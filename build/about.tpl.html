About this archive

This is an unofficial, volunteer-run collection of public records from the Irvington Union Free School District (Irvington, NY). It brings records that are spread across several district systems, some of them no longer online, together in one place with full-text search. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the district.

What's here

  • BoardDocs: every Board of Education meeting posted on the district's BoardDocs site since December 2011, with agendas, agenda item text, minutes and attachments. Scanned attachments were OCR'd.
  • Board meeting videos: machine transcripts of every recorded board meeting on the district's YouTube channels, with presentation slides pulled from the video and OCR'd.
  • District website: pages and documents from irvingtonschools.org.
  • Internet Archive: pages and documents from the district's earlier websites, as preserved by the Wayback Machine.
  • PTSA PAC minutes: public Parent Advisory Committee minutes posted by the Irvington PTSA.

Accuracy

Text here was extracted automatically. Meeting transcripts are machine-generated and contain errors, especially in names and numbers. Slide text and scanned documents are OCR output. Every page links to its official original (BoardDocs, the district website, a dated Internet Archive snapshot, or the YouTube video at the exact timestamp). Check the original before you quote or rely on anything.

Updates

The archive is refreshed regularly as new board meetings are posted. Each page shows the date of the record it came from.

Corrections and removal requests

These are public records from public meetings. If a page contains personal information that shouldn't be here, such as a student's private details, or you spot a transcription error that matters, please open an issue and it will be fixed or removed.

Data

All the extracted text is in the GitHub repository (data/text/ plus data/catalog.jsonl) for anyone who wants to search or analyze it offline.
