Current Practices
In an effort to provide for safe learning environments, IUFSD:
• Has comprehensive emergency plans that are developed with a security consultant
• Plans reviewed annually with security consultant and IPD
• Regular emergency drills are facilitated in each school
• Lock down, lock out, shelter in place, etc.
• Administrators participate in tabletop drills - February 1, 2018 with IPD
• 5 security guards
• One per school and one roving guard
• All staff members wear ID badges
• Buzzer systems at Dows Lane and Main Street

New Middle School Special Class
To best serve the needs of our students and to continue to educate them in
Irvington, a new special class model is being developed. The class will have one
full time special education teacher and the core content areas will be taught by
content certified staff in a Co-teach model.
The benefits of this class include:
Expanded ability to serve student needs in District
Creates flexibility for evolving needs of current students or those new to the District
• Offer a departmentalized learning experience with content expertise
• Students will participate in unified arts with peers
Offers an education in the Least Restrictive Environment (LRE)
