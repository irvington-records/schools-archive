Our Vision for Tomorrow
To achieve our vision, we will:
Provide students with a rigorous, comprehensive, enriched and diversified curricula
that will prepare students to achieve their personal best, and will integrate
technology in their learning.
Support educators through targeted professional learning and opportunity for
collaboration
Encourage innovation, creativity and risk taking to inspire a dynamic learning
environment
Foster the creation of a culture where students are active participants in society.
Strengthen local connections to and ownership of our schools.
* Ensure the fiscal health of the district and provide for a high quality learning
environment.

[02:10:38] 02-10-38.png
Transportation Options
Reviewed alternatives to improving pickup times without adding buses
• Scenarios included changing school start times to create route efficiencies
and to combine runs for K-5 and 7-12
• One option to start HS/MS at 7:25, Dows 8:25 and MSS at 9:05
• Second option was 45-60 minutes later without combining schools on runs to keep HS at
7:50am
Implications
• K-5 and 7-12 together, Parent concerns
If HS/MS start at same time:
» Traffic concerns at Campus
* Impact on Student programs at MS/HS
* Impact on Athletics/Clubs
• Implications for bargaining groups on start times
• Alignment with private/special education school runs
• Reducing mileage limits
• Law is 2 miles for K-8, 3 miles for 9-12
• Irvington voters approved .25 miles for K-5, 1.25 miles for 6-12
• Would require voter referendum to make changes
