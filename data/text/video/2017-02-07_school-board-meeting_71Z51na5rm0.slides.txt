not A2250

Special Education Financial Trends
Conclusions
Special Ed spending has increased yet spending per pupil has leveled from investments in
special classes and ICT program
Changes in Student needs
• Budgetary concern for today and future
Aid ratio declining from state
Spending per pupil viewed on the percent change basis, while showing variability, has
declined and sometimes is lower than Regular Ed
• Reflects program strategies to keep students in district where possible
• Reflects vulnerability of new high cost students moving in to district
• Majority of spending is tuition and salaries
• Continue to maximize special classes and student ratios
• Find appropriate placements to avoid stipulation agreements where advantageous
• Utilize consortium where possible
• Maintain strong tracking of student costs for management of expenses and forecasting
• Review programs for effectiveness relative to costs
Ensure proper coding of expenses for better benchmark comparisons
Maximize state aid and obtain all revenue due
