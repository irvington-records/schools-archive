The Design Process & Project Cost Estimates
UN'
Design process includes cost estimating at 30%, 60% and 90% design phases.
Where are we relative to original projections (capital project budget)?
• Value Engineering/Design Modifications
• Look for materials with same functionality but lower cost
• Efficiency of design to reduce costs
• Bid Alternates/Use of Other Purchasing Sources
• Seek outside bids (using state contracts/purchasing cooperatives) to compare pricing; reduces
contractor overhead
• Provides flexibility for awarding bids based on allowable budget
• Modification of Project Scale
• Hold off on certain aspects of project until funding picture is solid
• Current examples include Shed and paving basketball court at Dows

Bid Process
YORK
UNION
• Upon SED approval of design plans, bid documents are prepared and published
by H2M Architects
• Bids are advertised in the newspaper and through REVPLANS
• Rev is a bidding document repository to place design plans (bid specs) and manage bids
• Conduct a contractor walkthrough during the bid preparation period
• Led by Triton Construction and H2M
• Open bids in public - facilitated via Zoom
• Low Bidders - vetting process led by Triton/H2M
• Bid specifications met? Insurance, bond, OSHA certifications, etc.
• Review of scope with low bidder
• Reference checks
• Recommendation to award/reject bid presented to B&G committee including
which alternates to award
• Must award to low bidder unless lawful disqualifying reason can be substantiated
• May reject all bids and start over
