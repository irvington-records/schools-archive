IRVINGTON
Required Daily Health Screenings
Kristopher Harrison
To provide for the health and safety of all school community members:
• Staff members will complete a daily health survey before reporting to work
• Parents will be required to complete a daily health survey prior to their child
leaving for school.
• Surveys will include:
• Temperature taking
• Symptoms survey
• Travel survey
• Attestation to accuracy of information provided
• Nurses will monitor health daily and will implement quarantine procedures
when necessary

Health and Safety in School
Kristopher Harrison
• All students and staff will wear masks
• Regular mask breaks will be provided
• Schools will periodically sanitize all high-contact surfaces
• Proper hygiene practices will be mandated
• Traffic control process will be implemented (hallways and stairways)
• Lunch will be served in classrooms where possible to limit interaction
• Alternate locations will be added for secondary meal distribution/eating
• Meals will be available for remote learners
• Hand sanitizing and hand washing stations will be added throughout
each school
• Visitors to schools will be limited
virtually when possible
