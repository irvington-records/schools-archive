Understanding the Tax-Levy Cap
The Tax Levy Cap IS NOT a flat 2% !
It is a formula that includes:
• Allowable CPI (inflation) as set by NYS Comptroller
• Tax base growth factor
• Changes in other items: capital debt; PILOTS (Payments in
lieu of taxes); & excess increases in pensions

Demystifying the Fund Balance
• What is the fund balance?
• State allows 4% of budget to be retained by school districts
• End of year difference between expenses & revenue is closed to
fund balance
• How is it used?
o To cover emergency (unbudgeted) necessities
• Used for cash flow from July 1 until October (when District
receives tax revenue) to pay bills and salaries
What are the ramifications for using it?
• Not a sustainable source to balance budgets
o Could lower bond rating, resulting in higher interest rates
• District could receive "Fiscal Stress" designation from NYS
