Categories of Fund Balance
Fund Balance
• Unassigned Fund Balance
• Recommended amount is 4% of next year's budget
• Appropriated Fund Balance
• Funds designated for revenue for next year budget
• Approved in budget process
• Reserve for Encumbrances
• Incomplete orders at year end that carry over to
next year budget
• Amounts fluctuate from year to year
• Reserve Funds
• Restricted for specific purposes

Categories of Fund Balance
Current Reserve Funds
• Tax Certiorari
• Used to pay tax refunds on successful claims
• Capital Fund
• Used with voter approval for future project
• Worker's Comp
• Used for self-insured districts to pay claims
• Employee Benefits (EBALR)
• Used for contractual employee benefits upon retirement
More are allowed: ERS, Insurance, Repair, Property
Loss, Unemployment

Allowable Sources of Fund Balance
• Surplus resulting from expenditures not
exceeding revenues
• Borrowing
• Sale of building/land
• Unexpended capital project

[02:04:58] 02-04-58.png
Why Have Fund Balance?
• Office of State Comptroller/SED recommend
4% of next year budget as unassigned funds
as a measure of fiscal health
• Previously larger levels were allowed
• Cash flow - avoid borrowing for T.A.N. and
resulting interest costs
• Strong financial health leads to lower
borrowing rate for future capital projects

Tax Certiorari Reserve
• Currently at $1,336,831
• Money is added and subtracted each year
• In 2015-16, $2,528,165 was used and $873,515
was added
• Total exposure is $20,357,684 based on actual
filings but realistic exposure is approximately
$5-6,000,000 through settlements and court
proceedings.
• Reserve peaked in 2012-13 with addition of
$3,575,000 borrowing.
• Recommend maintaining current level or adding
to tax cert reserve as is possible to avoid future
borrowing.
