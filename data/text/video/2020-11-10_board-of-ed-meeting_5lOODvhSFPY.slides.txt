Varsity Philosophy
• Best players make the team regardless of grade level.
• Coaches select as many athletes as practical for their teams.
• Playing time and, sometimes, practice time may be disproportionate given the
competitive nature of varsity athletics.
• Varsity athletes are expected to demonstrate a higher level of skill and commitment,
and they should be prepared for a six-day-a-week commitment throughout
the season.
• Practices and competitions may be scheduled over a holiday, vacation
period and, on rare occasions, on Sundays.
• The varsity coach is considered to be the leader of the
program and is responsible for the communication and
connection with the junior varsity and modified programs.

Modified (Middle School) Philosophy
• Moditied programs provide an opportunity to learn new skills and test them in
competition.
• Cut vs No-Cut Policies
• Impacts playing time & level of competitiveness
• Increased demands on facilities & budget
• Modified coaches attempt to "play" all athletes while maintaining the integrity
and competitiveness of each competition.
• Exceptional 7th and 8th grade athletes may be considered for "playing up."
• Middle School athletes must qualify for high school athletic
programs after a screening process to determine physical maturity,
fitness and skill. (Athletic Placement Process)

The Athletic Placement Process (APP)
• The Athletic Placement Process (APP) is a program for evaluating students
who want to participate in sports at higher or lower levels.
• Parent/ Guardian Approval
• To initiate the process must provide written approval through FamilylD.
• Medical Evaluation
• Physical maturity is reviewed and determined by the District Medical Director, using Tanner
Scale ratings provided by private medical providers.
• Coaches Evaluation
• A skill evaluation form is completed by the coach of the team for which the
student desires to try out; evaluation includes consideration of past
observations of the student athlete and/or input from the student's
former coaches.

The Athletic Placement Process (APP)
• District Policy 7412 provides an opportunity to participate at an appropriate
level of competition based on readiness rather than age or grade with
certain restrictions listed below.
• No middle school athlete will be allowed to participate on a high school team if the
rosters are at capacity or if a high school athlete is cut.
• A 7th grade student will only be allowed to select and classify up to the varsity level
in non-contact sports that do not offer a JV program.
• 7th grade students may only classify up to the JV level in contact sports.
• 8th grade students may select and classify up to either the JV or
Varsity level upon meeting the State & district criteria.
