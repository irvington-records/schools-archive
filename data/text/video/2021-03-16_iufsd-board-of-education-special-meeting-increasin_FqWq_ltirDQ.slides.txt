Achieving Our Goal of Full Student Attendance
Planning, process and input played a critical role in developing a plan to
provide for a safe, academically sound return to full daily student attendance.
The recommendation will:
• Align with the three (3) tenets for planning:
• Vaccination of staff
• Access to surveillance testing
• Application of NYSDOH guidelines
• Provide for full in-person student attendance five (5) days/week in a phased
in approach
• Offer a revised 100% Distance Learning option
• Comply with all NYSDOH guidelines

Health, Safety and Mitigation
To ensure the health and safety of our students and staff:
• Social distancing will be enforced at a minimum distance of three (3) feet in classrooms
and six (6) feet in dining areas
• Desk barriers will be installed in all classrooms
• Mask wearing will be required at all times, except when one is eating
• Mask breaks will only be offered when necessary and will be outdoors
• Hallway traffic patterns will be adjusted
• Snacks will not be held in elementary classrooms
• Tents have been ordered and will be utilized to expand dining areas and provide for
outdoor learning spaces
• Contact tracing, quarantining and isolations will be enforced consistent with current
practices and DOH guidelines
• Surveillance testing will be conducted semi-monthly - twice a month - consent needed
• Arrival and dismissal procedures may be adjusted based on particular needs

Cafeterias and Lunchtime
I
To ensure that students are able to eat lunch in a safe space, changes will be
implemented in each school:
General Operations
• Pre-ordering lunches at the elementary schools will no longer be utilized. Students will obtain
lunch in the cafeteria or bring their own meal.
• Provide a minimum of six (6) feet of social distancing, utilizing desks and chairs.
• Ala carte snack offerings for elementary cafeterias will be phased in as staffing allows.
• Please deposit funds in student accounts - only needed for ala carte items. Breakfast/lunch
continues to be free of charge.
Dows Lane Elementary School
• Dining in either the cafeteria or gymnasium.
Main Street School
• Dining in either the cafeteria, auditorium or outdoors under tents.
Middle School/High School Campus
• Dining in either the cafeteria, the quad under tents or alternate locations.
• Flexible seating will be provided.

Surveillance Testing and the Use of Metrics
I
Testing Details
• BinaxNow RAT tests have been acquired through WCDOH.
• Tests to be administered by contracted service providers.
• Consent forms must be completed by parents.
• Appointments to be scheduled through a randomized process.
Testing Methodology
The District will facilitate surveillance testing semi-monthly. Every two weeks, 5% of the students and staff members
who are physically present on school premises will be tested. This means that a total of 10% will be tested monthly.
Evaluation of Surveillance Testing Data
The Irvington UFSD and the District Medical Director will review surveillance testing results on a semi-monthly basis
to evaluate the prevalence rates in the District and in each school. All testing data will be reported to the WCDOH
and NYSDOH on a semi-monthly basis.
The District rate of prevalence will be compared to the Westchester County rate or the Village of Irvington rate, whichever
is higher. If the Irvington UFSD rate is higher by 1% or more, the model of student attendance will be re-evaluated.
Illustration:
If the prevalence rate for Westchester County is 4% and the Village of Irvington is 5%, the rate for Irvington will be
applied. If the rate within the District or a single school was 6%, the District would implement measures to
mitigate potential viral spread.
Positive COVID Cases
Current District protocols, aligned with DOH guidelines, will be followed if/when an individual tests positive.

Phased In Increase of Full Student Attendance
The District will implement a phased in approach to increasing daily
student attendance:
1. IMS/IHS students with IEPs who are in ICT classes to begin four (4) days/week
attendance
2. Phase out full remote attendance on Flex Wednesdays
3. Phase out asynchronous learning on Flex Wednesdays
4. Phase in full daily student attendance by school campus

100% Distance Learning Option: Elementary
I
Elementary students will experience a "one room schoolhouse" approach:
• One half the students' day will be synchronous and one half asynchronous
• Three (3) certified, long-term substitutes will be assigned to provide scheduled,
synchronous instruction and support for learning
• Each long-term substitute will be responsible for remote students in two grades
(K-1, 2-3, 4-5)
• Grade-level teachers and instructional coaches will collaborate with the long-term
substitutes to ensure alignment between on-site and remote learning
• Cost of long-term substitutes:
• $7,625 Each
• $22,875 Total

100% Distance Learning Option: Secondary
I
Secondary students will experience a revised learning experience than in the
current hybrid model:
• Live stream of classroom instruction will continue for remote students
• Teachers will continue to use Google Classroom to communicate with students, post
assignments and provide learning resources.
• Teachers will continue to provide feedback to students:
• Live during class, during help sessions or by appointment
• Asynchronously through written feedback or with recorded feedback using Mote.
Please note that during class, teachers cannot provide the same level of personal
interaction while teaching with an increased number of students on-site.

Commitment to Location of Learning
Parents will be able to select location of learning, 100% in-person or 100%
remote:
• A commitment will be required for the balance of the 2020-21 school year
• Students will not be able to shift between modes of learning
• Hardships will be reviewed and evaluated by the Superintendent
• Students who are required to isolate or quarantine will be appropriately
accommodated
• 100% remote learning is not available for unexcused absences (vacation,
appointments, etc.)
• Remote student attendance is required and will be monitored
• Cameras must be turned on at all times

The Parent Pact
For the plan to increase daily, in-person attendance to succeed, to be
implemented in accordance with the timeline and for schools to remain open
in the proposed model, the home-school partnership is critical.
• Complete surveys ASAP - Data is needed to plan.
• Submit the COVID-19 surveillance testing consent form.
• Be transparent with COVID-19 exposures and travel plans.
• Follow all published guidelines regarding quarantine.
• Ensure students attend school, on time daily.
• Complete the Daily Health Screener..
Without this, schools cannot be open as proposed
• Be a partner and communicate!
