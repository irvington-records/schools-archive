Proposed Curriculum & Instruction Goal
Enhance curricula and instruction to promote academic excellence.
• Continue to align the existing curricula with the Common Core Learning
Standards (CCLS) and provide all educators with necessary professional
development;
Utilizing Rubicon Atlas curriculum mapping software, document
curricular shifts related to the CCLS;
Identify essential areas of curricular and instructional need and develop
plans for improvement:
Assess the use of technology and the related curricula to identify
opportunities to broaden and deepen student understanding and
application of information technology; and
• Improve curriculum communications with the community in order to
create better understanding of the changes in curriculum and to
encourage home and school partnerships.
