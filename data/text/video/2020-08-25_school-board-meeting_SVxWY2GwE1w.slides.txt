Proposal: K-1 Four (4) Half-Days/Week In-Person Learning
UNION FREE
SCHOOL DISTR
The District reconvened the Curriculum & Instruction Reopening Committee to evaluate the proposal for
Kindergarten and Ist grade to attend school for four (4) half days per week. The Committee determined
that it is viable to implement. Thus, with the Board's support, the K-l experience will be as follows:
This model will include:
• Students remain in two cohorts who attend separate AM & PM sessions
• Cohort A - AM session 8:00 am - 10:45 am
• Cohort B - PM session 11:45 am - 2:30 pm
• Students participate in on-site learning four (4) half-days:
• Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday
• Students also participate in asynchronous distance learning on the four days they attend school.
• Students will have a day of distance learning on Wednesdays.
Supported by administration and the K-l faculty, the model will provide for necessary continuity of learning
