We currently have 1 LMS for over a 1,000 students. This position has been
developed to support the needs of the library as well as a ripple effect in
technology and study skills. This position would allow for the increase of PLTW
and technology instruction at the MS and HS. Additionally, we would increase the
librarian staff at the Campus by .06 FTE. This person would also assume
responsibility for teaching a revamped study skills curriculum at the MS that will
focus on research skills, study strategies, and organization. This is a huge "bang
for the buck".
