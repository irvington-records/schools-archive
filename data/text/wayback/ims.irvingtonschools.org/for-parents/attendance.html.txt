Attendance
Absences or Late Arrivals: Lateness or absences should be reported to the attendance clerk at (914) 269-5313 or by sending an email to IMSattendance@irvingtonschools.org. Students who are absent from school can obtain homework information by checking Google Classroom. When students are absent for multiple days, arrangements can be made for a parent or sibling to pick up materials in the guidance office.
