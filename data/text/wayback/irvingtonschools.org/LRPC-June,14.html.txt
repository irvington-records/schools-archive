Irvington Board of Education
Long Range Planning Committee
Meeting No. 1: Minutes
June 14, 1999
- Don Marra opened the meeting by welcoming the attendees.  The committee members introduced themselves and stated their affiliation.
- Don Marra reviewed the Board of Education’s charge to the committee to analyze the challenges the District is facing as a result of anticipated growth in the student population.  He reported on the work that has taken place to date or is currently underway, including:
  - retaining Peter Gisolfi Associates as the architects for the Master Plan;
  - a preliminary traffic study at all three District Schools;
  - analysis of sites and buildings;
  - analysis of the amount of space used at each school and needed to meet projected enrollments.
- Dr. Vincent Beni summarized the Options that were presented in the Report of the 1998-99 Long-range Planning Committee to the Board of Education.
- Don Marra reviewed the enrollment projections.
- The architects reported on the status of the traffic study, buildings and site analyses, development of base site plans and building plans.
- The architects presented a draft of a document in which space requirements for "hypothetical" schools were compiled.  These lists of spaces were for a K-3 school, a Grade 4 to 5 school, a 6 to 8 Middle School, a 9 to 12 High School, and a 6 to 8/9 to 12 school which would share a common site.  These generic space requirements were then compared to the space which is currently available in the District.  The architects showed how the projected growth in the student population would impact the existing space throughout the District.
- The following schedule for additional Long-range Planning Committee meetings was set:
  - July 8th at 8:00 a.m. at the Middle School.  A tour of the school will take place after the meeting.
  - July 29th at 8:00 a.m. at the High School.  A tour of the school will take place after the meeting.
  - August 24th at 6:30 p.m. at Dows Lane.  A tour of the school will take place at 6:00 p.m.
