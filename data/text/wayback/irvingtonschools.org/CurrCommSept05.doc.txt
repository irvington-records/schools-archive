Curriculum Review Cycle

Ralph distributed the Curriculum Review Cycle for 2005-2006.  Attached to that sheet was the question template which was designed last year to guide each stage of the review cycle process.  Administrators, grade level leaders and chair people should use the template to guide their stage on the cycle review.


Textbook Selection Policy/Course Approval

Ralph redistributed the newly approved/adopted Board policies (6/7/05).  He told Committee members that these policies must be followed for future course approvals and book approvals.  Everything must be presented to the Curriculum Committee.

To be continued at the next meeting.

	Presenters:

a.	Corinne Daniels – Presentation on adoption of Open Court Phonics 
program at Dows Lane School.

Andrea Grundstein – Presentation on the designing of PAF materials for 
Grades 1-5.  The Kindergarten handwriting program will continue to be 
“Handwriting Without Tears.”

Questions:
When will fifth grade teachers get their books?
What is the expectation for fifth grade teachers?
Could Andrea make books available to parents whose children 
may need additional practice and assistance?

Ralph and Andrea responded to these questions.

Judy Pedi – Presentation on Library/Media/Technology curriculum for 
Grades 1-3.

Questions:
How will we be sure that the children receive an age appropriate aligned introduction to the research process.
How can we make sure that class time is still available to the 
classroom teachers in the computer lab? 
How can we be sure that the students are receiving instruction 
in the same programs that they did before the introduction of this 
special?

Ralph and Judy answered the questions and will consider next 
year’s lab usage schedule.  Ralph asked Judy to touch base with Jennifer DeLisi.




Carol LaBella – Presentation on adoption of Houghton-Mifflin spelling

Other

Joe Rodriguez spoke about the positive experience that the DLS staff and students had with the Math Trailblazers program.  Anne Schaeffer will continue to provide K-4 teachers with program support.

Christine Rosner will continue to provide push-in and pullout extended learning opportunities for students in Grades 3 and 4.

Eleanor Livesey has been hired to continue differentiated instruction for the elementary math representatives and secondary math teachers.  She will be meeting the secondary teachers on September 29th and the elementary teachers on October 6th.
