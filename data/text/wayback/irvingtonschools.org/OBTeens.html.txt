SOURCE http://irvingtonschools.org:80/OBTeens.html | DATE 2003-12-24 | TITLE The View On Board: Teen Drinking

Every one of us who cares about the children of our community worries about the ongoing challenge of teen drinking. We recognize that teenage alcohol and drug abuse is a widespread problem, and that our kids are as vulnerable as any of our neighboring communities. While there are many theories as to why this happens, there is no definitive answer. We do know that there are things our community can do, working together, to provide our teenagers with information, options and support in making healthy decisions. Your Board of Education is committed to being part of the solution, in coordination with the Village and other community organizations.
Recently, the District has added to its school psychologist staff, revamped the District Code of Conduct, reviewing the High School Athletic Code of Conduct and has committed to insuring strict adherence to that Code. A wide variety of school programs are dedicated to helping children make responsible decisions. For instance, the District and the PTSA are co-sponsoring the upcoming (Monday, February 10) workshop "Teenage Parties: Every Parent's Responsibility" featuring the acclaimed video "Dying High, Teens in the ER" and guest speaker Jeanine Pirro, Westchester County District Attorney. Don't miss this very important evening!
Other ongoing District programs include: ROADD ("Reduce our Adolescent Drinking, Drugging and Driving" mandatory for all students grades 10-12 including a parental workshop), Tobacco Education Cessation program, presentations to parents and students by Michael Nerney (a top drug education instructor) and the joint Village-District DARE program (Drug Abuse Resistance Education program for 4th and 8th graders).
The School District has also partnered with the Village of Irvington on a variety of cooperative ventures to give our children options. In addition to the summer "Open Air Center" Program, held nightly in the Middle School Parking Lot, the District has been an active participant in the Community Advisory Board (CAB), chaired by Village Trustee Isabel Milano. In late 2002, the District upgraded its participation on the CAB to Board Committee status with Trustees George Huzinec and Diane Mundree representing the Board of Education. The District is also represented by Superintendent Dr. Vincent Beni, High School Principal Dr. Scott Mosenthal, Middle School Principal Lauren Allan, High School Assistant Principal Joseph DeGennaro, Middle School Assistant Principal Raina Kor, and Main Street (Grades 4-5) Principal Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker. Other CAB participants include the PTSA, Police and Recreation Departments, IEF, Athletic Association, and representatives of the clergy. The Mission of the CAB is to "Coordinate the various community resources and activities to specific segments of the population with special emphasis on helping our children and adolescents grow up healthy, happy, and responsible."
At a recent Board of Education Meeting, the CAB announced an important first step in developing a plan to address the needs of our children. During the week of December 9, the CAB conducted a survey of our teens (Grades 6-12) using a questionnaire on attitudes and behaviors developed by the Search Institute. This anonymous and voluntary survey is designed to provide information on how our teens spend their time, their perceptions of community life and their participation in a wide range of risky behaviors. The survey results will be made public later this winter and will assist to establish priorities and design strategies for programs and services that will ultimately contribute to the healthy development of our kids.
Insuring the safety of our children is a top priority of the Board of Education, our District's Administration and Staff. By partnering with the Village, community and parents, we hope to make great strides in meeting this all-important goal. As always, the Board welcomes your feedback.
