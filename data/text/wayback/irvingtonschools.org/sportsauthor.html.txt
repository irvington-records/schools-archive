Students at the Main Street School got into the "sports" spirit recently when they greeted well known sports author Dan Gutman wearing sports jerseys and carrying their own copies of Gutman's books to autograph.
"The students really enjoyed the visit and came away with a great sense of what it takes to be a professional writer," said Main Street School Principal Don Kuhn. "Our students were energized by his visit."
Gutman, the author of more than 40 books including, "On the Field With Derek Jeter," "The Kid Who Became President" and "Babe Ruth and the Ice Cream Mess," to name a few, gave students tips on how they too can become "young authors" and better writers. "When you write a report for school, it's no different than what I go through when I write a book such as "Gymnastics" or "Baseball's Greatest Games," Gutman told students, as he talked about the important "research" process any writer must go through before starting a book.
"Maybe someday I'll be reading your books," added Gutman.
This program was sponsored by the Irvington PTSA.
