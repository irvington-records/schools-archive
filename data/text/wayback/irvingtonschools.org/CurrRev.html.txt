IRVINGTON SCHOOL DISTRICT
Curriculum Review Cycle
This Cycle has been adopted by the Irvington Board of Education to help guide curriculum
review for the next several years.  The plan directs our efforts by specifying which areas
should be reviewed each year.  One of the goals of establishing a cycle, a practice common to
many districts, is to help focus the efforts of staff on a limited number of areas each year,
and to keep professional development activities related to the areas of focus. Additionally,
the budget each year will reflect the recommendations of the Committee and the Board to
support specific curriculum areas.
