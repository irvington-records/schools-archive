Irvington Athletic Association
Update: May 2001
The IAA is currently in the midst of our 2001 Scholarship Fundraiser: a $10,000 vacation of a lifetime.  You decide where, when and for how long - After all, its your vacation!  Only 300 raffle tickets are to be sold at $100 each.  Imagine your dream vacation for just a $100 donation!  If you don't use all of the $10,000, don't worry: the balance remains for future use (there is no expiration date).  The raffle drawing is June 4th at the Senior Sports Banquet.  At that time, the IAA will also announce the 6 IAA Scholarship Winners funded by the raffle.  Remember that the raffle is limited to 300 tickets so don't get shut out!  To purchase your raffle ticket, contact any IAA board member or call Rick Rasulo at 591-5693.  Be sure to leave your name and number and someone will get back to you.
The IAA is also looking for new members.  We continue to serve our community through involvement in events such as the O'Hara Tournament and the Homecoming.  But these events require a great deal of help.  By giving as little as a few hours a year, you can make a big difference to the children of our community.
As always, the IAA urges the Irvington community to attend student athletic events, and give your support to our student athletes!
