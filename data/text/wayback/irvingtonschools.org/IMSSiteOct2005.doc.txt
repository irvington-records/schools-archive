SITE COMMITTEE MINUTES
Meeting Date:  10/18/05

Attendance:  Allicia Rios, Tracy Coulson, Scott Fried, Bridget Curran, Tina Savin, Stacey Kornfeld, Dorothea Marcus, Mary Saliani, David Zwiebel

Purpose of Committee – Scott Fried reviewed the purpose of the committee for new members and to clarify for existing members.  
A building committee that focuses on academics and global issues of a school, which includes parents, teachers, and administrators, is mandated by the state of New York.  Last year the site committee worked on an Academic Eligibility Policy for Athletics at the Middle School.  The focus of the committee is on projects to promote academic success.  

IMS ACTION PLAN – Scott introduced the Irvington Middle School Action Plan to parent members.  The Action Plan describes how the middle school building is going to implement the district’s Strategic Plan over the course of the year.  There are four goals in the plan and representatives from the building are serving on district committees focusing on each goal.  Teachers this year have also incorporated the one of the goals into their own professional teaching goals. Action Plan Goals:
ELA
Success of all students
Character Education – actually is being implemented this year where enduring understanding of the program Social Decision Making & Problem solving is infused within the curriculum.
Technology – use of Edline program as a means for communication with community and families
Buildings and Grounds

EDLINE – Scott reviewed the new program that the middle school is using which will eventually replace the IMS web page.  Each grade level has a page that is monitored by grade level leaders. The expectation this year is that all teachers will use the calendars on edline to post tests and projects.  By November, a middle school home page will be up and running.  At this time, teachers are not mandated to have a page for their classes.  Along with edline is a grading program called Intergrade Pro, which is replacing Think Wave.  It is a computerized grade book that allows teachers to post grades on line.  By the end of first quarter, all teachers using it will be trained and grades should be accessible on line.  

Parent suggestions for Middle School main page:
Extracurricular schedules
Mini-session dates
A day/ B day on calendars each month

FOCUS for the 2005-2006 school year – The Committee brainstormed some ideas that could be focus this year.  Some other concerns came up including the 6th grade Study Skills class and elimination of study halls at the 8th grade level, having parental input in the strategic plan/ or IMS Action Plan (character education goal).  One proposed idea to focus on this school year was the under use of the planner that each student purchases.  Currently, they are only being used for recording assignments, but can include useful tools for students like steps of the research process, editing marks resource, code of conduct, etc.  Members of the committee are encouraged to brainstorm ideas to focus on this school year to share at the next meeting Tuesday, November 15.
