The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library, April 23, 2003, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg,  Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman – Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mrs. Lauren Allan – Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane 
Gr. K-3 Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor – Middle School Assistant Principal, Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar – Director of Technology, members of the faculty and community. 

I  	CALL TO ORDER

The Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Janice Silverberg at 7:35 p.m. and the pledge of allegiance was given.

II	INFORMATION ITEMS

Dr. Beni reported that a trade union is “informational” picketing at the construction site.  There is no interruption to the school day or student activity.  Letters have been sent to each of the contractors at the site to put them on notice to meet their contractual obligations to the school district.

III	COMMENTS FROM RESIDENTS - None

  IV	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

A.   2003-04 Budget Adoption (Supplemental Log – Appendix A)
Dr. Beni stated that he was pleased with the budget which provides for a 9.6% tax increase and to recommend it’s adoption by the Board of Education.  Mrs. Silverberg reminded everyone of the amount of time, information gathering and meetings required to produce the proposed budget and that the Superintendent and his staff should take great pride in a job well done.   

A motion was made by Mrs. Diane Mundree, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to adopt the following Resolution:

“RESOLVED, that the Irvington Union Free School District’s 2003-2004 proposed budget be set at $35,388,153 and that this proposed budget be voted on by the community at the Annual Meeting on May 20, 2003 between the hours of 7:00 A.M. and 9:00 P.M. at the Irvington Middle School."
Vote:  5-0 Approved

Mr. Mandel noted that he is very proud that the Board has provided the incentive for teachers to pursue the difficult process of earning a National Teacher Certification as evidenced by Susan Dillon on tonight’s Personnel Agenda.  

B.   Policy # 5621:  Accounting of Fixed Assets – Second Reading
Policy discussion was tabled until the next Board Public meeting.

V           BUSINESS ITEMS

Construction Bid
A motion was made by Mr. George Huzinec, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany, to adopt the following Resolution:
“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the Flooring Bid -Contract No. HSNR1 to Elite Floors Inc. as the lowest responsive and responsible bidder with a base bid price of $771,000.00.”
Vote:  5-0 Approved
