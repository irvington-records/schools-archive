The following are the minutes of the Special Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the District Office Conference Room, August 26, 2003, with the following present:


Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Janice Silverberg and Mr. George Huzinec. 

I	CALL TO ORDER 

	The Special Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Penny Delany at 5:30 p.m. 

A motion was made by Mrs. Diane Mundree to adjourn to Executive Session for discussion of the employment history of a particular person, seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel.

The public meeting was reconvened at 9:00 p.m. by Mrs. Penny Delany

II       	ADJOURNMENT

	A motion was made by Mrs. Diane Mundree to adjourn at 9:00 p.m., seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel
