Administration Recruitment/Reorganization
Dear Parents/Guardians,
I am extremely pleased to announce to you the results of our administrative recruitment and reorganization efforts which have been planned throughout this spring. The district has certainly added to and expanded the strengths of our current leadership team. The result will be a more sharply focused approach to our curriculum and instructional needs on a K-12 basis. I am extremely proud of the individuals who have chosen to join our district.
New to the district are:
Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman will be the new Assistant Superintendent for Instruction. She will have overall responsibility for ongoing curriculum and staff development programs. Dr. Dolan-Waldman has extensive experience as a Math teacher, Middle School Principal and Central Office leader with significant involvement in the areas of curriculum, instruction and assessment. Dr. Dolan-Waldman currently serves as the Director of Professional Development and Technology at Southern Westchester BOCES and is a member of the New York State Commissioner's Staff and Curriculum Development Network.
Ms. Lauren Allen will be the Middle School Principal. Ms. Allen has been a teacher and administrator at the elementary, middle and high school levels. This former foreign language teacher currently is completing her eighth year as Principal of the Carrie E. Tompkins School in Croton-on-Hudson, having previously been the Assistant Principal at the George Fischer Middle School. She has also been a member of the prestigious Annenberg National Principals' Institute and the National School Reform faculty.
Mr. Joseph DeGennaro will fill the newly created Assistant Principal role at Irvington High School. Mr. DeGennaro currently serves as the Athletic Director and Interim Assistant Principal in the Hastings School District. He brings a wealth of experience with student matters and is an accomplished health and physical education teacher.
I am also pleased to announce expanded roles for the following members of the leadership team.
Dr. Dolores Garcia will be the Principal of grades 4 and 5 at Dows Lane and will have the overall responsibility for planning the move of these grades to the Main Street School in 2003. Creating this role now will enable us to return to a smaller school atmosphere at Dows Lane during the transition period. As a result, our children, staff and parents can work together to insure the new 4-5 Main Street School gains its own identity.
Ms. Susan Levey, currently the Director of Special Education, will now be the Director of Pupil Personnel Services, with the overall responsibility for the supervision and planning of all of our Academic Intervention Services, including Special Education, English as a Second Language, Guidance, Psychologists, and other academic support services.
This outstanding group of leaders will continue to work with Mr. Joseph Rodriguez (Dows Lane Principal Grades K-3), Dr. Scott Mosenthal (High School Principal), Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar (Director of Technology), Mr. Don Marra (Assistant Superintendent for Business) and myself as part of the district's Leadership Council. Along with our outstanding faculty and staff, we remain committed to insuring the best possible programs for your children.
I wish to express my gratitude to the parents who served on the interview committees and thanks to all of you for your wonderful support of our bond and budget this year.
Sincerely,
Vincent T. Beni
Superintendent
