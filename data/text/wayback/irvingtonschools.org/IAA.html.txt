Irvington Athletic Association
MISSION STATEMENT
The Mission of the Irvington Athletic Association is to build and enhance the athletic programs for the students of our community. We believe that a healthy sports program is the cornerstone of a community, building pride, civic engagement, and physical, mental and moral well being.
GOALS
We, the Irvington Athletic Association, are committed to supporting and improving student sports programs in Irvington by seeking to:
  - Work in partnership with the Irvington School District and the Village of Irvington Recreation Department.
  - To enhance community support and spirit for all athletic programs.
  - Promote good sportsmanship and encourage high standards of performance and conduct on and off the field, inclusive of players, coaches and spectators.
  - Establish a strong volunteer base.
  - To keep the community well informed about our sports programs schedules events and athletic accomplishments.
