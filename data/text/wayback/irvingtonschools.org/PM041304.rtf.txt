VII	PERSONNEL

	A.    RESIGNATIONS:

Vincent T. Beni – Superintendent of Schools – Effective 8/10/04 consistent with a letter of resignation dated April 13, 2004. 

Mrs. Delany reviewed some highlights of Dr. Beni’s contributions to the District while serving as Superintendent.  His entry plan included creation of a new curriculum review cycle, hiring of an Assistant Superintendent of Instruction, Master Plan for facilities, hiring a Superintendent of Buildings and Grounds, 5 Year Plan for financial projections, new Department Chair positions, hiring of Assistant Principals for each building, new and revised curriculum and staff diversification.  Mrs. Delany described Dr. Beni as a Superintendent with consummate administrative skills and thanked Dr. Beni for leaving our students with a wonderful school setting.  

Dr. Beni commented that the Board had a vision for the District and that a facilitator was needed.  The Board has accepted this challenge and the Administrators have taken it on.  Dr. Beni added that the District’s teaching staff rivals any in Westchester County with a very supportive community as demonstrated by their overwhelming support of the $47 million dollar bond vote in 2000.  Dr. Beni thanked the Board and the community saying that it was a privilege to serve in Irvington.  

Mr. Mandel said that it has been an honor to work with Dr. Beni, who came to the District in difficult times, and that he will never forget the solid planning that has taken place.  Mrs. Mundree added that Dr. Beni has done an incredible job and has accomplished everything that was outlined in the “search” brochure when he was hired.  Mrs. Silverberg thanked Dr. Beni for his remarkable accomplishment and his professionalism.  

A resident, Mrs. Carol Gutman, said that, with the hiring of Dr. Beni, the District “got the whole package” not just the new buildings and Master Plan implementation.

A motion was made by Mrs. Janice Silverberg, seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel, to accept Dr. Beni’s resignation.
Vote:  4-0
