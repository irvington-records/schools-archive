Irvington High School's Academic Challenge Team finished third and fourth in a recent county-wide tournament held at Horace Greeley H.S. in Chappaqua. Irvington High School's "A" team finished third and its "B" team finished fourth in the rigorous competition held on March 6th, when 26 different teams from area high schools competed against each other.
Irvington H.S.'s team is advised by Latin teacher Michele Cella. Team members include: captains Lee Stein and Brian Adelman and students Sam Abbott, Seth Feinstein, Eugene Petracca, David Imamura, Jeremy Neuberg, Sam Gordon, Gideon Mausner, Justin Eskind, Michael Brancato and Jeff Finkelstein.
