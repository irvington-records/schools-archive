Annual Report
Mission
The mission of the Irvington Education Foundation is to enhance the educational environment throughout the Irvington Public Schools by engaging the private support of citizens, parents, students, alumni, foundations and employers for the purpose of awarding grants to support special projects within the schools which significantly augment the school curriculum, but which cannot be paid for out of operating funds.
Message from the Board of Directors
Thanks to the exceptional generosity of the Irvington community, the Irvington Education Foundation is entering its fourth year and is playing an increasingly important role in supplementing the curriculum at all three of our schools. In partnership with our faculty, who submit grants for funding, we have supported programs in cultural arts, applied arts, language arts, science, math, technology, architecture, athletics and equipment procurement - and in the process touched every Irvington School District child in some form or another. As we enter the new century with an approved bond for a new Middle School, refurbished High School and plans for the reconfiguration of Dows Lane, we need to be creative and forward thinking. Our vision is to retain the best of the past while insuring that our children are exposed to cutting edge technology and the most innovative educational programs available to us.
Since its inception, the Irvington Education Foundation has granted over $235,000 to the District. This has enabled us to fund the return of the Irvington High School musical production for the last four years. It has given us the ability to include every 8th grade student in the award-winning National Geographic Jason project - an interactive environmental experience. We have funded a district-wide web-based weather monitoring and reporting curriculum that has encompassed math and science fundamentals in an innovative approach. Our elementary school students have been exposed to writing workshops, multi-cultural educational programs, habitat study and expanded interactive science, art and computer programs. Grades 3 and 5 incorporated math, science and team building skills into the construction of a life-size seaworthy craft that was auctioned off at this year's gala. And this is just a small sampling of the close to eighty grants funded from 1997-2000.
Our third successful fundraising gala, "Swing Into The Millennium" was held in November 1999 and raised an unprecedented $108,000. The remarkable support of this community has allowed this event to triple in both attendees and funds raised - allowing us to underwrite more innovative programs and broader initiatives.
But we've only just scratched the surface. Our goal, with your help is to keep pace with the rapid growth that our schools have experienced. To do that, now more than ever, we need the community's support.
