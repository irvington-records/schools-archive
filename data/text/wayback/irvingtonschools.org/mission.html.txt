Irvington Board of Education
Mission Statement 2000-2001
 
1999-2000 was a watershed year for the Irvington School District.  The Irvington community demonstrated its support for public education by overwhelmingly approving the $47 MM Master Plan bond proposal.  This plan, when completed, will ensure that we have the quality facilities we will need to educate all of our children in the 21st century.  In addition, last year we continued assembling a quality administrative team, implementing our multi-year technology plan, analyzing our long-term financial position and making progress in assisting our students to meet the new NY State assessments and graduation requirements.
For the 2000-01 school year, the Board of Education will continue to address the challenges that face our District.  In doing so, the Board will establish four sub-committees: Curriculum, Buildings and Grounds, Finance, and Community Relations.  Each of these sub-committees will have charges and action plans that will be periodically reviewed by the Board.  In addition, the Board as a whole will oversee matters relating to land use, personnel, negotiations and litigation.
This year, the major focus of the Board will be on instruction and curriculum development.  At the same time, the Board will continue to review the development of the Master Plan.
The unifying themes that will characterize all our efforts this year will be:
  - Educational Excellence
    - Assist all students to meet the new graduation requirements.
    - Continue implementation of our multi-year technology initiative.
    - Begin an analysis of other program needs impacted by our increased student population.
  - Policy
    - Initiate a multi-year review of District policies.
  - Communications
    - Maintain a high level of communications in order to continue the public's strong support for the Irvington School District.
