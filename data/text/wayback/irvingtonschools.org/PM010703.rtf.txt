Vote:  5-0 Approved

III	INFORMATION ITEMS

	Dr. Beni reported on the following:

Susan Dillon (Thorp) has been awarded the National Teacher certification.  She is the first Irvington Faculty member to earn this distinction and is one of five in Westchester County this year.  
The District has received an award of recognition from ASBO for  the Comprehensive Annual Financial Report (CAFR) for fiscal year ending June 30, 2001.
There is considerable focus in the region on the subject of teenage drinking.  Our students have completed a survey responding to questions regarding teen issues including drinking. 
The IEF is planning a fund raiser “sponsor a seat”.  This project would raise funds by “selling” seats in the new auditorium facility.  
Southern Westchester BOCES has been holding planning sessions with District staff for the three groups of students who will attend Irvington schools.  BOCES will be making a presentation at a future Board meeting.  
Compliments to our Administrative staff for their efforts in the transition planning process for the transition this year.

Mrs. Silverberg reported that the District web site will feature articles, etc. on current teenage issues.

  V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

Superintendent’s Report: 

Dows Lane K-3 Report – Mr. Joseph Rodriguez (Supplemental Log – Appendix A)
Mr. Rodriguez reviewed the work being done by the Kindergarten Committee.  The Committee is discussing the philosophy, length of school day and entrance cut-off date of the Kindergarten program.  Research and analysis is being done to examine current Kindergarten philosophies and practices.  A 



proposal being considered is a full day Kindergarten program beginning in mid-October of the school year.  

Mr. Rodriguez also reported on scheduling priorities for 2003-04 including the scheduling of large blocks of teaching time, scheduling consistency and common planning time.  

Another goal following the move of Grade 4-5 to the Main Street building, is to create more whole grade level activities, more cross grade level activities and to stress school wide themes emphasizing community and citizenship.

Following discussion, Mrs. Silverberg thanked Mr. Rodriguez for his report.  

Enrollment Projections 2003-04 – Dr. Vincent Beni (Supplemental Log – Appendix B)
Dr. Beni reviewed the enrollment for school years 1995-96 through 2002-03.  He also presented projections (low, medium and high) for the periods 2003-04 through 2010-11.  The District has grown within the projection estimates and it appears that the new construction will accommodate the student population for the foreseeable future.
