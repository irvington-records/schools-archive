Mrs. Janice Silverberg,  Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman – Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mrs. Lauren Allan – Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane

I	CALL TO ORDER

Mrs. Janice Silverberg called the Public Meeting to order at 7:40 p.m., and the pledge of allegiance was given. 

II	MINUTES OF MEETINGS

A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to approve the minutes of the Public Meetings of March 11 and March 25, 2003 and the Public Work Session of March 25, 2003.    
Vote:  5-0 Approved

The order of the Agenda was changed as follows:  

 V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

Superintendent’s Report:
1.  Recognition of National Merit Scholarship Finalists:  Ludi Chow, Tokufumi Noda, David Pomerantz

Dr. Beni welcomed the families and friende of the National Merit Scholarship finalists and remarked that these three (3) students are members of a very elite group of students nationwide.  Dr. Mosenthal introduced Ludi Chow, Tokufumi (Tommy) Noda and David Pomerantz.  Each student was presented with a framed certificate in recognition of their academic achievement.

2.  Recognition of Scholar Athlete Awards:  Girls Winter Indoor Track, Boys Winter Indoor Track,  Wrestling and Girls Basketball

Dr. Beni introduced Mr. McCormack for the introduction of the Girls Basketball Team, Boys Winter Indoor Track and the Wrestling Team.  Each of these teams have been recognized by New York State as having the second highest GPA in New York State with a team average of 90 or above.  

Mr. McCormack introduced Mr. Greg Ransom, Executive Director of Section One Athletics representing the New York State Public High Schools Athletic Association.  The Girls Track Team has been recognized by the NYSPHSAA as having achieved the highest team GPA (total of 167 teams) in New York State.  The team GPA was 98.917.  The team was presented with a banner suitable for hanging, a pin and a certificate for each of the team members.   

III	INFORMATION ITEMS 

	Dr. Beni reported on the following:
Meeting with state legislators in Albany on April 7, 2003 to discuss the unique situation in Irvington due to the increasing enrollment, cuts in state aid and certiorari claims.  
Thank you to the community for their participation in the Middle School career day last week.
There will be a march for public education in Albany on May 3, 2003.  Call the school district for information. 
Congratulations to the PTSA for a very successful International Night.





The results of the survey of Irvington teenagers has been received from the Search Institute.  There will be a discussion of the findings at a Board meeting in May or June.
Mr. Donald Kuhn will be named Interim Grade 4-5 Principal during Dr. Garcia-Blocker’s absence due to childcare leave.  
The April Spring Recess will not be shortened due to “snow” days.
The vote on the 2003-04 BOCES Budget and Board Member election will take place at the Board meeting of April 22, 2003.
Congratulations to the Board for presenting the Bond for a public vote.  The Bond proposition was approved by the voters as well as the proposition for transportation eligibility mileage reduction for Grades K – 5. 
Plans for the Main Street School roof replacement have been submitted to the State Education Department.

Mrs. Silverberg thanked the community for their support for the Bond proposition.   

IV	COMMENTS FROM RESIDENTS

Several residents commented on the need to maintain the multi-sensory program at Dows Lane Elementary.  Dr. Dolan-Waldman informed the group that the program will continue for next year.  The District will continue to evaluate the results.
