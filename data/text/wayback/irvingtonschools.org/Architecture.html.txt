SOURCE http://irvingtonschools.org:80/Architecture.html | DATE 2005-02-14 | TITLE Architecture Award

Irvington High School junior Christopher Macies placed first in the Architecture area of competition at the New York State Art Teachers Olympics for the Visual Arts, held recently in Saratoga, New York.
At the competition, where students competed from all over New York State, contestants were given a spontaneous architectural problem to solve in 1 and 1/2 hours. This year, the problem was to design a museum of Modern Art.
"It was a real challenge," said Christopher, who was the first Irvington student to participate in these Olympics. "The pressure was on, but I concentrated and did my best in the short time we had allotted to us." Christopher was also judged on his long-term project that consisted of designing a studio/residence for a famous architect or artist.
