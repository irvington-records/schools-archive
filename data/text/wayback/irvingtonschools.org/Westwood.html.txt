Westwood Agreement
**Reprinted here with the kind permission of the Irvington Viewpoint, first published in the October 1999 issue as a "School Matters" column, by Paul Mandel.
The Board of Education, in collaboration with the community, is finalizing a master plan to expand our school facilities. Most of the anticipated new construction will be on the High School campus. The Board has informed, and will continue to involve, residents. Much more will be communicated in this regard in the coming months.
There is an on-going issue affecting the High School campus, which predates our long range planning process. The Board is now pleased to announce an agreement with Westwood Development Associates that positively affects the safety and accessibility of the High School campus. This agreement is the result of years of discussion between the Village Planning Board, the school district administration, the developer and the community, and is clearly the best solution to a difficult and potentially divisive issue.
The background: Westwood is a proposed development to be located east of the High School. The proposal includes 16 homes on a tract of land on the hill above the High School football field. As the result of a 1982 lawsuit, this tract (and only this tract) was given the right of access over High School Drive. Over the years, concerns about safety led to exploration of an alternative access over "Link Road" (presently a paper road connecting upper High School Drive to Fieldpoint Drive). However, given the focus of the district's long range planning on expanding utilization of the High School campus, continued use of High School Drive as the access route to Westwood would appear preferable.
Safety has been and continues to be the top priority of the Board of Education. The agreement with Westwood Associates will improve the overall saefty of the campus and also assist in renovations of the roadway necessary for any building plan.
Key points of the agreement include: 1) Westwood will contribute $125,000 toward the improvement of High School Drive; 2) Westwood will re-construct the High School parking lot so that traffic will be routed along the north side of the existing lot rather than bisecting the parking lot as is currently done; 3) Westwood will widen the section of High School Drive located between the football and softball fields; 4) Westwood will construct a gated and unpaved emergency access road between High School Drive and Fieldpoint Drive.
The agreement provides the District with substantial funding to improve High School Drive to accommodate expanded facilities and additional staff and students. Significant improvements include: better drainage, the addition of speed bumps, and provision for emergency access should High School Drive be closed. In addition, pedestrians will no longer have to cross a road to get from the parking lot to the campus. These changes are especially important as we face increased vehicular and pedestrian traffic in the future.
There is more good news on the saefty of our campus: The New York State Department of Transportation finally granted the requests of the school district and the Village to install a traffic signal at the intersection of High School Drive and Broadway. This welcome addition will greatly improve traffic flow while reducing the risk of accidents at the intersection.
