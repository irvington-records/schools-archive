District Organizations
Several different groups make up the organization of our district. Some of these have official status, while others are voluntary. This summary aims to provide a simple outline of the responsibilities and roles of these groups, which maintain active liaisons among each other.
- BOARD OF EDUCATION:  The five members of the Board of Education have two major areas of responsibility:  policy and finance.  The Board sets, reviews and ensures implementation of policy for the district, holds bi-monthly public meetings, and presents a proposed school budget to the community each spring for the coming school year.  There is a public vote each May on the budget; at that time, members are elected to the Board for three year terms.
Contact: Penny Delany, Pres. 591-7039
- ADMINISTRATION: As required by NY State law, our district is headed by a Superintendent, and each of the three schools in the district has a Principal.  In addition, there is an Assistant Principal at Dows Lane, where enrollment is highest.  Other administrative staff includes an Assistant Superintendent for Business, a Director of Special Education, and a Technology Coordinator.  The Superintendent functions as the Chief Executive Officer of the district.  He or she reports to the Board and is responsible for implementation of policy and programs, while the Principals, who report to the Superintendent, oversee the day to day operation of their schools.
Contact: Superintendent Dr. Kathleen Matusiak 591-8501
- SITE COMMITTEES:  Each school has a site committee, which includes representatives of the faculty, parents, support staff, and the building Principal; at the High School, students also participate.  The Site Committees are mandated by State education law to address issues related to student achievement.  Parent members are elected each spring by the parent communities within each school. The Site Committees make annual reports, which may include specific recommendations, to the Board.
Contact: School offices for appropriate listings
- PTSA:  Our local Parent Teacher Student Association is part of a national volunteer organization whose overall mission is the enhancement of the welfare of children in our schools. As a service organization, the PTSA encourages parent and community participation in the schools through a variety of activities and events.  The PTSA also schedules programs each year aimed at informing and assisting parents, and enriching the experience of the students.  The district benefits from the support generated by PTSA activities for program and facilities enhancements. PTSA membership is open to all.	
Contact: Francie Malina 591-4126
- IRVINGTON EDUCATION FOUNDATION: The IEF was founded specifically to raise money to fund programs which enrich the curriculum but are beyond the scope of the school budget.  The Foundation, through its volunteer Board, solicits both individuals and corporations, via mailings and special events. The Foundation is eager to identify corporations with matching gifts programs or corporate foundations which support education.
Contact: Rhonda Billig 591-4916 or Roberta Samberg 591-5015
- IRVINGTON ATHLETIC ASSOCIATION: This group aims to support and enhance the athletic programs at our schools. The IAA, through volunteer activities, also seeks to increase community awareness of the value of athletics and participation in the sports program.
