IRVINGTON SCHOOL DISTRICT
Phase In of NYS Assessments
This information represents the planned changes in graduation requirements for students
throughout New York State.   The central point of these requirements is that schools are no
longer allowed to issued a "local diploma" reflecting local requirements.  Schools in every
district in New York are required to have students meet the same requirements for graduation.
As of June 2001, students must pass both the English and the Math State Regents exams in
order to graduate.  Next year there will be additional requirements as the State "phases in"
the new plan.  Part of the phase in includes starting with a lower passing score (55) and
then moving up to the expected standard score (65).  You will see notations about that in the
chart.  Each content area is targeted to come in at a different point; therefore, the chart
reflects a different pattern for each area until the students who are currently in 8th grade
reach graduation - 2005.  At that time, all students will be required to have completed 5
Regents exams at the 65 passing rate.  (There are some exceptions for Special Education
students.)
