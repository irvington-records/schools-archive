IRVINGTON SCHOOL DISTRICT
Central Office Move
Please be advised that on Thursday, July 1st the Irvington UFSD District Office will be moving from its' current location at 90 North Broadway to its' new location in the modular classroom buildings at Dows Lane.  To facilitate the move the District Office will be closed from noon on Wednesday, June 30th until Tuesday, July 6th.  District Office telephones and e-mail will not be in service during this time.  591-3036 will be available to report emergencies.
The main office in each school building will remain open June 30th through July 2nd.  The School District will be closed on Monday, July 5th in observance of the July 4th Holiday.  Principals and staff may be contacted in the usual manner.
