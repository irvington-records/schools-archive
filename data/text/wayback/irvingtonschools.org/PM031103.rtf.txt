The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library, March 11, 2003, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg,  Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman – Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mrs. Lauren Allan – Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane 
Gr. K-3 Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Dows Lane Gr. 4-5 Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor – Middle School Assistant Principal, Mrs. Susan Levey – Director Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar – Director of Technology, members of the faculty and community. 

I  	CALL TO ORDER

The Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Janice Silverberg at 7:06 p.m.

A motion was made by Mrs. Diane Mundree, seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel, to adjourn to Executive Session for the purpose of discussing the employment history of a particular person(s).
Vote:  5-0

The Public Meeting reconvened:

I	CALL TO ORDER

At 7:50 p.m. Mrs. Janice Silverberg called the Public Meeting to order from Executive Session, and the pledge of allegiance was given. 

II	MINUTES OF MEETINGS

A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to approve the minutes of the Public Meetings of February 4, February 11 and February 25, 2003 and the Public Work Session of February 25, 2003.    
Vote:  5-0 Approved

III	INFORMATION ITEMS

Mrs. Silverberg announced that on Saturday, March 8, 2003, Dr. Beni was honored by Fordham University as a distinguished alumnus.  Several Board and staff members attended the event. 

Dr. Beni thanked the Board, staff and community for the condolences and support that he received following the death of his father.

	Dr. Beni reported on the following:
Sample letters are available on the website for communicating with the Governor and State legislators regarding the proposed State budget.  
The PTSA and school district hosted a very successful meeting on March 4, 2003 concerning the issue of teenage drinking.
Thank you to the Village of Irvington for their assistance with the recent icing conditions on the High School hill driveway.
Ludi Chow, Tokufumi Noda and David Pomerantz have been named National Merit Scholarship Finalists out of 15,000 students nation-wide.
On March 25, 2003 the Superintendent’s Proposed Budget will be presented at a Public Board Meeting.  As of this date, the budget has not been finalized.
Reminder of the Bond Vote on April 3, 2003 for the purpose of restructuring the funding of capital projects.  There will also be a proposition to reduce the K-5 mileage eligibility for transportation.

Mr. Mandel commented that the students named as National Merit Finalists are “wonderful kids” who are 




achieving academically as well as being involved in music, athletics, peer leadership, etc.  He also remarked that the High School play, Babes In Arms, was a tremendous performance and very enjoyable.  Thanks were
 extended to the Village of Irvington for the use of the Irvington Town Hall Theater while the new High School Auditorium is being built.

IV	COMMENTS FROM RESIDENTS

Several residents commented on rumors regarding the 2003-04 budget, foreign language curriculum, class size, emergency evacuation and sheltering and Middle School transportation. 

  V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

Superintendent’s Report: 
Irvington Middle School Report – Mrs. Lauren Allan  (Supplemental Log – Appendix A)
Mrs. Allan and Ms. Raina Kor presented the Middle School update report by reviewing the seven (7) essential elements of middle level schools and programs including a detailed review of organization and structure, transition to the new school facility and the five year plan.

The transition planning to the new school will include tours for the faculty, students and the community.  The administrators of the Middle and High Schools are also working on schedule coordination.  

Ms. Kerry Moscato, Ms. Maria Tucci presented an overview of the Teachers College Writing Project.  Ms. Laurie Dyno discussed technology in the classroom.  

Mrs. Silverberg thanked Mrs. Allan and her staff for their presentation.
