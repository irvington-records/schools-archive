Letter to Parents from Superintendent Dr. Vincent Beni
Dear Parents and Guardians,
Thank you for your support and consideration during the recent crisis at Dows Lane. We are all saddened at the damage to the school.
This unfortunate incident, however, has brought to the forefront the many heroes and heroines who work to make the Irvington School District Community a special place. First and foremost, we must express our gratitude to our volunteer fire department. Their quick response ultimately saved our school from substantial damage. Their efforts were outstanding.
What would we have done without such an unbelievable faculty and staff? Their willingness to be flexible and roll up their sleeves enabled us to open school as quickly and efficiently as we did.
I must also congratulate the exceptional leadership of Joe Rodriguez, Lola Garcia and Don Marra (Assistant Superintendent) for the time and energy they devoted to ensure that our kids returned to the normalcy of the school as early as they did.
Finally, I must acknowledge the work of our construction management firm, JMOA Engineering, and Michael Orifici, its president. This group amassed an unbelievable amount of contractor support (more than 150 individuals) who cleaned and sanitized the building.
Now that two days have passed, we are able to assess the impact on our school. The cafeteria, administrative and staff offices, art room and music room will likely be out of commission for the rest of the school year. We are installing four new portable classrooms within two weeks to offset some of the program space loss. Two trailers will house the administration office. Joe Rodriguez will keep you apprised of decisions about necessary program changes.
Throughout the clean up, we were assisted by a safety and environmental consultant who was onsite for both days. She also visited the school on Wednesday to assess the situation. Her evaluation was instrumental in our decision to reopen the school.
The fire itself was limited to the cafeteria. There was extensive smoke in the ceilings of the two hallways in the original building. During the two day cleanup, the following steps were taken: all ceiling tiles were removed in the original building; all walls and ceilings in the original building were washed and sanitized; new ceiling tiles were installed in every classroom and the halls in the original building; all classroom materials including books, computers, furniture and supplies were dusted; all floors in the entire building were washed, mopped and polished; new carpeting was installed in some areas; the entire original building, including classrooms, was painted. In all, a monumental effort was undertaken to ensure that the children returned to a clean, safe school.
The kids' reactions on Wednesday were heartening. They were pleased to be back and amazed at the condition of their building. Some of you used the word "miracle" to describe what happened. I believe that it was the good will, cooperation, and commitment of several hundred people which restored Dows Lane to its normally inviting self for our kids.
Much work needs to be done to complete the repair and restoration of the campus. We are committed to doing that as expeditiously as possible.
Thank you so much for your outpouring of positive sentiment. We appreciate your support.
Sincerely,
Vincent T. Beni
