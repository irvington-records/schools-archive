 The Irvington School District is one of the fastest growing districts in New York State.
 The committee focused its attention on programs to prepare and support our district for
 this imminent expansion. One charge of the committee was to consider adding and
 developing interscholastic and extramural activities at both the middle and high schools.
 Additionally, we reviewed facility usage and needs as well as equipment and staffing.

The committee gathered information from similar districts in Westchester County. After
considering our district’s projected enrollment and facilities, the recommendations
included at the end of this report indicate what we believe will be necessary to enable
the district to provide the greatest opportunities for athletic growth for all our students. As
the needs of the district change, the committee will continue to look for solutions that will
enable us to serve the increasing school population.
                 DISTRICT ATHLETICS PHILOSOPHY


                 The philosophy statement below reflects the committee’s revision of the philosophy
                 statement developed by an earlier committee. The earlier statement is currently
                 included in our student handbook, but this revision will be included in future printings:


                        The aim of the athletic program is to engage students in activities that will
                        enrich their lives. The program should provide challenges and
                        opportunities for our students, regardless of social or economic
                        background. Through their participation, students will continually challenge
                        themselves in many ways.

                        Our athletic program is organized and administered with the welfare of the
                        individual in mind. It is conducted in the light of educational objectives that
                        cannot be compromised. It is based on the belief that athletic contests
                        should always be played with emphasis on developing social experience,
                        physical development, sportsmanship, and competitive skills. It is an honor
                        and privilege to play interscholastic sports and wear the Irvington uniform.
                        Therefore, students must behave appropriately and represent not only
                        themselves, but also their families, coaches, school, and community.

                        Students who choose interscholastic competition find great demand placed
                        on them. They are asked to sacrifice, to commit themselves to their teams,
                        to abide by the rules, to show discipline, and to exhibit sportsmanship.

B. Projected Additional Outdoor Facilities:

   It is projected that in the Spring of 2002 the Village of Irvington will open its
   Waterfront Park Field which will be used by Varsity, JV & Modified Softball, Modified
   Baseball, and possibly Soccer and Football.


C. Present Indoor Facilities:

   We are currently using the gymnasiums at Dows Lane, Middle School and the High
   School for a variety of interscholastic practices and games. These facilities are used
   to their full capacity.


D. Projected Indoor Facilities

   Upon the completion of the District’s Master Plan, there will be a new Middle School
   Gymnasium which will be incorporated into use as part of the athletic program.

The Athletic Director will continue to work with neighboring villages and schools to
secure the use of additional facilities, both indoor and outdoor, to supplement the
growing needs of our district. At present, the district uses facilities from both the Village
of Tarrytown and Mercy College on an as needed basis.
