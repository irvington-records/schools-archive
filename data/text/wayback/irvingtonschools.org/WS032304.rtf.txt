The following are the minutes of the Public Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Campus Presentation Room, March 23, 2004, with the following present:

Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mr. George Huzinec.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman – Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Ms. Raina Kor – Middle Interim School Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Main Street School Principal,  Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Elementary School Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Jacqueline O’Malley – Middle School Intermim Principal, Mr. Donald Kuhn – Main Street School Assistant Principal, Ms. Eileen McGuire – Dows Lane Elementary School Assistant Principal, Mrs. Sheralyn Gottlieb – Director Pupil Personnel Services,  faculty and members of the community. 

CALL TO ORDER

Mrs. Penny Delany call the Public Meeting to order at 6:43 p.m. and the pledge of allegiance was given.

Mrs. Delany welcomed everyone to the meeting which was scheduled for the appointment of the new Superintendent of Schools, Dr. Kathleen Matusiak.  Mrs. Delany said that she was thrilled with the successful search for a new Superintendent and the wonderful candidates who applied for the position.  Mrs. Delany commented that Dr. Matusiak is a wonderful fit for the District and that she has all of the abilities and qualities outlined in the profile that was created by the District. 

BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington 
Union Free School District hereby appoints Kathleen Matusiak 
Superintendent of Schools for a three (3) year period to commence 
July 11, 2004 through June 30, 2007 with a salary for the 2004-2005 
school year of Two Hundred Seventeen Thousand and No/100 
($217,000.00) Dollars subject to her receipt of New York State 
Certification to act as Superintendent of Schools or waiver of same 
from the State Education Department; and be it further 

RESOLVED, that the Board of Education hereby approves a contract 
of employment with Dr. Matusiak dated March 23, 2004 covering the 
aforementioned term and hereby authorizes the President of the Board 
to execute same.
 
A motion was made by Mrs. Janice Silverberg, seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel, to appoint Dr. Kathleen Matusiak as Superintendent of Schools, effective July 11, 2004.
Vote:  5-0 Approved

ADJOURNMENT

A motion was made by Mrs. Diane Mundree at 6:51 p.m., seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to adjourn.  The Public Work Session will follow at 7:30 p.m.

A welcoming reception was held in the Campus Library for Dr. Matusiak.  The Work Session convened at 7:46 p.m.
   
I             2004-2005 SUPERINTENDENT’S PROPOSED BUDGET - (Supplemental Log – Appendix A)

A.  Dr. Beni remarked that this is the 20th, and final, budget presentation of his career.  The Budget Vote and Board Member Election will be held on May 18, 2004 or June 8, 2004 if the legislature decides to postpone the vote.  

	The major factors affecting the 2004-05 budget are:
	
Bond payment for the new school construction
Special Education 
Health insurance


Transportation
Enrollment increase
New programs
Curriculum cycle
State Aid
Change in assessed value

Dr. Beni reviewed each of the major factors in detail.  The proposed budget provides for an increase of 3.6 staff members due to the rising enrollment and 4.9 to support proposed new programs.  The increase in staff will enable the district to maintain current class size.  The total budget is $38,450,252 which represents a tax increase of 9.48%.  Nearly 60% of the tax increase is devoted to instruction and curriculum.  
 
Key budget dates are:

April 13, 2004   - Instructional Budget Items
April 20, 2004   - Budget review (if necessary)
April 20, 2004 - Board budget adoption
May 4, 2004    - Budget Hearing
May 18, 2004  - Budget Vote

	B.  Mr. Reese presented the major areas of the Non-Instructional Budget:
	
General Support
Transportation
Community Service
Employee Benefits
Debt Service
Inter-Fund Transfers

This portion of the budget represents an 8.65% budget-to-budget increase.  Contributing to this are rising insurance costs, transportation cost increase of 18.5%, an 8% increased cost for employee benefits and the payment on the serial bonds for the Dows Lane 1993 addition and the community approved Master Plan.

Dr. Beni complimented Mr. Reese for his efforts in preparing this budget adding that this proposal moves the District ahead in it’s programs and facilities and maintains class size.
 
Following discussion and questions, Mrs. Delany thanked Dr. Beni, Mr. Reese and their staff for the presentation.

At 9:48 p.m. Mrs. Delany adjourned the Public Work Session.
