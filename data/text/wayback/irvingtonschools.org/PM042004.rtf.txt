VI	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

  2004-05 Budget Adoption
Dr. Beni began the discussion by saying that, by law, the Board must adopt a 2004-05 budget at this meeting.  No further adjustments to the Budget may be made after tonight’s meeting.  The Budget Hearing will be held on May 4, 2004.  

Following last week’s meeting, Scott Mosenthal has researched the questions presented regarding the hiring of an additional Guidance Counselor for the High School to be included in the 2004-05 budget.  From the data of districts surveyed, Irvington is currently about average in the ratio of number of student caseloads per guidance counselor.  Without the addition of an additional counselor, the District would be in the high end of the ratio range next year.  The budget impact to add a new counselor position would be an additional $95,000.  The student impact would be an increased amount of contact time between counselor and students allowing for more continuity and better connections.  The Board is unanimously in favor of adding a Guidance Counselor at the High School this year rather than waiting until next year.

A motion was made by Mrs. Janice Silverberg, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to adopt the following Resolution:

“RESOLVED, that the Irvington Union Free School District’s 
2004-2005 proposed budget be set at $38,545,252 and that this 
proposed budget be voted on by the community at the Annual Meeting 
on May 18, 2004 between the hours of 7:00 A.M. and 9:00 P.M. at 
the Main Street School.”

Vote:  5-0 Approved
