The following are the minutes of the Public Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library, September 24, 2002, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman - Assistant Superintendent Instruction, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mrs. Lauren Allan – Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Gr. K-3  Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Dows Lane Gr. 4-5 Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor - Middle School Assistant Principal, Mrs. Susan Levey – Director Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar – Director of Technology, George Kuntz - Superintendent of Buildings and Grounds, Michael Orifici - JMOA Engineering P.C., students and members of the community.

The Public Work Session was called to order by Mrs. Janice Silverberg 7:31 p.m. and the pledge of allegiance was given. 

1.  MASTER PLAN UPDATE

Mr. Michael Orifici reviewed three (3) key points at this point in the construction:
New construction in progress, or completed, is ahead of schedule.  Delivery of windows is scheduled for December and installation will begin.  The roofing has started as well as electrical, plumbing, HVAC and construction of wall studs for the Middle School and Cafeteria/Music/Science building. Concrete will be poured this week at the Theatre/Gym complex. 
The target dates ahead include the re-bid for the General Contractor for the High School Reconstruction. 
Reporting on the project financial status, the project is at 30% of completion.
Mr. Orifici noted that requests for Change Orders and issues arising from construction are lower than expected.  

A motion was made by Mr. George Huzinec, at 8:08 p.m., seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to adjourn the Work Session for the purpose of convening a Public Meeting to consider the Resolution to rescind and re-bid the General Construction Bid, HSR1.    

The Public Work Session was reconvened and called to order by Mrs. Silverberg at 8:10 p.m.

2.  Curriculum Cycle:  Summer Projects and Curriculum Implementation Plans

Dr. Dolan-Waldman reported on summer projects which the professional staff engaged in.  Among the topics addressed were the drafting of the K-12 Mathematics Curriculum and the World Language Curriculum.

The Curriculum Implementation Plan is in the second year of it’s cycle.  This year reviews will begin on Business Education, Art, Health, Physical Education and Science.

3.  Review of New York State Test Results (Supplemental Log - Appendix A)					

Dr. Dolan-Waldman reviewed the following:
The phase-in of the New York State Assessments.
Comparisons of four (4) years of English Language Arts (Grades 4 & 8) and Math (Grades 4 & 8) test scores.   
Review of a comparison of four (4) years of Regents results data.
The Board discussed how to use the test results to benefit our students.  Dr. Beni asked the Board and the public for feedback regarding getting this information out to parents along with information as to what the school district looks for, what action is taken and the follow-up to the analysis of the test scores.  

Mrs. Silverberg thanked Dr. Dolan-Waldman and everyone in attendance.  The meeting was adjourned at 10:21 p.m.
