New Senior Internship Program
- Seniors would take their final and/or Advanced Placement exams the week of May 15. They would intern from May 22 to June 14, working a minimum of four days per week (Monday-Thursday) and a minimum of five hours a day.
- Seniors would keep a daily timesheet (signed by the sponsor) and a daily journal to reflect on this experience.
- Seniors would be assigned a teacher mentor at the high school who would monitor their progress and serve as liaisons with the sponsors.
- Seniors would meet one day a week (Friday) at the high school to meet with their teacher mentors, write a weekly reflection, and share their experiences with other seniors.
- The mentors would contact their students’ sponsors weekly and conduct one site visit.
Dear Community Member,
It is with enthusiasm and anticipation that we write to you about a proposed program tentatively coming to Irvington High School—The Senior Internship. The purpose of the program will be to merge the energy and talents of our students with the experience and expertise of the workforce in our community. Our hope is that by combining careful, systematic organization from us with a willingness to provide an opportunity for meaningful work experience from the community, we will create positive, memorable learning experiences for both parties.
If you, or somebody you know, would be willing to sponsor a senior in this venture, you would be saying yes to giving one of our students a valuable opportunity—one they could possibly build on for the rest of their lives.
To be more specific:
The internship possibilities are endless. Obviously, we don’t want students to simply do busy work; we’re hopeful that the sponsors will make the internships as meaningful as possible given the short time frame.
Please join with us in this collaborative effort of school and community. If you are interested in sponsoring a senior, please call the high school principal’s office or return the attached sheet as soon as possible. Thanks!
Gratefully,
Scott Mosenthal
Principal, Irvington High School
