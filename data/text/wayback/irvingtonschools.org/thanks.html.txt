SOURCE http://www.irvingtonschools.org:80/thanks.html | DATE 2002-07-01 | TITLE Thank You

Second graders at the Dows Lane Elementary School in Irvington spent a recent afternoon writing original letters to NYC firemen and policemen who gave their all in rescue efforts at the World Trade Center disaster on Sept. 11, 2001.
A group of the students are pictured here holding copies of their letters which were delivered to the appropriate agencies: From left: Tansneem Sadeque, Caolyn Veilleux, Brett Pesce, Alexander Drath and Emma Semerad.
