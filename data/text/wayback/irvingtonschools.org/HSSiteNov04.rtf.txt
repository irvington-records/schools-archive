IRVINGTON HIGH SCHOOL SITE COMMITTEE
	Minutes of Meeting on November 16, 2004

Present:   Scott Mosenthal, Chris Tortu, Deborah Hardy, Kathleen Matusiak, Alison Romain, Zach Feinstein, Janet Williams, Laurie Chalk, Tristan Naumann, Rebecca Lazar, Lily Silpe, Katie Lushane, Joe DeGennaro, Anne Keary, Dalia May and Denise Fagann         

Scott reported that he and Kathleen attended the Emergency Preparedness Training sessions, the purpose of which was insure that appropriate safety conditions are in place to handle emergencies situations.  

Kathleen noted that the training included planning for every day issues as well, such as fire drills, the death of a member of the school community.

Scott reported that the Fall Musical, Lucky Stiff, was a great success.

Scott reported that the assembly presented by Ty Sells on alcohol and drug awareness was a success.  The students felt that his “message hit home.”  Administrators enjoyed it and felt he used humor very effectively to get his point across.  Parent turnout was somewhat low, but parents who attended reported it was a success.

Scott reported that there was a “Smoke-Out” planned for the upcoming week, but that, at this time, there was no drug/alcohol awareness event planned, maybe IEF would be interested in putting together a Parent Workshop.

Scott reported that Poet Lauretate, Billy Collins, was an excellent speaker enjoyed by all and stated that there was a suggestion about organizing a Poetry Slam.

Dalia reported that the trip to Pennsylvania to get the vote out was very interesting and the kids got a lot out of it, even Martin, who got a bucket of water dumped on him.

Scott, Tristan and Zach reported that Debate Assembly was very organized and well balanced.  The kids felt that it was informative.  Joe reported that the Connections Group following provided a good outlet for kids to get their individuals view heard.

Scott reported that there is a Connections Program planned for February involving Women’s Roles in Society.

Scott reported that over 20 members of the Staff are on ThinkWave and that, while not all students and parents felt it was necessary, some parents think it was very helpful, take advantage of it and are very happy it is available.

Joe reported that the Bowling Team is a huge success.  38 kids signed up for tryouts.  6 boys, 6 girls and 6 Alternates were selected.

Joe reported that the first Fencing Practice is set for this coming Saturday, and that Fencing is now very popular among the middle school students.  

Joe reported that there is an interest in curling.

Scott reported that the IEF and Artie McCormack have done a tremendous job in getting these new sports programs going.

Kathleen reported that the Emergency Preparedness training did not cover school security issues, noting that it is impossible to keep all the doors locked and that the district is working on an entry plan.   

 Kathleen asked for input from the Site Committee on the strengths and weaknesses of the school district..   Members of the Committee reported that the district is terrific, creative, warm, nurturing and like family.  The Committee discussed the fact that there should be more emphasis on creative writing and the concern with the growing drug and alcohol problem in the community.  There was also discussion about the math tracking program and in providing more challenging academic classes for some of the top students as well as the importance of providing classes for the other end of the group.

The next meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, December 21, 2004 at 3:30 p.m. 

The Meeting was adjourned at 5:05 p.m.

						Respectfully submitted, 

						Lily Silpe
