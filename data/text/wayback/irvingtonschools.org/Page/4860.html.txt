The District Wide Safety Plan which was adopted in September of 2020 has been amended to incorporate new laws (Section 27-c of the New York Labor Law and Section 2801-a(2)(m) of Education Law).  Essentially the changes request that the plan provide more detailed information on protocols for responding to a declared public health emergency involving a communicable disease. The revisions were coordinated by the District Wide Safety Team and our security consultant, Altaris Consulting.  Please click here to view the revised plan. Please forward any comments on this plan to Carol.Stein@irvingtonschools.org.  The plan will be re-adopted by the Board of Education during the February 9th Board of Education meeting.
