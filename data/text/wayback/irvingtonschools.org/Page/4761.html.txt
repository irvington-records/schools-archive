We continue to be very proud of you, Class of 2020! Although the pandemic has brought disappointment due to its impact on this special time that should be a celebration of so many wonderful accomplishments. This time has also given us greater perspective, strength and appreciation as well! We have been working closely with our Senior Class Officers, PAC, PTSA, District Leadership, the IEF, Booster Club, iAsk, our Village, the Irvington Police Department, the Recreation and Parks Department, Bridge Street Properties and all of our community partners to plan and offer end of year opportunities and experiences.
 
Today's communication reviews some of the information that was shared in earlier blasts: April 29th and May 8th and provides additional important updates regarding traditional end-of-year events such as graduation activities. There are a number of items that require action. Please review this blast carefully and please do not hesitate to contact me if you have any questions.   
 
Many thanks,
 
Ms. GM
 
 
Prom: Unfortunately, this year we are unable to host a traditional prom. Upon review of the Senior Survey and following up with senior class officers, the collective feedback we have received is that a virtual alternative is not desired.  
 
Senior Class officers will coordinate with Ms. Ippolito, the Senior Class Advisor, to host a class-wide Kahoot! on Wednesday, June 10th. Ms. Ippolito and Senior Class Officers will share details on the Seniors Class of 2020 Google Classroom page. 
 
Senior Awards & Scholarships: A list of students receiving Senior Awards and Scholarships will be shared with the community via a blast on Tuesday June 9. This will also be highlighted in the Graduation Video, and awards will be handed out at our Live Graduation Car Parade on June 20th. 
 
Live Graduation Car Parade Event June 20th!!!
Students and their families will be asked to arrive at the Riverfront lot at 9am (1 car per family). In partnership with the Irvington Police Department, there will be a car parade on Main Street and up Heritage Hill where celebrants and their families can see the beautiful personalized lawn signs. Students will pick up diplomas, Senior and Athletic Awards, personalized cupcakes sponsored by the IEF and goodie-bags provided by iAsk while being cheered on by teachers and staff as they drive through!
 
Photographers and videographers will be on hand to capture these special moments, which will be highlighted in our Graduation Video, which will be released at 8pm on June 20th! The IEF is also sponsoring a live stream of the Graduation Car Parade. Details to follow regarding how to access this live stream!
 
Graduation Video: IHS will celebrate commencement with a Graduation Video that will be shared with the community on Saturday, June 20th at approximately 8pm. Details regarding where you can watch the event will be shared as the date approaches.
 
Caps & Gowns: Graduation caps, gowns and tassels will be delivered directly to each student's home address via UPS. All packages are expected to be delivered by June 5.
 
If you have any questions, please contact Ms.Ippolito, Senior Class Advisor. 
If there are any issues related to your delivery, please reach out to our vendor, Oak Hall directly at 800-456-7623 or customerservice@oakhalli.com
