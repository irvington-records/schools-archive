COVID-19 Health Resources
- The Irvington UFSD is committed to providing healthy and safe spaces for its students to grow and learn and for its valued staff members to work. The COVID-19 pandemic has introduced very complicated times for all residents of the IUFSD and its employees. Just as the science related to this disease continues to evolve so does the guidance that is being provided by governmental agencies. As a result, the District values its partnership with the entire school community and endeavors to provide necessary resources to support one another’s efforts to keep our students and staff safe.
