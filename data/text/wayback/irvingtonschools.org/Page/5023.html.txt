COVID-19 Management Page
Page Navigation
- Since the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic, the Irvington UFSD has been committed to providing for the safest possible environment for its students and staff members. While there has been great progress in combating the virus, the District continues to be guided by four guiding principles: 
    - Ensure the health and safety of students and staff
    - Promote equity and accessibility to learning for all students
    - Provide instruction that meets the needs of all students
    - Support the social and emotional needs of all students and staff
The District will follow all official guidance issued by the NYS Department of Health, NYS Education Department, and Westchester County Department of Health. This page will serve as the central warehouse for all information related to the District’s management of COVID-19. Please regularly visit this page for all IUFSD plans, Official NYS guidance and District COVID-19-related communications. Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact your child’s school principal.
