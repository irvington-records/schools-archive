Irvington District-wide Safety Plan 2020-21 Adopted September 8th, 2020. School Safety and Security Updates
New Visitor Protocols The Irvington Union Free School District is commitment to providing the safest possible environments for our students and staff. To this end, the Board of Education and Administration are dedicated to making enhancements to our facilities and continue to seek opportunities to enhance our emergency readiness. This page shall serve as a portal for safety and security resources. To help ensure the safety of our schools, the District has implemented new Visitor Protocols that reflect the current COVID-19 situation. These protocols are effective immediately and we urge visitors to review them carefully before visiting the schools or District offices. While these practices will involve a more stringent "sign-in and screening process", they will provide for a safer environment for students, staff and visitors. We thank all visitors for your cooperation and patience.
