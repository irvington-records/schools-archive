SOURCE https://www.irvingtonschools.org/Page/4291 | DATE 2025-07-20 | TITLE School Counseling / Transcript Request Process (Current Students)

- Irvington High School now uses Naviance to process all collegebound transcript requests from current students. You must login to your Naviance account and under the Colleges section select the "manage transcripts" tab. Please see your counselor if you do not have your Naviance login information. If you need a transcript sent somewhere other than a post-secondary institution, please reach out directly to your School Counselor.
