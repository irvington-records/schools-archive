Dear Students and Families,
 
Happy Friday! Looking ahead, on Tuesday, June 30th at 12pm students, parents and guardians can log into Home Access Center (HAC) to view students’ final report cards and their transcript for the 2019-2020 school year. Please note, there will be no marking period average for marking period 4, for which students received letter grades: PS (Pass), PD (Pass with Distinction) and FL (Fail). Honor Roll and/or High Honor Roll distinctions were awarded to students during the first three marking periods but can not be awarded for the fourth marking period.  
 
If you have any questions related to HAC, please contact Ms. Herskowitz. If you have questions regarding an individual transcript please email your School Counselor. If you have questions related to your child’s grades, we ask that you please email your child’s teacher, who can respond to you when they return on September 2nd. Finally, as we shared earlier this month, student schedules for the 2020-2021 school year will be available on August 20th in Home Access.   
 
Many thanks,
Juliet
