Night Events
- Throughout the year, the School Counseling Department hosts events geared towards the current needs of our high school families. Our Evenings of Insights… The Counseling Department is excited to announce a series of invaluable evening programs designed to support our students and their families. These sessions will cover a range of crucial topics, including navigating the high school transition, navigating high school as it is happening, understanding the college application and review process, and providing insight into what happens behind the scenes in admissions committees. We encourage all parents and students to join us for these informative events, which are specifically tailored to equip you with the knowledge and tools needed for academic success and future planning. Join us for an evening with Nicholas Salinas, the Director of Financial Aid from Sarah Lawrence College.
