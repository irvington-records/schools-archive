Online Transportation Portal
- Student bus assignments are available the online transportation portal, which you can access via this link: myridek12.tylerapp.com. Log-in instructions may be found here Instructions for using Transportation Portal** The approximate opening date for the transportation portal is August 24, 2024. We will update the community via our newsletter when we have a definite date. Please check the portal frequently during the first few weeks of school since time adjustments may need to be made to allow for students being added to or moved from your route.
