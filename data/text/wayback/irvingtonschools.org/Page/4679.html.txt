Food Services Plan during extended school closure/Plan de aprovisionamiento de comida
- Food Services Plan during extended school closure - Updated 4/8/20 Beginning Wednesday April 8th, the distribution at the Main Street School parking lot will be from 9am - 10am. Food will then be distributed in the Tarrytown Crossing/Tallyrand parking lot near the playground from 10:15-11:15am. Both breakfast and lunch are available for a “Grab and Go” pickup. Parents may pick up food for their children. Additionally, every Friday we are distributing meals for Friday, Saturday and Sunday. Food is available for free for all Irvington UFSD students. This means that no child or family will be identified as being eligible for Free or Reduced Lunch when going to receive food. Students and families are asked to pick up the day’s food and return home and maintain social -distancing. Please continue to look for community messages and check our website for further updates.
