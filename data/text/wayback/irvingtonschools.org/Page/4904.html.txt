CRRSA/ARP Grant Allocations
The District presented preliminary plans for use of the Federal Stimulus money that will come to the District in the form of the CRRSA and ARP grants (see definitions below).  Please see the attached presentation made to the Board of Education and Community on June 8, 2021 for information on the District's preliminary plan for use of these funds.  All comments may be made to budget@irvingtonschools.org at any time.  Revisions to the plans are expected as we continue to refine our plans.
 
Recent grants include:
Coronavirus Aid, Relief and Economic Security Act (CARES) - $68,978 received and used for PPE/Technology devices in 20-21
 
Coronavirus Response and Relief Supplemental Appropriations Act (CRRSA)  also known as  Elementary and Secondary School Emergency Relief (ESSER) and Governor's Emergency Education Relief (GEER) - allocation of $206,709
 
American Rescue Plan (ARP)/ESSER III - allocation of $464,879
