Dear IHS Seniors & Families,
 
I hope this email finds you and your loved ones well. I am writing to share that at this time, we hope to proceed with being able to provide seniors with a traditional internship experience. I also want you to know that seniors should not take any action s until we make a final determination regarding the viability of the program/experience.
 
Please note that I adjusted deadlines so that students will not be submitting anything to mentors until early May, as opposed to early April. Each senior should receive an email from their teacher mentor on or before April 30th. If such an email is not received, please email me.
 
In addition to working with the District administration to determine the format for Senior Internships, I also continue to work with our District-level team to consider all other Senior Activities including Prom, Senior Dinner, and Graduation. To help inform our thinking and planning Mr. Samuelson,
Ms. Ippolito and I will be seeking opportunities to gather direct feedback and creative ideas from our students. Please look for communication regarding the upcoming events. I look forward to providing you with more information in the coming weeks. I thank you for your continued partnership and support and assure you that as we have new information related to any of the senior events, I will continue to reach out to you and share what I know.
 
Many thanks,
Juliet
