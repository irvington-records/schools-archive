School-Wide Expectations at Dows Lane
- Due to ongoing updates, please refer to the Dows Lane Buzz emails for the most up-to-date information. As part of our Character Building Program, we are helping students learn prosocial behaviors such as: 
  - Managing Impulsivity
  - Practicing Self-Reliance
  - Showing Respect and Kindness
  - Taking ownership for their actions and words
  - Cooperating
These are important Habits of Mind that students practice each and every day as part of our Dows Lane community.
