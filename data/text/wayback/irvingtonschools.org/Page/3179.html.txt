Inclement Weather Policies
- When Irvington UFSD schools are closed due to bad weather or hazardous driving conditions, no busing will be provided. If Irvington schools have a weather-related delayed start or early dismissal, busing will be provided accordingly. For students attending out-of-district schools: 
  - If your home district schools are closed due to weather conditions, no out-of-district busing will be provided.
  - If your home district declares a delayed opening, your child will also have the same delayed morning pick up, even if your out-of-district school does not have a delayed opening. (Usually school delays are two hours.)
  - If your out-of-district school has a delayed opening but your home district is open at its usual time, busing will be provided on a 2-hour delayed basis. Delayed openings will be accommodated as best as possible.
  - If your home district schools close early, all out-of-district PM bus runs will be conducted early.
  - Early dismissal declared by your out-of-district school will be accommodated as best as possible.
