Reopening Irvington UFSD
- The Irvington UFSD is committed to planning for the safe reopening of schools in September 2021. These efforts continue to be guided by four principles: 
  - Ensure the health and safety of students and staff
  - Promote equity and accessibility to learning for all students
  - Provide instruction that meets the needs of all students
  - Support the social and emotional needs of all students and staff
The District continues to follow all official guidance issued by the NYS Department of Health, NYS Education Department, Westchester County Department of Health and the Center for Disease Control. Further, the District also continues to utilize its Reopening Steering Committee to vet all plans. This page will serve as the central location for all information related to the reopening of schools. Please regularly visit this page for all official IUFSD plans and communications. Should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact your child’s school principal.
