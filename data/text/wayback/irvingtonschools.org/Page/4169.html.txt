Irvington UFSD Food Services
- Currently, the Irvington School District partners with Aramark to provide food services for our students and staff. The district participates in the National School Lunch program also known as Child Nutrition. Breakfast is available at the campus cafeteria for Middle and High School students between the hours of 7:30am - 9:30am and school lunch is available at all schools. Current meal prices: Breakfast/Lunch - Free

Gluten free offerings are available and allergies can be accommodated. Please contact Gloria Estrada at gloria.estrada@irvingtonschools.org to discuss your child’s needs. Universal Meals Participation Memo There are many ala carte items offered at the Campus Cafeteria for Middle and High School students as well. Please note that your child's end-of-year school lunch balance may be refunded, transferred to a sibling in the district, or donated to a student in need. Please email lunch@irvingtonschools.org with your preference. My School Bucks The District uses My School Bucks to allow parents/guardians to create a lunch account for their student and add funds periodically to these accounts. There is a small processing fee associated with this. Alternatively, students may pay in cash or have a check applied to his/her account.
