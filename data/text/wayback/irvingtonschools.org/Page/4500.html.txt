Master Schedule Information
January 23, 2019 Message
- During the week of January 14, we hosted a series of parent meetings to delve deeper into the proposed Master Schedule. At the January 14 and 16 workshops, parents worked in small teams to consider the benefits, concerns/questions and possible solutions regarding the schedule. We all came away with deeper insights and stronger ideas. On January 15 we hosted an open Q&A session that equally led to rich discussion. In order to support parents that were unable to attend, we recorded the Q&A session. Please click here to view the session.
