SOURCE http://www.irvingtonschools.org:80/honorband.html | DATE 2004-08-24 | TITLE Natl Honor Band

Three Irvington High School students were named 2004 National Honor Band Members. The students are Adam Shamosh (trumpet), Brian Corio (baritone saxophone) and Sam Abbott (piano).
These top student musicians were among the 100 selected from 2,200 applicants across the country. The three students will join others at the National Honor Band Festival Feb. 25-29 in Indianapolis, Indiana.
This is the 13th year that Irvington High School students have been selected for the National Honor Band. The selection process is a rigorous one, requiring students to each write an essay on musical achievements. They must also submit two professionally recorded CD's that demonstrate proficiency on the instrument they plan, as well as photographs of themselves performing.
"This is a tribute to the Irvington High School band program and the dedication of Irvington music teacher/ band instructor Larry Corio," said Nadine McDermott, Irvington music chair. "The school and the community share in their success."
