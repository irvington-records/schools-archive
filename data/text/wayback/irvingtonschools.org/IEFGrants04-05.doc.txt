We are pleased to present to the Irvington School Board the following gifts totaling $157,729 for the 2004-05 school year:

IRVINGTON HIGH SCHOOL
Grade 11: (L. Klein) - English – Repeat grant.   Students from 2 culturally, economically and geographically diverse communities will work together by attending a series of classroom workshops led jointly by teaching artists from the Manhattan Theatre Club and classroom teachers.  They will write, review, act and discuss with their peers.  This will promote an in-depth understanding of dramatic literature and performance.
Award: $5,376.

Grades 11: (L. Klein) - English – Shakespeare program designed by SUNY Purchase Performing Arts Department providing for in-depth analysis of the poet’s works with Theater Arts teachers.
Award: $4,570.


Grades 9-12: (A. McCormack) – Interscholastic Fencing - Physical Education -  This grant will provide another competitive athletic option for students during the winter.  There are currently 12 other schools offering fencing as an interscholastic sport.
Award:  $6,790.

Grades 9-12: (P. Costabile) – Architecture Club- This grant was submitted by Mr. Costabile and a student, Andy Lazar.  It will expand what students are able to do within the confines of the architecture classes that this Foundation initially funded. The club will meet on a weekly basis and designs will be collaborative.   
Award:  $4,000.

Grades 9-12: (A. Falk, D. May) – Character Education – World of Difference.  This grant continues the funding and expands the successful World of Difference program.  It allows students to develop leadership skills and addresses issues of diversity and tolerance. Last year, the program partnered with Gorton High School in Yonkers.
Award:  $2,100.

Grades 9: (S. Mosenthal) – Character Development- This grant formalizes a Senior  Mentoring program started last year which pairs interested seniors with incoming 9th graders who have been identified by the Middle School guidance counselors.  The students will meet weekly and work on organizations skills, tutoring and/or social skills.  Seniors will earn community service credit and 9th graders will benefit from additional support in a demanding environment.
Award:  $2,450.

IRVINGTON MIDDLE SCHOOL
Grade 6:  (S. Dillon, M. Tucci) - ELA and Social Studies - This is a repeat grant of the successful Medieval historical program providing workshops which focus on drama, music, dance, processions and celebrations.   
Award:  $13,546.  

Grades 6-8:  (N. Lieto) – Music - This grant will fund the purchase of large musical instruments enhancing and expanding the band program. Award:  $10,000. 

Grade 6:  (L. Stelmark, T. Savin) - Science - Repeat grant of the successful Jason Project.  This year however, at the suggestion of the science department, the program will be shifted to the 6th grade from the 8th grade.   This year students will be focusing on the Bayou in Louisiana 
Award:  $3,520.  

Grades 6-8:  [D. Vogel (parent)] - Theatre Arts - This is an expansion of a repeat grant which was submitted by a parent, will support an 8 week after school theatre program. The students will assist with script writing, editing and the process of problem solving.
Award: $11,940.40. 

Grade 8:  (J. Ippolito)  - Language Arts - Repeat grant of the successful study and performance of Shakespeare’s, A Midsummer Night’s Dream  with the Hudson Valley Shakespeare Players.   
Award: $10,300. 

Grades 6-8: (D. Schmertz) – Art – Last year the Foundation provided start up funds for a Photography Club.   A strong interest has been demonstrated by IMS students and funding has been continued for a second year. 
Award:  $7,676.
