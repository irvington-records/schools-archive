HEADLINES APRIL/MAY, 1998
VOTE ON MAY 19. Next Tuesday is the State-wide election day for all school districts. At issue is the approval of the proposed school budget for next year, and the election of School Board members. We have made every effort to present a budget that reflects a real commitment to education by adding teachers, maintaining acceptable class sizes, and funding our technology program, to name just a few items. This budget also includes for capital repairs and improvements and provides for meeting our foreseeable obligations for paying certs claims while holding the tax rate increase to 2.87%.
Two candidates are running for election to the Board of Education: George Huzinec and Joan Nelson are seeking the seats being vacated by Ellen Appelle and Paul Birgy, who chose not to run.
Your vote is very important to the on-going school budget planning process. A significant turnout to vote for this year’s budget will indicate community support for the direction and priorities of this Board of Education.
PERSON IN THE NEWS: Kathleen A. Fernald was appointed as the new Middle School principal at the May 5 Board of Education meeting. Ms. Fernald, who will begin her new position at IMS on July 1, is currently the Assistant Principal of the Robert E. Bell Middle School in Chappaqua. Prior to her six years in this position, she served a year as Interim Assistant Principal in the Bedford District. Ms. Fernald’s teaching experience includes private schools in New York; most notably, she was Assistant Head of the Upper School for the final three years of her ten years at the Nigtingale-Bamford School. She gained an additional two years administrative experience as Upper School Dean at the Springside School in Philadelphia before returning to New York and to public education. Ms. Fernald holds an MS from the University of Oregon, an MA from Columbia University and an EdM from Teachers College.
She was selected as one of four finalists for the position by a search committee which included three administrators, three parents and three teachers. The Board of Education members unanimously and enthusiastically chose Ms. Fernald as the best possible match for our district after interviewing her and visiting her current school. We warmly welcome Ms. Fernald to Irvington.
Of course, the other "person in the news" is Dr. Andy Kerfut, who is retiring as the first and only Principal of the Irvington Middle School after 22 years. We heartily thank Dr. Kerfut for his years of dedicated service to the district. He will be very much missed.
SO MANY THANK YOU’S have been extended to the PTSA that Mr. Hajek added up the organization’s various contributions to our schools, and came up with a grand total of $43,000 for this year alone. Thank you all!
