V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS
 
Superintendent’s Report  
1.  Secondary School End of Year Reports
Dr. Matusiak introduced Dr. Mosenthal, Mr. Fried, Mr. DeGennaro and Ms. Kor for the Secondary School End of Year Reports.  Mr. Fried described the secondary campus Middle and High Schools as working together and yet remaining as two distinct schools.  

8:10p.m:  Mrs. Silverberg joined the meeting.

Both schools received their Tri-State/Middle States Accreditation this year.  Mr. Fried reviewed the concepts of new teacher mentoring, the growing list of support services, progress on collaborative education strategizing and examples of IEF/PTSA grants which provide opportunity and enrichment for students.  

Dr. Mosenthal reported on courses and programs new in 2004-05, High School student achievement and implementation of the in-district alternative program.  The Middle School has imbedded the character education in the curriculum with both schools stressing a K-12 approach.  Each Principal reviewed the student, as well as, teacher collaborative efforts.  Planning for next year includes joint meetings of the Student Councils, Site Committees, shared staff recognition and shared special events.  

Mrs. Delany thanked the group for their presentation.
