The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Campus Presentation Room, September 21, 2004, with the following present:

Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Carol Gutman.  Also present:  Dr. Kathleen Matusiak - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro – High School Assistant Principal, Mr. Scott Fried – Middle School Principal, Mr. Donald Kuhn – Main Street School Interim Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Elementary Principal, Ms. Erika Raser – Committee on Special Education Subcommittee Chairperson, students and members of the community. 

I.  	CALL TO ORDER

The Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Penny Delany at 8:37 p.m., the pledge of allegiance was given.

II	MINUTES OF MEETINGS

A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to approve the minutes of the Public Meeting of September 7, 2004.  
Vote:  5-0 Approved

III	INFORMATION ITEMS  

	Dr. Matusiak reported on the following:
Six (6) of our students have been named National Merit Scholar Semi-Finalists.  They will be recognized at a future Board meeting.
Congratulations to Dr. Napolitano for organizing a very successful substitute teacher orientation.
Attended a meeting with several local realtors.  A “new resident” packet of information will be developed for prospective buyers.  A continental breakfast and a tour of the new buildings will be offered this fall.  
The staff is currently involved in “goal setting” for this school year.  

IV	COMMENTS FROM RESIDENTS

Brian Adelman, Senior Class President, reported on information that he has collected regarding parking lot usage in the Campus parking lots.  Dr. Matusiak said that a meeting of all concerned parties is being scheduled to discuss all of the different opinions and viewpoints regarding student parking (and parking issues in general) at the Campus.  Mr. Mandel complimented Brian on his initiative and great job in information collection.

V         BUSINESS ITEMS

Tax Certiorari Settlement – Pennybridge Manor
A motion was made by Mrs. Janice Silverberg, seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel, to adopt the following Resolution:  

“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District, pursuant to a tax certiorari settlement approved by the Board of Education in March 2004, authorizes the refund of taxes to Pennybridge Manor condominium in the amount of $173,599.44 from the Tax Certiorari Reserve.”
Vote:  5-0 Approved

Re-authorize Tax Certiorari Reserve
A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Janice Silverberg, to adopt the following Resolution:

“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District return $1,415,248 from the Tax Certiorari Reserve to the General Fund.”
Vote:  5-0 Approved



A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Carol Gutman, to adopt the following Resolution:

“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District appropriate $762,928 from the General Fund to increase the Tax Certiorari Reserve.”
Vote:  5-0 Approved

Lease Purchase Agreement – Computers
A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Janice Silverberg, to adopt the following Resolution:

“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District enter into a lease/purchase agreement for computer equipment purchases and authorize the Asst. Supt. for Business to sign the agreement."
Vote:  5-0 Approved

Lease Purchase Agreement – Telephone System
A motion was made by Mrs. Diane Mundree, seconded by Mrs. Carol Gutman, to adopt the following Resolution:

“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District enter into a lease/purchase agreement for the new telephone system that will be installed at Dows Lane and Main Street School by Independent Telecommunications Corporation and authorize the Asst. Supt. for Business to sign the agreement."
Vote:  5-0 Approved

VI	COMMENTS FROM THE BOARD - None 

VII	MEETING NOTIFICATION

The meeting will be a Public Meeting held on October 5, 2004 at 7:30 p.m. in the Campus Presentation Room.

VIII	ADJOURNMENT

Mrs. Janice Silverberg motioned to adjourn to Executive Session, for the purpose of discussing the employment history of a particular person, at 9:07 p.m., seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel. 
  	Vote:  5 - 0 Approved
