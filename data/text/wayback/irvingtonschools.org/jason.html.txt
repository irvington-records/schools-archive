SOURCE http://www.irvingtonschools.org:80/jason.html | DATE 2002-10-29 | TITLE Jason Project

Aneese Benferhat, a ninth-grader has been chosen to represent our region as a student argonaut for the internationally renowned JASON Project. Aneese was one of two finalists who made it to the state level from this region after competing against nine other state finalists.
As a student finalist, Aneese will travel to Alaska for two weeks (Jan. 25 - Feb. 4, 2002), where he will work with this year's JASON team and international scientists assembled together to engage students in an exciting and rewarding science and technology project. He will also get to be a presenter during a telecast which allows students to communicate with the scientists and become hands-on participants through a live "telepresence."
"I am very excited about this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity," said Aneese, who finds science one of his most loved subjects. "I look forward to being able to bring back to Irvington a lot to share with my fellow classmates and teachers."
