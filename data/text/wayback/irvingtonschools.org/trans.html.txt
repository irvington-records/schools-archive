Please Note: Families requiring transportation to Non-public (private and/or parochial) schools must apply for such transportation by April 1st. Applications are available in each school building, the Central Administration office or by contacting Doug Carter at 591-3052. Transportation requests received after the April 1st deadline will not be honored.
To be eligible for Non-public busing, the student must be at least 5 years old by December 1st and reside 15 miles or less from the school he/she will attend. The student must also be eligible for busing under the District's minimum distance eligibility standard (Grades K to 5-.25 mi.; 6 to 8-1.25 mi.; 9 to 12-1.5 mi.).
No busing will be provided to Non-public schools before September 1st, after June 30th, on legal holidays or on inclement weather days.
Questions regarding Non-public busing should be addressed to Doug Carter at 591-3052.
