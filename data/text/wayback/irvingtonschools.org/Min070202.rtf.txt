The following are the minutes of the Reorganization Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington Middle School Library, July 2, 2002, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  
Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, 
Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman - Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mrs. Susan Levey - Director Pupil Personnel Services.  Absent:  Mr. George Huzinec.  

I	CALL TO ORDER AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

The Public Meeting was called to order by Ms. Linda McMullan at 5:40 p.m. and the pledge of  allegiance was given.

II	OATH OF OFFICE - NEW SCHOOL BOARD TRUSTEES

Ms. Linda McMullan administered the Oath of Office to Mrs. Penny Delany, elected to the Board of Education, term expiring June 30, 2005.  Congratulations were extended to Mrs. Delany. 

III	ELECTION OF BOARD OFFICERS

	A.  President

Mr. Paul Mandel nominated Mrs. Janice Silverberg for the position of President of the Irvington Board of Education, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany.  Ms. McMullan called for the vote and Mrs. Silverberg was elected President.
	      Vote:  3 - 0 Approved    				

	B.  Administer Oath

Ms. McMullan administered the Oath of Office to Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Board President.     Congratulations were extended.

	C.  Vice President

Mrs. Diane Mundree nominated Mrs. Penny Delany for the position of Vice President of the Irvington Board of Education, seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel.  Mrs. Delany was elected Vice President.
                    Vote:  3 - 0 Approved			

	D.  Administer Oath

Ms. McMullan administered the Oath of Office to Mrs. Delany, Board Vice President.  Congratulations were extended

C.	Construction Bids

	1.  General Construction - HSR 1
	A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany, to adopt the following Resolution:

	“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District reject all bids for General Construction-Contract No. HSR 1,

	AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District authorizes the re-bidding of the General Construction-Contract No. HSR 1.”
	Vote:  4-0 Approved




2.  Mechanical Bid-Contract No. HSR 2

	A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to adopt the following Resolution:

	“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the Mechanical Bid-Contract No. HSR 2 to Capstone Enterprises as the lowest responsive and responsible bidder with a base bid price of $1,246,000.00.”
	Vote:  4-0 Approved

3.  Plumbing Bid-Contract No. HSR 3

	A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to adopt the following Resolution:

	“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the Plumbing Bid-Contract No. HSR 3 to Beneway as the lowest responsive and responsible bidder with a base bid price of $240,000.00.”
	Vote:  4-0 Approved

4.  Electrical Bid-Contract No. HSR 4
	A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany, to adopt the following Resolution:

	“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the Electrical Bid-Contract No. HSR 4 to Naber Electric as the lowest responsive and responsible bidder with a base bid price of $1,293,000.00.”
	Vote:  4-0 Approved	

5.  Roofing Bid-Contract No. HSNR 6
	A motion was made by Mrs. Penny Delany, seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel, to adopt the following Resolution:

	“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District correct the Roofing Bid-Contract No. HSNR 6 amount awarded to Hi-Tech Contracting Corp., on June 4, 2002, to $2,388,000.00.”
	Vote:  4-0 Approved

6.  Painting Bid-Main Street School
	A motion was made by Mrs. Penny Delany, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to adopt the following Resolution:
