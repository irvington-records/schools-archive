Alumni Corner
The Irvington School District has created this Alumni Corner to help its graduates stay in touch with each other and with the Irvington Schools Community. If you are interested in joining in, send your name, e-mail address and class to: alumni@irvingtonschools.org. Be sure to tell your friends to send in their e-mail addresses too!
