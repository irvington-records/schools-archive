The following are the minutes of the Public Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington Campus Presentation Room, September 30, 2003, with the following present:

Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mr. George Huzinec.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman - Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mr. Sheldon Levine – Middle School Interim Principal, Mr. Donald Kuhn – Main Street School Interim Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Elementary School Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor - Middle School Assistant Principal, Mrs. Susan Levey and Mrs. Sheralyn Gottlieb – Directors of Pupil Personnel Services, Mr. Micah Orifici – Interim Director of Technology, faculty members and members of the community.

The Public Work Session was called to order by Mrs. Delany at 7:38 p.m. 

Mrs. Delany commented on the fact that this was the first meeting in the Presentation Room on the new and renovated campus at 40 North Broadway.  Dr. Beni asked the meeting attendees for feed back and suggestions regarding the set-up of the room.  

The order of the Agenda was changed as follows:

Dr. Beni outlined the topics to be presented by Dr. Dolan-Waldman.  

ANNUAL PROFESSIONAL PERFORMANCE REVIEW PLAN

Dr. Dolan-Waldman presented the revised Annual Professional Performance Review Plan and summarized the changes made to the Plan by the review committee.  Major revisions include the standardized observation of teachers process and the inclusion of a new rubric for the observation and review of Pupil Personnel staff.

PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT PLAN

The Professional Development Plan, which defines goals for the professional staff, has also been updated.  Mr. Huzinec complimented all who participated in the process and commented that this plan is directly connected to the goals of the District.  					

CURRICULUM CYCLE REPORT

Dr. Dolan-Waldman reviewed the implementation of the curriculum cycle chart adding that the Technology review is being delayed for one (1) year due to the changes that have occurred this year as a result of the implementation of the Master Plan.  

There was discussion of the selection and implementation of a new mathematics curriculum.  “TERC” and “Trailblazers” are now being evaluated.  Dr. Beni pointed out that parent presentations will be made, possibly at PTSA meetings, to educate the parents on the specifics of the chosen curriculum.  Mrs. Delany said that parents need to feel more involved and that more communication would be helpful.  

This summer the curriculum work involved 57 teachers working on 16 difference project for a total of 292 days’ work  

Mrs. Delany thanked Dr. Dolan-Waldman for her presentations.

Mrs. Delany announced that there will be a Public Meeting held on October 7, 2003, at 7:30 p.m. in the Campus Presentation Room.

The meeting was adjourned at 9:15 p.m., to Executive Session, for discussion of the employment of a particular person.
