So, What has the Irvington PTSA done for the students in the District lately?
PTSA Sponsored Programs and Activities include:
- Parent Advisory Councils(PAC)
- Class Parents
- After School Enrichment Program
- Publishing Center at Dows Lane
- Authors and Illustrators Visits to Dows Lane and IMS
- Middle School Newsletter
- Student Directories for PTSA Members
- Yearbooks at Dows Lane and IMS
- Welcome Back BBQs At IHS
- International Week
- Health and Nutrition Week
- Earth Day
- Coat and Toy Drives for the Needy
- BINGO
- Book Fairs to fill Summer Reading Requirements
- Low Cost PSAT/SAT Review Courses
- Staff Recognition Days
- Special Education Support Committee
- Hospitality at Open House Nights
- The Winter Festival
- Pre-School Newsletters and Pre-School Carnival
- Speakers on Parenting Issues
just to name a few...
And did you know... over $16,000 in scholarships were awarded in to graduating seniors in 2000-2001!
