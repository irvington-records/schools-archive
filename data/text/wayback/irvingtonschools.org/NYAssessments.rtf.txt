APPROVED ALTERNATIVES TO REGENTS EXAMINATIONS


Note:  (MAS = Minimum Acceptable Score)



English Regents: 

Advanced Placement Language and Composition Exam (MAS = 3)
Advanced Placement Literature and Composition Exam (MAS = 3)

International Baccalaureate English A1 Standard Level Exam (MAS = 4)
International Baccalaureate English A1 Higher Level Exam (MAS = 3)


Math Regents: Alternatives to either Math A,  or Course I & Course II with a 65:

Advanced International Certificate of Education (AICE) Mathematics Examination (MAS = E)
Advanced Placement Calculus AB Examination (MAS = 3)
Advanced Placement Calculus BC (MAS = 3)

International Baccalaureate Mathematics Studies Standard Level Exam (MAS = 4)
International Baccalaureate Mathematics Methods Standard Level Exam (MAS = 4)
International Baccalaureate Mathematics Higher Level Exam (MAS = 3)
International General Certificate of Secondary Education (IGCSE) (MAS = A)

SAT II Mathematics Level IC (MAS = 470)
SAT II Mathematics Level IIC (MAS = 510)
