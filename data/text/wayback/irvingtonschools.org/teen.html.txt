SOURCE http://irvingtonschools.org:80/teen.html | DATE 2003-08-21 | TITLE Teen Award

Irvington High School junior Elizabeth Simons was recognized by the Fairfield/Westchester Chapter of the Crohn's and Colitis Foundation of America for her work as "Teen Chair" of the Pace Setter Three Mile Walk that took place earlier this fall at Irvington's scenic Hudson Riverfront Park. Elizabeth, who has been active for years helping to raise money for the Foundation, was credited with recruiting "teen" teams at other high schools in Westchester to join the Walk. Those eight teams of high school students raised close to $15,000 for the Crohn's and Colitis Foundation. Jessica herself raised $5,000 and the Walk itself, raised a total of $40,000 ---- money that will go to the Foundation that provides support for victims with intestinal diseases and funds research to someday find a cure for the disease.
Elizabeth and her whole family have been active in fighting the disease, which her older sister, Jessica, a 1996 graduate of Irvington High School, has suffered with since she was a young girl. "My sister has been so strong her whole life," said Elizabeth, who is also editorial page editor of the Irvington High School newspaper and a member of the school's Leadership Club. "She's my role model." Jessica Simons, who graduated from New York University, now attends Fordham University where she is pursuing a master's degree in education.
