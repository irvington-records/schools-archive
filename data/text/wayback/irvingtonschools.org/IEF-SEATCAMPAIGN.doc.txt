If “All the World’s A Stage”

Then why not invest in the best seat in the house…..

In the newly constructed Irvington High School/Middle School Theater

Don’t miss this once in a lifetime opportunity for a permanent place in the history of the Irvington Union Free School District



The Irvington Education Foundation is proud to sponsor, on behalf of the Irvington Union Free School District, the “Buy A Seat” campaign. Your purchase of a personalized engraved commemorative plaque to adorn a seat in the newly constructed Middle/High School Theater will enrich the academic and cultural lives of Irvington’s public school children. 

Just fill out and return the fill-in portion of the form below with your tax-deductible check. Be careful to insert both letters and spaces in each space provided. Each name will be placed on a beautifully engraved brass plaque to be recessed in the armrest of each seat. The name(s) of the individual(s) you choose to honor will be an enduring part of the beautiful new state-of-the-art theater, scheduled to be completed in late 2003.
Your tax-deductible contribution of $275 per seat will help accomplish the Foundation’s goals of awarding grants to our schools that enhance our students’ educational experiences and worldviews. A nominal portion will be used to
defray installation costs. Seats are limited and plaques will be sold on a first-come, first-served basis, so please return your form as quickly as possible. The IEF thanks you for preserving the legacy of excellence in the Irvington Union Free School District.
