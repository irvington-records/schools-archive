The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington District Office Conference Room, February 28, 2004, with the following present:


Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mr. George Huzinec. 

I	CALL TO ORDER

	The Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Penny Delany at 8:45 a.m.

	A motion was made at 8:45 a.m. by Mr. George Huzinec, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to change the location of the meeting to the Dolce Tarrytown House.
	Vote:  3-0 Approved

	A motion was made at 8:45 a.m. by Mrs. Diane Mundree, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to adjourn to Executive Session for the purpose of discussing the employment history of particular person(s). 
	Vote:  3-0 Approved

Mr. Paul Mandel and Mrs. Janice Silverberg joined the meeting at 9:00 a.m.

The Public Meeting reconvened at 2:00 p.m. 

II       	ADJOURNMENT

	A motion to adjourn was made at 2:01 p.m. by Mr. George Huzinec, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree.  
	Vote:  5-0 Approved
