Deborah Hardy, IHS's new guidance chair, was recently nominated by the New York State School Counselor Association (NYSSCA) to receive the prestigious Secondary Counselor of the Year Award, given out by the American School Counselor Association.
"It's a great honor to be recognized by your peers," said Hardy, who has served as a past president of the New York State School Counselor Association. Last year, Hardy received the "2001 Counselor of the Year Award" from the NYSSCA. The year before, the National Association of College Admissions Counselors named her "Counselor of the Year."
Before coming to Irvington High School this year, Hardy worked as a guidance counselor at Sleepy Hollow H.S. A resident of Yorktown, Hardy has two master's degrees; one in counseling from Long Island University, and another in educational leadership from the Bank Street College. Her bachelor's degree in foreign language and sociology is from Rider University.
