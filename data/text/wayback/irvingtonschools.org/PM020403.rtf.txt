  V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

Superintendent’s Report: 
Dows Lane 4-5 Report – Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker  (Supplemental Log – Appendix A)
Dr. Garcia-Blocker presented an update on the English Language Arts, Mathematics and Social Studies curricula.  The School Planning Team (SPT) has been discussing the Mission Statement of the Main Street 4-5 School, school environment, development of activities and clubs, lunch services, classroom utilization and use of facilities overall.  New programming being proposed includes world languages and library/research skills to be facilitated by a longer school day. 


 

There was considerable discussion of the proposed playground facilities at the Main Street school.  Resident concerns include an increase in traffic, proper drainage, disruptions to the environment after hours and the maintenance of property and equipment.  Mrs. Silverberg and Mrs. Delany assured the Ferris Street residents that the playground design is in the planning stages and that the Board wishes to listen to concerns and to remain good neighbors. 

Dr. Garcia-Blocker stated that the next meeting of the school planning committee will be held on February 13, 2003 and extended an invitation to the Ferris Street residents to attend.  

Mrs. Delany complimented Dr. Garcia-Blocker for the information packet given to the Board and, in particular, the Grade 4-5 Newsletter.  Mrs. Silverberg thanked Dr. Garcia-Blocker and the transition team for the presentation. 

Master Plan:  Update on Remaining Projects  (Supplemental Log – Appendix B)
Dr. Beni reported on the status of the Irvington Master Plan beginning with a chronology of events leading to June 2002 when the construction began.  The original funding plan was reviewed as well as the major changes since 1999:  state changes in the building aid distribution formula, the Dows Lane fire and the new major projects (Middle School roof replacement) which have been identified.  

Three options were presented for consideration:
Option A - increase the Capital Fund in the budget
Option B - borrow $995,000 for new projects
Option C - borrow $3.8 million for new projects, reduce the annual capital fund allocation in the budget, and therefore realize an earlier completion of the Master Plan

Dr. Beni discussed the cost effectiveness of Option C, the $3.8 million bond.  A question and answer period followed.  If the Bond, Option C, is chosen, the vote would be scheduled for April 3, 2003.  There will be further discussion at subsequent Board meetings.

Tax Certiorari Settlement – Betram J. Trent (d/b/a Bert Realty Co., Inc.)
A motion was made by Mr. George Huzinec, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to adopt the following Resolution:

“WHEREAS, law suits have been commenced to challenge the assessments on the property owned by Bertram J. Trent d/b/a Bert Realty Co., Inc. located at Station Road and South Buckhout Street in the Village of Irvington, and designated and described as Section 4, Sheet 7A, Block 0230, Lot 1 and Section 4, Sheet 7, Block 0230, P. 98 on the Tax Maps and Assessment Rolls of the Town of Greenburgh for assessment years 1996 through 2002; and

WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District, through its attorney, appeared in the pending litigation to protect the interests of the School District; and

WHEREAS, several meetings were held with the attorney for the petitioner, the Deputy Town Attorney for the Town of Greenburgh and counsel for the School District and various settlement proposals were discussed at length prior to arriving at a tentative settlement; and

WHEREAS, the School District’s counsel, the Town of Greeburgh Assessor and Deputy Town Attorney all recommend approving the tentative settlement; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that it is in the best interests of the School District to settlement this law suit with the following reductions in assessed values:

		Year		Original AV		New AV		Reduction
		1996		   $417,000		$397,000		  $20,000

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that Keane & Beane, P.C. is authorized to execute a Stipulation, Consent Judgment and any other relevant settlement documents consistent with this Resolution on behalf of the respondent-intervenor, the Irvington Union Free School District.”
