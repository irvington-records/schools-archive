Apply For Your STAR Tax Exemption
Since 1998, New York State has provided a program to reduce school property taxes for homeowners. This School Tax Relief (STAR) Program affords property tax relief while replacing the income to the school district with increased direct state aid. The Board of Education and the Administration of the Irvington School District wish to ensure that each and every resident is informed and takes advantage of this program.
To qualify for the "Basic" Exemption, you must merely own and live in a primary residence, defined as a one, two, or three family home, cooperative or condominium. In other words, every homeowner, not just senior citizens, now qualify for this exemption. In addition, senior citizens with incomes under $60,000 qualify for an "Enhanced" Exemption. However, the exemptions (both Basic and Enhanced) are not automatic: you must file an application if you have not already done so. The deadline for filing for an exemption for next year’s school taxes is June 1, 2002.
Below are answers to typical questions regarding the STAR program:
Who is eligible for the Basic Exemption? Every homeowner who lives in and owns a primary residence, defined as a one, two or three family home, cooperative or condominium.
Who is eligible for the Enhanced Exemption? Senior citizens, age 65 and older, with adjusted gross incomes of under $60,000.
Do I need to apply each year? You need to apply only once to receive the Basic exemption, unless you move to a new primary residence. In other words, if you applied last year, you need not apply again. However, senior citizens wishing to receive the Enhanced Exemption must submit proof each year that their adjusted gross income is under $60,000.
How do I receive the Basic Exemption? Send Form RP-425 (pdf format) (html format) and a copy of your driver’s license or other proof of residence such as a phone or utility bill (showing the address for which you are requesting an exemption) to the Greenburgh Town Assessor before June 1, 2001.
How do I receive the Enhanced Exemption? Send Form RP-425 , a copy of your driver’s license or other proof of residence such as a phone or utility bill (showing the address for which you are requesting an exemption), and a copy of your 1999 Federal Income Tax Form 1040 Page 1 and 2 to the Greenburgh Town Assessor before June 1, 2001.
Where do I send the forms? To the Greenburgh Town Assessor, P.O. Box 205, Elmsford, NY 10523 or visit the Assessor’s Office at 320 Tarrytown Road, Elmsford, NY. The phone number of the Assessor’s Office is: 914-993-1517.
Please take advantage of this program to reduce your School Taxes!
