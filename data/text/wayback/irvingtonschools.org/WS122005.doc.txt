The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Campus Presentation Room, December 20, 2005, with the following present:
Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Carol Gutman, Mrs. Robyn Kerner. Also present: 
Dr. Kathleen L Matusiak - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Ralph Napolitano - Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. Scott Fried-Irvington Middle School Principal, Mr. Donald Kuhn – Main Street School Interim Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez-Dows Lane Principal, Mr. Joe DeGennaro-Irvington High School Assistant Principal, Dr. Joel Klein, Interim Director Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Maddy Stern, Assistant Director of Pupil Personnel, members of the staff and community.  

Prior to the Public Meeting, the Irvington High School Chamber Ensemble, led by Mr. Alan Goldberg, performed several festive pieces for the members of the Board of Education and the audience.  Dr. Matusiak thanked Yea Na Hyun, Catherine Mulhall, Mari Nakamura, Anya Palkowski, Faye Wang, Max Dilthey, Jung Hee Schwartz and David Weinberger and Mr. Greenberg for a terrific performance.
 		
       I.	CALL TO ORDER
	The Public Meeting was called to order by Mr. Paul Mandel at 7:15 p.m., and the Pledge of Allegiance was said.

INFORMATION ITEMS
Dr. Ralph Napolitano reported that the District received funding approval on Title I A & D – Improve Academic Achievement, Title IIA - NCLB, Title IV A – Safe and Drug Free Schools, Title V A-Innovative Programs and Title II D, Enhancing Education through Teaching. The grants total $109,157.
Dr. Matusiak reported that several seniors have received early acceptance letters from such notable universities as Dartmouth and Yale. 
Dr. Matusiak and Mr. James Reese have been meeting with the principals this week to begin reviewing their budgetary needs for the 2006-2007 school year.  The community will be provided ample opportunity to ask questions and comment on the proposed budget.
 
 COMMENTS FROM RESIDENTS
      Mrs. Deb Weintraub announced that the P.T.S.A. will hold a general membership meeting on January 11, 2006 at 7:00 p.m. in the Campus Presentation Room. The meeting will provide a forum for the community to discuss what items they would like to see included in the proposed 2006-07 budget.  Co-Presidents Andrea Thompson and Deb Weintraub will be meeting with former P.T.S.A. presidents to review the format for the meeting.  

PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS:
A.	Superintendent’s Report
  
Professional Development Plan  (Supplemental Log – Appendix A)
		
		Dr. Matusiak introduced Dr. Ralph Napolitano for his report on the Professional Development Plan. 
Dr. Napolitano explained that the Professional Development Planning Committee’s mission is “To improve teaching and learning for all teachers and students.” The goals of professional development are in alignment with the District’s Strategic Plan and to incorporate curriculum development, professional development and teacher evaluation. Among the delivery models for professional development are Superintendent’s Conference Days, Consultants, out-of-district conferences and workshops, Mentoring Program, Study and Book groups and faculty/grade level/department meetings.
When measuring the effectiveness of the professional development plan, the District looks at changes on the student level as well as the evaluation of professional development.
