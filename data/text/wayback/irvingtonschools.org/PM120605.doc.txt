       I.	CALL TO ORDER

	The Public Meeting was called to order by Mr. Paul Mandel at 7:15 p.m., and the Pledge of Allegiance was said.

INFORMATION ITEMS

A.    Recognition of National Merit Semi-Finalists and Student Scholar Candidates

Drs. Kathleen Matusiak and Scott Mosenthal introduced two of the seven National Merit Semi-Finalists Abbe Sokol and Greg Sokol and presented them with Certificates of Achievement.  Ana Becker, Jeff Finkelstein, Danielle Frimer, Henry Liu and Ben Smail were unable to attend the meeting.  Jennifer Zeng and Nick Yuelys                 were presented with Certificates of Achievement for having been chosen as Student Scholarship Candidates.  
  Drs. Matusiak and Mosenthal congratulated the students for their outstanding achievements.

PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS:
A.	Superintendent’s Report
  
Dows Lane School Report and Action Plan (Supplemental Log – Appendix A)
Mr. Joe Rodriguez and Ms. Eileen McGuire presented the Dows Lane Elementary School Report and Action Plan.  The school is celebrating its 50th birthday with special events throughout the year. Mr. Rodriguez stated that there are a total of 571 students in the school.  He reviewed the English Language Arts curriculum that incorporates shared and guided reading as well as shared writing and interactive writing as well as independent reading and writing and is in alignment with the District’s Strategic Plan Goal 1.  

Mr. Rodriguez thanked the P.T.S.A  and the IEF for their continued support of many programs such as the  No Put Downs program, Famous Artists, Authors and Illustrators and Publishing Center sponsored by the P.T.S.A. IEF funded programs this year include a poetry workshop, American Sign Language, Junior Achievement and the Lincoln Center program.
