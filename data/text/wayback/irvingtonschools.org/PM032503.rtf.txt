The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library on March 25, 2003, with the following present:

Gr. K-3 Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Dows Lane Gr. 4-5 Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor – Middle School Assistant Principal, Mrs. Susan Levey – Director Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar – Director of Technology, faculty and community members. 

I  	CALL TO ORDER

The Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Janice Silverberg at 7:40 p.m.

Mr. Huzinec complimented the teachers who participated in the Middle School report given at the March 11, 2003 meeting and noted their enthusiasm for their work.
 
II	BUSINESS ITEMS

Resolution:  Main Street School Roof Replacement Funding
A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to adopt the following Resolution:

“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District appropriate $650,000 from the General Fund, fund balance, to provide funding for the Main Street School roof replacement project.”
Vote:  4-0 Approved

Resolution:  SEQRA Regulation – Main Street and Dows Lane Projects (Supplemental Log – Appendix A)
A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to adopt the Resolution naming the Irvington School District as the lead agency in the SEQRA process for the Type II projects of window replacement, field work and roof replacement.
Vote:  4-0 Approved
