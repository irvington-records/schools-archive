Don't Miss Out!!!  Seats are still available through the Irvington Education Foundation "Buy-A-Seat" Campaign. This is a once in lifetime opportunity to purchase a personalized engraved commemorative plaque to adorn a seat in the newly constructed Middle/High School Theater. The moneys raised will enrich the academic and cultural lives of Irvington's public school children. Tax-deductible contributions of $275 per seat will help accomplish the Foundations' goals of providing grants to our schools that enhance our children's educational experience and world view. A nominal portion will be used to defray installation costs. The IEF recently presented a wide range of grants that will benefit all of our children during the 2004-05 school year totaling over $170,000. This brings the IEF's grant total since its inception to over $1,000,000.  This money has directly benefited and enhanced the educational program of ALL of our children!  To learn more about the IEF, go to our web site and click Education Foundation.  Here you will find the list of grants for 2004-05 and prior years and the IEF's new endowment policy.
