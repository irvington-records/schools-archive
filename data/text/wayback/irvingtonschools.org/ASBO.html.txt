SOURCE http://www.irvingtonschools.org:80/ASBO.html | DATE 2004-08-07 | TITLE Financial Reporting Award

The Irvington School District has been awarded a "Certificate of Achievement for Excellence in Financial Reporting" by the Association of School Business Officials International.
The award, which is conferred to school districts who have met or exceeded the highest standards of financial reporting, was based on the district's comprehensive 2002 annual financial audit, prepared by the district's accounting firm -- Bennett, Kielson, Storch and DeSantis and Company of White Plains.
Irvington's Assistant Superintendent for Business and Facility Management James Reese said last year's 2002-03 district report provides interested community residents with a "clear and comprehensive picture of the district's fiscal health."
The Association of School Business Officials International, founded in 1919, is a professional association, which provides programs and services to promote the highest standards of school business management practices, professional growth, and effective use of educational resources.
