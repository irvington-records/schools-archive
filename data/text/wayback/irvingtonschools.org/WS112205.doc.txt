The following are the minutes of the Public Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Campus Presentation Room, November 22, 2005, with the following present:

Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Carol Gutman, Mrs. Robyn Kerner. Also present: 
Dr. Kathleen L Matusiak - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Ralph Napolitano - Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. Joe DeGennaro-High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor-Middle School Assistant Principal, Ms. Eileen McGuire-Dows Lane Assistant Principal, Dr. Joel Klein, Interim Director Pupil Personnel Services and members of the community.  Absent: Mrs. Diane Mundree.
 		
       I	CALL TO ORDER

	The Public Meeting was called to order by Mr. Paul Mandel at 7:15 p.m., and the Pledge of Allegiance was said.

     II       INFORMATION ITEMS

Dr. Matusiak introduced Ms. Carol LaBella, and Ms. Jean Snell, teachers at the Main Street School, sponsors of  the “Students Who Care” group, along with students Arielle Gur, Alex Goldsmith, Jenny Ferguson and Ilsson Jean. The students were invited by the Irvington Historical Society to look through boxes of archival items and create ways to display the items for the Children’s Room in the McVicar House.  
The O’Hara Basketball Tournament was held last weekend with teachers, administrators, students and community members playing for a great cause.
Jack Lazarus, a 4th grader from Main Street School, won the “Story Works” contest sponsored by Scholastic Magazine for a story he submitted.
The Middle School will be holding Student Council speeches and elections on Wednesday, November 23rd. Campaign posters are hanging throughout the school. 
Dr. Matusiak joined the Kindergarten classes at Dows Lane on November 22nd for their Thanksgiving feast that was prepared by parents. 

   III	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

Superintendent’s Report (Supplemental Log-Appendix A)
Main Street School Report and Action Plan
Dr. Matusiak introduced Mr. Don Kuhn, Interim Principal at the Main Street School to present the Main Street School Report and Action Plan.  

Mr. Kuhn stated that there are currently 155 4th grade students and 157 in the 5th grade.  There are nine new staff members at the Main Street School. The 4th grade math program, Trailblazers, has been implemented.  Planning for the 5th grade Trailblazers program will begin in January 2006 and implementation is scheduled for September 2006. The AIS enrichment program in 4th grade has begun. Mr. Kuhn reviewed the 4th grade ELA and math test scores, which show substantial movement from Level 3 to Level 4. The social studies and science scores also showed excellent student performance.
	 
 Mr. Kuhn highlighted the initiatives for each of the five goals that are part of the districtwide Five-Year Strategic Plan:
	     Goal 1:   Curriculum Mapping and Student Portfolios
     Goal 2:   Data warehousing and analysis of data as a tool to drive instruction; implementation of K-12  
                    Differentiated Instruction Plan        
     Goal 3:   No Put Downs program to promote character education
     Goal 4:   Training of staff and implementation of Edline 
Goal 5:  Tools for Schools, a program to monitor and address environmental concerns in and around the building 

Mr. Kuhn stated that the students continue to benefit from programs funded by the PTSA and IEF. Among the initiatives sponsored by the PTSA this year are No Put Downs, the Jason Project and the mobile computer lab.  The IEF has provided funding for the 5th grade Ecotowers program, Lincoln Center and Odyssey of the Mind program. 

A motion was made by Mrs. Janice Silverberg, seconded by Mrs. Carol Gutman, to adjourn to Executive Session for the purpose of discussing collective bargaining negotiations with the Districts’ legal counsel.
Vote: 4-0 Approved.               

The Work Session reconvened at 10:45 p.m.





       IV	ADJOURNMENT 
	Mrs. Janice Silverberg motioned to adjourn at 10:50 p.m., seconded by Mrs. Robyn Kerner.
Vote: 4-0 Approved
