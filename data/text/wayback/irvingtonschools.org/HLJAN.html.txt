HEADLINES JANUARY, 1998
SEARCHING.....
The search for a new Middle School principal is underway. The Middle School Site Committee initiated the process by working with Mr. Hajek to define the criteria for the position. Several residents responded to the Board’s invitation to make suggestions, as well; other comments are still welcome, and can be communicated to any member of the School Board, or to Mr. Hajek.
Our plan is to spread as wide a net as possible to attract applicants. Ads (in the New York Times and professional journals), personal contacts and networking, and a website posting are the major means of disseminating information about the opening. Then, a Search Committee made up of 3 administrators, 3 teachers and 3 parents will develop an interview format and thoroughly evaluate the applicants. Direct observation will be an important part of these evaluations. Finally, the 3 or 4 top candidates will be presented to the Board for a final round of intensive interviews. Our goal is to have a new principal named by mid-April to allow for a transition period.
Obviously, the choice of a new Superintendent is the most important challenge facing the district. We are committed to conducting a thorough, professional process of identification and evaluation of excellent candidates. That effort begins with a search for an appropriate consultant to assist us. The Board will be exploring the services offered by BOCES and the State School Board Association, as well as private consultants. As outlined in the district reorganization plan, we have allocated almost a full year for this effort, which should enable us to name a new Superintendent well before Mr. Hajek’s departure in December.
