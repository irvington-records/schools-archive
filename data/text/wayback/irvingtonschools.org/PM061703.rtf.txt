Gr. I	CALL TO ORDER

Mrs. Janice Silverberg called the Public Meeting to order at 7:37 p.m., and the pledge of allegiance was given. 

II	MINUTES OF MEETINGS

A motion was made by Mr. George Huzinec, seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel, to approve the minutes of the Public Meeting of May 20, 2003, Public Budget Hearing of May 20, 2003 and the Special Meeting of June 10, 2003.      
Vote:  5-0 Approved

III	INFORMATION ITEMS 

	Dr. Beni reported on the following:
Dr. Beni reported that the budget passed 546 yes, 178 no.  Dr. Beni thanked the community for their support.
The incoming Kindergarten enrollment is being monitored, class size is currently at 22 per section.  One section will be added.
Grades 1 and 2 enrollments are now also close to the high projections and being monitored.  An additional section may be added to Grade 1.  
The Unification Church is seeking tax exempt status which, if granted, will result in a $708,00 tax loss to the school district.  Based on the total current assessed value, there is a potential of a 3 ½ % tax increase to district taxpayers.  Dr. Beni said that this is a very serious issue and moving rapidly.

Mrs. Silverberg congratulated Mr. Mandel on being named the first recipient of the Jamie Sterling award.  

IV	COMMENTS FROM RESIDENTS - None

 V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

Superintendent’s Report:
1.  Recognition of Scholar Athlete Awards:  Girls Track and Field Team, Boys Track and Field Team 
Dr. Beni introduced Mr. McCormack for the presentation to the Girls Track and Field Team and the Boys Track and Field Team.  Each of these teams have been recognized by NYSPHSAA as having the highest academic average of any team in New York State.  Mr. McCormack reported that there are 
53, 650 teams in New York State vying for 29 academic awards and that Irvington has won four (4) of these awards.    

Mr. McCormack introduced Mrs. Margaret Pesick, representing the New York State Public High Schools Athletic Association.  The Girls Track and Field Team has been recognized by the NYSPHSAA as having achieved the highest team GPA, 96.7, first out of a total of 272  teams in New York State.  The team was presented with a banner suitable for hanging. 

Mr. McCormack presented the NYPHSAA banner to the Boys Track and Field Team for the academic achievement of being 1st out of 201 teams in the state with a 99.0 GPA.  Noted also was the achievement that, in addition to academic excellence, the team has a record of winning 104 dual meets 


over a period of nine (9) years.  Mr. Oley, track and field coach of 47 years, congratulated the team for their dedication to excellence in all areas.  

Master Plan Update:  Peter Gisolfi Associates, JMOA Engineering, P.C.
Mr. Scott Baird of JMOA Engineering, P.C. presented slides to describe the construction status at the Middle/High School campus.  The project is on, or ahead, of schedule.  

Dr. Beni reported that the “move” has begun.  The High School and Middle School are being packed, CSE has temporarily moved to the Main Street School Library and Transportation is occupying space in the District Office.  Asbestos abatement is set to begin shortly at the High School.
 
Dows Lane Grade K-3 Report:  Mr. Joseph Rodriguez (Supplemental Log – Appendix A)
Mr. Rodriguez presented the report developed by the Kindergarten Committee comprised of parents, teachers and an administrator.  The report discusses the Kindergarten child, how young children learn, Kindergarten philosophy, the Kindergarten program – extended or full day, Kindergarten eligibility, readiness/assessment and the content of Kindergarten. After considerable research and consideration, a full day Kindergarten program will begin at Dows Lane this Fall.  Mr. Rodriguez thanked the committee for their efforts and thoughtfulness.

Mrs. Silverberg thanked Mr. Rodriguez for his report.
