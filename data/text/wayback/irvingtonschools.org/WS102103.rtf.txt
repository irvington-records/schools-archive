The following are the minutes of the Public Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington Campus Presentation Room, October 21, 2003, with the following present:

Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mr. George Huzinec.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman - Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mr. Sheldon Levine – Middle School Interim Principal, Mr. Donald Kuhn – Main Street School Interim Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Elementary School Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor - Middle School Assistant Principal, Ms. Eileen McGuire – Dows Lane Elementary School Assistant Principal, Mrs. Susan Levey and Mrs. Sheralyn Gottlieb – Directors of Pupil Personnel Services, Mr. Micah Orifici – Interim Director of Technology, faculty members and members of the community.

Prior to the meeting, the PTSA hosted a brief reception in honor of the Irvington Board of Education members in observance of School Board Recognition Week.

The Public Work Session was called to order by Mrs. Delany at 7:38 p.m.  Mrs. Delany welcomed the students being honored for their scholastic achievement.  Dr. Beni added that this is a very select group of students, the district is very proud of them and is pleased and privileged to have them in our schools.   

RECOGNITION OF NATIONAL MERIT SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM HONOREES

Dr. Mosenthal explained that these students are in a group of less that 1% of high school students in the country and that their achievement is a real triumph for everyone involved.  Dr. Mosenthal introduced each of the National Merit Scholarship Program semi-finalists and presented them with a Certificate of Achievement from the district.  The honorees are:  Bernard Bluhm, Miriam Farber, Jenna Lazar, Frederica Lipmann, Eugene Petracca and Christine Wang.  These students will move on to the next level of the National Merit program.  The Board members congratulated each student and gave them each a Certificate of Achievement.  

Mr. Mandel commented that year after year our High School produces several semi-finalists and finalists, a very prestigious accomplishment.  Mrs. Delany thanked the students for coming.

The Agenda was changed as follows:

	Mrs. Francie Malina and Mrs. Debbie Weintraub, PTSA President and President-Elect respectively, gave each Board member a replica of the Tarrytown Lighthouse in acknowledgment of School Board Recognition Week.  Mrs. Malina thanked the Board members for their contributions to the district, leadership, advice, direction and guidance.  

STUDENT PERFORMANCE RESULTS

Dr. Beni informed the Board that the Board of Regents is taking significant action in approach to the Math A Regents exam as well as looking into the Physics and Global Studies exams.  Questions are being raised regarding the entire New York State testing system and students were really affected this year by difficulties with the testing program. 

Dr. Beni introduced Dr. Dolan-Waldman for the Student Performance review.  The focus of the student performance review conducted by the District is to:

Improve student performance
Review multi-year performance to see trends within content areas
Guide professional development
Guide curriculum development
Target interventions in specific content areas







Dr. Dolan-Waldman reviewed the compilations of testing data for each major content area (Supplemental Log – Appendix A).  The presentation included the impact of the reviews and changes that have been implemented.

ACADEMIC INTERVENTION SERVICES

Mrs. Levey and Mrs. Gottlieb reviewed the eligibility criteria for Academic Intervention Services for grades K – 12 and the types of assessments, including mandated testing, being utilized.  A list was reviewed of descriptions, by grade level, of the types of support services which may be provided to students in need.  

Also discussed were additions to personnel that the District has made, changes in the teacher/student ratio and the core group of specially trained teachers.  Individualized plans are developed for each student and revised as necessary.  Notification to parents of student progress are made quarterly, parent conferences are scheduled at least once each semester and suggestions are made to parents to encourage support and involvement in the home.     

Mrs. Delany thanked everyone for their presentations.

The meeting was adjourned at 10:30 p.m.
