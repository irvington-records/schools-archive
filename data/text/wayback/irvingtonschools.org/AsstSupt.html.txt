The Irvington Board of Education announced today it will appoint Dr. Ralph Napolitano as the district's new Assistant Superintendent of Schools for Instruction. Dr. Napolitano, who is presently the principal of Pleasantville High School, will start his new job this July. He replaces Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman, who retires June 30th.
The formal vote appointing Dr. Napolitano will take place on Tuesday, June 8th at the Board's next public meeting.
"During my visits to the Irvington School District, I was extremely impressed with the commitment of the Board of Education, Superintendent, staff and parents to the Districts' students," said Dr. Napolitano, a resident of Eastchester, who has served as principal of Pleasantville High Schol since 1995. "As Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, I look forward to collaborating with such outstanding people. Together, we will continue to advance the District's goals to provide an excellent educational experience for all students at every grade level."
Under his leadership, Pleasantville High School was named a New York State School of Excellence in 1999-2000. Prior to his career in Pleasantville, Dr. Napolitano served as principal of St. Catharine's Academy, a private school in the Bronx from 1987-95 where he was named "Compassionate Educator of the Year" by the Archdiocese of New York.
From 1979-87, he served as chairperson of the Department of Teacher Preparation at the College of Mount St. Vincent where he supervised an educational competency program for elementary, secondary and special education teachers. He also taught social studies to 7-9th grader students at the Frank Whalen Junior High School in the Bronx from 1977 to 1979 where he also served a a teacher trainer for staff development involving teaching reading in content areas. Dr. Napolitano earned his doctorate in administrative and instructional leadership from St. John's University in Queens, N.Y., where he also received his master's degree in secondary education and bachelor's degree in social studies.
