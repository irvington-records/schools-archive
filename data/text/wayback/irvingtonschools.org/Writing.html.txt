Irvington High School junior Arianna Boffey was recently accepted to attend one of the most prestigious writing conferences in the northeast --- the New England Writer's Conference ( also known as "Breadloaf"), held at Middlebury College in Vermont.
Arianna, who attended the four day conference last month, said "it was a great experience to be in a place where everyone is feeling the same enthusiasm for the craft of writing." "It was also wonderful to share ideas and get feedback on your writing from published authors."
To get accepted to the "Breadloaf" Conference, Arianna had to submit a writing sample. "Arianna is an extremely talented writer who has a wonderful eye for detail," said her English teacher Lee Klein. Although several hundred students apply to the program, only about 100 are accepted, based on their writing sample. "This is a great honor for Arianna and for the entire school district," added Klein.
