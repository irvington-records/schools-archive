III.	INFORMATION ITEMS

Dr. Matusiak introduced Mr. Ray Kuntz, Principal of the District’s new law firm, Kuntz, Spagnuolo, Scapoli and Schiro.  Dr. Matusiak stated that six law firms were interviewed in the Spring 2005 and that Kuntz, Spagnuolo, Scapoli and Schiro possessed the perfect mix of what Irvington was looking for in a new firm: local, well versed in municipal and education law, yet small enough to meet the District’s need for responsiveness when issues arise.

Mr. Kuntz thanked Dr. Matusiak and said that the firm was familiar with Irvington and the District’s reputation for excellence in education.  He and his firm look forward to working with the District.

Discussion of Proposal for Artificial Turfed Fields and Other Enhancements

Mr. Paul Mandel stated that the Board had asked Mr. Artie McCormack to prepare a curfew proposal for the existing and proposed fields and tennis courts.  Mr. McCormack, along with Dr. Scott Mosenthal, proposed the following curfew schedule:

Mid-March – end of June and August 15-November 30
Monday – Thursday: no later than 9:30 p.m.
Friday – Saturday: no later than 10:00 p.m.

December – Mid-March
Monday – Saturday: no later than 6:00 p.m. (unless weather permits for conditioning and practice).

July – August 15
Mr. McCormack stated that the District has no essential need for the fields during the summer, unless there is a special occasion, but his recommendation for a proposed curfew would be Monday – Saturday until 9:30 p.m.

A resident of Fieldpoint asked if consideration was given to the residents of Fieldpoint in developing the recommended curfew. Mr. McCormack responded by saying that the Board asked for his recommendations based on concerns expressed by residents of Fieldstone at a previous meeting, regarding the lighting of East Field, the parking area, and the noise factor.  

Mr. Howard Epstein presented a letter from 25 residents of Fieldpoint to the Board of Education outlining their concerns regarding the proposal to renovate and add lighting to East Field and the proposed tennis courts. The letter also provided recommendations on relocating the tennis courts and East Field to reduce the impact on the residents of Fieldpoint. The Board accepted the information. 
 
Residents of Riverview Road and Fieldpoint expressed their concerns regarding the drainage issue.  Mr. Mandel stated that the work on the driveway had been completed and that the architectural engineer is well aware of the previous problems and has taken measures in the design of the drainage systems to mitigate further drainage problems.  

Several residents expressed concern that the District was “biting off more than it could chew” regarding the impact that the funding of the proposal would have on taxes and next year’s budget discussions. Members of the Board reiterated their primary responsibility was to the educational programs of the District, but that physical education and athletics program are part of a well-rounded education. The Board continues to attempt to balance the academic needs with the athletic needs of the District. Due to the explosion of the athletics program, it became necessary to present a proposal to the community for enhancement of the District’s fields.  

The original timeline was to conclude discussions at the October 11th Public Meeting and a vote by the Board to adopt the proposal, but Mr. Mandel stated that more time might be needed for the environmental process and that the date might be pushed back. Mr. James Reese stated that the District has made no filings for SEQRA at this time. 

Mr. Mandel stated that an informational forum is scheduled for Saturday, October 1st at a special Board of Education meeting to allow for further discussion by community members, and that the Board is listening very carefully to everyone’s concerns.
