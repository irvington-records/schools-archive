Dr. Vincent Beni Named Head of Irvington Schools
Penny Delany, President of the Irvington Board of Education, announced that a nation-wide search for a new district Superintendent ended very close to home with the selection of Dr. Vincent Beni for the position.
Dr. Beni is presently the District Superintendent for Southern Westchester BOCES which provides services to the region's 35 school districts. During his three and a half years in this position, Dr. Beni implemented a new and comprehensive Strategic Plan, reduced administrative charges to member districts by 31%, and introduced a variety of shared services' opportunities in areas such as technology, curriculum development, arts and construction management. Dr. Beni served on the State Education Commissioner's Advisory Board on Standards and Graduation Requirements, and is responsible for ensuring that all schools in the region are prepared to raise academic standards to meet the new requirements.
In his previous position as Superintendent of Schools for the Hastings-on-Hudson School District (1988-1995), Dr. Beni effected a turnaround of a history of budget and bond defeats and put in place a multi-year fiscal plan. During his tenure, Hastings students demonstrated significant gains in achievement at all levels in all subject areas and the district accommodated a 38% increase in enrollment with only 7 additional teachers.
Dr. Beni began his career as a classroom teacher in the Bronx, and then taught and coached in Thornwood, NY. Before coming to Hastings, Dr. Beni served as Middle School Assistant Principal, District Curriculum Director and Assistant Superintendent in the Pearl River School District. Throughout, Dr. Beni has enjoyed a reputation as a dedicated educator and a gifted administrator, with a real talent for building leadership teams and working with community groups.
Dr. Beni is choosing to return to a school district because he is enthusiastic about working with faculty and parents to implement the standards he helped develop for all children. He decided to pursue the position in Irvington, where he will begin in January, 1999 because he sees Irvington as a community with a strong commitment to education where residents value the quality of their schools. Dr Beni says: "My record of personal commitment and professional accomplishments will enable me to provide creative, decisive leadership to enable Irvington to become the best small school district in New York State." Mrs. Delany concurs: "Dr. Beni has a unique combination of personal qualities and professional experiences that make him an ideal match for Irvington at this time. We're facing a big increase in enrollment -- he's handled that before. We're coming out of a real fiscal crisis and he knows how to manage that. And, as we are working to ensure that our K through 12 curriculum is responsive to the State standards, we'll have Dr. Beni, who helped develop those standards, to guide us. The Board is very enthusiastic about having Dr. Beni to lead our district into the next century."
Dr. Beni will replace Richard A. Hajek, who is retiring at the end of December, after 36 1/2 years with the District. Mr. Hajek has been Principal of Irvington High School for 25 years and will have served as both Superintendent and High School Principal for his final three years in Irvington.
