The following are the minutes of the Special Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Campus Presentation Room, July 27, 2004, with the following present:


Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mrs. Carol Gutman.  Also present:  Dr. Kathleen Matusiak – Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Ralph Napolitano – Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, members of the faculty and community.  Absent:  Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Janice Silverberg. 

I	CALL TO ORDER 

	The Special Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Penny Delany at 5:34 p.m. 

II	MINUTES OF MEETINGS
	
A motion was made by Mrs. Diane Mundree, seconded by Mrs. Carol Gutman, to adopt the Public Meetings of June 8, 2004, June 29, 2004 and the Public Work Session of June 22, 2004 as presented.
Vote:  3-0 Approved  

III		BUSINESS ITEMS

Stipulation of Settlement

A motion was made by Mrs. Carol Gutman, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to adopt the following Resolution as presented:

“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District hereby ratifies and approves the Stipulation of Settlement with regard to the claim of the parents of student no. 3741 and authorizes the Superintendent of Schools to execute same and take all actions provided for therein.”
Vote:  3-0 Approved

2004-2005 Warrant – Town of Greenburgh

A motion was made by Mrs. Carol Gutman, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to adopt the following:   

“MOVED, that the voters of the Irvington Union Free School District by a vote of 462 to 193 adopted a budget for the school year commencing July 1, 2004  in the amount of $38,545,252; that the sums as set forth in the forms of budget heretofore submitted to the voters be appropriated; that the taxes in the necessary amounts be levied therefore; that the Board members be authorized to execute a warrant therefore, and that the warrant be delivered to the Town authorities.”
Vote:  3-0 Approved

Dr. Matusiak commented on her first 2 weeks in the District by thanking the Administrators for all of their assistance, adding that she is thrilled with the addition of Dr. Ralph Napolitano, Assistant Superintendent for Instruction as well as Scott Fried, Irvington Middle School Principal.  Highlights of the first 2 weeks include:

meetings with the PTSA co-presidents, attorneys from Keane & Beane, Buildings & Grounds Committee and the Chamber of Commerce breakfast.
Exploration of the Middle States Accreditation process for all four schools in the District has begun.
planning for the Tri-States visitation is underway.
change in Public Relations personnel to provide better service and save money.  The person selected will meet with administrators during the retreat in August.
planning underway to recommend the hiring of a Human Resources specialist to streamline the many facets of personnel including mentoring, certification issues and SED mandates.
exploration of Cablevision televising.
the filling of many positions for 2004-05.  Interviewing is on-going and all positions are expected to be filled shortly.
Ms. Jennifer McArdle is the selection to fill the position of English Program Chairperson for the 2004-05 school year.

	Dr. Matusiak commented on the warm reception that she has received and is looking forward to a great year.
