The following are the minutes of the Public Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library, August 27, 2002, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman - Assistant Superintendent Instruction, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mrs. Lauren Allan – Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodrigues – Dows Lane Gr. K-3  Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Dows Lane Gr. 4-5 Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor, Middle School - Assistant Principal, Mrs. Susan Levey – Director Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar – Director of Technology, Mr. George Kuntz – Superintendent of Buildings and Grounds,  Mr. Peter Gisolfi and Mrs. Sandy Mintzes - Peter Gisolfi Associates, Mr. Michael Orifici & Mr. Scott Baird - JMOA Engineering, members of the community.  Absent:  Mrs. Diane Mundree  

The Public Work Session was called to order by Mrs. Janice Silverberg 7:32 p.m. 

1.  CONSTRUCTION UPDATE

	Dr. Beni introduced Mr. Peter Gisolfi and Ms. Sandy Mintzes from Peter Gisolfi Associates to present an update on the progress of the Master Plan design.  The drawings of the new buildings were briefly reviewed and samples of exterior building facades, roofs and accent colors were displayed and discussed.  Mr. Gisolfi also presented the suggested colors for interior walls, floors, doors and restrooms.  

	Mr. Orifici reported on the construction status, which is at or ahead of schedule, of each of the new buildings and also the summer High School demolition work.

Mr. Baird and Mr. Kuntz reviewed the summer work accomplished at the Main Street Middle School (Supplemental Log - Appendix B).  Mr. Kuntz said that during the roof and eave repairs additional damage was discovered and evaluated to be structural.  More repairs are required and will be addressed in the 5 Year Plan.

There was discussion of construction scheduling when students return to the High School campus.  

Mrs. Silverberg adjourned the Work Session to the Public Meeting at 8:25 p.m.
