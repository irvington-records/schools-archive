The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library, August 27, 2002, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman - Assistant Superintendent Instruction, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mrs. Lauren Allan – Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Gr. K-3  Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Dows Lane Gr. 4-5 Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor, Middle School - Assistant Principal, Mrs. Susan Levey – Director Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar – Director of Technology, Mr. George Kuntz – Superintendent of Buildings and Grounds,  Mr. Peter Gisolfi and Mrs. Sandy Mintzes - Peter Gisolfi Associates, Mr. Michael Orifici & Mr. Scott Baird - JMOA Engineering, and members of the community. I.  	CALL TO ORDER

The Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Janice Silverberg at 7:31 p.m., the pledge of allegiance was given.

Mrs. Silverberg adjourned to a Public Work Session at 7:32 p.m.
 
	Following the Work Session, Mrs. Silverberg called the Public Meeting to order at 8:25 p.m. 

II	MINUTES OF MEETINGS

A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany, to approve the minutes of the Public Meeting of July 2, 2002 and the Special Public Meeting of July 23, 2002
Vote:  4-0 Approved

III	INFORMATION ITEMS  (Superintendent’s Report below)
			
IV	COMMENTS FROM RESIDENTS - None	
 
 V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

  Superintendent’s Report
Dr. Beni updated the Board regarding the summer activities in the District:
Administrative Council Retreat
Meeting of the Administrative Council, Program Chairs, Team Leaders and Grade Level Coordinators to discuss plans for the pending moves, schedules, transportation, etc.
Continuing implementation of the curriculum cycle
Student performance discussions

Dr. Beni also reported the following:
Due to the retirement incentive, plans are underway for a massive recruitment effort for 2003-04
Special Education requests for hearings and subsequent litigations
The Community Advisory Board is exploring activities for  increased student support with participation from several community groups
Local legislators will be invited to tour the District during the week of September 16 which is Legislators Back To School Week
The Irvington girls swim team has merged with teams from Edgemont and Solomon Schechter and the season is underway

B.    Membership Dues:  Westchester-Putnam School Boards Association
	A motion was made by Mrs. Penny Delany, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to approve the payment of dues for the 2002-2003 school year to the Westchester-Putnam School Boards Association in the amount of $3,320.00.
	Vote:  4-0 approved

C.    NYSSBA Board of Directors Election
	A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to cast a vote for Anne Byrne, Area 10 nominee for the Board of Directors of the New York State School Boards Association.
	Vote:  4-0 Approved

	A motion was made by Mr.  George Huzinec, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany, to accept the donation of $1,000 from the J.P. Morgan Chase Foundation in recognition of Mr. Mandel’s 100 hours of volunteer service to the Irvington School District.
	Vote:  4-0 Approved

VI          BUSINESS ITEMS

Treasurer’s Report
A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to accept the Treasurer’s Report for June 2002 as presented.
       Vote:  4-0 Approved

B.    2002-03 Budget Adjustment
A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to adopt the following Resolution:

RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District increase the 2002-03 budget appropriations $50,000 for special education costs and increase the 2002-03 estimated revenues, property tax levy, $50,000.
Vote:  4-0 Approved

C.  	 2002-2003 Warrant - Town of Greenburgh
A motion was made by Mrs. Penny Delany, seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel, to approve the Warrant - Town of Greenburgh for the 2002-2003 school year for the collection of taxes in the amount of $26,731,831.00.
Vote:  4-0 Approved

D.	Auditor’s Recommendation:  2001-02 Budget
	A motion was made by Mrs. Penny Delany, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to adopt the following Resolution:

	RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District has determined that the entire Tax Certiorari Reserve is not needed and authorizes $500,000 of this reserve be released to the undesignated fund balance.
	Vote:  4-0 Approved

E.    Summer 2002 Transportation Contract
A motion was made by Mr. George Huzinec, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany, to approve the following:

“Motion to approve the transportation contract for summer school busing by Southern Westchester BOCES for the period July 8, 2002 to August 30, 2002 in the amount of $13,595.00.”
Vote:  4-0 Approved

F.    School Lunch Program - Bid Award
	A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany, to adopt the following Resolution:

	RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the School Lunch Program Management Services Bid to the Fine Host Corporation as the lowest responsive and responsible bidder with a base bid price of $0.372 per meal.
	Vote:  4-0 Approved

G.  	Bond Anticipation Note Bid Result
	Mr. Reese reported that TGH Securities has been awarded the bid for the $12.5 million Bond Anticipation Note (BAN) with an effective net interest cost of 1.4182%.  Funds will be received by the District on August 28, 2002.
