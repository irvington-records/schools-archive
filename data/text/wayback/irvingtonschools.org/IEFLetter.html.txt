September 21, 2001
Dear Friend and Neighbor:
The Irvington Education Foundation shares in the sorrow of the tragic losses that have resulted from the horrific events of September 11. Our deepest sympathies go out to all those affected.
While we considered canceling or postponing our October 27 Reach For The Stars 
fundraising gala, in light of what has occurred we now see this event as an opportunity 
to join together in support of our children.
As our nation undertakes the mission of securing our country's future, it is more important than ever to ensure the strength and health of that future by continuing to enrich our children's education. The mission of the Irvington Education Foundation is to raise funds to support special projects within all the Irvington public schools, which will enhance our children's educational experience.
The Irvington Education Foundation, together with the support of our community, is committed to dedicating its efforts to helping our children during this challenging time. More than ever, we encourage you to join us as we come together as a community on October 27.
Please respond promptly to the invitation you received last week along with the request for goods and services to be auctioned at the event, or for monetary donations. Also be sure to call or e-mail your table reservations.
We look forward to sharing an evening with you on October 27.
Sincerely,
Irvington Education Foundation Board and Gala Committee
