            MSS Begins Year With Annual Welcome Back BBQ
        
More than 500 students and families gathered on Sept. 10 for Main Street School’s annual Welcome Back BBQ, a tradition that brings the school community together to celebrate the start of a new year. 
Fourth and fifth graders enjoyed delicious food and sweet treats and plenty of dancing. The event provided an opportunity for everyone to reconnect with friends and play on the school’s playground. Special thanks to the parent volunteers, whose support with setup, cleanup and food and water service helped make the event a success.
