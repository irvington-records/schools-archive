Safety & Security
- The District is currently undergoing a Safety and Security Assessment, typically done every few years. Our District Level Safety Plan is posted annually by August 31 and can be found below. Each school building has a Building Level Safety Plan which remains confidential to the Safety Team of each building per law. We hold district wide Safety Committee meetings throughout the year to review important issues. Additionally, we are currently in the planning process for a capital project bond vote.  We intend to include several initiatives that will look to strengthen our facilities. Please stay tuned for more information as this process develops.
