            Interactive Lesson Builds Literacy Skills Through Multisensory Learning
        
Dows Lane Elementary School first graders recently began an engaging Fundations lesson focused on decoding and encoding words with glued sounds. The interactive session in Dina Lenhardt and Julie Giacomantonio’s class incorporated multisensory techniques, such as word mapping and magnetic tiles, to deepen understanding and participation.
