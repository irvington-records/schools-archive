             Tuesday, February 12 - BOE Meeting Rescheduled 
        
Due to inclement weather, the Irvington schools will be closed today, Tuesday, February 12, 2019. All school and district events are cancelled today.
The Board of Education meeting scheduled for tonight will be rescheduled for Wednesday, February 13, 2019 at 7:30 pm in the Campus Presentation Room (CPR in the library of the Middle School/High School Campus located at 40 N. Broadway, Irvington, NY 10533
