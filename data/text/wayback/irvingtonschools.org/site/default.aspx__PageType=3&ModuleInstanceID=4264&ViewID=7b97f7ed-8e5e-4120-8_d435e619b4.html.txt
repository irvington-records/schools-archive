Girls Swimming Wins Sectional Title
Girls Swimming Wins Sectional Title
The combined Sleepy Hollow-Hastings-Edgemont-Irvington (SHEDI) swimming team won the Sectional championship title at Felix Festa Middle School on Nov. 1. Irvington swimmers Grace Brady and Ava Franks were instrumental in the team’s success. Franks was a member of the winning 200-meter free relay, as well as the 400-meter relay that placed in third place. She also placed second in the 200-meter individual medley and the 100-meter breaststroke. Brady placed seventh in the 500-meter freestyle and 14th in the 200-meter freestyle. Both swimmers qualified to compete in the state championship, which will be held at Ithaca College on Nov. 16 and 17.
