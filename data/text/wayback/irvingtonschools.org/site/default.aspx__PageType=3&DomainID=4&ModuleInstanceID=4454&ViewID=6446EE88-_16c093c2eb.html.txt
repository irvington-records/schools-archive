Important: February 13, 2019 Board of Education Meeting Time Change
As a result of yesterday's storm and the subsequent District closure, the Board of Education meeting that was scheduled for yesterday, Tuesday, February 12th, was rescheduled for tonight, Wednesday, February 13th at 7:30 pm. The change in the meeting date introduced a few scheduling challenges that will result in the following shifts in tonight’s meeting times:
6:00 pm: BOE Meeting Call to Order and Anticipated Executive Session
7:00 pm: Conclusion of the Anticipated Executive Session
7:00-8:15 pm: Board will recess; no business will be attended to
8:15 pm: Public BOE meeting will be called back to order
When the meeting is called back to order at 8:15 pm tonight, the Board of Education will facilitate a brief discussion related to the Capital Project. This will then be followed by the published agenda that includes the first in a series of budget-related presentations, a presentation of the initial 2019-20 District calendar, and other items as identified within the agenda. The agenda can viewed on BoardDocs by clicking here.
We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause for the school community, but be assured that the Board of Education and the administration is making every effort to ensure that this week’s meeting is held as timely as possible and that all District business is attended to.
