            Dows Lane Students Learn About Book Making Process
        
Dows Lane Elementary School students were treated to a virtual visit by author Kevin Lewis on Nov. 12. During his visit, Lewis read aloud from his books “Chugga-Chugga Choo-Choo” and “My Truck Is Stuck!” He also shared his reading life and the process of book making. The interactive author visit was made possible thanks to the generous support of the Irvington Education Foundation’s Grant Committee.
