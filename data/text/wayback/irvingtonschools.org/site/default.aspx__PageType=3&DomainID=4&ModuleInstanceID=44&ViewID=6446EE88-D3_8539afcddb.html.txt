Fourth Graders Immerse Themselves in Lenape Culture
Main Street School fourth graders, who have been studying about the lives of local Native Americans, recently visited the Ward Pound Ridge Reservation in Cross River to bring their studies to life.
During the field trip, the students participated in an interactive educational program, called Indian Lore. They learned about the lives of local Native Americans, the Lenape, and how these local Eastern Woodland Indians lived during the period of first contact with the European settlers.
In addition, the students discussed three key aspects of Lenape life that included hunting, gathering and farming of food sources. They experienced village life in the museum’s traditional wigwam, and learned about the use of stone, wood, bone and other natural materials for tool manufacturing, as well as the games, toys and leisure activities of the Lenape people.
