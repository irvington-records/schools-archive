MSS Celebrates Fifth Graders at Moving Up Ceremony
Main Street School celebrated its fifth graders during a moving up ceremony on June 18. The event marked an important milestone as the students prepared to take the next step in their academic journey to Irvington Middle School.
The students took center stage throughout the event – leading the color guard, performing a song accompanied on piano by their peers, and sharing heartfelt memories from their fourth- and fifth-grade years.
Principal Joyce Chapnick and Superintendent of Schools Dr. Mara Ratesic shared inspiring remarks, while Irvington Middle School Principal David Sottile welcomed the rising sixth graders to their new school community. In her remarks, Chapnick commended the students for growing as readers, writers, scientists, historians, mathematicians, artists, musicians and critical thinkers, highlighting their readiness to embrace the next chapter of their education.
“Through your daily choices and learning, you have embodied the Portrait of a Graduate,” she said. “You have been courageous learners, willing to take risks, ask questions and grow from challenges. You’ve named the Habits of Mind and lived them — from persisting through difficult tasks, acting thoughtfully, thinking flexibly and reflecting metacognitively on both the process and the outcome of learning.”
