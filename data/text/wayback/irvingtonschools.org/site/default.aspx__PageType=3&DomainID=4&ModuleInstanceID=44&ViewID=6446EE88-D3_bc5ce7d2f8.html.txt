Eighth Graders Move Up to Irvington High School
Irvington Middle School eighth graders celebrated the culmination of their hard work over the last three years during a moving-up ceremony on June 22.
During the ceremony, student representatives Max Bloom, Isabella Ding and Alyssa Magallon addressed their classmates and reflected on their experiences in middle school. The graduates also heard from Superintendent of Schools Dr. Kristopher Harrison, Irvington Middle School Principal David Sottile and Irvington High School Acting Principal Michelleann DeFilippis.
Signifying the conclusion of one chapter and the beginning of another, the students were called one by one to accept their certificates, which were handed out by Sottile and Irvington Middle School Assistant Principal Allyson Daley. The students will embark on the next phase of their educational journey as high school freshmen in the fall.
“I am so proud of this eighth grade cohort,” teacher and team leader Gregg Pernick said. “They are kind, caring, hardworking and resilient. They will be missed in this school, but there’s no doubt that they’ve left their mark.”
