            Third Graders Prepare Songs for Choir Performance
        
In preparation for an upcoming choir performance, third graders sang “It’s All Right” by Jon Batiste in Genevieve Lotze’s music class. They have been focusing on the melody and rhythm of the lyrics, as well as learning about their own voices and understanding what it means to be an individual voice that contributes to and blends in with the larger vocal ensemble. The students are also learning about healthy practices, such as proper breathing, the importance of vocal warmups and performance posture.
