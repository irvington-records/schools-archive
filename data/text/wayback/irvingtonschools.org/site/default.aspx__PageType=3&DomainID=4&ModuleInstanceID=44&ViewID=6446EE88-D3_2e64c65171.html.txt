Irvington Schools Break Ground on Phase 2 Projects
The Irvington UFSD is pleased to have broken ground on Phase 2 of its capital project. Included in this work is the following:
Dows Lane:
- Renovation of the library and PLTW/STEM classrooms, to construct the new Creativity Suite.
- Installation of key card access system.
Main Street School:
- Renovation of the PLTW/STEM classroom and computer lab.
- Installation of air conditioning in the gymnasium.
- Renovation of the building’s cupola.
- Installation of a new power service line.
- Repair of a waste line.
- Installation of key card access system.
Middle School/High School Campus:
- Construction of a new high school entrance.
- Installation of security vestibules at the high school.
- Renovation of the campus library.
- Installation of new high school HVAC controls.
- Replacement of high school classroom univent heating units.
- Reconfiguration of the high school parking lot and campus traffic patterns.
- Construction of a maintenance storage facility.
- Installation of key card access system.
Please note that parking at the high school/middle school campus will be limited to the upper lots only for the foreseeable future as the District regrades and repaves. Additionally, while some trees had to be removed during this process, the District will plant new trees in September to replace those that had to be removed.
The District extends its appreciation to the school community for their patience and cooperation during this exciting time, as these facility upgrades will enhance learning, improve student and staff safety and expand ongoing energy-efficiency efforts.
