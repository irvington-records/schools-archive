On Wednesday, November 4, 2020, the District and Dows Lane Elementary School administrations facilitated a forum for the Dows Lane parent community; wherein, the details associated with the possible expansion of the AM/PM attendance model to the 2nd and 3rd grade was reviewed.  All Dows Lane parents are encouraged to watch the video of the meeting and review the presentation.
 
All Dows Lane parents will be emailed a survey to complete to provide the District with information critical to informing the determination as to whether the AM/PM attendance model will be implemented for all grades at Dows Lane Elementary School.
