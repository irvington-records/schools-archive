            Third Grader Earns Academic Achievement Award
        
Dows Lane Elementary School third grader Dylan Leffler has been recognized with the New York State Comptroller’s Student Achievement Award. He was recognized for being a student who is thoughtful, kind, and a leader in his grade level and around the entire school.
Each year, Comptroller Thomas DiNapoli's awards recognize students who show academic excellence and leadership potential through activities that improve their school or community.
