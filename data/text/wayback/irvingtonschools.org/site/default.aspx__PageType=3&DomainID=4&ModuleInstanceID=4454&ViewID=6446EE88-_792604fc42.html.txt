            October 12, 2021-Board of Education Regular Meeting
        
For the October 12, 2021  Board of Education Regular Meeting, the Board of Education Trustees and Administration will meet in person in the Campus Presentation Room, community members are not permitted to attend in person. Community members will be able to view the meeting and submit questions via Zoom. 
The Board of Education will permit up to 30 minutes for public comment. Those wishing to comment on topics that appear on the agenda or wish to pose a question to the Board of Education are invited to place their name and address in the Zoom Q&A. The Board of Education President will invite the respective members of the community to speak.  Each speaker will have up to three minutes to speak.  The Board of Education requests that the Q&A function not be used for other comments, communications or questions.
