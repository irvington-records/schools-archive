            October 22, 2020 Board of Education Meeting
        
For the October 22, 2020 Board of Education Special Meeting/Work Session, the Board of Education will meet in person in the Campus Presentation Room (CPR) on the main campus (40 N. Broadway, Irvington), beginning at 7:30 pm. 
No public attendees will be permitted to attend in person. Community members can view the meeting by following the link below. 
The Board of Education will permit up to 30 minutes for public comment. Those wishing to comment on topics that appear on the agenda or wish to pose a question to the Board of Education are invited to place their name and address in the Zoom Q&A. The Board of Education President will invite the respective members of the community to speak.  Each speaker will have up to three (3) minutes to speak.  The Board of Education requests that the Q&A function not be used for other comments, communications or questions.
