            8/18/20 Draft 2020-21 Code of Conduct - Posting for Public Comment 
        
The Draft 2020-21 Code of Conduct has been posted for public comment.  The draft Code of Conduct includes an addendum unique for the upcoming school year related to the implications of the COVID-19 pandemic on instruction and school operations.  Be advised that the Board of Education anticipates adopting the Code of Conduct at its August 25, 2020 meeting.  Should a member of the school community wish to comment on the proposed 2020-21 Code of Conduct, one is encouraged to email Dr. Harrison, Kristopher.Harrison@IrvingtonSchools.org, or the Board of Education, Board@IrvingtonSchools.org.
