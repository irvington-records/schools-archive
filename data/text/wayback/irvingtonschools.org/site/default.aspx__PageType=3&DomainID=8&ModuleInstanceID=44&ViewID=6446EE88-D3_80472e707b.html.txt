Dows Lane Students Enjoy PE in Newly Renovated Gym
Dows Lane Elementary School students have been working on reestablishing routines in their physical education classes and enjoying using the newly renovated gym. Tiffany Flynn and Marissa Stracuzzi said they have been challenging their students to different warmups on the racetrack.
“Students travel at different speeds in the same direction, practicing different locomotor movements such as walking, skipping, galloping, sliding, jumping, hopping, jogging and running,” Flynn said. “Due to the different COVID-19 restrictions and changes of space, we have not gotten to see students perform these skills until now, and it has been excellent practice to be able to teach these different locomotor movements.”
Flynn and Stracuzzi said they are excited to be working in the new space and look forward to all the exciting physical education activities they have planned for their students throughout the school year.
