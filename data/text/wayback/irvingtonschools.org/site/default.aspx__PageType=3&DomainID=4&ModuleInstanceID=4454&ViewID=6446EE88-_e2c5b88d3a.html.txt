On Monday, June 22nd, the Irvington UFSD facilitated its final COVID-19 surveillance testing clinic of the 2020-21 school year; wherein, 75 students and staff members were tested. The District is pleased to report that all test results were negative.
Over the course of this spring, nearly 700 students and staff members were tested through the District's surveillance testing program with all results being negative. This is a testament to the school community's commitment to implementing all recommended safeguards. 
The District extends its gratitude to all students, staff members and school families for their continued dedication to supporting the health of each other.
