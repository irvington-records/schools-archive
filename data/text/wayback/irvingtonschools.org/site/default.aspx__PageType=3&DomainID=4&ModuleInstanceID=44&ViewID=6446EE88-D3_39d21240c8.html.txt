            MSS Students Hike for Human Rights
        
Main Street School students participated in their Hike for Human Rights on June 5 and raised more than $800 for charity organizations. Throughout their walk, they carried signs they made in honor of their identities and shared information about causes that are important to them.
Principal Joyce Chapnick said the walk was a great success, as the students raised money for Midnight Run and Bulldog Family Fund. Specials thanks to the fourth grade leadership team for leading and facilitating the event.
