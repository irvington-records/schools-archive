            Students Treated to Popsicles With the Principals
        
Dows Lane Elementary School’s incoming kindergarten students enjoyed a frozen summer treat while meeting new friends and classmates during Irvington PTSA’s annual Popsicles with the Principals on Aug. 29. Principal Dr. Andrea Kantor, Assistant Principal Thomas Chickery, Superintendent of Schools Dr. Kristopher Harrison and Assistant Superintendent of Instruction and Human Resources Dr. Gail Duffy welcomed the new families to the community.
