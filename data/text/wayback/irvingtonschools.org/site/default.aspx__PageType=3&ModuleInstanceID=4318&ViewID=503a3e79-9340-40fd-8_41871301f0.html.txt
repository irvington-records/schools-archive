The Irvington UFSD District Office is located at the north end of Dows Lane Elementary School.  Visitors are able to park in the Dows Lane parking and are asked to enter through the North entrance of Dows Lane.  This entrance point is located near the Gymnasium.  All guests are required to report to the security greeter, present identification, and receive a guest badge that must be displayed while on District property.  We thank you in advance for your cooperation in helping keep our schools safe.
