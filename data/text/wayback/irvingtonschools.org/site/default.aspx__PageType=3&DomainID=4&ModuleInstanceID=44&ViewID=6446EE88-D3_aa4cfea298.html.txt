            Irvington UFSD Earns 2022 Best of Westchester Title
        
Irvington Union Free School District was voted the Best Public-School District in the county by Westchester Magazine’s readers in a new Best of Westchester list for 2022. This is the sixth time in the last seven years that the District has received the recognition.
“The Best of Westchester speaks to the success of our students and staff and is a symbol of the community’s pride in its schools,” Superintendent of Schools Dr. Kristopher Harrison said.
