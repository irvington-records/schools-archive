MSS Students Solve Problems During Global Math Week
Main Street School students solved a variety of math problems during their participation in the Global Math Project. They worked on the same mathematics problems that students from all over the world engaged in during Global Math Week.
“These problems are meant to illuminate how fun and interesting mathematical problem solving can be,” said Molly Stern, the K-5 math and science instructional coach “Participating in this mathematical exploration was a new, fun way to experience being a global citizen.
During Global Math Week, students worked with exploding-dot machines to learn more about the base-10 number system, watched a video about the structure of numbers in different languages, and some students even shared how numbers are said in different languages they speak at home.
