                Update on Face Mask Requirements

June 8, 2021


Dear Irvington School Community,

I am writing in follow up to my recent communications regarding the anticipated
revisions to the New York State Department of Health (NYSDOH) mask wearing
requirements. This much anticipated guidance was released on Monday night and states
the following:

“Effective June 7, 2021, face masks are not required to be worn outdoors on school
grounds, including during outdoor school sports. However, Responsible Parties may
choose to require face masks outdoors on school grounds, subject to the parameters of
this guidance. No student, teacher, staff member, or visitor may be prevented from
wearing an acceptable face mask voluntarily on school grounds.”

Please note that the revised guidance also indicates that masks must continue to be
worn indoors while on school grounds. The complete NYSDOH guidelines can be found
here.

Following a thorough review of the revised guidelines and, after consultation with the
Reopening Steering Committee, it has been determined that effective Thursday, June
10, 2021, while mask wearing will still be encouraged, the District will no longer
require masks to be worn outdoors on IUFSD grounds. This is inclusive of adults and
students and covers all outdoor activities, including outdoor athletics, as described in
the NYSDOH guidance. Those who wish to continue to wear masks outdoors will be
welcome to do so. However, it is important to note that proper social distancing still
remains of great importance and the school staff will make every effort to enforce it.

Also effective Thursday, June 10, 2021, barriers will be removed from the Middle School
and High School classrooms as they are no longer a NYSDOH requirement and impede
students' visual and auditory experiences in the classroom. In both schools proper social
distancing will be enforced. Unfortunately, the removal of barriers is not possible in the
elementary schools at this time due to furniture configurations that don’t provide the
required distancing.

As always, your support is greatly appreciated.

All the best,

Kris

Dr. Kristopher Harrison
Superintendent of Schools
