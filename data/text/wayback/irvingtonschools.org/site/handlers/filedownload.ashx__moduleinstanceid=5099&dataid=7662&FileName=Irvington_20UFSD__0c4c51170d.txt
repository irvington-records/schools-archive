Irvington Union Free School District
                   Reopening Plan Addendum #2

                                June 8, 2021

This Reopening Plan Addendum shall serve as a supplement to the April 2021
Addendum and will supersede the July 2021 Reopening Plan requirements
pertaining mask wearing outdoors on the Irvington UFSD campuses.

On June 7, 2021, the New York State Department of Health (NYSDOH)
updated it guidance pertaining to mask wearing requirements on school
grounds. This much anticipated guidance was released states the following:

“Effective June 7, 2021, face masks are not required to be worn outdoors on
school grounds, including during outdoor school sports. However,
Responsible Parties may choose to require face masks outdoors on school
grounds, subject to the parameters of this guidance. No student, teacher,
staff member, or visitor may be prevented from wearing an acceptable face
mask voluntarily on school grounds.”

Please note that the revised guidance also indicates that masks must
continue to be worn indoors while on school grounds. The complete
NYSDOH guidelines can be found here.

Following a thorough review of the revised guidelines and, after consultation
with the Reopening Steering Committee, it has been determined that
effective Thursday, June 10, 2021, while mask wearing will still be
encouraged, the District will no longer require masks to be worn outdoors
on IUFSD grounds. This is inclusive of adults and students and covers all
outdoor activities, including outdoor athletics, as described in the NYSDOH
guidance. Those who wish to continue to wear masks outdoors will be
welcome to do so. However, it is important to note that proper social

distancing still remains of great importance and the school staff will make

Also effective Thursday, June 10, 2021, barriers will be removed from the
Middle School and High School classrooms as they are no longer a NYSDOH
requirement and impede students' visual and auditory experiences in the
classroom. In both schools proper social distancing will be enforced.
Unfortunately, the removal of barriers is not possible in the elementary
schools at this time due to furniture configurations that don’t provide the
required distancing.
