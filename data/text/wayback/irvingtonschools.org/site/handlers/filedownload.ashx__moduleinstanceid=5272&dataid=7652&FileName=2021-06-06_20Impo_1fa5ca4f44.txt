       Important Update on Face Mask Requirements

June 6, 2021


Dear IUFSD Community,

On Friday, Governor Cuomo made statements regarding a change in the requirement for
students and staff members to wear face coverings on school grounds. These comments
then led to numerous media outlets reporting that the requirement will be lifted
tomorrow, Monday, June 7th. However, this is not correct.

Schools must follow written guidelines issued by the New York State Department of
Health (NYSDOH). At this time, the NYSDOH has yet to issue any change in
requirements. Additionally, today a representative from the Governor’s office
confirmed that there is no change in NYSDOH guidelines and masks must continue to be
worn at all times on school grounds.

Therefore, I want to confirm that there is no change in the requirements to wear face
masks in the Irvington UFSD. All students and staff members are required to continue
to wear face masks at all times while on school property or at school-sponsored
events.

Should there be any changes in the requirements, I will appropriately notify the school
community.


My best regards,

Kris
