  Distance Learning Design
While participating in Distance Learning:
 ● All students will participate in regular, scheduled synchronous learning following
   an assigned schedule

 ● Students at home will be learning along with students who are on-site

 ● Instruction will follow the pacing of on-site learning with similar assignments
   targeting the same learning objectives

 ● Small group and whole group synchronous learning will be scheduled
  Distance Learning Design, Continued
While participating in Distance Learning:
 ● Technologies are being explored to provide live streaming of the classroom for
   students learning from home

 ● Google Classroom and Google Meet will be utilized to support instruction and
   maintain effective communication

 ● Teachers will utilize technology such as Google applications and streaming as
   instructional strategies. However, details are still being ﬁnalized.
   Required Daily Health Screenings
To provide for the health and safety of all school community members:
 ● Staff members will complete a daily health survey before reporting to work

 ● Parents will be required to complete a daily health survey prior to their child
   leaving for school.

 ● Surveys will include:
    ○   Temperature taking
    ○   Symptoms survey
    ○   Travel survey
    ○   Attestation to accuracy of information provided

 ● Nurses will monitor health daily and will implement quarantine procedures
   when necessary
 Health and Safety in School
● All students and staff will wear masks
   ○ Regular mask breaks will be provided
● Schools will periodically sanitize all high-contact surfaces
● Proper hygiene practices will be mandated
● Trafﬁc control process will be implemented (hallways and stairways)
● Lunch will be served in classrooms where possible to limit interaction
● Alternate locations will be added for secondary meal distribution/eating
● Meals will be available for remote learners
● Hand sanitizing and hand washing stations will be added throughout
  each school
● Visitors to schools will be limited
   ○ Meetings will take place virtually when possible
Health and Safety on School Buses
●   All students and staff will wear masks

●   Social distancing will be implemented where possible

●   Buses will be wiped down between routes with a thorough cleaning daily

●   Siblings will be required to share seats

●   Completion of health survey prior to reporting to bus stops
