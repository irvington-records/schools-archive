Exploration of K-1 Four Half-Days/Week In-Person Learning

Through its planning process, the District did not advance a model that
would have students in school for in-person learning due to:

 ●   Concern for the ability to follow in-school social distancing
     requirements

 ●   Transportation limitations:

      ○   Additional cost

      ○   Social distancing

      ○   Routing needs

 ●   Anticipation of signiﬁcant increase in stafﬁng to provide
     appropriate supervision



                                                                          11
     Exploration of K-1 Four (4) Half-Days/Week In-Person Learning

Since the initial planning process, having heard signiﬁcant feedback from parents and
further exploration, the District is now investigating the possibility of having Kindergarten
and 1st grade attend school for four (4) days per week.

This model would include:

 ●   Students remain in two cohorts who attend separate AM & PM sessions

 ●   Students participate in on-site learning four (4) half-days:

      ○   Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday

 ●   Students also participate in asynchronous distance learning on the four days they
     attend school.

       Note: All grades have a half-day of distance learning on Wednesdays.


                                                                                                12
Exploration of K-1 Four (4) Half-Days/Week In-Person Learning

What will inﬂuence the District’s ability to implement this model?

  ● Ability to provide & afford transportation

  ● Ability to perform necessary cleaning between AM and PM sessions

  ● Learning must be for the full school day for attendance to “count”.
    Asynchronous, distance learning plans must be developed for the half-day
    students are at home

  ● Impact on child care needs & parent work schedules

                Note: Half-days will not be an additional option.
                  Only one model will be implemented for K-1.

Important Notes About K-1 Four (4) Half-Days/Week In-Person Learning

●   Students will remain in the assigned cohort (A-L/M-Z).

●   The distance learning component of the experience will be asynchronous and will need
    adult support and supervision (similar to homework).

●   Attendance and participation in the asynchronous, distance learning portion of the day
    will be monitored through login to Google classroom and completion of assignments.

●   The District will provide all learning supplies needed for assignments done at home.

●   The District will provide all technology needed to support asynchronous, distance
    learning.

Health & Safety



                  16
     School Visitors
To provide for the health and safety our students and staff:

 ●   Visitations to the schools will be greatly reduced

 ●   Volunteer opportunities will be eliminated

 ●   All meetings will be scheduled remotely when practicable

 ●   Visitors will be required to complete a health survey upon arrival and must provide
     information for contact tracing purposes

 ●   Vendor and contractor access will be limited and scheduled after-school when
     possible (with the exception of Aramark - our food service vendor)



                                                                                           17
 Social Distancing
Social distancing will be supported at all times:
 ●   No large-group gatherings will be held
 ●   All student desks will be placed a minimum of 6 feet apart
 ●   Students will be seated facing the same direction
 ●   Stairway access will be modiﬁed for one-way trafﬁc only, with the exception of
     emergencies
 ●   Hallways will be marked with a dividing line
 ●   Elementary students will eat lunch in their classrooms or the cafeteria to provide
     necessary spacing
 ●   Travel to/from other locations will be limited where possible
 ●   Use of outdoor space whenever possible
 ●   Signage and ﬂoor decals will be installed throughout each school to guide trafﬁc
 ●   Speciﬁc pick-up/drop-off procedures will be implemented at each school to reduce
     congestion/contact
                                                                                          18
     Face Masks
Face Masks:
 ●   The District will require that masks will be worn at all times except during mask breaks and
     lunch
 ●   Supervised mask breaks will be provided with social distancing
 ●   Masks will be worn during Physical Education & Recess
 ●   Students and staff members will be trained in the proper use of masks
 ●   Families are encouraged to provide their child with 2 comfortable masks of their choosing
 ●   The District will provide disposable masks when needed
 ●   Clear masks will be provided to reading teachers and some staff as appropriate for
     instructional purposes
Types of Masks Permitted:
 ● Cloth masks that securely cover the nose and mouth and ﬁt snugly against the sides of the
     face (cloth masks, surgical masks, etc.)
 ● Surgical masks
 ● Face shields may not be worn in place of a face mask, but may be worn in addition to a mask

     Speciﬁc guidance will be communicated to parents and posted on the website
                                                                                                    19
     Handwashing and Hygiene
The District will take proactive measures to encourage healthy practices by:
 ●   Providing instruction on proper handwashing procedures
 ●   Displaying reminders throughout each school
 ●   Installing handwashing stations in each school to eliminate congestion in restrooms
 ●   Providing instruction on proper coughing and sneezing hygiene
 ●   Providing hand sanitizer in all classrooms and in common spaces such as hallways
 ●   Posting resources on the District website for parents to support school practices
 ●   Closing water fountains and using only bottle-ﬁlling stations of which more have been
     added throughout the buildings
 ●   Not permitting the sharing of learning materials, devices, desks, etc.
      ○ Students will be provided with personal Chromebooks
 ●   Using barriers for services that require closer spacing, i.e. speech, OT, PT or where it is
     impractical to create necessary social distancing
                                                                                                   20
     Cleaning and Sanitization
The District will comply with all recommended cleaning protocols:

 ●   All schools will be deeply cleaned & sanitized twice per week
 ●   High-contact areas will be cleaned throughout the school day, including bathroom
     sinks and bottle ﬁlling stations
 ●   All surfaces will be treated with Bio Protect that does not permit virus to adhere to it
 ●   Classrooms will be regularly cleaned with a Clorox Disinfectant Sprayer or similar
     product
 ●   IMS & IHS desks will be covered with newsprint/butcher paper that will be changed
     between classes (pilot)
 ●   Teachers will be provided with disinfecting wipes



                                                                                                21
     Transportation
The District and the transportation contractor will take measures to support
health and safety:

 ●   Survey data informs the District that ridership will be dramatically reduced
 ●   The goal is to have students seated one per seat except for siblings who will
     be seated together
 ●   All students and staff must wear masks at all times
 ●   No eating on the bus (as per current practice)
 ●   Bus seats will be wiped down between routes by IUFSD custodians
 ●   Buses will be sanitized daily by the transportation contractor




                                                                                     22
  Food Services/School Lunch
The District will continue to support the proper nutrition of its students, while providing
for the health and safety of everyone:
 ●   Elementary students will eat in their classrooms or cafeteria to provide for distancing
 ●   Lunches will be delivered to the classroom and/or distributed through spaced pickup
     lines
 ●   Meals will be pre-packaged to limit contact
 ●   IMS & IHS cafeteria tables will be spaced further apart and students will not be
     seated across from one another
      ○ Alternate dining locations will be provided and supervised
      ○ All surfaces will be cleaned routinely and between lunch periods
 ●   Food service staff will undergo necessary training
 ●   Food preparation spaces and equipment will be sanitized daily
 ●   Lunches will be available for students engaged in distance learning
 ●   Free and reduced-priced meals will be provided to eligible students

Ventilation and Air Circulation
The District will take measures to ensure proper ventilation:

 ●   Classrooms have mechanical ventilation systems that provide the required
     air exchanges
 ●   All outside air dampers will be programmed/set for 100% fresh air until the
     weather cools
 ●   All air-handlers will have MERV-13 ﬁlters installed
 ●   Each classroom will have windows open to support fresh air circulation
      ○ Some windows don’t open as wide as others by design or for safety
         purposes
 ●   Classroom doors can be opened to aid air circulation
 ●   Each classroom will have air conditioning (central cooling or window units)

 Health Screening, Health Care & Treatment
● Daily health screening will be required before students report to school and bus
  stops and prior to staff reporting to work
   ○ An app will be introduced for the reporting of viral symptoms, fevers, contact
     with others who are ill per guidelines
   ○ Students and staff who are ill or were in contact with others who are will be
     required to stay home

● The District is fortunate to have expert nurses caring for our students who will
  monitor the health of students and staff

● Those who display signs of viral illness will be quarantined from others in a
  designated space until they can safely leave the school
   ○ All areas of contact will be immediately sanitized

● Routine health care will be provided; however, minor needs may be tended to
  outside the health ofﬁce

●   We have a standing relationship with the Westchester County Department of

Contact Tracing
The IUFSD will notify the state and local health department immediately upon being informed of any
positive COVID-19 diagnostic test result by an individual within school facilities or on school grounds,
including students, faculty, staff, and visitors to the District.

To assist the local health department with tracing the transmission of COVID-19, the District has
developed and maintains a plan to trace all contacts of exposed individuals in accordance with
protocols, training, and tools provided through the New York State Contact Tracing Program.

The District may assist with contact tracing by:
 ●   Keeping accurate attendance records of students and staff members
 ●   Ensuring student schedules are up to date
 ●   Keeping a visitor log that includes the date and time, address and phone number, and which
     area of the school they visited
 ●   Assisting the local health departments in tracing all contacts of the individual in accordance
     with the protocol, training, and tools provided through the NYS Contact Tracing Program
 ●   Informing local health department of staff member testing positive regardless of county of
     residence.
 ●   For all contact tracing, the DOH will require the name, address, and phone number as well as
     the name and phone number of the guardian of a student.

     What Will Happen if there is a Case of COVID-19?
If/when COVID-19 cases are discovered, in consultation with the local health department and
the school medical director/school physician the District will do the following:

 ●    Close or quarantine locations of potential exposure for 24 hours, if possible
 ●    Clean and disinfect locations as necessary
 ●    Provide DOH with information as required per contact tracing protocols (i.e. 14 days of
      quarantine)
 ●    Ensure student/staff member’s return to school meets DOH/CDC guidelines
 ●    Ultimate determination to close schools will be at the Superintendent’s discretion

Conﬁdentiality must be maintained as required by federal and state laws and regulations. Staff
should not try to determine who is to be excluded without guidance and direction from the
local health department.

               The District will always proceed with an abundance of caution.
