What changes are being considered?

● Core academic instruction in
  ELA and Math will occur during
  the portion of the day when
  students are on-site.

● Move all specials and PLTW to
  the portion of the day when
  students are at home.
Why are these changes being considered?
●   Experience with the am/pm model in Grades K and 1 has
    shown this to be beneﬁcial for the youngest learners

●   More consistency in face-to-face time with teachers will
    support a deeper relationship with their teacher and peers

●   Input from Grade 2 and 3 teachers

●   Parent feedback about the challenges of remote learning

●   More frequent on-site interactions with teachers and
    classmates

●   Reduce screen time for young learners
What would stay the same?
●   Your child’s teacher

●   Your child’s cohort assignment

●   Wednesdays remain fully remote

●   Cleaning Protocols
    ○   Classrooms and common areas will be cleaned between
        morning and afternoon sessions (extended to Grades 2 and 3)
    ○   Buses are cleaned between sessions and at the end of the day
What would change?
● Time spent in core academic learning is increased due to:
    ○   Fewer transitions
    ○   Specials and PLTW occur during time at home
    ○   Specials reduced from 40 to 30 minutes


●   Lunch/Recess
    ○   “To go” lunch for morning students
    ○   “Working” lunch in the classroom for afternoon students
What would change?
● Increased trafﬁc during mid-day transitions

● Mid-day pick-up and drop-off times will change to allow
  additional time for cleaning classrooms and teacher
  prep periods

● Students may need to share seats on the bus, efforts will
  be made to seat siblings together

How much will this cost?
●   Increased transportation costs
    ○   Approximately $46,500
    ○   Could be offset cost if eliminate bus monitor


● Increased cleaning cost
    ○   Approximately $12,000 - $20,000
    ○   Use of grounds staff might result in some reduced expectations
        for grounds appearance
What are the next steps?
● Survey Dows Lane parents for support of changing the schedule

● Present overall ﬁndings and make a recommendation to the
  Board of Education

● Potentially implement the new schedule at Dows Lane effective
  November 30th following the Thanksgiving break.

● Continue exploration of interest and feasibility to extend the
  am/pm model to Main Street School.
