In order to best support the health and wellness of students and their families and
the District’s staff members, the Irvington UFSD (IUFSD) has established operational
standards that comply with the New York State Department of Health (DOH) and
New York State Education Department (NYSED) guidelines.

1. General Health and Safety Measures:
   IUFSD will comply with DOH recommendations to promote behaviors that
   reduce the spread of illness. These actions include, but are not limited to: social
   distancing, wearing of face masks, frequent hand washing and sanitizing and
   frequent cleaning and disinfection.

   1A.   Social Distancing:
         The IUFSD will take numerous measures to support necessary social
         distancing including but not limited to the following:
          ●   It is expected that whenever possible, students and staff will remain a
              minimum of six feet apart ​and​ wear authorized face masks at all times
              (See 1B).
          ●   Specific protocols will be put in place to provide greater distance
              among students during Physical Education, Band, Orchestra and
              Chorus classes/programs.
          ●   The IUFSD has implemented a cohort model to reduce daily
              attendance in each school by 50%.
          ●   In each classroom, student desks have been placed six feet apart.
          ●   Hallway and stairway traffic has been modified to limit congestion.
          ●   Large group gatherings will be prohibited.
          ●   Elementary students will eat lunch in their classrooms or the cafeteria
              to provide necessary spacing.
          ●   Travel to/from other locations will be limited where possible.
          ●   Use of outdoor space whenever possible.
          ●   Morning drop-off and afternoon pick-up procedures have been
              modified to reduce congregating.
          ●   Social Distancing will be encouraged on school buses and only siblings
              will be permitted to share seats, where practicable.
          ●   Signage will be displayed in all school buildings to guide behaviors.

   1B.   Face Masks:
         The expectation is that students and staff will wear masks at all times with
         the exception of lunch, during facilitated mask breaks, and staff working in
         non-common spaces. Adherence to these protocols is critical for the safety
         and well-being of all members of our school community. Failure to comply
         with mask wearing protocols will constitute a violation of our Code of
         Conduct and will be subject to disciplinary consequences consistent with
         other health and safety related violations.

                                                                                         3
      Best Practices for Mask Wearing
           ● Students/staff should wash/sanitize hands before putting on a
              mask
           ●   Mask should cover nose and mouth and be secure under the chin
           ●   Mask should fit snugly against the sides of the face

      Masks During PE/Recess/Physical Activities
      Physical education classes will take place outdoors, when possible. Whether
      indoors or outdoors, students will be expected to wear a mask, unless they
      are participating in a supervised mask break. There will be mask breaks for
      students during PE classes.

      Types of Masks Permitted
           ● Cloth masks that securely cover the nose and mouth and fit snugly
              against the sides of the face.
           ●   Surgical masks
           ●   Face shields may not be worn in place of a face mask, but may be
               worn in addition to a mask.
           ●   Neck gaiters, vented masks, and bandannas ​are NOT permitted​.

      Mask Breaks
      Each school will facilitate “mask breaks”, during which time students shall
      remain at least 6 feet apart from one another. All mask breaks will be
      supervised by an adult.

      Mask Exemptions
      Students who are unable to tolerate wearing a face covering due to a
      medical condition, where the use of such a covering would impair their
      physical or mental health, will not be subject to the required use of a face
      covering. Documentation of such an impairment from a health care provider
      shall be required for an exemption and must be provided to the school
      nurse.

1C.   Hand Washing and Hand Sanitization:
      All students and staff members are required to wash and/or sanitize hands
      throughout the school day. To support this expectation, the following is be
      provided:
        ● Elementary students will receive direction on hand washing and hand
            sanitization.
       ●   Visual reminders will be displayed throughout each school.
       ●   Hand sanitizer dispensers have been installed in all hallways and
           classrooms.

                                                                                    4
             ●   Students will be guided to wash or sanitize their hands prior to and
                 following mask breaks, meal times, use of bottle filling stations, and use
                 of lavatories.
             ●   It is recommended that students apply hand sanitizer when exiting and
                 entering classrooms.
             ●   Additional hand washing stations will be installed in each school.

   1D. School Visitors:
       To provide for the health and safety our students and staff:
         ●       Visitations to the schools will be greatly reduced
         ●       Volunteer opportunities will be eliminated
         ●       All meetings will be scheduled remotely when practicable
         ●       Visitors will be required to complete a health survey upon arrival and
                 must provide information for contact tracing purposes
         ●       Vendor and contractor access will be limited and scheduled
                 after-school when possible (with the exception of Aramark - our food
                 service vendor)

   1E.   Cleaning and Sanitization:
         The District will comply with all recommended cleaning protocols:
         ●       All schools will be deeply cleaned & sanitized twice per week (Clorox
                 sprayer)
         ●       High-contact areas will be cleaned throughout the school day,
                 including bathroom sinks and bottle filling stations
         ●       All surfaces will be treated with Bio Protect that does not permit virus
                 to adhere to it for 90 days
         ●       Classrooms will be regularly cleaned with disinfectants
         ●       IMS & IHS desks will be covered with newsprint/butcher paper that will
                 be changed between classes (pilot)
         ●       Teachers will be provided with disinfecting wipes

2. Health Monitoring:
   The IUFSD will follow all recommended guidelines to support the health and
   well-being of all students and staff members. In doing so, it is essential for daily
   health screenings to occur before students and staff members leave to report to
   school/work and there must be effective communication to occur between
   parents and each school.

   2A. Required Daily Health Screenings:
        ● Prior to leaving for school, or before going to the bus stop, a daily health
           screening must be completed using the screening app. This
           self-screening must be completed by students and staff daily.
             ●   Students and staff members who answer “yes” to the following

                                                                                            5
          prompts must stay home:
          ○   Display flu-like or COVID-19 symptoms including: fever, cough,
              chills, stomach ailments, shortness of breath, etc.
          ○   Have been in contact with someone who has tested positive for
              COVID-19.
          ○   Have been in personal contact with someone displaying flu-like or
              COVID-19 symptoms.
          ○   Traveled to a state on the ​New York State quarantine list​.
          ○   Traveled to a country on the ​United States quarantine list​.
      ●   The health screening app can be downloaded to Apple or Android
          smartphones or by completing the web-based application posted on
          the District website. Directions and the link for the health screening
          app will be distributed before the first in-person student days.
      ●   Students for whom the daily health screening has not been completed
          will not be permitted to attend class. Parents will be contacted in each
          case and students for whom a daily screening is repeatedly not
          completed will be required to come to the school to pick up their child.

2C. COVID-19 Testing:
    IUFSD is not equipped or permitted to conduct COVID-19 testing.
     ●       We have a standing relationship with the Westchester County
             Department of Health (WCDOH), and in the event that testing is
             required, we will defer to the WCDOH for any required or relevant
             information.
     ●       Testing is readily available in our community.
     ●       Students, staff or visitors requiring COVID-19 testing should contact
             their health provider, a local urgent care, federally qualified health
             center or visit the NYSDOH website to locate a nearby testing facility.
     ●       The District will be able to provide expedited testing for staff members
             through its health insurance plan.
     ●       Local COVID-19 testing locations can be ​found by clicking here​.

2D. Contact Tracing:
    The IUFSD will notify the state and local health department immediately
    upon being informed of any positive COVID-19 diagnostic test result by an
    individual within school facilities or on school grounds, including students,
    faculty, staff, and visitors to the District.

     To assist the local health department with tracing the transmission of
     COVID-19, the District has developed and maintains a plan to trace all
     contacts of exposed individuals in accordance with protocols, training, and
     tools provided through the New York State Contact Tracing Program.

     The District may assist with contact tracing by:
     ●       Keeping accurate attendance records of students and staff members
     ●       Ensuring student schedules are up to date
     ●       Keeping a visitor log that includes the date and time, address and
             phone number, and which area of the school they visited
     ●       Assisting the local health departments in tracing all contacts of the
             individual in accordance with the protocol, training, and tools provided
             through the NYS Contact Tracing Program
     ●       Informing local health department of staff members testing positive
             regardless of county of residence.
     ●       For all contact tracing, the DOH will require the name, address, and
             phone number as well as the name and phone number of the guardian
             of a student.

   2E. What Will Happen if There is a Confirmed Case of COVID-19?
       If/when COVID-19 cases are discovered, in consultation with the local health
       department and the school medical director/school physician the District
       will do the following:
        ●       Close or quarantine locations of potential exposure for 24 hours
        ●       Clean and disinfect locations as necessary
        ●       Provide DOH with information as required per contact tracing
                protocols (i.e. 14 days of quarantine)
        ●       Ensure student/staff member’s return to school meets DOH/CDC
                guidelines
        ●       Ultimate determination to close schools will be at the Superintendent’s
                discretion
        ●       The District will always proceed with an abundance of caution.

   Important Note: Confidentiality must be maintained as required by federal and
   state laws and regulations. Staff should not try to determine who is to be
   excluded without guidance and direction from the local health department.

3. Academic Program and School Schedules
   The Irvington UFSD has developed extensive plans to provide a rigorous,
   comprehensive learning experience for all students. Each plan will comply with
   all DOH and SED requirements and will be implemented based upon New York
   State and DOH directives and local conditions. These plans provide for learning
   in the following models:

            ●   Hybrid:
                This model will allow for approximately 50% of student attendance,
                while remaining students will participate in Distance Learning.
                Students will attend school in alternating attendance in accordance
                with the Cohort Model. More details on the Hybrid and Cohort Models
                are below. While schools are operating in the Hybrid Model, parents
                will have the opportunity to enroll their children in a ​Distance
                Learning Option​ rather than them attending in person. (Note: the
                Distance Learning Option will follow a different schedule than the
                100% Distance Learning outlined below.)

            ●   100% Distance Learning:
                This model of learning will be implemented when 100% of students in
                a school or in the IUFSD will be required to learn from home. Here, all
                learning will be synchronous with teachers providing instruction by an
                established school schedule daily.

     3E. Technology to Support Learning:
         Equity of access to all aspects of learning is of critical importance for the
         IUFSD. Therefore, for the 2020-21 school year, the IUFSD will provide each

   4B. Transportation:
       In partnership with the Quad Village Transportation Cooperative and its lead
       agency, Ardsley UFSD Transportation Department and the transportation
                                                                                  13
    contractor, Royal Coach, specific plans have been put in place to provide for
    the safest possible transportation to and from school.
      ●   All students and staff must wear masks at all times
      ●   No eating on the bus (as per current practice)
      ●   Bus seats will be wiped down between routes by IUFSD custodians
      ●   Buses will be sanitized daily by the transportation contractor
      ●   Wear acceptable face coverings at all times on school buses (e.g.,
          entering, once seated and exiting)
      ●   Drivers will maintain a passenger log for each run to assist with
          contact tracing when needed.
      ●   Students whose physical or mental health would be impaired are not
          required to wear a face covering, and will be appropriately socially
          distanced at the rear of the bus.
      ●   Spare, clean cloth face coverings must be available to ensure all
          students wear cloth face coverings on the school bus.
      ●   Members of the same household may be seated in the same seat.
      ●   When possible, seating will be arranged to provide social distance on
          the bus.
      ●   Students will be loaded from the back to the front of the bus.
      ●   Students will unload from front to the back of the bus.
      ●   School bus drivers, riders, monitors and attendants may carry personal
          bottles of hand sanitizer with them on school buses.
      ●   When temperatures are above 45 degrees Fahrenheit, drivers will
          increase bus ventilation by opening the top hatches of buses or
          opening windows

4D. Athletics:
    The IUFSD will operate it’s interscholastic athletic program in compliance
    with all DOH, SED, New York State Public High School Athletic Association
    (NYSPHSAA) and Section One standards. However, the IUFSD will always
    make determinations in the best interest of its students, which may go
    above and beyond the precautions outlined by other entities. The IUFSD
    Director of Physical Education, Health and Athletics, Mr. John Buonamano,
    will provide all necessary updates to the athletic community. Related
    information will be posted on the ​IUFSD Athletics website​ and the
    Reopening IUFSD website​ as appropriate. Questions can be directed to Mr.
    Buonomano via email at ​John.Buonamano@IrvingtonSchools.org​.

4E. Facilities Usage:
    In order to provide the safest possible learning environment for our students
    and staff, use of all Irvington School District buildings by outside
    organizations is suspended indefinitely. Upon schools reopening, the use of
    all outdoor facilities will also be prohibited during the academic day and
    during after-school extracurriculars. This includes use of the High School
    track. As valued members of the Irvington Union Free School District, we ask
    your cooperation and support in providing our students and staff with a safe
    return to school.

4F. Ventilation and Air Circulation:
    Maintaining healthy learning environments is a top priority for the IUFSD. To
    this end, all recommended procedures will be followed to ensure that
    proper ventilation and air circulation is available in all spaces.​ ​The District
                                                                                   15
     will take the following measures to ensure proper ventilation:
      ●   Classrooms have mechanical ventilation systems that provide the
          required air exchanges
      ●   All outside air dampers will be programmed/set for 100% fresh air until
          the weather cools
      ●   All air-handlers will have MERV-13 filters installed
      ●   Each classroom will have windows open to support fresh air circulation
           ○   Some windows don’t open as wide as others by design or for
               safety purposes
      ●   Classroom doors can be opened to aid air circulation
      ●   Each classroom will have air conditioning (central cooling or window
          units) to maintain low humidity.
