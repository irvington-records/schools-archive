            First Graders Practice Mindfulness Yoga
        
Standing on their bright green yoga mats, Dows Lane Elementary School first graders moved through various yoga poses while learning to breathe deeply and relax on May 31. Certified yoga instructor and retired Dows Lane teacher Laurel Warager led the mindfulness and yoga lesson. Warager guided the students to practice warrior, tree and bridge poses as they participated with curiosity and confidence.
