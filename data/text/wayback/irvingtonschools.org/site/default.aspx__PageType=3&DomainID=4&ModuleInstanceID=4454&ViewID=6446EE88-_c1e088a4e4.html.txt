The Irvington UFSD is developing a Capital Project that will address critical needs across the District.  The proposed project includes necessary work at each school that addresses Safety and Security, Infrastructure needs, Energy Efficiency and the Modernization of Learning Spaces.  The Board of Education is hosting a special meeting specifically related to the Capital Project on Tuesday, February 26th in the CPR. For more information on this meeting and the project, please read Dr. Harrison's letter by clicking here.
