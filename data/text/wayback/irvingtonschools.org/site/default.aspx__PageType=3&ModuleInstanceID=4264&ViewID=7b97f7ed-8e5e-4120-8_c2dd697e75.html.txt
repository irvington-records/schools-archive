Zoe Maxwell Excels at NYSPHSAA Track and Field Championships
Zoe Maxwell capped her athletic career here at Irvington High by placing in the top six in two events at the New York State Public High School Athletic Associations Track and Field Championships at Cicero North High School on June 8th. Zoe placed 2nd in the triple jump and 6th in the 400 meters. In addition, Zoe competed in the New York State Federation meet on June 9th and took home a silver medal in the triple jump.
Zoe was also honored at the Con Edison Scholar Athlete Awards Dinner on June 6th.
