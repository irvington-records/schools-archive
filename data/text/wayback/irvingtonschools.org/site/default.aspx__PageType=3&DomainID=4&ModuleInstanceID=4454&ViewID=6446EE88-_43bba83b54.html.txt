Special Board of Education Meeting Wednesday, August 4th, 7:00 pm
On Wednesday, August 4, 2021, the Irvington UFSD Board of Education will hold a special meeting at 7:00 pm in the Campus Presentation Room (CPR), located at the Irvington MS/HS Campus. During this meeting the Irvington High School and District administrators will discuss the possible expansion of 1:1 Chromebook use to IHS and related technology plans for the 2021-22 school year. Additionally, the Buildings and Grounds Committee will provide updates on current status of the construction and will discuss the finances for the second phase of the capital bond project.
Please note: the Board of Education will be meeting in person and that the meeting will also be live streamed at https://youtu.be/7lGoRyxwzKA. Those interested in providing public comment must attend the meeting in person.
