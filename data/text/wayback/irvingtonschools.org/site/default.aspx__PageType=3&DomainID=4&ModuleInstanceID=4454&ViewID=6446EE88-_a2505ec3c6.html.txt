Dows Lane Elementary School will now implement its traditional arrival and dismissal procedures with vehicle driving down Dows Lane, the roadway, to access the school. However, on Monday from 9:30 am - 1:30 pm Dows Lane will be closed for paving. During this time, visitors will be able to access the school campus through the gate on Willow and Oak. Parents are asked to review Principal Kantor's email on the topic.
 
Thank you for your continued patience, cooperation and support.
