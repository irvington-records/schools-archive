Fifth Graders Move Up to Middle School
Main Street School celebrated its fifth graders during a moving-up ceremony on June 23 in the Campus Theater before an audience of friends, family, teachers and administrators.
The ceremony included the sharing of memories, a performance of a song and an acknowledgment of the teachers and PTSA members for their dedication and support. The students also heard remarks from Principal Joyce Chapnick and Superintendent of Schools Dr. Kristopher Harrison before receiving a warm welcome from Irvington Middle School Assistant Principal Allyson Daley.
“The moving-up ceremony was lovely,” Chapnick said. “It was simply awesome to be in person as an entire grade level and have family members present as well. The memories that students shared from their experiences during their time at MSS were really special.”
Special thanks to the staff on the Moving Up Day Committee for putting together the ceremony, and to all the grade teachers and parents for making the year memorable for the students.
