            Dows Lane Students, Staff Send Off Graduating Class
        
Dressed in their caps and gowns, members of Irvington High School’s Class of 2022 walked through the hallways of Dows Lane Elementary School on June 17 to the cheers of younger students, teachers and administrators. For many of the seniors, it was an opportunity to remember where their educational journey began 13 years ago and celebrate their accomplishments.
