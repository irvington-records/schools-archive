            Fifth Graders Enjoy a Day of Adventure
        
Fifth graders bonded with their classmates over a variety of activities during their field trip to Elmwood Day Camp on June 10. Throughout the day, they participated in arts and crafts, adventure park and playground, field day activities and swimming. They also enjoyed lunch together, including pizza, chips, fruit and water. Special thanks to the parent chaperones who joined for the day.
