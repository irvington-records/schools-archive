            MSS Students Go On a Path for Peace, Love and Equality
        
Main Street School students participated in a schoolwide Path for Peace, Love and Equality hike on June 8. During the walk, the students carried signs that they made to honor their identities and share information about causes that are important to them.
In addition, they raised more than $2,200 that will be donated to the Be a Friend Project, Dobbs Ferry Food Pantry and Welcoming Schools organizations. Special thanks to all the families for their generous support.
