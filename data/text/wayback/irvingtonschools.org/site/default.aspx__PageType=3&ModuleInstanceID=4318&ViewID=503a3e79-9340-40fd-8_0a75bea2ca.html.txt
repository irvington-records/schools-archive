Irvington Middle School
Irvington Middle School is located on the same campus at Irvington High School. Our building is the second building on the right as you come up the hill and our main entrance is located near the end of the large planter and turf area. Parking for short-term (15 minutes or fewer) visitors can be found along the outside of the median in front of the HS or in the gravel lot at the top of the hill beyond all of the school buildings. Our parking is limited, so we ask that you refrain from parking in any numbered spaces or those marked "MS", as they are reserved and needed for staff. All visitors should enter through our main entrance, show ID, and sign in. Following sign-in procedures, a greeter will direct you to the appropriate location.
