            8/18/20 Policy Revisions and Adoption for Public Comment
        
In light of shifts in instructional practice and school operations associated with the COVID-19 pandemic a number of policies are being revised or adopted.  The Board of Education is expected to adopt these policies at their August 25, 2020 public meeting.  If you have any comment on these policies, please feel free to email Dr. Harrison, Kristopher.Harrison@IrvingtonSchools.org or the Board of Education, Board@IrvingtonSchools.org.
