The Irvington UFSD is pleased to report that over 500 students and staff members were tested for COVID-19 on Thursday, October 14th and Friday, October 15th and that all test results were negative for the coronavirus.  The District extends its appreciation to everyone who participated, as it provided insight into the health of our school community.  
 
On Thursday, October 21st, the Irvington UFSD will facilitate its first round of surveillance testing; wherein, 20% of those who granted consent will be tested.  Those individuals who will be tested Thursday were chosen randomly.  Emails will be sent to students' parents and staff members today informing them of the testing appointment.
