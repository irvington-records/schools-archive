            May 5th COVID-19 Surveillance Testing Results
        
The COVID-19 surveillance testing that was facilitated on Thursday, May 5th, revealed four positive cases of the virus, three at Main Street School and one at Dows Lane Elementary School.  Those who tested positive have been notified directly by the District.
