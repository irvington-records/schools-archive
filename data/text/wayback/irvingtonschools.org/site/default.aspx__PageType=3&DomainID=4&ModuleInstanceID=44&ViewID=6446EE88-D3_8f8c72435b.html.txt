            Registering to Vote for the Irvington UFSD Budget Vote & School Board Election
        
During the current mandated school district closure and need for social distancing, personal voter registration may not take place in school districts.  Qualified Voters may register to vote with the County Board of Elections or by completing an online application with the New York State Department of Motor Vehicles.
Qualified Voter
A qualified voter must be 18 years old by June 9, 2020, a citizen of the United States and a resident with the Irvington School District for 30 days immediately preceding the vote, and not otherwise prohibited from voting. 
For further information, please contact Pauline Davies, District Clerk at pauline.davies@irvingtonschools.org.
