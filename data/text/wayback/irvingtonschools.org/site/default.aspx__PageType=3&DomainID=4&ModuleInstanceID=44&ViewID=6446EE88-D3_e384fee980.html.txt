            Dows Lane Third Graders Win Triple C Award
        
Dows Lane third grade students Eveleen Aziz, Ella Marcus, Jacob Snyder and Bailey Weiner were awarded the NYS Attorney General’s Triple C Award, sponsored by Attorney General Letitia James. They were recognized by Principal Andrea Kantor on April 27 in a ceremony at Dows Lane Elementary School. Students were nominated for this award for their courage, character and commitment to their academics, friends, school and community.
“We are very proud of all of our students, and today we were extra proud,” Kantor said.
Congratulations to the 2023 Triple C Award winners and their families. You are an inspiration to us all!
