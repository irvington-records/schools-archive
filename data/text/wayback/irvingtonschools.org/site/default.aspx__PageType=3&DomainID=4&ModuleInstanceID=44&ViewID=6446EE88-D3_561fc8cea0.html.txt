Irvington High School Seniors Sign College Commitment Letters
IHS seniors signed their college commitment letters yesterday at a ceremony attended by Principal Jon Hirsch, Superintendent of Schools Kristopher Harrison, Director of PE, Health and Athletics John Buonamano, faculty, coaches, students, friends and family.
Buonamano congratulated all for their hard work and dedication in all they did for the Irvington Bulldogs programs and for the accomplishment of playing their respective sports at the next level. Their coaches all praised them for their commitment to making their teams the best they could be and for leading by example on and off the field of play.
