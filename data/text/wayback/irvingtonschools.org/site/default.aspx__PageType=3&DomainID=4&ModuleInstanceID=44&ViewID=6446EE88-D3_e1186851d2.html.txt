            MSS Fourth Graders Celebrate End of Year With Picnic
        
Main Street School fourth graders celebrated the end of the school year with a picnic at Matthiessen Park on June 7. They walked down to the park with their class, where they spent the day together, played games and enjoyed lunch. Special thanks to Main Street School families for joining the students for lunch at the park and providing them with watermelon and ice pops.
