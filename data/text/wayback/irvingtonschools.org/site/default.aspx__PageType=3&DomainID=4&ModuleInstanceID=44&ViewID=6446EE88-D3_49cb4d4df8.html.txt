            Irvington Police Department Visits Dows Lane for Annual Halloween Safety Assembly 
        
Dows Lane Elementary students received a special visit from the Irvington Police Department during their annual Halloween Safety Assembly. Officers spoke with students about staying safe while trick-or-treating and shared tips to ensure everyone enjoys a fun and safe Halloween.
Officer Di Nardo also surprised students with glow-light bracelets from the department to help them stay visible while out after dark. 
“We value our partnership with the Irvington Police, and the students are always thrilled when they visit,” said Dr. Andrea Kantor, principal of Dows Lane Elementary School.  
The district extends its appreciation to the Irvington Police Department for their continued partnership and commitment to student safety.
