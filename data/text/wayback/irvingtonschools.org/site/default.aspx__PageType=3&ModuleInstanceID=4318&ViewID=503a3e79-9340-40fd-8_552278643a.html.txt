Please note that parking is limited on campus. High School visitors can park in our upper parking lot at the top of campus, past our Campus Gymnasium (the last building at the top of the hill). In addition, there are a select number of visitor spots available for 15 minute visits at the island in front of our main entrance. These spots are only available between 9:00am-1:00pm.
 
Please note that the lower parking lot is reserved for Faculty and Staff only.
