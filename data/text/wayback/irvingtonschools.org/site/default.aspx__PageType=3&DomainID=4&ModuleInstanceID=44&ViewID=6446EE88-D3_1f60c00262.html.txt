Main Street School Shines at Annual PTSA Talent Show
Main Street School students took center stage at this year’s PTSA Talent Show, filling the auditorium with energy, creativity, and school-wide pride. Led with tremendous heart and dedication by Suzy Kimmel, Kerry McGrath, and Dan Spitzer, the evening showcased the remarkable talents and confidence of the MSS students.  
The show featured 33 diverse acts, reflecting a wide range of interests and artistic expression. Students performed piano pieces, sang, danced, played instruments, demonstrated gymnastics and karate, delivered comedy sketches, presented artwork, created game shows and performance art, and brought original ideas to life on stage. Each act highlighted the individuality of our students and the supportive spirit of the MSS community.  
Families, teachers, and peers filled the auditorium with encouragement, creating the kind of warm, celebratory atmosphere that makes Main Street School so special. It was an evening filled with joy, connection, and inspiration.  
The PTSA Talent Show continues to be a beloved tradition at MSS, and this year’s performers shined brighter than ever. Congratulations to all of our students for sharing their gifts and to our dedicated organizers for creating an unforgettable night for the school community.
