Irvington High School Seniors Named AP Scholars
Fifty-six Irvington High School seniors have been recognized by the College Board as AP Scholars due to their outstanding success on Advanced Placement exams. The students took the AP exams in May 2019 as juniors after completing challenging college-level courses in the district in a variety of subjects.
Congratulations to the following students:
