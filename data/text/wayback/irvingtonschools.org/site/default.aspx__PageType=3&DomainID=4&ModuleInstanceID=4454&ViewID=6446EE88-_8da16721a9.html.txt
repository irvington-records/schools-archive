            January 6, 2022 COVID-19 Surveillance Testing Results
        
Thursday's COVID-19 surveillance testing yielded two positive results at Dows Lane.  Across the District, all other test results were negative.  Those who tested positive were notified by the District and will have further school-level follow up.
