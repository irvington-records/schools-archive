            Seniors Celebrate Journey with Dows Lane Elementary School Parade
        
Dressed in their white and green caps and gowns, members of Irvington High School’s Class of 2024 proudly paraded through the hallways of Dows Lane Elementary School on June 12 to the cheers of the younger students, teachers and administrators. For many of the seniors, it was an opportunity to remember where their educational journey began in Irvington 13 years ago and to celebrate their accomplishments.
Members of Irvington High School’s marching band led the parade. Irvington High School Principal Jon Hirsch and Dows Lane Elementary School Principal Dr. Andrea Kantor said they were proud of all students and cherish the annual tradition.
