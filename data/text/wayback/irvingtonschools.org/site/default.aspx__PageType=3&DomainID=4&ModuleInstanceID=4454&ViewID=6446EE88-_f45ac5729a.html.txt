            Additions to the 2019-20 Board Meeting Dates
        
The Annual Board and Administrative Roundtable Discussion originally scheduled for Tuesday, January 28, 2020 has been rescheduled for Tuesday, February 25, 2020, starting at 7:00 p.m. in the Campus Presentation Room.  Additionally, the annual school quality survey results will be presented on Tuesday, June 23, 2020, starting at 7:00 p.m. in the Campus Presentation Room.
