            MSS Fifth Graders Bond With Classmates Over Adventures
        
Main Street School fifth graders enjoyed a variety of adventurous activities and bonded with their classmates during their field trip to Elmwood Day Camp on June 7. Throughout the day, they participated in arts and crafts, adventure park and playground, field day activities and swimming. They also enjoyed lunch together, including pizza, chips, fruit and water. Specials thanks to the chaperones for accompanying the students to provide them with a great experience.
