Third Graders Perform Favorite Disney Songs
Dows Lane Elementary School third graders entertained audience members with their favorite Disney songs during their winter concert on Jan. 30.
“Music teacher Jo Anne Caroprese led students in an incredible performance that showcased our students’ beautiful voices and talent,” Principal Andrea Kantor said. “During the concert, faculty and staff members and families were seen singing and dancing on the side of the gym and in their seats.”
The students performed “We’re All in This Together” by Matthew Gerrard and Robbie Nevil, “Love Is An Open Door” and “Let It Go” by Kristen Anderson-Lopez and Robert Lopez, “A Whole New World” by Alan Menkin and Mac Huff, “Be Our Guest” by Alan Menken and Howard Ashman, and “Circle of Life” and “Hakuna Matata” by Elton John and Tim Rice.
