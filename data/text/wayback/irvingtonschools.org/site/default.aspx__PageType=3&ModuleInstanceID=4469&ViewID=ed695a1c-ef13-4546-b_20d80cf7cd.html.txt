APPOINTMENT SCHEDULING
SCHEDULE TIME WITH YOUR COUNSELOR ONLINE! NOW AVAILABLE!
We are thrilled to begin using the calendar scheduling program, Calendly, for future student/family appointments with counselors. Our goal is to make the process of connecting smoother. Using this system, you will have access to your counselor’s calendar in real time and will be able to make appointments that work with your schedule without having to wait for someone to connect with you.
