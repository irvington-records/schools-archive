Annual Irvington UFSD Survey Opens Today
Here at the Irvington Union Free School District, we know good things happen when we partner with our community. By working together, we can prepare all our students for a bright future and ensure our staff members’ professional success. That is why it is so critical that we hear from you through our School Quality Survey.
The survey asks for feedback on student and academic support, safety and behavior, school leadership, family engagement and more. The results will help us better understand our strengths and opportunities for improvement, so that we can work to meet the needs of every student.
The online survey is compatible with any computer, tablet, or smartphone. Parents and staff members will receive an email with directions as to how to complete the survey. Middle School and High School students will have the opportunity to complete the survey in school after spring recess. Community members can take the survey at https://survey.k12insight.com/r/SchoolQuality19. The survey is offered in English and Spanish. All responses are strictly confidential.
For more information about the survey, please contact Donna Friedlander at (914) 591-8501.
Thank you very much for your continued support and feedback.
