This morning, the Irvington UFSD facilitated its first round of COVID-19 surveillance testing; wherein, just shy of 5% of those learning on site participated. The District is pleased to report that there were no cases of COVID-19 detected.
The District extends its appreciation to the school community members who participated in today's testing.
