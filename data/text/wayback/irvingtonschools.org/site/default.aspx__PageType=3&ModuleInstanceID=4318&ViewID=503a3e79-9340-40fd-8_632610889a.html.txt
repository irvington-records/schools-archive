Main Street School is located in the heart of the village on Main Street.  Our parking is limited, so we ask that you refrain from parking in the school lot as the spots are reserved for staff; street parking is readily available.   All visitors should enter through our main entrance, show ID and sign in.  Following sign-in procedures, a greeter will direct you to the appropriate location.
