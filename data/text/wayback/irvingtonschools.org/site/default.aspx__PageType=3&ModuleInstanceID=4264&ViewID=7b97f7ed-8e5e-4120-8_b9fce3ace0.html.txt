Boys Cross Country Captures 3rd Place at State Championships
Congratulations to the boys Cross Country team for their 3rd place finish in Class C in the State Championships on Saturday. The team edged out Port Jefferson and Newfane by 1 point to claim 3rd place out of 11 teams. Top runners Ryan Flanagan and Alex Bjorgvinsson were 26th and 27th respectively out of the 131 runners in the race. A great end to an amazing season for the team and coaches Chris Barry and Deryl Thornton.
