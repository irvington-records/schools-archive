            Dows Lane Welcomes Incoming Kindergartners
        
Kindergartners and their families buckled up for a special bus ride around Dows Lane Elementary School’s parking lot on Aug. 29. They were welcomed by Principal Dr. Andrea Kantor and Assistant Principal Thomas Chickery, who explained bus safety and kindergarten routines and answered questions about the upcoming school year.
“There was even a little singing on some of the bus rides,” Dr. Kantor said. “The students and families were excited to board the big bus and hear all about kindergarten before the big day, next Tuesday, Sept. 6.”
Special thanks to the Irvington PTSA for greeting the kindergartners and new families and welcoming them with a plush bulldog gift.
