            Fourth Graders Celebrate End of Year With Picnic
        
Fourth graders celebrated the end of the school year with a picnic at Scenic Hudson Park on June 10. They walked down to the park with their class, where they spent the day together and welcomed their family members for lunch. Special thanks to the PTSA for coordinating ice pops and watermelon for the students.
