            Special BOE Meeting - Capital Project Update, Weds., March 13th, 7:30 pm
        
On Wednesday, March 13, 2019, the Board of Education will host a special meeting dedicated to the proposed capital project.  In this meeting, the Buildings and Grounds Committee will make revised recommendations for inclusion in the project. This meeting will feature important discussion that will inform the Board’s approval of the final scope of the project. As always, feedback from the school community will be welcomed and valued.
All community members are encouraged to attend this meeting.  For more information on the capital project, please visit the District website: www.IrvingtonSchools.org or click here.
