            Talented MSS Students Perform at Spring Concert
        
Main Street School students held their spring concert on June 9 in the campus theater on the Irvington Middle School and Irvington High School campus. Members of the fourth and fifth grade bands, fourth grade chorus, fourth and fifth grade orchestras, and Main Street School Singers performed a variety of repertoire.
The students worked diligently in preparation for the concert, under the direction of fourth grade chorus and orchestra director Nicole Allison and band and MSS Singers director Megan Hannon.
In addition, they presented an in-school concert for their peers during the school day on June 8.
