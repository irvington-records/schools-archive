Today, the District hosted its second round of COVID-19 surveillance testing. 108 individuals were tested, all of whom were negative! To date, we have tested nearly 220 people of whom have tested negative. The next round of testing will be on Monday, May 10 with invitations going out next Wednesday. 
Our thanks and appreciation for everyone’s support and participation in the testing process.
