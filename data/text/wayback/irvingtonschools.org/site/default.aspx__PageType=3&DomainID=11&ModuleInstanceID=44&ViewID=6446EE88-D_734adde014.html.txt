            Fourth Graders Welcome Third Graders to MSS
        
Main Street School fourth graders welcomed Dows Lane Elementary School third graders to their school to help support their transition to MSS in the fall. When they arrived at MSS, the third graders were paired with their fourth grade buddies, with whom they’ve been corresponding ahead of their visit. Then, the fourth graders led them on a tour of the building, including various rooms and offices, and participated in get-to-know-you activities and games. The students will engage in a variety of activities together from May 31-June 16.
