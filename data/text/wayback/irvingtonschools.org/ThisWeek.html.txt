This Week in Your School District
February 11, 2005
The next public meeting and work session of the Board of Education will be held on Tuesday, February 15 beginning at 7:30 PM in the Campus presentation room. The agenda, which will be on our web site on Monday under "BOE Agenda/Minutes" or by clicking http://www.irvingtonschools.org/Records.html will include a report on Pupil Personnel Services by Director of Pupil Personnel Services Sheralyn Gottlieb, a discussion regarding the proposal to enhance our athletic fields, and a discussion of the fixed costs in the 2005-06 Budget.
The next general membership meeting of the PTSA Special Education Parent group will be held on Thursday, February 17 beginning at 7 PM in the Main Street School Library. For additional information contact Donna Taylor at dgerardtaylor@aol.com, Penny Muise at lobo4@optonline.net or Barbara Gerson at begerson@optonline.net.
The PTSA Food and Menu Committee is looking for additional volunteers. This group will be meeting regularly and reviewing all aspects of food items that are available in the school cafeterias and vending machines. The committee is co-chaired by Pat Foung, Registered Dietitian and Esther Samra. If you are interested please contact Esther Samra at: esamra@bestweb.net or 332-4988.
The Irvington Schools community is proud to announce that five students have been named National Merit Finalists: Sam Abbott, Colin Bando, Romit Bhattacharya, Will Forster, and Eric Saar. This in incredible achievement and we wish them all the best in the final stage of this pretigious national competition.
Schools are closed for mid-winter recess from Monday, February 21 through Friday, February 25 so we wish you all an enjoyable, safe and relaxing break!
