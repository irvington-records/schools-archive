SOURCE http://www.irvingtonschools.org:80/PLA.html | DATE 2004-09-06 | TITLE Principal's Leadership Award

Noah Spitzer-Williams, an Irvington High School senior, was named the school's recipient of the Principal's Leadership Award and is now eligible for one of the 150 nation-wide scholarships of $1,000 each.
Noah, a popular peer leader and senior class president, plans to attend Tufts University's School of Computer Science in the fall where he plans to major in engineering. "Noah is one of the most positive and respected students in our school,'' said Irvington High School Principal Scott Mosenthal. "He is an outstanding leader who serves as an great role model for other students."
