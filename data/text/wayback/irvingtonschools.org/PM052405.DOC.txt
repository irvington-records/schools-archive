The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington Campus Presentation Room, May 24, 2005, with the following present:

Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Carol Gutman.  Also present: 
Dr. Kathleen Matusiak - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Ralph Napolitano - Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mr. Scott Fried – Middle School Principal, Mr. Don Kuhn – Main Street School Interim Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Principal, Ms. Eileen McGuire – Dows Lane Assistant Principal, Ms. Erika Trautman-Raser – Asst. Director of  Special Education, Mrs. Robyn Kerner – Trustee Elect, members of the community.  Absent:  Mr. Paul Mandel 

I.  	CALL TO ORDER

The Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Penny Delany at 7:35 p.m., and the Pledge of Allegiance was said.  Mr. Mandel is away on business.

II	MINUTES OF MEETINGS - None

III	INFORMATION ITEMS  

	Dr. Matusiak reported on the following:
Last evening was the Lower Hudson Council of School Superintendents annual dinner.  Two students, Romit Bhattacharya and Will Forster, were honored.
The new High School yearbook is available.
Deb Hardy has been named  Grade 6-12 Counselor of the Year by the American School Counselors Association.  The Board presented her with an achievement certificate.  Dr. Matusiak added that we are most fortunate to have her.

Mrs. Delany added a Thank You to Dr. Matusiak for working with the Special Education parents.  She added that the IEF Run/Walk for Excellence is scheduled for June 6th. 

IV	COMMENTS FROM RESIDENTS 

Deb Weinberg announced that the final PTSA general meeting of the year will be held on June 1, 2005 in the CPR at 9:30 a.m. 
Several students asked for clarification regarding the scheduling of Girls LaCrosse.  The sport is being phased in beginning this year with a modified team and JV beginning next year.

V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS
 
Superintendent’s Report  
1.  Elementary School End of Year Reports
Dr. Matusiak introduced Mr. Rodriguez, Mr. Kuhn and Ms. McGuire for the elementary school end of the year update.  Preparations are underway for the 2005-06 school year, with 3rd graders’ visits to  Main Street School, Kindergarten orientation and the Main Street 5th graders’ meeting with the principal and guidance counselors at the Middle School.  Mr. Rodriguez reported that follow-up meetings, informational surveys and training are continuing for the Trailblazers math program.  Main Street is also planning workshops and meetings with the consultant.

Ms. McGuire discussed the emphasis on character education.  Activities have been introduced about being a good citizen, how we show respect and caring and avoid “put downs”.  Mr. Kuhn reported on the status of the special education autistic class, collaborative teaching model, resource room and BOCES program at Main Street School.  




Many varied and special activities for students took place this year with many more planned for next year.  

Dr. Napolitano briefly reported on the 2004-05 ELA Test scores.

Following a question and answer period, Mrs. Delany and Dr. Matusiak thanked the administrators for their presentation.  

Human Resources Report
Dr. Matusiak introduced Deborah Legato, Human Resources Coordinator, for a presentation of an overview of Human Resources functions.

Mrs. Legato described the centralization of these functions including attendance, tenure, recruitment, certification, Workers Compensation and benefits, among others.  Future initiatives will include implementation of Finance Manager, a comprehensive software package, enhancements to the web page, development of  recruitment guidelines for administrators, and employee training.  

Mrs. Delany commented that Deborah is a wonderful addition to the staff and thanked her for the presentation.

VI          BUSINESS ITEMS

Treasurer’s Report –  None

Kitchen Equipment Bid
A motion was made by Mrs. Carol Gutman, seconded by Mrs. Janice Silverberg, to adopt the following Resolution as presented:

“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District reject the bid for Kitchen Equipment which was opened on May 4, 2005,

AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District authorize the re-bidding of the contract for Kitchen Equipment.”
Vote:  4-0 Approved

Campus HVAC Bid Award
A motion was made by Mrs. Carol Gutman, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to adopt the following Resolution as presented:




“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the Bid for Testing, Adjusting and Balancing of the HS/MS Campus HVAC System to D.L. Flow Tech Inc. as the lowest responsive and responsible bidder with a base bid price of $67,980.”
Vote:  4-0 Approved
