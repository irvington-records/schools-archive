Shop On-Line and Support the Irvington Education Foundation


 The Irvington Education Foundation in partnership with Schoolpop has created an on-line shopping mall for Irvington Union Free School District with over 250 top retailers, such as Barnes&Noble, Nordstroms, Dell, OfficeMax, and Buy.com.  
Just go to www.schoolpop.com,
Register and Select [Irvington Schools, Irvington, New York] and
Start shopping.
Every purchase made through Schoolpop will benefit Irvington Schools – at no additional cost.  Once Irvington Schools is designated and a purchase is made, the participating retailer will automatically rebate our school up to 20% of the total purchase price, excluding taxes and shipping.  

How Our School Can Benefit—

The combined purchasing power of persons connected to our school poses an incredible fundraising opportunity for our school.  Just think of the number of parents, grandparents, relatives, friends, neighbors, teachers, administrators…and their families, friends, and colleagues (nationwide) associated with our school.
