The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library, April 22, 2003, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg,  Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman – Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Mr. George Kuntz – Superintendent of Buildings and Grounds, Mr. Scott Baird – JMOA Engineering, P.C., Mrs. Sandy Mintzes – Peter Gisolfi Associates and members of the community.  Absent:  Mr. George Huzinec 

I  	CALL TO ORDER

The Public Meeting was called to order, from Executive Session, by Mrs. Janice Silverberg at 7:47 p.m. and the pledge of allegiance was given.

II	INFORMATION ITEMS

Dr. Beni reported that the state legislature may over-ride the Governor’s veto of the bill that would change the date of the school district’s Annual Meeting from May 20, 2003 to June 3, 2003.  This postponement is designed to provide additional time to allocate potential additional  funding for school districts to reduce the budget increase for taxpayers.  The Irvington School District would be minimally affected and will continue to follow the budget vote schedule for May 20, 2003 until the decision is made early next week.

Mr. Mandel commented that the New York State Budget should be in place by April 1st of each year.
