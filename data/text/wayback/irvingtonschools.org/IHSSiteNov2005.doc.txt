Notes: I.H.S Site Committee Meeting 11/13/05
Present: Janet Williams, Jessica Schwartz, Pat Tomasso, Anne Keary, Patty Potash, Dave Mintzes, Dalia May, Gideon Mausner (student), Joe DeGennaro, Scott Mosenthal


Site committee names to be sent to community at large: A decision was made to make contact information for adult members of all Irvington committees emailed via Paul Mandel’s listserv to help members of the community feel that their concerns and issues are better represented.
Edline off to a good start: The new Edline service was briefly discussed and thought to be most valuable when documents are posted.  It was also noted that most students (except Freshman) are not being encourage to go to Edline.  Integrated pro, which will make available individual student information (grades, missing or completed homework assignments), is not used by every teacher yet.  Scott Mosenthal cautioned that this is a work in progress.
Computer policy: Currently, the library is open to 4:30 p.m., so that students without access to computers at home may be at a disadvantage.  Those attending the school’s weekly instructional support meetings know which kids do not have access to technology.  We discussed whether there were ways for parents to donate computers.  It was decided that the Guidance Department should determine the need before we discuss next steps. 
The middle student:  The main discussion of the meeting was on the student who is  not reaching his/her full potential: the underachiever or the student who is not comfortable seeking help, perhaps.  The school does indeed have programs in place.  “Student of the Month” recognizes students that are striving hard and have a great outlook.  Each department picks several kids on a monthly basis, and they are honored in the principal’s message in the newsletter and through a letter sent home.  Scott also talked about his conversations with small groups of students to draw out their issues.
Nonetheless, many students will shy away from expressing themselves to the principal, guidance counselor or teacher.  It was agreed that we could try a student satisfaction survey as a way to get at perhaps other insights about why some students are not engaging their teachers or comfortable coming in for extra help.  Based on a survey instrument from the National Study of School Evaluation, as well as a prior survey used at Irvington some five or six years ago, Scott agreed to create a new, short survey and send it to Site Committee members for comments.  The survey, along with other efforts, may help show students how seriously the administration takes their opinions and to encourage them to reach out and get the attention they need.
Report cards: Because of the volume of students and numbers of envelope stuffers, the report card mailings went out in two batches. 
Upcoming events: Scott mentioned several events: among them, the winter concert, a dance performance and a basketball tournament.
Food: Many students are voting on cafeteria food with their wallets, by ordering lunch from local vendors.  Are these deliveries creating a safety hazard, or are they creating a management problem with the food service providers?  The Site Committee believed that the safety problem could be easily solved, if that in fact was the only issue.
