Also present: Dr. Kathleen L Matusiak - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Ralph Napolitano - Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Ms. Raina Kor-Irvington Middle Assistant School Principal, Ms. Eileen McGuire- Dows Lane Assistant Principal, Dr. Scott Mosenthal-Irvington High School Principal, Mr. Joe DeGennaro-Irvington High School Assistant Principal, Dr. Joel Klein, Interim Director Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Madee Stern, Assistant Director of Pupil Personnel, Mr. Artie McCormack-Director of Athletics, members of the staff and community.  

        I.	       CALL TO ORDER
The Public Meeting was called to order by Mr. Paul Mandel at 7:25 p.m., and the Pledge of Allegiance was said.

STUDENT RECOGNITON
Dr. Matusiak presented Scott and Casey DiCesare each with a Certificate of Athletic Accomplishments.  A video was shown of Scott’s pole vault jump at the June’s New York State Meet and Casey’s record- setting jump of 16.5’ at the Armory in December. Scott and Casey were winners of the Con Ed Award this year, one of the most prestigious awards in Westchester County for athletic achievements. Dr. Mosenthal, Mr. Artie McCormack and Coach Peter Oley spoke of both boys’ many accomplishments, academically and athletically, as well as their outstanding citizenship to both school and community.

COMMENTS FROM RESIDENTS
A parent thanked Dr. Joel Klein for arranging outside testing for her child; she also commented on busing. 
Deb Weintraub, PTSA Co-President, announced that as part of their General Membership   Meeting, a budget forum will be held on Wednesday, January 11th at 7:30 in the CPR. Other key events are:  February 1st – Health & Safety Committee hosting an inexperienced driver program for parents and on February 8th is for students; February 7th - Dows Lane 50th Birthday Party with former teachers and students; February 10th – Harlem Magic Masters in the A&A. Tickets will go on sale on January 21st.

      V.	       INFORMATION ITEMS
Dr. Matusiak thanked the PTSA for all they do throughout the year and mentioned that their after-school program offerings for the 2nd half of the school year look great.
The IEF Gala will be on January 28th and a number of new teachers and staff have received complimentary tickets.
The grant writing process is underway with meetings scheduled to look into the possibility of a collaborative grant between the District’s Wellness Committee and Stone Barns, a community literacy grant between the District, several local preschools and the Irvington Public Library, and a possible grant between the District and the Jacob Burns Theatre that would capitalize on the use of the District’s theatre.
David Enrenreich, a 6th grader, qualified through the State’s qualifying exam, to participate in the New York State Geography Bee. 
Dr. Matusiak visited with several 5th grade classes; she spoke to them about the budget process and read poetry to them.
Edline, the District’s web-based site, will be formally rolled out at the end of January. The site will contain the same information as the District’s current site, but with a different look and style.
At every Board of Education meeting from now until the budget vote in May, there will be a budget component to provide information and solicit input from the community. The proposed budget will follow the goals set forth in the Strategic Plan. The topics for these meetings will be announced in advance of the meeting dates. In addition to the regularly scheduled Board meeting, a Saturday and Thursday evening meeting will be scheduled.
The PTSA has met with the Principals to develop a Principal Wish List. 





 PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS:
                    A.     Superintendent’s Report:  

          1.  Fixed Costs   (Supplemental Log – Appendix A)
Mr. James Reese presented the 2006-07 Proposed Budget Fixed Costs and explained that the budget amounts could be variable. Fixed costs are those expenses that the District is obligated to pay, such as utilities, the District’s debt service, health insurance, the teacher’s retirement system, and employee benefits. Mr. Reese does not anticipate that there will be a reduction to the amount of State Aid the district receives as there was last year. The District has been holding discussions with the New York Power Authority to find ways to save on energy and perhaps take advantage of their energy conservation program whereby the Authority would lend the District money to do energy-saving modifications. Mr. Reese explained that the District should realize a significant reduction in the cost of telephone service due to the long-term agreement having just expired and now owing our system. 
	    
	          2.     Academic Intervention Services (AIS)   (Supplemental Log – Appendix B)
Dr. Ralph Napolitano presented an overview of the District’s AIS program. Dr. Napolitano     reviewed the number of students currently receiving AIS services, the staffing requirements and methods of delivery of AIS. Dr. Napolitano also reviewed each school’s criteria for AIS placement. 

There are a total of 398 students currently receiving AIS in the District, with 9.2 teachers     delivering services in ELA, Math, Science, Social Studies and English/Study Skills at the high school level. The 2006-07 school year’s needs will be based on the number of students who fail the State exams and, therefore, cannot be determined until June, 2006.  However, it is possible that an increase in services will be need at the Main Street School.
