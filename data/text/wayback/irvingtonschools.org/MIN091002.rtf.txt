 V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

  Superintendent’s Report - Opening of School Reports
Dr. Beni reported that the staff overcame many challenges and that the opening of school was very smooth.  The present enrollment is 1,960 with a total of 15 new teachers.  New this year is the girls merged swim team and girls soccer has changed seasons from Spring to Fall.  This year the Board meetings will focus on program presentations and construction updates.  
         
Dr. Beni asked each Principal to report on their opening days and to introduce the new faculty members in their building.  Dr. Mosenthal said that the High School year is off to a great start with an enrollment of 482.  9th graders have had orientation and the Code of Conduct and school rules have been reviewed with the student body.  Parking issues are being worked on by students and the administration.  Mrs. Allan reported Middle School enrollment of 478.  There were some scheduling issues as well as new lockers that arrived several days late.  The IMS Open House night is schedule for September 12, 2002.  Dr. Garcia-Blocker said that Grade 4 - 5 buses ran well, the cafeteria is up and running and that computer, mathematics and instrumental music instruction is underway.  All of the “specials” have their own classroom assignments this year.  Grade 4 -5 enrollment is 329.  Mr. Rodriguez reported the grade K - 3 enrollment is 641.  Kindergarten parents rode the buses with their children on the first day to ease the transition to school.  Many students have returned to Dows Lane from placements out of the District and are doing well.  

Dr. Beni commented on the following:
The staff is prepared to handle the observance of the September 11th anniversary.  
The Community Advisory Board has been very active and investigating many options for providing additional services and opportunities for the children of Irvington. 
There are fourteen (14) contractors on the High School campus.  There is a possibility of picketing on the east end of the campus to protest the hiring of non-union contractors (the District is required, by law to award bids to the lowest responsible bidder).
