The sixth annual O'Hara Invitational Youth Basketball Tournament will take place on November 9, 10, 11, 17 & 18. Once again the fourth annual Student/Faculty game will help kick off the tournament on Saturday, November 10 at 8 pm at the High School. Admission: Adults $3, Students $1. Last year the faculty put the pressure on in the final seconds and won the game for the first time.
The Charity game on Saturday, November 17, 8 pm at the High School will feature the "Heroes" vs. WFAN All Stars, including Chris "Mad Dog" Russo. We are proud to have NYC and local police and firefighters participating in the game. Admission: Adults $5, Students $3. There will be live auctions at halftime at both games. The Grand raffle is two tickets to the Super Bowl in New Orleans, February 3, 2002, with hotel accommodations for four nights. Raffles are $20 each or three for $50.
Volunteers are still needed at the food concessions, raffle table and admissions booth.
All proceeds from the Annual Basketball Tournament and raffles go to the O'Hara Foundation. The O'Hara Foundation is a not-for-profit public charity established as a means of memorializing Jack and Janet O'Hara and their daughter, Caitlin, who were tragically killed in the July 1996 explosion of TWA Flight 800. The O'Hara's are survived by their twin sons Brian and Matthew. The goal of the O'Hara Foundation is to keep the O'Hara spirit of giving and caring alive through donations to education, youth activities and community-related programs. Tax-deductible donations are welcome and can be sent to the O'Hara Foundation, P.O. Box 39, Irvington, NY 10533.
For further information regarding volunteering or donations, please call Vicki Lewin at 591-8611.
