The improvements afforded by the 2001-2002 District Budget will enable our schools to provide an educational program that our entire community will be proud of. The Budget is the result of months of work by our Superintendent and his administrative team culminating in a series of public presentations at Board of Education meetings this spring. Some of the key initiatives contained in the budget include:
- Educational programs - An increase in our teaching staff will help us meet our projected 6.3% enrollment increase We will maintain average class sizes at reasonable levels, continue to align our curriculum with the new NY state standards and provide additional resources to students with special needs. We will be able to augment course offerings K-12: there will be a new theater program in Dows Lane, a new orchestra program at the Middle School and 5 new Advanced Placement courses in the High School. A new Assistant Principal will help run a rapidly growing Middle School with an enrollment that will exceed 410. Finally, our Assistant Superintendent of Curriculum will continue her work of updating and articulating our K-12 curriculum.
- Master Plan/Space Needs - We will lease and install 2 additional modulars at the Middle School. With the initiation of site work this summer and construction later this year, this budget contains the first payments for the Master Plan Project.
- Technology - We continue our investment in educational technology by equipping each new class with the necessary workstations. Our District Technology Committee will create a grade-by-grade outline of competencies that each student should attain. Most significantly, we will maintain our concentration on staff training so that technology will be seamlessly integrated into the everyday education of our children.
All this and more results in a projected tax rate increase of 8.88% ($23.43 per $1,000 of assessed value) - in the middle of tax rate increases in Westchester. It is important to note that roughly half of this increase is the result of rapidly escalating contractual costs such as transportation medical insurance, and utilities. Even so, those who have applied for the STAR exemption might actually see a net tax reduction. Take the time to look through this brochure to familiarize yourself with the Budget.
