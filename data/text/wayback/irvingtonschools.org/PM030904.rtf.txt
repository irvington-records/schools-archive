III	INFORMATION ITEMS 

	Dr. Beni reported on the following:

V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

  Superintendent’s Report  

Math Curriculum Update:  Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman
Dr. Dolan-Waldman reported that the Trailblazers program has been selected as the math curriculum for the next school year.  With positive teacher recommendations, the teacher training has begun and classroom materials will be ordered.  Mrs. Silverberg added that this decision has been made following two (2) years of investigation and evaluation by the administration, teachers and parent groups.  Dr. Dolan-Waldman also reported that there are several Westchester districts that have implemented the Trailblazers program including Scarsdale (a site visit was made) and Bedford.  

A petition was presented to the Board with approximately 300 signatures opposing the implementation of the Trailblazers program and the elimination of the tracking system now in place in the elementary school.  Several parents spoke to the issue, representing all sides of the discussion of tracking.  


Mr. Mandel asked Dr. Dolan-Waldman which other “high performing” districts use a tracking system in the elementary grades.  Dr. Dolan-Waldman responded that, of approximately 40 districts surveyed, none have a tracking system in the elementary grades.

Mr. Huzinec’s statement highlighted the fact that the teachers and administrators are professionals who are key to making curriculum recommendations to the Board.    Mr. Mandel added that it is great to see a group of parents at the meeting for discussion of this issue.  

Dr. Beni said that, in choosing curriculum, decisions are made in a K – 12 perspective and that we should listen to what the teachers recommend.  He added that students rarely move out of the group that they were placed in while in the 3rd grade.  The budget for professional development has been substantially increased during the last 5 years demonstrating the investment of time and financial resources that the District has made for curriculum development in this high-stakes environment.  

Mrs. Delany thanked the Board and community for a positive discussion of the issue and reminded everyone present that the curriculum process and review is an on-going initiative.

Tax Certiorari Settlement – Pennybridge Manor Condominium
A motion was made by Mr. George Huzinec, seconded by Mrs. Janice Silverberg, to adopt the following Resolution:

“WHEREAS, law suits have been commenced to challenge the assessments on the property owned by Pennybridge Manor Condominium, a 73 unit townhouse style condominium development located on Sunnyside Place, for assessment years 1998 through 2003; and

WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District, through its attorney, appeared in the pending litigation to protect the interests of the School District; and

WHEREAS, several meetings were held with the attorney for the petitioner, the Deputy Town Attorney for the Town of Greenburgh and counsel for the School District and various settlement proposals were discussed at length prior to arriving at a tentative settlement; and

WHEREAS, the School District’s counsel, the Town of Greenburgh Assessor and Deputy Town Attorney all recommend approving the tentative settlement; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that it is in the best interest of the School District to settlement this law suit with the following reductions in assessed values:
