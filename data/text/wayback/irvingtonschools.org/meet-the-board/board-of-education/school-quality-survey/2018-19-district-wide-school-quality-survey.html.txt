2018-19 District-wide School Quality Survey
The Irvington Union Free School District presented the results from the 2018-19 District-wide School Quality Survey at the June 17h Board of Education meeting. The survey sought feedback about school experiences, student and academic support, safety and behavior, school leadership and family involvement.
The presentation included the feedback from middle school and high school students, staff, parents and community members who completed the survey.
As in prior years, K12 Insight, an independent research and communications firm that the District partnered with, administered the survey and compiled the results.
