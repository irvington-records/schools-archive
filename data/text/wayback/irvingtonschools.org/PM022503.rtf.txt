The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library, February 25, 2003, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg,  Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman – Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mrs. Lauren Allan – Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane 
Gr. K-3 Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Dows Lane Gr. 4-5 Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor – Middle School Assistant Principal, Mrs. Susan Levey – Director Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar – Director of Technology. 

I  	CALL TO ORDER

The Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Janice Silverberg at 7:10 p.m.

A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to adjourn to Executive Session for the purpose of discussing particular person(s).
Vote:  5-0

A motion was made at 7:35 p.m. by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to adjourn the Executive Session to the Public Work Session.  
Vote:  5-0

II	CALL TO ORDER

At 9:55 p.m. Mrs. Janice Silverberg called the Public Meeting to order from the Public Work Session. 

III	BUSINESS ITEMS

Construction Bid
A motion was made by Mrs. Penny Delany, seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel, to adopt the following Resolution:
“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the Locker Bid-Contract No. HSNR9 to Nickerson Corporation as the lowest responsive and responsible bidder with a base bid price of $164,792.”
Vote:  5-0 Approved
