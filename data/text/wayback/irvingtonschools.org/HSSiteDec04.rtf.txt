SITE MEETING MINUTES FROM DECEMBER 21, 2004 

Present: Janet Williams, Zach Feinstein, Dr. Dalia May, Jeanine Hart, Eden Univer, Katie Lapham, Joe DeGennaro, Alison Romaine, Dr. Scott Mosenthal, Becky Lazar, Tristan Naumann and Lily Silpe.

Minutes were reviewed and approved from the previous meeting, which was held in November.

A letter from Dr. Matusiak was distributed to the committee to thank everyone for allowing her to participate at our November meeting.
Scott opened a discussion on the various heat issues in the high school building. Most rooms were cold and a couple of rooms were extremely hot. Heat is inconsistent throughout the campus. Outside walkways are dangerous, especially for the younger students. The theater is so cold that performing in the facility is challenging. The new gym has the same problem. Joe and Scott attended a basketball game recently and had to wear their jackets. The heating company continues to work on the heating unit to properly regulate (Note: since our meeting, many of the heating issues have been resolved.)
A question was raised about providing a series of drug/alcohol speakers to continue to educate students and parents. Scott/Joe will speak to Student Assistance Counselor Lydia Fernandez to explore writing a grant to fund.
All newsletter/advertisements need to clarify if students and parents are expected to attend various night school sponsored functions. 
Scott updated the mid-year calendar. The second quarter ends on January 14th and mid-term week is January 25-28. Mid-term exams will count toward the third quarter grade. This is the first time the high school has elected to use a schedule like this. The request came from the high school faculty council to ease the January academic pressures for teachers and students.

Scott commented how difficult this week is because it is early decision week for seniors.
There was a leak in the atrium today. The ceiling needed to be opened up to search for the cause.   
Zach shared a few concerns in the music department. A student will have points removed from his/her grade if a lesson is missed in chorus…even with an excused absence. Also, students are let out late (after 3:12). Scott recommended that Zach speak to Ms. Gillespie and Mr. Corio. 
PARKING--Will juniors be permitted to park on campus once the seniors have gone on their internships? Will there be a lottery for seniors to park on campus next year? These questions will be discussed and answered at a future site meeting. 
Scheduling music 9th period has caused a decrease in extracurricular activity participation. Students that leave at the end of 8th period often will not come back for meeting that begins at 3:15. Additionally, the sports programs start at 3:30. Students would like to, if possible, explore putting the music classes in the middle of the day so all students leave school at the same time and extracurriculars can increase participation. There are many reasons why the schedule was changed this year all connected to improving the shared campus.
A comment was shared regarding some of the issues in the computer lab next to the library. Students are regularly using the lab unsupervised, a 2nd period class is assigned to the lab and the heat is unbearable because there are no windows. The other concern is the heat will eventually disrupt the computers effectiveness. Also, the AP Computer Science student needs access to the computers in this lab because it is the only lab that has the necessary software for the course. 
Driver Education Classes—why don’t we offer it here? Scott stated Mr. James Reese did not want the responsibility due to the liability. Scott also said that if there was enough interest, he will pursue offering the course with Central Office.
Academic Recognition-Do we do enough to recognize students? There is so much recognition for athletes in the newspapers…what can we do for academic achievement? We don’t have an honor society, but can we award by subject across all grades? Scott cautioned the group about awards. Often, backlash occurs. Deb Hardy will alert parents about certain organizations that are positive/negative. A list of high honor and honor roll students will be posted in the office.

At the January meeting—parents will report what students think about potential honor societies.
