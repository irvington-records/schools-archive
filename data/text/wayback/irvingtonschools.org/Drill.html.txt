Sheltering Drill
Dear Parents,
Issues concerning school safety have predominated the news over the past several weeks. Recent events nationally and in Westchester remind us that schools remain vulnerable to threats of violence. Last summer, the New York State Legislature passed a series of laws regulating school safety known as Project SAVE (Safe Schools Against Violence Education act). As part of our involvement in implementing Project SAVE, our district has expanded the scope of the District Emergency Team. That group of individuals has worked with security professionals to assess and review our school security practices.
Each year, all school districts are required to implement some aspect of their emergency management plans. In the past, we have generally done an early dismissal drill which is part of that plan. This year, however, we believe it is even more important to conduct a drill featuring another aspect of our plan - sheltering (moving the students to a safe indoor location) - to determine its viability. Our plan states that, given an emergency such as a gas leak, High School students would be directed to the Middle School gymnasium until the High School is determined to be secure. If the problem is at the Middle School, those students would be sheltered at the High School. Dows Lane students would walk to the Middle School and be sheltered in the auditorium and gymnasium. These plans have been developed with input from the police and other municipal officials as well as with our own staff including our transportation provider.
Our plan is to conduct an unannounced sheltering drill for each school between May 29 and June 8. We plan to have Middle and High School students return to their schools when their respective drills are completed. On another day, we will be dismissing Dows Lane students from the Middle School (on or about regular dismissal time) on the day of their drill. You will be informed about the Dows Lane drill through an emergency telephone chain.
We ask Dows Lane parents to please be sure you have given the school office your most up-to-date emergency contact information.
There will be a designated pick up area at the Middle School for parents whose children do not receive transportation. Please be aware that Main Street will obviously be very congested. There will be delays as we dismiss those children who are transported by bus.
We have discussed this drill with the PTSA leadership who agreed with us that the drill should be conducted. Our staff will assure the children that the event is only a drill.
As we prepare for next year, our District Emergency Team has made some other recommendations for our consideration. We are in the process of assessing them and will have public discussion prior to implementation. We want to keep our schools as safe as possible while retaining aspects of our friendly, inviting atmosphere at each building.
All of us should remember that schools still remain the safest place for kids. Our district will continue to research and implement programs which help students connect to their school, their peers and their teachers and recognize individual responsibility to the collective community.
Thanks for your cooperation as we conduct our drill. Please call your child's principal if you have any questions.
Sincerely,
Vincent T. Beni
