III	INFORMATION ITEMS  

	Dr. Matusiak reported on the following:

52 of our High School students have been named AP Scholars – an extraordinary accomplishment.
Met with the Grade 2 teachers as well as the IMS Site Committee today.
The second Technology Committee meeting was today; a draft plan will be presented to the Board shortly.
The budget process has begun with meetings with the building Principals.

Mrs. Delany reported that the Board met last week to set goals for the coming year and to discuss Board development so that the Board might better serve the community.

V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

  Superintendent’s Report – (Supplemental Log – Appendix A) 
Dr. Matusiak introduced Mr. Rodriguez and Ms. McGuire for the presentation of the Dows Lane Elementary School Report.  

Ms. McGuire outlined this presentation:  Student Population, Program, Building and Grounds, Trailblazers Implementation, Instructional Services Team and Special Events.  The student enrollment for 2004-05 is 600 grade K – 3 students with the entire building being utilized for instruction and activities.  The door and window replacement project has been completed.  

Mr. Rodriguez reviewed the implementation of the Trailblazers program including connections to Language Arts, the teaching of integrated Math and Science, New York State Standards, student assessment, staff development and enrichment.  Mr. Rodriguez said that the Trailblazers program has lived up to expectation and that parent feedback has been positive.  Ms. Christine Rosner, Elementary Math Teacher at Dows Lane, explained her role as well.

The purpose, team make-up and procedures of the Instructional Support Team (IST) was discussed by Ms. McGuire.  She also described some of the 22 different Fall activities that have taken place at Dows Lane this year.  

Mrs. Delany thanked Mr. Rodriguez, Ms. McGuire and Ms. Rosner for their presentation.
