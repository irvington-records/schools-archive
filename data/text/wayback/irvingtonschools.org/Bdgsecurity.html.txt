<FONT SIZE=4><B><P ALIGN="CENTER">BUILDING SECURITY POLICY</P></FONT></B>
<P ALIGN="CENTER">&nbsp;</P>
<h3>
<P>All buildings and property of the Irvington Public Schools are closed to the public between the hours of 10:00 p.m. and 6:30 a.m., with the following exception:  On those occasions when the school building and property are being used for activities approved in advance by the Board (or the administration when Board approval is not required) and which are scheduled to extend beyond 10:00 p.m., school buildings and property will remain open beyond 10:00 p.m. to those individuals in direct attendance at such activity until one-half hour after the closing time for such approved activity.</P>
<P>&nbsp;</P>
<P>Any person violating this resolution is subject to the penalties provided by law.</P>
</h3>
