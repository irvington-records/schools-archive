Master Plan Update
Over the past 3 months, several committees (Long-Range Planning, Buildings and Grounds, and Communications) have been quite busy working with various consultants to assist the Board as it develops a Master Plan for our facilities. There have been more than 20 meetings entirely focused on this project. The Long-Range Planning Committee (LRPC), representing the board, staff, administrators, parents, community members and students has performed an invaluable service, responding to reports presented by the district’s demographer, traffic consultant, architect and construction manager.
It is clear that the projected enrollment numbers will require significant facility enhancement.  The groups have discussed:
- The impact of increased numbers of students at all three schools;
- The need for additional or improved science laboratory, library, cafeteria, gymnasium and auditorium space, as well as classroom and laboratory space for an expanded technology program;
- The estimated cost per square foot and per student of various configurations of new and renovated facilities;
- The availability of land for a new school site, and the additional construction costs involved in site preparation;
- The SEQRA process for obtaining State approvals for any facility construction;
The committees recognized that our district has already far surpassed the enrollments of Dobbs Ferry and Hastings and will eventually be as large as Greenburgh, Mount Pleasant, Byram Hills and Rye. We are also much larger geographically than our neighboring districts.
Although the board is several steps away from finalizing a plan, the committees have been able to narrow the options identified in the original long-range planning report.  The LRPC believes the board should eliminate several options:
- K-4 DL, 5-6 MS, and 7-12 HS;
- K-2 DL, new 3-5, new 6-8, 9-12 HS;
- K-5 DL, new K-5, new 6-8 and 9-12 HS;
- K-3 DL, new 4-6, 7-8 MS and 9-12 HS;
- K-3 DL, 4-5 MS, new 6-8 and 9-12 HS.
(DL – Dows Lane School, MS – Main Street School, HS = High School campus)
There is one option which the committees believe would best address the district’s space and program needs: K-3 DL, 4-5 MS, 6-8/9-12 HS
We look forward to sharing with the community the outline of a plan which includes constructing a new 6-8 Middle School on the High School campus and reducing the overpopulation of Dows Lane by housing grades 4 and 5 at the Main Street building.
The Board will hold an important work session on September 28, at 7:30 p.m. in the Middle School Auditorium. At this work session, various consultants who have worked with the district’s committees, including the architect, construction manager, traffic consultant and financial advisor, will present components of this option to the Board. The Board will discuss the implications of this option for the district. Community members are strongly encouraged to attend this important meeting.
If you are interested in more detailed information, the minutes of the LRPC meetings are posted on our website: www.irvingtonschools.org. Copies of the minutes are available in each school or from the District Clerk in the Central Office.
