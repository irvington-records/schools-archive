Irvington communications consultant and former WPIX TV reporter Laurie Chock came to Irvington H.S. this week to share some of her communication skills with juniors facing the often dreaded college interview. "Like most challenges in life, the key is to break down the issues, and then they are a lot easier to conquer," said Chock who gave students important points to consider before they step into their interviews with college admission officials. "It is imporant to size control of the interview by not being passive. Remember, this is your time to get your message across."
Chock advised students to have "specific priority points" ready to talk about that will reveal their "heart and sole" to the interviewer.
She also told them how important it is to know things about the school and be prepared to ask their own probing questions of the interviewer. "Anticipate the questions and rehearse, rehearse, rehearse."
Chock, who is also the mother of an Irvington HS graduate (Class of 2000), and a current junior, volunteered to conduct two days of 40-minute workshops for some 30 students who gave up their lunch period to get tips on how to have a successful interview. "It was great to have an expert in the art of interviewing give our kids some real techniques they can use when they begin this college process," said IHS Guidance Chair Debra Hardy. "We thank Ms. Chock her for giving her time."
