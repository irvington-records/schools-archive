V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS
	
Superintendent’s Report:  
Dr. Matusiak reported that it has been very busy preparing for the students’ first day.  New Teacher Orientation on 8/30/05 welcomed 25 new staff members and that all positions are now filled. The new staff is enthusiastic and eager to begin the year. Dr. Matusiak thanked Dr. Napolitano for helping the new teachers feel acclimated to the District with his combination of warmth and humor. Presentations by the EAP, Deb Legato and an overview of lesson plan development were also part of Orientation.
The principals are doing a great job on their Action Plans and drafts will be forthcoming with regular updates to the Board.
Dr. Matusiak thanked the PTSA for the wonderful breakfast they hosted for the September 1st Superintendent’s Conference Day.  She also thanked the Jazz Band for doing a great job. The focus of the morning session was to explain the Strategic Plan to the staff. The rest of the day was spent in transition and department meetings.
The Technology Plan has been completed with the state-of-the-art computer rooms at the High School. Main Street School’s mobile labs have been delivered; there are 115 new computers and the smart boards  have been installed. 
Edline – needs have been identified, training has begun and the position of Technology Coordinator           has been posted.  “Home Page Advisor” will now be called  “Webmaster.”  All teachers will be required to have their own page by September, 2007.  
Site Committees: Seats on the various committees are still being filled.
The Superintendent has been informed that two students from New Orleans, victims of Hurricane Katrina, will be enrolling in the Irvington Schools. In a directive from Commissioner Richard Mills, school districts in New York must enroll students who were displaced due to the hurricane. Students will be considered “homeless” and school districts will be entitled to receive tuition reimbursement from the state.

Board Sub-Committee Reports

a.    Building and Grounds
Dr. Matusiak reported the following:
The heating and ventilation issues at the High School seem to have been resolved. The first cold day will be the test. 
There has been discussion about placing a freestanding kiosk in the atrium of the High School that would be both functional as well as an area to display student artwork.  
Mr. Reese reported that the drainage work at the High School has been completed.
The drainage problem at the Main Street School playground still persists. The proposals received for repair were over the bid threshold, therefore requiring the District seek bids for the project.  Discussions are ongoing with the architect to determine who is financially responsible for this problem.
 
Finance Committee	
Mr. Paul Mandel reported that the 3-Year Plan’s format has been changed and fiscal assumptions were reviewed and will continue to be updated throughout the year.  The external auditors, Bennett, Kielson, Storch. DeSantis & Co. has provided Mr. Reese with 3-year plan software that will enable him to update data at will.
Discussion on alternative funding options for the turf proposals will be discussed at the September 13th Board meeting. The Board will seek help from parents as well.

Ad Hoc Committee on Enhancement of Irvington Schools Athletic Fields
Mr. Paul Mandel reported that the 27-member committee, comprised of Board, community and faculty members as well as students, 4 times over the summer, communicated via e-mail and site visits were made by some members of the committee. Their report and PowerPoint Presentation will be made at the September 13th Board of Education Meeting.  In addition, Mr. Mandel, Dr. Matusiak and Ron Tetelman, President of Eberlin & Eberlin, will be meeting with residents of Fieldpoint to discuss the project and hear their concerns.

The Board of Education will have follow-up discussions on the proposal at the September 27th Board of Ed meeting as well as a Special Meeting on Saturday, October 1st at 9:00 a.m. Final discussions and approval of the proposal will be on the October 11th Board of Education meeting agenda.
