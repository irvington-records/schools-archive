VOTER REGISTRATION

To vote in Irvington School elections, you must be registered for either school elections or general elections.  If you are not registered, you may register for school voting at the District Clerk’s Office at 90 No. Broadway from 9:00 A.M. to 3:00 P.M. during regular school days, OR, register at the Special Voter Registration Day, Thursday, March 27, 2003 from 4:00 P.M. to 8:00 P.M. in the lobby of the Irvington Middle School.

A person qualified to register to vote must be a citizen of the United States, at least 18 years old, and a resident of the School District for at least thirty days before the vote.

     - Any person who is registered with the Westchester County Board of Elections in 
        the Irvington School District need not register separately with the School District.

     - Any person who voted in any of the Irvington School District elections any time
        since May 1999 is registered with the School District and need not register again.

     - Any person who is not registered with the County and did not vote in any of 
        the Irvington School District elections since May 1999 is not eligible to vote in the   
        School District’s April 3, 2003 Bond Vote (including those who have voted only in Village elections).

ABSENTEE BALLOTS
 
Absentee Ballots are available February 14, 2003 for registered voters who will not be in Irvington on April 3, 2003.  Apply in person in the District Clerk’s Office at 90 North Broadway for an application.  March 27th is the deadline for absentee ballot application if ballot is to be mailed to the voter.  April 2nd is the deadline for absentee ballot application if the ballot is to be picked up by the voter.  April 3, 2003 at 5:00 P.M. is the deadline for absentee ballots to be received by the District Clerk.  Call the District Clerk’s office at 591-8502 for further information.
