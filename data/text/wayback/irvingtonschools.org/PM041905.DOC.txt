The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington Campus Presentation Room, April 19, 2005, with the following present:

Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Carol Gutman.  Also present:  Dr. Kathleen Matusiak - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Ralph Napolitano - Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mr. Scott Fried – Middle School Principal, Mr. Don Kuhn – Main Street School Interim Principal Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Elementary Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor – Middle School Assistant Principal, Ms. Eileen McGuire – Dows Lane Elementary School Assistant Principal, Ms. Sheralyn Gottlieb – Director, Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Erika Trautman-Raser – Director of  Special Education, Mr. Arthur McCormack – Athletic Director, Mr. George Kuntz – Superintendent of Buildings and Grounds, students and members of the community.  

I.  	CALL TO ORDER

The Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Penny Delany at 7:37 p.m., the Pledge of Allegiance was said.

II	MINUTES OF MEETINGS - None

III	RECOGNITION

A.   Boys Fall Cross Country Team
Mr. McCormack reviewed the accomplishments of the Cross Country team over the past several years and introduced Mr. Gregg Ransom of the NYSPHSAA for the presentation of the Fall 2004 Scholar Athlete award for earning first place in New York State with a team academic average o 98.571%, first out of 240 schools.  He noted that this is the 3rd year in a row that the Cross Country Team has taken 1st place in New York State.  Mr. Oley accepted the award on behalf of the team.  He thanked the parents for their support and said that he is very proud of this team.  

Boys Winter Indoor Track Team
The team and coach Laura Petersen accepted the award for being 1st out of 129 schools with an academic team average of 97.917% for the winter 2004 season.  She noted also that the team has just won it’s 120th consecutive dual meet in an 11 year streak.  

National Merit Scholar Finalists
Sam Abbott, Colin Bando, Romit Bhattacharya, Will Forster, Eric Saar were introduced by Dr. Mosenthal.  Students in this group of National Merit Scholar Finalists represent less than 1% of the total of those taking the test.  Mrs. Delany presented each of the students with a certificate. 	

Special Olympics Gold Medal – Floor Hockey
Dr. Mosenthal introduced Frank Ragusa who won a gold medal in Tokyo, Japan representing the USA National Special Olympics Floor Hockey Team.

E.    Pole Vault State Champion
Dr. Mosenthal introduced Casey DiCesare who won the New York State Championship Pole Vault title with a personal best jump of 15”7”.

Dr. Matusiak and the Board congratulated all of the participants and their parents.

IV	INFORMATION ITEMS  

	Dr. Matusiak reported on the following:
The Irvington Middle School Play “On-Line” was a great success.
Mrs. Mundree and Dr. Matusiak attended the performance of the Main Street School’s version of “Charlotte’s Web”.

The UNICEF and Tsunami charity drives were a great success.  A total of $14, 850.00, grades K-8, was raised for these causes.   

V	COMMENTS FROM RESIDENTS
On May 3rd the PTSA, in cooperation with the League of Women Voters of the Rivertowns, will sponsor a debate for the candidates running for trustee of the Irvington Board of Education.  The event will be held in the Campus Presentation Room at 7:30 p.m.
A resident asked about details on the summer special education program and was referred to Sheralyn Gottlieb.

Board Sub-Committee Reports
Curriculum 
Dr. Napolitano reported that updates are being received from sub-committees and that work is being started on the Curriculum Review Cycle.  The Library/Media curriculum expansion is being looked at.

Safety Committee – The committee has met .  Key issues being looked into are the possibility of having a traffic officer assigned to the Campus intersection, “lockdown” procedures and review of Campus Security.

Finance – an Internal Auditor has been hired.

Buildings and Grounds – discussion of close out items, HVAC issues at the Campus, driveway drainage and playgrounds at Dows Lane and Main Street School. 

Communications – 2005-06 budget information is being prepared for the budget newsletter and other forms of public information.  Mr. Marra is heading up the village initiative to obtain cable TV access through a grant.

Policy Committee – The Facilities Use Policy is still undergoing revision and needs the fee structure.  Policies have been reviewed including food allergens, bullying and field trips.

Celebration Committee – A week of student/teacher activities has been planned for Teacher Appreciation Weed, May 1 – 7, 2005.
