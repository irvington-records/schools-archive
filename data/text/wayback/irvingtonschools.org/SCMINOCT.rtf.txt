Clarified purpose of holding joint meetings:
To share ideas and concerns regarding a “shared campus”
To determine a set of rules, norms, policies etc. that will be enforced throughout the campus

Sharing of data from other districts with a similar shared campus for their middle and high schools.  This information was collected by IMS Site Committee last year.  Please review this document for the next meeting.

Dress Code – Lauren and Joe distributed copies of each school’s dress code.
As they were compared it quickly became evident that in writing, they are very similar.  The IMS code describes “brief garment” in more detail than the IHS code.
Both codes prohibit the wearing of hats, the IHS code specifies they are prohibited in classrooms as “they are a sign of disrespect.”  A lengthy discussion ensured regarding hats.  Some feel they are a sign of disrespect; others do not.
How the codes are enforced in each school was discussed and differences were noted.  Student awareness of the codes and attitudes toward a dress code were debated.  Consequences for offenders were also shared.

During this conversation several questions were raised:
Why do we have rules?
(For the efficient and orderly operation of schools)
Should school be equated to the workplace, home or a mall?
What are schools obligated to teach and model beyond academics?
Can the campus run smoothly with two different sets of rules and standards?

Next Steps
Scott will discuss the dress code with the high school staff.  It’s important to assess their philosophy/beliefs on this topic before we proceed.
