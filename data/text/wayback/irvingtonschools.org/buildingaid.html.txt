Special Message about Building Aid
The recently enacted NYS budget included a minimum increase in State Aid to school districts. Our legislators and governor are now attempting to agree to a supplementary budget which would increase the amount of aid. A major point of contoversy is how to apportion building (construction) aid. There are several proposals made by the governor which would reduce aid to Irvington and other Westchester districts retroactively---changing the rules after communities have made decisions to build as they assumed that the promised state aid would be available. In fact, our representatives included an additional 10% aid to give an incentive to districts to provide appropriate space for our children.
Our Westchester delegation has strongly opposed this aid reduction, but needs your support and encouragement. Please call your local legislator (Sen. Spano-969-5194) (Assemblyman Brodsky-345-0432) to encourage them to maintain their support for keeping building aid at the promised level.
Please also call Gov. Pataki (518-474-8390 ), Sen.Bruno (518-455-3191) and Assemblyman Silver (518-455-3791) to express your concern about their proposals to reduce building aid.
This is a very serious situation which will potentially cost Westchester taxpayers hundreds of $$millions if these reductions are made.
Please call today!
