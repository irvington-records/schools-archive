The following are the minutes of the Public Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library, October 22, 2002, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman - Assistant Superintendent Instruction, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mrs. Lauren Allan – Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Gr. K-3  Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Dows Lane Gr. 4-5 Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor - Middle School Assistant Principal, Mrs. Susan Levey – Director Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar – Director of Technology, George Kuntz - Superintendent of Buildings and Grounds, Mr. Scott Baird - JMOA Engineering P.C., Mr. Errol Dawkins - Peter Gisolfi Associates, Mr. Ronald Longo - Keane & Beane, Ms. Stephanie Roebuck - Keane & Beane, faculty, students and members of the community.

The Public Work Session was called to order by Mrs. Janice Silverberg 7:45 p.m. and the pledge of allegiance was given. 

1.  MASTER PLAN UPDATE - Supplemental Log - Appendix A

	Mr. Baird presented slides showing the progress of the construction on the High School Campus.  The project is 25% complete.  The High School renovation project will begin when the General Construction contractor bid, currently under review, is awarded.  

Mrs. Silverberg thanked Mr. Baird for his update presentation.    

2.	Legal Update - Mr. Ronald Longo

Mr. Longo and Ms. Stephanie Roebuck of Keane & Beane, the school district attorneys, reviewed and discussed recent developments in legal issues and mandates in the areas of transportation and special education.  

	The transportation issues focused on were the April 1st deadline for out-of-district transportation requests and the requirements for establishing and changing distance requirements for transportation to and from school.  

	Mr. Longo and Ms. Roebuck explained the regulations regarding the Special Education Impartial Hearing process.  Mrs. Levey (Agenda item number 3 - Special Education Update) joined the discussion.  There was discussion of the state and federal laws regarding the Special Education hearings, dispositions and appeals.  

	Mrs. Silverberg thanked the participants for their report.

3.  District Transportation Report - Mr. James Reese and Mr. Douglas Carter, Supplemental Log - Appendix B

	Mr. Carter reviewed the current eligibility requirements for transportation, numbers of students being transported to in-district as well as out-of-district schools, costs for 2001-02 and 2002-03 and the double-tier scheduling to the four schools in the district that is currently in place.

	The proposed bus scheduling for 2003-04, which provides for the new Middle School/High School campus and Grades 4 and 5 being moved to the Main Street school, was presented.  The schedul is a triple-tier system which would require adjustments to the school day at each school.  District maps (Supplemental Log - Appendix C) were presented detailing bussing eligibility for Grades 4 and 5 from distances of .25  and .5 miles (current).  This issue is still being studied with particular attention to safety concerns.

	Mrs. Silverberg thanked Mr. Carter and Mr. Reese for the presentation and discussion.    

The next meeting will be a Public Meeting on November 12, 2002.  The meeting was adjourned at 10:11 p.m.
