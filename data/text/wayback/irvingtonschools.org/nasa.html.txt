NASA shuttle astronaut George Zamka, a former Irvington student, is coming back to his hometown to speak to students at the Dows Lane School in Irvington on Friday, May 3.
Zamka, who attended Irvington Middle School in the seventh and eighth grades, is now in the shuttle program at NASA. When he was a student in the district his favorite teacher was Scott Mosenthal, who taught English at the time. Dr. Mosenthal, or "Dr. Mo" as students call him, is now principal of Irvington High School. Zamka has kept in contact with "Dr. Mo" who he says, has inspired him and has had a "lasting, lifelong impression" on him.
Zamka will address Dows Lane students in two separate assemblies; at 8:45 -9:45 a.m. (grades 3-5) and again at 10:15 - 11:00 (grades 1 and 2)
