Welcome! We are delighted that you are interested in learning about career opportunities in the Irvington School District.  Please refer to the links below for information about positions and how to apply.
Instructional Vacancies
All teaching positions require that candidates be certified by the New York State Department of Education. For further information on certification, visit the Office of Teaching web site
 at http://www.nysed.gov/tcert/homepage.htm.  Applicants for our certified positions are required to apply using our on-line application process at www.pnwboces.org/teacherapplication.  For problems connecting to OLAS, please call Putnam Northern Westchester BOCES at 914-248-2358 or 800-435-3351 (for outside the metropolitan area).
