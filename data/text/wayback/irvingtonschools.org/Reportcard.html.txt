IRVINGTON SCHOOL DISTRICT
Report Card for 2004
The School Report Cards are developed by the New York State Education Department to compare
schools across the State in relation to some different measures that are used throughout the
State.  They do not reference any unique measures the districts choose to use .  The
information on the report card is taken from State tests such as the 4th and 8th grade Math
and English Language Arts tests, as well as from the High School Regents exams.  In addition,
they include information about numbers of students and other demographic information.  Please
note that the 2005 Report Card presents information from the 2003-04 school year.
