November 4, 2004

Dear Parent,
On Wednesday, November 10, the Irvington High School Key Club will host our first dance of the year from 7:00-10:00 p.m. Hence, I think it’s a good time to review with you our expectations regarding dances at the high school; I’d also like to make a few comments about unsupervised parties.
First, students are expected to arrive at the dance sober. This may appear to be a given, but we have had kids in the past who have shown up inebriated. If that is the caseWednesday, we will refuse admission  and give you a call. A bit of advice here: please look into any pre-dance party your child may be attending. I strongly suggest a phone call beforehand to make sure it’s properly supervised. Please note also that there will be a policeman on duty and five chaperones helping out.
Any guests must be brought to our attention beforehand (students should check with me or Mr. DeGennaro the day of the dance). The limit is one guest per Irvington student. Once a student checks in at the dance (the admission is $5.00), he/she may not leave and return later. 
The dance will end promptly at 10:00 p.m. Please make arrangements for your child to be picked up at that point if he/she does not drive. Since the chaperones are required to stay until the last student leaves, we’d appreciate your help with this.
Now, a few words on unsupervised parties. I have heard that there have been several instances in the past in which students—either deliberately or inadvertently—held parties at their homes when their parents were away. I have also heard that homes have been damaged as a result. I have several suggestions/concerns:
Never leave your child at home for a weekend unsupervised. It’s a recipe for trouble.
Please note that if a party occurs in your home while you are not there, you may be legally responsible for what happens should a student(s) leave your home inebriated and have an accident.
Stay informed as to your child’s whereabouts. If he/she says he/she is going to a party or sleeping over at a friend’s home, call the other parents beforehand.
	I am hopeful this dance will be a positive experience. I have told the entire student body that the future of dances at the high school depends on how responsible they are. Your help with this is appreciated. 
Please feel free to call if you have any questions.
Sincerely,

Scott Mosenthal
