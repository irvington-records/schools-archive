SOURCE http://www.irvingtonschools.org:80/natlmerit.html | DATE 2002-10-29 | TITLE Natl Merit

Four Irvington High School seniors have been named Commended Students in the National Merit Scholarship program (picture attached) l. to r. They are: Danielle Lerer, Kevin Litcofsky, Amy Padva and Jacob Cherry.
A letter of commendation from the school and National Merit Scholarship Corporation will be presented to these scholastically talented students. These four students join some 34,000 students who also received recognition for the exceptional academic promise throughout the nation.
Although they will not continue in the 2002 competition for Merit Scholarship awards, commended students placed among the top five percent of more than one million students who entered the 2002 competition by taking the 2000 PSAT.
