Midterm Week – Student Information
Midterms this year are Tuesday, January 21st through Friday, January 24​th​. There are three
time periods for classroom midterms each day with the exception of New York State Regents
exams which are given twice a day. Please review the midterm schedule and consult your
teacher if you have any questions.
Below are a few important aspects of midterm week that students should keep in mind:
    ● Students are expected to arrive 15 minutes prior to the start of the exam
    ● Students are strongly encouraged to leave all electronic devices at home including cell
        phones, iPods, ipads, video games, etc. Any student observed to be using any electronic
        device while taking an exam or on a supervised break runs the risk of having his/her exam
        invalidated. If a student must bring an electronic device to school he/she will be asked to
        place that device in a designated area inside the exam room and have it returned to
        him/her upon completion of the exam when he/she is leaving.
    ● Students are strongly encouraged to leave all bags and backpacks at home. If a student
        must bring a bag or backpack to an exam, he/she will be asked to place the bag in the front
        of the room and have the bag returned to him/her at the end of the exam when he/she is
        leaving.
    ● Students should have no more than two exams during one exam day. If a student does in
        fact have more than two exams in one exam day, he/she should speak to his/her teacher(s)
        to arrive at an amicable solution.

In case of inclement weather please review the following procedures.
Classroom Midterms
     ● In case of a delay – the entire first test block will be moved to Friday, January 24th .
     ● If school is closed –the entire schedule will be moved forward one day and the midterm
         schedule will extend to Monday, January 27th .For example: If school is closed on
         Wednesday, January 22nd , then Thursday, January 24th will follow the original Wednesday
         schedule, Friday January 24th will follow the original Thursday schedule, Monday, January
         27th will follow the original Friday schedule. If this should occur, please check the school
         website for updated information.
   in case of a delay or school closure on Friday, January 24th, tests will be moved to
Monday, January 27th ​. This will likely coincide with a regular classroom schedule. Should this
situation occur during Midterm week, more information will be provided.)
Regents Exams
     ● In case of delay – the exam will be given. The morning Regents exams will start at 10 am.
     ● If school is closed – we will NOT bring students in. The exam will be given in June, unless
         New York State decides to allow the exam to be given prior to June (which has happened
         before).

Buses
Regular AM High School bus service will be provided during midterm exams. A bus schedule will also be
available and posted on Edline. If you have any questions about midterms and midterm procedures,
please contact your teacher.
