          Irvington UFSD to Host Vaccination Clinic
                         Saturday, January 22, 2022
                            9:00 am to 12:00 pm
                        Dows Lane Elementary School

January 19, 2022


Dear Irvington School Community,

The Irvington UFSD is excited to again partner with ahma Rx, a locally-owned,
community-based specialty pharmacy, for an onsite vaccination clinic at the Dow's Lane
Elementary School this Saturday, January 22, 2022 from 9:00 am to 12:00 pm.

Ahma will be providing Pfizer booster shots of the COVID-19 vaccination for anyone 12+
(who received their second dose at least 5 months ago), as well as first and second doses
of the Pfizer vaccine for children aged 5-11. As the Pfizer vaccine is a two-dose
treatment, anyone receiving a first dose should be prepared to come back to Dow's Lane
in three weeks, February 12, 2022, and the pharmacy will be back on site to provide the
second dose as well. Please note: those attending the vaccination clinic Saturday
should enter through the Dows Lane main school entrance.

For those interested, we ask that you sign up each individual for individual appointments
here: ahma Rx-IFSD COVID Vaccination Registration.

On the day of the vaccination you'll also be asked to complete a Health Screening Form,
which can be downloaded here:

                     Vaccination Health Screening Form - Ages 5-11
                     Vaccination Health Screening Form - Ages 12+

Questions about the vaccination can be directed to Elmsford@ahmarx.com or you can
call or text the pharmacy at 914-840-5027.

For any adults who are interested in receiving a booster shot, the pharmacy also has
appointments available which can be made online, by clicking here.

My best regards,

Kris
