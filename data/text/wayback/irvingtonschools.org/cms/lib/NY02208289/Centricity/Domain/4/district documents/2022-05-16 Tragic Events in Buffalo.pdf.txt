                       Tragic Events in Buffalo

May 16, 2022


Dear Irvington School Community,

The hate-filled violence that struck the Buffalo community this weekend is deeply
upsetting and disturbing. To know that 10 innocent people lost their lives and others
were wounded based on one’s racist ideology is abominable. I am certain that this has
affected many in our community, as it has me. Through this weekend, I wrestled with
my feelings of anger and confusion as to how one could act in such a horrific way, and
sadness for the victims and their families.

This horrific event reminds us of why we as a school community are so deeply focused
on diversity, equity, and inclusion and serves as a motivation to continue this necessary
work. In doing so, together, we will make our community a better, more equitable place
and, in turn, have a positive impact on the global community. As we struggle to come to
terms with the tragic events of this weekend, let’s remember to be kind and uplift each
other, to be proud of the commitment to equity that we hold in our schools, and renew
our dedication to being agents of change in our community and in every community
beyond it, that we can impact.

Throughout our schools, our staff stands ready to support students who may be
experiencing emotions related to this event and the hatred associated with it. If you
believe your child may need support, please do not hesitate to reach out to the school
administrators or counseling staff who are available to help them process their feelings.
Additionally, I am providing helpful resources below to assist you in having conversations
with your child.

Today, and each day going forward, let’s come together as the strong, exceptional
community we are and rally around one another, standing up against instances of
wrongdoing occurring in our society, and allow difficult times to help us to learn, grow,
and become ever stronger.

Sincerely yours,

Kris

Dr. Kristopher Harrison
Superintendent of Schools
