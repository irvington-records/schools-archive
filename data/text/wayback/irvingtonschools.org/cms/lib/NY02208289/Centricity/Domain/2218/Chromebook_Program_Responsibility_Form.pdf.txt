School's Responsibility

    ☑ I understand the School’s responsibility during the Chromebook Program
    includes:
   ● Providing one Chromebook free-of-charge to each Student participating in the
      program.
   ● Providing reliable and robust Wi-Fi access to the Internet while on campus to the
      extent possible.
   ● Providing support via https://tinyurl.com/irvchromebookissues
   ● Providing students adequate opportunities to explore the Chromebook and Google
      Suite for Education while at school to the extent possible.

    Family's Responsibility

    ☑ I understand and agree to my family’s responsibility in the Chromebook
    Program which includes:
   ● Ensuring my child meets the expectations of the Chromebook Program as outlined
      in the Student Responsibility Form.
   ● Protecting the Chromebook, to the furthest extent possible, against damage, loss,
      or theft while ensuring the Chromebook is cared for as outlined in the Chromebook
       Care Instructions.

Financial Liability for Device Damage or Loss

☑ Parents/Guardians are financially responsible for the Chromebook and carrying case
issued to their child. In the event of damage, loss, or theft, parents will be liable up to the
full replacement cost of the Chromebook, currently valued at $385, and the carrying case,
valued at $50.

☑ If the Chromebook can be repaired, parents will be charged for the actual cost of the
repairs needed to return the device to its original condition. Repairs will be carried out by
our staff or a designee of our choosing.

☑ Failure to report damage, loss, or theft within a reasonable timeframe will result in full
charges for replacement or repair being levied.

☑ Failure to return the device upon withdrawal from the school district or at the end of the
academic year may result in legal action, including a report of stolen property filed with
local law enforcement.

  ☑ I understand and agree to the responsibilities stated above and on the Student
