MAIN STREET CAFETERIA EXPECTATIONS
Manage Impulsivity        Students will enter and exit the cafeteria calmly, utilizing designated entryways.
                          Students will keep hands and feet to themselves.
                          Students will walk in the cafeteria.
                          Students will limit movement to disposing of garbage, buying lunch/snack, getting water
                          and using the facilities.
Practice Self-Reliance    Students will come to lunch prepared (have lunch money, lunch box & coat).
                          Students will be mindful that they have 20 minutes to eat lunch.
                          Students will throw out garbage.
                          Students will take all personal belongs out of cafeteria.
Show Kindness             Students will greet and be courteous to cafeteria staff.
& Respect                 Students will use words and language that are kind (No cursing, name calling, verbal
                          threats or aggressive speech).
                          Students will use an appropriate voice level in the cafeteria.
                          Students will respond promptly to safety signals
Cooperate                 Students will promptly follow the directions of the adults in the cafeteria.
                          Students will remain seated until instructed to join the lunch line.
                          Students will self-advocate and ask for help when needed.
                          Students will help others to keep safe.


                                                PLEDGE of RESPECT
                                     I am a smart, special and valuable person.
                                            I respect myself and others.
                                    My words and actions are kind and honest.
                                        I accept only my best in all that I do.
                                              I am PROUD TO BE ME!
