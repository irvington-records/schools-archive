              An Important Community Update

May 3, 2019

Dear IUFSD Community Members,

As the school year draws to a close, the Board of Education would like to update
you regarding several important school district matters.

Review of Employee’s Attendance Records
On March 6, we informed the community that after an initial review by the Board
of Education’s legal counsel into certain attendance record discrepancies
associated with a (now former) District employee, we brought information from
that review to the attention of the Westchester District Attorney and the Office
of the NYS Comptroller. It is our understanding that their reviews of those
materials are continuing and may not be finalized for months. We will continue to
cooperate with all information requests from these agencies and to keep our
community informed of any important public developments.

We fully recognize from our individual and collective conversations with school
community members that some residents want more context about this matter.
Like you, we care deeply about both the quality and reputation of our schools,
and we know that many residents share these same values. At the same time,
matters that concern a government inquiry and personal rights to privacy carry
potential legal ramifications. As a result of this fact, the Board's legal counsel and
the independent agencies with which we are cooperating have recommended
that we share no additional information with the public at this time. We have
accepted these professional recommendations so that we will not otherwise
complicate or adversely impact the outcome of these reviews.

Independent HR Department Audit
Subsequent to the aforementioned review of attendance records discrepancies,
the Board of Education also initiated an independent review of the District’s
human resources practices and processes by hiring an objective third-party HR
professional. While we conduct audits like this periodically, we had not
previously planned an audit this year for the HR function; however, there was no
question about the necessity of conducting an audit at this time. We have been
told by the independent HR consultant to expect a finalized report in June, one
that will include a review of recommendations made in the prior 2012 HR audit
and the District’s implementation efforts related to those previous
recommendations. The auditor has shared that District staff have been fully
cooperative during the intensive audit process. We anticipate the audit will
result in recommendations for the improvement of certain HR systems, such as
those currently in place for monitoring employee attendance.

Freedom of Information Law (FOIL) Requests
As you may have learned from newspaper articles, social media, or general
conversation, the District has received an extraordinary number of formal
information requests in recent years, and particularly this year, known as FOIL
(Freedom of Information Law) requests. Because we are committed to
transparency and will always abide by the law, the District has, this year alone,
produced nearly 7,000 pages of documents pursuant to this law. Fulfilling these
requests requires hundreds of hours of legal and administrative time, which
translates to significant direct and indirect costs. In addition, reviews and
redactions, where appropriate and necessary, are conducted in a methodical and
time-intensive manner to preserve the District’s legal standing and avoid
potential litigation.

We have and will continue to work earnestly and transparently, we will always
abide by the law, and we will continue to fulfill the expectations of our
community by maintaining a high-performing and well-regarded school district.

Annual Budget Vote, Trustee Election, and Capital Bond Proposal
On May 21 from 7 a.m.- 9 p.m. at the Main Street School, the District will hold its
annual budget vote and trustee election. As we approach that day, we can
confidently report that the District is in excellent financial health and that our
proposed budget complies with the tax levy cap. In addition to voting on the
annual budget and electing Board of Education trustees, residents will be asked
to consider a special ballot question for a debt-neutral capital bond project
proposal to address the following areas:

      School safety,
      Maintenance and infrastructure needs,
      Energy conservation, and
      Learning space modernization.

For more information regarding the 2019-20 proposed budget and capital plan,
there are pages on our website homepage devoted to both that include a
calendar of upcoming informational presentations, Q&A's, and additional
supporting materials such as prior year financial actuals, purchasing practices,
planning reports, and more.

Communication with the Board and District
The IUFSD Board of Education is committed to maintaining strong, open
communications with District stakeholders. As elected officials and fellow
community members, we expect and welcome questions and feedback on District
matters.

Throughout the year, the Board invites you to attend Board of Education
meetings. If you cannot attend a meeting in person, YouTube videos of the
meetings are available on Boarddocs under each meeting's date. The videos are
posted a few days after each meeting at www.irvingtonschools.org under the
heading Meeting Agendas and Boarddocs. In addition, monthly Board Briefs email
newsletters contain direct links to the prior month’s Board presentations and
summarize Board actions in one place. If you do not currently receive the Board
Briefs newsletter you can subscribe to it by contacting Donna Friedlander at
donna.friedlander@irvingtonschools.org.

District Survey
Additionally, we encourage your participation in the current District survey,
which provides an important opportunity for stakeholder input. The deadline to
complete it has been briefly extended. As a parent or staff member you would
have received an email link; as a community member currently without children in
District schools, you can find a survey link on the homepage of the District
website at www.irvingtonschools.org.
