Coping with the Death of a Student or Staff Member

Most children experience the death of a family
member or friend by the time they complete high
school; and one in 20 children face the death of
a parent by age 16.1 The sadness and sense of
loss that results from the death of someone close
can impact significantly a student’s social and
emotional health as well as his or her ability to
learn at school. As such, bereavement is a very
common experience among school-age children
that holds important educational significance.
Unfortunately, school staff, including school
guidance counselors and other mental health
