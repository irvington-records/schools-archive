Code 45                  Portable Air Purifiers will be purchased to place in all school buildings in
Supplies and Materials   locations where transmission risk is greatest due to the activity of the space.
                         These air purifiers will reduce airborne pathogens and improve the indoor air
                         quality in the school facilities for students and staff.

                         Touchless faucets will be purchased and installed to reduce risk of virus
                         transmissions at high touch and contact areas.

                         Technology and Software will be purchased to support student learning which
                         will aid in regular and substantive educational interaction between students,
                         parents, and instructors. Upgrading Chromebooks will allow the district to
                         provide educational services to its students by delivering access to online
                         instruction and educational materials in remote settings, as well as, prevent
                         sharing of devices when in the classroom.

                         Security cameras will be purchased and installed to increase student and staff
                         safety throughout the buildings through monitoring of high traffic as well as
                         hidden areas. Security Cameras can also aid in contract tracing as it can verify
                         the proximity of individuals to any potential positive cases.
