2017-18 District, School and Department Goals
                                           Planning Strategically

In January 2017, a strategic framework was presented to provide a guiding framework for
District planning. The framework offers a series of six overarching goals:
   1.   Provide students with a rigorous, comprehensive, enriched and diversified curricula that will prepare students
        to achieve their personal best and will integrate technology in their learning.
   2. Support educators through targeted professional learning and opportunities for collaboration.
   3. Encourage innovation, creativity and risk-taking to inspire a dynamic learning environment.
   4. Foster the creation of a culture where students are active participants in society.
   5. Strengthen local connections to and ownership of our schools.
   6. Ensure the fiscal health of the district and provide for a high quality learning environment.




                                                   Goal Alignment

Each goal will be:
       Aligned with the District Theories of Action & Strategic Goals
       Reflect a well-articulated K-12 approach
       Defined through a series of District, School, and Department Goals
       Evolving to be evidenced based to identify successful attainment

Curriculum and Instruction:
      Implement instructional practices that elevate student thinking and understanding (ongoing goal)
      Develop a balanced assessment system that measures students’ content knowledge, skills and dispositional

Operations and Finance:
      Evaluate buildings and grounds needs and plan for long-range improvements
      Develop model for long-range financial planning to support strategic objectives
