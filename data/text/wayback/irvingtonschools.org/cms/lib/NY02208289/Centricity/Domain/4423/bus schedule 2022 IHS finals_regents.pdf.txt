Finals Week – Student Information
Finals this year are Tuesday, June 14th through Thursday, June 23rd. There are two time periods
for classroom finals June 14th, as well as some classroom finals given throughout various times
during the testing weeks. The New York State Regents exams are given up to twice a day,
beginning 6/15. Please review the final schedule and consult your teacher if you have any
questions.

Below are a few important aspects of finals week that students should keep in mind:
• Students are expected to arrive 15 minutes prior to the start of the exam.
• Students are strongly encouraged to leave all electronic devices at home including cell
phones, iPods, ipads, apple or internet capable watches, and any wireless or wired
accessories, video games, etc. Any student observed to be using any electronic device while
taking an exam or on a supervised break runs the risk of having his/her exam invalidated. If a
student must bring an electronic device to school, he/she will be asked to place that device in
their locker to be retrieved only upon completion of the exam when he/she is leaving.
• Students are strongly encouraged to leave all bags and backpacks at home. If a student must
bring a bag or backpack to an exam, he/she will be asked to place the bag in the front of the
room and have the bag returned to him/her at the end of the exam when he/she is leaving.
• Students should have no more than two exams during one exam day. If a student does in fact
have more than two exams in one exam day, he/she should speak to his/her teacher(s) to
arrive at an amicable solution.

Buses
Regular AM High School bus service will be provided during final exams. Additional bus runs
are provided as scheduled above and posted on the website.

If you have any questions about finals and final exam procedures, please contact your teacher.
