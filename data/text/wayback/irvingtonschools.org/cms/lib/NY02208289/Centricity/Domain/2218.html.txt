Welcome to the Technology Department!
- Technology is everywhere! It impacts every area of our lives and almost every part of our culture. It affects how we live, work, play, and, most importantly, learn. In our schools, technology has the ability to enhance relationships between teachers and students. It is our goal to ensure that technology improves every facet of teaching and learning in the Irvington Union Free School District. This page contains resources for our technology program here in the Irvington UFSD.
