   Irvington UFSD to Host COVID-19 Booster Shot
           Clinic for Children Ages 5 - 11

May 26, 2022


Dear Community Members,

We're excited to again partner with ahma Rx, a locally owned, community pharmacy, to
host a clinic for the administration of the COVID-19 booster shot for children ages 5-
11. Ahma Rx will be on site at Dow's Lane Elementary School, Friday June 10, 2022 from
3:00 pm to 6:00 pm and appointments will be required. If needed, additional
appointment times will be added to this date.

Ahma will be providing a single Pfizer booster shot for children aged 5-11, who received
their second dose at least 5 months ago. For those interested, we ask that you book
individual appointments here: ahma Rx-IFSD COVID Vaccination Registration.

On the day of the vaccination you'll also be asked to complete a Health Screening Form
which can be accessed and downloaded by clicking here.

Questions about the vaccination can be directed to Elmsford@ahmarx.com or you can
call or text the pharmacy at 914-840-5027.

For anyone that can not make this date, ahma Rx accepts walk-ins, Monday - Friday
11:00 am - 5:00 pm.

My best wishes,

Kris

Dr. Kristopher Harrison
Superintendent of Schools
