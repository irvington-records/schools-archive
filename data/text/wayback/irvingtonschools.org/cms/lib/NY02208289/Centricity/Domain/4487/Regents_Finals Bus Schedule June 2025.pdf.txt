Regents/Finals Busing Schedule

Regents/Finals Schedule June 2025

**This schedule will be in effect Monday 6/16-Friday 6/20

Home→School Buses

     ●​ All buses will do their regular AM morning rounds. Students who are taking 8AM
        exams will be picked up at their regular time.
     ●​ All buses will REPEAT their regular morning run four (4) hours later for students who
        are taking 12PM exams. (For example, if your student’s bus normally arrives at
        6:55AM, it will arrive at 10:55AM).

School→Home Buses

     ●​ Buses will depart IHS at 11:15AM to take students home following their exam
     ●​ A second round of buses will depart IHS at 3:15PM to take students home following
        their exam
     ●​ A late bus will depart IHS at 4:45PM to take students home on 6/17, 6/18, and 6/20

**This schedule will be in effect Monday 6/23-Wednesday 6/25


Home→School Buses

     ●​ All buses will do their regular AM morning rounds. Students who are taking 8AM

School→Home Buses

     ●​ A late bus will take students home at 12:45PM on 6/24 and 6/25
