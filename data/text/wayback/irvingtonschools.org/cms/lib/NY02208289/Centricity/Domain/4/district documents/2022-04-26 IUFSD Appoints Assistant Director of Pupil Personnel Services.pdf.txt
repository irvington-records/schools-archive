       Irvington UFSD Appoints Assistant Director of
                 ​Pupil Personnel Services

April 26, 2022


Dear Irvington School Community,

I am pleased to announce the appointment of Francis Tolan as the Assistant Director of
Pupil Personnel Services.

Throughout the rigorous and comprehensive search process, Francis continually
impressed everyone with his deep knowledge and resourcefulness. He demonstrated that
he values the importance of interpersonal connections and strong communication in
establishing partnerships with students and their parents, colleagues and members of
the school community. I am enthused to welcome Francis to the Irvington school
community and believe that he will have an immediate positive impact on the
department and our students’ experiences.

Francis most recently served as Interim Acting Assistant Principal at One World Middle
School in the Bronx, where he monitored the professional development series, oversaw
the special education department and led day-to-day school operations. He also worked
as a peer collaborative teacher at Leaders of Tomorrow Middle School, where he
developed lesson plans to support all students’ learning needs and led innovative and
student-centered programs. Prior to that, he served as a special education teacher at
the Globe School, as well as a fellow adviser at New York City Teaching Fellows. He
holds a bachelor’s degree in english and political science from the University of Notre
Dame, a master’s degree in education from City College of New York and a school
building leader license from the Center for Integrated Teacher Education.

Appointed today during a special Board of Education meeting, Francis will step into his
role beginning May 31st. Please join myself and the Board in welcoming Francis to the
Irvington school community.

Very truly yours,

Kris

Dr. Kristopher Harrison
Superintendent of Schools
