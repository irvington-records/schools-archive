                  Irvington UFSD Announces
                  ​Extended School Closures

March 16, 2020


Dear Irvington School District Families,

Today, Westchester County Executive, George Latimer, declared a state of
emergency due to the spread of the COVID-19 coronavirus. Included in his
executive orders was the closure of all Westchester County schools for 14 days.
The intention of this closure is to help mitigate the spread of the virus in our
schools. Therefore, the Irvington schools will have an extended closure through
March 31, 2020. At present, this information is subject to change based upon
ongoing updates.

Additionally, as the details of the State and Federal strategy addressing the
coronavirus continue to emerge, their recommendations are likely to
change. Therefore, families should prepare for the potential of longer school
closures. As a result of this, we ask for your continued flexibility and patience.
Information regarding any potential changes to this year’s District calendar will
be shared in the near future as our current priority is to focus on providing on-
going learning for our students and supporting the well-being of our school
community.

In the Irvington schools, we remain dedicated to serving the educational needs of
our students. In doing so, we have developed the plans outlined below to support
student learning while they are at home. Please be reminded that during the
school closures, all athletic and extracurricular activities are canceled.

Learning from Home

From March 18th through March 24th, teachers will be providing learning activities
designed to maintain current levels of academic skill. A separate communication
will be sent from each building outlining school-level plans.

On Wednesday, March 25th, instruction in new material may begin via a more
comprehensive learn-from-home approach. On this date, we are planning to shift
from maintaining learning to moving students forward with the established
curriculum. Students will be expected to engage in learning each day through a
variety of activities that may include live as well as asynchronous
instruction. Attendance will be tracked through participation and completion of
assignments. Specific information about accessing these lessons will be provided
by each building in advance of March 25th.

Families should check that they have a device and internet access to ensure that
students can fully participate in distance learning. If you have any concerns,
please reach out to your building principal via email for assistance.

School Nutrition Program
The District will establish two locations for Food Services Distribution: in
Irvington, the Main Street School parking lot and in Tarrytown, in the Tarrytown
Crossing/Tallyrand parking lot near the playground. Beginning on Wednesday, food
will be available for a “Grab and Go” pickup between 9:30 am - 11:30 am. As of
this time, we are planning on cold food only. Families may pick up both breakfast
and lunch at the same time. Parents may pick up for their children, but must
know the student number as we are required to track food distribution. Food will
be available for free for all Irvington UFSD students. Aramark, the District’s food
services provider, will be supplying the food which will comply with the required
components to meet National School Lunch guidelines. We will look to publish the
revised daily menu on our website in the near future. Please note that during the
food-distribution hours, congregating will be strongly discouraged. Students and
families are asked to pick up the day’s food and return home. This request is not
intended to dampen spirits, but rather to encourage social-distancing and prevent
the spread of the coronavirus.

We recognize that home-based learning is not a replacement for face-to-face
instruction, but it does provide an opportunity for students to continue to be
engaged in their learning during this time of crisis. If you have any questions
related to your child’s home-based learning, please email their teacher and/or
principal. Technological questions can be directed to the District’s student
technology help desk from www.tinyurl.com/irvchromebookissues.

We also recognize that students and families may need emotional support during
this very difficult time. If you are experiencing anxiety or stress and you feel you
need support, please call the Department of Community Mental Health office at
(914) 995-1900. In addition, the District’s mental health professionals will be
available to support you and your children. Counselors and psychologists can be
reached via email or you may call Gail Krieger at (914)269-5440.

As always, we sincerely appreciate your continued support and
understanding. Together we will get through this challenging time due to the
strength of our partnership as a school community.

Very truly yours,

Kris
