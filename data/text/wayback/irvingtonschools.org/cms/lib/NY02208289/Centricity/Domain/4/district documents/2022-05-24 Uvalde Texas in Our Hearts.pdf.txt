                    Uvalde, Texas in Our Hearts

May 24, 2022


Dear School Community Members,

I write today with exasperation and sadness, as we process yet another act of
violence. For the second time in as many weeks, we are shaken by senseless violence
that has taken the lives of innocent people. Today, we hold in our hearts those
personally affected in Uvalde, Texas.

The Irvington UFSD places the safety of its students and staff as its top priority. To this
end, there is consistent focus on school security and emergency preparedness. District
and school leaders work closely with the Irvington Police Department and the District’s
security consultant, Altaris, to evaluate security measures and to consistently practice
emergency protocols. This partnership will continue to inform our planning and
reinforce measures to provide the safest school environments possible.

In the days ahead, school staff members will be attentive to all student needs and our
school psychologists, social workers, and school counselors will be available to assist
anyone in need of extra support. Please note that each school will handle the subject
matter in a developmentally appropriate manner. Additionally, the Irvington Police
Department will have an increased presence on all school campuses.

Should you have any concerns for your child, please feel free to contact their school
principal or school counselor. Below, please find resources to help us during times of
violence.

As always, we will be strong together and continue to take all the steps necessary to
provide for the well-being of our students and staff.

Sincerely yours,

Kris

Dr. Kristopher Harrison
Superintendent of Schools
