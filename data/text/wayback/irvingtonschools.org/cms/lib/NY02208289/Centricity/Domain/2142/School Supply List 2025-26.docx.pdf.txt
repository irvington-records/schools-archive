SCHOOL SUPPLIES LIST
                                        IRVINGTON HIGH SCHOOL


General Supplies
        Large 3-ring binder (2” recommended) with dividers or folders to separate by subject with
        loose-leaf, pens/pencils. Students can have 1 binder per class if they prefer with dividers but it is
        not necessary.

Social Studies
        Global Studies I: 2 folders, three hole paper punch, white out, 100 index cards, see above for binder information
​       Global Studies II: see above for binder information
​       American Studies: 2 folders, see above for binder information
​       Economics: 2 folders, three hole paper punch, see above for binder information

Science

​       Physics: 30 cm ruler, protractor, Scientific Calculator
        Earth Science, Living Environment, Chemistry: four-function or scientific calculator (note: New York State
        regulations will not allow a graphing calculator for these courses)
        Regents and Honors Biology: 2 single subject 8.5 X 11 inch spiral bound notebooks; 2 rolls of tape; 1 glue stick; 1
        packet of 3 X 3 inch post its.

English
        Grades 9-11: five dividers, one pocket folder, three hole paper punch, see above binder information
        Grades 10 & 11: Composition notebook
​       Grade 12: notebook
​
Physical Education
        Appropriate athletic sneakers (no platform sneakers), shorts, T-shirt, sweat pants

Math
        All courses: dividers, ruler, graph paper, spiral notebook, see above for binder information
        Grades 9, 10, 11 & 12: Texas Instrument TI-84+ or TI-83+ graphing calculator.
​       AP Calculus: Texas Instrument TI-84+
        Geometry: Circle Master Compass

         Please note: Depending on the model, graphing calculators may range in price between approximately $90 and $150,
but are required for Regents and AP courses/exams. You may consider buying a used one. If this presents a financial burden,
the school has a limited number to be used in class. Please have your student notify his/her teacher in September. If
purchasing a new graphing calculator, please do not open package and keep the receipt in case the wrong calculator was
purchased.

World Language
        All courses: 5 section dividers, two-pocket portfolios, headphones. See above for binder information.
​       AP Spanish: pocket folder with prongs (link is for reference only)
        French: Multi-colored pens (ex. Flair)

Studio Art
        9” x 12” or 8” x 11” bound sketchbook
        Pink Eraser & four different types of pencils: HB, 2H, 2B & 4B
        Staedtler white eraser
        Kneaded Eraser
        Handheld pencil sharpener
