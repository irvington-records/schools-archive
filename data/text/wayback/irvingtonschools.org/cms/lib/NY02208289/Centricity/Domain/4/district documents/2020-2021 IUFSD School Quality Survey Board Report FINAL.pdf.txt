    Overview
    Positive school climate is an essential component of successful and effective schools. It is defined as shared beliefs, values, and attitudes that
    shape interactions between students, teachers, and administrators, while setting the parameters of acceptable behavior and norms for the
    school.

    The Irvington Union Free School District (IUFSD) School Quality Surveys asked parents, community members, staff members, and students in
    grades 6-12 for feedback on school climate, district leaders, and district initiatives. Additionally, the 2020-2021 survey asked for feedback on
    the impact of COVID-19 and the experience of distance learning. Results will be used to inform decisions at the campus and district levels.

    K12 Insight partnered with district team members to develop the surveys, which addressed the following topics:

           ▪   Learning Experiences
           ▪   Support for Students/Staff
           ▪   School Leadership
           ▪   Family Involvement
           ▪   Safety and Behavior
           ▪   Preparation for Distance Learning
           ▪   Communication
           ▪   Board of Education
           ▪   District Leadership Team
           ▪   Strategic Objectives

    Details of the Study and Understanding the Results
    The surveys were open June 7-23.

    Email invitations with unique survey links were sent to parents, community members, staff members, and students in grades 6-12. Parents and
    community members could also participate via a public link on the district’s website. Parents with children attending more than one district
    school were able to take the survey for each school. Reminders were sent on June 10, 15, and 23.

    The survey was translated into Spanish. Paper surveys were available on request.

    Pre-survey communications included emails, a phone script, a district website description, and social media posts.

    This report summarizes survey results and and breaks them down by parents/community members, staff, and students. This report compares
    results with those from 2018-2019 and 2017-2018. The survey was not administered in 2019-2020 due to COVID-19.

    Results do not reflect random sampling; therefore, they should not be generalized to all IUFSD parents, community members, staff members,
    and students in grades 6-12. Rather, results reflect only the perceptions and opinions of survey participants. School-level reports are also
    included in this project.

    Findings for each item in the report exclude participants who did not answer. In charts and graphs, data labels less than 5 percent are not
    shown. Percentages may not total 100 due to rounding.

     Net Promoter Score — District
     The district net promoter score (NPS) serves as a proxy for public confidence in the district and can potentially be connected to district
     growth. It is calculated by subtracting the percentage of detractors from the percentage of promoters, which gives a value between -100 and
     +100. A positive score means there are more people promoting the district than detracting from it.

     Key Insights
 •   98% of participating staff members rated their school as excellent or good, a 6 percentage-point increase from last school year. However, the
     rating fell for the other participant groups. 85% of participating parents rated their child’s school as excellent or good, a 1 percentage-point
     decrease, and 68% of participating students said the same, a 9 percentage-point decrease.

 •   The school net promoter scores increased by 10 points for participating parents and by 26 points for participating staff members. The district net
     promoter scores increased by 10 points for participating parents/community members and by 38 points for participating staff members.

 •   A majority of participants approved of the school-level handling of the response to COVID-19, with 82% of parents, 95% of staff, and 70% of
     students rating it as excellent or good. Similarly, 83% of parents, 94% of staff, and 70% of students rated the district-level response as excellent or
     good.

 •   The Communication dimension had the items with the highest agreement (Strongly Agree or Agree responses) for participating parents and staff.
     For instance, 94% of parents and 99% of staff said that Irvington UFSD communicated effectively and 93% of parents and 98% of staff said that
     the school maintained effective communication practices throughout the COVID-19 pandemic.

 •   This school year, Learning Experience items were asked for both the In-person and the distance/hybrid learning experiences. Overall, the in-
     person learning experience received higher agreement from all participant groups. Items with the greatest gaps in agreement included:
         • This school offers a variety of extracurricular activities (12 percentage points for parents, 12 percentage points for staff, and 22
             percentage points for students)
         • The school’s learning standards and expectations are clearly explained to students (10 percentage points for parents, 5 percentage points
             for staff, and 12 percentage points for students)
         • Students in this school receive individual support to address their academic needs (11 percentage points for parents, 6 percentage points
             for staff, and 15 percentage points for students)
