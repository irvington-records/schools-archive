Race, Culture, Gender, Individuality, and Inclusion for all at Dows Lane

Our mission at Dows Lane is:​ ​To create a nurturing learning environment where students thrive
and develop into caring, compassionate individuals and active learners.

Whole School Instruction:
  - Character Education and monthly themes related to ​Habits of Mind,​ such as Community,
      Working Together, Actions are choices we make every day, Fairness, Thinking Flexibly, Self
      Awareness/ Be Who You Are
  - School Counselor goes into each classroom with lessons about ​Habits of Mind​, Responsive
      Classroom, and ​Second Step​ curriculum
  - Assemblies that highlight character education themes and whole school values
  - Whole-school read alouds with books chosen for lessons about diversity, respect for others,
      and inclusion

Classroom Instruction:
   - Morning Meetings in every classroom to build community and a culture of self awareness,
      respect for others, acceptance and honoring of differences
   - Buddy-classes collaborate on different grade levels to form friendships with children in different
      grades and classes
   - Read aloud, discussions, and activities to help children learn to engage in discussions
      respectfully
         - Read alouds that mirror our students
         - Classroom libraries that are diverse and representative of all our students

Small Group and Individual:
  - School Counselor, Social Worker, and School Psychologists work with small groups or 1:1 with
      students and families who need additional resources or support

Professional Development:
   - Time together as a faculty to talk and discuss events and ideas, to read articles, and plan for
      work with students
   - Support and resources from the District Office- ​Welcoming Schools
   - In-school workshops for whole staff with ​GLSEN
   - Time to reflect and review our classroom libraries and school library and order more and new
      books to reflect our children
