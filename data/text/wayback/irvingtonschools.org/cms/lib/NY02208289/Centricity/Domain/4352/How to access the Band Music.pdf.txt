How to Access the Band Music on JW Pepper

1. Go to jwpepper.com (​https://www.jwpepper.com/sheet-music/welcome.jsp​)

2. Click on “Start Sharing” on the main page where it says Digital Share

3. Next, in the section where it asks “Have an access code?” paste this: ​6oLOdoEhGq0=

4. Then click “view music”

5. Scroll down the list until you see the song we are working on and click view music

6. It will automatically take you to the score. Go to the top left drop down and find the part
   you play.

7. Choose how you would like to see your part - you can view it a few lines at a time, in full
   screen, to fit screen...all up to you.
