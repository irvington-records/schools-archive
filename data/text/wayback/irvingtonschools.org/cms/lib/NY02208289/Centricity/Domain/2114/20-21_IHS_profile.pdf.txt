THE HIGH SCHOOL
    Irvington High School is located in the suburban Village of Irvington in southern                      CONTACTS
Westchester County, 20 miles north of New York City on the Hudson River. The majority
of its 6,500 residents are employed in management, professional and related occupations,
especially in the areas of finance, science and educational services.                                     ADMINISTRATION
