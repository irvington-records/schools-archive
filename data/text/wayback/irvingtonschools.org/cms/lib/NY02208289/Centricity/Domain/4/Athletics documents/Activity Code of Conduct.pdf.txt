IRVINGTON HIGH SCHOOL
          ACTIVITY CODE OF CONDUCT (revised Aug. 2017)

The purpose of the Irvington High School Activity Code of Conduct is to establish a
clear set of expectations for all students who participate in extracurricular activities
(athletics, performance arts, clubs, etc.). Because there is a clear possibility that
behaviors outside of the school day can have a significant impact on school activities, it
is imperative that students hold themselves to the same standards that they observe
during the school day. With that in mind, the Activity Code of Conduct mirrors the same
expectations outlined in the Irvington UFSD Code of Conduct and, as such, students
who participate in extracurricular activities will be held to the same standards before
and after school hours and when they are off school grounds as they would be held to
during the school day. In addition, infractions that take place during the summer can be
carried over into the fall and students not participating in an extracurricular activity may
be penalized during their next activity period.

Participation in extracurricular activities is a privilege extended to students who have
demonstrated satisfactory academic performance and appropriate behaviors. Students
who fail to meet these standards may be subject to removal from extracurricular
activities. Prior to the removal, the student and his/her parent(s) will have an
opportunity to meet informally with the official imposing the removal. Ultimately, it is
impractical and impossible to write every rule of conduct. Students are responsible for
their behavior and the consequences of any possible poor judgement.

Students who participate in extracurricular activities must observe the following
requirements:

1.      Any violation of the District’s Code of Conduct is a violation of the Activity Code
of Conduct. Therefore, while serving disciplinary consequences imposed according to
the UFSD Code of Conduct, a student will also be excluded from participating in
extracurricular activities. Further, any behavior on the part of the student that brings
discredit upon the school or team is subject to the penalties described in the Irvington
UFSD Code of Conduct.
2.      In the event that a student’s conduct warrants a removal from extracurricular
activities beyond the length of penalty imposed by the Irvington UFSD Code of
Conduct, the student will appear before the Activity Code of Conduct Review Board to
determine the length and nature of the exclusion from extracurricular activities. The
parameters of the exclusion will be determined by the Board and may extend beyond
the length of any disciplinary action imposed through the Irvington UFSD Code of
Conduct. The committee will be comprised of the Principal, the Assistant Principal, the
Athletic Director and/or a rotating, independent chairperson and an independent
guidance counselor.
3.      As stated above, students are accountable for their behavior during those hours
before and after school and even when they are not on school grounds. While incidents
during these times may not fall within the parameters of the Irvington UFSD Code of
Conduct, in the event that a student behaves in a way that is in violation of the spirit of
the code or is otherwise contrary to the morals and intent of the Code, the student’s
conduct may warrant an exclusion from extracurricular activities. Upon determination
by the Principal, the student will appear before the Activity Code of Conduct Review
Board to determine the length and nature of the exclusion from extracurricular
activities. The parameters of the exclusion will be determined by the Board and may
also extend beyond the length of any disciplinary action imposed through the Irvington
UFSD Code of Conduct.
4.      All students must adhere to the academic eligibility policy and be medically
eligible in order to participate.
5.      Students must be present in school prior to the beginning of third period (10:03
am) or have an excused legal absence other than illness in order to compete or
participate in that day’s activity, event or practice.
6.      Participants and their parents are financially responsible for all
uniforms/equipment issued to them. Students will not be permitted to sign up for
another activity/sport if they owe equipment or payment.
