               Important Announcement:
      Expanding Daily In-Person Student Attendance

March 11, 2021

Dear Irvington School Community,

It is with great enthusiasm that I report that our planning process to increase daily
student attendance has progressed well and concluded with recommendations for the
full return to in-person learning in advance of the date that I previously
communicated. While my recommendation will be formally presented to the Board of
Education at a special meeting on Tuesday, March 16th, 7:00 pm, it was of great
importance to me to provide the school community with a timely update.

The forthcoming recommendation will be to phase in full daily attendance, phase out
Flex Wednesdays beginning March 22nd, and increase attendance for ICT students as
outlined below.




   Health and safety precautions:
   The District will continue to follow all health and safety guidelines established by
   the New York State and Westchester County Departments of Health. In doing so,
   the District will continue to enforce necessary quarantines and isolations and will
   fulfill its contact tracing requirements. The major shift in our plans are related to
   the shifts in social distancing requirements. Applying existing NYSDOH guidelines,
   the District will:

         No longer require six (6) feet of social distancing in classrooms. Rather,
         where necessary student desks will be no less than three (3) feet apart.
         Classrooms will maintain a six (6) foot buffer at the front of the classroom
         for teachers.
         Each desk will have a barrier installed to further protect students and staff
         members.
         Tents have been ordered to provide additional outdoor seating during
         lunchtime and to offer supplemental learning spaces.

   To support the above-referenced modifications to the District’s student
   attendance plans, the custodial team will perform extensive cleaning on a daily
   basis. Additionally, all school buses will be cleaned between routes as is currently
   performed.

   Surveillance Testing:

   The District will facilitate surveillance testing semi-monthly. Every two weeks, 5%
   of the students and staff members who are physically present on school premises
   will be tested. This means that a total of 10% will be tested monthly. The
   surveillance testing program will begin the week of April 12th. While more details
   will be forthcoming, please see the consent form linked below, which is required
   for our child(ren) to be tested.

To help support school operations with more people physically present on each campus,
additional protocols will be put in place in each school that may introduce changes to
hallway traffic patterns, mask breaks and elementary snack time. As it is expected that
many parents will transport their children to school in personal vehicles, local traffic
management plans are being finalized at each school, in partnership with the Irvington
Police Department. Details regarding the traffic management plans will be
communicated directly to you by your child(ren)’s school principal.

In the coming days, parents will receive a survey that must be completed. This survey
will seek your formal commitment to in-person or distance learning and, for those
eligible for transportation, a determination as to whether your child(ren) will ride the
bus will be needed. Additionally, for students to participate in the surveillance testing
program, a consent form must be completed.

Over the coming days and weeks, I will provide a number of important updates, some of
which will require action on the part of all parents. So, please keep a close eye on your
email for these communications. As always, please know that I continue to be
appreciative of your support and grateful of the trust you have bestowed in the
District’s staff and in me.

Sincerely yours,

Kris

Dr. Kristopher Harrison
Superintendent of Schools
