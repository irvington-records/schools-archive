                  Important IUFSD COVID Updates

December 21, 2021


Dear Irvington School Community,

It is important that we are responsive to the changes that COVID-19 has recently brought
upon all of us. Across the region, we have seen the impacts of COVID-19 on businesses,
the entertainment industry, and our daily lives. Over recent weeks, we have also seen
a notable increase in the prevalence of the virus among students and staff. As a result,
the District is beginning to make adjustments to its current plans and is considering
additional options for the future.

While this communication is long, please take time to read everything closely as all of
the content is extremely important. Parental action is required in multiple areas!

There are four (4) important messages in this email:

   1. Reporting COVID-19 cases over the Holiday Recess;
   2. Change in how the District addresses quarantine through a Test to Stay program
      for students only;
   3. Eliminating the 24-hour waiting period for students who are required to
      quarantine to participate in remote instruction; and
   4. Preparing for fully remote instruction at any time after the Holiday Recess. The
      District is NOT planning to close for in-person learning. However, if the necessity
      arises through the District’s decision or by the decision of a governmental agency,
      we will be ready to proceed with high-quality synchronous virtual teaching and
      learning.

While this is all overwhelming, and our minds are focused on getting to the holiday
recess and celebrating with family and friends, it is important that the District continue
to be nimble and respond to the changing conditions around us.

Reporting COVID-19 Cases and Quarantine Orders During Holiday Recess
While the District is closed for the Holiday Recess, it is inevitable that there will be
cases of COVID-19 diagnosed. All parents are asked to report confirmed cases or
exposures to the District as soon as you are aware of a positive test result by completing
this form. For your convenience, a link to this form has also been posted on the District
website.

Revised Quarantine Practices
As a District, we recognize that there has been an increase in COVID-19 cases in the
school community. When students are required to quarantine, the District wants to
ensure that the impacts on learning are minimized. Therefore, the current 24-hour
waiting period to establish remote learning will be eliminated at IMS and IHS. Effective
Monday, January 3rd, secondary-school students who are required to quarantine will be
able to access remote learning the next school day. At the elementary level, the model
for remote learning differs from the secondary level. As a result, depending on the
time of day that notification is provided to the school and a student is confirmed to be
in quarantine, a student at the elementary level may need to wait to join the remote
classroom until the following school day.
Test to Stay
Effective Monday, January 3, 2021, the Irvington UFSD will begin to implement Test to
Stay (TTS). Test to Stay is an option for unvaccinated, asymptomatic students, but not
staff members per NYSDOH guidelines, who must quarantine as a result of being
identified as a close contact to an individual who is a lab positive case of COVID-19. In
TTS, an asymptomatic, unvaccinated student who has to quarantine can participate in
rapid antigen testing (RAT) on a daily basis in the District. Students participating in TTS
may not ride the bus to school. They must be driven to Dows Lane Elementary School
Gymnasium for testing. If a student tests negative, they will be permitted to 1) stay in
school that day and 2) will be permitted to ride the school bus home that day.
However, students participating in TTS will not be permitted to participate in any
extracurricular activities, as per NYSDOH restrictions. Additionally, students will have
to participate in TTS for seven (7) consecutive days. Testing will be administered by an
individual trained to do so.

For a student to participate in TTS, parents must grant consent for the testing to occur
at the school using the form linked below.

Important note: TTS will not impact those who are fully vaccinated, as those
individuals are already excluded from quarantining so long as they are asymptomatic.

TTS Q&A

When will TTS start?
IUFSD will begin the TTS program on Monday, January 3, 2022 for students ONLY, not
staff, who are required to quarantine.

Who is Eligible for TTS?
TTS is an option for students who must quarantine due to close contact to a confirmed
positive COVID-19 case. To be eligible, students must be asymptomatic. The District
will not provide testing for symptomatic individuals who must continue to quarantine
at home.

How do I have my child participate in TTS?
When a student is required to quarantine as a result of being identified as a lab
positive case of COVID-19, they will be able to participate in TTS. A TTS appointment
window will be made for individuals interested in participating. TTS will be held daily
in a central location, the Dows Lane Gymnasium from 6:45 am-7:45 am daily. Students
must be accompanied by their parent or guardian.

Parents must grant consent for their child to participate in the TTS program by
completing this form. Without such active consent, a student will be required to
quarantine if they are determined to be a close contact of a positive COVID-19 case.

How does TTS affect in-person attendance and participation in other school-sponsored
activities?
TTS is designed to allow students to participate with in-person instruction. It does not
apply to extra-curricular activities such as athletics, band, chorus, orchestra, and clubs
that occur outside of school hours.

Does TTS impact quarantining outside of school?
Students must remain under quarantine for the full 10-day period, while outside of
school, and wear a face mask at all times. Thus, the benefits of TTS only apply to daily
school attendance.

TTS Program Specifics

Length of Program:
Students must test every school morning for seven days. If the seventh day falls on a day
when school is not in session, testing on the eighth day is required. Individuals who test
negative on day seven, or later as noted above, no longer have to participate in the
testing. However, if they are exposed to another positive case during the TTA period,
the individual must restart the seven-day count. Individuals still remain under official
quarantine through day 10 and cannot participate in any non-academic activities. If one
does not participate in the TTS program, they will have to quarantine for 10 days.

Testing Location & Times:
All testing is conducted before school for each student. Individuals must be transported
to school on their own--they are not allowed to ride the school bus. Testing will be
conducted in the Dows Lane Elementary School Gymnasium daily from 6:45-7:45 am and
students must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

Test of Record:
TTS will utilize BinaxNOW Rapid Antigen Test, which is a Rapid Test (not a PCR). This is
the same test that was used during the surveillance testing the District administered in
the spring and is a lower nasal swab that will deliver results in 10-15 minutes. This test
will be considered the final test; subsequent testing will not be allowed to override the
original test. Individuals testing positive will be required to leave school promptly and
will be kept isolated until departure.

Program Capacity:
As with all operations, there are capacity limits. The District will administer the TTS
program until such time it does not have adequate staffing or tests available. Capacity
limitations will be evaluated on a weekly basis, and the community will be advised if
there are any changes to the program.

Preparing for Full Remote Instruction
The Irvington UFSD is NOT planning to shift to remote instruction. However, should
conditions locally or regionally require it, the District will be prepared. To support this
level of readiness, the District is taking the following steps this week:

   1. Chromebooks will be distributed to all students in kindergarten through 5th grade
      on Wednesday, December 22nd. Dows Lane parents will be emailed a digital form
      to complete to confirm receipt of the device. In the school, proper
      documentation will be completed to ensure the District has a record of which
      student has which device. Parental action required: All Dows Lane parents must
      complete this device form for their child.
   2. Students in grades 11 and 12 that had opted to not use a District-issued
      Chromebook and elected to use a personal device in school have one last chance
      to request a device. This special device distribution will be facilitated in the IHS
      atrium on Thursday, December 23rd between the hours of 9:00 am-12:30 pm.
      Those 11th and 12th graders newly requesting a device must complete this form.
   3. Cameras and microphones will be tested in all classrooms for proper functioning.
   4. Teachers will begin to bring instructional materials home with them to ensure
      they are prepared to shift to remote learning on short notice.
