To assist in planning for your summer, winter and spring vacations, the above dates are the athletic
season start dates for the upcoming 2022-23 school year.

In order for coaches and players to properly prepare for the start of their respective seasons, it is
imperative that all prospective student-athletes are in attendance for all tryouts and practices. Those
athletes who miss the tryout/practice periods, may not be selected to their team of choice and it will
have an impact on their eligibility to compete once games commence.
