          An Important Message from the
   Superintendent of Schools - Reopening Survey
             We Need Your Feedback!

July 15, 2020


Dear Parents and Guardians,

As you will recall from my earlier communications, the District is in the midst of
planning for the reopening of school in September. Just Monday, the New York
State Department of Health provided its guidelines to inform reopening plans and
today, the New York State Education Department will release its own
requirements. This information is a critical factor in how we plan to reopen the
new school year. Additionally, before the District finalizes its plans, we would
like to receive more feedback from the school community.

While our ultimate goal is to have all students return for full-time in-person
learning, we recognize that that may not be a viable option as meeting current
social distancing requirements for bus transportation and in instructional
spaces/hallways will be a significant challenge. Therefore, we need to devise the
best plans for a hybrid approach, which would be a combination of in-person
learning and distance learning. Thus, we have developed a survey for you to
provide your feedback as it relates to hybrid models of schooling and
transportation. I would greatly appreciate it if you would please complete this
survey by no later than 4:00 pm, Monday, July 20th. The results will help to
inform the District’s final recommendations for the reopening of school.

On Tuesday, July 21st, at 7:30 pm, the District Administration will present an
overview of the planning process by which we are developing reopening
recommendations. Final recommendations regarding reopening will be shared at a
later date. A link to this Zoom meeting will be posted on the District website and
on BoardDocs.

Thank you for your continued support.

Sincerely,

Kris

Dr. Kris Harrison
Superintendent of Schools
