MAIN STREET RECESS EXPECTATIONS
Manage           Students will enter and exit the playground calmly.
Impulsivity      Students will keep hands and feet to themselves.
                 Students will use playground equipment appropriately, i.e. jump ropes are for jumping,
                   monkey bars are for swinging and hanging.
                 Students will walk in the area around the monkey bars.
                 Students will avoid the shed, cars and the stairs by the cafeteria door.
Practice Self-   Students will know and follow the rules.
Reliance         Students will dress appropriately for the weather.
                 Students will gently place lunch bags in the designated area.
                 Students will tell adults when equipment leaves school property.
                 Students will take all personal belongings inside when leaving the playground.
Show Kindness &  Students will use words and language that is kind. (No cursing, name calling, bragging, verbal
Respect           threats or aggressive speech.)
                 Students will safely follow game rules.
                 Students will play fairly and include everyone.
                 Students will promptly return all equipment after play.
Cooperate        Students will follow the directions of all the adults.
                 Students will help others to keep safe and self-advocate when help is needed.
                 Students will stay where they can be seen by adults.
                 Students will line up promptly and quietly upon hearing the whistle.


                                               PLEDGE of RESPECT
                                    I am a smart, special and valuable person.
                                           I respect myself and others.
                                   My words and actions are kind and honest.
                                       I accept only my best in all that I do.
                                             I am PROUD TO BE ME!
