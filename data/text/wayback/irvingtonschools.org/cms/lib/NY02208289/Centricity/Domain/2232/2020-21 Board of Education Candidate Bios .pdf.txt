Irvington Board of Education
                             2020-21 Trustee Candidates

For the 2020-21 school year, the Irvington Board of Education has three
vacant three year terms. Registered voters of the Irvington UFSD community
will be asked to vote for three candidates. Below are the three community
members who are running for election. Voters will also have the opportunity
to write in candidates.


Brian Friedman

Brian has served one term on the Board of Education and currently is
President of the Board.​ ​ He is a graduate of Irvington High School and
returned to Irvington in 2005 with his wife, Amy.​ ​ They have two daughters
who attend Irvington schools, Emma (8th grade) and Jordan (4th grade).​ ​ He
is a lawyer and a partner in the litigation department of Epstein Becker Green
with a focus on complex commercial and employment litigation and financial
services regulatory matters.

Maura Gedid

Maura Gedid, parent of Luke (3rd) and Allie (5th), has served one term on the
board and is currently serving as second Vice President.​ ​ An entrepreneur, she
holds an MBA from New York University.​


Jayne Wissner

Jayne Wissner has been a member of the Irvington community for 18 years.
Growing up on Long Island, Jayne has lived in the New York metropolitan
area her entire life. Jayne moved to Irvington with her husband and two
young daughters who are both graduates of the Irvington Schools.​ ​Jayne is a
retired New York City schoolteacher who also worked as the union
representative for her school. Jayne currently works at Literacy Volunteers
teaching English to adults.​



Note: The biographical narratives were provided by the candidates.
