SCHOOL SUPPLIES LIST
                                       IRVINGTON HIGH SCHOOL
General Supplies
     Large 3-ring binder (2” recommended) with dividers or folders to separate by subject with loose-leaf,
     pens/pencils. Students can have 1 binder per class if they prefer with dividers but it is not
     necessary.

Social Studies
        Global Studies I: 2 folders, three hole paper punch, white out, 100 index cards, see above
        Global Studies II: see above for binder information
        American Studies: 2 folders, see above for binder information
        Economics: 2 folders, three hole paper punch, see above for binder information

Science
      All courses: USB flash drive, see above binder information
      Physics: 30 cm ruler, protractor, graphing calculator, 2 marbled composition books for labs
      Earth Science, Living Environment, Chemistry: four-function or scientific calculator (note: New York State
      regulations will not allow a graphing calculator for these courses)
      AP Biology: 5 function calculator (+, -, *, /, √); 2 single subject 8.5 X 11 inch spiral bound notebooks; 2 rolls of
      tape; 1 glue stick; 1 packet of 3 X 3 inch post its.

English
      Grades 9-11: five dividers, one pocket folder, three hole paper punch, see above binder information
      Grade 12: notebook

Physical Education
      Appropriate athletic sneakers (no platform sneakers), shorts, T-shirt, sweat pants

Math
       All courses: dividers, ruler, graph paper, spiral notebook, see above for binder information

       Grades 9, 10, 11 & 12: Texas Instrument TI-84+ or TI-83+ graphing calculator.
       AP Calculus: TI-89 is highly recommended.

        Please note: Depending on the model, graphing calculators may range in price between approximately $90
and $150, but are required for Regents and AP courses/exams. You may consider buying a used one. If this presents a
financial burden, the school has a limited number to be used in class. Please have your student notify his/her teacher
in September. If purchasing a new graphing calculator, please do not open package and keep the receipt in case the
wrong calculator was purchased.

World Language
      All courses: 5 section dividers, composition notebook, two-pocket portfolios, French/Spanish dictionary (for
      use at home). See above for binder information.




Studio Art
      9” x 12” or 8” x 11” bound sketchbook
      Pink Eraser & four different types of pencils: HB, 2H, 2B & 4B

      5 Function Calculator (+,-,*,/,√)
