Proposal of EWL for 2023-24
  Goals:
  ●   To introduce students in grades K-5 to the languages/culture of Spanish
      and French.

  ●   To develop speaking, listening and writing skills in target languages.

  ●   To build/foster an interest in learning a new language.

  ●   To provide an enriching, immersive learning experience.

  ●   To begin to build a foundation in World Language that can connect
      students earning the Seal of Biliteracy in High School by establishing a
      long and strong sequence.

Connecting the Dots
  Seal of Biliteracy:

  To earn the NYSSB, students must demonstrate Intermediate High proﬁciency in
  English and the required level of proﬁciency in one or more world languages set
  forth by the NYS Learning Standards for World Languages, adopted
  by the NYS Board of Regents in March 2021.



  World Language Students can earn 3 points toward the NYSSB
  in a number of ways, including:

   ●   Demonstrating proﬁciency on the AAPPL test (already
       administered in Irvington).

   ●   Demonstrating proﬁciency by completing a culminating project that is
       presented to a panel of 2.


                                                                                    10
Requirements to Earn the NY Seal of Biliteracy
 Students need to earn a total of 3 points in English and 3 points in World
 Language:

  ●   Completing coursework in English and/or a world language with an average of 85% or
      better;

  ●   Completing a Home Language Arts Program with an average of 85% or better;

  ●   Earning a set score on an approved assessment in English and/or a world language;

  ●   Demonstrating successful completion of coursework from a nation outside the U.S.; and

  ●   Completing and presenting a Culminating Project in English and/or a world
      language that demonstrates the required level of proﬁciency in all three modes of
      communication (Interpretive, Interpersonal, and Presentational).

Why Award the Seal of Biliteracy
●   Encourage students to pursue biliteracy, honor reading, writing, speaking and
    listening skills in a second language, that are attractive to future employers and
    college admissions.


●   Add recognition to transcripts to highlight a high level
    of proﬁciency for employers, military, post-secondary.


●   Celebrate the success and proﬁciency that Irvington
    students possess demonstrating intercultural
    competence needed for a global society.

Beneﬁts of Awarding the Seal of Biliteracy for Post-Secondary

 ●   College credit is given to students who earn the Seal:

       ○ 4 states, by law, offer college credits:
           ■ Illinois, Minnesota, Hawaii, Maine

       ○ 2 states, by policy, offer college credits:
           ■ Missouri and Mississippi (pending work)

 ●   Seal of Biliteracy is used for placement decisions in modern language courses
