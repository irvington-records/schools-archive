CRRSA Act (ESSER/GEER) Funding:
The Coronavirus Response and Relief Supplemental Appropriations Act, 2021
(CRRSA), was signed into law on December 27, 2020 and provides an additional
$54.3 billion for the Elementary and Secondary School Emergency Relief Fund
(ESSER II Fund).

❖   Irvington UFSD Allocation: $206,709 (no GEER)

❖   Funds may cover expenses from March 13, 2020 through September 30, 2023

❖   Application due June 15, 2021. Application revisions may be made as necessary.

American Rescue Plan Funding (ARP)

American Rescue Plan Funding (ARP):
The ARP ESSER Fund, authorized under the American Rescue Plan (ARP) Act of 2021,
will assist local educational agencies (LEAs), and more directly schools, in supporting
students who have been most severely impacted by the COVID-19 pandemic and are
likely to have suffered the most because of longstanding inequities in their
communities and schools, a situation exacerbated by the COVID-19 pandemic.
(NYSED)

    ❖ Irvington UFSD Allocation: $464,238

    ❖ Funds may cover expenses through September 30, 2024
          ➢ 20% of funds must be applied to assist with potential learning loss

    ❖ Application due date is TBD. Application revisions may be made as necessary.
