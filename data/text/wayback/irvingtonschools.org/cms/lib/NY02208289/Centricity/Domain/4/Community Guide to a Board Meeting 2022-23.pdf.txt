INTRODUCTION TO THE IRVINGTON UFSD BOARD OF EDUCATION

   WELCOME TO THE BOARD MEETING
   The Irvington Board of Education welcomes you. School board meetings are meetings of
   the board of education that are required to take place in public; however, they are not
   actually meetings of the public. Rather, they are business meetings held to conduct
   board business. Thank you for taking the time to attend a Board meeting and participate
   in the educational affairs of our District.

   AT THE MEETING

   The Board of Education, the official policy-making body of the Irvington Union Free
   School District, consists of seven members: residents elected at large by District voters to
   three-year, unpaid terms of office. The Board President, elected at an annual July
   organizational meeting, presides at all Board Meetings.

   Regularly joining Board members are the Superintendent of Schools, the Assistant
   Superintendent for Business, the Assistant Superintendent of Instruction and Human
   Resources and the District Clerk. Others may be invited to sit with the Board while making
   a presentation, providing information or during a work session. Please take an agenda and
   any other materials to enable you to follow the evening’s planned topics and business.
   Meeting agendas, presentations or other backup information may be accessed online by
   going to the District’s website, www.irvingtonschools.org, and clicking on the link to
   Boarddocs. http://www.boarddocs.com/ny/iufsd/Board.nsf/Public.

   All official business is enacted in public session. However, an executive session may be
   held before, during, or after a public meeting to discuss matters involving personnel,
   litigation, negotiations, or other matters as allowed under state education law.

   PUBLIC PARTICIPATION

   Each business meeting provides two opportunities when District residents may comment
   to the Board. The first, “Public Comments on Agenda Items” is the opportunity for public
   discussion of the agenda items planned for the day. The second period is one for general
   comments.

   Board meetings will contain at least one opportunity for public comment on the meeting
   topic, at the discretion of the Board President.
   If you plan to comment during the meeting:

       Please use the microphone, state your name and if you are a resident for the record
       Participants are asked to keep their comments to 3 minutes or less.
       Comment period will last no more than 30 minutes.
       All comments are to be directed to the BOE President.
       Comments and questions will be acknowledged, but will not be answered in session.
