IRVINGTON HIGH SCHOOL
          ACTIVITY CODE OF CONDUCT (revised August 2021)

The purpose of the Irvington High School Activity Code of Conduct is to establish a
 clear set of expectations for all students who participate in extracurricular activities
 (athletics, performance arts, clubs, etc.). Because there is a clear possibility that
 behaviors outside of the school day can have a significant impact on school activities, it
 is imperative that students hold themselves to the same standards that they observe
 during the school day. With that in mind, the Activity Code of Conduct mirrors the same
 expectations outlined in the Irvington UFSD Code of Conduct and, as such, students
 who participate in extracurricular activities can expect to be held to the same standards
 that they would be during the school day when it is after school hours or they are off
 school grounds. What’s more, infractions that take place during the summer can be
 carried over into the fall and students not participating in an extracurricular activity may
 be penalized during their next activity period.

Participation in extracurricular activities is a privilege extended to students who have
demonstrated satisfactory academic performance and appropriate behaviors. Students
who fail to meet these standards may be subject to removal from extracurricular
activities. Ultimately, it is impractical and impossible to write every rule of conduct.
Students are responsible for their behavior and the consequences of any possible poor
judgement.

Students who participate in extracurricular activities must observe the following
requirements:

   1. Any violation of the District’s Code of Conduct is a violation of the Activity Code
      of Conduct. Therefore, while serving disciplinary consequences imposed
      according to the IUFSD Code of Conduct, a student will also be excluded from
      participating in extracurricular activities. Further, any behavior on the part of the
      student that brings discredit upon the school or team is subject to the penalties
      described in the Irvington UFSD Code of Conduct.
   2. Of particular importance is the strict adherence to the district guidelines related to
      social distancing, the use of masks where required and all other related safety
      protocols. Failure to comply with these protocols may result in disciplinary action
      or suspension of privileges.
   3. In the event that a student’s conduct warrants a removal from extracurricular
      activities beyond the length of penalty imposed by the Irvington UFSD Code of
     Conduct, the student will appear before the Activity Code of Conduct Review
     Board to determine the length and nature of the exclusion from extracurricular
     activities. The parameters of the exclusion will be determined by the Board and
     may extend beyond the length of any disciplinary action imposed through the
     Irvington UFSD Code of Conduct. The committee will be comprised of the
     Principal, the Assistant Principal, the Athletic Director and/or a rotating,
     independent chairperson and an independent guidance counselor.
  4. As stated above, students are accountable for their behavior during those hours
     before and after school and even when they are not on school grounds. While
     incidents during these times may not fall within the parameters of the Irvington
     UFSD Code of Conduct, in the event that a student behaves in a way that is in
     violation of the spirit of the code or is otherwise contrary to the morals and intent
     of the Code, the student’s conduct may warrant an exclusion from extracurricular
     activities. Upon determination by the Principal or for incidents related to
     Athletics, determination by the Director of Athletics, the student will appear
     before the Activity Code of Conduct Review Board to determine the length and
     nature of the exclusion from extracurricular activities. The parameters of the
     exclusion will be determined by the Board and may also extend beyond the
     length of any disciplinary action imposed through the Irvington UFSD Code of
     Conduct.
  5. All students must adhere to the academic eligibility policy and be medically
     eligible in order to participate.
  6. Students must be in school prior to the beginning of third period (10:03 am) or
     have an excused legal absence other than illness in order to compete or
     participate in that day’s activity, event or practice.
  7. Participants and their parents are financially responsible for all
     uniforms/equipment issued to them. Students will not be permitted to sign up for
     another activity/sport if they owe equipment or payment.
