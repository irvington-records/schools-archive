Irvington Union Free School District
                                           FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:
                                            THE 2018-19 SCHOOL BUDGET


WHAT ARE BASIC BUDGET FACTS?
 2018-19 proposed budget amount: $61,348,175
 The proposed budget is compliant with the calculated tax levy cap.
 The budget-to-budget increase is 3.8%
 The estimated tax rate $19.41 per thousand of assessed value an increase of 1.46%.
 A tax calculator is on the District website to assist residents in calculating their school taxes.
 Voter approval: for the budget to be authorized it must receive 50+% majority of the votes on Election Day

WHAT FACTORS INFLUENCE BUDGET INCREASES?
 District legal or contractual obligations such as employee salaries, pensions, health insurance, and BOCES fees
 Unfunded or underfunded state mandates such as transportation, APPR requirements, Common Core Learning
  Standards implementation and special education services

WHAT STEPS HAS THE DISTRICT MADE TO CONTAIN COSTS?
 Since 2009, the District has eliminated 34.9 positions: 19.6 teaching positions, 15.3 staff members, 18 High School
  electives, and some elementary and middle school programs. In recent years, the Board and administration have
  been committed to restoring these reductions to deliver the highest quality experiences possible.
 In creating the 2018-19 budget, the District analyzed every line of the budget to ensure we are maximizing our
  budget dollars. In doing so, efficiencies have been realized through supplies and maintenance. Detailed, historical
  budget information is available on the District website.

WHAT IMPACT WILL THE 2018-19 BUDGET HAVE ON INSTRUCTIONAL PROGRAMS AND FACILITY MAINTENANCE?
There are no instructional programs or staffing cuts in the budget. There ARE additions to expand our current
instructional programs, such as the introduction of an elementary school guidance counselor, expansion of STEM (PLTW)
to grades K-2, increasing the number technological devices for students use, investing in school safety and security, and
providing for a second roving security guard at the MS/HS Campus. The proposed budget also supports on-going
buildings and grounds maintenance such as painting, carpet replacement, preventive maintenance, and necessary
repairs.

DOES MY VOTE MATTER?
Yes. The budget for the public schools impacts all residents of the Irvington school community. Casting your vote makes
your voice heard on the budget, which ultimately relates to the type of programming the District is able to provide our
students.
WHAT HAPPENS IF THE BUDGET IS NOT PASSED?
In the event that the budget does not receive the required majority of yes votes on May 15, State law provides the
Board of Education with two options: resubmit the budget to voters without any modifications or present a revised
budget. If the budget is not approved on the second attempt, the District MUST operate on a contingency budget.
When operating on a contingency budget, the total tax levy must be no greater than the current level and must
eliminate certain categories of expenditures, such as equipment. This would require a budget reduction of
approximately $2,200,000. To accomplish this would require reductions to programs and services provided district-
wide.

HOW DO I REGISTER TO VOTE, AND CAN I GET AN ABSENTEE BALLOT?
Residents of the Irvington School District must be registered with either the school district or the Westchester County
Board of Elections. If you are not registered, you may register for school voting at the District Clerk’s Office in the District
Office, 6 Dows Lane, from 9:00 A.M. to 3:45 P.M. during regular school days, OR, at the Special Voter Registration Day,
THURSDAY, MAY 10, 2017 from 4:00 P.M. to 8:00 P.M. in the lobby of the Main Street School. Remember, the deadline
to register to vote is May 10 in order to vote on TUESDAY, MAY 15, 2017.

In order to cast your vote via Absentee Ballot, a resident of the Irvington School District must complete an Absentee
Ballot Application prior to receiving the actual Absentee Ballot. Absentee Ballot Applications are available by calling the
District Clerk at 591-8501 or going to the District’s website: www.IrvingtonSchools.org and clicking on the 2018-19
Budget & Voter Information link.

Important dates to remember when requesting an Absentee Ballot:
 May 8, 2018 is the deadline for an Absentee Ballot Application to be received by the District Clerk if the ballot is to
   be mailed to the voter.
 May 14, 2018 is deadline for an Absentee Ballot Application to be received by the District Clerk if the ballot is to be
   picked up by the voter.
 May 15, 2018 at 5:00 P.M. is the deadline for Absentee Ballots to be received by the District Clerk in the District
   Office located at 6 Dows Lane.

WHEN IS THE VOTE AND WHERE CAN I REVIEW A COPY OF THE BUDGET?
The Annual School District Budget Vote and Board Member Election is Tuesday, May 15. The polls are open from 7:00
am until 9:00 pm. at the Main Street School, 101 Main Street, Irvington, NY. The budget and other supporting
documents can be viewed on the District website www.IrvingtonSchools.org or paper copies of the budget can picked up
in the District office. Specific questions can be directed to budget@IrvingtonSchools.org.
