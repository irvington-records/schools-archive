Information about the Fall 2022 Driver's Education Program at Maria Regina High School in
Hartsdale can be found here. The registration form is on the Maria Regina Website.
The Fall 2022 semester starts on October 4, 2022. The State Education Department is allowing
Driver Ed Programs to conduct behind-the-wheel training. The protocol as of now, and until the State
provides new guidelines, is that the parent/guardian has the option to opt in or out of the driving. If a
student opts out, their parent will sign a form stating that their child performed the driving portion of
Driver Education, under their supervision.

     Students have the option of doing in-person lectures or all-online assignments
      (asynchronous).
     We will be using Google Classroom for the All-Online students.
     Please understand that the tuition is the same for either lecture option, as this does not defray
      the cost of running the program.
     Students who successfully complete the course will receive the MV-285 (Driver Ed
      Completion Certificate). The MV-285 allows students to obtain a senior license at age 17.
     The aforementioned will be explained at the orientation for the students/parents.
      The orientation days are listed on the online registration form.
     The orientation will be in-person at Maria Regina H.S.
     The registration form is now available online.
