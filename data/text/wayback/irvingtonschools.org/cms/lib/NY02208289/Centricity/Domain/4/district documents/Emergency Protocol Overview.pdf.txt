Student Safety and
                                             Emergency Preparedness
                                             The Irvington Union Free School District considers emergency
                                             preparedness a top priority. To ensure we are equipped to respond
                                             in emergency situations, we maintain a comprehensive Districtwide
                                             safety plan that guides our staff and public safety partners to
                                             respond swiftly should an emergency occur. We provide ongoing
                                             crisis management training to school administrators and other key
                                             staff members throughout the District.
