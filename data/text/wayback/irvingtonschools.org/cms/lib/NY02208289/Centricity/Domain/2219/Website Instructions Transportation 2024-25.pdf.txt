Logon to the My Ride K-12 Website

Using a chrome browser, go to https://myridek12.tylerapp.com

   1.   Create an account by clicking on register




   2.   Add your student.

For Ardsley Families, use the ID number given to you.

For Dobbs Ferry Families, type DF-0000 then add your student id

For Edgemont Families, type ED- then add your student id

For Hastings Families, type HH- then add your student id

For Irvington Families, type IR-then add your student id
3.   Add your students’ last name




4. Click add student




5. Click back to students
After entering your student’s information, you will be brought back to the main page and you will see your students
record displayed




To locate bus information, click on details
