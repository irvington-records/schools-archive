                       Important Message for the Irvington School Community




January 25, 2019


Members of the Irvington School Community:

The Board of Education facilitated an emergency meeting today, January 25, 2019 to accept the
resignation of Jesse Lubinsky, Director of Technology.

In consideration of the District’s legal obligations, it is impermissible to share any specific details
regarding this matter at this time.

Please know that the Board of Education and the administration are determined to ensure that the
District’s operations and practices continue to reflect the values of our school community, are
consistent with best practices and are compliant with all applicable legal requirements.


Sincerely,

Mr. Michael Hanna, President
IUFSD Board of Education

Dr. Kristopher Harrison
IUFSD Superintendent of Schools
