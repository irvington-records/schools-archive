SCHOOL CLOSING PROCEDURES
Call the District’s main number at 591-8500 for updated announcements on school delays/closings. One of
the following two plans is used, depending upon the seriousness of weather conditions:
Plan A - If the weather is inclement (snow or ice) with improving conditions, the opening of school will be
delayed by two hours. Buses will follow this delayed schedule.
Plan B - If weather conditions are severe with no improvement in the forecast, all schools will be closed for
the day.
All announcements calling for either a delayed opening or closing of school for the day, will be made no
later than 7 a.m. In addition to the Emergency Notification phone message, the District’s recorded
phone message at 591-8500 as well as the District’s website: www.irvingtonschools.org, the
announcements will also be featured on WHUD radio station and website. Additionally, tune to News12
on Cablevison service or Fios1 News on Verizon Fios service, and the following local television news
channels – CBS and ABC. Please do not telephone the police for school information since these calls
could interfere with emergency police business.
In the event that school closings (due to weather or other emergencies) would bring the number of
school days below state minimum requirements, additional days will be added to the school calendar.
