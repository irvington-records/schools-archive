Irvington High School
                              Military Release Opt-out Form

IHS has a responsibility to provide military recruiters access to student recruiting information.
The National Defense Authorization Act requires local educational agencies that receive federal
funding to provide access to secondary school student names, addresses and telephone listings
upon a request made by military recruiters. Under this provision, a secondary school student or
parent of the student may request that such information not be released without prior written
parental consent. We are only required to release the information of 11th and 12th graders.
If you do not want your child’s name, address and phone listing released without written prior
consent, please submit to the main office by 9/30/25.

Please DO NOT release my child’s name to military recruiters.
