2022-23 COVID-19 Management Plan August
                               31, 2022


Overview

This document is our “one-stop resource” for how the District will manage COVID-19
during the 2022-23 school year. It will be updated as needed and in accordance with
changes in New York State guidance and District practices.

Please note that the COVID-19 Management Document covers the District-wide
approaches for managing COVID-19. School-specific requirements and guidelines will
be issued by each school, as necessary.

Please understand that COVID-19 and how we manage it requires flexibility and
patience with periodic changes in approaches and plans. Our commitment is to
promptly communicate any changes to our plans.

On August 11, 2022, the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) issued a
nationwide update on guidance for K-12 schools, early and education programs
regarding COVID-19. The District will always follow the guidance from the New York
State Education Department (NYSED) and New York State Department of Health
(NYSDOH). The most recent guidance from the respective State agencies can be
found here.

An Overarching Framework for Decisions

COVID-19 is a public health and medical concern, requiring educators to work within
the expertise provided by Federal, national, state and county public health and
medical authorities. We are dependent on state and county requirements and
guidance, which is informed by recommendations and guidance from the CDC and
the American Academy of Pediatrics.

The NYSDOH and the Westchester County Department of Health (WCDOH) regularly
provide updated guidance and requirements, which is integrated within the District’s
COVID-19 management plan. Please understand that media reports of guidance and
requirements will not be the basis of our decision-making.

The Irvington UFSD collaborates with other Westchester County school districts and
especially with the Quad Village School Districts to coordinate its plans for addressing
COVID-19. We will align with the state and county guidance. In turn, the

Current Procedures

   1.   IUFSD is Fully In-Person
        Student learning/attendance will be fully in-person for all students, grades K-12,
        all day, every day.

   2.   No Remote Option
        The District will not provide a Hybrid or Remote Learning option. Each school is
        committed to supporting student learning when they are out sick. Traditional
        means of communication regarding missed school work will apply during any
        COVID-19-related absences. This includes instructional resources being posted
        in Google Classroom and/or other means of communication as established by
        the schools.

   3.   Masks
        Effective March 2, 2022, mask-use became optional for all staff and students K-
        12. However, individuals who have tested positive for COVID-19 will have a
        required period of mask-use as follows:

              Individuals who test positive for COVID-19 are required to self-isolate for
              a period of five (5) days, with day “0” being the first day of symptoms or
              the positive test. Individuals may return to school/work following the
              five-day isolation if their symptoms have lessened and they have been
              fever-free for at least 24 hours. Upon returning to school, individuals
              should wear masks during Days 6 – 10 of their isolation period. This will
              be an honor system; we will not track the individual mask-use.

        Those who are more comfortable wearing masks are encouraged to do so. The
        school community respects each individual’s decision to wear, or not wear,
        masks. To this end, school community members are expected to respect the
        decisions of others. Those who do not respect the choices of others and fail to
        act responsibly will be subject to the consequences as outlined in the Code of
        Conduct.

   4. Vaccinations for Students
      The NYSDOH encourages vaccinations for all eligible students and staff
      members. In 2021-22 vaccinations were seen as a critical tool towards ensuring
      sustained, in-person schooling. In Irvington, a very high percentage of age-
      eligible students are fully vaccinated. Similarly, NYSDOH encourages boosters
      for eligible students and staff members.

        Fully vaccinated is defined as having the full COVID-19 vaccine regime (either
        one or two doses, based on the type of vaccine) and, if age-eligible, having
        received a booster. “Unvaccinated” is defined as either (1) not receiving the full
        COVID-19 vaccine regime (either one or two doses, based on the type of

5.   Vaccinations or Mandated Testing for Employees
     The New York State Department of Health (NYSDOH) no longer requires
     employees of school districts to demonstrate their vaccination status or
     participate in mandated weekly COVID-19 testing.

6. Physical Distancing & Contact Tracing
   Formerly known as social distancing, physical distancing within classrooms,
   lunchrooms and other internal spaces is no longer mandated by the NYSDOH.

7. COVID-19 Optional Surveillance Testing
   In 2021-22, the Irvington UFSD partnered with the Westchester County
   Department of Health (WCDOH) to facilitate surveillance testing. Participation
   in this program was optional. However, the District will not facilitate surveillance
   testing during the 2022-23 school year due to the high vaccination rates, lower
   prevalence of the virus, and lack of County and State funding to operate the
   program. Should conditions warrant the determination being reconsidered, the
   community will be appropriately notified.

8. Test-to-Stay (TTS) Program
   With quarantines no longer required, the District no longer operates a Test-to-
   Stay (TTS) program. Rather, the District will provide families and staff members
   home COVID-19 test kits, to the extent the District has supplies, to those
   experiencing COVID-19-like symptoms or unvaccinated individuals determined
   to be, or believe to have been, close contacts to a confirmed case of the virus. It
   is recommended that individuals who experience symptoms or those who are
   close contacts, participate in daily testing for a period of five (5) days after the
   last exposure.

9. Quarantine & Isolation Requirements
   Quarantine – Per the NYSDOH, the District is no longer quarantining exposed
   students or staff. Exposed, asymptomatic individuals, regardless of vaccination
   status, may remain in school. It is recommended that such individuals
   appropriately wear a well-fitted mask and undergo recommended testing.
   These individuals may participate in school-based extracurricular activities and
   may also continue to ride the school bus.

     Isolation – Individuals who test positive for COVID-19 are required to self-isolate
     for a period of five (5) days, with day “0” being the first day of symptoms or the
     positive test. Individuals may return to school/work following the five-day
     isolation if their symptoms have lessened and they have been fever-free
     without the use of fever-reducing medication for at least 24 hours. Upon
     returning to school, individuals should wear masks during Days 6 – 10 of their
     isolation period. This will be an honor system; the District will not track the
     individual mask-use.

10. Testing Requirements If Out With COVID-19 Symptoms
    Individuals who are ill should always stay home. Home testing or testing at a
    medical facility is encouraged at the onset of symptoms and the individual

   following day if their symptoms have improved and the parent/guardian has
   communicated with the school nurse about negative home COVID-19 test
   results. Home antigen COVID-19 tests are currently accepted to document a
   negative result. Free test kits are available in each school’s health office to the

11. Lunchroom & Meal Times
    Traditional lunch operations will resume in each school and students at the
    elementary schools will be provided snack time in accordance with the
    respective school practices. No physical distancing will be required and barriers
    will not be utilized in cafeterias.

12. Athletics
    Student athletes who are diagnosed with COVID-19 are no longer required to
    receive medical clearance to return to competition. Previously a cardiac
    clearance was required after a COVID-19 diagnosis and while it is no longer a
    requirement, it is recommended that individuals diagnosed with COVID-19
    communicate this to their primary care doctor and monitor for any cardiac
    symptoms as they return to normalized activity.

13. Barriers
    Barriers will not be installed on desks or in classrooms other than when
    necessitated by individual circumstances. Barriers may continue to be used in
    public-facing spaces, such as at the security desks and in building and central
    offices.

14. Transportation/Busing Procedures
    Mask use is optional on all school buses. Windows on school buses will be kept
    for additional air flow. Please provide proper outer clothing based on weather
    conditions to ensure your child’s comfort.

15. Ventilation and Facility Cleaning/Disinfection
    The District will continue to ensure air quality through the ongoing use of the
    upgraded ventilation systems. Additionally, the custodial staff will continue the
    additional cleaning and disinfection practices instituted last year, throughout
    each building multiple times each day.

16. Parent/Family Visitors and Meetings
    The District will resume pre-COVID-19 visitor practices that enable visitors to
    attend permissible school events, scheduled meetings, etc. In the spirit of
    security and safety, family and visitors must schedule an appointment ahead of
    time with specific teachers or administrators prior to reporting to the school.
    When in the building, visitors will need to go to the specific classroom or office
    only. Once in the school, visitors must wear a guest badge at all times. To
    support safe school operations, the District is not able to have visitors in the
    schools unannounced and unescorted. The following is a list of the
    District’s procedures regarding visitors::

    Spectators and audiences are allowed at athletic, musical and theater events
    (indoor and outdoor).
