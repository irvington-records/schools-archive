     A Follow-up Communication from the
         Irvington Board of Education
February 16, 2024

​Dear Irvington School Community,

In a statement released last night, the Board of Education advised the community of Dr.
Harrison’s retirement from the District, effective August 8, 2024. The Board would once
again like to thank Dr. Harrison for his nearly 12 years of tireless and dedicated service
to our District.

Now turning to the future and the Board’s plans for the upcoming school year, the Board
is eager to embark upon the process of finding a permanent Superintendent to lead our
District in the future. To that end, after carefully considering its options, the Board has
engaged the firm of Hazard, Young & Attea (“HYA”) to lead this comprehensive and
thorough search process. HYA is the same search firm the Board utilized in 2011, which
ultimately led to the Board’s selection of Dr. Harrison as our Superintendent. The Board
intends to immediately commence the search process so as to ensure that the very best
candidate is retained to serve as Superintendent for the Irvington schools.

The Board of Education appreciates and understands the importance of community
input and feedback in the search process. Please know that the process will be
thoughtfully planned to include opportunities for meaningful input from all school
community stakeholders.

Please stay tuned for details about the search process and opportunities to get involved,
which the Board will release in the near future.

Sincerely,
IUFSD Board of Education
