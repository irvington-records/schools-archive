                Important Message Regarding
                 ​School Safety and Security

October 12, 2023


Dear Irvington School Community,

I hope this message finds you and your families safe and well, especially in these
challenging days. I am sending this communication this afternoon in an abundance of
caution and to maintain open communications between our District and our
families. Our number one priority remains the safety and wellness of all of our students
and staff.

We recognize that during times like this there is misinformation and inappropriate
messaging being shared routinely on social media. While it is important to remain
vigilant, our actions must be guided by reliable intelligence, best practices, and
common sense. In working with our law enforcement partners, they have asserted that
there are currently no active, credible threats to New York State, including those
mentioned on recent social media posts.

Given the current climate, I believe it would be helpful to share our safety and security
protocols and practices.

Ensuring Safety and Security:

      Proactive Safety Measures - Our District continues to utilize proactive measures to
      enhance safety and security on a daily basis. We continually review and update
      our safety plans in collaboration with our law enforcement partners and security
      consultants to address potential threats and emergencies.

      Mental Health Support - We understand that these times can be emotionally
      challenging for students, staff, and parents alike. Our dedicated counselors,
      clinicians and support staff are available to provide emotional support to anyone
      who may need it. As some families have done this week, please know that you
      can always reach out to your school administrators or counselors if you or your
      child require assistance.

      Collaboration with Law Enforcement - We maintain a close working relationship
      with local law enforcement agencies. Our collaboration ensures that we are
      continuously monitoring for any potential threats and can respond swiftly, if
      necessary.

      Security Consultants - In addition to law enforcement, we also work with
      experienced K-12 security consultants, Altaris, who provide expertise and
      guidance in assessing and improving our security measures.

      Open Communication - We are committed to maintaining an open line of
      communication with our school community regarding safety and security
      concerns. We will keep you informed of any significant developments or changes
      in our security procedures.
Irvington is a resilient and caring community. By working together and staying informed
through reliable sources, we can navigate these challenges and ensure the safety and
well-being of our students and staff.

Should you have any questions or concerns, please feel free to reach out to us.

Sincerely,

Dr. Joel Adelberg
Acting Superintendent of Schools
