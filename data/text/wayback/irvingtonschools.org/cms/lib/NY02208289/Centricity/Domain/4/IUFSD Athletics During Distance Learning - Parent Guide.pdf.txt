Athletics During Distance Learning


As of March, 16, 2020 all athletic competitions and practices have been cancelled on the
guidance of the New York State Public School Athletic Association. You will receive
communication from the District if there are any changes.

Athletes are encouraged to stay active while schools are closed and are reminded that the
school facilities are closed.

For any questions regarding Athletics, please contact Stewart Hanson at
stewart.hanson@irvingtonschools.org
