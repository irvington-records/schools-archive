                          DEBATE CLUB




   The Debate Club welcomes all IMS students. In this club, we begin by
selecting compelling topics for discussion. Once a topic is chosen, students
  delve into research to deepen their understanding of the selected topic.
  Armed with knowledge, we then immerse ourselves in the art of debate.
  We use the Middle School Debate Format to engage in debate as well as
                        evaluate our fellow debaters.

When debating, students are on teams of 3– each standing at the "podium"
 (a desk) and delivering their points. At the appropriate time, students on
  the opposing team or in the audience can stand and ask for a "Point of
 Information" or participate in "heckling ". Join us for some fun and hone
                             your debate skills.

PALS
The   Peer    Assistance
Leadership     or    PAL
Program is a mentoring
program at IMS. End of year seventh graders
sign up to be PALS and these students meet
with end of year fifth graders when they visit
IMS for the first time in the spring before they
start middle school. PALS connect with their
assigned 6th graders on the first day of middle
school. The students eat a BBQ lunch sponsored
by the PTSA and then participate in a multitude
of activities throughout the afternoon, including
a tour of the campus.
