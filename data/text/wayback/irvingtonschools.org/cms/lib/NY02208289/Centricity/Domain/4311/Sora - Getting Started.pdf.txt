Steps to Setting up Sora on your device

1. Install the Sora App



2. Click Find my school



3. Click My school isn’t listed


4. Type irvington on the search line




5. Select Southern Westchester BOCES,
   Irvington High/Middle School




6. Select Irvington High/Middle School
   from the drop down list




7. Click Sign in using Irvington




8. Select Continue




9. Sign into your Irvington Google
   account
