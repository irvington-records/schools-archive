SCHOOL CLOSING PROCEDURES
Call the District’s main number at 591-8500 for updated announcements on school delays/closings.
One of the following two plans is used, depending upon the seriousness of weather conditions:
Plan A - If the weather is inclement (snow or ice) with improving conditions, the opening of school
will be delayed by two hours. Buses will follow this delayed schedule.
Plan B - If weather conditions are severe with no improvement in the forecast, all schools will be
closed for the day.
All announcements calling for either a delayed opening or closing of school for the day will be made
no later than 7 a.m. whenever possible. In addition to the Emergency Notification phone message,
the District’s recorded phone message at 591-8500 as well as the District’s website:
www.irvingtonschools.org, the announcements will also be featured on WHUD, WABC radio stations
and websites. Additionally, tune to News12 on Cablevison service and Verizon Fios service, and the
following local television news channels – ABC. Please do not telephone the police for school
information since these calls could interfere with emergency police business.
In the event that school closings (due to weather or other emergencies) would bring the number of
school days below state minimum requirements, additional days will be added to the school calendar.

Parent Notification System
The Irvington School District utilizes an instant alert service for emergency notifications or important
messages. IUFSD will continue to use Parent Square to send e-mails, text messages, and telephone
notifications to keep parents, community members and staff members informed about school
closings and emergency dismissals. Specific communications will be sent to each family regarding the
introduction of Parent Square. Your contact information contained in the District’s student
information system is used for contacting you regarding changes to the school schedule and other
critical news; therefore, it is important that you inform your child(ren)’s school office of any changes to
your contact information.


Professional Learning Early Release Day (PLRD)
On identified days during the months of, October through May, the schools will dismiss one hour early.
This early dismissal occurs to provide our faculty and staff with additional time for professional
learning, collaboration and curriculum development. The days are noted on the Calendar at a Glance
as PLRD.
