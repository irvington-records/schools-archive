IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT

                   APPLICATION FOR TRANSPORTATION TO A NON-PUBLIC SCHOOL
                                         2021-2022
This application for school bus transportation to a non-public school for 2021-2022 must be submitted by
April 1, 2021. New residents have 30 days from establishing residency to submit an application. A separate
form must be completed for each student, and a new application must be submitted for each school year. If
you have not yet confirmed your child’s acceptance at the school, you should still submit an application by the
April 1 deadline in order to be approved for busing. If you are considering more than one school, you should
submit a form for each school and then notify the transportation office of your final decision.

You may download this form and type your information directly into each field. Be sure to select “save as”
and return via e-mail to: transportation@ardsleyschools.org. Or you may print and send the completed form
to Transportation Department, Ardsley UFSD, 500 Farm Road, Ardsley, NY 10502; fax 914-295-5976.

Please Note:
In order to be eligible for transportation you must have provided proof of residency to Irvington UFSD and must reside within 15 miles of
the school listed above. There are no exceptions to this law. The District requires proof of age for all children entering kindergarten (please
attach a copy of the child’s birth certificate or bring it to the District office for verification). If after submitting this application you decide
not to send your child to this school, notify the transportation office at transportation@ardsleyschools.org or 914-295-5544. Be advised
that transportation is not provided for orientation days, after-school activities, or on Federal holidays. Please see district website for further
information.
