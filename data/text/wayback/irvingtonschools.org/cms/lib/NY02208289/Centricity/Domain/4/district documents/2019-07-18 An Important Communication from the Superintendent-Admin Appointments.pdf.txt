     An Important Communication from the
Superintendent - Administrative Appointments &
             Contract Settlement


July 18, 2019


Dear Irvington School Community,

It is with great enthusiasm that I share that at a special Board of Education
meeting on Tuesday, July 16, 2019, the Board appointed a number of new
administrators to fill vacancies that I communicated to you in recent
months. Each of the educational leaders appointed possess impressive skills,
knowledge and experience that will immediately prove to be impactful on our
students’ learning. Importantly, they have a remarkable passion for the education
of children and share our commitment to provide the very best educational
opportunities to every student, every day.

At Dows Lane, Assistant Principal Dr. Andrea Kantor, was appointed to serve as
Principal. She will partner with our new Interim Assistant Principal Linda Langiulli
to lead our outstanding school. Ms. Langiulli has many years of experience as a
leader and teacher. Together, Dr. Kantor and Ms. Langiulli will collaborate with
the Dows Lane faculty, staff and parent community to provide our students with
another incredible year.

Mr. Jason Strumwasser was appointed as the District-wide Director of
Technology. Using his years of experience as a teacher and leader in technology,
Mr. Strumwasser will collaborate with our faculty to enrich learning through the
integration and instruction of technology.

Mr. Stewart Hanson will serve as the Interim Director of Physical Education,
Health and Athletics. Mr. Hanson’s vast experience will enable him to provide
strong leadership in supporting our mission to encourage students to develop
healthy, active lives while also guiding our athletics program.

Finally, Dr. Iris Goliger was appointed to serve as the Assistant Director of Pupil
Personnel Services. Her deep experience and skill as a Committee on Special
Education Chairperson, School Psychologist and Clinical Psychologist will prove to
be a great resource for the Irvington school community.

These appointments follow a very thorough search utilizing the District’s rigorous
hiring process that includes multiple interview rounds and committee
interviews. The tenure-track administrators also participated in a clinical
experience that required them to demonstrate their expertise, as well as
committee interviews that included parents, teachers, staff, administrators and
Board of Education trustees. A final interview was also held with the Board of
Education.

The District is also pleased to announce the settlement of a new contract with
the custodial bargaining unit. The new contract extends through the 2023-24
school year and provides for responsible, fair compensation and benefits for our
dedicated custodians, cleaners, maintenance mechanics, and groundskeepers.

Please accept my best wishes for a wonderful summer.

Sincerely,

Kristopher Harrison, Ed.D
Superintendent of Schools
