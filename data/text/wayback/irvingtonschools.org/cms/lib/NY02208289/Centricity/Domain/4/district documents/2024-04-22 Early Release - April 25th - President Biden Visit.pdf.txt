       Early Release on Thursday, April 25th
           Anticipating Presidential Visit

Dear Irvington School Community,

This Thursday, April 25th, in anticipation of President Biden’s attendance at a
fundraiser in Irvington, we will be implementing our Early Release Schedule. We have
been advised by the Irvington Police Department to dismiss early in anticipation of
significant gridlock and road closures. We will be releasing students according to the
following schedule:
                            Dows Lane - 11:15 am
                            Main Street - 12:10 pm
                            IMS - 12:20 pm
                            IHS - 11:41 am

ALL after-school and evening activities in the Irvington Schools will be canceled as
well. Look for additional communications regarding the rescheduling of athletic events
and the IHS concert.

Sincerely,
Dr. Joel Adelberg
Acting Superintendent of Schools
