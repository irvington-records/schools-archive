  2019-20 BUDGET VOTE, CAPITAL PROJECT BOND REFERENDUM and
                   BOARD MEMBER ELECTION


VOTER REGISTRATION
To vote in ANY Irvington School referendums, including but not limited to the annual budget and member
election, you must be registered with the school district OR Westchester County general elections. If you are
not currently registered, you may register for school voting at the District Clerk’s Office, located in the
Administration Office, 6 Dows Lane, from 9:00 A.M. to 3:45 P.M. during regular school days, OR, the final
day to register in this year’s annual budget vote/member election and capital project referendum is
THURSDAY, MAY 16, 2019 at the Special Voter Registration Day, which is held from 4:00 P.M. to 8:00
P.M. in the lobby of the Main Street School.

A person qualified to register to vote must be a citizen of the United States, at least 18 years old, and a resident
of the School District for at least thirty days before the vote. Students who turn 18 by May 16, 2019 will be
eligible to register in order to vote on May 21, 2019.

        Any person who is registered with the Westchester County Board of Elections within the Irvington
        School District need not register separately with the school district.
         Any person who voted in any Irvington School District referendums since May 2014, need not
         register with the School District again.
        Any person who is not registered with the County and did not vote in any of
        the Irvington School District elections since May 2014 is not eligible to vote
        on May 21, 2019.

ABSENTEE BALLOT APPLICATIONS AND ABSENTEE BALLOTS FOR
MAY 21, 2019 BUDGET VOTE AND BOARD MEMBER ELECTION
A person must complete an Absentee Ballot Application prior to receiving the actual Absentee Ballot.
Absentee Ballot Applications are available by calling the District Clerk at 269-5011 or going to the District’s
website: www.irvingtonschools.org and clicking on the 2019-20 Budget & Voter Information link.

        May 14, 2019 is the deadline to submit an Absentee Ballot Application if ballot is to be mailed to the
        voter.
        May 20, 2019 is deadline to submit an Absentee Ballot Application if the ballot is to be picked up by
        the voter.
        May 21, 2019 at 5:00 P.M. is the deadline for Absentee Ballots to be received by the District Clerk.
