Communication During Distance Learning

The District will continue to update the community using our existing systems of emails,
robo-calls, Constant Contact and posting to the District website. District-wide
communications will come from the Superintendent of Schools; building-specific
communications will come from principals.

Teachers will communicate with students and families using email and Google Classroom.

Questions about Distance Learning

For general questions regarding distance learning, please contact Mary Ellis, Interim
Assistant Superintendent for Instruction and Human Resources at
mary.ellis@irvingtonschools.org

Questions about Special Education and Pupil Personnel Services

For questions regarding Special Education and PPS Services including counseling, nursing
and clinical support, please contact Gail Krieger, Director of Pupil Personnel Services at
gail.krieger@irvingtonschools.org

Questions about Technology

For general questions regarding technology, please contact our IUFSD Student Helpdesk
using the following link: ​www.tinyurl.com/irvchromebookissues

Questions about Your Child’s Health

Our nursing staff will continue to stay informed and participate in updates and emerging
developments that may impact the Irvignton community. Please email your school nurse if
you need to pick up medication or have a specific question regarding your child.
