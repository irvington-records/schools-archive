                       Irvington High School Parking Privilege Policy


PARKING

Only Seniors that have registered their cars and received a parking permit are allowed to park on
campus. Violators will booted/ticketed. Registered Seniors are only allowed to park their vehicles in the
upper lot. Seniors that are found to be parking anywhere other than the upper lot will receive a parking
ticket and a lien on their Home Access account. Juniors who are violating the parking policy will be
reported to the office where they too will receive a parking ticket and a lien on their Home Access
account and a delay on getting their Senior parking permit. Any cars parked in a Fire Lane will be
reported to the police department.

The High School Faculty parking lot and the Middle School Faculty parking lot (which include the spaces
along the track)-are restricted to faculty and staff ONLY. No students, no parents, no visitors. These
spaces are numbered and assigned to staff. Anyone other than staff parking in these spaces will risk the
possibility of being blocked in. Students parking in a staff member’s space will receive a parking ticket
and have their Home Access locked. Parents and/or visitors who are blocked in will need to wait until
staff member’s classes are finished and teacher is free to come out to the lot.



SPEEDING

Remember you are within a school zone that includes Middle School students walking about campus.
There are posted speed limits and there are STOP signs. By signing this you are agreeing that you will
drive safe and responsibly when driving on school property and if found to be speeding or driving
recklessly, you WILL lose your parking privileges and will surrender your parking permit.
Please know that reckless driving is always reported to the High School main office.



                            Enforcement is in place from 6:30am to 3:30pm.
