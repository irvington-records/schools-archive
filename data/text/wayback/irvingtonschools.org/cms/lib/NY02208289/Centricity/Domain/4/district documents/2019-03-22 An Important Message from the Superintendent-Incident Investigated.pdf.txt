             An Important Message from the
               ​Superintendent of Schools -
                   Incident Investigated

March 22, 2019


Dear Irvington School Community Members,

Last night district and school administration were notified that a student
reportedly made a threatening statement about doing harm on school
grounds, specifically at the MS/HS Campus.

This matter was promptly reported to the Irvington Police Department (IPD)
who immediately investigated. Through their investigation, the IPD has
determined that the threat was not credible and there is no risk to public
safety.

The safety of our students and staff are the District’s top priority. Please
know that the Irvington School District takes situations such as this one
seriously and will take necessary steps, including appropriate discipline, to
provide a safe learning environment. To this end, the strength of our
partnership with the Irvington Police Department is critical. Out of an
abundance of caution, there will be an increased police presence on school
grounds over the coming days.

We are also appreciative of the open lines of communication within our
school community that led to this matter being reported. As we have said
before, if you see or hear something that is not right, please report it!!

Very truly yours,




Kristopher Harrison, Ed.D
Superintendent of Schools
