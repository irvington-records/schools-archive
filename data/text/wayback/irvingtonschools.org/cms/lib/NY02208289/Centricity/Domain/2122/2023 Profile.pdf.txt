The High School
    Irvington High School is located in the Village of Irvington, a suburban community
in southern Westchester County, 20 miles north of New York City on the Hudson River.
                                                                                                            Contacts
The majority of its 6,500 residents are employed in management, professional and related
occupations, especially in the areas of finance, science and educational services.                          Administration

preparatory program and broad athletic, fine arts and extracurricular offerings. Fostering a             Dr. Kristopher Harrison
