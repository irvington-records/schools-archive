How to Report Your Child’s Absence



How to report your Child’s Absence As a reminder, all matters related to student attendance
need to be reported to Mrs. Adinaro by phone at 914-269-5483. It is important to call this
phone number to assure that an absence, lateness or early release is properly noted. If you call
another line, there is a chance that the attendance clerk may not get the message in a timely
manner, resulting in a possible unexcused absence. For your convenience, you may also email
Venus.adinaro@irvingtonschools.org regarding your child’s attendance. You may also read
information regarding attendance which can also be found in the IHS Student Handbook.
