SCHOOL CLOSING PROCEDURES
Call the District’s main number at 591-8500 for updated announcements on school delays/closings. One of
the following two plans is used, depending upon the seriousness of weather conditions:
Plan A - If the weather is inclement (snow or ice) with improving conditions, the opening of school will be
delayed by two hours. Buses will follow this delayed schedule.
Plan B - If weather conditions are severe with no improvement in the forecast, all schools will be closed for
the day.
All announcements calling for either a delayed opening or closing of school for the day, will be made no
later than 7 a.m. In addition to the Emergency Notification phone message, the District’s recorded
phone message at 591-8500 as well as the District’s website: www.irvingtonschools.org, the
announcements will also be featured on WHUD, WCBS, WABC radio stations and websites. Additionally,
tune to News12 on Cablevison service and Verizon Fios service, and the following local television news
channels – CBS and ABC. Please do not telephone the police for school information since these calls
could interfere with emergency police business.
In the event that school closings (due to weather or other emergencies) would bring the number of
school days below state minimum requirements, additional days will be added to the school calendar.


Parent Notification System
The Irvington School District utilizes an instant alert service, currently SEND WORD NOW/OnSolve, an e-mail,
text, and telephone notification service that keeps parents, community members and staff informed
about school closings and emergency dismissals. Your contact information contained in the District’s
student information system is used for gathering this contact information, therefore, it is important that you
inform your child(ren)’s school of any changes to your contact information. For further information, feel
free to call the Business Office at 269-5052.


Professional Learning Early Release Day (PLRD)
On identified days during the months of, October through May, the schools will dismiss one hour early. This
early dismissal occurs to provide our faculty and staff with additional time for professional learning,
collaboration and curriculum development. The days are noted on the Calendar at a Glance as PLRD.
** PLRD Dismissal will only be implemented if the District resumes 100% attendance or if in-person learning
   is in place during the IUFSD Reopening Plan.
