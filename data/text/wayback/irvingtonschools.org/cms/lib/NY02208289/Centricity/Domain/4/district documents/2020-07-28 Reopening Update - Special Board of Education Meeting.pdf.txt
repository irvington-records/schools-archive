                   Reopening Update
         A Special Board of Education Meeting -
         Wednesday, July 29, 2020 at 7:30 pm

July 28, 2020


Dear Irvington School Community,

I hope this letter finds you well, staying cool during this heatwave. Here in the
School District, we are busy preparing our Reopening Plan for the New York State
Education Department and the New York State Health Department. This
comprehensive plan will outline the steps the District will take to implement all
health and safety guidance and will provide details on how schools will operate in
the fall. The plan is intended to be flexible to allow the District to adapt to
changes in guidance and shifts in local environmental factors that could impact
school operations thus causing alterations to our learning plans.

Tomorrow, Wednesday, July 29th at 7:30 pm, there will be a special Board of
Education meeting during which I will provide recommendations for the hybrid
model of instruction (a combination of distance and in-person learning), as such a
plan is required by New York State. Next week, we expect that Governor Cuomo
will make an announcement that will direct the model under which learning
should occur statewide: hybrid learning, 100% distance learning or 100% in-person
learning. While I certainly believe that 100% in-person learning is neither
responsible or appropriate at this time, it is a model of learning for which we
must prepare.

The presentation and the subsequent discussion will focus on the recommended
model for hybrid instruction and the District administration will share an overview
of the many steps we are taking to provide for the health and safety of our
students and staff. Specific details related to student schedules and cohorts will
be confirmed at a later date by your child(ren)’s school.

Communication over the next month will be critical to ensure that the entire
school community is well prepared for the first days of school. With this in mind,
the District has developed a the Reopening Irvington UFSD webpage that will
become the central warehouse of all related communications. Please continue to
check your email for updates from your child(ren)’s principal(s) and me and visit
the website on a regular basis. Additionally, if you did not have a chance to
attend last week’s Board of Education meeting, you can view it by clicking here.

Please take the time to attend tomorrow night’s Board of Education meeting that
will be facilitated on Zoom. The community will have the opportunity to comment
during the course of the meeting. Community members can find the necessary
information to “attend” the meeting by clicking here or visiting the
Announcement section of the District website or BoardDocs for the details.

All my best,

Kris
