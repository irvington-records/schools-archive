Distance Learning Phase 3

As the school closure continues, we are preparing to enter Phase 3 of Distance Learning. During
the week of April 13th, teachers and building leaders will begin to implement portions of Phase 3,
with full implementation on Monday, April 20th.

During Phase 1, students were provided with assignments that were designed to maintain
current levels of skill and knowledge as teachers prepared to move into more active distance
learning models. In Phase 2, teachers designed learning experiences that moved students
forward in reaching learning goals already established in our course curricula. Teachers in core
content areas provided feedback to students and ensured that students were engaged and
connected to learning each day. During Phase 2, many teachers explored a variety of distance
learning tools and found ways to stay connected to their classes. For some this included
synchronous instruction and discussions, for some, it was posting videos and providing follow-up
assignments, for some assignments were posted and collected.

Over the past few weeks, it has become increasingly evident that students are yearning for live
contact with their peers and their teachers. Both teachers and parents report tremendous
satisfaction and enjoyment in having live interactions with students. We all need to see and hear
each other. As we move into Phase 3, all teachers will be holding online classes with their classes.

At the elementary level, teachers will have a minimum of three online sessions with students in
addition to the posing of daily independent work.
    ● In Kindergarten and Grade 1, these sessions will be approximately 20 minutes in length.
    ● In Grades 2-5, they will be approximately 30 minutes in length.

At the secondary level, teachers will be meeting with students for a minimum of two 30-minute
sessions using a modified building schedule:
    ● Classes that meet during periods 0-4 on A days will meet on Mondays
    ● Classes that meet during periods 5-9 on A days will meet on Tuesdays
    ● Classes that meet during periods 0-4 on B days will meet on Wednesday
    ● Classes that meet during periods 5-9 on B days will meet on Thursday

In addition to regularly scheduled instruction, all teachers will have scheduled extra help sessions
each day.

The following sample schedules are provided to give families an overview of what Phase 3 may
look like. As we enter full implementation, we anticipate adjustments to these schedules. Please
refer to communication from your child’s teachers and principals regarding their specific
schedules.
