                             TRANSPORTATION TO ATHLETIC COMPETITIONS
                                         OPT-OUT FORM


Where athletic competitions are held off school district grounds, it is expected that students will travel to and from
such competitions with their teams. However, parents may request that they be permitted to transport their
children or that their children be permitted to transport themselves to and/or from such competitions.


This form must be properly completed, signed and returned to the Athletic Office or School Administrative Office before any
student is permitted to be transported to and/or from athletic competitions as set forth above. Students will not be permitted
to “carpool” to and/or from athletic competitions.
