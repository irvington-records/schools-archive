SCHOOL CLOSING PROCEDURES
Call the District’s main number at 591-8500 for updated announcements on school
delays/closings. One of the following two plans is used, depending upon the seriousness of weather
conditions:
Plan A - If the weather is inclement (snow or ice) with improving conditions, the opening of
school will be delayed by two hours. Buses will follow this delayed schedule.
Plan B - If weather conditions are severe with no improvement in the forecast, all schools will be
closed for the day.

All announcements calling for either a delayed opening or closing of school for the day will be
made no later than 7 a.m. on radio stations WOR and WHUD in addition to the Emergency
Notification phone message, the District’s recorded phone message at 591-8500 as well as the
District’s website: www.irvingtonschools.org. Please do not telephone the police for school
information since these calls could interfere with emergency police business. For further
information, tune to News12, the local cable television station.
In the event that school closings (due to weather or other emergencies) would bring the number
of school days below state minimum requirements, additional days will be added to the school
calendar.
