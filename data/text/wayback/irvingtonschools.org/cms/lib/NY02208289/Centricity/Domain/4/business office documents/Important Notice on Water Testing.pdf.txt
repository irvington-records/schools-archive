Important Notice on Water Testing

October 17, 2016

Dear Members of the Irvington Community,

As you may already be aware, the District underwent testing for lead in the water this past school year.
With the safety of all students and staff in mind, the District took proactive action ahead of the new
legislation requiring districts to conduct this type of testing.

As of September, New York State legislation requires all school districts to test all water sources for
lead contamination. At the beginning of this school year, the District tested every faucet at Dows Lane
and Main Street Schools. The results received on October 13th revealed that seven of the 118 faucets
tested at Dows Lane were above the acceptable limit, and at Main Street School, five of the 28 faucets
tested were above the acceptable limit. All faucets above the acceptable limit were taken out of service
until remediation can be achieved. We are pleased to report that all of our drinking fountains
were found to be within the acceptable limits.

At Dows Lane, five of the seven faucets above the limit were bathroom sinks in classrooms, which are
rarely used. These classrooms all have a secondary sink outside the bathroom door that is used for
hand washing. Additionally, one of the three sinks in the library tested above the limit, as did a sink in
the cafeteria that is not used for food preparation but for washing pots. All locations have been taken
offline.

At Main Street School, two of the five affected sources were classroom sinks and have been taken out
of service. The other three are located in the bathrooms in the gymnasium and, due to their location,
cannot be easily shut down, as there would be no way for someone to wash his or her hands after
bathroom use. Therefore, these bathrooms will be closed until remediation is performed.

With the help of a consultant, the District will develop a full remediation plan. We are currently
awaiting the test results from our other buildings at the Middle School-High School Campus and will
update you once we have them. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact Carol Stein,
Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations, at 914-591-9780.

Please be assured that we will continue to take all steps necessary to protect the health of all members
of the school community. We thank you for your understanding and continued support.

Sincerely,


Kristopher Harrison, Ed.D.
Superintendent of Schools
