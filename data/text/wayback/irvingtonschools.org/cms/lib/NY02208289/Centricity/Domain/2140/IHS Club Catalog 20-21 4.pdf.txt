               Midnight Run is not a solution to

             families."

             At IHS we collect clothing,
             toiletries and food donations,
             prepare food, organize runs, and
             drive down to NYC to distribute
             materials. Given the unusual
             school year it is not safe for us

             us to go on runs. Therefore, until
             we figure out a more feasible
             and safer way to conduct
             midnight runs, we will be
             focusing on collecting donations
             and fundraising so that we are
             better prepared for runs in the
             future.
