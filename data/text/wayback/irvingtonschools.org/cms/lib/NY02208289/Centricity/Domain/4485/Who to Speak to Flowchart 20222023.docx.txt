WHO TO SPEAK TO IF YOU HAVE A QUESTION OR CONCERN
Please begin with your first point of contact and then proceed to the next person if you find your issue wasn’t resolved.

Assistant Principal - Sara Rust - Sara.Rust@irvingtonschools.org
It is always recommended to contact the appropriate staff members (as noted above) to address any concerns you may have. However, if you have a concern that relates to a broad-based group of students, such as an  entire grade level, or school, you can contact PAC, the PTSA-sponsored Parent Advisory Councils that meet monthly with the leadership team of each school to bring forward questions and/or concerns from the community.
