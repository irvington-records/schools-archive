IRVINGTON UFSD BOARD OF EDUCATION
                  COMMUNITY GUIDE TO A BOARD MEETING



   WELCOME TO THE BOARD MEETING
   The Irvington Board of Education welcomes you. School board meetings are meetings of
   the board of education that are required to take place in public; however, they are not
   actually meetings of the public. Rather, they are business meetings held to conduct board
   business. Thank you for taking the time to attend a Board meeting and participate in the
   educational affairs of our District.

   AT THE MEETING
   The Board of Education, the official policy-making body of the Irvington Union Free School
   District, consists of seven members: residents elected at large by District voters to three-
   year, unpaid terms of office. The Board President, elected at an annual July organizational
   meeting, presides at all Board Meetings.

   Regularly joining Board members are the Superintendent of Schools, the Assistant
   Superintendent for Business, the Assistant Superintendent for Curriculum,
   Instruction, and Assessment and the District Clerk. Others may be invited to sit with or
   address the Board while making a presentation, providing information or during a work
   session.

   Meeting agendas and other relevant materials will be available for you to follow the
   evening’s planned topics and business. A QR code will also be available to view the
   agenda and materials on your phone. Additionally, meeting agendas, presentations, or
   other board information may be accessed online by going to the District’s website,
   www.irvingtonschools.org, and accessing Boarddocs. Boarddocs contains all Board
   of Education meeting agendas. Boarddocs can be accessed directly by clicking the
   following link: http://www.boarddocs.com/ny/iufsd/Board.nsf/Public.


   All official business is enacted in public session. However, an executive session may be
   held before, during, or after a public meeting to discuss matters involving personnel,
   litigation, negotiations, or other matters as allowed under state education law.

   PUBLIC PARTICIPATION
   Each business meeting provides two opportunities when District residents may comment
   to the Board. The first, “Public Comments on Agenda Items” is the opportunity for public
   discussion of the agenda items planned for the day. The second period is one for general
   comments.

   If you plan to comment during the meeting:

    •    Please sign in with the District Clerk
    •    Please use the microphone, state your name and if you are a resident for the record
    •    Participants are asked to keep their comments to 3 minutes or less.
    •    Comment period will last no more than 30 minutes.
    •    All comments are to be directed to the BOE President.
    •    Comments and questions will be acknowledged, but will not be answered in session
