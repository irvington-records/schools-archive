WHAT IS A CAPITAL RESERVE?

 ●   The Capital Reserve was established to fund larger capital expenditures
     that could not be supported in the annual general fund budget and are
     typically smaller than projects that are included in a capital bond project.
     This reserve is one of our several reserve funds, each having a specific
     purpose.
 ●   The District established our capital reserve in 2015 to fund up to
     $1,500,000 and increased it to $10,000,000 in 2023. The establishment of
     the reserve fund was voter approved.
 ●   The reserve is funded through annual budget surpluses that are not
     allocated to other reserves, appropriations for following year budget,
     encumbrances or our undesignated fund balance.
 ●   These funds can only be used for capital improvements.
 ●   As of June 2024, the Capital Reserve amount is $4,034,571 which includes
     funding and interest revenue.

WHAT DID THE DISTRICT ENVISION USING THIS RESERVE FOR?
The District initially planned on using this reserve for the turf and track
replacement knowing they have an approximate 10 year life span. Funds
were also added to tackle other projects that are part of our long range
facilities plan. The use of a reserve saves the District money as it eliminates
the need to borrow and pay interest. Instead, the District is accruing
interest on the funds in the reserve account.

HOW CAN THE CAPITAL RESERVE FUND BE ACCESSED?
To spend from the Capital Reserve, voters must approve the spending
via a proposition on a ballot. The vote can occur at any time of the
year. We propose adding this proposition to the May 20th Budget/
Board Trustee election vote versus holding a separate vote for
this purpose to not incur additional vote costs.

WILL SPENDING FROM THIS RESERVE INCREASE TAXES?
This spending will strictly come from the funds set aside in the Capital Reserve
and will not impact the tax levy or tax rate. Unlike a Capital Bond Project/Proposition,
there is no borrowing associated with using the funds in the Reserve.


WHEN WILL THE WORK BE COMPLETED?
After a successful vote, the District will work with our Architects to create the
specifications needed to submit to the State Education Department so we can
receive building permits and eventually state aid on projects that are aidable. This
will take several months. Our best estimate is to plan on work commencing the
Summer of 2026. It is possible that once we have permits and can bid out the
work, some of this work might be able to get done over a break period.

We will look to minimize construction cost which also means grouping similar
work together (such as all paving and concrete repair) to get a favorable bid.

HOW WILL WE MANAGE EVENTS?

 ●   The District is cognizant that events may create traffic and noise and
     will take measures to mitigate any potential issues from field use
 ●   For larger scale events, the District intends to disallow parking on
     Heritage Hill Road
 ●   Outdoor PA system use will be restricted to School Athletics
     contests
 ●   Custodial staff are on hand for cleanup and more trash and
     recycling receptacles will be placed in area.
