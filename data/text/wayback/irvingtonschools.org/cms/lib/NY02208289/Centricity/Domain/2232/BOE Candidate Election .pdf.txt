Irvington Board of Education Trustee Candidates For the 2021-22 school year

The Irvington Board of Education has two vacant three-year terms. Registered voters of
the Irvington UFSD community will be asked to vote for two candidates. Below are the
two community members who are running for election. Voters will also have the
opportunity to write in candidates.

Susan Brunenavs is a 10 year resident of Irvington. She and her husband have two
sons, one at Dows and one at MSS. Susan has worked closely with district
administrators and faculty/staff through her roles with the PTSA, including her two year
term as Co-President. She’s served on many district committees and is currently on the
MSS Site committee. Susan worked in banking and communications and is a freelance
writer. She will work to ensure IUFSD maintains strong instructional experiences for
students, fiscal responsibility and community engagement.




Beth Propper is a 17 year resident of Irvington. She and her husband have a daughter
(IHS 2024) and a son (IHS 2015; NYU 2019). First elected to the board in 2018, Beth
has a JD and an MPA and has dedicated her career to serving the public interest in
NYC and Westchester. Her volunteer activities have provided her with deep insight into
a variety of school issues, from pre-K through the college application process. Beth
looks forward to continuing to serve our community’s best interests, building consensus
with transparency and communication.




Note: The biographical narratives were provided by the candidates.
