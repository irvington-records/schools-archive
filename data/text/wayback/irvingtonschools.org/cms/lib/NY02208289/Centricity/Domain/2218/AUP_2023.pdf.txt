Policy for the Acceptable and Safe Use of
                                            Technology and the Internet


The Board of the Irvington UFSD recognizes that the use of computer technology and the Internet has become an
important aspect of the educational environment. The Board wishes to promote the appropriate use of technology so as
to maximize the educational benefits for students and staff. This policy outlines appropriate use and prohibited activities
when accessing the District's technology resources and Internet access.

1. Acceptable Use
The use of District technology and Internet access must be consistent with the educational mission of the Irvington UFSD.
Examples of acceptable use include classroom activities, research, career development, and district communications.
Prohibited activities include accessing inappropriate content, illegal activities, threatening communications, sharing
credentials, unauthorized downloads, commercial use, and damaging data or systems.

2. Digital Citizenship
Users must abide by rules of acceptable online behavior, including proper language use, respecting privacy, following
copyright law, and protecting personal information.

3. Security
Users must report identified security issues and may not attempt unauthorized system access. Vandalism will result in
loss of privileges.

4. Monitoring and Privacy
District technology resources are not private. The District reserves the right to monitor use to ensure compliance with
policy.

5. Personal Devices
Users must allow monitoring of personal devices on District networks to ensure policy compliance.

6. Violations
Violations may result in discipline or legal intervention. Privileges may be limited, suspended, or revoked.

7. Disclaimers
The District makes no warranties about service or accuracy of information provided through its technology resources. The
District is not responsible for damages arising from policy violations.

8. User Agreement
Students and parents must sign an agreement to abide by this policy before being granted access.

9. Policy Review
The Superintendent or designee will review this policy annually and update as needed.

10. User Education
Students will receive age-appropriate digital citizenship and safety education. Staff development opportunities will be
provided on responsible technology use.

Each user of the technology resources of the Irvington UFSD, including students, faculty, staff or community member will
be required to complete and return the attached form before access to the system will be granted.
Failure to abide by the above rules and regulations will result in a suspension of technology access privileges,
