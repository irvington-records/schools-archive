                   District Capital Project Update

March 5, 2021


Dear Irvington School Community,


Since my last update on the progress of the Irvington UFSD capital project, there has
been a lot of forward movement toward launching construction on the Phase 1 projects.
Last summer we completed the installation of a new boiler at Dows Lane, and now I am
excited to share that we are breaking ground on the construction of the Campus
Learning Pavilion by Meszaros Field. This building will contain space for storage for our
athletic equipment, a treatment room for the Athletic Trainer, a concession stand,
public restrooms and an outdoor classroom space on the roof of the building. You will
start to see contractors at the MS/HS Campus, ably managed by Triton Construction, the
District’s construction manager. Areas under construction will close to only provide
access for contractors and to provide for student and community safety. During times of
construction, the District and its contractors will be mindful of student traffic patterns,
especially with regard to construction material deliveries, to ensure a safe environment
is maintained for all. At times, minor inconveniences may occur, which are inevitable,
but will be managed to limit the impact on traffic.

Then in late June, construction will begin on the campus enclosures, other
enhancements at the Middle School and Science buildings and much needed work at
Dows Lane Elementary. For a current update on the status of all capital projects and
more detail on individual projects, please click here.

As more updates are available and construction timelines are confirmed, the District
will provide the necessary details. In the meantime, please feel free to review project
details on the Capital Bond Project webpage. And, let’s all hope that good weather will
allow us to keep our project timelines on track!

Truly yours,

Kris

Dr. Kristopher Harrison
Superintendent of Schools
