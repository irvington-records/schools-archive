   The mission of the Irvington School
District is to create a challenging and
supportive learning environment in which
each student attains his or her highest
potential for academic achievement, critical
thinking and lifelong learning. Our schools
encourage the discovery and development
of students’ individual strengths, skills and
talents, and foster social and civic
responsibility. Four schools serve the
District to achieve that mission: Dows Lane
