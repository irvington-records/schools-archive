MESZAROS FIELD AND OLEY TRACK
                          RULES AND REQUIREMENTS
There is absolutely no:
 ❖      Metal cleats                        ❖       Colored sports drinks
 ❖      Sunflower seeds                     ❖       Pets of any kind
 ❖      Food                                ❖       Skateboards, bicycles, roller
 ❖      Gum                                         blades, or other non-
 ❖      Smoking or use of any tobacco               authorized recreational
        products at any time                        equipment
 ❖      Alcoholic beverages                 ❖       Use of any soccer goals during
 ❖      Use of grills or any BBQ                    lacrosse season
        equipment                           ❖       No lacrosse games or practices
 ❖      Use of drugs or any illegal                 without field netting in
        substances                                  place.

Any organizations using the facility are responsible for the following:
 ❖      Building use form approved by school district prior to usage. Please
        carry a copy of the form for Field Marshall review.
 ❖      Reporting of any damage or spills that may have occurred during use.
 ❖      Returning of goals or any approved school equipment to the proper location
        after use.
 ❖      Keeping all athletes and spectators off the high jump and pole vault mats.
 ❖      Ensuring that all athletes do not walk on the track surface with cleats
        and cross only at the designated mat coverings.
 ❖      Keeping all spectators off the tower and out of any district storage area.
 ❖      Ensuring all team participants have received these rules and regulations
        prior to field use and have agreed to abide by these rules. (A PDF file
        has been provided to you so that you may email it to all our participants.
        Please keep the email proof that you have distributed the file, if you
        choose to distribute the rules in this manner.)

Violators of these regulations will have their access revoked and will be
responsible for any related repair or replacement costs.   I, as the organizer,
agree to the terms above and that I will share a summary sheet of Irvington
U.F.S.D. Meszaros Field and Oley Track Rules and Regulations with my
organization. It is my responsibility to ensure that these rules and regulations
have been communicated to all teams, coaches, parents and players using Irvington
U.F.S.D property under our Facility Request.
