      Irvington High School Environment
The Irvington team works to create a safe and orderly environment that supports student
success, both in and out of the classroom. In this environment we strive to help you build
strong habits in the areas of Respect, Responsibility, Empathy, and Kindness, in school,
at school related events, and in the community, we will support you as you will:

    ޮ Work to the best of your ability by being on time to all classes, paying attention,
      and completing all work by clearly established deadlines.
    ޮ Treat others respectfully at all times, through both your actions and the way in
      which you speak to and about others in all forms including social media. Use of
      profanity, language that is vulgar or offensive to others, or words that are
      derogatory or make fun of others are unacceptable at IUFSD.
    ޮ Be truthful at all times, and encourage your friends to be truthful as well.
    ޮ Accept responsibility for your actions at all times.
    ޮ Respect school property, i.e. lockers, textbooks, Chromebooks, uniforms, etc.
    ޮ Ensure that fellow students feel safe and comfortable at IUFSD, and actively
      work to help those who feel uncomfortable or threatened in any way (physically,
      emotionally, etc.) by confiding in an adult who can intervene appropriately.
    ޮ Inform an adult in school if an incident begins outside of school and you believe
      it may continue in school.
    ޮ Use technology responsibly and for academic pursuits; you may only record,
      video/audio, or take pictures if given specific permission to do so.
    ޮ Understand and follow all the rules set forth in this handbook, as well as those
      established by your teachers.

                            The School Day
Busing
The school day begins at the bus stop. Students who live 1½ miles or further from the
high school are eligible for school bus transportation. Because safety is the first priority
on the bus, students can enter or exit the bus only when it has come to a complete stop.
All passengers must be in their seats when the bus is moving. The driver has authority on
the bus and will report any unacceptable behavior, which can result in a suspension of
bus privileges.

Senior Parking
Parking is a privilege for 6HQLRUVRQO\​. To be eligible for parking privileges seniors are
to complete a Parking Permit Registration form, and obtain a parking permit, which must
be displayed. Seniors may only park in the upper gravel lot. Seniors disregarding the
parking policies will be fined per fine schedule. In addition, the student’s HAC account
will be locked until fines are paid. Parking fines are as follows:
     Ɣ 1st Offense $25
     Ɣ 2nd Offense $50
     Ɣ 3rd Offense $75
     Ɣ 4th Offense $100
     Ɣ 5th or more - tow truck called at owner’s expense.
UNDERCLASSMAN ARE NOT PERMITTED TO PARK ON CAMPUS. IF AN
UNDERCLASSMAN IS FOUND TO BE PARKING ANYWHERE ON CAMPUS
DURING THE SCHOOL DAY THEY WILL BE FINED PER FINE SCHEDULE AND
WILL HAVE THEIR HAC ACCOUNT LOCKED UNTIL PAYMENT.

Access for Students with Handicaps
Irvington High School is accessible to all people.
Lockers
We encourage students to use their assigned locker to store their personal items.
Lockers (Gym)
Gym lockers willbe assigned at the beginning of the school year by the Physical

during PE classes and after- school practices and games.

Inclement Weather
We encourage you to register for automated email or phone messages regarding changes
to the school day schedule. In the event of school cancellation or delay due to snow or
for another emergency. Please contact Leann Serao in the high school office at
leann.serao@irvingtonschools.org to register for Instant Contact.

Lost and Found
If you’ve lost something or found something you should check the lost and found bins
located in the hallway off the Main Office, in the cafeteria or in the library. It is
suggested that any electronic devices, such as cell phones, should be brought to the main
office.

Working Papers
Students between the ages of 14 and 18 need working papers in order to obtain an
after-school job. Applications are available in the Counseling Office; they need to be
signed by a parent or guardian.

                                  Attendance
School attendance is both a right and a responsibility. The Irvington High School is an
active partner with students and parents in the task of ensuring that all students meet or
exceed the expectations of the New York State Learning Standards. As regular school
attendance directly correlates to academic success and contributes significantly towards
attaining a high school diploma, the high school has developed, and will revise when
necessary, the following comprehensive Student Attendance Policy.

      9th & 10th graders are not allowed to leave campus during the school day.

   11th & 12th graders are allowed to leave campus only during their free periods.

attendance emails.

Medical/Personal Appointments

Medical and personal appointments should be made before or after school hours.
However, when a student must miss school due to a medical/personal appointment, a
verification note/receipt must accompany the student when they return to school. When
arriving late due to an appointment, the student needs to sign in with their note/receipt.
When a student needs to leave school early for an appointment, the parent/guardian needs
to call the attendance office and the student needs to sign out and bring in their
note/receipt the following school day.
