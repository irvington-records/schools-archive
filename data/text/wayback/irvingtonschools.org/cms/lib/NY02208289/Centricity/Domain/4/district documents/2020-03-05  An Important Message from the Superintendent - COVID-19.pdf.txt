             An Important Message from the
               ​Superintendent of Schools -
              COVID-19 Coronavirus Update

March 5, 2020


Dear School Community Members,

We are aware that a neighboring school district has closed its schools for the
balance of the week to sanitize its facilities due to concerns related to the
coronavirus (COVID-19). Please be assured that I continue to be in regular
communication with the District physician, who is also in on-going contact with
the Department of Health. Through these communications and all known local
information related to COVID-19, we have been guided that there is no reason to
close any of the Irvington schools.

As a District, we are taking as many precautionary steps as possible to provide for
the well-being of our students and staff, which include the following measures:

      Sanitization of all classrooms and common spaces in each school multiple
      times each week (note: this scheduled overtime expenditure is being offset
      by budgeted snow removal costs that have not been utilized);
      On-going, daily cleaning/sanitization of all high contact items such as door
      knobs and handles, water fountains, bathroom fixtures, etc.;
      Installation of additional hand sanitizer stations in schools (supplies will be
      stocked as available through distributors);
      Daily sanitization of all school buses; and
      Initiation of planning for distance-learning options should schools need to
      be closed in the future for COVID-19 or other reasons.

Despite the growing number of cases of COVID-19, guidance remains consistent for
public schools. This includes precautionary steps for individuals who have traveled
to particular areas of the world (as identified by the CDC), the proper screening
of those who display illness and practicing good sense which includes:

      Voluntary home isolation – students and staff should stay home when ill,
      except to obtain medical care or necessities
      Respiratory etiquette – Cover coughs and sneezes; use shirt sleeve if tissue
      not available – Avoid touching eyes, nose, and mouth
      Hand hygiene – Regular and thorough hand washing with soap and water, or
      alcohol-based hand sanitizers (due to requirements for schools, the
      sanitizer in school is alcohol-free)

The District will continue to use the guidance of governmental agencies and
medical experts to inform planning and decision making. This also applies to
decisions associated with whether to move forward with scheduled field trips
outside the country and/or region. At this time, the District has not cancelled any
trips and is not cancelling gatherings of large groups of people. However, this may
change, based upon guidance and/or directives from the appropriate agencies or
experts.

The District will continue to maintain a COVID-19 Coronavirus resource page with
important links and documents related to this public health concern. This week,
we added an article that you may find useful that is designed to support parents
in speaking to the children about the virus. To access this article, please click
here.

I understand that these are concerning times for everyone and together we will
navigate this challenge and others that confront our community. I will continue to
keep you informed of new developments that affect our school community.
Should you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact me.

My very best,

Kris

Dr. Kristopher Harrison
Superintendent of Schools
