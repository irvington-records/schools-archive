In the event that there is a school closing due to weather or emergencies that would bring the number of school days below state
minimum requirements, students will be required to attend school on the following days beginning with March 25, 2024 and March
26, 2024. If six or more school closings are used then May 24, 2024 will be a school day. If additional days need to be made up Spring
Recess will be further shortened.
