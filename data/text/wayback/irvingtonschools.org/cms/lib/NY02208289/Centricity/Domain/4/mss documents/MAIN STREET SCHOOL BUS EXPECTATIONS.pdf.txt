MAIN STREET SCHOOL BUS EXPECTATIONS
Manage Impulsivity          Students will keep hands and feet to themselves.
                            Students will walk at all times and maintain space between the person in front
                             of them.
                            Students will remain seated with their seat belt fastened, facing forward, with
                             their feet on the floor at all times. (Students will not kick seats.)
                            Students will use appropriate voice level on the bus. (Please use level 1 and 2 voices
                             only.)
Practice Self-Reliance      Students will know and follow the rules.
                            Students will be prepared for their stop.
                            Students will take all personal belongings off the bus.
Show Kindness &             Students will greet the bus driver and monitor.
Respect                     Students will use words and language that are kind. (No cursing, name calling, verbal
                             threats or aggressive speech.)
                            Students will keep the bus clean.
Cooperate                   Students will follow the directions of the bus driver and monitor.
                            Students will self-advocate and ask for help when needed.
                            Students will help others to keep safe.


                                                PLEDGE of RESPECT

                                     I am a smart, special and valuable person.

                                            I respect myself and others.

                                    My words and actions are kind and honest.

                                        I accept only my best in all that I do.

                                              I am PROUD TO BE ME!
