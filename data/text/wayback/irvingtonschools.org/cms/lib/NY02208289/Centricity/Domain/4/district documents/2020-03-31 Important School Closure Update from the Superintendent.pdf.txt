       Important School Closure Update from the
              Superintendent of Schools

March 31, 2020


Dear Irvington School Community,

As some of you may have already learned by following Governor Cuomo’s press
conferences or through the media, the Governor’s recent executive order will
have great impacts on all schools that have Spring Break scheduled within the
next two weeks. Specifically, Executive Order 202.11 requires school districts to
“continue plans for alternative instructional options, distribution and availability
of meals, and childcare, with an emphasis on serving children of essential
workers.”

So what does this mean?

Contrary to the announcement that I made on Friday, March 27, 2020; wherein, I
communicated that the District would be closed April 6th through April 13th, the
District will continue with it’s distance learning plan rather than have Spring
Break. During this period of time, teachers will continue to guide distance
learning consistent with the practices that families and students are becoming
used to. What further complicates the instructional plan for next week is the fact
that there are a number of religious holidays that many of our staff members will
be observing, specifically Passover and Good Friday. As a result, I am advising the
school community that there may be limitations on teacher availability on given
days.

As we look to the days and weeks ahead, and recognize the likelihood of ongoing
closures, I hope that we can remain grateful for the good fortune that we all have
in our lives. Together, as a community we will navigate these challenges and
support one another. If you are ever in the need of assistance, please do not
hesitate to reach out to a school staff member, principal, our Social Worker, Gina
Menendez (Gina.Menendez@IrvingtonSchools.org) or me. We are dedicated to
doing everything possible to support you through any challenges you may face.
Finally, we do have numerous community members fighting COVID-19 and many
of us have friends and family members facing the difficult illness. I ask that you
keep those dealing with COVID-19 in your thoughts.

In the coming days, please remember to share your Irvington Spirit at
IrvingtonSpirit@IrvingtonSchools.org, follow us on Instagram at
https://www.instagram.com/irvingtonufsd/ and join me for my next Facebook
Live broadcast Friday, April 3rd at 12:00 pm at
https://www.facebook.com/IrvingtonUFSD/.

I thank you for your continued support and remain honored to be your partner
during these difficult times.

Every student, every day,

Kris
