  A Message from the Superintendent of Schools-
              Capital Project Update

July 31, 2020


Dear Irvington School Community,

It has been a while since the District had the opportunity to provide an update on
the capital bond project. Over recent months more critical issues have consumed
our communications; however, the District Administration and the Board of
Education’s Buildings and Grounds Committee has continued to advance the
project.

To refresh your memory this necessary capital project will provide for increased
safety and security, modernized learning spaces, enhanced energy efficiency and
improved district infrastructure. However, the impacts of COVID-19 have had
unfortunate and unforeseen delays on the required approval process that is led by
the New York State Education Department. Despite the delays in project approval
the District and its professional partners have met all timelines that we set to
achieve.

By design, the capital project was divided into two phases in which projects were
intended to be completed during the summer months of 2020 and 2021. As a
result of the pandemic, many of these projects will be delayed, but the District is
doing everything in its power to advance the project despite the SED’s delays in
its approval process.

By design, all safety and security projects were given top priority and will be
initiated as soon as approval is granted so long as the construction can be
completed safely. Thus, most significant construction will be limited to the
summers of 2021 and 2022. At this point in time, the only work that is confirmed
for this summer is the replacement of the Dows Lane boiler. For a current update
on the status of all of the individual projects included in this initiative, please
click here.

As more updates are available and construction timelines are confirmed, the
District will provide the necessary details. In the meantime, please feel free
review project details on the Capital Bond Project webpage.

Truly yours,

Kris

Dr. Kris Harrison
Superintendent of Schools
