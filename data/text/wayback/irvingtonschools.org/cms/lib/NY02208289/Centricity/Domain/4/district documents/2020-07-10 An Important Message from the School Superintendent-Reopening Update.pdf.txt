          An Important Message from the
   Superintendent of Schools - Reopening Update

July 10, 2020


Dear Irvington School Community,

I hope this message finds you well, enjoying the early days of summer. While we
typically think of summer recess as a time to slow down, we in the IUFSD have
been very busy planning for the reopening of school in September. This
communication is intended to provide you with important information on this
process.

Since early June, the District has been implementing a comprehensive planning
process to be ready for all aspects of the school experience regardless of whether
we are engaged in distance learning, on-site learning or a hybrid of the two. The
District’s planning process includes four committees that are representative of
our community by having among its membership teachers, nurses, staff,
administrators, Board of Education Trustees, parents, the school physician,
Irvington Police Officers and the District’s security and safety consultant, Altaris.
The four committees are comprised of a Steering Committee and three
subcommittees.

The committees are completing very focused work to develop recommendations
that will inform the creation of our reopening plan. This plan, which will be
completed by the end of July, will outline recommendations for all educational,
health and safety measures that may be implemented come September. Below,
please find a description of each committee’s responsibilities:

Steering Committee: The Steering Committee is charged with coordinating the
work of three subcommittees and supporting the work of the development of the
District’s reopening plan. The Steering Committee is meeting with representatives
of the three subcommittees who are presenting the development of their
recommendations. The most recent Steering Committee presentation/report
provides an overview of the District’s process to date, along with the areas upon
which the subcommittees are focusing.

Curriculum and Instruction Subcommittee: This committee is in the process of
developing recommendations that will outline models for instruction including an
enhanced model for distance learning (utilizing our experiences and community
feedback) and a hybrid model wherein students will attend school on a rotational
basis (i.e. 50% of students in attendance daily) employing health and safety
guidance/requirements from the respective governmental agencies. The
committee is also focusing on any necessary curricular modifications that have to
be made and are developing strategies for assessing our students’ learning needs
upon their return to school.

Social and Emotional Learning (SEL) Subcommittee: In addition to planning for
the academic needs of students, the IUFSD recognizes that the pandemic has
presented many members of our community with challenges in managing changing
school and work schedules, family dynamics, and health concerns. The SEL
committee is focused on developing recommendations for student and staff
support structures to facilitate the return to school including instructional
strategies and programs that will be implemented during the 2020-21 school
year.

Operations Subcommittee: The operations committee is responsible for
developing recommendations to provide for the health and safety of all staff
members, developing necessary protocols for cleaning and sanitizing school
facilities, coordinating any modifications to transportation services and addressing
food service needs.

While we are making great progress in developing our reopening plans, what has
been absent from this process is any formal guidance from New York State (NYS)
and the New York State Education Department (SED), which we look forward to
receiving next week. With this information in hand, we will be able to finalize our
operational recommendations for the reopening of school. To further inform our
decision making, please look for and complete an important parent survey related
to the reopening of school that will be emailed to you next week.

Finally, I look forward to presenting an overview of our planning process on July
21st at a Board of Education meeting that will be held via Zoom. Once Governor
Cuomo and the SED provide the necessary guidance, the District will finalize its
plans and present them to the school community. Details for these meetings will
be communicated in advance of the meeting dates.

As always, should you have any questions, please do not hesitate to contact me.

My best regards,

Kris

Dr. Kris Harrison
Superintendent of Schools
