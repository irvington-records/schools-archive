Important Voter Information from the District Clerk

May 21, 2020


Dear Irvington Community:

Pursuant to Executive Order No. 202.26 signed by Governor Cuomo on May 1,
2020, the 2020-21 Annual Budget Vote & School Board Election (Annual Meeting)
will be held on Tuesday, June 9, 2020.

Due to the COVID-19 pandemic, this year the school budget vote and Board of
Education trustee election will be conducted entirely by absentee ballot. The
requirement to complete an application has been suspended. Absentee ballots
will be automatically mailed to registered, qualified voters in the District and will
include a postage paid return envelope to assist the community members with
returning the absentee ballot by mail.

Absentee ballots will be in both English and Spanish and will contain a proposition
pertaining to the 2020-21 Irvington School District budget. The absentee ballots
will also contain the names of the individuals running for the three (3) open seats
on the Irvington Board of Education. The candidates for the three (3) seats are:

Brian Friedman
Maura Gedid
Jayne Wissner

Upon receipt of the absentee ballot:
      Voters must complete the absentee ballot
      Insert the absentee ballot into the affirmation envelope and seal it
      The voter must then write their name and address on the affirmation
      envelope and sign where indicated on the envelope
      The voter must insert the signed affirmation envelope into the postage paid
      return envelope pre-addressed to the District Clerk and mail it as soon as
      possible.

Please note that absentee ballots must be received by the District Clerk via US
Mail no later than 5:00 p.m. on June 9, 2020. Inspectors will assist with the
canvassing of the absentee ballot in the Dows Lane Gymnasium. Required health
and safety measures will be in place to protect our election workers.

During this time of social distancing and quarantine, there will be no personal
voter registration held at Main Street School. Prospective voters may register to
vote with the Westchester County Board of Elections by completing an online
registration application with the NYS Department of Motor Vehicles at:
https://dmv.ny.gov/more-info/electronic-voter-registration-application.
Registration should occur as soon as possible so as to be able to receive and return
an absentee ballot prior to 5:00 p.m. on June 9, 2020.

Important Date:
      The next Board of Education meeting will take place on Tuesday, June 2,
      2020 at 7:30 p.m. via Zoom. A Public Hearing on the 2020-21 Irvington
      School District budget, starting at 7:15 p.m., will precede this meeting.
      Details of this meeting will be posted on the District website.

Members of the community are encouraged to contact the District Clerk for
further information via email at: pauline.davies@irvingtonschools.org.

Sincerely,
Pauline Davies
District Clerk
