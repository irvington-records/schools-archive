Summer Driver’s Education Program at Maria Regina High School

Attached is information about the Summer 2022 Driver's Education program at Maria Regina High
School in Hartsdale. The registration form is located on the Maria Regina HS website under "about
MRHS" and "Driver's Education."

The summer 2022 semester will begin on July 12. 2022. The State Education Department is allowing
Driver Ed programs to conduct behind-the-wheel training. the protocol as of now and until the State
provides new guidelines is that the parent/guardian has the option to opt in or out of the driving, If a
student opts out, their parent will sign a form stating that the student performed the driving portion of
Driver Education under their supervision. This form will be provided to the parents at orientation.

Students have the option of doing in-person lectures or all online assignments.
They will be using Google Classroom for all online students.
Please understand that the tuition is the same for either lecture option, as this does not defray the
cost of running the program.

Students who successfully complete the course will receive the MV-285 (Driver Ed Completion
Certificate). The MV-285 allows students to obtain a senior license at 17.
