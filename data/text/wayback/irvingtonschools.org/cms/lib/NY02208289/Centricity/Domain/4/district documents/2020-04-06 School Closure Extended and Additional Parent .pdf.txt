              Extended School Closure and
         Additional Parent & Student Resources

April 6, 2020

Dear Irvington School Community,

I hope this message finds you well, having enjoyed the lovely weather over the
weekend. Over the last few weeks, the challenges facing all of us have required
us to dig deep in ourselves and find strength that we may not have ever had to use
before. We have had to endure new challenges, do things differently for our own
health and for the greater good and to place our trust in medical experts and
elected leaders to guide us through these uncertain times. In doing so, we are
building character and skills that will foster greater strength and a deeper level of
compassion that will serve us all well in the future.

We will need to continue to draw on our strength and continue to demonstrate
our collective perseverance as Governor Cuomo just announced that he has
extended the state-wide school closure through April 28th. During this time,
distance learning will continue.

While we are all indoors and unable to interact with those outside our homes,
there still are many things we can do to support our emotional wellness, enrich
our minds and have enjoyable times with our families. The IHS Guidance
Department has assembled many creative and enriching resources and activities.
Please click here to read their recent newsletter that is rich with ideas for all
ages.

I want to thank IUFSD Assistant Superintendent Mary Ellis for spearheading the
development of a new tool for the parent community, A Parent's Guide to
Distance Learning. The goal of creating these resources is to support our parent
partners in navigating this period of distance learning. Please know that we do not
believe these resources to be all inclusive, but we do think they will be helpful to
many parents. The District will continue to update this guide in the coming weeks
as our program continues to evolve.

To help the school community remain connected with all that is happening, I am
looking forward to hosting another Facebook Live update at 8:00 am, Wednesday,
April 8th. To join this live video update, please visit the District’s Facebook page:
www.Facebook.com/IrvingtonUFSD/.

Finally, to those celebrating holidays this week, I hope you are able to enjoy and
find ways to virtually connect with your family and friends.

Sincerely,

Kris

Dr. Kris Harrison
