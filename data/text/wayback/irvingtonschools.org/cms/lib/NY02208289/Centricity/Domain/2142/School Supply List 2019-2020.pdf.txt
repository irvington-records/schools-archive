SCHOOL SUPPLIES LIST
                                               IRVINGTON HIGH SCHOOL
                                                      2019-2020

H.S. Planners will be sold at the School Store located in the Middle School Aug 21-24, 9am – 2pm; Aug

General Supplies
        Large 3-ring binder (2” recommended) with dividers or folders to separate by subject with loose-
        leaf, pens/pencils. Students can have 1 binder per class if they prefer with dividers but it is not
        necessary.

Social Studies
        Global Studies I: 2 folders, three hole paper punch, white out, 100 index cards, see above
        Global Studies II: see above for binder information
        American Studies: 2 folders, see above for binder information
        Economics: 2 folders, three hole paper punch, see above for binder information

Science
        All courses: USB flash drive, see above binder information
        Physics: 30 cm ruler, protractor, graphing calculator, 2 marbled composition books for labs
        Earth Science: a 1.5 inch binder, a pack of dividers for binder, and a four-function or scientific calculator (note: New
        York State regulations will not allow a graphing calculator for these courses)
        Chemistry: four-function or scientific calculator (note: New York State regulations will not allow a graphing
        calculator for these courses)
        Honors Biology & Regents Biology: 2 single subject 8.5 x 11 inch spiral bound notebooks, 2 rolls of tape, 1 glue stick, 1
        packet of 3x3 inch post its, four-function or scientific calculator (note:New York State regulations will not allow a
        graphing calculator for these courses)
        AP Biology: 5 function calculator (+, -, *, /, √), 2 single subject 8.5 X 11 inch spiral bound notebooks; 2 rolls of tape; 1
        glue stick; 1 packet of 3 X 3 inch post its.
        Science Research: 1 5 inch binder, 2 packs of dividers for the binder

English
        Grades 9-11: five dividers, one pocket folder, three hole paper punch, see above binder information
        Grade 12: notebook

Physical Education
        Appropriate athletic sneakers (no platform sneakers), shorts, T-shirt, sweat pants

Math
        All courses: dividers, ruler, graph paper, spiral notebook, see above for binder information

        Grades 9, 10, 11 & 12: Texas Instrument TI-84+ or TI-83+ graphing calculator.
        AP Calculus: TI-89 is highly recommended.

         Please note: Depending on the model, graphing calculators may range in price between approximately $90 and $150,
but are required for Regents and AP courses/exams. You may consider buying a used one. If this presents a financial burden,
the school has a limited number to be used in class. Please have your student notify his/her teacher in September. If
purchasing a new graphing calculator, please do not open package and keep the receipt in case the wrong calculator was
purchased.

World Language
        All courses: 5 section dividers, composition notebook, two-pocket portfolios, French/Spanish dictionary (for use at
        home). See above for binder information.

Studio Art: 9” x 12” or 8” x 11” bound sketchbook (students can and should continue to use the
sketchbook started in Middle School) Pink Eraser & four different types of pencils: HB, 2H, 2B & 4B
