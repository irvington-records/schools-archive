The Irvington PTSA’s mission is threefold:
- To support and speak on behalf of the children and youth in our schools, in the community, and before governmental bodies;
- To assist parents in developing the skills they need to raise and protect their children;
- To encourage parent and public involvement in the public schools.
The Parent Advisory Council (PAC) is the PTSA's vehicle to bring parents' questions and concerns to the attention of the school administration. PAC is comprised of grade parents, PTSA Vice Presidents, the school principal and a teacher representative. PAC meets monthly at Dows Lane, Main Street School and Irvington Middle School.
The PTSA provides a great deal of support for school district children and faculty through programs that enrich the curriculum such as the After School Program, International Week, Health and Nutrition Week and low cost PSAT/SAT courses. Though our Authors/Illustrators program, we bring award-winning authors/illustrators to the schools to give presentations about their works and profession. Each child at Dows Lane publishes a literary work in a beautifully crafted book at our Publishing Center. We organize and fund numerous assemblies to enhance the curriculum as well. In the past, we have hosted a NASA astronaut, Peter Lourie who canoed the Hudson from beginning to end and numerous artists who work with the children to help them explore their own creativity.
A few evenings each school year, the PTSA holds parent educational programs and workshops. For 2003-2004, we will work closed with Irvington's Community Advisory Board (CAB) to provide programming that enhances our combined efforts. The PTSA's Special Education Support Committee also hosts programs on issues relating to children with special needs and parenting children with special needs.
We work closely with the community through on participation on the CAB, our coat and toy drives that benefit local charities and our Winter Festival that coincides with the Irvington Village's Holiday Happening. Furthermore, we publicize school budget and bond votes to insure that all school district residents are fully-informed of upcoming votes.
Each school year, the PTSA funds grants for programs that enhance the school curriculum. On average, we fund $25,000 in grants each year. Furthermore, each year we give over $10,000 in scholarships to graduating seniors. We finance our programs through various fundraisers such as gift wrap sales, Photo Day, Pizza Fridays at Dows Lane and Main Street School, our Mothers/Fathers Day Boutique and our Book Fairs. In May, we also host a Spring Fundraiser Luncheon.
Every student and parent in the Irvington School District benefits from PTSA programs and grants. This is possible because of the dedicated teachers, parent volunteers and Irvington community who generously giver their time and/or financial support.
