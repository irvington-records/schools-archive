Bond Facts Continued
Your Vote Counts on
February 3, 2000
- A person qualified to register to vote must be a citizen of the United States, at least 18 years old, and a resident of the School District for at least thirty days before the vote.
- Any person who is registered with the Westchester County Board of Elections in the Irvington School District need not register separately with the School District.
- Any person who has voted in any of the Irvington School District elections any time since May 1996 is registered with the School District and need not register again.
The Vote:
When:  February 3, 2000, 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM
Where:  Irvington Middle School
Absentee Ballots:  Call: 591-8502
Additional Information:   Call 591-8501 or 591-9780
Voter Registration:
To vote in the Irvington School Bond Vote, you must be registered for either school elections or general elections. If you are not registered, you may register for school voting at the District Clerk's Office at the High School 9:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m. during regular school days, OR, register at the Special Voter Registration Day, Thursday, January 27, 2000 from 4:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. in the lobby of the Irvington Middle School.
Any person who is not registered with the County or did not vote in any of the Irvington School District elections since May 1996 must register to vote in the School District's February 3, 2000 Bond Vote (including those who have voted only in Village elections).
For more information visit our website at: www.irvingtonschools.org
