State Approval to Commence Building Project
On Friday, May 4, the Irvington School District received a Building Permit from the New York State Education Department Office of Facilities Planning to commence with the implementation of its Master Plan. As a result, public bids for the site work portion of the project on the High School campus are now being solicited. Groundbreaking is expected to commence early this summer.
The site development portion of the project is the first step in a projected two year construction project which was approved by the voters in February, 2000. The Master Plan includes building a new middle school, a new science/technology/music/cafeteria addition to the High School, a new theater/ gymnasium complex, and renovations to the High School and other buildings in the district.
The site approval enables the district to commence its construction plan slightly ahead of its projected schedule. "We are pleased that our consultants, board committee, and administrative staff have worked closely with State officials to ensure that the site work is beginning when school is not in session," noted Board of Education President Paul Mandel. "Our commitment all along has been to insure a safe construction site for our students and staff, with a minimum impact on our school program."
Once bids are received and approved by the Board of Education, a specific construction schedule, including a date for a groundbreaking ceremony, will be developed and publicized.
For further information contact Dr. Vincent T. Beni, Superintendent of Schools, Irvington Union Free School District, 914-591-8501.
