The following are the minutes of the Public Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library, January 21, 2003, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg,  Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman – Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mrs. Lauren Allan – Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane 
Gr. K-3 Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Dows Lane Gr. 4-5 Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Mrs. Susan Levey – Director Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar – Director of Technology, faculty and members of the community. 

The Public Work Session was called to order, following Executive Session, by Mrs. Janice Silverberg at 7:45 p.m.
   
I             MASTER PLAN UPDATE 

Dr. Beni reported that the construction is ahead of schedule and that the new Middle School building will be ready for the September 4, 2003 opening of school.  He also said that the project is on budget and that the District is pleased with the quality of the work.  Mr. Baird of JMOA Engineering P.C., the Project Manager, presented the status of construction.  Highlights included:
	
Cafeteria/Music/Science building – windows are 95% installed, sheet rock (including taping and painting) is 75% complete and the masonry is 98% complete.
Middle School building – the light fixtures, fire alarm/PA system are being worked on, the tile installation is 90% complete and the painting is at 75%.
Theatre/Gym building – next steps include roofing, masonry, elevated deck and steps installation.

Mrs. Delany arrived at 7:55 p.m.

In addition, steel will be going up within the month at the Library/Gym/Arts wing and the gas line and electrical feeder line work is underway on the High School driveway hill.  

Mrs. Silverberg reviewed the components and objectives of the $3.8 million Bond which will be presented for a community vote on April 3, 2003

On April 3rd there will be a second Proposition for the reduction in the mileage for Grade K-5 transportation eligibility.

II            Curriculum Committee Update:  Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman
 
Dr. Dolan-Waldman discussed the minutes from the Curriculum Committee meeting held on January 29, 2003, the Irvington Insight articles and the presentation made to the PTSA on January 27, 2003 which outline student performance data.  

Mrs. Allan and the World Language sub-committee presented a report (Supplemental Log – Appendix A) including:  
current instruction, philosophy and program goals in Irvington
the sub-committee review process and benefits of early language instruction
recommendations for the 2003-04 school year
implementation, scheduling, staffing and budget requirements

Following questions and discussion, Mrs. Silverberg expressed the Board’s appreciation to Dr. Dolan-Waldman and the committee members for the work that went into the presentation. 

At 9:55 p.m. Mrs. Silverberg adjourned the Work Session to return to the Public Meeting.
