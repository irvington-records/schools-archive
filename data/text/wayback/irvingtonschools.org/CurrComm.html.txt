IRVINGTON SCHOOL DISTRICT
Curriculum Committee Introduction
The Curriculum Committee is comprised of parents, students, 2 Board members, a local college
representative, and staff from the District.  The goal of the Committee is to monitor the
many areas that are under review and discussion, and to help review curriculum proposals
prior to adoption.  This Committee is in its first year, with meetings held in October,
February and May.  These minutes reflect the overall discussion of the group and the
presentations that were made at the meeting.  Minutes from future meetings will be posted
here also.
