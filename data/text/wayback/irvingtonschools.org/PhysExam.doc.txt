IRVINGTON PUBLIC SCHOOLS

PHYSICAL AND IMMUNIZATION FORM

The New York State Law requires all students to be fully immunized against POLIO, DIPTHERIA, TETANUS, MEASLES, MUMPS AND RUBELLA.
HEPATITIS B is required for all children born on or after January 1, 1993.
VARICELLA is required for all children born on or after January 1, 1994.

A PHYSICAL EXAM performed by a licensed NYS physician is required of each child when entering KDG, 1ST, 3RD, 7TH, and 10TH grade.  In addition, all new students entering the district must also have a current physical.  Students participating in the interscholastic sports program and those students obtaining working papers must have a current physical on file.

EXCLUSION from school will result if the above requirements are not met.
