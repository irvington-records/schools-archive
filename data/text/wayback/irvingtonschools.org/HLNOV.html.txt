HEADLINES NOVEMBER, 1997
PERSON IN THE NEWS: DR. SCOTT MOSENTHAL
At this meeting, Board President Penny Delany and Superintendent Dick Hajek presented an overview of the administrative structure of the Irvington School district; the challenges we face in meeting new State standards, assessments, and graduation requirements; and the key role of the incoming High School principal.
The presentation culminated with Mr. Hajek’s recommendation that Dr. Mosenthal, who has taught in the Irvington school system since 1975 and served as Assistant Principal and chair of the English Department at the High School since 1984, be appointed High School Principal, to begin in July, 1998. Dr. Mosenthal holds a BA from Dartmouth College and both an MA and Ed.D. from Teachers College, Columbia University. In his present position, he is responsible for curriculum and staff development, teacher observation and evaluation, and has extensive student involvement. He holds a wide variety of school-wide and extra curricular responsibilities, most notably as coach for JV and Varsity teams, and as coordinator of the Peer Leadership and Community Service programs. His administrative accomplishments include developing the English Department Handbook, as well as designing and implementing standards and exit goals in grades 6-12.
This timing of Dr. Mosenthal’s appointment is consistent with the overall administrative reorganization plan the Board has developed. It will ensure cohesiveness during the transition process, while allowing the district to begin immediately to implement the new higher State education standards by addressing curriculum and scheduling modifications for the coming school year. It preserves the balance in the administration between the new vision of outside hires and appointments of able candidates from within the system. Given Dr. Mosenthal’s thorough familiarity with the district, staff, students and programs, his appointment as High School principal offers the district a tremendous boon especially as we anticipate a one-year search process for a new Superintendent, whom we hope will be appointed to begin in January, 1999.
There is also a positive financial impact for the district in this appointment. Dr. Mosenthal’s current salary, based on his 22 years experience and numerous team and club commitments, is over $105,000. His salary upon appointment as Principal will be set at $99,000 (slightly below the median for this region). However, as the teacher hired to replace Dr. Mosenthal’s line will be much lower salaried (estimate $45,000) the overall savings to the district will total over $60,000.
At the October 21 meeting, Dr. Mosenthal’s colleagues, as well as parents of his present and former students, offered enthusiastic support. The Board intends to formally vote on the resolution of Dr. Mosenthal’s appointment as High School principal to commence on July 1, 1998, at the November 4 Board of Education meeting.
