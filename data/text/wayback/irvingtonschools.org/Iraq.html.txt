SOURCE http://irvingtonschools.org:80/Iraq.html | DATE 2005-02-14 | TITLE Iraq

Irvington High School Senior Charlene Veras has spearheaded a schoolwide effort to send school supplies to Iraqi schoolchildren in need. "We have so many things that we don't even use, why not help people who need them," said Charlene, who helped get the word out with the help of Irvington H.S. social studies teacher Chris Barry.
Thanks to generous donations from her fellow students, families and staff, the school collected several boxes of paper, pens, chalk, paperclips, pencils and other basic supplies that were recently sent to Iraq. Mr. Barry learned about the program through the website IraqiSchools.com, a site dedicated to helping people gather and send school supplies to the children of Iraq.
The site is run by American soldiers stationed in Iraq -- many of whom stand guard protecting schools from looters when school is not in session, and the children from thugs and criminals when school is in session. "We take so many things for granted in the U.S., like getting an education in a safe setting," said Barry. "In Iraq this is not the norm." "Anything we can do to help, is sure to go a long way."
