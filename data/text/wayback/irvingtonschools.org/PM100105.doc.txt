The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Campus Presentation Room, October 1, 2005, with the following present:

Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mrs. Robyn Kerner. Also present: Dr. Kathleen Matusiak - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Mr. Artie McCormack, Health/Athletic Director, members of the community. Absent: Mrs. Carol Gutman, Mrs. Janice Silverberg.

  I	CALL TO ORDER
The Public Meeting was called to order by Mr. Paul Mandel at 9:10 a.m. and the Pledge of Allegiance was said.

II	INFORMATION FORUM
Mr. Mandel welcomed everyone and stated the purpose of this meeting was to provide the opportunity for members of the community who are unable to make the evening board meetings, to learn and comment on the proposal for artificial turfed fields and other enhancements. Mr. Mandel noted that the discussions on the proposal were to conclude at the October 11th Board of Education Public Meeting, but the Board is considering pushing back the original timeline. The Board will make public any change in the timeline.

Mr. Mandel introduced Mr. Ron Tetelman, President of Eberlain and Eberlain, architectural consultants to the District.  In response to public comment at previous meetings, Mr. Tetelman presented revised drawings of East Field and the proposed tennis courts.  The proposed revisions would eliminate the connection road and push back the tennis courts 15’, thereby doubling the distance from the Fieldpoint property line. The modifications to East Field include using cutouts for the bases and pitching mound and moving it approximately 15’ closer to the existing field.

 At the September 27th Board of Education Meeting, the Board approved the contracts to survey East Field and conduct test borings at East Field and Meszaros Field.  This work will start within the next week or so. The survey will confirm the property line between the District and Fieldpoint.

Residents of Riverview Road and Fieldpoint expressed concerns regarding drainage, lighting, noise and loitering. The Board of Education and the District is cognizant of their neighbors and will continue to listen to the community and work on ways to ameliorate any negative impact that the proposal might produce. 

Members of the community inquired about the impact on the District’s annual budget.  Mr. Mandel stated that the annual budget would include the increase for maintenance, debt service and lighting of the fields.  Currently, the District is paying very little in maintenance and it is hoped that the increase in electric costs will be offset by the reduction in transportation costs. At this time the fields are unsafe for play and very often, unplayable due to weather conditions.  

Mr. Mandel stressed that the Board of Education is committed to providing the students of Irvington with a quality education program and that if the District values a sports and athletics program, then the money spent on the fields and other enhancements, will be worth it.

III	MEETING NOTIFICATION
The next Public Meeting of the Board of Education will be on Tuesday, October 11, 2005 at 7:30 pm in the Campus Presentation Room.

IV     ADJOURNMENT
The meeting was adjourned at 11:05 a.m.
