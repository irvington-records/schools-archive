The Irvington High School Academic Challenge Teams (A) and (B) competed in the National Academic Challenge Team Championships held in New Orleans May 28-30.
Irvington was only one of four school districts to bring two teams; both teams went 3-1 in the round robin and made the playoffs. The A team players are Seth Feinstein, Lee Stein, Eugene Petracca and Sam Abbott. The B team players are Brian Adelman, David Imamura, David Neiditch, Jeff Finkelstein, Zach Feinstein and Jeremy Neuberg.
"This is a wonderful accomplishment for both our teams," said IHS teacher Michele Cella, who coaches the teams. Back in March, IHS's A and B teams finished in the top third and fourth respectively, at the countywide Academic Challenge competition held on March 6, where 26 different teams from area high schools competed against each other.
