The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library, September 24, 2002, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman - Assistant Superintendent Instruction, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mrs. Lauren Allan – Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Gr. K-3  Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Dows Lane Gr. 4-5 Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor - Middle School Assistant Principal, Mrs. Susan Levey – Director Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar – Director of Technology, George Kuntz - Superintendent of Buildings and Grounds, Michael Orifici - JMOA Engineering, P.C., students and members of the community.  

I.  	CALL TO ORDER

The Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Janice Silverberg at 8:08 p.m.  

II          BUSINESS ITEMS

Construction Bids
A motion was made by Mrs. Penny Delany, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to adopt the following Resolution:

“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District hereby rescind the award for the General Construction Bid-Contract No. HSR1 to Piazza Brother’s Inc. which was awarded July 23, 2002,

AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District authorize the re-bidding of the General Construction Bid-Contract No. HSR1."
       Vote:  5-0 Approved

III	ADJOURNMENT

Mrs. Diane Mundree motioned to adjourn at 8:10 p.m., seconded by  Mr. George Huzinec.
  	Vote:  5 - 0 Approved
