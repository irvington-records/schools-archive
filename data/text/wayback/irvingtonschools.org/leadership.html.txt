SOURCE http://irvingtonschools.org:80/leadership.html | DATE 2003-08-21 | TITLE Principal's Leadership Award

Irvington High School senior Ashley Mandel was selected this month by Irvington High School Principal Dr. Scott Mosenthal to receive the "Herff Jones Principal's Leadership Award" for her volunteer work at Children's Village in Dobbs Ferry.
Ashley, who has worked as a volunteer tutor to students at Children's Village for the last two years, is now the "coordinator of student volunteers." She is responsible for coordinating the schedules of over 50 Irvington High School students (freshmen-seniors), who volunteer after school and on weekends at the school for emotionally troubled youths. Ashley and the other volunteers spend time helping the children with homework, reading to them and playing games like checkers and chess.
Ashley said this Christmas, students at Irvington High School are collecting money to buy "Old Navy" fleeces for the students they tutor at Children's Village, who come from economically disadvantaged backgrounds. "It makes us feel good to do something special for the kids, who are so appreciative of any gifts they get," said Ashley, who is also a member of Irvington High School's Peer Leadership Program.
Dr. Mosenthal praised Ashley saying, "She and the others who give their time to these children are showing how much they care about those less fortunate. We are very proud of them."
