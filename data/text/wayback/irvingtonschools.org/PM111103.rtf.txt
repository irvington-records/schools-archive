The following are the minutes of the Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington District Office Conference Room, November 11, 2003, with the following present:


Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mr. George Huzinec.  Also present:  Ms. Jackie Roy, J.A. Roy Associates

I	CALL TO ORDER
	The Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Penny Delany at 7:00 p.m.

	A motion was made by Mrs. Janice Silverberg, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to adjourn to Executive Session for the purpose of discussing the employment history of a particular person(s). 
	Vote:  5-0 Approved

The Public Meeting reconvened at 9:00 p.m. 

II       	ADJOURNMENT

	A motion to adjourn was made at 9:00 p.m. by Mrs. Diane Mundree, seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel.
	Vote:  5-0 Approved
