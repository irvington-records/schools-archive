V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

  Superintendent’s Report  
Enrollment Report:  Dr. Vincent Beni
Dr. Beni reported that, using the October BEDS figures, the enrollment is 2,044.  There are no aberrations by grade.  The District continues to use the demographer’s report and the enrollment continues to reflect the mid-range projections.

Irvington High School Update:  Dr. Scott Mosenthal
Dr. Mosenthal presented an overview of the High School operation on the new shared campus. Facilities shared with the Middle School include the cafeteria, library, fields and gym, art, music and science rooms.  This sharing is feasible due to the similar schedules of the High School and Middle School. 

The High School enrollment is 523 with an additional 19 students attending the Magnet School.

New programs include a 3 year science research program, English Grade 11/12 Advanced Placement (3 sections), Advanced Placement Physics, Astronomy, college level French, enhanced/upgraded AIS 


program, Forensics and Model UN.  Activities new this year include J.V. Football, SADD, Gay/Straight Alliance and Senior Mentorship (41 senior class members working with 9th graders).

Items being scheduled for future discussion are:
Arrival/dismissal scheduling to ease traffic congestion.
Efficient use of the Cafeteria.
Shared programs and staff
Mentoring of Middle schoolers by 12th graders.
Adding more elective classes.  
Models for an Alternative Program.

Mrs. Delany thanked Dr. Mosenthal for his report.

Auditor’s Report:  Bennett Kielson, Storch, DeSantis & Company LLP
Mr. Les Storch briefly reviewed the Financial Statements and Supplementary Information with Independent Auditors’ Report for year ended June 30, 2003.  New this year is the requirement of two (2) different types of financial reporting statements.  Mr. Storch reported that the District is in excellent financial condition. 

Mr. Reese thanked Mr. Storch and Susan Friend for their work and efforts on this audit 

Mrs. Delany thanked Mr. Storch for his presentation.  

A motion was made by Mr. George Huzinec, seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel, to adopt the following Resolution:

“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District accept the Independent Auditors Report, performed by Bennett, Kielson, Storch, DeSantis & Company LLP, The Government Services Division of O’Connor Davies Munns & Dobbins, LLP, for the 2002-2003 school year.”
Vote:  5-0 Approved
