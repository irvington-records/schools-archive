Last year's Irvington High School "Yearbook" honoring the Class of 2002, earned second place in its class in the American Scholastic Press Association's "Annual Review and Contest Awards."
This is the first time a yearbook at Irvington High School earned this prestigious award. Yearbook teacher/advisors Francine DeDonato and Karen Palmer who were thrilled with the award, said "it goes to several hardworking senior students who worked on the yearbook for weeks and months last year. "
"These students were so dedicated and this award rewards them for their hard work and commitment," Ms. DeDonato added.
