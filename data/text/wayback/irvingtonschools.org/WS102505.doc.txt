The following are the minutes of the Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Campus Presentation Room, October 25, 2005, with the following present:

Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Carol Gutman, Mrs. Robyn Kerner. The Work Session was called to order by Mr. Paul Mandel at 7:32 p.m., and the Pledge of Allegiance was said.

Mr. Paul Mandel asked for a moment of silence for Mrs. Linda McMullan, retired District Clerk, who passed away on October 18th. Mr. Mandel said that Ms. McMullen was a terrific person and all that knew and worked with her will miss her.

Mr. Paul Mandel thanked Ms.Coddington for the Proclmation on behalf of the Board of Education.

Mr. Mandel welcomed the new teachers and thanked them for their hard work.

Student Performance Results  (Supplemental Log-Appendix A)

Dr. Ralph Napolitano presented the 2004-2005 Student Performance Results and congratulated administration and staff for their efforts on behalf of Irvington students. Dr. Napolitano reviewed the results and presented multi-year performance results showing trends in Advanced Placement, Regents, ELA, mathematics, science and social studies at the elementary and middle school assessments. Overall, the 2004-2005 results and trends are most impressive with mastery being achieved in many areas.

The elementary and middle school assessments results remain strong with significant improvement in eighth grade social studies as well as science and social students at the elementary level. Of note is 5th grade social studies where 99% of the students achieved mastery. At the middle and high school levels, the results are equally impressive. 

The results will help guide professional development and guide curriculum development. Teachers are receiving training in data analysis, which enables them to identify error patterns and remediate instruction more effectively to target interventions in some specific content areas.  

Mr. Paul Mandel thanked Dr. Napolitano for an excellent presentation.

Certiorari Settlement
A motion was made by Mrs. Carol Gutman, seconded by Mrs. Robyn Kerner, to approve the following Certiorari Settlement as presented.
Vote: 4:0 Approved.

WHEREAS, law suits have been commenced to challenge the assessment on the property owned by American Booksellers/DP 18 LLC, located at 828 South Broadway in the Town of Greenburgh and identified on the tax map as Section 3, Block 29, Lot P47 for assessment years 2001 through 2005; and

WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District, through its attorney, appeared in the pending litigation to protect the interests of the School District; and

WHEREAS, several meetings were held with the attorney for the petitioner, the Deputy Town Attorney for the Town of Greenburgh and counsel for the School District and various settlement proposals were discussed at length prior to arriving at a tentative settlement; and

WHEREAS, the School District’s counsel, the Town of Greenberg Assessor and Deputy Town Attorney all recommend approving the tentative settlement; and

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that it is in the best interest of the School District to settlement this lawsuit with the following reductions in assessed values:

		Year		Original AV		New AV	Reduction
		2001		   $480,250		$480,250	      -0-
		2002		   $480,250		$293,800	$186,450

		2004		   $480,250		$209,300	$270,950
		2005 		   $480,250		$209,300	$270,950
