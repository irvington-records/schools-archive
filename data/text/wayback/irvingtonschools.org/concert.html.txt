Irvington students and their families contributed close to $5,000 at a benefit concert held on January 9th at Irvington High School for the victims of the September 11 terrorist attack entitled, "United Forever: A Tribute to Freedom."
Irvington High School's instrumental and vocal groups under the direction of music teachers Larry Curio and Mary McCarthy performed several inspirational songs and other students performed duets. Student Council President Garett Wenig, hosted the event which filled the auditorium.
Proceeds will go to benefit "Bobby's Buddies," a charitable fund set up in honor of Irvington resident Robert Speisman, the 48-year-old father of three, who perished in the American Airlines Flight 77 after it was hijacked by terrorists who crashed the aircraft into the Pentagon.
Speisman's oldest daughter, Tara, 18, graduated from Irvington High School last year, where she was the valedictorian. She attends Georgetown University. His other two daughters are Haley, 10 and Brittany, 16, are students in the district. Mr. Speisman's widow, Rena, who organized "Bobby's Buddies" will determine how to allocate the funds.
Principal Scott Mosenthal said the concert also honored other former Irvington residents and victims of the Sept. 11th attack: former students Michael Collins and Michael Bane; former village resident Tim Stout, who lived in Irvington for 10 years, before moving to Dobby Ferry less than a year ago and Edward "Ted" Hennessy Jr., the husband of 1982 IHS graduate Melanie Salisbury, who all perished on Sept. 11. "The concert was a wonderful way to honor those whose lives were lost," said Mosenthal. "It was also a way to bring the community together in celebration of what these men represented."
For those who would like to make a donation, send them to "Bobby's Buddies" at P.O. Box 78, Irvington, N.Y. 10533.
