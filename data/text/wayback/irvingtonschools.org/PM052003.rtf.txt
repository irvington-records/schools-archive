III	INFORMATION ITEMS

	Dr. Beni reported on the following:
Reminded everyone of the Budget Vote on June 3, 2003 at the Irvington Middle School from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.
The State budget has been passed and therefore the proposed cuts in funding will not occur.  However,  funding has not been restored to current levels.
Acknowledgement of Mr. Reese’s efforts to obtain a $4,000.00 reimbursement from FEMA for expenses incurred in the clean-up of the February snow storms.  
On May 28, 2003 the Community Advisory Board will host a forum in the IHS Gym to discuss the results of the recent survey of Irvington teenagers. 
If the purchase of the land currently owned by the Unification Church is approved, the cost to a homeowner with a home assessment of $20,000 would be $75.00 per year.

Senior Citizen Tax Exemption
A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to adopt the following Resolution:

“Resolved, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, Town of Greenburgh, Westchester County, New York, adopt the Partial Tax Exemption for Senior Citizens as amended by recent legislative action.  The legislation provides a graduated maximum income exemption eligibility level of $29,899.99 for the granting of partial exemption from school district taxes to senior citizens.”
Vote:  5-0 Approved

Gifts and Donations
Barbara W. Ray
Dr. Beni informed the Board that a piano has been given to the District as a result of the settling of the estate of Mrs. Barbara W. Ray, a former long-time resident of Irvington.  If accepted by the Board, the piano will be kept in an appropriate storage facility pending the completion of the new Auditorium on the High School/Middle School campus.   

A motion was made by Mr. George Huzinec, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to accept the gift of a Steinway grand piano donated by Mrs. Barbara W. Ray.
Vote:  5-0 Approved
