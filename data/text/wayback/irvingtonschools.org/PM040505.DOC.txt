V	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

 Master Plan Expenditures Report (Supplemental Log – Appendix A)

Mrs. Delany introduced  Mr. Reese, Assistant Superintendent for Business for a review of the Master Plan funding and expenditures.  Mr. Reese presented a summary by contractor of the base bid and final contract amount including alternates, if any.  Following a lengthy discussion of change orders submitted while the construction was in progress, Mr. Reese stated that change orders approximating 5% of the overall cost is the industry standard.  A summary of contracts and cost by building was presented with the explanation of how the State Education Department summarized the entire project for approval and reporting purposes.  Mr. Reese also reviewed the SED regulations regarding the bidding and acceptance process in addition to the impact of the Wicks Law pointing out that there are major differences between school construction regulations and private construction.    The funding for the Master Plan began in 1999-2000 with the major portion being the February 2000 bond approval by the voters.

Mrs. Delany thanked Mr. Reese for his presentation and opened the discussion up to the audience.  A lengthy question and answer period followed.    
   
Alternative High School Program Update

Mr. Mosenthal and Mr. DeGennaro made a visit to the Magnet School to look at the program in depth to be able to assist students with the transition to Irvington High School.  They examined a typical student day in the current Magnet School setting to help in the planning for next year.  There was explanation and discussion of independent studies, internships and/or community service options.

Mrs. Delany thanked them for giving the Board a better prospective of the program to assist in their deliberations.
 
2005-06 Budget Discussion

Mrs. Delany reviewed the 2005-06budget process which started in the Fall of 2004.  The Board has listened to the community and has asked Dr. Matusiak to revisit and discuss each reduction with the appropriate staff.  The total budget is $41,975,779 resulting in a tax increase of 15.4%.  Mr. Reese reviewed the pie charts of “where the money goes” including salaries, benefits and unfunded mandates.  A contingency budget would have to be adopted after the rejection of the one (1) revote that is permitted.  This contingency budget would restrict what you can spend on specific budget items.   

The Board members discussed specific items in the budget, in particular the issue of the number of sections in the fourth and fifth grades at Dows Lane.  Four of the Board members are in favor of adding back (no reduction) a section in the 4th and 5th grade feeling that, in part, testing and transitional issues present the need for individual and group assistance in these grades.  Mrs. Delany expressed the opinion that 24/25 students is not an unacceptable number of students per class and would not be detrimental to the students.  The Board also commented on the resources required to continue to send students to the Magnet school rather than offering the program in-house.  

Discussion and questions followed.  Many resident expressed the opinion that the aide to the Nurse at Dows Lane should be provided for in the budget as well as an additional section to the 4th and 5th grades.  There were also questions regarding the direction and focus on Technology.  Dr. Matusiak responded that this will be reported on at a meeting in the near future.  Mrs. Delany thanked the audience and stated that the discussion will continue.
