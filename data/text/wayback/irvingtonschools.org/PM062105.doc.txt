PUBLIC WORK SESSION                                         June 21, 2005                                      

The following are the minutes of the Public Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington Campus Presentation Room, June 21, 2005, with the following present:

	Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Carol Gutman, 
	Mr. Paul Mandel.  Also present: Dr. Kathleen Matusiak-Superintendent of Schools, Mr. 	
	James Reese-Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Ralph Napolitano-Assistant
Superintendent for Instruction, Ms. Eileen McGuire-Dows Lane Elementary School Assistant Principal, Dr. Scott Mosenthal-High School Principal, Ms. Raina Kor-Middle School Assistant Principal, Dr. Joel Klein-Interim PPS Director.

I.	CALL TO ORDER

The Public Work Session was called to order to by Mrs. Penny Delany at 7:30 p.m. and the Pledge of Allegiance was said.

      MINUTES OF MEETINGS - None

III         INFORMATION ITEMS

Tribute to Penny Delany-Paul Mandel stated that Penny has worked long and hard for the community and our students.  Penny has seen the District through tremendous change in administration, teachers, building projects and New York State mandates.

Dr. Matusiak awarded Penny a “Shining Star” certificate highlighting her tremendous contribution to Irvington Schools.  Penny also received recognition from the Northern Westchester School Board Association and a proclamation from the Irvington School Community.

Diane, Janice and Carol also praised Penny for modeling poise, politeness and generosity.  She has set a positive tone for Board and community relations.

2005-2008- Strategic Plan Presentation

Dr. Matusiak thanked everyone who participated in the process.
An explanation of the process was provided, followed by an overview of each of the five goals and objectives in the Strategic Plan.  One or both of the Action Team facilitators explained this information in a PowerPoint presentation (attached).
The five goals are as follows:

To develop a consistent and effective K-12 ELA Program to improve  
student achievement, using the Tri-State report considerations;

To meet the specific needs of each student, K-12, and to ensure the optimal success of all students;

3.   To promote character education, good citizenship and sense of         
       community;

To ensure the implementation of the 2005-08 District Technology Plan;

5.    To ensure that the District’s buildings and grounds are kept safe,     
                                           clean, code compliant, and conducive to teaching and learning.

IV.	PROGRAM AND POLICY ITEMS

First reading on revised Facilities Use Policy (3280); revisions include:
proposed fee structure;
clarification regarding who can use the facilities and when;
guidelines for modified use of auditorium and fitness center. 
                
 	        There will be one more review of policy.

Questions were raised regarding facilities policy:

Will Girl Scouts have to pay for a classroom?
Will the policy preclude any agreement between the Village and school district?
Will we charge the Recreation Center, P.T.S.A. or Continuing Education program?
Does the fee schedule apply to people we did not charge before?

All questions were answered affirmatively.

Raina Kor explained how each school will begin working on a one year School Action Plan to align to the District’s three year Strategic Plan.

Dr. Matusiak explained that status reports will be presented to the Board on a semi-annual basis.  The Board congratulated Dr. Matusiak and the facilitators.  They found the plan to be very readable and systematic.  Mr. Mandel called the plan “brilliant”.

IV.	COMMITTEE REPORTS

	A.  Curriculum Committee – Members recently reviewed and evaluated their year.
	
B.  Buildings & Grounds – Items discussed included HVAC issues, muddy conditions    
      that need to be addressed on secondary campus, MSS playground, HS drive and Fritz             
      tile installation.

C.  Policy Committee – The process is going very well. The Board was recently provided 
      with a grid, indicating the status of all policies that have been approved, are pending,  
      or that need consideration in 2005-06.      

Mrs. Diane Mundree adjourned the Public Work Session at 9:30 pm.

PUBLIC MEETING                                      June 21, 2005

School Lunch Committee Minutes

The resident’s complaint about rodents was followed by a Westchester County Board of Health inspection.  There was no evidence of rodents.  School district was commended for maintenance of area. 

Review refrigeration at MSS – not a good situation due to lack of refrigeration.

   Cheerleading Team Proposal 

    Dr. Matusiak asked for a motion to place a proposal for a cheerleading team on agenda. Mr. Artie McCormack, Athletic Director, addressed the Board, stating that a parent requested consideration of this team at no cost to the District.

Mrs. Diane Mundree made a motion to approve, seconded by Mrs. Carol Gutman. 
Vote: 5-0 approved

               Following a lengthy discussion, the Board decided that, if approved, the team
should be co-ed and diverse.  That is, it should cheer for various girls’ and       boys’ sport teams at home games.

Mrs. Diane Mundree made a motion to approve Varsity Cheerleading as a sport at no cost to the District, co-education in composition with rotation of cheerleaders at only home games, seconded by Mrs. Carol Gutman.

Vote: 5-0 approved
