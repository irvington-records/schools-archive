The following are the minutes of the Special Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Conference Room, June 10, 2003, with the following present:


Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business. 

I	CALL TO ORDER 

	The Special Public Meeting was called to order by Mr. Paul Mandel at 6:57 p.m. 

II	CONSTRUCTION BIDS
	A.  Roofing and Masonry Restoration Bid
A motion was made by Mr. George Huzinec, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree, to adopt the following Resolution:  

“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the Roofing and Masonry Restoration Bid - Contract No. MS1 to Sea Breeze GC as the lowest responsive and responsible bidder with a base bid price of $628,000.”
Vote:  3-0 Approved
	
III       	ADJOURNMENT

	A motion to adjourn was made by Mr. George Huzinec, seconded by Mrs. Diane Mundree.	
	Vote:  3-0 Approved
