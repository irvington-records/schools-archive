The following are the minutes of the Public Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library, January 21, 2003, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg,  Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman – Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mrs. Lauren Allan – Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Gr. K-3 Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Dows Lane Gr. 4-5 Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Mrs. Susan Levey – Director Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar – Director of Technology, Mr. Scott Baird – JMOA Engineering, P.C. and members of the community. 

The Public Work Session was called to order by Mrs. Janice Silverberg at 7:47 p.m.
   
I             MASTER PLAN UPDATE - (Supplemental Log – Appendix A)

Mr. Baird of JMOA Engineering P.C. began this update presentation by commenting on the cold, windy weather that we are experiencing.  The project is moving at a slower pace due to the additional steps required by the temperature and the work crews being forced to proceed at a slower pace.  Warming stations are available throughout the site.  

In spite of the cold weather problems, the project as a whole is on or ahead of scheduled milestone dates.  The Cafeteria/Music/Science building is one (1) month ahead of schedule, the Middle School is two (2) months ahead and the Theatre/Gym building is due for completion in November 2003.    

Mr. Baird reviewed the status of the construction budget and reported that several of the final contracts are out for bid with award scheduled for late February. 

Dr. Beni pointed out that, in addition to the construction project, several other issues are being addressed such as the Main Street School roof repair, windows at Dows Lane and the Main Street School and the athletic fields which are subjected to extraordinary usage.  These projects are being discussed while keeping the focus on classroom space needed for September 2003.

Mr. Huzinec complimented the staff that was creatively responsible for the employment opportunity ad that appeared in the New York Times this past weekend.  

Mr. Mandel commented on several articles that appeared in the last issue of “On Board”.  Among articles that Mr. Mandel mentioned reported on the difficulty with state funding, the Governor’s veto of the removal of the District Superintendents’ salary cap and Grade 3 through 8 mandated testing.   

II            Technology Update (Supplemental Log – Appendix B)

Ms. Rajwar reviewed the District priorities:
Ongoing funding for technology
Continual evaluation of the technology plan
Staff development
Curriculum integration
Technical support
Access

Technology planning is underway for the new buildings on the Middle School/High School campus as well as for the renovated High School building.  

Following questions and discussion, Mrs. Silverberg thanked Ms. Rajwar for her presentation.
  





III   	Automated External Defibrillators Demonstration

	Mr. Reese reviewed the status of the Districts’ compliance with the mandate to provide an AED in each school building,  This is an unfunded state mandate with a cost to the District this year of approximately $21,000.00.

	Mr. DeGennaro reported that selected staff members have been certified in the operation of the AED and CPR and will require re-certification annually.  In addition, there are regularly scheduled inspections for the equipment.  Mr. DeGennaro explained the operation of the AED in the absence of the person scheduled to present the demonstration.     

Mr. George Huzinec motioned to adjourn to Executive Session for the purpose of discussing the employment history of a particular person(s) at 9:17 p.m., seconded by Mr. Paul Mandel.
Vote:  5 - 0 Approved

The Public Meeting was reconvened from Executive Session by Mrs. Silverberg at 10:03 p.m.

At 10:03 p.m. a motion was made and seconded to adjourn the Public Meeting.
