Notes: I.H.S Site Committee Meeting January 21, 2005
Present: Janet Williams, Joe DeGennaro, Eden Univer, Rebecca Lazar, Zach Feinstein, Deb Hardy, Tristan Naumann, David Imamura, Scott Mosenthal
Minutes from the last meeting were distributed and reviewed.
Zach reported that students are no longer being kept past the bell in music.
There have been problems with the heat; those problems are being addressed daily.
The midterm schedule is posted on the web and being mailed home this weekend. Copies have been posted in classrooms.
The students on the committee spoke out against the idea of posting the honor and high honor roll lists. Nonetheless, the lists will be posted since parents have requested it. The students spoke of surveying the student body about this idea and presenting their findings to the PTSA.
A question came up about publicity for student achievements. Scott noted that achievements are posted on the district website; he also sends that information to the district public relations person (Deb Kaiser). He will check to see if she forwards that information to the local newspapers.
There are plans for the Academic Challenge Teams to take on community volunteers in a fundraising effort later in the spring.
Next meeting: February 15 3:30 p.m. room 111. Refreshments: Janet. Notes: Janet.
