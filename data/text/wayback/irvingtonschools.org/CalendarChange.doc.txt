CORRECTION TO OUR PREVIOUS ANNOUNCEMENT



November 9, 2005




Dear Parents,

As you know, this school year, New York State assessments will be given in English Language Arts and mathematics in all grades from three to eight.  Due to the introduction of all of these new assessments, it is necessary to provide our teachers with appropriate scoring time to meet the deadlines established by the New York State Education Department.

On the District calendar that you received, the students were scheduled to have a half-day on January 12th.  This will now be a regular full day of school.  Instead, our Superintendent’s Conference Day will be changed to February 3rd.  There will be no school on that day.  These changes will enable our teachers the opportunity to grade all of the assessments in time to meet the State deadlines.

Similarly, we must also make an adjustment to our March schedule.  On the District calendar, the students were scheduled to have a half-day of school on March 16th.  This will now be a regular full day of school.  Instead, the Superintendent’s Conference Day will be changed to March 27th to give the teachers an opportunity to grade the assessments.  The children will have no school on March 27th.

As always, I thank you very much for your cooperation in this matter.

Sincerely,
