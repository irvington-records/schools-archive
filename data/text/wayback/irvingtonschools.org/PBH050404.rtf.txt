The following are the minutes of the Public Budget Hearing  of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington Campus Presentation Room, May 4, 2004, with the following present:

Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mr. George Huzinec.  Also present: Dr. Vincent T. Beni – Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman - Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Ms. Raina Kor - Middle School Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Main Street School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Elementary School Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Mr. Donald Kuhn – Main Street School Assistant Principal, Ms. Eileen McGuire – Dows Lane Elementary School Assistant Principal, members of the faculty and community. 

I.  	CALL TO ORDER
	
The Public Budget Hearing was called to order at 10:03 p.m. by Mrs. Penny Delany.
	
II	2004-2005 Irvington Union Free School District Budget

Dr. Beni asked if anyone had any comments or questions on the proposed 2004-2005 school budget.  

Mr. Mandel complimented the administration and staff for providing the district with a reasonable budget that 	also adds program content.

III	ADJOURNMENT

There being no further discussion, Mrs. Janice Silverberg motioned to adjourn at 10:06 p.m., seconded by Mr. George Huzinec.
  	Vote:  5 - 0 Approved
