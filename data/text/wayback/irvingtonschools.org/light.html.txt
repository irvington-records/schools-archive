IRVINGTON BOARD OF EDUCATION
Press Release: Traffic Signal to be Installed at High School Drive
	
	Superintendent of Schools Dr. Vincent Beni and Irvington Mayor Dennis Flood announced today that the New York State Department of Transportation (NYSDOT) has granted the Village of Irvington and the Irvington School District permission to install a traffic signal at the intersection of High School Drive and Route 9 (Broadway).
"We are very pleased the New York State Department of Transportation shares our concern for the safety and well-being of our students, parents, and staff," said Dr. Beni.  "The high school population is expected to double by 2005.  The installation of a traffic signal at this intersection will be a major improvement."
Mayor Dennis Flood shared Dr. Beni's enthusiasm upon learning of the NYSDOT decision.  "The Village of Irvington is very pleased with the Department of Transportation approval.  My staff will cooperate fully with the school district and the State of New York during the planning, bidding, and construction of this project."
Assistant Superintendent of Schools, Donald P. Marra, contacted the Department of Transportation last March to request a meeting to discuss the need for a traffic signal at the Broadway/Route 9 intersection.  Mr. Marra praised village resident and traffic expert, John Canning, for volunteering to meet with him and Department of Transportation representatives at a meeting in Irvington in April.  "John Canning's statistical analysis of the intersection and his professional expertise were extremely helpful in making a case for a traffic signal at the high school intersection," stated Marra.  Irvington Police Chief Richard Denike was also instrumental in providing information and support to the NYSDOT.
New York State approval was necessary because Route 9 is a state road.  The NYSDOT cannot legally install the signal due to the fact that High School Drive is not a public road.  The approval is granted either to the school or the village under a highway work permit.  Mr. Marra and Superintendent of Public Works, Mr. Robert Bauer, are in the process of designing a Request for Proposal (RFP) for the services of a traffic consultant engineer.  The consultant will design a system, file the proper documents with the State, write bids and specifications, review contractor bids, and supervise construction.
