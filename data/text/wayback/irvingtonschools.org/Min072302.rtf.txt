The following are the minutes of the Special Public Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the District Office Conference Room, July 23, 2002, with the following present:



Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mr. George Huzinec, Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman - Assistant Superintendent for Instruction. 


I	CALL TO ORDER 

	The Special Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Janice Silverberg at 9:35 p.m. 

II	BUSINESS ITEMS
	A.  CONSTRUCTION BIDS
	1.  General Construction
A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany, to adopt the following Resolution:  
“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the General Construction Bid-Contract No. HSR1 to Piazza Brother’s Inc. as the lowest responsive and responsible bidder with a base bid price of $4,487,000.00 plus bid alternates HSR1-A, B, C, D, E, F and G, in the net amount of $42,000, for a total bid award of $4,529,000.”
2.   Mechanical 
A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany, to adopt the following Resolution:  
“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the Mechanical Bid-Contract No. HSR2-Alternates A, B, C and D in the net amount of  $154,400 to Capstone Enterprises.”
Vote:  5-0 Approved
3.  Plumbing
A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany, to adopt the following Resolution:  
“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the Plumbing Bid-Contract No. HSR3-Alternates A, B and C in the net amount of  $100,000 to Beneway Inc.”
Vote:  5-0 Approved
4.  Electrical
A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany, to adopt the following Resolution:  
“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the Electrical Bid-Contract No. HSR4 Alternates A, B, C and D in the net amount of  $141,560 to Naber Electric.”
Vote:  5-0 Approved
5.  Asbestos Abatement
A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany, to adopt the following Resolution:  
“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the Asbestos Abatement Bid-Contract No. HSR5-Alternate A in the amount of  $53,960 to NSC Abatement Services.”
Vote:  5-0 Approved
6.  Windows
A motion was made by Mr. Paul Mandel, seconded by Mrs. Penny Delany, to adopt the following Resolution:  
“RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District award the Windows Bid-Contract No. HSNR 3 to Arrow Steel Window as the lowest responsive and responsible bidder with a base bid price of $3,041,400.”
Vote:  5-0 Approved
