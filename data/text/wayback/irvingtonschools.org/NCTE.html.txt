Irvington High School senior Elizabeth Boeckleman was named one of 665 outstanding writers in this year's National Council of Teachers of English (NCTE) Achievement Awards in Writing.
Ms. Boeckleman was selected from among more than 3,000 students who were nominated by English teachers across the country.
The 2001 NCTE Achievement Awards in Writing recognizes writing excellence based on the students' samples of their best prose or verse, and on impromptu themes that are written under supervision.
The NCTE writes that, "Elizabeth and other other winners are among the best student writers in the country."
