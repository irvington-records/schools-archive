The Honorable George E. Pataki
The Executive Chamber
State Capitol
Albany, NY  12224

March 12, 2003

Dear Governor Pataki

The proposed reductions to aid for public education in your budget are unacceptable to the residents of the Irvington Union Free School District.  Over the past seven years, our district has experienced the largest percentage growth in enrollment of any district between New York City and Albany – 51% or 658 students.  Using your proposal, aid to public education in Irvington during that same period will have increased by only $618,000.  These figures hardly approach the annual costs related to our growth including:  a new school, teachers, staff, principals, supplies, textbooks and technology.  Your proposed reduction of 12.6% in aid to Irvington is harmful to our children and community.

Instead, we hope that you will look carefully during this difficult financial period to a variety of sources to address the State’s fiscal problems.  These should include:

A review of broad-based sources of revenue which are less regressive than the property tax.
Minimal short term borrowing.
Efficiency in State operations.
No additional unfunded mandates.

Irvington residents have supported their schools with increases in the local property tax to match our enrollment growth.  If your budget were enacted, there would be an even greater reliance on local property taxes.  We are very concerned that we might lose (include some program or activity which you and/or your child value).

Please reconsider your proposed budget and replace it with one which assists districts like ours which have seen tremendous growth in the population as well as tremendous improvement of student performance on all State assessments.
