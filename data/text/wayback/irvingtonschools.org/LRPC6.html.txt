Review of District Options
The Long Range Planning Committee reviewed the entire range of options which would be available to the district for solving its space needs. By brainstorming the wide variety of potential responses to our student population, and then analyzing the implications of each, the committee hopes to assist the Board of Education in determining which option is the most likely to solve our space challenge in a way which:
- Preserves and enhances our educational excellence.
- Responds to the challenge with a district-wide focus.
- Provides for growth in a cost-effective manner.
- Solves the problem for many years to come.
- Allows the District flexibility to meet future challenges.
Below is an outline of the options which were analyzed by the committee, followed by a list of pros and cons for each.
Option I. Reconfigure Grades
- Move 8th Grade to High School (K-5, 6-7, 8-12)
- Move 5th Grade to Middle School and 8th Grade to High School (K-4, 5-7, 8-12)
- Kindergarten moved to High School or other location
Option II. Temporary Measures
- Buy more modulars
- Larger class size in Dows Lane
- Eliminate full-day kindergarten
- Program modification:
- Art/Music on wheels at Dows Lane
- Cutting Program at Middle School (Shop/Home Economics/Museum/Music Lab)
Option III. Reconfigure Administrative Offices
- Move Superintendent’s Office, Business Office, and CSE Office out of High School to modulars or to another location
Option IV. Scheduling
- Split sessions at Middle School and High School
- Run District year-round
Option V. Expansion/Renovation
- Build a second level over Middle School gym
- Add a floor to the High School
- Add a floor to the Middle School
- Add a third floor to Dows Lane
Option VI. Build a School
- Build a new 6th to 8th Grade Middle School next to the High School: share High School gym/auditorium and build a new library-multi-media center connecting building. Convert High School Library into additional classrooms. Downsize Dows Lane to be K-3; Convert Main Street School into 4th to 5th grade building
- Build a 4th to 5th grade annex on the site of the Dows Lane basketball courts
- Build a gym on Middle School field and a 4th to 5th grade annex on the site of Middle School gym or a 4th to 5th grade annex on the site of the Middle School field
- Buy land to build a new grade 6 to 8 Middle School, convert Main Street school into 4 to 5 grade annex and downsize Dows Lane to K to 3
