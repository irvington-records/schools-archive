SOURCE http://irvingtonschools.org:80/Celebrate.html | DATE 2005-02-14 | TITLE Celebrate Our Youth Day

The Irvington Community Advisory Board (CAB), which is comprised of seventy-five members ranging from school and village officials, parents, clergy, senior citizens, police department members, students from the Irvington Middle and High Schools, Immaculate Conception School, and representatives from many other organizations, including the O'Hara Foundation, is sponsoring a "Celebrate Our Youth Day" for the entire community on Saturday, May 8, from 2 PM to 10 PM.
This will be an all day event at Scenic Hudson Park bringing together our entire community. Special events, carnival booths, and friendly competition will dominate the day. Student performances will be scheduled during the entire day and evening.
CAB Chairperson and Village Trustee Isabel Milano commented, "This year's primary function of the CAB has been to focus on getting our kids more actively involved in the community as well as giving them a greater sense of value and purpose. This is being accomplished by having our CAB student representatives involved in all aspects of potential event decision making. The adult members of the CAB are very much aware of the fact that if we don't have the interest, commitment and involvement of our kids the chances for success are small."
"Celebrate Our Youth Day" is designed to focus on the positive things our youth bring to our community. Irvington youth volunteers are not only participating in this event but they are also actively organizing the day's events. Proceeds from the student run carnival booths will go directly to the Middle School and High School Class Activity Fund and sponsorships will help fund Community Advisory Board youth sponsored activities and programs.
Please plan on attending and participating in this wonderful event. A sign-up sheet for those interested in participating in a field event is attached. Complete the form and forward it to Athletic Director Artie McCormack at amccorma@irvingtonschools.k12.ny.us
