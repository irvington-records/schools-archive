HEADLINES MARCH, 1998
SUPERINTENDENT’S PROPOSED BUDGET FOR 1998/99
At the March 17 School Board meeting, Mr. Hajek presented his proposal for next year’s school budget. This budget emphasizes quality educational programs in the context of an overall financial goal of stabilizing the tax rate at a reasonable level. The new State standards, increasing student enrollment, and commitment to a long range technology program for the district were among the most significant factors addressed. The major components of the proposed budget are:
Technology:
*A Technology budget line of $250,000. will be established to ensure ongoing funding of the district’s long-term technology plan. For the coming year, an additional $150,000 will be allocated, allotting a total of $400,000. in year one of the plan for hardware/software, infrastructure, staff training, and a new Technology Coordinator.
At Dows Lane-
* Two classroom teachers and part time specialists will be added. This will result in reasonable class sizes (projected at six sections for grades 3 and 5, and seven sections for grades 1, 2, and 4 with 18 to 22 students per class in grades 1-5). This plan will also ensure that all students in those grades will have art throughout the school year, and extend the string component of the music program. The Kindergarten extended day/full day program with classroom aides will be maintained, with six sections and projected class sizes of 24 or 25. Extra periods of combined music and movement will be added to the Kindergarten program.
*A multi-sensory reading program will be initiated for one section of first grade.
*The Administrative Intern position will be maintained; Practice Teaching Placements will be developed with area colleges.
At the Middle School-
*New staff will be hired for Language/Unified Arts and for Foreign Language. A computer lab assistant will be added, thus providing the necessary supervision to keep the computer lab open for student use throughout the day.
*The math program will be revised to prepare students for the new graduation standards and the course work they will have to master in High School.
At the High School-
*Additional part-time staff will be hired for Social Studies and Foreign Language; other staff will be added for the Computer Lab and for a new Science Research Program.
*The Architectural Design Program will be revised.
For Special Education:
*Additional positions at the High School and Dows Lane.
Physical Education:
*Reinstatement of Freshman Basketball for both girls and boys.
Facilities:
*Dows Lane handicapped ramp, canopy from main building to modulars, repair gym floor, improve outdoor lighting; track repair and resurfacing at the High School.
The total cost of the proposed budget is $22,035,796., representing a tax rate increase of 2.87%.
Public discussion of the proposed budget is scheduled for the April 7, April 21 and May 5 School Board meetings. The budget vote and Board elections will take place on May 19.
