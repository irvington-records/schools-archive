SOURCE http://irvingtonschools.org:80/TrackAward.html | DATE 2005-02-14 | TITLE Top Scholar Athletes

For the third year in a row, the Irvington High School Boys Track and Field team was awarded the top Scholar Athlete team award in New York State . The team was the first out of more than 225 track and field teams in the state with an academic average of 99.4 percent.
That average was the highest of any state champ in the track and field category, according to IHS Athletic Director Arthur McCormack, who praised the team members, who he said, "excell both on and off the athletic playing field." "They are a great group of young men, who make us all proud," added McCormack, who also pointed out that four other IHS sring teams --- baseball, boys tennis, golf and girls track and field, also received NY State Scholar Athlete awards. "We congratulate all our athletes," he added.
The boys track and field team was recognized for their award at the June 8th Board of Education meeting. The team's members include: Brian Clark, Casey DiCesare, Scott DiCesare, Frank Fazzino, Bernard Bluhm, Brett Rogers, Colin Bando, Chris Jupierre, Geoff Campbell, Young Jo Fontaine, Romit Bhattacharya, Tyrik Overton, Andrew Shapiro, Aurelien Thompson, Ben Smail, Matt Mangiacotti, Dan Schade and Mark Lindberg.
In addition, the Varsity Boys Track team was Sectional (3rd consecutive year) and League champs (10th consecutive year). The Varsity Boys Tennis team garnered the league championship as well.
