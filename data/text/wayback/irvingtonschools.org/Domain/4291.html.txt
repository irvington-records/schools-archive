District Goals
Planning Strategically
- Each year, the District administration develops comprehensive plans to support the implementation of the Strategic Plan: Our Vision for Tomorrow. These goals are designed to address the District's Strategic Objectives as outlined in the Strategic Plan. In doing so, the District remains focused on its overarching Mission and Vision. Additionally, the District-level goals are complemented by school and departmental goals that put into operation certain District goals and identify local initiatives. Please take time to review the goals as they are designed to advance students' learning and school experiences.
