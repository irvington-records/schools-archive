School Safety and Security Updates
- 2025-26 District Wide Safety Plan Draft for Public Comment The Irvington UFSD has updated our District Wide and Building Level Safety Plans to include the additional procedures for sudden cardiac arrest notated in Desha's Law. As such, we are re-opening our District Wide Safety Plan for public comment. Please send any comments to Carol.Stein@irvingtonschools.org within the next 30 days. The revised plan will be placed on the Board of Education agenda for re-adoption at the December 2nd meeting. The revised District Wide Safety Plan can be found here, by clicking here. Please see below the Adopted District-Wide Safety Plan for the 2025-26 school year as developed by the District Safety Committee. This plan will remain in effect until the revised plan is adopted by the Board of Education. District Wide Safety Plan The Irvington Board of Education adopted the 2025-26 District-wide Safety & Emergency Management Plan at the July 1, 2025 meeting. Click here to view the 2025-26 District-wide Safety & Emergency Management Plan In partnership with our consultant, Altaris Security, the District developed an overview of its emergency protocols to guide the school community on our practices and to provide tips on what to expect should an emergency arise. Visitor Protocols The Irvington Union Free School District is committed to providing the safest possible environments for our students and staff. To this end, the Board of Education and Administration are dedicated to making enhancements to our facilities and continue to seek opportunities to enhance our emergency readiness. This page shall serve as a portal for safety and security resources. To help ensure the safety of our schools, the District continues to implement Visitor Protocols in an effort to provide a safer environment for students, staff and visitors. We urge visitors to review them carefully before visiting the schools or District offices. We thank all visitors for your cooperation and patience.
