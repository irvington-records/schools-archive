As part of our Character Building Program, we are helping students learn prosocial behaviors such as:
- Managing Impulsivity,
- Practicing Self-Reliance,
- Showing Respect and Kindness
- Ownership
- Cooperating
These are important Habits of Mind that students practice each and every day as part of our Dows Lane community.
