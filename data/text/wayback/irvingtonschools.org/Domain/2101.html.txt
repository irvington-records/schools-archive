Sports Information
- Registration for all interscholastic sports (Varsity, JV and Modified) is completed through our online system, FAMILY ID. All medical and emergency information should be entered and athletes must submit a current physical. The physical MUST be uploaded to the registration for each sport, every sport season. This will help expedite the approval process by the school nurse and medical director. Additional information can be located on the ATHLETIC page.
