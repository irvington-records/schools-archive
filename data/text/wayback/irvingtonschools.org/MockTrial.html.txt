SOURCE http://irvingtonschools.org:80/MockTrial.html | DATE 2005-02-14 | TITLE Mock Trial Win

The Irvington High School Mock Trial Team made it to the third round of the Mock Trial Competition held recently at the Westchester County Court House in White Plains.
The team competed by acting out trials in front of a judge at the Westchester County Court House. Irvington High School's Mock Trial Team is coached by teacher/advisor Erik Weiselberg. Student members include: Romit Bhattachayra, Erica Cooper, Zach Feinstein, Seth Feinstein, Will Forster, Joe Kamien, Amanda McCarthy, David Neiditch, Eric Saar, Alison Siegel, Jordan Suchow, Aurelien Thompson and David Imamura.
"The team has worked incredibly hard in preparing for the event," said Irvington H.S. Social Studies Chair Chris Barry. "The school and the Irvington community share in congratulating the team for its efforts."
