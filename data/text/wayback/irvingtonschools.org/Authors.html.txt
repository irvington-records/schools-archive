SOURCE http://irvingtonschools.org:80/Authors.html | DATE 2003-12-20 | TITLE Dows Lane Student Authors

Six Dows Lane Upper School students will soon be published authors when a book called "Dreamakers: Hopes and Aspirations of Children" comes out later this year. The book, published by Boyds Mills Press, a publisher of fine children's literature, will contain student writing samples selected from 1,000 entries submitted by schools throughout the country.
The Dows Lane students, who answered questions about their dreams for the future, include fifth-graders Anna Rembar, Samantha Duarte, Mary Clair, Emma Thacker and Laura Becker and fourth-grader Melissa Tierney.
Proceeds from the book will go to the Children's Aid Society, a non-profit organization in NYC that helps disadvantaged children.
"We are so proud our students are part of this special project which will also help other children reach their own dreams and goals," said Dows Lane Principal Dr. Lola Garcia.
