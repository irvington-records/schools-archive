The Board of Education is delighted to announce that we will be appointing Dr. Kathleen Matusiak as our new Superintendent of Schools. The official appointment will take place at the Board of Education meeting on Tuesday, March 23, and we invite all of you to join us at 6:30 pm, in the Campus Presentation room, for a reception in Dr. Matusiak's honor prior to the meeting. This appointment is the culmination of a year long process of identifying the strengths and needs of our district, developing a profile of our ideal candidate, and interviewing applicants. We are most appreciative of your thoughtful participation in the preliminary meetings with our consultant; it was essential that we understood your needs throughout the search process.
Dr. Matusiak has taught and served as an administrator at all educational levels. She is presently the Superintendent of the New Fairfield, CT school district, where she has worked for 5 ½ years. Prior to that, she was Assistant Superintendent of Secondary Education and K-12 Curriculum and Instruction in Danbury, CT. She came to that position after four years as the principal of a K-5 school in Watertown, CT. Dr. Matusiak's career includes teaching French and Spanish, and she has served as district wide Director of Language Arts and Reading; she is also a certified guidance counselor.
During her undergraduate years, Dr. Matusiak attended Simmons College and was awarded a Diplome from the Sorbonne. She holds graduate degrees from the University of Bridgeport, Fairfield University, and Teachers College, Columbia University, from which she received her Ed.D. in 1995.
As well as meeting our district's ideal of being a life long learner, Dr. Matusiak's experience meets the profile prepared by our district on every point. She has extensive experience in curriculum development, is a strong fiscal manager, has worked with a dual campus model, and is consistently described as a superb team builder and a tireless leader of great warmth and personal integrity. She grasps the essential importance of hiring, retaining, training and supporting her staff.
It speaks to the stature of our district that we received over 40 applications from very well-qualified educators for the position of Superintendent of Schools. Following an initial screening of the applications by our consultant, the Board extensively reviewed ten application packets, and selected six candidates to interview. We were most impressed with the caliber of these candidates, who represented districts in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut. Two candidates went on to a second, and then a third interview, as well as comprehensive reference checks. Dr. Matusiak is simply the best fit for our district, out of an excellent group of candidates.
Dr. Matusiak will immediately begin participating in the selection of our new Middle School principal and Assistant Superintendent for Instruction. As she has built her leadership team in New Fairfield "from scratch", she is eager to take on the challenge of filling these key positions as she begins her Superintendency here in Irvington.
Dr. Matusiak's family includes her husband, Fred, two grown children and two grown stepchildren; they are very enthusiastic about her accepting the position as our Superintendent.
In addition to her superb professional qualifications and extensive experience, Dr. Matusiak brings to the position of Superintendent of Schools enthusiasm, charm and a sense of humor. We look forward to introducing her to the community on March 23; she also plans several visits to the district during the remaining months of the school year. Of course, in September, Dr. Matusiak will schedule a series of meetings with school and community groups.
We anticipate that there will be some opportunity for Dr. Matusiak to visit the district schools this spring; she is eager to meet teachers and staff and begin the transition process. One of Dr. Matusiak's distinct strengths is her recognition of the key role teachers play in achieving true academic excellence.
We hope you will join us in welcoming Dr. Matusiak to our district.
