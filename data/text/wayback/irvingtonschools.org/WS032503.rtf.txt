The following are the minutes of the Public Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library, March 25, 2003, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg,  Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman – Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mrs. Lauren Allan – Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane 
Gr. K-3 Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Dows Lane Gr. 4-5 Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor – Middle School Assistant Principal, Mrs. Susan Levey – Director Pupil Personnel Services, Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar – Director of Technology, faculty and members of the community. 

The Public Work Session was called to order, following the Public Meeting, by Mrs. Janice Silverberg at 7:50 p.m.
   
I             MASTER PLAN UPDATE 

Dr. Beni reviewed  the chronology of the Master Plan from 1999 to the present including photographs of the current construction status on the High School campus.

Dr. Beni also reviewed the April 3, 2003 vote propositions:
Proposition 1 - $3,870,000.00 to provide funding for the Main Street School roof replacement, utilities and upgrades to the existing High School driveway and restructuring of the Master Plan financing to provide for an earlier completion of the projects and to take advantage of the current lower borrowing rates. 
Proposition 2 – reduce the mileage eligibility for busing for Grades K – 5 from .5 miles to .25 miles.  This proposition represents no additional expense to the District.

Dr. Beni reminded everyone that the vote will take place on April 3, 2003 at the Middle School from 7:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m. and that registration information and absentee ballots are available from the District Clerk.

Following the presentation, there was a period of discussion and questions.    
	
At 8:45 p.m., following the Master Plan presentation, the Board suggested a brief break prior to the budget presentation. 

Mrs. Delany arrived at 8:55 p.m.

II            2003-04 SUPERINTENDENT’S PROPOSED BUDGET (Supplemental Log – Appendix B) 

	Dr. Beni explained that the budget is the financial blueprint for the following school year and that this year’s budget was particularly complex in its development.  The budget process includes a three (3) year projection and forecasting and represents the collection of a great deal of information.  

	The major factors affecting the 2003-04 budget are:
Move to new facilities
Health insurance
State mandated employee retirement contributions
Utility costs
Bond payment
State Aid reduction
Lower interest rates on investments
Enrollment increase (4%)
Curriculum cycle
New programs
Special Education
4/3/03 Bond Referendum.

Dr. Beni reviewed each of the major factors in detail.  The proposed budget provides for a staff increase of 10 due to the rising enrollment and 1.4 to support proposed new programs.  The total budget is $35,271,893 which 



represents a tax increase of 8.9 % providing that the 4/3/03 Bond proposition is approved.  If the Bond is not approved the tax increase would be 13.5%.

Key budget dates are:
April 8, 2003   - Instructional Budget Items
April 22, 2003 - Budget review (if necessary)
April 23, 2003 - Board budget adoption
May 6, 2003    - Budget Hearing
May 20, 2003  - Budget Vote

Due to the lateness of the hour, the Non-Instructional portion of the budget will be presented at the April 8th meeting.

Following discussion and questions, Mrs. Silverberg thanked Dr. Beni and his staff for the presentation.

At 10:37 p.m. Mrs. Silverberg adjourned the Public Work Session.
