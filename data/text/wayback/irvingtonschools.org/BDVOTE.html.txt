Irvington Board of Education
Master Plan Update
The Irvington Board of Education School District unanimously adopted a
Bond Resolution at a public meeting on Tuesday, December 14, which will
enable the district to accomplish its Master Plan.  The Master Plan was
developed by the Board and district administrators over a 2 year period,
with considerable community input and discussion, to address the
district's rapidly growing student enrollment.  The $47,000,000 Bond
provides for the construction of a new 6-8 Middle School adjacent to the
High School, the construction of a separate gymnasium/theater complex to
the east of the new Middle School, the renovation and repair of our
existing facilities, and the renovation and repair of our athletic
fields.  The Board estimates that after State aid, BOCES rental
agreement and budget reductions, the net impact on an average homeowner
will be $285 per year over the life of the bond.
The public vote on this bond will take place on Thursday, February 3
from 7 AM to 9 PM in the Irvington Middle School.  In order to vote in
this election you must be a U.S. citizen, be 18 years of age or older
and be registered either with the school district or the Board of
Elections.  In addition, you must have been a resident of the district
for at least 30 days. Absentee ballots will be available beginning on
January 7, 2000.  In order to register to vote or receive an absentee
ballot, contact the Office of the District Clerk at 914-591-8502.
