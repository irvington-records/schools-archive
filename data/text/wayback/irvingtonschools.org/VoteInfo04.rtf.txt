VOTER REGISTRATION

To vote in Irvington School elections, you must be registered for either school elections or general elections.  If you are not registered, you may register for school voting at the District Clerk’s Office at the Administration Office 9:00 A.M. to 3:00 P.M. during regular school days, OR, register at the Special Voter Registration Day, Thursday,
May 13, 2004 from 4:00 P.M. to 8:00 P.M. in the lobby of the Main Street School.

A person qualified to register to vote must be a citizen of the United States, at least 18 years old, and a resident of the School District for at least thirty days before the vote.

     - Any person who is registered with the Westchester County Board of Elections in 
        the Irvington School District need not register separately with the School District.

     - Any person who voted in any of the Irvington School District elections any time
        since May 2000 is registered with the School District and need not register again.

     - Any person who is not registered with the County and did not vote in any of 
        the Irvington School District elections since May 2000 is not eligible to vote in the   
        School District’s May 18, 2004 Budget Vote and Board Members election (including those who have voted only in Village elections).

ABSENTEE BALLOTS 
Absentee Ballots are available April 22, 2004 for registered voters who will not be in Irvington on May 18, 2004.  Apply in person in the District Clerk’s Office at 90 North Broadway for an application.  May 11 is deadline for absentee ballot application if ballot is to be mailed to the voter.  May 17 is deadline for absentee ballot application if the ballot is to be picked up by the voter.  May 18, 2004 at 5:00 P.M. is the deadline for absentee ballots to be received by the District Clerk.  Call the District Clerk’s office at 591-8502 for further information.

SCHOOL BOARD CANDIDATE
Two seats are up for election; seats are currently held by George Huzinec and Diane Mundree.  Nominating Petitions are available at the District Clerk’s office March 17, 2004, and are due April 19, 2004.  Petitions must be signed by  25  qualified voters of the District.  The candidates with the highest vote count will be elected to serve a three-year term on a five member Board.
