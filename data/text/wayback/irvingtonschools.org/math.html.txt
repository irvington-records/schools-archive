The Irvington Middle School Math team came in first among Westchester schools in the New York Mathematics League Competition. The school's eighth-grade team scored 178 points, which put them in first place over Ardsley Middle School, which placed second, with 174 points. (Edgemont H.S. was third with 173 points and Rye County Day was fourth with 169 points.)
The competition is based on scores from 40 math questions answered by students during a 30-minute timed test taken earlier this year at their individual schools.
Winning members of Irvington Middle School's math team are: Henry Liu, Greg Sokol and Wendy Wang (each scored 36 out of 40). Eric Borum, Kevin John, Rachel Chasten, Andy Lazar and Margaret Leibovic (each scored 35). Math teacher Lisa Urban is the team's advisor.
