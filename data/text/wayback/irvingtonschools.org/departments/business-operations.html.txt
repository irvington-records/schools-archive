Business & Operations
Welcome to the Business & Operations Office for the Irvington UFSD!
Our department oversees the business and support operations that occur in the district, including Accounting, Accounts Payable and Receivable, Audit, Budget, Payroll, Treasury and Banking, Purchasing, Food Services (School Breakfast and Lunch) and Facilities.
