Interview Tips and Helpful Information
Interview Tips
Tips to Succeed in Your Next Interview:
- Research the District and school community
- Bring copies your résumé, notebook and pen
- Dress professionally
- Be on time and prepared
- Be confident and assertive, not aggressive
- Listen and be attentive and interested
- Answer questions, thoughtfully and completely
- Expand on your answers where appropriate
- Provide examples to illustrate your key competencies
- Consider questions that you may have about the position for which you are interviewing, the District, and school community
Security measures
A driver's license or State issued ID is required to enter each school building.
