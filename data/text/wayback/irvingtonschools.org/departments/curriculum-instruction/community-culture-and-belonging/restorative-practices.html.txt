Restorative Practices
The District has partnered with Cluster Community Services to learn more about Restorative Practices.  Restorative practices are focused on proactively building relationships and community. They also offer a path for responding to ruptured relationships in a way that centers accountability and the repair of harm. 
As part of the District’s commitment to welcoming and affirming schools, we will continue to revise our Code of Conduct to include restorative practices as a way to support our students.
