Computer Science, Engineering and Design
Project Lead The Way
The Irvington UFSD participates in Project Lead The Way, a nonprofit organization that provides transformative STEM learning experiences for K-12 students and teachers across the United States.
We currently participate in three PLTW programs: Launch (Grade K-5 students), Gateway (Grade 6-8 students), and Engineering (Grade 9-12 students)
In 2021-22, Dows Lane and Main Street Schools were named, Distinguished Programs joining schools nationwide with this honor!
