Free and Reduced Lunch Information
Applications for Free Lunch and Reduced Lunch may be submitted at any time during the year. Renewal applications must be done annually and received by September 30 to ensure no disruption or billings occur. Only a qualified meal counts towards the Free & Reduced Breakfast/Lunch program, although some ala carte items can be combined to count for a meal.
