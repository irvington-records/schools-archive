Application for Transportation to a Non-Public School
Transportation to a Non-Public School
School bus transportation: Residents of the district whose students will attend a non-public school in 2026-27 and require bus service must submit a request form online by April 1 (even if placement has not yet been confirmed) to the Ardsley Transportation Department. To be eligible, students must be at least 5 years old, in grades K-12 (not preschool) and the school must be within 15 miles of their home. You must also meet the minimum district mileage requirements.
If you are applying to multiple schools, a separate application must be submitted for each school.
To apply online, visit ardsleyschools.org and click on “Departments>Transportation>Transportation to a non-public school application”
If you will not need transportation next year, please let us know so we can remove your child from the list.
NOTE: Busing to non-public schools is not provided for orientation days, after-school activities, or on Federal holidays. Please be prepared to make alternate transportation arrangements. Please check the district calendar for all relevant dates.
