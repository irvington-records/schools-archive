IRVINGTON HIGH SCHOOL SITE COMMITTEE
Minutes of Meeting on October 2004

Present: Jaimie Allen, Joe DeGennaro, Rebecca Lazar, Dalia May, Scott Mosenthal, Alison Romain (minutes), Lily Silpe, Janet Williams, Eden Univer

Scott reported that Earth Science Regents scores and the sequence of the science curriculum were discussed at a recent Board of Education meeting.
Scott said that the senior class officers did a marvelous job of managing the process of allotting parking spaces.  34 students received one-semester permits.  There are no provisions for emergency parking.  Police are monitoring illegal parking on Circle Drive and Riverview Road.
Scott reported that he had spoken to Isabel Milano, Chair of the Community Action Board, about the lack of follow-up by the organizations who signed up student volunteers for community service.  She will contact the organizations.
Scott reported that proposed assemblies will be vetted by the Site Committee and that parents will be notified in advance.  The upcoming events are:
10/28: Motivational speaker, Ty Sells, on alcohol and drug awareness, as a part of Red Ribbon Week activities
11/4: the Poet Laureate for New York State
11/1: a mock election debate between the Young Democrat and Young Republican clubs, moderated by Mr. Barry.  The assembly will be followed by Connections and each social studies class will hold mock elections.
10/27: a preview of the Fall musical, Lucky Stiff
New business:
Scott reported that the Tri-State Consortium evaluators are coming tomorrow to begin a three-day review of our ELA program.  They will be interviewing students, parents, teachers, and administrators, and will shadow students in class.  The reviewers are from a peer group of schools.  This evaluation also fulfills the Middle States accreditation process for the high school.
Janet noted that occasionally, no one is at the security desk.  Scott agreed that it could happen because the two people assigned have additional duties.  Security is a priority of the superintendent and the Emergency Preparedness Committee will have training on November 16th and 17th.  Magnetic ID cards and video cameras are under discussion.
Lily questioned the high school’s policy on ranking.  Scott feels that it works to Irvington’s advantage not to rank its students and that many other schools in our area do not.  It forces the colleges to scrutinize our applicants more carefully.  Alison noted that those colleges that are inundated with tens of thousands of applicants may not take the time to do a thorough evaluation absent a class rank.
Becky reported that she had heard that the math sections of the recently administered PSAT included topics not covered except by level 4 classes.  Alison concurred.  Scott will alert the math department to this issue.
175 students will be entering 9th grade next year and staff levels will need to be increased.
Alison praised the fact that the upcoming assembly will address the timely issue of substance abuse, which she feels is a significant problem in the community.  Janet stated that we need an on-going program at the high school level, not just a one-time assembly.  A question was raised about the degree to which older students try to involve younger students in bad habits.  While all district teachers receive training to recognize signs of substance abuse, not all coaches are faculty members.
Committee members wish that more faculty members utilized Thinkwave.
Sign-up for the new fencing program will be underway soon.  Many students are interested.
Students are trying to organize a bowling team.
Scott reported for Tristan that he was concerned about which subscriptions the library had chosen to discontinue due to budgets cuts.
It was discussed where seniors can hang out when they do not have classes, because they are not permitted to loiter in the library and cafeteria.  Joe said that he will try to find an empty classroom.
The next Site Committee meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, November 16, 2004 at 3:30 pm.

The meeting was adjourned at 5:07 pm.

							Respectfully submitted,
