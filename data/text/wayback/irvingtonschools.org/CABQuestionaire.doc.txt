The purpose of the Community Advisory Board (CAB) shall be to coordinate various community resources and activities with specific segments of the population with special emphasis on helping children and adolescents grow up healthy, happy, and responsible.  It will provide a means for communication among various groups in the Village including the School District, Recreation Department, Police Department, Religious Organizations, Senior Citizen Organizations, and other groups recognized by the Village of Irvington.
