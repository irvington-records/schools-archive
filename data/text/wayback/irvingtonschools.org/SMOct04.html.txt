School Matters, October 2004
By Janice Silverberg
Over the years, community members have told us that they liked reading this "School Matters" column in the Irvington Viewpoint. The general consensus has favored a brief monthly summary of what has taken place at Board of Education meetings, as well as an update on district projects and issues. Although we can't replace our local newspaper, we can continue the column itself as part of our regular email service. In addition to this Board summary, our new Superintendent, Dr. Kathleen Matusiak will also write a monthly message, a practice she brings with her from her former district in New Fairfield, CT. A third column, "View on Board" will appear from time to time, with a more in depth analysis of a particular topic. Our intent is to keep the community informed about district news; we welcome your feedback and suggestions.
There is much good news to report in this first column of the school year! First of all, the transition to our new administrative team has been extremely smooth. Dr. Matusiak has been enthusiastically engaging the district, bringing her unique blend of keen intelligence, charm and warmth into and beyond our schools. She has met with a host of individuals and groups ranging from Village leaders to 2nd graders, developing perspective and appreciation of our district and community. Assistant Superintendent for Instruction Dr. Ralph Napolitano has focused on assessing our curriculum review cycle and working with the Curriculum Committee to ensure that our district remains true to its mission of academic excellence. Not only have these two superb administrators "hit the ground running", they have begun a dynamic working relationship with each other. We foresee an exciting period for our district, with these two at the helm.
During the first month of school, we celebrated several significant student accomplishments. Six Irvington High School seniors have been named National Merit Semi-finalists, matching last year's record. Congratulations to Sam Abbott, Colin Bando, Romit Bhattacharya, Will Forster, James Ryan, and Eric Saar. This is a distinct honor for these students, their families, and our district.
Obviously, our wind ensemble has taken the advice of the old adage to "practice, practice, practice", because they are on their way to Carnegie Hall. Led by Mr. Larry Corio, the ensemble is one of only six high school and two college ensembles invited to participate in a special concert at Carnegie Hall this coming spring.
Finally, kudos to our student government representatives, Joe Florio and Brian Adelman, who as members of the Transportation Committee, ably represented their constituency in obtaining parking privileges for seniors. Acknowledging the Committee's concerns for safety, sufficient staff parking and an adequate number of visitor spaces, Joe and Brian conducted an on-going survey of parking availability during the first weeks of school and made their case. Last week, 34 spots were allocated for student parking, and assigned by lottery. We congratulate our student leaders on their effective participation in this process. We also appreciate the cooperation of the Village administration, and the patience of our neighbors on Riverview Road and Circle Drive.
