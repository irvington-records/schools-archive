Notes: I.H.S Site Committee Meeting 9/21/04
Present: Janet Williams, Alison Romain, Lily Silpe, Anne Keary, Deb Hardy, Laurie Chock, Joe DeGennaro, Dalia May, Gabrielle Greenberg, Katherine Lapham, Eden Univer, Rebecca Lazar, Chris Tortu, Scott Mosenthal

After introductions, we discussed the following items:

Scott distributed a report on the high school from the past year. Included were test scores, student management statistics, new courses, honor roll and eligibility numbers, etc.

Several questions arose regarding the Community Service Program. There has been little or no follow-up from many of the organizations represented at last spring’s Community Service Day (two exceptions: Children’s Village and Habitat for Humanity). Scott will contact Isabel Milano to express our concern.


There were several questions regarding the new collaboration classes, in which special education teachers are paired with academic teachers. The goal of the program is to provide support in the classroom for special education students as well as general education students; in effect, these teachers are team teaching.

In response to a question: the new fencing team is being sponsored by the IEF and will begin in the winter.


Parking was brought up; there is a transportation committee meeting Thursday to decide whether students can park on the campus.

In response to a question about Driver’s Education: the district has decided not to sponsor this program due to liability issues.


Several assemblies are being planned. Last spring, I recommended to the superintendent that any assemblies would be brought before the Site Committee before the assemblies were held; I also recommended that I would notify parents of any upcoming asssemblies. Please let me know ASAP if you have any questions/objections regarding the two assemblies currently in the works:

--Lydia Fernandez, Student Assistance Counselor, is working with the PTSA to bring Ty Sells, a motivational speaker, to address students about the dangers of using drugs; this assembly is tentatively slated for the end of October. He has presented at a number of Westchester schools; I have called a principal at Harrison for a reference and tapes are being sent for review

--Alison Cooper-Mullin has arranged for Billy Collins, New York State Poet Laureate, to address the student body in November
Next meeting: October 18 3:30 p.m.
