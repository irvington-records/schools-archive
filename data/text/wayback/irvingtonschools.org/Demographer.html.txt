Demographer's Report
Executive Summary
- Westchester County school districts have experienced sharp increases in births and enrollments over the past fifteen years. Both the births and student enrollment in suburban Westchester are expected to remain high through at least the first decade of the new century.
- Irvington Town births have doubled over the past 16 years from a low of 55 births in 1981 to 110 births in 1997. (This is the highest number of births for Irvington since 1964 which was during the post-war baby boom).
- Irvington UFSD Kindergarten enrollment in 1986 was 82. Since then it has been increasing and this year will be 158 Kindergarten students. Further, the projected Kindergarten class for the year 2002 is 172, an all time high.
- These sharply larger Kindergarten entering classes of the past decade are now moving through the Elementary and Middle School grades and will soon cause High School enrollments to rise considerably. Overall, enrollment in grades K-12 has risen from 1101 in 1989 to 1597 in 1998, a rise of 496 students (a 46% growth in total enrollment).
- Single family residence building permits have recently risen within the School District, but while a modest number of new houses have been built, most of the School District's enrollment increase is a result of higher birth numbers and an in-migration of families with school-age children.
- This year, the Irvington Board of Education commissioned a study to forecast enrollment through the year 2006-07. The Middle Projection (of the three projections given to the Board) shows the following:

- In summary, Irvington's enrollment growth over the next several years will be substantial in both numbers and percentage. Most of this growth is built-in since much of the projected growth for the upper grades is already in attendence in the larger-sized earlier grades.
Capacity Analysis
- The School Board also commissioned a study of school building capacity based on how Irvington's three school buildings are currently used.
- Comparison of the capacities and enrollment forecasts make it apparent that by the year 2006-07, all three buildings will exceed their capacities.
|  | Current Capacity | Est. Enrollment | Est. Enrollment | 
| School | As Used | 1999-00 | 2006-07 | 
| Dows Lane K-5 | 916 | 918 | 949 | 
| Middle School 6-8 | 342 | 377 | 474 | 
| High School 9-12 | 354 | 387 | 626 | 
Clearly, some significant adjustments will be required to house Irvington's projected enrollment growth.
Notes:
1. All projected enrollments in this Executive Summary are drawn from the Middle Forecast: the one which the consultants believe is most likely to occur. The High and Low Forecasts are also possible but less likely.
2. Details and explanations underlying these enrollment projections and school building capacity numbers are contained in the full report given to the Irvington Board of Education in November, 1998.
