SOURCE http://www.irvingtonschools.org:80/IMSart.html | DATE 2002-12-24 | TITLE IMS Art Exhibit

The original artwork of several Irvington Middle School eighth-graders has been selected to be part of a special "Artist-Teachers, Students and Standards" Juried Art Exhibit at the College of New Rochelle from Jan. 25 through Feb. 10. The exhibit, which recognizes the artistic talents of both teachers and students, also explores how students' pieces reflect New York State standards in art education.
"We are so proud of our students and Diana Schmertz, our school's art teacher who worked so hard to create art work that reflect students' individual styles," said Irvington Middle School Principal Lauren Allan.
A closing reception for students, teachers, parents and friends will be held on February 10 from 2-4 p.m. at the College of New Rochelle's Mooney Center Exhibit Hall.
Following is a list of the participating students whose work will be on exhibit: Scott DiCesare - "Squiggles Anonymous" ( a self-portrait); Annelise Valerio- "Untitled" (a self-portrait in glaze, color and pattern); Joa Alexander - "Speechless" ( a free standing self-portrait bust); Nick Yuelys - "Afro Lamp" (a clay portrait); Amanda Luzzi, Jackie Turk, Erica Cooper, Nicole Raimondo, Genna Wiedeckehr and Danielle Link, who created a group mural that reflected aeme or emotion from the book, "A Lesson Before Dying" for an interdisciplinary project.
