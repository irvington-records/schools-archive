Irvington Education Foundation


We are pleased to present to the Irvington School Board the following gifts totaling $213,542.03 for the 2003-04 school year:

IRVINGTON HIGH SCHOOL
Grade 11: (L. Klein) - English – Students from 2 culturally, economically and geographically diverse communities will work together by attending a series of classroom workshops led jointly by teaching artists from the Manhattan Theatre Club and classroom teachers.  They will write, review, act and discuss with their peers providing them with an in-depth understanding of dramatic literature and performance.
Award: $2,912.

Grades 11-12: (M. Dunne) - Science- This grant will fund the expansion of the Physics AP Science program, providing students with necessary equipment that will enable them to design and perform their own experiment to answer questions that they proposed.  
Award:  $7,435.

Grades 10-12: (N. Viscogliosi) - Science- This grant will fund a 3 year intensive Science Research course which offers Regents credit and up to 12 college credits. This course emphasizes the development and disciplined use of scientific creativity. Seniors are expected to seek publication of his/her work in professional scientific journals.
Students will participate in the Intel competition. 
Award:  $13,815.

Grades 9-12: (L. Petersen) – Physical Education- This grant will enhance the physical education curriculum through the Tri Fit assessment system.  Each student will be provided with a personal fitness program designed to promote a lifetime of fitness and health. 
Award:  $10,869.

Grades 9-12: (S. Mosenthal) – Science- This grant will fund the purchase of electronic balances to be used in science classrooms.
Award:  $11,050.
