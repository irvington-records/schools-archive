Site Committee Minutes from Thursday September 27th, 2005

PTSA Food and Menu Committee invite Dows Lane Site Committee to work together on three goals:
One Snack Rule
How can we as a school enforce the one snack rule – either a cookie or an ice cream  or a bagel?  Though families are free to send any food they choose for their children, how can we ensure that children buy no more than one of these three as an after-lunch snack?
Candy as Incentive
Interested in encouraging school staff to discontinue using candy as a reward by developing a non-food reward suggestion list
Ellen Passov noted that children sometimes receive candy rewards following time with special services providers.  
Classroom Snacks
Interested in encouraging each classroom to have families rotate in providing snack for whole classroom, each day.  Idea is to limit big snacks and to promote healthy snacks
