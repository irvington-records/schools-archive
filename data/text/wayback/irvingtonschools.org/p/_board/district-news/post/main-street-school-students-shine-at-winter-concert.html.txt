Main Street School Students Shine at Winter Concert
Main Street School students filled the auditorium with music and enthusiasm during the school’s recent Winter Concert, a celebration of student learning, creativity, and community.
Families and guests were welcomed by Principal Joyce Chapnick and Assistant Principal Heather Shaughnessy, who thanked the community for joining the evening and highlighted the dedication students demonstrated in preparing for the performance. Over the past several months, student musicians worked with focus and commitment, learning not only musical notes and rhythms, but also the importance of collaboration, perseverance, and self-expression.
The concert showcased performances by the fifth grade orchestra, band, chorus, and the Main Street School Singers, each group presenting a diverse selection of music that reflected students’ growth and enthusiasm.
The fifth grade orchestra performed Appalachian Hymn by Soon Hee Newbold and Clog Dance by Elliot Del Borgo, demonstrating strong ensemble skills and expressive playing. Several student soloists were featured throughout the performance, highlighting individual musicianship within the larger group setting. The orchestra is led by Nikki Allison, who guided students in developing technical skill, confidence, and musical expression.
The fifth grade band energized the audience with Zombie Stomp by Rob Grice and Power Rock by Michael Story, showcasing rhythmic precision, dynamic contrasts, and teamwork across sections. The band, along with the fifth grade chorus and the Main Street School Singers, is led by Megan Hannon, whose instruction supports students’ musical growth across multiple ensembles.
The fifth grade chorus performed Young People of the World by Glen Everhart and Now or Never by Bon Jovi and Pitbull, emphasizing unity, confidence, and expressive vocal performance.
The Main Street School Singers concluded the evening with I Have a Voice, Haida, and A Million Dreams, blending musicality with meaningful themes of identity, community, and aspiration. Several students were featured in solo and small-group moments, adding depth and individuality to the performance.
Throughout the evening, students embodied the core values of The Irvington Way—taking courageous risks on stage, communicating through music, supporting one another as engaged community members, and reflecting pride in their growth as learners and performers.
The district extends its sincere appreciation to music educators Megan Hannon and Nikki Allison for their dedication and leadership. Ms. Hannon’s work with the fifth grade band, chorus, and 4/5 Main Street School Singers, and Ms. Allison’s guidance of the fifth grade orchestra, were evident in the quality and confidence of each performance.
The Main Street School Winter Concert served as a joyful reminder of the power of music to bring students, families, and the school community together in celebration of learning, creativity, and shared accomplishment.
