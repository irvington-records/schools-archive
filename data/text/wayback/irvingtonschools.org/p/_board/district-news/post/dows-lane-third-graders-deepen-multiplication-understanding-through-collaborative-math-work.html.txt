Dows Lane Third Graders Deepen Multiplication Understanding Through Collaborative Math Work
Third graders in Mr. Schwartz and Ms. Raymond’s ICT classrooms strengthened their multiplication skills through a hands-on exploration of decomposing pictorial arrays into expressions with three factors. As students examined how arrays can be broken apart and represented in different ways, they built a deeper understanding of the associative property while practicing how to clearly communicate mathematical thinking.
The lesson began with parallel teaching, allowing students to learn in two small instructional groups before returning to their seats to collaborate on the problem set. Working together, classmates shared strategies, discussed their reasoning, and supported one another through challenging problems.  
Throughout the activity, students demonstrated what it means to be Courageous Learners, an attribute or Irvington’s Portrait of a Graduate. They took risks, explained their ideas with precision, and persevered through moments of productive struggle. By expressing mathematical concepts clearly and accurately, they also strengthened their skills as Effective Communicators.  
The experience provided students with a meaningful opportunity to build confidence in multiplication while engaging in the collaborative learning that defines the Dows Lane classroom community.
