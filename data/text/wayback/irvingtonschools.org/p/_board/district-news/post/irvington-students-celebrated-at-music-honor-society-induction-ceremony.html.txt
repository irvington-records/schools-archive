Irvington Students Celebrated at Music Honor Society Induction Ceremony
Irvington Middle School proudly welcomed new members into its Music Honor Society during a formal induction ceremony held on November 25. The event celebrated students’ dedication to musical excellence, creativity, and service, highlighting the important role music plays within the school community and beyond.
The ceremony opened with student performances that showcased a range of musical styles and talents. The opening segment featured Early Music performed by Jagger Clark, followed by Sophia Orton performing Etude No. 1 by Philip Glass, Ella Marcus performing “Shy” from Once Upon a Mattress, and Camille Orton performing “Air” from Grieg’s Holberg Suite. These performances set the tone for an evening focused on artistry, commitment, and growth.
Principal David Sottile welcomed students and families, followed by remarks from Assistant Principal Allyson Daley, who led attendees in the Pledge of Allegiance and addressed the inductees. The audience then joined together in singing “America the Beautiful,” accompanied on piano by Jagger Clark.
As their names were read aloud, new inductees stood to be recognized before collectively reciting the Music Honor Society pledge, affirming their commitment to musical excellence, service, and leadership.
New members inducted into the Irvington Middle School Music Honor Society include: 
Aria Arinella-Rashid, Sydney Arnett, Liam Brandon, Lucy Buck, Jayanth Chandran, Ella Chen, Channing Chong, Mason Cohn, Frank DaCosta, Ryan Daly, Dakota Dawkins, Emmett Ditsky, Charlotte Dolinger, Gigi Dorment, Miranda Elder, Nora Estes, Natalie George, Kiran Gimenez, Luna Greenemeier, Oliver Hernandez, Tessa Hersom, Kayla Heslin, Scarlett Huang, Toby Lowenbraun, Amy Lu, Ava Magallon, Riggs Maher, Ella Marcus, Caitlin McGrath, Ansh Menon, Jack O’Hanlon, Camille Orton, Kimaya Punj, Jack Rafal, Oliver Rushton, Nico Russ, Sullivan Schreter, Linda Seery, Riona Shimizu, Mady Sherwin, Katriel Skerrett, Delia Snyder, Sophia Spagnuolo, Arjun Varanasi, Lucia Villavicencio, Teddy Weber, and Chatherine Weiss. 
The program also featured readings from student essay highlights, shared by Aahan Pathak, Freya Barakat, and Cassidy Reichgott, offering insight into students’ personal connections to music and their motivations for pursuing excellence in the art form.
Throughout the ceremony, students were reminded that membership in the Music Honor Society extends beyond performance. Music Honor Society advisor and program director Paul Scattaretico outlined student expectations, upcoming events, and community service opportunities, emphasizing the importance of giving back through music. Upcoming opportunities include performances at Atria Woodlands in Ardsley, assisting as ushers at school concerts, and a recording studio tour at Riverworks Recording Studios in Dobbs Ferry.
In his closing remarks, Mr. Scattaretico encouraged students to step outside their comfort zones, embrace leadership opportunities, and dedicate their time and talents to supporting others through music. Faculty expressed gratitude to school administrators, staff, and the music department for their continued support, and offered special recognition to the student performers and readers who helped make the ceremony a meaningful celebration.
Irvington Middle School congratulates all newly inducted members of the Music Honor Society and looks forward to the positive impact they will continue to make through music, leadership, and service to the community.
