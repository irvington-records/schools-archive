Irvington Second Graders Create Bold Geometric Snowflakes Through Radial Symmetry
Second Graders at Dows Lane brought winter into the art room as they designed their own geometric snowflakes using radial symmetry and cool color palettes. Guided by art teacher Ms. Arango, students experimented with shapes, patterns, repetition, and balance to create striking seasonal artworks.
As they planned, revised, and reflected on their designs, students practiced essential skills from Irvington’s Portrait of a Graduate. They explored creative risk-taking, communicated ideas about their artistic choices, and collaborated respectfully with classmates while working with shared materials.  
The finished snowflakes showcased not only artistic skill but also thoughtful decision-making and attention to detail. Their bold, cool-colored designs brightened the art room and highlighted the meaningful learning happening each day. 
Throughout this project, students strengthened their ability to think creatively, work responsibly, and express themselves –– key components of developing as Courageous Learners, Effective Communicators, and Self-Aware Individuals.
