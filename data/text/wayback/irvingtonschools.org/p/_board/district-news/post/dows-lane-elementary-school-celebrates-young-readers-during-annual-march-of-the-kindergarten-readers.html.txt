Dows Lane Elementary School Celebrates Young Readers During Annual March of the Kindergarten Readers
Dows Lane School students and staff came together to celebrate the joy of reading during the annual March of the Kindergarten Readers, a cherished tradition that highlights the school’s youngest learners and their growing love of books. 
During the event, kindergarten students proudly marched through the school holding their favorite books while classmates and teachers lined the halls, clapping and cheering in support. The celebration recognizes students who are just beginning to read or who have discovered a book they love, reinforcing the excitement and confidence that come with early literacy development.  
“It's one of our best days,” said Principal Andrea Kantor. “We are not only highlighting our love for reading but also coming together as a community to celebrate our students’ growth and courageous learning –– this is The Irvington Way.” 
The March of the Kindergarten Readers reflects Dows Lane’s commitment to fostering a supportive and inclusive school culture where students are encouraged to take risks, celebrate progress, and support one another. By honoring early reading milestones, the school reinforces the importance of literacy while building a strong sense of pride and belonging among its youngest readers. 
Through moments like these, Dows Lane School continues to nurture confident, curious learners and strengthen the connections that make the Irvington community so special.
