Irvington High School Seniors Return to Dows Lane for Annual Senior Walk
Members of Irvington High School’s Class of 2026 were greeted with cheers and applause as they walked the halls of Dows Lane Elementary School during the annual Senior Walk. The tradition provides seniors with an opportunity to return to where their educational journey began, reconnect with former teachers and reflect on how far they have come. Dows Lane Principal Dr. Andrea Kantor and Irvington High School Principal Jon Hirsch said the event remains a cherished tradition that highlights the strong connections within the Irvington school community.
