Irvington Middle School Presents Alice in Wonderland Jr. This Weekend
Irvington Middle School is heading down the rabbit hole this weekend with its winter musical, Alice in Wonderland Jr., a whimsical stage production featuring talents of IMS students both onstage and behind the scenes.
The musical brings the beloved story to life through colorful characters, lively music, and imaginative storytelling, showcasing the hard work and creativity of the Irvington Middle School cast and crew. Students have been preparing for months to deliver a fun and memorable theatrical experience for the school community.
Community members are invited to attend one of the following performances at the Irvington Campus Theatre:
- Friday, February 6 at 7:00 pm
- Saturday, February 7 at 5:00 pm
Irvington Middle School looks forward to welcoming families and community members to enjoy this enchanting production and to celebrate the dedication and talent of its student performers.
“A very merry unbirthday to us all!”
