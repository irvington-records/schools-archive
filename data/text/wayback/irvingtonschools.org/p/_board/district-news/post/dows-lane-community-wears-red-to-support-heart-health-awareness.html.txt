Dows Lane Community Wears Red to Support Heart Health Awareness
Earlier this week, student and staff at Dows Lane Elementary School filled the halls with red in support of Congenital Heart Defects (CHD) awareness and one of their own first grade students. 
The initiative began with the student, who encouraged teachers and classmates to wear red to help raise awareness about heart health. The response from the Dows Lane community was immediate and heartfelt, with classrooms across the building proudly participating. 
“He is a role model to all students and staff,” shared Dr. Kantor, Principal of Dows Lane. “Our community joined together supporting our loved student and our learning.” 
The day was a powerful example of The Irvington Way in action. Through empathy, leadership, and collective support, students and staff demonstrated how even the youngest members of the Irvington Union Free School District community can inspire meaningful action. 
Moments like these highlight the strength of Dows Lane’s school community and the importance of caring for one another.
