SOURCE http://www.irvingtonschools.org:80/latin2002.html | DATE 2003-04-19 | TITLE Latin Winners

Irvington High School students achieved notable standings in the National Latin Exam, given recently to students across the country. Sam Abbott and Brian Adelman received perfect scores and Kevin Litcofsky attained "summa cum laude'" distinction for four consecutive years on the same exam which tests students' competency of the Latin language.
Those students and others are also members of Irvington High School's Latin Team, which competes against other schools in Latin language and culture proficency. Others on the team include: Jim Ryan, John Tavolacci, Samuel Galgano, Martin Pepeljugosky, David Neiditch and Romit Bhattacharya.
