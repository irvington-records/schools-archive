Mark you calendars for the Irvington Artists in Concert, sponsored by the High School Latin Club. The concert will be held in the Irvington Town Hall Theatre on Saturday, May 20 beginning at 8 PM. This yearly extravaganza features local artists with international reputations representing a wide variety of musical styles, from classical to jazz, from old standards to original compositions. Local artists scheduled to appear include Diana Selenow, Marge Hone, Nenad Bach, Mary McCarthy and her husband Don Wiggins and Patricia Waters. Tickets, costing $12 for adults and $6 for students are available now at the Town Hall Theatre Box Office, Beckers and the High School Office.
The proceeds from the show go to benefit the Latin Club Scholarship Fund which helps graduating seniors with their college expenses by granting awards based on need and involvement with the club. Throughout the year, the Latin Club sponsors activities which raise money for national and local charities including UNICEF and Blythedale Children's Hospital.
The concert, which is a yearly event dating back to 1994, is also sponsored by Irvington Town Hall Theatre, Inc. which covers all expenses associated with the concert including donation of its facility for the evening and moving and tuning the High School piano. For further information, contact High School Latin teacher Michele Cella via the main High School phone number: 591-8500.
