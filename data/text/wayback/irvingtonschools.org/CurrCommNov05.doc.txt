2. Social Studies Textbooks for Grade 5

Ralph reminded everyone of the importance of following the Board Policy for textbook and workbook adoptions.  He distributed the completed summary packet on the social studies texts that are being 
requested by the fifth grade team for the Committee to review.  This packet will now be distributed to 
the Board of Education for their approval.

Once that happens the book order can be placed.  Ralph emphasized the importance of getting book reviews done in a timely matter so that they can be included in budgetary conversations and be ordered in a timely manner.  

3.  Curriculum Review Cycle

Principals, chairpersons and grade level leaders were reminded to review the specific phase of the curriculum review cycle each content area is in with their colleagues during Superintendent's Conference Day.  They should also consider the questions that are aligned to each of the phases for their response at a future meeting.  Summer work projects, textbook considerations, course approvals, etc. should be aligned to the curriculum assessment phase of the cycle.  For example, the following departments/ grades will be given priority next year when they are in their assessment phase: world languages, library media, and technology (formerly industrial arts). 







Ralph reminded everyone that when a content area is undergoing assessment, at the conclusion of 
that year, exit outcomes and technology integration also needs to be included in the curriculum report.

Chris asked a question regarding adding new electives:  In light of the fact that departments will not know if they are receiving additional staffing immediately, is it wise to prepare to introduce a new elective?  Response: It is always advisable to think about new courses in regard to student interest and faculty expertise.  Before adding a new elective all electives that are currently in place should be reviewed for enrollment.  If current course enrollments do not support continuation of a course, that class should be dropped and a new class should be added.  This does not involve additional staffing. 

4. Superintendent's Conference Day - November 3, 2005

Ralph thanked the principals and the chairpersons for all of their hard work on organizing this day for their staffs.  With new technology programs, curriculum programs and the Strategic Plan in place, teachers need the time to meet and discuss all new initiatives.

Ralph reminded the Committee that the January and March dates are being changed and extended.  The parents will be receiving a letter on these changes next week.  The principals will notify the PDP Committee regarding which teachers will not be used for scoring the ELA and mathematics tests.  
The PDP Committee in concert with the principals will help to organize the day for those teachers 
who are not involved in scoring.

5. Data Analysis Training 

Secondary administrators, department chairpersons and team leaders will be meeting with Mark Samis from the RIC to have their first training session in data analysis on November 4th. The elementary administrators and team leaders found their session with Marc very enlightening and useful.  They 
are looking forward to a second meeting with him in January.

6.  Consolidated Grants 

All Districts receive moneys to be used to fund some of their programs.  Ralph completed the grant applications this summer, but would like some help thinking about how these moneys could be used 
next school year.  He will bring information about the process to the Curriculum Committee at a later date.

Please remember that book approval, course approval and summer work projects will be prioritized according to the Curriculum Review Cycle.  After the content areas under assessment have been honored, the other areas will be considered.
