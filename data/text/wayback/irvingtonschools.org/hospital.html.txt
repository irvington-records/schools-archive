SOURCE http://www.irvingtonschools.org:80/hospital.html | DATE 2002-08-27 | TITLE Hospital

Irvington Middle School students presented a check for $5,000 to Bruce Komiske, executive director of the Children's Hospital Foundation at Westchester Medical Center, in Valhalla, N.Y. The school's Community Service Club raised the money by holding a Community Walk-a-Thon last spring. Pictured from l. to r.:eighth grade students Corrine Fisk, Greg Solol, and Jennifer Zeng, with Guidance Counselor Griffin Kelly, Principal Lauren Allan, Guidance Counselor Toni Kelly, Bruce Komiske and students Joshua Runes and Andy Lazar.
