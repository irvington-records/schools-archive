Vision Statement
The mission of the Irvington Union Free School District is to enable each student to discover and attain his or her highest potential in the realms of academic achievement, social responsibility and individual development. In order to achieve this mission, the District must provide its students with sufficient facilities, staff and enriched educational program.
It has become apparent that the District will soon face a severe shortage of building space for its burgeoning student population. The LRPC believes that in designing a response to properly address the issue of student population growth and space requirements, we must:
- Meet our District’s needs, for a projected 5-10 years.
- Provide a solution to our space problems from a district-wide perspective.
- Take into consideration our changing educational environment.
- Provide our students with a safe and healthy learning environment.
- Provide for growth in a cost-effective way.
- View the maintenance and enhancement of the Irvington School District’s educational excellence as our most important concern.
Furthermore, the LRPC believes that the District must consider the values listed below, which are central to maintaining and enhancing the educational excellence of the Irvington School District. To that end, the Irvington School District must:
- Maintain reasonable and appropriate class sizes.
- Provide the means to enable all students to meet the revised New York State learning standards.
- Provide a technologically rich environment.
- Furnish the appropriate space and facilities for specialized programs including music, art, and physical education.
- Provide a rich and varied co-curricular environment.
- Link our schools to the larger community.
- Consider the ability of the community to provide resources to its schools.
It is with this vision and these values as our guide that the LRPC presents its report to the Board of Education and the Irvington School District.
