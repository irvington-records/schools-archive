Mission Statement
Board of Education Charge to the
Long Range Planning Committee
The Mission Statement of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District declared that Long Range Planning would be a major focus for the Board during the 1997-98 school year. As a result, the Board formed a Long Range Planning Committee (LRPC) led by trustees Paul Mandel and Paul Birgy. This committee was charged to initiate a process to analyze the district’s long range planning needs. The goals of the committee included:
- Identifying the long range planning needs of the district.
- Forming a committee including community members to analyze district long range challenges.
- Utilizing this process to build community support to meet these challenges.
The primary District need identified by the LRPC was the rapid growth of our student population and its implications for building space, staffing needs and educational quality. Members of the LRPC included Board of Education Trustees Paul Mandel and Paul Birgy and Superintendent Richard Hajek. Community volunteers included: Gregory Fisk, George Huzinec, Isabel Milano, Byron Moss, Kenneth Nelson and Eileen O’Shea. As the committee continued its research and deliberations into the 1998-99 school year, the committee membership was expanded to include Superintendent-designate Dr. Vincent Beni and Assistant Superintendent for Business and Facilities Management, Donald Marra. At the beginning of the 1998-99 school year, George Huzinec became a Board of Education Trustee and Paul Birgy remained on the committee as a community member.
The committee held its first meeting on February 25, 1998. The LRPC was requested to present a report to the Board of Education detailing the range of options which the District might consider to deal with this issue. This report would be based on a thorough review of District resources and demographic projections and would be the basis for the Board of Education’s decision-making process on this important matter. The ultimate goals of the LRPC’s report were to:
- Identify and analyze a range of reasonable options (detailing both the positives and negatives of those options).
- Present the Board with recommendations regarding how to proceed in addressing this challenge for the long term.
- Begin the process of informing the community.
- Begin building a consensus on the critical nature of the space challenges to the future of our district.
