The mission of the Irvington Union Free School District is to provide the best education for the children of our community. To fulfill this mandate, we, the elected members of the Board of Education, must address a wide range of issues from the very local to those with state and even national import.
Ensuring the health, safety and well being of our children is the cornerstone of our mission. Following the tragedy of September 11, serious questions are being asked about the potential vulnerability of the Indian Point Nuclear Facility to a terrorist attack, and the potential deadly impact of such an attack on our region, our community and our children.
At this time of year, we are engaged in the annual budget planning process. We cannot, in reason or conscience, go about our usual business, estimating class size and tax rates, advocating changes in the Wicks Law of support for BOCES without addressing Indian Point. We are being asked about school safety, evacuation planning, emergency intervention, and the possibility of distributing potassium iodide in the event of an attack on Indian Point. Obviously, the safety of the plant and the adequacy of the planning in anticipation of disaster profoundly affect all of us, and our efforts on behalf of the best education of our children ring hollow if we ignore this reality.
We call upon you, our national, state and county leaders, to ensure that a safe, realistic emergency evacuation plan is in place for our region's schools. We ask that you provide clear guidelines for our schools on the public health issues that may result from an attack on Indian Point. Most importantly, we ask that you assure the communities in our region that the Indian Point Nuclear Facility can be safely operated and adequately secured.
We rely on your commitment to the people who live, work, and attend school in this region. We support your efforts to ensure the safety of our communities against the threat of a potential terrorist attack, even if that requires the decommissioning of the Indian Point Nuclear Facility.
Thank you.
Sincerely,
The Irvington Board of Education
