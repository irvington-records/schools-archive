The following are the minutes of the Public Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington High School Library, April 8, 2003, with the following present:

Mrs. Janice Silverberg,  Mrs. Penny Delany, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mr. George Huzinec, Mrs. Diane Mundree.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman – Assistant Superintendent for Instruction, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mrs. Lauren Allan – Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane 
Gr. K-3 Principal, Dr. Lola Garcia-Blocker – Dows Lane Gr. 4-5 Principal, Mr. Joseph DeGennaro - High School Assistant Principal, Ms. Raina Kor – Middle School Assistant Principal, Ms. Mrinalini Rajwar – Director of Technology, Mr. Arthur McCormack – Athletic Director, students, members of the faculty and community.   

The Public Work Session was called to order, following the Public Meeting, by Mrs. Janice Silverberg at 8:46 p.m.
   
I             2003-04 Superintendent’s Recommended Budget (Supplemental Log – Appendix A) 

	Dr. Beni reviewed the Recommended Budget summary.  The total 2003-04 recommended budget amount is $35,271,893 which represents a tax increase of 8.9%. 

	Two additional versions of the recommended budget have been prepared and presented for discussion which represent the inclusion of Teacher Aides at Dows Lane Elementary School.  The budget amount and tax rate increase is as follows:     
Including 1st Grade Aides:               $36,330,023, tax rate of 9.2%
Including 1st and 2nd Grade Aides:   $36,388,153, tax rate of 9.4%  

Dr. Beni introduced Mr. James Reese for the presentation of the Non-Instructional Items portion of the recommended budget.

Non-Instructional Items – Supplemental Log – Appendix B
Mr. Reese reviewed the major areas of the Non-Instructional Items portion of the budget in detail.  Substantial increases were noted as follows:
General Support – 16.8% increased operation of plant costs due to utilities services to new buildings, staff increase and insurance. 
Employee Benefits – due to mandated increase in employee retirement system costs and increases in hospital and medical insurance.  

The total Non-Instructional items portion of the recommended budget is $15,370,221 representing a 16.4% increase over the 2002-03 budget.

Mr. Reese explained that if the recommended budget is defeated twice, a contingency budget must be adopted.  A contingency budget is restricted to a 1.92% increase over the 2002-03 total budget.

Key budget dates are:
April 8, 2003   - Instructional Budget Items
April 22, 2003 - Budget review (if necessary)
April 23, 2003 - Board budget adoption
May 6, 2003    - Budget Hearing
May 20, 2003  - Budget Vote

Dr. Beni introduced Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman for the presentation of the Instructional Items portion of the recommended budget.

Instructional Items – Supplemental Log – Appendix C
Due to increased enrollment and program requirements, the 2003-04 budget includes 10.0 new teaching positions and the addition of a 1.0 Nurse’s aide.  A significant offset to the cost of the new teaching positions is the savings realized by teacher retirements as of 6/30/03. 





The budget provides for full-day Kindergarten, inroduction to World Language – Grade 5 and several new offerings at the High School.  Dr. Dolan-Waldman noted that the summer school program will be eliminated this year.  

The total Instructional cost for 2003-04 is $19,901,672 which represents a 2.3% increase over last year.

Following discussion and questions, Mrs. Silverberg thanked Dr. Beni, Mr. Reese and Dr. Dolan-Waldman for their  presentations.

Items on the agenda for the April 22, 2003 Board meeting include a Master Plan update and further 2003-04 budget discussion.  Mrs. Silverberg adjourned the Public Work Session at 11:50 p.m..
