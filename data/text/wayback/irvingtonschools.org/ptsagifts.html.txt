IRVINGTON PTSA GRANTS 2001-02
The Irvington PTSA is dedicated to the enrichment of our students and teachers in the District.  Towards this end, one of our many roles is to fund grants that will enhance the daily curriculum, education and everyday life of students and teachers at Dows Lane Elementary School, Irvington Middle School and Irvington High School.  Throughout the 2001-2002 school year, we have sought grants from teachers and administrators that are:
- Curriculum-based
- Beneficial to as many students in a grade as possible
- Grade-specific pilot programs that could be expanded if successful.
Following is a list of grants the Irvington PTSA wishes to donate to the School District for the current school year:
Dows Lane Elementary
- Fifth Grade Clearwater Hudson River program
- Fifth Grade Westward Expansion book series for all eight classrooms
- Third Grade Clearwater Hudson River program
TOTAL:  $3,200.00
Irvington Middle School
- Sixth Grade playground equipment
- Seventh Grade outdoor education program
- Language Arts Department "Good Conversation" author videos
- Health Fair Speaker Bill Ross
TOTAL:  $3,912.00
Irvington High School
- Special Education Outdoor Education equestrian program
- "Tribute to Freedom" concert refreshments
TOTAL:  $400.00
The grand total of these programs at all three schools equals $7,512.00, which it gives us pride to donate to the District.  We have great confidence that the psychic and scholastic value of these programs is exponential in relation to our monetary investment.  We look forward to continuing our work together.
Sincerely,
Millie Fisk
PTSA President
