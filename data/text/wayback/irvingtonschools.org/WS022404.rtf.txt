The following are the minutes of the Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington Campus Presentation Room, February 24, 2004, with the following present:

Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mr. George Huzinec.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent T. Beni – Superintendent of Schools, Dr. Jennifer Dolan-Waldman – Assistant Superintendent for Instruction,  Mr. James Reese – Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal,  Ms. Raina Kor – Interim Middle School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Elementary School Principal,  Mr. Joseph DeGennaro – High School Assistant Principal, Mr. Donald Kuhn – Main Street School Assistant Principal, Ms. Eileen McGuire – Dows Lane Elementary School Assistant Principal, members of the faculty and community. 

CALL TO ORDER
	
The Public Meeting was called to order by Mrs. Penny Delany at 6:32 p.m.

A motion was made by Mrs. Janice Silverberg, seconded by Mr. George Huzinec, to adjourn to Executive Session for the purpose of discussing the employment history of particular person(s).

The Public Work Session convened by Mrs. Penny Delany at 7:45 p.m., the pledge of allegiance was given.

Pupil Personnel Services Report – Supplemental Log-Appendix A

Dr. Beni introduced Ms. Gottlieb for the annual update report of the Pupil Personnel Services Department.  Ms. Gottlieb outlined the changes and transitions that have occurred this year in the CSE and CPSE, including monthly meetings with the parent group as well as the development of a website for parents, the move to new quarters which has provided space for testing and a conference room as well as space for staff and files.  The department has been reorganized with three (3) secretaries whose rolls have been redefined, cross-training of staff, file reorganization and revision of forms and procedures.  

The Pupil Personnel Services Department is comprised of psychologists, special education teachers, speech and language therapists, a hearing impaired teacher, occupational therapists, physical therapist, guidance counselors, ESL teacher, a grant writer, the Instructional Support Team, a behaviorist, teacher of the blind and teacher aides.  

Ms. Gottlieb updated the Board regarding the Academic Intervention Services and the Section 504 accommodation requirement for individual student special issues.  

Future developments include an autistic class for the Main Street School, creation of a class at the High School for emotionally disturbed students, a High School class with a focus on life skills and the incorporation of a co-teaching model (collaborative pairs).

Ms. Deborah Hardy, the Guidance Department Chairperson, reviewed the reorganization of the work assignments   in the High School Guidance Department as well as the procedural changes and restructuring of tasks for the clerical support.  Ms. Hardy also outlined the areas of responsibility and expertise of the counselors in their daily duties of addressing student needs.  In addition to on-going professional development, future goals include new programs, new forms, collaboration and curriculum changes, a college center for students, expansion of guidance services for the middle and elementary schools and involvement in the planning for a comprehensive K – 12 program.  The District shall consider hiring an additional 11th and 12th grade guidance counselor due to the rising enrollment and increased responsibilities of the Guidance Department.           
 
Tri-State Consortium Report – Supplemental Log-Appendix B

Dr. Dolan-Waldman described the Tri-State Consortium assessment process.  The consortium is a composed of a group of “critical friends” whose role is to assess the District for the purpose of developing and using an assessment model, communicating and sharing methods of bringing about continuous improvement and creating a vision of what is possible to achieve in the District.  The primary focus of the assessment model is student performance.







The Consortium is expected to make a  3 day site visit to the District in October of 2004.  Following the visit, a report will be provided to the District.  One of the goals of this process is to achieve accreditation for Irvington High School.  

Following discussion, Mrs. Delany thanked Dr. Dolan-Waldman for her presentation. 

The Work Session was adjourned to Executive Session, at 9:40 p.m., for the purpose of discussing the employment history of particular person(s).
