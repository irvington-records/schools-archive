The following are the minutes of the Reorganization Meeting of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Campus Presentation Room, June 29, 2004, with the following present:

Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mrs. Carol Keller Gutman.  Also present:  Dr. Vincent Beni - Superintendent of Schools, Mr. James Reese - Assistant Superintendent for Business, and members of the community and faculty.  Absent:  Mr. Paul Mandel   

I	CALL TO ORDER AND PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

The Public Meeting was called to order by Ms. Linda McMullan at 5:37 p.m. and the pledge of  allegiance was given.

II	OATH OF OFFICE - NEW SCHOOL BOARD TRUSTEES AND SUPERINTENDENT OF SCHOOLS

Ms. Linda McMullan administered the Oath of Office to Mrs. Diane Mundree, re-elected to the Board of Education, term expiring June 30, 2007 and to Mrs. Carol Keller Gutman, elected to the Board of Education, term expiring June 30, 2007.  Congratulations were extended to Mrs. Mundree and Mrs. Gutman.

III	ELECTION OF BOARD OFFICERS

	A.  President

Mrs. Diane Mundree nominated Mrs. Penny Delany for the position of President of the Irvington Board of Education, seconded by Mrs. Janice Silverberg.  Ms. McMullan called for the vote and Mrs. Delany was elected President.
	      Vote:  4 - 0 Approved    				

	B.  Administer Oath

Ms. McMullan administered the Oath of Office to Mrs. Penny Delany, Board President.  Congratulations were extended.  Ms. McMullan turned the meeting over to Mrs. Delany.  

	C.  Vice President

Mrs. Janice Silverberg nominated Mrs. Diane Mundree for the position of Vice President of the Irvington Board of Education, seconded by Mrs. Carol Keller Gutman.  Mrs. Mundree was elected Vice President.
                    Vote:  4 - 0 Approved			

	D.  Administer Oath

INFORMATION ITEMS

Dr. Beni reported on the following:
All graduation and moving up ceremonies went well.  Due to enrollment increases, some ceremonial procedures will need to be altered.
There will be discussion regarding the location of the High School graduation location next year.
Manhattanville College has expressed reluctance to host the Magnet School for 2005-06

Dr. Beni thanked the Board for their support during his Superintendency of the Irvington School District.
