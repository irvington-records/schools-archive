The following are the minutes of the Work Session of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, held in the Irvington Campus Presentation Room, December 9, 2003, with the following present:

Mrs. Penny Delany, Mrs. Diane Mundree, Mr. Paul Mandel, Mrs. Janice Silverberg, Mr. George Huzinec.  Also present:  Mr. James Reese – Assistant Superintendent for Business, Dr. Scott Mosenthal – High School Principal, Mr. Joseph Rodriguez – Dows Lane Elementary School Principal, Ms. Raina Kor – Middle School Assistant Principal, Ms. Eileen McGuire – Dows Lane Elementary School Assistant Principal and members of the community. 

The Public Work Session was called to order by Mrs. Delany at 7:40 p.m.  

Superintendent Search Consultant Report

Mrs. Delany welcomed everyone to the meeting and introduced Ms. Jackie Roy from J. Roy Associates who is conducting the search for a new Superintendent of Schools.

Ms. Roy began her presentation with a review of the search conducted five (5) years ago.  The challenges for the new Superintendent were identified at that time:  reconfiguration of buildings and/or expansion, the impact of the new state standards on the school budget and articulation of curriculum.  Meeting these challenges became a reality and, with the growth, building and increased staffing, a new vitality and energy has emerged in the school district.

Ms. Roy explained that she met with 14 groups (120 people) to gather input from the various elements of the District to assist her in the search.  She asked each group to share their thoughts about the strengths and challenges to the District and what we are looking for in a new Superintendent:

Strengths

Students do well
Facilities are outstanding
Community support
Enthusiastic teaching staff
District culture and traditions
Shared vision
Family oriented environment
Strides made in curricula
Parents are enthusiastic and supportive
Location 
Strong administration
Teacher/student relationships are “solid”

Challenges

Anxiety due to rapid growth and changes in the District
Additional issues to address
Keeping up with growing diversity
Stronger academic programs
Maintaining the “personal touch”

Ms. Roy reported that the qualities most often mentioned as desirable in the new Superintendent were:  imagination, vision, a person who is “hands-on”, good communicator, educational leader, experience in a “high achieving” district, team builder, fiscally prudent, should possess qualities of integrity, intelligence and credibility.

Mrs. Delany asked if anyone in the audience had comments or questions for Ms. Roy.  Questions were presented to Ms. Roy and answered as follows:

Have we allowed enough time for the search process and hiring?

Yes.  We are currently advertising in the New York Times, Education Week and on the AASA website.  This is a national search.  


Will more credence be given to a New York, or specifically Westchester County, candidate?

No.  However, the cost of living in Westchester County may be a factor in the candidates decision making process.  

Will Irvington be competing with other districts in the area looking for Superintendents?

No.  We do not anticipate any competition from other, similar districts in the area.

What are the challenges, as seen from the viewpoint of the Board?

Mrs. Delany replied that the biggest challenges are the curriculum, staff hiring and training, making everything “work”, fine tuning everything that is currently in place.

What is the search time-frame?

The deadline for applications is January 30, 2004.  The applications will be reviewed and forwarded to the Board.  Approximately 5 – 7 candidates will be interviewed by the Board prior to the February recess.  It is anticipated that a candidate will be hired by mid-March, 2004.

After this meeting, when will the next information session be held?

No further information will be forthcoming until a candidate has been chosen.  This is due to the confidentiality of the interview process.

There being no further questions or comments from the community, Mrs. Delany thanked everyone for coming.

The meeting was adjourned at 8:40 p.m.
