Course Offerings & Descriptions
The Counseling Department has created a series of short, grade-specific videos to make sure everyone has the resources and time they need to make thoughtful, informed choices. These videos review key topics like graduation requirements, post-high school planning unique to that year, and other essential information for the rising class. We strongly encourage both students and parents to watch the video corresponding to the grade your student will be entering next year (e.g., if your student is currently a 9th grader, watch the "10th Grade Course Video"). Below you will find the course catalogs per grade. As always, please see your counselor with all questions regarding the scheduling process.  Happy Scheduling!
