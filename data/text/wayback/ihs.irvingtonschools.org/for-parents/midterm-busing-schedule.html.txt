Midterm Busing Schedule
**This schedule will be in effect Tuesday January 20-Monday January 26**
Home→School Buses
- All buses will do their regular AM morning rounds. Students who are taking 8AM exams will be picked up at their regular time.
- All buses will REPEAT their regular morning round two (2) hours later for students who are taking 10:30AM exams. (For example, if your student’s bus normally arrives at 6:55AM, it will pick them up at 8:55AM).
- All buses will again REPEAT their regular morning run five (5) hours later for students who are taking 1PM exams. (For example, if your student’s bus normally arrives at 6:55AM, it will arrive at 11:55AM).
School→Home Buses
- Buses will depart IHS at 10:15AM to take students home following their exam
- Another round of buses will depart IHS at 12:45PM to take students home following their exam
- A third round of buses will depart IHS at 3:15PM to take students home following their exam
