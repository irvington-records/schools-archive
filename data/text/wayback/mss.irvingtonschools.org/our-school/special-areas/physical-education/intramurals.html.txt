Intramurals
The intramural program takes place on Monday (boys) and Friday (girls) mornings from 8:00-8:40am. The intramural program is a great experience for the students of Main Street School because it allows them to spend extra time participating in PE activities they enjoy such as kickball, soccer, basketball and wiffleball. Please contact Laura Shaw with questions at laura.shaw@irvingtonschools.org
