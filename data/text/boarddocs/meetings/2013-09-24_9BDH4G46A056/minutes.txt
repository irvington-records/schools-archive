Audit Committee of the Irvington Union Free School District (Tuesday, September 24, 2013)

	Generated by Elaine Cardea on Thursday, September 26, 2013

		Present: Mr. Robert Grados, Mr. Phil Whitney, Mrs. Maria Kashkin, Mr. John Montgomery. Mrs. Kerner arrived at 6:36 pm.

		Also present: Dr. Kristopher Harrison, Superintendent of Schools, Ms. Beverly Miller, Asst. Superintendent for Business, Mr. Tim Doyle, Bonadio and Company, LLP

		Call to Order

		Mr. Grados called the meeting to order at 6:30 pm

		Review the 2012-13 Audit Report

		Mr. Tim Doyle, the District's external auditor, presented the 2012-13 Audit Report and Findings to the Audit Committee. Mr. Doyle reviewed the audit findings and stated that it was necessary to conduct any single audits because the District did not reach the $500,000 threshold for federal grants. Mr. Doyle also reviewed changes in 2012-13 and 2013-14 regulations.

		Adjournment

		Motion was made by Phil Whitney, second by Maria Kashkin to adjourn the meeting of the Audit Committee at 7:00 pm.

		Motion Approved

		Yes: Robert Grados, Robyn Kerner, Phil Whitney, Maria Kashkin, John Montgomery
