“We spent time during our post observation conference discussing the value of explicit teaching
for both content and process. You demonstrated your ability to be explicit and teach the
content during this lesson, but a place of growth for you is developing your lessons around
process, more specifically group work/discussions. I commend you for taking the risk and
making groups, moving away from the EngageNY script. This is a first step. While in groups,
many students worked as individuals. To move past this you will need to develop expectations
for working in a group, accountable talk strategies; both of these could be accomplished using
protocols with your students.”
