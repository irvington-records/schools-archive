                2014-15 District Goals
1.   Develop a long-term plan, including a clarification of the
     District’s values and priorities, an assessment of its
     achievement of those values and priorities and the
     development of a detailed 5-year plan for attaining the values
     and priorities.
2.   Provide top-quality instruction for all students
3.   Develop and maintain a curriculum that offers all students
     the opportunity to attain their academic potential.
4.   Ensure that each school provides a rich and supportive
     environment for all students, welcoming all students,
     fostering their academic and social development, and
     preparing them well for the next phase of their education.
5.   Uphold high standards for District staff.
6.   Use all taxes and other funds raised by the District efficiently
     and effectively, and maintain the District’s fiscal health to
     balance the interests of current and future taxpayers.

                    Theory of Action 1
  If we provide students with rigorous, authentic learning
  experiences rooted in a comprehensive curriculum, then they
  will acquire the knowledge, skills and dispositions of successful
  21st Century learners that will prepare them to thrive in a rapidly
  evolving global society.
Goal
  O In order to develop successful 21st Century learners that will be
       prepared to thrive in a rapidly evolving global society, the
       Irvington School District will:
       O provide students with rigorous authentic, learning experiences
       O develop a comprehensive curriculum that includes:
          O aligned and articulated content
          O defined learning outcomes
          O a balanced and systematic approach to assessment
          O 21st Century skills and dispositions - problem solver, flexible
             thinker, collaborative learner, effective communicator,
             empathetic citizen, and self-reliant, reflective, creative risk-taker.

                   Theory of Action 2
  If we value and foster the professional learning and growth
  of all members of our school community, then we will build
  our collective capacity to support student success.
Goal:
  O In order to build our collective capacity to support student
        success, the Irvington School District will:
        O engage all members of our school community in the process
          of continuous improvement through the articulation of
          District goals
        O involve all members of the school community in learning and
          developing the knowledge and skills to support student
          success
        O provide educators with professional learning designed to
          support student growth
        O provide all staff with professional learning to develop the
          skills necessary to meet District needs.

                     Theory of Action 3
   If we engage in a systematic approach of classroom observation,
   collecting data and offering feedback to one another, then evidence-
   based decisions will promote reflective practice, inform instructional
   design and guide professional learning in order to continuously
   improve/advance student achievement.
Goal
   O In order to continuously improve student achievement through
       professional learning, reflective practice, and instructional design, the
       District will:
       O engage all K-12 instructional leaders in various approaches to classroom
         visitation to collect evidence of teaching and learning
       O utilize the Instructional Rounds protocol as a tool to assess our progress
         toward our relevant theories of action and in addressing instructional goals
       O use the Framework for Teaching as the benchmark for instructional best
         practices
       O use the Annual Professional Performance Review as one indicator of student
         performance and teacher effectiveness.
