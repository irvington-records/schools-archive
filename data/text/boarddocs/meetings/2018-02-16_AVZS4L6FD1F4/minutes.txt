Special Meeting (Friday, February 16, 2018)

Generated by Elaine Cardea on Monday, February 26, 2018

Members present

Maria Kashkin, Deborah Hargraves, David Graeber, Brian Friedman

Also present

Kristopher Harrison, Supt. of Schools, Carol Stein, Asst. Superintendent for Business, Artie McCormack, Athletic Director

1. Call to Order and Pledge of Allegiance

Maria Kashkin called the meeting to order at 8:15am and the Pledge of Allegiance was said.

2. Business of the Board

Action:  2.1 Agreement with County Center

BE It RESOLVED that the Irvington Board of Education hereby authorizes the Superintendent or his designee to execute an agreement with the Westchester County Center (“County Center”) for use of the County Center for semi-final basketball games involving Irvington High School (IHS) teams, provided that the IHS teams enter the semi-final tournament as the higher seeded team and, therefore, would become the host school; with the costs and expenses of the use of the County Center to be paid from gate receipts (except for the cost of officials) and capped at the amount of gate receipts received. A copy of such agreement shall be incorporated by reference within the minutes of this meeting.

Motion by David Graeber, second by Brian Friedman.

Final Resolution: Motion Carries

Yes: Maria Kashkin, Deborah Hargraves, David Graeber, Maura Gedid, Brian Friedman
