Dear Ms. Friedlander:

       Thank you for the opportunity to submit a proposal for service to the Irvington Union
Free School District (the “District”) as a qualified hearing officer to perform on-site
Superintendent Hearings.

        As is reflected in the enclosed resume, from February 1998 through December 2010, I
practiced in the field of education law representing public school districts throughout New York
State. As general counsel to public school districts, I routinely appeared in student disciplinary
matters, convened in accordance with Section 3214 of the New York State Education Law, and
in special education impartial due process hearings and appeals, convened in accordance with the
Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (“IDEA”), 20 U.S.C. § 1400, et. seq., Article 89 of
the New York Education Law, and Part 200 of the Regulations of the Commissioner of
Education. I also defended client school districts in administrative proceedings before the Equal
Employment Opportunity Commission, the New York State Education Department, the New
York State Public Employment Relations Board, the New York State Division of Human Rights,
the New York State Department of Labor, and the New York State Department of Environmental
Conservation.

       Since December 2010, I have worked exclusively as a certified hearing officer for public
school districts in New York State. I regularly preside over, and prepare written decision for,
student discipline hearings, employee discipline hearings and special education impartial due
process hearings.

        Based on the experience described above, I am qualified to serve the District as a hearing
officer to perform on-side Superintendent Hearings. My law firm, located in Purchase,
New York, is independent of the District. I have no conflict of interest with regard to any other
work performed by the firm for the District. I will respond to needs, questions, and/or concerns
of the District in a timely manner; in most cases on the same day, but never greater than twenty-
four (24) hours after the initial contact is made by the District.

        It is my understanding that all student discipline hearings will be conducted at the
District’s administrative offices located at 6 Dows Lane, Irvington, New York or at such other
location as the Superintendent of Schools may designate.

       I charge a flat fee of $400 per hearing, which includes professional services rendered at
the hearing and preparation of a written Report and Recommendation to the Superintendent of
Schools.

       Thank you for your time and consideration of this proposal.
