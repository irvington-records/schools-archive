This Agreement made this 24" day of June 2021 by and between:

IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT
party of the first part, hereinafter referred to as the "Board,” and

THE DEVEREUX FOUNDATION dba DEVEREUX ADVANCED BEHAVIORAL
HEALTH CONNECTICUT

party of the second part, hereinafter referred to as the "Contractar"

WITNESSETH: The Board is authorized by law to contract with institutions for the instruction of

children with disabling conditions, and

WHEREAS, the Contractor warrants that it has reviewed the individualized education program

(IEP) of each pupil to be enrolled in its school and represents that it will provide the
level of services identified in the IEP to meet the needs of such children with disabling
conditions,

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties hereto mutually agree as follows:

1.

2a.

The Contractor will provide adequate instruction, in accordance with the provisions
relating to eligibility of schools contained in Part 200 of the Regulations of the
Commissioner of Education of the State of New York (hereinafter the "Commissioner
of Education") regarding individuals with Disabilities Education Act and Section 504 of
the Rehabilitation Act of 1973, for children with disabling conditions approved by the
Commissioner of Education to attend Contractor's school, The school year is hereby
defined as July 1, 2021 to June 30, 2022. The Contractor will provide a special
education program for eligible children with disabling conditions placed in the
Contractor's school in accordance with the provisions of Education Law Sections 4407
and 4408 and Part 200 of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education for that
part of the school year for which such children are approved for placement in such
school by the Commissioner of Education and will operate and administer its special
educational program consistent with all the terms and conditions required under
Section 200.7(b) of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education.

In full consideration for the educational services to be rendered by the Contractor to
the Board for the period July 1, 2021 - June 30, 2022 of each school year under the
terms of this Agreement, the Board will pay to the Contractor for each child the agreed
upon tuition rate approved by the Commissioner of Education and approved by the
Director of the New York State Division of the Budget. Where such education is
provided for periods of less than the duration of the full September through June
program, consideration will be prorated accardingly, the specific amount to be fixed by
the State of New York (hereinafter the “State”).

In full consideration for the educational and maintenance services to be rendered by
the Contractor for the July through August program component, the Board will pay the
Contractor the agreed upon tuition rate approved by the Commissioner of Education

[OCR]
and approved by the Director of the New York State Division of the Budget and the
agreed upon maintenance rate approved by the Commissioner of Social Services of
the State and approved by the Director of the New York State Division of the Budget
for each child. Where such education and maintenance are provided for less than the
duration of the full July through August program, consideration will be prorated
accordingly, the specific amount to be fixed by the State.

3a. Payments will be made upon receipt of invoices, The Board will pay the Contractor the
most recent approved rates. Upon receipt of updated current rates, the Contractor will
invoice at the new rates and The Board will pay at the updated rates, The rates and
Payments shall be retroactive to the effective date. The rates as established by the
New York SED and the New York DSS.

b. For the purposes of this Agreement, the definition and calculation of enrollment and
attendance shall be in accordance with Section 175.6 of the Regulations of the
Commissioner of Education as determined by the Board.

4. The Contractor will obtain whatever releases or other legal documents are necessary
in order that the Contractor may render full and complete reports concerning the
education and progress of the pupil or pupils covered by the terms of this Agreement.
The Contractor will render such reports to pupils covered by the terms of this
Agreement, and will render such additional reports as may be required by the State
because of interest by the State in the progress of the pupil, or pupils, covered by the
terms of this Agreement. It is understood and agreed by the parties to this Agreement
that the full responsibility for obtaining such clearances rests on the Contractor, and
any failure to carry out such responsibility shall permit the Commissioner of Education
to withdraw approval for the placement of such child in such program in which event
this Agreement shall be canceled forthwith in regard to such child for whom such
releases are not submitted.

5. Pursuant to Section 4405 of the Education Law, payment of maintenance expenses
for residential pupils for the September through June program shall be made by the
county wherein each such pupil resides (or the city in the case of a county contained
in the City of New York) at the commencement of the school year (July 1) for which
maintenance is paid. The charge for maintenance may not exceed the amount
approved for such purpose by the Commissioner of Social Services of the State and
the Director of the Budget of the State. Such payment should be made on the same
terms and conditions as tuition, but the payment schedule will be determined by the
appropriate county or city.

6. No parent or guardian or any other person shall be required to make any payment for
tuition or maintenance on behalf of any child with a disabling condition covered by this
Agreement in addition to the payments made by the State and the county or city.

7. The Contractor will maintain the standards set forth under Section 200.7(b) of the
Regulations of the Commissioner of Education to preserve its status as an approved school
for the education of children with disabling conditions. It is understood and agreed by the
parties that failure to do so shall render this Agreement void, in which case the Contractor

This Agreement shall take effect as of July 1, 2021.

a. Either the Board or the Contractor may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days
prior written notice to the other party. Such notice shall be given in accordance with
the requirements for all notices pursuant to this Agreement set forth below.

b, The parties agree that either party's failure to comply with any terms or conditions of
this Agreement will provide a basis for the other party to immediately terminate this
Agreement without any further liability to the party which violated the Agreement.

c. In the event the Board or the Contractor terminates this Agreement with or without
cause, such termination of the Agreement shall not discharge the parties’ existing
obligations to each other as of the effective date of termination.

The Contractor hereby agrees to furnish to the State all reports, audits, etc. required to
make determinations as to eligibility under the provisions of the Regulations of the
Commissioner of Education. Such materials shall be furnished at such times as are
required by the State. Failure to submit required materials within ten (10) days of demand
or as required by regulation shall constitute grounds for the Commissioner of Education to
withdraw approval for such program.

The Contractor agrees to provide the State access to all relevant records which the state
requires to determine either the Contractor's or the Board's compliance with applicable
Federal or State statutes or regulations with the effect of law, which regulate either the
execution of the Agreement or the performance of obligations under the Agreement. The
Contractor agrees to retain all materials and records relevant to the execution or
performance of the Agreement in accordance with the provision of Section 74.21 of Valume
34 of the Code of Federal Regulations, but in no event less than six years from the date of
the Agreement.

The Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

The Board shall have all of its common law rights of set-off. These rights and powers shall
include, but not be limited to, the Board's option to withhold for the purposes of set-off any
monies due to the Contractor under this contract up to any amounts due and owing fo the
Board with regard to this contract, or amounts due and owing to the Board for any other
reason.
