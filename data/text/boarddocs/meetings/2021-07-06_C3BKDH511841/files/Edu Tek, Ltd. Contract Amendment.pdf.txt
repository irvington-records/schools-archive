AMENDMENT

AMENDMENT made this Ist day of July, 2021 to the July 8, 2020 Agreement,
between the IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (hereinafter
referred to as the “School District”) and EDU TEK LTD. (hereinafter referred to
as the “Consultant”).

WHEREAS, the parties entered into an Agreement dated July 8, 2020, establishing the
rights and responsibilities of the Consultant and the School District for services to be provided
by the Consultant to the School District; and

WHEREAS, the parties are desirous of providing for amendment of said Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, based upon the mutual covenants and understandings between the
parties, it is understood and agreed:

FIRST: EFFECT OF THE AMENDMENT

All the terms and provisions set forth at length in the aforementioned Agreement shall
continue in full force and effect during the term thereof (prorated where applicable based
upon the extension term set forth below), except as expressly modified herein.

SECOND: TERM

The second paragraph on page 2 of the Agreement as well as the Project Commencement
paragraph on Page 10 shall be amended to reflect an extension of the term of the
Agreement as follows: July 1, 2021 through July 31, 2021. Said amendment shall be
effective on July 1, 2021.

IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT

Dated: By:

Board President

EDU TEK LTD.
