     FIRST ANNUAL RENEWAL OF INDEPENDENT
 CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE IRVINGTON
UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT AND HMB CONSULTANTS,
                      LLC

        An original agreement (“Agreement”) was made between the Irvington Union Free School
District (the “District”) located at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, New York 10533 and HMB
Consultants, LLC (“HMB”) located at 3 Douglas Lane, Voorheesville, New York
12186 (collectively the “Parties”) on June 6, 2020. The Agreement term concludes on June 30,
2021.


       NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the covenants, agreements, and consideration
expressed in the Agreement, the Parties hereto mutually agree to complete this First Annual
Renewal to extend the Agreement term for a period of one (1) year commencing on July 1, 2021
and ending on June 30, 2022. Except as expressly modified by this First Annual Renewal, all
terms and provisions of the Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.


        The Parties agree to increase the compensation paid to HMB beginning on July 1, 2021
pursuant to Paragraphs 2 and 4 of the Agreement. Accordingly, the amount paid to HMB by the
District for the School Year 2021-22 shall be $3,750.00 increased by the NY/NJ CPI-U published
for the month of May, 2021 (+3.2%), or $3,870.00.



       IN WITNESS HEREOF, the Parties hereto have executed this First Annual Renewal of
Independent Contractor Agreement as of the day and year written below:
