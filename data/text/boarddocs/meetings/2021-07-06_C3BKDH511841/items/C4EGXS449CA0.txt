The only changes to the 2021-22 version of the IUFSD Code of Conduct is the removal of language in Section VIII(2)(c): "Knowingly and unlawfully possessing marijuana in violation of Penal Law § 221.05. A single violation of § 221.05 will be a sufficient basis for filing a PINS petition.” and in Section X “and cannabis” as the prior language is not consistent with current New York State Law.
