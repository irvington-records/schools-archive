Meeting: Jul 06, 2021 - 2021-22 Reorganization Meeting/Regular Meeting

Category: 18. Business Office Matters
Subject: 18.7 Lease Agreement-Copiers
Type: Action (Consent)

The existing lease ends in September 2021.  The new lease will result in a monthly savings of $1,200 with increased allowances for black & white and color copies and at a lower per unit price.  The district leases 15 machines.  All current equipment will be replaced on the new lease.  The maintenance agreement covers all consumable items including toner, developer and fuel oil, excluding paper and staples.  Funds are budgeted for this expense annually.
