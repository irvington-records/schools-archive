SHERYL BRADY

Sheryl Brady was appointed to the Southern Westchester BOCES Board of Education in August, 2019 to
fill an unexpired term. She has been a resident of White Plains for 33 years and is in her 13" year on the
White Plains Board of Education, having been first elected in 2007. On the White Plains Board of
Education, Sheryl has served on the Curriculum, Finance/Audit, Special Education, and Policy
Committees and is Board liaison to the PTA Council. All four of her children are proud graduates of the
White Plains Schools.

Shery! holds a Bachelor's degree from Purchase College and a Master’s and Ph.D. in Mathematics from
Cornell University where she was a Danforth Fellow. Formerly, she was an Assistant Professor and Chair
of the Mathematics Department at Purchase College, and currently she is the Manager of Operations at
Bennett International, an educational consultancy company.

An active member of the White Plains school and greater community, Sheryl previously served as
President of the Post Road PTA and PTA Council and was a member of the District's Strategic Planning
Core Team, Annual Budget Committee and District Enrichment Committee as well as the High School

Safety Team. She teaches Religious School and was a member of the Board of Trustees at Congregation
Kol Ami.
