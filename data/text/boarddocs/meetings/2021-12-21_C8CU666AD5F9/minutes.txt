3. Administrators' Update

Information: 3.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison reviewed the communication he sent out this afternoon regarding the "Test to Stay" program the District will be implementing after the Holiday recess. This will allow asymtomatic students only that test negative over a period of 7 days to remain in school for learning. Additional information and links are included in this communication and other communications that will be sent out regarding this program. This will allow students to stay in school but not participate in an extracurricular activities. The District is also addressing how they access remote learning during quarantine. There will be access to remote instruction the next school day for students in quarantine. The District is taking steps to prepare for a need to go to virtual learning. Although there are no plans to go to virtual learning, there will be device distribution this week in the elementary schools. Students in grades 11 & 12, students can use their own device or can request a District device. All links are in the communication sent today. There is a Google form to fill out is students test positive for COVD during the break. If students are told to quarantine, they should not come to school on January 3rd. Cases are relatively low in the District. Dr. Harrison wished everyone a relaxing and happy holiday recess
