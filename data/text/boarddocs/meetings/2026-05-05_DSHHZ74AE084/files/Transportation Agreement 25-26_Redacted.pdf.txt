                                 AGREEMENT

This Agreement, dated April 27, 2026, by and between the IRVINGTON UNION FREE
SCHOOL DISTRICT, with offices located at 6 Dows Lane, Irvington, New York, 10553,
and                                 a student who resides within the boundaries of the
Irvington Union Free School District.


       WHEREAS,                    (“Parents”), are the parents and natural guardians of    .

(“Student”), a student with a disability residing within the boundaries of the Irvington

Union Free School District (“District”); and

       WHEREAS, the Parents and the District previously entered an agreement dated

October 9, 2024, under which the Parents agreed to provide transportation to the Student

to and from

for the 2024-25 and 2025-26 school years and the District agreed to reimburse the Parents for

the cost of such transportation; and

       WHEREAS, the Parties desire to modify the agreement as it pertains to the 2025-

2026 school year; now

       FOR GOOD AND VALUABLE CONSIDERATION, IT IS HEREBY STIPULATED

AND AGREED by and between the parties and their attorneys as follows:

       1.     Parents, individually and on behalf of Student, hereby waive any rights

they may have for District-provided transportation to and from                   during the

period beginning July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2026, including, but not limited to,

specialized transportation, specialized vehicles, aides, monitors, behaviorists, etc.

       2.     The parties hereby terminate the October 9, 2024 agreement as it pertains to

reimbursement for transportation of the Student during the 2025-2026 school year.

       3.     In full consideration for the waivers herein, the Parties mutually agree that

for the 2025-2026 school year, the District shall reimburse the Parents for transportation

expenses for the Student between the Student’s home and                , in an amount not

to exceed $150 per day. The parties acknowledged and understand that the Parents have

secured the services of an independent (third party) transportation provider and may

provide transportation using such independent provider.

       4.     In order to obtain the reimbursement contemplated in Paragraph 3, the

Parents shall submit an invoice and proof of payment, on a monthly basis, and payment

to the Parents shall be made within twenty (20) days from receipt of an invoice and proof

of payment from the Parents. The invoice shall include a certification that Parents

provided and or arranged for transportation to the Student together with proof of

Student’s attendance at             and proof of continued residency within the District.

The District shall give the Parent notice of any invoice dispute within twenty (20) days of

its receipt and reserves the right to withhold payment pending the resolution of this

dispute.

       5.     The Parents acknowledge that they are transporting the Student on their

own behalf and will not hold themselves, or any other person or entity, as employees of

the District. The District is not contracting with the provider and has no supervisory or

other responsibility for the transportation provider. Neither the Parents nor any provider

with whom they contract shall be considered as having employee status and shall not be

entitled to participate in any of the District’s workers’ compensation, retirement, fringe

benefits, unemployment insurance, liability insurance, disability insurance, or other




                                            2

similar employee benefit programs. Similarly, neither the Parents nor any person or

entity with whom they contract, shall be considered as having employee status for the

purposes of any other rights, privileges or benefits of employment by the District.

       6.     The Parents hereby agree that this Agreement and the reimbursement of

Parents shall not constitute a pendency obligation of the District.

       7.     The District does not admit, and specifically denies, any wrongdoing or

violation of any law, statute, regulation or policy, and is entering into this Agreement

solely for purposes of amicably resolving this issue.

       8.     The Parents and the District hereby agree that, except as required by law or

except pursuant to the direction of the New York State Commissioner of Education or

pursuant to an order of a Court of competent jurisdiction, the terms of this Agreement,

the consideration paid hereunder, the documents and correspondence between the

parties and the discussions and negotiations concerning the Agreement are deemed

strictly confidential and shall not be disclosed by any party to any person or entity not a

party to this Agreement. Notwithstanding the above, the Parents and the District may

reveal information concerning this Agreement to attorneys, accountants and/or tax

advisors, and educational professionals and consultants, in order to obtain professional

advice and/or to enforce this Agreement.

       9.     The Parents and the District acknowledge that this Agreement contains the

entire understanding reached between them, and that neither has relied upon any

statements, representations, agreements or warranties, except as stated herein.        No

amendment or modification of this Agreement shall be valid or binding unless reduced

         10.   This Agreement is subject to the approval of the District’s Board of

Education.

         11.   This Agreement may be executed in counterparts and exchanged between

the parties with the full force and effect as if all signatures had appeared on one

document.

         IT IS ACKNOWLEDGED by the Parents that they have read and understand the

foregoing Agreement; that they have had the opportunity to discuss this Agreement with

an attorney; that they have had the opportunity to have all the terms and conditions of

this Agreement clearly explained to them by an attorney; and that they have freely chosen

to make and enter into this Agreement, such consent not having been induced by fraud,

duress or any other undue influence.
