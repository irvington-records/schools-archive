Track and Field Lighting Use
● Intent to balance the needs of the school community and the needs of the surrounding neighborhoods.
● Primary use will be for IUSFD and Irvington Rec
● Field lights will be used during the months of August, September, October, November, March, April and
  May.
● Night contests will be scheduled to begin no later than 7:00 pm from Sunday to Thursday and 8:00 pm
  Friday and Saturday.
● Lights will be off 30 minutes after the completion of post event breakdown and clean up, or by 10:30 pm
  for Friday and Saturday events and 9:30 pm for any weekday (including Sunday evening) events or
  practices.
● If a contest is delayed because of injury, overtime, weather, or a signiﬁcant public safety event the lights
  will be turned off no later than 30 minutes after the conclusion of post event breakdown and clean up..
● We will publish the dates for night contests on the District website at the beginning of each season
● The use of an outdoor Public Address System (PA) will be allowed prior to and during a contest and must
  conclude at the end of the contest.
● The use of a PA system during lighted use will be limited to IUFSD events.
