1500-R PUBLIC USE OF SCHOOL FACILITIES REGULATION

Facility Use Charges and Fees, Application Procedures and Use of the
Auditorium/Athletic Areas
Since the school budget is primarily intended to defray the expenses of
operating and maintaining the school and its facilities for the benefit of the
District's school children, charges will be made to non-school groups for the
use of school facilities based on the Board's approved “Schedule of Charges
for the Use of Facilities,” and “Custodial Service Fee.” These charges have
been developed to cover costs to the district and ensure that school funds are
not used to support private purposes, however worthy in themselves.

Upon request, user fees may be modified or eliminated by the
Superintendent during the hours between 3:00 p.m. and 10:00 p.m. on
regularly scheduled school days, where there is no identifiable cost to the
district. Recognizing that custodians will not be able to complete their
regularly scheduled assignments when assisting outside users, a Custodial
Service Fee will be charged unless it is determined that no additional staffing
or significant extra cleaning is required. This custodial fee will be determined
by the number of participants and the number of rooms requested In
addition, any other identifiable costs that would not otherwise have been
incurred by the district will be charged.

All users shall pay both the Use of Facility Fee and Custodial Service Fee for
use of facilities on Saturdays, Sundays and Holidays, with the exception where
a mutual exchange of facilities is possible between the school and the
organization requesting use of the facilities. On weekends, the custodial fee
charge will include one additional hour of time, past the requested hours of
use to accommodate operational needs.

When it is determined that a fee is to be charged, the district retains the right
to condition use upon an applicant depositing with the District a sum
equaling the estimated costs and fees associated with the proposed use ten
(10) days in advance of the requested use.

Groups involved in fund-raising will be charged according to the fee schedule
with the exception of annual charitable fund raising activities approved prior

to the revision of this Policy or those approved in writing in advance by the
Superintendent.

Application Procedure and Rules for Use of District Facilities
  A.​ All applications for use of school facilities shall be made through the
      online portal which can be found here
      https://www.irvingtonschools.org/departments/business-operations/faci
      lities/facilities-requests.
      All applicants are advised that consideration for the rental/use of space
      is completed on a first come, first served basis. The district will begin to
      accept applications for the subsequent school year on July 15th
      preceding the respective school year.
   B.​ The applicant must clearly and completely describe the intended use of
       the district facility in the application.
   C.​ The district may request a roster or group list of students/residents for
       the user group for
   D.​ All applicants must review the Facility Use Policy and Regulation prior
       to submitting the application. All applications must be signed by an
       authorized agent of the group or organization requesting use. The
       applicant’s signature on the application shall attest to the group or
       organization’s intent to comply with all Board policies and regulations
       and to use district facilities strictly in accordance with the use described
       in the application.
   E.​ All applicants must agree to assume responsibility for all damages
       resulting from its use of district facilities. Proof of adequate insurance
       must be provided at the time of application.
   F.​ Users must provide adequate adult supervision to ensure respectful use
       of the facility, safety of the participants, and ensure participants use only
       the facility area assigned. User must clean the premises and return the
       premises to the original condition found upon entering (with the
       exception of special setup by our custodial staff). Users are required to
       provide their own security and familiarize themselves with emergency
       exits and procedures. Prior to the start of the user’s event, an
       announcement should be made to the group regarding emergency
       evacuation procedures, including pointing out fire exits and responding
       to fire alarms.

   G.​ Permits shall be valid only for the facility, use, dates and time specified
       in the permit. No adjustment to the permit is allowed except with the
       prior written approval of the Superintendent (or designee). Permits
       shall not be transferable.
   H.​ The Superintendent (or designee) is authorized to alter or cancel any
       permit if it becomes necessary to use the facility for school purposes or
       for other justifiable reasons and extenuating circumstances. Whenever
       possible, adequate and timely notification will be given.
   I.​ With regard to scheduling activities, the district retains the right to give
       preference to groups and organizations which are associated with or
       sponsored by the district.
   J.​ Athletic equipment shall only be moved by district personnel only.
   K.​ If an outside organization use or activity results in damage to district
       equipment, fields, or facilities, the reserves the right to assess damages
       and issue invoices accordingly.
   L.​ Issuance of a permit shall not limit the right of access to the facility by
       district staff.
   M.​The use or sale of alcohol, tobacco, drugs or other intoxicants is strictly
      prohibited from district grounds and facilities.
   N.​ Any items left on premises by any group are not the responsibility of the
       District.


Fee Schedule
The fee schedule will be annually adopted by the Board of Education. Said
rates shall be posted on the district website. Custodial fees will be charged for
groups at a ratio of one (1) custodian to 100 participants and/or a rate of one (1)
custodian per major space area (gym, cafeteria, theater).

Campus Auditorium
  A.​ Most groups using the High School Auditorium will require the services
      of a district-approved stage manager. This determination is made after
      consultation with the facilities use coordinator. The fee for a stage
      manager will be paid by the group to the stage manager. When the
      program requires use of the full stage, light board, dressing rooms, etc.,
      a stage manager will be required. If the program requires only house
      lights, microphone and podium, the custodial staff can handle the

      arrangements and groups will be charged at an hourly rate with a three
      (3) hour minimum.
   B.​ Rental arrangements shall be made through the facilities use
       coordinator with a list of dates and times of use sent in advance
       (Facilities Use Form). The group will notify the facility use coordinator of
       the district-approved stage manager.
   C.​ User groups should determine, and specify on the application form, the
       nature of the equipment they need and the extent of the services
       required including the following:
         a.​ Use of lighting equipment
         b.​ Use of sound equipment
         c.​ Use of stage wing space and adjoining corridor(s)
         d.​ Dressing rooms
         e.​ List of equipment to be hung or mounted (lights and audio)
         f.​ Stage sets to be used and/or hung
   D.​ No equipment may be moved, changed or reset without consent of the
       district facilities manager.
   E.​ Nothing may be affixed in any manner (nails, screws or cleats) to any
       area of the stage or auditorium.
   F.​ No modifications may be made in the electrical panel or sound system
       without the express permission of the Assistant Superintendent for
       Business or his/her designee.
   G.​ The stage must be struck within twenty-four hours of the final
       performance and all equipment returned to its original place.
   H.​ Set-up time, if needed, shall be requested in writing on the facilities
       application form. Groups will be charged for all time that the
       auditorium is set up for their use and not able to be used by the district
       by other groups.

Field Markings
No user group shall be permitted to perform any field markings on any
district field unless approved by the district in its sole discretion, and any such
approved field markings shall be subject to an additional usage fee as such
fee may be set by the Board of Education from time to time. No field markers
are permitted on Meszaros Field.

Athletic Equipment
User groups must gain necessary approval to use district athletic equipment
such as goals. Athletic equipment shall be moved by district personnel only.
Should the user wish to use their own equipment, such use, as well as the
plan for storage of such equipment if on district property, shall be subject to
the approval of the district in its sole discretion.

Meszaros Field Rules and Regulation
To help preserve our community’s investment in the IUFSD athletic facilities,
the following standards must be followed. There is to be absolutely no:
         ●​ Metal cleats
         ●​ Seeds (example: Sunflower, Pumpkin, etc.)
         ●​ Food
         ●​ Gum
         ●​ Driving of stakes
         ●​ Tobacco and Vaping products
         ●​ Alcohol
         ●​ Sports drinks
         ●​ Pets
          ●​ Use non-authorized recreational equipment such as bicycles,
             skateboards, etc.
Any spills or damage must be immediately reported to an IUFSD staff
member who will implement corrective action. Violators of these regulations
will have their access revoked and will be responsible for any related repair or
replacement costs.

Track and Field Lighting Use
The following parameters for Field Lighting are set forth with the intent to
balance the needs of the school community and the needs of the
surrounding neighborhoods. The use of field lights at Meszaros Field/Oley
Track will be consistent with 1500 Public Use of School Facilities. Ordinarily,
field lights will be used during the months of August, September, October,
November, March, April and May.

Night contests will be scheduled to begin no later than 7:00 pm from Sunday
to Thursday and 8 pm Friday and Saturday. Lights will be off 30 minutes after

the completion of post event breakdown and clean up, or by 10:30 pm for
Friday and Saturday events and 9:30 pm for any weekday (including Sunday
evening) events or practices. If a contest is delayed because of injury,
overtime, weather, or a significant public safety event and cannot be
completed in time, the lights will be turned off no later than 30 minutes after
the conclusion of post event breakdown and clean up..

The Director of Physical Education, Health & Athletics will publish the dates
for night contests on the District website at the beginning of each season and
will announce any schedule changes on the website.The use of an outdoor
Public Address System (PA) will be allowed prior to and during a contest and
must conclude at the end of the contest. The use of a PA system during
lighted use will be limited to IUFSD events.

All questions on usage requirements should be directed to the Director of
Facilities.
