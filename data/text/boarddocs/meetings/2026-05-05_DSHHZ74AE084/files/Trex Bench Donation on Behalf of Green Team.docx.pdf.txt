April 29, 2026




Dear Irvington Board of Education,

Irvington Green Team recently completed a community-wide recycling project,
collecting plastic film which will be recycled and reused by the company Trex. Our
efforts have been rewarded with a Trex bench made from recycled plastic. We would like
to award the bench to Main Street School to celebrate their recycling and environmental
efforts. Principal Chapnick has accepted the offer and we hope you approve this gift.

Thank you,
Anna Plundo, volunteer, on behalf of Irvington Green Team
