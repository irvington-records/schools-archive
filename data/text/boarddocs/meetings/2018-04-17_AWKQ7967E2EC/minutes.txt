3. 2018-19 Budget Adoption

Discussion: 3.1 2018-19 Budget Overview

Dr. Harrison gave a final overview of the proposed 2018-19 budget and is grounded in the District's vision for tomorrow, focusing on ensuring that the very best is provided to students. The strategic objectives are linked many aspects of the budget that has been developed. Highlights of the budget include new. considerations which total $513,637 whichand invests in student opportunities, security and facility enhancements. Dr. Harrison again noted that the budget is very conservative and need to keep a careful, cautious eye moving forward. Proposed budget is $61,348,175, budget variance of $2,247,681 or a 3.8% variance. Dr. Harrison provided information on what it would mean to go to a contingent budget should voters defeat the proposed budget, i.e. $2.2 million would be required to be cut, allowing the budget to only grow by $24,433. However, he is very hopeful to earn the support of the community once again. The Budget Hearing will be held on May 1st at 7:15pm and May 15th will be the budget vote and trustee election. There are other community opportunities to learn more about the budget. There are also resources on the website available.

Action: 3.2 Adoption of 2018-19 Budget

Resolved, that the Irvington Union Free School District's 2018-19 Proposed Budget be set at $61,348,175 and that this proposed budget be voted on by the community at the Annual Meeting on May 15, 2018 between the hours of 7:00 am and 9:00 pm at the Irvington Main Street School, 101 Main Street, Irvington, New York 10533.

Motion by Maria Kashkin, second by David Graeber.
