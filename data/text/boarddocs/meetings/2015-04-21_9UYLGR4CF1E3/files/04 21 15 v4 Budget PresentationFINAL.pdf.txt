2015-16 Budget Considerations
    The 2015-16 proposed budget includes initiatives designed to enhance student opportunities and support the
    maintenance of District facilities. The considerations include:
     Increase Mathematics AIS teaching staff by .8 FTE at Dows and .2 Main St.
     Expand Project Lead the Way programming at the HS
     Introduce Project Lead the Way at the elementary and middle schools
     Expand Fundations phonics and handwriting program to grade 2
     Restore the 6-12 Literacy Coach
     Increase high school opportunity for students to attend Occupational Educational programs
     Increase special education teaching staff by 1.0 FTE
     Introduce a 6-12 Special Education Chairperson
     Increase school psychologist staff by .50 FTE
     Increase English as a Second (New) Language faculty by 0.5 FTE
     Restore full-time social worker position
     Increase high school clerical staff by 1.0 FTE
     Provide a security/greeter position at Dows Lane administration entrance
     Purchase necessary office and classroom furniture, and field equipment
     Restore a maintenance position
     Renovate to high school girls’ bathroom
     Perform necessary repairs to the District’s facilities



6

Continued Focus on Technology Instruction & Integration
 Supports technology integration via 5 year lease-purchase:
   180 Chromebooks and 6 mobile charging carts
   50 new computers
   Replacement of printers, SmartBoards, Projectors, etc.
 Technology lease purchase details:
     The District intends to enter into a 5 year lease-purchase agreement, in
     an amount not to exceed $195,000, for the purpose of purchasing
     technology equipment which will be solely used for instructional
     purposes. Funding is included in the 2015-16 budget to pay the first
     payment in an amount not to exceed $40,050 (principal & interest) for
     year 1 of 5. The first payment will be made prior to the end of the 2015-
     16 year.




7

Proposed Budget Plans for the Future
    Create a capital reserve fund
     A Capital Reserve Fund, like the District’s Tax Certiorari
      Reserves, are funds that are set aside for an express purpose
     Allows the District to save money for future construction
      projects or improvements to facilities.
     Such a fund cannot be created or spent without voter approval.
     Where do funds come from? Funds can be budgeted or allocated
      from fund balance.
