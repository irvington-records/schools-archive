1. LICENSE. Subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein, Licensor hereby grants to
Licensee certain limited use and access to certain portions of its Facility on certain dates and

public. Any normally restricted area use will need to be signed off by the applicable Licensor's
management prior to use.

2. PERSONS COVERED. Only Licensee’s members, prospective members, and invitees are
licensed by this Agreement to use the Facility.

3. TERM. The term of this Agreement shall commence on November 17 2026
(‘Commencement Date”) and shall expire on August 31°! 2027 (Expiration Date”). The period
beginning on the Commencement Date and ending on the Expiration Date is, hereinafter, the
“Term.”

4. LICENSE FEE. in consideration for the license granted pursuant to this Agreement, Licensee
shall pay to the Licensor all fees for all times specified in “Licensee’s Use Schedule,” and in
accordance with the payment schedule set forth therein (the “License Fee”), without set offs or
deductions. The License Fee shall also include such other amounts set forth in Licensee’s Use
Schedule. Licensee shall pay the full amount of the License Fee whether or not Licensee uses
the Facility for such times set forth in Licensee’s Use Schedule. Licensee Fees shall be due
and payable monthly in advance unless otherwise invoiced by Licensor.

5. LICENSOR’S DUTIES. Licensor will allow nonexclusive access to its Facility one (1) hour
prior to scheduled time, if necessary, all in accordance with the Licensor's customary practices.
For purposes of clarification, Licensee will not have access to the rink until the scheduled start
time.

6. LICENSEE’S DUTIES. Licensee’s duties shall include inspection of the Facility subject to this
Agreement. Licensee shall have the sole responsibility to inspect the Facility it is using prior to
each and every use (including, but not limited to, the ice surface, glass, boards, doors, gates,
spectator stands and locker rooms). Licensee shall immediately notify Licensor, prior to any use
of the Facility, of any potential problems or defects that may be observed by Licensee.
Furthermore, Licensee shall ensure that, during any use of a Facility, if a defect or safety hazard
is observed, Licensee will immediately cease all activity in the Facility until such time that the
defect or safety hazard has been corrected. Licensee shall have the sole responsibility for the
orderly crowd control and safety of the crowd during the licensed times set forth in Exhibit 1 and
such other times when it is using a Facility. Moreover, Licensee shall obtain an Assumption of
Risks and Indemnity Agreement of every participant (including the written consent of the parent
or guardian for minor participants) in a manner and form satisfactory to Licensor.

7. ASSUMPTION OF RISK. Licensee, and all its members, its prospective members, invitees
of Licensee and other persons that access or use the Facility under or pursuant to this Agreement
for any purpose hereby expressly assumes all risks and dangers incidental to such intended use.
These risks and dangers include, but are not limited to, the danger of being injured while on the
ice surface, pucks, hockey sticks, other players or skaters, skates, training apparatus, nets,
boards, wet surfaces outside the rinks, or any other element incidental to the use of the Facility
by Licensee or its members, prospective members, invitees or any other persons that access or
use the Facility under or pursuant to this Agreement for any purpose. This provision shall survive
the expiration or earlier termination of this Agreement.

                                     CODE OF CONDUCT

A.   Drug Policy:

     1. Licensor does not allow the use, sale, or possession of any illegal drugs at the
        Facility. All violators will be prosecuted.

     2. The use of tobacco, in any form, is prohibited within the Facility. Persons of legal
        age may use tobacco outside the Facility (i.e., smoke cigarettes, cigars, or pipes), to
        the extent legally permitted. Minors are prohibited from using any tobacco
        substances on the Facility's premises.

     3. Anyone under the influence of alcohol may not participate in any event within the
        Facility. All other alcohol laws set by the state will be enforced.

     4. Any person(s) found to be in violation of the drug, alcohol, or tobacco policies listed
        here may be excluded from the Facility.

B.   Coaches, managers, and other Licensee representatives will be required to control the
     conduct of their team's players both on and off the ice.

C.   The consumption of food and flavored drinks is prohibited unless prior arrangements
     have been made with the Licensor management.

D.   A Licensor supervisor will issue locker rooms in exchange for a valid driver's license or a
     set of car keys. The supervisor will inspect the locker room with the organization
     representative (the "key holder") before and after the use by each team. If there is any
     damage to said locker room(s) following the use, the organization will be held liable to
     repair said damages. Costs to be determined by Licensor management. Minors may not
     check out the locker rooms.

E.   All acts of vandalism performed by players, coaches, or spectators during the ice rental
     will be the financial responsibility of the Licensee. Facility's staff, at its own discretion,
     may prosecute the violator, prohibit him/her from entering the premises, and/or cancel
     the scheduled event.

F.   Licensor will not be responsible for any lost or stolen items.

G.   Absolutely no storage of hockey related equipment in the lobby and/or hallways. All
     hockey related equipment must be taken to the designated locker room or stored in a
     vehicle in the parking lot until a locker room is checked out to an authorized person.
     Teams are allowed to store their equipment in said locker room until the last person is
     ready to leave or until the next group/team arrives requiring the use of said locker room.

H.   Skaters must wait to take the ice until a referee or coach is present and is observing the
     activity.

I.   There will be no skaters allowed on the ice surface while the zamboni is in operation.
     Skaters may take the ice once the doors are closed.
