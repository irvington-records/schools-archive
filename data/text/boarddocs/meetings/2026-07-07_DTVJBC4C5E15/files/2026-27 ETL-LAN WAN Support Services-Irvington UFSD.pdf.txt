PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Working with the Irvington UFSD (District), Edu Tek Limited (Edu Tek) will provide comprehensive
Information Technology (IT) services at each school operated by the District (collectively referred to
herein as the Sites).

This Agreement shall start on July 1, 2026 and end on June 30, 2027, unless terminated sooner in
accordance with the provisions of this Agreement. Edu Tek will support the District’s Local Area
Networks (LAN’s) and Wide Area Network (WAN’s). This service includes support for all operating
systems currently in use at all Sites. The IT support described will be performed by:
       •       1.0 On-site Support Engineer Level 1
       •       2.0 On-site Network Engineer Level 2


The onsite team is supported by a Technology Services Coordinator (TSC) and/or an Operations
Manager (OM) who is also a top-tier engineer. The assigned TSC will provide executive level support
to the Director of Technology to ensure issues are resolved quickly and any necessary resources
are assigned to service the needs of the District. The TSC is available as needed and will be
provided to the extent necessary to support the Scope of Services outlined below.

Technology Management
       •   Make recommendations in the development of the District technology plan.
       •   Assist when requested in budget development by providing analysis of District’s
           networking needs and recommending hardware and network software to align with
           District needs and goals.
       •   Develop criteria and attend to details of the annual technology deployment plan.
       •   Meet with the District’s data consultant teams to discuss technology requirements for
           District participation in data warehousing.
       •   Assist in developing, implementing and supporting solutions as per the District
