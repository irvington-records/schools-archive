DOMINICAN UNIVERSITY NEW YORK
AFFILIATION AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT made as of the 1s! day of September, 2026 by and between the IRVINGTON
UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (hereinafter referred to as the “SCHOOL DISTRICT”), as the
party of the first part, having its principal place of business located at 40 North Broadway,
Irvington, New York 10533, and DOMINICAN UNIVERSITY NEW YORK (hereinafter referred
to as “UNIVERSITY”), as the party of the second part, having its principal place of business for
purposes of this Agreement at 470 Western Highway, Orangeburg, New York 10962.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, UNIVERSITY provides courses of study for those students preparing to
become occupational and physical therapists; and

WHEREAS, fieldwork experience and/or practicum are required and are an integral
component of becoming occupational and physical therapists; and

WHEREAS, SCHOOL DISTRICT is willing to accept said students from UNIVERSITY for
such purposes and upon the terms and conditions hereinafter set forth.

NOW, THEREFORE, it is mutually understood and agreed upon by and between the
parties hereto as follows

1 The term of this Agreement is for the period of September 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027,
unless terminated earlier as set forth herein. This Agreement may be renewed for additional one-
year periods upon mutual written consent of the Parties.

2. UNIVERSITY may send students in designated numbers and at such times as shall be
agreed upon by the parties, to SCHOOL DISTRICT for placement. The specific period spent at
SCHOOL DISTRICT will be consistent with the curriculum obligations for observation and field
work experience of students, as set out in the course description and objectives of the Program
and the ability of each SCHOOL DISTRICT school to accommodate them:

cy SCHOOL DISTRICT shall make its personnel, premises and facilities available to
UNIVERSITY’s students in the Program and shall communicate with UNIVERSITY’s staff about
the student's progress, in order to implement the Program at SCHOOL. DISTRICT, to the extent

necessary and feasible in SCHOOL DISTRICT’s sole discretion.

4 Subject to SCHOOL DISTRICT’s control of its own educational program, UNIVERSITY
shall be responsible for the planning and administration of the Programs including the
curriculum content, faculty appointments, student enrollment, accrediting, tuition, matriculation,
grading, and graduation. UNIVERSITY shall keep all records and reports on students’ Program
experience. In the event SCHOOL DISTRICT requires access to such records to demonstrate
compliance with any applicable law and/or regulation, UNIVERSITY shall cooperate with
SCHOOL DISTRICT to provide access to such records

[OCR]
5, UNIVERSITY shall designate a Program Director who shall be responsible for
coordination of the Programs. Such Program Director will select candidates for observation and
field work experience and shall forward the names of the students to SCHOOL DISTRICT prior
to the start of each semester, or as determined by SCHOOL DISTRICT. Approval and placement
of all candidates is subject to the discretion of SCHOOL DISTRICT. Prior to placement by the
Program Director, SCHOOL DISTRICT shall receive a letter from the student participant,
required documents and program guidelines from UNIVERSITY, the student participant's
resume and such other documents SCHOOL DISTRICT deems necessary for placement.

6 All of UNIVERSITY’s student Program participants shall observe and participate in the
Program under the direct supervision of SCHOOL DISTRICT

Ve The number of students to be assigred to the Program(s), cluding their schedules and
dates of assignment at SCHOOL DISTRICT, shall be mutually arranged and agreed to in advance
of their placement at SCHOOL DISTRICT. In the event that SCHOOL DISTRICT deems it
necessary to cancel a reserved space due to circumstances beyond its reasonable control, to the
extent possible, it agrees to notify UNIVERSITY at least one month in advance of the
commencement date of the student assignment or as soon thereafter as possible. If in SCHOOL
DISTRICT's discretion no student is accepted for assignment at SCHOOL DISTRICT, the Program
herein shall not be activated for that particular term. Notwithstanding the foregoing, SCHOOL
DISTRICT shall have sole discretion to determine whether any students shall be admitted to
SCHOOL DISTRICT for the Program. SCHOOL DISTRICT may terminate any student's
fieldwork, at any time for good cause, as determined by SCHOOL DISTRICT in its sole discretion.

8. SCHOOL DISTRICT shall make available appropriate observation and mentoring
experiences. The experiences provided shall be in accordance with all applicable Federal, State
and local laws, rules, regulations and ordinances and shall not infringe on any SCHOOL.
DISTRICT student or staff rights. All student observation and mentoring experience shall be done
under the supervision of a designated SCHOOL DISTRICT representative. Nothing herein shall
imply the students shall provide treatment or deliver services to students

9. In case of an emergency during the observation and mentoring experiences, SCHOOL
DISTRICT will seek emergency care for UNIVERSITY’s students and faculty via calling 911. With
the exception of emergency treatment or care, UNIVERSITY’s students and faculty will provide
for their own medical care needs.

10. The students shall not be deemed employees of SCHOOL DISTRICT and SCHOOL
DISTRICT shall pay no stipend or other consideration to students or faculty, nor shall
UNIVERSITY or its students pay any consideration to SCHOOL DISTRICT or its employees with
respect to this Agreement. UNIVERSITY, its employees and students shall not share or accept any
fee or gratuity from any SCHOOL DISTRICT students or students’ family for services provided
pursuant to this Agreement.

11 Transportation of students to and from SCHOOL DISTRICT shall be the sole
responsibility of the students.

12. The relationship of the parties hereto is that of independent contractors. Nothing in this
Agreement shall be deemed to constitute a partnership, joint venture, association or similar
relationship between the parties hereto or constitute either party as agent for the other for any
purpose whatsoever. Neither party shall have the authority to bind the other, or to contract in the
name of or create a liability against the other as against any third-party in any way or for any
purpose. Neither party shall be entitled to any benefits from the other party other than those
conferred under this Agreement

13. UNIVERSITY shall advise students of their responsibility to conduct themselves in
accordance with SCHOOL DISTRICT’s rules and regulations. UNIVERSITY will provide each
student with a copy of all such rules and regulations and the students will be responsible for this
information. SCHOOL DISTRICT reserves the right to dismiss at any time any student who is
unacceptable to SCHOOL DISTRICT for reasons of health, unacceptable practices or
performance, or whose condition or conduct jeopardizes tre well-being of the suudents or
employees of SCHOOL DISTRICT. Unless unusual or extenuating circumstances occur, such
dismissal shall not occur without prior consultation with UNIVERSITY. It is understood and
agreed that while on school grounds, UNIVERSITY’s students shall obey all of SCHOOL
DISTRICT’s rules and regulations and must follow all reasonable directives of SCHOOL
DISTRICT’s administrators and employees. UNIVERSITY shall furnish each of its employees and
students with a photo identification badge to be worn at all times while they are on-site at

SCHOOL. DISTRICT.

14, UNIVERSITY shall require that students have such physical examinations, tests, and
vaccinations as to determine that the students are not in the infectious stage of any communicable
disease, and are not habituated or addicted to a depressant, stimulant, narcotic, alcohol, or other
drugs or substances which may alter behavior or impair the performance of duties. UNIVERSITY
shall cooperate and direct students to make such records available, upon request by SCHOOL
DISTRICT, as are necessary to assure compliance with this provision.

15. UNIVERSITY’s students must be knowledgeable of the fact that all information obtained
during their observation and field work experience at SCHOOL DISTRICT must be kept
confidential in accordance with all applicable Federal and State laws and regulations including
but not limited to, Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (“HIPAA”), the
Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (“FERPA”), and New York Education Law § 2-d, and
must not be removed from SCHOOL DISTRICT without the approval of SCHOOL DISTRICT
UNIVERSITY, its employees, agents and stuclents agree that all information obtained in
connection with the services provided for in this Agreement is deemed confidential information.
UNIVERSITY, its employees, agents and students shall not use, publish, discuss, disclose or
communicate the contents of such information, directly or indirectly with third parties, except as
provided for in this Agreement. Such information shall not be released except as required by
governmental regulations. UNIVERSITY shall advise all students in the Program to respect the
confidential nature of all information which may come to them with regard to students receiving
treatment and/or therapy and SCHOOL, DISTRICT’s records in accordance with the Health
Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and other applicable laws, rules
and regulations. Both parties to this Agreement understand that they may receive and or come
into contact with protected health formation as defined by the Health Insurance Portability and
Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA). The parties hereby acknowledge their respective

17. UNIVERSITY’s faculty engaged in carrying out the provisions of this Agreement at
DISTRICT shall be advised by SCHOOL DISTRICT of its rules and regulations with which they
are expected to comply. UNIVERSITY shall provide each UNIVERSITY’s faculty member under
this Agreement a copy of such rules and regulations. At the request of SCHOOL DISTRICT,
UNIVERSITY shall immediately suspend from SCHOOL DISTRICT any faculty member who
fails to obey such rules. It is understood and agreed that while on SCHOOL DISTRICT grounds,
UNIVERSITY employees shall obey all of SCHOOL DISTRICT’s rules and regulations and must
follow all reasonable directives of SCHOOL DISTRICT’s administrators and employees.

18. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon fifteen (15) days’ written notice to the
other party. This Agreement may be terminated by SCHOOL DISTRICT in the event of a material
breach by UNIVERSITY, upon three (3) days’ written notice from SCHOOL DISTRICT to
UNIVERSITY. Students enrolled in or scheduled for the observation and field work experience at
SCHOOL DISTRICT will have the opportunity to complete their educational experience at
SCHOOL DISTRICT subject to the written approval of SCHOOL DISTRICT permitting
continuation and/or completion of the observation and mentoring work. Nothing in this
paragraph shall prohibit SCHOOL DISTRICT from terminating the Agreement on less than three
(3) days’ written notice when the breach by UNIVERSITY implicates the health, safety, and
welfare of the SCHOOL DISTRICT’s students and/or staff

19, Neither SCHOOL DISTRICT nor UNIVERSITY will discriminate against any individual
because of their age, race, creed, color, national origin, sexual orientation, gender identity or
expression, military status, sex, reproductive health decisions, disability, predisposing genetic
characteristics or marital status and will take affirmative action to ensure that each individual is
afforded equal opportunities without discrimination because of their.age, race, creed, color,
national origin, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, military status, sex,
reproductive health decisions, disability, predisposing genetic characteristics or marital status.

benefits will be derived by promoting the interests of both parties and each of the parties does,
therefore, enter into this Agreement with the intention of loyally cooperating with the other in
carrying out the terms of this Agreement and each party agrees to interpret its provisions insofar

30. Notwithstanding the provisions of this Agreement, nothing herein shall require
