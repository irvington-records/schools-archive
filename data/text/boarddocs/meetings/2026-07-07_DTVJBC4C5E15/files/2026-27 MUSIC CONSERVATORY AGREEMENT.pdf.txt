AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT made as of the 1+ day of July, 2026 by and between IRVINGTON UNION,
FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (hereinafter referred to as the “SCHOOL DISTRICT”), as the party of
the first part, having its principal place of business located 40 North Broadway, Irvington, New
York 10533, and MUSIC CONSERVATORY OF WESTCHESTER, INC. (hereinafter referred to
as the “SERVICE PROVIDER”), as the party of the second part, having its principal place of
business for purposes of this agreement at 216 Central Avenue, White Plains, New York 10606.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, SCHOOL DISTRICT is authorized by law to contract with independent
contractors for providing related services in accordance with New York Education Laws; and

WHEREAS, SERVICE PROVIDER is in the business of providing related services in the
area of music therapy; and

WHEREAS, SCHOOL DISTRICT desires that SERVICE PROVIDER provide music
therapy services of its supervised professional staff in an approved setting; and

WHEREAS, SERVICE PROVIDER is ready, willing, and able to provide related services
in the area of music therapy to SCHOOL DISTRICT.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained
in this Agreement, the parties hereto mutually agree as follows:

1. TERM OF AGREEMENT: This Agreement shall be in effect for the period July 1, 2026 to
June 30, 2027, unless terminated earlier, as set forth herein.

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES:

a. SERVICE PROVIDER shall provide music therapy services as set forth herein in
accordance with the State Education regulations. SERVICE PROVIDER shall provide
services to the students only on SCHOOL DISTRICT’s day of normal operation. The
program shall consist of student observation, student programming, staff training,
meetings with school personnel or student guardians, report writing, program.
development, and development of materials and/or teacher training preparation.
SERVICE PROVIDER shall conduct its program in a community center, library, or
designated school premises, as agreed upon by SCHOOL DISTRICT, or an
appropriate public place. Services shall not take place in a student's home unless an
adult of eighteen (18) years of age or older is present.

b. SCHOOL DISTRICT reserves the right to monitor and or evaluate the services
provided by SERVICE PROVIDER by conducting on site visits and observations,
surveys and or interviews with participants and stakeholders, document collection
and review and any other applicable means. Services must be appropriate, allowable
and consistent with SCHOOL DISTRICT and funding source requirements and
guidelines.
