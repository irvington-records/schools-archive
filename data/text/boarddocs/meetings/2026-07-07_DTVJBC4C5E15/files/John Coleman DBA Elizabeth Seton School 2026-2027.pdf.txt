This AGREEMENT made as of July 1, 2026 by and between the IRVINGTON UNION FREE
SCHOOL DISTRICT having a place of business at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, NY 10533
(the “District”) and JOHN A. COLEMAN SCHOOL D/B/A ELIZABETH SETON
CHILDREN’S SCHOOL, having a place of business at 300 Corporate Boulevard South,
Yonkers, New York 10701 (the “School”).

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 4402 of the Education Law of the State of New York the
District is required to provide special educational services for certain pupils residing within the
District who have handicapping conditions and who cannot be educated in special classes in the
public schools; and

WHEREAS, the District is duly empowered by law to enter into a contract with a non-
public school for the instruction of students with disabilities in those situations where the
District does not have an appropriate program available; and

WHEREAS, the School is a registered non-public school chartered by the Board of
~—- —.-Regents-of the-University-of the State-of New York as a non-profit-cducation-corporation-
authorized to establish, conduct, operate and maintain an educational program; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Education (the “BOE”) of the District has determined that
the School has an educational program which can provide instruction to enrolled student(s);
and

WHEREAS, the School has offered to provide such instruction on the terms and
conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein contained, the
parties hereto do agree as follows:

1. SCOPE OF SCHOOL’S SERVICES: The School shall provide instruction
and/or related services appropriate for student(s) placed by the District’s Committee on Special
Education (“CSE”) with the School. School services shall include, without limitation:

(a) Implementation of goals and objectives for the educational services
provided as per each individual student's individualized education program (“IEP”);

(b) Completion of educational progress reports regarding student achievement
of objectives as per report card schedules;

(c) Complete all areas of the student(s)’ IEP including but not limited to
student’s Present Levels of Performance, identifying the student’s strengths and weaknesses,
management needs, transition (if applicable), testing accommodations, program modifications;

[OCR]
(d) Provide the CSE with annual progress reports for each individual student
receiving service to be reviewed at each student's CSE meeting;

(e) Complete evaluations as per the request of the District’s Director of Special
Education or his/her designee, on an as-needed basis, at rates mutually agreed upon by both parties;

(f) Comply with any testing requirements upon notification of such review
dates; and

(g) | Comply with requests for transcripts, report cards, Regents examinations,
attendance sheets, and all other student records as the sending school may request, such materials
shall be provided within seven (7) days of the request.

‘The education provided by School shall be appropriate to the mental ability and physical
condition of the student(s) and in accordance with Commissioner’s Regulations, all applicable
Federal, State and local statutes, rules and ordinances. The School agrees to provide services in
accordance with the State Education Department’s (“NYSED”) regulations using only
professionals/service providers certified by the NYSED. All services shall be provided in strict
compliance with the student’s IEP. The School further agrees that all disciplinary measures for
disabled students will be conducted in accordance with applicable federal, state and local laws,

rules and regulations, = ~ ~

The School warrants that it has reviewed the IEP of each student to be enrolled in its
program(s) pursuant hereto, and represents that it is capable and willing to provide the services for
those student(s).

2. DISTRICT RESPONSIBILITIES: The District shall comply at all times with
the duties of school districts set forth in New York State Education Law $4402 and regulations
promulgated thereunder.

3. STUDENTS RECEIVING SERVICES: It is understood and agreed that services
will be provided by the School to those students who are referred by the District and accepted by
the School. It is acknowledged and agreed that all such students must have approval from the CSE
of the District for the requested services. The parties agree that the District shall have the right to
withdraw any student referred to the School for any lawful reason.

4. REPORTS: The School shall report in writing to the District, at least once each
year, summarizing the student's social and educational progress, stating the precise nature of the
program designated, and reporting all grades and attendance information. In addition, within the
timeframe not exceeding three (3) years from the date of placement, the School will be responsible
for providing the District with a current psychological, social history and medical examination.

5. TERM (SCHOOL YEAR); The term of this Agreement shall extend from July 1,
2026 to June 30, 2027, but shall be terminable by the District as to any one or more of student(s)
as set forth below.

6. RATE: The District shall pay tuition to the School at the NYSED’s most current
special education approved tuition rate per student (as outlined in Appendix A) upon the

presentation to it, on a monthly basis, by the School of proper invoices in a form acceptable to the
District. All such invoices must have a copy of the attendance record of the student(s) attached.
The parties hereto agree that total compensation to be paid for tuition to the School shall be limited
to the amount approved by the NYSED for the special education program and services set forth in
each student’s IEP and that the District will not be liable for payment of any sums in excess of the
amount so approved, except to the extent evaluations are completed at the request of the District,
then the mutually agreed-upon rates for the completed evaluations will also be paid to the School.

7. CONTACT BETWEEN SCHOOL AND DISTRICT PERSONNEL: Upon
reasonable notice, the School shall make its employees available to personnel designated by the
District for purposes of case discussions, personal observations, educational reviews, impartial
hearings and program visitations by prior arrangement between the personnel involved.

8, LICENSE AND AUTHORIZATION: The School warrants that it is duly
authorized to perform the services as described herein. The School warrants that it will provide
the District with licensed and qualified individuals. ‘he School further represents that such
services shall be performed by individuals that are licensed under the laws of the State of New
York, inclusive of the NYSED licensing requirements or by persons otherwise qualified to provide
services in accordance with all applicable laws and regulations.

(a) Exclusion from Health Care Programs. Each party represents and warrants
that it has never been excluded from participation in any state or federal health care program,
including, but not limited to, Medicare and Medicaid. Each party shall timely perform checks of
the following databases to determine if its employees and contractors are currently excluded,
debarred or otherwise ineligible to participate in federal health care programs (including, but not
limited to, Medicare and Medicaid) ot in federal procurement or non-procurement programs: (a)

General’s List of Restricted and Excluded Providers, Each part shall maintain documentation with
respect to these checks, and such documentation shall be made available to the other party upon
request, If, at any time during the term, a party or a party’s employee, staff member or contractor
is excluded, debarred or determined to be otherwise ineligible to participate in a federal health care
program, that Party shall immediately disclose this to the other party. The Party to whom the
disclosure is made shall have the right to immediately terminate this Agreement.

(b) — Assignment of Contractor’s Claims. The School hereby assigns to the
District all Medicaid reimbursement claims for payment for services rendered by School under
this Agreement. The District and School agree that only the District shall bill Medicaid and receive
any fees or charges for such services, which fees and charges shall accrue to the District. The
School shall remit to the District any payments School receives from third party payers for services
School renders hereunder.

(c) Medicaid Billing. The District shall provide or arrange for the provision of

all billing, collection and related accounting services required in connection with the provision of
the School’s services hereunder. The School shall provide the District with all information about

10. ATTENDANCE RECORDS: The School shall maintain monthly attendance
records and submit them to the District at the end of each month with the School’s invoice. If a
student has been absent for a period of five (5) or more consecutive days, the attendance record
shall indicate the reason for that absence.

13, DISCRIMINATION: Neither District nor School shall discriminate against any
individual because of his or her race, creed, color, sex, sexual orientation, national origin, religion,
age or disability.

14. GOVERNING LAW: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State
of New York. In addition, the parties hereby agree that any cause of action arising out of this
Agreement shall be brought in the County of Westchester.

15. SEVERABILITY: if any term, provision, covenant or condition of this
Agreement, or the application thereof to any person, place or circumstance, shall be held by a court
of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, unenforceable or void, the remainder of this Agreement
and such term, provision, covenant or condition as applied to other persons, places and
circumstances shall remain in full force and effect.

16. NOTICES: All notices of any nature referred to in this Agreement shall be in
writing and either sent by registered or certified mail postage pre-paid, or delivered by hand or
overnight courier, or sent by facsimile (with acknowledgment received), to the parties at the
addresses first set forth in the Agreement.

17. TERMINATION:

(a) — The District may terminate this Agreement upon the occurrence of any of
the following events:

i) Withdrawal by the District of all of its student(s) from the School
for any reason whatsoever;

Gi) Termination of the approval of the School by the Commissioner of
Education;

ii) Failure of the School to abide by the terms and conditions of this
Agreement; provided, however, that the School shall be provided
with thirty (30) days notice;

(iv) Changes in the student IEPs that require change of placement of all
students out of the School.

(b) The District may terminate this Agreement for any lawful reason.
(c) | The School may terminate this Agreement in accordance with New York

State Education Law and/or Commissioner's Regulations and for any lawful reason. Before any

Upon any such termination, the District shall pay to the School a pro-rata portion of the

18. NONWAIVER: No action or failure to act by School or the District shall
constitute a waiver of a right or duty afforded them under the Agreement, nor shall such action or
failure to act will constitute approval of or acquiescence in a breach thereunder.

19, AMENDMENT: This Agreement may be amended only by a writing executed by
both of the parties hereto.

20. ASSIGNMENT: Neither party may delegate any duties or assign any of its rights
under this Agreement without the prior express written consent of the other party, subject to any
necessary legal approvals, Any purported delegation of duties, assignment of rights or
subcontracting of work under this Agreement without the prior express written consent of the other
party is void abinitio.
