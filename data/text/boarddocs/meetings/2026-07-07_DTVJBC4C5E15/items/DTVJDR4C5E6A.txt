**If the roster for your event includes Irvington School District Residents then you are allowed a discount on the rate. The discount rate is directly related to the percentage of attendees who are Irvington School District Residents.
Discounts are applied as follows:
100% of roster is Irvington School District Residents receive a 75% discount to the rate.
75-99% of roster is Irvington School District Residents receive a 50% discount to the rate.
50-74% of roster is Irvington School District Residents receive a 25% discount to the rate.
