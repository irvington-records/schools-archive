Future Residents

      Upon proper written application and approval by the Superintendent of Schools or his/her
designee, the child(ren) of families who expect to become residents of the districtDistrict within
60 days and who have signed a lease for rental or a contract to buy or to build a residence in the

accordance with Board Policy 7130. The lease or contract will be submitted at the time of the

execute an acknowledgement that failure, for any reason, to provide appropriate evidence of
residency within said sixty (60) day period will result in the student(s)’ immediate removal from
school in the District and the parent(s)/legal guardian(s) responsibility for immediate payment of
tuition from the date of enrollment through the date of removal at the annual rate established by
the Board of Education pursuant to the formula set forth by the New York State Education
Department. It shall be the responsibility of the parent to provide evidence that he/she has
established residency within 60 days of the student(s)'s enrollment. The parent, at the time of
enrollment, shall sign an acknowledgement that failure, for any reason, to provide such evidence
within 60 days will result in parental responsibility for tuition from the date of enrollment. In the
case of extraordinary circumstances, the Superintendent, or the Superintendent's his/her
designee, may grant a thirty (30) day extension of time to substantiate residency.

     a)    When a Court has appointed a temporary or permanent legal guardianlegal guardian

           transfer of care, custody and control of the child. The Custodian Affidavit must be

Admission of Non-resident students on a Tuition Basis

      The Board of Education recognizes that the District's reputation and excellence in
providing education has resulted in interest in attending the District on a tuition basis. The
Board of Education will consider applications for non-tuition students on a case by case basis.
The Board of Education, or its designee, reserves the right to accept or reject any proposed
tuition student. The Board of Education, or its designee, will undertake consideration of such
applications only if:

     a)    Admission of tuition students shall not result in an increase in teaching staff.

     b)    The parent(s) or guardianlegal guardian(s) agrees shall be required to provide
           transportation for the student to and from school.

     c)    The admission of tuition students does not require additional facilities.

     d) d)      There is available classroom space within class size guidelines exists and with
        current levels of staffing.
     e) Enrollment of non-resident students is conditioned upon proper behavior in accordance
        with the District’s Code of Conduct. Any violation of the Code of Conduct by such
        student(s) which would result in any suspension from school shall result in the student’s
        immediate removal from attendance.
       The Administration shall develop and implement an application form incorporating the
criteria in this policy. Prior to consideration of the application, the parent(s)/guardianlegal
guardian(s) must sign a Release of Records for the student's current school. No more than ten
(10) tuition students shall be admitted per grade level.
