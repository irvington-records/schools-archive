SUBJECT: NAMING FACILITIES                                                                            Formatted: Font: 11 pt


The Board of Education shall have sole authority to name or rename district facilities, including     Formatted: Font: (Default) Arial, 11 pt
but not limited to buildings, athletic fields, tracks, libraries, gymnasiums, auditoriums/theaters,
and multipurpose rooms. The following guidelines will apply to requests for naming facilities.

  I.     Procedure

                               A.    Any member of the school district community or                   Formatted: Font: (Default) Arial, 11 pt
                                    the community at large can submit a request for
                                    a facility naming consideration.
                               B.   All requests for naming facilities shall be                       Formatted: Font: (Default) Arial, 11 pt
                                    submitted to the Superintendent and shall
                                    include reasons the facility should be named
                                    after the proposed individual. Also required
                                    shall be a biography of the individual to be
                                    honored, a letter of permission from the
                                    individual and/or the family and other pertinent
                                    information like the proposed design plans for
                                    signage and funding source.

                               C.   The Superintendent shall submit request to the                    Formatted: Font: (Default) Arial, 11 pt
                                    Board of Education for consideration. The Board
                                    shall vote for approval of the naming request.

         Facility Names
