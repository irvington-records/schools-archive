Policy Committee Minutes

July 24, 2013

Present: Maria Kashkin, Kris Harrison, Beverly Miller, Elaine Cardea

The committee reviewed the following policies to ensure that they are compliant with changes to laws and/or mandates. The Superintendent will ask the Board to waive the 2nd reading, as per Board policy, and approve policies #1510, #7310, #7314, and #7317 at the August meeting in order to have them in place for the beginning of school in September.

Review of Updated Policies as per new laws/regulations
a.     #1510 – Regular Meetings  - To allow Board members to participate in Board Meetings via video.
#7310 – School Conduct and Discipline – To comply with new DASA mandates
#7314 – Anti-bullying, Harassment & Hazing – To comply with new DASA mandates
#7317 – Dignity for All Students Act – To comply with new mandates 

II	Review of Possible Updates to Current Policies
#5634 – Naming of Facilities – The  policy was revised to clarify the role of the Board when 
       a request is submitted to name a facility.
#7131 – Admission of Non-Resident Students and Agreement – This policy was revised to 
       clarify the procedures when non-resident paying students are admitted to the district as 
       well as to satisfy the auditor’s recommendation to have parents sign an agreement.
#7316 – Cell Telephone and Electronic Signaling Devices – NYSSBA provided an updated 
 policy to address SED’s prohibition of cell phones in NYS assessments. The committee reviewed the suggested NYSSBA’s draft policy along with the District’s current Cellular Telephone & Electronic Signaling Device Policy. Dr. Harrison noted that he has asked the principals of each building to provide information as to what is permissible and the consequences in their buildings with respect to the usage of devices. Dr. Harrison also noted that he prefers the language in NYSSBA’s policy but the consequences will be incorporated into the Code of Conduct.

The District currently does not have the policies noted below. The committee began review of these policies. The District’s legal counsel provided the policies regarding student expression. 

Review of Sample Policies
6000 series – Disclosure of Wrongful Conduct (Whistleblower)
7000 series – School Sponsored Student Expression
7000 series – Student Personal Expression 

The Superintendent will follow up with the District’s legal counsel on several questions.
