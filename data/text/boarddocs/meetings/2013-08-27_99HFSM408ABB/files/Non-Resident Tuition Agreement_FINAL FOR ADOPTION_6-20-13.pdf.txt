                          IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT

                              NON-RESIDENT TUITION CONTRACT

        WHEREAS,          ____________________           and     ______________________,            are    the

parent(s)/guardian(s) (“parent(s)/guardian(s)”) of _________________ (the “student”); and

        WHEREAS, the parent(s)/guardian(s) have applied to the District for the student to be enrolled in

the Irvington Union Free School District (“District”) for the _____________school year; and

        WHEREAS, the parent(s)/guardian(s) acknowledge that, as a result of the parent(s)/guardian(s)

and student not residing within the District, the student is not legally entitled to a free education in the

District’s schools; and

        WHEREAS, the District is willing to permit the student to attend its schools for the

___________school year as a non-resident student conditioned upon the payment of tuition and pursuant

to provisions of Board of Education Policy 7130, which is incorporated herein and made a part hereof.

        NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS

        AGREED, that for the _____________school year, the student shall be enrolled in the District’s

schools. It is further agreed that in consideration of the foregoing, the parent(s)/guardian(s) shall be

responsible for the payment of tuition in the amount of ______________, as established annually by the

Board of Education pursuant to the formula set forth by the New York State Education Department. Said

tuition shall be paid in equal installments; with the first payment due no later than twenty (20) business days

prior to the commencement of the first academic semester and the second payment due no later than

twenty (20) business days prior to the commencement of the second academic semester. The parties

acknowledge that the above referenced tuition rate is subject to change by the State Education Department

in accordance with Part 174 of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education. In the event that the

tuition rate is changed during the term of this Agreement, the amount of tuition which the

parent(s)/guardian(s) is/are required to pay shall be increased or decreased to reflect the adjusted tuition

rate for the relevant period of the student’s attendance. The obligation to pay an increased or decreased

amount reflecting the adjusted tuition rate for the relevant period of each student’s attendance shall survive

the expiration of this Agreement.

        AGREED, that upon a failure to pay tuition as set forth above, the student will not be permitted to

continue attending the Irvington schools and the parent(s)/guardian(s) agree that an action may be brought

against him/her in the courts of the State of New York to recover any unpaid tuition owed, and in the event

the District is a prevailing party in such action, the parent(s)/guardian(s) herewith agree to pay the District

all costs incurred in the bringing of such action, including reasonable attorneys’ fees.

        AGREED, that the parent(s)/guardian(s) and the student hereby waive any rights they may have

under Education Law §3214 for any violation of the district’s Code of Conduct by the student that would

result in any suspension from the Irvington Union Free School District. Any such violation of the Code of

Conduct by the student shall be considered a material breach of this agreement. Upon such a violation, the

student shall no longer be entitled to attend the Irvington Union Free School District and all tuition

amounts due and owing to the District shall become payable immediately.

        AGREED, This Agreement may be terminated by District for educational or fiscal reasons upon

thirty (30) days written notice to the other party. In the event of such termination outstanding tuition due, if

any, shall be pro-rated. This provision shall not be construed as limiting the District’s ability to terminate

under any provision herein.

        AGREED, that the parent(s)/guardian(s) and student shall not assign, transfer or convey any of

their respective rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of District.

        AGREED, that this Agreement may be amended only in writing and signed by the parties.

        AGREED, that no action or failure to act by the parent(s)/guardian(s), student or District shall

constitute a waiver of a right or duty afforded them under the Agreement or at law or equity, nor shall such

action or failure to act constitute approval of or acquiescence in a breach thereunder, except as may be

specifically agreed in writing.

       AGREED, that this agreement shall be governed and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the

State of New York.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this agreement the day and year first set
forth above.
