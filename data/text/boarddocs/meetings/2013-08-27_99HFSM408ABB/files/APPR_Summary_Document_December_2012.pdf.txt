Teacher Evaluation:
The following document summarizes the three key components of the composite scoring for the new
teacher evaluation system. This information, in detail has been submitted to the State Education
Department and approved. The complete document is available on our Curriculum and Instruction home
page.

Background:
The new APPR was developed by a committee of teachers and administrators according to the
guidelines provided by the State Education Department (details of this guidance can be found on the
Engageny.org web site, http://engageny.org/resource/guidance-on-new-york-s-annual-professional-
performance-review-law-and-regulations/). Throughout the 2011-2012 school year, the APPR
Committee partnered to develop a plan to meet the expectations of the guidelines as well as the needs
of the Irvington School District. We submitted our APPR plan by July 1, 2012 as required. The final and
complete document includes extensive detail for the three categories summarized below which
Irvington teachers and administrators will receive their end of year evaluation.

Each teacher will receive a score based on the following formula. For each of these areas teachers will
receive a score. The scoring bands have been defined in the following sections.
     20% State Measure or SLO (Student Learning Objective)
     20% Local Measure
     60% Classroom Observation and Other Measures of Teacher Practice

Growth on State Assessments or Comparable Measures - 20%
For teachers in grades 4 - 8 Common Branch, ELA, and Math, NYSED will provide a value-added growth
score. That score will incorporate students' academic history compared to similarly academically
achieving students and will use special considerations for students with disabilities, English language
learners, students in poverty, and, in the future, any other student-, classroom-, and school-level
characteristics approved by the Board of Regents. NYSED will also provide a HEDI subcomponent rating
category and score from 0 to 25 points.

While most teachers of 4-8 Common Branch, ELA and Math will have state-provided measures, some
may teach other courses in addition where there is no state-provided measure. Teachers with 50 – 100%
of students covered by State-provided growth measures and teachers with 0 – 49% of students covered
by State-provided growth measures. Teachers with 50 – 100% of students covered by State-provided
growth measures will receive a growth score from the State for the full Growth subcomponent score of
their evaluation. Teachers with 0 – 49% of students covered by State-provided growth measures must
have SLOs for the Growth subcomponent of their evaluation and one SLO must use the State-provided
measure if applicable for any courses. (See guidance for more detail on teachers with State-provided
measures AND SLOs.)

Please note that if the Board of Regents does not approve a value-added measure for these
grades/subjects for 2012-13, the State-provided growth measure will be used for 20 points in this
subcomponent. NYSED will provide a HEDI subcomponent rating category and score from 0 to 20 points.

Where applicable teachers will receive a score from New York State. All other teachers will develop a
SLO (Student Learning Objective) based on pre and post assessment data.

point value.

Observation: There will be four designated observations for all tenured teachers. The following
framework will be used by all administrators:
     1 formal planned observation (full instructional period) – will include:
           o pre-observation
           o observation
           o post observation (reflection and conference)
     1 formal unannounced observation (full instructional period) – will include:
           o Observation
           o post observation (reflection and observation)
     2 walk-throughs –
           o Informal Feedback Form
           o Either teacher or administrator can request a follow up meeting

STATE-PROVIDED MEASURES OF STUDENT GROWTH – 20%
(25 points with an approved Value-Added Measure)
For principals in buildings with Grades 4-8 ELA, Math and/or High School courses with State or Regents
assessments, (or principals of programs with any of these assessments), NYSED will provide value-added
measures. NYSED will also provide a HEDI subcomponent rating category and score from 0 to 25 points.
 In order for a principal to receive a State-provided value-added measure, at least 30% of the students in
the principal's school or program must take the applicable State or Regents assessments. This will
include most schools in the State.
