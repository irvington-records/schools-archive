		Action (Consent), Policy - Adoption:  8.12 Adoption of Policies Without Required Two Readings:

		Mr. Montgomery is sensitive that Administration feels should be in place at the start of school and therefore the Board has been asked to waive the first read.

		#7310 - Student Conduct - Mr. Montgomery commented that there is a tendency to make policies read like legal language,specifically the repeated definition of harassment does not seem to streamline the policies.

		#7317 - DASA - No Comments

		#1510 - Regular Meeting Policy - No Comments

		#7131 Admission of Non-Resident Students - Mr. Montgomery noted that this revision has removed hearing rights for students that were in the previous version of the policy, leaving the District without any procedures for removing students from the District for disciplinary matters. Mr. Mountanos commented that the reason why most district's do not provide the right to hearing is that the law views students attending schools on a tuition basis as being a priviledge and is on a contract basis. It is very different from a resident student who has rights to their education. Mr. Montgomery asked if the District could provide for some process short of a hearing. Mrs. Kashkin noted that the District only commits to a year to year contract for non-resident tuition students.

		Concerns were raised by Mrs. Kerner regarding the aspect of the policy that allows for students who have been in Irvington for their high school years (grades 9-12) and move from Irvington during their senior year may continue in the school until graduation and fears that people might take advantage by moving out of the District, and insert a date as to when a parent moves and a student is allowed to continue.  Mrs. Kerner would also like discussion of the clause in the policy which states that a maximum of 10 students per grade be allowed to attend and which appears to be an arbitrary.  Mr. Mountanos recommends that there be no date be stated and that such requests be reviewed on a case by case basis, also stating that as a practical matter, when such requests are received late in the school year, the State Education Commissioner would allow the student to remain in the district until he renders his decision and most likely the student will be graduated before the Commissioner renders his decision. Mr. Mountanos says he advises clients to be general in their language when not regulated by state and/or federal law.

		Since only three Board members are in attendance, it was agreed to table the adoption of the Admission of Non-Resident students so that  all members are present to continue discussion.
