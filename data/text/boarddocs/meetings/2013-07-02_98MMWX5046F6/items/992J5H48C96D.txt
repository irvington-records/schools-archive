SED requires a Public Hearing whenever there are revisions to the Code of Conduct. The Code has been updated to reflect changes in the Dignity for All Students Act (DASA). The major change in DASA is that the definition of "harassment" is now defined to include bullying, cyberbullying and hazing. Other edits include expanding on 504 regulations as it relates to disciplinary actions (see page 26) and to demonstrates the similarities and differences between discipline of students under IDEA and those under 504 federal regulations.
