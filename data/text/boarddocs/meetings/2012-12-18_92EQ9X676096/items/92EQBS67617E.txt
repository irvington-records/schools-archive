Meeting: Dec 18, 2012 - Work Session/Special Meeting - 7:30 pm*
Date: 2012-12-18
Category: 5. Consent Agenda
Subject: 5.1 Approval of Consent Agenda
Type: Action (Consent)

To make more efficient use of meeting time, the Board of Education authorizes the use of a consent agenda as a part of the meeting agenda. The President of the Board will ask Board Members to identify any agenda items that Board Members wish to remove from the consent agenda to be voted on by separate motion. Board Members will also have the opportunity to ask/comment on any agenda item(s) prior to vote.
