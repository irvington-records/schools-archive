BUILDINGS & GROUNDS COMMITTEE
November 27, 2012    7:00 am
MEETING MINUTES
Present: Dr. Harrison, Maria Kashkin, Jim McCann, Gary Knowles, Beverly Miller

Review of projects currently under review by LAN -  The committee reviewed the list of open items and agreed that safety issues are a priority.  The condensate pump has been contracted for replacement at Dows Lane, and Gary is obtaining quotes to repair the retaining wall at Main St. School.  The committee also agreed that replacing the Andover controls on the Dows Lane boilers are a priority.  Other items under discussion were electrical upgrades at all buildings, Dows Lane renovations, firestopping, and refurbishing a student bathroom at the high school.  Administration will return to the committee with recommendations regarding the electrical projects following further investigation into cost efficiencies, energy performance contracts, and discussion with outside consultants.  Discussions on renovations at Dows Lane have been tabled pending receipt and review of the Space Utilization Study currently being performed by Dr. Paul Seversky. 

Traffic Calming Study - The HS Site Committee requested the district install speed humps on the road leading to East Field.  The District’s architects performed a Traffic Calming Study – which will be reviewed and discussed at the December 4th BOE meeting.  The committee discussed the architect’s recommendations for the installation of rumble strips and speed humps.  Mr. McCann inquired what authority the police have to deter speeding on that road.  Administration will initiate dialogue with the Village on ways we could become stronger partners in managing this situation.

Dows Lane – planter in rear of building - The committee reviewed a photo of a proposed planter to be donated and installed in front of the chiller at the rear of the Dows Lane School building.  The committee gave approval for Mr. Schepps to donate and place one planter and plants on the site.   Positioning of the planter will need the approval of Mr. Knowles.

East Field update– Additional testing was being performed the morning of November 27th, as required by DEC.  The district will not receive recommendations for remediation of the field until all test results have been received and analyzed.
Minutes taken by Beverly Miller
