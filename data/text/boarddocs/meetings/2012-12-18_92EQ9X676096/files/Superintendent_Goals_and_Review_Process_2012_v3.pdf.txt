Process


The Irvington BOE will model its evaluation after the New Jersey School Boards Association evaluation
form. According to this methodology, each Board member will separately fill out the evaluation form in
two parts to measure the Superintendent’s progress in achieving specific goals (part 1) and overall
leadership skills (part 2).

In part 1, each goal will be evaluated with a narrative description of the Superintendent’s progress against
that goal.

In part 2, the Board members will evaluate the Superintendent’s leadership skills in narrative format.

The completed form, including the aggregated comments from the five board members, will then be shared
with the Superintendent for comment.

The Superintendent’s annual goals will be presented at a public board meeting in the fall.

The Superintendent will also furnish a mid-year status report on our about the first of the year.

PART I: Goals

Instructional Leadership
The superintendent will lead the administration team in the enhancement and advancement of curricular
programs throughout the District. To achieve this goal, the administration will:
       Establish a cohesive team focused on data based decision-making, consistency in use of best practice,
       and a culture of customer service.
       Provide informational presentations at Board of Education meetings that include examples of
       current practice and desired improvements.
       Facilitate parent presentations and workshops to enable parents to be supportive partners in their
       child’s educational experience.
       Share relevant resources with the parent community via in person meetings, the District website
       and the District Facebook page with the result that the community is well versed on critical
       educational topics.
       Organize agenda-driven informal meetings in each school community to discuss current educational
       topics and the efforts that the Irvington Public Schools are making to address those issues.
       Lead the Board and the community in discussions to establish long-term curricula priorities for the
       School District.


District Infrastructure Goal

The superintendent will deploy a concise and future-focused vision to guide and prioritize decision making
related to District infrastructure. To achieve this goal, the administration team will:
       Provide an assessment of the technology infrastructure.
       Evaluate opportunities for energy conservation.
       Review safety and security at District facilities.
       Analyze and provide recommendations regarding future facility (building and grounds) needs.
       Develop a methodology to systematically evaluate costs and benefits of various programs and
       services.
       Outline financial priorities and constraints within the context of budgeting.


Personnel Leadership Goal.

       Develop hiring protocols for Administrative openings, and hire HS and MSS Principals.
       Provide leadership for administrators through professional development and modeling.
       Work to achieve teaching excellence across the District by ensuring resources are optimized, and
       learning opportunities are continually enhanced and improved.
       Foster a staff commitment to excellence in student performance and preparation for their future.

Part II: Leadership Standards


I. General Responsibilities

The Superintendent is the educational leader who promotes the success of all students, and ensures the
smooth operation of the district.

      Improves the quality of educational programs and services to students
      Recommends policies for Board adoption and implements the adopted policies
      Provides leadership in identification of priorities, and ensures all activities reflect those Board
      established priorities.
      Prepares and recommends long and short range plans for board approval, and implements those
      plans when approved.
      Promotes academic rigor that focuses on learning and excellence for the schools

II. Instructional Leadership

Develops and promotes instructional programs designed to optimize student, program and staff
achievement.

      Directs and supervises the administrative staff to assure the goals of the school are reflected in
      tits educational program and operations
      Encourages staff to develop programs, services and projects focused on improving student
      achievement
      Overarching responsibility for development and implementation of APPR and Common Core
      requirements
      Develops guidelines and directions for monitoring the effectiveness of existing and new
      programs. Ensures effective teaching techniques are in place and that all instructional resources
      are used to maximize student achievement.
      Monitors, evaluates and reports student achievement and utilizes research and assessments to
      improve the learning process
      Reviews and update the instructional program on a continuing basis and provides
      recommendations to the Board for the educational advancement of the District


III. Personnel Administration

Provides oversight for a comprehensive human resources program, including recruitment, retention,
staffing, organization and development.

      Provides direction and supervision in the development and implementation of sound personnel
      practices including clearly defined roles, duties responsibilities and current job descriptions
      Provides direction and acts as a resource in negotiations, and supervises administration of
      collective bargaining agreements.
      Assures all staff is evaluated annually in accordance with established procedures
      Recommends numbers and types of positions necessary for District

IV. Financial Management

Superintendent manages budget development, implementation and monitoring process that reflects
district goals.

       Assures district has long range financial and facility improvement plans which are updated
       annually.
       Oversees school facility management,
       Initiates and supervised development of annual budget, including parameter setting with the
       board based upon district goals.


V. Communication, Community Relations, Ethical Leadership

Establish effective communications not only with students, staff, parents and Board, but also with the
community as a whole. Promote a school-based climate of tolerance, acceptance and civility.

       Collaborate with families, community members, local government and media .
       Keep public informed about educational practices, trends, policies and problems in District.
       Respond to community feedback and build community support for the District
       Inform Board with timely communications and respond to concerns and needs of the Board and
       its members
