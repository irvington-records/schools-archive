                                      Irvington Union Free School District
                                       Board of Education Goals: 2012-13


Board Meeting Procedures Goal
Establish board meeting procedures and with the desired outcomes of:
       Building trust with the community by making a commitment to transparency and sharing the
       rationale behind decisions.
       Providing opportunities for meaningful engagement with community members on important
       issues that occur prior to the board making the decision.
       Informing the administration on what information is needed for decision-making.
       Sharing a process by which community members can have questions answered.
       Allowing the Board to conduct board work in a meaningful and productive manner.

Professional Development Goal
Focus time and attention on the professional growth of board members by:
       Establishing an induction and mentoring program for new board members.
       Participating in professional growth opportunities suggested by the Superintendent or sought out
       by board members.
       Conducting a board self-evaluation in May or June of 2013.

Board Communications Goal
The Board will make its business more transparent and improve its communication methods by:
      Using modern, digital communications to share information with the district in a timely fashion.
      These methods will include a more regularly updated website, improved email communications, a
      regularly updated Facebook page and video-taped Board meetings broadcasted on cable and via
      YouTube.
      Providing adequate notice to the community for upcoming Board agenda items.
      Hosting more frequent informal meetings with community members.
