Financial Climate and School Budgeting
 Budget responsibilities
   Maintain community’s investment in IUFSD
   Develop a responsible financial plan
   Contain tax increases


 Goals in school budgeting include:
   Preservation of necessary programs and
    resources
   Expand learning experiences
   Introduce new opportunities
   Invest in staff training
   Maintain facilities

What are our needs?
 Preserve existing programs
 Allow for program growth
 Introduce new learning experiences
 Build staff capacity through growth
  experiences
 Expand and increase the integration of
  technological learning opportunities
 Maintain and enhance facilities

Financial Leadership
 Cost containment
 Seek efficiencies
 Identify shared services
 Zero-based budgeting to be employed
