Proposed Resolutions Recommended for Adoption
1.   Sunsetting Resolution: NYSSBA opposes any legislative & regulatory mandates that
     would impose new unfunded or underfunded mandates. (Peru & Sachem School Boards)                   S

     districts to adopt an earlier start to the instructional year with those dates being

     Regents exams. (Peru School Board)

     provide reimbursement for all required administrative costs. (Peru School Board) *

12. Sunsetting Resolution: NYSSBA shall seek legislation to streamline the tenured
    teacher hearing process set forth in Education Law §3020-a in a way that preserves
    due process while addressing spiraling costs and the extraordinary length of time to                     S
    conduct hearings. (Peru School Board)
13. Sunsetting Resolution: NYSSBA supports all efforts to improve school safety by

14. Sunsetting Resolution: NYSSBA shall take a leadership role in raising awareness and
    understanding of the importance of all school districts’ leaders in understanding and
    taking action to address disproportionality in the education workforce and in student
    outcomes as it relates to diverse backgrounds. In carrying out this mission NYSSBA
    shall: (a) Develop training focused on cultural competency and diligently promote,
    encourage and facilitate access to all school boards; (b) Encourage school boards to
    invite discussions with students, parents, staff and the board to develop district                       S*
    action plans that address staffing, student outcomes and the school climate in a
    manner that improves cultural competency, diversity and inclusivity; and (c) Take an
    active role in collaborating with other groups, including but not limited to the New
    York State Board of Regents, NYSCOSS, SAANYS, NYSUT and other members of the
    Educational Conference Board to encourage them to jointly promote such training
    and conversations about their members and constituencies. (Peru School Board) *

     *Notes on RECOMMENDED items:
     #14. The sunsetting resolution on DEI initiatives could be updated to reflect progress over the past 5 years, but we
          are supportive of having a NYSSBA resolution relating to diversity, especially in hiring.
     #15. While recognizing that childcare is needed in many communities, schools are meant to be educational
          institutions. Our region needs to solve preK issues, and adding childcare could be difficult for districts.
     #17. While we support districts ensuring that naloxone is available, this is also an unfunded mandated that should
          continue to be determined on a local basis.
     #20. Preference is to wait for the graduation measures commission to report.
     #22. Need to know how this would affect 4410/SASD programs.
     #23. We support affordable digital access (through WiFi) for all. This resolution includes cellular, which is often a
          contentious local issue. This resolution does NOT include affordability, only access.
     #30. We support school meals for all students. To reduce the paperwork burden on parents and districts, the federal
          government should provide universal school meals. Priority should be given to funding students/families who
          need it most, potentially requiring an increase in the income level for FRPL.
     #31. Early voting was viewed as impractical for school districts (including higher cost, more access to school buildings
          and more work for district clerks). A future resolution supporting absentee ballots for any reason is a proven
          tactic for increasing school election turnout.
