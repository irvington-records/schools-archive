TO:      Board Presidents, Superintendents and District Clerks
FROM:    Karen Belanger, Westchester Putnam School Boards Association Executive Director
RE:      NOTICE: WPSBA VIRTUAL Annual Meeting & Vote on WPSBA Budget & Slate for 2020-21
DATE:    April 27, 2020

We are pleased to present information on the Westchester Putnam School Boards Association proposed
budget and the Board of Directors nominating slate for 2020-21, which was approved at WPSBA’s April
23rd meeting. This year’s budget presentation will be made by our Treasurer at the WPSBA VIRTUAL
Annual Meeting on May 28, 2020 (invitation attached).


The WPSBA dues increase will be limited to 1.5% for the coming school year – an annual increase of $14
to $66 for our member districts. We base our dues chart on the current-year tax levy, and thus take into
consideration both district size and district wealth (higher need districts receive a smaller percentage of
their revenue from the tax levy). WPSBA membership letters and dues invoices will be sent individually to
each district in July.

WPSBA works throughout the year to provide timely and relevant services, programming, and data
resources geared to the unique needs of our districts. In partnership with our school districts and the Lower
Hudson Education Coalition, we advocate for public education and the children we serve. In this
unprecedented year of school closures due to the COVID-19 outbreak, we have sought to bring information
and connections in a timely and safe manner to our member districts.

Please have your board of education vote on the WPSBA proposed Budget and Nominating
Committee Slate at your next board meeting. Your district’s ballot (which has been sent to your district
clerk) can be cast by a representative of your district via voice vote at the Virtual Annual Meeting, or the
signed ballot can be submitted via email prior to the May 28th meeting. As we require a quorum to
conduct business, please designate a representative to convey your board’s vote at our Virtual
Annual Meeting (and Town Call) at noon on May 28th (even if your district’s ballot was emailed).

We thank you for your ongoing support, and encourage you to complete our annual June e-survey. Your
feedback informs our programming, regional data analysis, communications, and advocacy.




Karen Belanger
Executive Director, WPSBA

WPSBA BUDGET- KEY LINE ITEMS:
 Income Categories:
      Membership Dues: Categories are based on school district’s current year tax levy. BOCES dues are based on budget. Special
       Act Public School districts have a flat rate.
      Program Fees: Revenue from programs for which WPSBA incurs costs for meal catering.
      Facts & Figure$ Sponsor Advertisements: Revenue includes sponsor advertisements in Facts & Figure$ and book sales,
        which offset the printing and mailing cost.
      LHEC & EPFP Administration: WPSBA receives a fee to serve as the administrative staff for the Lower Hudson Education
        Coalition (LHEC) and a fee to promote the NY Education Policy Fellowship Program (EPFP).
      Savings & Other Revenue: Use of savings, plus interest and other income.

 Expenditure Categories:
      Employee Compensation: Two FT employees: Executive Director & Program Coordinator; two PT (.2): Admin Associate &
       Finance Associate.
      Board of Directors and Committees: Costs related to WPSBA meetings, workshops, and no-fee programs, including: Board of
        Directors committee meetings; Prospective & New Board Member workshops; Current Issues; Legislative Forum; Advocacy
        Meetings; Special Act School District Taskforce; regional meetings; etc.
      Overhead and Professional Fees: Rent, office equipment and supplies, postage, telephone, technology, website, insurance,
       payroll, publications, in addition to bookkeeping, legal fees, and outside consultants.
      WPSBA Programs & Events: Direct costs for all programs and workshops, and for events for which there are meal catering
       costs. Soft costs such as personnel, overhead and related expenses are not directly allocated to most program or project
       expense categories.
      Conferences & Travel Expenses: Executive Director and President’s expenses for annual conferences, workshops, and
       meetings (NYSSBA, NSBA, etc.); memberships, subscriptions; staff training, meetings, and workshop expenses.
