4. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Information:  4.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison highlighted ceremonies within the Irvington community including the upcoming graduation ceremony for the Class of 2023 on June 17, 2023, and the Retiree and Tenure Reception that occurred prior to tonight's Board of Education meeting. Additionally, a sculpture honoring local enslaved Africans, entitled Yesterday, was unveiled on June 10, 2023. The sculpture is located on Main Street and will provide an opportunity for the District to integrate this work and the stories behind it into the student learning experience across all grade levels. Dr. Harrison also reminded the public to complete the annual school quality survey to help inform the District's planning in the years ahead.
