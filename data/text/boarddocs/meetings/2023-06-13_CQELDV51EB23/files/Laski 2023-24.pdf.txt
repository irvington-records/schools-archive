Re: Employment Letter Agreement
Dear Maria,

This letter serves as your Employment Agreement with the Irvington Union
Free School District (hereinafter “the District”) for the period of July 1, 2023 through
June 30, 2024. The Board of Education (hereinafter “the Board”) has been advised
that it is legally permissible to contractually obligate itself to provide you with the
wages and benefits described herein. It is acknowledged by you and the Board that
the termination of your employment is subject solely to various provisions of the Civil
Service Laws of the State of New York and nothing herein contained shall be deemed
to have modified the foregoing in any respect. It is acknowledged by you and the
Board that this Agreement negates any prior Agreement between you and the
Board that relates to your terms and conditions of employment. Such Agreements
are null and void by the execution of this Agreement. Therefore, based upon the
mutual understandings between you and the Board, it is understood and agreed as
follows:

Scope of Employment
Effective July 1, 2023, you shall be employed as Payroll Clerk.

Salary

Your regular base salary for the year July 1, 2023 through June 30, 2024 shall be
$75,275.

Career Increment

In addition to the foregoing, you shall receive a career increment in the
amount of $1,300 upon completion of ten (10) years of service; an additional $1,450
after completion of fifteen (15) years of service; and an additional $ 1,600 after
completion of twenty (20) years of service in the District.

[OCR]
Benefits

In addition to the annual compensation specified above, you shall be entitled
to receive the following enumerated benefits:

1 Work Year

In alignment with the CSEA Clerical contract, your work year for the 2023-2024
school year shall be twelve (12) months, except for the school holidays and recess
periods designated on the school calendar, vacation days, and use of sick, personal,
family illness days and bereavement days as well as any days off due to school
cancellation. You wilt work a forty (40) hour week, eight (8) hours per day, Monday
through Friday, including one (1) hour a day for lunch and will follow any other
provisions in the CSEA Clerical Contract.

2. Sick Leave

Effective July 1, 2023, you shall be entitled to thirteen (13) days of sick leave for
the 2021-2023 school year. Any unused sick leave days may be carried over and used
during any subsequent year of employment with no maximum accumulation.

Anon-cumulative reserve of thirty (30) working days will be available to you for
an extended illness. You may only utilize this reserve once in your lifetime. Extended
illness shall mean illness or disabitity for thirty (30) or more continuous working days.
This reserve can only be accessed after utilization of all your personal accumulated
sick leave. Thereafter, you shall receive the difference between your salary and the
amount paid for a substitute until long term disability becomes effective.

3. Personal and Other Leaves

Effective July 1, 2023, you shail be entitled to five (5) days for the purpose of
transacting or attending to personal business which cannot be attended to other
than on a school day during school hours. All personal business days shall require
reasonable notice and the reason for such absence. All unused personal days shall
be credited to sick time at the end of each fiscal year.

4. Family lilness - Death in the Family

Effective July 1, 2023, you shall be entitled to three (3) days of additional leave
with no deduction in pay for illness in the inmmediate family. You shall also be
entitled to three (3) days additional leave with no deduction in pay for death in the
immediate family. These days shall not be deducted from sick leave and you shall
not be entitled to accumulate any unused days under this section. The immediate
family is herein defined as father, mother, brother, sister, son, daughter, husband or
wife, grandparents, father-in-law, mother-in-law, brother-in-law and sister-in-law.

Ss. Vacation Leave

Effective July 1, 2023, you shall be entitled to fifteen (15) days of vacation leave
annually. Said vacation leave must be utilized during the summer months of July 1
through August 31. If you are unable to utilize said vacation leave days during July or
August due to your job responsibilities, you may, upon the prior approval of your
immediate supervisor, use your vacation leave at other times during the school year.
You shall be entitled to carry over any unused vacation days with no maximum
accumulation.

An employee with over two (2) years’ service, who terminates his or her
employment voluntarily, shall be entitled to the cash equivalent of 5/6 of one (1) day's
wage for each calendar month from July 1 preceding the date of termination
through the effective date of the employee's resignation.

Employees who have submitted a "resignation for the purpose of retirement"
from Irvington UFSD shall be granted the cash equivalent of one day's wage for every
accumulated vacation day earned during their employment period.

6. Health Insurance

Effective July 1, 2023, you shall have the opportunity to participate in the
health care plan offered by the Board of Education. Effective July 1, 2023, you shall be
required to contribute 7% of the applicable premium. Contributions to the cost of
premiums will be made through bi-weekly payroll deductions.

Should you receive health insurance from sources other than the District and
you opt out of the District's health insurance program for at least one year, you shall
receive a cash payment - payable in two equal installments - for the year you opt out
of the health insurance program based on the following:

Proof of 2 person or family coverage elsewhere: $5,844.48 annual payment
Proof of single coverage elsewhere: $3,506.71 annual payment

7. Retiree Health Insurance

You shall be entitled to health insurance benefits into retirement only after
completing ten (10) years of consecutive service in the District immediately
preceding your retirement. Upon your resignation from the District for purposes of
retirement into the New York State Employees Retirement System, you shall be
permitted to continue coverage under the District's health insurance program for
you and your spouse, subject to the terms and conditions of the District's health care
plan in effect. The District shall pay one-hundred percent (100%) of the health
insurance premiums for such coverage into retirernent. The District will only be
responsible for the basic Medicare Part B reimbursement amount. You shall also
have the option to cover any additional cost for coverage of dependents that you
wish to include subject to the terms of the District's health care plan in effect.

8. Welfare Fund

You shall be entitled to receive a welfare fund allocation in the amount of
$1,300, which may be utilized for Dental and/or Optical insurance coverage. Said sum
shall be contributed by the Board on your behalf to the Employee Benefit Fund of
the Irvington Union Free School District Clerical Unit.

9. Flexible Spending Account
You shall be entitled to participate in the District's flexible spending account.
10. Other Benefits

You shall also be entitled to receive the following benefits as set forth in Article
XVI the 2020-2027 Collective Bargaining Agreement between the District and the
Irvington Union Free School District Clerical Unit.

a. Life Insurance in the amount of $70,000
b. Disability Insurance

Written Agreement

By signing this letter, you acknowledge that this Agreement has been arrived
at jointly between yourself and the Board and shall not be construed against any
party hereto as being the drafter hereof or causing the same to be drafted. By
signing this letter, you acknowledge that this Agreement shall continue in full force

and effect for the term of your service to the District unless otherwise terminated in
writing.

Severability

If any provision of this Letter Agreement is determined to be contrary to law, it
is understood and agreed that such provision shall be deemed deleted and the
balance of this Letter Agreement without such deleted provision, if otherwise lawful,
shall remain in full force and effect. If such deleted provision involves compensation
or money benefit, you and the Board agree to negotiate as part of this Agreement in
place of such deleted provision a substitute of comparable value thereto, and in the
event of an impasse exceeding forty-five (45) days, either party hereto may submit
the issue for final disposition, to arbitration by the American Arbitration Association
pursuant to its rules, which Association shall be empowered to make an award of
comparable value or compensation as reasonably implements the intent of the
parties under the deleted provision.
