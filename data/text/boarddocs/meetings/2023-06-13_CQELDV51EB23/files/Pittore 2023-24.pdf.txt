Mr. Richard Pittore

Re: Employment Letter Agreement
Dear Rich,

Scope of Employment

Effective July 1, 2023, you shall be employed as the Director of School Facilities
WU.

Salary

Your regular base salary for the year July 1, 2023 through June 30, 2024 shall be
$123,510 plus $1,000 for a travel allowance for a total salary of $124,510.

Career Increment

You will receive a career increment (longevity) to follow the Irvington
Administrator's Association Agreement. Thus, you will receive $4,000 to reflect your
27 years of service.

Work Year

Your work year for the 2023-2024 school year shall be twelve (12) months.
Effective July 1, 2023, you shall be entitled to twenty three (23) days of vacation leave
annually. You may carry over up to a maximum of 23 days vacation and be
reimbursed upon leaving the district at a rate of 1/260 of your salary for each day. You
may cash in up to five (5) days of unused vacation time each year at the current per
diem rate. Vacation days may not be used in the two week period leading up to the
start of school unless expressly permitted by the Superintendent of Schools.

Health Insurance
The Board will provide you with Single, Two Person or Family Coverage Health
Insurance at the following cost to you:

Effective July 1, 2023: 18% of Premium

You will also receive a welfare fund allocation equivalent to teachers’ welfare
fund. For 2023-2024 the amount is $1,200.

Retiree Health insurance

You shall be entitled to health insurance benefits into retirement only after
completing ten (10) years of consecutive service in the District immediately
preceding your retirement. Upon your resignation from the District for purposes of
retirement under the New York State Employees Retirement System, you shail be
permitted to continue coverage under the District's health insurance program for
you and your spouse, subject to the terms and conditions of the District’s health plan
in effect. The District shall pay one-hundred percent (100%) of the health insurance
premiums for such coverage into retirement. The District will only be responsible for
the basic Medicare Part B reimbursement amount. You shall also have the option to
cover any additional cost for coverage of dependents that you wish to include
subject to the terms of the District's health plan in effect.

Written Agreement

By signing this letter, you acknowledge that this Agreement has been arrived
at jointly between yourself and the Board and shall not be construed against any
party hereto as being the drafter hereof or causing the same to be drafted. By
signing this letter, you acknowledge that this Agreement shall continue in full force
and effect for the term of your service to the District unless otherwise terminated in
writing.

Severability

If any provision of this Letter Agreement is determined to be contrary to law, it
is understood and agreed that such provision shall be deemed deleted and the
balance of this Letter Agreement without such deleted provision, if otherwise lawful,
shall remain in full force and effect. If such deleted provision involves compensation
or money benefit, you and the Board agree to negotiate as part of this Agreement in
place of such deleted provision a substitute of comparable value thereto, and in the
event of an impasse exceeding forty-five (45) days, either party hereto may submit
the issue for final disposition, to arbitration by the American Arbitration Association
pursuant to its rules, which Association shall be empowered to make an award of
comparable value or compensation as reasonably implements the intent of the
parties under the deleted provision.

Applicable Law

By signing this letter, you acknowledge that this Letter Agreement has been
entered into and shall be governed under and in accordance with the laws of the
State of New York, without reference to that state’s conflict of laws rules.

Entire Agreement

This Letter Agreement constitutes the full and complete Agreement between
you and the District and may not be altered, changed, added to, deleted from, or
modified except through mutual consent of you and the Board.
