WHEREAS, the Board has been advised that it is legally permissible for the
Board to contractually obligate itself to provide the terms and conditions of
employment herein more particularly described; and

WHEREAS, both parties acknowledge that the termination of the employment
of the Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human Resources, her employment
status as a probationary employee and whether she will be granted tenured status at
the conclusion of her probationary period are subject solely to the procedures set forth
at length in various provisions of the Laws of the State of New York regarding
probationary employees and nothing herein contained shall be deemed to have
modified the foregoing in any respect; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Education at its regular meeting to be held on the
day of June, 2023, by resolution adopted, intends to authorize the employment of the
Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human Resources and the provisions of
this Agreement relating to the compensation and other employment benefits which
are to extend to June 30, 2024, unless otherwise terminated pursuant to Education
Law §3019 and §3031 (during the Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human
Resources’ status as a probationary employee) and/or Education Law §3020-a (in the
event the Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human Resources is granted
tenure at the conclusion of her probationary period); and

WHEREAS, the parties acknowledge that, subject to the foregoing, the term of
this Agreement shall be effective from July 1, 2023 through June 30, 2024.

NOW, THEREFORE, based upon the mutual covenants and understanding
between the parties, it is understood and agreed:

fringe benefits, if any, shall be determined as follows:

a. On or about May 1, 2024, the Board shall meet to discuss the
Superintendent's recommendation, if any, as to what appropriate salary
increase and/or benefit modification(s) shall be made to the salary and
benefits provided in this Agreement.

BENEFITS AND WORKING CONDITIONS:

a. Work Year: The Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human
Resources shall work the full year, including recess periods and during
the summer, except for use of paid leave days, weekends and any state
or national holidays.

b. Vacation Leave: The Board shall provide vacation benefits as follows:

1.

The Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human
Resources shall receive twenty-eight (28) vacation leave
days per year, effective July Ist of each school year.

Said vacation leave must be utilized during the summer
months of July and/or August. In the event the Assistant
Superintendent for Instruction & Human Resources is
unable to utilize said vacation leave days during July or
August due to job responsibilities, she may, upon the prior
approval of the Superintendent of Schools, use said
vacation leave at other times during the year and/or she
may elect to receive the per diem cash value of unused
vacation leave days up to a maximum of seven (7) unused
vacation days per year. The Assistant Superintendent for
Instruction & Human Resources shall also have the option
to carry over up to a maximum of twelve (12) unused
vacation days into the following fiscal year. In no event shall
the Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human
Resources be permitted to exceed forty (40) days of
vacation leave (annual allotment of 28 days plus up to 12
carryover days) during any fiscal year.

Upon separation from employment with the District, the
Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human
Resources shall not be entitled to receive any cash payment
for unused vacation leave days.

Leave for Family Illness and Death in the Family (Bereavement): The
Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human Resources shall be
entitled to three (3) days leave without deduction in pay for illness in the
immediate family, effective July Ist of each school year. These days shall
not accumulate. For purposes of family illness days, the immediate
family is defined as: father, mother, brother, sister, son, daughter, or
husband.

In addition, the Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human
Resources shall be entitled to three (3) days leave without deduction in
pay for each occurrence of death in the immediate family. For purposes
of bereavement days, the immediate family is defined as mother, father,
sister, brother, children, mother-in-law, father-in-law, grandparents, or
husband. These days shall not accumulate.

Health Insurance: The Assistant Superintendent for Instruction &
Human Resources shall have the opportunity to participate in the health
care plan offered by the Board of Education. In order to participate in
said plan, the Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human
Resources shall be required to contribute towards the cost of the
premium for individual, two (2) person coverage or family coverage at
the following rate: Effective July 1, 2023 — 22%. In lieu of enrolling in the
District's health insurance plan, the Assistant Superintendent shall
receive a cash payment equivalent to 40% of the District's premium cost
for waiving the applicable coverage to be paid semi-annually in the same
manner as other similarly situated administrators.

Retiree Health Insurance: The Assistant Superintendent for Instruction
& Human Resources and her spouse shall be entitled to health insurance

coverage into retirement subject to the terms and conditions of the
District health plan and provided the Assistant Superintendent
Instruction & Human Resources retires from the Irvington School District
into the Teachers’ Retirement System (“TRS”) after no less than ten (10)
years of continuous service in the District. The Assistant Superintendent
shall contribute to the cost of the annual premium(s) for health
insurance coverage into retirement at the same percentage rate in effect
at the time of her retirement.

Life Insurance and Long-Term Disability Insurance: The Board will pay
the full cost of “term” life insurance that includes $100,000 life insurance
and a long-term disability insurance policy with a 90-day waiting period
with 60% of income payable to age sixty-five (65), due to disability
resulting from sickness or accident of the Assistant Superintendent for
Instruction & Human Resources. Notwithstanding the foregoing, upon
separation of employment from the District, this provision shall be of no
further force or effect.

Welfare Fund: The Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human
Resources shall be entitled to receive a payment of $1,000 as an
additional fringe benefit for Dental, Optical and other like benefits.

Professional Organizations: The Assistant Superintendent for Instruction
& Human Resources shall be eligible for reimbursement for the purchase
of membership(s) in up to three (3) professional organizations, which
have been approved by the Superintendent of Schools or his/her
designee. Reimbursement shall be contingent upon submission of
proof of payment.

Flexible Spending Account: The Assistant Superintendent for Instruction
& Human Resources shall be entitled to participate in the flexible
spending account established by the Board, with no funds to be supplied
by the Board.

Expense Reimbursement: The Assistant Superintendent for Instruction
& Human Resources shall be entitled to reimbursement for expenses
related to attendance at professional meetings or events held outside of
school hours, when such attendance is directed by the District, upon
submission of receipts substantiating such expenses.

Professional Duties: The Assistant Superintendent for Instruction &
Human Resources further agrees to devote her full time, skill, labor and
attention to her employment as Assistant Superintendent for Instruction
& Human Resources during the term of this Agreement, provided,
however, that by advance agreement with the Superintendent of
Schools, she may undertake consultative work, speaking engagements,
graduate teaching, lecturing or other professional duties or obligations
to the extent same shall not interfere with her duties as Assistant
Superintendent for Instruction & Human Resources. Any such
consultative work, speaking engagements, graduate teaching, lecturing

B WRITTEN AGREEMENT:

This Agreement shall continue in full force and effect from July 1, 2023 through
June 30, 2024, (subject to the Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human
Resources’ probationary and/or tenured status as set forth in the preceding
WHEREAS clauses) unless otherwise terminated, modified or extended by agreement
in writing between the parties.

4. SEVERABILITY:

If any provision of this Agreement is determined to be contrary to law, it is
understood and agreed that such provision shall be deemed deleted and the balance
of the Agreement without such deleted provision, if otherwise lawful, shall remain in
full force and effect. If any such deleted provision involves compensation or a money
benefit, the undersigned parties agree to negotiate as part of the Agreement in place
of such deleted provision a substitute of comparable value thereto, and in the event
of an impasse exceeding forty-five (45) days, either party hereto may submit the issue
for final disposition, to arbitration by the American Arbitration Association pursuant to
its rules, which Association shall be empowered to make an award of comparable
value or compensation as reasonably implements the intent of the parties under the
deleted provision.

5. EFFECTIVE DATE:

This Agreement shall be effective on the Ist day of July, 2023.
