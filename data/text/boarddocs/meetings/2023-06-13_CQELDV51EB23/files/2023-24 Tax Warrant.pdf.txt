Dear Town Comptroller:

I, Evan Gross, Deputy District Clerk of the Irvington Union Free School District, Town of Greenburgh,
County of Westchester, State of New York, do hereby certify that the following budget and tax items
for the 2023-2024 fiscal year, beginning July 1, 2023 and ending June 30, 2024 are true and accurate.
This is an official breakdown of the warrant for use for your collection of taxes.

At its meeting on June 13, 2023 the Board of Education adopted a motion containing the following
resolution:

MOVED, that the voters of the Irvington Union Free School District, by a vote of 466 to 127, adopted a
budget for the school year commencing July 1, 2023 in the amount of $73,079,120; that the sums as set
forth in the forms of budget heretofore submitted to the voter be appropriated; that the taxes in the
necessary amounts be levied therefore; that the Board members be authorized to execute a warrant
therefore, and that the warrant be delivered to the Town authorities.

The major budget categories and their respective amounts are listed below:

General Support                            $ 9,194,969
Instruction                                $ 39,732,355
Transportation                             $ 3,350,499
Community Services                                  0
Undistributed Expenses
 Benefits                    $ 17,015,116
 Debt Service                $ 3,711,181
 Transfers to Other Funds    $    75,000
