Action (Consent):  9.4 NYSPHSSA Referendum Ballot Vote

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District is a member high school of Section One, Inc. of the New York State Public High School Athletics Association (NYSPHSAA); and WHEREAS, the primary purpose of Section One is to provide opportunities for fair and safe interscholastic athletics for public school students within the geographic limits of the Section; and WHEREAS, the Executive Committee of Section One has recognized certain inherent advantages for non-public schools, including the ability to enroll student-athletes who reside outside of geographic limits and the capacity to attract athletes regardless of location based on athletic success; and WHEREAS, Section One has determined that the appropriate way to advance its primary purpose and address these inequities is to limit eligibility for sectional post-season tournaments to public schools; and WHEREAS, the Executive Committee has proposed specific amendments to the Section One Constitution and Bylaws to implement these changes while allowing non-public schools to compete in post-season play amongst themselves; and WHEREAS, a Referendum Ballot has been issued to all member schools to vote on these amendments; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District hereby: 1. Directs the district’s athletic and administrative leadership to cast a vote of YES on the referendum to amend the Section One, Inc. Constitution and Bylaws. 2. Authorizes the Chief School Officer/Superintendent, the High School Principal, and the Athletic Director to execute the required signatures on the official ballot. 3. Mandates that the completed and signed ballot be submitted to Todd Santabarbara, Executive Director of Section One, no later than the deadline of Friday, June 12, 2026.

Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education approve the Consent Agenda which includes 9-Business of the Board Items; 9.1-9.4; 10-Curriculum & Instruction Item 10.1; 11-Personnel Matters 11.1-11.11; 12-Business Office Matters 12.1-12.17.
