IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT
40 NORTH BROADWAY
IRVINGTON, NY 10533

This agreement made this 6° day of May, 2026, by and between Irvington Union Free School District, 40 North Broadway,
Irvington, New York, 10533, party of the first part, hereinafter referred to as the “Board,” and Mountain Lake Academy, 386 River
Road, Lake Placid, New York, 12946, party of the second part, hereinafter referred to as the “Contractor.”

WITNESSETH: The Board is authorized by law to contract with institutions for the instruction of children with
disabilities; and

WHEREAS, the Contractor warrants that it has reviewed the individualized education program (IEP) of each pupil
to be enrolled in its school and represents that it will provide the level of services identified in the IEP to meet the needs of such
children with disabilities.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties hereto mutually agree as follows:

1. TERM OF AGREEMENT: This Agreement shali be in effect for the period 07/01/2026-06/30/2027, unless
terminated earlier, as set forth herein.

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES: The Contractor will provide adequate instruction in accordance with the provisions
relating to eligibility of schools contained in Part 200 of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education of the State of New York
(hereinafter referred to as the “Commissioner of Education”) to attend Contractor’s school. The school year is hereby defined as July
1 to June 30. The Contractor will provide a special education program for eligible children with disabilities placed in the Contractor’s
school in accordance with the provisions of Education Law sections 4407 and 4408 and Part 200 of the Regulations of the
Commissioner of Education for the part of the school year for which such children are approved for placement in such school by the
Commissioner of Education and will operate and administer its special educational program consistent with all the terms and
conditions required under section 200.7(b) of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education. At all times the Contractor shall
abide by laws and regulations pertaining to confidentiality including, but not limited to, the Family Educational Rights and Privacy
Act (“FERPA”), Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (“IDEA”) and New York State Education Law Section 2-d,

3a. In full consideration for the education services to be rendered by the Contractor to the Board for the period July

— June 30" of each school year under the terms of this agreement, the Board will pay to the Contractor for each child the agreed

upon tuition rate approved by the Commissioner of Education and approved by the Director of the New York State Division of the

Budget. Where such education is provided for periods of substantially less than the duration of the full July through June program,
consideration will be prorated accordingly, the specific amount to be fixed by the State of New York (hereinafter the “State”)

b. In full consideration for the education and maintenance services to be rendered by the Contractor for the July
through August program component the Board wilt pay the Contractor the agreed upon tuition rate approved by the Commissioner of
Education and approved by the Director of the New York State Division of the Budget, and the agreed upon maintenance rate
approved by the Director of the New York State Division of the Budget for each child. Where such education and maintenance are
provided for periods of substantially less than the duration of the full July through August program, consideration will be prorated
accordingly, the specific amount to be fixed by the State.

4a. PAYMENT SCHEDULE: Payments for the school year shall be due in accordance with the following schedule:

(1) On or before September 20 of the school year, the total amount for the July through August portion of the
school year based upon actual enrollment and attendance date for the July through August component.

(2) The ten-month program shall be paid monthly, within thirty (30) days of receipt of the invoice based upon
enrollment and the attendance data.

(3) In the event that enrollment is for less than a full month, tuition will be prorated accordingly.

b. For the purposes of this agreement, the definition of calculation of enrollment and attendance shall be in
accordance with section 175.6 of the regulations of the Commissioner of Education as determined by the Board.

5. REPORTS OF STUDENTS: The Contractor will obtain whatever releases or other legal documents are necessary
in order that the Contractor may render full complete reports concerning the education and progress of the pupil or pupils covered by
the terms of this agreement. The Contractor will render such reports to the Board at any time that such reports are made to the parents
of the pupil or pupils covered by the terms of this agreement and will render such additional reports as may be required by the terms of
this agreement or as requested by the Board. It is understood and agreed by the parties to this agreement that the full responsibility
shall permit the Commissioner of Education to withdraw approval for the placement of such child in such program in which event this
agreement shall be canceled forthwith in regard to such child for which such releases are not submitted.

6a. Pursuant to section 4405 of the Education Law, payment of maintenance expenses for residential pupils for the
September through June program shall be made by the county wherein each such pupil resides (or the city in the case of a county
contained in the City of New York) at the commencement of the school year (July L) for which maintenance is paid. The charge for
maintenance may not exceed the amount approved for such purpose by the Commissioner of Social Services of the State and the
Director of the Budget of the State. Such payment should be made on the same terms and conditions as tuition, but the payment
schedule will be determined by the appropriate county or city.

b. BED HOLD-CSE REQUEST: In the event the Committee on Special Education (CSE) requests that a residential
bed be held for a student during any period of absence, and the applicable county Department of Social Services (DSS) does not
approve or pay the maintenance portion of the rate for such bed hold (including maintenance as defined under Education Law), the
Board shali be responsible for payment of the unpaid maintenance portion for the duration of the bed hold at the applicable approved
maintenance rate, Such Board payment shall be made notwithstanding any subsequent DSS decision, appeal, or reconsideration. For
purposes of this Agreement, “maintenance” shall have the meaning set forth in Education Law § 4401(3) and shall include room and
board and other maintenance-related charges approved under New York law and regulation. Maintenance rates for residential
programs shall be those annually established by the Commissioner of Social Services pursuant to Education Law § 4405 and related
regulations, including those concerning state aid and maintenance payments for approved providers set forth at 8 NYCRR § 200.9(h).

7. No patent or guardian or any other person shall be required to make any payment for tuition or maintenance on
behalf of any child with a disability covered by this agreement in addition to the payments made by the State and the county or city.

8. The Contractor will maintain the standard set forth under section 200,7(b) of the Regulations of the
Commissioner of Education to preserve its status as an approved school for the education of children with disabilities. It is understood
and agreed by the parties that failure to do so shall render this agreement void, in which case the Contractor shall be entitled to no
compensation from the Board other than any amounts already received for that portion of such school year.

9. The State, acting through the Department of Education or the Department of Audit and Control, shall have the
right to examine any or all accounts kept by the Contractor in connection with this agreement.

10. COMMISSIONER VISITS: The Contractor shall be subject to the visitation of the Commissioner of Education
or their designated representatives.

11. This agreement shall take effect as of the I* of July, 2026. This agreement may be terminated by written notice
by either party to the other and the Commissioner of Education not less than 90 days in advance of such termination. In the cvent the
Contractor or the Board terminates this agreement with or without cause such termination shal! not discharge the parties existing
obligations to each other up to and inctuding the date of termination of the agreement; except as to the obligations contained in
paragraphs $ and 12 of this agreement which shall survive such termination.

12. REPORTS TO STATE: The Contractor hereby agrees to furnish to the State all reports, audits, etc., required to
make determinations as to eligibility under the provisions of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education. Such materials shall
be furnished at such times as are required by the State. Failure to submit required materials within ten (10) days of demand or as
required by regulation shall constitute grounds for the Commissioner of Education to withdraw approval for such program.

13. The Contractor agrees to provide the State access to all relevant records which the State requires to determine
either the Contractor’s or the Board’s compliance with applicable Federal or State statutes or regulations with the effect of law, which
regulate cither the execution of the agreement or the performance of obligations under the agreement. The Contractor agrees to retain
all materials and records relevant to the execution or performance of the agreement in accordance with the provision of section 74.21
of volume 34 of the Code of Federal Regulations, but in no event less than six years from the days of this agreement.

14, This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

[OCR]
15, The Board shall have all of its common law rights of set-off. These rights and powers shall include, but not be
limited to, the Board's option to withhold for the purposes of set-off any moneys due to the Contractor under this contract up to any
amounts due and owing the Board with regard to this contract, or amounts due and owing to the Board for any other reason.

16. INSURANCE: Contractor shall maintain, in full force and effect, during all times this Agreement is in force,
the following types of insurance:

Commercial General Liability (GCL) Insurance: $1,000,000 per occurrence/$3,000,000 aggregate purchased from
an A.M, Best A-rated or better insurer, licensed in New York State. The policy will state that the organization’s coverage shail be
primary and non-contributory coverage for the District, its Board of Education, employees and volunteers with a waiver of
subrogation in favor of the District. The GCL must also include Sexual Misconduct and Assault coverage of at least $1,000,000. Such
Sexual Misconduct and Assault Coverage must be affirmed. If the CGL policy does not include coverage for the art therapy services
rendered to the district, a separate policy with the same liability limits must be purchased by the Contractor.

Professional Errors and Omissions Insurance: $1,000,000 per occurrence/$3,000,000 aggregate for the professional
acts of the consultant/Contractor performed under the contract for the District, If written on a “claims made” basis, the effective date
must pre-date the inception of the contract or agreement. Coverage shall remain in effect for three years following the completion of
the work. Automobile Liability: $1,000,000 combined single limit for owned, hired, borrowed and non-owned motor vehicles,
Umbrella/Excess Insurance: $5,000,000 each Occurrence and Aggregate.

Umbrella/Excess shali be on a fo!low-form basis over the required General Liability, Automobile, and Professional
Liability coverages. Workers’ Compensation: proof of coverage must be on the approved specific form, as required by the New York
State Workers’ Compensation Board. ACORD certificates are not acceptable. All such insurance shall reflect that the Board is an
additional insured by providing the District with endorsement CG 2026 or its equivalent. A completed copy of the endorsement must
be attached to the certificate of insurance. The Contractor agrees to indemnify the District for any applicable deductible and
self-insured retentions. The Contractor acknowledges that failure to obtain such insurance on behalf of the District constitutes a
material breach of contract. The Contractor is to provide the district with a certificate of insurance, evidencing the above requirements
have been met, prior to the commencement of work. The Board shall receive no less than 15 days written notice in the event of the
termination thereof.

17. INREMNIFICATION: Contractor shall indemnify, defend and hold the Board, its employees, officers, agents
and Board of Education harmless from any claims, liabilities, suits, proceedings and actions, of whatever name or nature as the same
may relate, in any manner, to the services provided by Contractor and its personnel to the Board pursuant to this Agreement. Said
indemnification and defense shall apply to any claim, liability, suit, proceeding and action in which the Board, its employees, officers,
agents and Board of Education may be named as a party, notwithstanding that the Contractor may deem said claim, liability, suit,
proceeding or action frivolous or without merit.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this agreement the day and year first above written.
