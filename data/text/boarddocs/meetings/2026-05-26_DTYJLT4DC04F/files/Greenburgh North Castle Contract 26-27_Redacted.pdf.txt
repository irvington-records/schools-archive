2026-2027 CONTRACT FOR EDUCATIONAL SERVICES

This Agreement made between:

GREENBURGH NORTH CASTLE UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (hereinafter,
“PROVIDER’) and Irvington UFSD (hereinafter, “DISTRICT”).

WHEREAS the DISTRICT desires to obtain certain services and work to be performed by the PROVIDER;
and the DISTRICT agrees to make payment(s) for services rendered; and

WHEREAS, the PROVIDER has the necessary qualifications and experience and agrees to perform the
services and work;

THEREFORE, it is agreed as follows:

1) TERM: The term of this agreement shall begin on 07 / 01 /2026 and shall end on the completion
of 06/30/2027. All the work to be performed by PROVIDER shall be completed, approved and
accepted on or before the date on which this agreement cnds. In no case shall the term of this
agreement extend beyond 06/30 /2027 or as further extended in writing, by mutual consent of all
parties.

2) SCOPE OF WORK: PROVIDER shall provide special classes and/or related services as
specified in the Individual Education Program for the pupil(s).

3) PERFORMANCE OF WORK: the DISTRICT shall make available to the PROVIDER
materials, equipment, supplies and other resources necessary to perform the work. The parties
collectively agree that such equipment, supplies and other resources shall not be extensive and the
PROVIDER shall not purchase any such materials without the prior approval of the DISTRICT
or if the PROVIDER does so, (s)he does so at his/her own expense. The work shall be deemed
completed when, in the judgment of the DISTRICT; it has been approved as being in compliance
with all aspects of the Scope of Work and has been accepted by the DISTRICT.

4) INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY: All intellectual property produced in the performance of the
work, including material which is or could be subject to a copyright, trademark or patent, shall be
the exclusive property of the DISTRICT.

5) PAYMENT FOR SERVICES: Payment(s) shall be made upon satisfactory completion, approval
and acceptance of the work and submission of an invoice or other documentations required by the
DISTRICT. Payment shall be made thirty days upon receipt of an invoice. Such payment can only
be modified through the written, mutual consent of the parties. The DISTRICT shall pay for
services at the tuition rate set by the New York State Education Department.

6) INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR: the PROVIDER is an independent contractor and shatl not
be considered an employee of the DISTRICT FOR ANY PURPOSE. As such, the PROVIDER is
solely responsible for scheduling, directing and completing the work. Under no circumstances will
PROVIDER be eligible or allowed to receive or participate in any benefit available to DISTRICT
employees. The DISTRICT will furnish the PROVIDER with an Internal Revenue Service Form
1099 and PROVIDER will be responsible to file such returns and reports and to pay such taxes or

[OCR]
other charges as may be associated with the payment(s) to the PROVIDER pursuant to this
Agreement.

7) INDEMNIFICATION AND CHAIN OF CUSTODY: Custody of students shall transfer from
THE DISTRICT (or its authorized transportation subcontractor) to THE PROVIDER upon the
student’s arrival and physical drop-off at the school or program site. THE PROVIDER shall retain
custody of the students during the school day, including all instructional periods, meals, recess, and
other program-related activities under its supervision, Custody shall transfer back to THE
DISTRICT (or its authorized transportation subcontractor) once a student boards the bus or other
authorized vehicle for transportation at the end of the school day.

THE DISTRICT shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless THE PROVIDER, including its officers,
employees, and agents, from and against any and all claims, demands, liabilities, damages, or costs
(including attorney's fees) arising out of or related to student transportation, including travel to and
from the program site, when such transportation is provided by THE DISTRICT or its subcontractor.

Conversely, THE PROVIDER shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless THE DISTRICT, including
its officers, employees, and agents, from and against any and all claims, demands, liabilities, damages,
or costs (including attorney's fees) arising out of or related to the custody, care, or supervision of
students during the regular school day, excluding times when students are being transported.

8) INSURANCE PROVISIONS: THE DISTRICT shail maintain, at its own expense, insurance
coverage that are equivalent to those required of THE PROVIDER. Each party shall provide the
other with mutual indemnification in accordance with the established chain of custody described
above.

THE DISTRICT shall further indemnify, defend, and hold harmless THE PROVIDER from and against
any and all claims arising from negligent acts or omissions by THE DISTRICT or any of its authorized
transportation subcontractors. THE PROVIDER shall be named as an unrestricted additional insured
under THE DISTRICT’s General Liability and Automobile Liability insurance policies.

Insurance coverage maintained by both THE DISTRICT and THE PROVIDER must include at
minimum:

a) Commercial General Liability: $1,000,000 per occurrence / $2,000,000 aggregate

b) Automobile Liability: $1,000,000 combined single limit

c) Workers’ Compensation: As required by New York State law
d) Professional Liability (if applicable): $1,000,000 per occurrence

Proof of insurance, including the additional insured endorsements for any transportation company or
subcontractor utilized by THE DISTRICT, must be provided to THE PROVIDER prior to the start of
services, All certificates of insurance must explicitly list THE PROVIDER as an additional insured and
must be maintained throughout the duration of the agreement.

Both parties shall comply with all applicable federal and New York State laws and regulations,
including the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), with respect to the custody and
transfer of student records and other confidential information.

[OCR]
9) NON- DISCRIMINATION; the DISTRICT and PROVIDER shall not illegally discriminate on
the basis of race, religion, color, ethnic background, gender, age, handicapping condition, sexual
orientation or any other basis which is protected by federal, state or local laws in the employment
of staff or in contracting with vendors, supplies or sub-contractors in the performance of the work.

10) TERMINATION; Upon the breach of any of the terms of this Agreement by PROVIDER and the
failure to cure the breach within ten (10) days after written notice, the DISTRICT may terminate
this agreement and all of its obligations to the PROVIDER. The DISTRICT may terminate this
Agreement without notice for breach of good faith, confidentiality or the provisions in paragraph
10 below.

11) SAVING CLAUSE: the PROVIDER shall not assign or transfer this Agreement, or any part
thereof, or any interest therein, without first receiving written approval from the DISTRICT. All
modifications of, or changes in, the terms of this Agreement must be in writing.

12) MISCELLANEOUS:

a. The PROVIDER shall not make any representations or commitments on behalf of the
DISTRICT.

b. The PROVIDER shall comply with all federal, state and local laws, rules and regulations that
apply to the performance of the work.

c. This Agreement shall be governed by and be interpreted in accordance with the laws of the
State of New York,

d. This written Agreement contains the entire agreement between the parties. There are no other
agreements or understandings concerning the terms of this Agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have hereunto set their names.
