                  BOARD OF EDUCATION OF THE IRVINGTON
                 UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT ("DISTRICT" or
                           "BOARD") AND THE IRVINGTON
                          ADMINISTRATORS' ASSOCIATION
                     ("ASSOCIATION") RESOLVING A CERTAIN
                          IMPROPER PRACTICE CHARGE

         WHEREAS, the BOARD and the ASSOCIATION are parties to a certain
Collective Bargaining Agreement ("CBA") dated July 1, 2006 through June 30, 2009,
and

          WHEREAS, the parties have completed negotiations for a successor collective
bargaining agreement and intend to execute a memorandum of agreement dated March
28,2012 CMOA") setting forth the terms and conditions for a contract with a duration of
July 1,2009 through June 30,2016; and

        WHEREAS, one of the terms of said MOA provides for the resolution of a
certain improper practice charge ("U-30245"), dated August 9,2010; and
          WHEREAS, the parties are desirous of codifying the terms and conditions of said
resolution by way of a separate side letter agreement;

          NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY STIPULATED AND AGREED BY
THE PARTIES AS FOLLOWS:

  1)      The pending improper practice charge dated August 9,2010, concerning merit
  pay is hereby withdrawn with prejudice.

  2) Each administrator who was employed for the full year during the 2010-11 school
  year and remains employed by the District as an administrator at the time of ratification
  ofthe parties' MOA, shall receive a payment of $6000. Such payment shall be made
  within sixty (60) days ofthe ratification ofthe parties' MOA in a separate check. The
  $6000 shall be added to the current salary of each ofthe eligible administrators and will
  be used to establish the new base salary for the 2011-12 school year.

   3)      Each administrator who was employed for the full year during the 2010-11 school
   year and remains employed by the District as an administrator at the time of ratification
   ofthe parties' MOA, shall receive aone -time bonus payment of $1000 on or about
   August 1 2012. Such one-time bonus payment shall not be applied to the base salary
      ofthe eligible administrators and shall be paid to eligible administrators in a separate
      check.
