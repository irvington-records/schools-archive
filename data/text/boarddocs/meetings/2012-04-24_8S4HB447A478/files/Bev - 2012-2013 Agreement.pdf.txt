               AGREEMENT made this _____ day of April, 2012, by and between
               BEVERLY MILLER (hereinafter referred to as “Assistant
               Superintendent for Business and Operations” and/or “Employee”),
               residing at 18 Kallen Avenue, Schenectady, New York 12304, and the
               BOARD OF EDUCATION of the IRVINGTON UNION FREE
               SCHOOL DISTRICT (hereinafter referred to as “Board”) with
               offices for the transaction of business located at 6 Dows Lane,
               Irvington, New York 10533.

        WHEREAS, the Board has been advised that it is legally permissible for the Board to
contractually obligate itself to provide the terms and conditions of employment herein more
particularly described; and

        WHEREAS, Beverly Miller was appointed to a probationary period for the position of
Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations pursuant to the Education Law of the State
of New York, effective July 1 , 2011; and

        WHEREAS, the aforementioned appointment was amended to render the probationary
period effective as of July 7, 2011; and

        WHEREAS, both parties acknowledge that the termination of the employment of the
Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations, her employment status as a probationary
employee and whether she will be granted tenured status at the conclusion of her probationary
period are subject solely to the procedures set forth at length in various provisions of the Laws of
the State of New York regarding probationary employees and nothing herein contained shall be
deemed to have modified the foregoing in any respect; and

       WHEREAS, the parties had previously entered into an Agreement for the 2011-12 school
year which was effective from July 7, 2011 through June 30, 2012;and

       WHEREAS, the parties desire to enter into a new employment agreement for the 2012-13
school year effective from July 1, 2012 through June 30, 2013;

         NOW, THEREFORE, based upon the mutual covenants and understanding between the
parties, it is understood and agreed:

1.     GENERAL PROVISIONS:

       The salary of the Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations for the 2012-2013
school year shall be $181,800.00. Increases in compensation and/or fringe benefits, if any, shall be
determined as follows:

       a.      On or about June 15, 2013, the Board shall meet to discuss the Superintendent’s
               recommendation, if any, as to what appropriate salary increase and/or benefit
               modification(s) shall be made to the salary and benefits provided in this Agreement.
               The decision of the Board shall be communicated to the Superintendent, and

            thereafter by the Superintendent to the Assistant Superintendent for Business and
            Operations prior to June 30, 2013.

     b.     On or about August 1, 2012, the Board shall compensate the Assistant
            Superintendent for Business and Operations in the amount of $2,000.00 as a one-
            time payment not to be added to base wages for meritorious service to the District.

2.   BENEFITS AND WORKING CONDITIONS:

     a.      The work year for the Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations shall be
     twelve12 months (July 1st - June 30th)), which work year shall encompass the approved
     school calendar and will provide for 22 vacation days to be used during the months of July
     and/or August (or at other times during the school year with the approval of the
     Superintendent of Schools) and shall exclude the use of leave time, if any, as more
     particularly set forth below.

     b.     The Board shall provide vacation benefits as follows:

                    1.     The Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations shall
                           receive twenty-two (22) vacation leave days per year, effective July 1st
                           of each school year;

                    2.     The Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations shall be
                           entitled to carry over up to five (5) unused vacation leave days for use
                           by the employee in the following school year;

                    3.     Upon separation from employment with the District, the Assistant
                           Superintendent for Business and Operations shall not be entitled to
                           receive any cash payment for unused vacation leave days.

     c.     The Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations shall receive twelve (12)
            sick leave days per year, effective July 1st of each school year.

                    1.     Sick leave days may be accumulated without maximum for purposes
                           of the employee’s use;

                    2.     Upon separation from employment with the District, the Assistant
                           Superintendent for Business and Operations shall not be entitled to
                           receive any cash payment for unused sick leave days.

     d.     The Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations shall receive five (5)
            personal leave days per year, effective July 1st of each school year, for the purpose of
            transacting personal business which cannot be conducted at any time other than
            during regular work hours.

                    1.     Unused personal leave days shall not be accumulated;



                                                                                                  2

                       2.     Upon separation from employment with the District, the Assistant
                              Superintendent for Business and Operations shall not be entitled to
                              receive any cash payment for unused personal leave days.

       e.      In lieu of enrolling in the District’s health insurance plan, the Assistant
       Superintendent shall receive a cash payment equivalent to 45% of the District’s premium
       cost for two person coverage, to be paid semi-annually in the same manner as other similarly
       situated administrators.

3.     WRITTEN AGREEMENT:

         This Agreement shall continue in full force and effect from July 1, 2012 through June 30,
2013 unless otherwise terminated, modified or extended by agreement in writing between the
parties.

4.     SEVERABILITY:

        If any provision of this Agreement is determined to be contrary to law, it is understood and
agreed that such provision shall be deemed deleted and the balance of the Agreement without such
deleted provision, if otherwise lawful, shall remain in full force and effect. If any such deleted
provision involves compensation or a money benefit, the undersigned parties agree to negotiate as
part of the Agreement in place of such deleted provision a substitute of comparable value thereto,
and in the event of an impasse exceeding forty-five (45) days, either party hereto may submit the
issue for final disposition, to arbitration by the American Arbitration Association pursuant to its
rules, which Association shall be empowered to make an award of comparable value or
compensation as reasonably implements the intent of the parties under the deleted provision.

5.     EFFECTIVE DATE:

       This Agreement shall be effective on the 1st day of July, 2012.

6.     ENTIRE AGREEMENT:

        This Agreement constitutes the full and complete agreement between the Board and the
Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations and may not be altered, changed, added to,
deleted from, or modified except through the mutual written consent of the parties.

7.     BOARD APPROVAL

        This Agreement is subject to approval by the Board of Education pursuant to a duly adopted
resolution.
