Category: 7. Business of the Board
Subject: 7.5 Establishment of an Insurance Reserve Fund
Type: Action (Consent)

Reserve management is an integral aspect of maintaining long term health of the district.  New York State General Municipal Law allows for the establishment of an Insurance Liability Reserve for certain uninsured losses, claims, actions or judgments for which the local government is authorized or required to purchase or maintain insurance. This will allow the District to plan and fund for future insurance claims.
