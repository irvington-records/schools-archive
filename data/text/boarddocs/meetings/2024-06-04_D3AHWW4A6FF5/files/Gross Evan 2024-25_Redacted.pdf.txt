WHEREAS, the Board has been advised that it is legally permissible for the
Board to contractually obligate itself to provide the terms and conditions of
employment herein more particularly described; and

WHEREAS, both parties acknowledge that the termination of the
employment of the School Business Administrator, his employment status as a
probationary employee and whether he will be granted tenured status at the
conclusion of his probationary period are subject solely to the procedures set forth at
length in various provisions of the Laws of the State of New York regarding
probationary employees and nothing herein contained shall be deemed to have
modified the foregoing in any respect; and

WHEREAS, both parties acknowledge that the School Business Administrator
may also be appointed to serve as the District Treasurer during a particular school
year. It is further acknowledged that termination from said position is subject solely
to various provisions of the Civil Service Laws of the State of New York and nothing
herein contained shall be deemed to have modified the foregoing in any respect. It is
further acknowledged that the School Business Administrator shall serve in said
District Treasurer position at the pleasure of the Board and may be dismissed at any
time without cause, upon thirty (30) days’ written notice; and

WHEREAS, both parties acknowledge that the School Business Administrator
may also be appointed to serve as the District Clerk during a particular school year. It
is further acknowledged that termination from said position is subject solely to
various provisions of the Civil Service Laws of the State of New York and nothing
herein contained shall be deemed to have modified the foregoing in any respect.;
and

WHEREAS, the Board of Education at its regular meeting held on the 4" day
of June, 2024, by resolution adopted, authorized the employment of the School
Business Administrator and the provisions of this Agreement relating to the
compensation and other employment benefits which are to extend to June 30, 2025,
unless otherwise terminated pursuant to Education Law §3019 and §303] (during the
School Business Administrator's status as a probationary employee) and/or
Education Law §3020-a (in the event the School Business Administrator is granted
tenure at the conclusion of his probationary period); and

WHEREAS, the parties acknowledge that, subject to the foregoing, the term
of this Agreement shall be effective from July 1, 2024 through June 30, 2025.

NOW, THEREFORE, based upon the mutual covenants and understanding
between the parties, it is understood and agreed:

1. GENERAL PROVISIONS:

The salary of the School Business Administrator for the 2024-2025 school year

shall be $144,068.

2. BENEFITS AND WORKING CONDITIONS:

a. Work Year: The School Business Administrator shall work the full year,
including recess periods and during the summer, except for use of paid
leave days, weekends and any state or national holidays.

b. Vacation Leave: The Board shall provide vacation benefits as follows:

1.

The School Business Administrator shall receive thirty-two
(32) vacation leave days per year, effective July Ist of each
school year .

Said vacation leave must be utilized during the summer
months of July and/or August. In the event the School
Business Administrator is unable to utilize said vacation
leave days during July or August due to job responsibilities,
he may, upon the prior approval of the Assistant
Superintendent for Business, use said vacation leave at
other times during the year and/or he may elect to receive
the per diem cash value of unused vacation leave days up
to a maximum of five (5) unused vacation days per year.
The School Business Administrator shall also have the
option to carry over up to a maximum of twelve (12)
unused vacation days into the following fiscal year. In no
event shall the School Business Administrator be
permitted to exceed forty-four (44) days of vacation leave
(annual allotment of 32 days plus up to 12 carryover days)
during any fiscal year.

Upon separation from employment with the District, the
School Business Administrator shall not be entitled to
receive any cash payment for unused vacation leave days.

cy Sick Leave: The School Business Administrator shall receive twelve (12)
sick leave days per year, effective July Ist of each school year .

1.

Sick leave days may be accumulated without maximum
for purposes of the employee's use;

A non-cumulative reserve of thirty (30) working days will
be available to you for an extended illness. You may only
utilize this reserve once in your lifetime. Extended illness
shall mean illness or disability for thirty (30) or more

School Business Administrator shall not be entitled to

Personal Leave: The School Business Administrator shall receive five (5)
personal leave days per year , effective July 1* of each school year, for
the purpose of transacting personal business which cannot be
conducted at any time other than during regular work hours.

1. Unused personal leave days shall not be accumulated;

2. Upon separation from employment with the District, the
School Business Administrator shall not be entitled to
receive any cash payment for unused personal leave days.

Leave for Family lilness and Death in the Family (Bereavement): The
School Business Administrator shall be entitled to three (3) days leave
without deduction in pay for illness in the immediate family, effective
July Ist of each school. These days shall not accumulate. For purposes of
family illness days, the immediate family is defined as: father, mother,
son, daughter, or spouse.

In addition, the School Business Administrator shall be entitled to three
(3) days leave without deduction in pay for each occurrence of death in
the immediate family. For purposes of bereavement days, the
immediate family is defined as mother, father, sister, brother, children,
mother-in-law, father-in-law, grandparents, or spouse. These days shall
not accumulate.

Health Insurance: The School Business Administrator shall have the
opportunity to participate in the health care pian offered by the Board
of Education. In order to participate in said plan, the School Business
Administrator shall be required to contribute towards the cost of the
premium for individual, two (2) person coverage or family coverage at
the following rate: Effective July 1, 2024 - 20%. In lieu of enrolling in the
District's health insurance plan, the School Business Administrator shall
receive a cash payment equivalent to 35% of the District’s premium cost
for waiving the applicable coverage to be paid semi-annually in the
same manner as other similarly situated administrators.

Retiree Health Insurance: The School Business Administrator and his
spouse shall be entitled to health insurance coverage into retirement
subject to the terms and conditions of the District health plan and
provided the School Business Administrator retires from the Irvington
School District into the Teachers’ Retirement System (“TRS”) after no
less than ten (10) years of continuous service in the District. The School

Welfare Fund: The School Business Administrator shall be entitled to
receive a payment of $1,000 as an additional fringe benefit for Dental,
Optical and other like benefits.

Professional Organizations: The School Business Administrator shall be
eligible for reimbursement for the purchase of membership(s) in up to
three (3) professional organizations, which have been approved by the
Superintendent of Schools or his/her designee. Reimbursement shall be
contingent upon submission of proof of payment.

Flexible Spending Account: The School Business Administrator shall be
entitled to participate in the flexible spending account established by
the Board, with no funds to be supplied by the Board.

Expense Reimbursement: The School Business Administrator shall be
entitled to reimbursement for expenses related to attendance at
professional meetings or events held outside of school hours, when
such attendance is directed by the District, upon submission of receipts
substantiating such expenses.

Professional Duties:The School Business Administrator further agrees
to devote his full time, skill, labor and attention to his employment as
School Business Administrator (and as District treasurer to the extent
applicable) during the term of this Agreement, provided, however, that
by advance agreement with the Superintendent of Schools, he may
undertake consultative work, speaking engagements, graduate
teaching, lecturing or other professional duties or obligations to the
extent same shall not interfere with his duties as School Business
Administrator. Any such consultative work, speaking engagements,
graduate teaching, lecturing or other obligations that are outside the
School Business Administrator's normal duties shall occur outside the
regular workday, unless expressly authorized by the Superintendent of
Schools. In the event the School Business Administrator shall receive
compensation from an outside agency for such consultative work,
speaking engagements, lecturing or other professional duties, for an

3. WRITTEN AGREEMENT:

This Agreement shall continue in full force and effect from July 1, 2024 through
June 30, 2025, (subject to the School Business Administrator's probationary and/or
tenured status as set forth in the preceding WHEREAS clauses) unless otherwise
terminated, modified or extended by agreement in writing between the parties.

4. SEVERABILITY:

lf any provision of this Agreement is determined to be contrary to law, it is
understood and agreed that such provision shall be deemed deleted and the
balance of the Agreement without such deleted provision, if otherwise lawful, shall
remain in full force and effect. If any such deleted provision involves compensation
or a money benefit, the undersigned parties agree to negotiate as part of the
Agreement in place of such deleted provision a substitute of comparable vatue
thereto, and in the event of an impasse exceeding forty-five {45) days, either party
hereto may submit the issue for final disposition, to arbitration by the American
Arbitration Association pursuant to its rules, which Association shall be empowered
to make an award of comparable value or compensation as reasonably implements
the intent of the parties under the deleted provision.
