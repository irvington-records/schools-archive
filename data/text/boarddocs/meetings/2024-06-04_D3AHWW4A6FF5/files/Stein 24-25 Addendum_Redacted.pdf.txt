FIRST AMENDMENT, made as of June 4, 2024, by and between CAROL STEIN
(hereinafter referred to as “Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations” and/or
“Employee”), residing at (is and the BOARD OF
EDUCATION of the IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (hereinafter referred to
as “Board”) with offices for the transaction of business located at 6 Dows Lane, Irvington, New
York 10533.

WHEREAS, the parties entered into an Agreement dated June 13, 2023, establishing the
terms and conditions of employment of the Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations
for the period of July 1, 2023 through June 30, 2025 (hereinafter referred to as the “Agreement”);

and
WHEREAS, the parties are desirous of amending said Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, based upon the mutual covenant(s) and understanding(s) between

the parties, it is understood and agreed:

1. All the terms and provisions set forth at length in the aforedescribed Agreement
shall continue in full force and effect during the term of employment of the Assistant

Superintendent for Business and Operations, except as expressly modified herein.
2. Vacation Leave

Effective July 1, 2024, Section 2(b) of the Agreement is hereby deleted in its
entirety and replaced with the following:

b. Vacation Leave: The Board shall provide vacation benefits as follows:

I. The Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations shall
receive thirty-two (32) vacation leave days per year, effective July Ist of each

school year.

2. Said vacation leave must be utilized during the summer months of
July and/or August. In the event the Assistant Superintendent for Business and
Operations is unable to utilize said vacation leave days during July or August due
to job responsibilities, she may, upon the prior approval of the Superintendent of
Schools, use said vacation leave at other times during the year and/or she may

elect to receive the per diem cash value of unused vacation leave days up to a

[OCR]
maximum of seven (7) unused vacation days per year. The Assistant
Superintendent for Business and Operations shall also have the option to carry
over up to a maximum of twelve (12) unused vacation days into the following
fiscal year. In no event shall the Assistant Superintendent for Business and
Operations be permitted to exceed forty-four (44) days of vacation leave (annual

allotment of 32 days plus up to 12 carryover days) during any fiscal year.

3. Upon separation from employment with the District, the Assistant
Superintendent for Business and Operations shall not be entitled to receive any

cash payment for unused vacation leave days.
3. Sick Leave

Effective July 1, 2024, Section 2(c) of the Agreement is hereby deleted in its
entirety and replaced with the following:

e. Sick Leave: The Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations
shall receive twelve (12) sick leave days per year, effective July Ist of each school

year.

I. Sick leave days may be accumulated without maximum for

purposes of the employee's use.

Ch A non-cumulative reserve of thirty (30) working days will be
available to the Assistant Superintendent for Business and Operations for an
extended illness. Extended illness shall mean an illness or disability of thirty (30)
or more working days. This reserve shall only be available on a one-time basis

and shall be in effect only after the accumulated sick leave has been used up.

3. Upon separation from employment with the District, the Assistant
Superintendent for Business and Operations shall not be entitled to receive any

cash payment for unused sick leave days.
4. Leave for Family IlIness and Death in the Famil reavement

Effective July 1, 2024, Section 2(e) of the Agreement is hereby deleted in its

entirety and replaced with the following:

[OCR]
e Leave for Family Illness and Death in the Family (Bereavement): The
Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human Resources shall be entitled to
three (3) days leave without deduction in pay for illness in the immediate family,
effective July Ist of each school year. These days shall not accumulate, For
purposes of family illness days, the immediate family is defined as: father, mother,

son, daughter, or spouse.

In addition, the Assistant Superintendent for Instruction & Human Resources
shall be entitled to three (3) days leave without deduction in pay for each
occurrence of death in the immediate family. For purposes of bereavement days,
the immediate family is defined as mother, father, sister, brother, children,
mother-in-law, father-in-law, grandparents, or husband. These days shall not

accumulate.

5. Board Approval

This Amendment is subject to approval by the Board of Education pursuant to a
duly adopted resolution.
