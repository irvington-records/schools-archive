UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT

June 4, 2024

Ms. Hana Gross

Re: Employment Letter Agreement
Dear Hana:

This letter serves as your Employment Agreement with the Irvington Union
Free School District (hereinafter “the District") for the period of July 1, 2024 through
June 30, 2025. The Board of Education (hereinafter "the Board”) has been advised
that it is legally permissible to contractually obligate itself to provide you with the
wages and benefits described herein. It is acknowledged by you and the Board that
the termination of your employment is subject solely to various provisions of the Civil
Service Laws of the State of New York and nothing herein contained shall be deemed
to have modified the foregoing in any respect. It is acknowledged by you and the
Board that this Agreement negates any prior Agreement between you and the
Board that relates to your terms and conditions of employment. Such Agreements
are null and void by the execution of this Agreement. Therefore, based upon the
mutual understandings between you and the Board, it is understood and agreed as
follows:

Scope of Employment

Effective July 1, 2024, you shall be employed as Athletic Trainer/Coordinator.
You certify and acknowledge that you are qualified to serve as an Athletic Trainer
pursuant to Section 135.4(c)(7)(i)(d)(1) of the Regulations of the Commissioner of
Education during the term of your employment. Additionally, you shall be employed
as the Athletic Coordinator under the supervision of the Athletic Director per the
ATC/Athletic Coordinator Role and Responsibilities job description.

Salary

Your regular base salary for the year July 1, 2024 through June 30, 2025 shall be
$70,562. The salary for the Athletic Coordinator role will be $15,000. Thus your total
salary will be $85,562.

Career Increment

In addition to the foregoing, you shall receive a career increment in the
amount of $1,300 upon completion of ten (10) years of service; an additional $1,450
after completion of fifteen (15) years of service; and an additional $1,600 after
completion of twenty (20) years of service in the District.

Benefits

In addition to the annual compensation specified above, you shall be entitled
to receive the following enumerated benefits:

1 Work Year

Your work year for the 2024-2025 school year shall be on an eleven (11) months
basis (effective July 1, 2024), except for the school holidays and recess periods
designated on the school calendar (other than days with athletic contests
scheduled), vacation days, and use of sick, personal, family illness days and
bereavement days as well as any days off due to school cancellation. You will work a
maximum of thirty-five (35) hours per week inclusive of one (1) hour per day for
meals. A schedule specifying hours and dates will be mutually agreed upon. The
trainer will be responsible for arriving at Irvington HS for 2:00 pm on school days and
will stay until the day's sporting activities are completed. This time will be adjusted
by you to also complete the Athletic Coordinator role. You will be required to work
weekend hours, as well as hours during school district break periods, contingent
upon the game and practice schedule as determined by the Athletic Director. Any
hours worked in excess of thirty-five (35) hours per week shall be paid at the hourly
contractual rate and must be pre-approved by the Athletic Director. In the event you
work less than thirty-five (35) hours for a particular week, said hour(s} may be applied
by the District in lieu of the payment as set forth in the preceding sentence.

2. Sick Leave

Effective July 1, 2024, you shall be entitled to twelve (12) days of sick for the
2024-2025 school year. Any unused sick leave days may be carried over and used
during any subsequent year of employment with no maximum accumulation.

Anon-cumulative reserve of thirty (30) working days will be available to you for
an extended illness. You may only utilize this reserve once in your lifetime. Extended
illness shall mean iliness or disability for thirty (30) or more continuous working days.
This reserve can only be accessed after utilization of all your personal accumulated
sick leave.

3. Personal and Other Leaves

Effective July 1, 2024, you shall be entitled to four (4) days for the purpose of
transacting or attending to personal business which cannot be attended to other
than on a school day during school hours. All personal business days shall require
reasonable notice and the reason for such absence. All unused personal days shall
be credited to sick time at the end of each fiscal year. In the third year of
employment and beyond, you shall be entitled to five (5) days for personal leave.

4. Family Illness - Death in the Family

Effective July 1, 2024, you shall be entitled to three (3) days of additional leave
with no deduction in pay for illness in the immediate family. You shall also be
entitled to three (3) days additional leave with no deduction in pay for death in the
immediate family. These days shall not be deducted from sick leave and you shall
not be entitled to accumulate any unused days under this section. The immediate
family is herein defined as father, mother, son, daughter or spouse. For death in the
family, the family is further expanded to include brother, sister, grandparents,
father-in-law, mother-in-law, brother-in-law and sister-in-law.

5. Vacation Leave

Effective July 1, 2024 you shall be entitled to five (5) days of vacation leave
annually. Said vacation leave must be utilized during the summer month of August.
If you are unable to utilize said vacation leave days in August due to your job
responsibilities, you may, upon the prior approval of your immediate supervisor, use
your vacation leave at other times during the school year. You shall be entitled to
carry over any unused vacation days with no maximum accumulation.

6. Health Insurance

Effective July 1, 2024, you shall have the opportunity to participate in the
health care plan offered by the Board of Education. Effective July 1, 2024, you shall be
required to contribute 7.5% of the applicable premium. Contributions to the cost of
premiums will be made through bi-weekly payroll deductions.

Should you receive health insurance from sources other than the District and
you opt out of the District's health insurance program for at least one year, you shall
receive a cash payment - payable in two equal installments - for the year you opt out
of the health insurance program based on the following:

Proof of 2 person or family coverage elsewhere: $6,487.38 annual payment
Proof of single coverage elsewhere: $3,892.44 annual payment

7. Retiree Health Insurance

You shall be entitled to health insurance benefits into retirement only after
completing twenty (20) years of consecutive service in the District immediately
preceding your retirement. Upon your resignation from the District for purposes of
retirement into the New York State Employees’ Retirement System, you shall be
permitted to continue coverage under the District's health insurance program for
you and your spouse, subject to the terms and conditions of the District's health care
plan in effect. The District shall pay the same contribution rate in effect at the time
of retirement for the health insurance premiums for such coverage into retirement.
You shall also have the option to cover any additional cost for coverage of
dependents that you wish to include subject to the terms of the District's health care
plan in effect. You will be solely responsible for any Medicare Part 8 required
payments.

Written Agreement

By signing this letter, you acknowledge that this Agreement has been arrived
at jointly between yourself and the Board and shall not be construed against any
party hereto as being the drafter hereof or causing the same to be drafted. By
signing this letter, you acknowledge that this Agreement shall continue in full force
and effect for the term of your service to the District unless otherwise terminated in
writing.

If any provision of this Letter Agreement is determined to be contrary to law, it
is understood and agreed that such provision shall be deemed deleted and the
balance of this Letter Agreement without such deleted provision, if otherwise lawful,
shalt remain in full force and effect. tf such deleted provision involves compensation
or money benefit, you and the Board agree to negotiate as part of this Agreement in
place of such deleted provision a substitute of comparable value thereto, and in the
event of an impasse exceeding forty-five (45) days, either party hereto may submit
the issue for final disposition, to arbitration by the American Arbitration Association
pursuant to its rules, which Association shall be empowered to make an award of
comparable value or compensation as reasonably implements the intent of the
parties under the deleted provision.
