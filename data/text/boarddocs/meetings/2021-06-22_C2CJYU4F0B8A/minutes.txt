7. Presentation/Discussion

Presentation: 7.1 Fund Balance Update and Discussion

Ms. Stein presented on the fund balance and reserves. The recommended unassigned fund balance is 4% for next year’s budget. We have four allowable sources of fund balance: surplus, borrowing, sale of building and unexpended capital projects. The unassigned 4% fund balance demonstrates fiscal health. A proper 4% fund balance plus reserves puts us in a strong financial position. Our audit statements resulted in a .158% IR, the lowest in long time. Our unassigned fund balance is $2,582,260. We have a total of $7 million in fund balances. In July we will know the final number. Based on these numbers it is recommended we add $150,000 to the capital reserve, add $500,000 to the workers comp reserve, and add $100,000 to the employee benefits liability reserve. Any additional surplus is recommended for the tax cert reserve. It is estimated $2.2 million go to reserves. In total this puts us at $1.7 million surplus revenue. The resolution for tonight is to fund the assigned category and agree to allocate the remaining surplus to different reserve categories.
