Yes: Michael Hanna, Maria Kashkin, Brian Friedman, David Graeber, Maura Gedid, Deborah Hargraves, Beth Propper

Michael Hanna let community members waiting outside the CPR that the public meeting was back in session and read the following statement:

"It has not been the practice of this Board, nor of other Boards of Education, to entertain public discussion of specific personnel matters. The reason for our inability to address this matter of concern is the Board’s important responsibility, imposed upon us by the Education Law of the State of New York, to protect the rights of students, staff, and faculty.

While the Board does not relish silence on this matter, to do otherwise would violate the law and would likely expose the District to liability."
