3. Administrative Report

Discussion: 3.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison introduces this as a special meeting. There has been tremendous progress made on the capital project over the summer. The sheet rock is laid, the bleachers are installed, the new bathrooms are installed, the roof is completed, the A/C work is underway, the flooring is coming for the new gym floor, and the sidewalks have been installed. The important topic on all of our minds are the COVID-19 protocols for the upcoming school year. Currently there has been no word from the State. We are awaiting guidance from the New York State Department of Health. We are starting to plan internally that we will open school with the same protocols as well closed school. There will be social distancing, a mask mandate and surveillance testing. We are awaiting further guidance.

4. Presentation/Discussion

Information: 4.1 Technology use in Irvington High School

Dr. Harrison introduced the presentation on "Instructional Technology Planning at Irvington High School: The Future of 1:1 Devices." Our technology objectives are linked to our broader district goals. As a result of the pandemic, all students and staff were given a district-purchased chromebook. We are now in the process of installing outdoor wifi on all of our campuses. During the pandemic, we introduced many technological advances in our classrooms. There were many advances we found beneficial that we will be keeping.

Dr. Duffy continued the presentation on technology. When we think about 1:1 teaching and learning, it has a lot of benefits for the students. Some of the tools that were most successfully used during remote learning were creative including graphic arts, movie creation, design and many other Web 2.0 tools. During COVID, the teachers were very engaged with Flipgrid, Padlet, Google Docs and Google Jamboard. This increased student participation. As educators, we need to identify the goals first and use the technology to meet those goals. We will continue using these tools as we move into the 2021-22 school year. Technology should not take the role of classroom teaching. We will continue to have ongoing professional learning. Discussion: 4.2 Capital Bond Project Update

Dr. Friedman reported that the capital project is moving on schedule in phase I. We expect, conservatively, $100,000 of the contingency budget to be returned after phase I. For phase II, adding the new estimates, we are now $516,000 over our total budget. This number contains many caveats, including potentially eliminating the Main Street school walkway project for $120,000. Eliminating the walkway project and adding in the contingency savings from phase I we are $296,000 over the entire budget. We are carrying phase II contingencies of $400,000 in the phase II budget. We are still awaiting incoming bids and will evaluate them based on the cost. If the bids come in too expensive, we will not move forward on these projects.
