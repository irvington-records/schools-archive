Our Foci, Our Progress…
2017-18 District Goals

  1.    Implement instructional practices that elevate student thinking and learning
  2.    Develop a balanced assessment system that measures students’ content knowledge, skill understanding and dispositional thinking
  3.    Design and introduce a new content management system that enhances communication between the school district and parents

2016-2017 Practices, Programs and Professional Development

It is through these three key entry points intended to promote learning and improve achievement that we plan for, address and assess the
implementation of goals. In any given year professional learning, practices and programs are designed as part of building level action plans. These plans
reflect specific steps that will be taken to accomplish the goal(s). In each of these three categories we plan for multi-year implementation. As a result,
we do not expect to make significant changes every year to all identified actions.

Theory of Action 2
If we value and foster the professional learning and growth of all members of our
school community, then we will build our collective capacity to support student
success.
Goal:
In order to build our collective capacity to support student success, the Irvington
School District will:
 ●   engage all members of our school community in the process of continuous improvement through the
     articulation of District goals
 ●   involve all members of the school community in learning and developing the knowledge and skills to
     support student success
 ●   provide educators with professional learning designed to support student growth
 ●   provide all staff with professional learning to develop the skills necessary to meet District needs.
