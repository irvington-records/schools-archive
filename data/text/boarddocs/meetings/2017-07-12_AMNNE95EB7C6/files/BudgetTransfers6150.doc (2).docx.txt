BUDGET TRANSFERS


The transfer of funds between and within functional unit appropriations of the General Fund is commonly required during the school year.  The Superintendent of Schools, in accordance with the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education, is authorized to make budget transfers between line item accounts, so long as the transfer for any one item does not exceed $10,000. All transfers in excess of $10,000 require prior Board of Education approval.  The Superintendent will report such budget transfers to the Board as an information item at its next meeting.

Internal transfers between the same object codes and/or building codes, whose detailed budgets were not part of the voter approved budget shall be approved by the Assistant Superintendent for Business or Superintendent’s designee and shall be exempt from reporting to the Board of Education.

Ref:	Education Law §1718
8 NYCRR §170.2(l)
