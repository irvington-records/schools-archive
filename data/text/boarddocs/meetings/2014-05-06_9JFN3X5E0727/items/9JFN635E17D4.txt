Subject: 2.1 Open Public Hearing - 2014-15 Budget
Type: Action
Attachments: May 6 Budget Presentation V6.pdf

Consistent with State Law, the Board of Education must conduct a budget hearing at least 7 days, but not more than 14 days, prior to the scheduled budget vote on May 20, 2014. The administration will provide a very brief overview of the financial information relating to the proposed 2014-15 budget. Members of the community will have an opportunity to comment and/or raise questions regarding the proposed budget.
