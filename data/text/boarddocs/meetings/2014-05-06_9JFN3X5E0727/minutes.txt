	2. Public Hearing - 2014-15 Budget

	Action: 2.1 Open Public Hearing - 2014-15 Budget

	Resolved, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District call to order the Public Hearing regarding the 2014-15 Budget.

	Motion by Phil Whitney, second by Maria Kashkin.

	Final Resolution: Motion Carries

	Yes: Phil Whitney, Robyn Kerner, Maria Kashkin, John Montgomery

	Dr. Harrison provided an overview of the budget process that has taken place over the last several months. He provided a recap of the 2014-15 proposed budget which the Board adopted at the April 24, 2014 Board Meeting. Dr. Harrison noted that the District has maintained one of the lowest budget increases over the past 5 years, with an average of 1.37%. However, significant reductions in programs as well as teaching and staff reductions were required to realize those low increases over the years. Dr. Harrison also noted that the proposed budget restores programs for students while balancing fiscal responsibility and educational quality. $576,631 in new budgetary considerations, including but not limited to restoration of some high school electives, new programs both at the high school and elementary levels, facilities renovations, and increases in professional development, is included in the $56,294,000 budget.

	The proposed budget calls for a tax rate increase is 2.91% and a budget to budget increase of 4.11%. The tax impact for the average assessed home would be $467.00

	There were no comments from the public.

	Action (Consent): 7.8 Establishment of Ali Nilsson Speechley Scholarship Award

	Resolution: WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District administers the Irvington Union Free School District; and WHEREAS, Ali Nilsson Speechley Scholarship Committee, hereinafter referred to as the “Donor”, wishes to honor the lifetime accomplishments and legacy of 1992 Irvington High School graduate Ali Speechley; and WHEREAS, the Donor desires to make a gift to the Board of Education in order to create a scholarship, to be known as the “Ali Nilsson Speechley Scholarship Fund”; and WHEREAS Education Law §§ 1709(12-a) and 2503(1) authorizes the Board of Education: [t]o take and hold in trust for the purpose of awarding scholarships in [the] schools any real estate transferred to it by gift, grant, bequest or devise, or any gift, legacy or annuity, of whatever kind, given or bequeathed to [the] board and apply the same, or the interest and proceeds thereof, according to the instructions of the donor or testator. NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District creates the Ali Nilsson Speechley Scholarship Fund as set forth below, based upon the understanding between the parties: 1. The Donor shall deliver to the Board, for deposit in the trust and agency account in the bank designated by the Board of Education the sum of five hundred dollars ($500.00) annually, which the Donor hereby gives to the Board forever and unconditionally, except as hereinafter set forth. 2. The scholarship shall be named the Ali Nilsson Speechley Scholarship Fund. 3. The scholarship shall be awarded annually in the amount of five hundred dollars ($500.00) to a deserving senior member of the Irvington High School Softball Team who will be attending college, has been an active participant on the Softball Team for more than one season, and who exemplifies the selfless and respected qualities always demonstrated by Ali Speechley. 4. The funds allocated to said scholarship shall come from such income or interest as is accumulated in a particular year plus such invasion of the principal as is necessary to issue said scholarship. 5. The details of the organization and administration of the Scholarship Fund will be left to the discretion of the Board of Education, while the annual recipient will be selected by the Irvington High School Scholarship Committee.

	Motion by Phil Whitney, second by Maria Kashkin.

	Final Resolution: Motion Carries

	Yes: Phil Whitney, Robyn Kerner, Maria Kashkin, John Montgomery
