History of Fiscal Conservatism
 Among the lowest average tax increases in
  Westchester County
 Average of a 1.37% budget increase over the past 5
  years

 Significant program and staff reductions since 2009
   19.6 teaching positions eliminated
   15.3 additional staff: administrators, clerical,
    custodial, maintenance, etc.
   18 high school electives
   Elementary and middle school programs




           2014-15 Proposed Budget Restores &
              Expands Student Experiences

2014-15 Budget is a Balanced Proposal
 Provides for additional growth in learning experiences

 Does not eliminate existing programs or personnel

 Invests in infrastructure & facilities

 Introduces enriching and supportive programs

 Expands technological opportunities

 Supports the growth & development of staff

 Restores electives at the High School

 Balances fiscal responsibility & educational quality
