	3. Presentations/Discussions

		Information:  3.1 2012-13 Budget Discussion: Superintendent's Office, District Clerk, Business Office, Debt Service, Insurance (Presentation)

		Mrs. Beverly Miller, Asst. Superintendent for Business, presented 1st draft of the proposed 2012-13 budget matters relating to the Superintendent's Office, the Business Office, the District Clerk's Office, Board of Education, debt service and insurance. The information Ms. Miller provided included both the current year budget, the estimated expenditures, the proposed 2012-13 budget and the +/- change. In addition, Ms. Miller also provided examples of what expenses are incurred under each budget code.

		Ms. Miller also provided the 2012-13 Schedule of Bond Payments, ERS Loan Payments and Lease/Purchase Agreements for computer technology. In addition, Ms. Miller reported that  expenses, such as Computer-Other expenses will be moved to other budget codes in order to maximize State Aid. (All budget lines/expenditures will be reviewed to ensure that the District is maximizing the State Aid revenue that the District is entitled to receive.

		In summary, the budgets presented tonight total $7,135,005, an increase of $221,365 over the 2012-13 budget.

		Following the presentation, Mrs. Robyne Camp, thanked Ms. Miller for the presentation, stating that it was easy to understand and comprehensive.

		Report:  6.3 Policy Committee Meeting 12-16-11

		Bob Grados reported that the Committee reviewed an update to the Conduct on School Property policy, a draft of the anti-bullying policy, and discussed the request to adopt a tree policy. It was the consensus of the Committee to not move forward with drafting such a policy as it is not the business of the school district, as well as it would be cost prohibitive to commit to annual inspections, etc. The district's trees will be taken care of on an as need basis. The Board welcomes involvement from community.

		Report:  6.4 Communications Committee Meeting 12-19-11

		Phil Whitney reported on meeting. Jesse Lubinsky will have Edline upgrade completed on January 3rd. Initially, the District page will be updated, with schools upgrading on a rolling basis.
