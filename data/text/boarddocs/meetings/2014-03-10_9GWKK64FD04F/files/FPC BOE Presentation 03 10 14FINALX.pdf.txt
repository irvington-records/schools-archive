                          Committee Charge


“The Field Project Committee has the charge of investigating
potential enhancements to the established baseline,
performing necessary research, and drafting and presenting a
report…”

To meet this charge, several questions were
formulated by district administration to
guide the process.


                                                               2

                             Questions/Conclusions


1. Restoring the playing surface at East Field to its historic
   grass playing surface was established as the baseline. Is
   one natural grass option preferred over another? Should an
   artificial turf be considered? Are there other
   considerations for East Field?

Committee Response: No (unanimous vote)

The committee concluded that having East Field natural grass
rather than artificial turf is in the best interests of the students,
the community, and the neighbors.

                                                                        3

                           Questions/Conclusions


2. Restoring Meszaros Field to its historic grass playing
   surface was also established as the baseline. Considering
   the documented history of maintenance and competition
   challenges, does artificial or natural turf better meet the
   needs of school programs and the community?

Committee Response: (7-3 vote for artificial turf)

The committee believes artificial turf better meets the needs
and specifically, the Committee prefers organic infill or a
hybrid product.

                                                                 4

                        Questions/Conclusions


3. Should the addition of lights be considered for
   any of our fields?

 Committee response: NO. (9-1 vote against lights at this
time but recommends the installation of conduit for
possible future use.)




                                                            5

                      Questions/Conclusions


4. Should replacement of Oley Track mirror the
   existing or should we consider alterations?

Committee response: Unanimous vote to replace as-is.

The Committee concluded that replacement should be done
within the footprint of the existing track.




                                                          6

                      Fields Project Committee Report


5. Are there other athletic facility improvements
   that should be considered?

Committee Response: Yes. Highlights are below:

• Widen the delineated playing field striping of Meszaros field to
  accommodate men’s lacrosse and soccer.
• Consider the addition of more bleachers on the home side of the field.
• Other minor considerations (athletic equipment, outsourcing
  maintenance) are detailed in a later slide and in the report.



                                                                           7

                                Conclusion


The committee has:

• investigated the potential enhancements to the
  established baseline
• performed necessary research
• prepared this report of its findings and recommendations

We respectfully ask the BOE to consider this report when
making a determination of the final scope of the athletic
facilities for inclusion in a possible bond referendum.

                        Further Response



An Analysis of Our Fields

In order to understand what is best for our schools
and community, the Committee examined the
natural grass fields that our school district has
available to its schools.




                                                      9

                           Analysis of Fields


Basic questions when examining the fields included:
• What type of sports are played by our students and
  community members?
• How many athletes play each sport?
• Which fields can support those sports?
• How often are the fields used?
• Do community members use our fields outside of the
   school year?
• What seasons are the sports played?
• When do the fields “rest”?
• What conditions are the fields in and how can they be
  maintained for optimal use?
• Does local weather affect playing conditions?
                                                          10

                             Analysis of Fields


Four Key Factors
1. The inventory of natural grass fields – both District and
   Village – available to our student athletes and
   community members, and their location

2. The size, type and condition of the fields

3. The use (and intensity of use) of the natural grass fields
   by our students and community members

4. Field maintenance

                                                                11

                       Analysis of Fields


The Committee concluded – and emphasizes - that
the District fields are interrelated and cannot be
individually reviewed or analyzed without looking
at them together as a system.

                                     Frequency of Use

Meszaros and the Dows Lane fields are programmed during the school day for
physical education classes.

• On average, from 8:30-3:00 p.m., our students use those fields 26 weeks/year
  for gym class.

• From 3:30-6:00 p.m. for 26 weeks/year (on average), Meszaros and the Dows
  Lane fields are scheduled for school sports, both as a practice field and a
  game field.

Student athletes begin using Meszaros and East fields 2 weeks before the start
of the school year, for approximately 4 hours/day, for pre-season training.

On weekends, both Saturday and Sunday, Meszaros is used primarily as a game
field for our student athletes and the community. From 8:30 a.m. – 6 p.m., on
average Meszaros and the Dows Lane fields are scheduled for use.

                    East Field: Natural Grass Factors


• Location and size of the field – out of sight, limits on use
  due to size and condition
• Primarily a practice field– not a game field
• Field not used for P.E. or recess
• Preserve natural green space
• Protect natural setting and surroundings
• Proximity to Field Point residences – believe residents
  will prefer natural grass
• Youth sports can still be played and fields lined
• Stormwater drainage will be enhanced with renovations
• Plan does not require cutting down trees


                                                                 18

                      East Field: Conclusion


East Field should remain a natural grass field. It is
in the best interests of the students, the
community, and the neighbors based on its historic
use, location, proximity to neighbors and other
factors previously discussed.




                                                        19

                 Meszaros Field: Current Factors

• Used for school and community events
• Used four seasons, year round, weather permitting
• Limited resting – November-February, July 1-August 15
• Previous studies by LAN of surface and drainage




                                                          20

                         Meszaros Field: Considerations

• Vendor meeting to review synthetic products

    • Artificial turf with crumb rubber infill
    • Artificial turf with a combination of crumb rubber and organic infill
    • Artificial turf with organic infill

The Committee compared artificial turf and natural grass and examined:
• environmental and safety issues
• cost analysis of various sports fields
• wear, durability and maintenance of field surfaces

Certain assumptions were made based on documents, consultants, and
discussions.



                                                                              21

                Meszaros Field: Conclusion



In sum, natural grass surrounded by trees is
nature’s best setting for our children and our
environment; however, when usage and
limitations of our fields are considered, a
natural grass field for Meszaros is not viewed
by a majority of the Committee as feasible.



                                                 22

                                      Oley Track


The Committee was informed that Oley track has reached
the end of its useful life and needs replacement.

• entire track needs to be resurfaced
    • includes the asphalt surface and rubberized running surface
• asphalt base is to remain

The rubberized track surface recommended for high school
level training and competition should be of the mid-grade
IAAF certified synthetic all-weather impermeable
system. The committee reviewed the samples provided by
two vendors, and agreed that it is the proper product to
recommend to the BOE for replacement of Oley Track.
                                                                    23

                               Other Improvements

1.   Widen the delineated playing field striping on Meszaros field to
     accommodate a regulation size men’s lacrosse/soccer field. This will
     require removing the 206-foot runway in the D-zone.
2.   Purchase and add lacrosse nets in the end zones of Meszaros so
     sports practice can occur while the track team is using the field.
3.   If Meszaros becomes an artificial turf field, the investment must be
     protected. Appropriate fencing, security cameras, proper signage
     posted with rules and regulations should be considered.
4.   Consider purchasing additional bleachers on the home side of
     Meszaros.
5.   Proper use of the tower should be explored, including using it to
     store maintenance equipment. (If the tower cannot store
     maintenance equipment, consideration should be given to
     purchasing a shed for such purposes.)


                                                                            24

                             Other Improvements

6. Chemical cleaners should not be used on any type of
   artificial turf field, if installed.
7. To properly maintain East as a natural grass field,
   outsourcing maintenance should be considered and a
   thorough plan and schedule be developed with professionals.
8. The deteriorating condition of Upper and Lower Dows must
be
   reviewed. Because our fields are interrelated and function as
   a system, it is critical that the Dows Lane fields are reviewed.
   BOE may want to consider forming a committee to review
   problems and recommend corrective action.
9. Examine the two other fields – Lower Lower Dows and Main
   Street School. Consideration should be given to improving
and
   remedying, if necessary, in the future.                            25
