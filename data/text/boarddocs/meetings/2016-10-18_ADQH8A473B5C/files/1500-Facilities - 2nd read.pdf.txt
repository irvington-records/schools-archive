                          PUBLIC USE OF SCHOOL FACILITIES

         While the district’s school buildings and grounds are maintained primarily for the
purpose of educating students within the district, the Board of Education recognizes that the
buildings and grounds are a valuable community resource and believes that this resource should
be available to the community for specific uses that will not interfere with educational
activities. This policy is intended to identify the uses that community groups may make of those
facilities, the conditions for such use and the application procedure for use of school facilities.

Permitted Uses

         District facilities may be used for the purposes listed below, subject to the conditions and
restrictions set forth in this policy.

A.     Instruction in any branch of education, learning or the arts.
B.     Public library purposes, subject to provisions of the Education Law.
C.     Social, civic and recreational meetings and entertainment, or other uses pertaining to the
       welfare of the community so long as such uses are non-exclusive and open to the general
       public.
D.     Meetings, entertainment and occasions where admission fees are charged, when the
       proceeds are to be spent for an educational or charitable purpose.
E.     Polling places for holding primaries and elections, for the registration of voters.
F.     Civic forums and community centers.
G.     Recreation, physical training and athletics, including competitive athletic contests of
       children attending a private, nonprofit school.
H.     Child-care programs when school is not in session, or when school is in session for the
       children of students attending schools of the district and, if there is additional space
       available, for children of employees of the district.
I.     Graduation exercises held by not-for-profit elementary and secondary schools, provided
       that no religious service is performed.

Prohibited Uses

        Any use not permitted by this policy is prohibited. In addition, the following uses are
specifically prohibited:

A.     Meetings, entertainments and occasions that are under the exclusive control of and the
       proceeds are to be applied for the benefit of a society, association or organization of a
       religious sect or denomination or of a fraternal, secret or exclusive society or
       organization, other than veterans’ organizations or volunteer fire fighters or volunteer
       ambulance workers.

Conditions of Use for District Facilities

A.     Use of district facilities may be permitted unless such facilities are in use for school
       purposes, or during educational programs. The district reserves exclusive and non-
       reviewable judgment to determine if a requested use would interfere with or disturb the
       district’s educational programs or subject the district to liability for inappropriate or
       inadequate supervision.
B.     To ensure that district facilities are preserved for the benefit of the greater district
       community, consideration will be given to community based groups. If there is a conflict
       in request for use of facilities, the following order will govern:
       1.       Irvington Union Free School District events and activities
       2.       Irvington Village and Recreation Department Programs
       3.       School District Resident Community Use
       4.       Outside Groups
C.     Applicants cannot transfer, or assign any approved use to another individual or group, or
       their own privileges will be deemed revoked.
D.     Where, in the judgment of the district, the requested use of district facilities requires
       special equipment or supervision, the district reserves the right to deny such use, or in the
       alternative, to condition such use upon the applicant’s payment of additional fees. Only
       authorized personnel shall operate district equipment.
E.     Use of district facilities will only be permitted where the organization provides the
       district timely evidence of adequate insurance coverage ($1,000,000 minimum) which
       shall name the district as “additional insured” to hold the district harmless from all
       liability, property damage, personal injuries and/or medical expenses. The district will
       exercise complete and unreviewable discretion regarding what constitutes adequate
       insurance coverage for each proposed use.
F.     Use of district facilities will only be permitted where the organization provides the
       district evidence of adequate adult supervision to ensure respectful use of the facilities.
       Users are required to provide their own security and familiarize themselves with exits for
       emergency situations.
G.     The Board reserves the discretion to deny use of district facilities described above, or to
       terminate use of district facilities:

               1.      By an applicant who has previously misused or abused district facilities or
                       property or who has violated this policy;

                 2.   For any use which could have the effect of violating the Establishment
                      Clause of the United States Constitution or other provisions of the United
                      States or New York State Constitutions;
                 3.   By any applicant where policies or practices do not conform to federal,
                      state and local anti-discrimination laws (however, the district must provide
                      equal access to the Boy Scouts of America and other designated patriotic
                      youth groups);
                 4.   For any use which, in the estimation of the Board, could reasonably be
                      expected to or actually does give rise to a public disturbance;
                 5.   For any use which the Board deems inconsistent with this policy;
                 6.   For any use by a private for-profit entity that has the direct or indirect
                      effect of promoting the products or services of such entity;
                 7.   In any instance where alcoholic beverages or unlawful drugs are sold,
                      distributed, consumed, promoted or possessed;
                 8.   By an applicant, including any individual associated with the
                      applicant, who has mistreated or been abusive, verbally or otherwise, to
                      district personnel; and
                 9.   For any use prohibited by law.

H.     Use of district facilities will be permitted only where the applicant agrees to pay the
       district a user fee according to Regulation 1500-R and the schedule adopted by the
       district to cover the costs and expenses associated with the requested use (heat,
       electricity, maintenance, custodial services, facility wear and tear, supervision,
       etc.). Additional fees may be associated with the use of any additional services or
       equipment. The district retains the further right to waive user fees for groups that are
       associated with or sponsored by the district.

I.     The Superintendent (or designee) is authorized to alter or cancel any permit if it becomes
       necessary to use the facility for school purposes or for other justifiable reasons and
       extenuating circumstances. Whenever possible, adequate and timely notification will be
       given.

J.     Facility use is subject to additional rules and conditions described in regulation 1500-R.
       Users must acknowledge that they have read and will adhere to this policy and the
       regulations as part of the application process.
