Background

●   Recent changes in legislation for teacher evaluation required all school districts to revise their
    APPR plans to be in compliance with the new 3012-d regulations. As in the past, our APPR
    Plan was developed by a committee of teachers and administrators according to the
    guidelines provided by the State Education Department (details of this guidance can be found
    on the Engageny.org web site)
●   During the 2015-2016 school year the APPR Committee partnered to develop a plan to meet
    the expectations of the guidelines, as well as the needs of the Irvington School District. The
    final and complete document includes extensive detail for the two evaluation categories
    upon which teachers and administrators will receive their end of year evaluation scores.

 ● Domain 1 - Planning and Preparation - 6 Components

Changes in What is Measured

State Approved Rubric: Danielson’s Framework for Teaching (2011 Revised Edition)
It is important to note that the domains included in the observation process have changed
significantly. The following bullets outline the key changes:
 ✓ All of Domain 1 will be scored as part of the observation process
 ✓ Domain/Component 4a will be scored as part of the observation process
 ✓ All remaining components of Domain 4 will not be scored anymore
 ✓ Domains 2 & 3 will continued to be scored as part of the observation process
 ✓ Domain 1 will be scored through the pre-observation planning form
 ✓ Domain/component 4a will be scored through the post-observation reflection form

Individual SLO’s vs. School-wide SLO’s
●   Individual SLO’s-
     ○ HS/MS - teachers of courses that end in a Regents exam:
           ■ If 50% or more of the teacher’s caseload is comprised of students taking the
               Regents, an individual SLO is required based on the Regents exam results
●   School-wide SLO - Based on the concept that all teachers contribute to the learning and
    performance of students, school-wide SLO’s reflect the collective efforts of our schools.
     ○ Dows Lane - District-wide SLO - 4th Grade Science Assessment
     ○ Main Street School - School-wide SLO - 4th Grade Science Assessment
     ○ Middle School - School-wide SLO - 8th Grade Science Assessment
     ○ HS - School-wide SLO - ELA Regents
