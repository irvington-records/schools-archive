Administrative Update:

Dr. Harrison gave an update on Homecoming. The annual parade and football game had to be canceled because the opponent team had a COVID-19 outbreak. He also gave an update on the required surveillance testing that will start soon in the District. 20% of the total District population will need to be tested in a quick and easy  self-administered saliva test. More information will follow from Dr. Harrison.

Dr. Duffy spoke about the plans for the upcoming Professional Learning Early Release Day (PLRD). Teachers will engage in a personal reflection activity regarding their own personal identities and the impact of their identities on relationships with their students.

Ms. Stein gave an update on Transportation and the fixes that are being implemented to start tomorrow morning.

5. Presentation/Discussion

Discussion: 5.1 Presentation-Creating Welcoming Schools Through SEL and Diversity, Equity and Inclusion

Board members asked several questions and Dr. Duffy and Dr. Harrison responded to the questions. Board members thanked Dr. Duffy for her presentation.
