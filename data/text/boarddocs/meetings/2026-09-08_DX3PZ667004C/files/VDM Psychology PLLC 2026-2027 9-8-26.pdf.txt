THIS AGREEMENT made as of the 11% day of August, 2026 by and between IRVINGTON

party of the first part, having its principal place of business located at 40 North Broadway,

10528.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, SCHOOL DISTRICT is authorized by law to contract with independent
contractors for providing related services; and

WHEREAS, SERVICE PROVIDER is in the business of providing neuropsychological
evaluation services to school districts; and

WHEREAS, SCHOOL DISTRICT desires that SERVICE PROVIDER provide such
neuropsychological evaluation services as set forth in this Agreement; and
