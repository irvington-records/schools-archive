Audit Committee of the Irvington Union Free School District (Tuesday, January 28, 2014)

		Present: Robert Grados, Robyn Kerner, Phil Whitney, Maria Kashkin, John Montgomery

		Also present:    Kristopher Harrison, Supt. Of Schools, Beverly Miller, Asst. Supt. For Business, Tanya Cronin and Donna Crowley, of Vanacore, DeBenedictus, DiGovanni and Weddell, the District's External Auditors.

		Procedural: 0.01 Call to Order

		Robert Grados called the meeting to order at 6:32 pm.

		Discussion: 0.02 Discussion of the 2013-14 Risk Assessment Audit Report

		Ms. Cronin and Ms. Crowley, representing the District’s External Auditors, Vanacore, DeBenedictus, DiGovanni and Weddell, presented the District’s 2013-14 Risk Assessment Audit Report dated 11/7/13 and addressed questions from members of the Board.

		Action: 0.03 Adjourn Audit Committee

		Motion to adjourn the Audit Committee by Phil Whitney, second Maria Kashkin.

		Final Resolution: Motion Carries

		Yes: Robert Grados, Phil Whitney, Robyn Kerner, Maria Kashkin, John Montgomery
