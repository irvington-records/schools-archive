2. Declaration of October 7, 2014 Bond Referendum Results

Action: 2.1 Declaration of October 7, 2014 Bond Referendum Results

Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education accept the results of the October 7, 2014 Bond Referendum as follows:

Yes: 1,095

No: 945

The ballot presented to voters read as follows:

That the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, in the County of Westchester, New York, (the “District”), is hereby authorized to construct improvements to all District school buildings and/or sites, and to expend $4,602,656 therefor;  (b) that a tax is hereby voted in an aggregate amount of not to exceed $4,602,656 to finance such cost, such tax to be levied and collected in installments in such years and in such amounts as shall be determined by said Board of Education; and  (c) that in anticipation of said tax, bonds of the District are hereby authorized to be issued in the aggregate principal amount of not to exceed $4,602,656 and a tax is hereby voted to pay the interest on said bonds as the same shall become due and payable.

Motion by Robert Grados, second by John Montgomery.

Final Resolution: Motion Carries

Yes: Robert Grados, Phil Whitney, Robyn Kerner, Maria Kashkin, John Montgomery
