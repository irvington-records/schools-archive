                    CONTRACT FOR EDUCATIONAL SERVICES

AGREEMENT made by and between the Board of Education of the Edgemont Union Free
School District, having its principal office at 300 White Oak Lane, Scarsdale, NY 10583
(hereinafter called the “Sending School District”) and the Irvington U.F.S.D., having its
principal office at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, New York 10533 (hereinafter called the
“Receiving School District”).

WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, the Sending School District has need of an appropriate placement for,
   Student #84034, (hereafter referred to as “the student”, and

WHEREAS, the Sending School District has determined that the Receiving School
District’s High School Special Class is appropriate for the student; and

WHEREAS, the Receiving School District is willing to enroll the student in its Special
Class, subject to the provisions of this Agreement,

IT IS AGREED as follows:

    1.      The Receiving School District herby agrees to offer the student a special
            education placement, including instruction and related services provided in
            accordance with the student’s IEP in a Special Class of the Receiving School
            District for the school year 2023-24.

    2.      The Sending School District agrees to pay Tuition in the amount of Ninety-
            nine thousand, three hundred ninety-three dollars and sixty-eight cents
            ($99,393.68) to the Receiving School District on behalf of the student. Tuition
            shall be computed as follows: $93,018.00 basic instruction in a special
            education classroom, $6,375.68 for the actual costs of speech and language
            therapy.

    3.      The Sending School District agrees to maintain responsibility for all necessary
            evaluations and reevaluations, unless contractual arrangements are made
            otherwise at an additional cost to the Sending District.

    4.      The Receiving School District agrees to provide the services as set forth in the
            child’s IEP.


    5.      The Receiving School District agrees to report to the Sending School District
            on the progress of the child as requested.

6.    The Receiving School District agrees to permit a representative or
      representatives of the Committee on Special Education of the Sending School
      District to visit the program in which the child is enrolled on reasonable notice.

7.    The Receiving School District agrees to comply with all federal and state
      statutes and regulations regarding the provision of educational services to
      students with disabilities. In addition, the Receiving School District warrants
      and agrees that members of its staff who have contact with the student shall
      have the appropriate fingerprinting and criminal history record checks in
      accordance with Part 87 of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education
      and shall provide the Sending School District with proof of same upon request.

8.    Nothing contained herein shall serve to transfer responsibilities for the child
      from the Sending School District to the Receiving School District except for
      the provision of the particular special education service contracted herein and
      any related ancillary duties such as report cards, progress reports, parent-
      teacher conferences, etc.

9.    Transportation to and from school is the Sending School District’s
      responsibility.

10.   It is understood and agreed that this Agreement is for the 2023-24 school year
      only, subject to the stay put provisions of the Individuals with Disabilities
      Education Act. Tuition and related services costs will adjust from one year to
      the next and details of such cost will be present in the form presented in
      Paragraphs 2 and 3 of this Agreement.

11.   Nothing herein shall be construed from preventing either party from taking
      such action, consistent with State and Federal law, as it may deem necessary,
      reasonable or appropriate in the case of a student whose continued presence in
      the educational program would constitute a danger to others, to himself, or to
      property, sufficiently serious to warrant immediate protective action.

12.   The Receiving School District, at its sole expense, shall procure and maintain
      polices of commercial general liability (CGL) and malpractice insurance to
      insure the Sending School District, its Board of Education, employees and
      volunteers against any claim for liability, personal injury, or death caused
      directly or indirectly by the Receiving School District in connection with the
      performance if its responsibilities under this Agreement; each such policy shall
      provide a minimum coverage of One Million Dollars ($1,000,000.00) per
      occurrence subject to an annual aggregate of Three Million Dollars
      ($2,000,000.00). The CGL policy shall include coverage for the negligent acts
      of employed health care and mental health professionals.

    13.    This Agreement may be terminated by the Sending School District on 15 days
           written notice should its Committee on Special Education determine that the
           Irvington High School Special Class is no longer appropriate for the child. In
           the event that enrollment is discontinued prior to the end of the school year,
           tuition and related services costs shall be computed in accordance with 8
           NYCRR 175.6 (b) and (c).

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Board of Education of the Sending School District and the
Board of Education of the Receiving School District have authorized this Agreement to be
signed by each of its respective officials.
