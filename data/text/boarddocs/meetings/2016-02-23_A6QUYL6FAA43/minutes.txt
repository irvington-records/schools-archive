	5. Presentations/Discussions

	Discussion: 5.1 Budget Presentation: Business, Debt Service, Buildings & Grounds, Transportation (Attachment)

	Dr. Harrison reviewed the objectives in developing the budget and again reiterated that the tax cap levy is only.12% over last year's budget, resulting in the push ahead budget being over $1 million dollars over the tax cap.

	Highlights of the following budget areas included:

-
		Board of Education: 4.40% budget variance ($3,048)

-
		Chief School Administrator: .52% budget variance ($1,871)

-
		Finance Budget: 1.62% budget variance ($9,681)

-
		Staff Budget Points: 8.99% budget variance ($33,054) increase includes addition of webmaster services and new website launch fees.

	With respect to the operations and maintenance budget points, there is a 5.66% increase ($217,097) but Dr. Harrison noted that maintenance and repairs are not "push ahead" in nature and includes needs that have been identified, such as request to purchase a new truck ($44,000) replacement. An increase of $37,276 in the custodial budget includes the request for the purchase tools and building repairs for all district facilities total $341,505. Capital building repairs total $208,350 that includes electrical upgrades, Maher Gym floor maintenance, irrigation system to lower Dows Field, etc. Other budget points include messenger and mailing services, which has decreased approximately $4,000, cyber-insurance policy as well as BOCES administrative costs have increased by $16,210.

	A budget decline in the transportation budget of $69,967 (-3.07%) is expected due to further efficiencies realized by the transportation coordinator by merging routes, etc. Debt service is also expected to be reduced by $117,981 due to bond refinancing and lower than projected expenses associated with the 2014 bond as well as transferring technology expenses to the equipment line. Concluding the presentation was a budget summary showing the push ahead budget to budget as well as the added expenses.

	Following the presentation, the Board asked clarifying questions as to what else might be needed in the area of maintenance, specifically with respect to the new fields that will be built, such as adding security cameras. Mr. Knowles noted that since there is no lighting on the field, cameras will not be ineffective, but motion detectors have been discussed with no conclusion at this time. Mr. Montgomery asked, off topic, what, if anything,  has been discussed regarding the use of the turf field by community members/teams. Mr. Knowles would like to have discussions on monitoring field use. Mr. Whitney suggested that sod might be budgeted for Dows Field in order to gain greater usage. The Buildings & Grounds Committee will discuss this area further and bring any additional budgetary considerations to the Board.

	To help better understand the budget lines for staff in each area, Mr. Hanna asked for the numbers of staff members are included in the various budget lines. The FTE will be added. Mr. Montgomery commented that he will need to view the entire budget before making any comments on specific budget items.

	Mr. Whitney noted that the budget for special education has increased from 2014-15 budget of $58,000 to proposed budget of $72,000 and asked what the impact would be if reduced back to $58,000. It was noted that some of increase is due to the mandate to use the electronic voting machines, as well as the policy review expense. Mr. Whitney would like to look further to perhaps realize some savings in that budget line. He would also like to look at the operations/maintenance budget which calls for a $200,000 increase and would like to be sure that all expenses are required for this year. He is also curious as to the shifts in the expenses between the private school transportation and special education transportation.

	Information: 5.2 Overview of Fund Balance Detail

	Fund Balance- Actual vs budget (attachment)

	Fund Balance - Historical Overview (attachment)

	Analysis of Tax Certioraries (attachment)

	The Board reviewed an analysis of the fund balance from the years 2011 - 2015 provided by Beverly Miller. She provided a brief explanation of how the amount placed in the fund balance is arrived at each year, which by law, maximum of 4% of budget can be placed in a fund balance per year. She noted that the District also has a tax cert reserve, liability reserves for employee benefits and workers compensation where monies from the fund balance have been transferred into as well. Questions relating to payment of tax certioraries from the tax cert reserve were also addressed as well as an analysis of the tax certioraries payments made and  the amount transferred into the reserve from the fund balance. She also noted that at this time, $411,083 will be needed to pay current settlements in the 2016-17 school year.

	Also reviewed was a budget vs actual from 2011 - 2015, highlighting revenues vs appropriations and encumbrances with the net revenue and expense and the increase or decrease to the fund balance for each year. There was discussion as to what would be the appropriate amount to place in fund balance, with Ms. Miller suggesting an appropriation of $500,000, noting that previously the amount has been $422,000 has been placed in fund balance over the last four years. Should the board decide to adopt a budget that is more in line with what is actually spent, it would decrease the amount at the end of the following year which will result in a reduction to the amount available to appropriate to the fund balance. Mr. Montgomery wonders what the right amount is for the fund balance, 4% or is it less. Ms. Miller cautioned the Board that when looking at budget lines, it is not advisable to budget closer to actual expenses and appropriate large amount from the fund balance in a particular year which might force the District to borrow funds to meet expenses in a particular year.
