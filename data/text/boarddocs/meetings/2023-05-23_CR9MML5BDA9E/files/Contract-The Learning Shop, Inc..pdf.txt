Homebound Instruction Contract between The Learning Shop, Inc. and

1. Basic Services: The Learning Shop will provide homebound instruction for children
who are unable to attend school.
© Learning Shop teachers will contact, and maintain continued communication
with the teachers of homebound students in order to facilitate desired
instruction. For long term absences, if the school desires, The Leaming Shop is
fully capable of instructing as per the New York State curriculum, on our own.
Grades will be sent monthly for your consideration.
The Learning Shop can also administer all exams as needed:
o Midterms, Finals, Regents, RCTs, etc.

2. The Learning Shop wil! provide homebound instruction for the district as follows:
© Not more than 5 hours per week for grades K-5, unless authorized by school.
© Not more than 10 hours per week for grades 6-12, unless authorized by school.
e For grades 6-12, a minimum of 2 hours per session, unless not
medically/academically sound or unless requested by school.

3. The tutoring rate is $67.50 per hour, for both remote and in person.

© The Learning Shop will send paperwork (attendance reports and lesson plans)
once a month along with an invoice.

e For a “no show”, The Learning Shop will charge the school for 1 hour,
The Learning Shop will make every attempt to make up missed hours.
Requested services outside the allotted teaching time, such as writing a report
for a CSE or attending a school meeting, is billable at $67.50/hr.

* Testing that requires same-day pickup and drop-off (Regents, RCTs, etc.) will
be billed one additional hour beyond the seat-time required. For a “no-show”
on a testing day the full time will be charged.

services to school districts; and

any tax obligation of SERVICE PROVIDER arising from the payments made under this Agreement
will be SERVICE PROVIDER’s sole responsibility. SERVICE PROVIDER will indemnify SCHOOL
DISTRICT for any tax liability, interest, and/or penalties imposed upon SCHOOL DISTRICT by any
