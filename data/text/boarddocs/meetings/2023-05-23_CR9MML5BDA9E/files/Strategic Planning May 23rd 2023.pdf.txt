Board of Education & Administrator Interviews

     The Strategic Planning process began with in-depth interviews with
     the Board Trustees and the Administrative Team to gauge their

Focus Groups




               3

Focus Groups - Overview
     With the assistance of Hazard, Young, Attea, & Associates (HYA), their
     partners in this pivotal work, the District facilitated 23 focus group
     over 6 days, comprised of over 180 students, parents, community
     members, Village leaders, staff, faculty, administration, and trustees.

     The results of these focus groups offered a snapshot of the District of
     today and the expectations of the District of tomorrow.

Strategic Planning Survey
     The purpose of the survey was to gain additional input from a broad range of
     stakeholders including alumni, administrators, community members, faculty and staff,
     parents/guardians, and students.

     The survey opened on February 3rd, 2023 and closed on February 19th, 2023. There
     were 1354, entirely or partially, completed entries received and the data was then
     analyzed by the College of Education Division of the University of West Georgia in
     conjunction with HYA. The findings were presented to the administration and Board of
     Education on March 7th, 2023.

     The overall response indicated that the District’s quality and effectiveness was above
     average in almost every area of the survey. And, while areas of growth and
     improvement were cited, complementary and positive responses were included. The
     statistical differences between strengths and needs was very small, which is another
     indication of overall satisfaction. Additionally, many areas identified for improvement
     are standing District initiatives.

Portrait of a Graduate

Portrait of a Graduate

   Courageous Learner - I eagerly pursue knowledge
     I have the skills and curiosity to continue to build upon my knowledge for a successful
     life along my chosen path. I think critically by evaluating and synthesizing information to
     draw meaningful conclusions. I know mistakes help me grow and am willing to take
     risks in my learning. I am a flexible and creative problem solver.

   Engaged Citizen - I actively and productively contribute to a larger society
     I examine issues from multiple perspectives and am aware of how my actions, thoughts
     and words impact my local, national and global communities. I recognize the diverse
     society in which we live and respect and appreciate the cultures, backgrounds and
     identities of my peers and their families. I am digitally responsible. I embrace others and
     actively seek to build community.




                                                                                                   13

Portrait of a Graduate
   Effective Communicator - I productively exchange ideas and collaborate
   with others
     I express myself clearly in my speaking and writing across a variety of mediums. I
     promote open and honest communication by being an active and respectful participant.
     I understand how non-verbal and visual communication can convey my thoughts and
     emotions.

   Self-Aware Individual - I possess the skills and tools to achieve personal
   happiness
     I understand myself and am able to foster my own physical, social and emotional
     well-being. I am reflective and have the confidence and resilience to overcome
     challenges. I use sound judgment and act with honesty, compassion, empathy
     and integrity. I strive to achieve my fullest potential and seek to integrate my
     passions into my studies, activities and other endeavors.

Draft Objectives & Mission




                             16

Draft of Strategic Objectives
     Student Success
      1.   Maximize the academic growth of each student to ensure attainment of
           essential skills and content.
      2.   Provide all students with a variety of pathways to identify and explore their
           passion and purpose.
      3.   Ensure a consistent and balanced assessment system that provides clear,
           timely, and meaningful feedback on student progress.
     4.    Provide relevant professional learning, which
           targets the needs of every staff member.
      5.   Engage in a cycle of program evaluation to
           ensure that the curricula is grounded in
           research, evidence, and data.




                                                                                           17

Draft of Strategic Objectives
     Learning Environment
      1.   Foster an affirming, welcoming and inclusive culture for students and staff.

      2.   Attract, continuously develop, and retain high quality and diverse educators and
           staff to serve the needs of all students.

      3.   Create an environment of support for educators to empower and inspire
           student engagement.

     4.    Develop social and emotional learning and skills to encourage risk taking,
           resilience, and adaptability for students and staff.

      5.   Identify school day schedules that optimize student experience and
           achievement.




                                                                                              18

Draft of Strategic Objectives
     Stewardship of Resources
      1.   Maintain sufficient fund balance to preserve and enhance equitable academic
           programming.

      2.   Ensure modern and well-maintained buildings and grounds that are utilized
           effectively to provide a safe, secure, and appropriate learning environment.

      3.   Improve and sustain technology infrastructure, furniture, and other supplies to
           align with instructional programs.

     4.    Uphold and promote sustainable practices.




                                                                                             19

Draft of Strategic Objectives
     Community Engagement
      1.   Improve protocols for two way communication with all stakeholders
           that are clear, consistent, and accessible.

     2.    Partner with families on their children’s progress and understanding of the
           scope and sequence of grade-level curriculum across all grades.

     3.    Create an inclusive and affirming environment in which stakeholders are heard
           and engaged in the continuous improvement process.

     4.    Develop strategic partnerships to enhance student engagement and learning.
