AGREEMENT

EDUCATIONAL AND RESIDENTIAL SERVICES

This agreement (“Agreement”) for educational services (“Special Education Program”) and
maintenance and residential services (“Residential Program”) for the 2023-2024 school year is
made this day of , 2023 (“Effective Date”) by and Irvington
Union Free School District (hereinafter referred to as the "SCHOOL
DISTRICT") having its principal place of business located at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, NY
10533 and the Board of Education of the Julia Dyckman Andrus Memorial, Inc. doing business as
Andrus Children Center (hereinafter referred to as the "SERVICE PROVIDER") having its
principal place of business located at 1156 North Broadway, Yonkers, New York 10701. The
SCHOOL DISTRICT and the SERVICE PROVIDER are each a “Party” and together, the
“Parties”.

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, pursuant to the New York State Education Law, the SCHOOL
DISTRICT is authorized to contract with institutions within the State of New York for
the instruction of students for whom the SCHOOL DISTRICT is unable to provide for
their education in the public schools; and

WHEREAS, the SCHOOL DISTRICT is in need of an appropriate
placement for certain students as specifically identified on Schedule A of this
Agreement (“Participating Students”), which is attached hereto and incorporated in this
Agreement by reference; and

WHEREAS, the SCHOOL DISTRICT has determined that Andrus’s Special
Education Program and Residential Program are appropriate for the Participating Students; and

WHEREAS, SERVICE PROVIDER is a registered non-public school chartered
by the Board of Regents of the University of the State of New York as a non-profit
education corporation authorized to establish, conduct, operate and maintain an educational

program; and

WHEREAS, the SCHOOL DISTRICT desires that SERVICE PROVIDER
provide instruction to the students placed at Julia Dyckhman Andrus Memorial; and

WHEREAS, SERVICE PROVIDER is ready and willing to accept the
Participating Students in its Special Education Program and the Residential Program, and provide
them with the Services (defined below) in accordance with the terms and conditions set forth in
this Agreement.

SCHEDULE B
SCOPE OF SERVICE

SERVICE PROVIDER shall provide to each Participating Student the services set forth in
the student’s Individualized Education Plans (“IEP”).

1. Special Education Program. SERVICE PROVIDER will enroll all of the participating
students listed on Schedule A attached hereto and incorporated herein by reference (“Participating
Students”), into its Special Education Program for the Term of this Agreement.

2. Maintenance/Residential Services. SERVICE PROVIDER will admit the Participating
Students indicated on Schedule A to be receiving Maintenance/Residential services into one of
SERVICE PROVIDER’s residential halls on the campus of the Andrus Children’s Center in
Yonkers, New York for the Term of this Agreement.

The following general principles apply to the services set forth above:

a. SERVICE PROVIDER shall provide adequate instruction, related services and/
or a facility to students during the school year. The education provided by SERVICE
PROVIDER shall be appropriate to the mental ability and physical condition of the children,
and in accordance with all applicable Federal, State and local statutes, rules and ordinances,
including, but not limited to, the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education of the State
of New York

b. SERVICE PROVIDER shall provide services in accordance with the State
Education's regulations, using only professionals certified by the New York State
Education Department. The residential treatment and educational services provided shall
be appropriate to the mental ability and physical condition of the children, and in accordance
with Commissioner's Regulations. In the event a child with an identified handicapped
condition is placed with SERVICE PROVIDER, SERVICE PROVIDER shall provide the
following services consisting of, but not limited to, the following: the completion of progress
reports regarding student achievement of objectives as per report card schedules; provide the
Committee on Special Education (CSE) with annual progress reports for each individual
student receiving service to be reviewed at each student's CSE meeting; complete evaluations
as per the request of the Director of Special Education on an as needed basis; comply with
any testing requirements upon notification of such review dates. All services shall be
provided in strict compliance with the student's IEP. SERVICE PROVIDER shall provide the
SCHOOL DISTRICT with a copy of any progress reports, testing and/or observation reports
prepared in connection with the students served.

c. SERVICE PROVIDER further agrees that all disciplinary measures for students
will be conducted in accordance with applicable Federal, State, and local laws, rules and
regulations.

d. SERVICE PROVIDER shall be responsible for appropriate staff orientation
and training for all its educational and supporting personnel, including, but not limited to,
in-service training related to the provision of educational services to handicapped children.

PAYMENT OBLIGATIONS

NOTICE; The rates stated below are subject to change if and when modified by the
applicable regulatory authorities and will be charged retroactively as applicable.

For the Special Education Program: The SCHOOL DISTRICT will pay SERVICE
PROVIDER tuition (“Tuition”) in equal monthly installments -- starting on the Special Education
Program enrollment date of the student(s) as determined by the student’s JEP through and
including the last day of the Term -- at the rates determined and set by the New York State
Commissioner of Education, These payments will be invoiced on the first school day of each
month and will be due and payable by the SCHOOL DISTRICT not later than the last school day
of each month. The current rate is: $10,804 for 7/1/22-8/3 1/22 and $64,823 for 9/1/22-6/30/23

For Maintenance/Residential Services: The SCHOOL DISTRICT will pay SERVICE
PROVIDER a fee (“Residential Fee”) in equal monthly installments -- starting on the first day of
the Term through and including the last day of the Term -- at the rates determined and set by the
New York State Office of Children and Family Services. These payments will be invoiced on the
first school day of each month and will be due and payable by the SCHOOL DISTRICT not later
than the last school day of each month. The current rate is: $515.24/ day, 10/1/22-3/31/23 for Reg-
Institution and $730.37/ day, 10/1/22-3/31/23for HTP-Restore, payable to SERVICE PROVIDER
only for summer months.

NOTICE: For any month in which a Participating Student is enrolled for only part of the
month, the Tuition and Residential Fees will be prorated.

Invoices. Each invoice shall include time sheets and attendance, types of services rendered
and fees payable and, if requested by SCHOOL DISTRICT in writing, shall identify the names of
the students who received services.

Disputed Fees. SCHOOL DISTRICT will provide SERVICE PROVIDER with notice of
any disputed invoice within ten (10) days after it receives the disputed invoice. Upon receiving
such notice, SERVICE PROVIDER and SCHOOL DISTRICT shall negotiate in good faith to
resolve the disputed issues. SERVICE PROIDER shall not have the right to withhold payment
pending the resolution of this dispute. Failure to dispute any invoice in the manner set
forth herein shall be deemed as an acceptance of the invoice by the SCHOOL DISTRICT.

Period of Enrollment, SCHOOL DISTRICT is required to pay SERVICE PROVIDER
for each Participating Student that is enrolled in the Special Education Program and/or the
Residential Program for the entire “period of enrollment” as defined in 175.6 of the
Regulations of the Commissioner of Education. Pursuant to those regulations, the period of
enrollment means that period commencing on the first day a Participating Student is enrolled in
and is physically present at, or “legally absent” from, the Special Education Program and/or
Residential Program. Legally absent means to be absent for personal illness, illness or death in
the family, impassable roads or weather, religious observance, quarantine, required court

be sent by regular mail.) Otherwise, this Certificate is valid for one year after this form is approved by the insurance carrier
or its licensed agent, or until the policy expiration date listed in Box 3c, whichever is earlier.

while the underlying policy is in effect.

business must provide that certificate holder with a new Certificate of Insurance Coverage for NYS disability and/
or Paid Family Leave Benefits or other authorized proof that the business is complying with the mandatory
coverage requirements of the NYS Disability and Paid Family Leave Benefits Law.

article and notwithstanding any general or special statute requiring or authorizing any such contract, shall not enter into
any such contract unless proof duly subscribed by an insurance carrier is produced in a form satisfactory to the chair, that
