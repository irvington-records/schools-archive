Meeting: Oct 26, 2021 - Special Meeting/Work Session
Date: 2021-10-26
Category: 5. School Board Recognition
Subject: 5.1 School Board Recognition
Type: Recognition

School Board Recognition Week in New York State is October 18-22, 2021 and to recognize the members of the Irvington school board for their time and commitment, Irvington Trustee Janice Silverberg, will present the Board with a Proclamation. Representatives from the PTSA, IEF, and district's bargaining units as well as the District will present members of the Board with tokens of appreciation.
