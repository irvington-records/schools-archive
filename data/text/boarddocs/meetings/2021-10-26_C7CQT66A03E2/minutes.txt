3. Administrators' Update

Information: 3.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison gave an updated on events happening in the District. He thanked everyone for their participation in the District's surveillance testing program Nearly 700 students and staff have been tested. All tests have been negative. Testing will continue every Thursday for approximately 20% of the people who have consented on Thursdays when school is in session. Emails will be sent to people that are going to be tested. The District is still working on rescheduling the Homecoming Football game. Potentially November 5th will be the Homecoming game. Details will follow when plans have been finalized. New York State has legalized the sale and possession of certain amounts of marijuana. Villages could opt out of allowing sales of cannabis in their communities. They had to opt out at this time. If they did not opt out now, they would not be able to opt at a later date.  Dr. Harrison thanked the village board for their impending action the health and wellbeing of the Irvngton children.

5. School Board Recognition

Recognition: 5.1 School Board Recognition

October 18 to 22 was New York School Board Recognition Week. Dr. Harrison thanked the board for their dedication to the Irvington School Community. Janice Silverberg, Village Trustee thanked (filling in for Mayor Smith) thanked the board for their service and delivered a proclamation from the Mayor. David Sottile thanked the Board on behalf of the Irvington Administrators Association for their service and partnership. Dr. Harrison spoke for James Groven who was unable to participate tonight. He asked Dr. Harrison to extend his sincere appreciation to the board for all the work they do on behalf of the children. Venera Giiannetto spoke on behalf of the entire IEF and thanked the Board for their hard work, dedication to our schools and children.  She also thanked the Board for their support of the IEF and their mission. The IEF presented a gift to each member. Delina Codey, PTSA Co-President thanked everyone for the hard work and especially over the past year while they learned and managed opening schools safely for students and staff. She thanked them for their time and for working to make our schools better, safer and more innovative.  The PTSA presented the board with a gift card to the Spirit Wear store. Dr. Harrison also presented the Board with a gift card to the Red Barn Bakery from the District and thanked the Board for all their contributions and teamwork. Dr. Harrison thanked all the guests for joining and being a part of this special celebration. David Graeber also thanked all the guests on behalf of the Board.

Due to the weather Mr. Friedman is running late and wants to deliver Buildings and Grounds Committee report. Dr. Harrison suggested changing the order of 6.1 Committee Reports and 7.1 Presentation.

Information: 6.1 Buildings and Grounds Committee Report
