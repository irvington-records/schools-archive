Meeting: Oct 20, 2020 - Special Meeting/Work Session
Date: 2020-10-20
Category: 5. School Board Recognition
Subject: 5.1 School Board Recognition
Type: Recognition

School Board Recognition Week in New York State is October 19-23, 2020.  To recognize the members of the Irvington UFSD School Board for their time and commitment, Irvington Mayor Brian Smith will present the Board with a Proclamation.  Representatives from the PTSA, IEF, the District's bargaining units, as well as the District itself, will present members of the Board with tokens of appreciation.
