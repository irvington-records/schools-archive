WITNESSETH: Upon annual approval of the Commissioner of Education, the District is authorized by
law to contract with approved nonpublic educational facilities within the State of New York when a public facility
for instruction has been indicated as not available because of the unusual type of handicap or combination of
handicaps, and

WHEREAS, in the judgment of the District, the Contractor can meet the need of said handicapped child,
re above captioned student

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties mutually agree as follows:

1. The Contractor will provide adequate instruction for handicapped children approved by the District to
attend Contractor’s school in accordance with the provisions relating to eligibility of school contained in Section
200 of the Regulations of the commission of Education of the State of New York.

2. In full consideration for the services to be rendered by the Contractor to the District under the terms
of this Agreement, the district will pay to the contractor an annual INTERIM tuition charge of $38,545.00, (this
figure is the interim rate and will be adjusted ) as approved by the Commissioner of Education for the education
of each handicapped child accepted for enrollment. Where such education is provided for periods of less thana
full school year, the consideration will be prorated on a monthly basis accordingly. Residentially placed students
will be billed for both tuition and maintenance charges (maintenance charges aré approved by New York State
Department of Social Services in conjunction with the Commissioner of Education). If a health management
para is required, as mandated on the IEP, the rate for the 2020/2021 school year is $30,000 per student

3. Payments shall be made monthly at the end of each month following submission of a properly
executed bill to the address of the District. Each bill must clearly show the name of the student(s) enrolled and
the dates of attendance for each month.

4. The contractor shall maintain daily attendance records in the same form required in the public
schools of New York State.

[OCR]
5. The Contractor will obtain whatever releases or other legal documents are necessary in order that the
contractor may render full and complete records concerning the education and progress of the pupil or pupils
covered by the terms of this Agreement. The contractor will render such reports to the District at least annually.
Included will be a copy of an individual education plan, prepared according to Regulations and any other such
reports to the District because of interest by the district in the progress of the pupil or pupils covered by the terms
of this Agreement.

6. The contractor will maintain its status as an approved school for the education of handicapped
children. Itis understood and agreed by the parties that failure to do so shall render this Agreement void, in
which case the Contractor shall be entitled to no compensation for the portion of the school year in which such
approval ceases to be maintained and shall reimburse the District any amounts already received for the portion
of such school year.

7. The District shall have the right to examine any or all accounts kept by the contractor in connection
with this Agreement.

8. The contractor shall be subject to to the visitation of the District and its designated representatives.
9. This agreement shall take effect on September , 2020 and terminate June 30, 2021

10. The Contractor hereby agrees to furnish to the District all reports, audits, etc., required to make
determinations as to eligibility under the provisions of Section 200 of the Regulations of the commissioner of
Education. Such materials shall be furnished at such times as are required by the District. Failure to submit
required materials within five days of demand or as required by regulations shall permit the District to cancel this
Agreement forthwith.

11. The Contractor hereby agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the District, its officers, employees and
agents against any and all liability, loss, damage, costs or expenses whatsoever which it or they may hereafter
incur suffer, be charged with or be required to pay by reason of the willful or negligent actions of the Contractor,
it servants, or agents, in undertaking the instruction contracted for under this Agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this Agreement the day and year first above written.
