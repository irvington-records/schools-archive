9. Business of the Board

Action (Consent), Recognition:  9.1 Recognition of IUFSD Employees for Provision of Essential Public Service During Covid-19 Pandemic

Resolution: Whereas, the Irvington Union Free School Board acknowledges that --while public school employees deserve recognition for their commitment to public school children year-round-- these unprecedented times require additional acknowledgement and appreciation for the invaluable work and daily efforts of all those who work within our schools to make sure all children succeed; Whereas, October 19-23 2020 has been designated “School Board Recognition Week” by the New York State School Board Association (NYSSBA), the Irvington Union Free School District (IUFSD) Board of Education believes that October 19-23, 2020 should be similarly utilized to recognize public school employees and to raise community awareness and understanding about the crucial role that public school employees play within a school district; and Whereas, it is only through the hard work, enthusiasm and dedication of district employees that IUFSD has been able to reopen and maintain the reopening of its schools for in-person and remote learning during the COVID-19 pandemic; and Whereas these essential employees all working together to support IUFSD public schools are committed to serving their school communities and are essential to the critical functions of the village, county, state, and nation during the pandemic despite the rapid intensification of the daily lives of all working to support IUFSD public schools; Whereas the safety, academic education, and social emotional learning of all children in our schools is of paramount importance; and Whereas a good public education is integral to the success and growth of our local communities, New York State and the United States of America; NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED that: The Board of Education of the Irvington School District recognizes October 19-23 2020 as Irvington Schools Employee Recognition week and in doing so honors the critical work of all IUFSD employees. We honor IUFSD employees in every role and at every level for their dedication to promoting the success of our children and our community.

Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education approve the Consent Agenda which includes 8.-Curriculum and Instruction Item 8.1, 9.-Business of the Board Items 9.1- 9.6, 10.-Personnel Matters Items 10.1- 10.9, 11.-Business Office Matters Items 11.1-11-4.

Motion by David Graeber, second by Beth Propper.

Final Resolution: Motion Carries

Yes: Michael Hanna, David Graeber, Maura Gedid, Brian Friedman, Beth Propper, Erin Bernstein, Jayne Wissner

Action (Consent): 9.2 Tax Certiorari Settlement - Stop and Shop

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District is a respondent in certain tax certiorari proceedings filed by The Stop & Shop Companies, Inc. pertaining to property identified on the tax roll as Section 7.230, Block 100, Lots 1 and 2 for tax years 2012 through 2019; and WHEREAS, the parties have negotiated a tentative resolution of the proceedings, as set forth in the consent judgment attached hereto; and WHEREAS, the Board has determined that it is in the best interests of the School District to resolve the proceeding in accordance with the Consent Judgment without further litigation; NOW, BASED UPON THE FOREGOING, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED that the Board hereby approves the settlement of the tax proceedings as set forth in the Consent Judgment; AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that Ingerman Smith, LLP is authorized to execute the consent judgment attached hereto, and any other relevant settlement papers necessary to effectuate the settlement; AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the School Business Manager shall be authorized to issue a refund payment in accordance with the Consent Judgment, not to exceed $101,704.47.
