Addressing the needs of East Field,
 Meszaros Field, and our Facilities
                      


           January 22, 2014

 East Field
   o As per NYSDEC and NYDOH guidance, the field requires remediation
     that, at a minimum, calls for drainage, a soil cap, and a new surface.
   o Due to the historic conditions, this must be accomplished.


 Meszaros Field & Oley Track
   o As per extensive study and the obvious state of degradation, the field
      requires improvement and the track must be replaced.

 Infrastructure
   o Building systems (like roofs) have a lifespan. We will always have a need
      for regular investment in our facilities which will exceed the support that
      a general operating budget can provide. These investments will result in
      energy conservation and utility cost reduction.

 Our school district field inventory is both lacking and
  substandard. Overuse degrades the quality of the fields.

 If we start from the position of merely restoring conditions
  to the natural state in which they operated before the
  discovery of the East Field historic conditions, we can
  establish a BASELINE of need for East Field.

 Remediate with soil cap and drainage
   o This is the minimum we must do for East Field
   o It would restore the field to playing condition
   o It would restore the surface to an improved natural grass state


 Remaining question
   o Do we replace the grass with artificial turf?
       • This is the more expensive option
       • This option increases playability
       • This option will be ready for September, 2015

  suitable for competition, and needs replacement.

 The entire track needs to be replaced, including the asphalt
  base and rubberized running surface.

 The replacement cost is $385k.

 Replace the track

 Our task tonight is to establish a baseline, or minimum
  amount of work that must be accomplished:
   o East Field cap and natural field replacement
   o Meszaros natural field and track replacement
   o Infrastructure needs district-wide


 Can we recognize that this is the minimum we must do in
  order to responsibly provide satisfactory athletic/
  community resources and building infrastructure?

 If we agree upon the baseline, we still must address the
  potential for enhancements to this baseline, some of which
  may add expense but increase usability (play time), lower
  maintenance costs, and expedite installation.

 How do we establish community input on this important
  aspect of the process?

 Appoint a Fields Enhancement Committee to examine these
  possible additions and make a report to the BOE.
       • Include up to 10 residents based on civic or professional
         experience
       • Examine the possible enhancements (artificial turf and lights)
         with the District’s engineers and industry professionals to
         construct a report
       • Present findings and report to the BOE in a public meeting
       • Address questions from the BOE and community


   o The BOE will consider the Fields Enhancement Committee’s
     report and make a determination on the final scope of the
     athletic facilities for inclusion in a bond referendum.

 Infrastructure/facilities projects

     facilitate discussion to introduce possible projects.
   o The BOE and community will offer opinion and present
     questions.
   o The B&G Committee will research questions and present a
     formal recommendation to the BOE in a public meeting.
       • Address questions from the BOE and community
   o Considerate of the B&G Committee’s recommendation, the BOE
      will make a determination on the scope of the
      infrastructure/facility projects for inclusion in a bond
