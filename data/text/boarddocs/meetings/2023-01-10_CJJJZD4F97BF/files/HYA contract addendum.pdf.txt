HYA Strategic Planning Process

Purpose

The purpose of this Addendum to the Letter of Agreement dated January 5, 2023 is to
modify Strategic Planning services provided by Hazard, Young, Attea and Associates
(HYA’), to Irvington Union Free School District (the “District”) and its Board of
Education (the “Board”) to support the Board in its effort to develop a strategic plan.
Custom survey development and analysis will be substituted for the standard HYA
Survey. All other provisions from the Letter of Agreement remain unchanged.

HYA Responsibilities
HYA shall provide the following services:

Collaboration on the development of survey questions with the Superintendent
and Board of Education.

Entry of all questions in the HYA Survey platform.

Provision of one draft on-line survey for review and proofreading prior to public
distribution.

Provision of link to on-line survey.

Update of survey completion numbers by sub-group at mid-point of survey
window.

Provision of survey in English only. If the district desires translation to other
languages a translation fee of $415 per language will be added.

Written survey analysis report completed by an HYA research associate. Analysis
will include quantitative data only and will include an overview, aggregate results
and results by identified Stakeholder groups (no more than six). In the event that
the District chooses to add open ended questions, results will be provided verbatim
by stakeholder group.

Questions from the standard HYA Strategic Planning survey have established
validity and reliability.

District Responsibilities

Collaboration on the development of survey questions with the HYA Associate.
Collaboration with the HYA Associate to determine Stakeholder groups (no more
than six).

Distribution of the link to the survey through email, social media, websites,
automated text messaging, through community organizations.
