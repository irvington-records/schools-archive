New Policy 9150 -
POLICY FOR MAINTAINING APPROPRIATE BOUNDARIES BETWEEN STUDENTS
AND SCHOOL EMPLOYEES AND OTHER SCHOOL-AFFILIATED ADULTS

Intent of This Policy
The relationship between the school employee and the student should be one of
professional cooperation and respect. All school employees have a responsibility to
conduct themselves in a manner that will maintain an atmosphere that is safe,
ethical, and conducive to learning. School employees must establish appropriate
personal boundaries with students and not engage in any behavior that is
unprofessional or that could reasonably lead to the appearance of impropriety.

Who is Covered Under This Policy
For the purposes of this policy, "school employees" include all individuals employed
by the District including but not limited to teachers, administrators, long term
substitutes and staff, including full and part-time athletic department staff and other
school-affiliated adults "Other school-affiliated adults" include but are not limited to,
interns, student teachers and employees of contracted service providers.

Prohibited Behavior
It is the policy of the Board of Education to prohibit any type of close personal
relationship between a school employee and a student that may reasonably be
perceived as unprofessional, including, but not limited to, the perception of a dating
relationship or that the school employee favors a particular student. School
employees shall not socialize with any student(s) or spend an excessive amount of
time with any student(s) when such conduct creates the impression to school
employees, other students, their parent/caregiver, or the public that an
unprofessional relationship, or inappropriate favoritism, exists. Socialization includes
in-person, telephone, and electronic communications.

Inappropriate school employee behavior includes, but is not limited to, frequent
personal communication with a student (via phone, e-mail, letters, notes, etc.)
unrelated to course work or official school matters; flirting; making suggestive
comments; dating;
request for sexual activity; unethical physical displays of affection; giving
inappropriate personal gifts; providing alcohol or drugs to students; inappropriate
touching; and engaging in sexual contact and/or sexual relations.

This policy applies regardless of whether the student or the school employee
initiated the inappropriate behavior, and whether or not the student welcomes the
inappropriate behavior and/or reciprocates the attention. Even if the student
participated "willingly" in the activity (regardless of the student's age), inappropriate

fraternization of school employees with students is against District policy and may
be in violation of professional standards of conduct and New York State Law.
However, inappropriate school employee conduct does not need to rise to the level
of criminal activity for such conduct to be in violation of District rules and subject to
appropriate disciplinary sanctions.

Electronic Communication with Students
Communication with students should take place through district-administered and
monitored communications systems, such as the district email system, the district
alert system or other approved district communication tools such as the Google
Suite of tools, including Gmail, Google Docs, Google Classroom, Google Chat and
SportsYou and through the district supported Student Square. No school employee
shall contact a student by any means other than district-administered systems.

School employees shall not have any social media connections with any student on
social media platforms, including but not limited to Instagram, Facebook, and
TikTok. Exceptions to this prohibition shall be made when the staff member and the
student have a familial relationship. Because alumni routinely maintain contact with
current students, school employees should exercise good judgment when
establishing online contact with alumni on social media websites.

If a school employee receives a request from a current student to connect or
communicate through a social media site, they should decline the request. The
following language is one suggested response: "Please do not be offended if I do not
accept or respond to your request. As a school employee/other school affiliated
adult, I am not permitted to interact with current students on personal social media
websites. If you do want to connect, please contact me through the District email."

Reporting, and Investigation
Any student(s) who believes that they, or another student, has been subjected to
inappropriate school employee behavior as enumerated in this policy should report
the incident to any other school employee, or to the Principal or Assistant Principal
of their school building.

School employees or third parties who have knowledge of or witness any possible
occurrence of inappropriate school employee/student relations shall report the
incident to the Building Principal or to the Superintendent of Schools. Anonymous
complaints of inappropriate fraternization of school employee(s) with student(s) shall
also be investigated by the District. Investigations of allegations of inappropriate
school employee-student relations shall follow the procedures utilized for
complaints of harassment within the School District. Allegations of inappropriate

school employee-student behavior shall be promptly investigated and will be
treated as confidential and private to the extent possible within legal constraints.

Notwithstanding the above, any school employee having knowledge of or
reasonable suspicion that another school employee may have engaged in
inappropriate conduct with a student that may constitute child abuse as described
in Policy 9620 Child Abuse in an Educational Setting, must also follow the District's
reporting procedures for such allegations as set forth in that policy.

If a student initiates inappropriate behavior toward a school employee, that school
employee shall document the incident and report it to their building principal or
supervisor immediately.

The District shall promptly investigate all complaints of inappropriate school
employee-student relations and take prompt corrective action to stop such conduct
if it occurs.

Exceptional Circumstances
If a school employee believes that a situation involving the safety of an individual or
group of students requires deviation from the guidelines set out above, they must
notify the Building Principal or the Superintendent of Schools in writing and obtain
written permission to proceed under modified guidelines.

Violations of Policy
Oftentimes, boundaries are crossed inadvertently and require nothing more than a
reminder to the school employee to maintain a boundary in the future. At other
times, the crossing of a boundary is indicative of a more serious problem and may
indicate an inappropriate relationship.

Any school employee who engages in inappropriate conduct with a student,
prohibited by the terms of this policy, shall be subject to appropriate disciplinary
measures up to and including termination of employment in accordance with legal
guidelines, District policy and regulation, and the applicable collective bargaining
agreement. A violation of this policy may also subject the school employee to
criminal and/or civil sanctions as well as disciplinary action by the State Education
Department.

Prohibition and Retaliation
The Board of Education prohibits any retaliatory behavior directed against
complainants, victims, witnesses, and/or any other individuals who participate in the
investigation of allegations of inappropriate employee-student relations. Follow-up
inquiries and/or appropriate monitoring shall be made to ensure that the alleged

conduct has not resumed and that all those involved in the investigation have not
suffered retaliation. Any act of retaliation is subject to appropriate disciplinary action
by the District.

Review and Dissemination
The Principal of each school and District Administration shall be responsible for
informing students, employees, parents/caregivers, and volunteers of the
requirements of this policy, including the duty to report, and the procedures
established for investigation and resolution of complaints. The presentation of this
policy to students shall be conducted in an age-appropriate manner. Further, school
employee training shall be provided to facilitate school employee identification of
possible behavior that may constitute inappropriate school employee/student
relationships.
