LETTER OF AGREEMENT
2026-2027 STRATEGIC PLAN IMPLEMENTATION SUPPORT

Purpose

The purpose of this Addendum to the Letter of Agreement (“Agreement”) is to document the
working arrangement between HYA Corporation (“HYA") and the Irvington Union Free School
District (“District”) to review progress on the District's Strategic Plan and identify potential updates
to plan objectives. This engagement is intended to support continued alignment with the Board of
Education's vision and District priorities and to guide the next phase of strategic plan
implementation.

Section t: HYA Responsibilities
HYA shail provide the following services:

e Strategic Plan Review and Workshop Preparation - HYA will collaborate with the
Superintendent to review progress on the District's current strategic plan and prepare for a
Board and district leadership workshop. This work will include up to three one hour virtual
planning meetings with the Superintendent to review plan progress, identify areas for
discussion, and develop the workshop agenda and materials.

« Board and District Leadership Workshop - On January 12, 2027, an HYA Associate will be
on site to facilitate a workshop with the Board of Education and district leaders. The
workshop will focus on reviewing progress toward the District's strategic plan goals and
objectives, discussing accomplishments and challenges, and identifying potential updates
to plan objectives to guide continued implementation.

° Off-Site Work - HYA will conduct off-site work to support the engagement, including
workshop planning, agenda development, preparation of materials, review of relevant
District information, and follow-up activities associated with the workshop.

Section Il: Fees and Payment Schedule
In consideration for the services described, the District agrees to pay HYA a consulting fee
of $6,000

2 50% ($3,000) invoiced upon execution of this Letter of Agreement

© 50% ($3,000) invoiced upon completion of the second planning session

Section Ill: Reimbursable Cost

* Printing and Postage: HYA provides all materials electronically. If the District requests
printed copies, it will reimburse costs associated with printing, binding, and shipping.

° Travel Expenses: The District agrees to reimburse travel costs associated with the
January 12, 2027 on-site session.

Section IV: Additional Services

The District may request additional days of service beyond those outlined in this Agreement,
including expanded support, additional workshops, or customized implementation tools. Such
requests may be made as part of this Letter of Agreement without requiring a formal addendum.
Additional days will be billed at a daily rate of $2,500.
