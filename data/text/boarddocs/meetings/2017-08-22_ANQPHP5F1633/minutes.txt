3. District Updates, News, Events

Information:  3.2 Final Logo Design

Dr. Harrison provided a recap of the logo design competition and voting process that was held at the end of last year. He shared the winning design which submitted by students, Alexandra Powell, Maya Castiblanco, Julia Herzog and

Katherina Kovalevskaya and which the Board of Ed approved of as well. Dr Harrison noted that the design has been tweaked (fonts, etc.) with input from Alexandra Powell and Chuck Routier, the district's design consultant, but that the final version remains true to the original design. A timeline of all the next steps to be taken will be created and shared with the Board.

Information:  3.3 Discussion of Community Surveys

Dr. Harrison commented that there have been discussions at previous Board meetings about the idea of conducting community surveys to gain input from stakeholders. Michael Hanna asked to reintroduce the idea with the full Board noting that a professional vendor would move forward this type of initiative, however, there is a cost of in the range of $20,000 which the Board must be comfortable with before moving forward. Mr. Graeber is in favor of a survey, but wants to consider how we will get people to take the survey in order to make the information received valuable. Mr. Hanna noted that several previous surveys have had a tremendous response, but it must be promoted to have people understand the motivation of the survey. Deb Hargraves believes it necessary to gather information as to the District needs, and is concerned about where money is spent forward, but would be supportive of a survey that would help the Board to better understand where community wants money to be spent. Catherine Palmieri noted that the challenge will be the expectation of stakeholders that something will be done with the information gleaned from conducting a survey. She also would be very cautious as to the language in the contract to be sure  it doesn't contain additional costs. She also suggested that a short survey be considered initially and if response rate is favorable and data valuable, could then continue with other, lengthier surveys. Determining who decides what questions are included in the survey is part of the process with a partnership with vendor, but must first gather estimates from vendors, form a working group to include administrators, board members, etc. Mrs. Kashkin is in favor as long as the parameters mentioned are set, especially the number of questions and to ensure that the data is valuable. The Board agreed to move forward with gathering initial information.
