              TEMPORARY CONSTRUCTION EASEMENT AGREEMENT


        THIS TEMPORARY CONSTRUCTION EASEMENT AGREEMENT (“Agreement”) is
made as of the ____ day of __________, 2017 by and between the Irvington Union Free School
District, having its principal office at 6 Dows Lane, Irvington, New York 10533 (hereinafter
“IUFSD” or “Grantor”), and the Village of Irvington, a New York municipal corporation having
its principal office at 85 Main Street, Irvington, New York 10533 (hereinafter “Village” or
“Grantee”).

                                       WITNESSETH

       WHEREAS, the IUFSD is the owner of certain real property in the Village of Irvington,
Town of Greenburgh, County of Westchester and State of New York, known as Heritage Hill
Road; and

        WHEREAS, the Village wishes to construct, at its expense, certain pedestrian safety
improvements in and along Heritage Road, including installing new curbs, replacing the sidewalk,
and relocating the pedestrian crossing activator, as more particularly described on the plans
entitled “Proposed Broadway/Heritage Hill Road Intersection Pedestrian Safety Improvement
Project,” prepared by James J. Hahn Engineering P.C., attached as Exhibit A and made part of this
Agreement (“Pedestrian Safety Improvements”); and

       WHEREAS, the Village wishes to obtain from the IUFSD a temporary construction
easement on, over, under, in and across Heritage Hill Road for the purpose of constructing the
Pedestrian Safety Improvements; and

         WHEREAS, the IUFSD is willing to grant such temporary construction easement subject
to the terms and conditions set forth in this Agreement,

        NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants contained in this
Agreement and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and legal sufficiency of which
are hereby acknowledged, the IUFSD does hereby grant to the Village of Irvington a temporary
easement in, on, over, under and across Heritage Hill Road for the purpose of permitting the
Village and its contractors to engage in such activities as may be required or convenient for, or
incidental to, the construction of the Pedestrian Safety Improvements, as shown on Exhibit A,
together with the free right to enter upon Heritage Hill Road at all reasonable times to perform the
foregoing activities without restriction or hindrance (“Easement”), subject to the following
conditions:

       (1) The Village shall bear all costs incurred in connection with construction of the
Pedestrian Safety Improvements, restoration of Heritage Hill Road and removal of all construction
equipment from Heritage Hill Road.

       (2) The Village shall promptly restore Heritage Hill Road in accordance with the plans

entitled “Proposed Broadway/Heritage Hill Road Intersection Pedestrian Safety Improvement
Project” (Exhibit A).

        (3) This Easement shall automatically terminate upon completion of the Pedestrian
Safety Improvements, restoration of Heritage Hill Road, and removal of all construction
equipment from Heritage Hill Road. The Grantee agrees to surrender all rights pursuant to this
Agreement upon expiration of this Temporary Construction Easement.

        (4) The Grantor and its heirs, successors, assigns, guests, invitees, licensees and students
shall have the right to cultivate the ground and the right to cross and re-cross the property more
particularly described on Exhibit “A” provided that such use of land shall not interfere with,
obstruct or endanger any rights granted to the Grantee hereunder.

        Grantee, for itself and its heirs, successors, assigns, guests, invitees and licensees,
contractors, and sub-contractors (referred to in this paragraph as “Releasors”) does hereby release
Grantor and its heirs, successors, assigns, guests, invitees, licensees and students (referred to in
this paragraph as “Releasees”) from any and all injuries, liabilities, loss, costs, damages and
expenses suffered by Releasors arising out of, or related to Releasors exercising the rights granted
hereunder, and Releasors agree to defend, indemnify and hold harmless Releasees from any and all
injuries, liabilities, loss, costs, damages and expenses (including attorneys’ fees) in any pending or
completed claim, action or proceedings, based upon, in whole or in part, the exercise by the
Releasors of the rights granted hereunder. This release and indemnification shall not apply to any
and all injuries, liabilities, loss, costs, damages and expenses caused by the Grantor’s negligence.

        The Grantor hereby covenants with the Grantee and warrants that it has lawfully acquired
of said subject land in fee simple, that Grantor has good, right and lawful authority to grant and
convey the said Temporary Construction Easement. The Grantee further agrees that incident to
and conditioned upon the granting of this Temporary Construction Easement, it will require itself
and its contractor(s) and/or sub-contractor(s) to name the Grantor as an additional named insured
on its and/or any hired contractor(s) and/or sub-contractor(s) insurance policy in relation to any
work done on the property of the Grantor more particularly described on Exhibit “A.”

       (5) This Agreement and all rights and obligations set forth herein shall run with the land
and shall bind and inure to the benefit of the successors and assigns of the respective parties to this
Agreement, until the termination of the easement pursuant to the terms of this Agreement.

        (6) This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the law of the
State of New York.

        (7) This Agreement may not be modified, changed, supplemented or terminated, nor may
any of its obligations be waived, except by written instrument signed by the party to be charged or
by it agent duly authorized in writing.

        IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have signed this Agreement the day and year
first above written.
