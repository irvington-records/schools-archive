4. Superintendent's Report

Dr. Harrison noted that Board members Catherine Palmieri and Deborah Hargraves are on vacation.

- Dr. Harrison provided an update on the construction projects taking place in the District: the turf has been installed on Meszaros Field and the infill will be installed later this week followed by the field goals, noting that it might be playable before the start of the school year.

- Oley Track has been paved, waiting for asphalt to cure to apply the running surface. Training will occur next week on field maintenance. A meeting will also be scheduled to review protocols and proper usage of the fields will also be scheduled. A special event will be planned for the reopening of Meszaros Field, perhaps for the Homecoming Game. East Field work will begin over Labor Day weekend and it is anticipated to be completed in November. The middle school courtyard upgrade is also underway.The other projects are completed or progressing well throughout the District.

- Dr. Harrison wanted to assure the community that it appears that it was stone dust that drained into a pond adjacent to the high school campus and hopes that it won't be a problem in the future once the drainage area is paved.

- He extended accolades to Carol Stein, Gary Knowles and the construction manager for their oversight and hard work with respect to the work taking place throughout the district.

- Dr. Harrison spoke to the guidance district’s received from the federal government on the need to provide gender neutral bathrooms and stated that the district has identified several single occupancy restrooms at the high school that will be converted to gender neutral which will be supportive of our student body.

- Parking will be an issue at the high school at the start of school with the ongoing construction which will affect the gravel lot where students (and staff) park. Other options for student parking are being explored, including offsite options for students to park. There are safety concerns if cars are allowed to park in the gravel lot.The community will be updated of any final plans.

- Dr. Harrison noted that enrollment at the kindergarten level is currently 131 with new families still registering. There will be another section added to keep the class size small at the kindergarten as has been historically true in Irvington.

With respect to the parking situation, Mr. Graeber noted that students often park in Cedar Ridge and asks if a communication can go out from the principal to students/parents to be respectful of the community, for safety issues as well as for the consideration of residents.

Curriculum/Instruction Update

Dr. Kor stated that the District will submit the APPR Plan to SED once one last signature is needed.  The State informed us that the Plan will be approved and is in compliance with the new directives.

A two day administrative retreat will take place next week, followed by new teacher orientation for two days where new staff will be introduced both to logistics of the district as well as our philosophy.

Mr. Graeber asked to have an overview of the new APPR Plan. Dr, Kor noted that a summary document will be provided of changes to the plan. He also asked when Rubicon Atlas will be available for parents so they may review their child's curriculum. Dr. Kor noted the role out will be discussed next week at the Administrators Retreat with communications to parents through open houses, etc. There will be information provided at a future Board meeting as well.

Business Office Update

Carol Stein updated the Board on the transportation, meeting which took place early today with Ardsley Transportation coordinators and have representatives from Royal Coach to review expectations, etc. Our routes are already established and on target for a successful start to the school year with respect to transportation. It was noted that many of previous drivers will be returning to work with Royal Coach so they are familiar with the district. Mr. Hanna asked if the Board could be provided with a matrix as to how the transportation program is going, perhaps sometime in September. It was noted that the Walking School Bus will start up  again in the fall.

Mr. Hanna also asked if they Board could get an update from the Village as to when the enhanced crosswalk at Broadway will be completed as well as the Dows Lane sidewalk. Dr. Harrison noted that it is the Village's objective to have the sidewalk completed prior to the start of the school year and the crosswalks will require design approval from the state.

Policy - 1st Reading: 6.1 1st Read - District Investments
