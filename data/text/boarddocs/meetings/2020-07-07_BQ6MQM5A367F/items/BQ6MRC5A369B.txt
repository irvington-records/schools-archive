Category: 4. Nominations, Election of Board Officers, Oath of Office for President and Vice Presidents, Line of Authority Within the Board
Subject: 4.3 Nomination and Election of Second Vice President of the Board of Education
Type: Action

The Board President will declare nominations open for the office of the Second Vice President of the Board of Education for the 2019-20 school year.  Should there be more than one nominee, the member with the majority of votes will thereby be appointed Second Vice President. The Board President shall administer the Oath of Office to the Second Vice President.
