Meeting: Jul 07, 2020 - 2020-21 Reorganization Meeting/Regular Meeting
Date: 2020-07-07
Category: 4. Nominations, Election of Board Officers, Oath of Office for President and Vice Presidents, Line of Authority Within the Board
Subject: 4.1 Nomination and Election of President of the Board of Education
Type: Action

The District Clerk will declare that nominations open for the office of the President of the Board of Education for the 2020-21 school year. Should there be more than one nominee, the member with the majority of votes will thereby be appointed President. The District Clerk shall administer the Oath of Office to the President and turn the meeting over to the Board President
