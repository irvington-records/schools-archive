NOW, THEREFORE, the parties hereto mutually agree as follows:

1. The Contractor will provide adequate instruction in accordance with the provisions
relating to eligibility of schoals contained in Part 200 of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education
of the State of New York (hereinafterreferred to asthe “Commissioner of Education”) to attend
Contractor’sschool. The school yearis hereby defined asJuly | to June 30. The Contractorwill provide a
special education program for eligible children with handicapping conditions placed in the Contractor's
school in accordance with the provisions of Education Law sections 4407 and 4408 and Part 200 of the
Regulations of the Commissioner of Education forthe part of the school year for which such children are
approved forplacement in such school by the Commissioner of Education and will operate and administer
its special educationalprogram consistent with all the terms and conditions required under section 200.7(b)
of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education.

2a. In full consideration for the education services to be rendered by the Contractorto
the Board forthe period September 1 — June 30 of each school yearunder the terms of this agreement, the
board will pay to the Contractor foreach child the agreed upon tuition rate approved by the Commissioner
of Education and approved by the Director of the New York State Division of the Budget the amountof
$2,731.80. Where such education is provided for periods of substantially less than the duration of the full
September through June program, consideration will be prorated accordingly, the specific amount to be
fixed by the State of New York (hereina fterthe “State”)

3a, Payments forthe School year shall be due in accordance with the following schedule:

(1) On or before September 20 of the school year, the total amount for the July
through August portion of the school year based upon actual enrollment and
attendance date forthe July through August component.

(2) The ten-month program shall be paid monthly, within thirty (30) days of billing
based upon enrollment and the attendance data.

(3) In the event that enrollment is for less than a full month, tuition will be prorated
accordingly.

b. For the purposes of this agreement, the definition of calculation of enrollment and
attendance shall be in accordance with section 175.6 of the regulations of the Commissioner of Education

4. The Contractor will obtain whatever releases or other legal documents are necessary in
order that the Contractor may render full complete reports canceming the education and progress of the
pupil or pupils covered by the terms of this agreement. The Contractor will render such Teports to the

5. Pursuant to section 4405 of the Education Law, payment of maintenance expenses for
residential pupils for the September through June program shall be made by the county wherein each such
pupil resides (or the city in the case of a county contained in the City of New York) at the commencement
of the school year (July 1) for which maintenance is paid. The charge for maintenance may not exceed the
amount approved forsuch purpose by the Commissioner of Social Services of the State and the Director of
the Budget of the State. Such payment should be made on the same terms and conditions as tuition, butthe
payment schedule will be determined by the appropriate count orcity.

6. No parent or guardian or any other person shall be required to make any payment for
tuition of maintenance on behalf of any child with a handicapping condition covered by this agreement in
addition to the payments made by the State and the county or city.

7. The Contractor will maintain the standard set forth under section 200.7(b) of the
Regulations of the Commissioner of Education to preserve its status as an approved school for the
education of children with handicapping conditions. It is understood and agreed by the parties that failure
to do so shall render this agreement void, in which case the Contractor shall be entitled to no compensation
for the Board any amounts already received for that portion of such school year.

8. The State, acting through the Department of Education or the Department of Audit and
Control, shail have the right to examine any orall accounts kept by the Contractor in connection with this
agreement,

9. The Contractor shall be subject to the visitation of the Commissioner of Education or
his designated representatives.

10. This agreement shall take effect as of the I* day of July, 2020. This agreement may
be terminated by written notice by either party to the other party and the Commissioner of Education not
less than ninety (90) days before the commencement of any succeeding school year Guly 1) subject to the
approval of the Commissioner of Education. In the event of such termination, the parties will adjust the
accounts dueand the Contractor will undertake no additional expenditures not already required. Upon any
such termination, the parties shall endeavor in an orderly mannerto wind down activities hereunder.

Education. Such materials shall be fumished at such times as are required by the State. Failure to submit
required materials within ten (10) days of demand or as required by regulation shall constitute grounds for
the Commissioner of Education to withdraw approval forsuch program.
