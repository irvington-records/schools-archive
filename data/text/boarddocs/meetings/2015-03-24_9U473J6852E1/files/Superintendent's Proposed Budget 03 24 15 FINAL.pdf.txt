Proposed Budget Invests in Our Facilities
     Continues focus on maintenance and upkeep:
      District-wide projects to repair and upgrade facilities
        Examples: electrical upgrades; lighting replacements; painting;
         repairing damaged ceilings; window shades; upgrade door locks; etc.
      Maintenance of grounds
        Examples: paving IMS parking lot; rebuilding loading dock; sidewalk
         repair; mulch; new snow removal equipment; new Campus courtyard
         walkways; etc.
      Large scale initiatives:
        Remodeling of a HS student restroom
        Creation of needed office space


10

 Proposed Budget Plans for the Future
     Create a capital reserve fund
      A Capital Reserve Fund, like the District’s Tax Certiorari
       Reserves, are funds that are set aside for an express purpose
      Allows the District to save money for future construction
       projects or improvements to facilities.
      Such a fund cannot be created or spent without voter approval.
      Where do funds come from? Funds can be budgeted or allocated
       from fund balance.
     Administrative Proposal:
     Seek Board of Education support tonight and thenVoter approval on May 19th, as the
     question must appear on a ballot for voter authorization.
