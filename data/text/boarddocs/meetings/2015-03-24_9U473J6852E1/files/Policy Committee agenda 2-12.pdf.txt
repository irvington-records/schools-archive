1.0     Current Policy Review (3.1-3.7 carried over from January 8 meeting)

        1.1    Information Security Breach and Notification
               NYSSBA recommends minor changes to update this policy regarding computerized
               data, and responsibility for securing hard copy files.

        1.2    Purchasing
               NYSSBA has updated policy to better reflect legal options for “piggybacking”
               purchases under various provisions of NYS law.

        1.3    Student Wellness
               NYSSBA has updated this policy to reflect the implementation of Healthy, Hunger-
               Free Kids Act of 2010 which went into effect 7/1/14.

2.0     Discussion of considering Credit Card policy

3.0     Student Health Services
        The regulations of the Commissioner of the DOH have been updated regarding
        immunization requirements. NYSSBA’s policy and regulation have been changed to align
        with the new regs. Also, NYSSBA’s policy incorporates Irvington’s current Immunization of
        Students (7511), Student Physicals (7512); Administration of Medication (7513); Health
        Records (7514); and Allergens – Management and Accommodation (7515). Upon adoption
        of NYSSBA’s Student Health Services, the current policies will be revoked.
