Students suspended from school are entitled to alternative education, in accordance with New York State law.  Although titled ‘homebound instruction,’ because it also addresses students out of school for medical reasons, this policy deals with an aspect of that requirement.

	This would replace current policy 8450 which was the District's former policy provider, Erie 1 Boces numbering format.
