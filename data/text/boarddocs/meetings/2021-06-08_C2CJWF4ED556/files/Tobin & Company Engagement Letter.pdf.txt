Dear Ms. Stein,

We are pleased to confirm our understanding of the services we are to provide for The Irvington
Union Free School District for the year ending June 30, 2021.

We will conduct a risk assessment. During our engagement we may interview district personnel
in order to gain a comprehensive understanding of these controls as they relate to the district's
financial operations and assets. We will conduct testing of these controls where required to
facilitate our understanding of them. We will report to you as to the effectiveness of these
controls and make recommendations accordingly. Our work will be conducted in accordance
with International Standards for the Professional Practice or Internal Auditing as promulgated by
the Institute of Internal Auditors.

In order to maintain our independence throughout the engagement and subsequent thereto, we
cannot and will not perform any management functions, make management decisions, or act or
appear to act in a capacity equivalent to that of an employee.

Because our engagement does not constitute an external audit or an examination made in
accordance with U.S. Generally Accepted Auditing Standards, we will not express an opinion, or
any form of assurance on the financial statements of the District.

If as a result of our engagement or through other means, matters come to our attention which
cause us to believe that the selected records and transactions are not presented in accordance
with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America, we will disclose
those matters in our report. Such Disclosure, if any, may not necessarily include all matters
which might have come to our attention had we performed additional procedures.

The District's management has the responsibility for the proper recording of the transactions in
the accounting records and for the preparation of financial statements in conformity with United
States Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP).

Our role is strictly limited to the engagement described in this letter, and we offer no assurance
as to the results or ultimate outcomes of this engagement or of any decisions that you may
make based upon our communications with you. You will be solely responsible for making all
decisions concerning the contents of our communications and reports, for the adoption of any
plans, and for the implementation of any plans you may develop.

You agree to assume sole responsibility for full compliance with all applicable federal and state
laws, rules or regulations and reporting obligations that apply to you, including the accuracy and
lawfulness of any reports you submit to any government regulator, authority or agency.

We agree to retain the documents, information and data you supply to us or that we develop for
three (3) years after we provide you with our report under any aspect of this engagement.

This agreement will remain in effect through the end of the fiscal year ending June 30, 2021 or
until it is terminated by either party on thirty (30) days written notice, with or without cause. In
the event of termination, the terms or this engagement shall survive and remain in effect.

We expect to begin our update of the existing risk assessment of your operations in June 2021.
The fee for the risk assessment for the year ending June 30, 2021 will be $7,500.

Our invoices for these fees will be rendered as work progresses and are payable on
presentation.

We appreciate the opportunity to be of service to you and believe this letter accurately
summarizes the significant terms of our engagement. If you have any questions, please let us
know. If you agree with the terms of our engagement as described in this letter, please sign the
enclosed copy and return it to us.
