		Action: 2.3 Naming of the Music Wing in honor of Lawrence Corio

		Resolution: Whereas, Mr. Lawrence P. Corio Began His Career as a Music Educator in the Irvington Schools in February 1983; and

		Whereas, During His Tenure of 29 Years, Mr. Corio’s contributions to the Irvington High School Music Program have been numerous, and include, but are not limited to, the following accomplishments:

		     - Developed one of the finest High School Bands in New York from Eight students in 1983 to 134 students in 2012

		     - Achieved Band and Wind Ensemble gold medals ratings of 5A and 6A+ at New York State Competitions

		     Conducted Irvington High School International Band performances in London, Belgium, Montreal and Toronto

		     - Conducted Irvington High School Band performances in the premier settings of The Kennedy Center in Washington, D.C. and New York City’s Carnegie Hall (Three times)

		     - Established the High School Advanced Placement (AP) Music Theory Course

		WHEREAS, Mr. Corio has touched the lives of thousands of Irvingtonions including students, their parents, community members, colleagues and administrators in many positive ways; and

		WHEREAS, Mr. Corio has submitted his resignation from the school district for the purpose of retirement effective June 30, 2012; and

		WHEREAS, the Board of Education believes that Mr. Corio’s contributions and his legend should be memorialized;

		NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education hereby recognizes the contributions of Mr. Corio and honors Mr. Corio by naming the Irvington High School Music Wing the: Corio Music Wing

		BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Proclamation be presented to Mr. Corio and that it be spread across the official minutes of this May 15, 2012 Board of Education meeting for all to see.

			Resolved, that the Board of Education move to a Consent Agenda and approve the consent agenda as presented.

			Motion by Robyne Camp, second by Phil Whitney

			Final Resolution: Motion Carries

			Yes: Robyne Camp, John Dawson, James McCann, Phil Whitney

		Action: 2.4 Approval to Establish a Scholarship in the Name of Lawrence Corio

		Resolution:Whereas, the Board of Education of the IRVINGTON UFSD has been asked to approve the formation of a Larry Corio Scholarship; and

		Whereas, the Larry Corio Scholarship Committee has advised the Board that upon the Board's approval of such scholarship that it would assume responsibility to conduct fund raising to provide funds for the scholarship; and

		Whereas, the scholarship will be used to honor annually a graduating senior from Irvington High School who intends to pursue a career in education; and

		Whereas, the Scholarship Committee will determine the amount of scholarship funds that will awarded to a scholarship; and

		Whereas, for the class of 2012, Mr. Corio will select the scholarship award recipient, and for subsequent graduating classes the recipient will be selected by a committee comprised of the high school band teacher and representatives of the guidance staff; and

		Whereas, the Scholarship Committee understands that the Irvington School District in supporting the creation of the scholarship cannot contribute any financial support to fund the scholarship or financial aid;

		Now Therefore, Be It Resolved, that the Board of Education hereby approves the formation of a Larry Corio Scholarship.

			Resolved, that the Board of Education move to a Consent Agenda and approve the consent agenda as presented.

			Motion by Robyne Camp, second by Phil Whitney

			Final Resolution: Motion Carries

			Yes: Robyne Camp, John Dawson, James McCann, Phil Whitney

	3. Business Office Matters

		Action (Consent): 3.1 SEQRA Resolution - Dows Lane Elementary School Capital Improvement Projects

		Resolution: WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District (“District”) has proposed to perform various capital improvement projects at Dows Lane Elementary School and WHEREAS, the Board wishes to fully comply with its obligations under the State Environmental Quality Review Act (“SEQRA”) and the regulations thereunder with respect to the proposed action, in its capacity as Lead Agency; and

		WHEREAS, the Board has carefully considered the nature and scope of the proposed action; and WHEREAS, upon review of the foregoing, the Board makes the following determinations:

		1.    The proposed actions involve performing various capital improvement projects at Dows Lane Elementary School.

		2.    The proposed project represents the replacement, rehabilitation or reconstruction of a structure or facility, in kind, on the same site within the meaning of 6 NYCRR § 617 (5) (c) (2); and/or alternatively represents a routine activity of an educational institution within the meaning of 6 NYCRR § 617.5 (c) (8); and/or alternatively represents maintenance or repair involving no substantial changes in the existing structure or facility within the meaning of 6 NYCRR § 617 (5) (c) (1).

		3.    The proposed action will in no case have a significant adverse impact based on the environment based on the criteria contained in 6 NYCRR § 617.7 (c) and are not otherwise a Type I action as defined by 6 NYCRR § 617.4.

		4.    The proposed action is a Type II action within the meaning of 6 NYCRR § 617.5 and are therefore not subject to review under SEQRA and the regulations thereunder.

		NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board finds and concludes that the proposed action is a Type II action within the meaning of 6 NYCRR § 617.5 and therefore are not subject to review under SEQRA and the regulations thereunder.
