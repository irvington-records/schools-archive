3. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Discussion, Report: 3.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison  welcomed the Board and public back after the holiday break. He congratulated our Middle School and High School students on successful winter concerts. He noted that the winter concerts are coming up: the Main Street School winter concert will be held on Jan. 17 and the Dows Lane concert will be held on Jan. 31, both at 7pm. The Main Street Spelling Bee is tomorrow, Jan. 9, and we wish all students the best of luck. The winners will be recognized at an upcoming Board of Education meeting. Dr. Harrison reminded everyone about the letter that was sent out to the District on Friday related to the capital project. The letter provides links to valuable resources that can be found on the District website and provide an outline of a number of community forums and BOE meetings where community input will be welcomed and valued. Dr. Harrison noted that we have advertised upcoming coffees in District Newsletters that will be hosted at local businesses. The first is next Thursday, Jan. 17, at the Black Cat Cafe at 10am. He asked that community members please sign up if they wish to attend to discuss district goals, budget and capital project. Important meetings upcoming include the Feb. 12 Board of Education meeting where we will discuss initial business and operations budget details and proposals and March 5, will be a discussion on the curriculum-related budget. Also at the March 5 meeting, we will have a discussion on the proposed district calendar for the 2019-2020 school year. Please note that this Thursday, Jan. 10 from 7pm to 9pm, is the Irvington High School Curriculum Night for 8th and 10th grade parents. Irvington High School Master Schedule workshops will be held on Jan. 14 at 7pm and Jan. 16 at 2pm. This is a great opportunity to learn more about the schedule and have your questions answered. In addition, there will be an open Q&A forum on Jan. 15 at 7pm in the High School theater.

Information: 3.2 Committee Reports

Communications -- Michael Hanna noted that the  Communications Committee met regarding supporting the Bond through communications via the website and mentioned the letter that went out to community last Friday regarding the Capital Project.

Curriculum -- David Graeber noted that the Curriculum committee will meet on Jan. 22 and will discuss Amplify Science and Early World Language and will provide an update at the next BOE meeting.

The PTSA is bringing back International Culture Night on March 1, more details to come. The PTSA General Membership Meeting is Jan. 23 at 7pm. The Irvington Public Library will be open late during mid-terms.

CAB -- Maria Kashkin noted that, at this morning's CAB meeting, they discussed the "Gray for a Day" initiative with students which most were aware of. They also discussed mid-terms and ways to reduce the stress around them. IHS Principal David Cohen answered students' questions regarding the Master Schedule. There were many well-thought questions and answers provided.

4. Capital Project Update

Discussion, Report:  4.1 Buildings and Grounds Committee Capital Project Update

Brian Friedman led the Presentation on behalf of the Buildings and Grounds Committee, noting that the scope of work is categorized as:

- Safety and Security

- Infrastructure/Project Assets/Storage/Other Improvements

- Energy Efficiency

- Modernizing Learning Spaces

Mr. Friedman explained that the work was identified as a result of the 2015 Building Condition Survey and 5 year Capital Plan, community-based committee work and review by the District Board Buildings and Grounds Committee with professional advice from the architectural/engineering firm and other partners. Each building and site was carefully inspected, with health and safety items receiving the highest priority. An estimated 90% of proposed projects are eligible for maximum State Aid reimbursement. Mr. Friedman noted that the goal is to remain budget neutral -- with no additional taxes. A Pre-Bond schedule and timeline was given with some important dates highlighted, including Feb. 7: Special BOE Meeting/Community Forum -- the District's Buildings and Grounds Committee will present the recommended project scope, Feb. 9: Community Forum, Feb. 12: Board/Community Q&A, and March 5: Finalization of scope of work/bond amount. Mr. Friedman stressed that the goal is to remain budget neutral  with no additional or new taxes.

Following the presentation, there was a discussion among the Board. Maura Gedid asked about the garage/facilities storage proposal and where the basketball court will be moved. Ms. Gedid also asked about LED replacement. David Graeber asked that the next presentation include photos of existing spaces that will be renovated in addition to the sketches. Deborah Hargraves  expressed her appreciation for the forward looking aspect regarding modernizing spaces. Michael Hanna asked about air conditioning.
