The District now utilizes NYSSBA Policy Service and is in the process of conducting a complete Policy Manual Review.  In addition, the Board must rescind the previously adopted policies. Please note that in some cases, NYSSBA's policies encompass one or more of the District's previous policies.

Please note that Policy #1500-Public Use of School Facilities and #1530 are not included in the series as they have been previously adopted by the Board.
