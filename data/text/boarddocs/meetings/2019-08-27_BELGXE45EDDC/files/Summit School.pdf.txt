2. SCOPE OF SERVICES: The Provider shall provide competent instruction foreach
enrolled student in accordance with the student's IEP, as approved by the School

maintain approval by the Commissioner of Education and the New York State

Provider shall also comply with all notice, reporting and recordkeeping requirements

3. PAYMENT:

a. In full consideration for the educational services to be rendered by Provider to
the School District for the period of this Agreement, the School District shall
pay to Provider for each student enrolled by the School District the tuition
rate set by the New York State Education Department. If the tuition rate for
the school year governed by this Agreement is not available at the beginning
of the school year, the School District shall pay the rate applicable to the
previous school year until a current rate is set, at which time the parties shall
adjust future tuition payments in accordance with the current rate. Provider
shall reimburse the School District for any overpayments and the School
District shall reimburse the Provider for underpayments made prior to
and in consequence of the current rate set for the school year governed by this
Agreement.

b. Ifthe New York State Education Department issues a Reconciliation Rate for
the school year governed by this Agreement, full consideration for the
educational services rendered by Provider to the School District shall be
governed by the Reconciliation Rate, and the School District shall pay tuition
in accordance with that Reconciliation Rate. Provider shall reimburse the
Schoo} District for any overpayments and the School District shall reimburse
the Provider for underpayments made prior to and in consequence of the
Reconciliation Rate set for the school year governed by this Agreement.

-¢. Ifa student's enrollment is terminated prior to conclusion of the school year
governed by this Agreement, the School District shall only be obligated to pay
the Provider for the period of the student's enrollment, and if enrollment is
terminated during any month in which the student was in attendance for less
than a full month, tuition shall be paid on a per diem basis for each day ofthe
month in which the student was in attendance.
