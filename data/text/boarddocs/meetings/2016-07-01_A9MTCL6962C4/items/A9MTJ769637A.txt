Meeting: Jul 01, 2016 - 2016-17 Reorganization Meeting/Special Meeting
Date: 2016-07-01
Category: 13. Community Comments and/or Questions
Subject: 13.1 Procedures for Community Comments and/or Questions regarding the School District
Type: Information

Members of the Community will be invited to offer comments or raise questions on any topic relating to the school district. Community members are reminded to direct comments or questions to the President of the Board and to limit comments or questions to approximately three (3) minutes.
