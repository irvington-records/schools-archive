Meeting: Dec 07, 2021 - Regular Meeting
Date: 2021-12-07
Category: 12. Community Comments and/or Questions
Subject: 12.1 Procedures for Community Comments and/or Questions regarding the School District
Type: Information

The Board of Education will permit up to 30 minutes for public comment. Those wishing to comment or pose a question to the Board of Education are invited to place their name and address in the Zoom Q&A. The Board of Education President will invite the respective members of the community to speak.  Each speaker will have up to three minutes to speak.  The Board of Education requests that the Q&A function not be used for other comments, communications or questions.
