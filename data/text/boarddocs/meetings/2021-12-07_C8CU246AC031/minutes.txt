5. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Information: 5.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison spoke about possible new COVD testing measures including a "test to stay"  and "testing out of quarantine" models.  The District is waiting for further guidance from the county. He also spoke about the upcoming vaccination clinic that will be held at Dows Lane.  Dr. Duffy recently presented at the Supporting the Whole Child Conference at Putnam Northern Westchester BOCES. Dr. Harrison and Dr. Kantor gave a presentation at the National Blue Ribbon School of Excellence Conference about Creating a Positive, Productive School Culture. Dr. Duffy spoke about work that is being done as part of professional learning in the book clubs at Main Street and Dows Lane. Teachers and administrators are reading Cultivating Genius written by Gholdy Muhammad.  The book provides a framework of equity with a cultural and historical perspective.
