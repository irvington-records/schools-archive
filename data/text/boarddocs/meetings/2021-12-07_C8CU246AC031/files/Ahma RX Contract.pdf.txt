                        IRVINGTON UFSD COVID-19 VACCINATION SITE
                           LICENSE AND INDEMNITY AGREEMENT


IT IS HEREBY AGREED, as of this 23rd day of November, 2021 (the "Effective Date"), between the
Irvington Union Free School District, having an address at 6 Dows Lane, Irvington, New York 10533 (the
“Licensor” or “School District”), and the Ahma RX (the “Licensee or Service Provider”) (the Licensor and
the Licensee are sometimes collectively referred to herein as the “Parties” and each, individually, as a
“Party”), having its principal place of business for the purposes of this Agreement at 35 Valley Avenue,
Elmsford, New York 10523, in consideration of the mutual promises contained herein and subject to the terms
and conditions set forth in this temporary, revocable license and indemnity agreement (the “Agreement”),
that:
   1. The Licensee, and its officers, employees, agents and contractors (“Licensee Parties”), are hereby
      authorized to establish a COVID-19 Vaccination Point of Dispensing (“POD”) in certain facilities of
      the Licensor located at the:
       Irvington Union Free School District, 40 North Broadway, Irvington, NY 10533 and
       As more particularly described in Exhibit A hereto (the “Premises”) to provide COVID-19
       vaccinations to students aged five (5) to eleven (11) years of age who are currently enrolled in the
       Irvington Union Free School District to receive the COVID-19 Vaccination (the “Authorized
       Purpose”).
   2. The Licensee is hereby granted a non-exclusive, temporary, revocable license (the “License”) to
      access and enter the Premises in order to engage in the Authorized Purpose on:

       Irvington UFSD-Dows Lane Elementary School
       December 8, 2021 and December 29, 2021
       Licensor agrees to allow Licensee to use the spaces in the Premises more particularly described in
       Exhibit A (the “Licensed Area”). The School District acknowledges and agrees that the Licensee
       will need reasonable access to the Licensed Area for pre-clinic set up 2 hours before the start of each
       of the above-mentioned dates, and for post clinic break down within 3 hours of the last vaccination
       on each of the above-mentioned dates. The Licensee will be permitted to remain on site for the
       purpose of vaccination until the last walk-in is vaccinated The number of vaccination stations and
       numbers of vials administered shall be in the sole discretion of the Licensee. The Licensee’s use of
       the Licensed Area for the Authorized Purpose shall be exclusive and no other uses shall be permitted
       in the Licensed Area while the Authorized Purposes is being performed. The parties on mutual
       consent may change the dates listed above.

   3. Licensee warrants that it, and its employees or contractors that perform the Authorized Purpose, have
      all relevant legal and medical qualifications to provide these services, including compliance with all
      applicable local, state, and federal law and any required licenses or permits.

   4. Licensee’s Covenants. The Licensee covenants that: (a) the Licensee and its employees and agents
      will take good care of the Licensed Area of the Premises and maintain same in a safe condition, free
      of all hazards that may have been created by the Licensee, (b) the Licensor shall have the right (but
      not the obligation) to enter the Licensed Area at any time for inspection purposes, (c) the Licensee
      shall have all medical waste removed in a manner consistent with applicable law, rules and
      regulations, (d) the Licensee shall perform the Authorized Purpose in a safe manner, (e) the Licensee
      will not, in connection with the Authorized Purpose in the Licensed Area, discriminate or permit
      discrimination against any person(s) or organization(s) because of race, sex, color, age, religion,
                                                                                                            1

   national origin, disability, marital status, military service, sexual orientation, gender identity or for
   any other reason prohibited by law, (f) the Licensee and its agents shall adhere to protocols
   recommended by the NYSDOH and the US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention to practice
   social distancing, use appropriate personal protective equipment (PPE), and maintain a safe
   environment for their staff at the site and to comply with guidance and rules issued by the State of
   New York and any executive orders issued by the Governor thereof related to COVID-19, when
   performing the Authorized Purpose.

5. Insurance.

   a. Notwithstanding any terms, conditions or provisions, in any other writing between the parties,
      LICENSEE hereby agrees to effectuate the naming of SCHOOL DISTRICT as an additional
      insured on LICENSEE’s commercial general liability and excess liability insurance policies. If
      the policy is written on a claims-made basis, the retroactive date must precede the date of the
      contract.

   b. The policy naming SCHOOL DISTRICT as an additional insured shall:

             i.     be an insurance policy from an A.M. Best rated "Secure" insurer,        licensed     in
              New York State.

         ii.         state that LICENSEE's coverage shall be primary and non-contributory coverage for
                     SCHOOL DISTRICT, its Board, employees and volunteers.

   c. SCHOOL DISTRICT shall be listed as an additional insured by using endorsement CG 2026 or
      equivalent. The decision to accept an alternative endorsement rests solely with SCHOOL
      DISTRICT. A completed copy of the endorsement must be attached to the certificate of insurance.

   d. The certificate of insurance must describe the specific services provided by SERVICE
      PROVIDER that are covered by the liability policies.

   e. At SCHOOL DISTRICT's request, LICENSEE shall provide a copy of the declaration page of the
      liability and umbrella policies with a list of endorsements and forms. If so requested, LICENSEE
      will provide a copy of the policy endorsements and forms.

   f. LICENSEE agrees to indemnify SCHOOL DISTRICT for any applicable deductibles and self-
      insured retentions.

   g. Required Insurance:

             i.      Commercial General Liability Insurance:
                     $1,000,000 per occurrence/ $2,000,000 aggregate with coverage for sexual
                     misconduct.

       ii.           Workers' Compensation and N.Y.S. Disability:
                     Statutory Workers' Compensation, Employers' Liability and N.Y.S. Disability
                     Benefits Insurance for all employees. Proof of coverage must be on the approved
                     specific form, as required by the New York State Workers’ Compensation Board.
                     ACORD certificates are not acceptable.

      object to the contents of the certificate or the absence of same shall not be deemed a waiver of
      any and all rights held by SCHOOL DISTRICT.

   i.   SCHOOL DISTRICT is a member/owner of the NY Schools Insurance Reciprocal (NYSIR).
        LICENSEE further acknowledges that the procurement of such insurance as required herein is
        intended to benefit not only SCHOOL DISTRICT but also NYSIR, as SCHOOL DISTRICT's
        insurer.

6. Licensor’s Covenants: The Licensor covenants that (a) it is duly authorized to allow the Licensee
   Parties to utilize the Premises and Licensed Area for the Authorized Purpose; and (b) it can, to the
   extent applicable, meet all of the requirements of the ADA and other disability laws or may use
   practices and procedures in order to accommodate individuals with disabilities as allowed under the
   ADA and analogous laws.

7. Fees and Costs:
   The School District agrees that no fee shall be charged the Licensee for use of the Licensed Area or
   otherwise under this Agreement.

   Neither Party will look to the other Party to cover any of the direct or indirect costs for services
   provided pursuant to this Agreement unless otherwise mutually agreed to by the Parties. If so agreed,
   the terms and conditions of any such other mutual agreement shall supersede this Agreement if any
   inconsistencies between the agreements exist.

8. Responsibilities Of The District: The School District agrees to the following responsibility and
   provisions at no charge to the Licensee:

   A.     The School District agrees to provide access to the Wi-Fi service and, if and to the extent
   possible, access to and use of hard-wired telephones and/or telephone and computer connections.

    B.      The School District agrees to provide the Licensee with the tables, chairs and signage for
    the clinics and agrees the Licensee may use the other furniture and furnishings located in the
    Licensed Area.

    C.      The School District agrees to provide the following services

                          Hot and cold potable water from the local supply sufficient for drinking,

                          Heat, ventilation and air conditioning to the premises as it currently exists

                          Trash collection with the exception of medical waste, which the Licensee
                           shall be responsible for.

9. Term and Termination: The term of this License will continue until June 30, 2022, unless sooner
   terminated pursuant to the following termination provisions: This Agreement may be terminated (i)
   upon mutual written consent of the Parties or, (a) in the case of a termination for convenience by
   Licensee, upon not less than 72 hours prior written notice, and (b) in the case of a termination for
   convenience by Licensor, upon not less than 72 hours prior written notice the exception that, if the
   clinics for the first COVID-19 Vaccine doses have been completed, the District shall not have the
   right to terminate for convenience under this provision until after the clinics for the second
   COVID-19 Vaccine doses have been completed. or;

    (ii) by the non-breaching Party, in the event of a material breach of this Agreement by the other Party,
    where the non-breaching Party gives written notice to the breaching Party, specifying in reasonable
    detail the material breach, and such breaching Party fails to cure the material breach within seven (7)
    days of receipt of the requisite notice; or

    (iii) the Licensee shall have the right to immediately suspend or terminate this License if necessary
    for public health and safety purposes.

10. Indemnification. The Licensee agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Licensor, its officers,
    employees, agents, and guests from any and all claims, demands, judgments, losses, costs, expenses
    and other liabilities arising out of or incident to the acts or omissions of Licensee and other Licensee
    Parties to the extent attributable to the negligence, gross negligence or intentional misconduct of its
    officers, employees, volunteers, and contractors, when acting within the course and scope of their
    employment in performing its services identified and conducted during the term of this Agreement,
    except to the extent arising from the acts or omissions of the Licensor, its agents, servants, employees
    and guests.

11. Notices. All notices required pursuant to the terms of this Agreement shall be in writing, and given to
    the Licensee and the Licensor at the addresses listed below for each Party and delivered (i) personally,
    (ii) by overnight carrier that provides a receipt evidencing delivery, (iii) by certified or registered mail,
    return receipt requested.
