Irvington Union Free School District
Monthly Financial Highlights

October 2021
Cash Balance

© Our current cash position is $31.68 million, increased by the District’s first tax payment. The remaining balances were offset by the normal
monthly check warrants, payrolls, and bond payment expenditures.

© The first tax payment of $29,416,160 was remitted to the District from the Town of Greenburgh. Tax payments are received between the
months of October through April as remitted by the Town. The two largest tax payments are received in October and February.

© State and Federal Aid cash received included final payments for 2020-21 IDEA and ESSA grants totaling $288,690 and the final 2020-21
BOCES aid payment of $317,416.

¢ Interest rates still remain low. As of October 2021, Chase was at 0.05% while NYLAF was at 0.005%. We will continue to monitor rates for

ways to maximize interest earnings, such as treasuries or CD’s. The District currently has one CD in the reserve fund with a maturity date of
03/04/22 in the amount of $245,000 at an interest rate of 0.15%.

Revenue:
* General Fund Revenue is currently at $62,905,809 or 94.5% of the budgeted amount. Included in this total is the full amount of the tax levy
revenue, which is recorded and recognized in full as per accounting standards. When the cash is received, it is booked against the receivable.
© We also recorded State Aid revenue of $1,992,961 through October. Of note, $1,726,824 of this amount was actually a recorded entry against
our TRS payment due for 2020-21. The State applies our aid payment directly against the amount due to TRS. No cash is received by the
District; however, this transaction is recognized as basic state aid revenue.

Expenditures:
¢ Actual General Fund Expenses totaled approximately $5.93 million for October. This brings the total year to date expenditures to
$14,986,019, which was approximately $770,000 greater than last year at this time.

¢ Included this month were debt payments of $145,000 principal and $11,500 interest payments for the 2008 Tax Cert Serial Bond and $34,321
payment for the 2008 Capital Serial Bond.
