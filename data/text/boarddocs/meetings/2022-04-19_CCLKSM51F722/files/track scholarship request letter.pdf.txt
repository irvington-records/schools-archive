Dear Members,

| am writing this letter as the Founder (1973) and Head Coach of The Westchester
Track Club. This multi-tiered running organization has had training programs and members on
the scholastic, collegiate, recreational, and Olympic Development level. The club has produced
3 Olympians over the years, and in 1984 | served as the Head Coach of the Somalian Olympic
Team at Los Angeles.

| lived in Irvington for many years, and was a good friend of Peter Oley, who of course the track
facility is named after. Also, Scott Mosenthal was a long time member of our club.
i continue to have friends in the area, including Irvington Head Track Coach Chris Barry.

The Westchester Track Club would like to award a $500 scolorship to a graduating senior
chosen by the Irvington High School track & field staff. This award would be for an individual
who has made a positive impact on the team and to his/her teammates, and not necessarily
for athletic achievement.
