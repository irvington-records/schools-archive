Action (Consent): 10.4 Establishment of the Westchester Track Club Scholarship Award

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District administers the Irvington Union Free School District; and WHEREAS, the Westchester Track Club, hereinafter referred to as the "Donor" desires to make a gift to the Board of Education in order to create a scholarship fund, to be known as the "The Westchester Track Club Scholarship"; and WHEREAS Education Law 1709(12-a) and 2503 (1) authorizes the Board of Education: to take and hold in trust for the purpose of awarding scholarships in [the] schools any real estate transferred to it by gift, grant, bequest or devise, or any gift, legacy or annuity, of whatever kind, given or bequeathed to [the] board and apply the same, or the interest and proceeds thereof, according to the instructions of the donor or testator. NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District hereby creates the above-named Scholarship Fund, as set forth below, based upon the understanding between the parties: The Westchester Track Club Scholarship 1. The Donor, shall deliver to the Board, an initial amount of $500 for deposit in the Special Revenue Scholarship account in the bank designated by the Board of Education. 2. The Scholarship Fund may be supplemented at any time by any donor(s) wishing to gift, grant, bequest or devise cash, cash equivalent or other property acceptable to the District or through any gift, legacy or annuity, of whatever kind, given, or bequeathed to the District for such purpose. 3. The scholarship shall be named the Westchester Track Club Scholarship. 4. The scholarship will be awarded in the amount of $500 to one graduating Irvington High School student chosen by the Irvington High School Track & Field staff. The student should be an individual who has made a positive impact on the team and to his/her teammates. 5. The funds allocated to said scholarship shall come from donations by any party at anytime which may be used to increase the scholarship or held for future recipients. 6. Details of the organization and administration of the Scholarship Fund will be left to the discretion of the Board of Education.

Motion by Erin Bernstein, second by Beth Propper.
