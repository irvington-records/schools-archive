Meeting: Jun 02, 2020 - Public Hearing/Regular Meeting
Date: 2020-06-02
Category: 13. Business Office Matters
Subject: 13.3 Tax Anticipation Note
Type: Action (Consent)

This resolution would allow the District to borrow to fund operations should the District have insufficient funds until tax revenues are received typically in October. A tax anticipation note is a short term borrowing that is paid back once tax revenues are received.
