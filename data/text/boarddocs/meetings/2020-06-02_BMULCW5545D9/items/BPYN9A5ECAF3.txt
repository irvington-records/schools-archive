Consistent with New York State Law, the Board of Education must conduct a Budget Hearing at least 7 days, but not more than 14 days, prior ot the scheduled budget vote on June 9, 2020. Members of the community will have an opportunity to comment on the proposed 2020-21 budget.

Community members can view the hearing by following the link noted in the header to this agenda. During the meeting, the Board of Education will pilot the use of the comment function in Zoom.  Those watching the hearing who wish to comment may do so using the chat function.  Also, should you have questions for the Board of Education or the Administration, you are asked to send them via email to: Board@IrvingtonSchools.org. The Board of Education and Dr. Harrison will receive these emails. Responses to questions will be posted on the website at a future date.
