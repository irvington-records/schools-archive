Meeting: Jun 02, 2020 - Public Hearing/Regular Meeting

Category: 14. Community Comments and/or Questions
Subject: 14.1 Procedures for Community Comments and/or Questions regarding the School District
Type: Information

During the meeting, the Board of Education will pilot the use of the comment function in Zoom.  Those watching the hearing who wish to comment may do so using the chat function during the appropriate time on the agenda.  Also, should you have questions for the Board of Education or the Administration, you are asked to send them via email to: Board@IrvingtonSchools.org. The Board of Education and Dr. Harrison will receive these emails. Responses to questions will be posted on the website at a future date.
