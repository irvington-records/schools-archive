2020-21 Three Phases of Distance Learning
●   Phase 1: Maintaining
     ○   During the initial closure, teachers placed focus on ensuring that the current levels of learning
         were maintained. This time was also spent building capacity in the staff to design and deliver
         instruction in new concepts and skills in a distant learning environment.

2020-21 Three Phases of Distance Learning
●   Phase 1: Maintaining
●   Phase 2: New Learning with a Flexible Schedule
     ○   Teachers, students and families shifted to engaging in new learning in a remote environment.
         This included both online google meets, engagement with instructional software and posting of
         independent assignments.

2020-21 Three Phases of Distance Learning
●   Phase 1: Maintaining
●   Phase 2: New learning with a Flexible Schedule
●   Phase 3: New Learning with Scheduled classes
     ○   To ensure that all students had online access to teachers, each building developed a schedule
         that would include live google meets, live extra-help periods as well continuing work with
         instructional technology and posted class assignments.

Synchronous and Asynchronous Learning
Synchronous: Teacher and students are interacting at the same time.

Asynchronous: Teacher and students are interacting at different times.

Dear IHS Families,

Give yourself a virtual high five!

You have made it through the first 3 weeks of “virtual learning"! As we head
into our fourth week of distance learning, we wanted to reach out, to not only
wish you and your family well, but to also remind you that although there is no
formal break next week, to take time for yourself! (Need some suggestions of

hi! We miss seeing you all and look forward to getting back to school as soon as
we can!

What We’ve Learned So Far
●   Distance Learning has been challenging for everyone.
●   Students, staff and families all need multiple forms of support.
●   Face-to-face connections with a teacher are important.
●   Some students struggle to learn in this environment while others thrive.
●   There is no “just right” amount of ________________.
●   Lessons need to be purposely adapted for distance learning.
●   Assessing in this environment presents unique challenges.
●   The impact of inequities in resources is magnified.
●   Learning how to mute and unmute is critical!

Wrapping up 2019-20
●   Seek feedback from all stakeholders through
    school and community surveys
●   Report student progress to parents
●   Where needed, adjust curriculum for the fall by
    identifying gaps in learning
●   Prepare pre-assessments to be used in
    September
●   Celebrate the many accomplishments of our
    students and our staff!

    ○   Reflect on what has been learned from Distance Learning Phases 1-3
    ○   Develop recommendations for Distance Learning 2.0
●   Social Emotional Learning Sub-Committee
    ○   Plan for the needs of families, students and staff on re-opening
    ○   Plan for ongoing support to meet anticipated challenges
●   Operations Sub-Committee
    ○   Make recommendations about the logistics of re-opening safely and within
        CDC, DOH and SED guidelines
