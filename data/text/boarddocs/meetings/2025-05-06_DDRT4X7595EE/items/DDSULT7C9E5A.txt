Meeting: May 06, 2025 - Public Hearing/Regular Meeting
Date: 2025-05-06
Category: 3. Public Hearing
Subject: 3.1 Public Hearing for the 2025-26 Budget, to open at 7:15 p.m.
Type: Procedural

Consistent with New York State Law, the Board of Education must conduct a Budget Hearing at least 7 days, but not more than 14 days, prior to the scheduled budget vote on May 20, 2025. Members of the community will have an opportunity to comment on the proposed 2025-26 budget.
