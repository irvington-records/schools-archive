                              Background
• To assess the satisfaction with the food service in the Irvington UFSD schools
  the Superintendent and the Wellness Committee developed a study to assess
  the opinions of:
   – Students
   – Parents
   – Teachers & Staff
• The study focused on Students in grades 4 thru 12
• Parents and Staff across all grade levels were invited to participate
• The survey was programmed and deployed with Survey Monkey research tools
• There was a high participation among each target group:
   – 859 Students began the study and 787 completed it – 57% participation
   – 369 Parents began the study and 295 completed it – 37% participation
   – 170 Teachers & Staff began the study, 136 completed it – 52% participation
• The surveys were collected between Wednesday, April 19 and Monday, May 2
•   All the data is available in the IUFSD Survey Monkey account for further analysis
                                                                                        2

                                Key Findings
•   Overall satisfaction with the food service is fairly low
•   It is lowest for Students in High School, and for Parents of students in 4th & 5th grades
•   Satisfaction is highest with the friendliness & knowledge of the FS staff, the
    atmosphere in the school cafeterias, and the speed & efficiency of the food service
•   Satisfaction is lowest for the overall quality of food, the ”healthy” food options
    available, and the range of food options offered
•   Students in the 4th & 5th grades rate the speed & efficiency of the service lowest
•   High School Students and the Staff give lower ratings to the overall quality and the
    range of food options offered
•   Of the foods served the made-to-order deli sandwiches get the highest quality ratings
•   Foods rated “Fair” or “Poor” in quality by 60% of Students are – fresh vegetables, pre-
    made sandwiches, pizza, and hot meals
•   The new features that are most likely to encourage students to eat at the school more
    often are “free samples & tastings of new foods” and “trials of new meals”
•   Many Parents and Students are willing to pay 20% more to get animal foods that don’t
    contain antibiotics or hormones. They are much less willing to pay extra for locally
    grown foods.                                                                         3
