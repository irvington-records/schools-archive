		Action : 6.2 Approval of Settlement Agreement

		Resolution: WHEREAS, an action was commenced and is currently pending in the Supreme Court, County of Westchester, captioned Irvington Union Free School District vs. Dearman Park Associates, Westwood Development Associates, et al., Index No. 23882/06. BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education, subject to the execution by all defendants and the satisfaction of any conditions precedent contained therein, hereby approves settlement of the action in accordance with the terms and conditions of a settlement agreement and further authorizes the President of the Board of Education to execute the subject settlement agreement, together with any non-material changes to the Exhibits of the Settlement Agreement as the President of the Board of Education deems reasonable.

		Mr. Grados reported that he had received a phone call from one of the defendants stating that there were further comments to the exhibits in the Settlement Agreement, attorney for the Defendants, Philip Maley, with respect to the property survey documents. Mr. Grados recommended that the Board vote on the resolution and recommended that the Board modify the resolution to provide him with the authority to execute the agreement as long as any changes that might be made to the exhibits are non-substantive in nature and only after all the defendants have signed the agreement as well. The Board agreed to vote on the amended resolution.
