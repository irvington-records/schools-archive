Category: 5. Business of the Board
Subject: 5.1 Emergency Repair Authorization
Type: Action (Consent)

The Meszaros turf field was damaged in the severe flooding that occurred on July 14, 2025.  These repairs will allow the fall sports season to begin on time.  The district's insurance will cover a portion of the repair cost, in totality with other damage sustained on that day.
