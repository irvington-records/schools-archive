                    MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT FOR EDUCATION
This Master Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made by and between The Devereux
Foundation d/b/a Devereux Connecticut (“Devereux”), a Pennsylvania-based nonprofit
corporation with its principal place of business at 444 Devereux Drive, PA 19085 and , Irvington
UFSD (the “School District”) with its principal place of business at 40 N Broadway, Irvington,
NY 10533.
WHEREAS, the School District has determined that Devereux is able to meet the educational
needs of individuals for which the School District is obligated to provide services and/or funding;
and
WHEREAS, the School District desires to place students in a Devereux school; and
WHEREAS, Devereux is able to provide services for the Regular School Year (9/1/25-6/30/26)
and/or Extended School Year (“ESY”) (7/31/25-8/31/25).
NOW, THEREFORE, for and in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained
in this Agreement, and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which
is hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:
1. Term and Termination.
   a. This Agreement shall begin on 7/31/25 and will terminate on 6/30/26.
   b. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon fifteen (15) days’ written notice during
      the time period of July 1-August 31 and thirty (30) days’ written notice to the other party
      during the time period of September 1-June 30. In the event of termination, by either party,
      the School District shall pay for, and Devereux shall provide, all contracted services up to
      the date of termination. The School District is solely responsible for the removal of all
      students as of the termination date.
   c. The School District shall pay Devereux for all services rendered even when services are
      rendered after the Termination date.
   d. If a student is discharged, for any reason, by any party, this Agreement shall automatically
      terminate with respect to the discharged student only as of the student’s last day of
      placement in a Devereux school. In the event of discharge pursuant to this paragraph, the
      Agreement will remain in full force and effect with regard to the School District and any
      other students placed pursuant to this Agreement.
2. Services and Payment.
   a. Devereux will provide Education Day services and Related Services as defined in this
      Agreement pursuant to the terms and conditions of Attachment A – Education and Related
      Services (“Attachment A”), attached to and incorporated into this Agreement.
   b. Devereux’s provision of services will:
          i. utilize certified teachers and/or staff who have the credentials minimally necessary
             to deliver the services required to implement the student’s Individual Education Plan
             (“IEP”); pursuant to applicable laws in the state in which the services are rendered;

                                                                                        Page 1 of 6

        ii. be implemented in full compliance with the student’s IEP and in conformity with all
            applicable provisions of State and Federal law;
        iii. be provided in instructional space that fully complies with all requirements of the
             applicable laws in the state in which services are rendered and all applicable County
             and minimal fire and building codes;
        iv. include progress monitoring in the manner required by the IEP of the student; and
        v. conform to the curriculum, assessment, and instructional team requirements of the
           applicable laws of the state in which services are rendered.
   c. In consideration for Devereux’s provision of services the School District agrees to pay the
      rates contained outlined in Attachment A.
   d. For each new student placed, or for any existing student for which the School District
      desires to change education service delivery, Devereux shall send to the School District, a
      “Student Placement Request Form.” The Student Placement Request Form shall specify
      each student’s placement, any Related Services (as that term is defined on Attachment A)
      the student will receive, and the effective date of the start of or change to the services. The
      Student Placement Request Form is a Microsoft Word form that will be sent electronically
      to one or more School District personnel with whom Devereux collaborates on student
      placement. All Student Placement Request Forms shall constitute attachments to this
      Agreement notwithstanding Section 13 of this Agreement and are hereby incorporated
      herein.
   e. For any student funded by the Pennsylvania Department of Education (“PDE”) for whom
      Devereux begins receiving funding from PDE (“4010 Funding”), the School District will
      be responsible for payment for any portion of the applicable fees pursuant to Attachment
      A that is not funded by PDE, including without limitation, for Education Day and Related
      Services. Devereux shall promptly notify the School District if a 4010 Funding placement
      becomes available for a student receiving services under this Agreement.
   f. Unless otherwise set forth herein Devereux shall not be responsible for the cost of any
      student’s medical, dental or pharmacy expenses, medical insurance premiums, clothing
      expenses, transportation to and from Devereux, and other expenses generally considered
      as personal to an individual.
3. Education Day.
   a. Extended School Year: The term Extended School Year (“ESY”) means the portion of the
      school year taking place primarily in the summer months pursuant to the published
      Devereux ESY Calendar, available upon request.
   b. Regular School Year: The term Regular School Year (“RSY”) means the portion of the
      school year taking place primarily in the fall, winter and spring months pursuant to the
      published Devereux RSY Calendar for the calendar year covered by this Agreement.
   c. Education Day: The term Education Day means a day during which an enrolled students
      receives education for at least one-half of the scheduled school day and includes all
      excused/authorized absences and unauthorized absences notwithstanding whether PDE

       remits payment for those days.
4. Visitation. The School District or its agents or employees, and any student’s parents and/or
   guardians shall have the right to visit and observe Devereux’s program and facilities during
   regular school hours pursuant to the applicable Devereux school handbook. Visitation must be
   scheduled prior to the visit.
5. Reports to School District. Upon request Devereux shall provide evidence of approval of its
   programs, together with a description of the programs and the types of students served,
   including instructions and special services to be provided to students. Devereux shall provide
   such additional information reports, as the School District may reasonably require to be kept
   informed of a student’s progress, including quarterly reports on forms provided by Agency and
   appropriate updated psychological, social and educational evaluations on or before June 1st of
   each year. Devereux shall promptly notify the School District in the event that Devereux's
   license or certificate to operate is revoked or suspended.
6. Student Records. Devereux will specifically maintain attendance records and progress
   reporting for each enrolled student. Upon withdrawal of a student from Devereux, all education
   records concerning the student may be returned to the School District upon request, provided,
   however, that Devereux may retain copies of such records consistent with its obligations under
   state and federal laws.
7. Transfers and Discharges.
   a. The School District shall fund all services provided to any student placed by the School
      District until the student is unenrolled, removed from the Devereux School, and no longer
      receiving Devereux Services.
   b. If payment responsibility for a student’s education services changes to an alternate agency
      while the student is enrolled at a Devereux school (for example, if a student’s family
      relocates to new district different from the School District that is a party to this Agreement),
      the School District is responsible to pay for all services rendered until such time that either
      (i) the alternate agency renders payment to Devereux; or (ii) the student is removed from
      the Devereux school.
   c. If, in Devereux’s sole professional discretion, continued placement in a Devereux school
      is no longer appropriate for a student, Devereux will issue a thirty (30) day discharge notice
      to the School District. The School District is solely responsible for identifying and securing
      a new placement for any student that is the subject of a discharge notice. Devereux will
      provide all reasonable support to the School District to identify an alternate placement. A
      thirty (30) day discharge notice issued by Devereux hereby constitutes a notice to partially
      terminate this Agreement with respect to the student being discharged, pursuant to Section
      1(b) herein. If a student continues to attend a Devereux school after the thirty (30) day
      discharge notice period ends, the School District shall reimburse Devereux the then
      prevailing Devereux standard rates for services rendered to the student. Devereux standard
      rates are available upon request.
8. Assignment by Devereux. Devereux may assign this agreement to an affiliated or controlled
   entity upon thirty (30) days written notice to the School District.

   but not limited to, information subject to HIPAA, FERPA and other related student

10. Insurance. In accordance with its usual practices, Devereux agrees to maintain worker's
    compensation, professional liability, comprehensive general liability and automobile liability
    insurance or coverage. Devereux will provide proof of coverage to the School District upon
    request. The School District shall provide proof insurance to Devereux upon request.
11. Independent Contractor Status. The relationship of the parties shall at all times be that of
    independent contractors and not as employer-employee.
12. Indemnification. Each party hereby agrees to indemnify, defend and hold the other party
    harmless from and against any and all claims, suits, actions, damages, judgments, liabilities,
    fines, penalties and expenses, including reasonable attorney’s fees and litigation costs, arising
    out of the indemnifying party’s alleged intentional or negligent acts or omissions. This
    provision shall survive any expiration, termination or non-renewal of this Agreement.
13. Entire Understanding. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the
    parties as to the matters contained herein and there are no terms, covenants, conditions,
    representations, warranties or agreements expressed or implied, oral or written of any nature
    whatsoever other than as herein contained.
14. Modification. No modification of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and
    signed by the parties hereto. No forbearance to enforce any provisions of this agreement, or
    waiver of any breach hereof, shall be deemed a waiver of any other provision of right hereunder
    or any subsequent breach of default.
15. No Waiver. The failure of any party hereto to exercise any right, power or remedy provided
    under this Agreement or otherwise available in respect hereof at law or in equity, or to insist
    upon compliance by any other party hereto with its obligations hereunder, and any custom or
    practice of the parties at variance with the terms hereof, shall not constitute a waiver by such
    party of its right to exercise any such or other right, power or remedy or to demand such
    compliance.
16. Severability and Situs.
   a. Any provision of this Agreement deemed void or unenforceable by law, shall be stricken,
      and all remaining provisions shall continue to be valid and binding. The Parties agree that
      in the event of a void or unenforceable provision, that such provision shall be replaced with
      enforceable language that expresses the original intention of the stricken provision.
   b. All disputes arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be governed exclusively by
      the laws of the Connecticut, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. All disputes
      arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be resolved exclusively in the appropriate
      court venued in Litchfield County, Connecticut.
17. Authorization. The undersigned individuals certify and represent that all necessary approvals
    or authorizations have been obtained from their respective organizations and that they are
    authorized to sign this Agreement on behalf of their organization.

Reimbursement:
Irvington UFSD will reimburse Devereux for the tuition and maintenance during the 2-month
period (July 31 – August 31). Irvington UFSD will reimburse Devereux for the tuition for the
10-month period (September 1 – June 30). Rates for tuition are established for the fiscal year by
NY SED. Rates for maintenance are established by NY OCFS.
The parties acknowledge that the tuition and maintenance rates in New York may change and
that retroactive increases or reimbursement may be necessary to assure appropriate payment of
services
1:1 rate - $45.00 per hour


Additional Terms and Conditions:
1. School District is responsible to reimburse Devereux for a minimum payment of half of the
   available ESY Education Days.
2. Payment for services begins on the date of admission and includes all days enrolled in the
   program, including but not limited to excused/authorized absences, hospitalization, and the
   first five (5) days of unauthorized absences.
3. If, at any time, new Related Services are made a part of the Student’s Individualized Education
   Plan (“IEP”), School District hereby acknowledges and agrees that Devereux will be under a
   legal obligation to provide those additional services, and will seek compensation from the
   School District for those services consistent with the rates stated above, with or without notice
   to the School District, or if the Related Service is not listed above, then consistent with
   Devereux’s then prevailing standard rates for the type of service added.
4. Payment is due within thirty (30) days from the date of the invoice sent to Agency. Late
   payments are subject to a finance charge equal to one percent (1%) per month on the unpaid
   balance, or the highest allowed by law, whichever is lower.
