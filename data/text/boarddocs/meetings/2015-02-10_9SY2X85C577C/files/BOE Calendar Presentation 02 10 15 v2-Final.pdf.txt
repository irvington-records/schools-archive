2015-16 District Calendar


    Irvington Union Free School District
             February 10, 2015

          Calendar Development Complexities
 Focus on maximizing learning
 Must accommodate SED requirements
  ◦ 180 days (inclusive of 4 Superintendent Conference Days)
  ◦ Testing calendar
 Align with contractual obligations
 Federal and religious holidays
 Consistency with BOCES and neighboring districts = new
  challenges this year

        Calendar Development Complexities
 Late Labor Day: September 7
 SED Established Rating Day: June 23
 Spring Break: districts taking different approaches:
   BOCES, Hastings, Valhalla, Edgemont aligned with IUFSD
   Dobbs Ferry, Ardsley: 2 long weekends rather than 1 week
     3/25-3/29 & 4/22-26
   Concern for poor attendance on 2 weeks as families may remove
    students from school for a week’s vacation

                                Enhancements
 Addition to Superintendent Conference Days:
   2 early-release and 2 early-dismissal days for professional learning
   Many other districts provide additional professional time, for
    example:
      Ardsley: 1 hour weekly
      Dobbs Ferry: 1 hour weekly & 1 early release day every 6 weeks
      Hastings: 1 hour monthly & three early release days

 Introduces opportunity for additional access to faculty & staff:
   Professional learning
   Curriculum development
   Grade-level and cross-grade level planning
   Reduces need to remove teachers from class

                       Enhancements (cont…)
 Currently have limited access to faculty and staff:
   All levels: One faculty meeting per month
   Elementary & Middle: one grade-level meeting per week
   Secondary: One department-level meeting per month
   All levels: non-instructional time used for special meetings and
    committees (i.e. Dows 30-min Teaching Time Thursdays)
