     Consideration #1:

 Instruction Budget Considerations
     Consideration #2:
     Increase Mathematics AIS teaching staff by 1.0 FTE
     Dows Lane: .8 FTE      Main Street: .2 FTE
     Rationale:
  Early identification and intervention is critical in a program that strives to ensure that all
      students are able to meet state standards, as well as Irvington’s academic expectations.
      Our current staffing at the elementary level is currently 2.0 staff members. This is a stark
      contrast to ELA, where our interventions are provided by 4.5 literacy specialists. Our
      recent shift in practice to an RtI model requires a more fluid approach that allows for
      increased levels of service to students based on data analysis. In order to meet the
      demands of the curriculum and prepare students for intermediate grade level learning, as
      well as middle and high school curriculum, increased support is necessary.
 Expense:
  $96,000

 Instruction Budget Considerations
     Consideration #4:
     Expand Fundations to grade 2
     Rationale:
  The Fundations program is designed to create a solid foundation of language, writing
      and phonics for students in grade kindergarten through third grade. Our introduction of
      Fundations at the K & 1 levels this school year is already being seen as a success.
      Consistency across the grade levels, as well as higher levels of progress much earlier in
      the year have confirmed our belief that this was an essential step for Dows Lane.
      Implementation is most often seen at the K-2 level. To ensure a solid approach and
      consistency, while meeting the needs of all students throughout the primary grades, we
      believe that it is important to add 2nd grade to the sequence of learning which will
      result in higher levels of success in reading and writing for all students.
 Expense:
  $22,014
        Professional Development:         $10,000

 Special Education Budget Considerations
 Consideration #1:
 Increase special education teaching staff by 2.0 FTE
 Rationale:
  Across the district we continue to see an increase in the needs of our students
     and the importance of ensuring high levels of support at all grades. This request
     encompasses the increase at both middle and high school:
      The High School currently has the largest numbers of IEP and 504 designated students, but in relative
       numbers, they have the fewest number of special education teachers. This significantly impacts students
       receiving the services they require, the scheduling process, and the overall ability to support students
       during what is the most critical time as students prepare for college and career.
      The Middle School currently supports students in an ICT model in ELA, mathematics, and social studies.
       As a result of the limitation of current staffing, students are not provided with the support in the science
       classroom. This creates a challenge for students who are learning high level content without the necessary
       classroom support, as well as for the teachers who are expected to meet the needs of all learners without
       the expertise of the special education teacher as a support.

 Expense:
  $192,000
      This code:                                  $137,172

 PPS Budget Considerations
 Consideration #2:
 Restore full-time social worker position in lieu of part-time community aide
 Rationale:
 Once a full time position, the decision to reduce the expertise of this role resulted
     in limitations for daily practice. A full time social worker would be able to fulfill
     the responsibilities of the community aide, but would also be able to provide
     counseling services, as well as other professional consultation and support.
Expense:
 $-0- No New Costs
      Cost would be offset by the elimination of part-time community aide and the
       reduction of one teaching assistant in 2015-16.
