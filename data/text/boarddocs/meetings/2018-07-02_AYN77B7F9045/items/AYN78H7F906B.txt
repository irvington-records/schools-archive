Subject: 7.1 Approval of Annual Resolutions/Special Meeting Resolutions
Type: Action (Consent)

To make more efficient use of time, the Board of Education authorizes the use of a consent agenda as part of its reorganization meeting agenda. If any Board member believes that an item in the annual resolutions consent agenda requires discussion, that Board member may request to remove the item from the consent agenda.
