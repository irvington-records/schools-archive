Category: 4. Nominations, Election of Board Officers, Oath of Office for President and Vice President, Line of Authority Within the Board
Subject: 4.2 Nomination and Election of Vice-President of the Board of Education
Type: Action

The Board President will declare nominations open for the office of the Vice President of the Board of Education for the 2018-19 school year. Should  there be more than one nominee, the member with the majority of votes will thereby be appointed vice president. The Board President shall administer the Oath of Office to the Vice President.
