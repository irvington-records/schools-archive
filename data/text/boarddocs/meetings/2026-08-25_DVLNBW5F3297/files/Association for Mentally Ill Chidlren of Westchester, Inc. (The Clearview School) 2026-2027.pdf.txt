INSTRUCTIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT, made this 1* day of July, 2026

by and between the Board of Education,

(hereinafter “School District”), Irvington UFSD,
having its principal place of business at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, NY 10533

and

Association for Mentally Ill Children of Westchester, Inc. (The Clear View

School)
(hereinafter “Provider’”)
having its principal place of business at 480 Albany Post Road, Briarcliff Manor, N.Y. 10510.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, pursuant to Education Law section 4402 (b), the School District is authorized by
Jaw to contract with institutions within the State of New York for the instruction of children with
special needs in those situations where the School District is unable to provide for their
education in special classes in public schools;

WHEREAS, Provider is a non-public school registered with and approved by the New York
State Education Department to establish, conduct, operate and maintain an educational program
for children with special needs;

WHEREAS, School District desires that Provider provide instruction to School District
students enrolled in the program operated by Provider;

WHEREAS, Provider warrants that it has reviewed for each student to be enrolled by the
School District the individualized education program (“IEP”), as prescribed by the School
Disirict’s Committee on Special Education (CSE) or Committee on Preschool Special Education
(CPSE) and represents that it will provide the level of services identified in the IEP to meet the
needs of each child, and

WHEREAS, Provider is capable of and willing to provide the level of services identified in
the IEP,

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained in
this Agreement, the parties hereto mutually agree as follows:

1. TERM OF AGREEMENT: The Agreement shall be in effect for the period
July 9, 2026 — June 25, 2027

unless terminated earlier, as set forth herein;

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES: The Provider shall provide competent instruction
for each enrolled student in accordance with the student’s IEP, as approved by the
School District CSE/CPSE. At all times during the contract period, the Provider
shall maintain approval by the Commissioner of Education and the New York
State Education Department to operate the educational programs in which each
student is placed. All services by the Provider shall be conducted in strict
compliance with applicable Federal, State and local laws, statutes and regulations,
including, but not limited to, any law, statute and regulation governing
disciplinary procedures. Provider shall also comply with all notice, remitting and
recordkeeping requirements of the New York State Education Department and all
other governmental regulators. Upon reasonable notice from the District, any
employee and/or teacher of Provider who provides services shall participate,
either in person or remotely, in CSE meetings concerning students served by the
Provider.

3. Termination: The Receiving School may terminate this agreement for just
cause and in accordance with New Yorl State Education Law and/or
Commissioner’s Regulations. Before effecting such termination, the Receiving
School shall provide the School District with sufficient advance written notice, so
as to allow the School District to secure alternate placement of the student(s).

4, PAYMENT:

a. In full consideration for the educational services to be rendered by Provider to
the School District for the period of this Agreement, the School District shall
pay to Provider for each student enrolled by the School District the tuition rate
set by the New York State Education Department. If the tuition rate for the
school year governed by this Agreement is not available at the beginning of the
school year, the School District shall pay the rate applicable to the previous
school year until a current rate is set, at which time the parties shall adjust
future tuition payments in accordance with the current rate. Provider shall
reimburse the School District for any overpayments and the School District
shall reimburse the Provider for any underpayments made prior to and in.
consequence of the current rate set for the school year governed by this
Agreement,

b. Ifthe New York State Education Department issues a Reconciliation Rate for
the school year governed by this Agreement, full consideration for the
educational services rendered by Provider to the School District shall be
governed by the Reconciliation Rate, and the School District shall pay tuition in
accordance with that Reconciliation Rate. Provider shall reimburse the School
District for any overpayments and the School District shall reimburse the
Provider for underpayments made prior to and in consequence of the
Reconciliation Rate set for the school year governed by this Agreement.

ec. Ifastudent’s enrollment is terminated prior to conclusion of the school year
governed by this Agreement, the School District shall only be obligated to pay
the Provider for the period of the student’s enrollment, and if enrollment is
terminated during any month in which the student was in attendance for less

. MONTHLY INVOICE: Provider shall submit an invoice for services

rendered on a monthly basis, identifying each student for whom services were
rendered during the month invoiced, and payment to Provider shall be made
within thirty (30) days from receipt of invoice from Provider. School District

reserves the right to withhold payment pending the resolution of this dispute.
Failure to dispute any invoice shall not be deemed as an acceptance and does not
act as a waiver of School District’s rights or prevent School District from availing
itself of any remedy at law or in equity.

.» EMPLOYER-EMPLOYEE RELATIONSHIP: This Agreement does

not create an employer-employee relationship between the parties. It is the
parties’ intention that the Provider shall be an independent contractor and not the
School District’s employee for any and all purposes, including, but not limited to,
application of the Fair Labor Standards Act, Federal Insurance Contribution Act,
the Federal Unemployment Tax Act, the Internal Revenue Code, the New York
State Revenue and Taxation Law, the New York State Workers’ Compensation
Law and the New York State Unemployment Insurance Law.
INDEPENDENCE OF PROVIDER: Provider shall retain sole and
absolute discretion in the judgment of the manner and means of carrying out the
service activities and responsibilities hereunder. The Provider agrees to be a
separate and independent enterprise from the School District. This Agreement
shall not be construed as creating any joint employment relationship between the
Provider and the Schoo! District, and the School District shall not be liable for
any obligation incurred by the Provider, if any, including, but not limited to,
unpaid minimum wages and/or overtime premiums.

. INCOME TAX DESIGNATION AND INDEMNIFICATION:

School District shall not withhold from sums payable to Provider under this
Agreement any amounts for Federal, State or local taxes, including income taxes,
employment taxes (Social Security and Medicare) and unemployment taxes.
Providers agrees that any tax obligations of Provider arising out of payments
made or services rendered under this Agreement shall be Provider’s sole
responsibility, and Provider shall hold harmless and indemnify School District for
same.

FINGERPRINTING AND BACKGROUND CHECKS: Provider
wattants and agrees that prior to employment all staff members who shall have
contact with School District students shall have the appropriate fingerprinting and
background checks performed as required by Part 87 of the regulations of the

10. INDEMNIFICATION AND HOLD HARMLESS CLAUSE:

Provider agrees that it shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless School District,
its officers, directors, agents and employees for all loss, costs, damages and
expenses, including attorneys’ fees, judgments, fines and amounts paid in

proceeding arising from any act, error or omission, misstatement, misleading
statement, neglect or breach of duties by Provider or any of its officers, directors,
agents or employees taken or made with respect to this Agreement.

11.INSURANCE: Provider shall purchase and maintain comprehensive general
liability and professional liability insurance in an amount not less than $
1,000,000 per occurrence/ $ 3,000,000 aggregate; automobile liability insurance
(any auto) (combined single limit/ each occurrence) in an amount not less than $
1,000,000; New York State Workers’ Compensation, Employers’ Liability and
NYS Disability Benefits Insurance for all employees. Provider shall use
reasonable efforts to provide prior written notice of cancellation or material
reduction in coverage to the School District. Provider shall provide to the School
District, upon request, certificates of insurance and shall use reasonable efforts to
meet the School District’s standards with respect to such policies and any
renewals or replacements thereof.

12. TERMINATION: This Agreement may be terminated by either party upon
thirty (30) days written notice to the other party. In the event of such
termination, the parties shall adjust the accounts due and payable to Provider for
services rendered, Provider shall not incur any additional expenses upon receipt
of School District’s notice of termination. If at any time during the term of this
Agreement the Agency believes that its program is unable to meet the needs of a
District student or unable to provide all the services as recommended on a
student’s IEP, the Agency shall, prior to exercising its right to terminate, notify
the District in writing of its concerns and the District will promptly schedule a
CSE meeting, if necessary, to review the program and student’s

13.ASSIGNMENT: Provider shall not assign, transfer or convey any of its
respective rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written
consent of School District.

14.GOVERNING LAW: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the
State of New York. Any claims or causes arising out of or in connection with the
Agreement shall be commenced in Supreme Court of the State of New York in
Ulster County.

15.SEVERABILITY: If any term, provision, covenant or condition of this
Agreement or the application thereof to any person, place or circumstance shall be
held by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, unenforceable or void, the
remainder of the Agreement and such term, provision, covenant or condition as
applied to other persons, places or circumstances shall remain in full force and
effect.

16.NO PRIOR AGREEMENTS: This Agreement constitutes the full and
complete Agreement between School District and Provider and supersedes all
prior written and oral agreements, commitments or understandings with respect
thereto. This Agreement may not be altered, changed added to, deleted from or
modified except through the mutual, written consent of the parties.

17.AUTHORITY: The undersigned representatives of the patties hereby
represent and wartant that each has full legal right, power and authority to enter
into this Agreement on behalf of the party each represents and to bind said party
with regard to the obligations hereunder.
