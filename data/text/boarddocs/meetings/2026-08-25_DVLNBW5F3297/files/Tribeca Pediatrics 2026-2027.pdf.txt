AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT made as of the Ist day of July, 2026, by and between IRVINGTON UNION FREE
SCHOOL DISTRICT ("SCHOOL DISTRICT"), as the party of the first part, having its principal place of
business at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, New York 10533, and Tribeca Pediatrics, PLLC ("SERVICE

PROVIDER"), as the party of the second part, having his principal place of business at 11 Park Place,
Suite 1200, New York, NY 10007.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, SCHOOL DISTRICT is authorized to contract with individuals for the provision of
physician evaluation services; and

WHEREAS, SERVICE PROVIDER is in the business of providing physician evaluation services; and

WHEREAS, SCHOOL DISTRICT desires that SERVICE PROVIDER provide physician evaluation
services to it; and

WHEREAS, SERVICE PROVIDER is capable of and willing to provide the within services to SCHOOL
DISTRICT.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained in this
Agreement, the parties hereto mutually agree as follows:

1, TERM OF AGREEMENT: This Agreement shall be in effect for the period July 1, 2026 to June 30,
2027, unless terminated earlier, as set forth herein.

2. ATTENDANCE AT TRAINING SESSIONS: SCHOOL DISTRICT reserves the right to direct
SERVICE PROVIDER and his employees to attend various training sessions in SCHOOL DISTRICT,
which session may include, but are not limited to mandated child abuse reporting, sexual harassment,
discrimination, blood borne pathogens, and "Right to Know" workshops. In addition, SERVICE
PROVIDER shall attend bi-monthly, 45-minute department meetings, IEP Direct training sessions or
other related professional development sessions. SERVICE PROVIDER shall not be permitted to invoice
SCHOOL DISTRICT for attendance at such sessions.

3. SCOPE OF SERVICES: SERVICE PROVIDER shall provide SCHOOL DISTRICT with the
following services:

a. Conduct careful health examinations annually of all students of SCHOOL DISTRICT that are
mandated by the State who do not present a family physician's certificate.

b. Conduct, as provided in the Education Law, such examinations as are required for the issuance
of employment certificates and vacation work permits.

c. Examine all teachers and other employees as required by the SCHOOL DISTRICT.

d. Upon SCHOOL DISTRICT request, review accident reports and medical excuses from any of
the physical activities connected with the SCHOOL DISTRICT's educational and extracurricular
program.

e. Review adaptive physical plans when requested.

[OCR]
£, Upon SCHOOL DISTRICT request, review and make recommendations to the school
administrators including, but not limited to, Director of Pupil Personnel Services and the Athletic

Director with respect to health literature and health related materials used in the SCHOOL
DISTRICT.

g. Upon SCHOOL DISTRICT request, recommend limitations on physical education for individual
students and in confidence, consult with parents, teachers, and students from time to time
concerning the same. Information shall be released to teachers on a need to know basis and the
physician shall release the minimum information necessary to protect the health and safety of the
student.

h. Upon SCHOOL DISTRICT request, recommend the exclusion or readmission of students in
connection with any infectious or contagious disease or otherwise.

i, Upon SCHOOL DISTRICT request, provide first aid to students and/or school employees.

j. Upon SCHOOL DISTRICT request, advise school administrators and school health service
personnel on urgent public health issues, to the extent of the physician's expertise and knowledge.

k. Review and make recommendations to the SCHOOL DISTRICT pertaining to the update of
policies governing procedures to follow in the event of an injury or emergency illness.

|. Be available during regular school hours by phone call from the nurse and registered nurses for
major injuries or unusual problems.

m. Act as liaison with local doctors, health agencies, and other health groups, as necessary.

n. Attend meetings of the SCHOOL DISTRICT's Health Department upon request of the SCHOOL
DISTRICT.

o. During the fall, winter, and spring, conduct individual/confidential health examinations of all
students who wish to participate in High School or Middle School athletics and have not received
clearance for participation from their private physicians including, but not limited to, cheerleading,
kick line and marching band. Such examinations shall occur in the presence of another individual.

p. Upon SCHOOL DISTRICT request, attend regular Committee on Special Education (CSE)
meetings.

q. Upon SCHOOL DISTRICT request, consult with school personnel and agencies on behalf of the
District regarding pupil abuse, neglect, and maltreatment

r. Upon SCHOOL DISTRICT request, provide medical supervision to the school health staff in
such preventive health screening programs as may be mandated by State Education Law or Public
Health Law.

s. Serve as the District's AED medical coordinator

t. SCHOOL DISTRICT reserves the right to monitor and or evaluate the services provided by
SERVICE PROVIDER by conducting on site visits and observations, surveys and or interviews
with participants and stakeholders, document collection and review and any other applicable
means. Services must be appropriate, allowable, and consistent with SCHOOL DISTRICT funding
source requirements and guidelines.

24, CONFIDENTIALITY and DATA SECURITY AND PRIVACY STANDARDS:

a. SERVICE PROVIDER, his employees, and/or agents agree that all information obtained in
connection with the services provided for in this Agreement is deemed confidential information.
SERVICE PROVIDER, his employees, and/or agents shall not use, publish, discuss, disclose, or
communicate the contents of such information, directly or indirectly with third parties, except as
provided for in this Agreement. SERVICE PROVIDER further agrees that any information
received by SERVICE PROVIDER, his employees, and/or agents during the course of the services
provided pursuant to this Agreement which concerns the personal, financial, or other affairs of
SCHOOL DISTRICT, his employees, agents, clients, and/or students will be treated by SERVICE
PROVIDER, its employees, and/or agents in full confidence and will not be revealed to any other
persons, firms, or organizations.

b. SERVICE PROVIDER acknowledges that he may receive and/or come into contact with
personally identifiable information, as defined by New York Education Law § 2-d, from records
maintained by SCHOOL DISTRICT that directly relate to a student(s) (hereinafter referred to as
"education record"). SERVICE PROVIDER understands and acknowledges that he shall have in
place sufficient protections and internal controls to ensure that information is safeguarded in
accordance with applicable laws and regulations, and understands and agrees that he is responsible
for complying with state data security and privacy standards for all personally identifiable
information (hereinafter referred to as "PII") from education records, and he shall:

i. limit internal access to education records to those individuals that are determined to have
legitimate educational interests;

ii. not use the education records for any purposes other than those explicitly authorized in this Agreement;

iii. maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the security,
confidentiality, and integrity of education records in his custody; and

iv. use encryption technology to protect data while in motion or in his custody from unauthorized
disclosure using a technology or methodology specified by the secretary of the United States Department
of Health and Human Services in guidance issued under Section 13402(H)(2) of Public Law 111-5 and
the National Institute of Standards and Technology Cyber Security Framework Version 1.1.

¢. SERVICE PROVIDER has no property or licensing rights or claims of ownership to PII, and
SERVICE PROVIDER must not use PII for any purpose other than to provide the services set forth
in the Agreement. Neither the services provided nor the manner in which such services are
provided shall violate New York law.

d. SERVICE PROVIDER further understands and agrees that he is responsible for submitting a
data security and privacy plan to SCHOOL DISTRICT prior to the start of the term of this
Agreement. Such plan shall outline how all state, federal, and local data security and privacy
contract requirements will be implemented over the life of the contract consistent with SCHOOL
DISTRICT's policy on data security and privacy, as adopted. Further, such plan shall include a
signed copy of SCHOOL DISTRICT's Parents' Bill of Rights and the training requirement
established by SERVICE PROVIDER for all employees who will receive personally identifiable
information from student records (hereinafter referred to as “student data").
