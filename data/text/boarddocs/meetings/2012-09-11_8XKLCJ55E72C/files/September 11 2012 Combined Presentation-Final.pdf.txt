Meszaros Field:
The Next Steps

     Assessing the Need for New Facilities

The need for improvements to Meszaros Field have been well
documented over the years:

• Poor drainage /field flooding results in cancelations
• Hard playing surface
• Struggle to maintain a quality natural turf
• Field usage demands, limit ability to maintain turf:
  • District athletics and physical education classes
  • Community athletic usage

                         Next Steps


The Board must review the available options to enhance the
athletic fields, which include:
  • Installation of a slit drainage system
  • Reconstruction of Meszaros Field with a natural turf surface
  • Conversion to artificial turf
  • Renovation /replacement of the track
  • Enhancement of East Field

      Installation of a Slit Drainage System

The installation of a slit drainage system may, according to LAN,
have limited effectiveness as a result due to the poor underlying
soil conditions.

Estimated cost:        $135,00 ‐ $165,000

Notes:
This project would have minimal disruption to athletic programs
and would result in field closure for approximately one month.

Reconstruction of the Field Using Natural Surfaces

The construction of a new, natural playing surface would include
the removal of surface material, addition of high‐quality organic
topsoil, installation of a slit drainage system and an irrigation
system, and re‐sodding the surface.

Estimated cost:        $465,000 ‐ $535,000

Notes:
The reconstruction of the Meszaros Field using natural turf
would require the field be closed for a minimum of two growing
seasons. This does not address the wear and tear that will
result from the significant usage and will require on‐going
maintenance.

Conversion of Natural Turf to Artificial Turf
The construction of an artificial turf playing surface would
include the removal of the existing surface material, installation
of a new stone base and drainage system, and the installation of
an outdoor rug infilled with a sand/rubber mixture.
Estimated cost:                 $1,200,000
Cost‐saving compromise:
Reduce conversion to only the playing area leaving the “D‐zone”
areas as natural turf.
Potential savings:              ($180,000 ‐ $240,000)
Notes:
With the construction of an artificial field, Meszaros Field would
be closed for approximately a 2‐3 months and with construction
potentially being initiated during summer months.

                   Track Renovations

The current track is nearing the end of its useful life and requires
renovation. It may be possible to cap the track, which would
include the application of a new rubberized surface. However,
the scope of construction associated with the field renovation
may result in damage to the track.
Cost estimates:
        Recapping:            $100,000
        Complete resurfacing: $465,000
Note:
A complete resurfacing will likely be necessary with the
construction of a completely new natural field or the installation
of an artificial turf field.
