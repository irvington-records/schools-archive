		Discussion, Information: 10.1 Continued Dialogue of Potential Renovations to Campus Athletic Fields

		Dr. Harrison noted that the subject of fields has a history in Irvington but this is an opportunity to revisit the issue. Dr. Harrison noted that Mr. Gary Knowles, Director of Facilities, had given a presentation at the previous Board meeting regarding the maintenance being done to maintain our fields, noting It is still a struggle to maintain the fields, with drainage being a major issue. Dr. Harrison also noted that the Board recognizes that a decision must be made with regards to athletic fields.

		Gary Knowles and Artie McCormack shared pictures of the athletic field's condition prior to the first football game and over the course of the day with several storms. Several parents came to cover the field prior to the game throughout the morning rain, then parents used blowers to dry the fields prior to the game which had to be suspended due to another storm. There was always a tremendous amount of rain on field during the game and it tore up the field; ponding also occurred on the field. The game had to be rescheduled and was finished at the Bronxville High School. Other sports were unable to utilize the field over the weekend. By Monday, there were a few minor wears but was found to be playable. By Sunday, there were some soft spots around the field, but had dried substantially. The field was used on Tuesday for play. The amount of lost home play last year was estimated at 25%. It was noted that we have not experienced much rain in August.

		Rich Wostbrock of LAN, architects of record, reported that LAN was asked by the District to look at the drainage problems and propose improvements, i.e. repair work, reconstruction of a natural grass field, artificial turf surface, and to assess  the track and potential impact due to field construction. Mr. Wostbrock reviewed their findings of field conditions, including underlying soil conditions.

		noting surface conditions were found to be firm and pitch was in line with natural turf. Perculation tests were conducted and found poor soil conditions .The surface of track has wear and is in need of recapping, but the base appears to be in good condition. The drainage system could be retrofitted with sand silt drainage system to help vertical drainage, however, it would not improve underlying soil condition. Mr. Wostbrock reviewed the possible options but the recommendation to the Board was to install artificial turf for field and obtain maximum play.

		Beverly Miller provided an overview of required next steps. Ms.Miller reviewed the needs of Meszaros Fields, usage summary, noting that 300-500 athletes use field per day and 100-300 use field over the weekend. Next steps for Board is to review available options:

-
			slip drainage system

-
			reconstruction of Meszaros Field - natural or turf

-
			East Field and Track

		Estimated costs  for drainage $135-165,000

		Natural Surface would cost approximately $465-$535,000 and would require two growing seasons

		To convert from natural turf to artificial - $1,200,000 or a partial turf would save approximately $180,000-$240,000. Work could be done during the summer. Project would include vertical drainage pipes and rain would go through the existing drainage patterns and current system.Track renovation would be $100,000 or $456,000 to reconstruct track.

		Ms. Miller reviewed the usage of East Field which is approximately four hours per day by school and community but is not being used to its maximum capacity due to poor conditions. She also reviewed a sample calendar as a natural progression of a capital project---starting in August with initial discussions and in three weeks develop guidance specs with guidance from the Board with a referendum in December of final scope. It would take approximately 14-16 weeks for SED to grant approval with work beginning in August 2013.

		Dr. Harrison noted that other challenges faced are scheduling public meetings or public forums and there are significant conflicts with any dates between now and October 2nd which is the next scheduled Board meeting. Mr. Grados thanked Dr. Harrison and Beverly Miller for putting this together so quickly.

		Mr. Whitney asked if possible to hook up to a sewer system to drain water off if using an artificial turf so as not to drain into pond. Mr. Wostbrock noted that the water would be required to travel a distance to get to drain off campus. He also noted change in amount of drainage is not that significant using artificial turf vs natural turf. Mr. Whitney feels we would need to take a look at potential impact on drainage system.

		Mr. McCann asked about results of perc test.It was found that there was no perc/drainage due to clay bottom and unless the field it is dug up, there is no ability to change underlying drainage. Noted that community has been talking about the fields for nearly 10 years and it comes down to getting it done right. With interest rate environment, a $2 million project, including a new track and artificial turf vs. $1.4 to do a natural turf or costing $40,000 a year. Artificial turf can help control drainage. In favor of artificial turf and reconstruction of track. A bond issue is maturing which has been costing $50,000 a year and would enhance our schools.

		Mr. Grados noted that the Board has a fiduciary responsibility to to keep district facilities in good form. Members of the Board expressed concerns regarding the tight timeline and the need to reach out to community members outside of school to get information to them. Mrs. Kerner will discuss ways to reach the entire community with the Board's Communications Committee, Other comments from the Board included the advantage of historically low interest rates and that a current bond will be expiring thereby freeing up capital to help pay for a bond. Mr.McCann asked that the financial adviser be contacted to provide information on bonds, i.e. 15, 20 year bonds and the real costs. The Board also discussed safety issues regarding any construction and that the optimal time would be to have work completed during the summer.

		It was the consensus of the Board to have Administration focus on addressing the drainage issue, resurfacing track with natural and artificial turf.
