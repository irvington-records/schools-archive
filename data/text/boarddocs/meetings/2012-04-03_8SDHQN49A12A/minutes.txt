		Action: 3.3 Adoption of 2012-13 School District Budget

		Dr. Roelle presented a brief overview of the proposed budget. The Proposed Budget of $51,156,000 is an increase of 1.65% and is $718 under the New York State Tax Cap. Dr. Roelle reviewed the staffing impact in the district and noted that 8.7 FTE positions will be reduced.The proposed budget also includes staffing additions of an Assistant Superintendent for Curriculum and Personnel, a special education teacher and teacher aide, as well as a .4 FTE AIS position at middle school and .6 floating sections to be used at the middle school/high school as needed.

		Board members had no additional comments from those made regarding the budget at the April 3, 2012 meeting.

		“RESOLVED, that the Irvington Union Free School District’s 2012-2013 proposed budget be set at $51,156,000 and that this proposed budget be voted on by the community at the Annual Meeting on May 15, 2013 between the hours of 7:00 A.M. and 9:00 P.M. at the Irvington Main Street School.”
