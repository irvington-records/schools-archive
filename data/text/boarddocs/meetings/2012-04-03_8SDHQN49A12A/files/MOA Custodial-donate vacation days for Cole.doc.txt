MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING between the Civil Service Employees Association, Inc. Local 1000, AFSCME, AFL-CIO CSEA Irvington Union Free School District Custodial Unit and the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District.  Representatives of the parties have reached agreement with regard to allowing Custodial Unit members to donate Family Illness days and/or Vacation days to an employee, Neil Cole, who has no accumulated time remaining as of this date and who, due to extenuating circumstances; illness of his son, is in need of consideration.  

It is agreed:
A maximum of 30 days can be donated to Neil Cole.
Each member may donate one day; if the maximum of 30 days is not achieved with the one day per member donation, then members may donate additional days until the maximum is reached.
Day(s) donated will be deducted from the family illness days or accumulated vacation days of the member making the donation.
Donated Family Illness days will be used before donated Vacation days.
Donated days will not be returned to the member making the donation.
Unit members will not be coerced or pressured into donating vacation time.
This Memorandum is a one time only consideration and will not set a past practice of precedent for the District

All other provisions of the July 1, 2010 – June 30, 2017 collective bargaining agreement between the parties shall be continued unchanged and in full force and effect.
