	BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that said employees who are not entitled to Part 30 bumping rights shall be notified, in writing, of their placement on a preferred eligibility list pursuant to the provisions of Section 3013 of the Education Law in the tenure area(s) of their employment being abolished; and
