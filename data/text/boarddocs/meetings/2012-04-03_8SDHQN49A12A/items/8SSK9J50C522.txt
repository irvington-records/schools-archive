Meeting: Apr 03, 2012 - Regular Meeting - 7:30 p.m.*
Date: 2012-04-03
Category: 8. Personnel Matters
Subject: 8.2 Resolution Regarding Withholding Annual Salary Increment for a Certain Teacher
Type: Action (Consent)

Whereas Article IV (C) of the Irvington Faculty Association Collective Bargaining Agreement provides the basis for the Board of Education to withhold the annual salary increase for certain teachers; and

	Whereas consistent with the protocol presented in the Collective Bargaining Agreement the Interim Superintendent of Schools has recommended that teacher #576 not be granted a salary increase for the 2012-13 school year.

	Therefore, Be it Resolved, that based upon the recommendation of the Superintendent of Schools, Teacher #576, shall not advance on the salary schedule to the next step upon the commencement of the 2012-13 school year and such teacher shall be maintained on Step 11 for the 2012-13 school year.
