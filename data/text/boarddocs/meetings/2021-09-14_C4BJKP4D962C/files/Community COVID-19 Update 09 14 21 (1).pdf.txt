Mitigation Efforts
The District is well equipped to support all guidelines issued by the appropriate New York State agencies: New
York State Department of Health (NYSDOH), Westchester County Department of Health (WCDOH) and New York
State Education Department (NYSED)

   ● Examples of requirements in each of our schools:
        ○ Face masks:
             ■   Must be worn indoors at all times
             ■   Must be worn by occupants on buses
             ■   Mask breaks will be provided
        ○ Social distancing:
              ■    Three (3) feet indoors, when possible, will be provided
              ■    Distancing of six (6) feet for eating, singing, playing wind instruments, PE, and recess
              ■    Limit any large gatherings
        ○ Hand sanitizer will be provided in all spaces and handwashing will be encouraged
        ○ Ventilation will continue to be a focus in school and on buses
             ■     Air puriﬁers added to cafeterias, music rooms and health ofﬁces
   ● Contact Tracing Protocols:
       ○   Protocols have been modiﬁed:
              ■   Asymptomatic, vaccinated individuals will not be quarantined if masks are worn
        ○    Close Contacts will be determined by:
               ■    Perimeter of three (3) feet for students and six (6) feet for adults
               ■    Contact time of 15 minutes in a 24 hour period

COVID-19 Vaccination
The COVID-19 vaccine has proven to lessen the effects of
COVID-19 on individuals.

 ● The District has coordinated vaccination clinics for its students and staff
   members and will continue to do so

 ● The District cannot require students or staff members to be vaccinated

 ● Staff members who are not vaccinated will have to be tested weekly

 ● District will partner with the WCDOH to facilitate surveillance testing

COVID-19 Testing
The District will facilitate weekly COVID-19 testing in partnership with
the WCDOH and Quadrant Biosciences. This testing will:

  ● Utilize the Clariﬁ saliva-based PCR test

  ● Tests will be administered in school

  ● Surveillance Testing will target 20% of students and staff weekly

  ● Results will be reported to the WCDOH for contact tracing purposes

  ● Consent will be required to participate

  ● Participation is strongly encouraged, but cannot be mandated

  ● Once Quadrant Biosciences furnishes a Memorandum of Agreement (MOA) and
    authorization from legal counsel is gained, the consent process will begin
    District-wide

Sick? When to stay home.
COVID-19 related symptoms that will require a medical evaluation and COVID-19 testing, even if
your child is experiencing just one of the following symptoms:
 ▪   Fever greater than 100.0 and/or chills
 ▪   Cough that is persistent
 ▪   Shortness of breath or difﬁculty in breathing
 ▪   Fatigue
 ▪   Muscle or body aches
 ▪   Headache
 ▪   New loss of taste or smell
 ▪   Sore throat
 ▪   Congestion or runny nose
 ▪   Vomiting and/or diarrhea

This list does not include all possible COVID-19 symptoms. Children and adolescents with the virus that
causes COVID-19 may experience any, all, or none of these symptoms.

If your child has a fever, please do not give medicine such as Tylenol or Advil and send them to school.
Should the fever return when the medicine wears off, you will be called to pick-up your child.

Please keep your child home until fever free for 24 hours (less than 100.0 without the use of
medicine), improvement in symptoms, and if prescribed by your healthcare provider, your child
has received the appropriate treatment.

                                      **All applies to staff members as well**

New: Acceptable COVID-19 Tests
Updated guidelines have introduced new standards for the
COVID-19 tests that will be accepted by the Irvington UFSD:

●   All students and staff members will need to provide a negative
    COVID-19 test after any illness with symptoms suggestive of a
    COVID-19 infection.

●   The IUFSD is only accepting NAAT tests (Nucleic Acid Ampliﬁcation
    Test), for example: PCR or rapid molecular tests. At this time the
    District is not accepting rapid antigen tests.

Public Board of Education Meetings
The State of New York has once again provided the opportunity
for public entities to meet virtually. Thus, there is a decision
before the Board of Education and Administration.

Should the Board meetings continue to be held in-person for everyone,
allowing for in-person community attendance and participation?

                                -OR-

Should the Board meetings continue to be held in-person for the Board
and Administration, while only permitting the community to view and
participate virtually, as was the case for much of 2020-21?
