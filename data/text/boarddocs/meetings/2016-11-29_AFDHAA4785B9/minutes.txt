6. Board Discussion

Discussion: 6.1 School Start Times

Catherine Palmieri noted that the purpose of this discussion is to address some of the challenges in the school scheduling. Dr. Harrison noted that neighboring districts made adjustments in their schedule and conversations began with administrators, mostly at the campus, looking at the constraints that there may be in making adjustments to start times. He asked if the concerns the Board has heard from parents is about the 7:50 start time or if it has to do with bus pickup times. Historically, there have been reductions in the number of buses that are run in an attempt to maximize the fewer buses which have led to early pickup times, some as early as 6:30-6:40am pickup and well ahead of the 7:50am start time. To solve the situation, additional buses will need to be put on the road which would increase transportation costs. Ardsley Transportation has been charged with offering different options to alleviate some of the concerns --- offering shortened routes but again this option would require additional buses.  Additionally, a survey will be sent to all schools in the area as well as BOCES asking for start/dismissal times as well as pickup/drop- off times. Dr. Harrison noted, anecdotally, that Irvington has one of the later dismissal times, making it difficult to make it to competitions.

Catherine Palmieri noted that the late start time of MSS was difficult for her as a parent but she always assumed that it was the busing challenge that drove the late start and perhaps both the early start time at high school and late start time at MSS are intertwined. Dr. Harrison also agreed as did Mr. Graeber that MSS start time is a challenge for working parents. Dows Lane start time also presents challenges due to 8:00am start time. Mr. Whitney thought that a later start time at the high school would give students more time to sleep and a way to address that adolescences require more sleep. Would it be possible to start at 8:30 and still have the opportunity for a full complement of extra-curricular activities after school? Dr. Harrison noted that school would have to go until 4:00 and would therefore interfere with students taking part in athletics and would need to make choices. It might also require students to be pulled out of classes to receive music instruction. It was noted that Dobbs Ferry now starts at 8:12am but they do not offer busing at the secondary level. Mr. Hanna did note that they have reported difficulty getting to athletic events.

Deb Hargraves would like to hear what are the ideal start times for  students, what are they tied to, i.e. teacher contract, etc. and then look at recommendations before looking at fiscal impacts. She noted that she often wondered why MSS was a late start time as opposed to another school. Mr. Hanna would also like to get the number of high school students who take the buses and if the buses were later, would ridership increase.

Dave Graeber asks if there could be any accommodations for early arrivals. Dr. Kor noted that there isn't early supervision available at elementary schools. It was also noted that currently, the district utilizes eight buses districtwide, which was reduced from 10 due to previous budget cuts.  Administration will follow up on questions posed by the Board.
