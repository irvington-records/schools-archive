3. Business of the Board

Action (Consent): 3.1 Main St. School - Bid Rejection Renovations at Main Street School SED #66-04-02-02-0001-016

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District received bids for Contract G-General Construction for the Main Street School Renovations Project (hereinafter referred to as the “Project”) on December 20 2021; WHEREAS, the School District’s Architect, Construction Manager and Administration have reviewed the bids submitted by the bidders for the General Construction contract in connection with the Project; WHEREAS, based upon such review, the Board of Education believes it to be in the best interest of the School District to reject all General Construction contract bids received for the Main Street Renovations Project and re-advertise for new bids for this Project at a later date; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Board of Education hereby rejects all bids submitted for the General Construction contract for the Main Street Renovations Project.

WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District received bids for Contract G-General Construction for the Main Street School Renovations Project (hereinafter referred to as the “Project”) on December 20 2021;

WHEREAS, the School District’s Architect, Construction Manager and Administration have reviewed the bids submitted by the bidders for the General Construction contract in connection with the Project;

WHEREAS, based upon such review, the Board of Education believes it to be in the best interest of the School District to reject all General Construction contract bids received for the Main Street Renovations Project and re-advertise for new bids for this Project at a later date;

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Board of Education hereby rejects all bids submitted for the General Construction contract for the Main Street Renovations Project.
