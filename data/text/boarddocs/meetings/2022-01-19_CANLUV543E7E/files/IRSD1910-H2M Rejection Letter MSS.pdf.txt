Dear Carol Stein:


On December 20, 2021, sealed bids were received by the Irvington Union Free School District for the above referenced
project and contract. As indicated on the attached bid tabulation, the low bid among the four (4) bids that were received was
for a total contract value of $1,093,000.00 (includes $30,000 General Contingency Allowance). There were also alternate
bid prices provided, items G1 through G3 for additional work items with a total bid price with alternates of $1,260,000.00.
This bid was submitted by Grace Contracting & Development LLC, West Haverstraw, NY.


H2M, along with Mr. Gary Knowles of the District and Mr. Kevin Sawyer of Triton Construction Corp., conducted an interview
with this bidder to assist the District in awarding this contract. This interview included discussions of the low bidder’s bid
submission, regarding their qualifications and references, to ensure the contractor’s complete understanding of the proposed
scope of work, their ability to perform the work, their financial stability to begin work, ability to meet the established completion
dates, availability of materials / labor and review the overall scope for compliance with the project specifications.


Upon review of the bids received, it is our professional opinion and recommendation to the Irvington Union Free School
District to reject all bids received for this contract and re-advertise and invite new bids.


Please call me if you have any questions concerning our recommendation at (914) 358-5623 x2064.
