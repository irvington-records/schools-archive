Based on the bid information provided by the low bidders, and pending any final review required by District Council, it is
our opinion that it would be in the Districts best interest to reject all the bids received for this project, and re-bid the
project again as quickly as possible.

A rebid will require an adjustment in the construction schedule timeline due to expected Material delays. We anticipate
the gym roof air conditioning unit will need to be installed in the fall when school is not in session. The balance of
construction work remains to be completed over the summer and will be made safe and occupiable for the first day of
school in September.

If you should have any questions, please do not hesitate to call.
