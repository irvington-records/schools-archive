6. Presentation

Information: 6.1 Presentation

Drs. Harrison and Duffy presented an overview of the proposed 2023-24 curriculum and instruction budget. All of the work is tied to the District's strategic objectives. The components of the budget included lines for instruction, library/media center, guidance, instructional technology, curriculum development, supervision, co-curricular and interscholastic athletics, special education and pupil personnel services. The budget expenditure represents an increase of approximately 7.6% by expenditure category.
