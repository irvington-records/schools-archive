                               LETTER OF AGREEMENT
            STRATEGIC PLAN REINVIGORATION AND IMPLEMENTATION SUPPORT

Purpose
The purpose of this Letter of Agreement (“Agreement”) is to document the working arrangement
between Hazard, Young, Attea & Associates (“HYA”) and the Irvington Union Free School District
(“District”) to support the reinvigoration and implementation of the District’s Strategic Plan. This
engagement is intended to ensure alignment with the Board of Education’s vision and to support
senior leadership in building actionable and sustainable implementation plans that reflect district
capacity and direction.

Section I: HYA Responsibilities
HYA shall provide the following services over the course of a four-day engagement (not to exceed
two days on site):

   •   Strategic Plan Review & Update
       HYA will collaborate with District leadership to review the current strategic plan, with the
       goal of updating objectives as needed and to prepare to re-engage stakeholders in the work.
       This phase includes virtual meetings with the Superintendent prior to October 15 to assess
       the current status of the plan, clarify goals, and co-develop materials and agendas for use
       during the on-site engagement.
   •   District Leadership and Faculty Engagement & Board Workshop
       On Tuesday, October 15, 2025, HYA will be on site in the District for a full day. During the
       day, HYA will work directly with district leaders, faculty, and staff to advance the planning
       process and build momentum for implementation. That evening, HYA will facilitate a Board
       of Education workshop focused on reviewing and updating the District’s directional
       strategic plan and reinvigorating the work following a pause caused by the superintendent
       transition.
   •   Leadership Implementation Planning Session
       HYA will conduct a second planning session to support the design of implementation plans
       that reflect district structures and capacity. This session may be conducted on site or
       virtually at the District’s discretion.
       Date: TBD
   •   Off-Site Work
       The remaining 1.5 contract days will be used for planning, preparation, material
       development, analysis, and post-engagement follow-up.

Section II: Fees and Payment Schedule
In consideration for the services described, the District agrees to pay HYA a consulting fee
of $8,500, based on a daily rate of $2,500 for 3.5 days of professional services. Payment will be
made in two installments:

    •   50% ($4,250) invoiced upon execution of this Letter of Agreement
    •   50% ($4,250) invoiced upon completion of the second planning session

Section III: Reimbursable Costs
A. Printing and Postage: HYA provides all materials electronically. If the District requests printed
copies, it will reimburse costs associated with printing, binding, and shipping.
B. Travel Expenses: The District agrees to reimburse reasonable travel costs associated with the
October 15, 2025 on-site session. If the second session is conducted on site, related travel costs will
also be reimbursed. HYA Associates do not seek reimbursement for meals.

Section IV: Additional Services
The District may request additional days of service beyond those outlined in this Agreement,
including expanded support, additional workshops, or customized implementation tools. Such
requests may be made as part of this Letter of Agreement without requiring a formal addendum.
Additional days will be billed at a daily rate of $2,500.

Section V: Business Relationship
HYA shall act as an independent contractor and not as an employee of the District. This Agreement
does not establish an employment relationship or entitle HYA to employee benefits.

Section VI: Term and Termination
This Agreement becomes effective upon signature by both parties and will remain in effect until the
agreed-upon services are completed or the Agreement is terminated by mutual consent.
