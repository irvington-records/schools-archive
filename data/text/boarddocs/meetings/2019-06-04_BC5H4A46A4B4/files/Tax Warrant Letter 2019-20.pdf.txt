Dear Town Comptroller:

I, Pauline Davies, District Clerk of the Irvington Union Free School District, Town of Greenburgh,
County of Westchester, State of New York, do hereby certify that the following budget and tax
items for the 2019/2020 fiscal year, beginning July 1, 2019 and ending June 30, 2020 are true and
accurate, This is an official breakdown of the warrant for use for your collection of taxes.

At its meeting on June 4, 2019 the Board of Education adopted a motion containing the following

resolution:

MOVED, that the voters of the Irvington Union Free School District, by a vote of 946 to 257,
adopted a budget for the school year commencing July 1, 2019 in the amount of $62,953,554; that
the sums as set forth in the forms of budget heretofore submitted to the voter be appropriated; that
the taxes in the necessary amounts be levied therefore; that the Board members be authorized to
execute a warrant therefore, and that the warrant be delivered to the Town authorities.

The major budget categories and their respective amounts are listed below:

General Support $ 7,339,396
Instruction $ 35,008,044
