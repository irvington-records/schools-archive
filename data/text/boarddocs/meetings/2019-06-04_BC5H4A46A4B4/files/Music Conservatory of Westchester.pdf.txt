Music Therapy Institute: Contract

This agreement betwecn The Irvington UFSD and The Music Conservatory of Westchester
contracts for ihe services of a music therapist to provide music therapy groups at $120 per session, 2
sessions per week for 30 weeks. In addition, a $200 fee for instruments, materials and administrative
fee/supervision of therapist will be included. Total cost is not to exceed $7,400.

Service will begin week of 9/9/19
Location: Irvington UFSD, High School and Middle Schoo!

Payment of this contract is due upon receipt of claim forms.

All checks are payable to the Music Conservatory of Westchester. Routine financial questions should
be directed to the Business Manager of the Music Conservatory of Westchester.

Issues related to the interagency collaboration are decided between Irvington’s Director of Pupil
Personnel and the Director of the Music Conservatory of Westchester.

Information and records of students will be made available by the district to contracted music therapist.
Confidentiality will be professionally respected.

Program implementation and supervision are the responsibility of the Director of the Music Therapy
Institute of the Music Conservatory of Westchester. The director and music therapists are employees
of the Music Conservatory of Westchester.

Sessions missed by the music therapist will be made up. If this cannot be accomplished because of
problems of coordination with the district, the contracted time will be used by the music therapist for
activities appropriate to the ongoing program,

Additional sessions may be contracted subject to negotiations between the Music Conservatory of
Westchester and the Irvington UFSD.

Please sign and return original.
