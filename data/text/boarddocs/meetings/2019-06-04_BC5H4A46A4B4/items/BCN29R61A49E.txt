Meeting: Jun 04, 2019 - Tenure/Retiree Reception/Regular Meeting
Date: 2019-06-04
Category: 7. Retirement Contribution Reserve Fund
Subject: 7.1 Establish a Retirement Contribution Reserve Fund
Type: Action

Reserve management is an integral aspect of maintaining long term health of the district.  The establishment of a Retirement Contribution Reserve will allow the District to plan for any large increases in pension contribution matches for ERS and TRS and minimize the impact to educational programs as we go through the budget process.  Once this reserve is established, the District will establish the sub fund for the TRS which will require a second resolution in accordance with new laws adopted with the NY State budget process. We anticipate this resolution to be on the June 18, 2019 agenda.
