CONTRACT FOR EDUCATIONAL SERVICES

The Board of Education for the Irvington Union Free Schoo! District appointed Katherine
Hough, M.D. (Pediatrics on Hudson LLC); 615 Broadway; Hastings-on-Hudson, NY
10706; 914-963-1663; as schoo! physician for the period commencing July 1, 2019 and
the terminating on June 30, 2020. The school physician will make inspections of students
attending the public schools in Irvington in accordance with (#902(1); 8NYCRR
#136.2©). Services will include the following:

Conduct all New York State mandated physical examinations of children,
including, but not limited to those for students in Pre-K, Kindergarten, First,
Third, Seventh, and Tenth grades who do no present a certificate of health from
their family physician.

Conduct such special physical examinations as may be appropriate before
placement of disabled children, as referred by the Director of Pupil Personnel
Services, Athletic Director, and/or Physical Education Coordinator.

Conduct athletic physical for students in Grades 7-12. The school physician has
the final decision on approving or not approving a student for a sport following a
physical examination.

Recommend adjustments of the educational program in accordance with
individual pupil’s health needs and consult with parents, teachers, and pupils from
time to time concerning the same.

Consult with School Nurse in their care for children found to have physical or
mental disabilities.

Consult with the School Psychologist, School Nurse, and other school personnel
in case conferences when such are indicated.

Interpret public health laws governing control of communicable diseases.
Periodically review polices and procedures governing the exclusion or
readmission of pupils in connection with infectious or contagious diseases. The
School Physician will carry medical malpractice insurance on its employee
physicians in limits of at least One Million Dollars ($1,000,000) per occurrence.
Three Million Dollars ($3,000.000) in the aggregate.

Act as consultant to schoo] administrators and school health personnel on medical
problems and public health procedures.

Consult with school personnel and agencies on behalf of the District regarding
pupil abuse, neglect, and maltreatment.
