A, GENERAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITES

1.The duties and responsibilities of the Teacher shall be those duties and responsibilities

prescribed for the position of Teacher in the Education law and other applicable statutes, laws, rules and/or
regulations and those duties and/or responsibilitics established by the Board pursuant to such
statues, laws, rules, and regulations including, but not limited to the following responsibilities:

a) To provide such vision services for children assigned by the Irvington Union Free School
Districts Committee on Special Education ("CSE") subject to students IEP.

b) To provide reports and communications to the IUFSD CSE with regard to such vision services.

c) To attend meetings or hearings of the IUFSD CSE when requested by such Committee.

d) To provide annual cvaluations and progress reports on all students treated during the year
to the CSE as requested.

¢) To perform such other dutics as the Teacher as may be required by the State Education
Department or as the Board may from time to time require.

2. In order to provide the services referred to in Paragraph "1" above, the Teacher agrees to be
available to serve on a regular basis, up to a maximum of ten (10) hours per week.

3. For providing such services, the Teacher shall be paid the sum of $140.00 per hour for maximum ten (10)
hours per week to be paid on the monthly basis based upon time sheets submitted by the Teacher, which must
include the date, time and name of the students(s) who received vision services during the prior month.

4. It is expressly understood that this Agreement does not create an employer/employee relationship between
the parties. ‘The Teacher is hired as an independent Contractor. This contract shall not entitle the Teacher to
any employce benefits. sick leave, vacation time or other benefits enjoyed by employees of the District, except
as stated hercin. Any missed vision services will be made up at the discretion of the Teacher.

B.GENERAL TERMS:
1 During the term of this agreement (2019-20 school year), the Teacher shall faithfully, diligently,

in accordance with accepted professional standards, perform and discharge the duties and
responsibilities of the Teacher for vision services to the District.
