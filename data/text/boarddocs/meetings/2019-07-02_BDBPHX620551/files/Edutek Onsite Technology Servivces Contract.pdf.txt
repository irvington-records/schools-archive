PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Working with the Irvington Union Free School District (IUFSD) Edu Tek Limited (ETL) will provide
comprehensive Information Technology services at each of the district’s sites.

This agreement shall start on July 1, 2019 and end on June 30, 2020. Edu Tek will support the
district’s Local Area Networks (LAN’s) and Wide Area Network (WAN’s). This service includes
support for all operating systems currently in use at all sites The IT support described will be
performed by:
   •    One Full-Time Level II and One Full-Time Level I Engineers

SCOPE OF SERVICES

    The scope of services contained in this agreement includes but is not limited to the following:
System Support & Maintenance
    •   Install and support computers (PC’s).
    •   Troubleshoot computer hardware issues.
    •   Maintain current Symantec Ghost image library for machine duplication or utilize
        Microsoft SCCM
   •    Install and configure printers.
   •    Troubleshoot printing issues.
   •    Install and configure ancillary computer hardware (i.e. scanners, LCD projectors,
        electronic whiteboards, keyboards, mice, probes and PDA’s).
   •    Provide basic software support and troubleshooting for desktop software applications.
   •    Interface with vendors to address warranty issues for hardware and software.
Networking Management
   •    Administer Microsoft Active Directory.
   •    Manage Google Apps for Education Domain
   •    Administer the district’s backup drives and tape rotation.
   •    Maintain and manage all district servers.
   •    Setup and manage user accounts and passwords.
   •    Perform annual network migration of students from building to building and between
        grades.
   •    Manage security policies (i.e. Student, Teacher & Administrative)
   •    Install servers, backup drives and network cards.
   •    Install and maintain anti-virus system.
Internet Support
    •   Deploy Antivirus definition updates on a regular basis.
    •   Configure routers and switches.
    •   Manage internet filtering requests.
    •   Configure & maintain firewall.

PROPOSAL ASSUMPTIONS
   •   The district will provide the necessary hardware and/or software or will reimburse Edu
       Tek for items procured to make repairs to computer equipment. Reimbursements will be
       made upon submission of a detailed invoice (with backup) sufficient to substantiate
       reimbursement.
  •    The district will provide Edu Tek with all necessary rights/passwords to administer the
       entire district’s network.
  •    District will maintain warrantees with manufacturers where possible.
  •    District will provide sufficient stock of replacement hardware to prevent excessive
       downtime.
  •    Non-contractual services requested that cannot be completed within the time allotted
       per this contract will be negotiated for separately and will be billed as additional services,
       if mutually agreed to in writing.
  •    The district will not be responsible for any employee benefits for Edu Tek employees.
  •    The district will provide a viable technology budget for the project and our services, which
       shall include contingencies for changes during implementation and other costs which are
       the responsibility of the district.
  •    The district will designate a representative authorized to act in the district’s behalf with
       respect to the project at the time of the execution of this agreement.
  •    Should this agreement be terminated, the district agrees that they are precluded from
       employing current or former Edu Tek Ltd. employees directly or through competing firms
       for the duration of the project or within twenty-four (24) months of the date of
       termination of this agreement, whichever is longer.
  •    If a project requires either weekend and/or after hours attention services shall not be
       rendered unless a fixed fee is negotiated for the service. Edu Tek will be reimbursed for
       all approved overtime expenses that are mutually acceptable and agreed to in writing,
       either monetarily or by credit for time on site upon submission of detailed invoices and
       prior written approval by the district.
  •    The district will provide an IT support structure, including staffing, commensurate with
       the size and requirements of the network.
  •    The district or Edu Tek may revoke this contract at any time for cause and with ninety (90)
       days’ written notice.
  •    The district may request a change of staffing at any time and EDU TEK will provide
       alternate staff with sixty (60) days’ notice.
  •    The district will make the worksite available during normal working hours to Edu Tek’s
       onsite staff all weekdays unless listed as a holiday in the SCHEDULE section below. This
       includes school vacation periods.

SERVICES NOT INCLUDED
The following services are not included in proposed basic services. These services can be
provided as an additional service when authorized, in writing, by the district. The services of a
licensed electrical contractor are not included in our services.
The district will continue to obtain the following services from a third-party provider or through
a separate contract and thus they would not be a part of Edu Tek’s base IT service:
    •   Internet Service
    •   Data Support Services
    •   Professional Development

Schedule

Daily & Weekly
Edu Tek will provide one (1) onsite, Full-Time Level II System Engineer and one (1) onsite, Full-
Time Level I System Engineer for forty (40) hours per week, less discretionary time described
below.
The Level I Engineers schedule will be from 7:00 am until 3:30 pm and they will receive ½ hour
for lunch unless another mutually agreeable schedule is determined.
The Level II Engineer schedule will be from 7:00 am until 3:30 pm and they will receive ½ hour
for lunch unless another mutually agreeable schedule is determined.
In addition to the services described above the district will also receive:
    •   On-site Operations Manager services not to exceed twenty (20) days per year


The use of this time will be dictated by the requirements of the onsite engineers and current
projects in the district.

Discretionary Time
For each full time Edu Tek Staff position held in district Edu Tek will receive two (2) weeks or ten
(10) days of discretionary time in addition to holidays (see below). This time will be used to fill
employee vacation and sickness requests. The time may also be used to provide training to our
onsite staff or to perform tasks not related to the district. Notice regarding the use of
discretionary time, in excess of two (2) days in duration, will be discussed with the district at
least two-weeks prior to commencement.

Vacations
So that the district has consistent on site coverage Edu Tek will make every effort to ensure that
employee vacations do not overlap. Edu Tek will make every effort to coincide employee
vacations with non-holiday district closures to provide consistent coverage throughout the
school year.

Sick Days
Edu Tek will make every effort to provide same day coverage for an ill employee who is unable
to be present on a given day. If we are not able to provide a substitute employee for a given day
the time absent will be counted against our discretionary time.

Holidays
Edu Tek Limited does not provide onsite coverage during the following holidays. (Note: These
holidays are not included in the discretionary time):
    •   Labor Day
    •   Columbus Day
    •   Veteran’s Day
    •   Thanksgiving Day and Friday after
    •   Christmas Eve Day
    •   Christmas Day
    •   New Years Eve Day
    •   New Years Day
    •   Martin Luther King Day
    •   President’s Day
    •   Good Friday
    •   Memorial Day
    •   Independence Day
Emergency School closures
School closures due to emergencies including snow do not count against our allotted
discretionary time. If the District Office is open on such days, Edu Tek employees are expected
to be present to work.

Staffing Definitions

    System Engineer Level One
    Certifications - MCSP, CAN, CLA or equivalent experience
    Skill Set:
    •   Ability to install Network operating system and perform simple operating system
        upgrades (not requiring any data or application movement/migration).
    •   Basic operating system troubleshooting
    •   Standard network/local printing and print queue installation and management.
    •   General network Administration (adding users, groups, shares, printers).
    •   Server hardware configuration.
    •   Rack device installations and cable management.
    •   SCSI attached storage subsystems.
    •   Implementation of Patch process for individual NOS.
    •   Non configurable/non manageable switch installations.
    •   Stand-alone Tape drive installations.
    •   Approved server based (non enterprise) tape backup software installation and
        configuration (specific agents may require a SE Level 2).
    •   General server diagramming and documentation

    System Engineer Level Two
    Certifications - MCSE, CNE, CLE or equivalent experience
    Skill Set:
    •   Ability to install Network operating system and perform more complex upgrades
        (application and data moves must be well defined and pre-approved)
    •   Advanced troubleshooting of operating system.
    •   Advanced network/local printing such as shared faxing and shared scanning.
    •   Advanced network Administration such as Directory and Domain troubleshooting.
    •   Cluster Hardware configurations
    •   Blade Server configurations
    •   Entry Level NAS/SAN (Fiber Attached Subsystems and Appliance NAS devices) in cluster
        configurations only. Does not include additional products for load balancing or active-
        active failure.
    •   Implementation of Patch process for Server group such as Microsoft Servers using
        bundled solutions such as SUS, WUS and Zenworks.
    •   Entry level managed/configurable switches not requiring routing, trouncing, or multiple
        manufacturer switch operation.
    •   Standalone tape drive installations including autoloaders.
    •   Approved server based (non enterprise) tape backup software installation and
        configuration and installation of approved backup agents including remote agents.
    •   Server Centric diagramming and documentation

Internetworking Engineer Level One
Certifications - CCNA or equivalent experience
Skill Set:
•   Entry Level Engineer with ability to configure and install Network devices such as switches
    and routers not requiring advanced configurations.
•   Troubleshooting basic switched and routed environments.
•   Layer 2 switch configurations including VLAN, Trunking, Channeling.
•   Basic router configurations using static routing, (no routing protocols or QoS)
•   Basic ACL's knowledge
•   Small to Medium Business basic Infrastructure design including IP addressing and
    Subnetting.
•   Router and switch image upgrades (that will not be impacted by routing protocols)
•   Basic internet and site-to-site connectivity (excluding VPNs)
•   General Network Diagramming and documentation

Internetworking Engineer Level Two
Certifications - CCNP or equivalent experience)
Skill Set:
•   Mid Level Engineer should have all of the skill sets of Level 2 plus
•   Troubleshooting of advanced switched and routed environments.
•   Layer 2 switch configurations including VLAN, Trunking, Channeling and advanced STP
    configurations.
•   Layer 3 switch configurations including Basic routing protocols.
•   Advanced router configurations including routing protocols EIGRP, OSPF, RIP, IGRP
•   Advanced ACL's knowledge and the ability to configure.
•   Basic wireless installations including basic authentication (WEP, MAC)
•   Basic Infrastructure design
•   Basic knowledge of SNMP, SYSLOG, DNS, NTP, TACACS/Radius
•   Router and switch image upgrades which can be impacted by routing protocols.
•   Site-to-site connectivity such as inter and intra site routing (Excluding VPNs)
•   General Network Diagramming and documentation

Security Engineer Level One
Certifications - Cisco Qualified Specialist or equivalent experience
Skill Set:
•   Entry Level Engineer with ability to configure and install basic security devices including

•   Basic firewall troubleshooting
•   Basic VPN configurations (Must be same devices for Site-to-Site), and User VPN using only

Security Engineer Level Two
