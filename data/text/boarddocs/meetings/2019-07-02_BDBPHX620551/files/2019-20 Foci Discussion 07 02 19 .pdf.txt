All Future Work Is Aligned With Our Vision for Tomorrow
All of our work will be aligned with the Strategic Objectives - Our Vision for Tomorrow
To achieve its mission and vision, and to provide for the future of its students, the Irvington Union
Free School District will:
 1.   Provide students with a rigorous, comprehensive, enriched and diversiﬁed curricula that will
      prepare students to achieve their personal best, and will integrate technology in their learning.
 2.   Encourage innovation, creativity and risk taking to inspire a dynamic learning environment.
 3.   Foster the social and emotional growth of all students and promote a culture where students are
      active participants in society.
4.    Support educators through targeted professional learning and opportunities for collaboration.
 5.   Strengthen local connections to and ownership of our schools.

6.    Ensure the ﬁscal health of the district and provide for a high quality learning environment.

Suggested Goal and Discussion Items
During the 2019-20 school year, we will engage in critical work
instructionally and operationally:

●   The instructional focus will remain committed to enhancing student
    learning experiences, developing curricula, and supporting teachers’
    development

●   The operational focus will address the capital project and future
    opportunities

Overarching Instructional Goals
Curriculum and Instruction:

●   Implement instructional practices that elevate student thinking and
    understanding

●   Develop a balanced assessment system that measures students’
    content knowledge, skills, and dispositional thinking

●   Increase the use of data to inform instruction and planning

The 2019-20 Instructional Focus
The Goals for the 2019-20 school year will be developed through the course of the
summer and will include initiatives related to:
 ●   Continued growth of the overarching goals
 ●   Target Strategic Objectives such as:
      ○ Development and introduction of the Elementary World Language Program
      ○   Finalization and implementation of the K-12 Guidance Plan
      ○   Update of the Technology Plan
      ○   Curricula development/alignment (Social Studies and Science)
      ○   Alignment and development of assessments
      ○   Increased, systemic use of data District-wide
             ● Collection and analysis of data K-12
      ○   Social and Emotional Learning (SEL)
      ○   Equity and access for all learners
 ●   Maximizing professional learning experiences
 ●   School Quality Survey

The Operational Focus
The Business and Operational Goals will continue to focus on District-wide
needs associated with ﬁnance and facilities.
●   Planning and design for the Capital Bond Project
     ○   Community partnership and engagement
     ○   Presentation of construction timelines and designs
●   Finalize Contract Negotiations
     ○   Custodians (CSEA)
     ○   Administrators (IAA)
●   Development of a Sustainability Committee
     ○   Stakeholder engagement
     ○   Identiﬁcation of opportunities
●   Continued Emphasis on Long Range Fiscal Planning and Reserve Management

Communications Focus to Continue
The District will continue to identify opportunities to increase
stakeholder engagement

●   Evaluation of communication practices

●   Identiﬁcation of enhancements to practices as necessary

●   Continue to bolster school identity and spirit
