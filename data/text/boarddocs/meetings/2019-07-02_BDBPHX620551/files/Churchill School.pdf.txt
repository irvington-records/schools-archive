SCHOOL & CENTER

AGREEMENT made this 1" day of September 2019, by and between ‘The Churchill Schoo! (herein
“Private School") and Irvington Union Free School District, 4 roadway, Itvi YY 10533

WHEREAS, the New York State Office of Education of Children with Handicapping
Conditions has found that the Private School is adequate to provide instruction adopted to the
mental attainments and physical conditions of the pupils whose names and addresses are listed in
Schedule "A", annexed hereto as a part hereof.

WHEREAS, the School District by law is authorized to contract with private schools
incorporated by and existing within the State of New York to furnish such educational facilities
for handicapped children.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION OF THE MUTUAL PROMISES HEREIN
CONTAINED, THE PARTIES HERETO AGREE AS FOLLOWS:

L The Private School agrees to furnish special classes at its educational facilities for
the instruction of pupils residing in the School District who are named in Schedule

nA",

2. The term of this Agreement shall extend from September 1, 2019 to June 30, 2020
but providing 180 educational days.

3. The School District shall pay tuition to the Private School at the annual rate of
36.499* per student, payable in 10 monthly payments of $3,469.90 per student.
4, This Agreement shall terminate immediately upon the occurrence of any of the
following:

A. Failure by the School District to remit tuition payments.

B. Termination of the approval of the Commissioner of Education of the Private
School.

Upon such termination, the Schoo! District shall pay to the Private School the pro-

rata portion of the monthly tuition for that part of the month when Services were
furnished prior to the termination,

*As set by the New York State Educational Department, Rate Setting Unit--and may be subject to
change.
