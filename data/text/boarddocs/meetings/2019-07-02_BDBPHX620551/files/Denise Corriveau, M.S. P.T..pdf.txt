GENERAL TERMS:

During the term of this agreement (2019-20 school year), the Therapist shall faithfully,
diligently, in accordance with accepted professional standards, perform and discharge the
duties and responsibilities of Therapist for PT services to the District.

The Therapist represents that he or she will, throughout the term of this appointment, hold
a valid license or of the credential to practice in the State of New York and proof of such
license or other credential will be furnished to the District Clerk upon request. It is expressly
understood that failure to hold and maintain such license shall be cause for the immediate
termination of this Agreement and the appointment of the Physical Therapist. The Physical
Therapist represents that he or she has been fingerprinted and been subject to a criminal
history background check and is not aware of any facta which would render the Physical
Therapist ineligible for employment by the District. The Physical Therapist authorizes the
District to request clearance for employment from the State Education Department.

The Therapist agrees that any individual providing professional services pursuant to this
agreement shall be fully qualified and duly licensed and, where applicable, shall comply and
be subject to the rules, regulations and responsibilities attendant to their position. The
Therapist shall not employ or engage any individual who is not appropriately licensed or
who’s right to practice has been revoked or restricted by the appropriate State licensing
authority.

The Therapist shall maintain professional liability Insurance (malpractice insurance) in the
minimum amount of One Million Dollars ($1,000,000) for a single claim and One Million
Dollars ($1,000,000) per year aggregate for all professional staff employed by the Therapist
who provides professional services to the District. This insurance will be maintained in full
force and in effect at all times.

The Therapist understands that in performing this Agreement, the therapist may have
access to confidential information in possession of the District or others, including, but not
limited to names, facts or information about individuals, businesses and families. The
Therapist may also have access to confidential information potentially including student
directory, personnel information and records, sensitive, confidential or internal School
District matters and other protected information. It is agreed that the definition of
confidential information includes all documentary, electronic or oral information made
known to the Therapist through any activity related to this Agreement. The Therapist
agrees not to reveal any confidential information and understands that any such disclosure
shall be considered a breach of this Agreement. The Therapist agrees that if a subpoena is
received for divulgence of confidential information, the Therapist shall notify the District
prior to divulging the same. This section shall survive termination of this Agreement.

This Agreement may be terminated at any time without cause by mutual agreement in
writing between the District and the Therapist or upon thirty (30) days written notice by
either party.

The Therapist acknowledges and agrees that he/she or its employees will be subject to
fingerprinting and a criminal history record check as may be required by the Education Law
of the State of New York. In such event, the Therapist agrees to cooperate with the school
district and to complete any and all necessary forms or procedures, all at no cost or expense
to the District.
