This AGREEMENT made as of July 1, 2019 by and between

THE YONKERS SCHOOL DISTRICT having a place of business at One Larkin
Center, Yonkers, New York 10701 and being located in the County of Westchester
(“School District”)

and

IRVINGTON UFSD, having a place of business at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, New
York 10533 and being located in the County of Westchester, (the “Receiving School”).

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the School District is duly empowered by law to enter into a contract
with the Receiving School for the instruction of students with disabilities in those situations
where the School District does not have an appropriate program available; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Education (the “BOE”) of the School District has found
the Receiving School has an educational program which can appropriately provide instruction
to enrolled student(s); and

WHEREAS, the Receiving School is desirous of providing such instruction on the terms
and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein contained,
the parties hereto do agree as follows:

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES: The Receiving School shall provide appropriate instruction
and/or related services to Yonkers resident student(s) placed by the Schoo! District’s Committee
on Special Education (“CSE”), Services shall include. without limitation:

a. Implementation of goals and objectives for the educational services provided as
per each individual student's individualized education program (“IEP”);

b. Completion of progress reports regarding student achievement of objectives as per
report card schedules;

c, Complete all areas of the student(s)’ [EP including but not limited to student's
Present Levels of Performance, identifying the student's strengths and
weaknesses, management needs, transition (if applicable). testing
accommodations, program modifications;

d. Provide the Committee on Special Education (“CSE”) with annual progress
reports for each individual student receiving service to be reviewed at each

student's CSE meeting;

[OCR]
e. Complete evaluations as per the request of the School District’s Director of
Special Education or his/her designee, on an as needed basis, at rates mutually

agreed upon by both parties;
f. Comply with any testing requirements upon notification of such review dates; and
g. Comply with request for transcripts, report cards, Regents examination,

attendance sheets, and all other student records as the sending school may request,
such materials shall be provided within seven (7) days of the request.

The education provided by Receiving School shall be appropriate to the mental ability
and physical condition of the studeni(s) and in accordance with Commissioner’s Regulations, all
applicable Federal, State and local statutes, rules and ordinances. The Receiving School agrees
to provide services in accordance with the State Education Department’s (“NYSED”) regulations
using only professionals/service providers certified by the NYSED. All services shall be
provided in strict compliance with the student’s IEP. The Receiving School further agrees that
all disciplinary measures for disabled students will be conducted in accordance with applicable
federal, state and local laws, rules and regulations.

It is acknowledged and agreed that the parties hereto will share necessary student IEPs
electronically via an appropriate and confidential web-based system (currently JEP Direct).
Upon termination, the Receiving School agrees to release these documents by “unsharing” them
via the web-based system.

The Receiving School warrants that it has reviewed the IEP of each student to be enrolled
in its program(s) pursuant hereto, and represents that it is capable and willing to provide the
services for those student(s).

2. REPORTS: The Receiving School shall report in writing, at least twice each year to the
School District, summarizing the student's social and educational progress, stating the precise
nature of the program designated, and reporting all grades and attendance information. In
addition, within the timeframe not exceeding three (3) years from the date of placement, the
Receiving Schoo! will be responsible for providing the School District with a current
psychological, social history and medical examination.

3. STUDENTS RECEIVING SERVICES: It is understood and agreed that services will
be provided to those students who are referred by the School District and accepted by the
Receiving School It is acknowledged and agreed that al! such students must have approval from
the CSE of the School District for the requested services. Receiving School shall receive
payment only for the students who actually attend Receiving School.

4, TERM (SCHOOL YEAR): The term of this agreement shall extend from July 1, 2019
to June 30, 2020, but shall be terminable by the School District as to any one or more of the
student(s) as set forth below.

3. AUDIT: Prior to the making of any payments hereunder, the School District may, at its
option, audit such books and records of the Receiving School to substantiate the basis for
payment. The School District will not withhold payment pursuant to this paragraph for more
than thirty (30) days after payment would otherwise be due, unless the School District shall find
cause to withhold payment or the Receiving School fails to cooperate with such audit. The
School District’s right to audit shall survive for a period of one year following termination
hereof. The School District has the right to audit the performance hereof, including without
limitation. to make site visits in order to review records, observe the performance of services
and/or to conduct interviews of staff and patrons, where appropriate and not otherwise prohibited

by law.

The Receiving School hereby agrees to cooperate with any investigation, resulting from
performance under this agreement, by the Yonkers Inspector General. Receiving School further
agrees to provide any additional information the School District may reasonably require in order
to respond to any audits associated with the services rendered to our students.

6. RATE: The School District shall pay tuition to the Receiving School at the NYSED's
most current special education non-resident approved tuition rate per student upon the
presentation to it, on a monthly basis, by the Receiving School of proper invoices in a form
acceptable to the District. All such invoices must have a copy of the attendance record of the
student(s) attached. It acknowledged that no payment will be made to the Receiving School
during any period which no students from the School District are in attendance. It is mutually
understood and agreed by and between the parties hereto that total compensation to be paid for
tuition to the Receiving School shall be limited to the amount approved by the NYSED for the
special education program and services set forth in each student’s IEP and that the School
District will not be liable for payment of any sums in excess of the amount so approved, except
to the extent evaluations are completed at the request of the School District, then the mutually
agreed upon rates for the completed evaluations also will be paid to the Receiving School. In the
event that any of the students are residents of the Richmond Children's Center, other chapter 721
Intermediate Care facility, Boards of Cooperative Educational Services (“BOCES”) or other
public schools the payment shall be made in accordance with the reimbursement schedule
provided by the NYSED.

It is acknowledged and agreed that cost to the School District under this Agreement shal]
be approved by the City’s Board of Contract and Supply (“BOCS”) and BOE as part of an
aggregate amount allocated for out of district special education programs. Any increase of said
amount shall require additional approvals.

16 AVAILABILITY OF PERSONNEL: The Receiving School shall make itself available
to personnel designated by the School District for purposes of case discussions. personal
observations, educational reviews, impartial hearings and program visitations by prior
arrangement between the personnel involved. The Receiving School will not unreasonably refuse
to make available an appropriate staff member who provides services to the student for
telephonic consultations at CSE meetings in order to assist the School District in providing
appropriate recommendations and placements for the student’s IEP.
