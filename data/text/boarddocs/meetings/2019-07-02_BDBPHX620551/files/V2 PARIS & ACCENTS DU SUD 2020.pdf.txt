             DAY 2: PARIS                                                                                   Thursday 02-Apr-2020
                                                        Bienvenue en France! Meet your French Prometour Tour Director upon arrival at the airport
                                                        Transfer to your hotel to drop off your luggage, check-in will be later
                                                        While in Paris, travel as the locals do by walking or taking public transportation
                                                        Enter Sainte Chapelle; a masterpiece of flamboyant Gothic architecture with its unique stained glass

                                                        Discover the Quartier Latin with your Tour Director, and make a stop at the Le Petit Prince Store, it is

                                                        Have lunch on your own
                           Metro de Paris               Have a guided tour of the Opera Garnier, probably the most famous Opera house in the world, and

                                                        Return to your hotel to freshen up and get ready for dinner
                                                        Have dinner with your group in a local restaurant
                       •        •       •       •       Overnight accommodation in Paris




             DAY 3: EXCURSION: VERSAILLES                                                                   Friday 03-Apr-2020
                                                        Breakfast at the hotel with your group
                                                        Board the RER and head to Versailles
                                                        With a local guide, visit the Château de Versailles, one of the most beautiful achievements of 18th-
                                                        century French art; some of the rooms open to visit are the Grand Apartments, the Royal Chapel and the
                                                        Hall of Mirrors
                                                        Explore the elegantly landscaped Gardens of Château de Versailles
                                                        Have lunch on your own
                                                        Board the RER and return to Paris
                     Chateau de Versailles              Discover Quartier Montmartre, known as "la Butte" and make sure to enter the Sacre Coeur Basilica
                                                        Have dinner with your group in a local restaurant
                                                        Overnight accommodation in Paris

                            •       •       •



             DAY 4: PARIS                                                                                   Saturday 04-Apr-2020
                                                        Breakfast at the hotel with your group
                                                        Climb to the top of the Arc de Triomphe, a monument that honors those who fought and died for France
                                                        and is the location of the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier
                                                        Stroll on the Champs-Elysées; it's over 1 mile long and is often described as the world's most beautiful
                                                        avenue
                                                        Admire the Egyptian Obelisk at the centre of the Place de la Concorde
                                                        Spend time in the Jardins des Tuileries, a wonderful place to watch Parisians go about their day, then
                                                        end your walk at Musée du Louvre's iconic glass pyramid
                                                        Have lunch on your own
                                                        Admire the architecture of Paris as you cruise along the River Seine on board a Bateau Mouche
                                                        Spend some time at the Eiffel Tower, always an iconic photo opportunity! (photo stop only)
                                                        Have dinner with your group in a local restaurant
                                                        Overnight accommodation in Paris

            DAY 5: PARIS - AVIGNON                                                                    Sunday 05-Apr-2020
                                                    Breakfast at the hotel with your group
                                                    Transfer by motor coach from your hotel to the train station
                                                    Take the TGV high speed train to Avignon
                                                    Transfer to your hotel to drop off your luggage, check-in will be done later
                                                    Have lunch on your own
                                                    With a local guide, visit the Palais des Papes, symbol of the church’s influence throughout the western
                                                    Christian world in the 14th century. It is the biggest Gothic palace in all of Europe
                                                    Have time in Avignon to investigate the tangle of tiny streets between Banasterie & Place des Carmes
                                                    Have dinner with your group in a local restaurant
                        Palais des Papes            Overnight accommodation in Avignon


                              •       •



             DAY 6: EXCURSION: PONT DU GARD & NIMES Monday 06-Apr-2020
                                                    Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group
                                                    Have your bus for the day
                                                    Stop at Pont du Gard; worth visiting for its historical importance, it was added to UNESCO's list of World
                                                    Heritage Sites in 1985
                                                    Photo stop at Pont du Gard, the famous three-level stone aqueduct that crosses the Gardon River Valle
                                                    Board your motor coach and continue on to Nimes, famous for its many Roman remains which are
                                                    mostly in an excellent state of preservation
                                                    Have lunch on your own
                                                    Follow your local guide on a walking tour of Nimes; see Porte Auguste, walk through Les Jardins de la
                         Pont Du Guard              Fontaine, pass by Castellum Aquae, and of course includes the Roman Arena and Maison Carrée, one
                                                    of the best preserved Roman ancient building in Southern France (Includes entrances)
                                                    Board your motor coach and return to Avignon
                    •     •       •       •   •     Have dinner with your group in a local restaurant
                                                    Explore the cafe-lined Place de l'Horloge, the site of the city's imposing Hotel de Ville and clock tower
                                                    Overnight accommodation in Avignon




             DAY 7: AVIGNON - ANTIBES - NICE                                                           Tuesday 07-Apr-2020
                                                    Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group
                                                    Board your motor coach and travel to Antibes
                                                    Walk around the beautiful town of Antibes; it has 16 miles of coastline, many parks such as the Exflora
                                                    Park, the Garoupe Lighthouse providing the best view, and a 16th-century star-shaped fort, Fort Carré
                                                    Have lunch on your own
                                                    Board your motor coach and continue on to Nice, the heart of the Côte d'Azur
                                                    With your Prometour Tour Director explore Vieux Nice; the Old Town is a maze of narrow streets
                                                    teeming with local life, buildings painted a faded Roman gold, flower-strewn squares, and traditional
                                                    boulangeries
                                                    Have dinner with your group in a local restaurant
                                                    Walk along Promenade des Anglais; paid for by Nice’s English colony in 1822, it follows the complete
                                                    4km sweep of the Baie des Anges
                    •     •       •       •   •     Overnight accommodation in Nice

                                                    Board your flight in Nice and begin the journey back to New York
