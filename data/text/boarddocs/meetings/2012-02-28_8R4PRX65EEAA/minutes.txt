	4. Presentations/Discussions

		Information:  4.1 Transportation Review - Mr. Joe Urbanowicz, Transportation Coordinator, provided the Board of Education with an update on services provided in the 2011-12 school year. (Presentation)

		Mr. Urbanowicz reported that the total savings for the 2011-12 school year total $228,610. To realize savings, three buses were eliminated, out of district private school bus runs were co-mingled, an out-of-district special education student moved from the district, and savings on fuel purchases all contributed towards savings.  Mr. Urbanowicz also noted that routes were made more efficient.

		In response to the presentation, Mr. McCann asked that the figures that were presented be verified by the Business Office and to report back to the Board.

		It was noted that a subcommittee of the Budget Task force is looking at possible options to present to the Board of Education regarding Transportation. Members of the Subcommittee are with with Mr. Urbanowicz to see what options are available to Irvington to reduce busing expense, i.e. increasing distances and reducing one bus by making scheduling changes.

	5. Work Session - Budget Workshop

		Discussion:  5.2 Follow-up to Board Budget Requests (Presentation)

		Dr. Roelle provided a review of the proposed budget and as per the Board's request at the February 7, 2012 Board meeting to review the recommendation to eliminate a 1.0 guidance position as well as the elimination of the grade 6 world language noted that restoration of a .5 guidance position would be $58,800. The proposed model would be that a .5 position would serve as Guidance Chair with no caseload, handle special education testing accommodation requests as well as arrange for evening programs. The case load would be approximately 200 students per counselor.

		Mrs. Miller noted that information provided by the State Education Department today concerning allowance for retirement expenses and a change in calculating debt would have the current proposed budget $59,718 under the Tax Cap which was originally at $34,000 under. However, several additions to the budget are now necessary, totalling $85,471, but a reduction in the Superintendent's  salary code of $10,000 requires an additional $75,471 in reductions to remain under the Tax Cap.
