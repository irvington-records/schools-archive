6. Discussion/Presentation

Presentation:  6.1 Reopening Schools Update

Dr. Harrison noted that schools are rescheduled to reopen in January using a multifaceted approach utilizing different methods of teaching.

Ms. Ellis discussed:

- Update on the review of assessment data to determine impact of the fall semester.  She noted that due to Covid, assessments are now online and are labor intensive.

- Review of 3 year benchmarks was conducted and results are consistent across the board.

- Flex Wednesdays appear to be working with teachers having a block of time in the afternoon for professional development.

- Process for contact tracing is in place with a debriefing to follow every case.

Dr. Harrison commented that there is no known case of Covid spread between students and teachers in the District to date.  Currently, specials are streamed to students resulting in class contact

between teachers and students.  The District keeps the community updated reporting Covid data on a daily basis.

Dr. Kantor noted that the technology challenge is difficult for the younger students as it is hard to sustain their attention.  The emotional support team currently works with students and faculty as needed.

Teachers are finding creative ways to work with students utilizing masks such as in Dance.

Ms. Chapnick commented that we are still able to provide students with the ability to connect with one another and provide them with the opportunity to join clubs and other activities.

Mr. Sottile noted the daily challenges to the Middle School and Ms. Daley made additional comments.

Ms. Gevargis-Mizimakoski commented that teachers and students are virtual and in person and coming up with creative ways to interact with one another.

Discussion and questions from Board of Education Members to School faculity.
