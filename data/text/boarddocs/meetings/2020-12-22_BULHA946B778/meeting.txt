Special Meeting/Work Session

Tuesday, December 22, 2020

-  Video

The Monthly Work Session will allow for the Administration and Board of Education to have presentations/discussions on a particular area(s) pertaining to the District and its programs/operations.
Irvington Board of Education
Irvington Union Free School District
The Board of Education will meet in person in the Campus Presentation Room (CPR)
MS/HS Campus
40 N. Broadway
Irvington, NY 10533
No public attendees will be permitted to attend in person. All members of the public can view the meeting via Zoom**
*6:30 p.m. - Anticipated Executive Session
7:30 p.m. - Special Meeting/Work Session
