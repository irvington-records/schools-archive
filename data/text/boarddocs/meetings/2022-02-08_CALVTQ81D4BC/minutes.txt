5. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Information: 5.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison presented that Irvington School District recently hosted World Read Aloud day at the Main Street School. Dr. Harrison also discussed a number of upcoming events. Finally, Dr. Harrison gave a District-wide COVID-19 update. He advised that we continue to work with NYS, Westchester County, school nurses to ensure that all measures are taken to provide for the health and wellbeing of our students and staff members.

Information: 5.2 Committee Reports

• Maura Gedid – Communications Committee – We spoke about the COVID-19 communications strategy.

• Brian Friedman – Curriculum Committee – We discussed two topics. We discussed the technology plan and the plan for board member visits into the schools.

• Erin Bernstein – Policy Committee – We discussed student’s funds management. We discussed internal controls.

• Jayne Wissner – District Safety Liaison Committee – We reviewed the new safety plans for students including medications, community drills and access for doors.

• Jayne Wissner – Community Advisory Board – We had two new trustees as guest speakers. We discussed local policy issues and how the impact taxes.

• Beth Propper – WPSBA Liaison Committee PTSA – We discussed amendments to the tax caps, there has been interest to keep the tax cap at 2%. Carryover as unused levy was discussed.

• Susan Brunenvas – Irvington PTSA Liaison Committee – We discussed several upcoming events, read-a-thon, virtual science fair, etc.

• Brian Friedman – Strategic Partnership Council – We discussed opportunities to enrich the experiences of students at all levels.

7. Presentation/Discussion

Presentation: 7.1 2022-23 Budget Development

Dr. Harrison presented an overview of Irvington’s budget process. He discussed import of considering tax cap compliance and ensuring the district maintains strong reserves. The Board will adopt the budget at its regular meeting April 19, 2022. Ms. Stein presented on the 2022-23 budget landscape. Action (Consent): 10.4 Bid Rejection Facilities Storage Building at High School SED #66-04-02-02-2-022-001

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District received bids for Contract G-General Construction for the Facilities Storage Building at the High School (hereinafter referred to as the “Project”) on December 20 2021; WHEREAS, the School District’s Architect, Construction Manager and Administration have reviewed the bids submitted by the bidders for the General Construction contract in connection with the Project; WHEREAS, based upon such review, the Board of Education believes it to be in the best interest of the School District to reject all General Construction contract bids received for the Facility Storage Building at the High School Project and re-advertise for new bids for this Project at a later date; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Board of Education hereby rejects all bids submitted for the General Construction contract for the Facility Storage Building at the High School Project.
