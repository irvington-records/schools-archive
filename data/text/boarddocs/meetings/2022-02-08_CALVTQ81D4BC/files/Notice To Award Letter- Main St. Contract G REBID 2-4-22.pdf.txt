Based on the above bid information, pending any final review required by District Council, the apparent responsible low
bidder is Grace Contracting and Development, Inc. for the Total Suggested Contract Amount indicated above.

The references provided by Grace Contracting and Development, Inc. gave no reason for disqualification. In addition,
the New York State Department of Labor has indicated that they are not debarred from doing public work, and there are
no known OSHA violations against this contractor in the past (7) seven years. Our Project scope review with this
contractor indicates an understanding of the project requirements and expectations. And the Companies bond strength,
financial stability, and insurance coverage meets or exceed contract requirements.
