3. Student Recognition

Mr. McCormack spoke of this year’s team and Coach Maher’s amazing leadership, along with Barbara Constantine and Mike Toolen and highlighted the Girls Basketball team’s accomplishments over the last eight years, which include six league titles, seven sectional titles, six regional titles, five state finals, four state championships and three federation championships.  This year’s team went on to the state finals but lost to Seton Catholic.  Four team members were recognized individually: Lindsay Halpin: All league, All Section, All State League and Sectional Tournament Most Valuable Player, Kelly Degnan was the Con Ed Award winner and Olivia Valdes was named State All Conference Player in the State Sectional Tournament and Heather Hall received the State Sportsmanship Award.  Coach Gina Maher spoke of how special this year’s team has been, especially since there were members from 8th grade to 12, helping each other, rallying around a team member who was hospitalized. She also noted that each member is also a Scholar Athlete which is very rare. The Sectional Title Plaque and Gold Ball were presented to the team.

4. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

- Dr. Harrison thanked the school community for supporting the 2017-18 budget, passing by 78% which sends a strong message for the support of the schools. He also congratulated Maria Kashkin who was elected to a 3-year term.

- The second proposition to increase the board to seven members was also approved and noted that nominating petitions are now available through the District Clerk and also provided a list of important dates in connection with the vote which will take place on July 11th.

- Update on the Logo selection process and committee, noting that students and staff will vote on 5 to 6 designs and finalists will go to the Board for their consideration.

- The rollout of the new homepage of the District’s website has been delayed due to the logo adoption process. The goal is to introduce the new website possibly in September.

- He noted that the students have had so many accomplishments across the school district, from musical performances, academic competitions or athletics and encouraged everyone to look at the weekly newsletter or Facebook to keep up with the accomplishments of the students.

Carol Stein provided an update on the work on East Field.

6. Discussions

To address the topics that have been brought up at past Board of Ed meetings or in communications with the Board and to be sure that topics are addressed, Dr. Harrison provided updates on the following:

- East Field and other field related updates will be continued

- Transportation concerns surrounding efficiency and early pick up times especially at the high school had been expressed by parents. A committee has been formed to review the concerns and meetings are being scheduled with administrators and representative group of parents as well as the transportation coordinator. The first meeting will focus on the scope of the committee and identify any possible changes that could be done in the short term as well as possible long term solutions. Ms. Hargraves also asked if the committee will review transportation for private schools and special ed transportation as she believes that these also need to be looked at for possible efficiencies. Dr. Harrison noted that there are funds available to have a consultant to review the bus schedules. Mr. Hanna would like to have regular updates including the direction the committee is going in as it would be good to track progress as it is a challenging topic. A communication strategy will be developed to communicate the progress the committee is making.

- Food Service - looking for opportunities to enhance existing program. Decision not to go for an RFP as there might be changes to the Federal program. Aramark is looking for ways to enhance and expand offerings. There will be pilots of additional meals offered. Aramark is taking ownership of the program and is very involved and receptive to working with the District. There will also be upgrades to the quality of the food provided in certain cases.

- Drug use concerns were addressed through a leadership letter expressing such concerns about the subject and meetings have been heldwith the Mayor, Police Dept. and iASK . District asked for greater partnership with the Village, the Police and iASK to move forward with a responsible educational program in the schools for next year. Looking to schedule a presentation at the end of September to unveil drug survey data related to students in Irvington and report the feedback from students from the 2014 and 2016 surveys and looking at the students' perception on the issue of drugs and drug use in Irvington. This will serve as a launching pad with possible community events as well as targeted programs for parents and students on the subject. Other opportunities are being explored to integrate learning for students. There will be a follow up letter to the community by the end of the year that will set forth expectations. Most of the funding will come from the iASK grant that has two more years (on the five year grant) as well as some rollover funds. As an aside, the President's proposed budget doesn't include funding for these types of programs which was previously talked about and would have a serious impact on programming. Mrs. Palmieri asked where the funding has been spent and if it were possible to have a listing of what they have funded through the grant money. Mrs. Palmieri expressed her frustrations that we have been educating our students about the dangers of drug use, dancing around the challenges but not sure that education is the answer and what is expected to change by spending the money. Dave Graeber also asked about working with adults as it is often the starting point where the use of prescription drugs begins. Also need to educate parents about hosting parties, prescription use or educating coaches about abuses and what they should do if they suspect students are using drugs. Catherine Palmieri believes that there should be consequences to behaviors as there doesn't seem to be at this time. Dr. Harrison believes there is a responsibility to educate our students, but there are dangers in transitions and society that are affecting perceptions in and around drugs, etc. and that there is value to continue educating our students. A forum was hosted last spring with the Village, an ER doctor and was a targeted effort. With respect to athletes, there is a review of our Athletic Code of Conduct, however, it is difficult if offences occur outside of school and school’s ability to intervene. The Village will need to be involved in these conversations.

- Booster Club - Another challenge has been associated with funding our athletic programs and there has been discussions about forming a fund raising vehicle to support aspects of the program that will support athletics and other clubs. There are legal aspects to address and approaches that have been taken will be discussed over the next couple of weeks. Deb Hargraves noted that she has sat in on meetings doesn’t think that there has been a clear message as to what the booster club should look like. It was discussed at the last meeting to run a few groups that will deal with the different aspects so that it isn't one person's responsibility to pull it together, as no one has wanted to take on the role as president. A mission statement is needed with clear expectations of what needs to be done. It was suggested that perhaps at the upcoming sports award dinner and scholastic achievement dinners that it be mentioned that there is an effort to get a club organized which might generate interest among parents.

- Dr. Harrison also touched upon other topics such as UBD/Rubicon, master scheduling at the HS, discipline statistics will be provided at the end of the year but to get a custom report is very difficult and have been trying to develop a tool to easily generate reports.

- Topics such Naviance and Code of Conduct have been mentioned but will need clarification as to what is being asked. Dr. Harrison provided an overview of the program and what it can provide. With respect to the Code of Conduct, Catherine Palmieri would like it to lay out expectations and what the repercussions would be if a student violates any aspects of the code.

- Dr. Harrison will provide an update on the athletic handbook but noted that there is good progress being made. Deb Hargraves would also like to discuss beautification efforts, but there other items to discuss and it was suggested that it would be an agenda item at the next B&G meeting and there is value to having all committees be strategic in approach but also to have regularly scheduled meetings. With seven members, it will be less a challenge to schedule ongoing committee meetings which will enable meeting objectives. A goal of the Board might be to define how committees can be made effective and outline priorities and objectives.

- Dr. Harrison updated the Board as to the several positions open in the district and the thinking of possibly restructuring the current position held by Patty Pancotto, possibly other responsibilities taken on by his new secretary as well as the security position at the District office.

	Mr. Graeber like follow-up again on the staff professional development plan for both teachers and administrators.

- Mr. Hanna noted that there was a commitment to develop a survey process. Dr. Harrison has reached out to other superintendents and it appears that they are done by outside vendors. Michael Hanna also mentioned the topic of gender equity has been raised, especially in the sciences and would like to see how Irvington is doing in that area.

	Mr. Whitney noted that the Board committed to developing a Board Handbook and he, along with Deb Hargraves, are editing the handbook and organizing it. He also noted that Deb Hargraves has done an amazing job in editing it and the document is in good shape, with some outstanding questions for legal. A first read should be done on June 6th and would try to have this completed and adopted on June 20th so it is available for the new Board members. He also suggests that the Board compare the edited version that is in GoogleDocs with that which was provided by the consultants as it is somewhat different. Deb Hargraves believes that this will be inclusive of information that is provided in the Handbook and other documents.
