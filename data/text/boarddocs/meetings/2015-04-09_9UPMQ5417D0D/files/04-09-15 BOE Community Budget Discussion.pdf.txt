Budget-Development Objectives
 Develop fiscally responsible budget that is mindful of the impact of the
  school budget on the entire school community
 Provide opportunity for:
     New and expanded student experiences
     Enhanced curriculum
     Professional development
     Maintain and improve facilities
 Sustain required and valued services and programs:
     AP courses, honors, electives
     Music programs
     Extracurricular experiences
       Clubs & athletics
     Bus monitors
     Special education
     Support services (i.e. Guidance & Student Assistance Counselor)

    Our Process
     January 27th: Principals reported on their school’s progress and
      projected future needs
     February 10th: Administration presented considerations for
      advancing curriculum, programs, and student experiences
     March 10th: Administration shared operational needs,
      anticipated revenue, and projected tax-levy cap
     March 24th: Superintendent’s Proposed Budget; approval of
      Capital Reserve Fund Proposition; and direction provided to
      Administration
     Tonight: Administration to present budget adjustments to
      accommodate Board tax rate targets

     Builds upon current programs and success
     Expands opportunity for technology integration
     Provides more support for all students
     Enhances infrastructure to compliment school operations
     Demonstrates fiscal responsibility

     Concern with linkage between increase of approximately $110,000 and the
      successful negotiation of a new APPR plan
