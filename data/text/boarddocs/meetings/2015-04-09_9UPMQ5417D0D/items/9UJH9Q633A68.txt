The regulations of the Commissioner of the DOH have been updated regarding immunization requirements. NYSSBA’s policy and regulation have been changed to align with the new regulations. Also, NYSSBA’s policy incorporates Irvington’s current Immunization of Students (7511), Student Physicals (7512); Administration of Medication (7513); Health Records (7514); and Allergens – Management and Accommodation (7515). Upon adoption of NYSSBA’s Student Health Services,  the current policies will be revoked.
