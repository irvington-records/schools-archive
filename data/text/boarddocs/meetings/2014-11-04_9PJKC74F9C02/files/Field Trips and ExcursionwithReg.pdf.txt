SUBJECT: FIELD TRIPS AND EXCURSIONS

       The Board of Education recognizes the desirability of providing off-campus experiences
which will reinforce or enhance the offered educational curriculum of the District. The
Superintendent of Schools, in consultation with the Building Principal, will determine the
frequency and content of school-sponsored field trips and excursions. Each student must secure
the permission of his/her parent and/or legal guardian before participating in such school-
sponsored field trips and excursions.

        Factors relevant in consideration of approval of such field trips may include the
relationship to the curriculum, benefits to students, the distance of the trip, availability of
transportation and personnel, sufficient liability insurance, the cost involved, weather conditions,
and full utilization of transportation. In order to make necessary transportation arrangements, all
requests for day field trips must be submitted to the appropriate Building Principal at least one
month prior to the field trip date.

        A field trip and excursion must be provided for all students involved in the curriculum or
District offered program to which the field trip and excursion is related. No student may be
excluded from a traditional curriculum-based field trip and excursion because of fees or costs.
Optional trips or excursions may be offered by the district for which the district may choose not
to fund. In such cases, the district will make reasonable efforts to identify fundraising efforts to
offset expenses. To the extent that particular students do not participate in a field trip and
excursion, an appropriate educational experience and proper supervision shall be afforded and
provided to the same non-participating students.

       On all school-sponsored field trips and excursions, provisions will be made for proper
supervision by school employees. Parents may assist or volunteer with such proper supervision.

Overnight or Long-Distance Field Trips

        Field trips and excursions in excess of one instructional day involving overnight and/or
international travel must be approved by the Board of Education prior to making any
commitments or arrangements. Requests for overnight trips should be made at least three (3)
months in advance of the planned event, while requests for international trips (except trips to
Canada) should be made at least six (6) months in advance of the date of departure.

         The Board of Education reserves the right to cancel any overnight and/or international
trip at any time prior to the date of travel if in its collective and sole discretion it believes that it
is not safe for students to travel to the planned destination.

Non-School Sponsored/Private Trips

       No person employed by the District shall solicit students to participate in any non-school
sponsored/private trip during school hours, on school grounds, or using school/district resources.

No individual or entity shall use the name of the District or any of its organizations in connection
with a non-school sponsored/private trip.

        Any employee of the District who leads, sponsors, or participates in a non-school
sponsored/private trip involving students of the District shall, prior to the trip, inform the
students, in writing, that the trip is not sponsored by the District and that the District shall not be
responsible for any aspect of the trip. A copy of the written notification shall thereafter be sent
to the Superintendent of Schools.

Transportation

        When the District provides transportation to students on a school-sponsored field trip and
excursion, extracurricular activity or any other similar event, it shall provide transportation back
to either the point of departure or to the appropriate school in the District unless extenuating
circumstances exist wherein the parents and/or legal guardians of the student makes a written
request of the building Principal for return transportation.

       Where intervening circumstances have made transportation back to the point of departure
or to the appropriate school in the District impractical, a representative of the District shall
remain with the student until such student’s parent or legal guardian has been contacted and the
student has been delivered to his/her parent or legal guardian.

FIELD TRIPS AND EXCURSIONS REGULATION


School Sponsored Field Trips

     The following guidelines and regulations are set to implement the planning of school-
sponsored field trips and excursions as part of, and directly related to, instructional activities:

     a)    All trips and excursions, and the arrangement therefore, must have the approval of the
           school Principal and the Superintendent of Schools utilizing the district field trip
           application form.

     b)    Pupils may be asked to pay all, or part of the expenses of field trips or excursions,
           provided arrangements can be made for the payment of trip expenses for pupils
           unable to do so. If some students do not participate in the field trip or excursion,
           acceptable educational alternatives must be provided.

     c)    Transportation arrangements for field trips or excursions must follow state
           regulations.

     d)    Each child who goes on a field trip or excursion must have written parental
           permission.

     e)       Supervision must be provided to provide for enriching, safe experiences and
           ensure to effective student management.

           Elementary Supervision on Field Trips and excursions:

           The Board requires that all Elementary trips (Kindergarten – 5) will have a 6 – 1
           student-chaperone ratio.

           Secondary Supervision on Field Trips and excursions:

             i. The Board requires that all in-state secondary trips (6-8) will have a minimum of
                    a 12 – 1 student-chaperone ratio.
            ii. The Board requires that all in-state secondary trips (9-12) will have a minimum of
                    12 – 1 student-chaperone ratio.
           iii. Out-of-state/overnight trips for secondary students (6-12) will have a maximum of
                    10 – 1 student-chaperone ratio depending upon activities and site visited.
           iv. Selection and use of chaperones should take into consideration the impact of staff
                    absences on total school program.

     f)       In case of an emergency or unanticipated danger, the Board reserves the right to
           cancel, modify or shorten any field trip or excursion before, during or after it has
           begun. Should the tour operator or other third party be unable or unwilling to refund
           any pre-paid costs, the Board will not be responsible for refunding any monies.
           While field trips may be related to curriculum, there is no requirement to participate,
           and non-participation will not impact your child’s grade.

g)       In situations where not all of the students of a class are invited to participate in an
     overnight field trip or excursion, it is the expectation of the district that meaningful
     instruction shall continue for the non-participating students and that a written plan
     shall be prepared prior to the trip outlining the procedures in-place to ensure that
     meaningful instruction for the non-participating students will not be compromised.

h)      It shall be the obligation and responsibility of any student participating in an
     overnight trip to make up any missed lessons and assignments.
