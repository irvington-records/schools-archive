	4. Superintendent's Report

-
		Dr. Harrison thanked everyone who attended the concussion management workshop last week, with approximately 45 community members in attendance. Speakers included Dr. Mark Herceg, Dr. Hough, and athletic trainers from Symmetry Physical Therapy.

-
		On Thursday, November 6th, the annual college aid night will be help for parents to begin to plan for financing college tuition.

-
		Over the past several months, there has been concerns regarding ED-V68 as well as the Ebola virus, and assured the Board and the community that the district is following guidelines from Department of Health and the District's physician. The nurses have been trained and reviewed best practices for following protocols. Appropriate cleaning products have been purchased in preparation should any outbreak of illness affect the District.

-
		Part 154 of the Commissioner's Regulations pertain to English language learners or bilingual education. The regulations have been revised and take effect in the 2015-16 school year and it is foreseen that there will be impacts on programs as well as budgetary implications due to these changes.

	Mr. Palermo provided an overview of the changes that the district will need to adhere to i.e. where the services are provided and the amount of time provided. The change includes having to provide services in the classroom, as opposed to the current model of pulling students out of class for delivery of services. Mr. Palermo noted that this will be particularly difficult in the secondary level with the elementary grades having the largest group of English language learners. Looking at our second language population, it is anticipated that an additional 1.0 FTE ESL teacher will be needed to provide the level of services required. Mr. Palermo also noted that there is a new requirement to provide an additional 90 minute session for two years to those students who have tested out of the program. Dr. Kor noted that this model will align with other push in services and support the students in the work that is taking place in the classroom. Dr. Harrison noted that this will be an underfunded mandated that the District will need to absorb the additional staffing costs.

	Technology Update

	Dr. Kor reported on the work that has been undertaken in the District in the area of technology since the presentation to the Board in June 2014 which has been to develop the plan as to what the teachers will be expected to do in the classroom regarding technology and how the District will support those requirements. A Technology Summit has been taking place during the week that covers the eight goals of the Technology Framework with teachers who will then bring back the work under each goal and then work with their building to develop a building-level plan. The ESTI are the standards that are being used to describe what technology integration looks like. There were approximately 20 staff members for each goal which took 3 hours for each session.

	Following Dr. Kor’s update, Board members made comments and had questions regarding the Technology Summit. Mr. Montgomery noted that approximately 420 hours of staff time was needed for attendance and asked about the instruction time for students whose teachers were participating. Dr. Kor noted that there were substitutes in the classroom. Mrs. Kerner noted that it appears that each goal is being putting it into action in the classroom then followed by formalization the goals. Dr. Kor noted that not all plans are ready to be implemented due to the need for further technology. Mrs. Kerner feels that a plan is being implemented but it is not being called as such and is looking to see where the Board fits into developing the vision and the goal of technology in the District. She noted that she didn't realize that the summit was going to be so time intensive and is just trying to understand the concept. Dr. Kor asked for clarity as to what Mrs. Kerner is asking and that it will be the teachers who need to integrate technology in the District. Mr. Whitney noted that the Board hasn't created the vision but rather that they desire to have a technology plan and asking the administration report on the plan.

	Dr. Kor noted that there will be budgetary implications to implement some of the options and new offerings which will be part of the 2015-16 budget discussions, such as expanding Project Lead the Way that could be brought to elementary, middle and high school.

	6. Business of the Board I

	Action, Policy - 1st Reading, Policy - Adoption: 6.1 Policy - #8460 - Field Trips and Excursions

	The Board is being asked to waive the second reading requirement in order to adopt the revised policy. The revision eliminates the Board being required to approve trips to Canada, especially in view of a proposed trip to Canada by the High school French Teachers.

	Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education hereby waives the second reading of Policy #8460 - Field Trips and Excursions due to the need to update said policy as per Policy #1410.

	Be It Further Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education hereby adopts Policy #8460 Field Trips and Excursions on the first reading in accordance with Board Policy #1410.

	Report: 6.2 Long Range Planning Committee - October 30, 2014

	Mr. Whitney reported that the committee, consisting of Mr. Whitney, Mr. Montgomery and Dr. Harrison, met on October 31, 2014. Mr. Montgomery who is part of the committee, reported that it was discussed to restart the long-term planning process and what type of input would be asked from the community. The committee will develop a first draft of a mission/vision goals which would then be brought to the full Board for discussion, leading to a second draft at which point the community will be for feedback and if need, further reworking with the end of the process resulting in a 3-5 year plan with specifics and the possibility of rolling over the district goals from year to year. Discussion with the community is anticipated to be scheduled for January with finalization by March 2015.

	Report: 6.3 Communications Committee Report - 10/24/14

	Mr. Whitney and Mrs. Kerner are members of the communications committee along with Dr. Harrison and met on October 30. The committee reviewed the communications during the bond referendum and then discussed what is needed in the District at this time, i.e. to hire a professional service to handle press releases and possibly posting to Facebook. The three vendors' proposals, BOCES, Syntax and a firm that is utilized by Hastings School District, were reviewed were approximately $10,000 for services which is an increase of $5,000 to the budget. The committee then discussed the merits of securing a professional service. Dr. Harrison noted that it is desirable to enhance the District's communications and that in doing so, this would be an opportunity to take a step forward and bring education to the forefront in these communications.

	Mrs. Kerner noted that it seems to her that five hours a week might be not enough time to do all that is proposed, therefore more hours will be need, increasing the costs

	much more than the $10,000, leading to her questioning as to how best to figure out a realistic number of hours that we are currently spending on communications. Dr. Harrison reported that the time budgeted will be closely monitored in order not to exceed a cap and a system will be developed to track time and expenses. Mr. Whitney suggested that Dr. Harrison might ask the vendors to present the costs as a retainer in order to contain the costs and not be open ended in order for the Board to feel comfortable that the expenses will not be exorbitant.

	Discussion: 12.2 Hiring Protocols

	This discussion is a continuation of the discussion on the hiring protocols that took place at the September 23, 2014 Board meeting. Mr. Whitney noted that at the Board’s recent retreat best practices in the area of hiring was discussed, and it was noted that there is no one particular number of final candidates that a Board would meet with. The facilitator also noted that it was unusual for the Board to review resumes of candidates, which has also been a request by some board members in the past.

	Mr. Montgomery also noted that he had discussed that he would want the Board to have the opportunity to meet with the administration to express their opinions and views regarding what they would want to see in a particular candidate pool. Mrs. Kashkin recalling a previous conversation regarding this topic, was that it is the principal who knows what would be needed in a candidate. Mr. Montgomery believes that it is the Board's responsibility to express their views as they are the ones that vote on the hiring. Mr. Whitney stated that the hiring protocol document is not one that the Board votes on whereas Mr. Montgomery believes that he could put forth a motion even though he believes that he would not be supported.

	One particular change will be for the Board to be represented when interviewing the Asst. Principals. Mrs. Kerner also requested that the Board meet with potential administrative interim hires. The other edit noted at the previous discussion at the September 23rd meeting will be identified and incorporated into the document.

	The Board reviewed the revised goals where goal #5 was reworded to read: Uphold high standards for District staff which the Board was in agreement with. Mr. Montgomery noted that these goals do not meet his expectations as there are no specific individual goals under each item, which would be a more 'to do' list which these goals are not. 	Final Resolution: Motion Carries

	Yes: Phil Whitney, Robyn Kerner, Maria Kashkin, John Montgomery

	Discussion: 12.2 Hiring Protocols

	This discussion is a continuation of the discussion on the hiring protocols that took place at the September 23, 2014 Board meeting. Mr. Whitney noted that at the Board’s recent retreat best practices in the area of hiring was discussed, and it was noted that there is no one particular number of final candidates that a Board would meet with. The facilitator also noted that it was unusual for the Board to review resumes of candidates, which has also been a request by some board members in the past.

	Mr. Montgomery also noted that he had discussed that he would want the Board to have the opportunity to meet with the administration to express their opinions and views regarding what they would want to see in a particular candidate pool. Mrs. Kashkin recalling a previous conversation regarding this topic, was that it is the principal who knows what would be needed in a candidate. Mr. Montgomery believes that it is the Board's responsibility to express their views as they are the ones that vote on the hiring. Mr. Whitney stated that the hiring protocol document is not one that the Board votes on whereas Mr. Montgomery believes that he could put forth a motion even though he believes that he would not be supported.

	One particular change will be for the Board to be represented when interviewing the Asst. Principals. Mrs. Kerner also requested that the Board meet with potential administrative interim hires. The other edit noted at the previous discussion at the September 23rd meeting will be identified and incorporated into the document.
