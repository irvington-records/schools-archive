Special Meeting (Monday, January 23, 2012)

Generated by Elaine Cardea on Monday, January 23, 2012

Board Members Present:  Mr. John Dawson, Mrs. Robyne Camp, Mr. Robert Grados, Mr. James McCann. Absent: Mr. Phil Whitney.

Also present: Dr. Robert Roelle, Superintendent of Schools, Ms. Beverly Miller, Assistant Supt. for Business, Mr. James Groven, President, IFA. Members of community and press.

1. Call to Order

         Mr. John Dawson called the meeting to order at 7:08 a.m.

2. Acceptance of Agenda

Action:  2.1 Motion to Accept Agenda

A motion was made by Mrs. Robyne Camp, seconded by Mr. James McCann to accept the agenda as presented.

Vote: 4-0 approved.

3. Comments From Members of the Community

        Mrs. Catherine Johnson commented on teachers wearing union tee shirts at meetings.

4. Business of the Board

Action:  4.1 Approval of Memorandum of Agreement between Irvington UFSD and the Irvington Teachers' Association (Presentation)

A motion was made by Mr. Robert Grados, seconded by Mrs. Robyne Camp, to approve the following resolution:

BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District hereby authorizes and approves the Memorandum of Agreement between the Board and the Irvington Faculty Association, dated January 9, 2012; and BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board of Education hereby authorizes the Superintendent of Schools to incorporate said Memorandum of Agreement into a more formal written agreement and further, authorizes the Superintendent of Schools to execute said formal written agreement.

Vote: 4-0 approved.

Mr. Dawson thanked the members of the Board of Education and the members of the Irvington Faculty Association for their hard work to achieve this contract. Also thanks were extended to former Board Members and Teacher Union members who  previously had worked on the negotiations .

5. Motion to Adjourn

Action:  5.1 Motion to Adjourn

       A motion was made by Mr. Robert Grados, seconded by Mr. James McCann, to adjourn the meeting at 7:12 a.m.

       Vote: 4-0 approved.
