	10. Presentations/Discussions

	Discussion: 10.1 Hiring Protocols

	Dr. Kor stated that the Hiring protocols document was The document outlines a series of steps that administrators follow, including forms that are used across the district, as well as what can and cannot be discussed during interviews. It also

	Dr. Kor addressed the questions and comments from the Board regarding the hiring protocol document, and stated that the document was generated over a year ago and represents best practices in hiring in the field of education, especially teacher hiring and ensures that the process is the same for each building and also ensures that everyone that sits on a hiring committee will know what is expected of them as members of the committee.

	Mrs. Kerner had hoped to have a broader discussion on the hiring of the administrators and believes that the Board didn't sign off on the process, i.e. the Board interviewing only one candidate and, in her view, it is the Board's role is to represent the community, and that in the past, the Board would interview 2-3 candidates. She would like to have a discussion with the Board on their thoughts on the current practices. Mrs. Kashkin noted that she is not comfortable judging a candidate on one meeting after they have gone through the extensive interview process and as for the hiring of teachers, she believes that the Board's involvement might not be appropriate as there are dynamics needed, level of experience, etc. and doesn't feel that she is prepared in making those kind of judgments.  Mr. Montgomery stated that he feels that an initial discussion was lacking with the Board about what qualifications they would like to see in a hirer for a particular position, mainly for administration but possibly for teaching staff as well, and also feels that the Board is excluded from the process and does not have a way of determining if a particular candidate is the right person. He is interested in having more information as the process progresses and for the Board to have a greater role. Mr. Montgomery also noted that a best practice noted in a NYSSBA document would be for the Board to have an initial discussion before the process begins.

	Questions were also asked regarding the selection of committee members, particularly parents, the advertising of positions as well as how the written portion of an interview is conducted.

	Mr. Whitney questioned if discussions must take place in an executive session as it pertains to qualities and qualifications of candidates and believes that the Superintendent is hired to hire our administrators but thinks that to some extent the Board is providing an oversight but not second guessing a choice. He also doesn't believe that the Board is skilled enough to hire educational professionals and wonders what would happen if there was a disagreement with the Superintendent's candidate.

	Both Mrs. Kerner and Mr. Montgomery would like to find a means to have the Board more active in the hiring process. Mr. Whitney noted that this will be a topic of discussion at the upcoming Board's retreat.

	Mr. Whitney also noted that the Board is not voting on the document and with Mr. Grados not present, the discussion should be tabled at this time. The discussion will continue on a future agenda.

	Discussion: 10.2 2014-15 District Goals

	Mr. Whitney thanked Mrs. Kerner and Mr. Montgomery for crafting the six aspirational goals being discussed. Mr. Montgomery prefaced the conversation by stating that the intent of the goals was to think through what is really cared about for the district as the starting point. The Board discussed the individual goals, and it was noted that the “aspirational” goals are broader than the previous versions. The goals include:

-
		Develop a long-term plan, including a clarification of the District’s values and priorities, an assessment of its achievement of those values and priorities and the development of a detailed 5-year plan for attaining the values and priorities.

-
		Provide top-quality instruction for all students.

-
		Develop and maintain a curriculum that offers all students the opportunity to attain their academic potential.

-
		Ensure that each school provides a rich and supportive environment for all students, welcoming all students, fostering their academic and social development, and preparing them well for the next phase of their education.

-
		Uphold high standards for District staff while ensuring that all staff who meet those standards have meaningful career opportunities, professional development, and a satisfying work environment.

-
		Use all taxes and other funds raised by the District efficiently and effectively, and maintain the District’s fiscal health to balance the interests of current and future taxpayers.

	The Board discussed the individual goals and possible redundancy with a long term plan that will be developed. The Board and administration continued to dialogue in an attempt clarifying certain language contained in the goals to be sure that the intent of the goals is clear, specifically goals 1 and 5. Several suggestions were made for rewording to achieve clarify. The suggestions will be addressed in the next version of the goals. Mr. Whitney also stated that he doesn't see a problem with aspirational goals aligning with long range goals.

	There was further conversation regarding the addition of sub-goals and Mrs. Kashkin feels that aspirational goals could roll over from year to year with sub-goals providing more specificity and provide objective review of attainment or progress. Mr. Montgomery noted that the process might need to be defined and to look at what the administrators are already focusing on before deciding on specific sub-goals looking at and defining what matters through conversations. Mrs. Kerner suggested that the administrators process this conversation and then have continued discussion with the Board as it reviews the priorities of the district and what areas should be the focus of sub-goals, i.e. foreign language, ELA, technology under the curriculum goal.

	Dr. Harrison stressed the importance of relaying the intent of a goal in order for administration to have a dialogue with the administrations in order form them also to be involved in the discussion on areas to prioritize, defining what success looks like for Irvington and then how the District measures up to that definition.
