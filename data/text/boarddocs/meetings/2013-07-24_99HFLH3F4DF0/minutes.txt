		Resolution: WHEREAS, Thomas Behrens, was appointed to a three (3) year probationary appointment in the Teaching Assistant tenure area, effective September 1, 2011; and WHEREAS, Mr. Behrens also possesses a New York State teaching certificate in the tenure are of Physical Education; and WHEREAS, effective February 6, 2012, Mr. Behrens requested and was granted a leave of absence from his Teaching Assistant position in order to voluntarily serve as a leave replacement teacher in the Physical Education tenure area until June 30, 2012; and WHEREAS, effective June 30, 2012, for reasons of economy and efficiency, the Board of Education abolished 1.0 FTE position in the Teaching Assistant tenure area; and WHEREAS, Mr. Behrens, the least senior employee in said tenure area, was excessed effective June 30, 2012 and placed on a preferred eligibility list in said tenure area pursuant to the provisions of Section 3013 of the Education Law; and WHEREAS, effective September 1, 2012, Mr. Behrens accepted a leave replacement teaching position in the Physical Education tenure area for the entirety of the 2012-2013 school year; and WHEREAS, effective June 18, 2013, the Board of Education created a 1.0 FTE position in the Teaching Assistant Tenure area, effective September 1, 2013; and WHEREAS, by resolution, duly passed on June 18, 2013, Thomas Behrens, the most senior employee on the preferred eligibility list in said tenure area, was recalled to said position, effective September 1, 2013, NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that Mr. Behrens’ tenure date in the Teaching Assistant tenure area is hereby extended as a result of the aforementioned leave of absence and layoff to January 30, 2016.
