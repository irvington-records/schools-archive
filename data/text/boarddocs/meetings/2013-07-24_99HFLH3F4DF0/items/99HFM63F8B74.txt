Meeting: Jul 24, 2013 - Special Meeting
Date: 2013-07-24
Category: 3. Consent Agenda
Subject: 3.1 Consent Agenda for Special Meeting
Type: Action (Consent)

To make more efficient use of meeting time, the Board of Education authorizes the use of a consent agenda as part of its special meeting agenda. If any Board member believes that any item on the consent agenda requires discussion, that Board member may remove the item from the consent agenda. The Board may take action, as it deems appropriate on the exempted item(s) in  separate motion(s).
