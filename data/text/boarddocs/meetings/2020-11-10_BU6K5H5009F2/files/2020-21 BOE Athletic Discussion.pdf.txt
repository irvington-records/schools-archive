2020-21 BOE ATHLETIC DISCUSSION
11.10.2020

Overarching Philosophy
●    Student participation in athletics is an integral part of education

●    Promote and ensure the value of education and fair play

 ●   Foster an environment in which student-athletes can cultivate and instill
     qualities of:
      ○   Leadership
      ○   Player development
      ○   Collaboration
      ○   Cooperation

 ●   Aim to build competitive and successful teams

 ●   Enhance positive student behaviors and relationships

 ●   Provide life lessons in sportsmanship, teamwork and competition

Varsity Philosophy
 ●   Best players make the team regardless of grade level.

 ●   Coaches select as many athletes as practical for their teams.

 ●   Playing time and, sometimes, practice time may be disproportionate given the
     competitive nature of varsity athletics.

 ●   Varsity athletes are expected to demonstrate a higher level of skill and commitment,
     and they should be prepared for a six-day-a-week commitment throughout
     the season.

 ●   Practices and competitions may be scheduled over a holiday, vacation
     period and, on rare occasions, on Sundays.

 ●   The varsity coach is considered to be the leader of the
     program and is responsible for the communication and
     connection with the junior varsity and modiﬁed programs.

Junior Varsity Philosophy
●   Strong emphasis on physical conditioning, reﬁnement of fundamental
    skills, elements, strategies and commitment of team play

●   Achieving a balance between continued team and player development
    while striving for victory

●   Student-athletes are expected to demonstrate a high level
    of dedication and commitment

●   Make a six-day-a-week commitment throughout the season

Modiﬁed (Middle School) Philosophy
 ●   Modiﬁed programs provide an opportunity to learn new skills and test them in
     competition.

 ●   Cut vs No-Cut Policies
      ○   Impacts playing time & level of competitiveness
      ○   Increased demands on facilities & budget

 ●   Modiﬁed coaches attempt to "play" all athletes while maintaining the integrity and
     competitiveness of each competition.

 ●   Exceptional 7th and 8th grade athletes may be considered for "playing up.”

 ●   Middle School athletes must qualify for high school athletic
     programs after a screening process to determine physical maturity,
     ﬁtness and skill. (Athletic Placement Process)

The Athletic Placement Process (APP)
● The Athletic Placement Process (APP) is a program for evaluating students
  who want to participate in sports at higher or lower levels.

  ○ Parent/ Guardian Approval
    ■ To initiate the process must provide written approval through FamilyID.

  ○ Medical Evaluation
    ■ Physical maturity is reviewed and determined by the District Medical Director, using Tanner
      Scale ratings provided by private medical providers.

  ○ Coaches Evaluation
    ■ A skill evaluation form is completed by the coach of the team for which the
      student desires to try out; evaluation includes consideration of past
      observations of the student athlete and/or input from the student’s
      former coaches.

The Athletic Placement Process (APP) (Continued)
 ● The Athletic Placement Process (APP) is a program for evaluating students
   who want to participate in sports at higher or lower levels.

   ○ Fitness Testing
     ■ a completion of a physical ﬁtness test administered by a certiﬁed physical education teacher
       who is not a coach of the sport for which the student will be trying out.

   ○ Tryout
     ■ Upon successful completion of all components of these tests, the student-athlete shall be
       eligible to try-out for the high school interscholastic team for which the student-athlete has
       been approved, at the level for which he/she has been approved, and in accordance
       with Policy 7412.

The Athletic Placement Process (APP)
● District Policy 7412 provides an opportunity to participate at an appropriate
  level of competition based on readiness rather than age or grade with
  certain restrictions listed below.

  ○ No middle school athlete will be allowed to participate on a high school team if the
    rosters are at capacity or if a high school athlete is cut.

  ○ A 7th grade student will only be allowed to select and classify up to the varsity level
    in non-contact sports that do not offer a JV program.

  ○ 7th grade students may only classify up to the JV level in contact sports.

  ○ 8th grade students may select and classify up to either the JV or
    Varsity level upon meeting the State & district criteria.

Impacts of COVID-19 Today & Tomorrow
Mission

Our top priority is to provide the safest possible re-entry to interscholastic athletics for our
student-athletes, coaches, support staff and spectators.

  ● Follow standards and guidelines provided by the Centers for Disease Control (“CDC”), the New York
    State Department of Health (“NYSDOH”) and local health departments, and the New York State
    Education Department (“NYSED”).

  ● Coordinating with best practices established by the New York State Public High School Athletic
    Association (NYSPHSAA) and the Section One COVID-19 Task Force.

  ● Operate in consultation with our District’s Medical Director to ensure proper
    measures are being considered with respect to our school community.

Impacts of COVID-19 Today & Tomorrow
Goals

The following goals are meant to provide a safe and enjoyable playing environment for all

 1.   Establish precautions, based on CDC, NYSDOH, NYSED, and local health department
      guidance, to help protect against the spread of COVID-19

2.    Educate All Involved Parties on Protocols

3.    Safely Reintroduce Student-Athletes to Physical Activity

4.    Safely Return to Interscholastic Competitions

5.    Provide Social/Emotional Support
