6. Discussion/Presentation

Information: 6.1 Presentation

The Research Director at K12 Insight, Mrs. Lauren Gonzalez, presented the results of the 2022 School Quality Survey. For this comprehensive survey, questions were asked of several different stakeholder groups (parents, community members, students and staff) regarding school district performance. The surveys were open from June 1-20th. The results of the survey showed positive responses across communication, academic standards and response to social-emotional needs. Some participants expressed a desire for increased academic rigor in the curriculum as well as a new method of communication. Additionally, improved communication was requested for students with IEPs.
