Safety Committee Meeting
Minutes
January 25, 2018

Present: Carol Stein, Arthur McCormack, Gary Knowles, David Cohen, David Sottile, Allyson Daley, Joyce
Chapnick, Florence Cerbone, Stephen DiGiovanni, Richard Pittore, Maura Gedid, Deborah Hargraves,
Chrys Paul, Michael Cerone, Michael Toolan and Doreen Morandi

Old Business:
Administration will have training next week on the security procedures. The Irvington Police are leading
the training for the district. The spring meeting of the Safety Committee will report on this training and
next steps for district training.

New Business:

Accident Reports:
The summary report shows accidents from September 2017 to January 18, 2018. There was discussion
about making the report in a data base format so the information can be sorted by location, building or
type of incident to see if there is a pattern to student accidents. The district will look to make these
changes going forward.

For the sports team practices and games, a request was made to note if the trainer was there at the
time of incident if possible.

Potential Capital Project Planning:
Carol opened the discussion by providing background that the district is in the preliminary stages of
planning for a future capital project. There are many facility and security needs that may not be
addressed within our current budget due to funding restraints and might require a bond. While there
will certainly be more opportunities for comments and suggestions, the District wanted some initial
thoughts from this committee for ideas for improving safety and security in all the schools. The
following areas were discussed.

Enclosing the Campus:
     David Sottile recommended the outdoor corridors be enclosed to make the entire school secure
        and for safe passage during extreme weather. He mentioned that during a lock out drill you are
        not allowed to send students outside, therefore, students cannot change classes during a lock
        out. David Cohen mentioned that a down side to enclosing outdoor corridors would cut off
        access to high school students and create more Middle School through traffic.

Security cameras and staff
    David Cohen asked for more security cameras, and at the main entrance a better set up for the
        security desk. The committee discussed in general that security greeters should screen visitors
        prior to entering the building but our current layouts don’t facilitate that practice.

       There was a suggestion by Michael Toolan for the District to have a School Resource Officer.
        One “SRO” could be shared by all schools in the District spending more time in the upper grades.
        He said the “SRO” would benefit students as well as administrators and could provide Safety
        Training as well as prevention education. It is noted, however, that personnel cannot be
        included with a bond proposition.
       Stephen DiGiovanni said the Campus could use more security on Staff Only and Concert days.

Traffic:
     David Sottile commented the traffic flow and volume is the greatest safety issue at the Campus.
         Student traffic and sun glare are concerns.
     Parking is an issue and cars are parking illegally blocking fire access.
     David Cohen suggested speed bumps.
     Stephen DiGiovanni asked about the feasibility of controlling access to the Campus at the hill
         with some sort of physical barrier (booth or gate) and suggested considering the ability to use
         the closed access road (Link Rd) as a potential solution. Carol noted there is a current legal
         agreement prohibiting its use.
     Maura Gedid asked if it was possible to have a drop off area at the bottom of the hill.
     It was suggested to have a traffic study done as part of the capital project planning

Classroom access:
     Florence Cerbone noted that substitutes don’t have keys for classrooms. A card key system is
        needed with daily “smart” badges for subs.
     Card keys would also allow more information on access (who, time of entry) and can easily be
        programmed to personalize allowable access for staff and students.
     There was a request for the District to find studies that link card key use and reported security
        incidents.
Storage:
     Artie said the Athletic Department needs proper storage for all sports equipment and funding
        for that storage should be part of the capital planning process. Proper storage will minimize
        incidents in accessing or moving equipment.
     Carol noted that classroom casework (cabinets) is inadequate, especially in the art and music
        rooms.

Carol said she will be presenting these comments to the Buildings & Grounds Committee as we begin
the work on the potential capital project planning process.
