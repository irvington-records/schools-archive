1.0      Develop Goals for 2017-18


        To have a sound policy manual that complies with all related Federal and NYS law and regulations
and aligns with the District mission, vision and Strategic Plan.

       It shall provide consistent direction to the Board of Education, Administration, faculty, staff,
students, parents, and community .

         To accomplish this, the committee will:

      ● Continue the revision of the policy manual
      ● Amend and develop policies as necessary to ensure effective governance of IUFSD.
