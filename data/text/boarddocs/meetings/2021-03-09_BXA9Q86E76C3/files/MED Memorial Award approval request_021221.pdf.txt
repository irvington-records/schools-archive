Members of the Irvington School Board,

| am writing on behalf of the members of the Irvington High School class of 1974 seeking your approval
to establish an award at the high school in memory of our classmate, Martha E. Dolan, who recently
passed away.

Martha was an incredible person: she broke barriers by becoming an engineer at a time when few
females entered the profession and over the course of her 30-year career contributed to the
development of several life-saving medical technologies. To honor Martha, we would like to establish
an award in her name at the high school: the Martha E. Dolan Memorial Award.

With this award, we would like to recognize a graduating senior who possess many of the qualities and
interests that defined Martha. Thus, the recipient should be a female in good academic standing with a
stated interest in pursuing a career in a STEM discipline, preferably in any engineering field. If more
than one student fits these criteria, then preference should be given to a student with financial need. If
additional criteria are needed to select the recipient, then preference should be given to an individual
who plays a sport at the high school or enjoys outdoor activities, such as hiking, skiing and sailing.

We would like to initiate the award at the end of the 2020/2021 school year. The award is proposed to
be a cash award in the amount of $1500. Members of the IHS class of 1974 have currently pledged
approximately $8,000, an amount sufficient to fund the award for at least 5 years. Should the board
approve the award, | anticipate that a significant percentage of these funds will be received shortly after
approval, with additional contributions being received over the lifetime of the award.

At the end of each school year, we would appreciate receiving an update regarding the award, including
the recipient’s name, selected college and preliminary course of study as well as notification of the
current balance in the award fund. Please send notification, either by email or regular mail, to:
