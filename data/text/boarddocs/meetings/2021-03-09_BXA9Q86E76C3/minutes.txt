Action (Consent): 10.4 Martha E. Dolan Memorial Award

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District administers the Irvington Union Free School District; and WHEREAS, the Martha E. Dolan Memorial Award, hereinafter referred to as the "Donor" desires to make a gift to the Board of Education in order to create a scholarship fund, to be known as the "Martha E. Dolan Memorial Award"; and WHEREAS Education Law 1709(12-a) and 2503 (1) authorizes the Board of Education: to take and hold in trust for the purpose of awarding scholarships in [the) schools any real estate transferred to it by gift, grant, bequest or devise, or any gift, legacy or annuity, of whatever kind, given or bequeathed to [the] board and apply the same, or the interest and proceeds thereof, according to the instructions of the donor or testator. NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District hereby creates the above-named Memorial Award, as set forth below, based upon the understanding between the parties: The Martha E. Dolan Memorial Award 1. The Donors of the Martha E. Dolan Memorial Award shall deliver to the Board, for deposit in the trust and agency account in the bank designated by the Board of Education. 2. The Memorial Award may be supplemented at any time by any donor(s) wishing to gift, grant, bequest or devise cash, cash equivalent or other property acceptable to the District or through any gift, legacy or annuity, of whatever kind, given, or bequeathed to the District for such purpose. 3.The scholarship shall be named the Martha E. Dolan Memorial Award in memory of Martha E. Dolan, Irvington High School Class of 1974. 4. The scholarship may be awarded annually in the amount of $1500 to one Irvington High School senior. The student must demonstrate the following character traits: female in good academic standing with a stated interest in pursuing a career in a STEM discipline, preferably in any engineering field. 5. The funds allocated to said Memorial Award shall come from donations by any party at anytime which may be used to increase the scholarship or held for future recipients at the discretion of the School Administration. The Memorial Award is intended to be initially funded at approximately $8,000 and the scholarship will continue to be awarded until funds no longer exist to fulfill the scholarship. When said such funds fall below $1500 and confirmation exists of no future funding, the School Administration will determine another senior scholarship fund to transfer funds to. 6. Details of the organization and administration of the Memorial Award will be left to the discretion of the Board of Education. Executive Content Last Modified by Patricia Roeder on February 22, 2021
