3. Student Recognition

Recognition: 3.1 Recognition of Fall Athletes

Arthur McCormack, Director of Athletics, acknowledged the achievements of our fall athletes, including the Boys Cross Country Team who placed 3rd in the state in the Class C Sectional Championship. He congratulated each team member: Alex Bjorgvinsson, Ronan Cypher, Henry Demarest, Ryan Flanagan, Aidan McClure, Ian McClure, Eric Parke and Lucas Turano, noting that Ryan Flanagan and Alex Bjorgvinsson earned All-County titles and Ryan also earned the Enterprise Runner of the Year distinction. In Girls Swimming, the combined high school team (SHEDI) placed 2nd in the state and are Sectional Champions. Grace Brady earned All League and State Qualifier titles. Ava Franks earned All League and All Section honors, State Champion in the 200 Free Relay and was named Enterprise Swimmer of the Year. In Girls Soccer, senior captain and 2-year starter, Miranda Farman earned All Section-Elite 12 title. In Volleyball, captain Mia Mascone earned All Section honors. In Boys Soccer, captain Anthony Avelino earned the All Section honors. The Board congratulated all of the athletes and Michael Hanna gave each a certificate honoring their individual achievements.

4. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Report: 4.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison noted the recent safety situation regarding a public safety matter that occurred in Dobbs Ferry  required the Irvington District to employ its emergency protocols. This situation was a first in many ways as we were responding to a matter out of district which put Irvington into alert -- not during the school day, but after dismissal. This was a great test of numerous aspects of our safety and security plans as we had to account for many students who were in different locations for various school-based programs, enrichment programs, and athletics. Dr. Harrison acknowledged the many staff members who enabled us to sharpen our communications to keep parents and the broader school community informed. Dr. Harrison acknowledged, with gratitude, the Irvington Police Department and the Village for their communications and support during this event.

In addition to the athletes that were celebrated tonight, Dr. Harrison, acknowledged Gina Maher - Irvington Girls Basketball Coach. On Saturday, Ms. Maher reached a major milestone in her career--700 wins, becoming the first girl's basketball coach in New York State Public High School Athletic Association's history to reach that milestone.

Dr. Harrison noted that next Tuesday, December 11 at 7 pm, there will be an informational event for the proposed new High School Master Schedule. Mr. Cohen sent out invitations to the Irvington Middle School and High School parent community and it's also announced in the weekly district newsletter. For community members looking to learn more, Dr. Harrison noted that resources are posted on the High School webpage.

Dr. Harrison also mentioned that there are many important upcoming events and reminded everyone to check the website and school communications to keep informed of the many upcoming events, including:

Report: 4.3 Buildings & Grounds Committee Capital Project Update

Brian Friedman spoke on behalf of the Buildings & Grounds Committee with an update on the overall scope of the work thus far. Representatives from H2M (architectural firm) were present to answer any questions. Work was identified as a result of the 2015 Building Condition Survey and 5-year Capital Plan, community-based committee work and review by the Board Buildings & Grounds Committee in partnership with the professional opinions of the Architect/Engineering Firm and other partners. Each building and adjoining site was carefully inspected, with health and safety items receiving the highest priority.

The scope of the work under consideration is categorized as:

• Safety & Security  • Infrastructure  • Energy Efficiency  • Protect Assets/Storage/Other Improvements  • Modernizing Learning Spaces

Safety & Security projects include:

• New Entry/Vestibule at High School

• Security Vestibules at the Middle School, Main Street School and Dows Lane

• Enclose the Campus – reduce entry points to locations with security vestibules

• Install additional security cameras

• Enhance signage at the Campus

• Improve exterior lighting at the Campus and Main Street School

• Install a card access door entry system for exterior and classroom doors with auto lockdown ability

Infrastructure work includes:

• Site work and paving throughout the District

• Roof replacement at Dows Lane

• Floor abatement at Main Street School and Dows Lane

• Renovate the girls bathroom at the High School

• Replace the waste line at Main Street School

Energy Efficiency updates include:

• Replace the boiler at Dows Lane

• Replace exterior wall and shade panels at the High School

• Replace classroom univents at the High School

• Upgrade controls for the univents at the High School

• Replace lights with LEDs district-wide

 • Install window solar and safety film district-wide

• Air Conditioning for: Maher Gym, Campus Gym, Main Street School Gym, Dows Lane Gym

Project Assets/Storage/Other Replacements being evaluated include:

• District Vehicle and Equipment Storage Building at Dows Lane (at current basketball court) and relocate the basketball court to be closer to playground

• Small Storage Building for athletic equipment with concession and bathrooms at the campus

• Bleachers (Code Compliant) and Pressbox at Meszaros Field

• Walkway for Bleachers to Athletic Storage

• Site work/utilities for Bleachers and Pressbox

• Standalone Outdoor Bathroom at Dows

Modernizing Learning Spaces:

• Renovating the boys locker room

• Casework for the art and music rooms for better storage of artwork, supplies and instruments

Items and details still to cost out – meetings in progress:

• Adding collaborative space in the High School and Middle School

• Address needs of the theater

• Updating Science/Computer Labs

• Reconfiguring Library spaces

Michael Hanna asked about the timeline. Brian Friedman said the goal is to have a fully formed scope of the project by the end of February, 2019.
