                                          AGREEMENT

      BY AND BETWEEN the BOARD OF EDUCATION of the ARDSLEY UNION FREE
 SCHOOL DISTRICT (“Ardsley”) with offices for the transaction of business located at 500 Farm
 Road, Ardsley, New York 10502, the BOARD OF EDUCATION of the DOBBS FERRY UNION
 FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (“Dobbs Ferry”) with offices for the transaction of business located at
 505 Broadway, Dobbs Ferry, New York 10522, the BOARD OF EDUCATION of the EDGEMONT
 UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (“Edgemont”) with offices for the transaction of business located
 at 300 White Oak Lane, Scarsdale, NY 10583, the BOARD OF EDUCATION of the HASTINGS-
 ON-HUDSON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT ("Hastings") with offices for the transaction of
 business located at 27 Farragut Avenue, Hastings-on-Hudson, New York 10706 and the BOARD
 OF EDUCATION of the IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (“Irvington”) with
 offices for the transaction of business located at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, New York 10533
 (collectively, Ardsley, Dobbs Ferry, Edgemont, Hastings, and Irvington shall be referred to as the
 “Quad Districts”) and ROYAL COACH LINES, INC. with offices for the transaction of business
 located at 1010 Nepperhan Avenue, Yonkers, New York 10703 (“Royal Coach”).

         WHEREAS, some or all of the Quad Districts and Royal Coach entered into Transportation
  Contracts for In-District home-to-school, Out-of-District, Summer, and Athletic and Field Trips, on
  June 28, 2019 (“Contracts”) in accordance with applicable New York State law and regulations; and
            WHEREAS, the Contracts contemplate transportation services in exchange for payment;
 and

          WHEREAS, the Quad Districts and Royal Coach have discussed their contractual
  relationship moving forward and the operational issues related to the transport of students of the
  Quad Districts and agreed to modify the Out-of-District and Summer contracts between the Quad
  Districts to Royal Coach; and

        NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the covenants, promises and agreements
 contained herein, the sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the Quad Districts and Royal
 Coach agree as follows:

     1. The Quad Districts agree to modify the Out-of-District and Summer Transportation Contracts
        (“Contracts”) rate schedule to be a minimum 4-hour bus effective July 2023, in consideration for
        Royal Coach agreeing to renew the Contracts with Ardsley and the Quad Districts/Edgemont for
        each of the next four (4) school years, as follows: (i) 2023-2024 school year at the regional
        consumer price index for the N.Y., N.Y.-Northeastern, N.J. area, based upon the index for
        all urban consumers (CPI-U) during the preceding twelve-month period; (ii) 2024- 2025
        school year at the CPI- U during the preceding twelve-month period; (iii) 2025-2026 school year
        at the CPI-U during the preceding twelve- month period; and (iv) 2026-2027 school year at the
        (CPI-U) during the preceding twelve- month period.

      2. In furtherance of Royal Coach's guarantees of contract renewals set forth in Paragraph 1,
         above, Royal Coach agrees to obtain and maintain a performance bond in the full amount of
         each of the Contracts and new contracts during the period of four (4) school years, 2023-2024,
         2024-2025, 2025-2026 and 2026-2027.
     3. This Agreement and all of its terms shall be governed by, interpreted under and construed in

      7. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each to be deemed an original, but all shall
         constitute one and the same written instrument. This Agreement may be executed
         electronically, via facsimile, and electronic, facsimile or photocopied signatures to this
         Agreement shall have the same force and effect as original signatures.
      8. This Agreement is subject to approval by the Board of Education of each school district
         comprising the Quad Districts.

        IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the undersigned have executed this Agreement effective as of
the First day of July, 2023.
