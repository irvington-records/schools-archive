                                                   Irvington Union Free School District
                                                       Monthly Financial Highlights

                                                                    March 2022
Cash Balance
    Our March cash position was $45.07 million, a decrease of $3.59 million compared to the prior month.
      Cash received in the Real Property & Other Taxes category included real property tax payment from the Town of Greenburgh for tax
       collection during the month of February totaling $736,688.
      State and Federal Aid Cash Receipts totaled $748,854 which included federal lunch reimbursement, and VLT Lottery aid payment,
       Commercial Gaming Grant Aid, Instructional Material Aid, Excess Cost Aid, Child Residential Program tuition and Summer School Aid.

      Cash disbursements during the month of March included normal monthly check warrants and two payroll runs.

Revenue:
    General Fund Revenue through March was $65,226,513 or 98.0% of the budgeted amount. This percentage is 0.3% less when compared to
      the same time last year.
      A number of State Aid payments were paid during March as listed above. These aid payments are generated from timely and accurate
       reporting on the annual ST3 and the System to Track and Account for Children (STAC). Remaining state aid payments includes portions of
       BOCES aid and Excess Cost Aid. These usually arrive in late June or during the next fiscal year. Any state aid not received by close of the
       current year will be recorded as a receivable in the current year.

      We project that we should exceed the budgeted amounts for the sales tax, tuition, and use of money and property lines. However, we also
       project that we will ultimately be below budget for health services revenue due to lower enrollment in the private school within the District.

Expenditures:
    Actual General Fund Expenses totaled approximately $4.91 million for March. This brings the total year to date expenditures to $40,058,292.
      6% of the budget remains including the outstanding encumbrances.

      The tax certiorari line is negative based on payments made from existing settlements. We will plan to have a budget amendment on a future
       agenda to fund this account code from the tax certiorari reserve to correct this negative amount. We also anticipate a budget amendment will
       be needed in the future to access funds from the worker’s compensation reserve for worker’s compensation expenses.
