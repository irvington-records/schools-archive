Meeting: Jul 10, 2012 - 2012-13 Reorganization Meeting/Special Meeting
Date: 2012-07-10
Category: 17. Personnel Matters
Subject: 17.3 Declination of Reduced Position
Type: Action (Consent)

WHEREAS, for reasons of economy and efficiency the Board of Education abolished a (1.0) FTE teaching position in the tenure area of Science (7-12) by duly passed resolution on April 3, 2012; and

	WHEREAS, the Board of Education simultaneously created a vacancy for a (.6) teaching position in the tenure area of Science (7-12); and

	WHEREAS, the least senior employee (employee #803) affected by said abolishment was offered the (.6) teaching position; and

	WHEREAS, said employee has declined the position.

	NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that said employee shall be placed on the preferred eligibility list for the tenure area of Science (7-12) for a period of seven (7) years commencing July 1, 2012.
