Meeting: Jul 10, 2012 - 2012-13 Reorganization Meeting/Special Meeting
Date: 2012-07-10
Category: 14. Consent Agenda
Subject: 14.1 Approval of Consent Agenda
Type: Action (Consent)

To  make more efficient use of meeting time, the Board of Education authorizes the use of a consent agenda as part of its special meeting agenda. If any Board member believes that any item on the consent agenda requires discussion, that Board member may remove the item from the consent agenda to the special agenda. The Board may take actions, as it deems appropriate on the exempted item(s) in a separate motion.
