SUBJECT: ANTI-BULLYING, HARRASSMENT AND HAZING POLICY                                                  Formatted: Left




The Irvington Union Free School District is committed to providing a positive, safe and
supportive learning and working environment that promotes respect, dignity and equality and is
free from bullying, hazing, harassment and discrimination. The Board recognizes that
harassment, bullying, hazing, andharassment, and discrimination bullying isare detrimental to
student learning and achievement. It Such conduct not only interferes with the mission of the
district District to educate its students and disrupts the operation of the schools but can also be
harmful to students . Such behavior affects not only the students who are the its targets of this
behavior but alsoas well as those individuals students who witness or participate andin witness
such acts.

To this end, the Board condemns and strictly prohibits all forms of bullying, harassment, hazing
and discrimination by District employees or students bullying on school grounds, school buses
and at all school-sponsored activities, programs and events, including those that take place at
locations outside the district but that materially and substantially disrupt the educational process
of the school environment or impinge on the rights of othersregardless of whether these
activities, programs and events are held on District property.

Definitions:

For the purpose of this policy, the term “harassment” is defined as the creation of a hostile
environment by conduct or by verbal threats, intimidation or abuse that has or would have the
effect of unreasonably and substantially interfering with a student’s educational performance,
opportunities or benefits or mental, emotional or physical well-being; or conduct, verbal threats,
intimidation or abuse that reasonably causes or would reasonably be expected to cause a student
to fear for his or her physical safety. The harassing behavior may be based on a person’s actual
or perceived race, color, weight, national origin, ethnic group, religion, religious practice,
disability, sex, sexual orientation, or gender identity or expression.

For the purposes of this policy, bullying includes systematic and intentional infliction of physical
harm or psychological distress on an individual or group of individuals. Bullying can encompass
unwanted purposeful, written, verbal, non-verbal, social or relational, or physical behavior that
has the potential to create long-term damage, cause discomfort or humiliation, or unreasonably
interfere with the individual’s school performance. is defined as a hostile activity which harms
or induces fear through the threat of further aggression and/or creates terror. Bullying may be
premeditated or a sudden activity. It may be subtle or easy to identify, done by one person or a
group. Bullying is often characterized by:

For the purposes of this policy, the term “hazing” among students is defined as any conduct
intended to have the effect of, or which should reasonably could be been expected to have the
effect of humiliating, intimidating or demeaning a student or endangering the mental or physical
health of a student in order for that student to join, participate in, or hold office in any
organization affiliated with any school or program operated by the District, including but not
limited to school sports, activities and clubs. Hazing behaviors include, but are not limited to,
the following general categories:
            a. Humiliation: conduct likely to cause embarrassment to or social isolation of the

The District aims to foster an educational setting wherein all students are treated with respect and
dignity. The school setting provides an opportunity to teach children, and emphasize among
staff, that cooperation with and respect for others is a key district District value. A program
gearedThe District’s goal will not only be aimed at to prevention is designed but also to not only
decrease further incidents of bullying, but and to help students build more supportive
relationships with one another by integrating the bullying prevention program into classroom
instruction. The District will create guidelines for training and will provide training to Sstaff
members in accordance with state law and the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education, to
raise awareness and sensitivity to acts of bullying, harassment, hazing and discrimination, to
enable staff to prevent acts of bullying, harassment, hazing and discrimination and to provide
intervention and reporting.

and The District will student will be sensitized, through district-wide professional development

       Learning about and identifying the early warning signs and precursor behaviors that may

       Establishing clear school wide and classroom rules about bullying, harassment, hazing

       Training adults in the school to respond sensitively and consistently to bullying,

Providing instruction in civility, citizenship and character education that emphasizes tolerance

Reporting and Investigating:

A student who believesStudents who believe that he/she has been have been bullied, parents
whose children have been bulliedharassed, hazed or discriminated against or other students who
observe have knowledge of bullying, harassing, hazing and discriminatory behavior are
encouraged to make a verbal and/or written complaint to a teacher, coach, bus driver, counselor,
supervisor, or administrator in accordance with the training and guidelines providedmust report it
to a staff member. All District personnel are responsible for reporting all complaints of bullying,
harassment, hazing and discrimination that they receive from students as well as any instances of
such behavior that they are aware of, to the principal of the school where the incident occurred.
When such conduct is of a sexual nature, as defined by the District’s Sexual Harassment Policy,
it must be reported to the designated Title IX Officer. In accordance with state law the Board
shall also appoint at least one staff member at each school building to be designated as a Dignity
Act Coordinator and be trained to handle human relations in the areas of race, color, weight,
national origin, ethnic group, religion, religious practice, disability, sexual orientation, gender
and sex.

At all times, complaints will be documented, tracked and handled in accordance with the
District’s policies and Code of Conduct, as applicable. Incidents will be included in the Violent
and Disruptive Incident Reporting (VADIR) system as required by the Regulations of the
Commissioner of Education, when applicable but in no case less than on an annual basis.

In order for the Board to effectively enforce this policy and to take prompt and corrective
measures it is essential that all victims and persons with knowledge of bullying, harassment,
hazing or discriminationand bullying report such behavior immediately. The Ddistrict will
promptly and fully investigate all complaints, formal or informal, verbal or written. To the
extent possible, all complaints will be treated in a confidential manner, although limited
disclosure may be necessary to complete a thorough investigation.


Disciplinary Consequences:

While the focus of this policy is on prevention, bullying, harassing, hazing and discriminatory
acts may still occur. In these cases, offenders will be given the clear message that their actions
are wrong and that the behavior must improvenot reoccur. Offenders will receive in school
guidance in making positive choices in their relationships with others. If appropriate and if
appropriate, disciplinary action will be taken by the administration in accordance with the
district’s Code of Conduct, as applicable. If the behavior rises to the level of criminal activity,
law enforcement will be contacted.

Non Retaliation:

All complainants and those who participate in the investigation of a complaint in conformity
with state law and district District policies, who have acted reasonably and in good faith, have
the right to be free from retaliation of any kind. The District expressly prohibits any retaliation
against complainants, victims, witnesses and/or any individuals, who initiate, testify, participate
or assist in the investigation of any allegation or report of bullying, harassment, hazing or

discrimination. District administrators will monitor participants in investigations and victims of
bullying, harassment, hazing or discrimination to ensure that the behavior has ceased, no
retaliation has occurred and support or counseling has been afforded to the individuals involved,
as needed.

Dissemination, Monitoring and Review:

This policy, or a plain language summary, shall be published in student registration materials,
student, parent and employee handbooks, and posted on the dDistrict’s website.

Each year, as part of the annual review of the Code of Conduct, this policy will be reviewed to
assess its effectiveness and compliance with state and federal law. If changes are needed,
revisions will be recommended to the Board for its consideration.

In addition, the Board will receive the annual VADIR data report for each building and for the
district District as a whole, with particular attention to the trends in the incidence of bullying,
harassment, hazing and discrimination. Based on the review of the data, the Board may consider
further action, including but not limited to modification of this policy and additional training.
