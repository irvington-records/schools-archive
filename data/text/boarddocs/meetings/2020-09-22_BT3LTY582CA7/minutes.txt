10. Business Office Matters

Action (Consent):  10.1 Door Hardening Resolution

Resolution: WHEREAS, under normal circumstances classroom, stair, and corridor doors must have vision panels to allow visual access into the next space; and WHEREAS, under normal circumstances all doors must not be blocked; and WHEREAS, in the event of an emergency the New York State Education Department has authorized the suspension of the above-requirements on a temporary basis; NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, in emergency situations, authorized and designated staff members may temporarily cover door vision panels and temporarily block doors to slow an intruder’s access to a particular room, as more particularly described in the applicable building-level emergency response plan.
