Meeting: Sep 22, 2020 - Special Meeting/Work Session
Date: 2020-09-22
Category: 9. Personnel Matters
Subject: 9.11 Adjustment of Probation End Date
Type: Action (Consent)

By providing verification of prior tenure, the probationary period of teachers may be reduced to three years where the teacher had previously been appointed to tenure in another school district within New York State. Related service providers do not fall under Section 3012-c or 3012-d in his or her final year of service.
