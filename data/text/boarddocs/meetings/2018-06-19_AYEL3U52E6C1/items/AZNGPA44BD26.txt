- $2,453,927 is the allowable maximum amount (4% of the 2018-19 budget) and recommended to be designated as Unassigned Fund Balance by the Comptroller

- $422,500 was presented during the budget discussions

- The Tax Certiorari reserve is used to make refunds on previous years' settlements.

- The Worker's Comp Reserve is used to pay current unbudgeted claims or hold for future worker compensation claims as the district is self insured for worker's compensation.

- The Capital Reserve will be used for future capital projects with voter consent.
