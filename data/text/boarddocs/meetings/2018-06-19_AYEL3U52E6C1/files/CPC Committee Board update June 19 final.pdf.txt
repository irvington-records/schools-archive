Why plan a Capital Project now?
The District has real needs
     • Addressing aging facility infrastructure needs
     • Safety and Security Improvements
     • HVAC needs
     • Improving academic spaces for flexible learning and new
        technology
     • Paving/Sidewalk projects
     • Addressing storage needs

Within the tax levy cap, the General fund budget cannot support these
big ticket expenditures

Why plan a Capital Project now?
Plan Capital Projects to align debt payments - reduce budgetary
and tax rate impact
     ● In 2022-23 debt payments drop by $1.2 million

     ● Utilize Bond Anticipation Notes (BAN) during project

       construction (interest and minimal principal payments)
       until then
     ● Align borrowing with construction phases

Borrowing costs are still relatively low: ~3% - 3.5%

Capital Project Planning Committee Goals

The Committee is an Advisory Committee that will make
recommendations to the Buildings and Grounds Committee who in turn
will make recommendations to the Board of Education
● Gather stakeholder input
● Contribute to list of needs/scope of work
   ○ Overall and for school/group each member represents
● Evaluate and assist in prioritizing scope of work

What can we do now to improve Security?

Short term considerations/In-Process
● Add Visitor Management System such as Raptor and strengthen visitor
   protocols and procedures; Visitor lanyards
● Number all windows and doors
● Add Flip charts with key security procedures to all classrooms and major
   gathering spaces
● Add radios to ensure all safety team members have one
● Add some additional cameras in key locations if existing wiring can support
● Install proximity door readers to key external doors for electronic access
   control
● Enhance safety/emergency kits per consultant’s recommendations
