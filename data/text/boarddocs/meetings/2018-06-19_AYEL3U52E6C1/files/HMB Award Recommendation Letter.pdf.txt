Dear Ms. Stein:

On Monday, June 11, 2018 at 9:30 am the Child Nutrition bids for operation with a Food Service Management
Company at the Irvington Union Free School District were opened. Aramark Educational Services, LLC, Whitsons School
Nutrition Corp., Chartwells Dining Service, Metz Culinary, and Sodexo were the five Management Companies who
received bid specifications. Aramark Educational Services and Whitsons School Nutrition were the two attendees at the
vendor conference and tour of the District on Wednesday, May 30, 2018.

Aramark Educational Services, LLC, and Whitsons School Nutrition Corp. were the two management companies
that responded. After thorough review of each bid proposal, the Aramark Educational Services, LLC proposal and bid
amount is, in our opinion, the lowest responsible.

The total meal price of the Aramark bid was $3.1513, which is lower than that of the Whitsons School Nutrition
Corp. bid of $3.4282. The Aramark individual meal price of $3.1513 for breakfast and lunch will allow the District to
receive a projected annual return above the $10,000.00 guaranteed return contractual arrangement.

| have enclosed a summary analysis of the bid for your review. Aramark will retain all the existing hourly food
service staff members at their current hours and will increase their wages for next year as required in the bid
specifications.

Upon thorough review of the proposal submitted by Aramark Educational Services, LLC, it is our recommendation
that the Irvington Union Free School District Child Nutrition Contract be awarded to Aramark Educational Services, LLC
for the 2018-19 School Year.

It was a pleasure to assist you and the Irvington Union Free School District with the Child Nutrition Bid Process.
