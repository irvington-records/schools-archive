Meeting: Apr 27, 2021 - Regular Meeting

Category: 6. Comments from Members of the Community
Subject: 6.1 Procedures for Community Comment on Topics that appear on the Agenda
Type: Information

Members of the community are invited to comment or ask questions of the Board on topics that appear on the Board of Education agenda.  Community members are reminded to direct questions to the President of the Board and to limit questions or comments to approximately three minutes.
