Meeting: Apr 27, 2021 - Regular Meeting

Category: 12. Community Comments and/ or Questions
Subject: 12.1 Procedures for Community Comments and/ or Questions regarding the School District.
Type: 

Members of the community will be invited to comment or ask questions of the Board on any topic related to the school district. Community members are reminded to direct questions to the President of the Board and to limit questions or comments to approximately three minutes.
