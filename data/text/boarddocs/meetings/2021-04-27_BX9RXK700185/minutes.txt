7. Discussion/Presentation

Information: 7.1 Capital Project Update

Board President, Mr. Friedman gave an update on  phase I and phase II of the Capital Project. Phase I renovations include: the Dows Boiler, the MS campus learning pavilion, the Dows Lane outdoor restroom and District Wide lighting and card access. Phase II renovations include: High School/Library Gym Arts Building  (LGA), Theater Gym Building, Main Street School renovations and the Dows Innovation Suite.
