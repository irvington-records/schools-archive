Submitted to the Board of Education for approval: June 19, 2012.



INTERNAL CONTROL RELATED MATTERS
      Auditor’s comment and recommendation: “During our audit, we found that duties assigned to
      the Treasurer did not provide the District with effective segregation of duties. The Treasurer has
      many responsibilities, some of which include completion of deposit slips, delivering the deposit to
      the bank, preparation of bank reconciliations, initiating wire transfers and recording transactions
      in the general ledger. Management has implemented procedures to mitigate these deficiencies,
      such as increased oversight by management.

        We recommend that management continue to review the responsibilities assigned to the
        Treasurer”

        Corrective action planned: The district’s accounting dept. is comprised of only 2 individuals(
        treasurer and accounts payable clerk), making it impossible to reassign the Treasurer’s
        responsibilities. The District has taken steps to create a system of checks and balances, utilizing
        management and secretarial staff, to provide a method of review of the Treasurer’s
        responsibilities.
                1.         The secretary to the Assistant Superintendent for Business receives all cash
                           and checks and prepares an independent log of the transactions. The
                           Treasurer prepares the deposit slip and makes the actual bank deposit. On a
                           monthly basis, the secretary matches her log to the cash receipts journal.
                2.         The Treasurer prepares the bank reconciliation which is then reviewed by the
                           Assistant Superintendent for Business who initials & dates as confirmation.
                3.         Wire transfers are signed by both the Assistant Superintendent for Business
                           and the Treasurer before implementation
                4.         While the Treasurer prepares and posts general ledger transactions, the
                           Assistant Superintendent for Business reviews and initials each entry.

        Contact person responsible for corrective action: Beverly Miller, Asst. Supt. For Business

        Anticipation completion date: Systems have been in place since at least September, 2011.

Extraclassroom Activity Funds
        1. Auditor’s comment and recommendation: “During our audit of the extraclassroom activity
            funds, we found that certain clubs did not have financial activity.”

   We recommend that management review all clubs to determine if they are active. Inactive
   clubs should be terminated and any unused funds should be transferred to another club
   according to Board policy.”

   Corrective action planned: All clubs are currently in the process of being reviewed. Inactive
   clubs will be closed and remaining funds transferred to another club.

   Contact person responsible for corrective action: Beverly Miller, Asst. Supt. For Business


   Anticipation completion date: June 30, 2012.




2. Auditor’s comment and recommendation: “We also found that management’s review of
   extraclassroom activity bank reconciliations was not documented.
   Through discussions with management we satisfied ourselves that the proper review of bank
   reconciliations occurred; however, we recommend that management document its review of
   extraclassroom activity bank reconciliations to ensure proper recording of cash balances,
   and to mitigate the possibility of any misappropriation of extraclassroom activity funds.”

   Corrective action planned: The Assistant Superintendent for Business reviews the bank
   reconciliations and documents the review, via initialing & dating.

   Contact person responsible for corrective action: Beverly Miller, Asst. Supt. For Business

   Anticipation completion date: System has been in place since at least September, 2011.
