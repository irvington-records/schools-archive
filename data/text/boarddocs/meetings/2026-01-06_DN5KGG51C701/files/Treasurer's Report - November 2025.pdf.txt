Irvington Union Free School District
Monthly Financial Highlights

November 2025

Cash Balance

¢ Our November overall cash position was $36.61 million, which is a healthy cash flow for this time of year. This amount was approximately the
$ 2.67 million higher when compared November 2024.

© Cash received in the Real Property & Other Taxes category included 3“ quarter 2025 sales tax totaling $267,948 from Westchester County and
a tax payment from the Town of Greenburgh for tax collection during the month of October in the amount of $1,770,866.

© State and Federal Aid Cash Receipts included the final BOCES aid 24-25 payment of $441,526, VLT Lottery aid totaling $34,959, and 25-26
ESSA Grant Drawdown totaling $15,542.

Revenue:

¢ General Fund Revenue through November was $73,568,490 or 92.9.0% of the budgeted revenue.

¢ The Use of Money and & Property mostly includes interest carnings from all District general fund bank accounts. The District has earned
$398,243 in interest earnings through November. The prior year, the District had an interest earnings budget surplus due to the high interest
rate environment. The 25-26 anticipated interest revenue earning budget was increased compared to the prior year as interest rates are still
relatively high (although rates are currently decreasing). This explains why November 2025 shows less percentage of revenue recognized at this
point of time when compared to last year. We will continue to work with our partners at NYCLASS for the best investment opportunities to
ensure we maximize our interest earnings revenue.

Expenditures:

e Actual General Fund Expenses totaled approximately $6.28 million for November. This brings the total year to date expenditures to
$24,488,980. Including outstanding encumbrances, 9% of the budget remains, which is a typical amount at this point in the year.

¢ Along with the normal monthly payroll and warrants, other expenses included bond principal and interest payments totaling $1,007,375.
