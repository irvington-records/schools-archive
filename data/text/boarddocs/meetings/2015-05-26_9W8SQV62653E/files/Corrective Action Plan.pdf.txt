IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT

CORRECTIVE ACTION PLAN FOR ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW OF THE CHILD NUTRITITON
PROGRAM, dated March 17, 2015

Identified in NYS Child Nutrition Program Administration letter dated April 28, 2015

Submitted to the Board of Education for approval, May 26, 2015:

NUTRITIONAL QUALITY AND MEAL PATTERN — Meal Components and Quantities

Auditor’s finding: “The production record includes a separate column for the number of planned
servings for each age/grade group, however, the production records supplied for the review
period, February 2015, indicate that the same food items were offered in the same portion size
to all students in grades 6-12.”

Agreed Upon Corrective Action Plan: “The SFA will work with the Food Service Management
Company, Aramark, to immediately begin planning the lunch menu in accordance with the
different age/grade groups. The food service director may continue to offer the same menu
items to all students however, different portion sizes must be offered to ensure the different
requirements are met....”.

Corrective action initiated:

The District is working with nutritionists to determine the best method of reaching the
requirements of the food based menu plan for both the 6-8 and the 9-12 age/grade groups.

It was determined that the addition of raisins or craisins would be a calorie “boost” for the 9-12
grade group to insure that they meet the menu plan requirements. This action has been
implemented as evidenced by the submission of the requested documents: Production records
and nutritional analysis for both middle school (grades 6-8) and high school (grades 9-12) for the
week of April 13-17,2015. Standardized recipes for raisins and craisins are also included with
this report.

Contact person responsible for corrective action: Dr. Kristopher Harrison, Superintendent

Completion date: April 13, 2015

NUTRITIONAL QUALITY AND MEAL PATTERN — Offer vs. Serve ~ Day of Review
Auditor’s finding: “ Signage was not posted explaining the components of the daily menu and
what students should select with each menu option to make a complete meal. Additionally, the


[OCR]
requirement that students must select at least a % cup of fruit or vegetable was not clearly
posted.”

Agreed Upon Corrective Action Plan: “The SFA will ensure signage is posted at or near the
beginning of the serving line in the cafeteria to clearly identify each reimbursable meal option

Corrective action initiated:

The District ordered five (5) signs from the Child Nutrition Program Office, at the
recommendation of the auditor. The signs were secured and posted in all building — 3 in the
Middle School/High School campus cafeteria, 1 in the Dows Lane cafeteria, and 1 in the Main St.
School cafeteria — by April 6, 2015.

Contact person responsible for corrective action: Dr. Kristopher Harrison, Superintendent

Completion date: April 6, 2015

TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE: SCHOOL BREAKFAST PROGRAM
Auditor's finding: “ The SFA began participating in the School Breakfast Program at the
Irvington High School in January 2015. Technical assistance was provided on the day of review
to the food service director regarding the meal pattern requirements included the offer versus
serve provision. “

Agreed Upon Corrective Action Plan: “It was discussed that students must be offered a full 1
cup portion of fruit each day at breakfast......”

Corrective action initiated:

The food service director immediately retrained the Aramark staff on offering 1 cup of fruit
and/or fruit juice at breakfast - students are allowed to select two % cup portions at no
additional cost to meet this requirement.

Contact person responsible for corrective action: Dr. Kristopher Harrison, Superintendent
Completion date: March 17, 2015

TECHNICAL ASSISTANCE: SUMMER FOOD SERVICE PROGRAM (SFSP) OUTREACH
Auditor’s finding: “ The SFA must inform families of the locations that operate the SFSP. The

SFA should provide families with the link to the CNKC website where families may look at a map
of locations, and should also provide the toll free number families can call to find a locations...”

Agreed Upon Corrective Action Plan: “...the SFA will inform families of locations that operate
the SFSP by listing the information on their June menu. The SFA will also post information
regarding the SFSP on their school website. The SFA will also ensure this requirement is met each
school year.”

[OCR]
Corrective action initiated:

A notice has been posted on the Irvington UFSD website, /rvingtonschools.org, under
Community.

The food service director will include the information on the June menus that are posted on the
District website.

Contact person responsible for corrective action: Dr. Kristopher Harrison, Superintendent

Completion date: June 1, 2015

Kristopher Harrison, Ed.D.
Superintendent
