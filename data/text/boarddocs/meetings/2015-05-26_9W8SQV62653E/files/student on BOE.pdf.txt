                                                                            By-Laws




SUBJECT:       NUMBER OF MEMBERS AND TERMS OF OFFICE


      The Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District shall consist of five
(5) members elected by the qualified voters of the School District at the annual election as
prescribed by law.

      Members of the Board of Education shall serve for three (3) years beginning July 1
following their election and each term shall expire on the thirtieth day of June of the third year.

     An ex officio member of the student body shall serve at public Board meetings and public
Board hearings if approved by the voters of the District as prescribed by law. This ex officio
member of the Board of Education shall have no vote in the proceedings of the Board of
Education nor will he/she attend executive sessions or be privy to any personnel or confidential
information.
