Dear Town Comptroller:

I, Elaine Cardea, District Clerk of the Irvington Union Free School District, Town of
Greenburgh, County of Westchester, State of New York, do hereby certify that the
following budget and tax items for the 2015/2016 fiscal year, beginning July 1, 2015 and
ending June 30, 2016 are true and accurate. This is an official breakdown of the warrant
for use for your collection of taxes.

At its meeting on May 26, 2015 the Board of Education adopted a motion containing the
following resolution:

MOVED, that the voters of the Irvington Union Free School District, by a vote of
835 to 373, adopted a budget for the school year commencing July 1, 2015 in the amount
of $57,664,000; that the sums as set forth in the forms of budget heretofore submitted to
the voter be appropriated; that the taxes in the necessary amounts be levied therefore; that
the Board members be authorized to execute a warrant therefore, and that the warrant be
delivered to the Town authorities.

The major budget categories and their respective amounts are listed below:

General Support                           $ 5,921,490
Instruction                               $ 31,935,635
Transportation                            $ 2,280,967
Community Services                                 0
Undistributed Expenses
 Benefits                    $12,336,196
 Debt Service                $ 5,134,712
 Transfers to Other Funds    $    55,000
Total Undistributed Expenses             $17,525,908
Total Expenditures                       $57,664,000

The Board of Education anticipates total revenue sources, other than Real Property Taxes,
of $6,314,402 including: Estimated State Aid of $3,285,502 and other income of
$3,028,900 including unexpended surplus funds in the custody of the Board as authorized
by the Board or required by law. The amount to be raised by taxes on the Real Property
of this School District is $50,927,598.         .
