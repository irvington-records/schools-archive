Meeting: May 26, 2015 - Work Session/Special Meeting - 7:30 pm*
Date: 2015-05-26
Category: 11. Community Comments and/or Questions
Subject: 11.1 Procedures for Community Comments and/or Questions regarding the school district
Type: Discussion

Members of the community will be invited to raise questions or offer comments on any topic relating to the school district. Community members are reminded to direct their questions to the President of the Board and to limit questions or comments to approximately three minutes.
