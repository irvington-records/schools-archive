                             BUILDINGS & GROUNDS COMMITTEE

                                     October 7, 2013 7:30 am

                                         MEETING MINUTES
Present: Dr. Harrison, Phil Whitney, Maria Kashkin, Gary Knowles, Beverly Miller

Discussion on outstanding capital projects– Discussion ensued regarding some outstanding capital
projects. The committee agreed that the priority items are: repairs to the Main St. School retaining
wall, emergency lighting upgrades at Main St. School, and emergency lighting upgrades at Dows Lane
School to meet SED compliance. Work will also be scheduled to seal trim caps in the HS Atrium and
cafeteria windows.

Gary will discuss wall repairs with our contractor, Mr. Bucci. Gary & Bev will work with LAN, the
district’s architectural firm, to create a separate RFP for the emergency lighting work. (It had previously
been incorporated into the Main St. School combined bid for radiator covers & emergency lighting,
which was rejected. The Dows Lane emergency lighting work had been incorporated in the plans for the
district office move & renovation work). The committee also reviewed some of the projects that have
been completed to date – repairs to the Main St. School steps, replace paving at the rear of Dows Lane
School, and boiler and steam trap work at Dows Lane.



Campus parking - update – Mr. Cohen has been working with the students to resolve the issue of
insufficient parking spots. A plan was created for students who have already received parking tags to
have alternate day parking privileges. A meeting will be held later this week with administration to
discuss the issuance of day-specific colored tags for students as well as monitoring the parking lots for
cars parked without authorization. It was suggested that visitors checking in at the security desks
provide the district with their license plate # & vehicle description as they would not have parking
permits.

 It was suggested that Dr. Harrison contact JCOS to see if there was the opportunity for some of our
staff/students to park in their lot.

 The upper lot, next to East Field, was discussed as a possible parking site. Currently, the lot is unusable
for that purpose as it would need an additional roadway (current pathway is basically for pedestrian
traffic to East Field), grading, installation of a guardrail, and lighting to be fully usable. The committee
determined the following resolutions to the parking situation: short term – alternate day parking
permits for students; medium term – grading of the upper lot, gravel resurfacing; long term –
comprehensive renovation plan for the upper lot, to be included in East Field renovations that would
include pacing, installation of a roadway, lighting, etc.
