                          BUILDINGS & GROUNDS COMMITTEE

                    September 30, 2014, 7:45 AM, at District Office

                                           MINUTES
In attendance: Maria Kashkin, Dr. Harrison, Beverly Miller, Gary Knowles

Absent: Phil Whitney

   1.   FIVE YEAR FACILITY PLAN:

   Lengthy discussion ensued regarding 2014-15 Five Year Facilities Plan. Discussion included
   actual and estimated repairs needed to our facilities, projected cost, and estimated time frames.
   After the inclusion of recommended changes, the finalized documents will be used in the
   creation of the annual School Report Cards which are presented to the Board.



   While the committee discussed the 2016-17, 2017-18, and 2018-19 years that are part of the
   Five Year Plan, they also recognized that many of the items and/or placement will change due
   to the mandated Building Condition Survey that must be completed by certified engineers
   during the 2015-16 school year. Additionally, it was discussed that as a result of projects
   included in the current bond, some of these projects may not be necessary as a result of other
   improvements. This survey will include the findings and recommendations of the engineers
   regarding the condition of our facilities.
