     2016 IUFSD ATHLETIC PARTICIPATION COMMITTEE REPORT


                                                                             March 18, 2016


SUMMARY

Athletics provide a healthy opportunity for students to participate in competitive physical
activity, to learn to work in teams, and to develop grit and confidence ­­ all of which can
offer a lifetime of benefit. Students are provided their first opportunity to participate in
competitive school athletics beginning in 7th grade with modified sports and, in later
grades, the opportunities expand with JV and varsity teams and additional sports.

In recent years, including 2015­16, several modified athletic teams experienced a
significant number of students trying out for a sport with a dozen or more students being
cut from a team. The committee reviewed participation numbers and considered
opportunities and challenges to improve the district’s ability to meet student interest in
participating in sports.

The athletic department created a 3­year historical dataset of student participation
including the number of students that tried out and the number cut from a team.
Additionally, a survey was shared with all 6th ­ 11th grade students to ascertain their 1st
and 2nd sport interest in 2016­17; the survey response was high with 430 responses.

The committee met twice to review the data and assess available practice and
competition options by sport. It also considered how other area districts handle sports
with high participation numbers; some districts have a no­cut policy with large teams
and others run additional teams composed of students with mixed­skill level, by age, or
by skill level. Additionally, the committee discussed programs offered by Irvington
Recreation, as well as, CYO and private leagues. The committee looked at balancing
participation with the benefit to individual student experience and the on­going success
of programs.

As is well known, Irvington athletics are constrained by limited field and gym space. Our
athletic director works closely with the Village Recreation Department to optimally share
gym and field space.

       Gym spaces include the Campus gym, Maher gym, MSS gym, and the Dows
       Lane gym. The Dows Lane gym floor is in very poor condition and not suitable for
       practices/games.

MODIFIED ATHLETICS
Modified Sports are the entry point for MS students to participate in school athletics. In
several modified sports as many as 15­20 students have been cut from a team during
the tryout process. Specifically, the sports are: boys soccer, girls volleyball, boys
basketball, girls basketball, and baseball. In some cases there are MS student who
have been cut in multiple seasons, effectively shut out of their preferred sports. It should
be noted there is a no­cut sport available in each season (Cross Country, Winter Track,
Spring Track and Field). However, available gym/field space, practice times, and
available competition can limit the district’s ability to run or expand a program.

VARSITY/JV ATHLETICS
Varsity/JV sports offer adequate availability for students to participate because of
additional sports and teams. In HS sports, if participation is high, both a JV and Varsity
team can be run. Where participation is low the JV team is not run and there is the
possibility of varsity athletes participating as independents (eg, boys swimming) or in
teams composed of students from other districts (eg, girls swimming, ice hockey). It was
noted that the upcoming 2016 varsity football team will likely be composed of a
substantial number of 9th graders.

FINDINGS

The committee finds that:

   1) The historical participation dataset should be maintained and the participation
      survey be continued going forward to support district and BOE planning and
      decision­making.

   2) Modified students should be advised prior to each season and during the tryout
      process that, if a sport they are interested in has limited space, to consider
      another sport they may be interested in. There is a no­cut sport each season.

   3) Based on high MS student interest and the availability of practice space and an
      appropriate competition schedule, the committee offers the following options:
      +Fall Sports: Addition of 2nd Girls Modified Volleyball Team.

DATA
HISTORICAL PARTICIPATION ANALYSIS: The athletic department created a 3 year
historical dataset of student sports participation and, cuts, if present, for all sports
teams.

2016­17 SPORTS PARTICIPATION SURVEY: Families with students in grades 6 ­ 11
were surveyed to ascertain what each student’s 1st and 2nd priority for participating in
sports for each season next year.
