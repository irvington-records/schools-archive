                            BUILDINGS & GROUNDS COMMITTEE

                         March 7, 2016, 7:30 AM, at District Office


                                               MINUTES
IN ATTENDANCE: Phil Whitney, Dr. Harrison, Deb Marinello, Gary Knowles, Beverly Miller

ABSENT: Catherine Palmieri



   1. REVIEW OF DOWS LANE PROPOSED “SARAH CARNAHAN GARDEN” :

       Deb presented landscape drawings of the proposed garden to the committee. Discussion
   ensued regarding the plants to be used. The committee approved the plans, with the direction to
   Ms. Marinello to discuss with the landscaper the need for deer resistant plants.

   2. Outstanding capital projects , including dust bowl:

        Decision was made to use artificial turf on the dust bowl and to include raised bed planter in
   the project. It is anticipated that the RFP’s for all remaining projects will be advertised next week. It
   is anticipated that the bid opening will be held about one month after initial advertising. The Board
   could make the contract awards sometime around late April. It is anticipated that most projects will
   begin this summer.

   3. Vendor participation in bids – how to increase:

       This was discussed with LAN to insure that broad advertising is in place for our projects.

   4. Hiring a construction manager

       The committee would like a construction manager to be hired to oversee the projects.
   Administration will discuss with LAN to determine timeframe for hiring and will advertise for the
   position once contracts have been awarded.

   5. Athletic schedule for fall – where will they play if Meszaros is closed?

       Dr. Harrison informed the committee that Artie has been in contact with the Village for probable
   use of their fields: soccer at Memorial Park,etc. Artie is discussing with Tarrytown & Mercy to see if
   we can use their fields for football.

   6. Maintenance costs of East Field new grass & Meszaros turf – budget implications

    Maintenance costs are included in the 2016-17 budget. The committee discussed the possibility
of a grounds foreman position to oversee fields. This would be a civil service position.

7. Review of 2016-17 B & G building repairs requested

    Committee reviewed prioritized lists of the requested B & G equipment and repairs. Copies are
attached. Discussion will be continued at the next meeting, as needed.

8.    Other: Phil raised the concern about the necessity of communications to students & our
     community regarding care & use of our new fields. We also need to address signage around the
     fields. This would need to be done prior to actual use, approx. the beginning of September. Erik
     Oley would be a good resource for communications regarding the new track. This item will be
     discussed at a more in-depth level at the next B & G meeting.
