Category: 9. Business Office Matters
Subject: 9.9 Field Trip - Grade 8 Washington, D.C. Trip
Type: Action (Consent)

The 8th Grade students will be taking a trip to Washington, D.C. between March 20-22, 2013.  The cost of the trip is paid for by the students and through fundraising activities.  Approximately 147 students will be going along with teachers and chaperones.  The estimated cost of the trip is $67,473 which is approximately $459 per student.
