SECURITY SERVICES

Arrow shall furnish security officer services and such other
services at the rates and locations more particularly set forth in
each Statement of Work (“SOW”) entered into from time to time,
in substantially the same form attached hereto as Annex “A”.

Such services will be provided in accordance with mutually
approved, written post orders set forth in each such SOW and
shall be subject to all of the terms and conditions of this
Agreement. The billing rates per service hour and/or for
equipment furnished, as reflected in any SOW, may be amended
only by mutual agreement of both parties in writing without
otherwise affecting the understanding contained in this
Agreement or such SOW.

Any short-term services (30 days) must be told to Arrow Security
prior to starting the work and be charged at special event/short
term service prices. In the event that any special and/or
emergency services (i.¢., not set forth in any then-current SOW)

twenty-four (24) hours’ prior written notice of termination of said
additional services. Overtime is only assessed when Officers work
more than 8 hours in a given day or more than 40 hours in a given
week. Arrow requires 48 hours prior notice for special events. No
overtime will be charged to client if 48 hours’ notice is given to
Arrow for special events. Overtime can be charged if client
requests current Officers work special events and Officers work
more than 8 hours in a day or 40 hours in a given week.

BILLING TERMS

Except as otherwise set forth in a SOW, Arrow will deliver to
Client, at the address specified in this Agreement, weekly invoices
for all work performed the previous week pursuant to each SOW.
Client shall pay such invoices within forty five (45) days of the
invoice date, without offset of any kind or nature whatsoever
unless such invoice needs to be adjusted for any reason.

Any invoice from Arrow shall be deemed conclusive proof of its
contents unless written objection to it is received by Arrow within
thirty (30) days of its issuance, in which event any claim or dispute
shall be deemed waived by Client. A statement of the number of
hours worked by each officer and/or time sheets will be made
available, at Client’s reasonable written request, provided the
request is received by Arrow within thirty (30) days of the last day
of the month in which the services were performed; provided,
however, that the payment of invoices is not conditioned upon
delivery of such statements/time sheets. Arrow Security utilizes
eHub / WinTeam electronic Officer check in / check out
automated software, which integrates into Arrow’s invoicing
WinTeam software. Arrow does not accept paper timesheets for
Officer check in / check out / invoicing verification. If Client
requires paper timesheets, an eight percent (8%) surcharge will
be added to each invoice as an administrative cost for such paper
timesheets.

In the event an invoice is not paid within forty-five (45) days of the
invoice date, Client shall pay to Arrow all costs and attorney’s fees
incurred by Arrow to collect such past due amount, and such
invoice shall accrue interest at a rate equal to the lesser of one
and one-half (14%) percent per month or the maximum legal rate
allowable by law beginning on the invoice date and continuing
until the date of payment.

Officer pay and bill rate adjustments will occur after New York
State Prevailing Wage Law adjustments on July 1** for each year of
the contract. Based on the New York State Prevailing Wage Law
increase or decrease, Arrow will adjust Officer pay rates in
accordance with New York State law and in turn will adjust bill
rates accordingly. Client understands that the bill rate adjustment.
is not a dollar for dollar increase as bill rates take into account
payroll taxes, insurance and other costs.

Any request by Client for additional services not otherwise
reflected in a SOW shall be delivered by Client to Arrow not less
than forty-eight (48) hours prior to the date such additional
services are needed. If less than 48 hours’ notice is given, such
services will be billed at the billable overtime rate set forth in the
applicable SOW. If the request of additional services exceeds five
(5%) percent of the base hours set forth in the SOW, then the
additional services shall be billed at the billable overtime rate
regardless of the amount of notice given. Such overtime rates
shall continue to apply until the SOW is amended in writing to
