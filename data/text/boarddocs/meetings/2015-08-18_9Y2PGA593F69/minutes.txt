	11. Discussions/Presentations

	Information: 11.1 Update on Smart Schools Bond

	Mr. Jesse Lubinsky provided the Board with an overview of the process that the District has undertaken to apply for the technology funding vis a vis the Smart Schools Bond Act which Irvington has been allocated $277,051 to improve educational technology and infrastructure. Mr. Lubinsky noted that the first step in the process was ask the Board to approve a 2014-18 Technology Plan (which it did at the June 16, 2015 meeting) followed by an investment plan that aligns with the approved plan. The Bond Act allows for funds to be allocated into six predefined categories.  Irvington’s Investment Plan would allocate the funds as follows:

-
		$179,845 for school connectivity (wi-fi for Dows Lane and Main Street);

-
		$97,206 for classroom technology (purchase of additional devices, i.e., Chromebooks, for student use.

	Approval of the Preliminary Smart School Bond Investment Plan by the School Board is required (on this meeting’s agenda), as is the posting of the Plan on the District’s website for at least 30 days to allow for all stakeholders to review the Plan, followed by a Public Hearing to enable stakeholders to comment on the Investment Plan. Administration will then complete the final SSB Investment Plan after which Board approval will be sought.
