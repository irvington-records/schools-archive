Meeting: Nov 22, 2022 - Work Session
Date: 2022-11-22
Category: 8. Business of the Board
Subject: 8.3 Senior Citizen Tax Exemption Income Level Adjustment
Type: Action

WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District has adopted resolutions in the past years with respect to tax abatements of real property taxes in accordance with enabling legislation enacted by the State Legislature; and

WHEREAS, in the 2021 regular session of the Legislature Chapter 558 was enacted, increasing the maximum eligible amount for tax abatements for senior citizens, which legislation requires any municipality who wishes to participate in the increased eligible maximum to adopt a resolution authorizing such an increase; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District finds it in the public interest to adopt resolutions pursuant to Chapter 558 of the Laws of 2021 increasing the amount of income that senior citizens can receive to the maximum allowable amount provided for in such Chapter:

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District hereby increases the base amount of income that senior citizens residing in owner-occupied residences can receive from a base amount of $29,000.00 to $50,000.00 as authorized by Chapter 558 of the Laws of 2021, where in State Legislature amended Section 467 (b) (1) and 467 (3) (a) to provide for such increase with a sliding scale thereafter; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that such maximum base amount of $50,000 authorized for owner-occupied pursuant to Section 467 (3) (a) of the Real Property Tax Law shall be used with a sliding scale provided for as follows:

			Income Level

			Percentage of Assessed Valuation Exempt from Taxation

			Up to $50,000.99

			50%

			$50,000.00-$50,999.99

			45%

			$51,000.00-51,999.99

			40%

			$52,000.00-$52,999.99

			35%

			$53,000.00-$53,899.99

			30%

			$53,900.00-$54,799.99

			25%

			$54,800.00-$55,699.99

			20%

			$55,700.00-$56,599.00

			15%

			$56,600.00-$57,499.99

			10%

			$57,500.00-$58,399.99

			5%

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this resolution authorizing increase pursuant to the authority of chapter 558 of the laws of 2021 shall take effect immediately and abatements granted thereunder shall apply as of the Town of Greenburgh taxable status date as of June 1, 2024.
