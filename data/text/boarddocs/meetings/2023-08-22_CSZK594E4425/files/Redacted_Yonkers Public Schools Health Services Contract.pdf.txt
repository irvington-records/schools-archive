YONKERS PUBLIC SCHOOL
CONTRACT FOR HEALTH AND WELFARE SERVICES

THIS AGREEMENT made this 6th day of June 2023 by and between Irvington Union Free School
District in the County of Westchester, New York, hereinafter called Referring District, and the Board of Education
of the Yonkers Public Schools, City of Yonkers, Westchester County, New York, hereinafter called Yonkers.

WITNESSETH, That whereas the Referring District has resolved that, pursuant to Section 912 of the
Education law, it entered into a contract with Yonkers for the purpose of providing, through Yonkers, health
services for those children residing within the Referring District and attending non-public schools in Yonkers, to
begin on September 6, 2022 and to the end on June 30, 2023.

NOW, THEREFORE, Referring District hereby agrees to pay to Yonker's the sum of $1,151.67 per pupil
for health services to be provided to each pupil listed in appendix A who are residents of the Referring District but
who attend non-public schools in Yonkers, See Appendix A attached.

The parties further agree as follows:

Services to be provided by Yonkers shall consist of the following:

U6 Such services may include, but are not limited to all services performed by a physician, physician
assistant, dentist, dental hygienist, registered professional nurse, nurse practitioner, schoo]
psychologist, school social worker or school speech therapist, and may also include dental
prophylaxis, vision and hearing screening examinations, the taking of medical histories and the
administration of health screening tests, the maintenance of cumulative health records and the
administration of emergency care programs for ill or injured students..

2. Annual medical inspection to same extent as provided for public school pupils, school nursing
service, vision and hearing tests, examinations for employment certificates, and examinations for
participation in athletics, notification of parents regarding defects and follow-up, instructions for
first-aid care for school emergencies.

3. Yonkers will furnish the following equipment to be used in providing such services if requested by
the authorities in charge of the non-public school: vision and hearing testing devices, health record

forms and first-aid supplies.

It is understood and agreed by and between the parties hereto that the services agreed to be supplied under
this contract shall not include any teaching service. It is further agreed that this contract shall not become valid and
binding upon either party hereto until the Superintendent of Schools of Referring District shall approve the same.
