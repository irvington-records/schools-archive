CONTRACT FOR SPECIAL EDUCATION SERVICES
2023-2024

Agreement made by and between the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District
having its principal office at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, New York 10533, (hereinafter called the “Sending
School District”) and the Pelham Union Free School District having its principal office at 629 Fifth Avenue,
Sanborn Map Building, Pelham, New York, 10803 (hereinafter called the "Receiving School District").

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS the Sending School District has need of an appropriate placement for a disabled student(s),
and

WHEREAS the Sending School District has determined that the Receiving School District’s Special
Education Program is appropriate for the child(ren), and

WHEREAS the Receiving School District is willing to accept the child(ren), a student(s) in its Special
Education Program, subject to the provisions of this Agreement,

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. The Sending School District hereby agrees to place the student(s) on attached Schedule “A” in the Special
Education Program of the Receiving School District for the year 2023-2024.

a. A student(s) may be added or deleted from the attached Schedule “A” at any time. In such event, the
payment amount owed by the Sending School District shall be adjusted accordingly.

2. The Sending School District agrees to pay the Receiving School District for this service the annual sum of
$89,426.00, to be adjusted to the rate that is set forth by the New York State Education Department for the
2023-2024 school year. This includes basic instruction, and consultation services provided within the
program, which money should be paid within 45 days of receipt of invoice.

3 The Receiving School District agrees to provide the services as set forth in the child’s IEP.

4. The Receiving School District agrees to report to the Sending School District on the progress of the
child as requested.

5 The Receiving School District agrees to permit a representative or representatives of the Committee on

Special Education of the Sending School District to visit the program in which the child(ren) is enrolled
on reasonable notice.

6. The Receiving School District agrees to comply with all federal and state statutes and regulations
regarding the provision of educational services to students with disabilities, subject to the provisions of
Paragraph “8” below.

Z Nothing contained herein, however, shall serve to transfer responsibilities for the child(ren) from the
Sending School District to the Receiving School District except ancillary duties such as report cards,
progress reports, parent-teacher conferences, etc.

8. Transportation to and from school is the Sending School District’s responsibility.

9. It is understood that this Agreement is for the 2023-2024 school year only, subject to the stay put
provisions of the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act. Tuition will adjust from one year to the next.

10. Nothing herein shall be construed from preventing either party from taking such action as it may deem

necessary, reasonable or appropriate in the case of a student whose continued presence in the
educational program would constitute a danger to others, to himself, or to property, sufficiently serious to
warrant immediate protective action.

11. The Sending School District shall maintain, in full force and effect, during all times this Agreement is in
force, the following types of insurance in the following amounts:

° Commercial General Liability Insurance
$1,000,000 per occurrence/ $2,000,000 aggregate.

Sexual misconduct coverage should be included.

. Workers' Compensation
Statutory Workers' Compensation and Employers’ Liability Insurance for all
employees.

. Excess/Umbrella Insurance

$3,000,000 each occurrence and aggregate

All such insurance shall reflect that the Receiving School District is an additional insured by using
endorsement CG2026 or broader. The Sending School District shall provide the Receiving School District
with a Certificate of Insurance that states this endorsement is being used. If another endorsement is used, a
copy shall be included with the certificate of insurance.

12; The Sending School District and the Receiving School District mutually agree to defend, indemnify, and
hold harmless each other from and against any and all liability, loss, damage, claim or action, to the extent
permissible by law, arising out of services provided.

13; This Agreement may be terminated by the Sending School District on 15 days written notice should its
Committee on Special Education determine that the Special Education Program is no longer appropriate
for
the child(ren).
