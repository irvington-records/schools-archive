The contract between the Irvington Union Free School District and the Greenburgh Graham is to provide educational and related services for the 2023-24 school year. The student number, rather than the name, has been provided in the resolution due to the confidential nature of this matter. The final tuition and maintenance rates are set by the NYS Education Department.
