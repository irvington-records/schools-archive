INTER MUNICIPAL COOPERATION AGREEMENT.

THIS AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") entered into as of the __ day of February,
2016, by and between the IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (hereinafter
referred to as "School District"), with offices for the transaction of business located at 6
Dows Lane, Irvington, New York 10533 and the VILLAGE OF IRVINGTON (hereinafter
referred to as "Village"), with offices for the transaction of business located at 85 Main
Street, Irvington, New York 10533,

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, under the provisions of Section 119-0 of the General Municipal Law,
each party has the power to enter into agreements for the performance one for the other
of their respective functions, powers and duties on a contract basis;

WHEREAS, the School District will undertake a project wherein existing
School District water runoff will be permitted and directed to flow from School
District premises under and through adjacent properties in order for the School
District to connect with, and discharge into, water drainage facilities system of such
neighboring properties (the “Project”);

WHEREAS, the Village requests, as part of the School District’s Project, that
the School District replace existing storm piping from Trails End to the existing
manhole in Fieldpoint Drive, as shown on Drawing E.01, prepared by LAN Associates
and attached to this Agreement as Exhibit “A”;

WHEREAS, the Village authorizes the School District to enter upon Village
property to complete the scope of work requested herein by the Village; and

WHEREAS, School District and Village wish to contract with one another in
accordance with General Municipal Law section 119-0.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein,
the parties agree as follows:

1. The Village does hereby grant, convey, transfer, deliver and release to the
School District a temporary easement under, on, and over the Village property shown
as “Trails End Connection,” for the purpose of constructing and installing drainage
pipe(s) and related structures,


[OCR]
2. The Schoo! District will contract with independent contractors for the
following scope of work requested by, and to be paid by, the Village:

a. That two (2) existing pipes will be replaced within Trails End and shall
both be increased in size from 18” to 24” diameter pipe; the first for a length
of ~110 feet, and the second for a length of ~42 feet;

b, That the existing 18” diameter pipe proposed to remain between the curb
inlet in Trails End and the Manhole in Fieldpoint Drive be replaced with 24”
diameter pipe; for a length of ~32 feet;

‘c. That the existing inlet in Trails End be modified to accept the new 24”
pipes;

d. That the existing manhole in Fieldpoint Drive be modified to accept the
new 24” pipes; and

e. All associated work related to a through d herein, including, trench
excavation, backfill, and surface restoration.

3. The School District shall promptly restore, or if necessary replace, to as
good condition as prior to entering upon said easement, any installations of any kind,
including but not limited to grass, landscaping, trees, bushes, in any manner disturbed
or damaged by the School District, its officers, employees, agents, servants or
contractors. Any re-seeding of disturbed property shall include overfill to maintain the
ground at its previous level.

4, The School District will prepare and maintain invoices detailing the
expenditure of all work completed for the benefit and at the request of the Village
under this Agreement. Invoices shall be submitted to the Village within 30 days of
completion of work. Invoices shall be sufficiently detailed to show the cost of work
performed. The Village shall pay such invoices within thirty (30) days

5. The term of this Agreement shall commence on February 24, 2016 and
terminate at the completion of the work described in paragraphs “2” and “3” above.

6. The School District agrees to give Village at least three (3) days’ notice
prior to commencing the initial construction under this easement.


[OCR]
7. The School District shall be responsible for all damage to life and property
due to negligent activities of the School District, her subcontractors, agents or
employees in connection with her services under this Agreement. The School District
specifically agrees that her Subcontractors, agents, or employees shall possess the
experience, knowledge and character necessary to qualify them individually for the
particular duties they perform. Further, it is expressly understood that the School
District shall indemnify and save harmless the Village, from claims, suits, actions,
damages and costs of every name and description resulting from the negligent
performance of the services of the School District under this Agreement, and such
indemnity shall not be limited by reason of enumeration of any insurance coverage
herein provided. Negligent performance of services, within the meaning of this Article,
shall include, in addition to negligence founded upon tort, negligence based upon the
School District's failure to meet professional standards and resulting in obvious or
patent errors in the progression of her work.

8, The School District is required to carry a general liability insurance policy
with limits of at least $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 aggregate. The Village of
Irvington shall be named as an additional insured, and a certificate of insurance must be
provided to the Village.

9. School District and Village, believing it to be in the best interest of their
taxpayers, do hereby authorize an inter-municipal cooperation agreement with and
between each other for the completion of the aforementioned work in exchange for the
Village’s payment to the School District for scope of work defined herein, in accordance
with applicable law and as provided for in this Agreement.

10. School District and Village shall each maintain all documents and records
created or maintained in connection with this Agreement for a period of six (6) years
after the termination of this Agreement. Each party agrees to make those documents
available for audit and inspection by any government official or agency with authority
and/or jurisdiction over the provision of the services described herein.

11, The School District is and shall at all times remain as to the Village wholly
independent. The personnel performing services under this shall at all times be under
the School District’s exclusive direction and control. Neither the Village nor any of its
officers, employees or agents, shall have control over the conduct of the School District
or any of its officers employees or agents, except as set forth in this Agreement.

12. The parties acknowledge that they will not hold themselves, their officers,
employees and/or agents out as employees of the other party. Neither the Village nor

14. Any alteration, change, addition, deletion, or modification of any of the
