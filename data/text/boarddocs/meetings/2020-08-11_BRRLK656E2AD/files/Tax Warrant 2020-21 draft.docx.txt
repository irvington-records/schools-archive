 Comptroller:

I, Pauline Davies, District Clerk of the Irvington Union Free School District, Town of Greenburgh, County of Westchester, State of New York, do hereby certify that the following budget and tax items for the 2020-2021 fiscal year, beginning July 1, 2020 and ending June 30, 2021 are true and accurate.  This is an official breakdown of the warrant for use for your collection of taxes.

At its meeting on August 11, 2020, the Board of Education adopted a motion containing the following resolution:

MOVED, that the voters of the Irvington Union Free School District, by a vote of 1,113 to 384, adopted a budget for the school year commencing July 1, 2020 in the amount of $64,556,500; that the sums as set forth in the forms of budget heretofore submitted to the voter be appropriated; that the taxes in the necessary amounts be levied therefore; that the Board members be authorized to execute a warrant therefore, and that the warrant be delivered to the Town authorities.

The major budget categories and their respective amounts are listed below:

The amount to be raised by taxes on the Real Property of this School District is $58,528,791.

The Board of Education anticipates other revenue sources of $2,049,123, estimated State & Federal aid of $3,556,086 and income of $422,500 from the unexpended surplus funds in the custody of the Board as authorized by the Board or required by law.
