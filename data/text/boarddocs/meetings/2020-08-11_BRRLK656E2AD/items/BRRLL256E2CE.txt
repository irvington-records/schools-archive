Meeting: Aug 11, 2020 - Regular Meeting
Date: 2020-08-11
Category: 6. Comments From Members of the Community
Subject: 6.1 Procedures for Community Comment on Topics that appear on Agenda
Type: Information

Tonight's Board of Education meeting will serve as one of three required public meetings for the public to comment on the District's proposed reopening plan. Those wishing to comment or pose questions on the reopening plan are invited to place their name and address in the Zoom Q&A. The Board of Education President will invite the respective members of the community to speak. Each speaker will have up to three minutes to speak. The Board of Education requests that the Q&A function not be used for other comments, communications or questions.
