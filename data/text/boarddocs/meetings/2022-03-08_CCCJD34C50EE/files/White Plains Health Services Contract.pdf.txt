CONTRACT FOR HEALTH AND WELFARE SERVICECS

THIS AGREEMENT made in duplicate January 31, 2022 by and between the Board of Education of Irvington UFSD (the Schoo!
District of Residence), County of Westchester, and the City of White Plains (White Plains City Schoo! District), Count of
Westchester.

WITNESSETH, that whereas the School District of Residence is duly empowered by the provisions of Section 912 of the
Education Law to enter into a contract for the purposes of providing health and welfare services ta the children residing in said
school district and attending nonpublic schools in the White Plains City School District to begin September 9, 2021 and to end
on June 24, 2022.

Now, therefore, the School District of Residence agrees to pay the White Plains City Schoot District the estimated sum of
$1,120.25 per pupil for health and welfare services provided to approximately 11 children residing in the School District of
Residence and attending nonpublic school in the White Plains City Schoo! District. It is further agreed that at the end of the
2021-2022 school year, the foregoing charge will be recomputed using actual expenditures for health and welfare service and
actual enrollment as of October 1, 2021, and that a refund or additional charge will be made as required on the basis of said
re-computation.

And the White Plains City School District hereby agrees with the School District of Residence as follows:

1, That the services to be provided shall include, as needed, the following: services performed by a physician, nurse,
school psychologist, school social worker, or school speech correctionist, and may also include vision and hearing test,
the taking of medicat histories, and the administration of health screening tests, the maintenance of cumulative health
records and the administration of emergency care programs for ill or injured pupils.

2. That the White Plains City School District will also furnish the following equipment to be used in providing such services
as requested by the authorities in charge of the nonpublic school: vision and hearing testing devices, health record
forms and first aid supplies,

It is expressly agreed by and between the parties hereto that the service agreed to be supplied under this contract shall not
include any teaching services.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have hereunto set their hands the day and year above written.
