Irvington Union Free School District
Monthly Financial Highlights

January 2022

Cash Balance

Our January cash position was $35.35 million, which was approximately $4.91 million higher than December 2021.

Cash received in the Real Property & Other Taxes category included a tax payment from the Town of Greenburgh for tax collection during
the month of December in the amount of $7,636,844 and STAR payment from New York State totaling $2,004,087.

State and Federal Aid Cash Receipts totaled $122,622 which included initial payments for 21-22 grants, federal breakfast reimbursement, and
VLT Lottery aid payment

Cash disbursements during the month of January included normal monthly check warrants and two payroll runs.

Revenue:

General Fund Revenue through January was $64,017,411 or 96.2% of the budgeted amount. This is approximately 3.5% higher of revenue
realized that at the same time last year.

The District received reimbursement for 19-20 CPSE administrative costs from Westchester County in the amount of $44,822. This was
accounted for in Miscellaneous Sources in the Revenue Report.

Health services invoices were sent out to the various districts in Januaty. Revenue should start to be reflected in February.

Expenditures:

Actual General Fund Expenses totaled approximately $4.74 million for January. ‘This brings the total year to date expenditures to $30,969,060.
7% of the budget remains including the outstanding encumbrances.

As a reminder, in February the Board approved a budget amendment to fund the tax certiorari account code from the reserve to correct the
negative amount. This will be reflected in next month’s reports.
