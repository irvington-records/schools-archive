The District received 3 bids for Contract G in a bid opening conducted publicly on February 18, 2022.  The District's construction manager, Triton Construction, and architects, H2M Architects and Engineers, have reviewed the bid proposals for adherence to the bid specifications.  They have met with the low bidders to review the project scope and have conducted reference checks. Award recommendation letters are attached.  A single prime contract is permitted when the total award amount is below $1,500,000.

At this time, no alternates are being awarded.  The District may elect to award Alternate G1 at a later date.
