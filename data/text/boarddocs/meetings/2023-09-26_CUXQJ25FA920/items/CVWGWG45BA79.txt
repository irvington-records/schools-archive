Meeting: Sep 26, 2023 - Work Session
Date: 2023-09-26
Category: 11. Business Office Matters
Subject: 11.6 Year End Fund Balance
Type: Action (Consent)

- $2,923,165 is the allowable maximum amount (4% of the 2023-24 budget) and recommended to be designated as Unassigned Fund Balance by the Comptroller

- $422,500 was presented during the budget discussions

- The Tax Certiorari reserve is used to make refunds on previous years' settlements/claims.

- The Worker's Comp Reserve is used to pay ongoing worker comp claims.

- The Capital Reserve will be used for future capital projects with voter consent.

- The Retirement Contribution Reserve will be used to offset future increases in the required ERS or TRS contribution rate in years where the rate increase could affect academic programs.

- The Employee Benefit Liability Reserve is used for contractual benefits to retiring employees.
