Subject: Consulting Services for Irvington Union Free School District – Landscape
         Planning and Implementation


Dear Carol,
I am pleased to provide you with a proposal for Landscape Architectural consulting services to
assist the Irvington Union Free School District (UFSD) with enhancements to the landscapes of
the district’s facilities that were begun in the summer of 2023.
Nolan Landscape Architects, PLLC (NLA) will assist the district with planning for continued
enhancements of the landscape of the High School/Middle School complex. Working with a
landscaping committee comprised of students, residents, educators and environmental
advocates, NLA will help implement landscape improvements consistent with the vision
developed in the fall/winter of 2022.
Scope of Services:

   •   NLA will meet with District’s landscaping committee on a periodic basis to develop
       strategies to implement more sustainable landscape practices.
   •   NLA will assist the Environmental Club and the District Grounds Staff with monitoring the
       Stormwater Basin Planting Pilot Project implemented in spring 2023, and with
       implementation of next phases: Maintenance of existing plantings; Fall/winter seeding of
       prepared plots; Future planting/seeding phases.
   •   NLA will assist the District in monitoring the health of trees obtained through the New
       York Power Authority Tree Program that were planted by District grounds staff in Fall
       2022.
   •   NLA will prepare plant lists for the District to use in obtaining appropriate plant material
       to implement the schematic planting plan for the High School entry area that NLA
       prepared in the Spring of 2023. NLA will be present on planting days to monitor the
       work, which will be performed by District Grounds Staff.

GENERAL PROVISIONS

Standard of Care: The landscape architectural services shall be performed with care and
diligence in accordance with the professional standards applicable at the time and in the
location of the project, and appropriate to its nature and scope.

Ownership of Documents: The landscape architect shall be deemed the author and owner of
all documents and deliverables developed pursuant to this agreement and provided to the Client
by the Landscape Architect (collectively, the “Design Materials”). Subject to payment by the
Client of all fees and reimbursable expenses owed to the Landscape Architect, the Landscape
Architect grants to the Client an irrevocable, non-exclusive license to reproduce the Design
Materials solely for the construction of the project.

Opinions of Cost: As the Landscape Architect has no control over the cost of labor, materials,
equipment, or services provided by others, or methods of contractors to determine prices in a
competitive bidding market, opinions of probable costs are based on qualified judgement and
are not a guarantee of actual construction costs.

Limited Construction Phase Services: The landscape architect shall not control or be
responsible for another’s means, methods, techniques, schedules, sequences or procedures, or
for construction safety or any other related programs, or for another’s failure to compete the
work in accordance with the plans and specifications.

Insurance: Owner agrees that all insurance provided by the contractor hired to perform the
work, including general liability, workers compensation, and property damage, shall be
endorsed to name Nolan Landscape Architects, PLLC as additional insured.

Client Responsibilities: As the Owner, you will be responsible for all application, review,
permits and fees associated with all aspects of obtaining a building permit from the Village of
Irvington, or other regulatory agencies with authority over the work .

Excluded Services: Surveying, engineering, or other related professional services are not
included in this scope of work.
