DAY 1:TRAVEL TO SPAIN                     Wednesday 20-Mar-2024

                   Transfer by motor coach from Irvington, NY to the airport.

                   Board your flight from New York and begin the journey to Madrid, Spain.




DAY 2:MADRID                                  Thursday 21-Mar-2024

                   Bienvenido a España!
                   Meet your Spanish Prometour Tour Director at the arrivals section of the airport.

                   Transfer by motor coach from the airport to your hotel to drop off your luggage, check-in will
                   be done later.

                   While in Madrid, travel as the locals do by walking and taking public transportation.

                   Head to Parque El Retiro with its ornate fountains, gardens, marble monuments, and open
                   spaces. It belonged to the Spanish Monarchy until the late 19th century and is now one of
                   Madrid's largest public parks. Go rowing on the pond in front of the magnificent Palacio de
                   Cristal.
                   (45 minutes, 4 people per row boat)

                   Have lunch on your own.

                   Enjoy a walk with your Tour Director along Calle Mayor. Take a look at the Cathedral de
                   Almudena, enter Mercado de San Miguel, admire the magnificent Plaza Mayor, and finish at
                   the Puerta del Sol.

                   Try Spanish Turron (nougat) at Casa Mira where it has been traditionally made since 1855.

                   Enjoy an authentic Paella dinner at a local restaurant with your group in Madrid.

                   Overnight accommodation in Madrid.




DAY 3:MADRID - CORDOBA                            Friday 22-Mar-2024

                   Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group.

                   Have a guided tour of El Palacio Real, the official residence of the Spanish Royal Family
                   while in Madrid.

                   Have lunch on your own.

                   Have a guided tour of Spain's largest bullring, Plaza de Toros de Las Ventas. Discover a
                   place which is emblematic of the heart and soul of bullfighting and where the world’s finest
                   matadors present their artistry and skill.

                   Board your motor coach and travel to Cordoba, the City of Three Cultures; Christianity, Islam,
                   & Judaism.

                   Enjoy dinner in a local restaurant with your group.

                   Overnight accommodation in Cordoba.

DAY 4:CORDOBA - SEVILLA                       Saturday 23-Mar-2024

                   Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group.

                   Enjoy a guided tour of the Cordoba Mezquita, an 8th century medieval Islamic mosque
                   converted into a Catholic cathedral and regarded as one of the most accomplished
                   monuments of Moorish architecture in Spain.

                   Have lunch on your own.

                   Explore the historical city center of Cordoba at yourown pace, it's a UNESCO World Heritage
                   Site.

                   Board your motor coach and travel to Sevilla, the capital of southern Spain’s Andalusia
                   region and a hotbed for flamenco dance, especially in the Triana neighborhood.

                   Discover the Plaza de Espana with your Tour Director. Located at the edge of the beautiful
                   Parque Maria Luisa, it was designed as the centerpiece of the 1929 Ibero-American
                   Exposition.

                   Enjoy dinner in a local restaurant with your group.

                   Overnight accommodation in Seville.




DAY 5:SEVILLA                                   Sunday 24-Mar-2024

                   Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group.

                   Enjoy a guided tour of the Cathedral of Seville, the largest Gothic building in the world, and
                   claims to contain the remains of Columbus.

                   Climb to the top of La Giralda, the Moorish Minaret is Seville's most recognizable monument
                   and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

                   Have lunch on your own.

                   Partake in a Flamenco Extravaganza! Start with a guided tour of El Museo del Baile
                   Flamenco, followed by a 45-minute Flamenco Dance Class, and end the visit by attending an
                   authentic non-tourist Flamenco Show.

                   Have an authentic tapas dinner in the Barrio Santa Cruz.

                   Overnight accommodation in Seville.




DAY 6:SEVILLA - GRANADA                         Monday 25-Mar-2024

                   Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group.

                   Board your motor coach and travel to Granada, in the foothills of the Sierra Nevada
                   mountains in Andalusia.

                   Have a guided visit of the magnificent Alhambra and Generalife Gardens. Explore beyond
                   the beautiful Moorish architecture to learn about the history of one of the most visited
                   attractions in the world.

                   Have lunch on your own.

                   Discover the Albaicín (Albayzin) Quarter, the oldest section of Granada with its narrow
                   cobbled streets and cármenes (Moorish-style houses), and admire the view from the Mirador
                   de San Nicolas.

                   Enjoy dinner in a local restaurant with your group.

                          Overnight accommodation in Granada.




DAY 7:GRANADA - MADRID                                Tuesday 26-Mar-2024

                          Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group.

                          Board your motor coach and travel to Madrid, the capital of Spain.

                          Enjoy a typical Spanish snack of Churros y Chocolate.

                          Learn the art of playing Castanuelas, it takes rhythm and fast fingers.

                          Dinner in a local restaurant with your group in Madrid.
  Churros con Chocolate   During dinner, enjoy the unique entertainment of a Tuna group, a band of men dressed in old
                          medieval costumes who sing traditional Spanish songs.

                          Overnight accommodation in Madrid.




DAY 8:MADRID - RETURN HOME                       Wednesday 27-Mar-2024

                          Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group.

                          Transfer by motor coach from your hotel to the airport.

                          Say a fond Hasta Luego to your Spanish Prometour Tour Director.

                          Board your transatlantic flight from Madrid and fly back to New York

                          Board your flight from Madrid and begin the journey back to New York.
