Dear Ms. Miller:

On November 10, 2015 at 11:00 a.m. bids were opened and publicly read out loud for the above-
referenced project. A total of two (2) bids were received for this project. A Bid Recording Form is made
Attachment No. 1 for your immediate reference. Upon reviewing the submitted bids Sun-Dance Energy
Contractors Inc. DBA Markley Mechanical is the lowest bidder with the base bid amount of $162,516.00.
A bid bond was submitted with the low bid.

Based on the fact that Markley Mechanical is the lowest responsible bidder and references are favorable,
it is LAN Associates, Engineering, Planning, Architecture, Surveying, LLP (LAN's) recommendation that
the Irvington Union Free School District award Contract No.2 to Markley Mechanical.
