Meeting: Oct 24, 2017 - Work Session/Special Meeting
Date: 2017-10-24
Category: 2. Board Recognition
Subject: 2.1 School Boards Recognition
Type: Recognition

School Board Recognition Week in New York State is October 23-27, 2017 and to recognize the members of the Irvington school board for their time and commitment, Irvington Mayor, Brian Smith,will present the Board with a Proclamation. Representatives from the PTSA, IEF, and district's bargaining units as well as the District will present members of the Board with tokens of appreciation.
