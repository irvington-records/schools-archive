Subject: 11.4 Contract - Service Provider
Type: Action (Consent)

The Irvington School District contracts with several service providers to provide a variety of related services for our special education students. We are also required to provide services to those students who attend the John Cardinal O'Connor School in Irvington. We then invoice the home districts of those students.
