Dear Carol Stein:

On December 20, 2021, sealed bids were received by the Irvington Union Free School District for the above referenced
project and contract. As indicated on the attached bid tabulation, the low bid among the six (6) bids that were received was
for a total contract value of $607,750.00 (includes $30,000 General Contingency Allowance). There were also alternate bid
prices provided, items G1 through G3 for additional work items with a total bid price with alternates of $836,150.00. This bid
was submitted by:

                                         IJZ Associates, Inc.
                                         428 Johnson Ave.
                                         Brooklyn, NY 11237

H2M, along with Mr. Gary Knowles of the District and Mr. Kevin Sawyer of Triton Construction Corp., conducted an interview
with this bidder to assist the District in awarding this contract. This interview included discussions of the low bidder’s bid
submission, regarding their qualifications and references, to ensure the contractor’s complete understanding of the proposed
scope of work, their ability to perform the work, their financial stability to begin work, ability to meet the established completion
dates, availability of materials / labor and review the overall scope for compliance with the project specifications.

Based upon our review of the contractor’s qualifications, we believe this contractor has met all the requirements outlined for
bidding and is therefore qualified to perform the work. As result of our findings, we offer the following recommendation.

                                         IJZ Associates, Inc. for a total bid contract price of $607,750.00 (Base
                                         Bid $607,750.00 without Alternate G1 through G3)

Please call me if you have any questions concerning our recommendation. Should the District proceed with award of the
contract, please notify our office so the conformed contracts may be prepared. If the District desires, we can issue a letter of
intent to this contractor so that the contractor can proceed to assemble the documents needed for contract signing and begin
to schedule the commencement of the construction. If you have any questions, please feel free to contact me at (914) 358-
5623 x2064.

Respectfully Submitted,
