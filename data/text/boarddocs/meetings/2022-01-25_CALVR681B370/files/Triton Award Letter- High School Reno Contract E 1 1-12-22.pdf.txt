Based on the above bid information, pending any final review required by District Council, the apparent responsible low
bidder is Talt Electric for the Total Suggested Contract Amount indicated above.

The references provided by Talt Electric gave no reason for disqualification. In addition, the New York State Department
of Labor has indicated that they are not debarred from doing public work, and there are no known OSHA violations
against this contractor in the past (7) seven years. Our Project scope review with this contractor indicates an
understanding of the project requirements and expectations. And the Companies bond strength, financial stability, and
insurance coverage meets or exceed contract requirements.

We are including the following attachments for you review: Contractor Bid Proposal, bid tabulation sheets, and Contractor
Check List. Upon the Board’s approval please send the attached NTP to the contractor by email and overnight mail on
School District letterhead.
