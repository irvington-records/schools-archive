Based on the above bid information, pending any final review required by District Council, the apparent responsible low
bidder is IJZ Associates, Inc. for the Total Suggested Contract Amount indicated above.

Department of Labor has indicated that they are not debarred from doing public work, and there are no known OSHA

understanding of the project requirements and expectations. And the Companies bond strength, financial stability, and
insurance coverage meets or exceed contract requirements.
