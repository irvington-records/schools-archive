District Updates

Dr. Harrison spoke about the states confusing messages today regarding the mask mandate. A temporary stay has been issued  by the appellate courts that masks will be required in school at least until the case is heard again. The District is awaiting clear and concise guidelines from New York State. In the meantime, all students and staff members need to wear masks in school until there are updates. Ironically, there were no COVID cases reported today. The District will be communicating a plan to distribute rapid antigen tests to students and staff. Saturday there was a vaccination clinic at the District where students received their first dose and booster shots. Future clinics will be held as needed.

Brooke Dunefsky has been named a finalist in the Regeneron Science Talent Search and was awarded $40,000 for her research she has performed in the Irvington IHS Science Research Project. She is one of the 40 finalists that will participate in a week-long competition in March where $1.8 million will compete for more that $1.8 million in awards. In addition, Nadia Parikka who started the Science Research Program, earned Regeneron Science Talent Search's Teacher of Merit award. Fifteen Irvington High School students were named Cove Value Award winners for the month of December in the category of perseverance. These students were honored for exhibiting true grit, determination and dedication despite difficulty.
