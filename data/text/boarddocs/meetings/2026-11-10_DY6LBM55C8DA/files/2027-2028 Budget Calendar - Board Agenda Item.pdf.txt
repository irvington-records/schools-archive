                                                  CALENDAR FOR 2027-28 BUDGET


 2026

 November 1-30          Appointments arranged with Director of Facilities to discuss Facility Requests

 November 10            Board adopts Budget calendar for 2027-2028

 November 16            Administrators and Directors receive budget materials & request forms

 December 14            Submit Budget memo & supporting documentation

 December 14-17         Superintendent and Assistant Superintendents meet individually with Principals,
                        Directors, Assistant Principals and Assistant Directors

  2027

 January 11             Final budget requests due in Business Office (including staffing requests)


 February 23            Board of Education Budget BOE presentation: Budget Landscape and Business Office,
                        Debt Service, Insurance, Buildings & Grounds, Transportation & Employee Benefits

 March 1                District to submit Tax Cap data to NYS Office of State Comptroller, Commissioner of
                        Education, and Commissioner of Taxation and Finance

 March 9                Board of Education Budget Discussion-Athletics/ Special Education/PPS,
                        Curriculum/Technology


 March 30               Board of Education Budget Discussion – Superintendent’s Budget

 March 30 - April 3     Print legal notices - four within seven weeks.

 April 13               Board of Education Budget Discussion, Potential Adoption

 April 20**             Board of Education adopts Budget

 April 21               Tax Report Card electronically submitted to SED, sent to local newspaper and appended
                        to budget - within 24 hours of Budget adoption

 ~May 1                 Budget book distributed to schools/website for display

 May 4                  Board conducts official Budget Hearing

 May 4-11               Six–day Budget Notice mailed to all qualified voters

 May 18                 Budget Vote and Board Elections



**Final adoption must occur by April 20 but can be adopted prior to this date
