                            Mercy University / Irvington UFSD

              MUTUAL EMERGENCY EVACUATION AGREEMENT


AGREEMENT made this _ day of                              2026 by and between MERCY
UNIVERSITY, with its main administrative office at 555 Broadway, Dobbs Ferry, New York
and the IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT ("Irvington UFSD"), with its main
administrative office at 6 Dows Lane, Irvington, New York.

                                     WITNESSETH

         WHEREAS, pursuant to Part 155.17 of the Regulations of the New York State
Commissioner of Education, each school district is responsible for creating and maintaining a
district-wide school safety plan and building-level emergency response plans; and

        WHEREAS, the comprehensive emergency preparedness measures specified in Part
155.17 require each school district to develop contingency plans for the rapid mass evacuation
of one or more school buildings as a result of a natural disaster, fire, serious violent incident,
or other type of emergency situation; and

       WHEREAS, Mercy University is authorized to enter into facility-sharing agreements
pursuant to § 202(a) of the New York Not-for-Profit Corporation Law and Irvington UFSD is
authorized to enter into facility-sharing agreements pursuant to Article 5-G of the General
Municipal Law of the State of New York, and

      WHEREAS, Mercy University and Irvington UFSD are geographically proximate in
Westchester County; and

        WHEREAS, Mercy University and the Board of Education of Irvington UFSD wish to
enter into an agreement whereby each party's facilities are mutually available to the other as a
short-term emergency evacuation site during emergency situations;

        NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the promises contained in this agreement, the
parties agree as follows:

Agreement for Use of Mercy University’s Parking Lots. Grounds, and Facilities

       1.      In the event it is necessary for Irvington UFSD to conduct an emergency
               evacuation of one or more of its school buildings, Mercy University will
               authorize the use of certain, parking lots, grounds, and facilities as emergency
               evacuation, relocation, and/or pick-up sites for students, teachers, and other staff
               members.

2.    Upon commencing an evacuation of a school building, the Irvington
      Superintendent of Schools or designee will communicate the evacuation plan
      and approximate number of students and staff members subject to emergency
      evacuation to Mercy University. The selection of specific Mercy University
      facilities for emergency evacuation use will be promptly made by the Mercy
      University Assistant Vice President for Campus Safety and Emergency
      Management or designee, and immediately communicated to Irvington UFSD.

3.    Mercy University will allow Irvington UFSD school buses and vehicles onto its
      parking lots and grounds for the purpose of relocating or picking up Irvington
      UFSD students during an emergency situation. Mercy University will permit
      access by Irvington UFSD students and staff members to such parking lots,
      grounds, and indoor shelter facilities if necessary.

4.    Mercy University will allow parents or guardians of Irvington UFSD students
      onto its parking lots and grounds, in their vehicles, for the purpose of picking up
      their children in the event of an emergency evacuation.

5.    Mercy University agrees to reserve its parking lots, grounds, and facilities for
      use by Irvington UFSD, as necessary, for the duration of the emergency situation.
      An emergency situation contemplated by this Agreement will generally be
      considered resolved when all evacuated/relocated Irvington UFSD students have
      been released to the custody of their parents or guardians.

6.    Mercy University will permit Irvington UFSD to use available traffic control
      equipment (i.e., traffic cones and/or barricades) as necessary to produce an
      orderly flow of traffic into and out of the designated pick up area. Irvington
      UFSD will be responsible, subject to input from Mercy University personnel, for
      controlling traffic flow into and out of school parking lots and grounds from the
      public roadway and for directing Irvington UFSD students and staff as necessary
      to protect their safety and welfare.

7.    Irvington UFSD will be responsible for supervising and monitoring its students
      and staff members while on Mercy University property. Release of students to
      parents or guardians will be supervised and controlled according to, and
      exclusively by, Irvington UFSD authorities, in accordance with established
      Irvington UFSD procedures.

8.    Irvington UFSD staff members will direct bus loading or egress and the orderly
      congregation of Irvington UFSD students on Mercy University parking lots and
      grounds.

9.    Each party agrees to designate an on-site liaison/emergency coordinator to
      facilitate communication and maintain an orderly implementation of this plan.

10.   Irvington UFSD shall obtain prior approval from the Mercy University Assistant
      Vice President for Campus Safety and Emergency Management, or designee,

            before entering Mercy University parking lots, grounds, or facilities. Access
            shall not be denied except for good cause.
     11.    This Agreement is subject to review annually on the anniversary date of its
            execution and may be terminated by Mercy University at any time upon written
            notice to Irvington UFSD. Additionally, either party may request a review of the
            Agreement as part of a post emergency re-evaluation upon 30 days notice, and
            such request shall be made in writing. Neither party may deny a post emergency
            re-evaluation except for good cause.

     12.    Irvington UFSD agrees to defend, hold harmless, and indemnify Mercy
            University, its employees, officers, agents, and servants from and against any
            and all claims, actions, suits, arbitrations, judgments and/or executions, or any
            other legal liability, including reasonable attorneys' fees, in respect to any
            negligent act, omission, or intentional misconduct of Irvington UFSD, its
            employees, agents, officers, contractors, or assigns during the use of Mercy
            University’s parking lots, grounds, or facilities.

      13.   Mercy University agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Irvington UFSD
            and its employees, officers, and agents from and against third-party claims
            for bodily injury, death, or damage to tangible property, including reasonable
            attorneys’ fees and defense costs, but only to the extent caused by the
            negligent acts or omissions or willful misconduct of Mercy University or its
            employees, officers, or agents acting within the scope of their duties in
            connection with Irvington UFSD’s use of Mercy University’s parking lots,
            grounds, or facilities under this Agreement. Irvington UFSD shall promptly
            notify Mercy University in writing of any claim for which indemnification is
            sought and reasonably cooperate in its defense; failure to give prompt notice
            shall relieve Mercy University of its obligations only to the extent materially
            prejudiced. Mercy University may participate in the defense with counsel of
            its choice, and no settlement imposing liability or obligations on Mercy
            University shall bind it without its prior written consent, which shall not be
            unreasonably withheld.


General Terms and Conditions

      14.   Nothing contained in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment
            or principal-agent relationship, or a partnership or joint venture, between the
            parties or any officer, employee or agent of either party, nor shall either party
            have the right, power, or authority to obligate or bind the other party in any
            manner whatsoever.
      15.   This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

      16.   This Agreement contains all of the terms and conditions agreed upon by the
            parties hereto in regard to the subject matter hereof, and no other agreement, oral
            or otherwise, shall be deemed to exist or bind either of the parties hereto or to
