Irvington Union Free School District
Monthly Financial Highlights

September 2026

Cash Balance

Our current overall cash position is $14.5 million, which declined by $3,987,377 from prior months.
The Town of Greenburgh’s first tax collection payment will be reflected in October. This will bolster our cash position.

State and Federal Aid cash received totaled $1,270,423, which included the final 25-26 General Aid payment and the first 26-27 General Aid
payments funded by lottery/gaming revenues.

Revenue:

General Fund Revenue is currently at $73,070,877 or 89.1% of the budgeted amount YTD, as the tax levy is recorded in full as revenue
received per accounting practices.

We also recorded State Aid revenue of $1,106,364 for September. However, this was a book entry against our TRS payment and thus no cash was
received. Rather than send the state aid money to the District and have the District in turn pay TRS, the State sends the funds directly to TRS to
ensure their payment.

The Use of Money mostly includes interest earnings from all District general fund bank accounts. The District has earned $110,320 in interest
revenue through the September 26-27 fiscal year and represents 13.4% of the overall forecasted revenue. We are trying to utilize our financial
instruments as best as possible with the current market fluctuation. We continue to receive an average monthly return rate of 3.58% on our
NYCLASS investments and further look for opportunities to ensure we are maximizing our potential earnings with our NYCLASS
Investment partners. We will analyze the U.S. Treasury market or the use of Certificate of Deposit with NYCLASS to determine the best
instrument for the highest rate.

Expenditures:

e

The Adjusted budget of $83,071,155 reflects the carryover encumbrances from the 25-26 budget in the amount of $599,195. These
encumbrances reflect purchase orders where the goods or services we not completed at fiscal year-end, June 30.

Actual General Fund Expenses totaled approximately $5,228,930 for September and $10,009,562 fiscal YTD. This is largely representing
salaries for staff, supplies necessary to start schoo] and a Bond Payment for the 2015 Capital Seal Bond in the amount of $156K.
