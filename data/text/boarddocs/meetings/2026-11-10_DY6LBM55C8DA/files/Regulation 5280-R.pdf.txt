5280-R ATHLETIC PLACEMENT PROCESS

5280-R

Athletic Placement Process

The Board recognizes that, in limited circumstances, a student may benefit from
participating in an interscholastic athletic program outside the student's usual
grade-level classification.

Consistent with applicable regulations, the District may utilize the New York
State Athletic Placement Process (APP) to permit:




   1. A student in grade eight to try out for and potentially compete on a senior
      high school athletic team; or
   2. A student in grades nine through twelve to try out for and potentially compete
      on an athletic team for grades no lower than seventh grade.

Participation through the APP is intended for the limited number of students who
may benefit from competing at a different grade level. No eighth grade student will
be allowed to participate on a high school team if there are high school students
being cut from that particular program. In accordance with NYS Commissioner’s
Regulations, the APP shall not be used to fill roster vacancies, provide additional
playing opportunities, replace a modified athletic program, or reward a student.

The District's use of the APP is discretionary and shall be implemented only in
accordance with applicable law, regulation, NYSED guidance, and procedures
established by the Superintendent or designee.

Students can be invited to participate in APP at any point in the athletic season.

APP Invitation and Consent

A student in grade eight may try out for a senior high school team through the APP
only upon receiving an invitation from the District or the District's Athletic Director or
Physical Education Director, as authorized by applicable regulation.

A request or recommendation from a student, parent or guardian, out of district
coach, teacher, or other individual does not itself constitute the required invitation.

Prior to participating in the APP, the student's parent or guardian shall provide
informed consent on a form meeting the requirements established by the
Commissioner of Education.

An invitation to participate in the APP provides an opportunity to try out and does
not guarantee placement on a team.


APP Readiness Determination

Parents or guardians must submit medical clearance from the student’s personal
physician or from the School District’s physician.

Students participating in the APP shall demonstrate sufficient readiness during
the standard team-selection process in the following areas:



   1.   Personal and social responsibility;
   2.   Sport-specific skill development;
   3.   Knowledge of the game; and
   4.   Physical fitness.

The District may consider research-based factors relevant to the student's risk of
injury and readiness to compete at another grade level, consistent with applicable
NYSED guidance and District procedures.

Such factors may include, as appropriate, the student's age, movement and
biomechanics, body composition, previous injury, grade of competition, training load,
position played, type of competition or practice, strength, player experience, level of
body contact, and environmental factors.

The District shall not subject a student participating in the APP to a separate
prerequisite fitness test, physical screening, or other assessment of the four
readiness factors before the student is invited to try out for a high school team when
such screening is prohibited by applicable regulation.




Team Selection

Once a student has been determined eligible to participate through the APP, the
student shall participate in the same team-selection process as all other students
trying out for that team.

The criteria used to select team members shall be applied uniformly to all students
who qualify to try out, regardless of grade level or whether a student is participating
through the APP.

An APP student shall not be required to demonstrate a skill, fitness standard, or
other selection criterion that is not also required of other students trying out for the
same team.
