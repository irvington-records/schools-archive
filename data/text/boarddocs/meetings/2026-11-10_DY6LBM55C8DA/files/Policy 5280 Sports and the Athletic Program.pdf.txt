POLICY 5280 - SPORTS AND THE ATHLETIC PROGRAM
The Board of Education recognizes that athletics are an integral part of a
well-balanced educational program and supports a broad interscholastic and
intramural athletic program that provides equitable opportunities for student
participation.

The District's interscholastic athletic program shall be conducted in accordance
with applicable New York State Education Law, the Regulations of the
Commissioner of Education, the rules and regulations of the New York State Public
High School Athletic Association (NYSPHSAA), and applicable District policies and
regulations.


Eligibility for Athletic Participation

Students participating in interscholastic athletics shall:

   1. Provide the required written consent of a parent or guardian;
   2. Provide proof of a health examination in accordance with applicable law and
      regulations, including any required health history;
   3. Be medically cleared for athletic participation;
   4. Satisfy all applicable eligibility requirements established by the Commissioner
      of Education, NYSPHSAA, and the District; and
   5. Comply with all applicable team, academic, behavioral, attendance, and
      eligibility requirements.

The District shall maintain procedures to ensure that students participating in
extra-class athletic activities meet applicable health and safety requirements.

A student who has been removed from athletic participation because of a suspected
traumatic brain injury, signs or symptoms indicating an increased risk of sudden
cardiac arrest, or an injury or medical condition that, in the judgment of the athletic
trainer or other qualified school health professional, warrants further medical
evaluation shall not resume participation until appropriate medical clearance has
been obtained in accordance with District procedures and applicable law.

This shall include, but is not limited to, injuries resulting in removal from a practice or
competition for which the athletic trainer recommends evaluation by a licensed
health care provider, including suspected sprains, fractures, breaks, significant
musculoskeletal injuries, or other conditions that may affect the student's ability to
safely participate.

The Director of School Health Services or District medical director may determine,
after consultation with the student's health care provider as required by law, that a
student with a severe health condition may not participate in extra-class athletic
activities when such participation would be detrimental to the student's health or
safety.

Students with activity limitations shall provide documentation from a licensed health
professional identifying any restrictions and the portions of the physical education or
athletic program in which the student may safely participate.
