Tri States Visitation

February 5- 7, 2025

           What is Tri States?
The Tri States Consortium is an alliance of
public schools which includes approximately
60 districts from New York, New Jersey, and
Connecticut committed to systems thinking
and collaborative inquiry as pathways toward
continuous improvement.
