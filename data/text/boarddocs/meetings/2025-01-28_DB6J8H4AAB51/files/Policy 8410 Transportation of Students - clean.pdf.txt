8410 TRANSPORTATION OF STUDENTS


It is the intent of the Board of Education to comply with the letter and spirit of the New
York State Education Law; with the regulations of the Department of Motor Vehicles and
of the Department of Transportation and with the Commissioner of Education's
regulations and decisions pertinent to student transportation, and these shall govern
any questions not covered by specific declaration of policy herein. Transportation
services shall be provided to meet the needs of in district students of the District within
specified limits and areas established by the Board of Education and approved by the
voters of the district. Our current voter approved mileage limits provide bus eligibility for
K-5 students living .25 miles from their home address to their school and Grades 6-12
students living 1.25 miles from their home address to their school.

The safe transportation of students to and from school is of primary concern in the
administration of the school bus program. All state laws and regulations pertaining to
the safe use of school buses shall be observed by drivers, students and school
personnel.

Every bus driver is required to report promptly any accident involving death, injury, or
property damage. All accidents, regardless of damage involved, must be reported at
once to the Transportation Coordinator. In addition, any incident which delays the
prompt transportation between home and school or between school and a school-
approved site, must be promptly reported to the Transportation Coordinator. The
Transportation Coordinator will immediately notify the Assistant Superintendent for
Business or Superintendent of Schools.

Requests for Transportation to and From Non-Public Schools

The parent or guardian of a parochial or private school child in grades K-12 residing in
the School District who desires that the child be transported to a parochial or private
school outside of the School District during the next school year must submit a written
request to the Transportation Department no later than April 1 of the preceding year,
or within thirty (30) days of moving into the District. . In accordance with Education Law,
non-public school transportation will only be provided up to a maximum of fifteen (15)
miles.    .

Transportation of Students with Disabilities

K-12 students with disabilities in the District shall be transported up to fifty (50) miles
(one way) from their home to the appropriate special service or program, unless the
Commissioner certifies that no appropriate nonresidential special service or program is
available within fifty (50) miles. The Commissioner may then establish transportation
arrangements.

Transportation of Non-Resident Students

The district shall not provide transportation to non-resident students who attend on a
tuition basis. It is the obligation of the parent/guardian or placing district to transport
non-resident students to and from school.

Transportation to School Sponsored Events
If the District provides transportation for student athletes participating in interscholastic
competition and students attending District sponsored educational field trips, students
will not be authorized to use any other means of transportation when participating in
these events, unless the Athletic Director or Principal or his/her designee authorizes
such alternate transportation. The Athletic Director or Principal shall require written
application prior to approval. As an exception to this policy, a coach may release a
student to the parent/guardian for transportation from an interscholastic event upon the
receipt of a written request from the parent/guardian.

Transportation to multiple addresses
While a student may have only one legal address for residency, it is acknowledged that
students may live with two guardians not at the same address due to family
considerations. The school district provides busing to a second guardian, as long as
the second guardian lives within the school district boundaries. A specific schedule
must be supplied to the Transportation Department for the school year and the District
will not make daily/weekly changes unless it is a permanent change for the school year.

Transportation to Child Care Programs
The District currently provides after school Transportation to approved, licensed Child
Care Program or School Age Program serving more than ten children and located within
the boundaries of the school district for students in Grades 1-6 . Alternatively, if a
parent has selected a caregiver location for pickup and or drop off and the caregiver
lives within the Irvington school district boundaries, this address may be used. A specific
schedule must be supplied to the Transportation Department for the school year and the
District will not make daily/weekly changes unless it is a permanent change for the
school year. The Board of Education has the right to suspend this service if warranted.
