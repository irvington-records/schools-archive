                   CLASSROOM USE AND MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT
                                         between
                                Irvington School District

                       Board of Cooperative Educational Services



1. This agreement, made this _______day of ________________ 2022, by and between The
   Irvington School District, an educational corporation having its office and principal place of
   business at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, NY 10533, hereinafter called the District and the
   Southern Westchester Board of Cooperative Educational Services, an educational
   corporation having its office and principal place of business at 17 Berkley Drive, Rye Brook,
   NY 10573, hereinafter referred to as SWBOCES.

2. The District hereby agrees to make available to SWBOCES for school purposes the services
   and facilities noted as follows:

               September 2022 - June 2023
               2 Classrooms ($24,366.00 per classroom) at the MS/HS
               1 Therapy Room ($6,749.00 per therapy room) at the MS/HS
               1 Office ($2,825.00 per office) at the MS/HS
               2 Clusters ($126,891.00 per cluster) (both located at the MS/HS)
               *Cluster = 3 Classrooms, 1 Office + 1 other purpose room
               Total Cost: $312,088.00

   The total service and facility cost for the year is $312,088.00. The 10-month portion will be
   paid in 2 installments. The 1st installment will be paid upon execution of this contract by both
   parties. The 2nd installment will be made by June 30, 2023.

3. The District will provide, as appropriate, for the special class pupils, school health services
   and the appropriate special instructional services that are available to the other pupils in the
   building – services such as appropriate academic programming, physical education, art,
   music, library, industrial arts, homemaking, etc. The specific services that are appropriate for
   each child or group of children will be determined by the building principal and the SWBOCES
   supervisor consistent with each student’s IEP. Thus, each child in a special program will
   participate in those aspects of the regular school program that are consistent with his/her IEP.

   The students and staff of SWBOCES occupying the District space shall have the use of normal
   and useful facilities of the District which are incidental to classroom instruction, including but

   not limited to the lavatories, cafeteria, gym, recreational areas, Library and access to computer
   lab, telephones, fax machines, internet and copiers as agreed upon and assigned.

   SWBOCES will provide speech, occupational and physical therapy, adaptive physical
   education, remedial reading, social work, psychiatric supervision and psychological services
   for these pupils as appropriate.

   SWBOCES agrees to validate that school children attending their programs at the District are
   either vaccinated, have been given exemptions, or are reviewing whether exemptions should
   be granted consistent with prevailing laws and regulations.

4. The District will supply custodial service, including utilities, for the classroom(s) and
   SWBOCES shall be supplied with the classroom furniture and the equipment necessary to
   conduct its educational program in the classroom.

5. SWBOCES hereby agrees to reimburse the District for, or to have repaired for its own account,
   all damage to the real and personal property of the district arising out of the use of the above
   premises by SWBOCES, except reasonable wear and tear. In the event of damage caused
   by SWBOCES to exposed asbestos containing material in the District, SWBOCES will not be
   responsible for the cost of the asbestos abatement and any related testing services.

6. WAIVE OF SUBROGRATION/INDEMNIFICATION/HOLD HARMLESS:

       A. lt is hereto agreed upon by all parties to this agreement that they will and each of them
          will agree to waive all rights in subrogation as against any other party to the
          Agreement.

       B. SWBOCES will indemnify and hold harmless the District for any activity arising out of
          use of the facility.

       C. The District agrees and accepts that it is responsible for all of the requirements of
          asbestos related statues and regulations including: “The Asbestos Hazard Emergency
          Response Act of 1986” “The Toxic Substances Control Act” and “School Asbestos
          Safety Act” and further agrees to hold harmless and indemnify SWBOCES for any
          claims, fines, penalties or exposures arising from such statues or regulations.

7. Insurance: SWBOCES shall purchase from and maintain in a company or companies lawfully
   licensed to do business in the State of New York such insurance as will protect SWBOCES
   and the District from claims set forth below for which SWBOCES may be legally liable, whether
   such operations be by SWBOCES or by anyone directly or indirectly employed by it.
   Notwithstanding any terms, conditions or provisions, in any other writing between the parties,
   SWBOCES hereby agrees to effectuate the naming of the District as an additional insured on
   SWBOCES’s insurance policies, with the exception of workers' compensation, N.Y. State
   disability and professional liability. If the policy is written on a claims-made basis, the
   retroactive date must precede the date of the contract. The policy naming the District as an
   additional insured shall:

       A. Purchase an insurance policy from an A.M. Best rated "secured" insurer, licensed in
          New York State.

       B. State that the organization's coverage shall be primary and non-contributory coverage
          for the District, its Board, employees and volunteers.

C. State that the policy affirmatively provides coverage for claims of negligent hiring,
   training and supervision, which may arise in the context of sexual molestation, abuse
   harassment, or similar sexual conduct.

D. The District shall be listed as an additional insured by using endorsement CG 2026 or
   equivalent. A completed copy of the endorsement must be attached to the certificate
   of insurance.

E. The certificate of insurance must describe the specific services provided by
   SWBOCES that are covered by the commercial general liability policy.

F. At the District’s request, SWBOCES shall provide a copy of the declaration page of
   the liability and umbrella policies with a list of endorsements and forms. If so
   requested, SWBOCES will provide a copy of the policy endorsements and forms.

G. Required Insurance:
       i.      Commercial       General     Liability  Insurance:   $1,000,000    per
               occurrence/$3,000,000 aggregate.
       ii.     Workers' Compensation and N.Y.S. Disability: Statutory Workers'
               Compensation, Employers' Liability and N.Y.S. Disability Benefits
               Insurance for all employees. Proof of coverage must be on the approved
               specific form, as required by the New York State Workers’ Compensation
               Board. ACORD certificates are not acceptable.

H. SWBOCES acknowledges that failure to obtain such insurance on behalf of the District
   constitutes a material breach of contract. SWBOCES is to provide the District with a
   certificate of insurance, evidencing the above requirements have been met. The failure
   of the District to object to the contents of the certificate or the absence of same shall
   not be deemed a waiver of any and all rights held by the District.

I.   The District is a member/owner of the NY Schools Insurance Reciprocal (NYSIR).
     SWBOCES further acknowledges that the procurement of such insurance as required
     herein is intended to benefit not only the District but also the NYSIR, as the District’s
     insurer.

J. In the event that any of the insurance coverage to be provided by SWBOCES contains
   a deductible, SWBOCES shall indemnify and hold the District harmless from the
   payment of such deductible, which deductible shall in all circumstances remain the
   sole obligation and expense of SWBOCES.

K. SWBOCES shall provide the District with evidence of the above insurance
   requirements upon execution of the within Agreement. SWBOCES further
   acknowledges that its failure to obtain or keep current the insurance coverage required
   by this Agreement shall constitute a material breach of contract and subjects
   SWBOCES to liability for damages including, but not limited to, direct damages the
   District sustains as a result of this breach. In addition, SWBOCES shall be responsible
   for the indemnification to the District of any and all costs associated with such lapse in
   coverage including, but not limited to, reasonable attorneys’ fees.

L. SWBOCES shall obtain and pay for insurance as may be required to comply with the
   indemnification and hold harmless provisions outlined under this Agreement.

   by either party shall be in writing and shall be validly given or made to the other party if
   personally served or if deposited in the United States mail, certified or registered, postage
   pre-paid, return receipt requested to the address as follows. If such notice, demand or other
   communication is served personally, it shall be effective immediately. If such notice, demand

   addressed to the party to whom such notice, demand or other communication is to be given

9. Severability: Any provision of this Agreement which is invalid, illegal, or unenforceable in any
   jurisdiction will, as to that jurisdiction, be ineffective to the extent of such invalidity, illegality,
   or enforceability, without affecting any remaining provisions hereof in such jurisdiction or
   render that or any other provision of this Agreement, invalid, illegal, or unenforceable in any
   other jurisdiction.

10. Discrimination Prohibited: Neither the District nor SWBOCES will discriminate against any
    individual because of his/her age, race, creed, color, national origin, sexual orientation,
    military status, sex, disability, predisposing genetic characteristics or marital status and will
    take affirmative action to ensure that each individual is afforded equal opportunities without
    discrimination because of his/her age, race, creed, color, national origin, sexual orientation,
    military status, sex, disability, predisposing genetic characteristics or marital status.

11. Non-Waiver: No course of dealing of any party hereto, no omission, failure or delay on the
    part of any party hereto in asserting or exercising any right hereunder, and no partial or single
    exercise of any right hereunder by any party hereto shall constitute or operate as a waiver of
    any such right or any other right hereunder. No waiver of any provision hereof shall be
    effective unless in writing and signed by or on behalf of the party to be charged therewith. No
    waiver of any provision hereof shall be deemed or construed as a continuing waiver, as a
    waiver in respect of any other or subsequent breach or default of such provision, or as a
    waiver of any other provision hereof unless expressly so stated in writing and signed by or on
    behalf of the party to be charged therewith.

12. HIPAA and FERPA Acknowledgment: Both parties to this Agreement understand that they
    may receive and/or come into contact with protected health information as defined by the
    Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 ("HIPAA") and information
    protected by the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act ("FERPA"). The Parties hereby
    acknowledge their respective responsibilities pursuant to HIPAA and FERPA, and, if
    necessary, shall execute a Business Associate Agreement in connection with such
    responsibilities.

13. Confidentiality: Both parties, their employees, and/or agents agree that all information
    obtained in connection with the use of facilities and services related thereto provided for in
    this Agreement is deemed confidential information. Both parties, their employees, and/or
    agents agree that they shall not use, publish, discuss, disclose or communicate the contents
    of such information, directly or indirectly with third parties, except as provided for in this
    Agreement. Both parties further agree that any information they, their employees and/or
    agents receive during the course of the use of facilities and services related thereto provided
    pursuant to this Agreement which concerns the personal, financial, or other affairs of one of
    the parties, its employees, agents, clients, and/or students will be treated by the parties, their
    employees, and/or agents in full confidence and will not be revealed to any other persons,
    firms, or organizations. In the event of a breach of the within confidentiality provision by one
    of the parties, the breaching party shall immediately notify the nonbreaching party and advise
    it as to the nature of the breach and the steps that have been taken to minimize said breach.
    The parties shall indemnify and hold each other harmless from any claims arising from one
    party’s breach of the within confidentiality provision. The parties further agree that the terms
    and conditions set forth herein shall survive the expiration and/or termination of this
    Agreement.

14. Governing Law: This Agreement shall be governed by, and interpreted and enforced in
    accordance with, the laws of the State of New York without regard to conflicts or choice of law
    provisions that would defer to the substantive laws of another jurisdiction. Each of the parties
    hereto consents to the jurisdiction of any state court located within the County of Westchester,
    State of New York, or federal court in Federal District Court for the Southern District of New
    York located in the County of Westchester, State of New York.

15. School Grounds and Rules: It is understood and agreed that while on school grounds,
    SWBOCES’ employees, students and/or agents shall obey all of the District’s rules, code of
    conduct and regulations and must follow all reasonable directives of the Districts’
    administrators and employees. In the event that a SWBOCES student engages in any
    misconduct for which discipline may be imposed, the District’s Superintendent and the school
    principal of the building in the which the program is housed shall be consulted by the
    SWBOCES principal as to the misconduct. SWBOCES shall make its best efforts in facilitating
    discussion between the District Superintendent and/or his designee and the SWBOCES
    principal and/or the resident district superintendent concerning any proposed penalty for such
    misconduct.

16. Administrative Meetings: Administrators for both parties shall meet on a regular basis to
    discuss issues of mutual concern. SWBOCES, its faculty, staff and/or agents shall be
    obligated to promptly communicate to the District any pertinent information concerning any
    misconduct and/or threats to the well-being of the District, its students, faculty, staff and
    invitees.

17. Emergency Contact Information: SWBOCES shall provide the District with the contact
    information to use in the event of an emergency involving one of SWBOCES’ students, faculty
    and/or staff. SWBOCES shall also furnish to the District a complete roster of students, faculty
    and staff that will be present on District property. SWBOCES shall be required to provide the
    District with updates to said roster as it changes. This includes, but is not limited to, new
    students that enter the program mid-year.

18. Emergency Medical Information: SWBOCES shall provide the District’s Superintendent, the
    Principal(s) of the District’s School Building(s), and/or the District’s nurse(s) assigned to the
    District’s School Building(s), with any known medical conditions of its students, faculty and/or
    staff to prepare the District in the event of a medical emergency; to the extent disclosure of

   such information is consented to in writing by the faculty member, staff member, parent or
   student (depending on the age of the student) and does not violate FERPA, or any other
   federal or state law. To the extent that The District’s Superintendent, Principal(s) and Nurse(s)
   shall treat these records as confidential and shall not disclose any of these records or the
   information derived therefrom to anyone else, including but not limited to other District
   administrators, teachers or staff without the prior written authorization of said parent, student,
   faculty member or staff member, except to the extent that disclosure is required to individuals
   providing emergency medical assistance and/or treatment to the applicable student, faculty
   member or staff member.

19. Compliance with Law: SWBOCES understands and agrees that it shall comply and be
    responsible for complying with all applicable federal, state, and local statutes, rules,
    regulations, codes and ordinances applicable to its use of the District’s facilities under this
    Agreement. The District understands and agrees that it shall comply and be responsible for
    complying with all applicable federal, state, and local statutes, rules, regulations, codes and
    ordinances applicable to the services provided to SWBOCES under this Agreement.

20. Representations and Warranties: SWBOCES represents and warrants: 1) that it has no
    obligations, legal or otherwise, inconsistent with the terms of this Agreement; 2) that the
    performance of this Agreement does not and will not violate any applicable law, rule or
    regulation or any proprietary or other right of any third party; and 3) that SWBOCES has not
    entered into or will not enter into any Agreement (whether oral or written) in conflict with this
    Agreement.

21. Lack of Enforcement: Historical lack of enforcement of any law, local or otherwise, shall not
    constitute a waiver of either parties’ responsibility for compliance with such law in a manner
    consistent with this Agreement unless and until the applicable party(ies) has received written
    consent for the waiver of such compliance from the entity responsible for the enforcement of
    such law.

22. Authorization to Enter Agreement: Both parties represent that that they have adopted a
    resolution authorizing execution of this Agreement, and the persons whose signatures appear
    on this Agreement on behalf of each of them expressly represent that they are authorized by
    each of their respective parties to execute this Agreement on its behalf and bind it to the terms
    and conditions of this Agreement.

23. No Punitive Damages: The District shall not be liable to SWBOCES for punitive damages on
    account of its termination of this Agreement with SWBOCES or any other alleged breach of
    this Agreement between it and SWBOCES and SWBOCES hereby expressly waives its right
    to claim such damages against the District.

24. No Assignment: Both parties agree not to assign, transfer, convey or sublet or otherwise
    dispose of this Agreement or its right, title and interest therein or its power to execute such
    Agreement, to any other person, firm or corporation without the previous consent in writing of
    the other party.

25. Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties
    hereto pertaining to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous
    agreements and understandings of the parties in connection therewith. No supplement,
    modification or amendment of this Agreement shall be binding unless executed in writing by
    the parties hereto.
