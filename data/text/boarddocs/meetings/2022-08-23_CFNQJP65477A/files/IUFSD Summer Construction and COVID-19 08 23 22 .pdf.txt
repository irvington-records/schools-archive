Construction and COVID-19 Update

     An update for the opening of the 2022-23 school year
                       August 23, 2022

Summer Efforts
This summer has been all hands on deck to prepare for the successful opening of
the 2022-23 school year. However, issues beyond the District’s control have
affected construction progress and school readiness including:

●   New COVID-19 guidance from the NYSDOH and NYSED just issued
●   Supply chain complexities have prohibited contractors from securing
    necessary items such as doors, windows, furniture, cabinetry, etc.
●   Worker shortages have delayed manufacturing of items needed to complete
    construction
●   Typical, unforeseen construction issues

COVID-19
The most recent NYSED and NYSDOH guidance provides for the following:

●   There will be no:
     ○   mask requirements
     ○   quarantine requirements for exposure
     ○   physical distancing requirements
     ○   contact tracing
     ○   restrictions on attendance at events
●   Those who test positive for COVID-19 will isolate for five days and wear a
    mask through day 10
●   No requirement to provide remote/virtual learning

Dows Lane Construction
The primary project at Dows Lane Elementary School was the renovation of the
media center (library) and the creation of two STEM/PLTW classrooms - creating
the Creativity and Innovation Suite.

●   All essential construction is completed
●   Delivery of lighting, some cabinetry, and furnishing delayed
●   Ceiling finishings to be installed once lighting is completed
     ○   Temporary lighting is installed
●   Cardkey access to be installed during the fall

Main Street School Construction
Numerous projects were undertaken this summer:
●   Parking lot resurfacing
     ○   Unforeseen conditions increased the project scope, cost, and timeline.
     ○   Completion by the start of school
●   Remodeling of the computer lab and STEM/PLTW classrooms
     ○   Construction will be completed by the start of school
     ○   Delays in cabinetry manufacturing and installation
●   Bell tower painting completed
●   Electric service upgrade
     ○   Installation of service lines completed, waiting for ConEd to complete the connection
●   Gymnasium Air Conditioning
     ○   To be completed during the fall, outside of school hours
     ○   Gym will be usable for instruction - ceiling penetrations will be completed by start of school
●   Cardkey access to be installed during the fall

MS/HS Campus Construction
Significant construction is ongoing in all areas

 ●    HS entrance and vestibule
         ○   Construction timeline was tight and project to be completed during September
         ○   Access to the HS will continue to be through the Maher Gym entry
 ●    Parking lot, roadway reconstruction
         ○   To be completed by the start of school
         ○   Manufacturing delays for light posts. Solar parking lot lights to be installed once posts are received.
         ○   Temporary lighting to be installed
 ●    Landscaping, tree planting to be completed in the fall
         ○   Best time for such plantings
         ○   Consultation with experts to select native plantings and to support pollinator pathways
 ●    HS univent units (HVAC) being installed and will be online
 ●    Media center remodeling - significant construction completed
         ○   Manufacturing delays for doors will require temporary inconveniences
         ○   Manufacturing delays for lighting may result in temporary lighting on the 2nd floor
 ●    Gymnasium air conditioning to be completed in the fall
 ●    Facilities building to be completed during the fall
 ●    Cardkey access to be installed during the fall

Patience and Cooperation, Please!
The delays associated with construction will require patience and cooperation from
all members of the school community. Inconveniences will be present for the next
month.
