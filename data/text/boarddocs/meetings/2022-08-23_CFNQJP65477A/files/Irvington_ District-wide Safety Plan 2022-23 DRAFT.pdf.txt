PURPOSE
Emergencies in schools are deﬁned as undesirable events that occur and have the poten al to cause
injury or illness to members of our school community or disrupt the orderly educa onal process. They
range from acts of bullying or harassment to catastrophic natural or man-made events. Emergency
management is the discipline of dealing with and avoiding risks. It is a discipline that involves preparing
for an emergency situa on or disaster before it occurs as well as suppor ng and rebuilding from the
emergency a er natural or human-made disasters have occurred.


Emergency management in our schools is the con nuous process by which our staﬀ, students,
administrators, parents, school groups, emergency responders and our community manages hazards in
an eﬀort to avoid or mi gate the impact of disasters resul ng from hazards. Preven ve measures and
good planning will reduce the likelihood that emergencies will occur and allow us to address those that
do in an expedi ous and eﬀec ve manner.


Districts are required to develop district-wide school safety and emergency management plans
designed to prevent and eﬀec vely manage such events to minimize the eﬀects of serious incidents
and emergencies. These plans also facilitate the coordina on of the District with local and county
plans and resources when incidents and emergencies occur.


The district-wide plan is responsive to the needs of all schools in the District and is consistent with the
more detailed building-level emergency plans. Districts are vulnerable to a wide variety of acts of
violence; and natural and manmade disasters. To address these threats, the State of New York has
enacted the Safe Schools Against Violence in Educa on (S.A.V.E.) law. Project S.A.V.E. is a
comprehensive planning eﬀort that addresses preven on, response, and recovery with respect to a
variety of emergencies in schools.


The Irvington Union Free School District supports the S.A.V.E. legisla on. As such, the Superintendent
of Schools, Board of Educa on, and the en re District staﬀ encourages and advocates on-going
district-wide coopera on in support of Project S.A.V.E.
