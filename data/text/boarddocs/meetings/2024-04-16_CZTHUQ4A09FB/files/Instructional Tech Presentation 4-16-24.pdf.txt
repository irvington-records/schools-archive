MEET YOUR COACHES

Peer feedback on group or individual projects while |
the work is in progress can be a beneficial practice. | 9
The Charrette Protocol, a technique adapted from

the architectural community, can be used to help
students improve their work In this protocol,

students present their work while it is in the
development stage, to a group of their peers and

solicit feedback and suggestions. The presenting

group silently listens and takes notes on the
improvements shared, This strategy fosters a
collaborative learning environment where students
support each other's growth, and promote a culture

of constructive feedback

If you want to brainstorm ways you can implement

this feedback protocol in your classroom reach out

to Nancy.
