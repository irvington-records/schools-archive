Dear Members of the Irvington Board of Education,
       We would like to receive approval of a new scholarship for the Phil DiRucco Senior
Sports Awards Banquet in memory of our son and brother, MICHAEL A. TARULLI. We are
honored that the Irvington Wrestling Tournament is named after him, and we will continue to
support the wrestling team.
        Michael was a student in the graduating class of 2010. He was an amazing son and
brother who always put his family first and was always the first person to help others in need.
While also a student at Irvington High School, he was on the Soccer Team, Football Team, Pole-
Vaulting Team, and the Wrestling Team. Michael received many athletic recognition awards such
as the Lower Hudson Athletic Conference All-League award (2006-07), creating a wrestling move
“TaRadle”, Best First Year in Wrestling at 119lbs (2006-07), Varsity Football Bill Dyer “GUNG HO”
Award (2009) and the Mike Mandel Award for Outstanding Commitment (2009-10). In addition
to his athletic commitments, he was a member of Tech Crew volunteering behind the scenes to
build and strike sets from the high school theater performances. Michael also received
scholarships for his character, perseverance, and commitment which was witnessed by many
administrators, teachers, and friends around the district. Outside of Irvington high school he
volunteered in our Church, The Immaculate Conception, and at local community events.
        To honor Michael’s legacy, we would like to establish a scholarship in his name for a
graduating high school student and we would like to name the award, “The Michael A. Tarulli
Memorial Scholarship.” This award should recognize a graduating senior who possesses the
same characteristics that defined Michael. The recipient of the award should be a scholar
athlete, role model for fellow peers, possess a positive moral character, have integrity,
professionalism, altruism, empathy and show kindness towards everyone. We would also like
the recipient to be someone who needs financial support and currently be on the Wrestling
Team.

         The award will be a $200.00 check which will be handed to a new recipient every year at
the Phil DiRucco Senior Sports Awards Banquet. After an applicant has been selected, we would
like to know who the recipient is, the college they will be attending, their intended major and
aspirations.
       Thank you very much for your time and consideration. We look forward to hearing from
you regarding the approval of “The Michael A. Tarulli Memorial Scholarship.”



Sincerely,


The Tarulli Family

Suzanne, Ralph,

Ralph (2007 graduate) and Peter (2019 graduate)
