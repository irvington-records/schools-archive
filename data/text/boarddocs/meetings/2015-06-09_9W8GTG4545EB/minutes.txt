	6. Presentations/Discussions

	Discussion: 6.1 Discussion of Instructional Coach Position

	The purpose of this discussion is to clarify what the Board’s objectives are with this new position that was approved in this year’s budget. The title of the position changed from secondary literacy coach to Instructional as it was felt that it would be more of a support to teachers in delivering quality in not only ELA but in Social Studies and Science as well with significant responsibility in supporting the teaching reading skills and writing across all Common Core courses beyond ELA literacy.  A job description is being created which will outline the responsibilities.  Dr. Harrison noted that the conversation tonight is to help inform the administration that they are on the right track to reinforce the Board’s objectives for this position and to ensure that the right candidate is chosen. As an aside, he noted that to date, not many resumes have been received.  The Board focused on what the potential candidate’s background should/would be and Dr. Kor emphasized that an ideal candidate would be someone with deep teaching experience, a master teacher. She also noted that the person would most likely be giving up tenure and it would be a leap of faith on their part to take on this position.  Mrs. Kerner noted that when the concept of this position was discussed during budget preparation, she viewed it as an attempt to raise the level of instruction as well as differentiating instruction for students and although the position is more secondary, she would hope that the person would be flexible and help at the lower grades if needed.

	Dr. Harrison concluded the discussion by stating that the real challenge will be in finding the right person and if unsuccessful in the search, there would need to be further conversations with the Board as to next steps.

	Discussion: 6.2 Discussion of Fund Balance

	Mr. Whitney initiated the discussion by explaining what a fund balance is, wherein a school district, by law, can maintain a maximum of 4% of the following year’s budget for emergencies, etc. and noted that Irvington has maintained the balance at the maximum. He also pointed out that in years’ past, Irvington has placed remaining any funds from the year’s budget into the tax certiorari reserve to pay for future settlements, but added the Board is not at liberty to discuss publically any ongoing negotiations pertaining to pending tax certiorari settlements.

	Ms. Beverly Miller, Asst. Supt. for Business stressed that the estimates for this year are preliminary and will not be final until the audit is completed sometime in July but noted that next year’s budget was reduced by $422,000 (the first budget item for 2015-16). The fund balance is also used for emergencies and to support district operations through the summer as the District does not receive tax revenue until October.  Any remaining funds will be allocated via Board discussion and resolution, such as being returned to tax payers via reduced tax rate.

	Ms. Miller recommended that any remaining funds beyond increasing the fund balance to the maximum allowable of 4% be put into the tax certiorari reserve as currently the pending settlements will exceed the balance in the reserve. She noted that Irvington has been hit hard by settlements and that she doesn’t see the end of the line.

	The Board then discussed how to best allocate the surplus. Both Mrs. Kashkin and Mr. Grados believe that it would be prudent to fund the tax cert reserve to avoid any financing fees that would be associated with having to bond settlements. Mrs. Kerner believes that with two years of surplus and tax rates increasing, she believes that refunding to community would help offset the tax impacts and would be fair to the community but also agrees to put the bulk into the reserve. Mr. Grados would like to know how much per household could be refunded. Mr. Whitney pointed out that it isn’t truly surplus when there are tax cert settlements that must be paid for.

	The Board will vote at the July 16th meeting for the allocation of the remaining funds.
