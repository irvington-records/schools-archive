6. Presentations/Discussions (Presentation)

Presentation: 6.1 Superintendent's 2017-18 Recommended Budget

Dr. Harrison provided an overview of the budget process undertaken thus far with respect to the 2017-18 budget. He noted the challenges facing the district, needs in curriculum and operations, and provides for a rigorous, comprehensive, enriched and diversified curricula along with providing support to the educators, etc. along with developing a fiscally responsible budget. The proposed budget does provide for expanded student experiences, maintains and improves facilities and sustains current programs across the district.

With respect to the tax cap, it is projected that the allowable tax levy increase will be 3.01%, which indicates growth. One impact on the budget is the loss of a PILOT (revenue in lieu of taxes) which will decrease as the property is now on the tax rolls. Review of other revenue streams include state aid which is still uncertain, tuition, increase of $145,000 of previously owed state aid from 2011, and it is hoped that there will be an increase in aid. Total revenue derived from other sources total $877,994 over last year's revenue, with $530,000 appropriated from the fund balance. The total budget is $59,207,994 which represents a 1.51% increase over this year's budget of $58,330,000.

Administrative adjustments have been made to the previous budget proposals, including but not limited to:

- reduce new Social Studies position to .4FTE;

- reduce $20,300 in teacher laptop allocation;

- no new proposed technology integration teacher;

- reduction of PLTW equipment/supplies;

- reduction of $90,000 in Chromebooks as Smart School monies will be applied;

- reduction of substitute expense with Educational Evaluator position --- $10,000;

- adjustments to health insurance/benefits based on proposed FTE deductions; and

- salary adjustments for new CSEA contract and grants

Dr. Harrison presented the revised vision for the additional data analyst position and the realigning of positions in the technology department as well as the technology & athletics clerical position. The proposed position will also be responsible for student registration, and website administration including Facebook pages.

A summary of budgetary considerations were highlighted, and non-negotiable items were noted with a total increase of $878,250. Dr. Harrison spoke to each of the new considerations, along with the rational and value added to the students and district. Budgetary impact trends to the community is difficult to predict due to the change in the equalization rates which are now 100% of full market value, as opposed to previously a 3.09% equalization rate in 2016-17. He provided the formula for community members to calculate their estimated taxes as well as how to compare the proposed increase or decrease to previous year's tax increase.

Board members made comments on the information presented, including concerns for including $530,000 allocated to the budget from the fund balance which had been discussed in previous years as to the pros and cons of applying such funds to reduce the tax impact. Carol Stein provided a brief overview of the different categories of the District's fund balances and other reserves including tax certiorari reserves. Mr. Whitney believes that this year's projections in ERS and TRS, etc. and should prepare for days where increases are necessary and it would be responsible to reduce to $422,000 as he believes this is not an extraordinarily difficult year and that it would be prudent to return money into the fund balance to ensure fiscal health of the District. Questions included how decision will be made to allocate teacher laptops with the request to know where they would be allocated prior to a final decision on the budget. There was discussion among the board and administrators surrounding the new data analyst position with respect to job responsibilities, including what the percentage would be of each of the three areas of responsibility, noting that there are different skill sets needed within the responsibilities. The board grappled with the responsibilities of the position and might need to rethink the position. With respect to the additional .4 FTE (from 1.) in a new Social Studies position, the goal is to relieve the core classes, however, if an elective was to be eliminated, current resources could be reallocated. In response to Mr. Whitney’s requested the rational of the additional Library Media Specialist as he is not entirely persuaded of the need for the position.  Mr. Sottile provided the desire to expand technology at the middle school which the position would allow for and Dr. Kor again reiterated that it was the intention to have two librarians when the campus was built, and would like to build a robust middle level program which would include research skills, study skills, etc. which flows within the vision of the District. She also noted that none of the positions are recommended randomly as most are to build back what was lost in previous budget cuts as well as building on the technology program, strong study skills program.

Dr. Harrison addressed Catherine Palmieri's question as to the reasoning for  certain items being non-negotiables, among which would be positions to grow the special education programs offered to students. Catherine Palmieri referenced that 13 teachers have been eliminated over the past five years and to look at restoring positions. She stated that her preference would be to eliminate the literacy coach, K-5 and restore social studies and math at the HS to provide some equity as she feels that the secondary programs have felt the brunt of the past cuts.

Mr. Hanna asked for updated trend data as to participation in the sports program as well as survey as to what students would like participate in. He also asked about the possibility to provide students with opportunities to participate in exchange programs. He expressed his concerns about equity based on gender and is curious if there is worth in conducting an audit of classes to be sure that there is opportunity for all students. Dr. Kor agreed that that is a topic that administrators too are curious about and will always look for ways to expand interest.

Questions from community were taken following the Board's questions and comments.

- Request for greater understanding of the budget for the community to understand the items, world language expansion, etc.

- Comments made regarding cuts over the past years and would hope that there aren't separate buckets for each school.

- Rational for reduction in social studies from 1.0FTE to .4FTE and maintaining the additional .6 FTE in math.

- Questions regarding data analyst position and gave a heads up that there is a segment of the community that is interested in growing the world language program in elementary school and funds should be found to provide instruction and reconsider position. Dr. Harrison agrees and that this will be addressed in time.

Dr. Harrison will send a list of follow-ups to the Board to be sure that everyone is in agreement.
