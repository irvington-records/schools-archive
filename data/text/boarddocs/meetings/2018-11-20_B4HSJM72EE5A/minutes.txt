3. Comments from Members of the Community

Information: 3.1 Procedures for Community Comment on Topics that appear on Agenda

A parent asked about early language instruction. Dr. Harrison noted that David Graeber gave an update at the last meeting regarding the different models that are being reviewed and that Dr. Kor will provide an overview at an upcoming meeting as we approach the budget review process.

4. Presentation/Discussion

Discussion, Presentation: 4.1 Curriculum an Instruction Plan

Dr. Kor provided an extensive overview of the current district instructional initiatives which highlighted significant curricular advancements at each school level. She also provided the district's newly developed Program Assessment model being employed through various programs. Further, Dr. Kor provided examples of important work that is being led by the district's instructional coaches. Here, she demonstrated how New York State standardized test data is being used to inform curriculum, instruction and professional learning.

Following the presentation, Dr. Kor fielded questions from the Board and President Hanna invited questions/comments from the public.
