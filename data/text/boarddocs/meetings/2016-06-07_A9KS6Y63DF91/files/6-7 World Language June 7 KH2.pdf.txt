         Shift in Department Focus

                         
 It is essential for students to possess a
  comprehensive set of communication skills in the
  target language. The 6-12 World Language program
  will provide students with a foundation in all aspects
  of communication to effectively speak, read, and
  write in the language which they are studying with
  an initial, specific focus on speaking.

    Why the Importance of Speaking?

                         
 Oral language provides the basis for both first- and
  second- language reading, since it is the foundation
  on which reading is built.
 As students develop their listening comprehension,
  they begin to make connections between oral
  language and the print that represents this oral
  language

            Increase in Speaking
                         
 While all four skills are important as part of a well
  balanced program – we had not had clearly defined
  goals for speaking – a shift in curriculum alignment
  now includes speaking targets with an expectation
  for students who remain in language being able to
  use the target language in authentic situations.
