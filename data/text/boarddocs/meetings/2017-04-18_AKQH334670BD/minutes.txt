5. 2017-18 Budget Overview/Adoption

Discussion: 5.1 2017-18 Budget Overview

Dr. Harrison reported that tonight is the culmination of the budget process that began back in October with the administrative team discussing needs in their buildings and they have been presented over the past three months, with the Board being asked to adopt the proposed budget. The May 2nd meeting will be the final opportunity for the public to comment on the budget which will be voted on by the community on May 16th. The budget focuses on student needs which align with the District's Strategic Plan as well as facility needs.

In addressing the feedback from the Board at the last meeting, the data manager position has been reduced to a $40,000 contracted data analysis service, rather than a $105,000 full-time position. The balance of the funds will be one-time costs for supplies to expand the PLTW program, professional development to support technology integration in primary grades and focus on additional sidewalk repairs or storage needs with final determination by the Buildings & Grounds Committee. Dr. Harrison reviewed the State Aid adjustments with a projected $3,893,909 in aid, which shows a slight decrease, but will be made up in aid from BOCES. The tax cap information, with a 3.01% allowable tax cap levy increase, was reviewed. Revenues and expenditures were reviewed along with a summary of the proposed budget by code. An overview of the additions to the budget were reviewed with a total in new considerations of $731,050, along with several items in the Business Office, central data, facilities and transportation considerations. Dr. Harrison addressed the cost avoidance by including positions that will allow students to remain in the District rather than being placed out of district at a significantly greater cost.

Due to the reassessment, the calculation of taxes will be done differently and a budget calculator will be put on the website to help the community calculate their taxes. He also noted that if the budget is defeated, the Board has the option of going out for a 2nd vote or going to contingency or, should the budget fail a 2nd time, a contingency budget would be implemented, causing the district to cut approximately $1.5.

The ballot on May 16th will have the budget proposition as well a second proposition to add two members to the Board and if approved, there will be a special election for the two members in June or July.

Action (Consent): 9.1 Establishment of the Irvington Historical Society Scholarship Fund

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District administers the Irvington Union Free School District; and WHEREAS, the Irvington Historical Society Scholarship Committee, hereinafter referred to as the "Donor" desires to make a gift to the Board of Education in order to create a scholarship fund, to be known as the "Irvington Historical Society Scholarship Fund"; and WHEREAS Education Law 1709(12-a) and 2503 (1) authorizes the Board of Education: to take and hold in trust for the purpose of awarding scholarships in [the) schools any real estate transferred to it by gift, grant, bequest or devise, or any gift, legacy or annuity, of whatever kind, given or bequeathed to [the] board and apply the same, or the interest and proceeds thereof, according to the instructions of the donor or testator. NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District hereby creates the above-named Scholarship Fund, as set forth below, based upon the understanding between the parties: The Irvington Historical Society Scholarship Fund 1. The Donor, Irvington High School, shall deliver to the Board, for deposit in the trust and agency account in the bank designated by the Board of Education the sum of One Thousand dollars ($1,000.00), with a 5 year commitment which would then be reviewed by the Society for another 5 years; which the Donor hereby gives to the Board forever and unconditionally, except as hereinafter set forth. 2. The Scholarship Fund may be supplemented at any time by any donor(s) wishing to gift, grant, bequest or devise cash, cash equivalent or other property acceptable to the District or through any gift, legacy or annuity, of whatever kind, given, or bequeathed to the District for such purpose. 3.The scholarship shall be named the Irvington Historical Society Scholarship Fund. 4. The scholarship shall be awarded annually in the amount of One Thousand Dollars ($1,000.00) to a graduating senior who has met the requirements: essay (may or may not deal with local history, but must include primary historical research), it is not necessary to take AP classes to be considered, there is no grade point average requirement, the student's work will be evaluated using a rubric designed by the Irvington Historical Society Scholarship Committee. The committee will also welcome input from teachers in the Social Studies department. 5. The funds allocated to said scholarship shall come from such income or interest as in accumulated in a particular year plus such invasion of the principal as is necessary to issue said scholarship. 6. Details of the organization and administration of the Scholarship Fund will be left to the discretion of the Board of Education.
