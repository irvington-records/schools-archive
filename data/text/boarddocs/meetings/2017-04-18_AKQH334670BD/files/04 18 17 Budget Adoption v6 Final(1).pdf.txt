                   Our Vision for Tomorrow
                                          3
To achieve our vision, we will:
❖ Provide students with a rigorous, comprehensive, enriched and diversified
  curricula that will prepare students to achieve their personal best, and will
  integrate technology in their learning.

❖ Support educators through targeted professional learning and opportunity for
  collaboration

❖ Encourage innovation, creativity and risk taking to inspire a dynamic learning
  environment

❖ Foster the creation of a culture where students are active participants in
  society.

❖ Strengthen local connections to and ownership of our schools.

❖ Ensure the fiscal health of the district and provide for a high quality learning
  environment.

                        Contingent Budget
                                          15

● If there was to be a majority “no” vote in May and a subsequent “no” vote
  in June if the Board chooses to have a second vote, it results in a
  contingent budget.
● A Contingent Budget requires the district to levy no more than the
  previous year levy. This would require approximately $1.5 million in cuts
  to this proposed budget and would result in a budget lower than 2015-16.
● The loss of PILOT revenue of $689,000 would not be recovered from the
  levy, which is a large part of the tax cap levy increase this year.
● A Contingent Budget would result in no new equipment, no community
  use of buildings and no capital improvements (unless emergency). Other
  necessary cuts would affect student programs.
● The district could deplete fund balance to offset cuts, leaving the district in
  a vulnerable financial position and designated in “significant fiscal stress”
  by the Office of State Comptroller
