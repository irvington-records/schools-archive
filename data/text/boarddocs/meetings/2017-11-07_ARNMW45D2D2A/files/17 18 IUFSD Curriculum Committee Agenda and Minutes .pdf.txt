1.0    Goal Setting

       The Curriculum Committee goal is to provide an opportunity for ongoing dialogue and
       communication with the BOE and community, in order to provide a deeper exploration
       and understanding of the needs related to curriculum, instruction and assessment.



       BOE Initial Areas of Interest:

       District PD Plan
       Overview of programs and timeline. Consideration of opportunities for a win win
       improving pd calendar and time in class of our professionals. Previously the BOE has
       understood calendar and contract as the main avenues for improvements. The
       introduction of the I\E period in a new secondary schedule provides another option, per
       David Cohen at the recent parent info session.

       Initiative/Program Assessment System
       Development of a system for assessing program/initiative progress/success.
       -Including hiring of budgeted data system consultant to support the work of the
       administration.

       Rubicon Atlas/Curriculum Development
       This is a key initiative of the district. Where do we stand?
