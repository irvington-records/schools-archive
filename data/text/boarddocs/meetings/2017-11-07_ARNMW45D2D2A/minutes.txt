5. Presentations/Discussions

Information: 5.1 Reconfiguration of Town Hall Theater Entrance

Present were Mayor Brian Smith, Trustee Janet Silverburg, architects John Malone and Earl Ferguson, representatives Laurie Chock and Jody Hansen of Irvington Town Hall Theater and it was noted that Board member, Deb Hargraves, also chairs the ARB (Architectural Review Board). Dr. Harrison provided a brief history of the conversations that have taken place on how the school and village can work together for tbenefit of the entire village and in particular with the theater. Several informal meetings have been held with village administration, representatives of the Theater Commssion and school district leadership to discuss potential for a Main Street location, and the relationsihp to Main Street School, property issues and mutal benefits that might be had. There have also been conversation with the Buildings & Grounds Committee with this being the first formal meeting to discuss moving forward with a feasibillity study which will focus on the Main Street option. Mayor Smith gave a history of the Irvington Town Hall Theater . He also noted that any funds for upgrades will be through fundraising, not through tax dollars. However, the upgrade would require a piece of land that belongs to the school district therefore, the purpose of this discussion is to gain feedback from the school board before moving forward. Mr. Malone noted that this is the feasibility phase and provided a rendering of the entrance concept along with the flow of pedestrian traffic. In addition, social spaces are being considered as part of the streetscape master plan but this would require a consideration by the school district of a land swap or easement to the Main Street School property that sits on Main Street next to Village Hall.

In addition, security issues associated with the MSS gym were discussed, playground area accessibility and the possibilty of connecting the MSS campus with the Theater which would offer possibilities of programmatic opportunities were also discussed. Dr. Harrison reiterated that these concerns have been discussed with the MSS principal, facilities personnel and this dialogue would be the beginning of a long process. Jody Hansen on the Theater Commissioner said that it is recognized that the theater is underutilized and that it would be a win win to have the school utilize the facility for concerts, etc. Traffic implications was discussed along with possiblet solutions. Deb Hargraves noted that it is an exciting design but there is concern that any events during the day will impact the village as the school will be in session and could create security concerns. Mr. Malone noted that the usage during the day would be more school-related events rather than outside events being held. She also sees a benefit to to try to incorporate connecting the gym to the main school building. The feasibility study could include the considerations/concerns that are raised by the school district. Dr. Harrison also feels that SED could be very burdensome and must be a consideration for the District, aside from the legal conversations that will be needed. He also noted that the district is grandfathered in and any upgrades could further complicate the process. Although Dr. Harrison is very excited for the village and such an opportunity but urges not to be shortsited with respect to the scope of design, with an pportunity for rooftop social space and amazing views of the river. He also suggests that air conditioning be part of the design as the impacts of not having air conditioning will hamper any expansion of programs during warm weather., Maria Kashkin expressed safety concerns for the school should there be daytime usage and to also would like to limit access to areas that would be hidden from view. The Board is supportive of moving forward and District's criteria would be incorporated in the design.
