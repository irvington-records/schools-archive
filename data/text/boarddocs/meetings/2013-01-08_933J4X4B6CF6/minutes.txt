	10. Presentations/Discussions

		Presentation:  10.1 2013-14 Budget Discussion: Business Office, Debt Service, Insurance, Buildings and Grounds, Transportation and Inter-fund Transfers (Presentation)

		Dr. Harrison stated that the District would be $1.7 Million over tax cap to maintain what is provided currently in our schools. A series of presentations will breakdown what makes up the total budget. Tonight's presentation will cover the business operations of the District. Mr. Grados noted that presentations will be made available online for those not able to attend.

		Ms. Miller reported that the budget will be presented line by line, covering Board of Education, District Office, including the Chief School Administrator, and Business Office expenses, including legal, human resources and public information, operations and maintenance. When looking at line items, the Board asked if some expenses were necessary or optional. Dr. Harrison reiterated that looking at the individual lines will be reviewed, discussed and revised as deemed necessary as the process continues. Also noted was an increase in the Legal line to better reflect actual expenses.

		Mr. Knowles presented the proposed operations and maintenance budgets. A maintenance position is being requested to work throughout the District in order to alleviate the need to hire outside services, such as repairmen and electricians. Mr. Knowles reviewed the custodial equipment requests throughout the District. In discussing the need to purchase a new truck that would be used to plow snow, Mr. McCann asked if a truck should be purchased this year in order to avoid possible closing or delays in school. Ms. Miller noted that NYSED regulations does not allow monies to be transferred into an equipment budget line without doing a resolution reappropriating funds. Administration will look into purchasing a new truck for this year. Ms. Miller noted that the buildings and grounds budget now includes the capital building repairs budget line which had  previously been kept in the inter-fund transfers budget line. Ms. Miller noted that this is the appropriate method for accounting for this budget line.

		Also presented was the proposed Transportation budget. All figures are calculated at CPI and will only change should we be required to go out to bid for transportation services. It was also noted that savings have been realized in consolidation of routes for private school students and transportation of special education students with our private carrier rather than contracting with BOCES---this year's savings is anticipated to be approximately $150,450. Ms. Miller reviewed the budget task forces' recommendations last year to reduce one bus at Dows Lane, removal of all bus monitors and a possible referendum to increase the distance. It would be necessary to decide soon if the Board would want to place a referendum before the voters to increase instances. Mr. Whitney opined that all possible savings should be looked at once the entire budget is presented as it would currently require 5% tax increase. Mr. Grados suggested that if the Board is going to consider elimination of bus monitors, it should be discussed earlier on so that parents can weight in pro or con. It was also noted that Irvington is the only quad district that  still has bus monitors. Potential increases would be additional special education students at $50,000 each or additional private school placements total approximately $18,000-20,000 each.

		The most significant change in the proposed budget is in Debt Service as one bond is expiring. Ms. Miller reviewed the 2013-14 Schedule of Bond Payments and reported that as previously requested by the Board to review possible refinancing current bonds to reduce interest rates, none of the current bonds are callable. Ms. Miller also reviewed the computer lease purchase agreements, noting that it is slightly lower due to lower principal and interest payments. The Debt Service budget lines will realize an 11.37% decrease, mostly due to the retiring bond.

		All the presentations tonight results in a budget of $12,609,000, a negative increase of -2.81% over the 12-13 school year, over the 2012-13 budget.
