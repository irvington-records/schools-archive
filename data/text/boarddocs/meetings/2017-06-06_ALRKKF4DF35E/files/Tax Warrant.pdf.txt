Dear Town Comptroller:

I, Elaine Cardea, District Clerk of the Irvington Union Free School District, Town of
Greenburgh, County of Westchester, State of New York, do hereby certify that the
following budget and tax items for the 2017/2018 fiscal year, beginning July 1, 2017 and
ending June 30, 2018 are true and accurate. This is an official breakdown of the warrant
for use for your collection of taxes.

At its meeting on June 6, 2017 the Board of Education adopted a motion containing the
following resolution:

MOVED, that the voters of the irvington Union Free School District, by a vote of
714 to 206, adopted a budget for the school year commencing July 1, 2017 in the amount
of $59,100,494; that the sums as set forth in the forms of budget heretofore submitted to
the voter be appropriated; that the taxes in the necessary amounts be levied therefore; that
the Board members be authorized to execute a warrant therefore, and that the warrant be
delivered to the Town authorities.

The major budget categories and their respective amounts are listed below:

General Support $ 6,484,119
