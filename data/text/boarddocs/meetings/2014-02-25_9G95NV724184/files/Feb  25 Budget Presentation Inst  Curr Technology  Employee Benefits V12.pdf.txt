Budget Development is a Process
 January 14th – Push Ahead Budget
     Using early projections, predicted that all existing
      programs and services could be maintained within cap
 January 28th – Administration, Business, Buildings &
  Grounds, & Transportation
   Projections remained within cap
 February 11th – Special Education, PPS, & Athletics
   As a result of increases in costs associated with special
    education placements and administrative
    recommendations, it appeared that we would exceed
    cap
 February 25th – Curriculum, Instruction, & Technology
   With new, updated tax base data from the NYS
    Comptroller, the tax levy cap is significantly higher than
    expected
   All previously presented needs and recommendations
    can be managed comfortably under the cap

Instruction Budget Considerations
Consideration #10:
Introduction of an early literacy approach in Kindergarten
and 1st Grade classrooms; including instructional materials
and professional development
Rationale:
 Our commitment as a district has been to increase
   consistency across classrooms.

   research based foundation for all students.

   a foundation for reading and spelling. It provides teachers

   sequential, and cumulative phonics/spelling program using
   multisensory teaching techniques.
