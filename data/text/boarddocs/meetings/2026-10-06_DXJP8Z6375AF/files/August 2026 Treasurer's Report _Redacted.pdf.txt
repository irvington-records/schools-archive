Cash Balance

Revenue:

© General Fund Revenue is currently at $125,886 or .2% of the budgeted amount YTD.

e The Use of Money mostly includes interest earnings from all District general fund bank accounts. The District has earned $80,763 in interest
revenue through the August 26-27 fiscal year and represents 9.8% of the overall forecasted revenue. This month, we are trying to utilize our
financial instruments as best as possible with the current market fluctuation. We continue to receive an average monthly return rate of 3.58%
on our NYCLASS investments and further look for opportunities to ensure we are maximizing our potential earnings with our NYCLASS
Investment pattners. We will analyze the U.S. Treasury market with NYLAF to determine the best instrument for the highest rate, other tax

revenue funds are received.

Expenditures:

e Actual General Fund Expenses totaled approximately $2,464,289 for September and $4,780,631 fiscal YTD. This is largely representing
salaries for the twelve-month staff during the summer months, insurance payments which are typically due at the beginning of each school
year and supplies necessary to start school.
