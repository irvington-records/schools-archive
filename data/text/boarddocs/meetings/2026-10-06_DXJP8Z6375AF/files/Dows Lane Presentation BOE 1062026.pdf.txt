Dows Lane: Authentic Learning




 Board of Education 10–6-26

Dows Lane - Authentic Learning
Authentic learning is:
 ● An educational approach that
   connects classroom lessons to
   real-world tasks and issues
 ● Engages students with hands-on
   learning experiences
 ● Is student driven and the teacher
   acts as a facilitator
