                                             AGREEMENT

        WHEREAS, Irvington Union Free School District (the “District”) and BBS Architects,
Landscape Architects and Engineers P.C. (“BBS”) entered into an Agreement dated on the 1st day of
July, 2025 (the “2025 Agreement”) pursuant to which BBS has provided architectural and engineering
services to the Irvington Union Free School District in connection with capital improvements projects;
and

     WHEREAS, the District has expressed a desire for BBS to conduct a Facility Study related to
HVAC systems throughout the district; and

       WHEREAS, BBS has submitted the attached HVAC Facility Study Fee Proposal dated August
17, 2026, detailing services related to conducting such a Facility Study; and

      WHEREAS, the District and BBS wish to memorialize the Agreement upon which BBS will
conduct the HVAC Facility Study described in the Fee Proposal Dated August 17, 2026.

NOW, THEREFORE, it is hereby agreed as follows:

1.     The District and BBS agree that BBS shall conduct the HVAC Facility Study as described in the
Fee Proposal dated August 17, 2026, in accordance with the terms of the 2025 Agreement.

2.      In consideration for the services to be rendered as described in the HVAC Facility Study Fee
Proposal dated August 17, 2026, the fee paid to BBS is a fixed fee of $19,500.

3.      It is hereby agreed, the rights and responsibilities of the District and BBS with regard to District
HVAC Facility Study shall, unless otherwise agreed to by the parties in writing, be in accordance with
the terms and conditions set forth in the 2025 Agreement which is deemed attached to and incorporated
into this Agreement, by reference.

4.      The 2025 Agreement and all of its terms, provisions, requirements and conditions shall continue
to apply to all of BBS’ services preformed pursuant to this Agreement, irrespective of when such work
is performed and/or completed, and irrespective of any changes in the nature or scope of the work.

5.      Except as specifically set forth herein, the 2025 Agreement between BBS and the District, its
terms, conditions, and provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
