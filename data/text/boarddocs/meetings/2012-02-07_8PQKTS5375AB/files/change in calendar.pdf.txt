Dear Parents,

In a recent decision from the New York State Education Department, several significant changes were
made to the upcoming schedule for the administration of both the state ELA and math assessments
planned for this April. Due to the changes in testing dates, we have had to adjust our calendars to
reflect the number of days of testing, as well as the actual test dates. In addition, these changes require
us to shift our scoring dates. Below you will find the new dates. Please take particular note of the
scoring days as these are days when schools are closed for students K-8.

Administration of the Assessments
English/Language Arts Assessment 3-8 April 17, 18, 19
Mathematics Assessment 3-8 April 25, 26, 27

Scoring of the Assessments

English Language Arts Assessment 3-8 Was Friday, April 27 is now Tuesday, May 1
Mathematics Assessment 3-8 Was Wednesday, May 2 is now Monday, May 7

April 27 and May 2 will be regular school days for all students. We are sorry for this late change, but
unfortunately this was a state-wide decision and local districts are mandated to adhere to the new
schedule.

Please contact me with any questions or concerns you may have.

Raina Kor
