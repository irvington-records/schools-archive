| believe Safe Driving is important to each of us. As a new driver, | realize how
important it is to fully concentrate on my own driving as well as the drivers around me.
Speaking on a cell phone while driving is a huge distraction which can cause tragic
results. Texting while driving is like driving with your eyes closed- need i say more?

| first met Jacy Good at a NFTY (North American Federation of Temple Youth)
convention in Dallas back in February, 2011. She shared her tragic story which i will
briefly summarize. She was on her way home from her college graduation when a truck
swerved to avoid an out of control car (which was driven by a distracted driver speaking
on his cell phone) The truck hit her car which resulted in the death of BOTH of her
parents, and her suffering a traumatic brain injury.

Anything that prevents your full concentration- {Eyes on the Road, Hands on the
Wheel } can cause a catastrophic life changing event, such as dismemberment,
Traumatic Brain Injury, and even death. As young adults, our lives are just beginning
with so much potential. Research shows that because our frontal lobes aren't fully
developed, as teens we don't always make the best decisions, and take unnecessary
risks . | feel that if we stress the importance of safe, non- distracted driving by having
Jacy share her story, my peers will get the message in a very interesting, effective
manner. | also would encourage my peers to spread the awareness/ importance of safe
driving amongst each other and our parents as well.

After Jacy's presentation, i am planning on giving my fellow classmates a survey to
measure the effectiveness of this presentation. | am hoping my fellow students will have
increased awareness of the extreme dangers of distracted driving and that they will
think twice before taking their eyes off the road, or having a phone conversation while
behind the wheel. . | will gladly present the results of this survey to the Board Members
of the IEF.

Jacy charges $1,000 for an appearance. The only equipment she requires are two
microphones OR one microphone on a stand and a projector. | am requesting a $500
grant and i intend to raise the other half through a bake sale, as well as having custom
novelty items printed and sold. Thank you so much for your consideration for this
project. | hope you will consider this as an important vehicle to help keep your children
safe!

Since the first presentation to high schoolers at Ardsley in June 2010, Steve and Jacy have
spoken at almost 75 high schools, colleges, temple congregations, safety organizations,
corporations, rotary clubs, summer camps and everything in between. They have given dozens
of interviews and press conferences, and appeared in print and media across the nation. In
addition to their first hand experience, Jacy and Steve have been trained on the extensive
science behind the dangers of distracted driving. We look forward to sharing our story with
Irvington and stay in touch if you need anything else from us.
