Meeting: Feb 07, 2012 - Regular Public Meeting - 7:30 p.m.
Date: 2012-02-07
Category: 9. Business Office Matters
Subject: 9.10 Contracts- Special Education Service Providers
Type: Action (Consent)

The Irvington School District contracts with several Service providers to provide a variety of related services for our special education students.  Therapy Source, Inc. will provide speech therapy, occupational therapy, physical therapy, audiology, sign language, ABA, and pyschological services at the rate of $60-$118 per hour for the 2011-12 school year.
