Meeting: Feb 07, 2012 - Regular Public Meeting - 7:30 p.m.
Date: 2012-02-07
Category: 9. Business Office Matters
Subject: 9.8 Field Trip - Grade 8 Washington, D.C. Trip
Type: Action (Consent)

The 8th grade students will be taking a trip to Washington DC between April 3-5, 2012.  The cost of the trip is paid for by the students and through fundraising activities.  Approximately 147 students will be going along with teachers and chaperones. The estimated cost of the trip is $66,000 which is approximately $449 per student.
