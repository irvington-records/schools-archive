	3. Appointment of Superintendent of Schools

		Action:  3.1 Resolution to Appoint the Next Superintendent of Schools - Roll Call Vote and Contract Signing

		BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District herewith appoints Mr. (Dr.) Kristopher Harrison as Superintendent of Schools, effective July 1, 2012, for a term to expire on June 30, 2017; and BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the President of the Board of Education is herewith authorized to execute the employment agreement (“Agreement”) between Mr. Harrison and the Board of Education, which Agreement has been previously reviewed and approved by the Board of Education.

		Motion by James McCann, second by Robert Grados.

		Final Resolution: Motion Carries

		Yes: Robyne Camp, John Dawson, Robert Grados, James McCann, Phil Whitney

		Prior to the vote to appointment of Dr. Harrison, Board members spoke of their enthusiasm and excitement in Dr. Harrison's acceptance of the position of Superintendent of Schools in Irvington. Board members referred to Dr. Harrison as a "visionary leader" and whose motto is "every student, everyday".

		Information: 3.2  Mr. James Groven, President of the Irvington Faculty Association and Dr. Scott Mosenthal, President of the Irvington Administrators' Association, congratulated and welcomed Dr. Harrison to the District on behalf of all staff members. Dr. Mosenthal presented Dr. Harrison with Irvington memorabilia.

		Mayor Brian Smith welcomed Dr. Harrison on behalf of the Village of Irvington and noted that very few events are as important as the appointment of a Superintendent. Mayor Smith noted that he looked forward to working closely with Dr. Harrison.

		Information:  3.3 Remarks from the Superintendent Designee

		Dr. Harrison thanked the Board for their confidence and trust in him to join the District. Dr. Harrison stated that he looks forward to collaborating with all constituents on the mission to "ensure that Irvington schools meets the needs of every child, every day" and is "sincerely thrilled to become part of the Irvington community."
