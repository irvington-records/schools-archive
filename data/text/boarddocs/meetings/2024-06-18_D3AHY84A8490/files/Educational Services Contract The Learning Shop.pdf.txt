Homebound Instruction Contract between The Learning Shop, Inc. and
The Irvington Union Free School District

1. Basic Services: The Learning Shop will provide homebound instruction for children
who are unable to attend school
e Learning Shop teachers will contact, and maintain continued communication
with the teachers of homebound students in order to facilitate desired
instruction, For long term absences, if the school desires, The Learning Shop is
fully capable of instructing as per the New York State curriculum, on our own.
Grades will be sent monthly for your consideration.
The Learning Shop can also administer all exams as needed:
o Midterms, Finals, Regents, RCTs, etc.

2. The Learning Shop will provide homebound instruction for the district as follows:
e Not more than 10 hours per week for grades K-5, unless authorized by school.
© Not more than 15 hours per week for grades 6-12, unless authorized by school.
* For grades 6-12, a minimum of 2 hours per session, unless not
medically/academically sound or unless requested by school.

3. The tutoring rate is $68.50 per hour, for both remote and in person.

¢ The Learning Shop will send paperwork (attendance reports and lesson plans)
once a month along with an invoice.
For a “no show”, The Learning Shop will charge the school for 1 hour.
The Learning Shop will make every attempt to make up missed hours.

* Requested services outside the allotted teaching time, such as writing a report
for a CSE or attending a school meeting, is billable at $68.50/hr.

© Testing that requires same-day pickup and drop-off (Regents, RCTs, etc.) will
be billed one additional hour beyond the seat-time required. For a “no-show”
on a testing day the full time will be charged.

4. The Learning Shop teachers will meet the New York State Instructional Mandate. Al!
teachers will meet applicable NYSED certification requirements and fingerprint
clearance. All employees must complete an OSPRA 102 (Clearance for Employment
Request) form which is available electronically.

The student’s home (an adult 18 years or older must be present)
The local library

Commercial General Liabitity Insurance
$1,000,000 per occurrence/ $2,000,000 aggregate.
Workers’ Compensation and N.Y.S. Disability
Statutory Workers’ Compensation, Employers’ Liability and N.Y.S. Disability

Sexual Molestation Coverage

$1,000,000 per occurrence/ $1,000,000 aggregate
Professional Liability Coverage

$2,000,000 per occurrence/ $2,000,000 aggregate
Auto Liability Coverage Non-Owned Automobiles
$1,000,000 combined single limit.

7. Terms of Agreement

July 2024~ June 2025

8. In all matters regarding instruction, curriculum, and method of administering
academic services, The Learning Shop will consult the appropriate school personnel
(Superintendent, Principal, Guidance Counselor, Teacher, etc) for advisement.
