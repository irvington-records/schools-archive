Dear Town Comptroller:

I, Evan Gross, District Clerk of the Irvington Union Free School District, Town of Greenburgh, County
of Westchester, State of New York, do hereby certify that the following budget and tax items for the
2024-2025 fiscal year, beginning July 1, 2024 and ending June 30, 2025 are true and accurate. This is
an official breakdown of the warrant for use for your collection of taxes.

At its meeting on June 18, 2024 the Board of Education adopted a motion containing the following
resolution:

MOVED, that the voters of the Irvington Union Free School District, by a vote of 389 to 103, adopted a
budget for the school year commencing July 1, 2024 in the amount of $76,088,100; that the sums as set
forth in the forms of budget heretofore submitted to the voter be appropriated; that the taxes in the
necessary amounts be levied therefore; that the Board members be authorized to execute a warrant
therefore, and that the warrant be delivered to the Town authorities.

The major budget categories and their respective amounts are listed below:

General Support                            $ 9,649,856
