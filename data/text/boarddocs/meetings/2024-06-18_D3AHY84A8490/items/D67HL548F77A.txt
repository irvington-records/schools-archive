Subject: 8.1 Nomination of Brian Friedman for the Everett R. Dyer Award for Distinguished School Board Service
Type: Action

Brian Friedman is being nominated for the Everett R. Dyer Award for Distinguished School Board Service.

The New York State School Boards Association's Everett R. Dyer Award for Distinguished School Board Service is presented annually to a current or former school board member, who, in the judgment of the NYSSBA Awards Committee, should be recognized for outstanding contributions to public education and children in his or her own school district.
