The Process
2022 - 2023: Learned about 3 Potential Math Programs & Chose 2 to Pilot
 ●   Engaged in an Illustrative Mathematics lesson shared by Josh Rosen (Grade 3 lesson on area)
 ●   Participated in a Eureka Math² lesson either at the K-3 level or the 4-5 level with Christine
 ●   Participated in a Reveal Math lesson either at the K-3 level or the 4-5 level with Christine
 ●   Visited Eastchester to see Reveal Math in action for Grade 2 and Grade 5
 ●   Selected 2 Programs, Eureka and Reveal to pilot in the 2023 - 2024 school year

2023 - 2024: Piloted Eureka Math2 and Reveal
 ●   Deﬁned the priorities and criteria for a new math program and examined the resources /
     online materials / and printed materials, considering these priorities
 ●   Piloted lessons on the same topic for each program (Apples to Apples)
 ●   Piloted a week or more of consecutive lessons from each program (Deep Dive)
 ●   Reﬂected as a math committee on the experiences of using each program
 ●   Chose a program
 ●   Molly will work with grade teams to introduce the new program to teachers.

2024 - 2025
 ●   Molly will support teachers in implementing this new program.

This new program has major improvements in
the following areas:
 ●   Student engagement
      ○   The design of the materials, activities, and teacher presentation resources are all
          focused on increased student engagement.

 ●   Assessment
      ○   There are pre-assessments with recommended supports / instructional tools based
          on the results, observational assessment sheets for ongoing assessment, topic-level
          quizzes, module assessments, and benchmark assessments all with rubrics.

 ●   Accessibility / differentiation
      ○   UDL principles were prioritized in the design so that the curriculum is accessible to
          all students.

 ●   Ease of use (and therefore horizontal alignment)
      ○   All materials are updated and available through the Great Minds website.
