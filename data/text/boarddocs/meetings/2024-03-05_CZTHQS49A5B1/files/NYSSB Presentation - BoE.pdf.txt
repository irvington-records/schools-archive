                 The NYS Seal of Biliteracy formally
New York State   recognizes students who have attained
                 a certain level of proﬁciency in two or
   Seal of       more world languages, one of which
                 must be English.
  Biliteracy
                 ➜ Nationwide program
                 ➜ Around 50% of schools in the Hudson Valley
                   region offer the Seal

● 30 total candidates
● Languages represented: Spanish, French, Mandarin,
  Korean, Hebrew, Latin, and English

In both English and a world language, students:
● Submit a piece of writing
● Present their work to a panel
● Participate in a Q&A session with panel members

Must demonstrate proﬁciency in both languages in the four modes
of communication: reading, listening, writing, and speaking

Each component is evaluated using rubrics developed by NYSED

                                                        SLIDESMANIA.COM

The recognition of attaining biliteracy becomes part of the high school
   transcript and diploma for these students and is a statement of
  accomplishment for future employers and for college admission
