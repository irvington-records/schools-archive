DESCRIPTION OF SERVICES TO BE PROVIDED FOR ASSISTIVE TECHNOLOGY
EVALUATIONS AND TRAININGS WITH WIHD:

A comprehensive assistive technology evaluation by the WIHD Assistive Technology Team.
The comprehensive evaluation may require the services of one or more of the following
disciplines: Speech-Language Pathologist (Augmentative/Alternative
Communication/Instructional Technology); and/or Occupational Therapist (Access/Instructional.
Evaluation fee will be determined as follows:

One discipline: $1,380.00
Two disciplines: $1,890.00
Travel cost per school visit $25 under 20 miles

$50 over 20 miles

Please note that in the event an evaluation has to be re-written (in medical or educational format)
there will be an additional charge of $225.

Trainings can include: observation of student in school activities; adult and peer interaction;
meetings with team members (parent, paid professionals, teaches, and related service providers);
and hands-on training sessions with devices. Training fees are as follows:

$225.00 per hour or $560 for 3 contracted hours. Travel cost per school visit is
$25.00 up to 20 miles and anything over 20 miles is $50.00
