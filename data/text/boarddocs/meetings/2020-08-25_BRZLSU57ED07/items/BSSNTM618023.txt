Subject: 12.4 Contract - Metropolitan Center for Research on Equity and the Transformation of Schools
Type: Action (Consent)
Attachments: Irvington UFSD - NYU Contract executed.pdf

The partnership with NYU Metropolitan Center for Research on Equity and the Transformation of Schools will provide for the following:  Superintendent's Conference Day keynote presentation and school-level breakout sessions, Culturally Responsive Sustainable Education lead learner training, 8 school-level workshops (2 at each school), Root Cause Analysis that will assess District programs, practices, policies and student opportunities to ensure equity and access and the development of an equity-focused strategic plan.  The contract cost will be supplemented by outside grants and a donation by the Irvington Faculty Association (IFA).
