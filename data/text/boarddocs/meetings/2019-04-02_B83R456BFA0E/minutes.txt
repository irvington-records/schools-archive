3. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Report:  3.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison welcomed everyone and acknowledged the great season for the Girls Varsity Basketball team.  He also put in a plug for the Middle School production of Aladdin Jr.  Dr. Kor spoke about the success of the 4th Grade Annual Science Fair.

Information:  3.2 Committee Reports

Michael Hanna stated that he would like to move the Committee Reports to the end of the meeting.

4. Comments From Members of the Community

Information:  4.1 Procedures for Community Comment on Topics that appear on Agenda

Michael Hanna opened the floor to the community.  There was follow-up on the leaf blower issue from the prior meeting and a community member spoke of her support for EWL for Irvington Schools.

5. Discussion/Presentation

Dr. Harrison provided modifications to the proposed 2019-20 budget, based on the feedback provided by the Board of Education and the community.  Michael Hanna thanked the Administration for their hard work. Discussion ensued regarding various items on the budget. An active discussion took place regarding the FLEX/FLES EWL programs.

Michael Hanna opened the floor for community comments regarding the budget.  Six Community Members spoke at this time.

There was a brief update from the Trustees on their Committee Reports.
