                                                                                                      3/6/19
                               Irvington Middle School
                        3 Day Washington D.C. Capital City Tour



Wednesday, April 10, 2019
6:45 AM Buses (3) to report to school
7:15 AM Departure – stop for lunch enroute at group’s expense
12:30PM Arrive in Washington D.C. - Join Tour Escorts at Air and Space Museum
1:00 PM Visit Air & Space Museum/ National Mall and Smithsonian Museums
3:00 PM Visit US Holocaust Memorial Museum Permanent Exhibit
5:30 PM Dinner at Buca Di Beppo
7:30 PM Illuminated Monument Tour to include Lincoln, Vietnam and Korean War Memorials
10:00PM Check in to Hilton Hotel, 6550 Loisdale Road, Springfield, VA / 703-971-8900
10:30PM Security to monitor hallways

 Thursday, April 11, 2019
 7:30 AM Breakfast at hotel
 9:30 AM Admission to the Newseum
12:00PM Lunch at Smithsonian (meal vouchers provided)
12:45/1:15 Admission to African American Museum
 4:30 PM Tour of Monuments and Memorials around Tidal Basin – FDR, Jefferson & MLK Memorials
 5:30 PM Dinner at Joe Theismann’s
 8:00 PM Ghost and Graveyard tour of Old Town Alexandria
10:30PM Security to monitor hallways

Friday, April 12, 2019
7:30 AM Breakfast at the hotel and check out
9:30 AM Visit Arlington National Cemetery to see the Changing of the Guard at the Tomb of the
          Unknown Soldier as well as visit the gravesites of the Kennedy Brothers and the crew of the
          Challenger Space Expedition
12:30PM Lunch at Shops at National Place (coupon provided)
2:00 PM Gallery Passes to see Congress in session (arranged by Tour Escorts)
3:30 PM Departure for home
8:15 PM Arrival back at school
