                            BUILDINGS & GROUNDS COMMITTEE

                                      June 12, 2013 9 am

                                       MEETING MINUTES



Present: Dr. Harrison, Maria Kashkin, Gary Knowles, Beverly Miller



    1.   The committee reviewed the outstanding projects that were discussed at the June 5th meeting.
         The projects are:
            a. HS lighting on walkways – price quote $13,934.00. The committee approved the work
                be done as soon as possible as this is a safety issue.
            b. Replacement of bluestone & steps on campus – Bev contacted LAN to obtain the
                estimated cost for this project. To date, we do not have a response. She will contact
                them again.
            c. Replace Andover controls at Dows Lane – price quote was $300,000 - $350,000. This far
                exceeds available funds. Gary is meeting with another vendor on June 18th to get a
                second opinion of the work needed and a cost estimate.
            d. Repair/replace certain exterior doors on HS/MS campus – price quote $9,779.46. The
                committee approved the work to begin as soon as possible as this is a safety issue.
            e. Repair/replace hardware on interior fire doors on campus - the work is scheduled to be
                done and is being paid through General Fund building repair funding.



    2.    Grounds Maintenance – The committee discussed the grounds maintenance scheduled to be
         performed during the summer. Gary also make the committee aware of the shortage of
         custodial labor during the summer months due to vacations, etc.



    3.    The committee was informed that Larry Schepps has been installing perennial plants behind
         Dows Lane without permission from the District. The committee recommended that a letter be
         sent to Mr. Schepps forbidding any landscaping installations without the express written
         consent of the Superintendent.

Minutes taken by Beverly Miller
