Dear Board of Education,

Every year the proceeds from the Otto Awards is donated to a specific charity or
organization voted on by the faculty. This year as a faculty we decided to donate
all the proceeds from the Otto Awards 2013 to the Scott Mosenthal scholarship
fund. Since Scott has been the founder and creator of the Otto Awards, we
thought that the best way to honor him is to donate the money to a scholarship in
his name.

Claudia Rodriguez
Otto Awards Treasurer
