Dear Ms. Miller:

On Monday, June 10, 2013 at 9:00am the Child Nutrition bids for operation with
a Food Service Management Company at the Irvington Union Free School District were
opened. Aramark Educational Services, LLC, and the Whitson’s School Nutrition Corp.
were the two management companies who attended the vendor conference and tour of the
District on May 31, 2013.

Both Aramark and Whitson’s responded, and after a thorough review of each bid
proposal, the Aramark Educational Services, LLC proposal and bid amount is, in our
opinion, the lowest responsible.

The total cost per meal of the Aramark bid was $2.9436, which is lower than
Whitson’s bid of $3.3202. Both bids will allow the District to receive an amount in
excess of the guaranteed return as stipulated in the specifications.

I have enclosed a summary analysis of the bid for your review. As the incumbent
management company within the Irvington Union Free School District, you are aware of
Aramark’s operational and financial capabilities. Aramark will retain all of the current
food service staff members at their current hours and will increase their wages for next
year as required by the information provided on Schedule G-1 within the bid
specifications.

Upon a thorough review of the proposal submitted by Aramark, it is my
recommendation that the Irvington Union Free School District Child Nutrition Contract
be awarded to Aramark for the 2013-14 School Year.

It was a pleasure to assist you and the Irvington Union Free School District with
the Child Nutrition Bid Process.

Sincerely,

Vice President, HMB
