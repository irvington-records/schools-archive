Meeting: May 03, 2016 - Public Hearing/Regular Meeting*
Date: 2016-05-03
Category: 9. Business of the Board
Subject: 9.3 Adjustment to Rate of Pay for Certified Election Inspectors
Type: Action (Consent)

As NYS school districts are now mandated to utilize the electronic scanning machines, we must also use certified election inspectors to operate the machines.  The County sets the rate of pay at $225 per day. The rate will be prorated for inspectors who work only a portion of the full day. The Chief Election Inspector is paid at the rate of $250 per day. Several workers who are not certified will only work the voter books and will be paid at the previously approved rate of $9.50 per hour.
