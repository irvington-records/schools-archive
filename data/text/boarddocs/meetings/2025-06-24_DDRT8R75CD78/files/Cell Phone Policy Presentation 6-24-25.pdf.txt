HeadsUp Rivertowns PTSA ‘Smartphones in School’ Survey

    ● 497 Responses

    ● 70% of respondents children do not have a smartphone

    ● Majority plan on giving students a smartphone in grade 8 or later

    ● Large majority of respondents in favor of no access to
      smartphones during the school day

    ● Large majority of respondents wanting workshops on how to
      control apps, digital safety support, and screen time support

Stakeholder Feedback:
What are the benefits of a bell to bell cell phone ban?

       ● Fewer distractions/compulsions

       ● More engagement, focus

       ● More interaction with each other in a personal way

       ● More eye contact

       ● More attention paid in class

Stakeholder Feedback: What are the losses?

     ● There is a “comfort in having phone”

     ● Students aware they can’t have phones, creates tension
       “students against the system” not signing up for this, just
       being taken away

     ● This will be at big transition, particularly for IHS

     ● Parents to start the conversation at home now

     ● Partnership will be needed

Stakeholder Feedback:
How should students bringing phones and watches to school
store them to be in compliance with the new policy?

     ● Have them put it in their locker and as an escalation, if they
       don’t put it in their locker then they have to put it in a pouch for
       a month

     ● Use the honor system, if they don’t comply must put it in pouch

     ● Central collection point - logistically puts a load on the staff - a
       question of liability, we are taking expensive personal property

     ● Storage solution is not simple - complicated - liability

Stakeholder Feedback:
How should students bringing phones and watches to school store them
to be in compliance with the new policy?
  ●   Should be put in your locker so it can be easily accessed at the end of the day

  ●   Even if we come up with the best possible system [some students] are still
      going to sneak

  ●   For a lot of students we have given them phones and then we are just taking
      them away [but if] we come from a place of empathy and get teenagers on
      board, maybe not punitive and try to explain to them - students are smart

  ●   Explain why this has to happen - the honor system respects their agency
      which will create more of a “buy in” instead of “you broke the rules”

  ●   Seniors are about to go out into the world and have to implement
      [moderation] without any scaffolding, better they learn to do that in school

Stakeholder Feedback:
How should students be directed to contact parents if necessary?

    ● Stressful to not have phones during emergency but it is not
      better for kids to have phones during an emergency - can
      spread misinformation - then spreads from parent to parent,
      which the school has to correct

    ● Parents need to know and trust that the school is “handling”
      any emergency

    ● Access to a dedicated landline at IHS- make it fun

Proposed Policy

      ● Dows/MSS: no smartphones or watches, if found will be
        confiscated and parents will be contacted

      ● IMS/IHS: locker storage

      ● Incorporating use of lockers at IHS

      ● Students with IEPs, Section 504 Plans, or documentation from
        medical practitioners specifically requiring use of electronic
        devices, may do so as specified.

      ● Evaluate over time, seek to support individual students while
        assuming most students will comply
