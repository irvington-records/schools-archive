Drew Coburn
Has 25+ years experience in branding, advertising and integrated communication at agencies such as Landor,
Young & Rubicam and Grey Worldwide among others. He’s worked on strategy for Pfizer, Kraft, J&J, Bristol-My-
ers, Aventis and The New York Times.

Drew holds a BA from Princeton, a Masters in International Affairs from Columbia and a masters in Educational
Counseling from Mercy College.


Chuck Routhier
Has 25+ years of design experience and has worked on comprehensive branding programs for Hertz, Blackber-
ry, Burger King, Capital One, Lipitor, Merrill Lynch, and Morgan Stanley. Chuck was creative director at Landor
Associates in NYC, then Vice President/Creative Director of Brand Anatomy.

He holds an MFA in Graphic Design from Yale University and teaches at Mercy College. He has also taught
design at Yale, Parsons and Maine College of Art and designs and crafts lighting and furniture.

Branding IUFSD / Strategy




Irvington is a small, intimately connected school district and
community on the Hudson River…where teachers, families,
and administrators come together around students.

To provide a vibrant education that develops the whole child,
as individuals, and a nurtured and nurturing member of a
close-knit community.

A vibrant education, both in the immediate moment of their
lives and as a steadfast foundation to support a lifetime of
future endeavors.
