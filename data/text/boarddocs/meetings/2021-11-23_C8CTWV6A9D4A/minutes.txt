(Item added at meeting) 6.5

Motion to approve Monument Commission, Creation and Installation

Whereas, in partnership with the Irvington Historical Society, a group of community members  known as “Commemorate” has raised funds to  commission a monument made of granite and bronze, to be created by artist Vinnie Bagwell, to commemorate the contributions of enslaved Africans who worked and toiled in the geographic area that would become known as Irvington (coterminous with the Irvington School District and the Town of Greenburgh); and,

Whereas, a monument commemorating the contributions of enslaved Africans prior to their delayed manumission in New York State, aligns with the mission, goals, and commitment of the Irvington Union Free School District to provide an education that fosters critical thinking and allows its students to develop a deep and broad  understanding of New York, American and World History, within a school community and culture that values diversity, equity, and inclusion; and

Whereas, in accord with the New York State Culturally Responsive Sustaining Education framework, such a monument will foster students’ abilities to connect across lines of difference, elevate the history of historically marginalized voices, and serve to empower students as agents of social change; and

Whereas, as public meetings of the Irvington Union Free School District Board of Education providing for full discussion of the plans for the monument’s creation and installation with opportunity for public comment have been provided at school board meetings on January 26, 2021 and November 23, 2021; and,

Whereas, the Village of Irvington has agreed to accept responsibility for all maintenance of the monument; and,

Whereas, “Commemorate” has agreed to accept responsibility for all costs associated with the commissioning, creation, insurance, and installation/reinstallation of the monument and,

Whereas, “Commemorate” has agreed that the Irvington Union Free School District and the Irvington Board of Education will have final approval of the appearance, location and language of any and all educational plaques or other explanatory materials sought to be affixed to District property in relation to the monument;

THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Irvington Board of Education approves the installation of the monument to be created by artist Vinnie Bagwell as commissioned by “Commemorate” at the designated staked location on the southwest corner of the Main Street School property.

Motion by Maura Gedid, second by Beth Propper.

Final Resolution: Motion Carries

Yes: Maura Gedid, Brian Friedman, Beth Propper, Erin Bernstein, Jayne Wissner, Susan Brunenavs
