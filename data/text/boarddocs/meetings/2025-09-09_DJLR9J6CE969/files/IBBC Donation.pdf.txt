                                                      Memo​
                                       Bulldog Booster Club Donation


To: Dr. Mara Ratesic, Superintendent​
From: John Buonamano, Director of Athletics​
Date: 9/8/25​
Subject: Acceptance of Bulldog Booster Club Donation – Football Headset System

Dear Dr. Ratesic,

I am writing to request approval to accept a donation from the Bulldog Booster Club for the purchase of an
updated football headset system, the Cobalt Plus Football model.

This system provides our coaching staff with reliable, real-time communication across the sideline and press box,
which is essential for coordinating game management, ensuring player safety, and enhancing overall team
performance. The new system will directly benefit our athletes by improving the efficiency and accuracy of
coaching decisions during competition.

The donation replaces our former headset system, which has experienced repeated malfunctions and has reached
the end of its expected life capacity. The Booster Club’s generosity ensures that our program can continue to
operate at a high standard without placing additional financial strain on the district.

I respectfully request BOE approval to officially accept this donation.

Sincerely,




​
John Buonamano​
Director of Athletics
