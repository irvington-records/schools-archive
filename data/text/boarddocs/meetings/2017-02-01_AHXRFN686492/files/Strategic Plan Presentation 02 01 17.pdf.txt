After Some Challenging Fiscal Years, We Are Restoring Program Health

•   Some challenging budget years following the
    crisis of ’08 caused us to cut spending in several
    categories, which we have since worked to
    restore

•   Believing in intervention, and to provide for our
    most challenged learners, we have invested
    every year in Special Education and in
    Guidance & Social Work

•   We have begun to restore our budget in
    maintenance, but our buildings and grounds are
    challenged and will likely need additional future
    investment or a possible bond

•   In addition to the budget, we sought and passed
    a bond to invest in several areas of our facilities,
    including fields, vestibules, and other
    infrastructure needs

OUR VISION FOR THE FUTURE

Our View of the 21st Century
• Our students will need to be global citizens in a world that is growing ever smaller and
  more complex

• Our students will need to demonstrate ease with technology, to meet the demands of
  future workplaces and social environments

• Our students will, therefore, need to be flexible thinkers, problem solvers, effective
  communicators, and empathetic citizens

• Academic rigor in all content areas will be key to student success in the future

Backed by Global Educational
Research
In 2015, the Organization for Economic Development studied
education results across the globe and found that, generally
speaking, “the smartest countries tended to be those that
•    have acted to make teaching more prestigious and selective;
•    helped schools establish cultures of constant improvement;
     and
•    applied rigorous, consistent standards across all
     classrooms.”

Other OECD global studies have come to similar
conclusions. Research done in 2010 concluded...that “three
things matter most”:

    1) Getting the right people to become teachers
    2) Developing those people into effective instructors, and
    3) Ensuring the system is able to deliver the best possible
       instruction for every child


                                                                   Source: http://www.oecd.org/pisa/   11

                             Our Mission

   The mission of the Irvington School District is to create a challenging and
supportive learning environment in which each student attains his or her highest
 potential for academic achievement, critical thinking and life-long learning. Our
   schools encourage the discovery and development of students’ individual
     strengths, skills and talents, and foster social and civic responsibility.

Our Core Beliefs and Vision
We believe in

• A community (educators, students, parents, community members) that fosters curiosity,
  understanding, innovation, compassion, creativity, social emotional development, academic
  excellence and achievement.

• A culture that acknowledges and respects individual differences, and provides for multiple
  opportunities for teacher and student learning and innovation.

• Schools that provide local-to-global learning experiences through an in-depth, broadly
  defined and interdisciplinary curriculum, supported with meaningful teaching practices and
  service opportunities.

• Students who demonstrate their learning through varied and diverse learning and
  assessment opportunities which measure all valued knowledge, skills and dispositions and
  attend to their needs, passions, strengths, interests, and learning styles.
                                                                                       13

 Our Mission is Built on a Theory of Action, with Underlying
 Strategies & Action Plans to Achieve our Goals

• We have defined goals that focus our resources on our
  highest priorities. Each goal is further defined through a
  series of objectives.

• Our objectives, together with the key measures,
  constitute in a measurable way, steps toward meeting a
  particular goal.

• We will gauge achievement of our objectives and goals
  through a series of key measures. The key measures
  are quantifiable indicators that demonstrate our
  progress towards each objective.

• We have identified annual stretch targets or
  “benchmarks” of performance for each key measure.

To Achieve Our Vision, We Will
•   Provide students with a rigorous, comprehensive, enriched and diversified curricula that will
    prepare students to achieve their personal best, and will integrate technology in their
    learning.

•   Support educators through targeted professional learning and opportunities for
    collaboration.

•   Encourage innovation, creativity and risk taking to inspire a dynamic learning environment.

•   Foster the creation of a culture where students are active participants in society.

•   Strengthen local connections to and ownership of our schools.

•   Ensure the fiscal health of the district and provide for a high quality learning environment.

We will… Provide students with a rigorous, comprehensive, enriched and
diversified curriculum that will prepare students to achieve their personal
best and will integrate technology in their learning.
In order to develop successful 21st century learners that will be prepared to thrive in a rapidly evolving global
society, the Irvington School District will:

• Provide students with rigorous authentic, learning experiences

• Develop a comprehensive curriculum that includes:
    aligned and articulated content with defined learning outcomes
    a balanced and systematic approach to assessment
    21st Century skills and dispositions-problem solver, flexible thinker, collaborative learner, effective
     communicator, empathetic citizen, self-reliant, reflective, creative, risk-taker

• Invest in the administration, faculty and staff to ensure the district is able to hire, grow, and deliver an
  enriching, comprehensive learning experience

• Provide professional development to support teachers to deliver a rigorous learning experience

• Implement a process of continuous instructional & curricular improvement
