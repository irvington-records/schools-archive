Name: Levi Segall
Human Right: Climate change
Claim: Climate change is causing disasters which are causing many people to die

Ahh! A hurricane! Ahh! A wildfire! What’s going on?! The answer is simple: climate
change. Climate change is causing our planet to get hotter and hotter. And what’s behind
this? Actually, it’s not a “what” — it’s a “who.” That “who” is us. The good news? We are
also the ones who can stop it, but only if we take action together.

For starters, climate change is fueling extreme wildfires. In California, rising temperatures
and drier conditions have made forests highly flammable, leading to devastating
wildfires. Scientists have found that higher temperatures caused by global warming are
drying out vegetation, making wildfires much more likely to start and spread. These fires
are destroying homes, displacing wildlife, and claiming lives. Did you know that between
2003 and 2020, around 18,000 wildfires erupted in California alone? And yes, humans
are a major part of the problem.

Secondly, in Florida, warmer oceans caused by climate change are fueling stronger and
more frequent hurricanes. These storms are not only more destructive but also deadlier.
Did you know hurricanes are now three times more frequent than they were 100 years
ago? Hurricane lan in 2022, for instance, caused horrible flooding, with storm surges
reaching 10 to 15 feet in some areas. Some other storms have caused water levels to
rise so high that they’ve reached the roofs of houses!

These disasters may sound like scenes from a horror movie, but they’re very real—and
they’re happening because of us. The only way to stop them is for us to take
responsibility and make changes. Small actions like walking to school, reducing our use
of fossil fuels, and conserving energy can make a big difference. So, how do you feel
about climate change now? Let’s take a stand and stop it—who’s with me?!
