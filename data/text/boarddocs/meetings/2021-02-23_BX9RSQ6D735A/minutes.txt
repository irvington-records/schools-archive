10. Business of the Board

Action (Consent): 10.1 Establishment of the Claudia Rodriguez Scholarship

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District administers the Irvington Union Free School District; and WHEREAS, the Claudia Rodriguez Scholarship Committee, hereinafter referred to as the "Donor" desires to make a gift to the Board of Education in order to create a scholarship fund, to be known as the "Claudia Rodriguez Scholarship"; and WHEREAS Education Law 1709(12-a) and 2503 (1) authorizes the Board of Education: to take and hold in trust for the purpose of awarding scholarships in [the) schools any real estate transferred to it by gift, grant, bequest or devise, or any gift, legacy or annuity, of whatever kind, given or bequeathed to [the] board and apply the same, or the interest and proceeds thereof, according to the instructions of the donor or testator. NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District hereby creates the above-named Scholarship Fund, as set forth below, based upon the understanding between the parties: The Claudia Rodriguez Scholarship 1. The Donor, Claudia Rodriguez Scholarship Committee shall deliver to the Board, for deposit in the trust and agency account in the bank designated by the Board of Education. 2. The Scholarship Fund may be supplemented at any time by any donor(s) wishing to gift, grant, bequest or devise cash, cash equivalent or other property acceptable to the District or through any gift, legacy or annuity, of whatever kind, given, or bequeathed to the District for such purpose. 3.The scholarship shall be named the Claudia Rodriguez Scholarship. 4. The scholarship may be awarded annually to one or more Irvington High School students. The Committee/ School Administration may choose not to award a scholarship that year. The student must demonstrate the following character traits: Spirited, fun-loving, committed, caring toward all, empathetic and a true friend. The recipient should, if possible, also be someone who might not otherwise be recognized for their academic prowess, sports ability or artistic talent. The student would exhibit great potential moving forward and someone recognized as having qualities of strength and persistence. 5. The funds allocated to said scholarship shall come from donations by any party at anytime which may be used to increase the scholarship or held for future recipients at the discretion of the Claudia Rodriguez Scholarship Committee or School Administration. The scholarship is intended to be funded at a minimum of $50 or base amount per year until funds no longer exist to fulfill this minimum. When said such funds fall below $50, the scholarship Committee/ School Administration will determine another senior scholarship fund to transfer funds to. The Claudia Rodriguez Committee may decide to increase the amount of a single award, or award more than one scholarship, per year. 6. Details of the organization and administration of the Scholarship Fund will be left to the discretion of the Board of Education.

Motion by Beth Propper, second by Jayne Wissner.

Yes: Michael Hanna, David Graeber, Maura Gedid, Brian Friedman, Beth Propper, Erin Bernstein, Jayne Wissner

Action (Consent): 10.2 Resolution for High Risk Sports

Resolution: WHEREAS, on or about February 2, 2021, Westchester County Health Commissioner, Dr. Sherlita Amler, authorized the resumption of high risk sports in Westchester County, subject to compliance with various health and administrative guidelines; and WHEREAS, Commissioner Amler has required each school district to provide written authorization to the Westchester County Department of Health (“WCDOH”) as to the District’s intended involvement in high risk sports; NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District herewith authorizes the participation of its students in high risk sports, effective February 1, 2021, in accordance with the guidelines set forth by the NYSPHSAA and the WCDOH. BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District establish a COVID surveillance testing program in which all high-risk student athletes shall participate. BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District herewith authorizes the resumption of the following specific high risk sports and required student grade level. Winter Sports Varsity & JV Wrestling Grades of eligible student-athletes Grades 9-12 and those in middle school who qualify through the Athletic Placement Process (APP) and in compliance with the Irvington Athletics Department guidelines.
