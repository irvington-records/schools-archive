Meeting: Feb 23, 2021 - Regular Meeting
Date: 2021-02-23
Category: 11. Community Comments and/or Questions
Subject: 11.1 Procedures for Community Comments and/or Questions regarding the school district.
Type: Information

Members of the community will be invited to raise questions or offer comments on any topic related to the school district. Community members are reminded to direct questions to the President of the Board and to limit questions or comments to approximately three minutes.
