Sen. Andrew Stewart-Cousins put forth resolutions on  the floor of the NYS Senate recognizing the following Irvington Athletic Teams who were designated Student Athletes and will present students and coaches with certificates:

Irvington High School Boys Indoor Track Team on receiving the 2016 Winter Scholar Athletic Team Champion Award by the NYSPHSAA(New York State Public High School Athletic Association, Inc.)

Resolution J5347

Irvington High School Girls Indoor Track Team on receiving the 2016 Winter Scholar Athletic Team Champion Award by the NYSPHSAA

Resolution J5348

Irvington High School Field Hockey Team on receiving the 2015 Fall Scholar Athletic Team Champion Award by the NYSPHSAA

Resolution J5349

Irvington High School Boys Cross Country Team on receiving the 2015 Fall Scholar Athletic Team Champion Award by the NYSPHSAA

Resolution J5350

Irvington High School Boys Soccer Team on receiving the 2015 Fall Scholar Athletic Team Champion Award by the NYSPHSAA

Resolution J5351

Irvington High School Girls Cross Country Team on receiving the 2015 Fall Scholar Athletic Team Champion Award by the NYSPHSAA
