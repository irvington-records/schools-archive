Meeting: Jan 12, 2016 - Regular Meeting
Date: 2016-01-12
Category: 12. Business Office Matters
Subject: 12.4 Acceptance of Innovation Fund Grant Awards
Type: Action (Consent)

1. Student Art Initiative Murals Grant – Collaboration of Art Club members to create murals representing literary imagery and quotes; submitted by Sydney Cuff, Julia Herceg, Rebecca Mesonjnik and Sophia Vatakis; Grant Award: $900.

	2. Festival of One-Act Plays – The Theater Troupe will hold a festival of one-act plays representing both published and student-written plays; submitted by Clara Montgomery and the Theater Troupe; Grant Award: $4,200.

	3. Sandy Hook Promise – Provide timely education and awareness for students to prevent gun violence in school and our community; submitted by Daniel DeMatteo; Grant Award: $1,000.

	4. Environmental Education and Clean-Up – Educate students and the community on the importance of preserving local nature and promote this idea through specific clean-up days at Halsey Pond; submitted by Julia Sabatino; Grant Award: $626.

	5. Geotagging Environmental Project - Set up a Quick Response smart tag system at a local one-mile hiking tree trail so that hikers can access tree information on their smartphone or tablet; submitted by Sydney Cuff; Grant Award: $1,162.

	6. Environmental Club Marker Recycling – Collection program for recycling used Crayola markers; submitted by Cleo Davidowitz and the Environmental Club; Grant Award: $741.

	7. Youth Gender Equality Conference – Create a larger community for gender equality groups and clubs in area high schools; submitted by Zachary Gallin; Grant Award: $1,500.

	8. Robotics Club – Provide robotics program that educates, offers support and supplies tools and materials; submitted by Mikhail Dorokhov and Justin Tunis; Grant Award: $1,799.
