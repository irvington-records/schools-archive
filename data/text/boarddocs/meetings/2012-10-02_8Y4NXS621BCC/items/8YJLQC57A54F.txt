Meeting: Oct 02, 2012 - Regular Meeting - 7:30 p.m.*
Date: 2012-10-02
Category: 10. Personnel Matters
Subject: 10.11 Appointment of Main Street School Interim Principal
Type: Action (Consent)

BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District herewith rescinds the July 10, 2012 resolution appointing Eileen Casey as the Interim Principal of the Main Street School effective August 20, 2012 through June 30, 2013.

	WHEREAS, the Board of Education is desirous of employing a retired administrator to serve in the capacity of Interim Principal of the Main Street School for the period of August 20, 2012 through June 30, 2013; and

	WHEREAS, the employment of retired individuals is governed by Sections 211 and 212 of the New York State Retirement and Social Security Law; and

	WHEREAS, pursuant to said provisions of law, the Board has been granted a waiver from the New York State Department of Education permitting it to employ said retired administrator for the 2012-2013 school year.

	NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District hereby appoints Eileen Casey as the Interim Principal of the Main Street School, effective August 20, 2012 through June 30, 2013; and

	BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that Ms. Casey shall be compensated at the rate of $135,000 for the term of her appointment without any fringe benefits, except those required by law; and

	BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that said appointment is subject to the execution of a written agreement waiving the acquisition of tenure and seniority rights under the Education Law, as approved by District counsel.
