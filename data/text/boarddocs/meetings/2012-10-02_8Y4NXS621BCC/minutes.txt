	5. Board President's Report

		Discussion: 5.1 Discussion of Athletic Fields

		Mr. Grados reported that a districtwide mailing was sent today to all residents in the Irvington School District regarding the  discovery of what appears to be construction debris on East Field, and the steps that were taken immediately upon discovery, as well as the current status. Mr. Grados noted that it is an important fact that the debris found on East Field  is not uncommon for this area.  Mr. Grados reported that Dr. Harrison met with all school officials; Phil Whitney and Bob Grados spoke personally with Fieldpoint residents whose property is adjacent to the District's property, as well as the mayor, representatives of AYSO, the PTSA Executive Board and the media. The debris was found when perc tests were being conducted on East Field as part of the Board's pursuing a possible referendum to upgrade the District's athletic fields. Mr. Grados noted that the Board will continue to keep the community informed about the status of East Field but as of now, the community has the same information as the Board.  Mr. Grados stated that the Board and Administration's first priority is the safety of children. The Department of Environmental Conservation has been approached and a meeting is scheduled for October 11 with DEC and District representatives. The Board will report back to the community as the path becomes clearer after meeting with the DEC what next steps are needed to rectify the situation on East Field.

		Board members were in agreement that any project for Meszaros Field is on hold and that the priority is to address whatever needs to be done to East Field to make it safe. But noted once DEC advises the District of needed remediation to East Field and its associated costs, the Board will again address the needs of Meszaros Field. Mr. McCann suggested that while DEC is conducting their own review of East Field, that LAN can continue to cost out options that were previously requested. The Board agreed that it would not be possible to have a bond vote in December but will report back to the public on a possible new timeline.
