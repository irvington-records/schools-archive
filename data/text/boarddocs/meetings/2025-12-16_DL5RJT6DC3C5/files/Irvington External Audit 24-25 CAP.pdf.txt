                              Irvington Union Free School District

      Corrective Action Plan for Audited Financial Statements Year Ended June 30, 2025

For each recommendation included in the audit report, the following is our corrective action(s)
taken or proposed.



941 Filing Recommendation: We recommend that the District monitor payroll tax liabilities to
ensure compliance with all applicable tax deposit deadlines.

Implementation Plan: The Payroll Clerk will receive training by the Treasurer on payroll tax
liability deadlines to ensure compliance with tax deposit deadlines. The Treasurer will review
the payroll tax submission on the IRS website prior to submission by the payroll clerk to ensure
accuracy and timeliness.

Implementation Date: October 2025



Extra-classroom – Timeliness of Deposit Recommendation: We recommend that the client
properly documents when cash is received to ensure deposits are made timely.

Implementation Plan: The Central Treasurer will reinforce to all Student Club Advisors, during
the annual fall meetings with the advisors and throughout the year, the importance of sending
all cash deposits to the Business Office as soon as it is received to ensure the cash gets to the
bank timely. Additionally, the Central Treasurer will remind all clubs to use the the proper extra
classroom receipt forms and not utilize prior year and outdated forms. The District also
emphasizes the use of MySchoolBucks for collecting payments to minimize the need for cash
collections/deposits.

Implementation Date: December 2025
