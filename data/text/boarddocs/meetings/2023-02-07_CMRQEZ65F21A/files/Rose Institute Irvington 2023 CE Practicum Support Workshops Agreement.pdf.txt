               ROSE INSTITUTE FOR LEARNING AND LITERACY

                                  and
                 IRVINGTON UNION FREESCHOOL DISTRICT

       ORTON-GILLINGHAM CLASSROOM EDUCATOR PRACTICUM
                SUPPORT WORKSHOPS AGREEMENT


This Agreement made as of January 20, 2023 by and between Irvington Union Free School
District, 6 Dows Lane, Irvington, New York 10533 and Manhattanville College, School of
Education (“COLLEGE”), 2900 Purchase Street, Purchase, New York 10577, Federal
Identification Number 13-1740469.

The organizations wish to serve students, teachers, and schools by furthering the preparation of
teachers to effectively develop literacy and promote academic achievement.

A. Description of Services: Manhattanville College

         The ROSE INSTITUTE FOR LEARNING AND LITERACY will offer at IRVINGTON,
         Professional development support for teachers participating in the Orton-Gillingham
         Classroom Educator practicum.

         The first session will take place on January 31, 2023. Two additional half-day sessions
         will be scheduled during March and May.

         Irvington and the college will mutually explore the desirability and feasibility of further
         coursework.

         IRVINGTON will pay COLLEGE $1800 for each full day of support and $900 for each
         half-day. Should the college and the school agree to continue to offer workshops or
         courses for professional development and/or official graduate credit in the future, the fee
         will be re-evaluated by the College.

         IRVINGTON will be invoiced by The Rose Institute for Learning and Literacy and
         payment will be sent to the Institute.

 B. Description of Services— IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT

      IRVINGTON will recruit participants and will assist with registrations and, as may be
      requested by the College, verification of registrants’ eligibility.

C. Term and Termination

   a) This Agreement will become effective on its execution and will continue in full force and
      effect until terminated. The initial term of the Agreement will be from January 20, 2023 to
      December 30, 2023. The program will be evaluated and may be modified accordingly and
      continued or discontinued thereafter.

   b) Either party, upon thirty (30) days written notice, can effect termination of this Agreement
      without cause. Upon termination, neither party shall have liability to the other party, except
      as otherwise set forth herein. Should the program be terminated once the course has begun,
      officially registered students may continue to complete the course on campus or other
      active college location where the course may be available.

D. Confidentiality of Records

      Any records, reports or other documents provided by IRVINGTON or COLLEGE shall
      remain the property of IRVINGTON or the COLLEGE, as applicable. Any records, reports
      or other documents provided in confidence by either party shall be kept confidential in
      accordance with applicable laws, rules and regulations. This includes, but is not limited
      to, confidentiality of records, evaluations, critiques, case files, and materials.

      IRVINGTON acknowledges that certain information about Manhattanville’s students
      may be contained in records maintained by the IRVINGTON and that this information
      can be confidential by reason of the Family and Educational Rights and Privacy Act of
      1974 (20 U.S.C. 1232g) and related college policies unless valid consent is obtained from
      the students or their legal guardian. Both parties agree to protect these records in
      accordance with FERPA and College policy. To the extent permitted by law, nothing
      contained herein shall be construed as precluding either party from releasing such
      information to the other so that each can perform its respective responsibilities.


E. Independent Contractors

      Both parties agree that no MANHATTANVILLE employee is an employee of
      IRVINGTON and that no IRVINGTON employee is an employee of
      MANHATTANVILLE. Both parties are independent contractors to each other’s
      compliance obligations.

H. Fingerprinting

      Successful fingerprint clearance by the New York State Education Department for all
      individuals providing tutoring for students in the Irvington Union Free School District will
      be obtained.
