2018-19 IUFSD District Calendar


       Irvington Union Free School District
                  March 6, 2018

          Calendar Development Complexities
 Focus on maximizing learning
 Must accommodate SED requirements
  ◦ 184 days (inclusive of up to 4 Superintendent Conference Days)
  ◦ Testing calendar
 Align with contractual obligations
 Federal and religious holidays
 Consistency with BOCES and neighboring districts
 Provides for 4 snow days

                       Calendar Details
 Labor Day: September 3
 First Student Day: September 4
 SED Established Rating Day: June 26
 December Break: December 24-Janaury 1
 Winter Break: February 18-22
 Spring Break: April 15-22
 Memorial Day: May 24-May 28 (Fri-Tues)
 Last Student Day: June 25
 Professional Development:
   Conference days: Aug 29 &30, Oct 9, & Mar 15
   New Model: 1 hour early release two Wednesdays each month
     6 hours of additional professional learning time

                                Enhancements
 Addition to Superintendent Conference Days:
   2 – 1 hour early release days per month October through May
   First and third Wednesdays
   Many other districts provide additional professional time, for
    example:
      Ardsley: 1 hour weekly
      Dobbs Ferry: 1 hour weekly & 1 early release day every 6 weeks
      Hastings: 1 hour monthly & three early release days

 Increases opportunity for additional access to faculty & staff:
   Professional learning
   Curriculum development
   Grade-level and cross-grade level planning
