Commitment to Safety
We are committed to providing the safest school environments possible.

 ● Over recent years, the district has taken steps to enhance security
 ● The District is implementing near-term enhancements
 ● Outlining longer-range, major enhancements



                What do we do now? What do we plan to do?

Critical Partnerships
The District maintains a close partnership with Irvington Police Department

 ●   Youth officers
 ●   DARE Program
 ●   Participation in training and drills
 ●   Pedestrian safety discussions - District-wide
 ●   Members of District Safety Committee
 ●   CAB/iASK Board
 ●   Regular physical presence in schools
 ●   Consistent communication with administration

Supporting Social and Emotional Needs
 The District has a variety of resources and programs to support students:

  ●   6-12 guidance counselors
  ●   New proposal: K-5 guidance counselor
  ●   K-12 social worker
  ●   School psychologists
  ●   Meditation programs 6-12
  ●   Mindfulness and Habits of Mind focused learning
  ●   IMS/IHS Character Building Facilitators (CBF)
  ●   IHS Peer Leadership

Current Practices
In an effort to provide for safe learning environments, IUFSD:

 ●   Has comprehensive emergency plans that are developed with a security consultant
      ○   Plans reviewed annually with security consultant and IPD
 ●   Regular emergency drills are facilitated in each school
      ○   Lock down, lock out, shelter in place, etc.
      ○   Administrators participate in tabletop drills - February 1, 2018 with IPD
 ●   5 security guards
      ○   One per school and one roving guard
 ●   All staff members wear ID badges
 ●   Buzzer systems at Dows Lane and Main Street

Importance of the Emergency Plans
●   Comprehensive, yet flexible
●   Developed with experts
●   Practiced regularly
●   Importance of confidentiality

Near-term Enhancements
In the coming days and weeks, the District will:

 ●   Increased police patrols and presence at each school
 ●   Emergency classroom door security measures/resources
 ●   Additional campus security guard
 ●   Provided additional learning for all staff in partnership with the IPD
 ●   Implement enhanced guest protocols
 ●   Complete security audit
 ●   Gather feedback to inform next steps

Large-Scale Enhancements
As discussed in recent BOE meetings, the District is planning a capital bond
project. This project will be grounded in school security and may include:

 ●   Enclosure of the MS/HS Campus
 ●   Increased entrance security at each school
 ●   Installation of extensive video surveillance at all schools
 ●   Swipe card access system
 ●   Advanced guest registration system
 ●   New phone system
 ●   Physical features, bollards, etc.

Large-Scale Enhancements
Large-scale enhancements largely require:

 ●   Thoughtful planning and design
 ●   Engagement with security experts, engineers, and architects
 ●   Funding - Capital Bond Referendum/public vote
 ●   NYSED approval
 ●   Bidding process
 ●   Construction

Next Steps
It is critical for the District to be thoughtful and make wise investments to
enhance security:

 ●   Security audit
 ●   Take steps to implement enhancements now
 ●   Community engagement related to large-scale improvements
