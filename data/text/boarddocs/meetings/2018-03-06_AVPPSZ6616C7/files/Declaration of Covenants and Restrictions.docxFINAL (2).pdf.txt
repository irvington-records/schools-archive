                DECLARATION OF COVENANTS AND RESTRICTIONS


      THIS COVENANT is made the ____ day of _________________ 2018, by the Irvington
Union Free School District (the “District”), having its District Office at 6 Dows Lane, Irvington,
New York 10533.

        WHEREAS, the District is the owner of certain property referred to as the East Field area
of the Irvington High School and Middle School Campus, located at 40 North Broadway,
Irvington, New York 10533, being more particular described in Appendix “A” (the “East
Field”); and

        WHEREAS, solid waste construction and demolition debris has been identified
comingled within fill material beneath the East Field, which is believed to constitute an “old
landfill”; which said landfill was in operation sometime after 1965 and ended sometime after
1969 but before 1974; that records of the facility have been filed with the NYSDOH contained in
the District’s permit application for installation of a storm water drainage system within the
footprint of the “old landfill”; and that is not subject to permitting obligations of 6 NYCRR Part
360; and

         WHEREAS, the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation (the
“Department”) has approved a Landfill Disturbance Plan (the “LDP”) to be implemented in
connection with construction of certain drainage facility improvements in and around the East
Field, in order to mitigate threats to the environment presented by such construction and
demolition debris fill material and such plan requires that the East Field be subject to certain
restrictive covenants;

        NOW THEREFORE, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School
District, acting for the Irvington Union Free School District and its successors and/or assigns,
covenants that:

       First, the East Field subject to this Declaration of Covenants and Restrictions is as shown
on the map attached to this declaration as Appendix “A” and made a part hereof.

       Second, unless prior written approval by the Department or, if the Department shall no
longer exist, any New York State agency or agencies subsequently created to protect the
environment of the State and health of the State’s citizens, hereinafter referred to as “the
Relevant Agency,” is first obtained, there shall be no construction, excavation, use or occupancy
of the East Field which threatens the integrity of the top two feet of soil or which results in
unacceptable human exposure to underlying fill material.

       Third, the owner of the East Field shall not excavate, disturb, remove, or otherwise
conduct construction activities that will entail contact with fill material at the East Fields deeper
than two feet, unless in each instance the owner first obtains a written waiver of such prohibition
from the Department or Relevant Agency.

         Fourth, the owner of Fields shall prohibit the Fields from ever being used for purposes
 other than restricted residential (including athletic and recreational field uses), commercial or
 industrial uses, as those terms are used in Part 375 of Title 6 of the New York Code, Rules and
 Regulations, and from farming or vegetable gardening (except for raised box vegetable
 gardening upon approval of the Department or Relevant Agency), unless an express written
 waiver of such prohibition is first obtained from the Department or Relevant Agency.

          Fifth, the owner of the Fields shall provide a periodic certification, prepared and
 submitted by a professional engineer or environmental professional acceptable to the Department
 or Relevant Agency, which will certify that the institutional and engineering controls put in place
 remain in place since the previous inspection, comply with the SMP, and have not been impaired
 in their function or purpose.

         Sixth, the owner of the Fields shall continue in full force all controls set forth herein,
 unless the owner first obtains permission to discontinue such controls from the Department or
 Relevant Agency.

        Seventh, this Declaration shall modify the deed of record on said property and is and
 shall be deemed a covenant that shall run with the land and shall be binding upon all future
 owners of the East Field, and shall provide that the owner and its successors and assigns consent
 to enforcement by the Department or Relevant Agency of the prohibitions and restrictions set
 forth herein, and hereby covenant not to contest the authority of the Department or Relevant
 Agency to seek enforcement.

         Eighth, any deed or conveyance of the Fields, or any portion of the Fields, shall recite,
 unless the Department or Relevant Agency has consented to the termination of such covenants
 and restrictions, that said conveyance is subject to this Declaration of Covenants and
 Restrictions.
