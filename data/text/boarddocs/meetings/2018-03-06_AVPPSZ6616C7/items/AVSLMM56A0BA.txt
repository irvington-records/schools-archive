WHEREAS, on or about June 9, 2014, LAN Engineering, Planning, Architecture, Surveying, LLP (LAN) the environmental and planning consultants for the Irvington Union Free School District (the “District”) petitioned the New York State Department of Environmental Conservation for coverage under the Part 360 Landfill Disturbance Exemption on behalf of the Irvington Union Free School District in accordance with 6 NYCRR 360 1.7 (b) (9) in regards to the District’s project involving the installation of a storm water drainage system partially within the footprint of an inactive landfill which underlies the East Field area of the Irvington High School and Middle School Campus located at 40 North Broadway in the Village of Irvington, Westchester County and

WHEREAS The New York State Department of Environmental Conservation by letter dated July 15, 2014 determined that the activities and site improvements described in the Fill Encroachment Summary dated 5/6/14, revised 7/3/14, prepared by Richard A. Wostbrock; Field Improvement Plan, dated 6/14, revised 7/3/14, prepared by Richard A. Wostbrock; and Construction Details dated 6/14, revised 7/3/14, prepared by Richard A. Wostbrock made the Irvington Union Free School District project as aforementioned eligible for exemption from 6NYCRR Part 360 Solid Waste Management Facilities Regulations pursuant to the provisions of 6 NYCRR 360 7.1 (b) (9) subject to certain terms and conditions and

WHEREAS there is a condition requiring that the property deed shall be modified to include a provision indicating the period of time during which the property has been used as a landfill, describing the wastes contained within and noting that records of the facility have been

filed with the Department. The deed shall also reference a map which shall be filed with the

County Clerk and which shall clearly indicate the limits of the landfill area within the property

boundary. The deed must also indicate the requirement to maintain the required 2 foot thickness

of soil cover material; and

WHEREAS, Declaration of Covenants and Restrictions, detailing all of the aforementioned conditions is to be signed and recorded with the County Clerk on the property at

40 North Broadway in the Village of Irvington, Westchester County wherein it modifies the

deed of record for 40 North Broadway in the Village of Irvington, Westchester County

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education (“Board”)

hereby adopt a resolution to approve the Declaration of Covenants and Restrictions as attached

hereto to be filed with the Westchester County Clerk’s Office on the property located at 40 North

Avenue Irvington, New York; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Board of Education hereby authorizes the

President of the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District to sign said

Declarations of Covenants and Restrictions and all transfer documents necessary for the

recording of same with the Westchester County Clerk’s Office.

_______

*

As part of the East Field capital project, the NYS Department of Environmental Conservation requires the property deed to be modified to include the period of time that the property was previously used as a landfill.  The resolution permits the Board president to sign the attached Declaration of Covenants and Restrictions and all associated documents as reviewed and prepared by our legal advisors.
