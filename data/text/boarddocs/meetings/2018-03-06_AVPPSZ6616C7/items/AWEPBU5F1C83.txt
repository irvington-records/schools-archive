Meeting: Mar 06, 2018 - Regular Meeting
Date: 2018-03-06
Category: 10. Business Office Matters
Subject: 10.3 Contract for Architectural and Engineering Services for District-Wide Pre-Referendum Capital Projects
Type: Action (Consent)

The District recognizes the services of an architect will be needed as we begin our capital project planning,  The District distributed a Request for Proposal (RFP) for Architect Services and received proposals from four interested firms.  The Building and Grounds Committee of the Board subsequently met to review the proposals and interview selected firms.  The selected firm is H2M Architects & Engineers, based out of White Plains.  This firm has extensive school experience in our geographic region and comes with strong recommendations.
