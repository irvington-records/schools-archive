                          The Model

 The philosophical underpinnings of the Consortium’s model center
  on systems thinking: that is, it focuses on the degree to which a
  district is consistently aligned with the expectations of the
  assessment model’s eight indicators throughout the system.
 Further, the Consortium is devoted to the analysis of multiple forms
  of student assessment data to advance instructional practices and
  programs.
 Over the course of three days in the Irvington Middle and High
  Schools, the visiting team examined evidence provided by the
  district in the form of written documentation, videos, student work,
  and we led interviews with district office personnel, building level
  administrators, teachers, students, parents, and Board of Education
  members.

                 Essential Questions


 To what extent is the World Language 6-12 curriculum
  effectively sequenced and articulated within and across
  languages?

 To what extent does the World Language program balance
  age appropriate skill development, the acquisition of content,
  and higher level thinking?

 To what extent does our program support the development of
  a set of skills and/or dispositions (risk taker, effective
  communicator, and flexible thinker) that encourages students
  to use the language in advanced or authentic experiences?

                     Indicator #1
            Performance Based-Assessments
                   Commendations:

 The World Language Department has a solid foundation in place that
  currently provides students with opportunities to integrate higher order
  thinking skills in specific tasks and activities in order to enhance
  student learning in the process of building capacity to create
  performance based-assessments.

 Teachers at all levels, 6-12, provide varied curriculum materials that
  offer students creative, purposeful, and long-term performance-based
  tasks that focus on transfer and application of knowledge across
  disciplines.

 Teachers have initiated the process of developing and implementing
  rubrics that describe expectations and guidelines for performance based
  tasks.

                       Indicator #1
              Performance Based Assessments
                    Recommendations:

 In order to enhance student learning through PBA it is essential that the
  world language department agree upon and outline the structure of a
  PBA that is interdisciplinary and includes student demonstration of
  language learning.

 Consider developing a collaborative process that involves all languages
  in analyzing current performance tasks to determine their link to
  authentic assessments. The LASW (Looking at Student Work) protocol
  is a method that guides teachers through the process of identifying best
  practice and giving and receiving feedback.

 Collaborate to review and refine the existing rubrics in all languages
  with the intent of creating a department rubric consistent across
  languages and the language modes (interpretive, interpersonal and
  presentational). Additionally, Begin to gather student exemplars of
  assessments, tasks, assignments that can be used in conjunction with the
  rubrics for benchmarking.

               Indicator #2
Student Metacognition in the Learning Process
              Commendations:

 There are examples of opportunities for students to self-reflect
  within specific language levels. (Spanish 2 midyear reflection and
  student goal setting)

 Teachers use various strategies to elicit metacognitive thinking about
  their own language acquisition. (i.e. French Middle School ‘Can Do’
  statements)

 Teachers create opportunities for students to reflect on how their
  skills can inform their own learning. Examples include: The Family
  Tree Project Reflection asking how the skills can be used in other
  topics; creating a list of cognates in the classroom; and searching for
  cognates at home, student choice in readings, assessments, and
  exercises, Spanish Salsa articles).

                 Indicator #2
  Student Metacognition in the Learning Process
              Recommendations:

 Consider a collaborative process to define and integrate
  metacognition into the rigor of language acquisition. An agreed
  upon definition and what it would look like in the classroom is
  essential throughout the department.
 Continue to implement the self-assessment component within
  projects that challenge students to understand how to think about
  their own thinking. In addition to what a student learns in terms of
  skills and proficiency, the chance to look closely at one’s own
  learning process will deepen student growth and knowledge.
 While students do reflect on their reactions to characters and
  scenarios, we recommend reviewing and revising these opportunities
  by expanding expectations to include reflections of their personal
  learning process.

                        Indicator #3
                  Student Performance Data
                      Commendations:

 The district’s students perform well on the Checkpoint A and B
  assessments.

 Teachers monitor student progress on Student Learning Objectives
  through pretest/midterm analyses.

 Latin students participate in national competitions and exams.

                      Indicator #3
                Student Performance Data
                   Recommendations:

 Consider engaging the world language teachers in identifying the
  questions that are important to them (and to the system), and
  generating data that would be helpful in answering those questions.

 Consider doing a crosswalk of the state assessment data and data
  generated internally through performance based assessments and
  SLO assessments: do the data from both sources tell the same story
  about what students know and can do?

 Consider bringing the department together around the analysis of
  data at least once or twice per year, with an eye toward generating
  potential changes in curriculum and instruction as a result of the
  analysis.

                       Indicator #4
                Curriculum and Instruction
                     Commendations:

 There is a wide range of evidence of lesson plans and activities,
  tasks and assessments that represent the current instruction in all
  languages.

 There is a beginning of a conceptual understanding of UBD
  designed unit plans presented as representative artifacts of
  curriculum.

 There is a genuine desire on the part of teachers to incorporate
  cross-curricular connections.

                       Indicator #4
                Curriculum and Instruction
                   Recommendations:

 Consider revamping unit plans with differentiated activities, tasks,
  and or assessments to align to local, state or national benchmarks
  with existing student exemplars.

 Consider adopting a systematic process of collecting, sharing and
  comparing data across languages and levels in order to inform
  instruction.

 Continue developing Rubicon Atlas Curriculum maps and connect
  more consistently to the ACTFL National Standards and the
  Common Core.

                        Indicator #5
                    Professional Learning
                      Commendations:

 The district incorporates a systematic professional learning process
  that is based on current student and teacher needs related to district
  goals.

 Individual educators are involved in professional learning that is
  embedded, collaborative and reflective, based on teacher and student
  needs related to district goals.

 There is an eagerness that is shared amongst members of the World
  Languages department to continue their professional growth by
  means of innovative and effective resources.

                        Indicator #5
                    Professional Learning
                     Recommendations:

 Consider developing a systemic, 6-12 professional learning process
  that is based on teacher/student providing time and resources to
  ensure that professional learning is language specific, collaborative,
  embedded and reflective.

 Consider developing a professional learning plan for all language
  teachers that uses supervision and process to align professional
  learning goals and instructional strategies to promote optimal
  student results.

 Consider analyzing student results based on the educators’
  participation in systemic, 6-12, language oriented, professional
  learning. There should be a direct correlation between the
  professional learning and the students’ results.

                     Indicator #6
         Equitable Support for Student Needs
                  Commendations:

 The department has created a transparent system for placing students
  into honors classes. There is room in the system for flexibility through
  the acceptance of overrides.

 The number of students exempted from foreign language has
  significantly declined over the past ten years.

 Members of the professional teaching staff are regularly available to
  provide additional support to students who request it. (Independent
  study opportunities).

                     Indicator #6
         Equitable Support for Student Needs
                 Recommendations:

 Consider developing a formal system for generating and monitoring
  student data. (Who are the students entering honors? Why have
  honors? Who are the students who do not continue with studying a
  language?)

 Consider augmenting the use of formative data to inform
  instructional decisions.

 Consider increasing the use of differentiated instruction and
  student-centered instructional methodologies within the classroom
  to meet the needs of the diverse range of learners. Provide related
  professional development opportunities to support the use of
  differentiation

                   Indicator # 7
    Shared Vision and Environment for Change
                Commendations:

 In 2008, the world language department developed a shared vision
  statement that outlines shared goals both horizontally and vertically
  for all language students.

 Students in world languages demonstrate confidence, enthusiasm,
  and dedication to learning and using world languages throughout
  middle/high school and beyond.

 Many teachers in the world language department have created
  classroom learning environments that encourage students to take
  risks and take ownership of the target languages.

                   Indicator # 7
    Shared Vision and Environment for Change
                Recommendations:

 Collaborate to review and refine the mission statement of the world
  language department to reflect district goals.

 Create a collaborative process that allows and encourages teachers
  to think about the vision for the teaching of world languages grades
  6-12.

 Consider the use and implementation of progressive technology to
  promote the teaching of world languages.

                      Indicator #8
             Parent and Community Support
                    Commendations:

 The district has in place practices that include parents in the larger
  conversation about world language choices, the selection process for
  courses and levels and course expectations.

 There is a wide range of opportunities for parents to be involved in all
  facets of the world language program: Ed Line, e-mail, newsletters,
  class letters, Open House, information about TV channels and
  subscriptions and specific language events (French Week).

 The Board of Education and the broader community demonstrate
  support of the language program. The Irvington Education Foundation
  raises funds to support enrichment programs.

                      Indicator #8
             Parent and Community Support
                   Recommendations:

 Continue to find ways to leverage local organizations to bolster
  authentic opportunities for students; such as trips, guest speakers, study
  abroad programs.

 Consider creating a process to collect data about parent awareness of
  the curriculum and course offerings.

 Continue to review and refine the potential to partner with the quad
  village to expand opportunities for teachers and administrators
  (technology integration, exchange programs, local events).

 Goal #7 – Technology Framework -

             Technology Goal #7:
        Global Education and Citizenship

 Teachers and students will use technology to promote engagement
  in the global society. Global citizenship will be an underlying focus
  throughout all curriculum areas in an effort to prepare students for
  engaged living in a culturally diverse and rapidly changing world.

Purpose:
 Enabling young people to participate in shaping a better-shared
  future for the world is at the heart of global education. An emphasis
  on the unity and interdependence of human society can support the
  development of a sense of self and appreciation of cultural
  diversity. Technology can facilitate the opportunities for teachers
  and students to engage across continents and with people of all
  cultures and backgrounds.
