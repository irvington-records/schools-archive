Audit Committee of the Irvington Union Free School District (Tuesday, October 10, 2017)

Members present

Michael Hanna, Maria Kashkin, Catherine Palmieri, Deborah Hargraves, David Graeber, Maura Gedid, Brian Friedman

Also present

Kristopher Harrison, Carol Stein, Jaime Cote

1. Call to Order

Michael Hanna called the meeting to order at 6:30pm.

2. Motion to Enter into Executive Session

Action:  2.1 Anticipated Executive Session

Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education enter into Executive Session with a representative from the Bonadio Group, the District's auditor, for the purpose of discussing the draft of the District's 2016-17 Audit Report.

Motion by Maria Kashkin, second by Maura Gedid.

Final Resolution: Motion Carries

Yes: Michael Hanna, Maria Kashkin, Catherine Palmieri, Deborah Hargraves, David Graeber, Maura Gedid, Brian Friedman

3. Adjournment

Action:  3.1 Motion

Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education adjourn the Executive Session and reconvene and adjourn the Audit Committee.

Motion by David Graeber, second by Maria Kashkin.

Final Resolution: Motion Carries

Yes: Michael Hanna, Maria Kashkin, Catherine Palmieri, Deborah Hargraves, David Graeber, Maura Gedid, Brian Friedman
