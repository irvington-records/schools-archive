Subject: 10.3 Contract - COVID 19 Testing
Type: Action (Consent)
Attachments: COVID TESTING AGREEMENT Camp Mohawk final.pdf

This agreement will allow the District to conduct the random testing required to keep schools open should the District be in a yellow, orange or red zone as designated by the State of NY.  Camp Mohawk will conduct a drive through testing process, provide health care workers to administer the test and perform the necessary setups and materials.  At this point, it is expected that the County Health Department would provide the test kits.  We would like to acknowledge that Camp Mohawk will be graciously providing this at cost with no additional markup to the District.
