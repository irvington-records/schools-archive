5. Presentation/Discussion

Presentation:  5.1 Superintendent's Proposed Budget Presentation

Dr. Harrison presented the modified proposed budget.  Dr. Harrison stated that this presentations will put the final considerations before the Board and the Board will be asked to adopt the budget tonight.  After the presentation the Board engaged in further discussions with the Administration.

Presentation:  5.2 Presentation of Proposed 2019-20 School Calendar

Dr. Harrison presented modifications to the proposed school calendar.  Discussion took place after the presentation between the Board of Education and Dr. Harrison, particularly regarding the PLRDs.

6. 2019-20 Budget Adoption

Action:  6.1 Adoption of 2019-20 Budget Adoption

Resolved, that the Irvington Union Free School District's 2019-20 Proposed Budget be set at $62,953,554.00 and that this proposed budget be voted on by the community at the Annual Meeting on May 21, 2019 between the hours of 7:00 am and 9:00 pm at the Irvington Main Street School, 101 Main Street, Irvington, New York 10533.

Motion by David Graeber, second by Maura Gedid.

Final Resolution: Motion Carries

Yes: Michael Hanna, Maria Kashkin, Deborah Hargraves, David Graeber, Maura Gedid, Brian Friedman, Beth Propper (Vote by Roll Call)

Action: 6.2 Approval of 2019-20 Property Tax Report Card

Resolved, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District adopts the 2019-20 Real Property Tax Report Card for the Irvington Union Free School District appended to the budget, which shall be filed with the State Education Department within 24 hours of the adoption of the budget, no later than 24 days before the annual election and budget vote.
