	4. Presentations/Discussions

		Discussion:  4.1 Follow-up to Discussion on Conducting a Space Utilization Study

		Mr. Whitney reported that the purpose of conducting a space utilization study is to determine what the District's needs will be going forward with the possibility of closing Main Street School. Ms. Miller reported that she received two proposals: Dr. Paul Seversky, a retired Superintendent, has conducted many such studies. The second proposal from Legacy Group,  work with architects as well as on capital projects. Dr. Seversky's focus is maximize existing buildings whereas Legacy Group appears to be geared towards capital projects, conducting walkthroughs of buildings to determine conditions. In addition, the Legacy Group would have a broader spectrum creating a steering committee of community members. Dr. Seversky would offer such a focus group as an additional cost option. Dr. Seversky's cost to conduct the study is $13,950 ($17,975 with a focus group) and Legacy Group is $25,000.

		Ms. Miller reviewed how both would conduct enrollment projections for 10 years. Both would present the information to the public at the conclusion of the study.

		In Ms. Miller's opinion, she believes that Dr. Seversky could do the job at half the cost but is looking for guidance from the Board.

		Mrs. Robyn Kerner agreed with Ms. Miller that cost is a factor and initially Dr. Seversky's proposal is thorough and would provide the the information the Board is seeking. Both Maria Kashkin and Phil Whitney agreed and asked Ms. Miller to finalize the contract with Dr. Seversky.

		Discussion, Information:  4.2 Report of Current Condition of Meszaros Field

		Mr. Whitney noted that this discussion is a  first step in a long process, noting that the fields are difficult to maintain and there are inherent difficulties such as the drainage. He noted that Mr. Grados had asked that the fields be a priority this year.

		Mr. Gary Knowles, Director of Facilities, presented photos of current conditions of Meszaros Field and noted that they are looking better than they have in a long time. He outlined the field usage and noted that the District has three football teams, 4 soccer teams and three lacrosse teams using the field. AYSO and recreation football games are played on Meszaros Field. In addition, both the middle school and the high school use field for physical education classes.

		Mr. Knowles reviewed previous years' maintenance plans vs. current maintenance, i.e core aerating, verti-drain aerating and over seeding. The issues are poor drainage and lack of down time due to the overuse of the fields. Perc tests have been taken to determine how long it takes water to seep into the field. There is also a layer of 6-10 inches of clay which also prohibits drainage. He noted that it has been recommended that the track be resurfaced and if it is not done soon, we would be looking at replacing the track at higher costs.

		With regards to East Field - there are stones that are surfacing throughout East Field. All three football teams use for practice as well as an adult weekend team and club soccer teams utilize the field. The only maintenance currently being done on East Field is moving and removal of debris. The field needs grading, irrigation and top dressing and re-seeding.

		Mrs. Kerner suggests that we pull the data from past years to review and asked if the architect noted any changes in drainage due to the additional construction that has taken place since the last study of drainage issues.

		Dr. Harrison reviewed what a schedule would look like if the Board decides to present a bond referendum. Working backwards, the Board would need to engage in dialogue immediately, exploring possible options and engage with the architect to identify proposals and plans, cost estimates for the Board's review, and seek community input. The schedule presented includes a target date of November 27th to conduct a bond vote. The Board would need to make decision as to path to take at the October 2nd meeting. The timeline also included, pending positive vote, that final drawings and specifications would be submitted to the State Education Department (SED) by January 10, 2013 and approval by May. Request for Proposals (RFP's) would be published in June and bids award by June 17th in order to begin working by the first week in July. Dr. Harrison noted that it would require "laser light focus" to begin renovations of fields by June 2013. Dr. Harrison also suggested that the board might look at approving a more modest project such as retrofitting of our current drainage system. Due to new technologies---slip drains. This would be less costly, but that there are no guarantee of effectiveness.

		The Board asked questions regarding SED approval schedules. It was also noted that any work to the drainage issues might not be aidable.

		Dr Harrison noted that only conversations have taken place regarding Meszaros Field. There are a lot of factors to consider in addition to defining scope and engaging the community, such as having to take the field out of service for a period of time which would impact athletics and recreation programs. The Board would have to consider many other issues if it is decided to go with a natural field.

		What is the budgeted amount to maintain the fields this year? This will be provided to the Board Also fall vs rest of year expenditures.

		Mr. McCann asked what the importance of starting work in July? Summer is best time to do work. Mr. McCann said that this should not be a pressure point in making any decisions and asked what are the options as a step 2 to tonight's presentation. There could be 6-7 options and would want rough costs to each option. Would also like to bring in discussion about renovating East Field as well. In response to the starting date, Phil Whitney noted that one reason for the timeline that was presented, would be to have field for 2013-14 season. Robyn Kerner also noted that it is not a good idea to tie in with budget vote so that November is about the latest you could do vote.

		Dr. Harrison noted that another benefit in having large scale construction project conducted during the summer as opposed to having students, visitors, would be a major safety benefit. An appropriate next step would be to collect the report from architect and follow up with the Buildings and Grounds Committee and to expand on tonight's discussion. Mrs. Kerner feels it will be necessary to set up additional board meetings to discuss options, etc. with architect so that the entire board is involved in the discussion. Mrs. Kerner asked if District will continue with previous architect or plans to issue an RFP? It was noted that the same engineer who analyzed the drainage five years ago is doing the current analysis. Jim McCann also requested that a more thorough perc test done in order to fully understand the drainage problems that exist which will instill confidence in report.

		The Board requested that the architect draw up possible options and to include East Field. The Board will also review different time frame options for a field renovation project.
