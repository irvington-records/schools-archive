Dear Town Comptroller:

I, Elaine Cardea, District Clerk of the Irvington Union Free School District, Town of Greenburgh,
County of Westchester, State of New York, do hereby certify that the following budget and tax items
for the 2018/2019 fiscal year, beginning July 1, 2018 and ending June 30, 2019 are true and accurate.
This is an official breakdown of the warrant for use for your collection of taxes.

At its meeting on June 5, 2018 the Board of Education adopted a motion containing the following
resolution:

MOVED, that the voters of the Irvington Union Free School District, by a vote of 689 to 205, adopted
a budget for the school year commencing July 1, 2018 in the amount of $61,348,175; that the sums as
set forth in the forms of budget heretofore submitted to the voter be appropriated; that the taxes in the
necessary amounts be levied therefore; that the Board members be authorized to execute a warrant
therefore, and that the warrant be delivered to the Town authorities.

The major budget categories and their respective amounts are listed below:

General Support                            $ 7,146,346
Instruction                                $ 33,892,145
Transportation                             $ 2,419,477
Community Services                                  0
Undistributed Expenses
 Benefits                    $ 13,624,069
 Debt Service                $ 4,218,138
 Transfers to Other Funds    $     48,000
Total Undistributed Expenses              $17,890,207
Total Expenditures                        $61,348,175
