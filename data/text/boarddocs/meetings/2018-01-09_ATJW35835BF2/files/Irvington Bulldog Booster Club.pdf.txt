What Is The Purpose Of The Irvington Bulldog Booster Club?

The purpose of the Booster club is to coalesce and organize the tremendous community
support for Irvington Bulldog athletics. We are forming an independent 501-c-3 tax exempt
non-profit organization comprised solely of volunteers. We will work with the school
administration, coaches, teachers, students, student athletes and community at large to be
an organization which promotes the development of our Irvington student athletes to be the
best they can be, on and off the playing field. We will seek to raise funds in support of non-
budgetary needs for athletic programs, leadership development for our student athletes and
to foster best possible experiences for all of our students and student athletes.

The Booster Club will look to have a presence through concessions and merchandise sales at
most home athletic events where possible and appropriate. We will look to create additional
revenue through a Booster club membership program and future fundraisers in 2018.

As an organization solely focused on the betterment of our students and student athletes,
we also want to make clear that the Booster club will have no role as an advisory group to
the athletic program or its administration. Matters pertaining to coaches, student athletes’
playing time, budgetary considerations and all other athletic program management
decisions are within the purview of the administration and the booster club will have no say
in these matters.

Who Can Join The Booster Club?

In consultation with the Irvington High school leadership, particularly Principal David Cohen
and Athietic Director Artie McCormack, the club will consist of alumni, parents and the
broader school community. Anyone can support this mission by joining the Booster Club,
donating, and volunteering. We have already received several offers from parents to
volunteer their time at home events, participate in helping to create unique ways to
fundraise for our program. We look forward to support from the broader Irvington
community as we begin our efforts. Please feel free to reach out to any board member if
you are interested in participating in the Booster club in any capacity.

Mission Statement

The Irvington Bulldog Booster Club is dedicated to fostering a positive and spirited
community environment by working to enhance Bulldog pride and to

encourage participation in and attendance at athletic programs of the Irvington School
District. Together with parents, coaches, students, teachers, and administrators, the
Irvington Bulldog Boosters will provide opportunities for our student athletes and coaches
and work to enhance student sportsmanship, leadership and character.

The Bulldog Boosters will seek to organize strategic fundraising to ensure important
financial resources for all sports teams, leadership development, athletic programs and
athletic facilities of the Irvington School District.

The Booster club board will be hosting a meeting on Tuesday, Dec 12 @ 7pm in the
High School theatre to discuss the goals of the Booster Club and to answer any
questions. We look forward to a great turnout and a productive conversation as
we roll out into the Irvington community.

Email us with questions, suggestions or comments at: Irvingtonbbc@qmail.com

Booster Club Spending Protocol

The Irvington Bulldog Booster club is committed to enhancing the student athletic programs of
Irvington high/middle schools. In accordance with our mission and in conjunction with our
independent association with the Irvington High School administration, the Booster club will
strive to provide financial support for our athletic programs, The booster club will only consider
written funding requests which follow the guidelines specified below. No requests will be
considered unless in writing; all spending will be subject to a majority vote of the Booster club
executive committee or the entire board of the Booster club as specified below.

° All requests must be submitted in writing on the request form attached. No verbal, or ad
hoc, request will be considered.

° All requests must be vetted and approved by the Athletic Director prior to consideration
by the Booster club. Approval by the Athletic Director DOES NOT constitute approval
by the booster club, however no requests will be considered by the booster club
UNLESS the athletic director has approved the request

° The Booster club will look to fund requests focused on items outside of the athletic
department budget which are intended to advance the overall athletic program by team
and/or sport. Examples of such items include: safety gear, training equipment,
additional sport specific equipment for practices/games, sport specific senior day
celebrations, end of season sport awards, leadership/sportsmanship education and/or
Sorums

° No requests which sponsor or subsidize players’ personal property (warm ups,
enhancements to uniforms) or entertainment (defraying costs of in-season team dinners,
other entertainment) will be considered by the Booster club for spending approval. **
exceptions may be considered for situations of student family financial hardship

° No requests will be considered to subsidize athletic program staff personnel costs or
compensation- i.e. volunteer coaches

° All expense requests <= $500 will be voted on by the executive committee of the Booster
club- Co-Presidents, Treasurer, Secretary, plus at least one other member of the Booster
club board. A majority (3 of 5) is necessary for an expense to be approved. Any expense
request > $500 must be approved by a majority of the entire Booster club board.
