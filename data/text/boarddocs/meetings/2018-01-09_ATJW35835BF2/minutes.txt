3. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Report:  3.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison reported on the following:

- The amazing job the Facilities Department and Gary Knowles did braving the cold temperatures during the snow clean up of last week's storm.

- The District has already used 3 of 4 snow days built into the calendar. If necessary will use May 25th as a make up day.

- A District-wide survey is being developed in conjuction with K 12 Insight that will collect and analyze feedback on perceptions of school experiences. The goal is to have the survey go out right after the Winter recess and have results back by the end of March.

- Reminded everyone to check the District's Facebook page and other school communications for upcoming District, IEF and PTSA events.

Dr. Kor spoke about Professional Development  2 hour delayed start on January 16th.

Carol Stein reported on the following:

- The Business Office is preparing for the upcoming Child Nutrition Administrative Review by NYSED which is conducted every 3 years.

- Budget preparation is ongoing

- RFP for an Architect Bid closed today. Next step will be for Buildings and Grounds Committe to interview proposers.

- The District will hire a Food Service consultant to help develop the RFP for a Food Service company. The consultant has the knowledge and experience needed to develop an appropriate RFP for the 5 year contract.

Maria Kashkin suggested the principals notify parents that there is only one snow day remaining so the parents won't be surprised.

3.2 Committee Reports

Buildings & Grounds-Catherine Palmieri reported on the 12/18/17 meeting and that the Architect proposals were due today. Now the committee can start to look at the projects.

Policy-Deborah Hargraves reported on the12/22/17 meeting, the committee continues to work through and update policy manuel. They are also working on the Governance handbook with legal counsel to make sure policies and handbook are in agreement.

Communications Committee-Michael Hanna reported on the 12/15/17 meeting. The new District website will be launched in the Spring. The committee is looking to update and change the newsletters that are published to get the community more interested in reading them.

Wellness Committee-Carol Stein reported the 1-4-18 meeting was postponed due the snowstorm and is rescheduled for 1-18-18. At that meeting four new members of the community will be introduced and they will be talking about the upcoming RFP.

Mr. McCormack spoke about combining fund raising resources and surveying the community to find the right people for the positions in the Booster Club. A general booster club will bring about community enthusiam and spirit. Mr. Mascone reported the progress the IBBC Board has made this school year. The IBBC is currently conducting parent outreach. The IBBC has developed guidelines for the coaches to request funds. Spending requests will come from coaches, approved by the Athletic Director and then be sent to the IBBC club for funding.
