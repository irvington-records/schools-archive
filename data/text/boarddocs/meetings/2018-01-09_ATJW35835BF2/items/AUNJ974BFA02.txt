Category: 10. Business Office Matters
Subject: 10.4 Child Nutrition Bid Development
Type: 

Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education enter into a contract between the Irvington Union Free School District and HMB Consultants, in the amount of $12,500 for services to develop specifications for the District's Child Nutrition Program for 2018-19 and authorizes the Superintendent to execute the contract.
