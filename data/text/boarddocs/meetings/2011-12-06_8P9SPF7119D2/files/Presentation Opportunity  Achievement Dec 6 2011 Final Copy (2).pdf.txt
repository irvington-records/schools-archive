Achievement
"We need to move beyond our horse and
buggy mentality in educational instruction and
assessment, and to admit students to college
not just on the basis of their academic skills
with a few other extras thrown in, but rather on
the basis of a student's overall potential to
make a positive difference to the world. (pp. ix–
x)“ Sternberg, R. (2010) College Admissions for the 21st
Century, Harvard Press.
