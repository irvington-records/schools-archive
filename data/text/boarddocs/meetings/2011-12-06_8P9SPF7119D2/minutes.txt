Information:  3.1 News from the

Schools

Phil Whitney provided an update on activities in the schools as well as student and sport team achievements.

Information:  3.2 Superintendent Search

Update

Mr. John Dawson reported that the Board finalized the characteristics for the new Supterintendent. HYA, the Search Consultants' team is now gathering resumes for possible candidates. Mr. Dawson noted that the search is on target with search timeline.  Dr. Roelle noted that the Profile Leadership Report is now on the website.

Information, Presentation:  3.3 School Report Card: Irvington School District Student Achievement - 2010-11 School Year - Supplemental Log - Appendix A

Dr. Kor provided an update on the 2010-11 student achievement based on the NY State Report Card. She noted that this is only one way to look at student achievement as well as looking at how our students are engaged in other activities, including extracurricular activities. Dr. Kor provided an overview of how Irvington is preparing students to live in a global society.

Mr. Palermo spoke to the special education programs and their integration into the classroom to experience the full breadth of curriculum.

Dr. Kor reported that the State often changes the exams, dates, cut points, etc. which makes it difficult to compare data from year to year. Dr. Kor encourages one to compare cohorts, i.e. last year fourth grades and this years fifth graders.

The principals at each building reported on their students' assessment data as well as the various opportunities for their students, both academically and extracurriculur.
