5. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Information: 5.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison provided District updates. First, it was noted that Inclusive Schools Week is held each year during the first full week in December. This event is an important opportunity for educators, students and parents to discuss ways that schools can improve their ability to educate all children. This year's theme is "unite with the community." Dr. Harrison also presented on a holiday project between the Village of Irvington and the Irvington High School Art Department; wherein student artists are repainting wooden soldiers to present a more diverse "troop." Next, it was reported that before the holidays, the District will distribute 4 rapid COVID-19 tests to each student. Following, an announcement was made regarding a technology issue. It was also noted that the District will adopt a new communication platform called ParentSquare, Finally, it was noted that the new solar lighting in the high school parking is now online. Lastly, looking ahead to the December 20th board meeting, we look forward to honoring our fall student athletes and teams that earned state championship titles this year. The students, their coaches and their families will be present at the next meeting.
