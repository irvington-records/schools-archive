                              Irvington Union Free School District

      Corrective Action Plan for Audited Financial Statements Year Ended June 30, 2022

For each recommendation included in the audit report, the following is our corrective action(s)
taken or proposed. For recommendations where corrective action has not been taken or
proposed, we have included the following explanations.

School Lunch Fund Recommendation: We recommend that the School District take all steps
necessary to utilize the excess portion of this fund balance in the upcoming fiscal year.

Implementation Plan: The Assistant Superintendent for Business reviews the needs of the Food
Services program monthly with the Food Services Director. Often equipment is needed and the
lunch fund is accessed. Additionally, the District is saving for a possible cafeteria furniture
remodeling project as our cafeteria is outdated and needs enhancements. We are also looking
into new locations for seating, which would require furniture, equipment and point of sale
terminals.

Implementation Date: Ongoing



Other Postemployment Benefits Recommendation: We recommend that the School District
verify all the information on the census data agrees with personnel records before submission
to the actuaries in order to obtain an accurate valuation of OPES liability. We also recommend
that the School District ensure that all personnel records of past and current employees are
maintained on file.

Implementation Plan: The Business Office and Human Resources Departments perform internal
reviews of this data and will continue to cross check information based on all known sources.

Implementation Date: Next Census collection



Capital Projects Fund - Project Length Schedule Recommendation: We recommend that a
review of all capital projects be undertaken to determine the status of completion and the
proper disposition of unspent funds.

Implementation Plan: The Assistant Superintendent for Business and School Business
Administrator will review all capital projects and status of completion. We anticipate all
construction to be completed by the end of the 2022-2023 fiscal year.

Implementation Date: June 2023

   Corrective Action Plan for Audited Extraclassroom Statements Year Ended June 30, 2022

For each recommendation included in the audit report, the following is our corrective action(s)
taken or proposed. For recommendations where corrective action has not been taken or
proposed, we have included the following explanations.


Extraclassroom Activity Funds Recommendation: We recommend that the district begin to
make deposits within 72 hours of receipt.

Implementation Plan: The School Business Administrator/Treasurer will review the proper cash
handling procedures for extraclassroom funds with the High School and Middle School Faculty
Club Advisors, especially timeliness of sending cash to the business office. It should be noted
that deposits brought to the local branch are not recorded until the following business day as
there are no longer tellers available to accept funds and funds are deposited to a drop box.

Implementation Date: November 2022



Extraclassroom Activity Funds Recommendation: We recommend that the School District
investigate and review these balances and take the appropriate action to close out these old
amounts. A determination of the status of a club with no financial activity should be made to
determine the proper disposition of funds. This action will deter clubs from being inactive in
future years.

Implementation Plan: Due to the pandemic, many student clubs were unable to conduct
meetings or generate financial activity during the recent fiscal years. The School Business
Administrator/Treasurer, who is also the Central Treasurer for the ECA funds, will review the
district extraclassroom procedures with the Faculty Club Advisors including the status of student
clubs. Faculty Advisors will advise students to record meeting minutes to document current and
future club activity to help the District determine inactive clubs during the fiscal year.

Implementation Date: June 2023
