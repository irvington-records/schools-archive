IRVINGTON Superintendent of Schools

UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT

Unit Name: Irvington Union Free School District
Audit Report Title: Irvington Union Free School District - Fixed Assets
Audit Report Number: 2022M-89

For each recommendation included in the audit report, the following is our corrective action(s)
taken or proposed. For recommendations where corrective action has not been taken or proposed,
we have included the following explanations.

Audit Recommendation:

1 Approve the disposal of all fixed assets and IT equipment purchased for $300 or more prior
to disposal as required by Board policy.

Implementation Plan: This is our customary practice but we acknowledge that one disposal was
missed. The Business Office will work more closely with the Technology Department and
Buildings and Grounds to ensure all assets that are planned to be disposed are placed on the
Board agenda for Board approval prior to disposal. To note, at the recent November 9, 2022
meeting, the Board of Education approved a Declaration of Obsolete/Surplus Equipment
authorizing the disposal of assets as per Board policy. A complete listing of iterns and items tags
was included.

Implementation Date: November 2022

Audit Recommendation:

2. Ensure a full inventory and appraisal of District property, equipment and materials are
performed every three years as required by Board policy.

Implementation Plan: The Business Office will schedule a full inventory with the valuation
consultants for Spring of 2023. The School Administration and Board of Education will discuss if the
Board policy should be changed for a full inventory audit every five years to match the District's
current practice or whether funds should be budgeted to obtain a full inventory every three years.
Implementation Date: March 2023

Audit Recommendation:

3. Ensure the Treasurer provides fixed asset tags to the building custodians, and the fixed
assets are tagged prior to use.

Implementation Plan: The Director of Facilities will notify the Treasurer of new equipment
purchases upon requisition approval. The Treasurer will provide an asset tag to the Director of
Facilities to be tagged on the asset prior to use.

Implementation Date: October 2022

Audit Recommendation:

4. Ensure the Treasurer maintains accurate and up-to-date fixed asset inventory records,
including the cost, date of purchase, serial number and location, to account for and
monitor District fixed assets.

Implementation Plan: The Treasurer will complete the template provided by the valuation vendor
for all additions and disposals throughout the year using the data from the District's new asset
management system. Additionally, the template will be cross referenced to the District's financial
management system by the Assistant Superintendent for Business to ensure all assets are up-to-
date.

Implementation Date: June 30, 2023

Audit Recommendation:

5. Ensure the iT vendor obtains a certificate of disposal from the company sanitizing and
disposing District IT equipment.

implementation Plan: While this is our current practice, the Assistant Superintendent for Business
and Treasurer will communicate with and monitor the Technology Department to ensure that a
certificate of disposal is provided with disposal of District IT equipment. The Treasurer will maintain
the certificate with the fixed assets files. If items marked as obsolete and board approved for
disposal are retained for parts, we will make note of this, as then no certificate would be available.
Implementation Date: November 2022
