Action (Consent): 12.2 Contract-Out of District Transportation Contract with Royal Coach

Resolution: BOARD RESOLUTION WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District is required to provide student transportation to resident students including those attending schools outside of the School District’s boundaries; WHEREAS, the District Administration has identified the Out-of-District Transportation Contract between Ardsley Union Free School District and Royal Coach (the “Ardsley Out-of-District Transportation Contract”) as an option for the provision of such transportation to the District’s students; WHEREAS, the Ardsley Out-of-District Transportation Contract permits piggybacking by other school districts subject to the approval of the Board of Education of the Ardsley Union Free School District; WHEREAS, the District Administration undertook an analysis of the costs associated with the use of the Ardsley Out-of-District Transportation Contract through piggybacking; WHEREAS, the analysis demonstrated that the School District would achieve cost savings by its use of the Ardsley Out-of-District Transportation Contract through piggybacking; and WHEREAS, by resolution, the Board of Education of the Ardsley Union Free School District authorized the School District to utilize the Out-of-District Transportation Contract via piggybacking. NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that piggybacking onto the Ardsley Out-of-District Transportation Contract achieves costs savings to the School District. NOW THEREFORE, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the costs associated with piggybacking onto the Ardsley Out-of-District Transportation Contract are appropriate for the School District. NOW THEREFORE, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District hereby authorizes the School District to enter into a contract with Royal Coach Lines for Out-of-District Transportation pursuant to the terms and conditions of the Ardsley Out-of-District Transportation Contract through piggybacking; NOW THEREFORE, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District authorizes the Board President and the Superintendent of Schools, where required, to execute and file all documents necessary to effectuate such contract.

Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education approve the Consent Agenda which includes 9.-Curriculum and Instruction Item 9.1; 10.-Business of the Board Items 10.1- 10.6; 10.-Personnel Matters Items 11.1- 11.5; 11.-Business Office Matters Items 12.1- 12.10.

Motion by Michael Hanna, second by Beth Propper.

Final Resolution: Motion Carries

Yes: Michael Hanna, David Graeber, Brian Friedman, Beth Propper, Erin Bernstein, Jayne Wissner

Not Present at Vote: Maura Gedid
