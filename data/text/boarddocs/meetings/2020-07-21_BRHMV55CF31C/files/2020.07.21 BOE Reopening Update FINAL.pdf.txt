   Promote equity and accessibility to learning for all students.


Provide instructional delivery that meets the needs of all students.


Address the social-emotional needs of all students and employees.

  C&I Subcommittee Areas of Focus
● Identify models of learning that support high-quality, rigorous learning
  for students as well as dedicated time for professional collaboration and
  learning.

● Plan for professional development that will support teachers in
  establishing a positive classroom environment with high expectations for
  learning in all possible instructional models.

● Consider the opportunities and challenges for ﬁeld trips, co-curricular
  and extra-curricular activities and events.

● Consider how the operational needs for social distance will impact
  teaching and learning.
