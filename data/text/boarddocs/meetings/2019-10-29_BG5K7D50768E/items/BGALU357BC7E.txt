Meeting: Oct 29, 2019 - Special Meeting/Work Session
Date: 2019-10-29
Category: 5. School Board Recognition
Subject: 5.1 School Board Recognition
Type: Recognition

School Board Recognition Week in New York State is October 21-25, 2019.  To recognize the members of the Irvington UFSD School Board for their time and commitment, Irvington Mayor Brian Smith will present the Board with a Proclamation.  Representatives from the PTSA, IEF, the District's bargaining units, as well as the District itself, will present members of the Board with tokens of appreciation.
