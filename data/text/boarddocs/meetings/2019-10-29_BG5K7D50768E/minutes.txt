5. School Board Recognition

Recognition:  5.1 School Board Recognition

Dr. Harrison began the meeting by acknowledging the Board of Education Trustees.  He then opened up the floor to representatives from the PTSA, the IEF and the IAA.  All gave words of appreciation and token gifts to each of the Board Members.  Following these presentations, Dr. Harrison read a Proclamation on behalf of Irvington Mayor Brian Smith.  This was followed by Dr. Harrison presenting token gifts of appreciation to each Board Member on behalf of the District.

Michael Hanna stated that October is National Principal Month and wanted to recognize and acknowledge all the hard work and dedication of each building Principal.

9. Business of the Board

Action (Consent):  9.1 Tax Certiorari Settlement - Holy Spirit Association

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District is a respondent in certain tax certiorari proceedings filed by Holy Spirit Association of the Unification of World Christianity pertaining to Parcels 2.20-53 and 1.220-128-28.5 for tax years 2014 through 2018; and WHEREAS, the parties have negotiated a tentative resolution of the proceedings, as set forth in the consent judgment attached hereto; and WHEREAS, the Board has determined that it is in the best interests of the School District to resolve the proceeding in accordance with the Consent Judgment without further litigation; NOW, BASED UPON THE FORGOING, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED that the Board hereby approves the settlement of the tax proceedings as set forth in the Consent Judgment; AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that Ingerman Smith, LLP is authorized to execute the consent judgment and any other documents necessary to effectuate the settlement; AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that upon approval by the Court, the Assistant Superintendent for Business shall be authorized to issue a refund payment in accordance with the consent judgment.

Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education approve the Consent Agenda which includes 9.-Business of the Board Items 9.1- 9.5; 10.-Personnel Matters Items 10.1-10.3; 11.-Business Office Matters Items 11.1- 11.2.

Motion by David Graeber, second by Maria Kashkin.
