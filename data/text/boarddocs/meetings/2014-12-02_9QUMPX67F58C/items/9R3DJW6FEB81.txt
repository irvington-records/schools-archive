Subject: 11.6 Tax Certiorari Settlement - 111 North Broadway
Type: Action (Consent)

When the BOE originally approved the settlement in March, it was anticipated that the reduction would be reflected in the 2014-15 tax roll.  However, the final signed settlement did not meet the deadline.  Therefore, 111 North Broadway was over-assessed for the current year and paid excess taxes which now must be refunded.
