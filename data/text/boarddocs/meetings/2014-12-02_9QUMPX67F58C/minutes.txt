	4. Superintendent's Report

-
		Dr. Harrison reported that the PTSA has recently taken an interest in advocacy efforts and in partnership with New York Suburban Coalition for Public Education (NYSCPE), they are preparing to launch a letter writing campaign to legislatures. Their focus will be on the Gap Elimination Aid (GEA) that has cost Irvington approximately $2.7  in aid over the past three years. Other efforts will be concerning the field testing that the State is considering a requirement of all districts to participate in. On behalf of the PTSA, Michael Hanna, thanked the Board dealing with the issues that have been surfacing over the past years, including the tax cap, reduced state aid, etc. that is out of their control. There is an effort to reach out to the State in order for them to hear parents and community concerns. The upcoming letter writing campaign will begin shortly and information will be available on the PTSA's website. Dr. Harrison noted that the Board might be asked to consider possible resolutions that would memorialize their position on the issues.

-
		Dr. Harrison also noted that Dr. Kor is working with the principals and Department Chairs of the World Language Department to process the meaning of the Tri State World Language Review Report and to identify the appropriate next steps.

-
		The State Ed Department recently released a report entitled “Where Are They Now?”, referring to the graduating class of 2012 and where the individual students are currently. They utilized college clearinghouse data to track the students and it appears that the data might be flawed causing concern that their data sets do not accurately reflect a student’s college/career/military status. Miscalculations have been reported on the District’s data, i.e. on our graduation rate. Calls are being made to parents to find out where the students are at this time. The findings will reported to the Board upon completion of identification.

-
		On November 20th, the fall book club focused on The Gate Keeper, and thanked the PTSA, especially Megan Russin, who hosted at her home, as well as Dave Cohen, Deb Mariniello and Emily Colman. A spring book club is also being planned.

-
		There are many events coming up in the next week, including the Warnock Basketball Tournaments, concerts, etc.

	5. Board Committee/Liaison Reports

	Information: 5.1 Curriculum Committee - 11/18/14

	Mrs. Kerner reported that the Curriculum Committee members, Maria Kashkin, Raina Kor and Kris Harrison, discussed professional development with regard to the number of days that are scheduled during the year, and the work being completed during those times was outlined by Dr. Harrison and Dr. Kor. Administration is working to identify time for staff to receive professional development, such as opportunities to have more early release times or delayed openings, and noted that some area school districts use early release and/or delayed openings to conduct staff development. Mr. Montgomery would like further discussion on the needs of staff development to ensure the time is well spent.

	Also discussed by the committee was the revised elementary report card that accommodates the Common Core, with the majority of changes in the area of ELA. A detailed guide was developed by Dr. Kor to assist parents in understanding the terminology in the guide.

	As an aside to the committee report, Dr. Harrison noted that developing the 2015-16 school year calendar will be a challenge as Labor Day is on September 7th. Several scenarios will be presented for the Board’s consideration.
