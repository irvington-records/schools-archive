A Strategic Vision

 The Irvington Strategic Vision was developed with the input of the Irvington school
 community to develop a framework to:

  ●   Develop a common path for our future
  ●   Name our aspirations as a school community
  ●   Establish objectives to enhance the learning experience
  ●   Identify indicators of success
  ●   Support current initiatives
  ●   Inform decision making in the development of:
        ○ Curriculum and instruction
        ○ Extra- and co-curricular programs
        ○ Facilities
        ○ Budget

Living the Vision

 Bringing a strategic plan to life means that across the District we are implementing a
 way of thinking … a way of being.

  ●   Planning will be seen and measured through the lens of our Strategic Objectives
  ●   A process has been developed by which we will structure our planning to put the

Strategic Objectives in Action

 The Strategic Vision has come to life through targeted planning that will be reflected in:

  ●   Development of annual District, School and Department Goals
       ○   Alignment with specific objects - published in a singular document
       ○   Goals will be outcome-based with indicators of success
       ○   Annual updates will be presented and published
  ●   Proposal of new initiatives to expand or enhance student experiences
  ●   District sub-plans:
       ○   Curriculum and Instruction (i.e. professional development, technology, etc.)
  ●   Facility plans

Strategic Objectives
 The Irvington Strategic Vision names six, overarching objectives that will be employed to
 achieve its mission and vision.

  1.   Provide students with a rigorous, comprehensive, enriched and diversified curricula that
       will prepare students to achieve their personal best and will integrate technology in
       their learning.
  2. Support educators through targeted professional learning and opportunities for
     collaboration.
  3.   Encourage innovation, creativity and risk taking to inspire a dynamic learning
       environment.
  4. Foster the creation of a culture where students are active participants in society.
  5.   Strengthen local connections to and ownership of our schools.
  6. Ensure the fiscal health of the district and provide for a high quality learning
     environment.

Limitations to a Strategic Plan
 A quality plan must be flexible to allow a school district to respond to unforeseen or
 emerging needs, while remaining focused on its visionary tenants.

 While limitations may not be known at this time, they may include:
  ●   Funding restrictions: tax cap, state aid, required pension contributions, health care costs,
      NYS or Federal budgets
  ●   Introduction of new State and Federal mandates
  ●   Shifts in student needs
  ●   Environmental factors
  ●   Maintenance of a responsible and sustainable budget
       ○   Consideration of future District obligations

How Will We Know?
To understand our progress, it is essential to create a culture that values the role of the
assessment of student learning and use of data to inform decision making.
     How will know we understand growth, achievement and programmatic progress?

Our initiative embraces a wide range of opportunities to understand our success, which
supports and informs each Strategic Objective:
 ●   Daily classroom instruction in how teachers understand student growth
 ●   Creation of benchmark assessments to measure the effectiveness of curriculum and
     instruction
 ●   Use of standardized assessment
 ●   Administration of surveys to entire school community
 ●   Development of a program assessment model to evaluate whether initiatives are
     attaining identified objectives
