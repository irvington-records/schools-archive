Irvington Union Free School District
Monthly Financial Highlights

December 2025

Cash Balance

Our December overall cash position was $31 million, which is a healthy cash flow for this time of year.

Cash received in the Real Property & Other Taxes category is Greenburgh’s ‘Tax Collection payment for $598,859.77. These funds are still
from the first half for payment of property taxes due. While we record the full revenue in July, we receive the funds over time.

State and Federal Aid Cash Receipts included is Excess Cost Aid of $359,720 and VLT Lottery aid totaling $34,959.

Revenue:

General Fund Revenue through December was $73,937,322 or 93.4% of the budgeted revenue.

The Use of Money and & Property mostly includes interest earnings from all District general fund bank accounts. The District has earned
$431,116 in interest earnings through December. The prior year, the District had an interest earnings budget surplus due to the high interest
rate environment. The 25-26 anticipated interest revenue earning budget was increased compared to the prior year as interest rates are still
relatively high (although rates are currently decreasing). This explains why December 2025 shows less percentage of revenue recognized at this
point of time when compated to last year. We will continue to work with our partners at NYCLASS for the best investment opportunities to
ensure we maximize our interest earnings revenue.

Misc. Sources is at 96 % of the budget, as we received funds owed from prior years and tax payments for bisected propetties.

Expenditures:

Actual General Fund Expenses totaled approximately $6.17 million for December. This brings the total year to date expenditures to
$30,655,170. Including outstanding encumbrances, 9% of the budget remains, which is a typical amount at this point in the year.

Under General Support and Benefits Category, both the Tax Certiorari Expense and Other Benefits will need a Budget Adjustment transfer
from Reserves set aside for these expenses.
