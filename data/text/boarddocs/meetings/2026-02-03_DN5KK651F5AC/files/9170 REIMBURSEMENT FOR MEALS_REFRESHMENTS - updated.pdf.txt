9170 REIMBURSEMENT FOR MEALS/REFRESHMENTS

Irvington Union Free School District officials and employees are entitled to reimbursement for
necessary expenses incurred in the performance of their official duties and/or as outlined in
collective bargaining agreements.

Travel Outside of District

It is the position of the New York State Comptroller's Office that meals of public officers and
employees generally should not be reimbursed or paid by the municipal entity unless the officer
or employee is traveling outside his/her regular work area on official business for an extended
period of time. All requests for reimbursement for such expenditures must be appropriately
documented with an itemized receipt and information showing the dates and purpose of the
outside of District travel. Prior approval of the Superintendent must be obtained.

Staff Meetings/Board Meetings/Emergency Meetings

The Board of Education recognizes that at certain times it may be appropriate to provide meals
and/or refreshments at District meetings which are being held for an educational purpose and
where events prevent attendees from taking off during mealtime for food consumption because
of a pressing need to complete business. Prior approval of the Superintendent must be obtained
for food and beverages provided at meetings or activities which will be charged to the District.

Any such expenditures must be appropriately documented with an itemized receipt and
information showing the date and purpose of the meeting, who attended the meeting and why
the attendees needed food and/or refreshments to conduct School District business. These
requirements must be met for meals/refreshments provided by the school lunch fund or local
vendors, charged to the District and/or reimbursed to a School District official.

District Events

The Board of Education also recognizes that at certain times it may be appropriate to provide
meals and/or refreshments at District events. Examples of authorized categories of
expenditures include but are not limited to staff on teacher orientation day at the beginning of
each year, staff recognition day, Superintendent's Conference Day, receptions for volunteers
and student recognition events.

Any such expenditures must be appropriately documented with an itemized receipt and
information showing the date and purpose of the event. Prior approval of the Superintendent
must be obtained.

The Board of Education authorizes the following maximum reimbursement amounts for
breakfast, lunch, and dinner that are associated with approved attendance at conferences,
conventions and workshops, per Policy 9170. Original receipts must be submitted in order to be
approved for reimbursement:

Breakfast         $15.00
Lunch             $17.00
Dinner            $35.00
