The Irvington School District contracts with several service providers to provide a variety of related services for our special education students and John Cardinal O'Connor School students. Therapy Source, Inc. recruits and compensates, as independent contractors, speech-language pathologists, occupational therapists, physical therapists, psychologists, and similar service providers (each, a "Therapist").
