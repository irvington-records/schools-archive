Subject: 9.5 Adoption of the District Wide Safety Plan
Type: Action (Consent)
Attachments: District Wide Safety and Emergency Plan 2024-25.pdf

On Monday, July 15th, the State Board of Regents approved proposed updates to Commissioner's Regulation 155.17 Education Law. These proposed updates were made as a result of feedback received from stakeholders during the initial Board of Regents hearing in April 2024. These newest changes went into effect on July 31st, 2024. Because these changes became law after the District Plan was Board approved, the District was required to re-approve the plan. The re-approval process required a new 30-day public comment period which has now been completed.
