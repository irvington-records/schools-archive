ORTON-GILLINGHAM ASSOCIATE LEVEL PRACTICUM

This Agreement made as of October 22, 2024, by and between IRVINGTON UNION FREE
SCHOOL DISTRICT (“IRVINGTON”), 6 Dows Lane, Irvington, NY 10533, and The Rose
Institute for Learning and Literacy, Manhattanville University School of Education
(‘MANHATTANVILLE”), 2900 Purchase Street, Purchase, New York 10577, Federal
Identification Number 13-1 740469.

The organizations wish to serve students, teachers, and schools by furthering the preparation of
teachers to effectively develop literacy and promote academic achievement.

A. Description of Services: Manhattanville University

The ROSE INSTITUTE FOR LEARNING AND LITERACY, MANHATTANVILLE
will offer at Irvington for professional development credit, a supervised practicum to
facilitate the implementation of the Orton-Gillingham approach at the Associate Level.
The practicum will use a virtual mentoring model and will follow all requirements stated
by the Academy of Orton-Gillingham Practitioners and Educators and The Rose Institute
for Learning and Literacy. These requirements include submission of lesson plans and 5
videos. A total of 5 videos will be submitted by May 30, 2025. Two support sessions
will be included, one kick-off and one closing.

Twelve teachers will participate in the practicum.

Irvington and Manhattanville wili mutually explore the desirability and feasibility of
further coursework.

Coursework will be offered for professional development credit only. Irvington will
determine what benefits accrue to its faculty for this course at its sole discretion.

The practicum fee will be covered through Co-Ser with PNW BOCES.

C. Term and Termination

This Agreement will become effective on its execution and will continue in full force and effect
until terminated.

Either party, upon thirty (30) days written notice, can effect termination of this Agreement without
cause, Upon termination, neither party shall have liability to the other party, except as otherwise
set forth herein. Should the program be terminated once the course has begun, officially registered
students may continue to complete the course on campus or other active MANHATTANVILLE
location where the course may be available.
