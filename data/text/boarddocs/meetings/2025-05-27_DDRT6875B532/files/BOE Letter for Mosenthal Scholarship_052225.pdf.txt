Dear Trustees,

I am writing on behalf of the Irvington NY PTSA to seek your approval to establish the Dr.
Scott Mosenthal Scholar-Athlete Scholarship Award. The proposed award will recognize
one male and one female matriculating student-athlete who has demonstrated
outstanding athletic achievement while maintaining above-average academic
performance.

The PTSA will provide $500 in funding annually to award two recipients $250.00 each. The
scholarship will be independently administered by the IUFSD administration along with its
existing scholarship awards.

Sincere thanks for your consideration and look forward to hearing from you regarding the
approval of this scholarship.


Sincerely,



Sonal Vasudev
President, Irvington NY PTSA
