                    The Role of BOCES in New York State
• In 1948, the New York State legislature created Boards of Cooperative Educational Services (BOCES) to provide shared educational
  programs and services to school districts within the state.
• With BOCES as a partner, school districts can be strategic in deploying shared programs that serve students from all districts regardless
  of their enrollment, income, or size of tax base.
• Today there are 37 BOCES that are partnering with nearly all the state’s school districts to help meet students’ evolving educational needs
  through cost-effective and relevant programs. SWBOCES is Celebrating its 75th year of servicing the Sole Supervisory District of
  Westchester Country.

• The 37 BOCES are divided up into 12 Joint Management Team (JMT) Regions. We are in the Lower Hudson JMT region servicing
  Putnam County, Rockland County, and Westchester County.

• Each JMT has a Regional Information Center (RIC) that services that region. Our regional JMT RIC is the Lower Hudson Regional
  Information Center (LHRIC).

• Boards of Cooperative Educational Services (BOCES) are continuously looking to the future to develop and evolve programs that align
  with the needs of their students, school districts and communities. We are proud of our role in this work.

• BOCES membership is not currently available to the “Big Five” city school districts: New York City, Buffalo, Rochester, Yonkers, and
  Syracuse.

• The BOCES network has the ability to use municipalities and divisions of the state with school districts in the provision of cost-effective
  services.

              The Role of BOCES in New York State
The District Superintendent

    • A District Superintendent is the Chief Executive Officer (CEO) of an individual BOCES. The District
      Superintendent is responsible for both the BOCES and its Component Districts. A District Superintendent
      also serves as the Commissioner of Education’s representative to the region.

District Superintendents can provide:

    • Liaison Services – The District Superintendent facilitates communications between the New York State
      Education Department and local districts, as well as other public and private agencies.

    • Superintendent Searches – Upon request, the District Superintendent may assist component school
      districts in their selection process of a new school superintendent. The District Superintendent at the
      BOCES may serve as a consultant for recruitment, screening and evaluation of candidates.

    • Consultant Services – Drawing from various resources, the District Superintendent is available to consult
      with school districts on a variety of educational topics including:
         • Board-administration relationships
         • New York State Board of Regents Standards
         • State education department commissioner’s regulation interpretations and clarifications
         • Management and planning
         • School boundaries
         • Reorganization and mergers

Serving 77 high schools and 65 middle schools in Dutchess, Putnam, Rockland, and
Westchester counties, providing support for the administration and management of athletic
programs

• Administer the scheduling of interscholastic athletic contests
• Coordinate the assigning of athletic officials
    • Negotiate the contract with the Section One Certified Officials Association
    • Manage the officials’ payment plan for member schools
    • Support the recruitment and retention of athletic officials
• Optional Services: 3rd party athletic software vendors
    • Sport Registration & Medical Clearance (Family ID)
    • Video Analysis (Hudl)
    • Concussion Management (ImPACT)
• Serve as the office of Section One, Inc. of the New York State Public High School Athletic Association
    • Facilitate 36 Section One championship events
    • Responsible for ensuring schools adhere to the rules, regulations, bylaws, and eligibility standards of
      NYSED and NYSPHSAA
    • Organize and facilitate membership-driven initiatives to enhance the student-athlete experience
    • Provide equitable and safe competition for the students of public, private and parochial member schools
