Models of ICT

   ●   Team Teaching: Both co-teachers deliver instruction to the whole group at the
       same time.
   ●   One Teach, One Observe: While one teacher leads the lesson, the co-teacher
       collects specific data about the students
   ●   Station Teaching: Teachers divide content and students
   ●   Parallel Teaching: Two co-teachers teach the same content to separate groups
       simultaneously.
   ●   Alternate Teaching: One teacher works with the large part of the class while the
       co-teacher works with a smaller group.
   ●   One Teach, One Assist: one teacher leads instruction while the co-teacher
       circulates providing unobtrusive help as needed.
