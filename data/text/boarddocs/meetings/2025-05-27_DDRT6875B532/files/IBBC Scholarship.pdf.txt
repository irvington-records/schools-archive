Dear John,

We are writing this letter as the Co-Presidents of the Irvington Bulldog Booster
Club.

The Irvington Bulldog Booster Club (IBBC) is dedicated to fostering a positive
and spirited community environment by working to enhance Bulldog pride and
encourage participation in and attendance at athletic programs of the Irvington
Union Free School District. Together with parents, coaches, students, teachers,
and administrators, the Irvington Bulldog Boosters will provide opportunities for
our student-athletes and coaches and work to enhance student sportsmanship,
leadership, and character. The Bulldog Boosters will seek to organize strategic
fundraising to ensure important financial resources for all sports teams,
leadership development, athletic programs, and athletic facilities of the Irvington
Union Free School District.

We would like to award two $1000 scholarships called The Bulldog Boost Award
to two deserving athletes who exemplify the values of the Irvington Portrait of a
Graduate through their athletic journeys. These athletes routinely show resilience
and curiosity in their growth, embracing diversity and community on and off the
field, communicating openly and respectfully with coaches and teammates, and
leading with self-awareness, integrity, and compassion. These athletes
consistently contribute to team goals, uphold the highest standards of work ethic,
and build a team culture rooted in trust, respect, and unity.

Thank you for your consideration!

Maggie Kadro and Courtney Graeber
Co-Presidents IBBC
