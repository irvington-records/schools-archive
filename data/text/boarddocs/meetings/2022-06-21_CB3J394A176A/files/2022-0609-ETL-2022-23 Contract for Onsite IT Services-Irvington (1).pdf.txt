PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Working with the Irvington UFSD (District), Edu Tek Limited (Edu Tek) will provide comprehensive
Information Technology (IT) services at each school operated by the District (collectively referred to herein
as the Sites).

This Agreement shall start on July 1, 2022 and end on June 30, 2023, unless terminated sooner in
accordance with the provisions of this Agreement. Edu Tek will support the District’s Local Area Networks
(LAN’s) and Wide Area Network (WAN’s). This service includes support for all operating systems currently
in use at all Sites. The IT support described will be performed by:
        •       2.0 On-site Systems Engineer(s) Level I
        •       1.0 On-site Systems Engineer(s) Level II
        •       1.0 On-site Data Engineer(s) Level I
        On-site Project Operations Manager services not to exceed twenty (20) days per year.

SCOPE OF SERVICES
The scope of services contained in this agreement includes but is not limited to the following:

Technology Management
        •   Make recommendations in the development of the District technology plan.
        •   Review technology budget proposal and service contracts for alignment with plan
            recommendations.
        •   Assist in budget development by providing analysis of District’s networking needs and
            recommending hardware and network software to align with District needs and goals.
        •   Develop criteria and attend to details of annual technology deployment plan.
        •   Assist in the procurement of technology equipment utilizing available vendors in accordance
            with State bids as part of the annual deployment plan.
        •   Meet with the District’s data consultant teams to discuss technology requirements for District
            participation in data warehousing.
        •   Assist in developing, implementing and supporting solutions as per the District Technology
            Plan for NIST/WISP/CSOP/SAN/Disaster Recovery Systems, alternative email solutions and
            compliance with NYS electronic records retention policies.
        •   Support technology needs of Board of Education meeting

Inventory Management
        The assigned Edu Tek staff will update the inventory of the District’s technology equipment
        during the term of the Agreement in accordance with the District’s inventory procedures set
        forth in Exhibit 'A.' While Edu Tek’s team will make updates to and manage the inventory as set
        forth in this Agreement to provide assistance for asset management and the maintenance of an
        accurate inventory, Edu Tek will not be responsible for the use of equipment by the District’s
        personnel and students.

         v. where the student data will be stored (described in such a manner as to protect

            protected and privacy risks mitigated.

        System Engineer Level One
        Certifications - MCSP, CAN, CLA or equivalent experience Skill Set:
        •   Ability to install Network operating system and perform simple operating system upgrades
            (not requiring any data or application movement/migration).
        •   Basic operating system troubleshooting.
        •   Standard network/local printing and print queue installation and management.
        •   General network Administration (adding users, groups, shares, printers).
        •   Server hardware configuration.
        •   Rack device installations and cable management.
        •   SCSI attached storage subsystems.
        •   Implementation of Patch process for individual NOS.
        •   Non configurable/non manageable switch installations.
        •   Stand-alone Tape drive installations.
        •   Approved server based (non enterprise) tape backup software installation and configuration
            (specific agents may require a SE Level 2).
        •   General server diagramming and documentation.


        System Engineer Level Two
        Certifications – ITIL Foundation, MCSE, CNE, CLE or equivalent experience Skill Set:
        (minimum of 3 years)
        •   Ability to install Network operating system and perform more complex upgrades (application
            and data moves must be well defined and pre-approved).
        •   Advanced troubleshooting of operating system.
        •   Advanced network/local printing such as shared faxing and shared scanning.
        •   Advanced network Administration such as Directory and Domain troubleshooting.
        •   Cluster Hardware configurations.
        •   Blade Server configurations.
        •   Entry Level NAS/SAN (Fiber Attached Subsystems and Appliance NAS devices) in cluster
            configurations only. Does not include additional products for load balancing or active- active
            failure.
        •   Implementation of Patch process for Server group such as Microsoft Servers using bundled
            solutions such as SUS, Kaseya, WUS and Zenworks.
        •   Entry level managed/configurable switches not requiring routing, trouncing, or multiple
            manufacturer switch operation.
        •   Standalone tape drive installations including autoloaders.
        •   Approved server based (non enterprise) tape backup software installation and configuration
            and installation of approved backup agents including remote agents.
        •   Server Centric diagramming and documentation.
