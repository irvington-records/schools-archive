Understanding
Fund Balance/Reserves

June 21, 2022

Categories of Fund Balance
Fund Balance

• Unassigned Fund Balance
  o   Recommended amount is 4% of next year’s budget

• Appropriated Fund Balance
  o Funds designated for revenue for next year’s budget
  o Approved in budget process


• Reserve for Encumbrances
  o Incomplete orders at year-end that carry over to next year’s budget
  o Amounts fluctuate from year-to-year


• Reserve Funds
  o   Restricted for specific purposes
  o   Funds assist with long range financial planning and future
      budgetary “surprises”



                                                                          1

  Categories of Fund Balance (cont.)
  Current Reserve Funds
   • Tax Certiorari
     o   Used to pay tax refunds on successful claims

   • Capital Fund
     o   Used with voter approval for future projects

   • Worker’s Comp
     o   Used for self-insured districts to pay claims

   • Employee Benefits Liability (EBALR)
     o   Used for contractual employee benefits upon retirement

   • TRS/ERS
     o   Used for contractual retirement contribution matches set by NYSTRS
         and NYSERS


More are allowed: Insurance, Repair, Property Loss, Unemployment

                                                                              2

 Allowable Sources of Fund Balance

• Surplus resulting from expenditures not exceeding revenues

• Borrowing

• Sale of building/land

• Unexpended capital project




                                                               3

  Why Have Unassigned Fund Balance?
• Office of State Comptroller/SED recommend 4% of the next year
  budget as unassigned funds as a measure of fiscal health


• Previously larger levels were allowed

• Cash flow – avoid borrowing for T.A.N. and resulting interest costs

• Strong financial health leads to lower borrowing rate for future
  capital projects


• Ability to weather any potential storms




                                                                        4

Why Have Reserves?
• Used for long range planning – known costs
  o   Worker’s Comp tail claim payments
  o   Tax Certiorari refund payments
  o   Contractual payments to retirees


• Used for long range planning – known projects such as turf
  replacement

• Used to manage economic fluctuations
  o   TRS/ERS rates increasing can result in severe budgetary pressure
  o   Inflation effects - example utilities

Tax Certiorari Reserve
• Began year with $3,369,291

• Money is added and subtracted each year

• Transferred $991,471 to General Fund to pay out on settlements for 2021-22

• Total exposure is ~$28,000,000 based on actual filings but realistic
  exposure is approximately $5-$6,000,000 through settlements and court
  proceedings

• In 2012-13, District borrowed $3,575,000 to augment reserve. Final payment
  for this bond is in 2023-24

• Recommend maintaining current level or adding to tax cert reserve with
  remaining surplus funds available to avoid future borrowing

Funding Recommendations
• Fund to 4% limit for Unassigned Fund Balance

• Add $300,000 to Capital Reserve

• Add $200,000 to Worker’s Comp Reserve
  o   Relieve need for future budgetary increases and allow for settlements on
      tail claims
  o   Net increase is $115,800 as $84,200 was subtracted

• Add $200,000 to Reserve for Employee Benefits and TRS/ERS Reserves

• Add any remaining surplus funds to Tax Certiorari Reserve
  o   Estimated to be around $2,000,000
  o   Approximate net increase of $1,008,529 as $ 991,471 was utilized this year


Estimates are based on current financials and accrual estimates. Final
numbers available end of July/August as audit is completed.
