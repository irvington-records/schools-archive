                  Irvington UFSD Inventory/Device Distribution Procedures
Requirements
       •    This Procedure is provided to any Irvington UFSD Employee capable of ordering
            technology.
All Technology orders, hardware or software is reviewed and approved by EduTek and The Director of
     Technology, prior to a requisition being created. The approval will be in written form and must be
     sent with the requisition for approval by the Irvington Business Official. Hardware / Software
     Approval form.
        • Any User receiving a device issued to them will fill out a Device 1:1 form. See Attached,
            Student Device 1:1 Google form or staff form.
        • Devices can only be removed from the asset management system by EduTek.
        • Inventory, Asset management, Collection, and Distribution take priority to all but network
            outages or Irvington Superintendent deemed task.
        • This procedure can only be changed via written signed consent by both Irvington
            Superintendent and a Principle of EduTek.


Ordering of Technology
       •   Requests for hardware or software review are provided to EduTek and the Director of
           Technology through the Helpdesk.
           • Any user requesting a device, software, or other technology request, must enter a
                helpdesk ticket under the Technology needs category.
                    • Director of Technology will collaborate with the Building Administrator to gain
                        approval and provide quotes based on specifications, with attached Hardware
                        Approval form.
       •   EduTek, reviews the request for hardware or software
           • If hardware or software meets Irvington current environment a Hardware / Software
                Approval form is approved, saved to the Technology share drive, emailed to the
                requestor, and the Helpdesk ticket updated and closed.
           • If the request does not meet the District’s current technology standard the request is
                then escalated to the Director of Technology.
       •   The District’s Business official reviews requisition with attached Hardware / Software
           Approval Form.
           • Confirms Shipping and Attention is to 40 North Broadway Irvington NY, 10533 Attn.
                EDUTEK Technology Office.
       •   Purchase order is forwarded to the vendor, Director of Technology, and EduTek.
       •   Irvington Business Office Provides EduTek VIA Intra-District mail a copy of the PO with
           appropriate quantity of Asset Tags for all hardware for items over $5000 or the asset limit
           set by the board annually. Edutek will continue to use the Edutek tags for all items to be
           maintained in inventory.


Receiving new Hardware
       •   Hardware is received and delivered by District facilities to EDUTEK Technology Office.
       •   After receipt of newly ordered devices and peripherals, the EduTek onsite IT team will:
           • Confirm receipt of hardware by cross referencing the PO with packing slips.
           • Scan packing sheet and email to Director of Technology and the Technology
               Department Office Assistant.
           • Only EduTek Staff will unbox, asset tag, and enter appropriate information/data in the
               current Asset Management System

Distributing Devices

Fixed Devices (Classroom/Office/Cart Devices)


      •   Hardware distribution plan to be provided to EduTek by the Director of Technology.
          • Distribution plan shall include Schedule, Device type, locations ,Hardware to be Asset
             recovered.
          • Distribution plan shall be finalized prior to July 1st.
          • Director of Technology shall provide a plan to the Superintendent or designee for
             approval.
          •
      •   Hardware will be deployed and set up based on the distribution plan.
          • Hardware only to be deployed into spaces ready to receive devices.
          • Wirings will be neatly managed and devices will be tested for functionality.
          • Hardware will not be set up on Student desks permanently, or as a stop gap measure
              without written consent of the Superintendent.
      •   EduTek shall update the Asset management system as devices are distributed.
      •   EduTek shall update the Asset management system with Hardware to be Asset recovered if
          hardware is deemed unusable or not able to be repaired . See Asset Recovery procedure
          below.
Mobile Devices (Laptop/Chromebook)

      •   Distribution Plan and Rosters
          • Distribution plan shall include granular deployment schedule, Device type, userbase.
          • Distribution plan shall be finalized prior to July 1st.
          • Finalized student and staff rosters shall be provided by 1st week of August.
          • District Registrar shall provide a list of New registrants after August 1st.
          • EduTek will have additional devices available for users on the Registrar's list.
          • A device will only be provided to users on either the Finalized roster or Registrar list.

Maintenance of Devices

   •   EduTek will Track via the asset management system
          • Repairs, or insurance claims
          • Parts replaced
          • Cosmetic damages that are found
                   • Sticker residue, that may not be removable
                   • Obvious scratches/dents that do not render the device unusable.
   •   Provide Audit log To director of Technology as requested.
          • Audit log shall include Timestamped records
          • Reports shall be within Asset Management software limitations.

Inventory maintenance

Room Based Devices

      •   A physical inventory of all room-based devices will be conducted for three (3) weeks over
          the final two (2) weeks of the calendar year and the first (1) week of January.
      •   Two of the onsite Technicians will be dedicated to the inventory and unavailable for
          desktop support or projects based on a mutually agreed upon schedule..
      •   Inventory shall include mobile devices assigned to carts or rooms.
      •   A check of Devices Allocated to users will not be part of this inventory.
      •   EduTek To provide a report of missing or damaged devices to DOT & Superintendent for
          review.


User Devices

      •  An Inventory of user-based devices will commence following device collection.
      Two of the onsite Technicians will be dedicated to the inventory and unavailable for desktop
         support or projects.
      • A reconciliation of received devices will be conducted and a report shall be provided to the
         Director of Technology and Superintendent of schools.
      • Director of Technology, or School Admin shall notify users of missing devices.
      • EduTek to disable devices at the direction of the Director of Technology.
      • Director of Technology to provide a report of Users approved to keep district devices.

Asset Recovery

      •   Director of Technology shall provide a written plan of Hardware to be recovered.
      •   Edutek to make recommendations for devices to be discarded based on IT expertise
      •   EduTek to Properly deprovisioned in the inventory management tool and Google Admin
          Console.
      •   EduTek to provide a report to the Irvington Business official in spreadsheet format of
          devices to be asset recovered.
      •   Report shall include Make, Model, Asset tag(s), Serial number.
      •   Devices will not be recycled until approval by the Board of Education and Irvington
          Business official approves in writing.
