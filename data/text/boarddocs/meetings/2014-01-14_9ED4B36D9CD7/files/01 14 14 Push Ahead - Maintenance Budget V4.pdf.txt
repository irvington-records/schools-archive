Financial Leadership

 o   To understand financial limitations, a push-ahead/
     maintenance budget has been calculated to project fiscal
     limitations.

 o   The push-ahead budget projects what it would cost to
     maintain all the existing services and personnel in the
     upcoming school year

Maintenance budget details
 All data is based upon known data and estimates (based on
  professional guidance)
 Shifts in key figures can change
 Target is to provide a budget that balances academic
  excellence with fiscal responsibility
 Throughout the budget process, additions and subtractions will
  be made based on Board and Administrative recommendations
 Process for budget development:
   Component-by-component review
   Administrative and Board recommendations
   Determination for inclusion in the Superintendent’s Proposed Budget
