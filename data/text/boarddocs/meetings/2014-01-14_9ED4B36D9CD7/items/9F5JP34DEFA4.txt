The team of elementary administrators and Assistant Superintendent, Dr. Kor, will provide a presentation that will detail the shifts of our elementary school programs as result of the required implementation of the Common Core Learning Standards. Specific examples will be included that will highlight how instruction has changed as well as the expectations for what students are learning.
