Action (Consent): 9.2 Tax Certiorari Settlement-GEJ

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District was a respondent in certain tax certiorari proceedings filed by GEJ Tarrytown, LLC pertaining to property identified on the tax roll as Section 1.140, Block 94, Lot 5.2 for tax years 2016 through 2021; and WHEREAS, on March 22, 2022, the Board adopted a resolution authorizing a settlement and payment of the refunds due at the time (from 2016 through 2020 assessments); and WHEREAS, due to a delay in the court approval of the settlement, taxes were levied on the original 2021 assessment rather than the reduced assessment; and WHEREAS, the Board desires to amend the resolution to increase the total refund amount to include the refunds associated with the 2021 assessment; and NOW, BASED UPON THE FORGOING, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED that the Board hereby amend the foregoing Board Resolution adopted on March 22, 2022 to reflect a total refund not to exceed $636,130. AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Assistant Superintendent for Business shall be authorized to issue an additional refund payment in a sum not to exceed $90,704.
