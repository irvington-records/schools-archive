New Calendar Development Goals

   To be more culturally responsive by recognizing additional
    holidays that are celebrated by community members

   Increase opportunities to support SEL, by systemically
    providing for a better school-life balance by eliminating
    homework over certain school closures and not having
    assessments on the school days that follow.

Calendar Development Complexities

    Focuses on maximizing learning

    Meets SED requirements
      o 184 days (inclusive of up to 4 Superintendent Conference Days)
      o Testing calendar

    Aligns with contractual obligations

    Accounts for federal and religious holidays

    Maintains consistency with BOCES

    Provides for 4 snow/emergency-closure days

2023-24 Calendar Details
                     Labor Day: September 4th

                     First Student Day: September 5th

                     SED Established Rating Day: June 26th
                      o   Mandated final day of the year

                     December Break: December 25th - January 2nd

                     Winter Break: February 19th – 23rd

                     Spring Break: March 25th – April 1st

                     Memorial Day Weekend: May 24th – May 27th

                     Juneteenth: June 19th

                     Last Student Day: June 25th

                     Professional Development:
                      o   Conference days: August 30th & 31st, November 7th, March 15th
                      o   Professional Learning Release Days (PLRDs)

Highlight: Schools closed for students on Election Day, no homework in observance of Diwali, Lunar New Year
and Eid al-Fitr (if falling on a weekend no homework on the next school day)
