COMMENTS:
 #5. Debated the necessity of this resolution given the expansion of mandated reporters in the last 5 years.
 #8. Debate over the importance of drills for safety preparedness vs. the time required and the anxiety and stress for
      students (also noted that current NYS law requires 8 evacuation drills and 4 lockdown drills).
 #9. Discussed how to ensure that the community (perhaps beyond only board trustees) determines the need.
 #11. Debate over the need for guidance and data privacy guardrails for AI vs. fears that legislation can be very
      inflexible and hard to change as AI continues to evolve. A more targeted resolution calling for guidance from
      NYSED on use of AI in the classroom and in district operations was more positively received.
 #13. Perspective is that NYSSBA should not be spending time on whole milk, and this is not really the purview of local
      board members.
 #14. NYS is already doing a statewide survey on recess. Calendar days are currently more of an issue.
 #15. Although we agree with the need for additional funding for expensive special education placements, we believe
      it should be part of the reworking of the Foundation Aid formula, available to all districts and include funding
      for FAPE to age 22.
 #16. While we understand the difficulty of finding local papers nowadays, older community members (non-parents)
      are more likely to see school information in newspapers, and school districts could be open to accusations of
      “hiding” information if it is difficult to find on their website.
 #17. We believe that NYSSBA should be pushing to keep “save harmless” rather than offering alternatives.
 #18. We believe that NYSSBA should be pushing to keep “save harmless” for all districts.
 #19. Although supportive of any source of funds for CVA settlements, we would like to see a solution available to
      more school districts.
 #22. Despite a consensus over the need for financial literacy, we oppose asking legislators to create a curriculum
      mandate. As the Board of Regents are already planning on requiring financial literacy as part of the graduation
      measures process, the learning standard will be required.
 #23. Concern expressed that school will now be pushed into taking on the job of ensuring health care for children.
      Discussion included concerns over what medications should be prescribed in a school environment, what kind
      of follow-up would be needed, what availability of mental health resources would be needed and the potential
      liability on districts.
