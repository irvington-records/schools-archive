6. Retirement Contribution Reserve Sub-Fund

Action:  6.1 Establish a District Retirement Contribution Reserve Sub-Fund

WHEREAS, Section 6-r of the New York State General Municipal Law was amended to allow school districts that participate in the New York State Teachers’ Retirement System to establish a sub-fund within its retirement contribution reserve fund to finance retirement contributions to the New York State Teachers’ Retirement System and/or to offset all or a portion of the amount deducted from the moneys apportioned to the school district from the State for the support of common schools pursuant to Education Law �521; and

WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District participates in the New York State Teachers’ Retirement System; and

WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District established a retirement contribution reserve fund on June 4, 2019 entitled Retirement Contribution Reserve Fund.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District hereby establishes a sub-fund, entitled Irvington Union Free School District Retirement Contribution Reserve Sub-Fund, within its retirement contribution reserve fund, subject to the requirements of New York State General Municipal Law �6-r, effective immediately.

Motion by Maria Kashkin, second by Beth Propper.

Action (Consent):  11.5 Resolution to Join PERMA, a Municipal Group Self-Insurance Program

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Authorized Representative of the Irvington UFSD desires to secure the Irvington UFSD's obligation to provide workers' compensation benefits, as applicable, through participation in a group self-insurance program of which the Irvington UFSD will be a member, The Authorized Representative of the Irvington UFSD, duly convened in regular session, does hereby resolve, pursuant to, and in accordance with the provisions of Section 50 of the New York State Workers Compensation Law and other applicable provisions of law and regulations thereunder, as follows: Section 1. The Authorized Representative (hereinafter "Representative") of the Irvington UFSD does hereby resolve to secure the Irvington UFSD's obligation to provide workers' compensation benefits, as applicable, through participation in a group self-insurance plan of which the Irvington UFSD will be a member; Section2. The Representative of the Irvington UFSD does hereby resolve to become a member of the Public Employer Risk Management Association, Inc., a workers' compensation group self-insurance program for local governments and other public employers and instrumentalities of the State of New York; Section 3, In order to effect the Irvington UFSD's membership in said group self-insurance program, the authorized officer of the Irvington UFSD is hereby authorized to execute and enter into the Public Employer Risk Management Association Workers' Compensation Program Agreement, annexed hereto as Exhibit A, on behalf of the Irvington UFSD. This resolution shall take effect immediately.
