Meeting: Jun 18, 2019 - Special Meeting/Work Session
Date: 2019-06-18
Category: 11. Business Office Matters
Subject: 11.4 Year End Fund Balance
Type: Action (Consent)

- $2,518,142 is the allowable maximum amount (4% of the 2019-20 budget) and recommended to be designated as Unassigned Fund Balance by the Comptroller

- $422,500 was presented during the budget discussions

- The Tax Certiorari reserve is used to make refunds on previous years' settlements.

- The Worker's Comp Reserve is used to pay current unbudgeted claims or hold for future worker compensation claims as the district is self insured for worker's compensation.

- The Capital Reserve will be used for future capital projects with voter consent.

- The Retirement Contribution Reserve will be used to offset future increases in the required ERS or TRS contribution rate in years where the rate increase could affect academic programs.
