Meeting: Jun 18, 2019 - Special Meeting/Work Session
Date: 2019-06-18
Category: 8. Business of the Board
Subject: 8.1 Naming of the Irvington Middle School Store in Honor of Mary and John Bachrach
Type: Action (Consent)

Mr. and Mrs. Bachrach have been beloved members of Irvington Middle School and the broader Irvington community for decades.  In fact, 2018 marks the 40th year of their service to our school. Ms. Bachrach was a teacher at IMS from 1978 until 1999. Following her “retirement”, Mary has continued to serve the students and staff of IMS as the advisor to our Student Council through 2011, an active member of our Sunshine Committee,  leader of our School Store, and in countless other volunteer roles to support the students and staff of IMS. Her impact on the lives of her students, both in and out of the classroom, has been evident through the many students who repeatedly volunteer their time to set up and run the School Store with her during summer months, those that fondly share their memories of the lessons learned in her classes (including several current Irvington staff members), and the student experiences that she has had a hand in creating and facilitating over the years such as our annual Halloween Dance, the Pennies for Patients drive, and the Moving Up Ceremony.  In all of these endeavors, and many more, Mary has given of her time and spirit to promote the success of IMS and our students.
