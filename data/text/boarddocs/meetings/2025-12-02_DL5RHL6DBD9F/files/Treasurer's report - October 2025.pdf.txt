Irvington Union Free School District
Monthly Financial Highlights

October 2025
Cash Balance
© Our current overall cash position is $39,994,013 million, increased by the District’s first tax payment. This was $1.7 million more than last year
at this time.

© The first tax payment of $32,956,164 was remitted to the District from the Town of Greenburgh. This represents approximately 48.49% of
our tax levy. Tax payments are received between the months of October through April as remitted by the Town. The two largest tax payments
are received in October and February.

© State and Federal Aid cash received included final payments for 2024-25 IDEA and ESSA grants totaling $275,659, Video Lottery Terminal
(VLT) Aid in the amount of $34,959, State Lunch payments totaling $45,103. As the District is now providing universal free meals, the
reimbursement from New York State will be higher than previous years.

© Interest rates decreased month over month, as expected. The NYLAF average monthly rate dropped 4.80% to 3.923%. Chase decreased from
1.87% to 1.53%. We will analyze the U.S. Treasury market with NYLAF to determine the best instrument for the highest rate.

Revenue:

¢ General Fund Revenue is currently at $71,914,071 ot 90.9% of the budgeted amount. Inchided in this total is the full amount of the tax levy
revenue, which is recorded and recognized in full as per accounting standards. When the cash is received, it is booked against the receivable.

© The Miscellaneous Sources category is higher year over year as the District collected on prior year-old receivables owed from another District.
The Business Office continues its attempt to collect old receivables even after the auditors suggest to write as uncollectable.

Expenditures:

¢ Actual General Fund Expenses totaled approximately $6.88 million for October. This brings the total year to date expenditures to $18,205,057
which was $1,632,323 million more than last year at this time.

¢ Included this month was an interest debt payment of $ 22,122 for the 2015 Capital Serial Bond.
