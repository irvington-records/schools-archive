INTE) ICIPAL AGREEMEN'

This Intermunicipal Agreement (“IMA”) made and entered into this 24th day of
November, 2025, by and among the IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT
(hereinafter referred to as “Irvington”), having its principal place of business located at 6 Dows
Lane, Irvington, New York 10533, and the DOBBS FERRY UNION FREE SCHOOL,
DISTRICT (hereinafter referred to as “Dobbs Ferry”) having their principal place of business
located at 505 Broadway, Dobbs Ferry, New York 10522, collectively referred to as “the
Parties”.

WHEREAS, the foregoing Parties have determined to enter into this IMA pursuant to
Education Law§§ 1709 and 3625 and New York General Municipal Law § 119 for the purpose
of establishing a sharing services and resources for the purposes of interscholastic athletics; and

WHEREAS, municipal corporations of the State of New York may enter into
agreements for the performance among themselves or one for the other of their respective
functions, powers and duties on a cooperative or contract basis; and

WHEREAS, in respect to the mutual consideration of the parties, as more fully set forth
herein, the Parties wish to share services and resources related to interscholastic fencing teams;
and

WHEREAS, the Parties have each determined that it would be in their best interests
and the best interests of their students to share services and resources related to interscholastic

fencing teams.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the
Parties agree as follows:

1. The Parties all represent to each other that they are authorized, pursuant to both
Article IX, Section 1 of the State Constitution and Article 5-G of the General Municipal Law to
enter into intergovernmental/intermunicipal agreements.

2. The Parties, believing it to be in the best interests of their taxpayers, to hereby
authorize intermunicipal cooperation among each other for the sharing of services and resources
related to interscholastic fencing teams.

3. Term. The term of this IMA shall commence on November 24, 2025, in
accordance with the calendars of the Parties, and terminate on March Ist, 2026.

4. Scope of services to be provided by Irvington. Irvington will provide the

following:

A. Access by Dobbs Ferry athletes to its fencing coaching staff;

o

Safe, scheduled access to training facilities;

Cc. Integration of Dobbs Ferry athletes into established practice plans and team
routines;

Dz. Qualified coaching staff to supervise and train all participating athletes; and

E. Communication of practice schedules, match schedules, team expectations and
safety requirements to Dobbs Ferry UFSD in a timely manner.

5. Scope of services to be provided by Dobbs Ferry Dobbs Ferry will provide
the following:
A. Equipment and uniforms for Dobbs Ferry fencing athletes

B. Transportation for all Dobbs Ferry fencing athletes to and from
Irvington for all practices and matches;

Cc. A chaperone at all matches (home and away) involving Dobbs Ferry athletes;

D. Dobbs Ferry athletes will follow all Irvington team rules, coaching
directions, safety protocols and NYSPHSAA/Section 1 regulations.

E. Reimbursement to Irvington pro rata based on the number of students on
the team for the costs associated with the fencing program, including, but
not limited to the cost of coaches, equipment and facility-related
expenses. Irvington will invoice Dobbs Ferry at the conclusion of the
season.

6. Each Participating District shall be responsible for obtaining any needed
permissions, consents or waivers from the New York Public High School Athletic Association.

7. The Parties shall comply and be responsible for compliance with all federal,
state, and local statutes and rules including, but not limited to, the rules and regulations of the
New York Public High School Athletic Association in regard to interscholastic competition of

its own fencing team.

8. Insurance. The Parties shall maintain in full force and effect during the Term of
this Agreement: (i) Workers’ Compensation Insurance, Employers Liability and New York State
Disability Benefits Insurance for the statutory limits as prescribed by the laws of the State of
New York; (ii) comprehensive general liability insurance (including sexual misconduct) with
limits of liability of $1 million for each occurrence, $2 million in the general aggregate and $2

million for products/completed operations; and (iii) Automobile Liability: $5,000,000 combined

[OCR]
single limit for owned, hired and borrowed and non-owned motor vehicles. Each party shall
provide a copy of the insurance certificates to the others at least ten (10) days prior to the
commencement of the term of this IMA and shall further provide prior written notice of the
cancellation, termination or modification of said policies. Dobbs Ferry agrees to name Irvington
as an additional insured.

9. In the event that any of the insurance coverage to be provided by any party
contains a deductible, the Parties shall indemnify and hold each other harmless from the
payment of such deductible, which deductible shall in all circumstances remain the sole
obligation and expense of each party.

10. The Parties shall provide each other with evidence of the above-referenced
insurance requirements upon execution of this IMA. The Parties further acknowledge that the
failure to obtain or keep current the insurance coverage required by this IMA shall constitute a
material breach of this IMA.

11. Prior to commencement of the within services, the Parties shall obtain and pay
for insurance as may be required to comply with the indemnification and hold harmless
provisions outlined under this IMA.

12. Notices: Any notices to be given under this IMA by any party to another may be
affected by personal delivery in writing or by mail, registered or certified, postage prepaid with
return receipt requested. Each party may change the address by written notice in accordance with
this paragraph. Notices delivered personally shall be deemed communicated as of actual receipt;
mailed notices shall be deemed communicated as of two (2) days after mailing. Notice shall be

13. Termination. This IMA may be terminated by any Participating District upon
thirty (30) days’ written notice to all other parties. In the event of termination, the Participating

District shall be liable for and make payment of all sums due and owing for services rendered up

[OCR]
to the effective date of termination by the Participating District.

This IMA may be terminated by Irvington upon 30 days’ written notice to each
Participating District at the time the notice of termination is issued by Irvington. Upon
termination, the Parties shall undertake all necessary steps to wind down activities hereunder.
The Parties will be liable for and make payment of all sums due and owing for services
rendered up to the effective date of termination.

14. Indemnification and Hold Harmless Provision. The Parties further agree that
each shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the others, its board of education officers,

directors, agents, and employees, for all loss, costs, damages and expenses, including attorneys’
fees, judgments, fines and amounts paid in settlement in connection with a terminated, pending

or completed action, suit or proceeding arising from any act, error or omission, neglect or breach
of duties by that party or any of its board members, officers, directors, or agents taken or made
with respect to this IMA.

15. Discrimination. Services provided pursuant to this IMA shall be provided
without regard to race, creed, color, sex, sexual orientation, national origin, religion, age or
disability.

16. | Governing Law. This IMA shall be governed by the laws of the State of New
York.

17. Severability. If any term, provision, covenant or condition of this IMA, or the
application thereof, to any person, place or circumstance shall be held by a court of competent
jurisdiction to be invalid, unenforceable or void, the remainder of this IMA and such term,
provision, covenant or condition as applied to other persons, places and circumstances shall
remain in full force and effect.

18. This IMA constitutes the full and complete IMA among the Parties and
supersedes all prior written and oral agreements, commitments or understanding with
respect hereto.

19. This IMA has been mutually arrived at and shall not be construed against any
party as being the drafter or causing this IMA to be drafted.

20. Any alteration, change, addition, deletion or modification of any of the
provisions of this IMA or any right any party has under this IMA shall be made by mutual
agreement of the Parties in writing and signed by all Parties.

21. This IMA may not be assigned by any party without the prior written

[OCR]
authorization of the non-assigning parties.

22. Nothing contained in this IMA shall be construed to create an employment or
principal-agent relationship or partnership or joint venture, among the Parties and any officer,
employee, servant, agent or independent contractor of any party; nor shall any party have the
right, power or authority to obligate or bind the other party in any manner whatsoever.

23. The undersigned representatives of the Parties represent and warrant that they
have the full legal rights, power and authority to enter into this IMA on behalf of the respective

school districts and bind the same with respect to the obligations and terms contained herein.

This IMA shall not become binding until approved by the Parties’ respective Boards of
Education by resolution at a duly convened public meeting.

24, This Agreement may be executed in more than one counterpart, each of which
will be deemed an original, but all of which will constitute one and the same instrument.

25. This Agreement may be executed with original signatures or by facsimile or
