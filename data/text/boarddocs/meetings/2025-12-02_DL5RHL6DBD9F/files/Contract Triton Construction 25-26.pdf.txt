Mrs. Stein,

Per your request, Triton Construction is happy to provide a proposal to continue our construction
management services for your school athletic field lighting and surface upgrades.

This proposal provides a part time level of services and staffing at an hourly rate, up to 20 hours a week,
as may be required for the successful completion of the work.

We propose the following:

We will provide one part-time on-site staff member during your construction project at a total cost not to
exceed $109,000.00. Services will be tracked on an hourly rate via timesheets and billed monthly.
Attached are the hourly rates which include all staff, operations costs, standard corporate insurances, and
earnings.

We will provide an Amendment to our previous executed Agreement,

SECOND AMENDMENT OF

AGREEMENT BETWEEN OWNER AND CONSTRUCTION

MANAGER

WHEREAS Irvington Union Free School District (hereinafter referred to as the
"Owner") with offices for the transaction of business located at 40 North Broadway
Irvington, NY 10533 and Triton Construction Company LLC. (hereinafter referred to as the
“Construction Manager” and/or “Triton”) with offices located at 550 7" Ave. 14th Floor, New
York, New York 10018 entered into an Agreement for Construction Management Services,
dated May 2018 (the "Agreement"); and

WHEREAS the Owner and Construction Manager wish to amend the Agreement to
expand the scope of services by the Construction Manager for additional compensation.

WHEREAS the parties agreed that the Agreement was amended under the first
Amendment as follows:

1. The Construction Manager will be compensated for providing 4 additional months
of full-time on-site construction supervision from June 15, 2022 thru October 15,
2022 and 2 Months of pre-construction services, both of which is beyond the
Construction Management services as set forth in the Agreement.

a.

The additional compensation paid by the Owner for the 4 additional months of
Construction services will be $23,493.00 paid monthly, for 4 Months, for a total
of $93,972.00.

The additional compensation paid by the Owner for the 2 additional months of
pre-construction services will be $11,308.00 paid monthly, for 2 Months, for a
total of $22,616.00.

The total additional compensation paid to the Construction Manager will be
$116,588.00.

There will be no retention withheld from these payments.

2. All terms and conditions of the Agreement, as amended by the First Amendment,
shall remain in full force and effect.

NOW THEREFORE, the parties agree that the Agreement shall be amended a second
time as follows:

1. The Construction Manager will be compensated for providing additional part-time
on-site construction supervision which is beyond the Construction Management
services as set forth in the Agreement.

The additional compensation paid by the Owner for the additional
Construction services to complete the work will be an agreed upon hourly rate,
to be paid monthly (see Hourly Rate of Staff Attachment A).

The total additional compensation paid to the Construction Manager for the
