                              INTER-MUNICIPAL AGREEMENT

        THIS AGREEMENT made and entered into this 25th day of March, 2026, by and between
the BOARD OF EDUCATION OF SCARSDALE UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT, with
offices located at 2 Brewster Road, Scarsdale NY 10583 (hereinafter referred to as “Scarsdale”)
and the BOARD OF EDUCATION OF IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT
(hereinafter referred to as “Irvington”), with offices at 6 Dows Lane, NY 10533.

                                       W I T N E S S E T H:

        WHEREAS, Scarsdale and Irvington are each duly organized as a school district under the
laws of the State of New York, and as such are legally authorized to transport their resident children
to and from extracurricular activities and programs funded by each school district; and

        WHEREAS, Scarsdale and Irvington are authorized under the laws of the State of New
York to enter into contracts with each other to share the costs of transporting their resident children
to and from extracurricular activities and programs funded by each school district; and

      WHEREAS, Scarsdale and Irvington mutually desire to transport their students to and from
a computer programming competition sponsored by Siena College for high school students across
New York State.

        NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY AGREED, by and between Scarsdale and Irvington
as follows:

       1.      Scarsdale will arrange to transport students from Scarsdale High School’s Coding
               Club and Irvington High School’s Computer Science Class, as well as each school’s
               faculty or club’s faculty advisors, participating in the Siena College computer
               programming competition to and from the competition scheduled for Friday, April
               17, 2026.

       2.      Irvington agrees to reimburse Scarsdale for its proportionate share of the mileage
               and costs to Scarsdale for providing the bus and driver.

       3.      In the event of an accident, the parties agree that Scarsdale will be responsible for
               communicating with the appropriate law enforcement authorities, preparing and
               filing all forms, and communicating with Irvington to ensure the parents/guardians
               of the students riding the bus are notified in a prompt and timely manner.

       4.      Each party shall, to the maximum extent permitted by law, defend, indemnify and
               hold harmless the other party for any demand, claim, action, proceeding, judgment,
               costs or expenses, including reasonable attorney’s fees, which arise from the
               performance of services under this Agreement, unless it arises from the indemnified
               party’s negligence.

5.      Each party will be responsible during the term of this Agreement for purchasing
        and maintaining comprehensive automobile liability insurance to provide their
        respective students and faculty advisers with protection from and against claims for
        damages due to bodily injury, sickness, death, and/or property damage, including
        the loss of use resulting therefrom, which arises from the services being performed
        and furnished hereunder. Each party shall, prior to the commencement of services
        under this Agreement, deliver to the other party a certificate of insurance that
        evidences that such insurance is in full force and effect, and shall maintain such
        insurance throughout the term of this Agreement.


6.     This Agreement is effective for the period March, 2026 through June 30, 2026.

7.      Any modification of this Agreement must be in writing and executed with the
     same formality as the Agreement herein.
