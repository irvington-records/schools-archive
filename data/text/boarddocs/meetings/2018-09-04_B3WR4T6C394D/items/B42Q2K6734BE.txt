Category: 7. Nomination and Election of 2nd Vice President to the Board
Subject: 7.1 Nomination and Election of 2nd Vice President to the Board
Type: Action

The Board President will seek the nomination for the 2nd Vice President of the Board of Education for the 2018-19 school year. Should there be more than one nominee, the member with the majority of votes will thereby be appointed vice president. The Board President shall administer the Oath of Office to the 2nd Vice President.
