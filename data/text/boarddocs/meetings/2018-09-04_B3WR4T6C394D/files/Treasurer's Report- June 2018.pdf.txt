                                                       Monthly Financial Highlights

                                                                    June 2018
Cash Balance
    We ended the fiscal year with a cash position of $8,323, 918, of which $7,871,326 is in the General Fund. This position reflect our committed
       and undesignated reserves and we will be “borrowing from our reserves” for cash flow to support operations until our tax revenue comes in
       October.
      Our School Lunch account is healthy with a balance of $181,105. We will be utilizing some of our fund balance for new equipment for our
       food services program.
Revenue:
    We ended the year with $60,397,426 or 101% of the budgeted amount. This is slightly higher than the budget due to prior year State aid
       payments based on actual expenditures from the prior years.
Expenditures:
    Actual General Fund Expenses for June totaled approximately $8.6 million, which is slightly higher than prior month due to the balloon
       payment. The balloon payment allows the teachers to elect to receive their pay checks in 25 pays by including 4 payroll checks versus the 21
       pay from September through June. This brings the total year to date expenditures to $59,208,259. The remaining encumbrances of $18,683
       will be carried over to 2018-19 and the remaining amount of $691,874 will go toward fund balance and reserves.
