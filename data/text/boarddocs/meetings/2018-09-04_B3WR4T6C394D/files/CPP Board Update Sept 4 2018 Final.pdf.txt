 ● Gather accurate information to make informed decisions

 ●   Allow time for community feedback
 ●   SEQRA process
 ●   Allow enough time to create Board consensus

Project Timeline (After the Successful Vote)
June 2019 - December 2019*
 ● H2M prepares detailed construction documents
January 2020 - October 2020*
 ● State Education Department (SED) reviews, approves and issues building
      permits
November 2020 - March 2021*
 ● Bidding and Awards
June 2021
 ● Large-scale construction starts
Some smaller projects might be able to begin in Summer 2019 and 2020.
 ● Less involved construction documents and/or SED review required
 ● Fast track process for SED if available
*Some more in-depth projects will require a longer period for construction documents and thus will push back
these dates. However, the period for bidding & permitting is fairly long and the target of June 2021 should still be
attainable. Alternatively, a final construction phase of June 2022 may be targeted.                                8
