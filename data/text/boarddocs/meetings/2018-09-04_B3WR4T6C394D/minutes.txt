3. Administrator's Update

Information:  3.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison, Superintendent of Schools, remarked on an exciting first day of school and reported that transportation ran smoothly/pretty close to on-time for first day. On his tours through all the schools, the excitement and school spirit were evident.

He also talked about a district-wide meeting that was held last week on all facets of security. A representative from our security advisory company, Altaris, gave a presentation that was well received. Dr. Harrison also spoke about upgrades that are planned to make our schools safer including installation of security cameras and ongoing training. He asked that we all check our school calendars and school district newletters for upcoming events. Dr. Harrison was contacted by the Village Administrator on behalf of the Governor's office inviting him and a group of students to the opening ceremony at the Governor Mario M. Cuomo Bridge which he noted was a great opportunity for the District and these students to be part of New York State history.

Dr. Raina Kor, Superintendent for Curriculum/HR gave a brief update on some work that went on over the summer. Nancy Deakin led a two-day summer Institute on Literacy and Christine Rosner led a two-day Math Institute. Dr. Kor noted that it's this kind of work that lays the foundation for work that can be done for the year, securing a partnership with teachers ensuring that instruction will be applied from day one.

Carol Stein, Assistant Superintendent for Business, spoke about the updates to the food services program: Dows Lane has a full serve which includes all 5 food groups and two hot entree choices. The Campus cafeteria has also made some chages to its serving area, highlighting the salad bar a little more. The deli bar now offers paninis. She said internal marketing will be done to let the families know of the changes this year and there already is information regarding these changes on the website.

Michael Hanna, Board President, mentioned that NYSSBA reached out with an invitation for Board Presidents to have an opportunity to meet with Congresswoman Nita Lowey. He attended the meeting at her White Plains office. It was a good opportunity to talk about concerns regarding federal tax issues as well as security concerns.

4. Discussions/Presentations

Presentation:  4.1 Capital Project Update

Dr. Harrison and Carol Stein gave an in-depth overview of the Capital Project plans and goals, including work to date.  In addition to enhancing security, Dr. Harrison said they are looking into how to improve our facilities to accomodate learning styles as well as managing repairs and updates that are needed/anticipated. Energy-efficient and eco-friendly improvements are also under consideration.

Veronica Byrnes, Senior Architect from H2M architects + engineers explained the process and timeline involved in the bond process. She explained the difference in "Type 1", "Type II" and "Unlisted"  SEQRA project classifications and answered questions regarding the bond process and timeline.

Carol Stein gave a presentation on the project, scope of work identified to date and changing vote date to allow the recommended time to get cost estimates and community feedback.

After a successful vote, H2M will provide detailed construction documents between June 2019 and December 2019.

January 2020-October 2020: State Education Department (SED) reviews, approves and issues building permits

November 2020 - March 2021: Bidding and Awards

June 2021: Large-scale construction begins.

Some smaller projects might be able to begin in summer 2018 and 2020.

Michael Hanna spoke about the scope of the process, that we must be "forward thinking" in planning for work needed to carry the district through the next decade.
