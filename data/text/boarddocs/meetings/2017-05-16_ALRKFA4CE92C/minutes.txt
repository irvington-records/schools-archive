4. Business of the Board

Action:  4.1 Set Time and Date of Special Election

Resolved, that at the May 16, 2017 Annual Meeting, the residents of the Irvington Union Free School District approved the referendum to increase the Irvington UFSD School Board from the current five (5) members to seven (7) members for the terms beginning July 12, 2017 - June 30, 2020; and

Be It Further Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education hereby sets the Special Meeting to elect two (2) new members of the Irvington Board of Education for Tuesday, July 11, 2017 from 7:00am to 9:00pm at the Main Street School, 101 Main Street, Irvington, New York.

Motion by Phil Whitney, second by David Graeber.

Yes: Phil Whitney, Catherine Palmieri, Michael Hanna, David Graeber
