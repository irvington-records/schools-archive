4. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Report: 4.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison reported that:

- the school year has gotten off to a great start and thanked the staff and administration for their contributions in making the first days a success;

- the school closure on Friday, September 8th was due to a police search taking place in the Irvington woods and noted that he was in communication with the police in the early morning and the matter had  not yet been resolved when it was necessary to make the call to close schools;

- highlighed the upcoming events in the District, including open houses, and an important program on September 27th which is sponsored by the Community Advisory Board and iASK, targeting drug use and prevention. The program will be held at the Town Hall Theater at 7:00pm. The evening will provide an overview of two years worth of student surveys and followed with a panel of experts to discuss related topics. This will be a first in a series of programs that the District is committed to bring to the community over the next several years;

- a booster club is being formed, a not-for-profit organization to support our athetics program and to bolster bulldog spirit;

- WiFi is being installed at the MSS and will allow more technology in the classrooms.

Dr. Kor reported that NY State has now reduced testing from three to two days which will require tjhat the school calendar be revisited to determine which two days make the most sense to hold the testing. At this time the administrators are discussing the options and will communicate to the community. As an aside, she noted that the District encourages parents to send children to school on testing days, especially in light of this reduction, and believes the testing data does inform programming.

Carol Stein updated the Board on transportation with respect to balancing the routes to help alleviate the early start time at the high school and man parents felt there were positive changes, but to totally alleviate the early buses would require adding additional buses which would have a budgetary impact. The first few days did go smoother than in past years. With respect to registration of new students, in a two week period prior to school, 21 students registered as well as two kindergartners. 1749 was the enrollment at the start of school.
