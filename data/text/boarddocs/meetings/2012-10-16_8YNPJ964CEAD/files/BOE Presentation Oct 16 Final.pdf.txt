Annual Professional Performance Review
Teacher evaluation system based on the following
 values and components:
   60% - Classroom observation based on New York State
    approved rubric as well as other measures which reflect
    teacher practice including portfolios, evidence binders
    and goal setting.
   20% - State Assessment Data or District Developed
    Student Learning Objectives (SLO).
   20% - Locally selected measures of achievement using
    standardized data, collected through a consistent and
    organized system.

                  What the
     New York State Education Department
       Hopes Will Come from This . . .
 By revamping our evaluation and professional development
  programs, we’re taking steps to ensure that every student
  has world-class educators in the classroom, and that every
  teacher has world-class principals to support them and help
  them grow professionally.
 This new performance management system ties together
  three priorities that are naturally interdependent: measuring
  performance, recognizing outstanding work, and providing
  teachers and principals with focused professional
  development and mentoring.

  Based on the selected domain, explain your goal
  using the SMART Goal Model (see below)
 My goal is to focus on the various assessments I use in the _____ grade math
  program – these will include both assessments of learning and assessments for
  learning. It is important to assess student learning prior to teaching so that the
  lessons are planned to match the needs of the students. It is also critical to be aware
  of how well the different individuals in the classroom are grasping the concepts and
  in what particular area each person needs further explanation and practice; this
  requires ongoing assessment. This assessment needs to be tied into the instruction
  and, in fact, be part of the instruction so that learning is being achieved through the
  assessment.
 With the arrival of the Common Core Learning Standards there is greater emphasis
  placed on understanding the deeper mathematical concepts in addition to being able
  to arrive at the correct answers. Therefore, the assessments have to be designed to
  reveal if a student has learned the “why” as well as the “how.” These assessments
  may be brief and targeted, or they may be larger projects in which students play an
  active role in the design itself; in addition, students benefit from being part of
  developing a rubric used to evaluate them, giving them ownership with a clear
  understanding of the desired outcomes.
 Finally, it is necessary to have assessments at the end of a unit so the instructor
  knows if the end goals have been met or if they will need further explanation later in
  the year, rather than just refreshing. Assessments are a critical part of the learning
  process from start to finish, and my goal is to use them more effectively this year.

What is your plan for achieving your goal(s)?
 Plan assessments prior to unit and have a clear idea of how students
  will be assessed throughout
 Refine my own project ideas to give me a keener understanding of
  how individuals are progressing
 Investigate creative ways in which assessments are already being
  implemented by speaking to other professionals
 Explore websites and subscribe to a professional journal to learn of
  what new ideas are being developed and to what purpose
 Encourage students to play an active role in assessment design
 Keep individual records of student performance
 Require students to reflect upon their own strengths and
  shortcomings through the Learning Lab model
 Reflect on the effectiveness of the assessments and considering how
  they may be improved for future instruction                   Reflective

What evidence will you provide to demonstrate that
you have achieved your goal(s)?
 I will include assessments that I have used at different times of
  instruction, evidence of knowledge gathered from other professionals
  as well as professional writing, student work that was used in the

  record keeping. This evidence will be developed and collected
