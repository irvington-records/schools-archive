WPSBA DISCUSSION OF GOVERNOR’S BUDGET PROPOSAL

Governor offered $991 million in restored education aid.
Various state education groups including NYSSBA, Superintendents, and Business Officials are
requesting around $2.2-2.4 Billion.
Ultimately the final number is negotiated by the two leaders of the two legislative branches and
the Governor. The higher education funding is as a priority the more likely the number is
increased, as happened to certain degree last year.

Additionally Governor’s proposed budget includes $150 million for a tax credit. This syphons
money from public to private schools. High wealth donors can donate up to $1 million to private
/ parochial schools.

Separately, a parochial group is asking for an increase in publicly subsidized transportation from
15 to 25 miles with 90% reimbursement. As we’ll review during the budget process, this is not
an insignificant cost.

STATE REGENTS

Chancellor Meryll Tisch, an At-large Regent is leaving, along with Regent Bottar of Syracuse.
Applications by candidates has closed. New Regents are elected by the General Assembly.
Apparently the vote will occur on March 8th. Typically the At-large Regent is from NYC as they
have a large delegation in the assembly. There’s been a bit of a shake-up with the Regents over
the past 2 years with much more robust discussion about education policy. Depending on who is
selected this may further advance that trend.
