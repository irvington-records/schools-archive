             IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT
                       Board of Education

                                     Policy Committee
                                      February 1, 2016

                                       Minutes/Notes


The Committee met from 8 – 9:30 am. Catherine Palmieri, Kris Harrison, Raina Kor, Beverly
Miller, Elaine Cardea, and Scott Palermo were in attendance.

Focus of Work:

The committee has begun its review of the NYSSBA proposed policy revisions that are a
part of the policy manual overhaul.

Leading up to this meeting, the administration reviewed and made edits to NYSSBA
proposed policies. These policies were uploaded to Google Docs to allow committee
members to pose comments and suggest edits in advance of the meeting.

These policies have been written to include Irvington-specific details; include information
pertaining to current revisions or additions to regulations and law; and, where possible,
have melded multiple policies to reduce the overall number of polices.

During the meeting the committee began to review section 0000, which include policies
on:
     Mission statement and vision (on hold pending completion of strategic planning
      process)
     Equal opportunity and Nondiscrimination
     Sexual harassment
     Sexual harassment and bullying prevention
     HIV/AIDS
     Accountability
     Board self-evaluation
     Evaluation of the Superintendent

The Committee completed approximately 50% of this section.

Next Steps:
The Committee will meet again on February 24th at 4:30. During this meeting, we will
complete section 0000 and begin the review of 1000.

Once a section is completed, it will be forwarded to the entire BOE and then added to an
agenda for the appropriate readings.
