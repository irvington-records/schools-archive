                 IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT
                           Board of Education

                                    Communications Committee
                                        February 3, 2016

                                            Minutes/Notes


The Committee met from 1-2 pm. Phil Whitney, Michael Hanna, Kris Harrison, and Jesse Lubinsky
were in attendance.

Focus of Work:

The Committee has been focused on enhancing the District’s digital communication tools such as
the Website, Mobile page, Facebook page, and newsletters.

Website
Following discussion at a prior meeting, school staff has taken steps to:

       remove outdated or irrelevant content
       reorganize and prioritize information
       improve the “mapping” associated with the information available on the mobile page to
        improve the user experience

The Committee discussed future enhancements for the website which is to include:

       further “cleaning”
       possible selection of new content management system to replace Edline
            o This would occur at future date
            o More information will be forthcoming

Site Analytics

The committee also discussed website analytics as Jesse installed Google Analytics on the site.
While the tool did register a few thousand “hits” each day, it is unable to measure the depth of use
due to limitations associated with Edline’s structure.

Facebook Page

The page continues to provide a good balance of news and updates from across the District. The
committee discussed the possibility of shortening posts and adding more voice.

Next Steps

The Committee will meet again after winter break to continue its dialogue about the digital
communication tools and will also discuss budget and bond project communications.
