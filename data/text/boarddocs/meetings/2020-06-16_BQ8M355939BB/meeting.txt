* 7:30 p.m. - Anticipated Executive Session
* Annual Meeting will take place immediately following the canvassing of the absentee ballots for the 2020-2021 Budget Vote and Member Election
The community can observe the canvassing of the Budget Vote & School Board Election ballots through the following video-only link:
https://zoom.us/j/94836079319?pwd=ZFVBbTJHSVNSL1BlZGNOSUhFOXVmQT09

Then, the Board of Education will meet to accept the results of the vote/election. Due to the fact that the District does not know when the canvassing will be concluded, it is estimating that at 9:00 p.m. the BOE will meet to accept the results of the election.
