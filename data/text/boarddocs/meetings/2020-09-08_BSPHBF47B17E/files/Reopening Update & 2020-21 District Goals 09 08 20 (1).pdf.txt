       Superintendent’s Conference Days
Three Superintendent’s Conference Days were held, September 2-4, to
prepare for the reopening of school. During these days, professional
learning/training was offered in the following areas:
   ●   Health and safety
   ●   Social and emotional needs/learning
   ●   New school/District protocols and procedures
   ●   Technology
   ●   Instructional design to support hybrid and distance learning
   ●   Equity, diversity and inclusion

     Chromebook Distribution
The District will ensure equity and access to learning for all students by
providing each student with a Chromebook and internet access, where
needed.
●   Due to manufacturer's’ production delay, the IUFSD implemented a modiﬁed

     ○   Students without devices at home for school use
     ○   Students enrolled in 100% distance learning
     ○   IMS students (due to 1:1 program)
     ○   Teachers and aides to deliver instruction

●   861 Chromebooks distributed to date
●   Once all Chromebooks are received (ETA 2 weeks), another distribution will
    be facilitated

    Daily Health Screening Requirements
Each student and staff member will be required to complete a daily
health self-screening questionnaire prior to leaving for school/work.

●   App is available for download in the Apple App Store and Google play

●   Web-based application posted on the website

●   Students for whom the screening was not completed will not be
    permitted to attend class until health is veriﬁed
     ○   Repeat occurrences will result in parents having to pick student up from school

       Interscholastic Athletics
IUFSD will follow all guidance issued by NYSDOH, NYSPHSAA & Section 1
● High School Cross Country, Field Hockey, Soccer, and Girl’s Tennis are permitted to begin Tuesday,
  September 29th
    ○ No open workouts or practices are authorized prior to September 29th
    ○ Section 1 guidance on games forthcoming
● High risk fall sports (Football & Volleyball) and their ability to practice is being reviewed by Section 1
    ○ No high risk practices/games until Governor gives consent
● Section 1 Modiﬁed Committee will meet to evaluate the status of modiﬁed fall sports
● Season schedules to be modiﬁed per Section 1 guidance
● Medical Clearance for participation
    ○ All students must have previously approved health examination for school attendance from the
      2018-2019 or 2019-2020 school years
    ○ Parental permission and updated health history form on FamilyID within 30 days of sport start date.
● Speciﬁc Athletics Department health & safety protocols will be implemented
● Training to be provided to all coaches by the Athletic Director, Athletic Trainer and School Nurse
● Speciﬁc details to be sent to parents and student-athletes by the Athletics Department                       10

     Overarching Goal
The IUFSD primary goal is to:

 ● Provide for the health and safety of every student and staff member

 ● Support students’ and staff members’ emotional well-being

 ● Support teachers in the delivery of hybrid and remote instruction

 ● Ensure a successful reopening

          Primary Areas of Focus District-wide
The following three goals have been developed and are aligned with the District
Theories of Action and Strategic Objectives:
Goal 1:   Develop a well-articulated, aligned K-12 Social-Emotional Learning (SEL) plan.
           ● District-wide professional learning and planning supported by CASEL

Goal 2:   Create a welcoming school community for all students by ensuring that IUFSD programs and
          experiences are equitable and provide access to opportunities for all students, especially Black,
          Indengenous and People of Color (BIPOC)
           ● District-wide professional learning supported by NYU Metropolitan Center
           ● Root cause analysis
           ● Development of a strategic plan

Goal 3:   Assessment: District-wide focus on measuring: student growth and success, program
          effectiveness and the use of data to inform instruction
           ● Professional development and support by Diane Cunningham and IDE.
