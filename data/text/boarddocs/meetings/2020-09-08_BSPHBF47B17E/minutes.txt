Action (Consent): 10.5 Tax Certiorari Settlement - Cornelia Bagarotti

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District is a respondent in certain tax certiorari proceedings filed by Cornelia Bagarotti, pertaining to property identified on the tax roll as Section 1.200, Block 120, Lot 1.L7 and Section 7.230, Block 101, Lot 4.L1 for tax years 2018 through 2020; and WHEREAS, the parties have negotiated a tentative resolution of the proceedings, as set forth in the consent judgement attached hereto; and WHEREAS, the Board has determined that it is in the best interests of the School District to resolve the proceeding in accordance with the Consent Judgement without further litigation; NOW, BASED UPON THE FORGOING, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED that the Board hereby approves the settlement of the tax proceedings as set forth in the Consent Judgement; AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that Ingerman Smith, LLP is authorized to execute the consent judgement attached hereto, and any other relevant settlement papers necessary to effectuate the settlement; AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the School Business Manager shall be authorized to issue a refund payment in a sum not to exceed $56,458.33 in accordance with the Consent Judgement.
