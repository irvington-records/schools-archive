Audit Committee of the Irvington Union Free School District (Tuesday, February 9, 2016)

	Generated by Elaine Cardea on Friday, February 12, 2016

	Members present

	Phil Whitney, Catherine Palmieri, Robert Grados, John Montgomery, Michael Hanna

	Also present

	Kristopher Harrison, Superintendent of Schools, Beverly Miller, Asst. Supt. for Business

	Procedural: Call to Order

	Mr. Whitney called the Audit Committee to order at 10:10pm.

	Discussion: 2015-16 Internal Audit

	The purpose of the Audit Committee meeting was to have the Committee determine what area of focus the District's internal auditor should focus on for the 2015-16 Risk Assessment Audit. Beverly Miller, Asst. Supt. for Business, outlined the various options for the Board's consideration:

-
		high risk - security controls in information technology (Ms. Miller noted that all districts have this designation)

-
		payroll/personnel

-
		accounts payable/cash distribution

-
		extra classroom activities fund

-
		food service - point of sale system

	The Committee discussed the possible areas to focus the audit on, including special education, technology security, effectiveness of the District's bid process. Ultimately, the Board agreed to have the risk assessment audit focus on special education compliance as it relates to reporting, auditing of revenue and receipts to ensure that all monies due or owed are in order.

	Action: Adjourn Audit Committee Meeting

	Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education adjourn the February 9, 2016 Audit Committee Meeting.

	Motion by Robert Grados, second by Catherine Palmieri.

	Final Resolution: Motion Carries

	Yes: Phil Whitney, Catherine Palmieri, Robert Grados, John Montgomery, Michael Hanna
