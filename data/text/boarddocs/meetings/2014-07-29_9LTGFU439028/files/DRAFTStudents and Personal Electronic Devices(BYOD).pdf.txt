SUBJECT: STUDENTS AND PERSONAL ELECTRONIC DEVICES BRING YOUR
OWN DEVICE (BYOD)


        The Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District recognizes that there
are personal electronic devices that have educational applications such as tablets, I-Pads, e-
readers, calculators, voice recorders, digital cameras, and music listening devices. In some
instances a “smart phone” may include applications that permit these functions. These personal
electronic devices shall be allowed to be used in classrooms if the following criteria are met:

              The teacher has authorized use of personal electronic devices either in his/her
               classroom generally or for a particular exercise.
              The student uses the personal device to access the Internet or authorized
               applications through the District’s network, under the terms of its Computer Use
               in Instruction.

        The Board acknowledges that personal electronic devices such as laptops, tablet
computers, e-readers, cellular phones, pagers, and 2-way communication systems can be a
positive means to facilitate communication; however, the display and/or use of such devices can
cause disruption to the educational process.

       Therefore, all disruptive use of personal electronic devices is prohibited during school
hours unless otherwise authorized. Such disruptive may include, but is not limited to:

       a) Any use in a classroom, laboratory, library, study hall, gymnasium, and/or other
          designated educational area.
       b) Any use in hallways, corridors, stairways, elevators, bathrooms, and/or other building
          common areas.
       c) Permitted use that interferes with or otherwise disrupts the telecommunications,
          computer network, and/or other electronic operating systems of the Irvington Union
          Free School District.
       d) Any use during any local test or State assessment. During any local test or State
          assessment, all personal electronic devices, unless otherwise permitted during a local
          test, shall be rendered powered off and assigned proctors, monitors, and school
          officials shall have the right to collect such prohibited personal electronic devices
          prior to the administration of, and for the duration of the test or assessment.

Therefore, and to prevent such disruption to the educational process, the display and/or use of
personal electronic devices shall be governed by the Code of Conduct and/or student handbooks
issued by each school.

        The Irvington Union Free School District is neither responsible nor liable for any stolen,
lost and/or damaged personal electronic device.

        Misuse of any personal electronic devices will result in its confiscation. Moreover, some
uses of personal electronic devices may constitute a violation of the District’s Code of Conduct,
and in some instances, a violation of local, State, and/or federal law. To the extent any use of
personal electronic devices constitute a violation of local, State, and/or federal law, the District
shall cooperate with law enforcement officials as appropriate and necessary.
