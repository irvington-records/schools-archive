                           IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT
                                   2013-14 District Goals


Instructional Goal:
Establish high expectations and accountability for quality teaching in every classroom and for every
student.
      Regularly address issues regarding underperforming teachers, to optimize student learning.

Curriculum Goal:
Enhance curricula and instruction to promote academic excellence.
    Continue to adapt the existing curricula with the Common Core Learning Standards (CCLS) and
       related mandates, while maintaining higher standards where possible;
      Identify essential areas of curricular and instructional need and develop plans for improvement;
      Assess the use of technology in instruction and develop coherent K-12 technology curriculum
       action plan and begin implementation where possible;
      Assess District’s approach to college preparedness and admissions, and initiate improvements;
      Improve curriculum communications with the community in order to create better understanding
       of the changes in curriculum and to encourage home and school partnerships.

Facilities Goal:
Address facility needs to ensure District buildings and grounds are conducive to programmatic needs.
     Determine the most appropriate use of the existing school facilities, and utilize the 2012-13 Space
         Utilization Study;
      Develop an action plan for the athletic facilities/fields.

Long-Range Planning Goal:
Devise long-range plans to guide future work across the District.
    Define the indicators of a successful school experience;
      Review and refine the mission and vision of the District;
      Create meaningful instructional and strategic objectives to inform the efforts of the Board of
       Education, Administration, Faculty and Staff;
      Evaluate the District’s financial health and devise a framework to inform future financial
       decisions;
      Identify opportunities for enhancing the District’s operations and infrastructure.




Adopted: November 5, 2013
