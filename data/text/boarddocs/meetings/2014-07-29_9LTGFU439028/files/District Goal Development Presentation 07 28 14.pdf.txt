2014-15: Goal Discussion
           July 29, 2014




                           1

The mission of the Irvington School District is to create a
challenging and supportive learning environment in which
each student attains his or her highest potential for academic
achievement, critical thinking and life-long learning. Our
schools encourage the discovery and development of
students’ individual strengths, skills and talents, and foster
social and civic responsibility.




                                                                 2

Instructional Goal:
Establish high expectations and accountability for quality teaching in every
classroom and for every student.

Curriculum Goal:
Enhance curricula and instruction to promote academic excellence.
 Continue to adapt the existing curricula with the Common Core Learning
  Standards (CCLS) and related mandates, while maintaining higher
  standards where possible;
 Identify essential areas of curricular and instructional need and develop
  plans for improvement;
 Assess the use of technology in instruction and develop a coherent K-12
  technology curriculum action plan and begin implementation where
  possible;
 Assess District’s approach to college preparedness and admissions, and
  initiate improvements;
 Improve curriculum communications with the community in order to
  create better understanding of the changes in curriculum and to
  encourage home and school partnerships.


                                                                               3

Facilities Goal:
Address facility needs to ensure District buildings and grounds are
conducive to programmatic needs.
 Determine the most appropriate use of the existing school
  facilities, and utilize the 2012-13 Space Utilization Study;
 Develop an action plan for the facilities/athletic fields.


Long-Range Planning Goal:
Devise long-range plans to guide future work across the District.
 Define the indicators of a successful school experience;
 Review and refine the mission and vision of the District;
 Create meaningful instructional and strategic objectives to inform
  the efforts of the Board of Education, Administration, Faculty and
  Staff;
 Evaluate the District’s financial health and devise a framework to
  inform future financial decisions;
 Identify opportunities for enhancing the District’s operations and
  infrastructure.
