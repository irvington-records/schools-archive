	10. Presentations/Discussions

	Discussion: 10.1 Discussion of Draft Policies: Student & Personal Electronic Devices/Bring Your Own Device (BYOD); Field Trips & Excursions; Computer use in Instruction Acceptance Use Policy

	Mr. Whitney noted that the only new policy that Board is being asked to adopted is the Bring Your Own Device (BYOD) policy. The purpose of discussion is for the Board to make any final comments and editing suggestions prior to the final version presented for approval at the August Board Meeting. Comments were made regarding the language in the BYOD that pertains to the disruptive use and asks that the language be edited for clarity as well as a clearer definition of "disruptive". Mr. Grados will work on the language from a legal standpoint. The District's intent was to create a matrix in which teachers will allow permission for their students to bring their own device for the first year in order for teachers to adjust to such use in their classes. Mrs. Kerner is concerned that a student could have a majority of teachers that will permit the use but one that doesn't and it will be necessary to identify a process for a parent or student to have a discussion if they feel it necessary to utilize a device. Mrs. Kashkin commented on the need to be a consistent policy with regard to use e-readers. Dr. Harrison agreed that this will be a learning process with changes as needed.

	Mr. Montgomery noted that he is uncomfortable with the fact that costs and benefits of technology in education haven’t been defined and would like to have analysis before instituting a policy that provides for students utilizing technology more extensively.

	There was also discussion on the intent and interpretation of the policy, with Mrs. Kashkin believing that the purpose of the policy was to acknowledge that students have devices that they might use in the classroom and not speaking to instructional use by teachers whereas  Mr. Montgomery interprets the policy as opening the door for teachers to utilize devices in their classroom as part of their instruction and he would not be comfortable to allow that until there is further analysis of the benefits. Dr. Harrison stated that the purpose of the policy was to aid students in their personal style of learning. Mr. Whitney believes that there needs to be more substance in the policy and it doesn't address how this policy will be used, therefore creating the concern on the part of the Board. Mr. Montgomery also noted that the policy doesn't provide for what is appropriate at different levels, i.e. elementary vs. secondary and should provide that information. Mr. Grados believed that the intent was only at the middle/high school level and that it should be stated within the policy. Dr. Harrison agrees that there needs to be a supplemental document and/or regulation but also feels that we must prepare our students for their future education and life outside of school and to provide many experiences. He noted that many school districts are moving forward with providing for technology in the classroom. He also noted that teachers have been permitting this type of use for years and that the policy is formalizing and in some ways protecting the District.

	Another concern voiced by Mr. Montgomery is the issue of equity, whether students are financially unable or parents do not want child to bring the device, that we must be able to provide devices to students. Mr. Whitney would like to have another discussion on August 26th and vote to adopt at the September Board meeting.

	Policy #3160 – Computer Use in Instruction Acceptable Use Policy – A few edits were suggested to both the policy and regulation which will be made.

	Discussion: 10.2 Discussion of 2014-15 District Goals

	Mr. Whitney would like to have the District goals adopted much sooner this year in order to send a clear message to staff as to what the focus of the school year will be. Dr. Harrison noted that the District continues to stand by many of the objectives that were established last year, especially with regards to the curriculum related goals. Last year, the facilities goals focused on addressing facility needs and the community will be asked to vote on a referendum to address the needs, therefore he believes the goal can be eliminated at this time, however, the long-range planning should be continued for the 2014-15 school year. Also discussed was reinstituting curriculum review cycle.

	Robyn Kerner noted that she would like to see goals more as a reach, above and beyond, not day to day responsibilities when using words like assess, and how to set ourselves apart from others and enhance our students’ education. In looking at 2013-14 goals, Mr. Whitney would like to tighten them up to use for the 2014-15 and possibly make them more aspirational as opposed to task oriented
