5. Presentation/Discussion

Discussion: 5.1 Instructional Technology and Project Lead the Way (PLTW)

Dr. Kor and Jesse Lubinsky gave an in-depth presentation titled: Technology: Moving Forward.  Dr. Kor noted how far the District has come with technology in our schools in the last five years. This presentation focused on four (4) areas:

1. Vision and Goals

2. Instructional Focus

3. Policies and Compliance

4. Infrastructure

Dr. Kor spoke about our vision for Technology Moving Forward: All students should possess essential technology skills and participate in critical technology learning experiences that will equip them for success in our schools and prepare them for the challenges of their future.

Jesse Lubinsky spoke about the 2014-2018 Technology Framework Goals 1-7 and how they are being applied across the district:

Goal #1: Mobile Student Centered Computing -- All learners will have access to technology in an anytime, anywhere, one-to-one computing environment as appropriate and relevant to instructional goals.

Goal #2: Interactive Multimedia Capable Peripherals--All learners will have access to technology that allows for consumption and interaction with a variety of information and multimedia.

Goal #3: Professional Development--Professional Development (PD) will be a top priority for the success of all learners. PD must be integrated within all content areas and grade levels. Also, PD must be ongoing due to the simultaneous learning of how to use technology, the integration of technology in instruction, and the continual emergence of new and improved technologies and practices. PD must be differentiated to address the needs, aptitudes, and styles of adult learners. It is expected that all staff members will seek out professional development with a technology structure that engages, encourages, and empowers all learners.

Goal #4: Electronic Resources--Electronic resources and tools must be readily available to support contemporary teaching and learning environments.

Goal #5: Curriculum Integration--The International Society for Technology in Education, National Educational Technology Standards and the New York State Educational Technology Standards will continue to be integrated into all K-12 curricula.

Goal #6: Innovation--The District is committed to promoting the growth of an innovative culture among students and staff.

Goal #7: Global Education and Citizenship--Teachers and students will use technology to promote engagement in the global society. Global citizenship will be an underlying focus throughout all curriculum areas in an effort to prepare students for engaged living in a culturally diverse and rapidly changing world.

Following the presentation, the Board engaged in a Q&A with the presenters and shared their thoughts. Dr. Kor noted that technology has dramatically shifted across the District and there are many examples of growth and integration of technology. Dr. Kor also stated that we are not investing in technology for technology's sake, but for the opportunities it presents to enrich teaching and learning.
