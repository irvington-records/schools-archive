Meeting: Oct 23, 2018 - Special Meeting/Work Session
Date: 2018-10-23
Category: 3. School Board Recognition
Subject: 3.1 Board Recognition
Type: Recognition

School Board Recognition Week in New York State is October 22-26, 2018. To recognize the members of the Irvington School Board for their time and commitment, Irvington Mayor, Brian Smith, will present the Board with a Proclamation. Representatives from the PTSA, IEF, and district's bargaining units as well as the District will present members of the Board with a token of appreciation.
