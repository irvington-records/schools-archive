Ms. Stein,

On January 28, 2026, bids were received and opened publicly for the above-mentioned project. Upon receipt of the bid results, Triton
Construction reviewed each of the contractor’s qualifications. Five (5) bids in total were received for this project.

A bid review meeting was held February 3, 2026, with the low bidder, American Athletics Courts, Inc., BBS Architects, Landscape
Architects, and Engineers, P.C., a representative from the District and Triton Construction. Our project scope review with the
contractor indicates an understanding of the project requirements and expectations. Based on our review of the contractor’s bid,
qualifications, and successful scope review, we see no reason why a contract should not be awarded to American Athletics Courts,
Inc., as noted below.

          Running Track Replacement, Field Turf Replacement, and Bathroom & Classroom Renovations at Irvington High School
          Contract GC-2 –Running Track

          American Athletics Courts, Inc.
          2050 Rte. 206
          Vincentown, NJ 08088

          Base Bid:           $ 514,400.00 (includes a $10,000 allowance)
          Alternate #1:       $ 2,000.00
          Total Bid Price:    $ 516,400.00

Based on the information above, pending any final review required by District Counsel, the apparent responsible low bidder is
American Athletics Courts, Inc. from Vincentown, NJ. The total contract being recommended is FIVE HUNDRED SIXTEEN THOUSAND
FOUR HUNDRED DOLLARS AND ZERO CENTS ($516,400.00).

Upon the Board’s approval, Triton will provide the District with a Notice of Award to be sent to the contractor both by email and
overnight mail on School District letterhead.
