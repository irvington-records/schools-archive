    Project

Ms. Stein,

On January 28, 2026, bids were received and opened publicly for the above-mentioned project. Upon receipt of the bid results, Triton
Construction reviewed each of the contractor’s qualifications. Seven (7) bids in total were received for this project.

A bid review meeting was held February 3, 2026, with the low bidder, MCT AT Service Inc., BBS Architects, Landscape Architects, and
Engineers, P.C., a representative from the District and Triton Construction. However, after further review, we discovered that the
contractor was missing the required Subcontractor information in their bid. This makes their bid incomplete and therefore we
recommend the disqualification of MCT AT Service Inc.

Due to this disqualification, we all met with Pierotti Corp., who was the second lowest bidder. Our project scope review with the
contractor indicates an understanding of the project requirements and expectations. Based on our review of the contractor’s bid,
qualifications, and successful scope review, we see no reason why a contract should not be awarded to Pierotti Corp., as noted below.

          Running Track Replacement, Field Turf Replacement, and Bathroom & Classroom Renovations at Irvington High School
          Contract GC-1: Girl’s Bathroom and Classroom Casework

          Pierotti Corp.
          84 Calvert St.
          Harrison, NY 10528

          Base Bid:           $ 274,400.00 (includes a $5,000 allowance)
          Alternate #1:       $ 69,000.00
          Total Bid Price:    $ 343,400.00

Based on the information above, pending any final review required by District Counsel and the disqualification of MCT AT Service Inc.,
the apparent responsible low bidder is Pierotti Corp., from Harrison, NY. The total contract being recommended is THREE HUNDRED
FORTY-THREE THOUSAND FOUR HUNDRED DOLLARS AND ZERO CENTS ($343,400.00).

Upon the Board’s approval, Triton will provide the District with a Notice of Award to be sent to the contractor both by email and
overnight mail on School District letterhead.
