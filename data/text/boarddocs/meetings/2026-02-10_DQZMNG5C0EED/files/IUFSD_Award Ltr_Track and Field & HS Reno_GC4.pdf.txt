Re: Running Track Replacement, Field Turf Replacement, and Bathroom & Classroom Renovations at Irvington High School
    Project
    Contract GC-4: Combined Bid

Ms. Stein,

On January 28, 2026, bids were received and opened publicly for the above-mentioned project, which combines GC-2 and GC-3
contracts. Upon receipt of the bid results, Triton Construction reviewed each of the contractor’s qualifications. Four (4) bids in total
were received for this project.

A bid review meeting was held February 3, 2026, with the low bidder, LandTek Group, LLC, BBS Architects, Landscape Architects, and
Engineers, P.C., a representative from the District and Triton Construction. Based on our review of the contractor’s bid, the total
combined costs exceed the combined totals of GC-2 and GC-3 as separate contracts. Therefore, we recommend not awarding any
contracts for GC-4.

If you should have any questions, please do not hesitate to call.
