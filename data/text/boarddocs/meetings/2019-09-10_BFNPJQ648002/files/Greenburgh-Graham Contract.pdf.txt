This agreement made the 1" day of July, 2019, between the Irvington UFSD, County of
Westchester, New York, herein called the “School District,” and, Greenburgh-Graham UFSD.,
County of Westchester, State of New York, herein called the “Private/Public School”.

WHEREAS, the School District is duly empowered by law to enter into a contract with
the Private Schools or Public Schools for the instruction of disabled children in those situations
where the School District does not have an appropriate placement available, and

WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the School District has found the Private/Public
School has an educational program which can appropriately provide instruction to the pupil(s)
whose names, addresses and birthdates are listed on Addendum “A,” annexed hereto and made a
part hereof.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein contained, the
parties hereto do agree as follows:

1. The Private/Public School agrees to provide special classes and/or related services as
specified in the Individual Education Program for those pupil(s) whose names appear
on Addendum “A.”

2. The term of this agreement shall extend from 7/1/19 to 6/30/20, but shall be
terminable by the School District as to any one or more of the pupil(s) named herein
as of the end of the month by notice in writing to the Private School received by it
during such month.

3. The School District shall pay tuition to the Private/Public School at the New York
State Education Department’s approved rate per pupil(s) upon the presentation to it
by the Private/Public School of proper invoices in a form acceptable to it. The
Private/Public School shall present to the School District monthly invoices for
services rendered in the preceding month. It is mutually understood and agreed by
and between the parties hereto that total compensation to be paid to the Private/Public
School shall be limited to the amount approved by the New York State Education
Department for such services and that the Irvington UFSD will not be liable for
payment of any sums in excess of the amount so approved. In the event that any of
the children identified in Addendum “A” are residents of the Richmond Children’s
Center, or other chapter 721 Intermediate Care facility, the payment shall be made in
accordance with the reimbursement schedule provided by the State Education
Department.

[OCR]
. The Private/Public School shall report in writing, at least twice each year to the
School District, summarizing the pupil’s social and educational progress, stating the
precise nature of the program designated, and reporting all grades and attendance
information. In addition, within three (3) years from the date of placement, the
Private/Public School will be responsible to provide the Irvington UFSD with a
current psychological, social history and medical examination.

. The Private/Public School shall make itself available to personnel designated by the
School District for purposes of case discussions, personal observations, educational
reviews and program visitations by prior arrangement between the personnel
involved.

. The School District may terminated this agreement upon the occurrence of any of the
following events:

A. Withdrawal by the School District of the pupil(s) whose name(s) appear on
Addendum “A” from the Private/Public School for any reason whatsoever.

B. Termination of the approval by the Commissioner of Education of the
Private/Public School.

C. Failure of the Private/Public School to abide by all of the terms and
conditions of agreement.

. This Private/Public School may terminate this agreement for just cause and in
accordance with New York State Education Law and/or Commissioner’s Regulations.
Before effecting such termination, the Private/Public School shall provide the School
District with sufficient advance written notice, so as to allow the School District to
secure alternative placement of the pupil(s).

. Upon such termination, the School District shall pay to the Private/Public Schoo! a
prorate portion of the monthly tuition for the part of the month when services were
furnished prior to the termination.

. This agreement between the School District and the Private/Public School may not be
altered, varied or contradicted except by a similar writing executed by both of the

parties hereto.
