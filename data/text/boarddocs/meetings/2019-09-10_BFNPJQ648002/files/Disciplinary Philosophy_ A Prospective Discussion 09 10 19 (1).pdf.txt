Disciplinary Philosophy:
   A Prospective Discussion
       September 10, 2019

Purpose of a Code of Conduct
A code of conduct is developed to provide for a safe learning
environment that is conducive to teaching and learning by:

●   Fostering responsibility
●   Promoting social, emotional and physical wellness
●   Supporting learning and a productive culture
●   Respecting all members of the school community
●   Setting standards for students and staff
●   Communicating a school community’s values

Considerations
Each situation is unique and evaluated based on the following:
●   Code of Conduct standards
●   Nature of the incident
●   Developmental stages of the students involved
●   Health and safety of the the individuals and the school community
●   Personal and/or known special needs
●   Prior experiences - discipline can be progressive
●   How to provide learning for individuals and/or school community
●   Interventions that may support current needs and alleviate
    reoccurrence

Approaches Employed to Address Infractions
The following approaches are utilized to address infractions:
●   Proactive learning experiences
    ○ SEL, character education, Habits of Mind/dispositions, Health curriculum, SAC &
      community programs
●   Providing a positive school culture
●   Conferences with students, parents, staff, etc.
●   Peer mediation
●   Counseling:
    ○ School Counselors, School Social Worker, Student Assistance Counselor, Psychologists
●   Prescribed learning experiences
●   Detention*
    ○ Before school, after school, recess
●   In-school suspension*
●   Out-of-school suspension
    ○ Short and long term                                    *limitations to be discussed

Student-Centered Supports and Interventions
The District is committed to supporting students and their families.
Services include but are not limited to:
 ●   School-based interventions
     ○ Teacher, administrative, RtI & MTSS process
 ●   Counseling:
     ○ School Counselors, School Social Worker, Student Assistance Counselor, Psychologists
 ●   Coordination with external services and resources
 ●   During suspensions, the following interventions may be implemented:
     ○   Home instruction
     ○   Student Assistance Counselor services
     ○   Drug screening
     ○   Substance abuse evaluations and treatment
     ○   Independent counseling
     ○   Targeted learning experiences

Suspension
The use of suspensions should only be utilized in signiﬁcant Code of
Conduct infractions.
●    Principal suspension - up to 5 days
●    Superintendent’s suspension - over 5 days - results in a Disciplinary Hearing
●    Students are afforded due process in accordance with law and policy
●    Appeal process is available

The “costs” of suspensions:
Personal Impacts                          Fiscal Impacts
 ●   Lost instructional time               ●   Home instruction
 ●   Social & emotional                        ○ Up to 10 hours/week - $675/week

 ●   Challenges for families               ●   Disciplinary hearing
                                               ○ Legal costs - $245/hour
                                               ○ Hearing ofﬁcer - $200/hour* (*aidable)

Opportunities to Enhance Approach
The District is eager to implement more progressive,
student-centered approaches to better support student learning
while reducing the impacts of traditional disciplinary consequences.
Opportunities can include additional, restorative practices such as:
 ●   Community service
 ●   Development of Public Service Messages
 ●   Community learning experiences
 ●   Comprehensive counseling services
 ●   In-school suspension program development
       ○ Academic and counseling services

Challenges:
 ●   Stafﬁng to lead and supervise experiences
 ●   Space to host programs
