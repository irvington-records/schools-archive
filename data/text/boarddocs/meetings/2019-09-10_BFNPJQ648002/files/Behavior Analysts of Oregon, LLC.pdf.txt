Whereas the BOARD, desires to retain the services of the SP to provide Behavior Analytic
Services In the Irvington Free Union School District. Whereas, the SP agrees to furnish
these services to the BOARD; and now, therefore, these parties agree to be bound by the
following terms and conditions.

TERMS OF AGREEMENT
It is understood by and between both parties that:

The BOARD shall pay the SP at the following rate for services rendered pursuant to this
agreement: $175.00 an hour for direct and indirect services. Billing will be processed in
Increments of fifteen minutes for all direct services and indirect services.

Direct Services are defined as services that occur inside the district, home of a student in
the district or in service to a district student in an out of district placement. Direct services
Include: student observation, student programming, staff training and meetings with
school personnel or student guardians.

Indirect services are defined as services that occur outside of the district and include:
report writing, program development, development of materials and / or teacher training
preparation.

Not to Exceed $45,000 annually.

Services are to be provided at the request of the BOARD’s Director of Pupil Services or
his/her designee between July 1, 2019 and June 30, 2020.
