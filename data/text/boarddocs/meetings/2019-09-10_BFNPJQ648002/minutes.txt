6. Comments From Members of the Community

Information:  6.1 Procedures for Community Comment on Topics that appear on Agenda

After Dr. Harrison's presentation and the discussion between Administration and Board members, Brian Friedman allowed for any students in the audience to go to the microphone.  No students went at this time.  There was some more commentary and discussion from Board members and then Brian Friedman allowed for community comment.  Two adults and 1 student spoke.

7. Presentation/Discussion

Discussion: 7.1 Disciplinary Philosophy Discussion

The order of the agenda was changed slightly, with Dr. Harrison's presentation/discussion coming ahead of the Committee Reports.

Dr. Harrison gave a presentation and lead a discussion on the District's Disciplinary Philosophy.  There was much discussion between Dr. Harrison and the Board of Education members.

Action (Consent): 9.2 Tax Certiorari Settlement - DCD Realty Holdings, LLC

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District is a respondent in certain tax certiorari proceedings filed by DCD Realty Holdings, LLC pertaining to property identified on the tax roll as Section 1.180, Block 102, Lot 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 11 for tax years 2017 and 2018; and WHEREAS, the parties have negotiated a tentative resolution of the proceedings, as set forth in the Consent Judgment attached hereto; and WHEREAS, the Board has determined that it is in the best interests of the School District to resolve the proceeding in accordance with the Consent Judgment without further litigation; NOW, BASED UPON THE FORGOING, IT IS HEREBY RESOLVED that the Board hereby approves the settlement of the tax proceedings as set forth in the Consent Judgment; AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that Ingerman Smith, LLP is authorized to execute the Consent Judgment attached hereto, and any other relevant settlement papers necessary to effectuate the settlement; AND BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Assistant Superintendent for Business shall be authorized to issue a refund payment in accordance with the Consent Judgment.

Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education approve the Consent Agenda which includes 9.-Business of the Board Item 9.1-9.3; 10.-Curriculum and Instruction Item 10.1; 11.-Personnel Matters Items 11.1-11.11; 12.-Business Office Matters Items 12.1-12.8.
