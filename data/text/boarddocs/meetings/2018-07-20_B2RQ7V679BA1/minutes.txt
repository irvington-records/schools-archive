7. Discussion

7.1 Carol Stein, Asst. Supt for Business discussed Fund Balance

The Assistant Supt for Business presented a draft of the 2017-18 financial close and resulting fund balance determinations.  As discussed in June, the district will fund the Undesignated Fund Balance to the Office of State Comptroller recommended limit of 4% of next year's budget. This is an increase of $89,907.  The district will fund $100,000 toward our Capital Reserve, $50,000 to our Worker's Comp Reserve, and the remaining surplus to our Tax Certiorari Reserve.  While the surplus (annual revenue less expenses) is not quite finalized, we estimate the amount of $507,631 to the Tax   Certiorari Reserve.  The Tax  Certiorari Reserve will actually decrease by $91,647 as there were refunds paid from the reserve in 2017-18. Total Fund Balance will decrease by approximately $32,000 which is an improvement from the prior three year average decrease of $615,977. Maintaining adequate levels of reserves is important for the financial stability of the district and to relieve budgetary pressure with any large swings in workers comp expenses, contractual payments for retiring employees and tax certiorari refunds.
