Subject: 12.1 Authorization to Transfer from the Liability Reserve Fund to General Fund
Type: Action (Consent)

Allocate to budget code A1930.400-00-0000. Transfer to General Fund from Liability Reserve Fund.

Reserve management is an integral aspect of maintaining long term health of the district.  New York State Education Law allows for the establishment of a Liability Reserve to cover liability claims incurred by the School District. This will allow the District to plan and fund for future liability claims.
