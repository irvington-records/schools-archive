Meeting: Nov 26, 2024 - Work Session
Date: 2024-11-26
Category: 12. Business Office Matters
Subject: 12.2 Authorization to Transfer from Workers Compensation Reserve to General Fund
Type: Action (Consent)

Allocate to budget code A9040.800.00.0001. Transfer to General Fund from Workers Compensation Reserve.

While the District is fully insured for new Worker's Comp claims, we remain self funded for claims prior to July 1, 2019, also known as tail claims.
We plan to use the Workers Compensation Reserve to fund all tail claims during fiscal 2024-25. This will require budget amendments periodically throughout the fiscal year.
