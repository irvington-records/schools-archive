MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT

This Agreement made this 4 day of Tanuasry 2024, by and between
IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (hereinafter referred to as “SCHOOL DISTRICT”}, as the party
of the first part, having its principal place of business located at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, New York
10533, and the Shames Jewish Community Center on the Hudson, (hereinafter the “JCC”) as the party of
the second part, having his/her/its principal place of business for the purposes of this Agreement at 371
5. Broadway, Tarrytown, New York 10591.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the JCC has operational needs and service work that can be completed by
students;

WHEREAS, SCHOOL DISTRICT desires that JCC allows select SCHOOL DISTRICT students to
perform these services as part of their educational program; and

Whereas, JCC is ready, willing, and able to provide the location where the SCHOOL
DISTRICT students can perform these services;

NOW, THEREFORE for good and valuable consideration the receipt of which is
acknowledged, it is hereby agreed as follows:

1 This Agreement shall be in effect for the period January 9, 2024 through June 30, 2024
and it may be extended at the expiration of the initial term in a writing signed by the parties.

2. JCC agrees to permit the SCHOOL DISTRICT students in the SCHOOL DISTRICT’s
Vocational Independence Program class to perform support services such as unloading and
storing deliveries, light janitorial services and other such support work deemed appropriate as
part of their educational vocation program and abilities and as supervised by SCHOOL DISTRICT -
and/or and JCC staff;

3. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR: All employees of JCC shall be deemed employees
of JCC for all purposes and JCC alone shall be responsible for their work, personal conduct,
direction, and: compensation. JCC acknowledges that it will not hold itself, its officers,
employees and/or agents out as employees of SCHOOL DISTRICT. JCC shall not be considered
as having employee status and shall not be entitled to participate in any of SCHOOL DISTRICT’s
workers’ compensation, retirement, fringe benefits, unemployment insurance, liability
insurance, disability insurance, or other similar employee benefit programs.

4. This Agreement may be terminated on 30 days prior written notice by one party
to the other party.

6. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York. Each of
the parties hereto consents to the jurisdiction of any state court located within the County of
Westchester, State of New York or federal court in Federal District Court for the Southern
District of New York,

7. This Agreement may be modified or amended in writing signed by all parties.

8. To the fullest extent permitted by law, the SCHOOL DISTRICT agrees to defend,
indemnify and hold harmless the JCC its officers, agents or employees against all claims,
demands, actions lawsuits, costs, damages and expenses, including attorneys’ fees, judgments,
fines and amounts, arising from any act, omission, error, recklessness or negligence of the
SCHOOL DISTRICT, its officers, agents or employees in connection with the performance of
services pursuant to this Agreement. The obligations pursuant to this provision shall survive
the termination of this Agreement.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the JCC agrees to defend, indemnify and hold harmless
the SCHOOL DISTRICT, its officers, agents or employees against all against all claims, demands,
actions lawsuits, costs, damages and expenses, including attorneys’ fees, judgments, fines and
‘amounts, arising from any act, omission, error, recklessness or negligence of the JCC , its
officers, agents or employees in connection with the performance of services pursuant to this
Agreement. The obligations pursuant to this provision shall survive the termination of this
Agreement.
9, This Agreement may be executed in counterparts.

10. Any services performed by a party pursuant to this Agreement shall be without
fee or cost to the other party, the intent being that compensation to the respective parties will
be roughly equivalent and that each side receives considerable benefit based on the services
performed under this Agreement.
