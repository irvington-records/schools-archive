Project Timeline

Going live during the school year (October through May) has many advantages. This includes access to district
resources, the ability to schedule immediately in Infinite Campus without incurring additional costs, and access to
the legacy system through the end of the school year. In contrast, a summer Go-Live (June through September) has
the advantage of starting fresh with little disruption during the school year, but it does provide resource challenges,
often the need to extend licensing for the legacy system, and an immovable date with high stress levels as deadlines
become hard to meet over the summer.

A short (18 weeks) project with a focus to complete all tasks effectively and without running systems in parallel is
the preferred and best practice method. That said, some districts opt to run parallel with two systems and schedule
in Infinite Campus for the following schoo! year. This option will require additional data conversion, project
management, and professional development effort. In addition, selecting a longer rollout period will impact the
overall effort (Example: Engaging a project manager for 18 weeks vs. 8 months may result in a significant effort and
cost increase).

Data Conversion Process and Scope

Methodology

Custom follows an iterative conversion process for data to be extracted, transformed and loaded (ETL process) from
the legacy data source. A new Infinite Campus site will be created with each conversion cycle. It is Custom’s
experience that three* conversion cycles are optimal for achieving clean and accurate data.

Third Party Integrations and Customizations

Third party integrations and Customizations (scripts and reports) are handled as new development projects. A
requirements document needs to be completed and signed-off by the district. Development will only start once a
signed document is received. Any costs not included within the implementation project will require an additional
purchase order from the district.

The timeline required to develop, implement, and test integrations and customizations depends on the time of the
year and the status of the data conversion (extracts cannot be created or tested without data). Four to six weeks is
the standard turn-around time for any extract. This time starts once a requirement document is signed and a PO or
payment method is secured and at no time prior.

Critical and required integrations can be developed to coincide with the Go-Live date, but in general, integrations,
Custom reports and scripts will only be deployed once a live site is available. Developing the requirements may start
earlier.

All customizations and integrations are dependent on converted data. The district is required to review and approve
all data related to each customization.

Developing Ad-hoc reports (a tool that includes pass-through SQL query options) and extracts configured with the
Infinite Campus Data Extract utility are considered customizations.

All integration and customizations included as part of the implementation must be scheduled for completion within
90 days of Go-Live.
