Meeting: May 07, 2019 - Public Hearing/Regular Meeting*
Date: 2019-05-07
Category: 2. Public Hearing for 2019-20 Budget
Subject: 2.1 7:15pm Public Hearing for 2019-20 Budget
Type: Procedural

Consistent with New York State Law, the Board of Education must conduct a Budget Hearing at least 7 days, but not more than 14 days, prior to the scheduled budget vote on May 21, 2019. Members of the community will have an opportunity to comment on the proposed 2019-20 budget.
