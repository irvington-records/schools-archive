Subject: 13.3 Donation - PTSA Grants
Type: Action (Consent)

The Irvington Board of Education accepts the following PTSA grant:

Name of Grant	 	School	 	 Amount
HS Band Trip to Philadelphia	 	HS	 	 $       4,550

The HS Band, Wind, Jazz, and Flag team will be traveling to Philadelphia for a music tour and city experience from March 28-March 29th.  One of their performances will be before a Flyers game at the Wells Fargo Area.  The PTSA has generously approved a grant towards the cost of the hockey tickets for each student.  We are extremely grateful for their unbelievable support of the music program and allowing these performance experiences to be possible.
