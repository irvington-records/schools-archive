                                                        Student Reflections
1) How would you compare your experiences in this math class to your experiences in
   previous math classes?

  Some other responses…

  My experience in this math class is very different from my experience in previous math
  classes. It is a lot more hands on, which helps me learn and grow as a student. In past
  years, we did a lot of individual work at our seats on worksheets, or a lot of note-taking.
  This year we were able to build more of a community because we were all working
  together to solve a problem.

  I get to work with other people more which is helpful because we get to share ideas.


  I think this math class is better because you can answer questions that need more
  thinking and you can solve these problems in different ways. I think it is good that we
  can solve problems in different ways, work with other people, and hear what they think.

2) How does the Building Thinking Classrooms approach help you overcome challenges
when solving problems?

Some other responses…

It helps overcome problems because I can hear other people’s opinions and see different
points of view.
It helps me overcome challenges when solving problems because if I get stuck, there are
other people at different levels that can chime in.

The dry erase boards allow you to start with an idea, then if it is wrong, you can just
erase it and move on. Working with others helps as well, maybe they see something you
don't.

3) How does working in randomly selected collaborative groups impact your learning?

  Some other responses…

  It impacts my learning by observing other people’s techniques and problem solving
  strategies to improve on my own.
  It has helped me be able to create new ideas for problem solving, it has made me open to
  new ideas.
  Everybody has different abilities so everybody is able to contribute in their own way.

  Working in randomly selected groups helps since you work with different people and
  everybody is special and has different strengths. Another reason is that if you are
  always working with your friends, your focus might be affected. Last but not least, you
  get to know other people every time, which strengthens your relationships with each
  other.

4) How does completing tasks on the whiteboards compare to working on an individual
task at your desk?

Some other responses…

Completing tasks on whiteboards is much more engaging than working on an individual
task at my desk. It engages me socially by allowing me to collaborate with other students to
come up with ideas. It also makes the problem seem more interesting and I am less likely to
get frustrated when I am working with other people. I also love using whiteboards in
general, because they don't feel as permanent as pen and paper.
