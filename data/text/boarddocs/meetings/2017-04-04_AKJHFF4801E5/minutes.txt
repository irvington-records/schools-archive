6. Presentations/Discussions

Discussion: 6.1 Discussion of 2017-18 Proposed Budget

Dr. Harrison spoke again of budget process and that of the six meetings held on the Budget, this meeting is the most critical as the Board must provide final direction to the administration on the budget. Dr. Harrison addressed the questions that were asked at the previous board meeting including cost-benefit analysis for proposed special class, athletics participation, technology needs, staff shifts over time, the proposed data position and its responsibilities and highlighted proposed budget modifications. With respect to the proposed special class, the proposal to maintain the students in the district rather than placing outside the district, will realize a cost savings of $757,302 as out of district tuition would be approximately $1,005,675.

In the area of athletic participation, Dr. Harrison noted that any additional teams causes many associated costs, as well as ensuring that facilities are available for any particular sport, indoor or outdoor. An overview was provided of the number of participants in varsity, JV and modified, along with the number of cuts along with the level of interest communicated by students for next year.

In response to question regarding technology, Dr. Harrison noted that in a school district, it is a complicated system that includes network devices such as switches, instructional devices such as Chromebooks, monitors, Smartboards, etc. as well as administrative devices such as PCs and printers. Teachers also need to access devices. He reviewed total spending over the last four years and anticipated purchases for 2017-18. Previously, purchases were done via leases, but rules around leases have changed just as devices might be more affordable to purchase outright rather than lease. The number of devices that have been purchased as well as the potential purchases through 2019-2020 were highlighted. Dr. Harrison noted that some purchases have been made by the PTSA,IEF and SED in the past. To maintain infrastructure and devices would be approximately $274,950. A pilot program would begin at the high school to purchase devices for teachers.

Staffing changes were addressed and Dr. Harrison addressed the proposed data manager/analyst and the vision for that position. Over the last two weeks, responsibilities for the proposed position have been realigned with the two existing positions. The position would be housed in the District Office to work closely with District administrators.

There has been adjustment in the area of special education expenses with opportunities to have the proposed educational evaluator be .5FTE and .5FTE for a proposed 1/2 day special class for grades 2 & 3. There would be reductions in an aide and reduce a teaching assistant to an aide. This ½ day class would serve a student who would be placed out of district as well as other students. Total savings would be $107,500. There is also opportunity to add a revenue stream by accepting tuition-paying students into the program.

Dr. Harrison believes the budget touches every student in the district. The budget to budget increase would be 1.32%.
