                   Our Vision for Tomorrow
                                          3
To achieve our vision, we will:
❖ Provide students with a rigorous, comprehensive, enriched and diversified
  curricula that will prepare students to achieve their personal best, and will
  integrate technology in their learning.

❖ Support educators through targeted professional learning and opportunity for
  collaboration

❖ Encourage innovation, creativity and risk taking to inspire a dynamic learning
  environment

❖ Foster the creation of a culture where students are active participants in
  society.

❖ Strengthen local connections to and ownership of our schools.

❖ Ensure the fiscal health of the district and provide for a high quality learning
  environment.

  Adjusted Proposal - Special Education Expenses
                                    16

There are different timelines for budget process vs. decision-making related
to student needs determined through CSE. Often this results in changes in
placement both within and out of district. As a result, the following
opportunity exists:
●   Create ½ special class for grades 2 & 3
●   Eliminates need for 1 out-of-district placement
●   Reduce 0.5 FTE Aide
●   One Teaching Assistant to become an Aide
Modification to the prior proposal:
● 1.0 FTE for Educational Evaluator adjusted to:
    ○ Educational Evaluator .5 FTE & .5 special class

          Adjusted Proposal - Cost Savings
                                    17

Elimination of out-of-district placement, reduction of .5 FTE Aide, & TA
adjustment introduces a cost savings:

Tuition:                                        $ 85,500
.5 Aide:                                        $ 13,000
TA adjustment:                                  $ 9,000

Total savings:                               ~ $107,500

Savings applied to reduce fund balance allocation from $530,000 to
$422,500.

The adjusted Superintendent’s Budget is now $59,100,494.
This represents a reduction of $107,500 from the total budget presented
on March 28, 2017 of $59,207,994
