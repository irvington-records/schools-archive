            SIDE LETTER AGREEMENT BY AND BETWEENTHE
            BOARD OF EDUCATION OF THE IRVINGTON UNION
            FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT, Virginia Diamond, AND THE
            IRVINGTON FACULW ASSOCIATION REGARDING
            LUNCH BREAK(S) FOR NURSES

WHEREAS, the collective bargaining agreement ("CBA") between the
lrvington Union Free School District ("District") and the lrvington Faculty
Association ("lFA") for the period of July 1, 2023 - lune 3C,2C24 is silent with
respect to the lunch break to be afforded to Nurses; and

WHEREAS, New York State Labor Law, Section 162 requires the District to
provide Nurses rntr at least thirty (3O) minutes for a meal break; and

WHEREAS, said statute permits an exception for certain employees to remain
on site and on call throughout their meal break where the nature of the job
calls for such conditions; and

WHEREAS, the District and the IFA both acknowledge that Nurses fall within
this permitted exception provided each Nurse is given the opportunity to
revoke their consent to work through their meal break; and

WHEREAS, the parties have engaged in collective bargaining m an effort to
create acompensation mechanism for the above for lhe 2023/24 school year;

NOW, THEREFORE, the District, Virginia Diamond and the IFA agree as follows:

     I     For the 2023/24 school year, unit member Varginia Diamond
           herewith agrees to work, remain on site and on call throughout her
           meal break. Unit member Virginia Diamond shall receive payment
           in an amount equal to one-half of the applicable hourly rate (3O
           minutes) for each day said coverage responsibilities are actually
           provided. A time sheet shall be submitted on a monthly basis
           indicating the actual days such coverage is provided.

     2     Virginia Diamond understands and acknowledges that this waiver
           may be revoked in writing at any time and said payment shall
           cease effective with the date of said revocation. Notwithstanding
           the foregoing, Virginia Diamond shall provide at least five (5) school
           days' written notice of said revocation to the Building principal.

     3.    Other than as specifically set forth herein, this Agreement shall not
           serve as a precedent for any of the parties in any forum. This
           Agreement may not be used and/or cited by any of the parties in
           any future grievance, arbitration or litigation except for the
           purposes of enforcing the specific terms of this Agreement.

      4      This Agreement is subject to the review and acceptance of the
             Board of Educationby formal resolution, duly executed. Absent
             said approval, this Agreement shall be null and void.

      5.     The parties herewith acknowledge that this Agreement represents
             the full, final and complete resolution of this matter.

      6.    The Association and its members herewith agree that a violation,
            misinterpretation, or inequitable application of the terms and
            conditions of this Side Letter Agreement shall be subject to the
            grievance and arbitration procedures set forth in the collective
            bargaining agreement between the Association and the District.

      7     This Agreement shall not be altered except through mutual
            written agreement by the parties.
      8     This Agreement shall sunset in all respects and shall be null and
            void effective June 30, 2024. Should the parties wish to renew such
            an Agreement in future years, it must be approved by both the
            Association and the Board of Education.
