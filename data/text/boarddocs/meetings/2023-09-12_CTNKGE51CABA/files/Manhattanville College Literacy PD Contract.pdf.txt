ORTON-GILLINGHAM MENTORED PRACTICUM AGREEMENT

This Agreement made as of August 31, 2023, by and between Irvington Union Free School
District (“Irvington”), 6 Dows Lane, New York 10533, and Manhattanville College, School of
Education (“College”), 2900 Purchase Street, Purchase, New York 10577, Federal Identification
‘Number 13-1740469.

The organizations wish to serve students, teachers, and schools by furthering the preparation of
teachers to effectively develop literacy and promote academic achievement.

A. Description of Services: Manhattanville College

The ROSE INSTITUTE FOR LEARNING AND LITERACY (Manhattanville) will offer
at Irvington for professional development credit, a supervised practicum to facilitate the
implementation of the Orton-Gillingham approach in the classroom. The practicum will
use a virtual mentoring model, will be under the supervision of an Orton-Gillingham
Fellow, and will follow all requirements stated by the Academy of Orton-Gillingham
Practitioners and Educators and The Rose Institute for Learning and Literacy, These
requirements include submission of lesson plans and 5 videos. A total of 5 videos will be
submitted by May 30, 2024. It is a two-semester practicum with the first semester being
September through December 2023 and the second being January through May 2024.

Six teachers will participate in the practicum.

Irvington and Manhattanville will mutually explore the desirability and feasibility of
further coursework.

Coursework will be offered for professional development credit only. Irvington will
determine what benefits accrue to its faculty for this course at its sole discretion.

The practicum fee will be $1,500 per participant. Required materials for this course will
be shared by and purchased directly through the instructor, who may also offer

b)

This Agreement will become effective on its execution and will continue in full force and
effect until terminated. The program will be evaluated and may be modified accordingly
and continued or discontinued thereafter.

Either party, upon thirty (30) days written notice, can effect termination of this Agreement
without cause. Upon termination, neither party shall have liability to the other party, except
as otherwise set forth herein. Should the program be terminated once the course has begun,
officially registered students may continue to complete the course on campus or other
active college location where the course may be available.

D. Confidentiality of Records

Any records, reports or other documents provided by Irvington or Manhattanville shall
remain the property of Irvington or Manhattanville, as applicable. Any records, reports or
other documents provided in confidence by either party shall be kept confidential in
accordance with applicable laws, rules and regulations. This includes, but is not limited
to, confidentiality of records, evaluations, critiques, case files, and materials.

Irvington acknowledges that certain information about Manhattanville’s students may be
contained in records maintained by the Irvington and that this information can be
confidential by reason of the Family and Educational Rights and Privacy Act of 1974 (20
U.S.C. 1232g) and related college policies unless valid consent is obtained from the
students or their legal guardian. Both parties agree to protect these records in accordance
with FERPA and Manhattanville policy. To the extent permitted by law, nothing
contained herein shall be construed as precluding either party from releasing such
information to the other so that each can perform its respective responsibilities.

E. Independent Contractors

Both parties agree that no Manhattanville employee is an employee of Irvington, and that
no Irvington employee is an employee of Manhattanville. Both partics are independent
contractors to each other’s compliance obligations.
