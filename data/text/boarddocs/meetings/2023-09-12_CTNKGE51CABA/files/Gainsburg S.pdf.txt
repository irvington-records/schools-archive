SIDE LETTER AGREEMENT BY AND BETWEENTHE
BOARD OF EDUCATION OF THE IRVINGTON UNION
FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT, Sharon Gainsburg, AND
THE IRVINGTON FACULTY ASSOCIATION
REGARDING LUNCH BREAK(S) FOR NURSES

WHEREAS, the collective bargaining agreement ("CBA") between the
Irvington Union Free School District ("District") and the Irvington Faculty
Association ("IFA") for the period of July 1, 2023 - June 30, 2024 is silent with
respect to the lunch break to.be afforded to Nurses; and

WHEREAS, New York State Labor Law, Section 162 requires the District to
provide Nurses what least thirty (30) minutes for a meal break; and

WHEREAS, said statute permits an exception for certain ermployees to remain
on site and on call throughout their meal break where the nature of the job

calls for such conditions; and

WHEREAS, the District and the IFA both acknowledge that Nurses fall within
this permitted exception provided each Nurse is given the opportunity to
revoke their consent to work through their meal break; and

WHEREAS, the parties have engaged in collective bargaining m an effort to
create acampensation mechanism for the above for the 2023/24 school year;

NOW, THEREFORE, the District, Sharon Gainsburg and the IFA agree as
follows:

1 For the 2023/24 school year, unit member Sharon Gainsburg
herewith agrees to work, remain on site and on call throughout her
meal break. Unit member Sharon Gainsburg shall receive payment
in an amount equal to one-half of the applicable hourly rate (30
minutes) for each day said coverage responsibilities are actually
provided. A time sheet shall be submitted on a monthly basis
indicating the actual days such coverage is provided.

2. Sharon Gainsburg understands and acknowledges that this waiver
may be revoked in writing at any time and said payment shall
cease effective with the date of said revocation. Notwithstanding
the foregoing, Sharon Gainsburg shall provide at least five (5)
school days' written notice of said revocation to the Building

Principal.

3. Other than as specifically set forth herein, this Agreement shall not
serve as a precedent for any of the parties in any forum. This
Agreement may not be used and/or cited by any of the parties in
any future grievance, arbitration or litigation except for the
purposes of enforcing the specific terms of this Agreement.
