Benefits

In addition to the annual compensation specified above, you A be entitled to
receive the following enumerated benefits: i

1 Work Year

In alignment with the CSEA Clerical contract, your work year for the 2023/24
school year shall be twelve (12) months, except for the school holidays and recess
periods designated on the school calendar, vacation days, and use of sick, personal,
family illness days and bereavement days as well as any days off due to school
cancellation. You will work a forty (40) hour week, eight (8) hours per day, Monday
through Friday, including one (1} hour a day for lunch and will follow any other
provisions in the CSEA Clerical Contract.

2. Sick Leave

You shall be entitled to six (6) days of sick leave for the 2023/24 school year
prorated to five (5) based on the start date of September 18th, then nine (9) in 2024/25
and thirteen (13) in 2025/26 and beyond. Any unused sick leave days may be carried
over and used during any subsequent year of employment. A non-cumulative reserve
of thirty (30) working days will be available to you for an extended illness. You may only
utilize this reserve once in your lifetime. Extended illness shall mean illness or disability
for thirty (30) or more continuous working days. This reserve can only be accessed after
utilization of all your personal accumulated sick leave.

3. Personal and Other Leaves

You shall be entitled to two (2) days for the purpose of transacting or
attending personal business, which cannot be attended to other than on a school day
during school hours for the 2023/24 school year, prorated to one and a half (1.5) days
based on the start date of September 18th. In 2024/25 personal leave will be four (4)
days and in 2025/26 and beyond, personal leave will be five (5) days. All personal
business days shall require reasonable notice and the reason for such absence. Alll
unused personal days shall be credited to the sick time at the end of each fiscal year.

4. Family illness - Death in the Family

You shall be entitled to three (3) days for the 2023/24 school year prorated to two
and one-half (2.5) days of additional leave with no deduction in pay for illness in the
immediate family. For subsequent years, you will be entitled to three (3) family illness
days. These days shall not be deducted from sick leave and you shall not be entitled to
accumulate any unused days under this section. You shall also be entitled to three (3)
days additional leave with no deduction in pay for death in the immediate family. The
immediate family is herein defined as father, mother, brother, sister, son, daughter,

husband or wife, grandparents, father-in-law, mother-in-law, brother-in-law and
sister-in-law.

5. Vacation Leave

Effective July 1, 2024, you shall be entitled to eight (8) days of vacation leave

annually. Said vacation leave must be utilized during the summer months of July)
through August 31. If you are unable to utilize said vacation leave days during July or
August due to your job responsibilities, you may, upon the prior approval of your
immediate supervisor, use your vacation leave at other times during the school year.
You shall be entitled to carry over any unused vacation days with no maximum
accumulation. After one full complete year of service, you shalt be entitled to ten (10)
days of vacation leave annually. After the completion of five (5) years of service, you shall

be entitled to fifteen (15) days of vacation leave annually.

An employee with over two (2) years' service, who terminates his or her
