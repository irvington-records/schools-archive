3. Recognition of Fall Athletes

The Board recognized five student athletes for their athletic achievements in the Fall 2017 season: Eric Pasternack, Boys Varsity Soccer All Section, Jess Greene and Miranda Farman, Girls Varsity Soccer All Section, Zoe Maxwell who among her many accomplishments, was also named Con Ed Scholar Athlete of the Week, League MVP and reached a milestone of scoring over 100 goals in her high school career. Chris Barry, Boys Cross Country Team coach, stated thateach of the students being recognized tonight are not only great athletes, but great people as well. He iintroduced Luke Carmosino for his accomplishments on the Boys Cross Country Team this year, which included winning the Class C championship, placing 5th in the State meet , placing 3rd overall under extremely difficult conditions, and had the best finish overall in Irvington's 63 year history. He qualified for the Federation Meet and finished 25th, running 16.36 on a 5K course. The Board congratulated all the athletes and presented them with certificates

4.0 Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Report:  4.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison reported on the following:

- Congratulated the MS/HS band for a tremendous concert last week and thanked Mrs. McComber and Mr. Scatarettico for their leadership.

- The High School Counseling Dept. is hosting an evening event on December 7 at 7:00pm to provide families with a better understanding of the college admission process with a panel discussion following.

- The Best of Westchester competition has kicked off and would like to retain title of Best Public School District in Westchester which we have been named two years in a row.

- The January 9th Board meeting will introduce the leaders of the new Booster Club and at the January 23rd meeting will consist on updates on the Strategic Plan.

Information:  4.2 Update on Smart School Act Investment Plan

Jesse Lubinsky, Dir. of Technology, provided an update on the Smart Schools Investment Plan, part of the Smart Schools Bond Act that provided Irvington with a grant totalling $277,051 to improve educatonal technology and/or infrastructure improvements. As way of background, the Board of Education approved a resolution on June 28, 2016 to allocate $185,833 for the installation of Wifi networks at Dows Lane and Main Street School and allocate $91,218 for classroom technology, i.e. the purchase of 316 Chromebooks, 316 Google Chrome Management Console Licenses and 63 Tech Tub Trolley. Mr. Lubinsky will move forward with the second round of purchasing and he is confident that the State will approve as the plan has been thoughtfully developed and is consistent with the District's Technology Plan that is in place.

Information:  4.3 Committee Reports

Buildings & Grounds: Catherine Palmieri reported on the 11/15/17 meeting where the following was discussed: current debt service review, borrowing options,RFP for architectural services, and reviewed various types of bollards (safety barriers).

Dr. Harrison reported that at the December 18th meeting, the committee will begin to discuss a possible bond project and timeline for moving forward. It will also be necessary to look at the management of our debt service, what is sustainable as well as debt neutrality would need to be looked at along with all factors with respect to any new laws both on the Federal level and state level.

Communications Committee: Michael Hanna reported on the November 20th Communications Committee and thanked Maura Gedid for helping draft a resource for the community as to what to expect when coming to a Board meeting. Board Briefs (the Board's monthly newsletter) now links all presentations as well as PAC minutes for parents to easily access them and lcommittee will continue to look at other ways to improve transparency. Michael Hanna also reported that a Google Account has been created so that when communications from the community are responded to, the responses will be linked in Board Briefs for the community to view.

Curriculum Committee: discussion on ongoing initiatives: understanding assessment development, approaches to addressing technology in instruction with a follow up at the January meeting, an overview of professional development taking place in the District was provided by Dr. Kor.

Policy Committee: Deb Hargraves reported that the committee is still working on the 4000 series, Curriculum, with focus on Response to Intervention (RTI) and changing the language in the policy as well as looking at the new terminology. Ms. Hargraves noted that a complete policy review is being undertaken, which is a slow, methodical, laborious task, but progress is being made.

Mr. Hanna noted that members often attend regional events, and that recently, Deb Hargraves and Maura Gedid attended a Technology Summit hosted by WPSBA which focsed on student engagement in 21st Century schools. Deb Hargraves  provided an overview of the summit where approximately 10 presentors from schools in the area described what they believe is on five year horizon with respect to instruction and technology and how their school is approaching different aspects of technology, i.e. physical space, STEM and conversations and reflections with teachers and what technology means to each. The concept and definition of "personalized learning" was discussed at length.
