                         Smart​ ​Schools​ ​Investment​ ​Plan​ ​Update

Through​ ​the​ ​Smart​ ​Schools​ ​Bond​ ​Act,​ ​the​ ​Irvington​ ​UFSD​ ​was​ ​allocated​ ​a
total​ ​of​ ​$277,051.​ ​The​ ​goal​ ​of​ ​the​ ​Act​ ​is​ ​to​ ​improve​ ​educational​ ​technology​ ​and
infrastructure​ ​to​ ​improve​ ​learning​ ​and​ ​opportunity​ ​for​ ​students​ ​throughout
the​ ​State.​ ​Based​ ​on​ ​guidance​ ​from​ ​NYSED​ ​on​ ​how​ ​to​ ​spend​ ​these​ ​funds,​ ​each
district​ ​was​ ​to​ ​submit​ ​an​ ​Investment​ ​Plan​ ​which​ ​allocated​ ​their​ ​respective
funds​ ​into​ ​several​ ​predefined​ ​categories​ ​and​ ​uses.​ ​Additionally,​ ​expenditures
were​ ​required​ ​to​ ​address​ ​clear​ ​needs​ ​that​ ​are​ ​reflected​ ​through​ ​the​ ​district’s
technology​ ​plan.

On​ ​June​ ​28th,​ ​2016,​ ​the​ ​Irvington​ ​UFSD​ ​Board​ ​of​ ​Education​ ​approved​ ​a
resolution​ ​allocating​ ​our​ ​Smart​ ​Schools​ ​Bond​ ​Act​ ​award​ ​funds​ ​from​ ​New​ ​York
State​ ​in​ ​the​ ​categories​ ​of​ ​School​ ​Connectivity​ ​and​ ​Classroom​ ​Technology​ ​as
follows:

Our​ ​application​ ​to​ ​spend​ ​the​ ​funds​ ​allocated​ ​in​ ​the​ ​School​ ​Connectivity
category​ ​was​ ​approved​ ​in​ ​June​ ​2017​ ​and​ ​funded​ ​the​ ​expense​ ​of
implementing​ ​Wifi​ ​networks​ ​at​ ​Dows​ ​Lane​ ​Elementary​ ​School​ ​and​ ​the​ ​Main
Street​ ​School.

During​ ​the​ ​2017-2018​ ​budget​ ​cycle,​ ​the​ ​Board​ ​of​ ​Education​ ​and

on​ ​Chromebooks​ ​to​ ​supplement​ ​the​ ​District’s​ ​plan​ ​for​ ​increasing​ ​technology
access​ ​to​ ​students​ ​in​ ​classrooms.​ ​We​ ​will​ ​be​ ​moving​ ​forward​ ​with​ ​the​ ​second
round​ ​of​ ​our​ ​Smart​ ​Schools​ ​Bond​ ​Act​ ​purchasing.
