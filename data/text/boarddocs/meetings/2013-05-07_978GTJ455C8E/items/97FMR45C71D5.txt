Category: 8. Business of the Board
Subject: 8.3 Approval of Westchester Putnam School Boards Association's 2013-14 Budget and Board of Directors
Type: Action (Consent)

Westchester Putnam School Boards Association by-laws provide that every Member of record of the Association, through authorization of one member of its Board of Education, shall be entitle to one vote upon each proposition coming before the annual meeting which will be held at its annual meeting held on Thursday, May 30, 2013.
