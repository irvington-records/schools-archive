Meeting: May 07, 2013 - Public Hearing/Regular Meeting - 7:30 p.m.
Date: 2013-05-07
Category: 1. Public Hearing - 2013-14 Budget
Subject: 1.1 Open Public Hearing
Type: Action, Procedural

Consistent with State Law, the Board of Education must conduct a budget hearing at least 7 days, but not more than 14 days, prior to the scheduled budget vote on May 21, 2013. The administration will provide a very brief overview of the financial information relating to the proposed 2013-14 budget. Members of the community will have an opportunity to comment and/or raise questions regarding the proposed budget.
