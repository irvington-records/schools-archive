Meeting: May 07, 2013 - Public Hearing/Regular Meeting - 7:30 p.m.
Date: 2013-05-07
Category: 7. Consent Agenda
Subject: 7.1 Approval of Consent Agenda
Type: Action (Consent)

To make more efficient use of meeting time, the Board of Education authorizes the use of a consent agenda as a part of the meeting agenda. The President of the Board will ask Board Members to identify agenda items that Board Members wish to remove from the consent agenda for further discussion. Such item(s) will be removed for such discussion and will be presented in a separate motion.
