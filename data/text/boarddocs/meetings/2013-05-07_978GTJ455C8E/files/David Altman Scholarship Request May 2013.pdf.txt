Memo
To: Dr. Harrison, Board of Education
From: Scott Mosenthal
Date: May 1, 2013
Re: David Altman Scholarship Request

        The David Scott Altman Scholarship Committee requests that the School Board
establish and administer a David Scott Altman Scholarship Fund. Monies have been
donated to date through the generosity of family and friends to create a scholarship to
honor David Altman, who graduated from Irvington High School in 1994 and recently
passed away on February 9, 2013.
        The scholarship committee would like the School Board to make an annual award
to a deserving senior as determined by the David Scott Altman Scholarship Committee
and accordingly pledge the School Board the sum of $5,000.00 per year for the following
seven years (2020).
The recipient of this award will demonstrate
Athleticism
Leadership
Scholarship
Magnetism
Selflessness
Intelligence
Sense of humor
Compassion
Friendliness to all.

       The recipient is to be selected by the high school principal and the David Scott
Altman Committee.
       The scholarship award will be presented at the high school senior awards
ceremony, with the criteria for the award announced at the ceremony in the spring of
2013.
       In closing, David has two nephews and one niece that attend the Irvington
Schools system. It would make them very proud to know that a selected senior that
possesses their uncle’s wonderful qualities will be honored by receiving the David Scott
Altman Scholarship fund. The Committee hopes the Board of Education will approve this
scholarship fund to honor the life of David Altman.
