RIDER TO AGREEMENT

Irvington Union Free School District
(hereinafter referred to as “School District”)
40 North Broadway
Irvington, New York 10533

-and-

Junior Bus Tours, Inc.
(hereinafter referred to as “JBT”)
935 Route 34, Suite 3C
Matawan, New Jersey 07747

The terms and conditions of this Rider supersede and take precedence over the
terms of the Tour Confirmation Agreement and General Responsibilities and
Policies attached hereto. The Agreement is made up of this Rider and the annexed
documents. To the extent the terms of this Rider conflict with the terms of the Tour
Confirmation Agreement and/or the General Responsibilities and Policies, the
terms of this Rider shall govern.

The following provisions of the Tour Confirmation Agreement shall be modified
as indicated:

a. The Group Name shall be deleted and replaced with the following: “Irvington
Union Free School District on behalf of Irvington Middle School.”

b. The fourth paragraph of the General Responsibilities and Policies shall be
modified by adding the following: “Notwithstanding the foregoing, JBT has
the duty to use its best professional judgment in contracting or associating with
any third party that provides services to the School District.”

c. The third sentence of the sixth paragraph of the General Responsibilities and
Policies shall be deleted and replaced with the following: “The fee is to be the
final cost for the services contemplated in this Agreement. Neither the School
District nor participants shall be required to pay and shall not be liable for any
additional costs or fees that may be incurred during the terms of this
Agreement, including, but not limited to, the costs associated with: fuel, fuel
surcharges, mechanical repairs, tolls, cost of driver and his/her services,
gratuities, parking, licensing, traffic or criminal violations, insurance or
overtime usage due to traffic or conditions not the cause of the School District.
Fees and expenses in addition to those provided for in this Agreement must be
requested of the School District and agreed to by the School District prior to
the School District owing or providing any compensation for same.”

d. The fourth sentence of the sixth paragraph of the General Responsibilities and
Policies shall be deleted and replaced with the following: “Changes to the
itinerary may be made by Junior Tours, with the consent of the School District,
if deemed necessary or desirable.”

e. The fifth paragraph of the General Responsibilities and Policies shall be
modified by adding the following: “However, JBT shall utilize suppliers
providing travel services and accommodations it has reason to believe are
competent, and hereby represents that if it has used said suppliers in the past
for similar Tours, their services have been acceptable to JBT.”

f. The first sentence of the seventh paragraph of the General Responsibilities and
Policies shall be deleted and replaced with the following: “JBT specifically
disclaims any and all responsibility for the general supervision and behavior
of tour participants, except that JBT staff will act with professionally reasonable
care and obey all applicable laws and regulations.”

g. The eighth paragraph of the General Responsibilities and Policies shall be
deleted and replaced with the following: “This Agreement shall be governed
by, and interpreted and enforced in accordance with, the laws of the State of
New York, and any disputes shall be heard in a court of competent jurisdiction
in the State of New York.”

3, The following provisions shall be added to the Agreement:

a. This Agreement shall commence upon the date of approval by the Board of
Education and terminate after performance of the services contemplated herein
and completion of the field trip to Washington, D.C. scheduled for April 9, 2025
to April 11, 2025, but no later than June 30, 2025, unless terminated earlier as
set forth herein.

b. JBT acknowledges that all buses used for this tour are equipped with seat belts
and that the driver acknowledges that seat belt use is required for students
eight years of age and above.

c. JBT shall provide proof of adequate insurance, in the limits set forth herein,
for the hotels and motorcoach companies with whom JBT contracts or
schedules to provide services for the School District.

d. All employees of JBT shall be deemed employees of JBT for all purposes, and
JBT alone shall be responsible for their work, personal conduct, direction, and
compensation, JBT acknowledges that it will not hold itself, its officers,
employees and/ or its agents out as employees of the School District. JBT is

. JBT will not discriminate against any individual because of his/her age, race,

action to ensure that each individual is afforded equal opportunities without
discrimination because of his/her age, race, creed, color, national origin, sexual
orientation, military status, sex, disability, predisposing genetic characteristics
or marital status.

JBT shall purchase from and maintain in a lawfully licensed insurance
company or companies such insurance as will protect JBT and the School
District from claims set forth below for which JBT may be legally liable,
whether such operations be by JBT or by anyone directly or indirectly
employed by any of them, or by anyone for w hose acts any of them may be
liable. Notwithstanding any terms, conditions or provisions, in any other
writing between the parties, JBT hereby agrees to effectuate the naming of the
School District as an additional insured on the JBT’ insurance policies, with the
exception of workers' compensation and professional liability. The policy
naming the School District as an additional insured shall:
i. Be purchased from an A. M. Best rated “A - X” insurer, licensed as
in “admitted carrier” in New York State.
ii. Contain a 30-day notice of cancellation, non-renewal or reduction
of coverage.
iii. State that the company's coverage shall be primary coverage for the
School District, its Board, officers, employees and volunteers.
iv. Be memorialized in a certificate of insurance provided to the School
District.

In the event that any of the insurance coverage to be provided by JBT

JBT further acknowledges that its failure to obtain or keep current the
insurance coverage required by paragraphs “e,” “f” and “g” shall constitute
a material breach of contract and subjects JBT to liability for damages,
including but not limited to direct, indirect, consequential, special and any
other damages the School District sustains as a result of this breach. In
addition, JBT shall be responsible for the indemnification to the School
District of any and all costs associated with such lapse in coverage,
including but not limited to reasonable attorneys’ fees. JBT shall not be
considered “approved” until it has obtained all insurance required under
this specification and such insurance has been reviewed and accepted by
the School District.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the parties agree that they shall
indemnify and hold harmless each other, its and their officers, directors,
and employees for all loss, costs, damages and expenses, including
attorneys’ fees, judgments, fines and amounts paid in settlement in
connection with a terminated, pending or completed action, suit or
proceeding arising from any negligent act, error or omission, or breach of
duties by the indemnifying party or its officers, directors, or employees
taken, made, arising out of or resulting from the performance of the services
under this Agreement or from any violation by said party of the terms of
this Agreement. This obligation shall not be construed to negate, abridge,
or otherwise reduce any other right or obligation of indemnity that would
otherwise exist as to any party or person described in this paragraph.

Neither party shall be liable to the other for punitive damages.

JBT agrees not to assign, transfer, convey or sublet or otherwise dispose of
this Agreement or its right, title and interest therein or its power to execute
such Agreement, to any other person, firm or corporation without the
previous written consent of the School District.

JBT understands and agrees that it shall comply and be responsible for
complying with all applicable federal, state, and local statutes, rules,
regulations, codes and ordinances. JBT shall conduct criminal background
checks for all its employees who go on the tours and provide proof of same
to the School District upon request.

Historical lack of enforcement of any law, local or otherwise, shall not
constitute a waiver of JBT’ responsibility for compliance with such law ina
manner consistent with its agreement with the School District unless and
until JBT has received written consent for the waiver of such compliance
from the School District responsible for the enforcement of such law .

Except as expressly provided in the Agreement between the School District
and JBT, duties and obligations imposed by such Agreement and rights
and remedies available thereunder shall be in addition to, and not a
limitation of duties, obligations, rights and remedies otherwise imposed or
available by law, or in equity or by other agreement, and such rights and
remedies shall survive acceptance of JBT’ work and/ or any other
termination of JBT’s Agreement with the School District.

No action or failure to act by the School District or JBT shall constitute a
waiver of a right or duty afforded them under the Agreement, nor shall
such action or failure to act constitute approval of or acquiescence of a
breach thereunder, except as may be specifically agreed in writing.

This Agreement may be terminated by the School District for any reason
upon seven (7) days written notice to JBT. In such event, the School District
will not be refunded any paid fees but will not incur any additional fees or
liabilities.

All notices, demands, requests, consents, approvals, offers, statements and
other instruments or communications required or permitted to be given
hereunder shall be in writing and shall be deemed to have been received
when personally delivered, or when mailed by first class registered or
certified mail, addressed to each party at the address set forth above.

Incase any provision of this Agreement should be held to be contrary to, or
invalid, under the law of any country, state or other jurisdiction, such
illegality or invalidity, shall not affect in any way, any other provisions
hereof, all of which shall continue, nevertheless, in full force and effect in
any country, state or jurisdiction in which such provision is legal and valid.
