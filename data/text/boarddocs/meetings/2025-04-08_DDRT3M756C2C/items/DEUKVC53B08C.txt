Meeting: Apr 08, 2025 - Regular Meeting
Date: 2025-04-08
Category: 13. Business Office Matters
Subject: 13.2 Municipal Cooperation Resolution
Type: Action (Consent)

PMA Financial, LLC (PMA), which oversees the New York Liquid Asset Fund (NYLAF), has merged with Public Trust Advisors, LLC, which oversees the New York Cooperative Liquid Assets Securities System (NYCLASS). As a result of the merger of these companies, there is now an overlap in service providers to both NYLAF and NYCLASS. After careful consideration and thorough evaluation of the best path forward, both NYLAF and NYCLASS Board of Directors have agreed to merge the Cooperative Programs out of the best interest of the Participants. This resolution will allow the District to utilize the new combined fund for our investments.
