Strategic Objectives
   Student Success
    1.   Maximize the academic growth of each student to ensure attainment of
         essential skills and content.
    2.   Provide all students with a variety of pathways to identify and explore their
         passion and purpose.
    3.   Ensure a consistent and balanced assessment system that provides clear, timely,
         and meaningful feedback on student progress.
    4.   Provide relevant professional learning, which
         targets the needs of every staff member.
    5.   Engage in a cycle of program evaluation to
         ensure that the curricula is grounded in
         research, evidence, and data.

Student Success
   Focus for 2023/2024
    ●   Directly connected to the Portrait of a Graduate and aligned to the on-going work
        in the areas of SEL, DEI and use of data to inform instruction.
    ●   Provides continuity between ongoing work of the District and the Strategic Plan.

   Creation of Implementation Plan
    ●   Working with the Instructional Leaders (District/building administrators,
        department chairs, grade level coordinators, instructional coaches)
    ●   The implementation plan will include both strategies to be implemented and
        strategic indicators that will measure the success of the plan created.
    ●   Updates to be shared at the November 21st as well as at the June 18th BOE
        meetings.
    ●   While the primary focus of implementation for 2023-24 will begin with Student
        Success, we recognize that all four strategic priorities are intrinsically linked.
