FIRST AMENDMENT, made as of November 21, 2023, by and between the
BOARD OF EDUCATION of the IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL
DISTRICT (“BOARD” or “DISTRICT”) with offices for the transaction of business
located at 40 North Broadway Irvington, New York 10533 and DR. JOEL
ADELBERG, (“DR. ADELBERG” or “ACTING SUPERINTENDENT”) with an

address 0 is

WHEREAS, the parties entered into an Agreement dated August 28, 2023, establishing
the terms and conditions of employment in connection with DR. ADELBERG’S service as the
ACTING SUPERINTENDENT for the DISTRICT (hereinafter referred to as the “Agreement”);
and

WHEREAS, the term of employment set forth in said Agreement runs from August 28,
2023, through November 22, 2023, unless sooner terminated in accordance with the provisions of
said Agreement; and

WHEREAS, the parties are desirous of amending said Agreement for the purposes of
extending said term of employment and providing certain paid leave time to DR. ADELBERG;

NOW, THEREFORE, based upon the mutual covenant(s) and understanding(s) between
the parties, it is understood and agreed:

i All the terms and provisions set forth at length in the above-referenced Agreement
shall continue in full force and effect during the term of employment of the ACTING
SUPERINTENDENT, except as expressly modified herein.

2. Term of Employment:

The term of employment set forth in Article 1(a) shall be revised so as to extend through
June 30, 2024, subject to Article 1(b).

Bs Benefits and Working Conditions:

a. The work year shall encompass the approved school calendar. The workday
for DR. ADELBERG shall be consistent with the workday of other similarly situated
administrators. DR. ADELBERG shall be credited with seven (7) days of paid personal
leave and seven (7) days of paid sick leave. Upon resignation or termination of his
employment with the DISTRICT, DR. ADELBERG shall not be compensated for any
unused personal or sick days. DR. ADELBERG shall be permitted to take said paid leave
days as needed and on a reasonable basis, so long as he advises the President of the Board
of Education in advance. DR. ADELBERG shall not be paid for any additional days he
does not work.

b. DR. ADELBERG shall not be required to report to work on those legal holidays
outlined in the school calendar.

Cc DR. ADELBERG shall not be entitled to any other fringe benefits, including, but
not limited to paid leave (other than as specifically set forth above), redemption of unused
leave time, insurance coverage, pension or retirement contributions.
