                            BUILDINGS & GROUNDS COMMITTEE

                         April 18, 2016, 7:30 AM, at District Office


                                              MINUTES
IN ATTENDANCE: Phil Whitney, Dr. Harrison, Catherine Palmieri, Gary Knowles, Beverly Miller



   1. Project Manager – timing of hiring:

      Project Manager (Clerk of the Works) cannot be hired until after contracts are awarded. The
   Committee agreed to advertise the position as 20 hrs. per week @ $100 per hr. for an est. 12-16
   weeks. Committee will be sent a draft of the advertisement prior to its being placed in the
   newspaper. Bev will work with LAN to send advertisements to potential candidates.

   2. Review of bids and implications for overall budget:

       Bids that were received on April 15th were reviewed. It will be recommended to the Board to
   award bids for HS Meszaros & Track work to include alternates #2,3,4,5 and contract 1A; Middle
   School Dust Bowl, Dows Lane Univents and boiler controls; and Middle School Vestibules . The
   bathroom renovations were tabled pending further discussion.

   3.   Dust bowl changes :

        Committee reviewed current specs and determined that no changes are needed at this time.

   4.   Estimate for enlarging existing sidewalk at HIS near loading dock:

       Discussion ensued regarding plans to partially remove existing wall to create a sidewalk and to
   retain 3 parking spots. District will request estimate from LAN

   5. Dows Lane sidewalk repairs:

       Committee approved contacting the Village to request their pursuit of the property owner at the
   corner of El Retiro and Dows Lane for sidewalk repairs.

   6.   Air conditioning noise at Dows Lane School:

        Item tabled for future discussion

   7.   Additional signage at crosswalk at Heritage Hill junction:

        Gary will research crosswalk signage and report back to the Committee

8.   NEW ITEM – HS Environmental Club:

    Gary was contacted by the HS Environmental Club. They requested permission to install 5 trees
between the outdoor classroom and the garden. Catherine will contact the club for additional
information and to share the current plans of the Beautification Committee.

9.   HS Island:

    Question was raised as to maintenance on this area for 2016-17. Gary assured the Committee
that the work is scheduled for next year.

10. 8th grade gift:

     Catherine informed the Committee that she was contacted by some 8th grade parents who
would like to do a gift to the District as a celebration of the 8th grade graduation. More information
will be shared as it develops.



Respectfully submitted:



Beverly Miller
