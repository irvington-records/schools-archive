3. Presentation/Discussion

Presentation:  3.1 District Long-Range Planning

Dr. Harrison, Dr. Kor and Ms. Stein led an informative presentation on Long-Range Planning, which was an outgrowth and evolution of the District’s Strategic Plan: Our Vision for Tomorrow. In the presentation, Dr. Harrison spoke of the importance of long-range planning and how it helps to inform and guide the work of the Board of Education and District.

The presentation identified a series of themes for which the District will focus: Instruction, Social Emotional Learning, Pupil Personnel Services and Special Education, Technology, Athletics, Safety and Security, and other District-wide needs. Each of the themes were reviewed and related needs were outlined. These needs are all intended to support continuous improvement of student achievement and school-time experiences. Additionally, Ms. Stein presented one scenario of a long-range financial plan. The financial projection, while necessarily based on a number of assumptions, will help to inform broader planning across the District.

The Board of Education and members of the community engaged in discussion and asked a number of related questions.
