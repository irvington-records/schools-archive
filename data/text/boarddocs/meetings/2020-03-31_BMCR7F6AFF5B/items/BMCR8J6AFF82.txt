Community members can view the meeting by following the link noted in the header to this agenda. However, those watching the meeting will not be able to comment or pose questions.  Should you have questions for the Board of Education or the Administration, you are asked to send them via email to: Board@IrvingtonSchools.org.  The Board of Education and Dr. Harrison will receive these emails. Responses to questions will be posted on the website at a future date.
