YORKTOWN CENTRAL SCHOOL DISTRICT
2019-2020 CONTRACT FOR HEALTH AND WELFARE SERVICES

This agreement made in duplicate this 13th day of January 2020 by and between the Board of Education, Irvington Union
Free School District, County of Westchester, New York, (the School District of Residence) and the Board of Education of
the towns of Yorktown, New Castle and Cortlandt, County of Westchester, New York (Yorktown Central School District).

WITNESSETH, that whereas the School District of Residence is duly empowered by the provisions of Section 912 of the
Education Law to enter into a contract for the purposes of providing health and welfare services to the children residing in
said school district and attending non-public schools in the Yorktown Central School District, to begin on September 3, 2019

and to end on June 26, 2020. prorated fer & months

NOW, therefore, the School District of Residence agrees t6 pay the Yorktown Central School District the sum of $1,289.67
per pupil for health and welfare services provided to 2 child(ren) residing in the District of Residence and attending non-
public schools in the Yorktown Central School District. It is further agreed that at the end of the current Yorktown school
year the foregoing charge will be recomputed using actual expenditures for health and welfare services and actual enrollment
as of October 1, and that a refund or additional charge will be made as required on the basis of said recomputation.

And the Yorktown Central School District hereby agrees with the School District of Residence as follows:

1. That the following services to be provided shall include, but not be limited to, services performed by a physician,
dentist, dental hygienist, nurse, school psychologist, school social worker, and school speech correctionist; taking of
medical histories; vision and hearing tests; examination for employment certificates; notification of parents regarding
defects and follow-up; instructions for first aid care for school emergencies.

2. The Yorktown Central School District will also furnish the following to be used in providing such services as
requested by the non-public school authorities: Supplies and equipment for use by physician, school nurse,
psychologist, dental hygienist, social worker, and speech correctionist (i.¢., Scales, Vision and hearing testing devices,
Health record forms, First-aid supplies and all other readily transportable equipment and supplies pertaining to
delivery of services).

It is expressly agreed by and between the parties hereto that the services agreed to be supplied under this contract shall not
include any teaching services.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have hereunto set their hands the day and year above written.
