AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT made this 2nd day of March, 2020 by and between IRVINGTON UNION
FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (hereinafter referred to as “SCHOOL DISTRICT”), as the party of the
first part, having its principal place of business located at 6 Dows Lane, Irvington, New York
10533, and Bard College Institute for Writing & Thinking (hereinafter the “CONSULTANT”) as the
party of the second part, having his/her/its principal place of business for the purposes of this
Agreement at PO Box 5000, Annandale-On-Hudson, NY 12504.

WITNESSETH;

WHEREAS, CONSULTANT is in the business of providing Administrative
Consultancy /Mentor services to school districts; and

WHEREAS, SCHOOL DISTRICT desires that CONSULTANT provide such Administrative
Consultancy /Mentoring services as set forth in this Agreement; and

WHEREAS, CONSULTANT is ready, willing, and able to provide such services to
SCHOOL DISTRICT;

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained in this
Agreement, the parties hereto mutually agree as follows:

1, TERM OF AGREEMENT: This Agreement shall be in effect for the period September 1, 2019
through June 30, 2020 unless terminated earlier, as set forth herein.

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES: CONSULTANT shall provide consulting services to the SCHOOL
DISTRICT as follows:

1) CONSULTANT shall provide writing workshops with the Irvington Middle School and
High School ELA staff.

3. PAYMENT SCHEDULE: In full consideration for the consultancy and workshop services to
be rendered by CONSULTANT to SCHOOL DISTRICT for the period of this Agreement, SCHOOL
DISTRICT will pay CONSULTANT a lump sum amount of $24,475 for this consulting work with
Edith Winthrop Teachers Center paying the remaining balance of $17,500.

SCHOOL DISTRICT. CONSULTANT is retained by SCHOOL DISTRICT only for the purposes
and to the extent set forth in this Agreement, and his/her/its relationship to SCHOOL DISTRICT
shall, during the periods of her services hereunder, be that of an independent contractor,
CONSULTANT shall not be considered as having employee status and shall not be entitled to

determined to have legitimate educational interests;

authorized in this Agreement;

iii, maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to
protect the security, confidentiality and integrity of education records in its

iv. use encryption technology to protect data while in motion or in its custody
from unauthorized disclosure using a technology or methodology specified
by the secretary of the United States Department of Health and Human
Services in guidance issued under Section 13402(H)(2) of Public Law 111-5.

c. CONSULTANT further understands and agrees that it is responsible for submitting
a data security and privacy plan to the SCHOOL DISTRICT prior to the start of the
term of this Agreement. Such plan shall outline how all state, federal and local data
