                        BUILDINGS & GROUNDS COMMITTEE

                       April 15, 2015 7:30 AM, at District Office

                                           MINUTES




1. DOWS LANE OUTDOOR LEARNING CENTER (Sarah Carnahan Outdoor Learning Space)

  Reviewed preliminary plans, as presented to Gary by Delina Codey. After careful review and
  discussion, the committee advised Gary to contract Ms. Codey with the following direction as
  provided by the committee: (1) propose that the project be done in phases due to the many
  facets of the project - First phase would be the installation of the terraced seating and
  bluestone area, and (2) revamp the proposed plantings to only include plants & shrubs that are
  deer resistant – suggest input from a professional landscaper.

2. CAPITAL PROJECTS (update)

  Committee was informed of the additional project delays due to the backlog of projects at SED.
  It is now projected that the District will not receive project approvals until sometime between
  September – December.


3. CAPITAL PROJECT – DOWS LANE (update)

  Committee was informed that the initial “kick-off” meeting was held with the architect,
  contractor, and administration. The project is on target to begin July 1st with an anticipated
  finish date of August 21st.



4. INNOVATION FUND GRANT

  Committee was provided an overview of project approvals that relate to the Buildings &
  Grounds Committee. Dr. Harrison will keep the committee apprised on any progress on the
  projects.
