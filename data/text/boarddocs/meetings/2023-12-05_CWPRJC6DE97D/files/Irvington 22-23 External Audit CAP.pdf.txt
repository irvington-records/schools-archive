                               Irvington Union Free School District

  Revised Corrective Action Plan for Audited Financial Statements Year Ended June 30, 2023

For each recommendation included in the audit report, the following is our corrective action(s)
taken or proposed. For recommendations where corrective action has not been taken or
proposed, we have included the following explanations.

School Lunch Fund Recommendation: We recommend that the School District take all steps
necessary to utilize the excess portion of this fund balance in the upcoming fiscal year.

Implementation Plan: The Assistant Superintendent for Business reviews the needs of the Food
Services program monthly with the Food Services Director. The District will continue its
equipment replacement efforts, including expanding food service to an atrium location at our
high school which requires new equipment to meet refrigeration and food safety standards.
The Assistant Superintendent for Business is also meeting with architects and furniture
representatives for a new cafeteria design and furniture options at our cafeterias. An excess
fund balance plan was submitted to and approved by Child Nutrition.

Implementation Date: June 2024



Bid Requirements Recommendation: We recommend that the School District declare
emergencies in the board minutes when they arise to support the exemption from the bidding
process

Implementation Plan: The District agrees with this recommendation. Going forward, the
Assistant Superintendent for Business and District Clerk will ensure there will be an emergency
declaration in the board minutes to support the exemption from bidding in emergency
situations, if required.

Implementation Date: December 2023



Liability Reserve Establishment Recommendation: We recommend that the School District
consider establishing a liability reserve so that future liability claims can be funded in advance
of required payments.

Implementation Plan: The Assistant Superintendent for Business will recommend to the Board
to establish this reserve during the fiscal 2023-2024 fiscal year.

Implementation Date: June 2024
