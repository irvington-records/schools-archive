Subject: 12.2 Surplus Inventory - 2008 Dodge Ram 3500 Heavy Duty Dump Truck
Type: Action (Consent)

The 2008 Dodge Ram 3500 Heavy Duty 4X4 dump truck with 25,156 miles and 9ft Meyers plow has severe rust and rot with nonfunctioning parts and has ignition issues and wiring damage.

The cost of the numerous repairs needed to get the truck back on the road far exceeds the value of the vehicle.
