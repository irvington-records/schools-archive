AGREEMENT FOR SPECIAL EDUCATION OF DISABLED PUPILS

Under the provision of Section 4402 2(B) of the Education Law, THIS AGREEMENT made on
this Ist day of September 2024, between the Irvington School District, having its principal
place of business at 6 Dow Lane, Irvington, NY 10533 (hereinafter referred to as “School
District”), and The Community School, a domestic non-profit corporation, having its principal
place of business at 11 West Forest Avenue, Teaneck, NJ 07666 (hereinafter referred to as the
“Contract School”),

WHEREAS, the Contract School conducts an educational program for students with disabilities,
and

WHEREAS, the School District is interested in having certain of its children educated in said
program, and

WHEREAS, the Contract School does certify that its program is approved by the Education
Department of the State of New York,

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties mutually agree as follows:

1. The Contract School will provide adequate instruction for students with disabilities as per
the student’s Individualized Education Program (“IEP”) approved by the School
District’s Board of Education. At all times during the term of the Agreement, the
Contract School shall maintain approval by the New York Commissioner of Education
and the New York State Education Department to operate the educational programs
where students are placed.

2. The Contract Schoo! will provide instruction on a regular ten (10) month school calendar,
which will commence on September 4, 2024 and end on June 30, 2025. The school day
will consist of a minimum of five and one-half (5.5) hours of instructional contact.

3. In full consideration for the services to be rendered by the Contract School to the School
District under the terms of this Agreement, the School District will pay to the Contract
School the amount of $336.33 per child per day. Said tuition will be paid in ten (10)
monthly installments at the end of each month, September through June, upon receipt ofa
proper invoice from the Contract School setting forth the name(s) of the child(ren) who
were enrolled for the prior month, Where a child’s enrollment is for less than a full
month, the Contract School will be compensated at the per diem rate based on the number
of days in the month and the number of days in which a child is enrolled.

4. The Contract School shall maintain daily attendance records in the same form as is
required in public schools of the State of New York and submit proof of monthly
attendance with the proper invoice for tuition payments.

5. The Contract School will obtain such releases and other legal documents as are necessary
in order that the Contract School render full and complete reports concerning the

education and progress of the students covered by the terms of this Agreement. At no
cost to the School District, the Contract School will render reports to the School District
at any time such reports are made to parents of students and will render such additional
reports as may reasonably be required by the School District regarding, among other
things, student progress. Such progress reports will be rendered, in a format established
by the Contract School, before December 15", April 15" and June 15" of the regular
school year. Should the Contract School fail to maintain its status as a State-approved
school for the education of students with a disability, this Agreement shall become void
and the Contract School be entitled to no compensation for any period in which it did not
have State approval.

6. The Contract School shall maintain throughout the term of this Agreement a current
Professional Liability insurance policy issued by an insurance company authorized to do
business in the State of New York in an amount and form acceptable to the Board of
Education and shall provide the Board of Education with a Certificate of Insurance with
respect to such coverage upon execution of this Agreement naming the Irvington School
District as an additional insured. If such policy shall lapse, for whatever reason, during
the term of this Agreement, the Board of Education reserves the right to immediately
terminate this Agreement without any further notice to the Contract School. If no such
certificate is required by State regulations, the Contract Schoo! must assure the Board of
Education that the service provider is qualified and experienced to perform the duties
outlined in this Agreement.

7. The School District’s Board of Education or its representatives may visit the school
conducted by the Contract School.

TERMINATION The School District may terminate this Agreement upon the
occurrence of any of the following events:

a. Withdrawal by the School District of the pupil(s) attending the Contract School
for any reason whatsoever. If withdrawal is voluntary, without cause, fifteen (15)
days written notice must be provided to the Contract School.

b. Termination of the approval by the Commissioner of Education of the Contract
School.

c. Failure of the Contract School to abide by all of the terms and conditions of the
Agreement.

The Contract School may terminate this Agreement for just cause and in accordance with
New York State Education Law and/or Commissioner’s Regulations. Before effecting such
termination, the Contract School shall provide the School District thirty (30) days advance
written notice, so as to allow the School District to secure alternative placement of the

pupil(s).

Upon such termination, the School District shall pay the Contract School a prorate portion of
the monthly tuition for the part of the month when services are furnished prior to the
termination.

RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN PARTIES Contract School is engaged by the Board of
Education only for the purpose and to the extent set forth in this Agreement and its relation to the
Board of Education shall, during the period of its engagement and service hereunder, be that of
an independent contractor, and as such shall be free to dispose of such portion of its entire time,
energy and skill as such and is not obligated to devote hereunder to the Board of Education as it
sees fit and to such persons, firms or organizations as it deems advisable. Contract School shall
not be considered under this provision of this Agreement, or otherwise, as having an employee
status or as being entitled to participate in any plans, arrangements or collective bargaining
agreements by the Board of Education pertaining to, or in connection with any salary, term or
condition of employment, health insurance, workers compensation or unemployment insurance
or similar benefits as provided for regular employees. The work contemplated herein must meet
the approval of the Superintendent of Schools or his designee and shall be subject to the general
right of the Superintendent of Schools of inspection and supervision to secure the satisfactory
completion thereof. Contract School is not to be considered an agent or employee of the Board
of Education for any purpose and it is not entitled to any benefits that the Board of Education
provides to employees. Contract School will be solely and entirely responsible for its acts during
the performance of this Agreement. The work and services provided for berein shall be
performed by Contract School, and no other person shall be engaged upon such work or services.
