                        MAHOPAC CENTRAL SCHOOL DISTRICT

                 2024-2025 CONTRACT FOR EDUCATIONAL SERVICES

         This Agreement is made by and between the Irvington Union Free School District, having
its principal place of business at 6 Dows Lane, Irvington, New York (herein after referred to as the
“Sending School District”) and the Mahopac Central School District, having its principal place of
business at 100 Myrtle Ave., Mahopac, New York (herein after referred to as the “Receiving
School District”).

                                         WITNESSETH:

       WHEREAS, pursuant to New York state Education Law, the Sending School District is
authorized to contract for special services or programs as the Sending School District deems
reasonable and appropriate for students with disabilities after consideration of the
recommendations of the local committee on special education (“CSE”); and

        WHEREAS, the Receiving School District is a public school authorized by New York
State to conduct, operate and maintain an educational program and provide special education and
related services to students with disabilities; and

       WHEREAS, the Receiving School District operates the Falls Academy which is a
therapeutic middle school that offers students a unique, innovative, and comfortable learning
environment that engages students and fosters social, emotional and academic growth; and

        WHEREAS, the Sending School District has need of an appropriate placement for a
student identified in Schedule A (herein after referred to as the “Student”), who has been identified
by the Sending School District as a student with a disability; and

        WHEREAS, the Sending School District has determined that the Receiving School
District and the Falls Academy is an appropriate placement for the Student; and

         WHEREAS, the Receiving School District is willing to accept the Student as a student in
its Falls Academy program, subject to the provisions of this Agreement.

        NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained
in this Agreement, the parties hereto mutually agree as follows:

       1.      TERM: This Agreement shall be in effect for the period of July 1, 2024 to June
30, 2025, unless terminated earlier as set forth herein, and not including Extended School Year.

       2.      SCOPE OF SERVICES: During the term of this Agreement, the Receiving
School District shall provide a special education program and/or services to the students listed in
Appendix A of this Agreement in accordance with the New York State Education Department’s
(“NYSED”) regulations, using only professionals certified by NYSED. The Receiving District

                                                 1

shall charge for some services pursuant to the schedule in Appendix B. The Receiving School
District shall provide adequate instruction and/or related services as set forth in the Student’s 2024-
2025 Individualized Education Program (“IEP”), with the exception of assistive technology,
subject to any limitations noted in the Receiving School District’s letter accepting the Student.
Sending School District shall provide any assistive technology set forth in the IEP.

In the event that the Sending School District, upon the recommendation of its CSE, wishes to
modify the instructional program and/or related services (i.e. type, frequency, and/or location)
provided to the Student during the term of this Agreement, the Sending School District shall
present its request for such modifications to the Receiving School District. The Receiving School
District shall review such request and shall determine whether it is able to meet the request. The
Receiving school District shall notify the Sending School District of its determination within ten
(10) business days and shall advise same of any changes in costs associated with any modification
made to the program and/or services. Any agreement with respect to an increase or decrease in
related services or change of program shall be in writing and shall be made an addendum to this
Agreement.

        3.      TUITION PAYMENTS/INVOICES: In consideration for the special education
program to be rendered by the Receiving School District to the Sending School District for the
period of this Agreement, upon presentation of an invoice by the Receiving School District, the
Sending School District will pay for the educational program at the non-resident tuition rate set by
the State Education Department. If the tuition rates for this school year are not available at the
beginning of the school year, the Sending School District shall pay the rates applicable to the
previous school year until new rates are set, at which time the parties shall adjust future tuition
payments so that the Receiving School District will be paid in accordance with the rates applicable
to the current school year. Services will be provided at the additional rate outlined in Appendix
B.

       Receiving School District will submit an invoice for services rendered on a monthly basis,
and payment to Receiving School District shall be made within thirty (30) days from receipt of
invoice from Receiving School District. If the Sending School District disputes the content of any
invoice received from the Receiving School District, the Sending School District must notify
Receiving School District of the dispute within twenty (20) days of its receipt of the invoice.

        No parent or guardian or any other person shall be required to make any payment for
services on behalf of any child covered by this Agreement. Receiving School District and its
employees shall not share or accept any fee or gratuity from the student or student’s family for
service provided pursuant to this Agreement.

        4.      INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR: All employees of Receiving School District
shall be deemed employees of the Receiving School District for all purposes and Receiving School
District alone shall be responsible for their work, personal conduct, direction, and compensation.

                                                  2

Receiving School District acknowledges that it will not hold itself, its officers, employees and/or
agents out as employees of the Sending School District.

         5.     TERMINATION: This Agreement may be terminated by either party upon
thirty (30) days written notice to the other party. In the event of such termination, the parties will
adjust the accounts due and payable to Receiving School District for all services rendered.
Receiving School District will not incur any additional expenses upon receipt of Sending School
District’s notification that Receiving School District’s services have been terminated. Upon any
such termination, the parties shall endeavor in an orderly manner to wind down activities
hereunder. All reports and services due to the Sending School District will be completed by the
Receiving School District within thirty (30) days of the termination date. Following the expiration
or termination of this Agreement, the Receiving School District shall have no further obligation to
provide a placement, a program or any services to the Sending School District’s student enrolled
through this Agreement except as required by State and Federal laws and regulations when the
parent or guardian of such a student invokes pendency as part of an Impartial Hearing.

        6.       RIGHT TO EXAMINE RECORDS/OBSERVATION/INSPECTION: The
Receiving School District agrees to cooperate to the extent required under the law with the State
and its Agents and provide all information to them as may be required for the State and its Agents
to fulfill their auditing or other legal responsibilities, including those reports required to make
determinations as to eligibility under the provisions of the Regulations of the Commissioner of
Education. Receiving School District agrees to provide the State access to all relevant records
which the State requires either Receiving School District’s or Sending School District’s
compliance with applicable Federal or State statutes or regulations with the effect of law, which
regulate either the execution of this Agreement or the performance of obligations under this
Agreement.

       Receiving School District shall be subject to the visitation of the Commissioner of
Education or his/her designated representative.

         Sending School District shall have the right permitted under the law to examine any and
all relevant accounting records as they pertain to tuition and related services.

        7.      SAVE LEGISLATION: Receiving School District understands and agrees that it
is responsible for complying with all applicable Federal, State, local statutes, rules and ordinances
including the New York State Safe Schools Against Violence in Education (“SAVE”) legislation.
Receiving School District shall adhere to all requirements and protocols as established by Sending
School District and NYSED, including, but not limited to, fingerprinting. Receiving School
District further agrees and understands that all individuals providing services under this Agreement
must be cleared by NYSED in accordance with the provisions contained in the SAVE legislation
prior to providing services to students of the Sending School District. In the event the Receiving
School District utilizes a third-party provider or employee who has not obtained fingerprinting

                                                  3

clearance with the State Education Department, the Sending School District shall have the right to
immediately terminate this Agreement.

        8.      ATTENDANCE RECORDS: Receiving School District shall maintain daily
attendance records which shall be submitted to the Sending School District at the end of each
month. If a child has been absent for a period of five (5) or more consecutive days, the attendance
record shall indicate the reason for that absence.

        9.     REPORTS OF STUDENTS: Receiving School District shall obtain whatever
releases or other legal documents that are necessary in order that Receiving School District may
render full and complete reports concerning the education and progress of the Student covered by
the terms of this Agreement. Receiving School District agrees to retain all materials and records
relevant to the execution or performance of this Agreement in accordance with its Records
Retention policy, but in no event less than six (6) years from the end of the Students last date of
enrollment in the Receiving School District.

        10.    VISITS BY SENDING SCHOOL DISTRICT: The Receiving School District
agrees to permit a representative or representatives of the CSE for the Sending School District to
visit the Receiving School District and observe the Sending School District’s student in the
program in which the student is enrolled at the Receiving School District. Such visitation requires
reasonable advance written notice by the Sending School District.

        11.    CSE MEETINGS: The Receiving School District agrees to make relevant
personnel available to participate in person or by telephone in meetings of the Sending School
District’s Committee on Special Education if reasonable prior notice of such meetings is provided
to the Receiving School District.

        12.     STUDENT DISCIPLINE: Receiving School District reserves the right to
suspend the Student from its program if the Student violates the Receiving School District’s Code
of Conduct. The Receiving School District agrees that all disciplinary measures for disabled
students will be conducted in accordance with applicable Federal, State and local laws, rules and
regulations. To the extent practicable Receiving School District shall notify Sending School
District prior to determining to suspend the Student. When prior notice is not practicable, the
Receiving School District shall notify the Sending School District of the suspension within one
business day. In the event that a hearing is required for the suspension under Education Law
Section 3214, the Sending and Receiving School Districts shall mutually agree on which District
shall conduct the hearing. If the Sending and Receiving School Districts are unable to reach an
agreement, the Receiving School District reserves the right to conduct the hearing. If a
manifestation team meeting is required as a result of or in conjunction with the suspension, the
Sending School District shall promptly convene a manifestation team meeting regardless of which
School District conducts the hearing, if a hearing is required.



                                                4

        13.    STUDENT TRANSPORTATION: The Sending School District shall be
responsible for providing all students listed in Appendix A of this Agreement with transportation
to and from school.

        14.     STUDENT PRESCRIPTIONS: Where applicable, Sending School District shall
obtain all medical prescriptions from the parent/guardian of students referred to Receiving School
District. Sending School District shall forward copies of these prescriptions to Receiving School
District upon its written request.

       15.     Nothing contained herein shall serve to transfer responsibilities for the Student from
the Sending School District to the Receiving School District except for the provision of the
educational services per this Agreement.

        16.    CONFIDENTIALITY: Both parties acknowledge and agree to comply with all
laws, rules and/or regulations as applicable, and pertaining to the confidentiality of information
obtained, transmitted, reviewed, generated, requested, provided, maintained and/or otherwise
utilized in connection with this Agreement. This shall include, but not be limited to, the
requirements of the Individuals with Disabilities Act (“IDEA”), the Family Educational Rights
and Privacy Act (“FERPA”) and any concurrent federal and/or state law, rule or regulation. The
Receiving School District agrees that it will not disclose confidential information to third parties
except as provided for and necessitated under this Agreement.

         17.      MUTUAL INDEMNIFICATION AND HOLD HARMLESS: Both parties
further agree that they shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other party to this Agreement
including its officers, directors, administrators, employees, and agents from and against all claims,
loss, liabilities, suits, proceedings, actions, costs, damages, and expenses, including but not limited
to reasonable attorney’s fees, arising out of or resulting from any willful, reckless, or negligent
acts or omissions of the indemnifying party. This obligation shall not be construed to negate,
abridge, or otherwise reduce any other right or obligation of indemnity which would otherwise
exist as to either party.

        18.      INSURANCE: Upon written request by the Sending School District, the Receiving
School District will provide to the Sending School District a copy of the Receiving School
District’s certificate of insurance.

       19.     COVID-19: The Receiving School District has established protocols and
expectations consistent with Federal, state, and local guidance to mitigate the risk of infection of
COVID 19 by students and employees. The Sending School District acknowledges that despite
such precautions, a student may still be infected by COVID-19, and, having independently
evaluated and reviewed the risks of the student being exposed to or infected by COVID-19, the
Sending School District has determined to place the student with the Receiving School District
with full knowledge of the risks.

     Students will be expected to follow all Receiving School District protocols pertaining to
COVID 19 while at the Receiving School District.

       Due to COVID 19 and its impact, the method of instructional delivery may change
throughout the school year (for example, instruction may be provided via in person classes, remote
learning, and/or a hybrid of the two methods). The Receiving School District will provide all IEP
services to the fullest extent practicable and in accordance with State guidance regarding flexibility
with respect to the implementation of IEP services until schools return to normal operating
conditions.

       20.     NOTICE OF LITIGATION: In the event litigation is initiated in connection with
the provision of services and obligations pursuant to this Agreement, either party shall promptly
give written notice of such to the other party within three (3) business days.

        21.     NON-DISCRIMINATION: Services provided under this Agreement shall be
performed without regard to race, creed, religion, national origin, color, sex (including gender and
gender identity), sexual orientation, age, disability or any other category protected under federal
and/or state law.

        22.     NOTICES: All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in

        23. ASSIGNMENT: It is expressly understood that no party may assign their rights,
duties and/or obligations under this Agreement without the written consent of the other party.

        24. WAIVER AND CONSENT: The failure of either party to insist upon performance
of any of the terms, conditions and covenants herein shall not be deemed a waiver of any rights or
remedies that the party may have, and shall not be deemed a waiver of any subsequent breach or
default in their terms, conditions and covenants herein contained. If either party’s consent is

necessary pursuant to the terms of this Agreement, such consent shall not be unreasonably
withheld.

       25. GOVERNING LAW: This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of
New York. Any litigation between the parties have venue in a court of competent jurisdiction in
the County of Putnam in the State of New York and federally the District Court in Westchester,
New York.

       26. SEVERABILITY: If any term, provision, covenant or condition of this Agreement,
or the application thereof to any person, place, or circumstance, shall be held by a court of
competent jurisdiction to be invalid, unenforceable or void, the remainder of this Agreement shall
remain in full force and effect.

        27. EXECUTION: This Agreement may be executed in one or more counterparts, all of
which shall be considered one and the same document as if all the parties had executed a single
original document.

        28. ENTIRE AGREEMENT: This Agreement contains the entire agreement between the
parties and may only be amended in a writing that is signed by both parties and attached to this
Agreement as an Addendum.

       29. AUTHORITY: The undersigned representatives of each party hereby represent and
warrant that the undersigned has full legal rights, power and authority to enter into this Agreement
on behalf of his or her respective party.

       IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Board of Education of the Sending School District and the
Board of Education of the Receiving School District have authorized this Agreement to be signed
by each of its respective officials.
