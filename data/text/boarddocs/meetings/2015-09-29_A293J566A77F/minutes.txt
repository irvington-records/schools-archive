	Discussion: 5.8 Development and Implementation of Strategic Plan (Attachment)

	Mr. Whitney began the discussion by noting that creating a strategic plan has been discussed over the past seven years and reviewed the purpose of developing such a plan, the process, the participants but also stressed that at the end of the process it will be the Board who will ultimately have the final say as to the creation of the long term plan for the District from all the data/input gathered by the community, etc. Dr. Harrison provided the Board with the objectives of undertaking a strategic plan development process and how stakeholders will be engaged in the process including community forums, in online data collection process as well as the formation of a cross-role learning committee. He is recommending Dr. Giselle Martin-Kniep, a recognized leader in the field of education, to facilitate the process. He believes this process is vital and will provide direction across the District as well as the framework for decisions to be made in the future. The cost associated will be $24,000 which includes onsite facilitation, offsite development, travel, etc.

	Mr. Whitney noted that the cross-role learning committee will be a broad representation of the community and the Board will not be involved in picking members as Dr. Martin-Kniep will facilitate that process but the Board will intervene if the committee members look to be the same voices. There will also be Board representation on this committee. Mrs. Palmieri also asked what will be done to communicate this to make sure that it reaches the community at large. Dr. Harrison noted that there is a press release to go to the papers, will ask the Village to spread the word, etc.

	Mrs. Palmieri also feels that community members need to have basic information as to where the District is at this time, a baseline as a jumping off point. Dr. Harrison will speak with Dr. Martin-Kniep who indicated that she didn't necessarily agree with that approach. Mr. Hanna agrees that a baseline is necessary as part of the process.

	A lengthy conversation followed among the Board members as to who would be responsible for ultimately setting the goals, with both Mr. Montgomery and Mrs. Palmieri agreeing that they view it as the Board’s role to craft the plan, using the data gathered by the committee and used as a foundation. Mr. Montgomery would like to see more weight on the process of collecting data which he feels is the most important process for the Board and to make sure the committee presents the Board with findings rather than providing conclusions. Mr. Hanna would like the plan to remain relevant over several years and that those who are charged with carrying out the points of the plan should also be involved in the drafting of the plan. He’d like to hear from students as well.

	Mr. Montgomery expressed that he is comfortable with this approach in a number of ways, but not sure that recommended facilitator is the right person for this and is afraid that it will provide only a middle of the road approach rather than a plan fit for a district such as Irvington. Other concerns expressed related to a narrow representation of the community. The conversation concluded with the agreement to modify the concepts as outlined and how to work with the Board to make sure they are satisfied with the outline and process. Mr. Montgomery believes the process should move forward but the Board must be kept informed all along the way with direct contact with Dr. Martin-Kniep.
