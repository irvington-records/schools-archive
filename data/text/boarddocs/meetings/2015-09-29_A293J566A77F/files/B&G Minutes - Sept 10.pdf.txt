                            BUILDINGS & GROUNDS COMMITTEE

                                 Thursday September 10, 2015
                                           8:00 AM
                                        District Office


                                               MINUTES
In attendance: Phil Whitney, Catherine Palmieri (via phone), Dr. Kris Harrison, Gary Knowles, Beverly
Miller



    1. CROSSWALK PROPOSAL - A recommendation was presented to the District to create a
       crosswalk from the top of Heritage Hill to the High School walkway. Several suggestions
       reviewed by the District and our architect were not viable due to traffic patterns. At the
       meeting, it was suggested that the District look into the possibility of removing part of the wall
       near the loading dock to create a walkway. This will be reviewed with our architect. This item
       will appear on the next B & G agenda for further review.



    2. DOWS LANE CHANGE ORDER – During any construction project situations arise than necessitate
       revisions to the original plans. All of the revisions have been incorporated into one change
       order. Phil requested that a document be created that provides more detail on each item and
       to outline the cost differences against the budget. The change requires Board approval.

    3. CAPITAL PROJECTS – UPDATE ON TIMELINE – The District has finally received State approval of
       the capital projects. Our architect has created an aggressive timeline to (1) complete the Main
       St. gym roofing project this fall and (2) to obtain the best prices on the remaining projects by
       going out to bid early for the work that is to commence next summer. The District will work
       with the architect to finalize all plans and put them out to bid as soon as possible.

    4. REMAINING DOWS LANE MODULAR UNIT – There is one modular unit remaining behind Dows
       Lane School which is used as a maintenance garage. The committee discussed possible
       replacement and location sites. Gary will do an inventory of Dows’ maintenance needs as well
       as the needs of other outside equipment. The District will work on creating a list of potential
       sites, taking into account workspace needed and the pros & cons of each location. This item will
       appear on the next B & G agenda for further review.

    5. EAST FIELD DRAINAGE EASEMENT/PROPERTY LINES – There has been a lot of administrative
       work taking place with legal counsel regarding property lines between Fieldpoint and East Field,

    and obtaining drainage easements. The District is working with legal counsel to obtain all the
    proper documentation to proceed with the capital project.

6. AIR CONDITIONING FOR CLASSROOMS – The committee needs to have an analysis done of the
   needs of the respective schools, including prioritizing areas, electrical implications and financial
   requirements. Gary will discuss with building principals and present his findings at the next B&G
   committee meeting.

7. GIRLS VARSITY BASKETBALL SIGN – The committee reviewed and discussed the size and style of
   a new sign, to be hung under the campus sign at the bottom of Heritage Hill, which would
   incorporate all of the winning years of the team. The style will match the existing campus sign.
   Dr. Harrison and Gary will review two size options and make a decision on the most visually
   acceptable size.
