SEPTEMBER 2015


LIAISON REPORT: COMMUNITY ADVISORY BOARD!
By Michael Hanna!
!
For those who aren't familiar with the Community Advisory Board, it is a fantastic
coalition that includes representatives of the village including our Rec Department,
Police, and Village Board, our schools including the Superintendent, the HS principal
and guidance, local organizations including the PTSA, and about two dozen students
from the middle school and high school. It’s co-chaired by Tanya Hunt and Allyson Felix. !
!
Most importantly, the CAB meetings offer an amazing opportunity for students to raise
concerns that are heard directly by community advocates and decision-makers. CAB
works to support and address those concerns.!
!
Thanks to the award of a substantial 5 year “Drug Free Community” grant!
it’s mission has been supercharged to improve communication and strategies to
improve alcohol/drug awareness, education, and prevention. As part of the award,
which includes $125K a year over 5 years, the organization has been created the iASK-
CAB coalition [, and I’m cribbing a bit from informational materials,] the coalition will
sponsor youth-based training and activities throughout the year that range from health
class instruction at the high school to a pre-Prom “Red Carpet” event and transportation
to the event venue. In addition, they are launching a new website and social media
platforms to provide information and resources for both parents and teens.!
!
Undoubtedly it would benefit the BOE to hear from members of CAB, including
students, about their work in and insights about our schools and community.
