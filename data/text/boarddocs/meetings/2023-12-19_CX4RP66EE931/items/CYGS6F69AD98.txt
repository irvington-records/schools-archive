Category: 9. Personnel
Subject: 9.3 Approval for Establishment of Substitute Teacher After School Hourly Rate
Type: Action (Consent)

The contractual rate for substitutes is set annually on a daily rate basis. With the onset of the after school program and any other hours beyond the work day, the need for an hourly rate exists.
