Details of the Study and Understanding Results
The Irvington Union Free School District (IUFSD) School Quality Surveys asked parents, staff members, and students in grades 6-12 for feedback on various aspects of the district and its schools. K12 Insight partnered with district team members to develop the surveys, which addressed the following topics:

Learning Experiences
Support for Students/Staff
School Leadership
Family Involvement
Communication

The surveys were open June 4-27. 

Email invitations with unique survey links were sent to parents, staff members, and students in grades 6-12. Parents could also participate via a public link on the district’s website. Parents with children attending more than one district school were able to take the survey for each school. Reminders were sent throughout the survey window. The survey was translated into Spanish. 

This report summarizes district-level survey results and breaks them down by stakeholder group. This report compares results with those from 2021-2022 and 2020-2021. 

 K12 Insight uses census sampling, which provides data reflective of all voices in the community. While all parents, staff, and students in grades 6-12 were invited to take the survey, not all participated. Statistical tests designed to infer the perceptions of a larger population from a smaller sample size are not appropriate; rather, descriptive statistics provide the most accurate representation of the data. Therefore, the findings herein cannot be generalized beyond the participants. Nevertheless, this study offers valuable insights about the perceptions of parents, staff, and students in grades 6-12.Additional reports provide school-level results.

Findings for each item in the report exclude participants who did not answer. In charts and graphs, data labels less than 5 percent are not shown. Percentages may not total 100 due to rounding.

[notes] Participating parents and staff members had strong favorability (Strongly Agree or Agree responses) in the Communication dimension. For example, 93% of parents and 94% of staff members said that Irvington UFSD communicated effectively. There was lower agreement among students for this item, partly due to one in five (21%) responding Don’t Know. 
Both parents and staff said their most frequently used resource for information was their weekly school-specific newsletter and the top two preferred methods of communication were email and text messages. 
There was an increase in favorability among participating parents and staff members on items in the Safety and Behavior dimension. For example, 70% of parents and 98% of staff members said that school staff are aware of the safety and security procedures, 10 and 7 percentage-point increases, respectively. 78% of parents and 89% of staff members said that students are aware of the safety and security procedures, 8 and 5 percentage-point increases, respectively. And 88% of parents and 92% of staff members said that schools are safe, 9 and 16 percentage-point increases, respectively.
