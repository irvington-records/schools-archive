HASTINGS ON HUDSON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT
27 FARRAGUT AVENUE
HASTINGS ON HUDSON, NEW YORK 10706

CONTRACT FOR EDUCATIONAL SERVICES

Agreement made by and between the Board of Education of the
Hastings on Hudson Union Free School District, having its
principal place of business at 27 Farragut Avenue, Hastings on Hudson,
New York 10706 (hereinafter referred to as “Receiving School
District”), and Irvington Union Free School District having its principal
place of business at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, New York 10533
(hereinafter referred to as the “Sending School District”).

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the Sending School District has a need of an
appropriate placement for the student(s) identified in Exhibit A
(hereinafter, the “Student”), who is a student with a disability;
and

WHEREAS, the Sending School District has determined that the
Receiving School District’s Communications Development
program is an appropriate program for the Student; and

WHEREAS, the Receiving School District is willing to accept and
enroll the Student in its Communications Development program,
subject to the provisions of this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties mutually agree as follows:

1. The Receiving School District has accepted and is willing to
enroll and instruct Student in its Communications
Development program for the 2019-2020 school year,
beginning on September 3, 2019 and ending on June 25,
2020. Provided both parties execute this Agreement, the
terms of this Agreement are effective commencing on the first
date on which the Student of Sending School District began
attending Receiving Schoo! District irrespective of when each
party executes this Agreement.

[OCR]
2. The Receiving School District agrees to provide the programs
and services set forth in the IEP of each Student as it exists
on the start date of this Agreement, with the exception of
assistive technology and subject to any limitations noted in
the Receiving School District's letter accepting the Student.
The Sending School District shall provide any assistive
technology set forth in the IEP. Implementation of any
modification to the Student’s IEP after the start date of this
Agreement that adds new programs or services or increases
the duration or frequency of existing programs or related
services will be the responsibility of Sending School District
unless the Receiving School District is willing and able to staff
with existing service providers the new and/or modified
programs and/or services.

3. The Sending School District agrees to pay the Receiving
School District for the program, services and instruction
provided pursuant to this Agreement, the New York State
approved 2019-2020 tuition rate for the Receiving School
District's Communications Development program. Unless and
until New York State sets or approves the 2019-2020 tuition
amount for Receiving School District, using the current
approved rates, Sending School District agrees to pay the
Receiving School District tuition in the amount of
$140,981.83 for the program, services and instruction
provided pursuant to this Agreement, which tuition amount
shall be paid in four (4) installments within forty-five (45)
days of Sending School District's receipt of an invoice for each
such installment. Once the 2019-2020 approved tuition rate
is known, if Sending School District has overpaid, the
Receiving School District shall issue a credit against any
tuition stilt due or a refund to the extent any overpayment
credit exceeds the remaining tuition balance and if the
Sending School District has underpaid tuition, Receiving
School District shall issue any invoice to Sending School
District for any additional sums due to Receiving School
District, which inveice the Sending School District will pay
within forty-five (45) days of receipt of the invoice.

4. The Sending School District’s principal contact person shall be
Gail Krieger, Director of Pupil Personnel Services, and all
correspondence, reports and inquiries regarding this
Agreement shall be directed to that person. The principal

[OCR]
contact for the Receiving School District shall be Laura
Sullivan, Director Special Education Services.

5. The Receiving School District agrees to report to the Sending
School District on the progress of the Student on a regular
basis; however, the Sending School District may request
written reports quarterly during the 2019-2020 school year.

6. The Receiving School District agrees to permit a
representative or representatives of the Committee on Special
Education of the Sending School District to visit the
Communications Development program in which the Student
is enrolled if reasonable prior notice of the visit is provided to
Receiving School District.

7. The Receiving School District agrees to comply with all federal
and New York State statutes and regulations regarding the
provision of educational services to students with disabilities.

8. Nothing contained herein, however, shall serve to transfer
responsibilities for the Student from the Sending School
District to the Receiving School District except for the
provision of the particular special education program(s) and
related services contracted for herein and any related
ancillary duties such as report cards and progress reports.

9. The Receiving School District agrees to make relevant
personnel available to participate by telephone or in person in
meetings of the Sending School District’s Committee on
Special Education if reasonable prior notice of such meetings
is provided to the Receiving School District.

10. Nothing herein shall be construed as preventing either
party from taking such action as it may deem necessary,
reasonable or appropriate in the case of a student whose
continued presence in the educational program would
constitute a danger to others, to him/herself, or to property,
sufficiently serious to warrant immediate protective action.

11, Transportation to and from school is the Sending School
District’s responsibility.

12. It is understood and agreed that this Agreement is for the
2019-2020 school year only, subject to the stay put

13. Both parties acknowledge and agree to comply with all
laws, rules and/or regulations as applicable and pertaining to
the confidentiality of information obtained, transmitted,
reviewed, generated, requested, provided, maintained and/or
otherwise utilized in connection with this Agreement. This
shall include but not be limited to the requirements of the
Individuals with Disabilities Education Improvement Act
(“IDEIA”), the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act
(“FERPA”), Section 2-d of the New York State Education Law
and any concurrent federal and/or state law, rule and/or
regulation. The Receiving School District agrees that it will
not disclose confidential information to third parties other
than the Student's parent(s) or guardian(s) except as
required by law or validly issued subpoena. Receiving School
agrees that if it receives a subpoena for divulgence of
confidential information, it shall notify the Sending District
prior to divulging the same, unless the subpoena specifically
prohibits such notification. This provision will survive the
termination and/or expiration of this Agreement.

14. Services provided under this Agreement shall be
performed without regard to actual or perceived race, creed,
color, sex, sexual orientation, weight, national original, ethnic
group, religion, religious practice, gender, age or disability.

15. Any and all notices demands or other communications
required or desired to be given hereunder by either party
shall be in writing and shall be validly given or made to the
other party if personally served or if deposited in the United
States mail, certified or registered, postage prepaid, return
receipt requested to the address set forth above. If such
notice, demand or other communication is served personally,
it shall be effective immediately. If such notice, demand or
other communication is given by mail, the same shall be
effective when received, but in any event, it shall be effective
no later than four (4) days after deposit in the United States
mail addressed to the party to whom such notice, demand or
other communication is to be given, at the address set forth
above. Either party hereto may change its address for

[OCR]
purposes of this paragraph by written notice given in the
manner provided above.

16. The Receiving School District warrants and agrees that
members of its staff who have contact with the Sending
School District’s Student shall have the appropriate
fingerprinting and background checks in accordance with Part
87 of the Regulations of the New York State Commissioner of
Education.

17. The Receiving School District reserves the right to suspend
the Student from its program if the Student violates the Code
of Conduct of Receiving School District. The Receiving School
District agrees that all disciplinary measures for disabled
students will be conducted in accordance with applicable
Federal, New York State and local laws, rules and regulations.
To the extent practicable Receiving School District shall notify
Sending School District prior to determining to suspend the
Student. When prior notice is not practicable, the Receiving
School District shall notify the Sending School District of its
suspension of the Student within 24 hours. In the event that
a hearing is required for the suspension under Education Law
Section 3214, the Sending and Receiving School Districts
shall mutually agree on which School District shall conduct
the hearing. If the Sending and Receiving School Districts are
unable to reach an agreement, Receiving School District
reserves the right to conduct the hearing. If a manifestation
team meeting is required as a result of or in conjunction with
the suspension, the Sending School District shall promptly
convene a manifestation team meeting regardless of which
School District conducts the hearing (if a hearing is required).

18. This Agreement may be terminated by the Sending School
District on fifteen (15) days prior written notice should its
Committee on Special Education determine that the program
at Receiving School District is no longer appropriate for the
Student, the Student ceases to reside within the Sending
School District, or the Student withdraws from the Sending
School District. Upon such termination, Receiving School
District shall be paid for all services provided in accordance
with this Agreement through the date of termination.

19. Subject to the Individuals with Disabilities Education
Improvement Act, this Agreement may be terminated by the

20. The Sending School District agrees to indemnify, defend
and hold harmless the Receiving School District, its officers,
directors, agents, administrators, elected officials or
employees from any claims, liabilities, suits, proceedings,

employees. The obligations pursuant to this paragraph shall
survive the termination and/or expiration of this Agreement.

21. The Receiving School District agrees to indemnify, defend
and hold harmless the Sending School District, its officers,
directors, agents, administrators, elected officials or
employees from any claims, liabilities, suits, proceedings,
costs, damages, expenses and actions, including reasonable

directors, agents, administrators, elected officials or
employees. The obligations pursuant to this paragraph shall
survive the termination and/or expiration of this Agreement.

22. In the event that the parent or person in parental relation
to the Student pursuant to this Agreement files a request for
an impartial hearing or administrative complaint or initiates
litigation in connection with such services, the party receiving
such request, complaint or litigation shall promptly give
written notice of same to the other party. The parties further
agree that the terms set forth in this paragraph shall survive
the expiration and/or termination of this Agreement.

23. No failure by either party to insist upon the strict
performance of any term, covenant, agreement or provision
of this Agreement or to exercise any right or remedy upon a
breach thereof, and no performance of any services by the
other party during the continuance of any such breach, shall

[OCR]
constitute a waiver of any such breach or of any such term,
covenant, agreement or provision.

24. No amendment, change or modification of this Agreement
shall be valid unless in writing, signed by the parties hereto.

25. This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding and
agreement between the parties and any and all prior
agreements, understandings and representations are merged
herein and are of no further force and effect.

26. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State
of New York. Any litigation between the parties shall be
venued in an appropriate court located in Westchester
County, New York.

27. The undersigned individuals warrant that they are duly
authorized agents who possess the legal right and authority
to enter into this Agreement and bind each party with respect
to its obligations set forth herein.
