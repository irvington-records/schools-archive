Meeting: Apr 05, 2022 - Regular Meeting
Date: 2022-04-05
Category: 11. Personnel Matters
Subject: 11.3 Appointment of Special Education Teacher
Type: Action (Consent)

WHEREAS, Michelle Sensi, a tenured elementary and reading teacher began an assignment (40% or more) in the special education tenure area in September 2019; and

WHEREAS, Ms. Sensi has continued the assignment (40% or more) in the special education tenure area during the 2020-2021 and 2021-2022 school years; and

WHEREAS due to these assignments, Ms. Sensi is entitled to a separate probationary period in the special education tenure area;

NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that upon the recommendation of the Superintendent of Schools, Ms. Sensi shall be appointed to a three (3) year probationary period in special education effective September 1, 2019 and shall be eligible for tenure in special education on August 31, 2022.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the District seniority records shall be amended to reflect Ms. Sensi's accrual of seniority in the area of special education, effective September 2019.
