Monthly Financial Highlights

February 2022

Cash Balance

Our February cash position was $48.66 million, which was approximately $16.9 million higher than last month.

Cash received in the Real Property & Other Taxes category included real property tax payment from the Town of Greenburgh for tax
collection during the month of January totaling $17,572,948. Additionally, the district received the County Sales Tax in the amount of
$238,483.

State and Federal Aid Cash Receipts totaled $191,576 which included federal lunch reimbursement, and VLT Lottery aid payment, initial
payment for the ARP ESSER 3 grant.

Cash disbursements during the month of February included normal monthly check warrants and two payroll runs.

Revenue:

.

General Fund Revenue through February was $64,507,926 or 96.7% of the budgeted amount. This percentage is 0.2% compated to the same
time last year.

The District received and recorded the fourth quarter sales tax. Westchester sales tax has been higher year over year. We currently project to
record higher sales tax revenue at the end of the year than budgeted.

The District routinely monitors interest rates. With rates slightly incteasing, we were able to secure a short 4 month T-bill investment at .29%
for $10,000,000. This will result in approximately $8,800 additional interest earnings for the District.

Expenditures:

Actual General Fund Expenses totaled approximately $3.98 million for February. This brings the total year to date expenditures to
$35,150,310. 7% of the budget remains including the outstanding encumbrances.
