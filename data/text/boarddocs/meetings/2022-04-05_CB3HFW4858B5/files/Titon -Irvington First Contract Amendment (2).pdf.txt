                              FIRST AMENDMENT OF
AGREEMENT BETWEEN OWNER AND CONSTRUCTION MANAGER

        WHEREAS Irvington Union Free School District (hereinafter referred to as the
 "Owner") with offices for the transaction of business located at 6 Dows Lane Irvington,
 NY 10533 and Triton Construction Company LLC. (hereinafter referred to as the
 “Construction Manager” and/or “Triton”) with offices located at 30 East 33rd Street,
 11th Floor, New York, New York 10016 entered into an Agreement for Construction
 Management Services, dated August 29, 2018 (the "Agreement"); and
       WHEREAS the Owner and Construction Manager wish to amend the Agreement to
 expand t h e scope of services by the Construction Manager for additional compensation.
        NOW THEREFORE, the parties agree that the Agreement shall be amended as
 follows:
       1. The Construction Manager will be compensated for providing 4 additional
          months of full-time on-site construction supervision from June 15, 2022 thru
          October 15,2022 and 2 Months of pre-construction services, both of which is
          beyond the Construction Management services as set forth in the Agreement.
          a. The additional compensation paid by the Owner for the 4 additional
             months of Construction services will be $23,493.00 paid monthly, for 4
             Months, for a total of $93,972.00.
          b. The additional compensation paid by the Owner for the 2 additional
             months of pre-construction services will be $11,308.00 paid monthly, for 2
             Months, for a total of $22,616.00.
          c. The total additional compensation paid to the Construction Manager will be
             $116,588.00.
          d. There will be no retention withheld from these payments.
       2. All terms and conditions of the Agreement, as amended by the First
          Amendment, shall remain in full force and effect.
       IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this First Amendment to
the Agreement on the dates filled in below.
