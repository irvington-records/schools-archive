TIER
  1
         Project : Convert HS Gym & Maher
                Gym Lighting to LED
   Project summary
   The High School Gym & the Maher Gym lighting use sodium halide
   bulbs. The lighting is very poor, expensive and cannot be quickly turned
   on or off. This proposed project converts the light fixtures to LED.


   Project Rationale
   The primary benefit is energy savings as well as quality of life. The
   estimated annual cost savings is $5,595 or 72% of the total annual
   lighting cost. The quality of life improvement is an intangible benefit. This
   will also reduce the carbon footprint of the District.
