THIS AGREEMENT entered into as of the ____ day of ____________, 2014, by and
between the BOARD OF EDUCATION of the ARDSLEY UNION FREE SCHOOL
DISTRICT (“Ardsley”) with offices for the transaction of business located at 500 Farm
Road, Ardsley, New York 10502, the BOARD OF EDUCATION of the DOBBS FERRY
UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (“Dobbs Ferry”) with offices for the transaction of
business located at 505 Broadway, Dobbs Ferry, New York 10522, the BOARD OF
EDUCATION of the HASTINGS-ON-HUDSON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT
(“Hastings”) with offices for the transaction of business located at 27 Farragut Avenue,
Hastings-on-Hudson, New York 10706 and the BOARD OF EDUCATION of the
IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (“Irvington”) with offices for the
transaction of business located at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, New York 10533
(collectively referred to as the “Quad” or the “parties”).

                                      RECITALS

WHEREAS, the members of the Quad have entered into discussions pertaining to the
administration and management of transportation services provided to resident pupils
attending schools within each of the Quad school districts or non-public schools; and

WHEREAS, the parties believe that it is in the best interest of their taxpayers to share
resources to provide transportation management services; and

WHEREAS, the parties wish to contract with one another for the operation and
provision of transportation management services to each party’s residents in
accordance with General Municipal Law § 119-o; and

WHEREAS, the parties recognize what their respective rights and obligations will be
under the contract;

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants set forth herein, the
parties agree as follows:

1.     This Agreement shall take effect on July 1, 2014 and shall remain in effect until
June 30, 2015.

2.    Notwithstanding the term of agreement set forth herein, the parties shall have
the option to renew the Agreement for additional one-year periods upon consent and
adoption of a resolution authorizing renewal by each board of education. Notice of
non-renewal must be given to the Ardsley Union Free School District by February 15th.

3.     Ardsley, Dobbs Ferry, Hastings and Irvington, each believing it to be in the best
interest of its taxpayers, do hereby authorize inter-municipal cooperation and assistance
with and among each other for the consolidation of their transportation management

services and administration of the transportation management by a single
transportation coordinator as more fully described herein.

4.    The parties agree to cooperate with one another in furtherance of the provision
and operation of the transportation management program.

5.    The transportation coordinator shall perform the following duties:
      a. develop and manage of safe and reliable transportation system of pupil
         transportation that is cost-efficient for all members of the Quad;
      b. maintain an effective safe transportation program which includes but is not
         limited to receiving monthly reports from contractors relative to the following
         safety measures: reporting the number of hours of safety training each bus
         driver and monitor receives, the number of “reportable” school vehicle
         accidents per NYS Department of Motor Vehicles guidelines, reporting the
         school bus inspection failure rate as determined by the NYS Department of
         Transportation;
      c. ensure compliance by all contractors that the bus drivers and bus monitors
         are appropriately trained and meet the minimum guidelines of NYSED and
         NYS Motor Vehicle and Traffic Laws;
      d. ensure compliance by all contractors with all NYS Education Laws, NYS
         Motor Vehicle and Traffic Laws and any and all federal, state, and local laws,
         rules and ordinances applicable to school transportation services;
      e. maintain documentation concerning the number of school bus breakdowns
         that impact service.
      f. Maintain documentation of all complaints regarding service reliability for the
         school districts of the Quad.
      g. perform a self-evaluation of the transportation program; develop efficient
         routes, bus stops and time schedules in consultation with member school
         districts of the Quad.
      h. The members of the Quad shall provide student data for transportation
         purposes in a timely fashion in the format requested by the transportation
         coordinator.

6.     The transportation coordinator shall develop specifications for the provision of
transportation services to the members of the Quad on a cooperative basis. Such
specifications shall be prepared in accordance with all applicable federal, state and local
laws, rules, regulations and ordinances. Upon written consent of the members of the
Quad, the transportation coordinator may utilize a consultant for purposes of
developing the transportation specifications at an additional expense to the Quad
Districts, the cost of same to be shared equally by the members of the Quad. In no event
shall any bid or request for proposals be issued without the review and approval of all
members of the Quad.



                                            2

7.     The transportation coordinator shall consult with each member of the Quad to
determine its needs and solicit their feedback concerning the transportation
specifications developed by the transportation coordinator.

8.     The transportation coordinator shall be responsible for publication requirements
for bids or solicitations that are unique to any member of the Quad in connection with
competitive bidding or other solicitations to which such publication requirements
apply.

9.     The participation by the members of the Quad in the transportation bid or
request for proposals shall be voluntary. Members shall advise the transportation
coordinator of participation no later than February 1st. Participation in this Agreement
shall not prevent members of the Quad from issuing bids or solicitations for
transportation services independently of the Quad cooperative. Members reserve the
right to independently utilize specifications, bids or solicitations prepared by the
transportation coordinator and reserve their rights to award contracts individually and
on their own behalf.

10.    The parties to this Agreement understand and agree that each member of the
Quad reserves the right to make any investigation it deems necessary into a bidder’s or
offeror’s responsibility and qualification to provide the transportation services
requested in the procurement solicitation. Each Quad member’s determination as to the
responsibility or qualifications of a bidder or offeror shall be final, as to that member.

11.    The governing board of each Quad member shall make an award of the resultant
contract to the successful bidder or offeror in accordance with applicable law, policy
and this Agreement.

12.    In addition to the foregoing responsibilities, the transportation coordinator shall
be responsible to work cooperatively with each member of the Quad to develop a
transportation budget for the Quad which must clearly itemize costs by the
participating members. The transportation budget developed is subject to review and
approval by the members of the Quad.

13.    On an annual basis, the transportation coordinator shall review the level of
financial support and/or other resources necessary for the administration of the
transportation program and will propose increases or decreases to the members of the
Quad for review and approval prior to the Program budget preparation process and
prior to the school budget preparation process. Proposals received will be reviewed
and approved by each Board of Education on an annual basis.

14.     Through the efforts of the transportation coordinator, the Quad shall achieve
efficiencies on behalf of the participating school districts through economies of scale


                                            3

and by identifying the most cost effective means for providing transportation (e.g. use
of BOCES-owned buses and vans or contractor-operated vehicles). On or before
February 15th of each year, the transportation coordinator shall provide a report to all
participating members of the occasions when the transportation coordinator saved the
Quad members money.

15.    The transportation coordinator shall oversee the contractors awarded
transportation contracts pursuant to any transportation bid or request for proposals
issued by the participating members.

16.    The transportation coordinator shall act as liaison between the participating
members of the Quad and the contractors awarded transportation contracts by the
participating members. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the transportation coordinator
shall communicate with and keep updated the Assistant Superintendent for
Business/Business Manager for each participating member.

17.    The transportation coordinator shall be and at all times remain an employee of
the Ardsley Union Free School District. Ardsley Union Free School District shall be
responsible for all wages, benefits and supervision of the transportation coordinator.
Nothing in this agreement shall be construed as creating an employee-employer
relationship between the other members of the Quad and the transportation
coordinator.

18.    In consideration of the cooperative services provided for herein, the members of
the Quad shall pay a proportionate share of the cost for provision of transportation
services and the administration of the transportation program by the transportation
coordinator as set forth in Appendix “A”. In the event that any member of the Quad
opts not to utilize the services described herein, the cost allocation shall be revised and
the member shall only be responsible for the pro-rata share of the fees commensurate
with the services utilized by the member.

19.    The parties’ obligations hereunder shall be performed in accordance with all
applicable laws, rules, regulations and ordinances and requirements of the New York
State Education Department.

20.    The parties agree to purchase and maintain an insurance policy to protect each of
them from claims under workers’ or workmen’s compensation, claims for damages
because of bodily injury, occupational sickness or disease, or death of employees, claims
for damages insured by usual personal injury liability coverage or injury to or
destruction of tangible property, including loss of use resulting therefrom for which the
either party may be legally liable. Insurance shall be required as follows:

       a. Commercial General Liability Insurance: $1,000,000 per occurrence and
          $2,000,000 aggregate.
       b. Commercial Excess Insurance: $3,000,000 per occurrence and $3,000,000
          aggregate.
       c. Workers’ Compensation and N.Y.S. Disability: Statutory Workers’
          Compensation, Employers’ Liability and N.Y.S. Disability Benefits Insurance
          for all employees.

Each party shall furnish to the other certificates evincing its procurement of such
policies. Policies shall contain a thirty (30) day notice of cancellation.

22.    The parties agree to maintain all documents and records created or maintained in
connection with this Agreement for a period of six (6) years after the termination of this
Agreement. Each party agrees to make those documents available for audit and
inspection of any government official or agency with authority and/or jurisdiction over
the provision of the services described herein.

23.   Any alteration, change, addition, deletion or modification of any provision of
this Agreement or of any right any party has under this Agreement must be made by
the mutual assent of the parties in writing and signed by each party.

25.   This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York. If any
portion of this Agreement is found by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid or
unenforceable, the remainder of this Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.

25.   This Agreement constitutes the full and complete Agreement between and
among the parties and supersedes all prior written and oral agreements, commitments
or understandings with respect thereto.

26.    This Agreement has been mutually arrived at and shall not be construed against
either party as being the drafter or causing this Agreement to be drafted.

27.    The undersigned representatives of each party hereby represents and warrants
that they are officers, agents, or directors of the party for whom they sign with all legal
rights, powers and authority to enter into this agreement on behalf of the respective
parties and bind them with respect to all obligations enforceable against them in
accordance with the terms contained herein.
