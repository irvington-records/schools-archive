	10. Presentations/Discussions

	Discussion: 10.1 2014-15 Budget Discussion (Presentation)

	Dr. Harrison reported that over the course of developing the proposed budget, the tax cap was substantially reduced from the original figures that the state had provided. He recapped the information provided at the March 18th meeting, stating that where the Board felt strongly about certain items, they have been added back into the budget, such as scheduling flexibility at the high school, a crisis TA training at Dows Lane and school bus monitors. The total of these items are $155,778. Dr. Harrison is asking the Board to reconsider the full-time athletic trainer that had been removed as he feels that it is a very important service for our students. The cost of the trainer is $30,000. These proposed additions equal $185,778. There were other reevaluations to the proposed budget and items totaling $203,036 were removed from the budget, such as deletion of a 1.0 FTE IMS teacher, reduction to custodial supplies, reduction of BOCES fees, lower projected teaching salaries, postponing repairs to Dows Lane’s windows as well as a reduction in the transportation contract and a reduction in the NYSIR insurance fees. With the noted shifts of additions and reductions, the budget is now $26,982 under the tax cap, with a budget increase of 3.82% and a tax rate levy increase of 3.31%.

	The Board now will need to consider the additional $132,985 in state aid that Irvington is to receive through the state’s budget. Several options include add backs to the budget of items that were eliminated, funding capital projects that wouldn't need to be bonded or to reduce the tax levy, and would equate to a .28% in the levy, from 3.31% to 3.03%.

	Members of the Board then spoke to individual items contained in the proposed budget. Comments included, but are not limited to:

-
		Mr. Grados would be in favor of applying the additional state aid to capital projects as well as the .40FTE Music.

-
		Mr. Whitney would be in favor of adding back the .40FTE band/music position as it is a challenge to manage the number of students in the program and the students would feel a positive impact. He also stated that he is not in favor of supporting the communications allocation ($18,000) and would rather the money benefit classroom expenses.

-
		Mr. Montgomery asked about the tax cap and how it affects future tax cap rates and those budgets, stating that he favors doing soft costs that won't need to be removed from future budgets due to constricted caps. He is also in favor of adding back the 40FTE music but is still hesitant about the high school writing program and the database management position, due to the lack of clarity, as to how the positions will provide benefit students. He doesn't feel there has been an adequate conversation as to what programs we would want to add and that spending the money in these areas is premature until further conversations are held. Other questions still remain regarding the athletic trainer and the communications request.

	Mrs. Kerner noted that her priorities are always what will most positively impact the students as well as the fiscal health of the district, concurring with the others that the .40FTE music will be a benefit to the students. She also feels that the $18,000 for an outside consultant for communications would not directly impact the students.

	She would like to see if there could be additional funding to bolster the guidance department. She is also concerned about future budgets and perhaps best to spend money to get close to the cap, and reducing the fund balance by $400,000 should there not be extra money this year to replace the funds that are earmarked to offset the tax levy, needing to be cognizant of risks associated with reducing the fund balance. Mr. Montgomery would like to see funding for tax certs in a budget line.

	Discussion: 10.2 Fields and Facilities Discussion (Presentation)

	Dr. Harrison provided a recap of the recommendations made by the Field Project Committee, noting that the Board concurred that East Field should be surfaced with natural turf and install drainage and irrigation. The remaining options the Board must decide is what type of playing surface should be put on Meszaros Field, i.e. organic infill, hybrid infill, and the surface on Oley track, as well as other recommendations made by the FPC, i.e. lacrosse netting, storage shed, bleachers and some type of irrigation at Lower Dows. He also recapped the facilities recommendations made by the B&G Committee. Items under consideration include energy savings projects, quality of life, safety projects. Dr. Harrison reviewed the estimated debt service and budget impacts of any new bond that is approved through a voter referendum.

	Two independent turf vendors provided an overview of their artificial turf products: FieldTurf and GeoTurf. They also addressed maintenance of their products and warranty questions.

	The Board discussed the facilities recommendations and adjustments have been made. Mr. Grados is in favor of the Tier I projects as presented. Mr. Montgomery noted that many of the facilities recommendations are items related to the gym which could be seen as a negative in the community. The Board discussed the timeline for decision-making as it relates to the vote for the bond, possibly removing the MSS gym lighting might be advisable. Mr. Grados expressed that he hopes that the community will realize that all items are in the best interest for the district and are items that have been neglected over the years. Mrs. Kerner feels that there are other items that could be taken care of instead of the recommended remediation to the dust bowl.

	Mrs. Kerner noted that the grass cost information provided was not updated as was the artificial turf calculations, and this is based on some information that has been provided. She feels that it might no longer be a given that in order to sustain our needs that we must use artificial turf and feels that looking to natural grass is worthwhile to see if grass could handle the needs.She asks if the focus should also be on making sure that all our fields are usable as they are a system and shouldn't just focus on the two fields.
