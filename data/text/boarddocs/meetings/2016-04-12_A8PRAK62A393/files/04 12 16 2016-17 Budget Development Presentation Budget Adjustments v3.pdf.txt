                                      4


 Maintain a proposal that is under the tax-levy cap
 Include an additional modified volleyball team

  to the recommended classrooms

   Budget Adjustments & Considerations
                                    5

Included in new proposal - net budget decrease $4,000
 Additional modified volleyball team: est. cost $9,700
 Decrease original proposal world language faculty at IHS by 0.2 FTE:
  est. cost ($13,700)
   0.2 FTE (rather than 0.4) will provide smaller class sizes and
     scheduling flexibility
   Elementary world language will be tabled with discussion at a future
     meeting

Not integrated into proposal:
 Air Conditioning
   Facility concerns could present greater expenses
   Project can be revisited at a future date
 Contracted field maintenance: $36,000
