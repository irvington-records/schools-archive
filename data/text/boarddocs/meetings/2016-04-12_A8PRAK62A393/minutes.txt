	5. Discussion

	Discussion: 5.1 2016-17 Proposed Budget (Attachment)

	Dr. Harrison noted that this will be the last opportunity to make any changes to the proposed budget prior to the adoption of the recommended 2016-17 budget on April 19, 2016. The proposed budget balances programs with fiscal responsibility. Dr. Harrison provided an overview of the adjustments to the original proposed budget which now includes an additional modified volleyball team in the amount $9,700, reduction of proposed 0.4 FTE in world language to 0.2 FTE increase in world language which will affect the high school by possibly having smaller class sizes as well as scheduling flexibility, noting that the current position will now be 0.8 FTE. With respect to introducing a language program at MSS for 5th graders, this will be further discussed at a future board meeting. With respect to the request to purchase and install 40 air conditioners in the district for $100,000, this has not been included as there were facility concerns that would need to be addressed, however the project will be visited at a future date. With respect to including funds for field maintenance, outside firm would cost approximately $36,000.

	Dr. Harrison reported that the proposed budget is a 1.10% budget to budget variance from last year's budget and will be $4,304 under cap. He then reviewed the tax calculation, noting that Irvington has a negative tax cap of -0.07% or $37,080, the only district in Westchester with a negative tax cap. With changes in assessed value, the tax rate increase would be 1.24%, the lowest in six years. By way of example, with the average home assessed at $803,075, there would be a $213 increase in taxes.

	Other considerations such as the previously mentioned air conditioners, 1.0 FTE library media specialist, 1.0 FTE technology teacher and a 0.5 FTE psychologist are not included in the proposed budget. An outside ground maintenance would require $32,000  to be allocated from the fund balance, which would bring the amount from the fund balance to $532,000 or staff personal would require an addition $87,000 be allocated from the fund balance.

	Comments from Community

-
		Eric Oley commented on the need for field maintenance and noted his strong recommendation that the Board hire an outside grounds maintenance in order to gain their expertise, help train staff as to proper maintenance of fields.

-
		Alison Waguespack, co-president of the PTSA asked if the PTSA could help offset an expense, such as modified volleyball team or supplies or in another area as they are in a position to help fund something (approximately $10,000).

	Board Discussion

	Mr. Grados agrees with Mr. Oley, however, he asks if there is a way to work the cost of position into the existing budget to remain under the tax cap rather than adding to the amount taken from the fund balance. Mr. Montgomery believes that it will require either tightening up certain budget lines which would then possibly leave less to be put towards the tax certiorari account.

	Mr. Montgomery had questions with respect to world language and the contracted field maintenance. He is happy that the world language program will be augmented, however, will still have faculty member not being full-time. It was noted that the 0.4 was reduced to 0.2 FTE as administration felt it was not needed at this time. Mrs. Palmieri noted that it was not the intent of the Board to reduce it but that the 0.4 would enhance the program. Their concern is to be sure that the District can retain the part-time employee.  The trade-off was the addition of the modified volleyball but Mr. Montgomery doesn't believe that that (0.2 FTE language teacher) necessarily needs to be the trade-off.  Mr. Grados asks if the additional of the 0.2 FTE will move the program forward, agreeing that smaller class sizes would be beneficial. Mr. Hanna noted that he trusts the administrators to make these decisions, but would like to identify staff we would like to retain, and to do so earlier in the process going forward. Dr. Harrison believes that it would be best to identify the Board's priorities and if items that are valued, the conversation needs to cover how to pay for those values, but it will also require increased use of fund balance. Mr. Whitney also believes that the administration needs to make staffing decisions and must be owned by administration. Mr. Montgomery would like to see class sizes by grade and would like the Board to tackle the broader discussion of what the objectives of the world language program are and how to evaluate the program.

	With respect to the grounds maintenance position, Dr. Harrison highlighted the current staff’s responsibilities with respect to the maintenance of the fields, i.e., irrigation of fields, top dressing, etc. but noted that staff members do not have expertise in field maintenance, such as a contractor who would provide strategies to maintain healthy turf, etc. This might not be perfect solution but would expand upon what current staff now performs and will provide needed expertise. It was clarified that the outside firm would take over all aspects of hands on maintenance except for the mowing of the lawns. Mr. Hanna also sees this addition as providing more time for current staff to handle their other responsibilities. Dr. Harrison believes it is a wise investment to have expertise providing labor and overseeing the maintenance fields. The conversation then focused on how to pay the $36,000, whether it be taken from fund balance, a trade off or taken from the end of the year surplus. Dr. Harrison did state that this year's budgeting was the tightest since he's been in Irvington, especially in the area of special education tuitions, which could exceed the budget, or if another section needs to be added, funds would need to be taken from fund balance. To look for reductions in anticipated costs, could look at salary lines but may not have reductions as in previous years. Mr. Whitney asked if there were items within the B&G budget to reduce in order to cover the field maintenance costs, but this would only defer costs to next year which could be another difficult year.

	With respect to possible tax certiorari liabilities, potentially and outlay of $1 million in settlements, Mr. Montgomery asked how much is being placed in the tax certiorari reserve, if there would be a surplus this year. AT this time, Ms. Miller cannot say with certainty that there will be a surplus of $1 million, but confident there will be at least $400,000 in surplus, and noted that currently there are $400,000 in known settlements, with an additional $600,000 in possible settlements. It might be necessary to take out a BAN to cover the settlements. Mr. Montgomery noted that the recent tax assessment might cause the tax certs to be reduced in the future.

	To recap, the administration recommends using the $32,000 from the fund balance to include the field maintenance,

	Mr. Montgomery and Mr. Grados, Mr. Hanna agree that along with the cooperation of the PTSA of $15,000, would be willing to add grounds maintenance as well as the additional 0.2 FTE for world language, which would require only $30,000 to cover the net difference. it was unanimous  amongst the Board to add $30,000 in order to cover field maintenance and 0.4 FTE world language.

	Mr. Hanna wanted to reiterated that we are looking at a tough year next year and to be mindful of that when supporting this budget.
