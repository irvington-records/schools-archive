What is a Capital Reserve?

The Capital Reserve was established to fund larger capital expenditures that could not be
supported in the annual general fund budget and are typically smaller than projects that are
included in a capital bond project.

The District established our capital reserve in 2015 to fund up to $1,500,000 and increased it to
$10,000,000 in 2023. The establishment of the reserve fund was voter approved.

The reserve is funded through annual budget surpluses that are not allocated to other
reserves, appropriations for following year budget, encumbrances or our undesignated fund
balance.

As of June 2024, the Capital Reserve amount is $4,034,571 which includes funding and
interest revenue.

What did the District envision using this reserve for?

The District initially planned on using this reserve for the turf and track
replacement knowing they have an approximate 10 year life span. Funds were
also added to tackle other projects that are part of our long range facilities plan.
The use of a reserve saves the District money as it eliminates the need to borrow
and pay interest. Instead, the District is accruing interest on the funds in the
reserve account.

How can the Capital Reserve fund be accessed?

To spend from the Capital Reserve, voters must approve the spending via a
proposition on a ballot. The vote can occur at any time of the year.

We propose adding this proposition to the May 20th Budget/Board Trustee
election vote versus holding a separate vote for this purpose to not incur
additional vote costs.

Will spending from this reserve increase taxes?
This spending will strictly come from the funds set aside in the Capital Reserve and will not
impact the tax levy or tax rate. Unlike a Capital Bond Project/Proposition, there is no
borrowing associated with using the funds in the Reserve.


When will the work be completed?
After a successful vote, the District will work with our Architects to create the speciﬁcations
needed to submit to the State Education Department so we can receive building permits and
eventually state aid on projects that are aidable. This will take several months. Our best
estimate is to plan on work commencing the Summer of 2026. It is possible that once we
have permits and can bid out the work, some of this work might be able to get done over a
break period. We will look to minimize construction cost which also means grouping similar
work together (such as all the paving and concrete repair) to get a favorable bid.

What are the beneﬁts of including lights on Meszaros Field?

Student and Community Engagement!

 ●   Lighting extends ﬁeld availability into the evening, effectively doubling the hours for
     both in-district and out-of-district athletic events.

 ●   Would allow us to hold Friday or Saturday night competitions, enhancing community
     engagement during the regular season

 ●   Currently we rent lights for fall homecoming events like ﬁeld hockey, football, and soccer.
     This allows for the expansion of spring sports with "Light Up the Night" events, similar to
     homecoming, for sports such as ﬂag football, lacrosse, and track and ﬁeld,

 ●   Provide increased opportunities for Irvington youth and recreational programs and
     greater availability for scheduling.

When will the lights on Meszaros Field be in use?

 ●   The District intends to use the lights primarily for Irvington Athletics

 ●   The latest lights would be on are 9:30 pm weekdays and 10:00 pm for Friday/Saturday use

 ●   Irvington Rec/Youth Sports may apply to use the ﬁeld lights

 ●   The months of lights use will be August, September, October, November, March, April
     and May

 ●   The District will publish a schedule of events where the Field Lights will be in use on the
     District website ahead of each season

Upon approval of the capital reserve proposition and installation of the ﬁeld lights, the
Facilities Use Policy/Regulation will be revised accordingly.
