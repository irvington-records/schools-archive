Eastchester Union Free School District
Contract for Health & Welfare Services

THIS AGREEMENT, made on this day of , 2025 by and between the
Board of Education of Irvington Union Free School District, Village of Irvington, Westchester County, New York,
hereinafter called the Party of the First Part, and the Board of Education of the Eastchester Union Free School
District, Town of Eastchester, Westchester County, New York, hereinafter called the Party of the Second Part.

WITNESSETH: WHEREAS the Party of the First Part has resolved that pursuant to Section 912 of the Education Law,
it enter into a contract for the purpose of providing, through the Party of the Second Part, health and welfare
services for children residing in its own school district but attending non-public schools in the Eastchester UFSD to
begin on September 1, 2024 and to end on June 30, 2025.

NOW, THEREFORE, the Party of the First Part hereby agrees to pay the Party of the Second Part the sum of
$1,543.85 per pupil for health and welfare services to be provided for approximately 1 pupil who is a resident of
the district of the Party of the First Part but attends a non-public school located in the district of the Party of the
Second Part; and the Party of the First Part agrees to pay the Party of the Second Part a pro-rata share for any
students who are not enrolled for the entire school year, based upon the length of enrollment.

And the Party of the Second Part hereby agrees with the Party of the First Part that the health and welfare services
provided under Section 912 shall consist of the following: annual medical inspection, school nursing services,
vision and hearing tests, examination for employment certificates, examination for participation in athletics,
scoliosis examination, notification of parents regarding defects and follow-up, instructions for first aid care of
school emergencies, psychological and psychiatric services, and social counseling services.

The Party of the Second Part will also furnish the following equipment to be used in providing such services if
requested by the authorities in charge of the non-public school: vision and hearing testing devices and health
record forms.

It is expressly agreed by and between the parties hereto that the services agreed to be supplied under this contract
shall not include teaching services.

It is mutually agreed that this contract shall not become valid and binding on either party thereto until the same
shall be approved by the district Superintendent of Schools.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have hereunto set their hands this day and year above written.
