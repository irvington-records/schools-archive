Category: 9. Business of the Board
Subject: 9.2 Capital Reserve Proposition
Type: Action (Consent)

Capital reserve proposition to utilize capital reserve funds for District improvements. Since the funds to be expended are from the 2015 Building Capital Reserve Fund, approval of this Proposition will not require a tax levy upon the real property of the School District.
