          Need for Additional Clerical Position


   John Cardinal O’Connor School
   Significant increases in Medicaid billing/reporting requirements
   Increased numbers of CSE/CPSE/504 Meetings
   Increased state reporting requirements
   STAC reporting
   Homebound Instruction
   Increased number of contracts for services
   DASA Responsibilities
   Nurse Sub procurement
   Response to Intervention (RTIm Direct)
   McKinney Vento (Homeless) reporting

    Need for Additional High School Special Education Teacher



   Currently five high school special education teachers teach an extra period to cover the needs
   We have 7 Teachers at IMS and only 5.3 at the IHS for one more grade level
   Increased need for departmentalized special classes
   Reduces the need for out-of-district placements
   1/3 of students with IEPs are at the HS
   1/2 of students with 504 plans are at the HS

                Need for Additional Elementary Special Education Teacher


   We have needed two K Integrated Co-Teaching (ICT) classes for the past two years
     We may need to continue that trend
     It is difficult to predict what will be needed for four year olds next year this early in the school year


   We have had a significant number of students with disabilities move into the third grade which may result in a
    need for two sections in fourth grade next year
