                               Safety Committee Meeting
                                       Minutes
                                     Jan. 16, 2013

Present: Beverly Miller (chairperson), Will Baisley, Florence Cerbone, Artie McCormack, Scott
Mosenthal, David Sottile, Rich Pittore, Gary Knowles, Dr. Harrison, Maria Kashkin, Scott
Palermo, Rich Pittore, James Groven, Randy Lichtenwalner and Debbi Centofanti
Absent: Laurie Noonan, Jamie Wilson and police

The meeting opened with a review of the minutes from the Oct. 24, 2012 meeting.

Old Business
     Speed Humps – They are on hold for now until we know what is being done with the east
       field.
     Additional camera by southwest corner of campus where students go down the hill? Gary
       received one quote for $14,000. He is seeking other quotes.
     Is the camera at the intersection by the Maher gym working? Edutek needs to plug it in.
     Since the new window hardware was installed, David S.is concerned with decreased air
       flow in classrooms and also no egress windows in the smaller rooms. Can windows be
       opened from the top. Gary is meeting with architect tomorrow and will look into these
       issues.

New Business
    Lock down Drills- NY State law requires that you conduct two emergency drills each
      year but we are doing them on a monthly basis.
    Security- Dr. Harrison, Beverly and Gary are meeting with security firms that deal with
      school districts and then will make recommendation to the board. The security firm
      would do an assessment, make recommendations, and update our emergency plans. Also
      staff development would be done over the next two years. Some ideas being looked at are
      swipe card access and walkie- talkies that are tied into PA system.
    Discussion on the system of locking classroom doors. Keys are specific to a room. Don’t
      want to have too many master keys. Doors should be locked when no class in session.
    Main St. School doors are old and can only be locked from the outside so they are
      keeping them locked. Can’t use hold open devices on the doors because they are against
      fire code. Look into magnetic strips that go on the door strikers and door stays unlocked
      until you remove the strip and then door is locked.
    Current walkie-talkies have a limited range.

Accident Reports
    Dows Lane – 1, Main St.-13, Middle School – 6, HS – 15 (13 from sports)
       Consistent with prior years
