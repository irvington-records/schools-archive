	3. Recognition of Students

		Recognition:  3.1 Winners of the 6th Annual Main Street School Spelling Bee

		Dr. Harrison introduced the winners and runners up of the Main Street School's 6th Annual Spelling Bee and acknowledged the hard work of all the students who particpated in the Bee.   Ms. Casey, Interim Principal, noted that there was a great deal of competition with lots of studying of 500 words. The Bee is a joint effort with the PTSA who provided tee shirts, water bottles and medals and ribbons. Ms. Casey expressed that she was proud of how the students were very supportive of each other and reflected everything that is taught in their character education program.

		The winners and runner ups were presented with Certificate of Recognition from the Board of Education. The 4th Grade Winner was Alexandra Oster, and Nicholas Papapanou was the runner up. Max Edelman was the 5th Grade winner and Aiden Meaney and Arushi Parekh were the runner ups.

		Recognition:  3.2 Varsity Girls' Soccer Team for Achieving the Highest GPA among Girls' Soccer Teams in New York State

	      Prior to introducing the Girls Soccer Team, Dr. Harrison reported that the team acheived the highest GPA in the State, 98.833%, and congratulating the team for representing the athletic program and high school very well. Mr. Rich Silverstein of the NYSPHSAA (New York State Public High School Athletic Association) presented the NYS Scholar Athlete Award to the team. Mr. Silverstein, Assistant Executive Director for Section I Athletics, presented a banner to the team and noted that being recognized as a state champions is a feat beyond incredible but that the team of 16 girls placed first among the 467 teams in the State  with a GPA of 98.833%, the highest average earned in the 22 year history of the program. He also noted that this is the 26th award as State Athlete for Irvington ,and the first for the Girls Soccer Team. Mr. McCormack, Athletic Director, introduced the members of the team that were present as well as Coach Tiffany Flynn. Mr. McCormack also added that all Irvington fall teams were recognized as Scholar Athletes, each one having achieved at least a 90% GPA.

	10. Presentations/Discussions

		Presentation:  10.1 2013-14 Budget Discussion: Athletics, Special Education and Pupil Personnel Services

		Dr.Harrison reiterated that the budget process involves reviewing the budget line by line and identifying areas that need to be increased, or possibly reduced, and prioritizing what is needed to support  students' needs.

		Athletics

		Mr. McCormack, Athletic Director, reviewed the interscholastic athletics budget, noting that coaching salaries are proposed to be higher, due to increases in coaches as well as settled contracts. Overall, there is a 8.37% increase to cover a request for a full time athletic trainer that is needed, uniforms, and travel to competitions, etc. He is also requesting that the JV Field Hockey coach be reinstated as well a partial reinstatement of the JV Boys Lacrosse Coach. been very beneficial to the overall programs.

		He reviewed achievements in athletics, including but not limited to: 10 league champions, two NYS Scholar Athlete Champions, 24 New York State Scholar Athlete teams, two Con Ed Scholar Athletes, two sectional champions and two county champions.

		Mr. McCormack also noted that there is a need for a third ice hockey team as there is an increase in interest. In response to a question regarding fundraising, Mr. McCormack projected that 50-60% of the teams currently fundraise and Mr. McCann would like a list of the teams that do not currently fundraise, stating that everyone must begin to participate in supporting the programs.

		Special Education and Pupil Personnel Services

		Mr. Palermo addressed the special education and pupil personnel budget. Two special education teachers and a clerical position are being requested. Mr. Palermo addressed the need for the additional clerical position, among the reasons for the need are significant increases in Medicaid billing/reporting paperwork, the increased enrollment of the John Cardinal O'Connor School, an increase in the number of CSE/CPSE\504 meetings, along with other mandated reporting requirements.

		Also, the need for a special education high school teacher is needed and over the last two years, students have come to the high school needing special classes, which further stresses the teachers' load. Currently, all teachers are teaching a 6th class as allowed by contract and noted that there are fewer teachers at the high school than at the middle school and that 1/3 of students with IEP's are at the high school. Mrs. Kerner stated that it will be important to look at all the programs and current resources and effectively utilize current staff in more efficient ways so as not to increase staff.

		With respect for the request for an additional ICT classroom teacher for fourth grade, Mr. Palermo noted that there has been a significant increase in the students with disabilities moving into the District, especially in third grade this year. In addition, there might be a need for an additional ICT class for kindergarten but it is difficult to determine that at this time. There has been an increase in kindergarten students coming to the District and also in the complexities of their issues.

		Mr. Palermo reviewed the tuition budget lines, noting that an 8% increase is generally used each year to cover private/agencies and BOCES placements. Overall, there is an increase of $81,478 in these budget lines. There is an overall increase of 7.33% in the special education budget lines and a 3.87% in pupil personnel services.

		Dr. Mosenthal addressed the current 1.0 guidance position with .5 being paid by BOCES for the first time this year, but BOCES may not renewing the contract next year. He outlined three reasons to extend this to 1.0 FTE: 1) the chair would pick up 50-60 student caseload, freeing other counselors, 2) would connect with students and parents and 3) hard to imagine what a .5 position would look like, not to mention that there would be a very small pool of counselors for a .5 position.

		Mrs. Miller reported that with all presentations given, there is a 1.64% overall increase for the 2013-14 budget. Next meeting will cover curriculum, instruction, etc.

		Stephanie Plaut asked if there was a trigger point that increased our number of students joining athletics and also asked about what type of fundraising would be considered and if possible to take a look at costs per pupil for some of the more expensive sports, such as ice hockey. Dr. Harrison did note that areas are going to be reviewed, especially the types of fund raising that would be possible for teams to undertake to reduce the burden on taxpayers.
