                        BUILDINGS & GROUNDS COMMITTEE

                    October 26, 2015, 7:30 AM, at District Office


                                          MINUTES



1.   Crosswalk Proposal –currently being reviewed by our architects for viability and cost
     estimate.



2. Remaining Dows Lane Modular Unit –

        a. Committee reviewed a list of district-wide maintenance storage requirements.
        b. Committee than reviewed some options, short-term and long-term, of the needs
           district- wide for outside maintenance and athletic storage.
        c. Short-term options: Gary and Bev will obtain est. cost for a small shed to replace the
           current modular unit. Cost to include architectural fees (as it will need SED
           approval), modular demolition costs, cost of new shed and installation.

                     It was noted that a shed replacement of similar size to the modular (approx.
        310 sq. ft.) does not address the long term needs of the District)



3. Air Conditioning for Classrooms –
       a. Committee reviewed a list, compiled by Gary and the principals, of the principals’
          priority areas of classrooms needing AC: Dows = 7; Main St. = 12; Middle School =9;
          High School = 12, totaling 40 classrooms designated as #1 priorities.
       b. Total cost of project would be approx. $100,000:

                    Electrical upgrades to accommodate the above classrooms were $62,500 per
                    estimate received from our electrical contractor.

                    AC units are $790 for small units and $899 for large AC units (which most
                    rooms would require). Our maintenance staff would install the units.

        c.    Also discussed was a “central AC concept” for the Middle School. There is currently
             some ductwork already in place. However, per Gary, this concept would cost
             hundreds of thousands of dollars as it would require a new chiller, new air handlers,
             and other work to be operational.

4. Campus Patio –

    The Irvington PTSA presented a plan for a 26 x 20 patio in the quad (where there are
existing picnic tables). It would be surrounded by a cobblestone border set in concrete. The
base would be comprised of engraved bricks that the PTSA would sell, on a continual basis, as
a fundraiser. Open areas would be filled with plain bricks and would be replaced with engraved
bricks as they are sold. Per PTSA,” This fundraiser would hopefully raise money that can be
used for district projects, while already beginning the beautification process with building the
patio.”

The Buildings & Grounds Committee supports this project.

Board approval of this project will appear on a forthcoming agenda.
