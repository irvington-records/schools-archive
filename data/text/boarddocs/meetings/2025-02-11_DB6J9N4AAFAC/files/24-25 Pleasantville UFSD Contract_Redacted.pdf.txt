CONTRACT FOR SPECIAL EDUCATION SERVICES 2024-2025
Non-Resident Tuition
AGREEMENT made by and between the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District
having its principal office at 6 Dows Lane, Irvington, NY 10533 (hereinafter called the “Sending School
District”) and the Pleasantville Union Free School District having its principal office at 60 Romer Avenue,
Pleasantville, New York 10570 (hereinafter called the “Pleasantville UFSD”).

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS the Sending School District has need of an appropriate placement for Student #21674, a disabled
child, and

WHEREAS the Sending School District has determined that the Pleasantville High School Therapeutic
Support Program is appropriate for the child and

WHEREAS, the Pleasantville UFSD is willing to accept the child as a student in its Pleasantville High School
Therapeutic Support Program, subject to the provisions of this Agreement,

IT IS AGREED as follows:

1. The Sending School District hereby agrees to place Student #21674 in the Pleasantville High School
Therapeutic Support Program in the Pleasantville UFSD for the 2024-25 school year.

2. The Sending School District agrees to pay the Pleasantville UFSD for this service the annual sum of
$70,690.00 prorated rate of $45,948.50 for remaining 117 days, as detailed in the annual tuition
summary worksheet that accompanies this contract. Tuition costs are inclusive of general or foundation
aide payable to Pleasantville UFSD.

3. The Pleasantville UFSD wiil charge, and the Sending District will pay for any requested evaluations,
including but not limited to psychological, educational, speech, occupational therapy, physical therapy,
assistive technology, or augmentative communication, that are deemed necessary by the CSE, as well as
any assistive technology and/or instructional equipment as set forth in the child’s Individualized Education
Program (IEP).

4, The Pleasantville UFSD agrees to provide the services as set forth in the child’s Individualized Education
Program (IEP).

5. The Pleasantville UFSD agrees to report to the Sending School District on the progress of the child as
requested.

6. The Pleasantville UFSD agrees to permit a representative or representatives of the Committee on Special
Education of the Sending School District to visit the program in which the child is enrolled on reasonable
notice.

[OCR]
The Pleasantville UFSD agrees to comply with all federal and state statutes and regulations regarding the

provision of educational services to students with disabilities, subject to the provisions of Paragraph “9”
below.

8. Nothing contained herein, however, shal] serve to transfer responsibilities for the Child from the Sending
School District to the Pleasantville UFSD except for the provision of the particular special education service

contracted herein and any related ancillary duties such as report cards, progress reports, parent-teacher
conferences, etc.

9. Transportation to and from school is the Sending School District’s responsibility.

10. It is understood and agreed that this Agreement is for the 2024-25 school year only. Tuition will adjust
from one year to the next.

. Nothing herein shall be construed from preventing either party from taking such action as it may deem
necessary, reasonable, or appropriate in the case of a student whose continued presence in the educational

program would constitute a danger to others, to himself/herself, or to property, sufficiently serious to
warrant immediate protective action.

12. This Agreement may be terminated by the Sending School District on 15 days written notice should its

Committee on Special Education determine that the designated program is no longer appropriate for the
child,

13. This Agreement may not be altered, varied or contradicted except by a similar writing executed by both of

the parties to this Agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Board of Education of the Sending School District and the Board of Education

of the Pleasantville UFSD have authorized this Agreement, as signified by the signature of the respective
officials.

Sending School District, Irvington Union Free School District
