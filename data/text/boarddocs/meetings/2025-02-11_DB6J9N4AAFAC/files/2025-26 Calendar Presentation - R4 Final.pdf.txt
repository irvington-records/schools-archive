New York State Testing Schedule

       Grades 3-8 ELA, Math, Grade 5-8 Science
          o Testing Window - April 6th, 2026 to May 15th, 2026

          o Scoring Dates - Completed by May 22nd, 2026



       Final dates for the June 2026 Regents Exams
          o June 9th, 2026* - first administration of the new Regents Exams in ELA and Algebra II

          o June 10th, 2026* - first administration of the new Regents Exams in Physical Science: Chemistry

          o June 17th, 2026 through June 26th, 2026 - including 8th grade Regents




  Note:       No state exams will be given on June 19th, 2026 in observance of Juneteenth
              June 26th, 2026 is Rating Day - no state exams will be given on this date

  * Administration of these exams takes place early in the month to allow time for score collection and standard setting in order to be able to
    post the conversion chart by June 26th, 2026 (Rating Day)

2025-26 Calendar Details
     Labor Day: September 1st

     First Student Day: September 2nd

     SED Established Rating Day: June 26th
      o    Mandated final day of the year

     December Break: December 22nd - January 2nd

     Winter Break: February 16th – 20th
      o   Inclusive of Presidents Day and Lunar New Year

     Spring Break: March 30th – April 3rd

     Memorial Day Weekend: May 25th

     Juneteenth: June 19th

     Last Student Day: June 25th – End of Year Half Days reduced to only the 24th & 25th

     Professional Development:
      o    Conference days: August 27th & 28th, November 4th (Election Day), March 20th
      o    Professional Learning Release Days (PLRDs)

     Holiday closures carried over into the 2025-26 calendar
      o    Diwali - October 20th
      o    Eid al-Fitr - March 20th School will be closed for students. Superintendent’s Conference Day for staff.
