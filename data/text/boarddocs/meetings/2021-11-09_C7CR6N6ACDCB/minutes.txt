5. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Dr. Harrison said a very well attended Homecoming parade and game finally took place over the past Friday night. There were lots of community members in the new stands. He thanked John Buonomano, Deb Mc Cumber, Lauren Cutignola and Michael Berkowitz for their help and support for this very successful event. The Bulldogs won the game. He also provided an update that the District is working with the Westchester County DOH to hold a vaccination clinic for children 5 to 11. He will share the information as soon as it is available.

Dr. Harrison said the Dows Gym is now fully operational and students are now having their Physical Education classes in the renovated gym. He reminded everyone to stay COVID safe during the upcoming Thanksgiving holiday. Veterans Day will be celebrated this week. Dr. Harrison and  David Graeber thanked the Veterans for their service.

Information: 5.1 District Updates, News, Events

Information: 5.2 Committee Reports

Buildings & Grounds

Carol Stein and Brian Friedman were absent so Dr. Harrison gave the Buildings and Grounds Committee report. He said that the District is getting ready to go out to bid on Phase 2 projects. He said there are major staff shortages in the Facilities division of SED. While the District is still awaiting permits we have enough documentation to go out to bid. The District will also go out to bond soon and get low interest rates.

Communications Committee

Maura Gedid and Beth Propper gave an update from the Communications Committee. They are working on the website and are going to reach out to Diana Cassidy (Technology Club) to get some high school students involved in the website.

Curriculum Committee

Jayne Wissner reported that the committee got an SEL update from Dr. Duffy. They identified activities and supports for adult learners.

Policy Committee

Jayne Wissner said the committee is continuing to review the Technology policies. Areas of need are structure and redundancy. Next up is review of the cell phone policy.

Safety Committee

Jayne Wissner said there was an important discussion about community use of the school grounds during the school day. Many classes and activities are taking place outside. The Safety Committee is asking the Policy Committee to come up with a firm policy regarding community members being on school property during the school day. Dr. Harrison said he communicated these expectations in his weekly newsletter and asked the village to send out a similar communication. New York State School Boards does not have a model policy. Dr. Harrison's recommendation is that the committee drafts its own policy and have it vetted by District legal counsel.

Community Advisory Board (CAB)

There were two meetings with students. At the first meeting they talked about using time management to help prevent anxiety. The students enjoy extracurricular activities and count that as self-care. At the second meeting they talked about giving thanks. Students said they were thankful for being back in school in person, able to see their grandparents and celebrating achievements especially the athletics teams. She said the O'Hara Center has a program to remove invasive species.

Village of Irvington

David Graeber thanked the Trustees that are leaving and welcomed the newly elected ones. He said the Board of Education looks forward to working with all the Village Trustees.

Negotiations Committee

Dr. Harrison said there was a very productive organizational meeting with the IFA negotiations team. Looking forward to a collaborative process that will result in a fair contract with both parties.
