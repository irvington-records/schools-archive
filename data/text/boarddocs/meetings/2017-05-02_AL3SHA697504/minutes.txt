2. Public Hearing for 2017-18 Budget

Dr. Harrison reported that the proposed 2017-18 school budget is $59,100,494, meets the tax cap, and carries a tax rate of $18.92 per $1,000 of assessed value. Residents will need to calculate their taxes due to the reassessment. There is a tax calculator available on the District's website and can compare their taxes He noted that the budget supports all district programs and maintains grounds and facilities of all buildings. There are staffing increases which supports educational programs, including but not limited to the restoration of an elementary ELA teacher, additional MS/HS Library Media Specialist, .6 increase of .4 HS Math, .4 increase HS Social Studies, expansion of STEM to 5th grade, a roving security guard at the campus. Registration and absentee ballot information was provided. One Board seat is open, with Mr. Whitney not seeking reelection. There is an additional proposition to increase the board from five to seven members. No date has yet been set for the special election should the voters approve. Any questions can be emailed to budget@irvingtonschools.org

6. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Dr. Harrison. Dr. Kor and Ms. Stein provided the following updates:

- Logo contest has been launched as per April 18th BOE discussion. Details can be found on the district website and submissions must be sent to Elaine Cardea and received by 5:00pm;

- Congratulations to students who participated in the HS Band concert last week. It was a wonderful performance. Congratulations also to the art students for a very impressive art show;

- Complications are brewing for next year's calendar. SED recently issued a series of three different memos to clarify issues around the number of school hours in a school week, i.e. 27.5 for elementary and 25 for secondary. They are being challenged by groups around the state but could be a potential problem for Irvington with respect to early release days at the end of the year as well as the kindergarten program that has a modified schedule for a week at the beginning of the year, which helps with the transition, but state is applying the same standard to the grade and would not be counted. Since kindergarten is not a mandated program, not sure how it will be enforced. NYSUIT and other professional organizations are lobbying SED. If SED does not amend these clarifications, there will need to be modifications to next year's calendar. The Board and community will be kept apprised of situation. Mr. Hanna suggested reaching to our Regents.

- Update on East Field - construction is in full swing and looking at window of potentially July of 2018 to be completed but if weather cooperates it could be sodded before summer vacation. There are weekly meetings to keep everyone on track.

- Dr. Kor reported on last Friday’s early release where teachers continued to develop their units of study as well as began to develop the next generation of Science standards. District is working with a consultant who is very knowledgeable. The curriculum will need to be redesigned at the middle school level due to the changes in the standards. At the high school, it will be the approach to learning as opposed to curriculum redesign. Rollout will take place over three years and are very excited to begin the work. (Mr. Graeber would be interested in seeing what the professional development plan is around the Strategic Plan to see what the work would look like over the year.) Dr. Kor clarified that the work will be more curriculum development and the professional development will follow.

- Carol Stein is working through a plan to begin work on a transportation committee. Parents will be on the committee, as well as an administrator and transportation personnel. Will be reaching out within the week to get started.

- Currently, district is In the fourth year of five year contract with food services and looking to make some changes, such as providing all white meat chicken. Looking at how well items are selling and looking at the changing standards of the national lunch program but not sure how New York will alter their mandates. There is an opportunity to share more information vis a vis the website, etc.
