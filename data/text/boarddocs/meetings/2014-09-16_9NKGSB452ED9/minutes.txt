	7. Presentations/Discussions

	Discussion: 7.1 2014-15 District Goals

	The Board had a lengthy review and discussion of the current version of the draft District goals, which are less detailed and more aspirational (broader) then in previous versions, following the discussion at the September 9th meeting. The members also agreed that it is necessary to articulate the purpose of district goals, i.e. should be reflection of what is best for the district, how they might parallel a long range plan, reflection of a bigger mission of what we want to be as district. Aspirational goals could cross over several years, while smaller action/deliverable sub-goals could be included and achievable annually but also be fluid as needs/areas are prioritized.  Also discussed was how the District goals, superintendent goals and individual building goals dovetail together and that having a long-range plan might also help to guide district goals. It was also noted that goals should not encompass day to day work being done, such as stating curriculum mapping or review of a Tri-State Report.

	Dr. Harrison also agreed with the concept of having broader district goals with administration defining action items to meet those broader goals. He also cautioned that the language needs to be targeted and not judgmental, i.e. avoiding words such as effective, which then requires measuring and defining such words.

	Mrs. Kerner and Mr. Montgomery will work together to hone the language of the goals to represent the discussion and the Board will continue to work towards finalization at the October 14th meeting.

	Discussion: 7.2 Discussion of Board Meeting Calendar/Presentations

	Dr. Harrison reviewed the board meeting calendar and schedule of presentations for the balance of the school year.  With regard to the building –based curricular presentations that are scheduled to begin on April 21st, Mrs. Kerner had requested that these presentations be moved up to February or March, in order to coincide with budget development so as to better understand what might be needed for budgetary purposes.

	Members also requested that along with the above, an overview be provided of the status of initiatives that were funded through this year’s budget, including implementation of new programs and if expectations are being met and if any items should be eliminated.

	Both Dr. Harrison and Dr. Kor noted that having these types of presentations earlier in the year, substantial information to be presented on a particular initiative or program but is willing to look at what might be presented in order to include in any budget discussions.

	There was also a request to have an update on the Guidance  Department and perhaps provide a recap of the 2014 college application/placement process, possibly including where students applied and college acceptance, etc. Dr. Harrison asked the Board to  further clarify what  information they would like to be provided. Mr. Montgomery, for himself, would like to assess the achievement in the college placement process but not sure how it would connect with a presentation.

	Dr. Kor will look into how best to gather the information and if Naviance would be able to provide any statistical information.
