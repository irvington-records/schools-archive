Category: 4. Comments from the Community
Subject: 4.1 Procedures for Comments from Community
Type: Information

Members of the community will be invited to comment or ask questions of the Board on topics that appear on the agenda. Community members are reminded to direct questions to the President of the Board and to limit questions or comments to approximately three minutes.
