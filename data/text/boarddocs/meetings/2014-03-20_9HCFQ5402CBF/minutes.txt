	3. Business of the Board

	Discussion: 3.1 Continued Discussion of Fields and Facilities (Presentation)

	An overview and cost implications of projects were presented along with the recommendations made by the Field Project Committee (FPC). The current proposed plan for East Field will meet DEC/DOH requirements and will be maintained to meet continued compliance, and natural turf as recommended. Artificial turf was the committee’s recommendation for Meszaros using organic or hybrid organic infill. Oley Track was recommended to use the existing footprint and resurface the track. Other items related to athletics were also recommended such as lacrosse netting, storage shed, bleachers, lighting at Meszaros, etc. The total cost of the recommended renovations total $3,008,853 and additional recommendations total $405,000.

	Dr. Harrison reviewed the Buildings & Grounds committee’s recommended items that were categorized as: safety, energy conservation, preemptive and quality of life and broken into two tiers, but not in any particular rank order. Tier 1 recommendations total $1,974,700. Tier two recommendations total $587,700 or $2,562,400 total. Dr. Harrison noted that the impact of a bond would be offset by expiring debt at the end of the 2014-15 school year and provided the cost of various levels of bond amounts.

	Mr. Grados noted that the conversation will continue at the April 1st Board of Education meeting which will also include the 2014-15 proposed budget. A meeting has been scheduled for April 9th in order to be sure that everyone's questions and comments are addressed. Mr. Grados also noted that community members have communicated to the Board questions and the administration will post all the questions and responses.

	Action: 3.2 Adoption of East Field Remedial Option

	WHEREAS, on or about August 27, 2012, LAN Engineering, Planning, Architecture, Surveying, LLP (LAN), the environmental and planning consultants for the Irvington Union Free School District (the “District”), discovered non-native construction and demolition debris fill material to be present beneath “East Field” on the High School and Middle School Campus, located at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, NY; and

	WHEREAS, on or about that date the District directed closure of East Field until LAN completed an investigation of that fill material and District representatives met with the Department of Environmental Conservation (DEC) and Department of Health (DOH) of the State of New York to determine appropriate remedial measures to be undertaken at East Field so as to allow it to be restored to use as an athletic field; and

	WHEREAS, during 2012 and 2013 LAN Associates undertook several investigations of East Field and met several times with DEC and DOH in furtherance of the above goals so as to ensure that any remedy implemented by the District would be protective of the health, safety and welfare of the District’s students, staff, residents and other users of East Field and of the environment; and

	WHEREAS, at a meeting with DEC and DOH on February 21, 2014, a conceptual remedial option was identified by LAN Associates, DEC and DOH that meets all of the above goals (the “Remedial Option”) and that, in summary, would require the District to:

	1.         Cover East Field with approximately 1-foot of clean fill to enhance the protective cap to 2-feet, and install a natural grass playing surface;

	2.         Install appropriate drainage to ensure the long term stability of East Field; and

	3.         Apply for a DEC approval of a “Landfill Disturbance Plan”, a copy of which is attached hereto (“Plan Approval”); and

	WHEREAS, on or about March 13, 2014, the Irvington Field’s Project Committee made a recommendation to the Board of Education to adopt the Remedial Option;

	            THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education (“Board”) hereby adopts the Remedial Option, in concept, and subject to further modification as this Board deems appropriate once a more comprehensive Remedial Option plan is presented; and

	BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board of Education hereby authorizes LAN Associates to undertake work necessary to complete the attached Plan Approval from DEC; and

	BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that pursuant to the State Environmental Quality Review Act statute and regulations (“SEQRA”), the Board of Education has determined that the Project is a Type II action pursuant to Section 617.5 of Title 6 of the New York Codes, Rules and Regulations (“NYCRR”) and not subject to SEQRA.
