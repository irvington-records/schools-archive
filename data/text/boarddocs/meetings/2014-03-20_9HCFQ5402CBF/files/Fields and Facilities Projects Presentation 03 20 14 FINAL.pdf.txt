Fields Recap
The Field Project Committee (FPC) recommends:

 Replace the East Field playing surface with natural turf; install
  drainage; and install irrigation
   The use of 1 ft clean fill to be combined with 1 ft of existing
     material to create a 2 ft protective cap is expected to satisfy
     DEC/DOH requirements
 Replace the Meszaros Field playing surface with artificial turf
  using either organic or organic hybrid infill
 Replace Oley Track with a new running surface

Facilities Recap
The Buildings and Grounds Committee evaluated needs
across the district to formulate its recommendations, which
include:
   8 Energy Saving Recommendations

   2 Pre-emptive Recommendations

   4 Quality of Life Recommendations

   2 Safety Recommendations



      The BGC did not attempt to prioritize the projects in rank
      order. Rather, the BGC separated the projects into Tier 1
      “Recommended” and Tier 2 “Not Recommended at This
      Time”
