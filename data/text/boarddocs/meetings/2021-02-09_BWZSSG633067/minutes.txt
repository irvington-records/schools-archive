Action (Consent): 8.2 Authorization of Student Participation in High Risk Athletics

Resolution: WHEREAS, on or about February 2, 2021, Westchester County Health Commissioner, Dr. Sherlita Amler, authorized the resumption of high risk sports in Westchester County, subject to compliance with various health and administrative guidelines; and WHEREAS, Commissioner Amler has required each school district to provide written authorization to the Westchester County Department of Health (“WCDOH”) as to the District’s intended involvement in high risk sports; NOW THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District herewith authorizes the participation of its students in high risk sports, effective February 1, 2021, in accordance with the guidelines set forth by the NYSPHSAA and the WCDOH. BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District establish a COVID surveillance testing program in which all high-risk student athletes shall participate. BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District herewith authorizes the resumption of the following specific high risk sports and required student grade level. Winter Sports -Varsity & JV Boys Basketball -Varsity & JV Girls Basketball -Varsity Ice Hockey (Merged Program) Fall II Sports -Varsity Football -Varsity & JV Volleyball Spring Sports -Varsity & JV Boys Lacrosse Grades of eligible student-athletes Grades 9-12 and those in middle school who qualify through the Athletic Placement Process (APP) and in compliance with the Irvington Athletics Department guidelines.

At the request of the Board, the resolution was amended to include the following:

"BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District establish a COVID surveillance testing program in which all high-risk student athletes shall participate."

It was noted at the meeting the vote on 8.2 should not have been part of the Consent Agenda but require a separate vote.
