Category: 4. Public Hearing-2020-21 District-Wide Safety Plan
Subject: 4.1 Public Hearing for the updated 2020-21 District-Wide Safety Plan
Type: Procedural

The District Wide Safety Plan which was adopted in September of 2020 has been amended to incorporate new laws (Section 27-c of the New York Labor Law and Section 2801-a(2)(m) of Education Law).  Essentially the changes request that the plan provide more detailed information on protocols for responding to a declared public health emergency involving a communicable disease.

Section 2801-a(6) requires that the district-wide safety plan and any amendments be available for public comment at least thirty (30) days prior to its adoption, and may be adopted by the school board (or governing body) only after at least one (1) public hearing that provides for the participation of school personnel, parents, students and any other interested parties.

The amended District-Wide Safety plan was posted on the Irvington UFSD website for public comment on January 6, 2021 in advance of the Public Hearing and adoption by the Board of Education at this meeting.
