   1) Describe your project in detail. Please be specific, including how students will engage in the
      activity and how you will measure whether or not this project has been successful.

   Starlab is a portable planetarium that can be brought into school and set up in a relatively short
   time . It is an inflatable dome capable of accommodating up to 35 students. It provides a wide
   variety of night skies . The program would be tailored to specific curriculum areas in grades K-2
   as describe below.

    NY State Elementary Science Standard 4: The Physical Setting
    Students will understand and apply scientific concepts, principles, and theories pertaining to the
    physical setting and living environment and recognize the historical development of ideas in
    science.
    Key Idea 1:
    The Earth and celestial phenomena can be described by principles of relative motion and
    perspective.
    Major Understandings:
    1.1a Natural cycles and patterns include:
    • Earth spinning around once every 24 hours (rotation), resulting in day and
    night
    • Earth moving in a path around the Sun (revolution) , resulting in one Earth year
    • the length of daylight and darkness varying with the seasons
    • weather changing from day to day and through the seasons
    • the appearance of the Moon changing as it moves in a path around Earth to
    complete a single cycle
    1 1b Humans organize time into units based on natural motions of Earth
    • second , minute, hour
    • week, month
     1.1 c The Sun and other stars appear to move in a recognizable pattern both daily and
    seasonally .

2) What specific classroom/school needs does your project addresses? How does the
   proposed project build on existing programs? Please include how this project incorporates
   the curriculum and is linked to State and/or national standards.

   We would use the Starlab introductory unit, Patterns in the Sky-Simple Constellations, for
   our kindergarten students. This provides an overview of the stars visible in this area. First
   and second grade have reading activities that center on myths and legends which can be
   connected to stories told in the Starlab. The theme for first grade would be Greek Mythology
   and for 2nd grade it is Native American Mythology. These will help the youngsters grasp how
   these people derived their stories, myths and legends and utilized them to better understand
   the world around them. Each program would be presented in a single day.


3) How will you publicize your project?

    We can publicize it through the email "This Week in the Irvington Schools" and perhaps
    when photos are taken, they could be a part of the quarterly mailing from the
    superintendent.

4) How will your project benefit students?

    The students will gain a dramatic visual understanding of our solar system and universe
    along with activities directly related to the classroom curriculum.
