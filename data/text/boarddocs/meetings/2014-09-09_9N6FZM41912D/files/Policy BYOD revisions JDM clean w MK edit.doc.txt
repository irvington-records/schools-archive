STUDENTS AND PERSONAL ELECTRONIC DEVICES
BRING YOUR OWN DEVICE (BYOD)


The Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District recognizes that certain personal electronic devices (“PEDs”) have educational applications.  Such PEDs may include but are not limited to smart phones, tablets, iPads, e-readers, calculators, voice recorders, digital cameras, and music listening devices. PEDs that permit educational functions shall be allowed to be used in classrooms only when all three of the following criteria are met:

The principal has authorized use of PEDs with specified educational functions.in any school building.
The teacher has authorized use of PEDs with specified functions and characteristics in his/her classroom generally or for a particular exercise.
The student uses the PED only to connect wirelessly to the District’s network or to use authorized applications on the PED, under the terms of District’s policy on Computer Use in Instruction.

Students shall not use PEDs to access the Internet, except permitted access through the District’s network.

This policy applies only to students in Irvington Middle School and Irvington High School.  The Board does not believe that use of PEDs should be permitted in earlier grades.

	Use of PEDs should not be allowed to create inequities in educational opportunity within Irvington schools.  If students do not own suitable PEDs for permitted educational activity, as defined above, the District shall make available District-owned devices for those students for the educational activities requiring the PEDs.  

 	The Board acknowledges that the display and/or use of such PEDs can cause disruption to the educational process.  Therefore, “disruptive use” (as hereinafter defined) of PEDs is prohibited during school hours as follows unless otherwise authorized.  For purposes hereof, “disruptive use” may include, but is not limited to:

Any use in a classroom, laboratory, library, study hall, gymnasium, and/or other designated educational area, except under the three criteria for permitted PED use above. 
Any use in hallways, corridors, stairways, elevators, bathrooms, and/or other building common areas.
Permitted use that interferes with or otherwise disrupts or interferes with the proper functioning of the telecommunications, computer network, and/or other electronic operating systems of the Irvington Union Free School District.
Any use during any local test or State assessment.  During any local test or State assessment, all PEDs, unless otherwise permitted during a local test, shall be powered off and assigned proctors, monitors, and school officials shall have the right to collect such PEDs prior to the administration of, and for the duration of, the test or assessment.

Therefore, and to prevent such disruption to the educational process, the display and/or use of PEDs shall be governed by the Code of Conduct and/or student handbooks issued by each school.   

The Irvington Union Free School District is neither responsible nor liable for any stolen, lost and/or damaged PED.

	Misuse of any PEDs in violation of this Policy or the Code of Conduct will result in its confiscation.  Moreover, some uses of PEDs may constitute, in some instances, a violation of local, State, and/or federal law.  To the extent any use of PEDs constitutes a violation of local, State, and/or federal law, the District shall cooperate with law enforcement officials as appropriate and necessary.
