                           IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT
                                   2014-15 District Goals


Instructional Goal:
Maintain high expectations and accountability for quality teaching in every classroom and for every
student.

Curriculum Goal:
    Develop and maintain curricula to promote academic excellence. Continue to –revise curricula
       utilizing Understanding by Design and document all K-12 curricula in Rubicon Atlas, while
       identifying opportunities for improvement utilizing internal and external processes such as those
       identified in the Tri States Consortium World Language Program Review Report;
      Identify opportunities to expand and enrich learning experiences;
      Assess the use of technology in instruction and develop coherent K-12 technology curriculum
       action plan and begin implementation where possible (Needs revision to reflect current work
       and technology framework);
      Continue to build a rich college preparedness program to equip graduates for the college
       admission process and a successful transition to the post-secondary environment (Name
       specifics).

Infrastructure Goal:
Outline process to revise and update the IUFSD policy manual.


Long-Range Planning Goal:
Devise long-range plans to guide future work across the District.
    Define the indicators of a successful school experience;
      Review and refine the mission and vision of the District;
      Create meaningful instructional and strategic objectives to inform the efforts of the Board of
       Education, Administration, Faculty and Staff;
      Evaluate the District’s financial health and devise a framework to inform future financial
       decisions;
      Identify opportunities for enhancing the District’s operations and infrastructure.
