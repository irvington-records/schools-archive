 IUFSD’s Commitment to SEL Programming:
 Process, Planning & Budget
The District is committed to the development and alignment of its SEL
programming. This will include the following:
1. Process: On February 28th, a professional team from CASEL will visit IUFSD and
   review current SEL programming; assess alignment of current programming; and
   recommend opportunities for enhancement.

2. Planning: The District will review and consider CASEL’s recommendations and
   develop a multi-year plan of action to ensure the implementation of a
   comprehensive SEL experience for all students. An SEL committee will be developed
   for this purpose.

3. Budget: For the 2020-21 ﬁscal year, the District will allocate $25,000 to support
   professional learning, purchase of SEL/instructional materials and support SEL
   student programs

         What’s Causing the Rise in Teenagers
         with Severe Anxiety?

High expectations and pressure to succeed

Between standardized testing and a culture of achievement, today's
youth can feel pressure to succeed in ways previous generations did not.

A survey done every year by Higher Education Research asks incoming
college freshmen if they feel overwhelmed by all they have to do. In 2016,
41% of students said "yes" compared with 28% in 2000 and 18% in 1985.

                   Source: American Academy of Pediatrics (Copyright © 2019)

         What’s Causing the Rise in Teenagers
         with Severe Anxiety?

A world that feels scary and threatening

We've seen an increase in school shootings, with resultant drills and
lockdowns in schools. We've seen shootings in public places. There have
been terrorist attacks here in the US and around the world taking many
lives. From just watching or reading the news, it is reasonable for anyone
to feel afraid in public spaces that previously would have felt safe.


                   Source: American Academy of Pediatrics (Copyright © 2019)

         What’s Causing the Rise in Teenagers
         with Severe Anxiety?

Social media

Today's children and teens are constantly connected to social media. It's
not surprising that their self-esteem―and worldview ―becomes
connected to responses to social media posts. It's hard for them not to
compare their life and social connections to what they see others
posting on social media.


                   Source: American Academy of Pediatrics (Copyright © 2019)

The Role of a School Psychologist

● Conduct cognitive assessments in preparation for CSE meetings
● Provide group and individual counseling (RTI or CSE mandated)
● Conduct student observations
● Engage in consultations with teachers, support staff and families
● Complete behavioral assessments
● Provide case management
● Perform crisis intervention and management
● Conduct risk assessments

                                                                      10

The Role of an Elementary School Counselor

● Plan and deliver classroom instruction supporting Social
  Emotional Learning

● Provide individual and small-group counseling/coaching

● Provide crisis counseling

● Engage in consultations with teachers, support staff and families




                                                                      11

The Role of a Secondary School Counselor
● Plan and deliver classroom instruction supporting Social
  Emotional Learning

● Provide individual and small-group counseling/coaching

● Collaborate with students in the development of schedules

● Provide college counseling

● Provide transition planning

● Engage in consultations with teachers, support staff and
  families



                                                              12

The Role of a Student Assistance Counselor

● Promote awareness of various drugs, trends and other harmful
  issues (posters, verbal and written information)

● Provide on-going education and training of students and faculty
  through workshops, speakers and classroom presentations

● Participate with community organizations to facilitate educational
  and preventive initiatives

● Provide individual and group counseling with students presenting
  with at-risk behavior




                                                                       13

The Role of a School Social Worker
● Advocate for families and students
● Consult with all school personnel to support a positive school climate
● Conduct risk assessments
● Assist parents in accessing and utilizing school and community resources
● Support student attendance and engagement through home visits,
  collaboration with school personnel, and direct student support

● Provide crisis intervention and management
● Provide direct support to staff in meeting student needs
● Mobilize family, school and community resources to support individual
  students and families                                                      14
