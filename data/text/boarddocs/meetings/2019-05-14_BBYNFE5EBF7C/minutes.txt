4. Presentation/Discussion

Information:  4.1 2018-19 Elementary End of Year Update

Dr. Kor began the Elementary End of Year Update. Deb Mariniello and Dr. Andrea Kantor then presented the Dows Lane summary. There were some questions from the Board of Education, directed towards data in the presentation. Deborah Hargraves would like to merge the data from the May 7th presentation, into tonight's presentation, so that the Board has more comparative data. Dr. Kor provided additional insight into the data collected. Additional questions/comments from the Board of Education Trustees took place.

Joyce Chapnick presented the Main Street School summary. Conversation and questions between Board members and Joyce ensued.
