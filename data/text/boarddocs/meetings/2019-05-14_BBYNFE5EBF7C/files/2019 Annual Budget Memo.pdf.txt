TO:        BOARD PRESIDENTS, SUPERINTENDENTS AND DISTRICT CLERKS
FROM:      Lisa Davis, Westchester Putnam School Boards Association Executive Director
RE:        NOTICE: WPSBA ANNUAL MEETING, PROPOSED BUDGET and NOMINATING SLATE VOTE
DATE:      April 30, 2019

We are pleased to present information on the Westchester Putnam School Boards Association
proposed budget and the Board of Directors nominating slate for 2019-20.

The WPSBA Board of Directors approved the proposed budget at its April 25th meeting. The WPSBA
Treasurer will present a financial report and budget overview at the WPSBA Annual Meeting and
Dinner Program on May 30, 2019 at Crabtree Kittle House in Chappaqua, NY (invitation attached).

After over a decade of flat dues, the WPSBA dues categories will increase by 1.5% next year. We base
our dues chart on the current-year tax levy, and thus take into consideration both district size and
district wealth (higher need districts receive a smaller percentage of their revenue from the tax levy).
WPSBA membership letters and dues invoices will be sent individually to each district in June.

WPSBA works throughout the year to provide timely and relevant services, programming, and data
resources geared to the unique needs of our districts. In partnership with our school districts and the
Lower Hudson Education Coalition, we advocate for public education and the children we serve.

Please have your board of education vote on the WPSBA proposed budget and Nominating
Committee Slate at your next board meeting. Your district’s ballot (which has been sent to your
district clerk) can be cast in person at the Annual Meeting, or the signed ballot can be submitted via
email prior to the May 30th meeting.

We thank you for your ongoing support, and encourage you to complete our annual June e-survey.
Your feedback informs our programming, regional data analysis, communications, and advocacy.




Executive Director, WPSBA

WPSBA BUDGET- KEY LINE ITEMS:
 Income Categories:
     Membership Dues: Categories are based on school district’s current year tax levy. BOCES dues are based on budget. Special Act Public
      School districts have a flat rate.
     Program Fees: Revenue from programs for which WPSBA incurs costs for meal catering.
     Facts & Figure$ Sponsor Advertisements: Revenue includes sponsor advertisements in Facts & Figure$ and book sales, which offset the
       printing and mailing cost.
     LHEC/EPFP Administration: WPSBA receives a fee to serve as the administrative staff for the Lower Hudson Education Coalition (LHEC)
       and a fee plus expense reimbursement for Lisa Davis to co-coordinate the NY Education Policy Fellowship Program (EPFP).
     Savings & Other Revenue: Interest and other income.

 Expenditure Categories:
     Employee Compensation: Two FT employees: Executive Director & Program Coordinator; two PT (.2): Admin Associate & Bookkeeper.
     Board of Directors and Committees: Costs related to WPSBA meetings, workshops, and no-fee programs, including: Board of Directors
       committee meetings; Prospective & New Board Member workshops; Current Issues; Legislative Advocacy Committee; Special Act
       School District Taskforce; regional meetings; etc.
     Overhead and Professional Fees: Rent, office equipment and supplies, postage, telephone, technology, website, insurance, payroll,
       publications, in addition to bookkeeping, legal fees, interns, and outside consultants.
     WPSBA Programs & Events: Direct costs for all programs and workshops, and for events for which there are meal catering costs. Soft
      costs such as personnel, overhead and related expenses are not directly allocated to most program or project expense categories.
     Conferences & Travel Expenses: Executive Director and President’s expenses for annual conferences, workshops, and meetings (NYSSBA,
       NSBA, EPFP, regional organizations); memberships, subscriptions; staff training, meeting, and workshop expenses.
