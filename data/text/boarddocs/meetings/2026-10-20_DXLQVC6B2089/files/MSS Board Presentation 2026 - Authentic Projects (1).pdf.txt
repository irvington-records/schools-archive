Student successes included…
●   Students worked collaboratively to plan and create their
    math games while practicing teamwork and communication
    skills.
●   Students creatively incorporated math concepts, skills, and
    vocabulary into fun and engaging games and engaged in
    problem posing.
●   Students gave and received feedback from teachers and
    peers to revise and improve their work.
●   Students demonstrated pride and ownership in their ﬁnal
    game presentations.

          Teacher Reﬂections on the Process
●   Game creation was highly engaging for students.
●   Providing clear structures, models, and scaffolds helped students stay
    organized and allowed the project to run smoothly.
●   A challenge was balancing student independence with the level of support
    some groups needed for collaboration, time management, and
    problem-solving.
●   Supporting productive group work pushed us to step back at times and
    allow students to work through challenges on their own before intervening.
●   Overall, the experience was rewarding because students showed creativity,
    perseverance, and pride in their ﬁnal products while strengthening both
    math and collaboration skills.
