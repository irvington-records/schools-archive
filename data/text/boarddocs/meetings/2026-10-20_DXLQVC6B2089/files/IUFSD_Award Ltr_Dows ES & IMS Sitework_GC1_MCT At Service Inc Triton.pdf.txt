Ms. Stein,

On October 13, 2026, bids were received and opened publicly for the above-mentioned project. Upon receipt of the bid results, Triton
Construction reviewed each of the contractor’s qualifications. Five (5) bids in total were received for this project.

A bid review meeting was held October 14, 2026, with the low bidder, MCT At Service Inc., BBS Architects, Landscape Architects, and
Engineers, P.C., a representative from the District and Triton Construction. Our project scope review with the contractor indicates an
understanding of the project requirements and expectations. Based on our review of the contractor’s bid, qualifications, and successful
scope review, we see no reason why a contract should not be awarded to MCT At Service Inc., as noted below.

          Capital Sitework Improvements at Irvington Middle School & Dows Lande Elementary School

          MCT At Service Inc.

Based on the information above, pending any final review required by District Counsel, the apparent responsible low bidder is
MCT At Service Inc., from New York, NY. The total contract being recommended is EIGHT HUNDRED THIRTY ONE THOUSAND SEVEN
HUNDRED DOLLARS AND ZERO CENTS ($831,700.00).
