Dear School Board Members:

I would like to start a scholarship in honor of my mother.

The Leona B. Platzman Memorial Scholarship

Criteria:
1. Interest in pursuing health sciences
2. Maintaining GPA of at least 3.3
3. Has volunteered his/her time in health field
4. Compassionate hard-working senior
5. Short 100-200-word paragraph as to why you deserve this scholarship.

Amount $1,500 pledge


My mother was born in Brooklyn to Polish immigrants that escaped during the
Holocaust. Went on to college and settled in Tarrytown as a homemaker raising 4
children that graduated from the Irvington UFSD. She was a strong believer in
education. She believed that there were no boundaries in life, only the ones that you
yourself create. My mother developed stage 4 breast cancer yet survived over 20 years
to welcome 12 grandchildren. She then developed other health issues, Parkinson's,
dementia and cardiac issues.

My family would like to recognize an IHS senior interested in a health sciences career.
We have a particular interest in contributing to the growth of talented students in
the community in which we reside.

It would be an honor to award a student a scholarship in my mother's memory.

Sincerely,

Michael Platzman
