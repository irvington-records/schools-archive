As per the Irvington Faculty Association Contract
Article IV (D, 4):
Teachers may apply for changes to their salary column only once per school year, either in the fall semester or in the spring semester. All transcripts for fall semester changes must be received by November 1st in order to be eligible for lane movement retroactive to September 1st in accordance with past practice and all transcripts for the spring semester must be received by April 1st in order to be eligible for lane movement retroactive to February 1st in accordance with past practice.
