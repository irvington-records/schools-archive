SIDE LETTER AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE
BOARD OF EDUCATION OF THE IRVINGTON UNION FREE
SCHOOL DISTRICT ("DISTRICT" or "BOARD") AND THE CIVIL
SERVICE EMPLOYEES ASSOCIATION, INC., LOCAL 1000,
AFSCME, AFL-CIO, IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL
DISTRICT CUSTODIAL UNIT ("ASSOCIATION") CONCERNING
COMPENSATORY TIME
WHEREAS, the DISTRICT and the ASSOCIATION are parties to a Collective Bargaining Agreement, dated July 1, 2010 through June 30, 2017 ("CBA"); and
WHEREAS, the CBA is silent as to the manner in which emergency school closings will be handled, with the exception of such closings due to snow; and
WHEREAS, on or about October 29 and October 30, 2012 New York State declared a State of Emergency due to Hurricane Sandy; and
WHEREAS, due to said State of Emergency, DISTRICT schools were closed on said dates; and
WHEREAS, on or about October 30, 2012, the DISTRICT determined that it required the services of ASSOCIATION members; and
WHEREAS, certain ASSOCIATION members reported to work on said date but due to the State of Emergency, certain ASSOCIATION members were unable to report to work; and
WHEREAS, the ASSOCIATION has demanded that those ASSOCIATION members who were able to report to work on October 30, 2012, be compensated in some manner for the time worked; and
WHEREAS, in an effort to avoid the time and expense of potential litigation, the parties have engaged in collective bargaining negotiations concerning the resolution of this matter.
NOW,  THEREFORE,   IT   IS   HEREBY   STIPULATED   AND   AGREED   BY  THE PARTIES AS FOLLOWS:
Those ASSOCIATION members who were unable to report to work on October 30, 2012
due to the State of Emergency shall not be penalized in any manner, including but not
limited to the charging of any contractual leave time or docking of pay for said day.
The DISTRICT shall grant each of the following ASSOCIATION members eight (8) hours
of compensatory time in consideration of the hours worked on October 30, 2012:
a.	Angel Aviles
Those ASSOCIATION members entitled to compensator)' time, as specified in paragraph
"2" above, shall be required to utilize said time no later than June 30, 2013.  The failure to
utilize this compensatory time by June 30, 2013 will result in the ASSOCIATION member's
forfeiture of said time.  The use of compensatory time shall require prior approval from the
ASSOCIATION member's immediate supervisor and the Assistant Superintendent for
Business. It is further understood that said time must be utilized in no less than ½ day
increments, i.e. ASSOCIATION members shall be entitled to utilize said time as either one
(1) full day or two (2) ½ days.
In consideration of the above, the ASSOCIATION and its members herewith agree that no
cause of action, including but not limited to grievances, shall be filed in connection with this
Agreement, except for purposes of enforcing the specific terms of this Agreement.   The
ASSOCIATION and its members herewith agree that only a violation of the terms and
conditions of this Agreement by the DISTRICT shall be subject to the grievance and
arbitration  procedures   set  forth  in   the   CBA  between  the  ASSOCIATION   and the DISTRICT.
The parties acknowledge that this Agreement represents the full, final and complete resolution of this matter.
This Agreement shall not serve as a precedent for any of the parties, in any forum. This
Agreement may not be used and/or cited by any of the parties in any future grievance,
arbitration or litigation except for the purposes of enforcing the specific terms of this
Agreement.
This Agreement shall not be incorporated into any successor collective bargaining agreement
between the parties and is entered into solely with respect to the State of Emergency
Declared on October 30, 2012.
This Agreement shall expire and be of no further force or effect at the close of business on
June 30, 2013.
