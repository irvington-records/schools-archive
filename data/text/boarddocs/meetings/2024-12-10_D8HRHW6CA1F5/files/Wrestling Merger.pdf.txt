Dear Dr, Ratesic,

Iam reaching out to bring to your attention the need for your signature on the attached document regarding
athletic program merger for the upcoming 2024-25 winter season.

Iam proposing to merge our modified wrestling program with Hastings to provide a more robust and beneficial
experience for our athletes. Currently, we only have two students registered, and over the past three years,
participation has not exceeded five athletes. Merging with Hastings will allow our wrestlers to have training
partners within their own weight class, which is essential for their skill development, safety, and enjoyment of
the sport.

Additionally, a dedicated coaching staff will be available to support them consistently, as our varsity coaches have
been stretched between varsity and modified responsibilities. This collaboration presents the safest and most
productive path forward for our modified wrestling program.Once these documents are signed, please include
them in the agenda for the next Board of Education meeting for final approval.

Should you require any further clarification or have any questions, please do not hesitate to reach out to me.

Sincerely yours,
