 How are students supported through a 504 Accommodation Plan?

General education students with 504 Accommodation Plans receive supports
  that are designed to ensure access to the general education curriculum.
   Recognition of the impact of identified impairments is the foundation of
development of accommodations that serve as a blueprint to remove barriers
                             to student access.

    How are students supported through the CSE process?

Special education supports are rooted in practices that collaboratively advance
 each student’s unique educational journey through innovative, inclusive, and
   culturally responsive systems that continuously adapt to meet students’
                evolving needs throughout their time in Irvington.
