Subject: 11.1 Contract - Graybar Electric Company, Inc.
Type: Action (Consent)
Attachments: Brigthcore-Graybar Services Agreement for Irvington UFSD Final.pdf

This agreement will allow Brightcore Services, as a supplier for Graybar, to assist H2M with the design of the Conversion to LED Lighting Project, which is part of the Capital Project.  Brightcore Services has specialized expertise on LED lighting projects and participates with Sourcewell Purchasing Cooperative.  This agreement references the future purchase of the lighting material through this purchasing cooperative, but not the installation of the lights, which will be bid out at a later date.
