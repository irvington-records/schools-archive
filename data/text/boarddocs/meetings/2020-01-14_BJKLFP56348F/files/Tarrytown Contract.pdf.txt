TUITION CONTRACT

(PUBLIC RECEIVING SCHOOL WITH PROGRAM FOR DISABLED
STUDENTS)

This Agreement made this 9 day of July, 2019 by and between the Board of Education of the
Irvington Union Free School District (“Sending District”) having its principal place of business
located at 6 Dows Lane Irvington, NY 10533 and Tarrytown Union Free School District,
(“Receiving School”) having its principal place of business for purposes of this Agreement at 200
North Broadway, Sleepy Hollow, New York 10591.

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, pursuant to Education Law §4402(2)(b) the Sending District is authorized to
contract for special services or programs as the Sending District shall deem reasonable and
appropriate for students with disabilities after consideration of the recommendations of the

local committee on special education ("CSE"); and

WHEREAS, the Tarrytown Union Free School District is a public school authorized by New
York State to conduct, operate and maintain an educational program and provide special
education and related services to students with disabilities; and

WHEREAS, the Sending District has found the Receiving School adequate to provide such
special education instruction and/or related services and is desirous of having the Receiving
School provide such instruction and/or related services to certain students with disabilities

residing within the Sending District; and

WHEREAS, the Receiving Schoo) acknowledges that it has ceviewed the Individualized
Education Program(s) ("IEP") of the student(s) to be served pursuant to this Agreement and
warrants that it is capable of providing and will provide the level of instructional and related
services required by each applicable IEP;

NOW, THEREFORE, it is mutually agreed by and between the parties as follows:

{. Term of Agreement: The term of this Agreement shall be from July 1, 2019 to August
16, 2019 unless terminated earlier as set forth herein. It is understood and agreed that
this Agreement is for the 2019-2020 school year only, subject to the stay put provisions of
the Individuals with Disabilities Education Improvement Act. Tuition may be adjusted
from one year to the next.

2, Services:

a. The Receiving School agrees to provide the services as set forth in the IEP of each
student listed in Appendix “A” as such IEP(s) exists on the start date of this
Agreement, with exception of assistive technology and subject to any limitations
noted in the Receiving School’s letter accepting the student, Implementation of
any modification to the student(s)’s IEP after the start date of this Agreement that
adds new programs or services or increases the duration or frequency of existing
programs or related services will be the responsibility of Sending District unless
the Receiving School is willing and able to staff with existing service providers
the new and/or modified programs and/or services.

b, The Receiving School agrees to report to the Sending District on the progress and
attendance of the student on a regular basis; however, the Sending District may
request written reports no more often than three times yearly for grades K-5 and
quarterly at the secondary level during the school year.

¢. The Receiving School agrees to permit a representative or representatives of the
Committee on Special Education of the Sending District to visit the program in
which the student is enrolled if reasonable prior notice of the visit is provided to
Receiving School.

d. The Receiving School agrees to make relevant personnel available to participate by
telephone ia meetings of the Sending District's Committee on Special Education if
reasonable prior notice of such meetings is provided to the Receiving School.

€, Nothing contained herein, however, shall serve to transfer responsibility for the

of the particular special education service contracted herein and any related
ancillary duties such as report cards, progress reports and parent-teacher
conferences.

3. Payments

a, The Sending District will pay the Receiving School the annual tuition fees set
forth in Appendix "A" in ten (10) equal monthly installments.

b. Invoices shall be submitted to the Sending District on a monthly basis by
Receiving School.

c. The Sending District may add or remove, with the prior written approval and
consent of the Receiving School, any student at any time during the term of this
Agreement. Payment for students that are added or removed shall be prorated
for the time the services were provided to the student(s).

4, Termination: This Agreement may be terminated by the Sending District on fifteen (15)
days prior written notice should its Committee on Special Education determine that the
Receiving School's program is no longer appropriate for the student. Upon such
termination, Receiving Schoo! shall be paid for all services provided in accordance with
this Agreement through the date of termination on a pro rata basis.

The Receiving School may not terminate this Agreement or its application with regard to
any individual student(s) except upon fifteen (15) days’ notice to the Sending District.
Such termination may only take place after consultation with the Sending District,
including but not limited to consultation with the Sending District's Administration and
Committee on Special Education ("CSE") In the event of termination, the Receiving
School shall cooperate with the Sending District to wind down any activities hereunder
and all reports or other materials due to the Sending District from the Receiving School
shall be promptly completed. Nothing in this paragraph shall be construed to limit the
Receiving School's rights to remove students from the academic setting in accordance with
law. Nothing herein shall be construed from preventing the Receiving School from taking
action as it may deem necessary, reasonable or appropriate in the case of a student whose

Incidents; The Receiving School shall notify the Sending District promptly of any

Project SAVE: The Receiving School shall be responsible for compliance with the
requirements of Project SAVE and the SAFE SCHOOL ACTS ("Project SAVE"), with
respect to any person providing services by or through the Receiving School under the
terms of this Agreement. All persons providing services by or through the Receiving
School under the terms of this Agreement shall have received appropriate fingerprinting
clearances required by law prior to providing services to the students of Sending District.
In the event a provider of the Receiving School, who provides services to a student of the
Sending District, has not obtained required fingerprinting clearance with the State
Education Department, the Sending District shall have the right to immediately terminate
this Agreement.

Hold Harmless: The Receiving School agrees to defend, indemnify and hold harmless
the Sending District, its Board of Education, agents, officers, trustees, attorneys and
employees from and against all claims, damages, losses and expenses, including but not
limited to attorneys’ fees, arising out of or resulting from the performance of this
Agreement. This provision shall survive termination of this Agreement. This obligation
shall not be construed to negate abridge, or otherwise reduce any other right or obligation
of indemnity which would otherwise exist as to any party or person described in this

paragraph,

The Sending District agrees to defend, indemnify and hold harmless the Receiving
School, its Board of Education, agents, officers, trustees, attorneys and employees, from
and against all claims, damages, losses and expenses, including but not limited to
attorneys’ fees, arising out of or resulting from the performance of this Agreement. This
provision shall survive termination of this Agreement. This obligation shall not be
construed to negate, abridge, or otherwise reduce any other right or obligation of
indemnity which would otherwise exist as to any party or person described in this
paragraph.

Notice of Litigation: In the event litigation is initiated in connection with the provision
of services under this Agreement, the parties shall promptly give written notice of same
to each other.

Licensing: The Receiving School will ensure that its staff possesses any requisite
licenses and qualifications to perform the services contracted for. The Receiving School
shall verify the existence and validity of licenses and credentials and in the event the
required ticense and/or certification of any agent, contractor or employee of the
Receiving School providing services under this Agreement is revoked, terminated,
suspended or otherwise impaired, the Receiving School shall immediately notify the
Sending District. Upon written request, copies of such credentials shall be made available
to the Sending District by the Receiving School.
