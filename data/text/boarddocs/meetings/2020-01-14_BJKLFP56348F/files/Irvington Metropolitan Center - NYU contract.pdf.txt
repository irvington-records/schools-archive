Appendix A: Scope of Work

Metropolitan Center for Research on Equity and the Transformation of Schools-IESC
and Irvington Union Free School District

Irvington Union Free School District in partnership with the New York University's
Metropolitan Center for Research on Equity and the Transformation of Schools-Jnnovations in
Equity and Systemic Change (IESC) will engage in a collaborative training with the district from
February 2020 to April 2020.

This partnership project will be comprised of the following strand of work:
1. Provide an overview of Culturally Responsive Sustaining education (CRS-E) at Irvington
Middle and High School

Scope of Work:

The proposed scope of work will focus on: (1) developing common language around CR-S
education, including race, ethnicity, culture, and language and its’ impact on student outcomes,
and (2) examining the role that social identities, power, and privilege play in shifting individual
and systemic inequalities in schools. Metro Center’s-IESC will conduct a total of 2 separate, 1
hour day trainings with Irvington Middle and High School. Each training will build an
awareness of how race, identity, power, and privilege create education barriers that have
historically created educational inequities for historically marginalized children and families.

Through the brief trainings educators will: 1) develop the start to an awareness of CR-SE,
including importance of race, ethnicity, culture, and language in schools; and 2) examine the role
that identity, power and privilege have in schools.

Timeframe

This contract is for 3 months of collaborative work between Irvington Union Free School
District and NYU Metro Center IESC. Once Irvington Union Free School District approves
the contract, work will begin in February 2020 and continue through April 2020.

Cost
