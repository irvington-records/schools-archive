	2. Work Session of the Board of Education - Presentation/Discussion

	Discussion: 2.1 Continued Discussion of Fields Project Committee Recommendations (Presentation)

	Dr. Harrison provided an overview of the Fields Project Committee’s five recommendations for improvements to the District’s athletic facilities as well as the necessary remediation East Field was provided, including several other recommendations made by the committee for the Board to consider when developing the scope of a possible referendum. A summary of the updated estimated costs for the field projects was also provided: East Field - $1,211,328 – the original estimate was $1,216,028, however, LAN reduced their fees from 7% to 6.5%); Meszaros Field - $1,797,525 with a total estimated cost of $3,008,853. Additional recommendations total $325,000. Dr. Harrison provided feedback on questions that have been posed

	Presentation: 2.2 Buildings & Grounds Facilities Recommendations (Presentation)

	Mr. Whitney thanked Peter Manouvelos and Rich Westbrock, of LAN for their assistance in providing cost estimates for the recommended projects as well as Beverly Miller and Gary Knowles who are members of the Building & Grounds Committee. Mr. Whitney presented an overview of the process that the B&G Committee undertook in order to present the Board of Education with a list of recommended capital projects, their associated costs, rationale and proposed prioritization for the Board’s consideration for possible inclusion in a proposal for a bond referendum.

	Of the 16 projects that the Committee presented for consideration, eight are connect to energy savings projects, two are pre-emptive in nature, four are concerned with quality of life and two for safety. It was noted that the committee separated the projects into Tier I projects (recommended) and Tier II projects, which are not being recommended at this time.

	The projects that are recommended include:

-
		Rewiring campus lights to allow shutoff

-
		Convert Dows Lane and MSS gym lights to LED

-
		Replace univents & upgrade HVAC

-
		Upgrade boiler controls at campus/MSS

-
		Move District Office to Dows Lane

-
		Add vestibules to IMS entrances

-
		Replace MSS gym roofs

-
		Renovate dustbowl between MS and gym building

	The estimated costs of the Tier I projects is $1,974,700.

	Projects in Tier II included:

-
		Creation of a parking lot at upper lot with gravel and lights

-
		Renovation of high school boys and girls bathroom

-
		Installation of speed humps on hill

-
		Install radiator covers at MSS

-
		Convert HS gym lights to LED

	The Tier II projects totaled $587,700.

	Mr. Whitney and Mrs. Kashkin also provided a project summary for each Tier I project along with the rationale for recommending the project. It was also recommended that the renovation to the boys and girls bathrooms could be considered for funding out of the 2014-15 operating budget.

	Mr. Whitney and Ms. Kashkin responded to comments and questions from the Board regarding the information provided in the presentation.

	Next steps for the possible facilities improvements include moving the HS lighting conversion from Tier II to Tier I as well as a possible addition of a “Project Manager” temp position to oversee the project in its entirety. The Board will continue to review and discuss the recommended facilities’ projects at its March 20th meeting.
