5. Presentation/Discussion

Discussion: 6.1 District Goals Presentation

Dr. Harrison gave an overview of our goals for this year that are integrated into our District's Strategic Plan, with 6 key objectives:

1.  Provide students with a rigorous, comprehensive, enriched and diversified curricula that will prepare students to achieve their personal best, and will integrate technology in their learning.

2.  Encourage innovation, creativity and risk taking to inspire a dynamic learning environment.

3.  Foster the social and emotional growth of all students and promote a culture where students are active participants in society.

4.  Support educators through targeted professional learning and opportunities for collaboration.

5.  Strengthen local connections to and ownership of our schools.

6.  Ensure the fiscal health of the district and provide for a high quality learning environment.

Dr. Harrison noted that Our Vision for Tomorrow takes our Mission another step in terms of how we plan to reach our goals.

2018-19 Goal Overview has 3 key focus areas including:

1. Strategic Leadership -- Dr Harrison highlighted identifying outcomes and opportunities to increase stakeholder engagement, enhancing communication to  inform, engage and educate District stakeholders while expanding on the Irvington UFSD District brand.

2. Instructional Leadership: Curriculum and Instruction -- Dr. Harrison noted that Dr. Kor is overseeing this area which includes implementing instructional

3. Financial and Operational Leadership: Business and Operations initiatives support the Strategic Plan and the District's finances and operations.

Dr. Harrison spoke about key objectives which include advancing the Strategic Plan by identifying opportunities to increase stakeholder engagement, i.e., how we can distinguish Irvington as a different School District. He also spoke about enhancing communications to educate and inform the community on the great work we do. Our new website is getting positive feedback and work continues to improve the site to make it an informed, rich resource for everyone in our school community. Dr. Harrison noted that he is currently in the process of working with the PTSA to set up a variety of events with community members at local venues within Irvington to have informal discussions.

Dr. Kor, Assistant Superintendent for Curriculum/HR, spoke on the topic of Instructional Leadership -- including the implementation of instructional practices that elevate student thinking and understanding as well as developing a balanced assessment system. Dr. Kor noted that staff is clearly, cohesively and thoughtfully continuing with Unit Planners and expressed excitement about our new PLRD (Professional Learning Release Day) program that will enable teachers the time commitment to focus on instruction and learning, something that will be a benefit to all. Dr. Kor also spoke about the rollout of the new K-2 PLTW (Project Lead the Way) curriculum. Alyssa Fischer, who taught Kindergarten, 1st and 2nd grades here, is now a teacher for the K-2 PLTW which has just launched. Jesse Lubinsky has taken on the leadership of PLTW in addition to Technology.

Dr. Harrison spoke about the ongoing use of data and that the District has expanded its Data Manager role and is in the process of developing a portal which enables the addition of data sets that teachers can access and update with test scores. This will provide one common place where important instructional and learning data can be stored and accessed. Moving forward, we will utilize this data to inform instruction and planning.

Carol Stein Assistant Superintendent for Business, spoke about Business & Operations Initiatives. Ms. Stein said that their job is a support role, focusing on financial resources to achieve the District Goals. Ms. Stein noted that last year, we focused on facilities needs to inform the Capital Bond Planning Project and settled the food service contract. This year the focus in on 3 main areas:

1.  Long term financial plan development -- includes history/trend analysis while considering key sensitive areas such as Teacher Retirement System (TRS) and    Employee Retirement System pension rates.

2.  Capital Bond Project -- which is likely up for a vote in the spring and still needs a lot of work in terms of narrowing the scope.

3.  Settlement of contracts -- with CSEA Custodial Unit and IAA.
