    The Process
 On February 10th the Administration presented proposals related to
  curriculum and instruction.
 Tonight, focus shifts to business operations, revenue and tax cap.
 Business operations include:
    Technology; transportation; business office; debt service;
     buildings and grounds; insurance; & employee benefits
 The Board of Education and Administration will engage in
  significant dialogue.
 Importance of public participation

Budget-Development Objectives Review
     Develop fiscally responsible budget that is mindful of the
      impact of the school budget on the entire school community
     Provide opportunity for:
       New and expanded student experiences
       Enhanced curriculum
       Professional development
       Maintain and improve facilities
     Sustain required and valued services and programs:
       AP courses, honors, electives
       Music programs
       Extracurricular experiences
          Clubs & athletics
       Bus monitors
       Special education
4

    Challenges in Meeting Objectives Review
      Limitations as a result of the Tax Levy Cap
      Uncertainty of State Aid
        State aid increases unknown at this time
      Maintenance of responsible tax rate
      District’s financial obligations:
        Pension
        Benefits
        Salary
        Debt service
        NYS, SED & Federal mandates

 Operations and Maintenance Considerations
     Consideration:
     Addition of one security greeter at Dows Lane

     Rationale:
     Providing for a safe and secure learning environment is a top priority in our
     schools. With the move of District Offices to Dows Lane, without the addition of
     an additional security greeter contractors and visitors with District-level business
     will be required to pass through the school chaperone free. This plan will provide
     for one greeter at the new Dows Lane entrance and the District Office entrance
     (current Dows Lane entrance). Additionally, the new greeter will be able to offer
     assistance to handicapped entrance for Dows Lane and provide necessary reception
     services for the District Office.

Planning Ahead: Building a Capital Reserve Fund
  A Capital Reserve Fund, like the District’s Tax Certiorari
   Reserves, are funds that set aside for an express purpose
  Allows the District to save money for future construction projects
   or improvements to facilities.
  Such a fund cannot be created or spent without voter approval.
  Where do funds come from? Funds can be budgeted or allocated
   from fund balance.
 Administrative Proposal:
 Seek Board of Education support and then Voter approval on May 19th, as the question must
 appear on a ballot for voter authorization. Within the 2015-16 budget, deposit an initial
 $25,000 utilizing the existing fund balance. Then at the end of each fiscal year, the Board
 and Administration contemplate viability of enhancing the reserve account.
