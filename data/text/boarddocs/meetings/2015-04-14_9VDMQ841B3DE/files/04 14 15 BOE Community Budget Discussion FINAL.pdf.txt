Budget-Development Objectives
 Develop fiscally responsible budget that is mindful of the impact of the
  school budget on the entire school community
 Provide opportunity for:
     New and expanded student experiences
     Enhanced curriculum
     Professional development
     Maintain and improve facilities
 Sustain required and valued services and programs:
     AP courses, honors, electives
     Music programs
     Extracurricular experiences
       Clubs & athletics
     Bus monitors
     Special education
     Support services (i.e. Guidance & Student Assistance Counselor)
2

    Our Process
     January 27th: Principals reported on their school’s progress and
      projected future needs
     February 10th: Administration presented considerations for advancing
      curriculum, programs, and student experiences
     March 10th: Administration shared operational needs, anticipated
      revenue, and projected tax-levy cap
     March 24th: Superintendent’s Proposed Budget; approval of Capital
      Reserve Fund Proposition; and direction provided to Administration
     April 9th: Administration presented budget adjustments to
      accommodate Board tax rate targets of 4%, 3.75%, & 3.5%
     Tonight: Administration will present additional modifications to fulfill
      the BOE’s new charge



3

    The “Charge” for the Administration
     Include the new consideration of restoring an English-Language
      Arts coach to support teachers and enhance instruction
     Strive to maintain a tax rate increase of approximately 3.5%




4

    Discussion Items
     Addition of ELA Coach:
       Grades 6-12
       Focus of effort to support literacy instruction across the curricula
     Possibility of replacing proposed additional Dows Lane/District
      Office security greeter with a part-time clerical employee:
       Cost would be comparable
       Limitations/concerns:
         Must provide with breaks and lunch, while greeter has lunch and breaks at their
          post
         Still need to provide coverage for remainder of the day
       Recommendation: introduce a security greeter, but evaluate this
        concept or other opportunities for the 2016-17 school year
