AGREEMENT

Contract Terms

Authority and Financial Responsibility

The completion of this form constitutes an agreement by and between FlexSchool (“the

School’) and Irvington Union Free School District (“the District”) whose signatures below

demonstrate they have the authority to enter into this contract in connection with education of
(‘the Student’) for 8th grade beginning on February 24, 2025 for the duration

of the academic year ending June 11, 2025. This contract shall become binding between both

parties when signed by the Founder and an authorized representative of the District.

Services

FlexSchool agrees to provide the District with the applicable FlexSchool course curriculum
and teaching lessons for Student. In connection with the provision of the curriculum, the
Student (accompanied by his Irvington teacher) shall be permitted to attend certain Flex
School classes virtually and participate in such classes as if he were a FlexSchool student.

Notwithstanding the Student’s participation in FlexSchool classes, the Irvington teacher
accompanying the Student shall be considered the Student’s teacher of record and shall be
responsible for associated teaching duties including, but not limited to, taking attendance,
assigning grades, parent-teacher conferences, IEP progress Reports, etc. The FlexSchool
staff shall consult with the Irvington teacher, as reasonably necessary, in connection with
such teacher activities.

Withdrawal and Reduction/Reimbursement of Fees

This agreement may be terminated by the District or by FlexSchool upon 30 calendar days’
written notification to the other party. If the District does not provide adequate notification
according to applicable state laws, FlexSchool may, for billing purposes, add the number of
enrolled days within the 30 calendar days’ notification period so that the tuition charged the
District reflects compliance with the 30 calendar days’ notice requirement. If FlexSchool does
not provide adequate notification according to applicable state laws, then the District is only
responsible for tuition up to and including the pupil's last day of attendance.

Dismissal
FlexSchool reserves the right to dismiss a student for failure to comply with any of its policies
or regulations, including those in the Family Handbook.

Family Handbook

2024-25 FlexSchool Family Handbooks will be distributed before the beginning of school and
become part of this contract. Parent(s) and/or legal guardian(s) and students will be asked to
sign the handbook thereby agreeing to abide by all policies and regulations of FlexSchool. In
the event that there exists a conflict between any term, condition, or provision contained
within this Agreement and in any policy or regulation contained within the FlexSchool Family
Handbook, the term, condition, or provision contained within this Agreement will control.

School Closings
Since school closings have been built into annual costs, the District agrees to pay fees during
months when a closing occurs.

Leave of Absence
The district is still liable for payment under the fee schedule if the student needs to take a
physical or mental leave of absence.

Conflict Resolution

It is understood that any controversy or claim arising out of or relating to this contract, or the
breach thereof, shall be settled by arbitration administered by the American Arbitration
Association in accordance with its rules, and judgment on the award rendered by the
arbitrator(s) may be entered in any court having jurisdiction thereof. By entering into this
agreement, the parties are giving up their right to have any dispute relating to this contract
decided in a court of law before a jury, and instead accept the use of arbitration.
