CONTRACT FOR HEALTH AND WELFARE SERVICES

This agreement made in duplicate this 12" day of February, 2020 by and between the Board of Education of the Irvington
U.F.S.D., County of Westchester, New York and the Board of Education of the Somers Central School District, County of
Westchester, New York.

WITNESSETH, that whereas the School District of Residence is duly empowered by the provisions of Section 912 of the
Education Law to enter into a contract for the purposes of providing health and welfare services to the child/children residing in
said school district and attending non-public schools in the Somers Central School District to begin on September 1, 2019 and to
end on June 30, 2020.

Now, therefore, the School District of Residence agrees to pay the Somers Central School District the estimated sum of
$1,188.11 per pupil for health and welfare services provided to approximately 1 child/children residing in the District of
Residence and agree that at the end of the 2019-2020 school year the foregoing charge will be recomputed using actual
expenditures for health and welfare services and actual enrollment as of October 1, 2019 and that a refund or additional charge
will be made as required on the basis of said re-computation.

And the Somers Central School District hereby agrees with the Irvington U.F.S.D. as follows:
|. That the services to be provided shall consist of annual medical inspection, school nursing service, vision and hearing
tests, examinations for employment certificates for students, examinations for participation in athletics, notification of

parents regarding defects and follow-up.

2. That the party of the second part will also furnish the following equipment to be used in providing such services as
requested by the authorities in charge of the non-public school: scales, vision and hearing devices, first aid supplies.

It is expressly agreed by and between the parties hereto that the services agreed to be supplied under this contract shall not
include any teaching services.

IN WITNESS HEREOF, the parties have hereunto set their hands the day and year above written.
