This agreement made thisd\ Say of NaqQuav 4, 2020 by and between the Board of Education
of Irvington Union Free School District, party of the first part, hereinafter referred to as the “Board” and
Mountain Lake Academy, 386 River Road, Lake Placid, NY 12946, party of the second part, hereinafter
referred to as the “Contractor.”

WITNESSETH: The Board is authorized by law to contract with institutions for the
instruction of children with handicapping conditions and

WHEREAS, the Contractor warrants that it has reviewed the individualized education
program (IEP) of each pupil to be enrolled in its school and represents that it will provide the level of
services identified in the IEP to meet the needs of such children with handicapping conditions.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties hereto mutually agree as follows:

1. The Contractor will provide adequate instruction in accordance with the provisions
relating to eligibility of schools contained in Part 200 of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education
of the State of New York (hereinafter referred to as the “Commissioner of Education”) to attend
Contractor’s school. The school year is hereby defined as July | to June 30. The Contractor will provide a
special education program for eligible children with handicapping conditions placed in the Contractor’s
school in accordance with the provisions of Education Law sections 4407 and 4408 and Part 200 of the
Regulations of the Commissioner of Education for the part of the school year for which such children are
approved for placement in such school by the Commissioner of Education and will operate and administer
its special educational program consistent with all the terms and conditions required under section 200.7(b)
of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education.

2. In full consideration for the education services to be rendered by the Contractor to the
Board for the period September | — June 30 of each school year under the terms of this agreement, the
board will pay to the Contractor for each child the agreed upon tuition rate approved by the Commissioner
of Education and approved by the Director of the New York State Division of the Budget the amount of
$2,731.80. Where such education is provided for periods of substantially less than the duration of the full
September through June program, consideration will be prorated accordingly, the specific amount to be
fixed by the State of New York (hereinafter the “State’?)

3a. Payments for the School year shall be due in accordance with the following schedule:

(1) On or before September 20 of the school year, the total amount for the July
through August portion of the school year based upon actual enrollment and
attendance date for the July through August component.

(2) The ten-month program shall be paid monthly, within thirty (30) days of billing
based upon enrollment and the attendance data.

(3) In the event that enrollment is for less than a full month, tuition will be prorated
accordingly.

[OCR]
b. For the purposes of this agreement, the definition of calculation of enrollment and
attendance shall be in accordance with section 175.6 of the regulations of the Commissioner of Education
as determined by the Board.

4, The Contractor will obtain whatever releases or other legal documents are necessary in
order that the Contractor may render full complete reports concerning the education and progress of the
pupil or pupils covered by the terms of this agreement. The Contractor will render such reports to the
Board at any time that such reports are made to the parents of the pupil or pupils covered by the terms of
this agreement and will render such additional reports as may be required by the terms of this agreement. It
is such understood and agreed by the parties to this agreement. It is understood and agreed by the parties to
this agreement that the full responsibility shall permit the Commissioner of Education to withdraw approval
for the placement of such child in such program in which event this agreement shall be cancelled forthwith
in regard to such child for whim such releases are not submitted.

5. Pursuant to section 4405 of the Education Law, payment of maintenance expenses for
residential pupils for the September through Jie program shall be made by the county wherein each such
pupil resides (or the city in the case of a county contained in the City of New York) at the commencement
of the school year (July 1) for which maintenance is paid. The charge for maintenance may not exceed the
amount approved for such purpose by the Commissioner of Social Services of the State and the Director of
the Budget of the State. Such payment should be made on the same terms and conditions as tuition, but the
payment schedule will be determined by the appropriate count or city.

6. No parent or guardian or any other person shall be required to make any payment for
tuition of maintenance on behalf of any child with a handicapping condition covered by this agreement in
addition to the payments made by the State and the county or city.

7. The Contractor will maintain the standard set forth under section 200.7(b) of the
Regulations of the Commissioner of Education to preserve its status as an approved school for the
education of children with handicapping conditions. It is understood and agreed by the parties that failure
to do so shall render this agreement void, in which case the Contractor shall be entitled to no compensation
for the Board any amounts already received for that portion of such school year.

8. The State, acting through the Department of Education or the Department of Audit and
Control, shall have the right to examine any or all accounts kept by the Contractor in connection with this
agreement.

9. The Contractor shall be subject to the visitation of the Commissioner of Education or
his designated representatives.

10. This agreement shall take effect as of the 6th of January, 2020. This agreement may
be terminated by written notice by either party to the other party and the Commissioner of Education not
less than ninety (90) days before the commencement of any succeeding school year (July 1) subject to the
approval of the Commissioner of Education. In the event of such termination, the parties will adjust the
accounts due and the Contractor will undertake no additional expenditures not already required. Upon any
such termination, the parties shall endeavor in an orderly manner to wind down activities hereunder.

11. The Contractor hereby agrees to furnish to the State all reports, audits, etc., required
to make determinations as to eligibility under the provisions of the Regulations of the Commissioner of
Education. Such materials shall be furnished at such times as are required by the State. Failure to submit
required materials within ten (10) days of demand or as required by regulation shall constitute grounds for
the Commissioner of Education to withdraw approval for such program.

12. The Contractor agrees to provide the State access to all relevant records which the
State requires to determine either the Contractor’s or the Board’s compliance with applicable Federal or
State statutes or regulations with the effect of law, which regulate either the execution of the agreement or
the performance of obligations under the agreement. The Contractor agrees to retain all materials and

[OCR]
records relevant to the execution or performance of the agreement in accordance with the provision of
section 74.21 of volume 34 of the Code of Federal Regulations, but in no event less than six years from the
days of this agreement.

13. This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

14. The Board shall have all of its common law rights of set-off. These rights and powers
shall include, but not be limited to, the Boards option to withhold for the purposes of set-off any moneys
due to the Contractor under this contract up to any amounts due and owning the Board with regard to this
contract, or amounts due and owing to the Board for any other reason.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties hereto have executed this agreement the day and
year first above written.
