CONTRACT FOR HEALTH and WELFARE SERVICE

THIS AGREEMENT made in duplicate this 3 day of February 2020 by and between
the Board of Education of Irvington UFSD (the School District of Residence),
County of Southern Westchester

of the City of White Plains (White Plains City School District), County of Westchester.

WITNESSETH, that whereas the School District of Residence is duly empowered by the provisions of Section 912

of the Education Law to enter into a contract for the purposes of providing health and welfare services to the children
residing in said school district and attending nonpublic schools in the White Plains City School District to begin
September 3 2019 and to end or June 30, 2020

Now, therefore, the School District of Residence agrees to pay the White Plains City School District the estimated

sum of $1,044.27 per pupil for health and welfare services provided to approximately 8 children
residing in the District of Residence and attending nonpublic schools in the White Plains City School District. It is
further agreed that at the end of the 19-20 school year, the foregoing charge will be recomputed using actual
expenditures for health and welfare service and actual enrollment as of October 1,2020 _and that a refund or

additional charge will be made as required on the basis of said recomputation.

And the White Plains City School District hereby agrees with the School District of Residence as follows:

1. That the services to be provided shall include, as needed, the following: services performed by a
physician, nurse, school psychologist, school social worker of school speech correctionist, and may
also include vision and hearing tests, the taking of medical histories and the administration of health
screening tests, the maintenance of cumulative health records and the administration of emergency
care programs for ill or injured pupils.

2. That the White Plains City School District will also furnish the following equipment to be used in
providing such services as requested by the authorities in charge of the nonpublic school:

Vision and hearing testing devices, health record forms and first aid supplies.

It is expressly agreed by and between the parties hereto that the service agreed to be supplies under this contract
shall not include any teaching services.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have hereunto set their hands the day and year above written.
