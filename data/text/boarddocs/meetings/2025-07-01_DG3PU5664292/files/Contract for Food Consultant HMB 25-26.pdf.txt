   A. Introduction

        HMB Consultants, LLC (“HMB”), in its capacity as an independent contractor of the
Irvington Union Free School District (“District”), proposes to provide consulting services
(“Services”) to the District related to the District’s Child Nutrition Program (the “Program(s)”).

         HMB, will provide the services of consultants who have the experience, skills, experience,
knowledge, and competency in an effort to enable the district to experience a school year with
nutritious, appealing, student acceptable food service as well as a positive financial outcome for the
District. HMB will evaluate the performance of the contracted Food Service Management Company
(“FSMC”) awarded the contract with the district.

   B. Scope Of Proposed Services

ONSITE OBSERVATION AND REVIEW

        Onsite visits will allow for a review and evaluation of the Child Nutrition Programs operated
by the FSMC. HMB will work with the district to set a schedule compatible to the needs and
timeline of the district for the 2025-26 School Year.

        HMB will complete the USDA-required on-site annual self-reviews prior to the Feb 1 st
deadline. The remaining visits will be available at the discretion of the Assistant Superintendent
for Business. HMB will furnish a written summary report to the district within 15 business days
after each visit.

SERVICES:

   •   Menu Evaluation – Analyze monthly menus for variety, quality, and conformity to the
       contractual requirements at both the Elementary and Secondary levels and provide
       recommended adjustments to the Food Service Director and Assistant Superintendent for
       Business. HMB will also ensure that the FSMC meets all USDA guidelines at each grade
       level for all meal components.

   •   Meal Participation – HMB will draft reports that analyze both breakfast and lunch
       participation per individual building and District-wide, comparing the current month to the
       same month of the previous year, if desired. This is an excellent tool for the District to
       identify which buildings may need attention to boost meal participation.

   •   Labor – Ensure that all staffing positions, wages, and benefits are maintained as per the
       bid specifications and Labor Schedules therein. Additionally, HMB Consultants can
       provide an analysis of the meals per labor hour, an indicator of the efficiency of staffing.

   •   Quality Assurance and Self-Reviews – Conduct the USDA-required Annual Self-Review
       in each building. Additionally, we will complete an internal checklist of contract compliance
       areas that we identified within the bid specifications to ensure the FSMC is adhering to
       them. This evaluation and self-review of each building is required by NYSED prior to
       February 1st of each year.

    •    Student Advisory and Wellness Committee Meetings - HMB will attend and facilitate,
         if desired, meetings with students, parents and administrators to glean input into menu
         offerings. HMB will attend and participate in Wellness Committee meetings throughout
         the year when coordinated in advance with our onsite schedule.

    •    Profit and Loss Statement Analysis – Analyze the FSMC’s profit and loss statement in
         detail to ensure that the guaranteed return stipulated in the contract will be achieved.
         Provide projected C-Fund revenue so the District may budget Child Nutrition Program
         expenditures.

    C. Consulting Fees

       HMB will provide consultation at the rate identified below. This on-site rate includes onsite
days throughout the September – June 2025-26 school year. Billing will be generated three times
per year or monthly whatever the district desires.

         6 Days per year - $6,000.00

       The above fees include all expenses incurred by HMB Consultants including travel and
supplies. These fees do not include costs incurred voluntarily by the district at the
recommendation of HMB, such as equipment or other related expenses.

       Fees are payable as billed, upon satisfactory completion of work performed pursuant to
any agreement entered between HMB and the District.
