SIDE LETTER AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE
BOARD OF EDUCATION OF THE IRVINGTON UNION
FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (“DISTRICT” or “BOARD”)
AND THE IRVINGTON ADMINISTRATORS’
ASSOCIATION (“ASSOCIATION”) CONCERNING
JOYCE CHAPNICK, JON HIRSCH, ANDREA KANTOR,
ALLYSON DALEY, AND DAVID SOTTILE

WHEREAS, Article VI(A)ofthe parties’Collective Bargaining Agreement,
forthe period ofJuly 1,2024 - June 30,2030 (“CBA”), contains a provision setting
forth the work year and vacation leave for members of the bargaining unit;
and

WHEREAS, Joyce Chapnick (“Ms. Chapnick”), Jon Hirsch (“Mr. Hirsch”),
Andrea Kantor (Dr. Kantor’), Allyson Daley (“Ms. Daley”),and David Sottile (Mr.
Sottile”) are mem bers ofthe ASSOCIATION and are bound by the provisions of
the parties’ CBA;and

WHEREAS, the District's scheduling of Ms. Chapnick, Mr. Hirsch, Dr.
Kantor, Ms. Daley, &Mr. Sottile fora Professional Development Session on June
30, 2025, requires their presence on a day they had previously designated for
vacation for either the fullday ora halfday;and

WHEREAS, the parties have engaged in collective bargaining for the
purpose of providing additional com pensation to the above-referenced unit
members for attending said Professional Development Session on June 30,
2025;

NOW, THEREFORE, it is hereby stipulated and agreed by the parties as
follows:

1. Ms. Chapnick,Mr.Hirsch,Dr.Kantor,and Ms. Daley shallbe compensated
for one (I)additionalday of work at their perdiem rate ofpay based upon
a work year of 240 days (/240th) for their attendance at the above-
referenced Professional Development Session on June 30, 2025. Said
previously scheduled vacation day shall be deducted from each unit
mem ber’s 2024/25 allotment.

2. Mr. Sottile shall be com pensated for one half(V/2) ofan additional day of
work at his per diem rate of pay based upon a work year of 240 days
(/240th) for his attendance at the above-referenced Professional
Development Session on June 30, 2025. Mr. Sottile’s previously
scheduled one half (1/2) vacation day shall be deducted from his 2024/25
allotment.

3. This Agreement is subject to the review and the acceptance ofthe Board
of Education by formal resolution, duly executed. Absent said approval,
this Agreement shallbe nulland void.
