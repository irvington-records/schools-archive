The contract between the Irvington Union Free School District and the Hawthorne Cedar Knolls School District is to provide educational services for the 2022-23 school year. The student number, rather than the name, has been provided for in the resolution due to the confidential nature of this matter. The final tuition and maintenance rates are set by the NYS Education Department.
