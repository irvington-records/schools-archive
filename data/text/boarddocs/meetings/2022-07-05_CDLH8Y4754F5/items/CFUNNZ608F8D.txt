This is a first read for the following policy:

Code of Conduct

Following a comprehensive review by school district stakeholders inclusive of community members, parents, students, faculty members, administrators, and Board of Education Trustees, revisions have been proposed to the Code of Conduct for it to be culturally responsive and reflective of current values.  As the District continues to expand its restorative practices initiative, further revisions will be made and presented to the school community.
