Student Confidentiality/Suppressed Data: To ensure student confidentiality, the Department does not publish data for groups with fewer than five students or data that would
allow readers to easily determine the performance of a group with fewer than five students. When fewer than five students in a group are tested, percentages of tested students
scoring at various levels are suppressed for that group and for the next smallest group. The racial/ethnic groups display this suppression with a S. All other groups display this
with a dash. However, the performance of tested students in racial/ethnic groups for which the data are suppressed is aggregated and shown in the Small Group Total row.
