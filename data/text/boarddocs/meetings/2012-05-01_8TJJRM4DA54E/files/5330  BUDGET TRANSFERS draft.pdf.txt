SUBJECT: BUDGET TRANSFERS

The transfer of funds between and within functional unit appropriations of the General Fund is
commonly required during the school year.

The Superintendent, in accordance with the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education, is authorized
to make budget transfers between line item accounts, so long as the transfer of any one item does not
exceed ten percent (10%) of the amount appropriated by the Board for that category. All transfers in
excess of ten percent (10%) require prior Board approval.

For clarification, regulations allow transfers between contingent expenditure codes and/or transfers
from non-contingent expenditures codes to contingent expenditures codes. Regulations do not allow
the transfer of funds from contingent expenditure codes to non-contingent expenditure codes, or
transfers between non-contingent expenditure codes.

In the event that a situation arises which requires an immediate budget transfer in an amount greater
than may be authorized by the Superintendent, the President of the Board of Education is authorized to
approve the transfer.

Lists of all transfers between function and / or object codes as detailed on the voter approved budget,
and authorized by the Superintendent, shall be incorporated into the monthly financial report as an
informational item for the Board of Education. Internal transfers between same object codes and/or
building codes, whose detailed budgets were not part of the voter approved budget, shall be approved
by the Superintendent’s designee and shall be exempt from reporting to the Board of Education.
