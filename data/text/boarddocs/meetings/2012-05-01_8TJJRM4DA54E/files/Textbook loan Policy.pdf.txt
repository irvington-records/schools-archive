SUBJECT: TEXTBOOK LOAN PROGRAM

        The following rules and regulations shall govern the loan of textbooks to all
public and non-public students of the District.

   1. Textbooks

       a.      The definition of a textbook under the textbook loan program shall be in
               accordance with Section 701 of the NYS Education Law.

   2. Procedures for Inventory Control

            a. In order to accurately determine which textbooks can be filled from
               existing inventory, the Irvington Union Free School District shall maintain
               a district-wide annual inventory and shall be in accordance with the
               guidelines set forth in the publication stated above.
            b. The District shall establish proper procedure for annually inventorying all
               textbooks.
            c. All loaned textbooks must be returned no later than June 30th of a school
               year. Textbooks not returned by either public or non-public students, shall
               be invoiced for the replacement cost of the textbook. Textbooks returned
               damaged as to be not usable shall be invoiced for the replacement cost of
               the textbook.
            d. To the extent required under Education law 3202, a Board of Education
               may not withhold textbooks from a student who fails to return loaned
               textbooks from a prior school year.


TEXTBOOK LOAN TO PUBLIC SCHOOL STUDENTS

        The following rules and regulations shall govern the loan of textbooks to students
attending the District’s public schools.
    1. Upon the recommendation of the Superintendent of Schools, the Board of
        Education shall designate the textbooks to be used in district schools.
    2. Building-level principals shall be responsible for submitting textbooks requests to
        the Business Office.
    3. Students shall receive their textbooks at their respective schools, and shall return
        them to the same place at the end of the school year.

TEXTBOOK LOAN TO NON-PUBLIC SCHOOL STUDENTS

        The following rules and regulations shall govern the loan of textbooks to residents
of the District attending non-public schools.

   1. District Residents

       a.      Non-public school students requesting to be loaned textbooks from
               Irvington Union Free School District must be District residents in
               accordance with District Policy No. 7130.
       b.      Prior to submitting a request for the loan of textbooks under this policy,
               non-public school students must submit proof of residency- as established
               in a manner consistent with State Law and the Regulations of the
               Commissioner - and be approved by the District Registrar as an approved
               resident.

   2. Enrolled Student

       a.      Non-public school students requesting to be loaned textbooks from
               Irvington Union Free School District must submit proof of enrollment in a
               non-public school.
       b.      Said proof of enrollment must be executed by an individual from the non-
               public school authorized to provide such proof of enrollment.
       c.      Said proof of enrollment must be attached to the textbook loan request.


   3. Loan Requests

       A Loan Request Form may be obtained through the district’s website , mail or
   picked up at the Business Office of the Irvington Union Free School District.
       a.     All Loan Request Forms must be submitted to the Business Office of the
              Irvington Union Free School District no later than June 1st.
       b.     All Loan Request Forms must include all required information in order for
              it to be processed.
       c.     Once all textbook loan requests have been assembled, the District shall
              compare all requests with the supply of textbooks available in the
              District’s inventory, and available funding.
       d.     The District will purchase and distribute textbooks on an equitable basis
              according to the New York State Textbook Loan Program guidelines
              “Recommended Procedures for Textbook Purchases, Loans, and Inventory
              Control”, published by the NYS Education Dept., Div. of State Aid.
