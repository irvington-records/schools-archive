	4. Presentations/Discussions

		Presentation: 4.1 Presentation of 2010-2011 School Year Report Card (Presentation)

		Dr. Raina Kor provided an overview of Irvington students' performance in the 2010-11 State Assessments, Regents, and AP courses. Dr. Kor also included historical data, comparison data for 11 other area districts as well as data for six years of State assessments for the current 9th grade cohort. Dr. Kor noted that the State Education Department revised the cut scores in  2010, making those scores appear lower than in previous years. She also noted that it is difficult to compare scores over the course of five years given the fact that the State changes the assessments year to year.

		With regard to the 2011 4th grade Mathematics data, Dr. Kor noted Irvington falls within the mean of the 18 other districts compared. She also noted that there are seven different math programs utilized amongst the 18 other districts. Based on the data, Irvington should be proud of the math scores. Mr. McCann noted that identifying the number of classified students would be helpful when looking at data.

		English Language Arts -Between 2009 and 2010 the cut points changed significantly and therefore the numbers appear to have decreased. Irvington ranked 7th when compared to the other 18 area districts.

		Science assessment is given at grade 4 and 8. Irvington students do consistently well. It was noted that 8th grade students in accelerated science do not take the state assessments (approximately 40 students), but take earth science regents instead.

		Social Studies - Mr. McCann made the observation that the middle range of scores are where the majority of our students fall when looking at the 2011 Global History Regents data. Dr. Kor noted that the administration does look at the data and often will drill deeper when there seems to be some varying numbers in an attempt to understand the causes and where changes are warranted. Dr. Kor also noted that when looking at the data, teachers will very often look at the individual students' scores to see if they can identify the cause of a lower than expected score. Robert Grados noted that assessments are only one indicator of how our students are doing, especially when an individual child's performance is investigated.

		World Languages - Irvington students do well in world language.

		Dr. Roelle shared that when looking at comparison data, what is gleaned is that all districts do quite well and Irvington is consistent with these districts.

		The Board asked how the data will be used and if goals might be set from analyzing the data to drive curriculum changes, etc. Dr. Roelle stated that that statistical analysis will be very useful especially given the APPR and that the District might want to join with other districts to use the services of a statistical analyst. Dr. Kor noted that the Common Core Standards will revamp all the state assessments.

	5. Comments From Members of the Community

		Members of the community were also interested in how the District will use the data with regard to driving curriculum as well as looking at teachers' performance.
