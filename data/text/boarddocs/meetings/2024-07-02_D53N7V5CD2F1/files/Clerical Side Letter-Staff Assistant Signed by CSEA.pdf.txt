AGREEMENT BY AND BETWEEN
THE BOARD OF EDUCATION OF THE
IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT AND
THE IRVINGTON CIVIL SERVICE EMPLOYEES’ ASSOCIATION
CLERICAL UNIT REGARDING THE VOLUNTARY
RECOGNITION OF A CIVIL SERVICE POSITION

WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free Schoo! District
(“District”) and the Irvington Civil Service Employees Association Clerical Unit
(“Association”) are parties to a certain written agreement (“CBA”) for the period of July
1, 2020 — June 30, 2027; and

WHEREAS, the parties’ CBA contains a Recognition Clause setting forth the
titles represented by the Association (“Article I”); and

WHEREAS, on June 27, 2024, the District created a new Civil Service position
with the title, Staff Asststant-IT; and

WHEREAS, the District has advised the Association of its desire to voluntarily
recognize the title of Staff Assistant-IT and include it within Article I as one of the titles
represented by the Association; and

WHEREAS, the parties have engaged in collective bargaining concerning the
inclusion of said title within the bargaining unit; and

NOW, THEREFORE, the District and the Unit agree as follows:

1, The District and the Association hereby agree to modify the CBA by adding
the title of “Staff Assistant-IT” to Article | of the CBA effective with the date
of execution of this Side Letter Agreement and/or the creation of said position
by the Board of Education (“Board”), whichever is later. The parties herewith
acknowledge that the Board’s creation of the position is contingent upon the
approval of same by Westchester County Civil Service.

2. The work year for said title shall be twelve (12) months and said title shall be
placed on Database Assistant/Secretary to School Office/Principal Column of
the 12 Month CSEA Clerical/Aides salary schedule.

3. The parties acknowledge that this side letter agreement is subject to approval
by the Board. Absent said approval, this agreement shall be null and void.

4. The Association and its members herewith agree that only a violation of the
terms and conditions of this Side Letter Agreement shall be subject to the
grievance and arbitration procedures set forth in the CBA.

5. Other than as specifically set forth herein, this Agreement shall not constitute
a practice or policy nor serve as a precedent for any of the parties, in any
forum. This Agreement may not be used and/or cited by any of the parties in
any future grievance, arbitration or litigation, including but not limited to the
Public Employment Relations Board (“PERB”), except for the purposes of
enforcing the specific terms of this Agreement.

6, The parties herewith acknowledge that this Agreement represents the full,
final and complete resolution of this matter.
