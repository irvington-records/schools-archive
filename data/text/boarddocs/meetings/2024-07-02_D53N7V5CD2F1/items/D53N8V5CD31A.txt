Category: 8. Consent Agenda I: Approval of Annual Resolutions
Subject: 8.1 Consent Agenda I
Type: Action (Consent)

To make more efficient use of meeting time, the Board of Education authorizes the use of a consent agenda as a part of the meeting agenda. A Consent Agenda allows for one motion to approve multiple items and such items are identified as such on the Agenda. The President of the Board will ask for a motion to approve the Consent Agenda and will ask if  Board member(s) wish to have discussion and/or clarification on specific item(s) contained within the Consent Agenda. Following the discussion, the Board will then vote on the Consent Agenda motion. In addition, a Board member may request to have an item removed from the Consent Agenda in order to vote on the motion separately.

Please note that unless otherwise indicated all appointments, adoptions, approvals, designations and/or authorizations are for the 2024-25 school year.
