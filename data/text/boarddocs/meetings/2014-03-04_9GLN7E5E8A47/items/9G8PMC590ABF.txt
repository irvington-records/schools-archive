Category: 9. Business Office Matters
Subject: 9.3 Health Contracts - Out of District
Type: Action (Consent)

As provided by Section 912 of the State Education Law, the Irvington School District is responsible for providing health services to Irvington resident students who attend private/parochial schools in other school districts.
