High Expectations for our Schools, Staff & Students
For our schools to meet our students’ needs we must:
     Provide students with rigorous authentic, learning
      experiences rooted in a comprehensive curriculum, to
      acquire the knowledge, skills and dispositions necessary
      to be successful beyond Irvington

     Value and foster the professional learning and growth of
      all members of our school community

     Provide opportunities to promote the growth of interests
      and talents

     Offer well maintained learning environments and school
      facilities

District Goals Informed the Budget Development
Instructional Goal:
 Establish high expectations and accountability for quality teaching in every classroom and
    for every student.
Curriculum Goal:
 Enhance curricula and instruction to promote academic excellence.
       Continue to adapt the existing curricula with the Common Core Learning Standards
        (CCLS) and related mandates, while maintaining higher standards where possible;
       Identify essential areas of curricular and instructional need and develop plans for
        improvement.
 Assess the use of technology in instruction and develop a coherent K-12 technology
    curriculum action plan and begin implementation where possible;
 Assess District’s approach to college preparedness and admissions, and initiate
    improvements;
 Improve curriculum communications with the community in order to create better
    understanding of the changes in curriculum and to encourage home and school
    partnerships.
Facilities Goal:
 Address facility needs to ensure District buildings and grounds are conducive to
    programmatic needs.
       Determine the most appropriate use of the existing school facilities, and utilize the
        2012-13 Space Utilization Study;
       Develop an action plan for the athletic facilities/fields.

Proposed Budget Expands Student Opportunities
Continuing to expand academic programs by:
 Elementary Schools:
   Increases mathematics Academic Intervention Services to
    support students’ needs
   Introduces Fundations to provide a systematic means of
    instructing K-1spelling and phonics
 Secondary Schools:
   Creates a 9th grade writing program
   Increases English Academic Intervention Services
   Adds an additional MS co-teacher for 6th grade social
    studies and 7th&8th grade science
   Provides for 2 additional HS sections for scheduling flexibility
   Offers additional professional development for the guidance
    department

Proposed Budget Invests in Arts & Student Activities
 Offers a 0.4 FTE increase in the music department to offset
  prior years’ reductions & provide more individualized
  instruction
 Restores the MSS school play to offer theatrical
  experiences for our students
 Reinstates 2 IMS student clubs to enrich student learning
  experiences
