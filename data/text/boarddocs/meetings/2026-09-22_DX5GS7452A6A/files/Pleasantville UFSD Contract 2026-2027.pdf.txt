CONTRACT FOR SPECIAL EDUCATION SERVICES - 2026-2027
Non-Resident Tuition

AGREEMENT made by and between the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School
District having its principal office at 6 Dows Lane, Irvington, New York 10533, (hereinafter called the
“Sending School District”) and the Pleasantville Union Free School District having its principal office
at 60 Romer Avenue, Pleasantville, New York 10570 (hereinafter called the “Pleasantville UFSD”).

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the Sending School District has determined that one or more of its students with disabilities
may require placement in an appropriate special education program outside of the district;

WHEREAS, the Sending School District has identified one or more specialized programs operated by
Pleasantville Union Free School District (“Pleasantville UFSD”) as appropriate placements for such
student(s), in accordance with recommendations made by tts Committee on Special Education (CSE);

WHEREAS, Pleasantville UFSD is willing to consider acceptance of referred students from the Sending
School District into its specialized programs, subject to the terms and conditions of this Agreement;

IT 1S AGREED as follows:

1. The Sending District hereby enters into agreement with Pleasantville UFSD for the 2026-2027
school year to place and educate student(s) recommended by the Sending District’s CSE and
accepted by the Pleasantville UFSD in designated specialized programs operated by Pleasantville
UFSD. This agreement shall apply to any and all such placements made during the school year.

2. The Sending District agrees to pay Pleasantville UFSD the annual sum identified for each enrolled
student, as detailed in the individual tuition summary worksheet(s) appended to this Agreement.
Tuition rates are inclusive of general or foundation aid payable to Pleasantville UFSD. Payment
terns shall follow the schedule and method established in the appendices. The Pleasantville UFSD
will charge, and the Sending District will pay for any requested evaluations, including but not limited
to psychological, educational, speech, occupational therapy, physical therapy, assistive technology, or
augmentative communication, that are deemed necessary by the CSE, as well as any assistive
technology and/or instructional equipment as set forth in the child’s Individualized Education
Program (IEP).

3. The Pleasantville UFSD agrees to provide the services as set forth in each individual child’s
Individualized Education Program (IEP).

4. The Pleasantville UFSD agrees to report to the Sending School District on the progress of each
individual student as requested, but not more than quarterly.

[OCR]
5. The Pleasantville UFSD agrees to permit a representative or representatives of the Committee on
Special Education of the Sending School District to visit the program in which the child is enrolled
on reasonable notice.

6. The Pleasantville UFSD agrees to comply with all federal and state statutes and regulations
regarding the provision of educational services to students with disabilities, subject to the provisions
of Paragraph “9” below.

7. Nothing contained herein, however, shall serve to transfer responsibilities for the Child from the
Sending Schoo! District to the Pleasantville UFSD except for the provision of the particular special
education services contracted herein and any related ancillary duties such as report cards, progress
reports, parent-teacher conferences, etc.

8. Transportation to and from school is the Sending School District’s responsibility.

9. Itis understood and agreed that this Agreement is for the 2026-2027 school year only. Tuition will
adjust from one year to the next and is based on the individual recommendations of the student’s IEP.

10. Nothing herein shall be construed from preventing either party from taking such action as it may
deem necessary, reasonable, or appropriate in the case of a student whose continued presence in the
educational program would constitute a danger to others, to himself/herself, or to property,
sufficiently serious to warrant immediate protective action.

11. This Agreement shall remain in full force and effect for the duration of the 2026-2027 school
year. However, the Sending School District may withdraw an individual student from a designated
program upon fifteen (15) days’ written notice to Pleasantville UFSD, should its CSE determine that
the program is no longer appropriate for that student. Such withdrawal shall not affect the validity or
enforceability of this Agreement with respect to any other students placed, or who may be placed,
during the term of the Agreement. In addition, if the Pleasantville UFSD requests that the Sending
School District’s CSE schedule a program review to consider possible changes to the student’s
current program, such CSE meeting shall be promptly scheduled.

12. This Agreement may not be altered, varied or contradicted except by a similar writing executed
by both of the parties to this Agreement.
