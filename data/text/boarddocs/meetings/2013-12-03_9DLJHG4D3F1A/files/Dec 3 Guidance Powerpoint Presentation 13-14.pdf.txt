                LONG TERM GOALS

• Foster a strong partnership between student, family and
  school community
• Instill and develop confidence in our responses to support
  student achievement and growth
• Consistently analyze and assess the comprehensive
  instructional curriculum to best align students to a 21st
  century global society
• Continuously identify and implement systems to maximize
  our ability to support students, parents and school personnel
  in an effort to best prepare students for their experiences
  beyond high school

                SHORT TERM GOALS

• Analyze the college process and develop plans for improvement
• Analyze and improve our scheduling process to better align our
  course offerings to the individual and comprehensive needs of our
  students career and college goals
• Further engage the school community by establishing an
  environment of proactive communication and student advocacy to
  support the academic, social/emotional and college/career growth
  of all our students
• Streamline our data reporting systems, i.e. Eschool Plus/Home
  Access, to enhance efficiency and accuracy
• Increase the guidance department’s overall capacity to support
  student growth and success by creating and providing strategic
  opportunities for professional development
• Foster stronger collaboration with the faculty in order to better
  guide students in their academic decisions

                        Where are we?
• Over the past 5 years, Irvington student college applications and
  acceptances have demonstrated the following trends for the nation’s most
  selective colleges according to US News & World Report and Barron’s
  Review:
   o 1,435 applications resulted in 363 acceptances
   o Trends show we are at or above the national acceptance rates for
      most colleges on this list including:
        • Brown University, University of Pennsylvania, Cornell, Dartmouth,
          Columbia, University of Chicago, NYU, Emory, Georgetown and
          Northwestern
    o Trends show we are below the national acceptance rates for some
      select colleges on this list including:
        • Harvard, Princeton, Yale, Amherst, Boston University, Middlebury
