Meeting: Dec 03, 2013 - Regular Meeting - *6:30 pm
Date: 2013-12-03
Category: 9. Personnel Matters
Subject: 9.1 Resolution Regarding Leave of Absence and Voluntary Transfer of Tenure Area
Type: 

WHEREAS, Joan Burns, a tenured Reading teacher, was assigned to 1.0 FTE teaching position in the tenure area of Reading during the 2011-2012 school year; and

	WHEREAS, Ms. Burns also possesses a New York State teaching certificate in the tenure area of Special Education; and

	WHEREAS, during the 2012-2013 school year, Ms. Burns voluntarily served in a split tenure assignment consisting of (.4) Reading and (.6) Special Education; and

	WHEREAS, during the 2012-2013 school year, Ms. Burns took a leave of absence from (.6) of her Reading position in order to effectuate the aforementioned assignment; and

	WHEREAS, the District filled (.5) of the aforementioned Reading vacancy with a long-term substitute teacher with a valid New York State Reading certificate; and

	WHEREAS, the District determined not to fill the remaining (.1) of the aforementioned Reading vacancy during the 2012-2013 school year; and

	WHEREAS, Ms. Burns has requested to voluntarily serve in the Special Education tenure area on a full-time, i.e. 1.0 FTE, basis during the 2013-2014 school year; and

	WHEREAS, in order to effectuate the aforementioned assignment, Ms. Burns has requested a leave of absence from her 1.0 FTE Reading position for the 2013-2014 school year, pursuant to the terms of a certain Side Letter Agreement between the Board of Education (“Board”), Ms. Burns and the Irvington Faculty Association (“Association”); and

	WHEREAS, the long-term substitute appointed to fill the (.5) Reading vacancy during the 2012-2013 school year has been appointed to continue in said assignment during the 2013-2014 school year; and

	WHEREAS, the District has determined not to fill the remaining (.5) Reading vacancy during the 2013-2014 school year,

	NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the District’s records shall be revised to accurately reflect the foregoing; and

	BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Board of Education hereby approves the Side Letter of Agreement between the Board, Ms. Burns and the Association regarding a voluntary transfer of tenure area and a leave of absence.
