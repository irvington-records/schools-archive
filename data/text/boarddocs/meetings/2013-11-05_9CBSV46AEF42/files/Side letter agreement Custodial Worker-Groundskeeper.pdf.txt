SIDE LETTER AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE
BOARD OF EDUCATION OF THE IRVINGTON UNION
FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (“DISTRICT” or “BOARD”)
AND THE CIVIL SERVICE EMPLOYEES ASSOCIATION,
INC., LOCAL 1000, AFSCME, AFL-CIO, IRVINGTON
UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT CUSTODIAL UNIT
(“ASSOCIATION”)

WHEREAS, Article II of the parties’ Collective Bargaining Agreement, dated July 1, 2010
through June 30, 2017 (“CBA”), contains a provision regarding the recognition of titles in the
Association; and

WHEREAS, said provision recognizes the Association as the exclusive negotiating agent for
the Custodial and Maintenance Staff members of the District; and

WHEREAS, the District has identified an operational need for a Custodial
Worker/Groundskeeper; and

WHEREAS, said title was newly created via resolution by the Board of Education, duly
passed, on October 8, 2013; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Education has determined that there is a community of interest
between said title and the Association; and

WHEREAS, notwithstanding the foregoing, said title has not yet been formally recognized
by the Board of Education as part of the Association; and

WHEREAS, the parties have engaged in collective bargaining with respect to the terms and
conditions of employment for said title.

NOW, THEREFORE, IT IS HEREBY STIPULATED AND AGREED BY THE
PARTIES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The Board of Education agrees to voluntarily recognize the Association as the
exclusive bargaining representative for the title of — Custodial
Worker/Groundskeeper, effective October 8, 2013, via passage of a formal
resolution, which resolution is attached hereto as Exhibit “A”.

2. The parties agree that the salary schedule(s) applicable to the title of Custodial
Worker/Groundskeeper is/are attached hereto as Exhibit “B”.

3. The job description for the title of Custodial Worker/Groundskeeper, as
promulgated by the Westchester County Department of Civil Service, is attached
hereto as Exhibit “C” for informational purposes.

4, The parties agree that all terms and conditions of employment for Association
members, as set forth in the CBA, shall be applicable to Custodial
Worker/Groundskeeper employees.

CUSTODIAL WORKER/GROUNDSKEEPER

GENERAL STATEMENT OF DUTIES: Performs building cleaning, maintenance and minor repair tasks,
as well as maintenance of school district grounds; does related work as required.

DISTINGUISHING FEATURES OF THE CLASS: Under direct supervision, the duties of this class involve
the efficient and economical performance of building cleaning, which may involve the performance of
minor repair and maintenance tasks. In addition, incumbents of this position perform routine work in the
maintenance of school grounds. Supervision may be exercised over Maintenance Laborers, Laborers
and Cleaners.

EXAMPLES OF WORK:: (Illustrative Only)

When assigned to Custodial Duties:

‘Sweeps, mops and waxes floors; washes walls and windows, dusts and performs other cleaning duties;
Dusts desks, woodwork, furniture and other equipment;

Cleans and mops lavatories and locker rooms and replaces soap or towels;

Empties wastebaskets and collects and disposes of refuse;

Moves and arranges chairs, tables and other furniture or equipment;

Maintains cleanliness and sanitation of swimming pool by checking temperature, chlorine levels,
vacuuming;

Assists with heavy work in kitchen and cafeteria;
Performs a variety of errands and related custodial tasks;

May make minor repairs to furniture, electrical fixtures, windows and shades; locks, faucets, heating
system and other equipment.

May undertake routine maintenance tasks related to building operating systems, i.e.; changing filters, etc.;

When assigned to Groundskeeping activities:

Mows, reseeds, rolls and fertilizes lawns and athletic fields;
Cares for flowerbeds;

Rakes leaves;

Trims hedges, trees and shrubs;

Operates a variety of grounds maintenance equipment;

EXAMPLES OF WORK: ((llustrative Only)

REQUIRED KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS, ABILITIES AND ATTRIBUTES: Working knowledge of building
cleaning practices, supplies and equipment and the ability to use them economically and efficiently;
working knowledge of the accepted procedures and practices of grounds maint enance work; familiarity
with the operation and maintenance of heating systems; ability to make minor plumbing, electrical,
carpentry, and mechanical repairs and perform a variety of routine maintenance tasks; ability to
understand and carry out simple oral and written directions; willingness to perform custodial and other
manual tasks; willingness to work outside during inclement weather; thoroughness; dependability;
physical condition commensurate with the demands of the position

Either: (a) one (1) year experience in
cleaning; or (b) one (1) year experience in grounds maintenance work; or (c) a satisfactory equivalent
combination of the foregoing experience sufficient to indicate the ability to do the job.

SPECIAL REQUIREMENT FOR APPOINTMENT IN SCHOOL DISTRICTS:

In accordance with the Safe Schools Against Violence in Education (SAVE) legislation, Chapter
180 of the Laws of 2000, and by the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education, candidates
for appointment in schoo! districts must obtain clearance for employment from the State
Education Department prior to employment based upon a fingerprint and criminal history

background check.
