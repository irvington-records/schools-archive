     1) State the specific classroom/school needs your project addresses. How does the proposed
        project build on eXisting programs? Please include how this project incorporates the
        curriculum and is linked to State and/or national standards.
         At the current time, we do not have a local weather station that students in the
         school district can use to access local weather data. The Weather Station and
         WeatherBug Schools Program from Earth Networks will support STEM
         initiatives in a variety of ways, ultimately putting authentic, real-time data at
         everyone's fingertips.

        There are three major components to this grant, the Weather Station, the
        WeatherBug Achieve web-based program and an Online Weather Center.
        The implementation of WeatherBug will begin with the WeatherBug Standard
        Package but in the future will have the ability to add two very important
        components to this system, the High-Definition Camera and the Lightning
        safety solution.

        Currently grade six science, grade eight Earth Science and high school Earth
        Science teach components of weather and climate. In planning for the future,
        the WeatherBug Program has teaching implications to the fol/owing Next

2) Describe your project in detail. Please be specific, including how students will engage in the
   activity and how you will measure whether or not this project has been successful. Indicate
   methods you will use to accomplish and publicize your project.
   The cost of the WeatherBug Schools Program includes three major
   components:
   a) a weather station - that will be installed on our IMSIIHS campus rooftop
   b) a Web-based program that displays real-time weather data collected from
      the weather station along with interactive tools and lessons
   c) the costs of installing and setting up the WeatherBug Schools Program
      (installing the weather station on the roof and connecting it to WeatherBug
      Network Appliance.)
   The WeatherBug Standard Package includes:
   a) a state-of-the-art weather station that tracks 27 different parameters from
      our campus rooftop in real-time
   b) the WeatherBug Network Appliance - an IP enabled device that connects
      easily to the Internet, provides fast transmission of the data from our
      weather station, has a 72-hour battery life and automatically reboots as
      needed
   c) all necessary cabling and connections
   d) LIFETIME site-license to WeatherBug Achieve, an award-winning,
      Web-based program that incorporates live weather data into dynamic and
      interactive tools and lessons
   e) Interactive tools and activities - These include the LCD Display Unit, the
      Online Weather Center and the Safety Alerting Tool.

   Once the system is installed and up and running, every teacher and student in
   our school district will have immediate access to WeatherBug Achieve and the
   Online Weather Center. I have spoken to Gary Knowles and Steve Amy and
   have been assured that our facilities and technology network can
   accommodate the weather station and WeatherBug Achieve (web-based
   instructional program). Part of the grant request covers the costs for the
   installation and set-up of the system by WeatherBug to assure that we can
   take advantage of the program benefits immediately.

   As soon as the grant is approved, we can begin to share the program benefits
   with administrators, stUdents and families in the community by sharing some
   of the applications and websites.

3) Describe how your project will benefit students.
   There are a number of benefits that the WeatherBug Schools Program will
   provide:

   a) it will enable students to keep track of local weather and compare and
   contrast the data to other WeatherBug Schools. There are about 8,000 other
   weather stations that data can be accessed from locally and nationally.
   b) it provides students with access to weather alerts, weather forecasts, radar,
      camera images and historical observations via a WeatherBug hosted page,
      which is customized for our school district
   c) it provides examples of how technology and engineering are used in the
      study of weather and climate
   d) it stimulates and engages students with a Web-based program that
     incorporates live weather data into dynamic and interactive tools and
     lessons

Will the project be continued at the end of this grant? If so how will it be funded?
   We will have a lifetime site-license to the WeatherBug Achieve software
   curriculum and access to 8,000 Weather Stations and 1,200 cameras located at
   schools nationwide.

    Although there are no additional costs once the system is installed and up and
    running, there are two additional components that we might want to consider
    adding/purchasing in the future. One is the WeatherBug Total Lightning
    Solution which includes a sensor that connects to the existing weather station
    and provides advanced notification of lightning as a result of the detection of
    in-cloud and cloud to ground lightning. The other is a high-definition camera
    that can IIbring our weather station data to life". The costs of adding both of
    these components is between $6,500 and $8,000 depending upon the type of
    camera package.

4) If your project will result in a product, what will become of it?
   Once the weather station is installed and connected to our technology
   network, a customized WeatherBug hosted Web-based Online Weather Center
   that is set-up by WeatherBug will be created. We will have a lifetime site-
   license to this software and weather system data providing an exciting,
   real world learning opportunities for the students.

5) Will volunteers be required to implement this program? What plan do you have to recruit
   these volunteers? Will you require help?
   No volunteers are needed to implement the program because we are paying to
   have the equipment installed and set-up.
   The technology department will be able to help trouble-shoot any problems wit
   the support of the WeatherBug.
