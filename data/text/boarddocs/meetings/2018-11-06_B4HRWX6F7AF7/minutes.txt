3. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Report: 3.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison noted that he, Dr. Kor and Ms. Stein attended the High School Drama Club performance of Radium Girls on November 2. He also acknowledged the IFA sponsored "Dinner and a Show" event on November 1 which was a great success. Seniors from the community were treated to dinner and the show. The event was well received by all and there is already talk of doing it again next year. Dr. Harrison acknowledged the outstanding performance and the event overall and thanked the students, volunteers and staff for a great evening.

Dr. Harrison acknowledged the combined Sleepy Hollow-Hastings-Edgemont-Irvington (SHEDI) swim team that won the sectional championship title at Felix Festa Middle School on November 1. The girls volleyball team made it to the sectional semi-finals, losing 3-1 to Ardsley -- a tremendous season for the team overall. The Irvington boys cross-country team captured the Class C Sectional Championship title on November 3, with 7 runners placing in the top 30 out of 130 overall. The girls cross-country team placed fifth in Class C. Dr. Harrison extended congratulations to all of our fall student athletes and noted that the Board will acknowledge them at a future Board meeting.

Information: 3.2 Committee Reports

B&G: Maria Kashkin reported that the committee met on October 23, with representatives from H2M (architectural firm), and they prioritized the list of potential projects for the Capital Bond and reviewed cost estimates for each. The committee prepared a list of questions for H2M and Triton (construction manager). The B&G committee will meet again this Thursday (November 8). There will be a presentation to the Board after they have all the needed details. Deborah Hargraves added that the committee worked with Administrators on a very long list and prioritized the top 14 items.

Communications: Maura Gedid reported that the focus of the meeting on October 24 was how to support the Bond proposal with Communications to the public. The committee is currently working on developing a plan of action as well as the timing for each action, including utilizing the website effectively and the scheduling of public forums. She added that the committee received updates from the administration related to the ongoing enhancements to the website.

Curriculum: David Graeber noted that the committee met on October 31 and discussed 3 key items, 1) Elementary World Language Planning overview  2) Amplify (K-8 Science program currently being piloted) and 3) Debrief on the October 23 technology presentation. Regarding the Elementary World Language Planning overview, David noted that a committee of 15, including Dr. Kor and teachers from all elementary grade levels, have been engaged to inform the process. Dr. Kor will give an update on this at a future meeting. The Amplify training for K-1 teachers was completed earlier, middle school was completed last week, and there will be additional updates from Dr. Kor forthcoming.

Policy: Deborah Hargraves noted that the committee continues its work on the 4000 series, with only a few policies to review in that series. The objective of this committee is to get through the entire policy manual by the end of the school year. Dr. Harrison noted that the committee is aiming for a first read of the 4000 Policy series in December.

Liaison Reports:

Community Advisory Board:  Maria Kashkin noted that a very engaged group of students from both the Middle School and High School met and discussed the Homecoming event which included getting pledges from students to make better choices. Students discussed having tables at Homecoming to promote different events. Mrs. Kashkin also reported that the Irvington Village drug take-back day took in over 8 pounds of medication. The Irvington Police Department runs this program every 3 months, but there is a permanent receptacle at the police station where unused medication can be brought for safe disposal at any time.

District Safety: Dr. Harrison noted that the committee spoke about district safety initiatives in regard to the Capital Project and the District's ongoing efforts to make schools safer. The committee discussed the current approach to make both student and staff safety training and drills more thorough and effective.

Food Service/Wellness -- Carol Stein noted that the full serve offering at Dows is going well and is being closely monitored. Ms. Stein noted that the consultant has been helpful in advising what to look for regarding food selection and content to ensure compliance with the contract and federal standards. The recycling initiative is also going well throughout the District.

Michael Hanna thanked those Board members who participated in the recent NYSSBA Convention and Education Expo and noted that Dr. Harrison and Jesse Lubinsky gave a Presentation on Student Agency that was very well received. Dr. Harrison reported that a number of Irvington students were at the convention representing the Education Foundation Innovation Fund and they did an outstanding job. Already, the District has received about a dozen inquiries from other schools about the Innovation Fund concept, the projects they're working on, and other information so they can initiate opportunities in their schools. Dr. Harrison noted that this is a point of pride for our students and our District.
