	Discussions

	Michael Hanna noted that the cost of removal of the modular storage units that are located behind Dows Lane wasn’t part of the Bond but wonders if now is an opportunity to have them removed which would benefit the art classroom that faces the modular. He also noted that comments have been made to him by neighbors. Ms. Miller noted that cost would be an issue as their removal isn’t a budgeted item and serve as 1/2 garage for equipment and the other as storage space for the PTSA. She also noted that there was another unit behind Dows Lane but was moved near East Field and is utilized as storage for outdoor athletic equipment as well as building & grounds equipment. However, the units are not climate controlled and therefore condensation is causing the equipment to rust and mold.

	Dr. Harrison stated that the objective for the upcoming budget would be to include funds to erect a metal garage which would need SED approval. The estimated cost would be between $20,000-30,000. Mr. Grados felt that it would be worth the cost if this would get rid of the unsightly units and Mr. Montgomery asked if the Board could resolve finding the money and move forward with SED approval. Dr. Harrison also noted that the architects and engineers would need to be involved.  He also noted that PTSA would need storage for their supplies.

	Mr. Whitney also reported that Nina Dawson, teacher and Village Historian, contacted him asking if the Board wanted to sponsor an ad for a brochure that the Historical Society is producing. Mr. Whitney thought $50 per member. Mr. Montgomery noted that he isn’t thrilled being asked for such a donation. Other members agreed to the donation of $50.

	Dr. Harrison reported that he will be sending the Board a copy of the proposal of the firm to handle the security analysis/review of our computer network.
