Minutes of the March 8, 2019 Board of Education Meeting

Special Meeting (Friday, March 8, 2019)

Members present

Michael Hanna, Deborah Hargraves, David Graeber, Maura Gedid, Brian Friedman, Beth Propper

Also present Dr. Kristopher Harrison, Dr. Raina Kor and Carol Stein.

Meeting called to order at 8:05 AM

1. Call to Order and Pledge of Allegiance

Procedural:  1.1 Call to Order

Michael Hanna called the meeting to order at 8:05 am

Procedural: 1.2 Pledge of Allegiance

The Pledge of Allegiance was recited.

2. Acceptance of Agenda

Action:  2.1 Acceptance of Agenda

Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education accept the agenda for the March 8, 2019 Board Meeting as presented.

Motion by Deborah Hargraves, second by Brian Friedman.

Motion Carries

Yes: Michael Hanna, Deborah Hargraves, David Graeber, Maura Gedid, Brian Friedman, Beth Propper

3. Business of the Board

Action:  3.1 Independent Audit Agreement

BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education herewith authorizes the Board President to execute an agreement between the Board of Education and PCS Leadership Inc. to conduct an independent audit of the District’s Human Resources practices and procedures.

Motion by Deborah Hargraves, second by Maura Gedid.

Motion Carries

Yes: Michael Hanna, Deborah Hargraves, David Graeber, Maura Gedid, Brian Friedman, Beth Propper

4. Next Public Meeting

Information:  4.1 Meeting Notification

The next meeting of the Irvington Board of Education will be on Wednesday, March 13 at 7:15 p.m. in the Campus Presentation Room.

5. Adjournment

Action:  5.1 Motion to Adjourn

Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education adjourn the March 8, 2019 Special Meeting.

Motion by David Graeber, second by Maura Gedid.

Final Resolution: Motion Carries

Yes: Michael Hanna, Deborah Hargraves, David Graeber, Maura Gedid, Brian Friedman, Beth Propper

Submitted by Doreen Morandi, District Clerk, Pro Tem
