IUFSD Athletics Overview
       January 24, 2017

Philosophy of Athletic Department
 The Irvington Athletic Department believes that participation in athletics’ is an integral part of
 education. Our goal is to promote and ensure the value of education, school spirit, and fair play.
 Our program is designed to meet the needs of students who seek a variety of experiences that
 will foster the growth of positive student behaviors and relationships while providing life lessons
 in sportsmanship, teamwork and competition. While we take great pride in our achievements,
 we do not condone successes that compromise sportsmanship and student wellness.

Section One and BOCES
● We are a member of Section One and pay dues to
  BOCES based on our programs.
● Conferences and Leagues are developed by Section
  One.
● Schedules are based on school size, geography, and
  sometimes ability.
● Officials are assigned to our games though BOCES.
● Conference Alignment changes based on BEDS each
  year.

Overview of Coaching Staff
● 56 Teams/58 Coaches
● 60% of coaches are not Irvington School District Staff
● Coaching requirements- Fingerprinting/CPR/First
  Aid/Concussion/Coaching Courses
● Hiring Process- Coaches are appointed each season
● Assistant Coaches- 5 varsity teams and 2 modified
  teams with Asst Coaches
● Stipends tied to the teachers contract

Modified Sports
● Participation is the goal, but it is not a
  guarantee that all playing time will be equal.
● Skill development should be stressed.
● Team strategies are developed.
● Commitment-weekdays after school no
  weekends or holiday vacations.
● No playoffs

Junior Varsity Sports
● More competitive than modified and playing
  time is not structured the same as modified.
● Skill development stressed
● Team strategies continue to be developed.
● Commitment-practice time longer and more
  games than modified.
● Preparing athletes for the varsity level.
● No playoffs

Varsity Sports
● Commitment at this level is even higher than
  lower levels- off season workouts and longer
  season.
● Develop skills to enhance individual and team
  performance.
● Quantity of playing time is not guaranteed.
● Preparing some for play at the collegiate level
● Tournaments, League, Sectional, Regional, &
  State Championships are goals.

Tryouts
● Depending on the number of athletes out for a
  particular team, cuts may be necessary at any level.
  ○ However, there is non-cut sport each season.
● Tryout process will take place at the beginning of the
  pre-season with a guarantee of at least a three-day
  tryout period.
● Roster sizes vary depending on the sport and level.
● Process being developed by committee to outline
  practices and to ensure transparency

Athletic Placement Process (APP)
●   NYSPHSAA process that allows 7th and 8th graders to tryout for the high
    school teams.
●   Steps that are involved-

        No cuts on the HS team they are trying out for

        Maturity screening with appropriate tanner score approved by the school MD
        Physical Fitness test
        Tryout period

●   If a student passes the APP test but does not make the high school team,
    the student is still eligible for modified sports.
●   If a student is placed on the HS team, playing time should be appropriate.

Team Practice Details
●   Varsity and JV Practice weekdays,weekends, holiday breaks; Modified
    practices only weekday, no weekends or holiday breaks.
●   High school practice will range from 90 minutes to 2 hours; modified
    practices should be no longer than 90 minutes.
●   Practice schedules are flexible and should be communicated by the coach
    on a weekly basis. Schedules will be set based on available facilities which
    may depend on game schedules of other teams.
●   Practice locations can be at any one of the district fields or gyms as well or
    at one of the village fields. Some sports have practice locations at off site
    venues ( swimming and ice hockey).
●   Coaches should be communicating practice expectations at the beginning
    of each season.

Medical Clearance
● FamilyID- Online registration of emergency and medical
  information.
● Information letter and directions distributed each season.
● Physicals are valid for one year .
● All information required for clearance will be reviewed by the nurse
  and school doctor.
● Parents will be able to check to see if information was received and
  student is cleared.
● Students are not eligible to begin practice until forms are in and
  have been approved by the medical staff.

Athletic Trainer
● Contracted service
● Works each weekday from 2pm until last contest or
  practice is completed
● Works weekends as per game and practice schedule
● Member of the Concussion Management team and
  administers baseline and post injury concussion tests;
  manages the Return to Play concussion protocol.
● Maintains constant communication with the
  school nurses, coaches, parents, and athletes.

Concussion Testing and Protocols
●   Concussion testing is given to all HS athletes.
●   Permission for concussion testing will be included in the online registration
    process.
●   IMPACT baseline tests will be done for all sports.
●   Sideline concussion pre-assessments will be done in addition in contact
    sports.
●   Testing will be done by the Athletic Trainer at the beginning of the
    pre-season practices.
●   The Concussion Management team will meet to address concerns or
    issues with testing protocols.
●   The Return To Play protocols will be followed once an athlete has been
    cleared by the school MD.
