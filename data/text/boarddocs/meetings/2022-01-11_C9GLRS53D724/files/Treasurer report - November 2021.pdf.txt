Irvington Union Free School District
Monthly Financial Highlights

November 2021

Cash Balance

Our November cash position was $27.50 million, which was approximately $4.53 million higher than November 2020.

Cash received in the Real Property & Other Taxes category included 3" quarter 2021 sales tax totaling $198,513 from Westchester County and
a tax payment from the Town of Greenburgh for tax collection during the month of October in the amount of $850,867.

State and Federal Aid Cash Receipts included $76,644 of payments for federal and state school lunch reimbursement and $13,991 for VLT
Lottery aid.

Revenue:

General Fund Revenue through Novembet was $63,949,456 or 96.1% of the budgeted amount.

State Aid revenue recorded through November was $2,835,741. This includes $2,541,623 of this amount recorded entry against our TRS
payment due for 2020-21. As a reminder, the State deducts our aid payment against the amount due to TRS. No cash is received by the
District, however, this transaction is recognized as revenue. Any remaining amount due to TRS is paid by the District directly.

Miscellaneous sources category included E-rate reimbursements for prior year in the amount of $106,339. E-rate reimbursement amounts
depend on prior year expenses for certain telecommunications and internet purchases.

Expenditures:

Actual General Fund Expenses totaled approximately $4.79 million for November. This brings the total year to date expenditures to
$19,776,533. Including outstanding encumbrances, 8% of the budget remains.

The tax certiorari line is negative based on payments made from existing settlements. We will plan to have a budget amendment on a future
agenda to fund this account code from the tax certiorari reserve to correct this negative amount.
