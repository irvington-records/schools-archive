Phase 2 bids - Award Process

❖   Triton Construction/H2M Architects met with all low bidders to review their
    bids and ensure the contractor could meet the project specifications
❖   Each bid package was reviewed for completeness and adherence to the bid
    specifications
❖   Based on these reviews, Triton makes recommendations to award bids
❖   B&G Committee reviewed bids and alternate prices to determine we would
    remain in the voter approved capital project amount of $18,867,640.
