5. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Information: 5.1 District Updates, News, Events

COVID Updates

Dr. Harrison said the "Test to Stay" program is up and running for unvaccinated students who have been determined to be a close contact of a confirmed COVD case. The District is also conducting the weekly surveillance testing. The District anticipates new guidance from Westchester County Department of Health regarding a reduction in insolation and quarantine times and changes in contact tracing. All updates will be communicated to the Irvington UFSD school community. Dr. Harrison is working to facilitate another clinic for boosters and first time vaccinations for everyone in the Irvington UFSD school community. Westchester County is now testing and giving vaccinations at the Westchester County Center. He said the days and times are listed on the Westchester County website and Facebook page and will be on the Irvington IUFSD Facebook page. Dr. Harrison thanked the entire school nursing team, administrators, school counselors and all of the teachers for their extensive effort to keep everyone safe.

- Main Street School held their Spelling Bee and the winners will be invited virtually to be recognized by the Board of Education.

- Irvington Senior, Brooke Dunefsky has been recognized in the Regeneron Science Talent Search. Brooke is one of 300 students out of over 1800 applicants that has been awarded a $2,000 scholarship. She will move onto the next round where they will pick 50 finalists that will complete for $1.8 million in scholarship money. This competition includes students from the United States, Puerto Rico and eight other nations. Dr. Harrison said he was very proud of our Science Research program students and faculty for the outstanding work they produce.

- On February 2nd, the District is hosting a Zoom based program with  iASK-CAB. This program will give the results of the most recent substance abuse survey. They will look for trends over time, assess risk factors and compare with national and local data. Parents can sign up for the Zoom based presentation via links in the District newsletter, secondary school newsletters and on District social media.

- The District will be celebrating Martin Luther King Day and Black History Month with activities and learning. In the elementary schools there will be a combination of classroom and school-wide activities, Secondary schools will participate in curriculum-based activities with teachers leading learning activities in the classrooms.

- Today, Dr. Gail Duffy and two of teacher leaders, Michael Berkowitz and Sara Rust co-led the second group of staff members in Culturally Responsive Education.  Currently the District is collaborating with a professional partner in this work and next year it will be an Irvington owned program.

- The Irvington UFSD Marching Band will play the National Anthem at the Mets game on Sunday, May 15, 2022. Tickets will be available for purchase through media resources.

Information: 5.2 Committee Reports

Buildings & Grounds Committee-Brian Friedman gave an update on Phase 1 & 2 Capital projects. The presentation can be found here.

Communications Committee-Susan Brunenavs said they worked on a process to respond to community emails sent to the Board of Education.

CAB-Jayne Wissner said they talked about the substance abuse survey results. There was a successful mini De-Stress Fest held and there would be another one in the Spring. The spotlight was a on community member George Diaz of Geordanes. He spoke about the history of Geordanes. They will be spotlighting an Irvington community member every week. They working on a campaign to bring the community together. They are going to come up with a slogan, lawn signs, keychains, etc.

Village of Irvington-David Graeber welcomed Ms. Burgos and Mr. Bard as new Village Trustees. Mr. Bard will be the new liaison for the Village of Irvington.
