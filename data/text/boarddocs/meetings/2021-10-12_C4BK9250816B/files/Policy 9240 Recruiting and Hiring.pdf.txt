RECRUITING AND HIRING

NEW NOTE: We have provided optional text to emphasize the district’s commitment to a diverse
and culturally competent staff.
OLD NOTE: This policy addresses recruiting and hiring practices generally. Please make sure
to customize where appropriate, especially where indicated by the [bold, bracketed] text. This
policy reflects the Commissioners Regulations regarding obtaining § 211 waivers for employment,
with language specifying the good faith effort that boards must undertake when filling open

The Board of Education believes that the quality of the district’s employees in large part
determines the quality of the education offered to the district’s students. As the employer for the
school district, the Board will provide and maintain qualified and certified instructional and
support personnel to carry out the educational programs of the district. The Board is committed
to working to build and sustain an equitable and inclusive work environment where cultural
diversity is celebrated and valued. We believe diversity benefits and enriches the development of
all of our students, staff, and faculty.                                                                 Formatted: Font: 14 pt

The Assistant Superintendent for Instruction and Human Resources shall implement and
maintain a high-quality recruiting and hiring program to attract, secure and retain the best-qualified
staff to meet the needs of students and the district. The District has developed and shall maintain
comprehensive guidelines for the hiring and recruitment of employees. This document,
Administrative Guidelines for Recruitment and Selection of Certified Staff, shall be implemented
for all certified personnel and will be updated as needed by the administration. Should material
changes be necessary, they will be presented to the Board of Education for informational purposes.


New or Revised Positions

The Assistant Superintendent for Instruction and Human Resources will develop
recommended qualifications for all new positions in the district and review the qualifications for
all existing positions as necessary. The Superintendent must approve all recommended
qualifications for all new and existing positions. The Board must approve the qualifications for all
new positions in the district and revisions of the qualifications for existing positions.

The Assistant Superintendent for Instruction and Human Resources shall refer all proposals
for the creation or reclassification of all unclassified (non-instructional) positions and a statement
of the duties for these positions to the Westchester County civil service authority for
classification.

The Assistant Superintendent for Instruction and Human Resources shall develop job
descriptions that incorporate the qualifications and job duties for all positions in the school district.
The Superintendent must approve the job descriptions for all positions in the district.

Recruiting

The district will seek the most qualified candidates for vacant positions by recruiting from a variety
of sources, including present staff. District employees may apply for all positions for which they
meet the certification and other stated qualifications.

NEW NOTE: We have modified the language of the first sentence to better mirror policy 0100,
Non-Discrimination and Equal Opportunity, and have added the second optional sentence to
emphasize a commitment to recruiting, hiring and retaining a diverse and culturally competent
staff. Some strategies for increasing diversity in your workforce are to (1) recruit specifically at
schools and other venues known for high levels of diversity; (2) hire staff for one capacity and
work to develop their potential to fill other roles; and (3) identify current district students with the
potential to become future staff members and support them in that regard.

The Board and its employees will adhere to the practice of not discriminate in recruiting and hiring
personnel without regard to on the basis of age, color, creed, disability, weight, marital status,
national origin, race, religion, sexual orientation, gender (including gender identity and gender
expression), sex or any other status protected by federal or state law. The Board is also committed
to recruiting, employing, supporting and retaining racially, ethnically and linguistically diverse –
as well as culturally competent – administrative, instructional, and support personnel.


NOTE: The text below addresses the previously noted Commissioner’s Regulation.


The Board will practice due diligence in finding the most qualified persons for any
open positions. The Board recognizes that some specialized positions are difficult to
fill because of shortages of qualified candidates. In rare instances, the Board may
need to hire a retired public employee, in which case it will follow the
Commissioner’s regulations for securing a §211 waiver. When recruiting for an
open position the Board will first undertake a thorough and good faith search for a
certified and qualified individual who will not require such a waiver for
employment.

Hiring

Through standard recruiting and hiring procedures, the Assistant Superintendent for Instruction
and Human Resources will ensure that candidates for district employment meet all the
qualifications set for the position sought. The district will comply with all the requirements of the
Education and Civil Service laws, including any fingerprinting requirements.

The Superintendent will recommend individuals for employment in the school district. The
Assistant Superintendent for Instruction and Human Resources will recommend individuals
for employment to the Superintendent. The Superintendent must recommend all individuals for
employment to the Board. The Board must approve all individuals to be employed by the school
district.
