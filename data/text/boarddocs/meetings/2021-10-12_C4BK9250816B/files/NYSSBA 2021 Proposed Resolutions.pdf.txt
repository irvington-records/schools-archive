Proposed Resolutions Recommended for Adoption
1.   Sunsetting Resolution: NYSSBA calls upon the state Legislature and the
     Governor to reform NYS education funding in accordance with the following
     tenets to ensure adequate resources to public schools in the state of NY by         S
     providing for: adequacy, equity, flexibility, predictability, and clarity.
     (NYSSBS Board of Directors)

2.   Sunsetting Resolution: NYSSBA supports proposals affecting public
     education that: Provide access to programs that prepare students to be
     college-ready; Achieve equity and adequacy in funding; Promote efficiency
     and cost-effectiveness; Advance high expectations for all students; Embrace         S
     innovative approaches and assessments; Foster community engagement and
     regional cooperation. (NYSSBA Board of Directors)
3.   Sunsetting Resolution: NYSSBA support proposals that would allow students
     with disabilities to be tested at their developmental age.                          S
     (NYSSBA Board of Directors)

4.   Sunsetting Resolution: NYSSBA support proposals that would increase state
     and federal reimbursements for school meals. (NYSSBA Board of Directors)            S
5.   NYSSBA support proposals that would generate new state revenues with the            S*
     dedicated purpose of funding education. (NYSSBA Board of Directors)              (group was
                                                                                        divided)
6.   NYSSBA support legislation to amend the education law to permit voters to
     obtain absentee ballots for school district elections and budget votes when
     scheduled on a day that coincides with a religious holiday and to continue to       S
     permit districts to alter the date of the election and budget vote if the
     uniform budget date falls on a religious holiday. (The Nassau BOCES Board)
7.   NYSSBA work with the Office of the State Comptroller & other policymakers
     to ensure that Comptroller audits are based on statutory guidelines or
     provisions of state law, rather than subjective standards and preferences.

     (The Newark School Board)

8.   NYSSBA supports updates to the Open Meetings Law to allow for School
     Board Trustees to attend meetings remotely via video conferencing without
     disclosing their location and without permitting public in-person access to an      S
     individual trustee’s remote location. (The Hendrick Hudson School Board)

9.   NYSSBA supports legislation to amend the Open Meetings Law to provide
     enhanced opportunities for a public body to conduct a business meeting
     using remote technology, authorize committees of a public body to meet                   S*
     through videoconferencing without a fixed location for the meeting,                   (group was
     eliminate the requirement to disclose the location of members attending                 divided)
     remotely and that the public can attend at those remote locations.
     (Croton-Harmon School Board and Albany City School Board)

10. NYSSBA supports the revision of the Child Safety Zone statute and
     implementing regulations of the State Education Department to establish a
     more flexible and meaningful set of criteria for the determination of a Child            S
     Safety Zone for which a school district may provide student transportation as
     an ordinary contingent expense. (Croton-Harmon School Board)
11. NYSSBA calls upon the state Legislature and the Governor to provide funding
     for assistance to school districts to allow them to make and implement
     decisions in the best interest of the global environment, including and                  S
     especially in facilities improvements, transportation, and upkeep.
     (Voorheesville School Board)

12. NYSSBA support legislation at the state and federal levels for their
     appropriate governmental agencies to assume the fiscal responsibility for
     student nutrition, shifting that financial burden away from public schools and
     thereby increasing the allocation of resources to enable all districts to                S
     provide nutritious meals and snacks for all students without incurring
     negative impacts upon their annual budgets. (Washingtonville School Board)
13. NYSSBA supports state legislation that evaluates the efficacy of, and requires
     use of accurate, recent data in future implementation of Foundation Aid.                 S
     (Shenendehowa School Board)

14. NYSSBA have as a legislative imperative, an addition to the New York State
     Public Health Law, that all immunizations required of students under the
     provisions of New York State Public Health Law 2164 be required of all school            S
     staff where medically appropriate for adults. (The Ulster BOCES Board)
15. NYSSBA supports state legislation to establish guidelines for school districts
     for identifying and capturing data and creating metrics for initiatives that             S*
     mitigate the damaging effects associated with climate change so as to more
                                                                                           (group was
     effectively communicate expectations and monitor outcomes when planning                 divided)
     and implementing school budgets. (Shenendehowa School Board)
16. NYSSBA support legislation that abolishes state school level expenditure
    reporting. (Washingtonville School Board)                                                 S
17. NYSSBA supports state aid eligibility for school districts that are obligated to
    pay tuition reimbursement for unilateral private placements. (Freeport School Board)      S

    Proposed Bylaw Amendment: The annual business meeting shall be held in
    person during the annual school board convention, unless the health and or
    welfare of the participants is in jeopardy. The membership shall be notified
    of the date, time and location of meeting, as determined by the Board of                   O
    Directors at least 6 months in advance. Said meeting shall be held not earlier
    than September 1st, nor later than November 30th of each year.
    (Nassau BOCES Board)

18. NYSSBA shall support laws and procedures that ensure teachers and school
    employee background checks are thorough, cover out of state
    incidents, have the ability to continually report incidents throughout an                  O
    employee’s career and also include psychological testing.
    (Newark School Board)

19. NYSSBA seek legislative and/or policy changes to streamline the teacher
    certification process for teachers from out of state and those with previous               S
    teaching experience. (Newark School Board)
20. NYSSBA support the proposed NY Health Act and any legislation at the state
    or federal level that provides single payer healthcare for all New Yorkers.                O
    (Onteora School Board)

21. NYSSBA support legislation that requires every child in NY State, aged 0-21,
    be covered for free under the Child Health Plus Program. (Onteora School Board)            O
22. NYSSBA support legislation which requires the State of New York to hold
    school districts harmless for employee and retiree health care increases that              O
    exceed the Consumer Price Index. (Onteora School Board)
23. NYSSBA support legislation that creates a process for tenure review and
    renewal occurring every five years throughout the career of all tenured
    public school employees. The process will include student, parent and                      O
    colleague feedback, and will not be driven by test scores, and is intended to
    be instructive, not punitive. (Onteora School Board)
24. NYSSBA should encourage laws, regulations and policies that promote
    alternatives for school districts when purchasing non-instructional services               S
    that do not give BOCES an unfair competitive advantage. (Ardsley School Board)
25. NYSSBA oppose proposals to allow school district voters to use non-excuse
    absentee ballots upon request, for school board elections and budget votes.                O
    (Washingtonville School Board)

       * Notes on items where consensus was divided:
       #5- Revenues that provide a net increase in education funding are welcome. Alternatively, there were fears of
       association with cannabis legalization or that new revenues will supplant current aid.
       #9- Additional flexibility in Open Meetings Law is welcome, especially the ability to have committees meet
       virtually. Alternatively there were concerns expressed about the long term consequences of virtual meetings.
       #15- Focus on environmental sustainability is positive. Alternatively, data collection and creating community
       expectations around climate change could create a new unfunded mandate.
