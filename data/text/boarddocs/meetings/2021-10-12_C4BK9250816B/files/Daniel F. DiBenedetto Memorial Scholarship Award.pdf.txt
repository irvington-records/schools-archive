Dear Members of the Irvington Board of Education,

1 am writing to seek your approval to establish an award at the high school in memory of my
brother, Daniel F. DiBenedetto, who recently passed away. Daniel was a member of the
Irvington High School Class of 1993. Daniel was an amazing person, who was extremely
devoted to his family, who put others before himself, and who was always willing to lend a
helping hand. While in high school, Daniel was a member of Varsity Athletes Against Substance
Abuse (VAASA), was a member of the Varsity Soccer and Baseball teams, and received the All
League Award for his soccer performance.

To honor Daniel, I would like to establish an award in his name at the high school called “the
Daniel F. DiBenedetto Memorial Award.” This award would recognize a graduating senior who
embodies many of the characteristics that defined Daniel. Accordingly, the recipient of the
award should be a scholar-athlete, should be a role model for fellow classmates and
underclassmen, should show kindness and empathy for people, and should exhibit good
citizenship, selflessness, and altruism. Preference should be given to a student in financial need.

I would like to have the first award be given at the end of the 2021-2022 school year. The award

would be $1,000 cash and, after Board approval, I will fund the award with $5,000, an amount
that will be sufficient for at least the next 5 years.

If/when approval is given, it would be greatly appreciated if you can provide me with an update
at the end of each school year that summarizes the recipient of the award, the college that the

recipient will be attending, the recipient’s intended major, and the balance in the fund.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I look forward to hearing from you regarding the
approval of this award.

Sincerely,

Anthony DiBenedetto
