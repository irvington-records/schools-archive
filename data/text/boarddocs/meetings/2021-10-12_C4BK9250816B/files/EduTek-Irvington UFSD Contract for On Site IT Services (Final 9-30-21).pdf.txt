PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Working with the Irvington UFSD (District), Edu Tek Limited (Edu Tek) will provide comprehensive
Information Technology (IT) services at each school operated by the District (collectively referred to herein
as the Sites).

This Agreement shall start on October 1, 2021 and end on June 30, 2022, unless terminated sooner in
accordance with the provisions of this Agreement. Edu Tek will support the District’s Local Area Networks
(LAN’s) and Wide Area Network (WAN’s). This service includes support for all operating systems currently
in use at all Sites. The IT support described will be performed by:
        •       2.0 On-site Systems Engineer(s) Level I
        •       1.0 On-site Systems Engineer(s) Level II
        •       1.0 On-site Data Engineer(s) Level I
        On-site Project Operations Manager services not to exceed twenty (20) days per year.

SCOPE OF SERVICES
The scope of services contained in this agreement includes but is not limited to the following:

Technology Management
        •   Make recommendations in the development of the District technology plan.
        •   Review technology budget proposal and service contracts for alignment with plan
            recommendations.
        •   Assist in budget development by providing analysis of District’s networking needs and
            recommending hardware and network software to align with District needs and goals.
        •   Develop criteria and attend to details of annual technology deployment plan.
        •   Assist in the procurement of technology equipment utilizing available vendors in accordance
            with State bids as part of the annual deployment plan.
        •   Meet with the District’s data consultant teams to discuss technology requirements for District
            participation in data warehousing.
        •   Assist in developing, implementing and supporting solutions as per the District Technology
            Plan for NIST/WISP/CSOP/SAN/Disaster Recovery Systems, alternative email solutions and
            compliance with NYS electronic records retention policies.
        •   Support technology needs of Board of Education meeting

Inventory Management

        The assigned Edu Tek staff will update the inventory of the District’s technology equipment during
        the term of the Agreement in accordance with the District’s inventory procedures set forth in
        Exhibit “A.” While Edu Tek’s team will make updates to and manage the inventory as set forth in
        this Agreement to provide assistance for asset management and the maintenance of an accurate
        inventory, Edu Tek will not be responsible for the use of equipment by the District’s personnel
        and students.

System Support & Maintenance
        •   Install and support computer equipment, including computing devices (e.g. desktops,
            Chromebooks, laptops) and tablets, utilized by the District’s students and employees.

        •   Troubleshoot computer hardware issues.
        •   Maintain current device image library for machine duplication or utilize Microsoft
            SCCM/InTune.

            management tools.
        •   Troubleshoot printing issues.
        •   Install and configure ancillary computer hardware (i.e. printers, scanners, keyboards, mice,

Confidentiality and Data Security and Privacy Standards:
            a. Edu Tek agrees, on behalf of itself and its employees, and/or agents that, in the course of
               performing services, it may have access to information that is deemed confidential
               information under New York Education Law Section 2-d, for example, teacher or principal
               data and student data. Edu Tek, its employees, and/or agents shall not use, publish,
               discuss, disclose or communicate the contents of such information, directly or indirectly
               with third parties, except as provided for in this Agreement. Edu Tek further agrees that
               any information received by Edu Tek, its employees, and/or agents during the course of
               the services provided pursuant to this Agreement which concerns the personal, financial,
               or other affairs of District, its employees, agents, clients, and/or students will be treated
               by Edu Tek, its employees, and/or agents in full confidence and will not be revealed to
               any other persons, firms, or organizations.
            b. Edu Tek acknowledges that it may receive and/or come into contact with personally
               identifiable information, as defined by New York Education Law Section 2-d, from records
               maintained by District that directly relate to a student(s) (hereinafter referred to as
               “education record”). Edu Tek shall maintain reasonable administrative, technical and
               physical safeguards to protect the security, confidentiality and integrity of personally
               identifiable student information in its custody in accordance with applicable laws and
               regulations, and understands and agrees that it is responsible for complying with state
               data security and privacy standards for all personally identifiable information from
               education records, as and to the extent applicable to Edu Tek.
            c. It is anticipated that education records will be stored only in District systems (and not in
               Edu Tek’s custody), unless required for performance of services or as requested by the
               District. To the extent education records are in Edu Tek’s custody, it shall:
                     i. limit internal access to education records to those individuals that are determined

         ii. not use the education records for any purposes other than those explicitly

        iv. use encryption technology to protect data while in motion or in its custody from

   Law Section 2-d, Edu Tek as a third party contractor is responsible for providing the
   District with supplemental information to be included in the District’s Parents’ Bill of

         ii. how Edu Tek will ensure that subcontractors, persons or entities to whom Edu

        iii. that student data in Edu Tek’s possession will be returned or destroyed upon
             expiration of the Agreement;
        iv. if and how a parent, student, or eligible student may challenge the accuracy of
            the student data that is collected; and
         v. where the student data will be stored (described in such a manner as to protect
            data security), and the security protections taken to ensure such data will be
            protected and privacy risks mitigated.

     standards provision and unauthorized release of student data, Edu Tek shall immediately
     notify the District and advise it as to the nature of the breach and steps Edu Tek has taken
     to minimize said breach. Said notification must be made within seven (7) days of the
     breach. In the case notification to a parent or eligible student is required by applicable
     law, Edu Tek shall promptly reimburse the District for the cost of such notification.
g. In the event that Edu Tek fails to notify the District of a breach, said failure shall be
   punishable by a civil penalty of the greater of $5,000 or up to $10 per student, teacher
   and principal whose data was released, provided that the maximum penalty imposed shall
   not exceed the maximum penalty imposed under General Business Law, section 899-
   aa(6)(a).

Confidentiality Training
Edu Tek acknowledges that federal and state laws protect the confidentiality of personally identifiable
information of the District’s students as well as its teachers and principals. Edu Tek represents and
warrants that any officers, directors, employees or agents of Edu Tek, who will have access to student,
teacher and/or principal data of the District, have received or will receive training on the federal and state
laws governing confidentiality of such data prior to obtaining access to such data.
Compliance with Law/Fingerprinting
Edu Tek shall comply, at its own cost and expense, with all applicable Federal, State and local statutes,
rules, regulations and ordinances including, but not limited to, the New York State Safe Schools Against
Violence in Education (SAVE) legislation, the Federal Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (“FERPA”)
and Section 2-d of the New York State Education Law (which concerns privacy and security of student,
teacher and principal data). Edu Tek shall adhere to all requirements and protocols as established by the
District and the New York State Education Department applicable to the services to be provided under
this Agreement. Edu Tek also acknowledges and agrees that its officers, directors and employees
performing on-site services for the District under this Agreement are subject to fingerprinting and a
criminal history record check as required by the SAVE legislation and other sections of the Education Law
of the State of New York. Appropriate written proof of such clearance shall be provided to the District
prior to such individuals providing services to the District. Edu Tek agrees to cooperate with the District
and to complete any and all necessary forms or procedures to obtain required fingerprinting and criminal
history checks, all at no additional cost or expense to the District. In the event that Edu Tek utilizes an
individual who is required to and who has not obtained fingerprinting clearance with the New York State
Education Department, the District shall have the right to immediately terminate this Agreement.
