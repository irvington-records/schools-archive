5. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Information: 5.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison gave an update on surveillance testing that will begin this week. All individuals that signed up will be tested this week. He also gave an update on Homecoming. John Buonamano is working to find an opponent for either a night or day game.

He said at the October 26, 2021 meeting, the Board of Education Trustees will be recognized by various community partners for their dedication to the students of Irvington UFSD.

Dr. Duffy said that the District’s Twitter account will be up and running soon. It will highlight and celebrate the work of the District students. The Twitter handle will be @IrvingtonUFSD.

Ms. Stein gave a Capital Project update. She said fans were able to sit on the new bleachers during recent competitions and the railings have been installed at the Campus Learning Pavilion. She said the Dows Gym floor installation is moving forward and will be completed by the end of October. Other projects are being completed as materials become available.

Buildings & Grounds

Brian Friedman said that B & G committee will be working with the Commemorate Irvington  committee for placement of the enslaved  Africans memorial at the Main Street School. He also said bids will be going out next month for Phase II of Capital Projects.

Beth Propper gave an update on what the Communications Committee has been working on. She said they have been working on “health”, or COVID-Related, communications to the community and reviewing the website for accessibility.

11. Business of the Board II

Action (Consent): 11.1 Establishment of the Daniel F. DiBenedetto Memorial Scholarship Award

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District administers the Irvington Union Free School District; and WHEREAS, Anthony DiBenedetto, hereinafter referred to as the "Donor" desires to make a gift to the Board of Education in order to create a scholarship fund, to be known as the "Daniel F. DiBenedetto Memorial Scholarship Award"; and WHEREAS Education Law 1709(12-a) and 2503 (1) authorizes the Board of Education: to take and hold in trust for the purpose of awarding scholarships in [the) schools any real estate transferred to it by gift, grant, bequest or devise, or any gift, legacy or annuity, of whatever kind, given or bequeathed to [the] board and apply the same, or the interest and proceeds thereof, according to the instructions of the donor or testator. NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District hereby creates the above-named Scholarship Fund, as set forth below, based upon the understanding between the parties: The Daniel F. DiBenedetto Memorial Scholarship Award 1. The Donor, Anthony DiBenedetto shall deliver to the Board, an initial amount of $5,000 for deposit in the Special Revenue Scholarship account in the bank designated by the Board of Education. 2. The Scholarship Fund may be supplemented at any time by any donor(s) wishing to gift, grant, bequest or devise cash, cash equivalent or other property acceptable to the District or through any gift, legacy or annuity, of whatever kind, given, or bequeathed to the District for such purpose. 3. The scholarship shall be named the Daniel F. DiBenedetto Memorial Scholarship Award. 4. The memorial scholarship will be awarded annually in the amount of $1,000 to one graduating Irvington High School student. The student should be a scholar-athlete, a role model for fellow classmates and underclassmen, show kindness and empathy for people and should exhibit good citizenship, selflessness and altruism. Preference should be given to a student in financial need. 5. The funds allocated to said scholarship shall come from donations by any party at anytime which may be used to increase the scholarship or held for future recipients. The scholarship is intended to be funded at a minimum of $1,000 per year until funds no longer exist to fulfill this minimum. When said such funds fall below $1,000, the scholarship Committee/ School Administration will determine another senior scholarship fund to transfer funds to. 6. Details of the organization and administration of the Scholarship Fund will be left to the discretion of the Board of Education.
