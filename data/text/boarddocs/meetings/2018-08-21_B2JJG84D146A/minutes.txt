3. Discussion/Presentation

3.1 Charitable Trust Discussion

Assistant Superintendent for Business, Carol Stein, discussed the potential of establishing a Charitable Trust to enable residents to receive a tax credit for donations to the Irvington School District. The Board decided to take Ms. Stein's and Legal's recommendation that we wait for IRS approval before proceeding further with resolutions.

3.2 Security

Dr. Harrison, Superintendent of Schools, gave an intro to the ongoing Safety and Secuity plan and improvements for the School District. Carol Stein, Assistant Supt for Business gave a detailed account of the measures in place and future plans to enhance the security of the School District including:

- New sign in/Visitor Managment proceduries

- Visitor lanyards to identify ALL visitors

- Public-facing notifications of Emergency Protocol Communications

- Equipping each Satey Team with a portable Radio

- Emergency "Go Kits" enhanced per recommendations from security consultant

- Staff Training for all Irvington USFD staff on August 29, 2018

- New Visitor Protocols reviewed/enforced with Security Personnel

- Installing window shades in certain locations

- Adding concrete planters/walls or rocks to increase play area border security

- Numbering of doors, exterior and interio (in process)

- Numbering classroom windows (in process)

- Cameras at entrance ways

- 6-hour training session for each building level safety team

- Continued drills per SED guidelines

3.3 Reorganization of Board Leadership Structure

The Administration and the Board discussed the viability of adding a 2nd Vice President to the Irvington UFSD School Board.

Trustee Deborah Hargraves left the meeting at 7:40pm

WHEREAS; the Irvington Union Free School District pursuant to the authority granted in General Municipal Law, Article 5A (Public Contracts), Section 103 desires to participate in the U.S. Communities Cooperative Purchasing Alliance. Said Alliance is sponsored by the New York State Association of Counties; Association of School Business Officials International; the National Association of Counties; the National Institute of Governmental Purchasing; the National League of Cities; and the United States Conference of Mayors;

WHEREAS; Section 103 of the General Municipal Law permits Irvington Union Free School District to make purchases of apparatus, materials, equipment or supplies, or to contract for services related to the installation, maintenance or repair of apparatus, materials, equipment, and supplies, may make such purchases, or may contract for such services related to the installation, maintenance or repair of apparatus, materials, equipment, and supplies, as may be required by Irvington Union Free School District therein through the use of a contract let by the United States of America or any agency thereof, any state or any other county or political subdivision or district therein if such contract was let to the lowest responsible bidder or on the basis of best value in a manner consistent with this section and made available for use by other governmental entities;
WHEREAS; the Irvington Union Free School District desires to participate for the purpose of fulfilling and executing its respective public governmental purposes, goals, objectives, programs and functions;
WHEREAS; the Irvington Union Free School District has reviewed the benefits of participating in this program and based on this review has concluded the program will provide the best value to taxpayers of this Irvington Union Free School District through the anticipated savings to be realized;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, by the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District is authorized to participate in the U.S. Communities Government Purchasing Alliance and that the Irvington Board of Education or designee is authorized to register for the U.S. Communities program on behalf of Irvington Union Free School District;
