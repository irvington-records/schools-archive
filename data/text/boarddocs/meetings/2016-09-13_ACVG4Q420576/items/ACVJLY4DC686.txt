Category: 9. Business of the Board
Subject: 9.3 Appointment of Chief Emergency Officer
Type: Action (Consent)

Recent changes in legislation related to emergency planning and response, now requires that school districts designate a Chief Emergency Officer who will ultimately assure compliance with the regulations.
