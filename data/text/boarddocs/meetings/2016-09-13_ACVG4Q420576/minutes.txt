4. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Dr. Harrison shared the following:

- He thanked everyone in the community for their outpouring of support of the Rosenberg family as well as the staff for making themselves available to students, faculty and community. This will be a healing process and everyone will continue to support one another.

- He acknowledged Ms. Chapnick for keeping in communication with the parents whose children were on the Clearwater Sloop which broke down during a fifth grade trip yesterday. Carol Stein ensured that the ship was worthy to go back out today to take another group of students. Ms. Chapnick acknowledged the staff and chaperones for keeping calm and cool and letting the students know that they were safe.The school psychologist did meet with some students who sought out support and reinsurance.

With respect to transportation, this year was the smoothest in five years with only a few glitches. Pleased to have Royal Coach providing the transportation again this year.

- The principals will visit the next board meeting to discuss the goal work taking place across the district along with a personal approach for each building. - An update on the bond projects, with Meszaros Field completed, school projects completed and East Field work starting this week. Mrs. Stein noted that the field maintenance training will provided to staff. Greenplay has offered to maintain Meszaros Field this year which will allow us to watch and determine if our staff can take over the maintenance or will need to contract out for maintenance with our staff handling the day to day maintenance. Also investigating the possibility of installing security cameras around the field. Signage will be finalized that will spell out rules and use of the fields.

6. Presentations/Discussions

Mr. Timothy Doyle represented the District’s independent auditing firm, Bonadio & Co. at the Audit Committee that was held prior to the Board meeting and presented the results of the four audits conducted for the year ending June 30, 2016: Internal Control Matters, Extra-classroom Audit and Basic Financial Audit and reported that the District received an unqualified or unmodified opinion rating on all basic financial statements, which is the highest level of assurance that can be given. Mr. Doyle noted that the audit was conducted in accordance with Governmental Auditing Standards and that on internal controls over financial reporting that could have material effect on financials that none were found nor were any non-compliance issues found.

However, the Extra-Classroom Fund Audit which is required to be conducted separately, did contain a qualified opinion with respect to the cash received within the district and the timeliness with which the receipts are submitted to the treasurer. He stressed that this is a standard report that is received among districts.

14. Board of Education Discussion

Mr. Whitney raised the question from previous year regarding the possibility allowing student athletes to be exempt from participating in gym which would give them a study hall, thus providing more time for their studies. He would like to have administration respond to be sure that the question is given the due diligence. He believes that Bronxville might have adopted this practice. Dr. Harrison responded that that there would be impacts on the PE program but also has considered the philosophical approach and ethical challenge as you wouldn't be able to give the same consideration to other students who might participate in other extra-curricular activities, such as musicians, those who participate in plays, etc. Dr. Kor also stressed that PE is a curriculum and have asked Artie McCormack and Dave Cohen to look at possibly redesigning the curriculum, and want to make sure respectful of all programs and what are the goals of the program. Mr. Graeber asked if the situation has been looked at from the homework angle. Both Mr. Graeber and Mrs. Palmieri also agreed with Dr. Harrison's characterization of taking into account other students who participate in many extracurricular activities.
