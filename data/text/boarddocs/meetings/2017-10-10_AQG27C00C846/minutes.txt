3. Presentation

Information, Report: 3.1 Overview of 2016-17 Auditors' Report/Opinion

Carol Stein introduced Jaime Cote from Bonadio & Co. LLP, the District’s independent auditing firm, who shared the Financial Statements as of June 30, 2017 along with the  Independent Audit Report and Extra Classroom Activity Funds Statement audit. The opinion of the audit was that the financial statements present fairly in all material respects and are in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the USA. He provided financial highlights which included:

- General Fund Balance as of June 30, 2017: $5,635,259 of which $422,500 is designated for subsequent year’s expenditures and $161,424 for carryover of encumbrances from the previous year.

- Unassigned Fund Balance: $2,364,020 and represents 4% of statutory limit.

- Increase to Tax Certiorari ($1.5), Workers Compensation (341,273) and Capital Projects ($301,340 reserves.

Mr. Cote noted that the reserves approved by the Board and are reasonably stated and that the fund balance is being managed appropriately as mandated by SED. Mr. Cote acknowledged that management was prepared for the audit to be undertaken in July. He reviewed the post retirement obligations (3,173,350) and noted that all governments in NYS must report their share of NYS pension liabilities, however amounts due are payable by NYS to District pensioners and that the school district does pay attention to their future obligations.

A qualified opinion was reported with respect to the extra-classroom activities fund due to the “insufficient accounting controls are exercised over cash receipts at the point of collection to the time of submission to the Central Treasurer. Accordingly,m we were unable to obtain sufficient audit evidence over such receipts beyond the amounts recorded. Mr. Cote did stress that at least 95% of school districts incur this opinion due to the timing of the audit. The report will be made public after the meeting.

Dr. Harrison gave accolades to Carol Stein and her staff for their efforts as there is a tremendous amount of work that goes into preparing for the audit. The Board thanked Ms. Stein for her efforts.

Information: 4.2 Proposed NYSSBA Amendments/Resolutions

Mr. Hanna provided a background of the process that is undertaken with respect to amendments/resolutions submitted to NYSSBA for their approval or rejection at the annual NYSSBA convention by member districts after which time, those approved are submitted to the legislature for their consideration. Mr. Hanna noted that Irvington submitted a resolution this year calling for an amendment to the laws governing the timeline to hold a special election of Board Members, and was accepted by NYSSBA to be placed on the ballot. To determine which resolutions/amendments that area school districts' support, oppose, support if amended or no decision reached, area school districts’ Legislative Action Committee (Michael Hanna and Maura Gedid), along with Westchester Putnam School Boards Association (WPSBA)  meet to review, discuss and reach consensus.  It is at that point that each school district will vote on those prior to the convention and a vote will be cast by the district’s delegate in attendance.  It was noted that only Dr. Harrison will be in attendance this year, therefore, no vote can be cast by the District.

After board members discussed the process and questions were asked about some of the individual items, it was determined that insufficient time was allowed for the Board to discuss the individual amendments/resolutions, the potential impact on districts, both financial and operational, and reach an informed opinion in order to vote. It was agreed that in the future, sufficient time will be allocated for members to be able to reach informed decisions prior to voting as a Board.  Those supported by Westchester Putnam School Board's Association are then submitted to the legislation to support modification and elimination to current laws, others may be supported if resolution is amended, opposed, or that no decision was reached on an individual resolution. Mr. Hanna noted that Irvington has a resolution on the ballot asking for the legislation to amend the laws surrounding holding a special election for additional members.
