Subject: 3.1 Approval - Milburn Flooring Mills
Type: Action

Milburn Flooring Mills will restore the Maher Gym floor as the proposal includes 4 passes of sanding, one coat of sealing, three finishing coats and installing the logos (Gina Maher and Bulldog).  It also includes lines for badminton.  The total contract is $30,376 and arises from the Ed-Data Contract for time and materials, this proposal exceeds $25,000 needing approval from the Board of Education.
