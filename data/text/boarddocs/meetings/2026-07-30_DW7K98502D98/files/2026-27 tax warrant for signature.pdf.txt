Dear Town Comptroller:

I, Carol Stein, District Clerk of the Irvington Union Free School District, Town of Greenburgh, County
of Westchester, State of New York, do hereby certify that the following budget and tax items for the
2026-27 fiscal year, beginning July 1, 2026 and ending June 30, 2027 are true and accurate. This is an
official breakdown of the warrant for use for your collection of taxes.

At its meeting on July 30, 2026, the Board of Education adopted a motion containing the following
resolution:

MOVED, that the voters of the Irvington Union Free School District, by a vote of 367 to 120, adopted a
budget for the school year commencing July 1, 2026 in the amount of $82,471,960; that the sums as set
forth in the forms of budget heretofore submitted to the voter be appropriated; that the taxes in the
necessary amounts be levied therefore; that the Board members be authorized to execute a warrant
therefore, and that the warrant be delivered to the Town authorities.

The major budget categories and their respective amounts are listed below:
