To make more efficient use of meeting time, the Board of Education authorizes the use of a consent agenda as a part of the meeting agenda. A Consent Agenda allows for one motion to approve multiple items and such items are identified as such on the Agenda. The President of the Board will ask for a motion to approve the Consent Agenda and will ask if  Board member(s) wish to have discussion and/or clarification on specific item(s) contained within the Consent Agenda. Following the discussion, the Board will then vote on the Consent Agenda motion. In addition, a Board member may  request to have an item removed from the Consent Agenda in order to vote on the motion separately.

8. Business Office Matters

Action (Consent): 8.1 Year End Fund Balance

Resolution: Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education retain $3,298,878 of the 2025-26 year fund balance as an Unassigned Fund Balance for 2026-27 and appropriate $422,500 as Assigned Fund Balance for 2026-27 Revenue. Any remaining fund balance will be deposited to one or more of the following reserves: Tax Certiorari Reserve, not to exceed $2,000,000, Retirement Contribution Reserve, not to exceed $400,000, Employee Benefit Liability Reserve, not to exceed $400,000, Liability Reserve, not to exceed $1,000,000, Insurance Liability Reserve, not to exceed $150,000, and any remaining surplus funds to be deposited to the Capital Reserve, which is anticipated to not exceed $2,000,000.

- $3,298,878 is the allowable maximum amount (4% of the 2026-27 budget) and recommended to be designated as Unassigned Fund Balance by the Comptroller

- $422,500 was presented during the budget discussions

- The Tax Certiorari reserve is used to make refunds on previous years' settlements/claims.

- The Worker's Comp Reserve is used to pay ongoing worker comp tail claims.

- The Capital Reserve will be used for future capital projects with voter consent.

- The Retirement Contribution Reserve will be used to offset future increases in the required ERS or TRS contribution rate in years where the rate increase could affect academic programs.

- The Employee Benefit Liability Reserve is used for contractual benefits to retiring employees.

- The Liability Reserve will be used for any settlements on legal matters.

- The Insurance Liability Reserve will be used to cover deductibles or over insurance policy limits, if/when that may occur.

The fund balance presentation on June 23, 2026 provided approximate funding recommendations to each of these reserves, however, the final amounts will be determined once the financial statements for the 2025-26 school year are prepared.
