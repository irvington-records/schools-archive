The NYS Department of Civil Service has developed the NY HELPs Program to assist agencies with filling critical positions. This statewide program temporarily removes NYS Civil Service Exam requirements for many titles. This means that candidates will not be required to take the NYS Civil Service Exam for these titles and can be eligible for consideration by meeting the minimum qualifications as stated in the job posting for each title.
