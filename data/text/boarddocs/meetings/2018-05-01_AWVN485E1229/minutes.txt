2. Public Hearing for 2018-19 Budget

Michael Hanna called the Public Hearing to order at 7:24pm.

Dr. Harrison provided the details of the proposed 2018-19 Budget which will be voted on by the community on Tuesday, May 15 2018 from 7:30am - 9:00pm in the Main Street School. The proposed budget totals $61, 348,375 and is a 3.8% increase over last year's budget and is tax cap compliant. The tax rate is estimated to be $19.41 per thousand of assessed value, or a 1.42% increase. Dr. Harrison noted that the budget does have several new initiatives, including enhancing security of the District's facilities, such as a second roving security guard, funding of tax certs as well as a pilot program to restore the late bus at the MS/HS campus. With respect to curriculum, the PLTW continuum will be completed with a K-2 teacher, addition of an elementary guidance counselor and restoration of assistant athletic coaches and a creation of a 7/8 grade special class, which is cost neutral. There have been a number of public forums over the course of the budget process to provide an opportunity for community members to ask questions with several others in the next few weeks.

There were no comments from the community. The Public Hearing was closed at 7:31pm.
