Category: 10. Business Office Matters
Subject: 10.7 Contract for Health Services-Harrison Central School District
Type: Action (Consent)

As provided by Section 912 of the State Education Law, the Irvington School District is responsible for providing Health and Welfare Services to Irvington resident students who attend private/parochial schools in other school districts.
