     THIS OPT-IN AGREEMENT (THE “OPT-IN AGREEMENT”) TO
PARTICIPATE IN THE WESTCHESTER COUNTY SCHOOL BUS STOP ARM
SAFETY PROGRAM IS MADE AS OF THIS 22nd DAY OF October, 2024, BY AND
BETWEEN


       THE COUNTY OF WESTCHESTER, a municipal corporation of the State of New
       York, having an office and place of business in the Michaelian Office Building, 148
       Martine Avenue, White Plains, New York 10601 (hereinafter referred to as the “County”),
       and
       THE IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT, a school district of the State
       of New York, having an office and principal place of business at 40 North Broadway,
       Irvington, NY 10533 (hereinafter referred to as the “School District”).

       and
       BUSPATROL AMERICA LLC, a limited liability company, having its principal place
       of business at 8560 Cinder Bed Road, Suite 100, Lorton, VA 22079 (“BusPatrol” or
       “Contractor”).
                                             RECITALS

       WHEREAS, pursuant to New York Session Law Chapter 145 of the Laws of 2019, the
County enacted Local Law 6-2023 (the “Local Law”) to establish a demonstration program (the
“Program”) imposing vehicle owner liability for failure of an operator thereto to comply with
Section 1174 of the New York Vehicle and Traffic Law (“VTL”) when meeting a school bus
marked and equipped as provided in subdivisions 20 and 21-c of section 375 of the VTL and
operated in the County pursuant to Section 1174-a; and

        WHEREAS County has entered into an agreement with BusPatrol, dated June 10, 2024,
(hereinafter the “Agreement”) to install and operate the Program through, among other things, the
installation of school bus photo violation monitoring systems on school buses that can be used to
automatically produce recorded images of a vehicle at the time it is used or operated in violation
of Section 1174-a of the VTL; and

        WHEREAS, the County and Participating School District are authorized, pursuant to New
York Session Law Chapter 145 of the Laws of 2019, as amended by New York Session Law
Chapter 56 of the Laws of 2024, and the Local Law, as amended from time to time (collectively,
referred to as the “Stop Arm Law”), to enter into an agreement for, among other things, the
installation, maintenance, and use of school bus photo violation monitoring systems on school
buses owned or operated by the school district or privately owned and operated for compensation
under a contract with such school district; and

        WHEREAS, Participating School District desires to enter into this agreement to authorize
the County, through its contractor BusPatrol, to install, maintain and use the school bus photo
violation monitoring systems on school buses operated within the Participating School District as
authorized by the Stop Arm Law.

       NOW, therefore, it is mutually agreed by and between the Parties hereto as follows:

1. Purpose. This Opt-In Agreement constitutes a formal, binding agreement between the
   Participating School District, the County and BusPatrol (together, the “Parties”), for the
   installation, maintenance, and use of school bus photo violation monitoring systems on school
   buses owned or operated by the Participating School District or privately owned and operated
   for compensation under a contract with such Participating School District in accordance with
   the Stop Arm Law (the “BusPatrol System”).
2. Authorization. The Parties’ execution of this Opt-In Agreement will serve as authorization
   for the County, through its contractor BusPatrol, to install, maintain and use the BusPatrol
   System on buses owned and operated by the Participating School District or privately owned
   and operated for compensation under contract with such Participating School District
   according to the terms of this Opt-In Agreement.
   The Participating School District represents and warrants that it has not entered into an
   agreement with any city, town or village within the County to participate in a school bus stop
   arm program.
3. Payment. In accordance with the Stop Arm Law and the Agreement, the County will be solely
   responsible for paying BusPatrol for the installation, maintenance and use of the BusPatrol
   System on buses owned and operated by the Participating School District or privately owned
   and operated for compensation under contract with such district as set forth in the Agreement.
   Nothing in this Opt-In Agreement shall be deemed to amend the Agreement. Participating
   School District will have no responsibility for payment of any amounts due to BusPatrol for
   the installation, maintenance or use of the BusPatrol System under this Opt-In Agreement.
4. Responsibilities of the Parties:
           a. BusPatrol. BusPatrol is responsible for:
              i.  providing all equipment and services necessary to install, maintain and use
                  the BusPatrol System;

              ii.    Installing the BusPatrol System in a safe and secure manner;

              iii.   Installing the BusPatrol System so that it is out of reach of the children and
                     any other occupants of the bus;

              iv.    Installing the BusPatrol System so that its installation and use do not interfere
                     with the operation of the school bus;

              v.     Providing for remote monitoring on the BusPatrol System with periodic
                     checks to detect any problems with the BusPatrol System and promptly
                     correcting, repairing and addressing any problems detected;

              vi.    Promptly repairing and replacing any part of the BusPatrol System, as
                     necessary, throughout the term of the Opt-In Agreement so that it remains in
                     good condition and proper working order; and

                                                  2

   vii. Complying with all applicable federal, state and local laws, rules, regulations,
        orders or ordinances applicable to the Opt-In Agreement or the work to be
        performed under the Opt-In Agreement.

b. County. County is responsible for establishing the Program pursuant to Stop Arm
   Law, including, but not limited to:
      i. Arranging for County Enforcement Technician to review evidence
          packages and approve or disapprove potential notices of violation of the
          Stop Arm Law;
       ii. Installing signage provided by BusPatrol and as set forth in the Local Law
           in conformance with standards established in the Manual of Uniform Traffic
           Control Devices;
      iii. Reviewing and approving BusPatrol invoices for payment, in accordance
           with the Agreement; and
      iv. Submitting an annual report on the results of the BusPatrol System to the
          governor, the temporary president of the senate and the speaker of the
          assembly.
c. Participating School District. Participating School District is responsible for:
        i. Providing BusPatrol or its agents with access to buses owned or operated
           by the District beginning on the installation start date to be mutually agreed
           upon by the District, the County and BusPatrol with the understanding that
           the Parties want the maximum amount of buses outfitted with Enforcement
           Cameras to the extent reasonably possible. If Participating School District
           does not own and operate the buses customarily used on the routes in its
           district, then Participating School District shall use reasonable efforts to
           enter into an agreement with the private owner(s) and operator(s) of those
           buses to allow BusPatrol to install and operate its equipment on such buses,
           at no cost to BusPatrol. If Participating School District does not enter into
           such agreement with the private owner(s) and operator(s), or if any private
           owner or operator fails to provide BusPatrol will access to school buses, the
           County or BusPatrol or Participating School District, at its option, may
           terminate the Opt-In Agreement;
       ii. Providing BusPatrol with ongoing access to any and all BusPatrol
           equipment installed on buses owned or operated by the Participating School
           District or its third-party bus operator, as reasonably necessary for
           BusPatrol to operate, maintain, and, upon expiration or termination of the
           Opt-In Agreement, remove, the BusPatrol Equipment and BusPatrol
           Systems;
      iii. Providing BusPatrol with electronic copies of school bus routing
           information, in Excel or CSV format, if possible, for the purpose of
           identifying high risk routes and prioritizing an installation schedule;

                  iv. To the extent practicable, use reasonable efforts to maintain the routes
                      identified in Subsection 4(c)(iii);
                   v. Using all reasonable efforts to properly store, secure, maintain, and repair
                      the school buses when not in use to reasonably safeguard the BusPatrol
                      System;
                  vi. Appointing a designated point of contact, identified above, who shall be
                      authorized to act on behalf of the Participating School District on all matters
                      relating to this Opt-In Agreement and Participating District’s use of and
                      participation in the Program;
                 vii. The Participating School District shall use reasonable steps to ensure that
                      all buses are equipped and operated in compliance with the specifications
                      required by the Federal, State, or local law, including New York State VTL
                      § 375 to the extent applicable, and provide BusPatrol with certificates of
                      inspection issued by the New York State Department of Transportation
                      (DOT) pursuant to section 140 of the New York Transportation Law and
                      the safety rules and regulations promulgated thereunder, and other
                      documents or declarations pertaining to the inspection of the buses by the
                      DOT;
                viii. The Participating School District shall provide the County with any
                      information concerning the buses and routes necessary for the County to
                      meet its reporting requirements under the Stop Arm Law; and
                  ix. The Participating School District shall provide BusPatrol with a list of the
                      school buses for which the BusPatrol Equipment will be installed and the
                      model of each such school bus.
5. License, Restricted Use. The Participating School District grants to BusPatrol a license to
   use the school buses to install the BusPatrol System, including BusPatrol Equipment and
   BusPatrol Software and other BusPatrol Intellectual Property (collectively “BusPatrol
   Intellectual Property”), solely for purposes of carrying out this Opt-In Agreement. This license
   shall continue for so long as this Opt-In Agreement remains in effect and shall expire
   immediately upon termination or expiration of this Agreement. BusPatrol shall immediately
   cease any and all use of the BusPatrol Intellectual Property on the school buses upon
   termination or expiration of this Opt-In Agreement.
   The Participating District agrees that it will not use the BusPatrol Intellectual Property for any
   purpose. Participating School District will not disclose the BusPatrol Intellectual Property to
   any third parties without the prior express written permission of BusPatrol; will not make any
   modifications to the BusPatrol System; and will not attempt to disassemble, de-compile or
   otherwise perform any type of reverse engineering to the BusPatrol System or cause any other
   person to do any of the foregoing.
   Any image or images captured by BusPatrol Systems shall be inadmissible in any disciplinary
   proceeding convened by the Participating School District or any school bus contractor thereof,
   and any proceeding initiated by the department of motor vehicle involving licensure privileges

                                                 4

   of school bus operators. Any school bus photo violation monitoring device mounted on a
   school bus shall be directed outwardly from such school bus to capture images of vehicles
   operated in violation of Section 1174 of the VTL, and images produced by such device shall
   not be used for any other purpose.

6. Nothing in the preceding paragraph shall prohibit the Participating School District from
   entering into a separate written agreement with BusPatrol for the installation of Non-
   Enforcement Cameras in its school buses. Non-Enforcement Cameras means internal cameras
   and other related equipment, including audio recording equipment, installed by BusPatrol on
   the school bus to allow authorized personnel within the Participating School Districts to
   monitor student safety on board the bus. The Parties acknowledge and agree that the County
   shall have no responsibility or liability with regard to Non-Enforcement Cameras.
7. Reporting. Participating School District, acting by and through the Superintendent of Schools
   of such District, or his or her designee shall provide any report required of the Participating
   School District pursuant to Stop Arm Law, to the State or any official thereof. BusPatrol and
   County agree to work with the Participating School District to provide any information or other
   reasonable assistance necessary for the Participating School District to prepare and submit any
   required reports.
8. Restrictions on Access to Enforcement Data. In accordance with the Stop Arm Law and the
   Agreement, the Parties agree that BusPatrol will implement controls and configure the
   BusPatrol System to safeguard enforcement data generated by the external cameras and other
   components of the BusPatrol Stop Arm Enforcement Solution as follows:
           a. Pursuant to Section 1174-a (a)(3)(i) of the Vehicle and Traffic Law, BusPatrol will
              implement controls and configure the BusPatrol system to: (i) prevent Participating
              School District from accessing any photographs, microphotographs, videotapes,
              other recorded images and data from the BusPatrol Systems; (ii) to provide for the
              proper handling and custody of such photographs, microphotographs, videotapes,
              other recorded images and data produced by such systems; and (iii) to provide for
              the forwarding of such photographs, microphotographs, videotapes, other recorded
              images and data to the County for the purpose of determining whether a motor
              vehicle was operated in violation of Section 1174-a of the VTL and imposing
              monetary liability on the owner of such motor vehicle therefor.
           b. BusPatrol will implement controls and configure the BusPatrol Systems to ensure
              that all photographs, microphotographs, videotapes, other recorded images and data
              produced by the BusPatrol Systems shall be destroyed (i) ninety days after the date
              of the alleged imposition of liability if a notice of liability is not issued for such
              alleged imposition of liability pursuant to the Local Law or (ii) upon final
              disposition of a notice of liability issued pursuant to the Local Law.
9. Insurance and Indemnification. BusPatrol agrees to procure and maintain insurance naming
   the County and the Participating School District as additional insured, as provided and
   described in Schedule “A,” entitled "Standard Insurance Provisions", which is attached hereto
   and made a part hereof. In addition to, and not in limitation of the insurance provisions
   contained in Schedule “A”, the Contractor agrees:
