PROGRAMS IN THE UNITED STATES



  The Nation’s Most Trusted and Deployed School Bus Stop-Arm Safety Program
  BusPatrol is the most trusted and deployed school bus stop-arm safety program nationwide.
  We proudly collaborate with hundreds of school districts and municipalities, safeguarding over
  1.3 million students across 16 states with our services deployed on more than 26,000
  contracted school buses.

  Our support extends across the spectrum of fleet sizes, catering to the smallest fleets comprising
  a few buses to the largest ones, which range from 500 to 1,000 buses. Notably, an impressive
  80-85% of our clientele entrust us with the modernization of their fleets by incorporating
  internal cameras. This technology is particularly embraced by school bus drivers who, while
  ensuring road safety, cannot simultaneously monitor the interior of the school bus.
