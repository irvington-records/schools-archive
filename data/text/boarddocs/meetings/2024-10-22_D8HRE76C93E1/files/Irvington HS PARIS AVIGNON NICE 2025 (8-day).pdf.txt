  DAY 1:TRAVEL TO FRANCE                         Wednesday 12-Feb-2025

                            Board your motor coach at your school and transfer to the airport (JFK/LGA).

              Board your flight from New York and begin the journey to France.




DAY 2:PARIS                              Thursday 13-Feb-2025

              Bienvenue en France!
              Meet your French Prometour Tour Director at the arrivals section of the airport.

              Transfer by motor coach to the hotel to drop off your luggage, check-in will be later.

              While in Paris, travel as the locals do by walking or taking public transportation.

              Stop in front of Musée du Louvre to admire its famous glass pyramid.

              Stop at a Parisian boulangerie to watch how French baguettes & croissants are
              made.
              (tasting included)

              Have lunch on your own.

              With your Tour Director, discover some of the highlights of the Quartier Latin such
              as Luxembourg Gardens, the Pantheon, Sorbonne University, the famed
              Shakespeare & Co. bookstore, and the Petit Prince Store dedicated to St-Exupéry's
              masterpiece.

              Enter the iconic Cathédrale Notre-Dame de Paris and admire its rose window &
              French Gothic architecture, it's as impressive inside as it is outside. (non-guided)
              (entrance is subject to the reopening date)

              Enjoy dinner with your group in a local restaurant.

              Overnight accommodation in Paris.




DAY 3:PARIS                                  Friday 14-Feb-2025

              Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group.

              Visit Musée d'Orsay. Originally a railway station, the collection boasts some of the
              best and most-known 19th and 20th century works from artists like Renoir, Cezanne,
              Degas, Monet, and Van Gogh. (guided) (this museum is subject to availability)

              Have lunch on your own.

              Take a funicular to the top of Montmartre hill to discover Quartier Montmartre with
              your Tour Director, it was and still is the source of inspiration to many artists such as
              Pissarro and Delacroix.

              Enter Sacré Coeur Basilica to admire its mosaic interior, take a selfie at the Wall of
              Love, and spend time in the always lively Place du Tertre.

              Learn the art of French cooking with a group hands-on cooking class.

              Take care in what you prepare, this is going to be your meal!

                        Overnight accommodation in Paris.



DAY 4:PARIS                                        Saturday 15-Feb-2025
Château de Versailles

                        Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group.

                        Board a local RER train and travel to Versailles.

                        Discover Château de Versailles at your own pace, the extravagant Sun King Palace
                        has some spectacular rooms such as the Grand Apartments, the Royal Chapel, and
                        Hall of Mirrors.
                        (audio guide)

                        Explore the elegantly landscaped Gardens of Château de Versailles.

                        Have lunch on your own.

                        Board a local RER train and return to Paris.

                        Head to Place du Trocadéro , it's a must-go-to for all photo seekers, with the Eiffel
                        Tower providing the perfect backdrop.

                        Cruise along the River Seine on board a Bateau Mouche to admire the architecture
                        of Paris.
                        (on board commentary / audio-guide )

                        Enjoy dinner with your group in a local restaurant.

                        Overnight accommodation in Paris.



DAY 5:PARIS - AVIGNON                                Sunday 16-Feb-2025
Travel by TGV Train

                        Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group.

                        Transfer by motor coach from your hotel to the train station.

                        Admire the French countryside as you travel up to 320 km/h on a TGV high-speed
                        train to Avignon.

                         Have lunch on your own.
                        ​(purchase sandwiches & snacks before boarding the train)

                        Transfer by motor coach to the hotel to drop off your luggage, check-in will be done
                        later.

                        Visit the Palais des Papes. It is the largest Gothic palace in Europe and was the
                        symbol of the church’s influence throughout the western Christian world in the 14th
                        century. (guided)

                        Enjoy dinner with your group in a local restaurant.

                        Overnight accommodation in Avignon.



DAY 6:AVIGNON - NICE                                Monday 17-Feb-2025
Aix-en-Provence & Grasse

                        Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group.

                  Board your motor coach and travel to Aix-En-Provence, a city where many great
                  artists have come for inspiration, including Paul Cezanne.

                  Discover Aix-en-Provence from onboard the tourist train. Departing from the
                  Cézanne statue listen to informative commentary about its rich cultural and historical
                  heritage as you admire must-see monuments like Fontaine de la Rotonde at Cours
                  Mirabeau, the roi René statue, and La Tourreluque. (on board commentary / audio-
                  guide)

                  Have lunch on your own.

                  Board your motor coach and continue on to Grasse, considered to be the world's
                  capital of perfume.

                  Visit Parfumerie Fragonard and Boutique to learn how French perfumes are made.
                  (guided)

                  Board your motor coach and continue on to Nice, the heart of the Côte d'Azur.

                  While in Nice, travel as the locals do by walking or taking public transportation
                  (Nice bus & tramway).

                  Enjoy a group pizza dinner in the heart of Vieux Nice, it’s always interesting to see
                  how each country makes this well-known dish!

                  Overnight accommodation in Nice.



DAY 7:NICE                                    Tuesday 18-Feb-2025

                  Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group.

                  At Confiserie Florian see how the famous Bonbons Nicois are made from fruits and
                  flowers of the area, and then enjoy a blind taste-test challenge!

                  With your Tour Director explore Vieux Nice. The Old Town is a maze of narrow
                  streets teeming with local life, buildings painted a faded Roman gold, flower-strewn
                  squares, and traditional bakeries.

                  Have lunch on your own.

                  Go to Parc du Chateau and admire the spectacular panorama of Nice and the Baie
                  des Anges (round trip elevator).

                  Make a memory moment of your visit to Nice by taking a group photo in front of the
                  I Love Nice sign. (using your own cameras)

                  Enjoy dinner with your group in a local restaurant.

                  Overnight accommodation in Nice.



DAY 8:NICE - RETURN HOME                 Wednesday 19-Feb-2025

                  Enjoy breakfast at the hotel with your group.

                  Transfer by private motor coach to the airport.

                  Say a fond Au Revoir to your French Prometour Tour Director .

                  Board your flight from Nice and begin the journey to New York.

                  Board your motor coach at the airport (JFK/LGA) and transfer to your school.
