                                        Tour Confirmation Agreement
This Tour Confirmation Agreement (this "Agreement") is between Junior Bus Tours, Inc. ("Junior Tours") and
Irvington Middle School (the "Group"). Junior Tours and the Group agree that:

TOUR PACKAGE
Junior Tours will provide the Group a tour of Washington D.C. from April 9-11, 2025, with the following:
* Round-trip transportation via motorcoach
* 2 night’s accommodations
* 2 breakfasts at your hotel
* 2 lunches ($20 cash allotment per person)
* 2 dinners
* Tour Guides for each bus while in Washington D.C.
* Sightseeing tour as per itinerary
* Free chaperones in the ratio of 1 to 8 paying participants in double occupancy
* 1 Extra hotel room
* $1000 travel stipend
* Promotional materials: forms, baggage tags, travel organizer, 2 t shirts and 1 hoodie sweatshirt
* All admissions, taxes and tips while on tour
* Insurance- Junior Tours, Inc. maintains professional liability insurance policy

NUMBER OF PARTICIPANTS TOUR COST AND PAYMENT PROCEDURE
The all-inclusive cost on quad occupancy is $750 per person. The Tour cost is based on 120+ paying participants.
Should the final number of paying participants fall below 120 paying participants, Junior Tours reserves the right to
increase the tour cost and/or adjust the Tour Package accordingly.
The total tour cost shall be paid according to the following deposit schedule (Should the Group require additional time
to make these payments, please contact Junior Tours):
        1st Deposit:    $250 per person is due 11/15/2024
        2nd Deposit:    $250 per person is due 12/15/2024
        Balance:        $250 per person is due 2/15/2025


ROOMING LIST - ADDITIONAL ROOMING CHARGES / AIRLINE FEES
The Rooming List should be forwarded to Junior Tours as soon as it becomes final, but not later than 6 weeks prior to
the Group’s departure date.

The Tour Package cost includes double occupancy rooms for free chaperones and quad occupancy rooms for paying
participants. Any paying participant opting for (i) triple occupancy will pay an additional $75 per person; (ii) double
occupancy will pay an additional $175 per person; and (iii) single occupancy will pay an additional $350 per person.

GENERAL RESPONSIBILITIES AND POLICIES
Standard Cancellation Policy: The Group Leader has the sole discretion to make all decisions on behalf of the Group in regard to the Tour and
whether to reschedule or cancel the Tour if conditions warrant a change. Any decision reached by the Group Leader and Junior Tours is binding
on all registered tour participants. All payments received, less non-recoverable deposits, will be applied to the new tour if the Tour is
rescheduled. All payments received, less non-recoverable deposits and a $200 per person booking fee, will be refunded if the Tour is canceled.

If an individual Tour Participant wants to cancel from a scheduled tour, they must notify Junior Tours in writing. Junior Tours will retain:
25% of the Tour Price if cancellation notice is received 90 days or more prior to departure.
50% of the Tour Price if cancellation notice is received more than 46-89 days prior to departure.
100% of the payments received if cancellation notice is received 45 days or less prior to departure.

Junior Tours recommends that each person purchase the NTA Travel Protection Insurance Plan. The insurance allows individuals to receive
additional compensation above and beyond what they would be entitled to under the Standard Cancellation Policy. Individuals who purchase the
insurance are entitled to a 100% refund of any monies lost after applying the Standard Cancellation Policy if they can document that they are
cancelling for a ‘covered reason’. Covered reasons include, but are not limited to, things such as unexpected illness, injury or death to insured, a
Traveling Companion or an Immediate Family Member, travel supplier bankruptcy, weather that ceases all travel services for 48+ hours, traffic
accident enroute to the departure, jury duty, subpoena or quarantine. Additional information, including terms and conditions, can viewed at
https://nta.aontravelprotect.com
