Action (Consent):  9.7 Agreement with the County of Westchester for Participation in the Stop Arm Program

Resolution: WHEREAS, pursuant to New York Session Law Chapter 145 of the Laws of 2019, the County enacted Local Law 6-2023 (the “Local Law”) to establish a demonstration program (the “Program”) imposing vehicle owner liability for failure of an operator thereto to comply with Section 1174 of the New York Vehicle and Traffic Law (“VTL”) when meeting a school bus marked and equipped as provided in subdivisions 20 and 21-c of section 375 of the VTL and operated in the County pursuant to Section 1174-a; and WHEREAS County has entered into an agreement with BusPatrol, dated June 10, 2024, (hereinafter the “Agreement”) to install and operate the Program through, among other things, the installation of school bus photo violation monitoring systems on school buses that can be used to automatically produce recorded images of a vehicle at the time it is used or operated in violation of Section 1174-a of the VTL; and WHEREAS, the County and Participating School District are authorized, pursuant to New York Session Law Chapter 145 of the Laws of 2019, as amended by New York Session Law Chapter 56 of the Laws of 2024, and the Local Law, as amended from time to time (collectively, referred to as the “Stop Arm Law”), to enter into an agreement for, among other things, the installation, maintenance, and use of school bus photo violation monitoring systems on school buses owned or operated by the school district or privately owned and operated for compensation under a contract with such school district; and WHEREAS, Participating School District desires to enter into this agreement to authorize the County, through its contractor BusPatrol, to install, maintain and use the school bus photo violation monitoring systems on school buses operated within the Participating School District as authorized by the Stop Arm Law. THEREFORE, the Board of Education authorizes the Board President to sign the formal, binding agreement between the Participating School District, the County and BusPatrol (together, the “Parties”), for the installation, maintenance, and use of school bus photo violation monitoring systems on school buses owned or operated by the Participating School District or privately owned and operated for compensation under a contract with such Participating School District in accordance with the Stop Arm Law (the “BusPatrol System”).

Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education approve the Consent Agenda which includes 9-Business of the Board Items; 9.1-9.7; 10-Curriculum & Instruction Item 10.1-10.2; 11-Personnel Matters 11.1-11.13; 12-Business Office Matters 12.1-12.7.
