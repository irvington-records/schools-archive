AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE DAYTOP PREPARATORY SCHOOL
AND IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT

THIS AGREEMENT made on 6 November 2019 between The Daytop Preparatory School, Rockland 620 Route

303, Blauvelt NY 10913, (hereinafter called “DPS”) and Irvington Union Free School District. 6 Dows Lane
Irvington, NY 10533 (hereinafter called “Referring Schoo! District”),

SSETH:

WHEREAS, DPS is a registered nonpublic school chartered by the Regents of the University of the State of New
York as a non-profit educational corporation to establish, conduct, operate and maintain an educational program, grades
seven through twelve, for children of school age who are involved in the Samaritan Daytop Village, Inc, rehabilitation
program; and

WHEREAS, the Referring School District, is duly empowered by law to enter into a contract with DPS for the
instruction of children of school age; for grades 7 through 12, and

WHEREAS, the scope of services provided shall include academic services in accordance with State Education
regulations, using teachers certified by the New York State Education Department. The program consists of core
instruction for grades 7 — 12 in English, Mathematics, Social Studies, Science, and other major courses of study as agreed
with the Referring School District. All pupils will receive 5.5 hours of instruction per day, These services will be
provided in the facility closest to the pupil’s home in either Daytop Rockland or in Daytop Suffolk; and

WHEREAS, DPS shall maintain monthly attendance records which shall be submitted to the Referring School
District at the end of each month. If a child has been absent for a period of five (5) or more consecutive days, the
attendance record shall indicate the reason for the absence to the extent known to DPS, and to the extent permitted by law;
and

WHEREAS, DPS will furnish written reports of each pupil’s educational progress to the Referring School
District. DPS will render such reports at any time that such reports are made to the parents of the pupil covered by the
terms of this agreement, and will render such additional reports as may be required by the Referring School District. At a
minimum, all reports shall be furnished at the end of each semester and at the termination of the Agreement; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Referring School District has determined that DPS will provide
instruction to the pupil whose name, address and birthday is listed on Addendum “A” annexed hereto and made a part
hereof.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein contained, the parties hereto do agree as
follows:

6 DPS shall provide a program of instruction to Pupils pursuant to the laws of the State of New York and
the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education, specifically for this pupil whose name(s) appear(s) on
Addendum “A” annexed hereto and made part hereof.

2. DPS shall report to the Referring School District concerning cach pupil’s educational program and
progress in writing at the end of each semester, i.e., January 30th and June 30th, or upon termination of
this Agreement. DPS shall render additional information upon request by the Referring School District.

3. DPS shall make its personnel available to personnel designated by the Referring School District for
purposes of educational reviews and program visitations by prior arrangement between the personnel
involved,

[OCR]
4, The term of this agreement shall be the period within which said pupil is enrolled in The Samaritan
Daytop Village, Inc. rehabilitation program, except that the term of this Agreement is not to exceed the
2019-2020 School Calendar of the DPS, and is to run concurrently therewith.

5. The Referring School District shall pay tuition to DPS at the rate of $ 300 per pupil per day, regardless of
attendance, pursuant to paragraph 7 herein.

6. ‘The Referring School District shall be responsible for payment for each day of enrollment of each pupil
whose name is listed on Addendum “A” prior to termination of this Agreement. For billing purposes,
days of service shall conform to the DPS school calendar.

7. Payment by the Referring School District shall be due within 30 days of receipt of the invoice from DPS.

8. For Pupil information, the parties acknowledge that the agreements, authorizations and consents permitted
herein are in full satisfaction of, and in compliance with the provisions of all Local, State and Federal
laws relating to the undersigned’s rights to confidentiality as sel forth in 42 U. S. C. 290dd, 42 CFR Part
2, and the Health Insurance Portability & Accountability Act (HIPAA), of 1996 42 U. S.C. 1320d et seq.,
45 CFR Parts 160 and 164, and The Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), 20 U.S.C. §
1232g; 34 CFR Part 99, 34 CFR § 99.31, or otherwise.

9. This Agreement shall terminate immediately upon any of the following occurrences:

a. Discharge of the pupil from the Samaritan Daytop Village, Inc. rehabilitation program;

b. Termination of the Charter of DPS by the Regents of the University of the State of New York;

c. Determination by DPS that it cannot provide an appropriate educational placement for the Pupil
pursuant to the Education Law of the State of New York and the Regulations of the Commissioner of
Education;

d. Failure by the Referring School District to abide by all of the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

10. This Agreement between DPS and the Referring School District may not be altered, varied or
contradicted except by a similar writing executed by both parties hereto.

IN WITNESS WHEREOEF, the parties hereto have signed this Agreement the day and year first written,

Referring School District The Daytop Preparatory School
