5. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

Information: 5.1 District Updates, News, Events

Dr. Harrison spoke to reopening school in September with reference to NYS state and Westchester county guidelines. Our reopening plan considers the national CDC guidelines as a framework for reference. We are confident in our ability to re-open safely. The re-opening Steering Committee has vetted these plans, the committee includes input from parents, teachers and Dr. Huff, the school medical advisor. We are still awaiting adjustments from the state and the county. There is a universal mask mandate for all people entering schools. We will not be using barriers in classrooms. We will be doing contract tracing and necessary follow-up. In partnership with the Department County of Health, we will use a saliva-based PCR test. We will be limiting large group meetings and pivoting to virtual meetings instead. We will continue with proper cleaning and sanitation protocols. Vaccination status will be requested for contract tracing purposes. There will be daily screening of athletes, especially high-risk athletes. Once this guidance is issued, we will make any necessary adjustments to the plan. The hope is to have the final plan out to everyone by the end of next week.

Dr. Duffy reported on several exciting activities upcoming for the 2021-22 school year. First, there will be a two-day administrative retreat planned at the O'Hara Nature Center. This will be an opportunity for the staff to connect with each other, reflect, review goals and identify new priorities and gain strength for the coming school year. At the new staff orientation on September 1st we will be meeting all of our new, incoming teachers. At this meeting there will be an overview of district goals, and teachers will meet with district staff and with their mentors. Teachers will also have time to set-up their classrooms and goals for the year. Dr. Harrison and Dr. Duffy will also speak virtually to all staff, welcoming them for the new school year.
