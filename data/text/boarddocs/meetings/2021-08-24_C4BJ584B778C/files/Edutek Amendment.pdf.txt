SECOND AMENDMENT

AMENDMENT made this 5th day of August, 2021 to the July 8, 2020 Agreement,
between the IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT (hereinafter referred to as
the “School District”) and EDU TEK LTD. (hereinafter referred to as the “Consultant’).

WHEREAS, the parties entered into an Agreement dated July 8, 2020, establishing the rights and
responsibilities of the Consultant and the School District for services to be provided by the Consultant to
the School District; and

WHEREAS, the parties entered into a first amendment to said Agreement dated July 6, 2021
extending the term of the Agreement as follows: July 1, 2021 through July 31, 2021; and

WHEREAS, the parties are desirous of providing for a second amendment extending the term of
the Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, based upon the mutual covenants and understandings between the parties,
itis understood and agreed:

FIRST: EFFECT OF THE AMENDMENT

All the terms and provisions set forth at length in the aforementioned Agreement shall continue
in full force and effect during the term thereof (prorated where applicable based upon the
extension term set forth below), except as expressly modified herein.

SECOND: TERM

The second paragraph on page 2 of the Agreement as well as the Project Commencement
paragraph on Page 10 shall be amended to reflect an extension of the term of the Agreement as
follows: July 1, 2021 through September 30, 2021. Said amendment shall be effective retroactive

to July 1, 2021.
IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT
Dated: By:
Board President
EDU TEK LTD,

Dated: 8/5/21 week _ Matthew Orifici
