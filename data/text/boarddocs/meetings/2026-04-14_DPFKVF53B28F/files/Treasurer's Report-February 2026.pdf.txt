Irvington Union Free School District
Monthly Financial Highlights

February 2026
Cash Balance:

© Our February overall cash position was $50.5 million, which is a healthy cash flow for this time of year.

© Cash received in the Real Property & Other Taxes category is Greenburgh’s Tax Collection payment for $19,601,867.18. These funds are
from the second half of the fiscal year, for payment of property taxes due. While we record the full revenue in July, we receive the funds over
time. The district has also received $277,583 from Westchester County for the 4" Qtr. 2025 sales tax collected from municipalities.

© State and Federal Aid Cash Receipts included IDEA 611 Grane Aid $74,580, Lunch Aid of $55,204. and VLT Lottery aid totaling $34,959.01

Revenue:

@ General Fund Revenue through February was $74,675,587 or 94.3% of the budgeted revenue.

© The Use of Money & Property mostly includes interest earnings from all District general fund bank accounts. The District has earned
$871,318. in interest earnings through February. This month, a good portion of our NYCLASS investment CD’s had matured giving a
favorable increase in interest earnings revenue. By investing in NYCLASS CD’s, the District was able to lock in a 6-month interest rate at
4.075%, compared to average monthly interest rate of 3.566% for February. Prior year, the District had an interest earnings budget surplus
due to the high interest rate environment. The 25-26 anticipated interest revenue earning budget was increased compared to the prior year as
interest rates are still relatively high (although rates are currently decreasing). This explains why February 2026 shows less percentage of
revenue recognized at this point of time when compared to last year. We will continue to work with our partners at NYCLASS for the best
investment opportunities to ensure we maximize out interest earnings revenue.

© Misc. Sources is at 96.8 o of the budget, as we received funds owed from prior year tax payments for bisected properties and collect prior
year JCOS related services reimbursement from a neighboring School District.

Expenditures:

¢ Actual General Fund Expenses totaled approximately $9.65 million for February. This brings the total year to date expenditures to
$41,690,427. Including outstanding encumbrances, 7% of the budget remains, which is a typical amount at this point in the year.

¢ Under General Support and Benefits Category, both the Tax Certiorari Expense and Other Benefits will need a Budget Adjustment transfer
from Reserves set aside for these expenses.
