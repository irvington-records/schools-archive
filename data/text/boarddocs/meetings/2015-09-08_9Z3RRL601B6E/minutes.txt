	4. Superintendent's Report and Opening of School Reports

-
		Dr. Harrison reported that spirits are very high across the district and he noted that enrollment is up from June. It is currently 1,791 in contrast to 1,753 in June, with over 70 new families in Irvington.

		Dr. Harrison commented on the first days of school with regard to transportation were more complex than in previous years, including having a new bus company. All concerns are being addressed with the bus company and Ardsley Transportation. There has been improvement today with most buses arriving on time and will continue to monitor to ensure that our expectations are being met.

-
		With respect to the computer breach that occurred at the close of the 2014-15 school year, there is still student discipline and legal issues that prevent too much being reported. The security assessment conducted over the summer found few critical issues and determined that the District’s infrastructure and protocols are compliant with standard industry practices. Recommendations from the security audit are being implemented that were identified through the analysis, including staff training on security measures including establishing passwords, system will force staff to change passwords every 30 days, and two factor authentication of administrators' credentials. Outdated programs and open relay found have been shut down, etc. Other protocols are being implemented on the management side to further ensure that our systems secure.

-
		Final approvals have been received from SED for the projects that the community approved last October. Timelines will be mapped out with the RFP process beginning as early as this winter. The removal of the old district office as well as the reconstruction of the Dows Lane offices, nurse’s office and District Office took place over the summer and completed on time. Some work might be done earlier or possibly over Christmas break.

-
		Dr. Harrison highlighted several student recognitions: Madison Carelli and Conor Winton were awarded the Millie Kibrick Service Award; Clara Montgomery and Ethan Weinstein have been chosen to participate in the NYSSMA All-State Music Conference. Clara will perform with the String Orchestra and Ethan will join the Wind Ensemble. The students will be recognized by the Board for their accomplishments at a future board meeting.

	Action: 6.1 Approval of Superintendent's Employment Agreement

	Mr. Whitney noted that it is the Board's most important responsibility to evaluate the superintendent and which the evaluation or contents are not shared or discussed with the public. After checking with other districts, he found that this is an area which is not discussed publicly and doesn’t believe that Irvington should be any different. He also stated that the superintendent’s current contract is due to expire in June, 2017, and that the contract before the Board calls for a two year extension to the current agreement. He also noted that new to this contract is that there is no stated compensation amounts for the future years so that current board does not make such determinations but that future Boards will determine compensation. 	11. Board of Education Discussion

	Mr. Whitney noted that last year's district goals were created over multiple meetings and believes that they can be rolled over for the 2015-16 school year as they are still as pertinent and will dove-tail with the strategic planning process. Mr. Montgomery noted that the purpose of last year’s goals was to encapsulate what the Board felt the District should be doing and through the Superintendent's goals to witness the goals’ achievements. They were meant to be very high level. Ms. Palmieri asked about how the outcomes would be assessed to see if goals are being met and the need for a mechanism to review and measure if they are being achieved. Ms. Palmieri looks at them as both vision and goals. Goals must be quantified in order to develop a matrix for evaluation. Mr. Whitney believes that time would be better spent focusing on a strategic plan. Mr. Montgomery agrees but also to spend time on developing goals for the Superintendent and be more proactive as a Board. Mr. Hanna also believes that developing a strategic plan is a more important discussion. There will be further discussion and approval at the September 29th meeting on this subject.

	The Board discussed future discussion items for agendas: Mr. Hanna would like to talk about the management and revenue brought in by campus theater. He noted that the theater is under Bev Miller's responsibility, the staff reserves it through her office and that it is rented from outside organizations and feels that there might be opportunity to bring in a manager to help faculty in-house but also work with outside groups, asking if the Board might be interested in giving permission to administration to explore this potential and would we be willing to raise the cost of renting the space to outside organizations. Mr. Montgomery asked for data regarding that the current utilization. Mr. Hanna would like to provide a CAB report and a WPSBA update. This will be done at the next meeting.

	Mr. Montgomery would like to see what other topics for discussion. Mr. Whitney will share the draft of future presentations/discussions with the rest of the Board.

	Ms. Palmieri realizes that buses are well used by DL and MSS but has the perception that they are not overly used by the HS and asked if an audit has been completed on actual ridership. She also feels that the community has let us know that they are concerned about our computer security and proposes that a subcommittee be formed to oversee and focus on that aspect of our technology. Regarding the site committee advertisement, she is not sure if it is clear what the Site Committee is responsible for.

	Ms. Miller addressed Ms. Palmieri's question on transportation utilization and shared information on undertaking an analysis of transportation usage. Mr. Whitney asked Ms. Miller to determine what data can be provided by the Ardsley Transportation. Mr. Grados spoke of the conversation several years ago about eliminating transportation at the high school level due to low ridership. Ms. Palmieri noted that she is also interested in this with regard to adjusting the start time for the high school and what would be the implications for the middle school, etc.

	Mr. Montgomery asked what communications are given to new families to Irvington to guide them through various aspects of the District and could include information on the site committee, etc. The communications committee will discuss.
