        IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT


                            2014-15 District Goals



  1. Develop a long-term plan, including a clarification of the District’s
     values and priorities, an assessment of its achievement of those values
     and priorities and the development of a detailed 5-year plan for
     attaining the values and priorities.

  2. Provide top-quality instruction for all students.

  3. Develop and maintain a curriculum that offers all students the
     opportunity to attain their academic potential.

  4. Ensure that each school provides a rich and supportive environment for
     all students, welcoming all students, fostering their academic and social
     development, and preparing them well for the next phase of their
     education.

  5. Uphold high standards for District staff.

  6. Use all taxes and other funds raised by the District efficiently and
     effectively, and maintain the District’s fiscal health to balance the
     interests of current and future taxpayers.




Adopted: 11/4/14
