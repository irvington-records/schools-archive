Subject: 13.1 Cooperative Purchasing Agreement - The Interlocal Purchasing System (TIPS)
Type: Action (Consent)

There is no fee to join this purchasing cooperative.  This cooperative solicits bids which specify piggybacking which is required by GML, for district use. This creates better pricing with greater competition. This cooperative can be used in lieu of individual district bids which could result in higher prices, with fewer bidders participating. The use of cooperatives is not applicable in all buying situations but opens options for the District.
