    ^ Notes on Recommended Resolutions:
    #3 Concerns about legislators or Board of Regents mandating curriculum, but resolution targeted at school boards.
    #4 Advocacy position may improve teacher retention. Alternatively, funds could be used to improve teacher salaries which
        would help with teacher recruitment and retention.
    #6 In a high salary region, added flexibility on BOCES district superintendent compensation would be helpful in recruitment.
    #8 Amending the current limit on building aid for a 5-year period will incent long-term capital planning and can be cost
        effective for districts.
    #12 While we do not support a state mandate on cell phone usage, we support funding for policies implemented by districts.
    #15 While recognizing the importance of children learning to swim, the resolution would siphon funds from general
        education to support only one type of athletic facility. Partnerships with local swimming facilities are an alternative.
    #16 While providing families with an opt out of busing will be more efficient, cost effective and environmentally friendly, we
        support ensuring that all students have access to transportation services when needed.
    #22 Although we were supportive of the resolution it may be unnecessary as advocacy has already been undertaken by
        NYSSBA and LHEC, and NYSED has so far declined to bring the proposals back to the Board of Regents.
    #23 While school calendars are increasingly difficult to manage, we prefer that districts are allowed more flexibility with the
        days/hours requirements, not just by extending opening and closing days of the school year.
