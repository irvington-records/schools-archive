        IUFSD
Safety Enhancements
       2013-14

Objective
The District holds the responsibility of providing the safest
possible environment for its students to learn and for its
employees to work.

This responsibility includes, but is not limited to:
• Facility maintenance
• Effective communication
  • Internally and externally
• Emergency preparedness
  • Development and maintenance of detailed emergency plans
  • Practice various drill scenarios
  • Staff training

Addition: Security Greeters
Security greeters are now employed at each school in the
District.
• Outside security firm hired to provide security greeters with
  industry experience
• All doors are locked at the elementary schools
• Campus buildings are open, but visitors are directed to the
  security desk
• Each visitor must sign in and present valid identification at the
  security desk
• Visitor passes are issued to each visitor
• Passes expire in 24 hours

Addition: Two-way Radios
• To improve communication in and around our schools, staff
  now have two-way radios that function without interference
  and “dead-zones”
• Radios have been assigned to:
  •    Administrators
  •    Security desks
  •    Nurses
  •    Head custodians & custodians
  •    Outside recess/PE
  •    Irvington Police Department who performs daily communication
      checks with the schools

Addition: Electronic Entry Systems
To increase security at Main Street School and Dows Lane
Elementary School:
• An electronic entry (buzzer) system has been installed at Main
  Street School
  • The security desk and main office have access to the system
• Additional access to the electronic entry (buzzer) system has
  been installed for Dows Lane security desk
• This system is not practical for the Campus. We continue to
  explore options to enhance security.

Safety and Security Assessment
In early 2013, the District contracted with a security firm, CLPS,
to assess our emergency plans.

CLPS’ assessment informed the development of new District and
school-level emergency plans, adjusted security protocols, and
identified facility improvement needs.

Recommendations have been implemented District-wide.

Updated: Safety Plans
• Building Plans:
  • A specific safety plan that has been shared with their staff (not for
    public distribution)
  • Building-level Safety Team
  • School Emergency Response Team
  • Post-Incident Response Team
• Emergency drills are scheduled on a regular basis at each school
  • Police participation
  • Each drill is debriefed to increase efficiency and safety
• Created “Grab and Go” bags for each building principal – includes
  emergency supplies (radios, flashlights, emergency vests, student
  contact information)
• Nurses already had “Grab and Go” bags – District has worked to
  insure they are all comparable and meet requirements

DISTRICT-WIDE SAFETY TEAM
• Meets quarterly to discuss any concerns associated with safety and
  security.
• The committee has representatives from across the community
  including:
  • Administrators
  • Police Department
  • Fire Department
  • Transportation Department
  • Teachers
  • Parent Representatives
  • Head custodians
  • CLPS representative

Continued Focus
• Significant progress has been made in the past nine months to
  improve security and safety in our schools.

• We will continue to explore methods to enhance safety and security.

• We strive to be proactive, but are now better prepared to respond
  to a variety of situations.

• At the Middle School and High School, we must continue to assess
  the “open campus” concept

 The work of school safety, like our commitment to providing an
                excellent education never ends!

Thank you!
