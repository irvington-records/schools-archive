	Resolution: RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington School District hereby authorizes members of the Board of Education and the District Clerk to attend conferences pursuant to Section 77-b of the General Municipal Law. BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that all actual and necessary expenses of travel, means and lodging and all necessary tuition fees incurred in connection with attendance at a conference shall be charged against the School District and the amount thereof shall be audited, allowed and paid in the same manner as are Claims against said District.
