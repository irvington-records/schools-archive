John DeSantis

of Hastings-on-Hudson was elected to the BOCES Board in 1996, after having served for 18
years on the Hastings-on-Hudson Board of Education, including two terms as President. He
received an Award of Excellence for School Board Service from the Mid-Hudson School
Study Council. Mr. DeSantis is currently the Senior Director of Technical Services and
Special Projects for the school of Arts & Sciences at New York University. He has also served
on the Executive Board of the Greenburgh Council of Civic Organizations. Mr. DeSantis was
also a co-founder of the New York University Federal Credit Union which was established in
1982. He also served as Chairman on that Board for an additional 10 years. In addition,
Mr. DeSantis served as a representative of the New York University Administrative

Management Council for 30 years and as a New York University Senator for four two year
terms.
