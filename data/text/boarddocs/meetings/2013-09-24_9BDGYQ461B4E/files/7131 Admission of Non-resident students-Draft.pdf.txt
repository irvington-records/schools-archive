SUBJECT:       ADMISSION OF NON-RESIDENT STUDENTS

     It is the policy of the Board of Education that the District shall not permit any non-resident
student to enroll in the District’s schools except in the following circumstances:
Future Residents

      Upon written application and approval by the Superintendent of Schools or his/her
designee, the child(ren) of families who expect to become residents of the District and who have
signed a lease for rental or a contract to buy or to build a residence in the District shall be
admitted to school during the semester in which they make application subject to the following:
At the time of application for enrollment, the parent(s)/legal guardian(s) shall be required to
furnish the District with a copy of the lease or contract that establishes their future residence
within the District within sixty (60) calendar days of the student(s)’ enrollment, the
parent(s)/legal guardian(s) shall be required to substantiate that they have become residents of
the District in accordance with Board Policy 7130. At the time of enrollment, the parent(s)/legal
guardian(s) shall also be required to execute an acknowledgement that failure, for any reason, to
provide appropriate evidence of residency within said sixty (60) day period will result in the
student(s)’ immediate removal from school in the District and the parent(s)/legal guardian(s)
responsibility for immediate payment of tuition from the date of enrollment through the date of
removal at the annual rate established by the Board of Education pursuant to the formula set
forth by the New York State Education Department. In the case of extraordinary circumstances,
the Superintendent, or his/her designee, may grant a thirty (30) day extension of time to
substantiate residency.

     Unless and until such time as residency is substantiated as set forth above, transportation of
the student to and from school shall be the sole responsibility of the parent(s) or legal
guardian(s).

Former Residents

      Any student whose parent(s) or legal guardian(s) move out of the District on or after May
1st of any school year may apply, in writing, to the Superintendent of Schools or his/her designee
for approval to complete the school year without payment of tuition. The determination of
whether to grant or deny such application rests solely with the District. Continued attendance
shall be conditioned upon proper behavior in accordance with the District’s Code of Conduct.
Any violation of the Code of Conduct by the student(s) during the period of continued
attendance which would result in any suspension from school shall result in the student’s
immediate removal from attendance. The student(s) are entitled only to minimal due process
such as Notice and an opportunity to respond prior to termination.
      A student who has completed grades 9, 10 and 11 in the Irvington High School and whose
parent(s) or legal guardian(s) move out of the District prior after the start of the second semester
of their senior yearto the completion of the student(s) senior year, may apply, in writing, to the
Superintendent of Schools or his/her designee for permission to complete the senior year without
payment of tuition. Continued attendance shall be conditioned upon proper behavior in
accordance with the District’s Code of Conduct. Any violation of the Code of Conduct by the

student during the period of continued attendance which would result in any suspension from
school shall result in the student’s immediate removal from attendance.

      In the event approval is granted by the Superintendent of Schools or his/her designee for
continued attendance, as set forth above, the transportation of student to and from school shall be
the sole responsibility of the parent(s) or legal guardian(s).
Student not living with a parent or legal guardian

      Pursuant to Education Law Section 3212, the residence of a student is presumed to be the
residence of his/her parent(s)/legal guardian(s). However, in certain limited circumstances this
presumption may be rebutted. The following are the limited circumstances under which that
presumption may be overcome:

     a)    When a Court has appointed a temporary or permanent legal guardian and that legal
           guardian is a resident of the District.

     b)    In certain circumstances, a child who resides with someone in the District other than
           his/her parent(s)/legal guardian(s) may be eligible to attend the District’s schools free
           of tuition if the District determines that the student’s parent(s)/legal guardian(s) have
           relinquished total (and presumably permanent) care, custody and control of the child
           to another individual(s) residing within the geographic boundaries of the District. In
           such case, it shall be incumbent upon the child’s parent(s)/legal guardian(s) and the
           proposed custodian(s) to establish that the requisite transfer of care, custody and
           control has been made.

           Accordingly, the District shall require the child’s parent(s)/legal guardian(s) to
           complete a Parent Affidavit concerning the transfer of care, custody and control of the
           child to someone residing within the District. The Parent Affidavit must be signed
           and notarized. The District shall also require the person(s) to whom care, custody
           and control have been transferred, to complete a Custodian Affidavit concerning the
           transfer of care, custody and control of the child. The Custodian Affidavit must be
           signed and notarized.

           Legal guardian(s) and Custodian(s) should be aware that any student who moves into
           the District (without his/her parent(s)/legal guardian(s)) for the primary purpose of
           attending the District’s schools shall be deemed a non-resident student and may be
           permitted to enroll upon the payment of tuition, in accordance with the below
           provisions, i.e. “Admission of Non-resident students on a Tuition Basis”.

     c)    Exchange students placed in the Irvington Union Free School District by a recognized
           exchange program and approved by the Superintendent of Schools.
     If permission to attend school tuition-free is denied by the Superintendent, an appeal may
be made to the Commissioner of Education for review.

Admission of Non-resident students on a Tuition Basis
     The Board of Education recognizes that the District's reputation and excellence in
providing education has resulted in interest in attending the District on a tuition basis. The

Board of Education will consider applications for tuition students on a case by case basis. The
Board of Education, or its designee, reserves the right to accept or reject any proposed tuition
student. The Board of Education, or its designee, will undertake consideration of such
applications only if:
     a)      Admission of tuition students shall not result in an increase in teaching staff.
     b)      The parent(s) or legal guardian(s) shall be required to provide transportation for the
             student to and from school.

     c)      The admission of tuition students does not require additional facilities.
     d)      There is available classroom space within class size guidelines and with current levels
             of staffing.

     Enrollment of non-resident students is conditioned upon proper behavior in accordance
with the District’s Code of Conduct. Any violation of the Code of Conduct by such student
which would result in any suspension from school shall result in the student’s immediate
removal from attendance. The student(s) is entitled only to minimal due process such as Notice
and an opportunity to respond prior to termination.

       The Administration shall develop and implement an application form incorporating the
criteria in this policy. Prior to consideration of the application, the parent(s)/legal guardian(s)
must sign a Release of Records for the student's current school. No more than ten (10) tuition
students shall be admitted per grade level.

Tuition Rate and Payment
a)   Tuition Rates: The tuition rate will be established annually by the Board of Education in
     pursuant to the formula set forth by the New York State Education Department.

b)   Payment of Tuition: Payment shall be made no less than twenty (20) business days in
     advance of each semester during a given school year. No child will be allowed to attend
     school in any semester where payment has not been made, as provided above. In the event
     payment is made by check, which is not honored for any reason, all fees incurred by the
     District in connection with same shall be immediately paid by the parent(s)/legal
     guardian(s) and may result in removal of the student from the District’s schools.

c)   Yearly Basis: Non-resident tuition students are admitted for one (1) school year. The
     Board of Education will make determinations for continued enrollment in subsequent years
     in accordance with the provisions of this Policy.
