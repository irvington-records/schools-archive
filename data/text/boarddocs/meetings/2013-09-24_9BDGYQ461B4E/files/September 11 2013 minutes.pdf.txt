Present: Dr. Harrison, Maria Kashkin, Phil Whitney (via telephone), Gary Knowles, Beverly Miller

    1. RFP bids received for bluestone replacement on the campus – A decision was tabled on a
       recommendation to the Board as additional information was needed. Beverly will contact LAN

       under discussion.
    2. Painting of campus athletic tower – Paula Morley has presented a proposal to the District for
       the Friends of Football group to volunteer to paint the athletic tower, including painting a
       bulldog logo onto the tower. A decision was tabled pending discussion with Ms. Morley
       regarding logo design considerations and quality control of execution. Additionally, the district
       would need to be named as an additional insured on their insurance coverage. Gary will contact
       Ms. Morley to obtain additional information.
    3. Painting of paw prints on campus hill driveway – A request was received from Paula Morley to
       allow the Friends of Football group to volunteer to paint paw prints on the campus hill driveway.
       The committee approved with the condition that the work be performed with Gary’s
       supervision. Gary will contact Ms. Morley to arrange the painting.

5. Update on campus window hardware – There have been complaints from middle school
   teaching staff regarding the new window hardware that was installed. Concerns have been
   room temperature; methods of egress in case of emergency. Per Gary, the cost to convert
   current windows to escape windows (per staff requests for 4 windows) is $3,600.00 per window.
   Upon additional review by LAN, it was determined that the district is in compliance with all SED
   codes regarding egress. The committee agreed to not proceed with recommending any changes
   to the current system as we are in compliance.

   The cost to accommodate the teaching staff’s requests for additional ventilation would cost
   $18,500 to revise the current window hardware to open an additional 3” on all windows, except
   along the walkway (1st and 2nd floors). The reason for installing the replacement hardware was
   for safety reasons. The committee does not agree to proceed with any revisions to the current
   hardware configuration. Should room temperatures be an ongoing concern, the committee
   recommended that Gary reviewed each concern, on a case-by-case basis, for the need to
   purchase room fans.
