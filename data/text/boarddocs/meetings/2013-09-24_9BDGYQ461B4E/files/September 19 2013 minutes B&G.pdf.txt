                            BUILDINGS & GROUNDS COMMITTEE

                                 September 19, 2013 7:30 am

                                        MEETING MINUTES
Present: Dr. Harrison, Phil Whitney (via telephone), Gary Knowles, Beverly Miller

    1. RFP bids received for bluestone replacement on the campus – Phil & Gary toured the sites on
       Sept. 13th. Upon review of the district’s options, the committee agreed to recommend to the
       Board of Education awarding the contract to the lowest responsible bidder, Raines & Nagler.



    2. Painting of campus athletic tower – The decision regarding painting of a logo on the tower is
       still tabled. However, the committee had agreed to allow the Friends of Football to proceed
       with their offer of donating the work of painting the athletic tower – 2 sides green, 2 sides white
       – with the anticipated completion to be before Homecoming Weekend. An outside contractor,
       who is a regular contractor for the District, performed the work and it was completed before
       Homecoming.



    3. Update on campus hill lighting project – Under the Safe Routes to Schools grant, the Village
       will be installing lights on the campus hill. However, before that work can begin, it was
       discovered that the current wiring on the District’s light poles is inadequate for the new lights. It
       requires #4 wiring but our poles have #6 wiring which is in poor condition and needed replacing.
       The District received a quote for approx. $13,000 to upgrade our pole wiring. It is anticipated
       that the wire replacement work will begin the week of Sept. 23rd. The electrical contractor will
       coordinate both projects to be done simultaneously.
