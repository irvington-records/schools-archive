SUBJECT: NAMING FACILITIES

The Board of Education shall have sole authority to name or rename district facilities, including
but not limited to buildings, athletic fields, tracks, libraries, gymnasiums, auditoriums/theaters,
and multipurpose rooms. The following guidelines will apply to requests for naming facilities.
  I.      Procedure

                               A.    Any member of the school district community or
                                    the community at large can submit a request for
                                    a facility naming consideration.
                               B.   All requests for naming facilities shall be
                                    submitted to the Superintendent and shall
                                    include reasons the facility should be named
                                    after the proposed individual. Also required
                                    shall be a biography of the individual to be
                                    honored, a letter of permission from the
                                    individual and/or the family and other pertinent
                                    information like the proposed design plans for
                                    signage and funding source.

                               C.   The Superintendent shall submit request to the
                                    Board of Education for consideration. The Board
                                    shall vote for approval of the naming request.
  II.    Criteria for
         Facility Names
                               A.    In evaluating such requests, the Board shall
                                    consider the individual’s contributions to the
                                    District together with their length of service.
  III.   Plans for
         Signage
                               A.   Upon Board approval of the request to name the
                                    facilities, proposed design plans should be
                                    submitted to the Superintendent of Schools
                                    who, in collaboration with the Director of
                                    Facilities, will present them to the Buildings and
                                    Grounds Committee for review.
                               B.   Upon recommendation by the Buildings and
                                    Grounds Committee, the Board of Education
                                    shall approve such design plans, including the
                                    approval to affix building plaques to any District
                                    facility.
                               C.   The plaque may be purchased with District
                                    funds authorized by the Board or through funds
                                    received by the District from students,

                      community, or parent groups or from individuals.
