Irvington 1130

1130 NEWS MEDIA RELATIONS

The Board of Education invites and welcomes the active participation of all forms of
media, print and electronic, in educating the public and improving education within
the district and the wider community. The Board and Superintendent of Schools will
make every reasonable effort to cooperate with the media by providing accurate
information about district operations, to the extent permissible by statute and
regulation.

The Board President is designated as the spokesperson for the Board when the
Board is making a statement on an issue. All statements of the Board will be
released through the Office of the Superintendent and/or the District Clerk. No other
member of the Board individually will speak for, or in the name of, the Board unless
by explicit direction of the Board. Board members should emphasize to the media
when asked to speak as a Board member that they can only speak as private citizens
unless they have been empowered by the Board to speak for it.
If Board members choose to blog on their own or if the member maintains a social
networking presence, the Board member must ensure that it is clear that the
postings do not represent the Board as a whole.

The Superintendent is designated as the spokesperson for the district.

All staff intending to release information to the media must first notify the
Superintendent. The Superintendent shall establish all necessary procedures to
govern day-to-day interactions between the schools and the news media. No other
employee or contractor of the District will speak for, or in the name of, the District
unless by explicit direction of the Superintendent. The Superintendent will establish
all necessary procedures to govern day-to-day interactions between the District
schools, and the media and the community.
