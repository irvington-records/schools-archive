                               Safety Committee Meeting
                                       Minutes
                                     Oct. 23, 2013

Present: Beverly Miller (chairperson), Dr. Harrison, Jimmy Ruffler, Artie McCormack, David
Cohen, Rich Pittore, Gary Knowles, Maria Kashkin, Randy Lichtenwalner, David Sottile, Kari
Carlson, Stephen DiGiovanni, Florence Cerbone, Jamie Wilson, Mike Morano, Mike Toolan, Vic
Granuzzo and Debbi Centofanti
Absent: Jimmy Ruffler, Artie McCormack, Laurie Noonan

The meeting opened with a review of the minutes from the June 12, 2013 meeting.

Old Business
     Glass in Wooden Doors at MSS – Over 20 need to be replaced. Angel has being doing it
       time permitting. Stationary panes at main entrance have to be replaced also and might
       need a contractor to do it.
     Netting for lacrosse field- Artie researching.
     Windows – MS – 4 rooms do not need a rescue window according to SED regulations.
       Discussion commenced with Middle School still concerned that students do not have a
       way out. Dr. Harrison noted that the window could be broken if necessary and these
       were not the only rooms in the district that didn’t have an egress to the outside.
     Safe Routes to School – Walkways are done with the exception of some minor areas that
       Gary is addressing. The lights on the High School hill have been installed and just
       waiting for Con Ed to hook up with main line. The district will pursue implementing a
       walking bus program, per the Safe Routes to School grant.
     East Field- Should have recommendation of remedy from DEC in a month or so. No
       immediate reopening of the east field in the near future. We will probably need to have a
       bond resolution.
     Security – An outside security firm was hired and provides security greeters for each of
       the 4 building entrances. New protocols are in place for visitors. All the doors that can
       be locked are except for some at the campus. Portable radios are in use and are
       connected with administration and police. Artie monitors the security greeters and does
       walk-throughs of the buildings. Grab-n-go bags are in each school with supplies and
       student information in them. The District is always looking into new security measures
       and concerns.
     Bluestone repair at the Campus – an outside contractor removed the damaged and
       cracked bluestone and replaced it with concrete. This was a safety hazard.

New Business
    Dows Lane – In the older section of the school most rooms only have 2 outlets in them;
      therefore they are using power strips. Gary will look into the feasibility of an electrical
      upgrade and bring to Buildings & Grounds meeting.
    NYSIR Reports & recommendations- discussed items that needed corrections. Gary will
      be getting quotes for 2 areas of the campus that need to be addressed- Sidewalk Pole
      Covers and Sidewalk Curbs.
    Dows Lane- recommended that a notice be sent to parents regarding the procedures for
      the drop off area in the morning.

      The Village DPW informed Gary that the village was applying for a grant to get a
       transfer station for the Main St. School and then a generator so it could be used as an
       emergency shelter.
      Transportation – a few issues on private special ed. buses that were resolved. No current
       issues for the safety committee.

Accident Reports
    Dows Lane – 1, Main St.-0, Middle School – 5 ( 1 from sports), HS – 24 (22 from sports)
       Consistent with prior years

Respectfully submitted,
Debbi Centofanti
