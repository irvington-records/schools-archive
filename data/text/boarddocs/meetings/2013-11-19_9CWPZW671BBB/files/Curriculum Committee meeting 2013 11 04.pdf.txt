Summary of Curriculum Committee meeting, Nov. 4, 2013

The Curriculum Committee consists of Dr. Harrison, Dr. Kor, Ms. Kerner, and myself. We had
our first meeting on Nov. 4.

The meeting focused on issues related to the transition to the Common Core Learning
Standards. Dr. Kor showed us samples of a unit plan for Grade 5 ELA. This sample was on
Fantasy.

We had a broad discussion on the adoption of the Common Core. This included the distinction
between standards and curriculum, and the work that is being done, particularly in K-5, in
developing detailed curriculum plans around the standards.

We also discussed our plans for the committee’s work during the year. We are planning to
cover the following areas:

       Changes in the elementary math curriculum

       Curriculum mapping and related options

       Definition of the skillset & knowledge our graduates should possess, and how this filters
       back into instructional and curriculum goals for K-12.

       APPR and raising the bar on instruction

We have also raised the issue of our writing curriculum.

John Montgomery (Nov. 19, 2013)
