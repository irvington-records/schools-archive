Through this community solar program, the District would lease our roofs to Brightcore Energy who would install and maintain solar panels at their cost and provide the District lease payments.  The District would also receive credits on our Con Edison costs.  Brightcore won the bid by the New York Power Authority (NYPA) to be the exclusive solar developer in this program for Westchester County schools.  Upon completion of the analysis, the next step will be to enter into a formal lease agreement for ten years with options to renew for an additional ten years and then an additional five years.
