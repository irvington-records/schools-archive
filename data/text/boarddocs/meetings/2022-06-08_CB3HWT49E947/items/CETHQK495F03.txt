Meeting: Jun 08, 2022 - Regular Meeting
Date: 2022-06-08
Category: 9. Business Office Matters
Subject: 9.13 Authorization to Transfer from Workers Compensation Reserve to General Fund
Type: Action (Consent)

While the District is fully insured for new Worker's Comp claims, we remain self funded for claims prior to July 1, 2019, also known as tail claims.  The payments for existing tail claims now has exceeded budgeted funds and therefore, the reserve is needed.
