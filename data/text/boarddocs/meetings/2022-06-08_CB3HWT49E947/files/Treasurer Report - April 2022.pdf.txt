Irvington Union Free School District
Monthly Financial Highlights

April 2022

Cash Balance

Our April cash position was $40.41 million, a decrease of $4.66 million compated to the prior month.

Cash received in the Real Property & Other Taxes category included real property tax payment from the Town of Greenburgh for tax
collection during the month of February totaling $967,828.

State and Federal Aid Cash Receipts totaled $283,827 which BOCES Aid and the CRSSA closeout funds.

Cash disbursements during the month of April included normal monthly check watrants, two payroll runs, and multiple bond principal and
interest payments.

Revenue:

General Fund Revenue through April was $65,943,686 or 99.1% of the budgeted amount. This percentage is 0.6% higher when compared to
the same time last year.

We anticipate to receive about $238,100 for 3“ quarter sales tax in May. We expect both 3" and 4" quarter sales tax revenue will be higher
than projected. Thus, we will have a surplus in this revenue category.

Miscellaneous sources is higher than budgeted mainly due to higher E-rate reimbursement.

Expenditures:

Actual General Fund Expenses totaled approximately $6.41 million for April. This brings the total year to date expenditures to $46,464,834.
6% of the budget remains including the outstanding encumbrances.
Approximately $1,988,665 of bond principal and interest were paid in April. This consisted of thtee different bond payments.

‘The tax certiorari line is negative based on payments made from existing settlements. Other Insurance line is negative for worker's
compensation payments. We will plan to have a budget amendment on an upcoming agenda to fund these accounts from their respective
reserve funds.
