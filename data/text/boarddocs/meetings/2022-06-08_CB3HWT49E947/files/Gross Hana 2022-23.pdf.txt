Re: Employment Letter Agreement
Dear Hana:

This letter serves as your Employment Agreement with the Irvington Union Free School
District (hereinafter “the District") for the period of July 1, 2022 through June 30, 2023. The
Board of Education (hereinafter “the Board") has been advised that it is legally permissible
to contractually obligate itself to provide you with the wages and benefits described herein.
Itis acknowledged by you and the Board that the termination of your employment is subject
solely to various provisions of the Civil Service Laws of the State of New York and nothing
herein contained shall be deemed to have modified the foregoing in any respect. It is
acknowledged by you and the Board that this Agreement negates any prior Agreement
between you and the Board that relates to your terms and conditions of employment. Such
Agreements are null and void by the execution of this Agreement. Therefore, based upon
the mutual understandings between you and the Board, it is understood and agreed as
follows:

Scope of Employment

Effective July 1, 2022, you shall be employed as Athletic Trainer. You certify and
acknowledge that you are qualified to serve as an Athletic Trainer pursuant to Section
135.4(c)(7)(i)(d)(1) of the Regulations of the Commissioner of Education during the term of
your employment.

Salary

Your regular base salary for the year July 1, 2022 through June 30, 2023 shall be
$63,983.

Career Incr.

In addition to the foregoing, you shall receive a career increment in the amount of
$1,300 upon completion of ten (10) years of service; an additional $1,450 after completion
of fifteen (15) years of service; and an additional $ 1,600 after completion of twenty (20)
years of service in the District.

Benefits

In addition to the annual compensation specified above, you shall be entitled to
receive the following enumerated benefits:

1. Work Year

Your work year for the 2022-2023 school year shall be on an eleven (11) months basis
(effective July 1, 2022), except for the school holidays and recess periods designated on the
school calendar (other than days with athletic contests scheduled), vacation days, and use
of sick, personal, family illness days and bereavement days as well as any days off due to
school cancellation. You will work a maximum of thirty-five (35) hours per week inclusive of
one (1) hour per day for meals. A schedule specifying hours and dates will be mutually
agreed upon. The trainer will be responsible for arriving at Irvington HS for 2:00 pm on school
days and will stay until the day's sporting activities are completed. You will be required to
work weekend hours, as well as hours during school district break periods, contingent upon
the game and practice schedule as determined by the Athletic Director. Any hours worked
in excess of thirty-five (35) hours per week shall be paid at the hourly contractual rate and
must be pre-approved by the Athletic Director. In the event you work less that thirty-five (35)
hours for a particular week said hour(s) may be applied by the District in lieu of the payment
as set forth in the preceding sentence.

2: Sick Leave

Effective July 1, 2022, you shall be entitled to twelve (12) days of sick for the 2022-
2023 school year. Any unused sick leave days may be carried over and used during any
subsequent year of employment with no maximum accumulation.

A non-cumulative reserve of thirty (30) working days will be available to you for an
extended illness. You may only utilize this reserve once in your lifetime. Extended illness shall
mean illness or disability for thirty (30) or more continuous working days. This reserve can
only be accessed after utilization of all your personal accumulated sick leave. Thereafter,
you shall receive the difference between your salary and the amount paid for a substitute
until long term disability becomes effective.

3. Personal and Other Leaves

Effective July 1, 2022, you shall be entitled to four (4) days for the purpose of
transacting or attending to personal business which cannot be attended to other than on
aschool day during school hours. All personal business days shall require reasonable notice
and the reason for such absence. All unused personal days shall be credited to sick time at
the end of each fiscal year. In the third year of employment and beyond, you shall be
entitled to five (5) days for personal leave.

4. Family Iliness - Death in the Family

Effective July 1, 2022, you shall be entitled to three (3) days of additional leave with
no deduction in pay for illness in the immediate family. You shall also be entitled to three
(3) days additional leave with no deduction in pay for death in the immediate family. These
days shall not be deducted from sick leave and you shall not be entitled to accumulate
any unused days under this section. The immediate family is herein defined as father,
mother, brother, sister, son, daughter, husband or wife, grandparents, father-in-law, mother-
in-law, brother-in-law and sister-in-law.

5. Vacation Leave

Effective July 1, 2022 you shall be entitled to five (5) days of vacation leave annually.
Said vacation leave must be utilized during the summer month of August. If you are unable
to utilize said vacation leave days in August due to your job responsibilities, you may, upon
the prior approval of your immediate supervisor, use your vacation leave at other times
during the school year. You shall be entitled to carry over any unused vacation days with
no maximum accumulation.

6. Health Insurance

Effective July 1, 2022, you shall have the opportunity to participate in the health care
plan offered by the Board of Education. Effective July 1, 2022, you shall be required to
contribute 7.5% of the applicable premium. Contributions to the cost of premiums will be
made through bi-weekly payroll deductions.

Should you receive health insurance from sources other than the District and you opt
out of the District's health insurance program for at least one year, you shall receive a cash
payment - payable in two equal installments - for the year you opt out of the health
insurance program based on the following:

Proof of 2 person or family coverage elsewhere: $5,411.56 annual payment
Proof of single coverage elsewhere: $3,246.95 annual payment

7. Retiree Health Insurance

You shall be entitled to health insurance benefits into retirement only after
completing twenty (20) years of consecutive service in the District immediately preceding
your retirement. Upon your resignation from the District for purposes of retirement into the
New York State Employees’ Retirement System, you shall be permitted to continue
coverage under the District's health insurance program for you and your spouse, subject to
the terms and conditions of the District's health care plan in effect. The District shall pay the
same contribution rate in effect at the time of retirement for the health insurance premiums
for such coverage into retirement. You shall also have the option to cover any additional
cost for coverage of dependents that you wish to include subject to the terms of the
District's health care plan in effect. You will be solely responsible for any Medicare Part B
required payments.

8. Welfare Fund

You shall be entitled to receive a welfare fund allocation in the amount of $1,191, (a
prorated amount based on the eleven month work calendar) which may be utilized for
Dental and/or Optical insurance coverage. Said sum shall be contributed by the Board on
your behalf to the Employee Benefit Fund of the Irvington Union Free School District Clerical
Unit.

9. Flexible Spending Account
You shall be entitled to participate in the District's flexible spending account.
10. Other Benefits

You shall also be entitled to receive the following benefits as set forth in Article XVI
the 2020-2027 Collective Bargaining Agreement between the District and the Irvington
Union Free School District Clerical Unit.

a. Life Insurance in the amount of $70,000
b. Disability Insurance

Written Agreement

being the drafter hereof or causing the same to be drafted. By signing this letter, you
acknowledge that this Agreement shall continue in full force and effect for the term of your
service to the District unless otherwise terminated in writing.

Severability

If any provision of this Letter Agreement is determined to be contrary to law, it is
understood and agreed that such provision shall be deemed deleted and the balance of
this Letter Agreement without such deleted provision, if otherwise lawful, shall remain in full
force and effect. If such deleted provision involves compensation or money benefit, you
and the Board agree to negotiate as part of this Agreement in place of such deleted
provision a substitute of comparable value thereto, and in the event of an impasse
exceeding forty-five (45) days, either party hereto may submit the issue for final disposition,
to arbitration by the American Arbitration Association pursuant to its rules, which Association
shall be empowered to make an award of comparable value or compensation as
reasonably implements the intent of the parties under the deleted provision.

Applicable Law
By signing this letter, you acknowledge that this Letter Agreement has been entered

into and shall be governed under and in accordance with the laws of the State of New
York, without reference to that state’s conflict of laws rules.

Entire Agreement

This Letter Agreement constitutes the full and complete Agreement between you
and the District and may not be altered, changed, added to, deleted from, or modified
except through mutual consent of you and the Board.
