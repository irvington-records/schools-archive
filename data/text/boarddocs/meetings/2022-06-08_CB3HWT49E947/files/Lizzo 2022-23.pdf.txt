IRVINGTON Superintendent of Schools

UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT

June 8, 2022

Ms. Sara Lizzo

Re: Employment Letter Agreement

Dear Sara:

This letter serves as your Employment Agreement with the Irvington Union
Free School District (hereinafter “the District”) for the period of July 1, 2022 through
June 30, 2023. The Board of Education (hereinafter “the Board”) has been advised that
it is legally permissible to contractually obligate itself to provide you with the wages
and benefits described herein. It is acknowledged by you and the Board that your
employment status and the termination of your employment as the District Clerk are
subject solely to various provisions of the Laws of the State of New York and/or
Westchester County regarding the employment status of District Clerks and nothing
herein contained shall be deemed to have modified the foregoing in any respect.
Therefore, based upon the mutual understandings between you and the Board, it is
understood and agreed as follows:

Scope of Employment
Effective July 1, 2022, you shall be employed as District Clerk, Registrar and

Accounts Receivable Clerk and perform all the duties associated with the
appointment of this position.

Salary
The regular annual base salary for the 2022-23 school year shall be $58,750 plus

a $9,500 stipend for night BOE meetings and Election Days.

Career Increment
In addition to the foregoing, you shall receive a career increment in the amount

of $1,300 upon completion of ten (10) years of service; an additional $1,450 after
completion of fifteen (15) years of service; and an additional $1,600 after completion of
twenty(20) years of service in the District.

Benefits
In addition to the annual compensation specified above, you shall be entitled
to receive the following enumerated benefits:

[OCR]
1. Work Year

In alignment with the CSEA Clerical contact, your work year shall be twelve (12)
months, except for the school holidays and recess periods designated on the school
calendar, vacation days, and use of applicable sick, personal, family illness days and
bereavement days as well as any days off due to school cancellation. Your will work a
forty (40) hour week, eight (5) hours per day, Monday through Friday including one (1)
hour a day for lunch, plus night BOE meetings and any School District Budget and
School Board Trustee Election days.

2. Sick Leave

You shall be entitled to nine days (9) in 2022-23 and thirteen (13) in 2023-24
and beyond. Any unused sick leave days may be carried over and used during any
subsequent year of employment. A non-cumulative reserve of thirty (30) working
days will be available to you for an extended illness. You may only utilize this reserve
once in your lifetime. Extended illness shall mean illness or disability for thirty (30) or
more continuous working days. This reserve can only be accessed after utilization of
all your personal accumulated sick leave.

3. Personal and Other Leaves

You shall be entitled to four (4) days for the purpose of transacting or attending
personal business, which cannot be attended to other than on a school day during
school hours for the 2022-23 school year, and in 2023-24 and beyond, personal leave
will be five (5) days. All personal business days shall require reasonable notice and the
reason for such absence. All unused personal days shall be credited to the sick time at
the end of each fiscal year.

4. Family Illness - Death in the Family

You shall be entitled to three (3) days for the 2022-23 school year of additional
leave with no deduction in pay for illness in the immediate family. You shall also be
entitled to three (3) days additional leave with no deduction in pay for death in the
immediate family. These days shall not be deducted from sick leave and you shall not
be entitled to accumulate any unused days under this section. The immediate family
is herein defined as father, mother, brother, sister, son, daughter, husband or wife,
grandparents, father-in-law, mother-in-law, brother-in-law and sister-in-law.

5. Vacation Leave

Effective July 1, 2022, you shall be entitled to seven (7) days of vacation leave
annually. Said vacation leave must be utilized during the summer months of July 1
through August 31. If you are unable to utilize said vacation leave days during July or
August due to your job responsibilities, you may, upon the prior approval of your
immediate supervisor, use your vacation leave at other times during the school year.

You shall be entitled to carry over any unused vacation days with no maximum
accumulation. After one full complete year of service, you shall be entitled to ten
(10) days of vacation leave annually. After the completion of five (5) years of service,
you Shall be entitled to fifteen (15) days of vacation leave annually.

An employee with over two (2) years' service, who terminates his or her
employment voluntarily, shall be entitled to the cash equivalent of 5/6 of one (1) day's
wage for each calendar month from July 1 preceding the date of termination through
the effective date of the employee's resignation.

Employees who have submitted a "resignation for the purpose of retirement"
from Irvington UFSD shall be granted the cash equivalent of one day's wage for every
accumulated vacation day earned during their employment period.

6. Health Insurance

Effective July 1, 2022, you shall have the opportunity to participate in the health
care plan offered by the Board of Education. You shall be required to contribute 6.75%
of the applicable premium. Contributions to the cost of premiums will be made
through bi-weekly payroll deductions.

Should you receive health insurance from sources other than the District and
you opt out of the District’s health insurance program for at least one year, you shall
receive a cash payment — payable in two equal installments — for the year you opt out
of the health insurance program based on the following:

Proof of 2 person or family coverage elsewhere: $5411.56 annual payment
Proof of single coverage elsewhere: $3246.95 annual payment

7. Retiree Health Insurance

You shall be entitled to health insurance benefits into retirement only after
completing twenty (20) years of consecutive service in the District immediately
preceding your retirement. Upon your resignation from the District for purposes of
retirement into the New York State Employees Retirement System, you shall be
permitted to continue coverage under the District’s health insurance program for you
and your spouse, subject to the terms and conditions of the District's health care plan
in effect. The District shall pay the same contribution rate in effect at the time of
retirement for the health insurance premiums for such coverage into retirement.
There will be no obligation of the District for Medicare Part B reimbursement. You
shall also have the option to cover any additional cost for coverage of dependents that
you wish to include subject to the terms of the District's health care plan in effect.

8. Welfare Fund

You shall be entitled to receive a welfare fund allocation in the amount of $1300
per year which may be utilized for Dental and/or Optical insurance coverage. Said
sum shall be contributed by the Board on your behalf to the Employee Benefit of the
Irvington Union Free School District Clerical Unit.

You shall be entitled to participate in the District’s elective flexible spending

10. Other Benefits

You shall also be entitled to receive the following benefits as set forth in

a. Life Insurance in the amount of $70,000
b Long Term Disability Insurance

Written Agreement
By signing this letter, you acknowledge that this Agreement has been arrived

at jointly between yourself and the Board and shall not be construed against any party
hereto as being the drafter hereof or causing the same to be drafted. By signing this
letter, you acknowledge that this Agreement shall continue in full force and effect for
the term of your service to the District unless otherwise terminated in writing.

Severability
If any provision of this Letter Agreement is determined to be contrary to law, it

is understood and agreed that such provision shall be deemed deleted and the
balance of this Letter Agreement without such deleted provision, if otherwise lawful,
shall remain in full force and effect. If such deleted provision involves compensation
or money benefit, you and the Board agree to negotiate as part of this Agreement in
place of such deleted provision a substitute of comparable value thereto and in the
event of an impasse exceeding forty-five (45) days, either party hereto may submit the
issue for final disposition, to arbitration by the American Arbitration Association
pursuant to its rules, which Association shall be empowered to make an award of
comparable value or compensation as reasonably implements the intent of the
parties under the deleted provision.

Applicable Law
By signing this letter, you acknowledge that this Letter Agreement has been

entered into and shall be governed under and in accordance with the laws of the State
of New York, without reference to that state’s conflict of law rules.

Entire Agreement

This Letter Agreement constitutes the full and complete Agreement between
you and the District and may not be altered, changed, added to, deleted from, or
modified except through mutual consent of you and the Board.
