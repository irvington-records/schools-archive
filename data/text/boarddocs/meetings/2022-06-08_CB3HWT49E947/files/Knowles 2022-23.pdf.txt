Re: Employment Letter Agreement
Dear Gary:
Scope of Employment

Effective July 1, 2022, you shall be employed as The Director of School Facilities,
Operations and Maintenance.

Salary

Your regular base salary for the year July 1, 2022 through June 30, 2023 shall be
$138,994, which includes $1,000 for a travel allowance.

Career Increment

You will receive $3,650 at the beginning of the 20" year of service in the District plus
an additional $1,825 in the beginning of the 25" year of service.

Retirement Incentive

Upon completion of twenty (20) years of service credited in the New York State
Employees Retirement System and a minimum of ten (10) years in the Irvington Schools,
and if you are eligible to retire under the New York State Employees Retirement System
and submit to the District an irrevocable resignation for the purpose of retirement and in
fact retire under the New York State Employees Retirement System, you shall be eligible for
a one-time payment of 25% of annual salary (base salary plus career increment).

Work Year

Your work year for the 2022-2023 school year shall be twelve (12) months. Effective
July 1, 2022, you shall be entitled to 23 days of vacation leave annually. You may carry over
up to a maximum of 23 days vacation and be reimbursed upon leaving the district at a rate
of 1/260 of your salary for each day. You may cash in up to five (5) days of unused vacation
time each year at the current per diem rate.

Health Insurance

The Board will provide you with Family Coverage Health Insurance at the following
cost to you:
Effective July 1, 2022: 18% of Premium

You will also receive welfare fund allocation equivalent to teachers’ welfare fund. For
2022-2023 the amount is $1,200.

Should you receive health insurance from sources other than the District and you
opt out of the District's health insurance program for at least one year, you shall receive a
cash payment - payable in two equal installments - for the year you opt out of the health
insurance program based on the following:

Proof of 2 person or family coverage elsewhere: $5,000 annual payment
Proof of single coverage elsewhere: $3,000 annual payment

Retiree Health Insurance

You shall be entitled to health insurance benefits into retirement only after
completing ten (10) years of consecutive service in the District immediately preceding your
retirement. Upon your resignation from the District for purposes of retirement under the
New York State Employees Retirement System, you shall be permitted to continue
coverage under the District's health insurance program for you and your spouse, subject to
the terms and conditions of the District's health plan in effect. The District shall pay one-
hundred percent (100%) of the health insurance premiums for such coverage into
retirement. The District will be only be responsible the basic Medicare Part B
reimbursement amount. You shall also have the option to cover any additional cost for
coverage of dependents that you wish to include subject to the terms of the District's
health plan in effect.

Other Benefits

You will receive the following benefits comparable with those listed in the Irvington
Administrators’ Association:
Memberships
Education Allowance
Access to Flex plan
Life Insurance
Disability Insurance
Eligibility for Sick Bank

Written Agreement

By signing this letter, you acknowledge that this Agreement has been arrived at
jointly between yourself and the Board and shall not be construed against any party hereto
as being the drafter hereof or causing the same to be drafted. By signing this letter, you
acknowledge that this Agreement shall continue in full force and effect for the term of your
service to the District unless otherwise terminated in writing.

Severability

If any provision of this Letter Agreement is determined to be contrary to law, it is
understood and agreed that such provision shall be deemed deleted and the balance of
this Letter Agreement without such deleted provision, if otherwise lawful, shall remain in
full force and effect. If such deleted provision involves compensation or money benefit, you
and the Board agree to negotiate as part of this Agreement in place of such deleted
provision a substitute of comparable value thereto, and in the event of an impasse
exceeding forty-five (45) days, either party hereto may submit the issue for final disposition,
to arbitration by the American Arbitration Association pursuant to its rules, which
Association shall be empowered to make an award of comparable value or compensation
as reasonably implements the intent of the parties under the deleted provision

Applicable Law

By signing this letter, you acknowledge that this Letter Agreement has been entered
into and shall be governed under and in accordance with the laws of the State of New York,
without reference to that state’s conflict of laws rules.

Entire Agreement

This Letter Agreement constitutes the full and complete Agreement between you
and the District and may not be altered, changed, added to, deleted from, or modified
except through mutual consent of you and the Board.
