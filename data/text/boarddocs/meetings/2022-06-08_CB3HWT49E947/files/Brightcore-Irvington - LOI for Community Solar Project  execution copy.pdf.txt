Dear Carol,

This cover letter, together with the attached summary of terms (the “Term Sheet” and collectively
with this cover letter this “LOI”), set forth certain terms of a proposal by Brightcore Energy, LLC
(“Brightcore”) to Irvington Union Free School District (“Irvington”) (each, a “Party” and
collectively, the “Parties”) with respect to the development, construction, installation, financing,
ownership and operation of a portfolio of rooftop and potentially carport solar PV projects that are
intended to participate in ConEdison’s community solar program (the “Project”), to be located at
sites in Irvington’s district (collectively, the “Site”) (collectively, the “Transaction”). In March of
2021, the New York Power Authority (“NYPA”), in collaboration with Westchester County and
its municipalities, ran RFP #Q1-7147SR to support the procurement process for implementing
solar and energy storage projects under a best value approach among solar and storage developers.
Brightcore was selected as the exclusive solar developer in this program for Westchester County
Schools.

Following the execution of this LOI, (i) the Parties shall use good faith efforts to negotiate and
enter into either a Solar Lease Agreement and related documents for the Transaction (the
“Definitive Agreements”) and (ii) concurrently therewith, Brightcore shall use reasonable efforts
to develop and prepare the Project for submission into Con Edison’s community solar program
(the “Development Activities”). Except for (i) the terms contained in this paragraph, and (ii) the
provisions in Section II of the Term Sheet labeled “Binding Terms” (collectively, the “Binding
Terms”), this LOI does not create and is not intended to create a binding and enforceable contract
between the Parties. The Parties shall not be bound to any Transaction unless and until all
necessary approvals have been obtained and the Parties have negotiated, approved, executed and
delivered the appropriate Definitive Agreements. This LOI does not include all material terms
that would be included in the Definitive Agreements, and either Party may propose additional
and/or different terms than those summarized herein for inclusion in the Definitive Agreement.

This LOI and the terms and conditions herein shall be effective between the Parties upon approval
and execution by each of their respective duly authorized governing boards, officers or
representatives. If the terms and conditions of this LOI are in accord with your understanding,
please sign and return the enclosed counterpart of this LOI to the undersigned.

                         this LOI by the parties and will end two (2) years from such execution date

Exclusivity:        In consideration of the time, effort and expense to be undertaken by
                    Brightcore in conducting a due diligence investigation of the contemplated
                    Transaction, in performing the Development Activities and in preparing and
                    negotiating the Definitive Agreements (and any related documentation),
                    until the earliest occurrence of: (i) two (2) years from the date hereof, (ii)
                    termination of this LOI by Brightcore, or (iii) the execution and delivery
                    of the Definitive Agreements, neither Irvington nor any agent or party
                    representing Irvington (including, without limitation, attorneys,
                    accountants, consultants, financial advisors and investment bankers) shall,
                    directly or indirectly, solicit, initiate, knowingly encourage, participate in
                    negotiations with, enter into any agreement with, any third party concerning
                    any rooftop or parking canopy solar PV project at the Site (“Exclusivity
                    Obligation”).

                    In the event the Parties do not enter into Definitive Agreements by the
                    expiration date of this LOI, neither party shall have any further obligation
                    to the other party.

Termination:        Either party shall have the right to terminate this LOI upon fourteen (14)
                    days’ prior written notice to the other party. Upon such termination, neither
                    party shall have any further obligation to the other party, except if Irvington
                    terminates this LOI, the Exclusivity Obligation shall survive until the
                    Expiration Date.

Counterparts:       This LOI may be executed and delivered in electronic form, such as
                    PDF/electronic mail or facsimile, and in counterparts, each of which
                    counterpart shall be deemed to be an original, but all of which together
                    shall constitute one document.

Governing Law:      This LOI shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws
                    of the State of New York without giving effect to any conflicts of law
                    principles.

Entire Agreement:   This LOI sets out the Parties’ complete understanding as of the date
                    hereof with respect to this LOI and the Transaction and supersedes all
