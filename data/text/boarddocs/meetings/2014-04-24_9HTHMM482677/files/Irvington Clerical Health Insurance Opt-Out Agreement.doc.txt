MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
BETWEEN THE
IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT
AND 
CSEA, SCHOOL DISTRICT CLERICAL UNIT

1.  	Article XVI, paragraph “K” of the existing collective bargaining agreement covering the period from July 1, 2010 through June 30, 2015 (“Agreement”) between the Irvington Union Free School District (“District”) and CSEA, School District Clerical Unit (“Clerical Unit”), provides for a payment to eligible unit members who opt-out of the health insurance plan (“health insurance opt-out”) as follows:

“Full-time employees who are currently receiving family health insurance from the District and new full-time employees when first eligible for health insurance and who opt out of the District’s health insurance program for at least one year, shall receive a cash payment of $3,500 or 50% of premium, whichever is greater, for the year they opt out of the health insurance program.

Effective July 1, 2012, eligible full-time employees shall receive a cash payment of an amount equal to forty-five (45%) percent of the premium.  Effective July 1, 2013, said amount shall be reduced to forty-four (44%) percent.  Effective July 1, 2014, said amount shall be reduced to forty-three (43%) percent.”


2.  	The District and the Clerical Unit agree that the clear language of Article XVI, paragraph “K” provides that the health insurance opt-out is limited to only those eligible full-time employees currently receiving family health insurance, including 2 person, and not to those eligible full-time employees receiving individual health insurance.   

3.	Notwithstanding, it is undisputed that the health insurance opt-out has been extended by the District to eligible full-time employees receiving either family or individual health insurance from the District.    

4.  	The District and the Clerical Unit agree that the District shall continue to provide the health insurance opt-out to those eligible full-time employees currently receiving family, 2 person and individual health insurance from the District through the expiration of the collective bargaining agreement on June 30, 2015.  

5.	Effective July 1, 2015, those eligible full time employees receiving individual health insurance coverage shall no longer receive the health insurance opt-out specified in Article XVI, paragraph “K” of the Agreement, unless otherwise negotiated by the parties in the successor agreement to the existing collective bargaining agreement.


6.	This Agreement is subject to approval by the Board of Education pursuant to a duly adopted resolution.
