	10. Review of Proposed Budget/Adoption of the 2014-15 Proposed Budget (Presentation)

	Prior to the Board’s vote on adopting the 2014-15 proposed school budget, Dr. Harrison provided an overview of the budget process that began in January, noting that the proposed budget does not eliminate programs, invests in infrastructure and provides exciting opportunities for students while balancing fiscal responsibility with educational quality. The proposed budget of $56,294,000 has an estimated tax rate increase of 2.91%, which is the lowest in four years and the averaged assessed home would see an increase of $467 in their taxes.

	Board members Phil Whitney, Robyn Kerner and Bob Grados all commented on the budget process and thanked administrators and teachers for their help in developing the process. Mr. Whitney noted that he was pleased with the budget and the rigorous process, and he and Mrs. Kerner requested to have ongoing, periodic updates from administration as to whether the new spending (on programs) has had the desired effect.  Mrs. Kerner wanted to highlight that the District continues to make a commitment to small class sizes and that the Board heard from community members as to their desire to maintain bus monitors which were added back into the proposed budget. She would like to see better upfront prioritizing of the District’s needs for programs, etc.

	Mr. Montgomery stated that he continues to have concerns regarding the proposed 9th grade writing class and how it is going to be structured after meeting with Dr. Kor, Mr. Cohen and Dr. Harrison. He stated that he would prefer to spend the same amount of money on tax certs while looking at existing programs to increase students’ writing skills. He also noted that he is less concerned with the increase to the athletic trainer but is still not clear as to how the additional time is going to be spent, i.e. concussion management, at games, etc.  Mrs. Kerner noted that she agrees somewhat with Mr. Montgomery’s concerns with the writing progam and would challenge the administration to also look at current core programs, both at high school and middle school.

	Action: 10.2 Adoption of the 2014-15 Proposed Budget

	Resolved, that the Irvington Union Free School District's 2014-15 proposed budget be set at $56,294,000 and that this proposed budget be voted on by the community at the Annual Meeting on May 20, 2014 between the hours of 7:00 am and 9:00 pm at the Irvington Main Street School.
