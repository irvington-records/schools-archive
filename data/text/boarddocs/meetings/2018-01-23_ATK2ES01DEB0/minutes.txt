3. Presentation/Discussion

Dr. Harrison started the round-table discussion by noting that the District Mission Statement is the foundation of the work that occurs throughout the District. This year's goals have been developed in schools and in departments throughout the District and are closely linked to the Strategic Objectives and are aligned for Grades K-12.

For 2017-18 there are three categories that encompass the goals:

- Communications-increasing communications, launching a new website in the spring and introducing a refreshed District identity vis a vis a new logo.

- Operations and Finance Goals-evaluate buildings and grounds needs and develop for long range plans and develop plans for financing

- Curriculum and Instruction-focus is on the continuation of goals and goals are linked to the Strategic plan.

During the course of the Roundtable the following was noted:

David Sottile, Principal, Middle School spoke about one of the two primary goals at the Middle School. Instruction that elevates student thinking. He also reported Middle School was able to expand the technology program for 8th grade students by offering a new elective course, Green Architecture. Green Architecture is a specialized unit in the Project Lead the Way Program and is a course that is beyond the 7th and 8th grade foundation courses. Mr. Sottile noted that IMS is seeking to introduce more elective opportunities for 8th graders. He also spoke of science instruction at the Middle School that is inquiry based rather than a process approach to a lab task. The students consider a "phenomenon" (as it's called in the standards) and through investigation, collaboration and learning they get a deeper understanding of what causes the phenomenon to occur.

Mr. Sottile requested that late buses be restored to the Middle School to enable more students to participate in after-school activities. Currently students who rely on the school bus to get home from school are limited in after school homework help, activities and clubs.

Allyson Daley, Assistant Principal, Middle School spoke about the second curricular goal related to assessments and how it connects to student thinking. She reported the Middle School is making progress towards their goal of building a system to target and assess the academic and behavioral needs of the students (which is related to Response to Intervention (RTI)). Supporting this work teachers participated in grade-level training workshops to build knowledge of RTI. Aimsweb (a screening tool for Mathematics and Reading) has been introduced and implemented in 6th grade. The 6th grade students are taking the first Aimsweb screening this week. Ms. Daley also spoke of the IMS Data Team's progress sharing that they will be observing the Main Street School Data team as a part of their professional learning.

David Cohen, Principal, Irvington High School spoke about the progress towards school goals. In mathematics, teachers are benefitting from their professional learning and coaching through Metamorphosis. Many benefits have been seen in planning and lesson implementation as a result of coaching and the review of student work. The four learning goals knowledge, skill, meaning making and transfer has become an important part of the actual lesson and closing activities. He reported that the teachers at the high school are fully utilizing Google Classroom and using Chrome books in their classes for independent and collaborative learning. He said the high school could use more devices moving forward to support student and staff needs. He also requested additional Art Department staffing for next year to support the development of the Fine Arts Program.

He reported the scheduling committee is working on developing the new master schedule with input from students and faculty and administrators. The high school would benefit from flexible spaces and furniture to accommodate a new master schedule as a result of the possibility of having more students eating lunch at the same time. Potentially the new schedule with have longer periods or block scheduling which will allow students to dig deeper into learning. On a different note, Mr. Cohen explained there is increased need and interest in the Sophomore class for participation Vocational-Technical Education programming that needs to be considered in the budget process.

Matthew Samuelson, Assistant Principal Irvington High School said that it is critical for teachers to articulate the goals of a lesson. He gave an example that the Bard protocols (English Department professional learning) ask students to explore thinking through writing and closing activities in lessons are now including much more self-reflection. In the process, students are encouraged to hone in on specific wrong answers, figure out where they made their error and use feedback to plan better in the future. He also mentioned teachers at the high school are also using RTI protocol to fine tune and help struggling students.

Dr. Harrison noted during budget development, they are looking for creative and flexible ways to use staff to support a larger cohort of students with special needs that are entering the secondary level.

Deb Marinello, Principal, Dows Lane School spoke about the level of commitment the teachers and staff have in teaching thinking at Dows Lane students. Teachers are working collaboratively in each other's classrooms and looking at each other's students' work to determine how to deepen student thinking. She shared their work around Character Building and that they have added a layer this year that speaks to the Strategic Plan "to engage students as active participants in their school and in their culture." A monthly student-led assembly gives students a chance to show and teach what they have learned to other students. She reported that during a Faculty meeting teachers went to each other's classroom to look for evidence of the essential question and student thinking. Her budget requests include a Guidance Counselor (shared K-5) to foster social-emotional learning and to meet NYS requirements, as well as a "Peaceful Playground." Peaceful Playground is a direct installation into the existing playground and creates structures for students to engage with each other and they can use the Character Building skills. Ms. Mariniello spoke about the work at Dows Lane around assessments. Every teacher and staff member is learning how to use Aimsweb data to develop student learning goals. She would like to expand the "Project Lead the Way" program to grades K-2. There are two new (Science based) clubs the Garden Club and The Maker Thinking Club for 1st grade. The Maker Thinking Club had 87 students sign up and she is looking to develop lunch time clubs.

Andrea Kantor, Assistant Principal, Dows Lane School shared that Kindergarten teachers are working with the Literacy Coach to create yearlong unit plans that include student thinking, teaching thinking and all the other skills students need to be successful in Literacy. She reported that all teachers have had RTI Tier 1 interventions training and are differentiating instruction in the classroom to meet all students' needs.

Dr. Kor said in order to round out the STEM program, there is a need for a teacher for grades K-2 and there is a need for another position to support integration of technology for K-12 for students and faculty.

Dr. Harrison spoke about re-envisioning the District Clerk position. He suggested that the position of District Clerk should be expanded to assume duties such as registration and FOIL requests that are currently handled by Assistant Superintendent Carol Stein.
