Category: 8. Business of the Board
Subject: 8.7 Donation - Susan Reilly Memorial Bench
Type: Action (Consent)

This gift will be a round bench that wraps around a maple tree, overlooking the field and river on the Dows Lane Elementary School site.  The intention is that stories can be read or created under the tree.  A small plaque will be attached to the back of the bench.
