Information: 6.2 External Audit Report

Mr. Jeffrey Shaver from PFK O'Connor Davies presented on the 2021-22 external audit.

Information: 6.3 NYSSBA Advocacy resolutions

Board Vice President Beth Propper facilitated a discussion on the 43 proposed resolutions presented by NYSSBA. The board then voted on which resolutions they wished to support, with the following results:

NYSSBA Resolution 33-43, excluding resolution 41. Unanimous oppose.

NYSSBA Resolution 41. 4 support, 2 oppose.

NYSSBA Resolution 10. 4 support, 2 abstain.

NYSSBA Resolution 15, 31. Unanimous oppose 15, support 31.

NYSSBA Resolution 16. Unanimous oppose.

NYSSBA Resolution 21. Unanimous oppose.

NYSSBA Resolution 22. Unanimous oppose.

NYSSBA Resolution 26. Unanimous support.

Ms. Propper cast the Board's vote at the NYSSBA Annual Business Meeting on October 17, 2022.
