Proposed Resolutions Recommended for Adoption
1.   NYSSBA support adding fully state funded arts and music education as a common
     school branch that should be incorporated into the public-school curriculum to                ND*
     provide a more well-rounded education for children. (Dutchess BOCES)
2.   Sunsetting Resolution: NYSSBA support meaningful reforms to the tax cap levy
     formula. (NYSSBA Board of Directors)                                                             S
3.   Sunsetting Resolution: NYSSBA support reforming the system of educator discipline
     to cap the length of time educators are waiting 3020-a proceedings are paid.                     S
     (NYSSBA Board of Directors)

4.   NYSSBA support the state enhancing support for school districts in response to
     cybersecurity threats that endanger student and staff personal data and school                   S
     safety. (NYSSBA Board of Directors)
5.   NYSSBA support a requirement that districts be fully funded to employ a sufficient
     number of mental health professionals to properly serve the needs of the students                S
     of the districts of New York State. (NYSSBA Board of Directors)
6.   NYSSBA supports a revision to transportation statutes to permit a School District to
     provide aidable transportation outside voter approved limits, so long as doing so
     would not result in an additional cost to the School District and would not displace             S
     any students otherwise entitled to transportation. (East Meadow School Board)
7.   NYSSBA supports a comprehensive reform of the Foundation Aid Formula.
     (Freeport School Board)                                                                          S
8.   NYSSBA supports Universal Pre-K for 3- and 4-year-olds statewide. (Onteora School Board)        O*
9.   NYSSBA shall petition NYSED and the Commissioner thereof to include, as a
     mandatory prerequisite to being employed by a school district in the State of NY as a
     licensed security guard, that said individual receives as part of their training and            O*
     licensure a separate and specific credential signifying training and knowledge of the
     laws, rules, and regulations applicable to schools. (The Sayville and Sachem School Boards)
10. Sunsetting Resolution: NYSSBA support raising the allowable undesignated fund
    balance for school districts. (Freeport School Board)                                             S
11. NYSSBA shall seek legislative changes to allow component school districts to hold
    the BOCES administrative budget vote and BOCES BOE election on a date of their
    own choosing between April 16 and April 30, rather than a single uniform date                     S
    selected by the BOCES board of education president. (Orleans/Niagara BOCES Board)

14. NYSSBA supports amendments to the NYS General Municipal Law governing reserve
    funds to authorize school districts to be able to borrow from one or more of their
    existing reserve funds in order to cover temporary cashflow shortfalls attendant to               S
    the annual delay between the levy and collection of taxes. (Massapequa, Hicksville, Carle

    regulations providing oversight over special education parent and rules of conduct
    and enforcement procedures for same. (Massapequa, Hicksville, Carle Place, Island Trees, and     O*
    Oyster Bay-East Norwich School Boards)

16. NYSSBA supports legislation at the state level that would allow for school board
    members to receive a stipend for their work as a trustee. (Beacon School Board)                ND*
17. NYSSBA joins statewide advocacy efforts to support healthy, high-quality school
    meals through the Child Nutrition Reauthorization (CNR). (Hicksville School Board)                S
18. NYSSBA support legislation to fully fund or reimburse school districts for tax
    exemptions for veterans. (Fayetteville-Manlius School Board)                                      S
19. NYSSBA support legislation to fully reimburse districts for all investments in
    electrical buses, including all infrastructure. (Fayetteville-Manlius School Board)               S
20. NYSSBA support legislation at the state and/or federal level that would reform the
    current gun laws to strengthen background checks, implementing red flag laws, as
    well as laws that will restrict guns from those under the age of twenty-one.                   ND*
    (Plainview-Old Bethpage School Board)

21. NYSSBA support legislation to amend the Education Law to standardize the
    deadlines for filing of candidate petitions for the various categories of school                  S
    districts. (Croton-Harmon School Board)
22. NYSSBA will support legislation to establish the last date for candidates to file a
    petition after withdrawal of a candidate so that such date is sufficiently in advance
    of the date of an annual meeting to provide school district clerks with enough time               S
    to prepare for the election, including printing of machine-readable ballots and
    absentee ballots. (Croton-Harmon School Board)
23. NYSSBA seek to initiate legislation at the state or federal level that would provide
    dedicated funding to be allocated for school safety measures including staffing.                  S
    (Wilson and Brunswick School Boards)

24. Sunsetting Resolution: NYSSBA supports legislation that would provide finalizing the
    ballot for positions on a small city school district board on the day after a 10-day
    period for filing and reviewing of any objections to nominating petitions.                        S
    (Albany School Board)

25. Sunsetting Resolution: NYSSBA supports legislation that would amend Article 18-A
    of the General Municipal Law to permit school districts, at their option, to be
    necessary parties to payment-in-lieu-of-taxes (PILOT) agreements. (Albany and Taconic             S
    Hills School Boards)

    about their children’s educational programs and should require school districts to                                O
    promote parental involvement by providing access to curricula and instructional
    materials, and legally protect a parent’s right to withdraw a child from portions of
    school curriculum. (Massapequa, Hicksville, Carle Place and Locust Valley School Boards)

    immunization. (Massapequa, Hicksville, Carle Place, Sachem, and Locust Valley School Boards)                      O

        *Notes on items where views were divided or different from NYSSBA Resolutions Committee:
         #1. While all fully support arts education, discussion focused on the tradeoff between the negative of a
              curricular mandate and the positive of requiring this mandate be fully funded.
         #8. All opposed a mandate for district-provided prekindergarten, while supporting #28 to ensure funding is
              available for a quality program, even in high-cost, space-constrained districts.
         #9. It is unclear as to what training is necessary. Our advocacy suggests that this is not the most critical need
              in terms of school security.
         #15. Difficult for all special education parents to afford a trained advocate.
         #16. Discussion centered around view that all funds should go directly to students vs. ensuring that board
              service is available to all with childcare/work expenses covered by stipend.
         #20. Debate over whether gun control is appropriate topic for school board advocacy. Gun control issues
              affect schools, but are also a broader societal issue, not specific to school districts.
         #41. All would like to see help for school districts to work toward later start times locally for the benefit of
              student health, however, a statewide mandate on school start times was not supported.
