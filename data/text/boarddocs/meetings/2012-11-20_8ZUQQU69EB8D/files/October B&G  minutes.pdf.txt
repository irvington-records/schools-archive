                           BUILDINGS & GROUNDS COMMITTEE

                                   October 23, 2012 7 am

                                       MEETING MINUTES
Present: Dr. Harrison, Maria Kashkin, Jim McCann, Gary Knowles, Beverly Miller



   1. Gary reviewed projects that have been completed since July 2012 (copy attached). Mr. McCann
      raised a few concerns: (1) the 12” drain on the hill is spilling over during heavy rain storms –
      Gary will check it; (2) it was suggested that the southwest corner of the HS building have a
      camera installed to monitor the wooded section frequented by students – Gary will review; and
      (3) Mr. McCann informed the committee of a new product on the market – drainage pipes with
      built-in clean-outs. Mr. McCann suggested that this concept be looked into for our district as
      they would need fabricating to fit our existing pipes.
   2. Gary informed the committee of a request from the MS Art Dept. (Diana Schmertz) regarding
      the installation of a mosaic mural on the exterior wall between TG & Maher gym. Mosaics
      would be created by 8th graders. After discussion, approval was given to proceed with guidance
      from Gary regarding the proper type of building adhesion and a framework to surround the tiles.
   3. Larry Schepps has offered to donate 2 trees & planters to be installed at the rear of Dow’s Lane
      Elem. School, outside of the cafeteria. The committee requested that Mr. Schepps provide
      pictures of the proposal, prior to granting approval, to determine whether the size is acceptable
      to the committee.
   4. Beverly reviewed the current status of seven capital projects (copy attached) which are under
      the direction of our architectural firm.
   5. Larry Schepps, during the summer, contacted the school regarding the Dow’s Lane chiller noise.
      The district installed condenser fan cones to reduce the noise level.
   6. East Field/Meszaros Field – discussion ensued regarding the current status of the athletic fields.
      Dr. Harrison informed the committee of the soil surface sampling to take place next week. Any
      major work on Meszaros Field would probably not be able to be done until summer 2014.
   7. Future concerns:
          a. Discussion ensued regarding how to minimize the damage to interior school walls that
               necessitates repainting. Further discussion with Dr. Harrison will be needed as the
               problem entails building-level decisions.
          b. HS gutters do not function properly, allowing roof water to cascade over the gutters
          c. HS quad-side wall (nurse’s office) has a water leaking problem that has not, to date,
               been able to be solved.
          d. Schedule building walk-thru for Jim & Maria with Gary.
          e. HS/MS Dust Bowl – having a negative impact on equipment and building maintenance
               (hard to keep buildings clean with grit/dust blowing in)

Minutes taken by Beverly Miller
