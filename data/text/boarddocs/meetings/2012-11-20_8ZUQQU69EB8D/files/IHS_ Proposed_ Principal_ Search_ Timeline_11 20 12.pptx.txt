Search Committee Profile 
The Search Committee will include the following representatives:

One Board of Education member
Three Parents (two nominated by the PTSA, one by the IEF)
Four Irvington Faculty Association members
Two Irvington Administrators Association members
One Civil Service Employees Association member
One High School student (nominated by the student government)
Assistant Superintendent of Instruction and Human Resources
