Completed Maintenance Projects for the 2012/13 School Year

Dows Lane

Wall repair and painting of rooms 102, 217, 218, 219, 220, 221, and 222.

Paved dumpster area.

Repaired roof above room 200.

Installed new scoreboard on Upper Dows Field.

Repainted lines in the parking lot.

Removed the zip line standards in the playground area. Replaced them with seesaws.
Flame treated the stage curtains.

Main Street

Repaired storm drain between the MSS and the Gym building.

Cleaned and cleared gutters and down spouts.

Repaired damaged stair treads on the west stairwell.

Repaired damaged walls and painted all classrooms on the Main Office floor.
Painted the Health office and bathroom.

Middle School

Painted classrooms and corridors in the A and B wings.
Installed padding on the pillars in the recess area.
Repaired the large exterior door near the Main Office.

High School

Installed solar panels.

Paved the High School hill.

Replaced the asphalt curbing with concrete on the High school hill.

Painted the south stairwell.

Painted the Music wing hall and office.

First phase of fire stopping has been completed.

Gutters and down spouts on the TG building have been cleared and cleaned.

Replaced the broken window in the atrium.

Replaced the northwest side Campus gym doors.

Installed security camera outside the north end of the boy’s locker room in the TG building.
