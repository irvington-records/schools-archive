	Information: 3.1 District's Update, News and Events

		Dr. Harrison reported the following:

-
			Two seniors have earned scholarships to Division One schools.

-
			On Friday, November 23, a pipe broke at Main Street with water cascading through the windows. Dr. Harrison  recognized Gary Knowles and the rest of custodial and maintenance  staff who mobilized quickly to prevent damage to classrooms, etc. Dr. Harrison also  acknowledged teachers who helped with the team effort.

-
			Kindergarten students at Dows Lane said the Pledge of Allegiance which was played on WHUD, the Mike and Casey show.

-
			East Field testing is continuing and hopefully the final phase will be completed over the next week. The public will be updated following information received and shared with DEC, including the necessary remediation work. Dr. Harrison noted that the storms interfered with the testing, but are moving forward.

-
			Possible changes to School Calendar - It will be important to discuss the school district calendar given the closure of five days due to Hurricane Sandy. District has not hit the 180 day requirement but having April 1 and June 21 as student days instead of Supt. Conference Days would meet requirement,  but since it is only late fall, it is important to have conversation as to approaches to be proactive mode and not behind later in the year. Need to recoup instruction days, not just compliance with state aid. The legislature will probably not meet before January and any bills introduced will require that school districts utilize all vacation days prior to seeking a waiver from the 180 days. Need to also consider that Main Street School lost an additional day due to broken pipe. Superintendent is recommending to the Board to consider school being in session Wednesday-Friday of February break (20-22), which would provide latitude of two snow days. Dr. Harrison is also looking to establish a commitment that if there are many snow days, it would be necessary to look at March recess which is filled with two significant holidays and propose school to be in session  March 27 and 28 as a possibility. The Board should consider the options and begin to advise the community of what is being considered. Part of the process does include consultation with bargaining units and meeting is scheduled for Tuesday, November 27 to begin the dialogue. Dr. Harrison hopes to make a decision by the next Board meeting, December 4. There is also more opportunity for instructional time if February break is reduced rather than the March break. Looking at next year, we need to be a little more proactive in building in more snow days. Members of the Board acknowledged that there will most likely be a high percentage of absenteeism as families will take the February break and it was suggested that homework packets be given in advance. Also, the Board has heard concerns expressed by parents about the loss of instructional time and that work needs to be made up, especially for students taking AP classes.4. Presentations/Discussions

		Information, Presentation: 4.2 IHS Principal's Search Timeline (Presentation)

		Dr. Harrison reported that there had been a previous conversation as to the process of hiring the high school principal and shared a somewhat revised time line of the process which would begin in January with recruitment and end approximately April 23rd with his recommendation to the Board and BOE approval with a target of May/June to introduce the new principal in the community and the district.

		Also noted was that Dr. Harrison and Dr. Kor will paper screen approximately 10-20 resumes if pool permits and identify six to seven candidates to bring to the committee with three finalists interviewed by Dr. Harrison then

		bring finalists to Board. If there are two candidates who are both highly desireable, it might be necessary to provide performance based activities and leadership activities if there are two candidates who are both highly desirable.

		Mr.Whitney asked if a search firm might be considered. Dr. Harrison noted that it was considered but he would rather look to district resources and talent to preserve resources and believes that we have talent within the district to undertake the responsibility. The process for MSS search will be staggered along with this.

		In reviewing the possible advertising outlets to use, Jim McCann noted that the District did not use the NY Times when recruiting the Superintendent. He also suggested that if Robyn and Maria have the time that they both be in the preliminary rounds of the interview committee.

		Robyn Kerner asked how  parents who have some experience with recruiting will be able to participate. Mr. Grados noted that the committee cannot be too big and to use a similar process when recruiting the Superintendent with focus groups--- that is where they (parents) can provide input. Should a suitable candidate not be found, Dr. Harrison believes that enought time has been built in to do a second round of recruiting, but he is optimistic that a desirable candidate will be found.
