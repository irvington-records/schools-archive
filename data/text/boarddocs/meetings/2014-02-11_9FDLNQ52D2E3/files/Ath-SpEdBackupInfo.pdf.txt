PUPIL PERSONNEL SERVICES 2014-15

BUDGET CONSIDERATIONS

SOCIAL WORKER/COMMUNITY AIDE +$27,525

Increase part-time position to a full time position. This position was

cut to .50 several years ago. Due to economic difficulties in the past few
years having a negative impact on many Irvington families, the community
aide has been overtaxed, on a prt-time basis, working to connect them with
resources so that their children come to school better equipped to

learn. The community aide also completes Social Histories for the

CSE and will aid in providing transition services to students graduating

or aging out of the District.
