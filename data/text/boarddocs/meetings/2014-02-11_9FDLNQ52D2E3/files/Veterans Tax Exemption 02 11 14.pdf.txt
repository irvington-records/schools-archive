 Veterans’ Exemption Option

• Signed into law on December 2013
• Authorizes certain tax exemptions from certain school taxes
  for eligible veterans. Income is not a criteria – exemption is
  on a certain percentage of the assessed value of a residence
• BOE must decide by May 1 – taxable status date – for
  applicability to the 2014-15 school year
• Process - BOE must have a public hearing before considering
  adoption of exemption
• Irvington has approximately 182 veterans who would be
  eligible for this exemption
