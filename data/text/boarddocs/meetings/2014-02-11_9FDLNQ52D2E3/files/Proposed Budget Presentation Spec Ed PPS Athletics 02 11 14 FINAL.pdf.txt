Special Education Budget Considerations
Consideration #1:
Create Asst. Director of PPS & Eliminate CSE/CPSE Chairperson

Rationale:
 Asst. Director position would be.85 FTE in Special Ed. Budget
  & .15 FTE in Special Aid grant
 Responsibilities of the Dept. have increased over time and the current
   CSE/CPSE Chairperson is currently functioning as an Asst. Director
   without the capability of performing supervisory responsibilities
 Title change is needed to perform these duties


Expense:
 ($6,962)
     This code: ($4,973)
     Benefits: ($1,989)




                                                                    5

Special Education Budget Considerations
Consideration #2:
Addition of 1.0 FTE Crisis Teaching Assistant

Rationale:
 We have an increase in the number of students with
   behavioral needs

   TA to be trained to work with the behavioral specialist & the school
    psychologist to modify inappropriate behavior patterns in order to
    retain students within the District vs. outside placement

Expense:
 $42,000
       This code: $30,000
       Benefits: $12,000

Special Education Budget Considerations
Consideration #3:
Increase in behavioral intervention services

Rationale:
 Resources via BOCES for one day per week to address student
  needs. Goal will be to help our teachers learn the skills to
  better support students’ behavioral needs

Expense:
 $35,000
