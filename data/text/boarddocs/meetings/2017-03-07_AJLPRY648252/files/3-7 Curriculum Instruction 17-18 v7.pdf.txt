                  Our Vision for Tomorrow
                                          4

To achieve our vision, we will:
❖ Provide students with a rigorous, comprehensive, enriched and diversified
  curricula that will prepare students to achieve their personal best, and will
  integrate technology in their learning.

❖ Support educators through targeted professional learning and opportunity for
  collaboration

❖ Encourage innovation, creativity and risk taking to inspire a dynamic learning
  environment

❖ Foster the creation of a culture where students are active participants in
  society.

❖ Strengthen local connections to and ownership of our schools.

❖ Ensure the fiscal health of the district and provide for a high quality learning
  environment.

              Budget Development Objectives
                                           5

This budget presentation will focus on the following goals:

   Provide students with a rigorous, comprehensive, enriched and diversified
    curricula that will prepare students to achieve their personal best, and will
    integrate technology in their learning.
   Support educators through targeted professional learning and opportunity for
    collaboration
   Encourage innovation, creativity and risk-taking to inspire a dynamic learning
    environment
   Foster the creation of a culture where students are active participants in society.

Therefore, we will:
● Utilize the budget to provide for rigorous and enriched learning experiences
  that are rich with technology
● Continue to support the growth of our faculty
● Promote creativity in a culture that values global learning

                                         28
Consideration 2:
Data Manager/Analysis Position: 1.0FTE
Rationale:
In recent years, New York State has increased the data reporting requirements for
districts and has accelerated timelines for reporting existing data elements. In
order to effectively report the data, district point-personnel must also function as
the lead on district student information systems. The effective management and
expectations for compliance have increased the need for a position that reflects a
sophisticated skill set. This increase in data collection and reporting also provides
an opportunity for the district to utilize data analysis as a way to examine
programs and effectiveness.


Expense:
$75,000 in this budget, +$30,000 in benefits

    Proposed Instructional Technology Budget
              New Considerations
                                        29

Consideration 3:
Addition to Edutek contract to increase technical support by .5
FTE
Rationale:
Over the past several years, we have continued to increase the number of devices
we support while also increasing our overall reliance on technology to help drive
instruction. These increases have led to an increase in the number of technology
support tickets we have received (we matched entirety of our total for the 2015-
2016 school year in December of 2016 for the 2016-2017 school year). We need
to increase our support in order to effectively maintain our infrastructure.


Expense:
$35,000

   leader.
