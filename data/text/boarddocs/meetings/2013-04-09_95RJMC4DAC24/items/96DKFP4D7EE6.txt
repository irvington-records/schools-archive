Subject: 9.9 Tax Certiorari Settlement - Reckson - 560 White Plains Road
Type: Action (Consent)

WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District is a party respondent in certain tax certiorari proceedings commenced by the property owners of property located at 560 White Plains Road, Tarrytown, Town of Greenburgh, New York, a/k/a Tax Map number 7.160-42-6 for assessment years 2007-2012; and

	            WHEREAS, the parties to said litigation have attempted to narrow their differences; and

	            WHEREAS, the Irvington Union Free School District, through its attorney, reviewed the relative merits of the petitioner’s claims and appeared in the pending litigation to protect the interests of the School District; and

	            WHEREAS,  meetings were held with the attorney for the petitioner, the Deputy Town Attorney for the Town of Greenburgh, and counsel for the School District and various settlement proposals were discussed to arriving at a tentative settlement; and

	            WHEREAS, the School District’s counsel, and the Town of Greenburgh,  recommend approving the tentative settlement,

	            NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that it is in the best interests of the School District to settle this law suit with the following reductions in assessed values:
