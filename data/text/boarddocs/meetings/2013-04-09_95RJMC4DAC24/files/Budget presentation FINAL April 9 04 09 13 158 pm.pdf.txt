The 2013-2014 Budget Picture
 Despite past reductions and as result of the tax levy cap:
  Restoration of student opportunities is hindered
  Program growth is limited
  Maintenance of existing programs would exceed the tax cap
   by approximately $2,000,000

 Cost escalation beyond Board control:
  Pension Contributions
  Health Insurance

  Tax Certs
      Potential exposure through 2013-14
  Special Education Services

Efficiency Initiatives Included in the
Superintendent’s Proposed Budget
 Identified nearly $500,000 in reductions
 Renegotiated copier leases - $100,000 savings
 New workers’ compensation self insurance initiative -
  $90,000 savings
 Energy conservation initiative
 State grant for records management: est. $50,000-$60,000
  over two years
 Centralized district mailing system – eliminated four postage
  meters; negotiated lower fees for postage meter: $12,000

   Increased BOCES purchasing
   Quad Village Collaboration

      Professional development
      Parent programs

Irvington Union Free School District
Superintendent’s Proposed Budget
2013-2014
 Provides for additional growth in learning experiences

 Does not eliminate existing programs or instructional
  personnel
 Invests in infrastructure & facilities

 Supports the growth & development of staff

 Expands technological opportunities

 Adds full time Assistant Principal at Dows Lane to support
  student and instructional needs
 Balances fiscal responsibility and educational quality
