« General Fund Revenue through April was $77,509,291 or 97.9% of the budgeted revenue.

© The Use of Money mostly includes interest earnings from all District general fund bank accounts. The District has earned $894,097. in interest
earnings through April. This is a 103.3% of forecasted revenue, indicating we will have a surplus in this category due to continued higher interest

average monthly return rate of 3.52% on our NYCLASS investments and further look for opportunities to ensure we are maximizing our potential
earnings with our NYCLASS Investment partners.
