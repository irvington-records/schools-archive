Minutes of the February 7, 2019 Board of Education Meeting

Special Meeting (Thursday, February 7, 2019)

Members present

Michael Hanna, Maria Kashkin, Brian Friedman,Deborah Hargraves, David Graeber, Maura Gedid (arrived at 8:10) Beth Propper

Also present

Dr. Kristopher M. Harrison, Superintendent of Schools, Dr. Raina Kor, Asst. Supt for Instruction and Human Resources, Carol Stein, Asst. Supt for Business and Operations, Gary Knowles, Director of Facilities, Veronica Byrnes and Eric Neiler from H2M Architects + Engineers, Kevin Sawyer from Triton Construction, John LaPlaca from Altaris  Consulting Group and members of the community.

1. Call to Order/Pledge of Allegiance

Action: Procedural:  1.1 Call to Order

Michael Hanna called the meeting called to order at 8:03 pm

Action: Procedural:  1.2 Pledge of Allegiance

The Pledge of Allegiance was recited.

2. Acceptance of Agenda

Action:  2.1 Acceptance of Agenda

Resolved, that the Irvington Board of Education accept the Agenda for the Feb. 7, 2019 Special Meeting as presented.

Motion by Deborah Hargraves, second by Brian Friedman.

Final Resolution: Motion Carries

Yes: Michael Hanna, Maria Kashkin, Brian Friedman, Deborah Hargraves, David Graeber, Beth Propper

Maura Gedid arrived at 8:10 pm

3. Presentation/Discussion

Information:  3.1 Capital Project Recommendations

Brian Friedman of the Building and Grounds Committee presented the Capital Project Recommendations for the scope of work to be included in the proposed capital project bond referendum on May 21, 2019.  Additional information and input was provided by Veronica Byrnes, Eric Neiler, Kevin Sawyer and John LaPlaca.  Comments and questions from the Board of Education and the Administration were permitted after each section.

4. Comments from Members of the Community

Information:  4.1 Procedures for Community Comment on Topics that appear on Agenda

Michael Hanna allowed questions/comments from the community prior to completion of the presentation.  Approximately eight community members took the opportunity to speak.

5. Next Public Meeting

Information: 5.1 Meeting Notification

The next meeting of the Irvington Board of Education will be held on Wednesday February 12, 2019 at  7:30 pm in the Irvington High School/Middle School Campus Presentation Room, 40 N. Broadway, Irvington, New York 10533.
