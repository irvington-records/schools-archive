Category: 10. Personnel Matters
Subject: 10.2 Appointment to the School District's Board of Registration for 2013-14
Type: Action (Consent)

The State Education Department requires the Board of Education to designate four (4) qualified voters of the District to constitute a Board of Registrars, no later than the thirtieth (30th) day following the budget vote and election.
