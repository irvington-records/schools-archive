IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DISTRICT
PLUMBING BID

The Irvington Union Free School District is accepting bids for labor and material rates for
all plumbing repairs and maintenance and any new plumbing work.

Qualifications-The Bidder must be a licensed Contractor in New York State and be able
to work in Westchester County.

Bidder must meet all qualifications and must meet all criteria on the general requirements
page to work in the Irvington UFSD.

Bidder must provide a reference list including commercial work as well as examples of
work done in public schools.

Bidder must be in business for at least the past five (5) years under the same name.
Bidder must be able to respond to the District within one (1) hour of being contacted.

Labor Rates-The Bidder will provide hourly labor rates for straight time, overtime,
weekends and holidays. Bidder must list specific rates and titles for laborer, supervisor
and/or any other category of worker.

Straight time is considered to be Monday - Friday from 8:00AM to 4:00 PM

Overtime is considered to be Monday - Friday from 4:00PM to 8:00AM

Weekends are considered to be Saturday and Sunday.

Holidays are considered to be holidays specified by the Federal Government.

Materials-The contractor must provide material costs either at the retail (supplier
catalog) price or a percentage above or below the retail (supplier catalog) price.

This bid will be in effect through June 30, 2011 and may be renewed for an additional
year (7/1/12 — 6/30/13) at the sole discretion of the School District.
