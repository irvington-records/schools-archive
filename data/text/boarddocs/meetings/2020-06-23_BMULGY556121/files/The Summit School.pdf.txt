WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, pursuant to Education Law section 4402 (b), the School District is authorized by
law to contract with institutions within the State of New York forthe instruction of children with
special needs in those situations where the School District is unable to provide for their
education in special classes in public schools;

WHEREAS, Provider isanon-publicschool registered withand approved bythe New York
State Education Department to establish, conduct, operate and maintain an educational
program for children with special needs;

WHEREAS, School District desires that Provider provide instruction to School District
students enrolled in the program operated by Provider;

WHEREAS, Provider warrants that it has reviewed for each student to be enrolled by the
School District the individualized education program ("IEP"), as prescribed by the School
District's Committee on Special Education (CSE) orCommitteeon Preschool Special Education
(CPSE) and represents that it will provide the level of services identified in the IEP to meet the

needs of each child, and

WHEREAS, Provider is capable of and willing to provide the level of services identified in the
IEP,

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained in this
Agreement, the parties hereto mutually agree as follows:

1. TERM OF AGREEMENT: The Agreement shall bein effect for the period

July 1, 2020 to June 30,
2021

unless terminated earlier, as set forth herein;

2. SCOPE OF SERVICES: The Provider shall provide competent instruction for each
enrolled student in accordance with the student's IEP, as approved by the School
District CSE/CPSE. At all times during the contract period, the Provider shall
maintain approval by thc Commissioner of Education and the New York State
Education Department to operate the educational programs in which each student is
placed. All services by the Provider shall be conducted in strict compliance with
applicable Federal, State and local laws, statutes and regulations, including, but not
limited to, any law, statute and regulation governing disciplinary procedures.
Provider shall also comply with all notice, reporting and recordkeeping requirements
of the New York State Education Department and all other governmental regulators,

3. PAYMENT:

a. In full consideration for the educational services to be rendered by Provider to
the School District for the period of this Agreement, the School District shall
pay to Provider for each student enrolled by the School District the tuition
rate set by the New York State Education Department. If the tuition rate for
the school year governed by this Agreement is not available at the beginning
of the school year, the School District shall pay the rate applicable to the
previous school year until a current rate is set, at which time the parties shall
adjust future tuition payments in accordance with the current rate. Provider
shall reimburse the School District for any overpayments and the School
District shall reimburse the Provider for underpayments made prior to
and in consequence of the current rate set for the school year governed by this
Agreement,

b. Ifthe New York State Education Department issues a Reconciliation Rate for
the school year governed by this Agreement, full consideration for the
educational services rendered by Provider to the School District shall be
governed by the Reconciliation Rate, and the School District shall pay tuition
in accordance with that Reconciliation Rate, Provider shall reimburse the
School District for any overpayments and the School District shall reimburse
the Provider for underpayments made prior to and in consequence of the
Reconciliation Rate set for the school year governed by this Agreement.

.c. Ifa student's enrollment is terminated prior to conclusion of the school year
governed by this Agreement, the School District shall only be obligated to pay
the Provider for the period of the student's enrollment, and if enrollment is
terminated during any month in which the student was in attendance for less
than a full month, tuition shall be paid on a per diem basis for each day ofthe
month in which the student was in attendance.

4. MONTHLY INVOICE: Provider shall submit an invoice for services rendered ona
monthly basis, identifying each student for whom services were rendered during the
month invoiced, and payment to Provider shall be made within thirty (30) days from
receipt of invoice from Provider. School District shall give notice of any invoice
dispute within twenty (20) days of its receipt and reserves the right to withhold
payment pending the resolution of this dispute. Failure to dispute any invoice shall

5. EMPLOYER-EMPLOYEE RELATONSHIP: This Agreement does not create an

employer-employee relationship between the parties. It is the parties' intention that

the Provider shall be an independent contractor and not the School District's
employee for any and all purposes, including, but not limited to, application of the

Fair Labor Standards Act, Federal Insurance Contribution Act, the Federal

Unemployment Tax Act, the Internal Revenue Code, the New York State Revenue

and Taxation Law, the New York State Workers' Compensation Law and the New

York State Unemployment Insurance Law.

INDEPENDENCE OF PROVIDER: Provider shall retain sole and absolute

discretion in the judgment of the manner and means of carrying out the service

activities and responsibilities hereunder. The Provider agrees to be a separate and
independent enterprise from the School District. This Agreement shall not be
construed as creating any joint employment relationship between the Provider and
the School District, and the School District shall not be liable for any obligation
incurred by the Provider, if any, including, but not limited to, unpaid minimum
wages and/or overtime premiums.

7. INCOME TAX DESIGNATION AND INDEMNIFICATION: School District
shall not withhold from sums payable to Provider under this Agreement any amounts
for Federal, State or local taxes, including income taxes, employment taxes (Social
Security and Medicare) and unemployment taxes. Providers agrees that any tax
obligations of Provider arising out of payments made or services rendered under this
Agreement shall be Provider's sole responsibility, and Provider shall hold harmless
and indemnify School District for same.

8. FINGERPRINTINGANDBACKGROUND CHECKS: Providerwarrants
and agrees that prior to employment all staff members who shall have contact
with School Districtstudents shall have the appropriate fingerprinting and
background checks performed as required by Part 87ofthe regulations ofthe
Commissioner of Education of the State of New York.

9. INDEMNIFICATION AND HOLD HARMLESS CLAUSE: Provider agrees that
it shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless School District, its officers, directors,
agents and employees for all loss, costs, damages and expenses, including attorneys’
fees, judgments, fines and amounts paid in settlement in connection with a
terminated, pending or completed action, suit or proceeding arising from any act,
error or omission, misstatement, misleading statement, neglect or breach of duties
by Provider or any of its officers, directors, agents or employees taken or made with
respect to this Agreement.

10. INSURANCE: Provider shall purchase and maintain comprehensive general
liability and professional liability insurance in an amount not less than $1,000,000
per occurrence /$3,000,000 aggregate; automobile liability insurance (any auto)
(combined single limit/each occurrence) in an amount not less than $1,000,000;
Statutory Workers' Compensation, Employers' Liability and NYS Disability Benefits
Insurance for all employees.
