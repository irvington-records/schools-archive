THIS AGREEMENT made this, 15, day of ul-yao20 and between the BOARD OF EDUCATION OF
THE IRVINGTON UNION FREE SCHOOL DI. RICT (hereinafter referred to as the "District") having its
principal offices located in the village of Irvington, County of Westchester, State of New York and Denise
T. Corriveau, M.S., P.T. (hereinafter referred to as the "Therapist")

residing at 204 Tamarack Lane, Brewster, NY 10509 (914) 320-4792.

WHEREAS, by a resolution passed by the Board of Education of the any ton Union Free School
District (TUFSD) (hereinafter referred to as the "Board") at its meeting on. 2020 the "Pediatric
Physical Therapist” (PT) was appointed for the District, subject to the following terms and conditions:

A. GENERAL DUTIES AND
RESPONSIBILITES
1. The duties and responsibilities of the PT Therapist shall be those duties and responsibilities

prescribed for the position of Physical Therapist in the Education law and other applicable statutes, laws,
tules and/or regulations and those duties and/or responsibilities established by the Board pursuant to such
statues, laws, rules, and regulations including, but not limited to the following responsibilities:

a) To provide such PT services for children assigned by the Irvington Union Free School
Districts Committee on Special Education ("CSE") subject to students IEP.

b) To provide reports and communications to the TUFSD CSE with regard to such pediatric
physical therapy services.

c) To attend meetings or hearings of the IUFSD CSE when requested by such Committee.

d) To provide annual evaluations and Progress reports on all students treated during the year
to the CSE as requested.

e) To perform such other duties as the Therapist as may be required by the State Education
Department or as the Board may from time to time require.

2 In order to provide the services referred to in Paragraph "1" above, the Therapist agrees to be
available to serve on a regular basis depending on the needs of the students.

3. For providing such services, the Therapist shall be paid the sum of $75.00 per 30 minute sessions
to be paid on the monthly basis based upon time sheets submitted by the Therapist, which must include the
date, time and name of the students(s) who received physical therapy services during the prior month.
Sessions other than 30 minutes will be prorated based on the 30-minute session rate.

4. It is expressly understood that this Agreement does not create an employer/employee relationship
between the parties. The Therapist is hired as an independent Contractor. This contract shall not entitle the
Therapist to any employee benefits, sick leave, vacation time or other benefits enjoyed by employees of the
District, except as stated herein, Any missed therapy services will be made up ‘at the discretion of the
Therapist.

B. GENERAL
TERMS:

L, During the term of this agreement (2020-21 school year), the Therapist shall faithfully, diligently,
in accordance with accepted professional standards, perform and discharge the duties and
responsibilities of the Therapist for PT services to the District.

3. The Therapist agrees that any individual providing professional services pursuant to this

4. The Therapist shall maintain professional liability insurance (malpractice insurance) in the

minimum amount of One Million Dollars $1,000,000) for a single claim and One Million Dollars
($1,000,000) per year aggregate for all professional staff employed by the Therapist who provides
professional services to the District. This insurance will be maintained in full force and effect at all times.

3% The Therapist understands that in performing this Agreement, the Therapist may have access to
confidential information in possession of the District or others, including, but not limited to names, facts
or information about individuals, businesses and families. The Therapist may also have access to
confidential information potentially including student directory, personnel information and records,
sensitive, confidential or internal School District matters and other protected information, It is agreed
that the definition of confidential information includes all documentary, electronic or oral information
made known to the Therapist through any activity related to this Agreement. The Therapist agrees not to
reveal any confidential information and understands that any such disclosure shall be considered a
breach of this Agreement. The Therapist agrees that if a subpoena is received for divulgence of
confidential information, the Therapist shall notify the District prior to divulging the same. This section
shall survive termination of this Agreement.

6. This Agreement may be terminated at any time without cause by mutual agreement in writing
between the District and the Therapist or upon thirty (30) days written notice by either party.

7. The Therapist acknowledges and agrees that he/she or its employees will be subject to
fingerprinting and a criminal history record check as may be required by the Education Law of the
State of New York. In such event, the Therapist agrees to cooperate with the school district and to
complete any and all necessary forms or procedures, all at no cost or expense to the School District.

8. Reports will be prepared and records maintained by the Therapist as requested by the CSE with
copies of reports submitted to the CSE as requested.

9. Annual review and interim reports will be provided to the District as requested. Data for the
reports will be collected as part of the treatment sessions when one month notice is provided by the
District.

10. This Agreement constitutes the entire Agreement between the parties and contains all the
agreements between them with respect to the subject matter hereof.
