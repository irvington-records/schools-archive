A. GENERAL DUTIES AND

RESPONSIBILITES

1, The duties and responsibilities of the Teacher shall be those duties and responsibilities

prescribed for the position of Teacher in the Education law and other applicable statutes, laws, rules
and/or regulations and those duties and/or responsibilities established by the Board pursuant to such
statues, laws, rules, and regulations including, but not limited to the following responsibilities:

a) To provide such vision services for children assigned by the Irvington Union Free School
Districts Committee on Special Education ("CSE") subject to students IEP.
b) To provide reports and communications to the JUFSD CSE with regard to such vision services,
c) To attend meetings or hearings of the IUFSD CSE when requested by such Committee.
d) To provide annual evaluations and progress reports on all students treated during the year
to the CSE as requested.
e) To perform such other duties as the Teacher as may be required by the State Education
Department or as the Board may from time to time require.

Zs In order to provide the services referred to in Paragraph "1" above, the Teacher agrees to be
available to serve on a regular basis, up to a maximum of ten (10) hours per week.

3), For providing such services, the Teacher shall be paid the sum of $140.00 per hour for maximum ten
(10) hours per week to be paid on the monthly basis based upon time sheets submitted by the Teacher, which
must include the date, time and name of the students(s) who received vision services during the prior month.

4. It is expressly understood that this Agreement does not create an employer/employee relationship
between the parties. The Teacher is hired as an independent Contractor. This contract shall not entitle the
Teacher to any employee benefits, sick leave, vacation time or other benefits enjoyed by employees of the
District, except as stated herein, Any missed vision services will be made up at the discretion of the Teacher.

B. GENERAL
TERMS:

1. During the term of this agreement (2020-21 school year), the Teacher shall faithfully, diligently,
in accordance with accepted professional standards, perform and discharge the duties and
responsibilities of the Teacher for vision services to the District.

5; The Teacher understands that in performing this Agreement, the Teacher may have access to

breach of this Agreement. The Teacher agrees that if a subpoena is received for divulgence of

shall survive termination of this Agreement.

6. This Agreement may be terminated at any tine without cause by mutual agreement in writing

1. The Teacher acknowledges and agrees that he/she or its employees will be subject to

8. Reports will be prepared and records maintained by the Teacher as requested by the CSE with
copies of reports submitted to the CSE as requested.

9. Annual review and interim reports will be provided to the District as requested. Data for the
reports will be collected as part of the treatment sessions when one month notice is provided by the
District.

10. This Agreement constitutes the entire Agreement between the parties and contains all the
agreements between them with respect to the subject matter hereof.
