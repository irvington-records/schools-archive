An Important Message from the Board of Education

June 25, 2020


​Dear Irvington Community

The Irvington Board of Education condemns racial injustice and inequality. We
believe that these injustices must be remedied to secure a more equal
society. We must ensure that our schools are anti-racist and that we are all
addressing implicit biases in our everyday actions.

The core values of the Irvington School District are to provide a safe, welcoming
and nurturing educational environment for all students; and to inspire life-long
learning in our children. Over the years, the District has provided K-12
programming that teaches tolerance, understanding and the acceptance of
differences in a variety of contexts. We can and must do more.

The District is currently strengthening its Social Emotional Learning (SEL) by using
recommendations developed this year with a leader in the SEL field, Collaborative
for Academic and Social Emotional Learning (CASEL). The District is also in the
process of developing a partnership with The Metropolitan Center for Research on
Equity and the Transformation of Schools in order to perform a root cause analysis
of current District practices and programs and ultimately improve our work
around diversity and anti-racism education.

Your elected School Board is committed to working with the administration to
examine everything from equity in instructional resources to the discipline
process. We look forward to all school community stakeholders bringing their
passion and dedication to this difficult but necessary work. We stand together to
fight inequity and injustice and want to especially thank our current students and
alumni for their efforts in channeling their rightful outrage and pain into positive
steps toward change.

Sincerely,

The Irvington Board of Education
