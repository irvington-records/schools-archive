Irvington Union Free School District
Unauthorized Disclosure of Personally Identifiable Information Procedure §121.4

A complaint is made by the use of paper form and submitted to the data privacy officer (DPO), or via electronic form here.
Information is entered into the data tracking form and a digital folder and physical folder are created to investigate and respond to the complaint. The physical folder resides in the Education Center, technology department file cabinet.
Within 2 business days: Acknowledgement of complaint is sent to the complainant by the IUFSD Data Privacy Officer, or designee via e-mail.
An investigation is commenced by IUFSD Data Privacy Officer or designee. An Ad Hoc review team is formed.
Report of investigation and letter to the complainant is written and filed by the IUFSD Data Privacy Officer or designee.
Within 60 days: Provide the parent or eligible student, teacher, principal or any other staff member of the educational agency who filed a complaint with its findings OR where the response may compromise security or impede a law enforcement investigation, the educational agency shall provide the parent, eligible student, teacher, principal or any other staff member of the educational agency who filed a complaint with a written explanation that includes the approximate date when the educational agency anticipates that it will respond to the complaint.
