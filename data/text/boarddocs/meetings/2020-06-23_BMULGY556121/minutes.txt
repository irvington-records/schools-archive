7. Board of Education Statement

Action:  7.1 Approval of Letter to Community

Whereas the Irvington Board of Education condemns racial injustice and inequity and believes that any such injustices must be remedied to secure a more equal society;

Whereas the Irvington Board of Education is committed to ensuring that its schools are anti-racist and that all people and organizations much address implicit biases in everyday actions;

Whereas the Irvington Union Free School District maintains its dedication to its core values to provide a safe, welcoming and nurturing educational environment for all students, and to inspire life-long learning in our children;

Whereas the Irvington Board of Education values the K-12 programming that teaches tolerance, understanding and the acceptance of differences in a variety of contexts;

Whereas the Irvington Board of Education is committed to expanding this commitment to anti-racist education, eliminating social injustices and implicit bias and increasing instruction to diversity;

Therefore, be it resolved that the Irvington Board of Education approves the dissemination of the attached letter stating its beliefs herein.

This letter is posted on the Irvington UFSD website and in BoardDocs.

12. Business Office Matters

Action (Consent):  12.1 Establishment of the Multi-Linguist Scholarship Award Fund

Resolution: WHEREAS, the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District administers the Irvington Union Free School District; and WHEREAS, Michele Cella and Collette Dupre, hereinafter referred to as the "Donors" desire to make a gift to the Board of Education in order to create a scholarship fund, to be known as the "Multi-Linguist Scholarship Award Fund"; and WHEREAS Education Law 1709(12-a) and 2503 (1) authorizes the Board of Education: to take and hold in trust for the purpose of awarding scholarships in [the) schools any real estate transferred to it by gift, grant, bequest or devise, or any gift, legacy or annuity, of whatever kind, given or bequeathed to [the] board and apply the same, or the interest and proceeds thereof, according to the instructions of the donor or testator. NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Board of Education of the Irvington Union Free School District hereby creates the above-named Scholarship Fund, as set forth below, based upon the understanding between the parties: The Multi-Linguist Scholarship Award Fund 1. The Donors shall deliver to the Board, for deposit in the trust and agency account in the bank designated by the Board of Education the sum of One Thousand dollars ($1,000.00), with a 1 year commitment and then, afterwards, establish a GoFundMe web page (or similar) to continue the tradition. 2. The Scholarship Fund may be supplemented at any time by any donor(s) wishing to gift, grant, bequest or devise cash, cash equivalent or other property acceptable to the District or through any gift, legacy or annuity, of whatever kind, given, or bequeathed to the District for such purpose. 3. The scholarship shall be named the Multi-Linguist Scholarship Award. 4. If sufficient funds permit, the scholarship shall be awarded annually in the amount of Five Hundred Dollars ($500.00) each to a graduating male and female senior who have individually met the requirement of a love and commitment to languages and their culture. This award is not solely intended for the outstanding academic scholar, bu rather for the male and female students who embrace learning other languages and who remain receptive to experience the various cultures of their studies, and may have financial need. These two recipients would during his/her entire tenure in the Irvington School District, complete the sequences of two or more languages by the end of his/her senior year. Additionally, these students may continue the study of language(s), perhaps with study abroad during his/her college career. 5. The funds allocated to said scholarship shall come from such income or interest as in accumulated in a particular year plus such invasion of the principal as is necessary to issue said scholarship. 6. Details of the organization and administration of the Scholarship Fund will be left to the discretion of the Board of Education.
