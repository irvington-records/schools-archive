Meeting: Jun 23, 2020 - Special Meeting/Work Session
Date: 2020-06-23
Category: 6. Comments from Members of the Community
Subject: 6.1 Procedures for Community Comment on Topics that appear on Agenda
Type: Information

Community members can view the meeting by following the link noted in the header to this agenda. During the meeting, the Board of Education will use of the comment function in Zoom.  Those watching the hearing who wish to comment or pose a question regarding an item that appears on the agenda may do so using the chat function during the appropriate time on the agenda.  Also, should you have questions for the Board of Education or the Administration, you are asked to send them via email to: Board@IrvingtonSchools.org. The Board of Education and Dr. Harrison will receive these emails. Responses to questions will be posted on the website at a future date.
