The contract between the Irvington School District and the Greenburgh-Graham UFSD is to provide educational services beginning July 1, 2020. The student number, rather than the name, has been provided for in the resolution due to the confidential nature of this matter. The final tuition and maintenance rates are set by the NYS Education Department.
