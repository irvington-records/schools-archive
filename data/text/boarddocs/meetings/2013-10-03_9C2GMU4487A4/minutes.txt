	2. Discussion

		Discussion:  2.1 Discussion on Draft Policies: Non-School Sponsored Student Expression and School Sponsored Student Expression

		Mrs. Kerner thanked everyone for coming as it is important topic and stated that the purpose of the meeting is for the (Board and Administration) to get a sense as to how everyone else feels about the proposed policies, noting that the Board will continue its discussion at the Board Meeting on Tuesday night, October 8th.

		Dr. Harrison noted that there was a passionate Board meeting (September 24th) and it was clear that there was need for further opportunity to engage all, including the student body in the dialogue, therefore, this meeting was scheduled during the day to allow students to attend and participate in the conversation.

		Dr. Harrison again reviewed the traditional policy review cycle and noted that the policies being discussed first appeared on the August 27th Board meeting agenda for Board discussion. Both Dr. Harrison and the Board agreed that they were not prepared to move forward with the policies to be sure that they are representative of the district and will serve the students well. Dr. Harrison noted that the Board will again discuss the policies at their meeting on October 8th and that today is an opportunity to hear from students and community. Dr. Harrison again noted that the reason for a policy such as this is the need to strike balance for students expressing themselves, with the obligation of the District and BOE to protect the rights of the entire student body but also believes that students have an important voice as well as parents and it is important to hear their thoughts in order to ensure that the Board's governance meets the needs of the District as well as the community.

		There were approximately 60 students and parents in attendance. Several questions were concerning the role and purpose of the Board's policy committee. Mrs. Kashkin, who sits on the Policy Committee, stated that she doesn't see her role on the committee to decide what to put in front of the Board but to Mr. Montgomery noted that the entire Board must vote ______________
