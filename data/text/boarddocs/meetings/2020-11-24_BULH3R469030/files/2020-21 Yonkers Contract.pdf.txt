This AGREEMENT made as of July 1, 2020 by and between

       THE YONKERS SCHOOL DISTRICT having a place of business at One Larkin
       Center, Yonkers, New York 10701 and being located in the County of Westchester
       (“School District”)

and

       IRVINGTON UFSD, having a place of business at 40 North Broadway, Irvington, New
       York 10533 and being located in the County of Westchester, (the “Receiving School”).


                                       WITNESSETH:


       WHEREAS, the School District is duly empowered by law to enter into a contract
with the Receiving School for the instruction of students with disabilities in those situations
where the School District does not have an appropriate program available; and

        WHEREAS, the Board of Education (the “BOE”) of the School District has found
the Receiving School has an educational program which can appropriately provide instruction
and related services to enrolled student(s); and

       WHEREAS, the Receiving School is desirous of providing such instruction and related
services on the terms and conditions set forth herein.

        NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants herein contained,
the parties hereto do agree as follows:

1.      SCOPE OF SERVICES: The Receiving School shall provide appropriate instruction
and/or related services to Yonkers resident student(s) placed by the School District’s Committee
on Special Education (“CSE”). Services shall include, without limitation:

       a.     Implementation of goals and objectives for the educational services provided as
              per each individual student's individualized education program (“IEP”);

       b.     Completion of progress reports regarding student achievement of objectives as per
              report card schedules;

       c.     Complete all areas of the student(s)’ IEP including but not limited to student’s
              Present Levels of Performance, identifying the student’s strengths and
              weaknesses, management needs, transition (if applicable), testing
              accommodations, program modifications;

       d.     Provide the Committee on Special Education (“CSE”) with annual progress
              reports for each individual student receiving service to be reviewed at each
              student's CSE meeting;

       e.      Complete evaluations as per the request of the School District’s Director of
               Special Education or his/her designee, on an as needed basis, at rates mutually
               agreed upon by both parties;

       f.      Comply with any testing requirements upon notification of such review dates; and

       g.      Comply with request for transcripts, report cards, Regents examination,
               attendance sheets, and all other student records as the sending school may request,
               such materials shall be provided within seven (7) days of the request.

         The education provided by Receiving School shall be appropriate to the mental ability
and physical condition of the student(s) and in accordance with Commissioner’s Regulations, all
applicable Federal, State and local statutes, rules and ordinances. The Receiving School agrees
to provide services in accordance with the State Education Department’s (“NYSED”) regulations
using only professionals/service providers certified by the NYSED. All services shall be
provided in strict compliance with the student’s IEP. The Receiving School further agrees that
all disciplinary measures for disabled students will be conducted in accordance with applicable
federal, state and local laws, rules and regulations.

        It is acknowledged and agreed that the parties hereto will share necessary student IEPs
electronically via an appropriate and confidential web-based system (currently Frontline
Educaton). Upon termination, the Receiving School agrees to release these documents by
“unsharing” them via the web-based system.

         The Receiving School warrants that it has reviewed the IEP of each student to be enrolled
in its program(s) pursuant hereto, and represents that it is capable and willing to provide the
instructional and related services for those student(s).

2.      REPORTS: The Receiving School shall report in writing, at least twice each year to the
School District, summarizing the student's social and educational progress, stating the precise
nature of the program designated, and reporting all grades and attendance information. In
addition, within the timeframe not exceeding three (3) years from the date of placement, the
Receiving School will be responsible for providing the School District with a current
psychological, social history and medical examination.

3.     STUDENTS RECEIVING SERVICES: It is understood and agreed that services will
be provided to those students who are referred by the School District and accepted by the
Receiving School It is acknowledged and agreed that all such students must have approval from
the CSE of the School District for the requested services. Receiving School shall receive
payment only for the students who actually attend Receiving School.

4.      TERM (SCHOOL YEAR): The term of this agreement shall extend from July 1, 2020
to June 30, 2021, but shall be terminable by the School District as to any one or more of the
student(s) as set forth below.

5.      AUDIT: Prior to the making of any payments hereunder, the School District may, at its
option, audit such books and records of the Receiving School to substantiate the basis for

payment. The School District will not withhold payment pursuant to this paragraph for more
than thirty (30) days after payment would otherwise be due, unless the School District shall find
cause to withhold payment or the Receiving School fails to cooperate with such audit. The
School District’s right to audit shall survive for a period of one year following termination
hereof. The School District has the right to audit the performance hereof, including without
limitation, to make site visits in order to review records, observe the performance of services
and/or to conduct interviews of staff and patrons, where appropriate and not otherwise prohibited
by law.

        The Receiving School hereby agrees to cooperate with any investigation, resulting from
performance under this agreement, by the Yonkers Inspector General. Receiving School further
agrees to provide any additional information the School District may reasonably require in order
to respond to any audits associated with the services rendered to our students.

6.      RATE: The School District shall pay tuition to the Receiving School at the NYSED's
most current special education non-resident approved tuition rate per student upon the
presentation to it, on a monthly basis, by the Receiving School of proper invoices in a form
acceptable to the District. All such invoices must have a copy of the attendance record of the
student(s) attached. To receive payments, Receiving School shall submit invoices via email to
Raquel.Lopez@yonkersny.gov. It is acknowledged that no payment will be made to the
Receiving School during a period where students are not in attendance for ten (10) consecutive
days or longer. During any necessary remote learning periods called for health and safety, the
Receiving School will continue to provide instruction to students under contract and enrolled
with the Receiving School through remote methods unless the student is formally withdrawn
from the District and this contract is terminated. No changes to billing may occur for remote
instruction methods during mandatory closure for health and safety reasons. It is mutually
understood and agreed by and between the parties hereto that total compensation to be paid for
tuition to the Receiving School shall be limited to the amount approved by the NYSED for the
special education program and services set forth in each student’s IEP and that the School
District will not be liable for payment of any sums in excess of the amount so approved, except
to the extent evaluations are completed at the request of the School District, then the mutually
agreed upon rates for the completed evaluations also will be paid to the Receiving School. In the
event that any of the students are residents of the Richmond Children's Center, other chapter 721
Intermediate Care facility, Boards of Cooperative Educational Services (“BOCES”) or other
public schools the payment shall be made in accordance with the reimbursement schedule
provided by the NYSED.

       It is acknowledged and agreed that cost to the School District under this Agreement shall
be approved by the City’s Board of Contract and Supply (“BOCS”) and BOE as part of an
aggregate amount allocated for out of district special education programs. Any increase of said
amount shall require additional approvals.

7.      AVAILABILITY OF PERSONNEL: The Receiving School shall make itself available
to personnel designated by the School District for purposes of case discussions, personal
observations, educational reviews, impartial hearings and program visitations by prior
arrangement between the personnel involved. The Receiving School will not unreasonably refuse
to make available an appropriate staff member who provides services to the student for

telephonic and/or consultations at CSE meetings via video-enhanced platforms, in order to assist
the School District in providing appropriate recommendations and placements for the student’s
IEP.

8.      LICENSE AND AUTHORIZATION: Receiving School warrants that it is duly
authorized to perform the services as described herein. Receiving School warrants that it will
provide the School District with licensed and qualified individuals. Receiving School further
represents that such services shall be performed by individuals that are licensed under the laws of
the State of New York, inclusive of the NYSED licensing requirements or by persons otherwise
qualified to provide services in accordance with all applicable laws and regulations. Receiving
School shall certify that all such individuals possess documentation evidencing such license or
other qualifications as required by Federal, State or local statutes, rules, regulations and orders.

9.     MEDICAID COMPLIANCE:

       a.      Exclusion from Health Care Programs. The Receiving School represents and
               warrants that it has never been excluded from participation in any state or federal
               health care program, including, but not limited to, Medicare and Medicaid.

       b.      Assignment of Contractor’s Claims. Receiving School hereby assigns to the
               School District all Medicaid reimbursements claims for payment for services
               rendered by Receiving School under this Agreement. The School District and
               Receiving School agree that only the School District shall bill and receive any
               fees or charges for such services, which fees and charges shall accrue to the
               School District. Receiving School agrees not to bill Medicaid directly for any
               services provided to Yonkers student(s). Receiving School shall not be entitled to
               and shall not seek any compensation or benefits from the School District’s
               students, their insurers or any other source, other than compensation provided by
               the School District under this Agreement. Receiving School shall promptly remit
               to the School District any payments Receiving School receives from third party
               payers for services Receiving School renders hereunder.

       c.      Medicaid Billing. The School District shall provide or arrange for the provision
               of all billing, collection and related accounting services required in connection
               with the provision of Contractor’s services hereunder. Receiving School shall
               provide the School District with all information about services rendered in such
               form and substance as is required for School District to bill and collect for the
               services provided by Receiving School hereunder. Receiving School covenants
               and agrees that upon request of School District, Receiving School will cooperate
               with School District and provide such additional information and documentation
               as may be reasonably requested by School District to allow School District to bill
               and collect for the services provided by Receiving School hereunder and/or
               respond to third party audits associated with the services provided by Receiving
               School hereunder. Further, Receiving School will ensure that all services, reports,
               evaluations, and session notes are signed and/or certified by a qualified and/or
               certified provider as defined by Medicaid.

       d.      Corporate Compliance. Receiving School understands and acknowledges that the
               School District has a corporate compliance plan (the “Plan”) and that the School
               District promotes a compliance attitude with a culture that seeks to foster the
               prevention, detection and resolution of instances of misconduct. A copy of the

               Plan is available on the School District’s website
               https://www.yonkerspublicschools.org/cms/lib/NY01814060/Centricity/domain/7
               0/docs/policy/Section-6000.pdf. In furtherance thereof, Receiving School shall
               comply with the Plan throughout the term of this Agreement. Receiving School
               shall immediately notify the School District’s compliance officer of any violation
               of applicable law, regulation, third party pay or requirement or breach of the Plan
               of which Receiving School becomes aware during the term of the Agreement.

       e.      In addition, Receiving School hereby covenants and agrees that it has received,
               and that it will continue to receive throughout the term of this Agreement, annual
               compliance program training. Such compliance program training shall include,
               but not be limited to, mandatory reporting obligations to the School District’s
               compliance officer, an overview of the fraud, waste and abuse laws, medical
               necessity determinations, quality of care standards and whistleblower protections.

10.     SAVE LEGISLATION: The Receiving School understands and agrees that it is
responsible for complying with all applicable Federal, State, local statutes, rules, and ordinances
including the New York State Safe Schools Against Violence in Education (SAVE) legislation.
Receiving School shall adhere to all applicable requirements and protocols as established by the

services under this Agreement must be cleared by the NYSED in accordance with the provisions

School must submit a complete roster of names and social security numbers for all applicable
service providers who will be or may be providing services to the School District pursuant to this
Agreement, for the School District's verification of such providers' clearance status.

11.     ATTENDANCE RECORDS: Receiving School shall maintain monthly attendance
records which shall be submitted to the School District at the end of each month with invoice. If
a student has been absent for a period of five (5) or more consecutive days, the attendance record
shall indicate the reason for that absence. In the event of such an extended absence the
Receiving School must notify the School District within two (2) days following the fifth
consecutive absence.

13.     CONFIDENTIALITY. Receiving School understands that in performing this
Agreement he/she/it may have access to confidential information in possession of the School
District or others, including, but not limited to names, facts or information about individuals,
businesses and families. Receiving School may also have access to confidential information,
potentially including student directory information; personnel information and records;
information regarding sensitive, confidential or internal School District matters and other
protected information. It is agreed that the definition of confidential information includes all
documentary, electronic or oral information made known to Receiving School through any
activity related to this Agreement. Receiving School agrees not to reveal any confidential
information and understands that any such disclosure shall be considered a breach of this
Agreement. Receiving School agrees that if he/she/it receives a subpoena for divulgence of
confidential information, he/she/it shall notify the School District prior to divulging the same.
The parties further agree that the terms and conditions set forth in this Confidentiality section
and all of its subparts shall survive the expiration and/or termination of this Agreement. Without

     To limit internal access within Receiving School to the education records of the School
        District as well as to the student, teacher and/or principal data of the School District to
        those individuals that are determined to need such records or data to perform the services
        set forth in this Agreement;
To not disclose any personally identifiable information to any other party, unless:

        (c)     To safely store any data concerning the students, teachers and/or principals of the

                           Parents’ Bill of Rights for Data Privacy and Security

         The Yonkers City School District is dedicated and committed to protecting the privacy and
security of student, teacher, and principal data. In accordance with New York State Education Law
Section 2-d, the Yonkers Public School District hereby sets forth the following Parents' Bill of Rights for
Data Privacy and Security, which is applicable to all students and their parents and legal guardians. New
York State Education Law Section 2-d and the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (“FERPA”)
protect the confidentiality of personally identifiable information. The following is the Yonkers Public
Schools Bill of Rights for Data Privacy and Security.

         The Yonkers City School District, in recognition of the risk of identity theft and unwarranted
invasion of privacy, affirms its commitment to safeguarding student personally identifiable information
(“PII”) in educational records from unauthorized access or disclosure in accordance with both State and
Federal law. The Yonkers City School District establishes the following parental bill of rights:

         1. Student PII will be collected and disclosed only as necessary to achieve educational purposes
in accordance with State and Federal Law.
         2. A student's personally identifiable information cannot be sold or released for any marketing or
commercial purposes by the district or any third party contractor. The district will not sell student
personally identifiable information and will not release it for marketing or commercial purposes, other
than directory information released by the district in accordance with district policy.
         3. Parents have the right to inspect and review the complete contents of their child's education
record.
         4. State and federal laws, such as New York State Education Law § 2-d and the Family
Educational Rights and Privacy Act, protect the confidentiality of students’ PII. Safeguards associated
with industry standard and best practices, including, but not limited to, encryption, firewalls, and
password protection, must be in place when data is stored or transferred.
         5. A complete list of all student data elements collected by the State Education Department is
available for public review at http://nysed.gov.data-privacy-security or by writing to: Chief Privacy
Officer, New York State Education Department, 89 Washington Avenue, Albany, New York 12234.
         6. Parents have the right to have complaints about possible breaches and unauthorized disclosures
of student data addressed. Complaints should be directed to:
                  Deputy Superintendent, Yonkers Public Schools, One Larkin Center, Yonkers NY
                  10701 or send an email to BORComplaints@yonkerspublicschools.org.
                  b. Complaints can also be directed to the New York State Education Department online at
                  http://nysed.gov.data-privacy-security, by mail to the Chief Privacy Officer, New York
                  State Education Department, 89 Washington Avenue, Albany, New York 12234 or by
                  email to privacy@mail.nysed.gov or by telephone at (518) 474-0937.

        7. Parents have the right to be notified in accordance to applicable laws and regulations if a
breach or unauthorized release of their student’s PII occurs.

         8. Parents can expect that educational agency workers who handle PII will receive annual
training on applicable federal and state laws, regulations, educational agency’s policies and safeguards
which will be in alignment with industry standards and best practices to protect PII.

        9. In the event that the District engages a third party provider to deliver student educational
services, the contractor or subcontractors will be obligated to adhere to State and Federal Laws to
safeguard student PII. Parents can request information about third party contractors by contacting Deputy
Superintendent, Division of Teaching and learning, Yonkers Public Schools, One Larkin Center, Yonkers
NY 10701 or send an email to BORComplaints@yonkerspublicschools.org.

        New York State, through the New York State Education Department, collects a number of student
data elements for authorized uses. A complete list of all student data elements collected by the State is
available online through http://www.p12.nysed.gov/irs/sirs/documentation/NYSEDstudentData.xlsx for
public review online. You may obtain a copy by writing to: Office of Information & Reporting Services,
New York State Education Department, Room 863 EBA, 89 Washington Avenue, Albany NY 12234.

         10. For purposes of further ensuring confidentiality and security of student data, as an appendix to
the Parents’ Bill of Rights each contract Yonkers Public schools enters into with a third party Consultant
shall include the following supplemental information:
         a. the exclusive purposes for which the student, principal or teacher data will be used;
         b. how the third party Consultant will share the student, principal or teacher data with, if any, will
abide by data protection and security requirements;
         c. When the agreement expires and what happens to the student, teacher, or principal data upon
expiration of the agreement;
         d. If and how a parent/guardian, student, eligible student, teacher, or principal may challenge the
accuracy of the student data or teacher/principal data that is collected; and
         e. Where the student, teacher, or principal data will be stored (described in such a manner to
protect data security), and the security protections taken to ensure such data will be protected, including
whether such data will be encrypted.

         The Yonkers Public Schools Parents’ Bill of Rights for Data Privacy and Security reflects
information available as of this document's creation. The District Bill of Rights is subject to revision and
is projected for revision in accordance with further guidance received, including guidance through
NYSED. Additional information is available on the New York State Education Department website.
http://www.p12.nysed.gov/docs/parents-bill-of-rights.pdf
