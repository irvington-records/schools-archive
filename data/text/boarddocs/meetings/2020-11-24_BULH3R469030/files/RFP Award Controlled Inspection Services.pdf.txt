4 hours on-site time totally ending before 12:00 PM or starting after 1:00 PM. Client will be billed for each
discipline performed on site.

G. Overtime rates are applicable after eight (8) hours, before 7:00 AM or after 3:30 PM, Monday through Friday,
and are charged at one and one-half (1%4) times the hourly rate. Saturday services are charged at two (2)
times the full day rate for up to the first eight hours. Any Holiday or Weekend (Saturday or Sunday) second or
third shift work has commensurate pricing.

H. For fabrication shop inspections all travel expenses and, if necessary, accommodations will be additional. Shop
inspections will be billed at an overtime rate of one and one-half (1%) times.

|. Nuclear density tests are included in the unit rates provided for up to the first 10 tests per day. Any additional
density testing will be billed at three dollars pertest.

J. Allinspections must be requested prior to 2:30 PM the previous working day. Inspections requested after this
time will be provided on a first-come, first-serve basis and will be billed at overtimerates.

K. All following day inspection cancellation requests must be made prior to 3:30 PM the previous working day.
Inspection cancellations requested after this time will be billed based upon the unit rate for the cancelled
inspection.
