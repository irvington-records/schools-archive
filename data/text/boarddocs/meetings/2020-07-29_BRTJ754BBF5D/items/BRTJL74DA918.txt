Meeting: Jul 29, 2020 - Special Meeting
Date: 2020-07-29
Category: 5. Comments from Members of the Community
Subject: 5.1 Procedures for Community Comment on Topics that appear on Agenda
Type: Information

The Board of Education will permit up to 30 minutes for public comment. Those wishing to comment on topics that appear on the agenda are invited to place their name and address in the Zoom Q&A. The Board of Education President will invite the respective members of the community to speak.  Each speaker will have up to three minutes to speak.  The Board of Education requests that the Q&A function not be used for other comments, communications or questions.
