Action:  3.1 Appoint Acting Superintendent of Schools

BE IT RESOLVED that the Board of Education herewith appoints Dr. Gail Duffy to serve as the Acting Superintendent of Schools effective immediately in accordance with the District's protocol(s) for the vesting of power in the event certain District officials are absent from their positions; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that said appointment shall continue until further notice and/or action by the Board; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Board of Education herewith authorizes and approves the payment of an additional stipend at the daily rate of $137.50 for each day of service in said position; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that all other terms and conditions of Dr. Duffy's employment agreement with the District for the 2023/24 school year shall remain unchanged notwithstanding the above-referenced appointment as the Acting Superintendent of Schools.

Motion by Jayne Wissner, second by Susan Brunenavs.
