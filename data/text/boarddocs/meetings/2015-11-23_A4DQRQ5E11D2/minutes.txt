Special Meeting (Monday, November 23, 2015)

	Generated by Elaine Cardea on Monday, November 23, 2015

	Members present

	Also present

	Kristopher Harrison, Supt. of Schools Beverly Miller, Asst. Supt. for Business, member of the community

	1. Call to Order

	Mr. Whitney called the meeting to order at 8:00am. The Pledge of Allegiance was said.

	2. Strategic Planning Committee Members

	Mr. Whitney reported that one of the students to serve on the committee informed the Board that he could not participate due to a conflict on Dec. 8th. Mr. Whitney opened the dialogue with the Board to discuss finding an alternate student, but stated that it might be challenging to do so given tomorrow is the first committee meeting. Mr. Hanna suggested that if an alternate is selected, the student could observe tomorrow and be appointment at tomorrow night’s meeting.

	The second matter under discussion was what role the senior district office administration would have on the committee, observer or participant. It was noted that the facilitator, Giselle Martin-Kniep felt strongly that the superintendent should be present. Ms. Palmieri expressed her thoughts that as CEO of the District, the Superintendent should be present as he will ultimately be responsible for the administration of the plan. Mr. Grados and Mr. Hanna concurred that Dr. Harrison should be present at the meetings, as much as would be feasible given his other responsibilities. Mr. Montgomery disagreed, stating that his presence might impact staff and parents and influence outcomes. He again expressed his discomfort with the process and what the final product will yield. It was noted that the Board will receive regular updates from the facilitator to be kept informed of the work of the committee. It was reiterated that it will be the Board’s responsibility to review and make any changes to the strategic plan that they will ultimately adopt. Mr. Hanna noted that the process for developing as well as the final strategic plan had been discussed at numerous public Board meetings.

	A community member asked why the strategic planning meetings will not be open to the public, as she feels strongly that the community should be part of the process and not advisory in nature. (Note: Board advisory committees are not subject to the Open Meetings Law). In response, Mr. Whitney reported that the issue can be discussed further at tomorrow’s Board meeting, but stated that the committee is indeed serving as advisory inasmuch as it will be the Board’s responsibility to determine the final Strategic Plan. Request to have it on record that that question was not fully addressed.
