strengths, skills and talents, and foster social and civic responsibility.

Technology Goals 2022-25
➢ Technology Goal #1: All learners will have equitable access to a range of
 digital resources appropriate for instructional goals and all learning styles,
 using an anytime, anywhere, supportive computing environment.

➢ Technology Goal #2: All learners will gain and develop foundational
 technology proﬁciency, media literacy skills, and the ability to practice
 digital citizenship across all grade levels and content areas.

➢ Technology Goal #3: All staff members will be provided with ongoing
 differentiated professional development targeted to their individual needs,
 grade level/content area with a continual focus on emerging and innovative
 technologies.

Why Filtering?
As part of the Federal grant process called E-Rate, public schools and libraries that
receive funding are required to purchase ﬁltering tools (ﬁrewalls) and software to
protect end-users from inappropriate content for school consumption
(obsecenity, pornography, other content harmful to minors).

As districts shifted to mobile devices, schools and libraries, under the E-Rate
program, were required to extend ﬁltering to those devices to work off campus as
well.

GoGuardian

Since the summer of 2022, the District moved toward a new ﬁltering system,
GoGuardian. The previous vendor, Lightspeed, was unable to meet the District’s
needs for compliance ﬁltering for self-harm, violence, and bullying. Furthermore,
the District took reactive steps based on feedback about the inability to ﬁlter by
grade level and school to ﬁnd a better platform.

Communications regarding device acceptable use were updated with the new
vendor’s name, which all returning students’ parents saw in the new registration
process.

Technology Usage

Technology usage varies by school-level, grade-level, content, and
course:
●   Its use is consistently reviewed by staff with adjustments being made

●   Traditional forms of teaching and learning are not replaced by technology.
    Rather, it is extended, enriched, or increases engagement

●   Use and instructional practice will continue to evolve to maximize learning,
    meet student needs, and fulﬁll curricular standards
