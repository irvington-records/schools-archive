                        3 Day Washington D.C. Capital City Tour


 Wednesday, April 1, 2020
 6:45 AM Buses (3) to report to school
 7:15 AM Departure – stop for lunch enroute at group’s expense
12:30PM Arrive in Washington D.C. - Join Tour Escorts at Air and Space Museum
 1:00 PM Visit Air & Space Museum/ National Mall and Smithsonian Museums

 Thursday, April 2, 2020
 7:30 AM Breakfast at hotel
 9:30 AM Gallery Passes to see Congress in session

 8:00 PM Ghost and Graveyard tour of Old Town Alexandria
10:30PM Security to monitor hallways

Friday, April 3, 2020
7:30 AM Breakfast at the hotel and check out

          Challenger Space Expedition
1:00 PM Lunch at Shops at National Place (coupon provided)
2:00 PM Departure for home
7:30 PM Arrival back at school
