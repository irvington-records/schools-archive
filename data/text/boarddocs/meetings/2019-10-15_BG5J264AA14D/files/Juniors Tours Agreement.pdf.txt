                                       Tour Confirmation Agreement
This Tour Confirmation Agreement (this "Agreement") is between Junior Bus Tours, Inc. ("Junior Tours")
and Irvington Middle School (the"Group"). Junior Tours and the Group agree that:

TOUR PACKAGE
Junior Tours will provide the Group a tour of Washington D.C. from April 1-3, 2020, with the following:
* Round-trip transportation via motorcoach
* 2 night’s accommodations at Hilton Springfield
* 2 breakfasts - at your hotel
* 2 lunches – meal vouchers
* 2 dinners – request Buca Di Beppo and Theisman’s
* Tour Guide for each bus 24 hours/day in Washington D.C.
* Sightseeing tour as per itinerary
* All admissions, taxes and tips while on tour
* Free chaperones in the ratio of 1 to 8 paying in double occupancy
* $500 cash stipend
* Promotional materials: forms, baggage tags, travel organizer, 2 t shirts and 1 hoodie sweatshirt
* Insurance- Junior Tours, Inc. maintains professional liability insurance policy

NUMBER OF PARTICIPANTS TOUR FARE AND PAYMENT PROCEDURE
The all inclusive price on quad occupancy is $565 per person. The Tour price is based on 120+ paying
participants. Should the final number of paying participants fall below 120 paying participants, Junior Tours
reserves the right to increase the tour fare and/or adjust the Tour Package accordingly.

The total tour fare shall be paid according to the following deposit schedule (Should the Group require additional
time to make these payments, please call Junior Tours):

        1st Deposit:    $150 per person is due 10/15/2019
        2nd Deposit:    $150 per person is due 12/15/2019
        Balance:        $265 per person is due 2/15/2020

ROOMING LIST - ADDITIONAL ROOMING CHARGES
The Rooming List should be forwarded to Junior Tours as soon as it becomes final, but not later than six weeks
prior to the departure date.

Tour package price includes double rooms for complimentary chaperones. Any other person opting for (i) single
occupancy will pay $100 per person per night; (ii) double occupancy will pay $50 per person per night; and (iii)
triple occupancy will pay $25 per person per night.
