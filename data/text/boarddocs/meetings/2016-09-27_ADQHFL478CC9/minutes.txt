3. Administrators' Update/Board Committee & Liaison Reports

- Dr. Harrison acknowledged that Mr. Palermo announced his retirement and the Board will accept it later in the meeting.

- With respect to the field projects, the delay was due to materials backordered from manufacturer. There is a huge domino effect when changes and shifts do impact the work in the District.

Teachers’ goals are directly related to the building goals that have been created by the principals. 6. Presentations/Discussions

Presentation: 6.1 2016-17 District and School Goal. (Presentation)

Dr. Harrison began the presentation noting that the focus of this presentation was not only to provide the Board with an overview of each of the buildings’ goals but to provide professional learning for the Board to appreciate the dialogue of what is taking place in each of the schools.The mission statement speaks to creating a challenging and supportive learning environment, as well as to foster social and civic responsibility. The beliefs and commitments are borne from the three Theories of Action that were developed three years ago. Dr. Kor spoke to the foci for the 2016-17 school year and are contained in three primary goals:

- To implement instructional practice to elevate student thinking and understanding;

- To develop a balanced assessment system that measures students' content knowledge, skill, understanding and dispositional learning; and

- To design and introduce a new content management system to enhance communication with the parent community.

At the heart of the learning and the commitment that the district has made is the professional development that has taken place over the five years that includes Understanding by Design which is instructional design as well as curriculum mapping through Rubicon Atlas, which is a written curriculum so that all students have the same experiences, and through assessments which will prove that that is happening throughout the district. Teachers then bring their individual passion via their lesson plans and activities.

Mr. Graeber commented that parents, especially K-8, have expressed the desire to see what the scope of work will be for the year in order to better help their child and he's not sure if parents understand what balanced literacy is which would help their children more directly. He would also like to see more specific goals, especially Goal 3 with regard to assessment reflecting something more directly and concretely.

Following the introductory dialogue, principals and assistant principals spoke to how their building is working to implement instructional practices and each highlighted specific programs that are in place to elevate student thinking, the assessment systems that are in place to measure the students' content knowledge, skill, understanding and dispositional thinking and provided examples such as observable as well as formative assessments, student self-assessment, digital on-line assessment, etc. They each highlighted other focuses that are taking place in their building to enhance each students' educational experiences. Examples include, but are not limited to:

- Bard Writing Program and Metamorphosis at the high school and middle school;

- accountable talk/Socratic Seminar and PLTW at Main Street School; and

- UBD curriculum development/implementation, math stations, accountable talk and PLTW at Dows Lane.

Administrators also provided examples of other ways their school expands educational experiences for all students. For instance, at the high school level, student leadership and expression are being cultivated and topics such as student wellness and dealing with stress are being discussed, moving to the Google platform with Google Classroom and increasing student access to technology. Mr. Cohen Mr. Samuelson spoke to the use of assessments at the high school and the ways in which students are assessed. Across the District, there is great focus on expanding the integration of technology in the classrooms with the use of Google Classroom. It was noted that the use of Google Classroom also allows for submission of work and allows for efficiency and opportunity for feedback by the teacher on an ongoing basis.

The focus at IMS is looking at the whole child. Structures in place at IMS to support student learning includes professional learning, grade level learning sessions that will enhance students' experiences. Mr. Sottile noted that the collaborative nature among teachers at the middle school helps to frame the success to pursue this year's goals. Ms. Daley also spoke of the curriculum mapping and the shift now to implementation with the use of essential questions to construct daily lessons to push thinking of students and using the structures in place with teachers refining their practices and lessons by collaboration with their peers.

Ms. Chapnick reiterated and reinforced the information that was shared by high school and middle school administrators and the ways that MSS prepares the students for middle school then onto high school which illustrates the continuum of our K-12 program, with a common language to all faculty and then trickles down to students and the message is consistent throughout so that students know what the expectations are for them. MSS includes using technology in their global citizenship goal, PLTW as well as student support, both for social and emotional learning which emphasizes expectations viś a viś Habits of Mind, academic intervention through RTI. An important focus at MSS continues to be to help children learn who they are along with what it means to be a good citizen.

Deb Mariniello noted that the similarities among the schools is deliberate and by design and also noted that curriculum and assessment go hand in hand. The work now at Dows Lane is implementing the curriculum writing work that has been undertaking over the years, is now being implemented. Also of note, they are proud and excited to be delivering English as a New Language (ENL) as a co-teaching model as the belief is that it is better transfer of knowledge when the support is done within the classroom along with their peers. Expectations of student behavior are reinforced through morning meeting, along with the development of dispositional thinking and mindfulness as part of their character building program.

In the area of technology for 2016-17, Jesse Lubinsky spoke to the districtwide focus on communications with the focus on launching a new website in the Spring 2017. The District is partnering with School Wire to develop a user-friendly and interactive design for the website. Focus groups will be created having stakeholders identify additional needs, and to communicate what is wanted in the website and

Dr. Harrison noted that the goals discussed tonight will be linked to the Strategic Plan and noted the impressive body of work that is going on in the District. He thanked the administrators for the work that they are committed to and that it is moving the district forward. Mr. Hanna noted that this was a deeper, richer conversation at the beginning of the year than in previous years.
