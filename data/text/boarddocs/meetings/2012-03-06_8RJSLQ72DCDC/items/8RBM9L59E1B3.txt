Category: 9. Business Office Matters
Subject: 9.3 Contracts- Architectural/Engineering Services
Type: Action (Consent)

The District solicited Bids for architectural/engineering services for various minor capital projects.  Several firms submitted proposals which were reviewed by a committee comprised of administration, board members and 2 community members. After interviews and discussion the decision was made to recommend LAN Associates.
