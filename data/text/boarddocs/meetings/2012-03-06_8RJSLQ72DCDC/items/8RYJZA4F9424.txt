Date: 2012-03-06
Category: 9. Business Office Matters
Subject: 9.6 Health Contracts - Out of District Students
Type: Action (Consent)

As provided by Section 912 of the State Education Law, the Irvington School District is responsible for providing health services to Irvington resident students that attend private or parochial schools in other school districts.
