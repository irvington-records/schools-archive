    Recommendations

     Continue to streamline the personnel files and tracking
     systems

     Monitor certification and seniority areas

     Restructure appointment information

     Set up systematic processes for Board appointments
     including part time or extra pay positions

     Increase current substitute pool and review electronic
     substitute calling options

+
    Recommendations continued

     Update current use of technology systems such as FM,
     OLAS and MLP

     Maintain electronic seniority list

     Develop guidelines for recruitment and selection to
     ensure open process for all applicants

     Provide updated information to staff regarding
     compliance officers and ensure yearly mandated
     training occurs in each school building (Sexual
     Harassment and Hazcom)

     Provide compliance officers training to ensure
     appropriate workplace investigations

+
    Final recommendation

     Given the amount of oversight required for instruction, it
     may be beneficial to reinstate the position of Assistant
     Superintendent of Curriculum and Instruction.

     In addition to curriculum work, the position could
     oversee the in-service and graduate credit approvals,
     trainings and compliance as well as supervise the
     clerical position in human resources.
