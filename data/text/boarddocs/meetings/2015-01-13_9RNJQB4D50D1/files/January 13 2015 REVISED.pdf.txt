 All students should possess essential technology skills and
participate in critical technology learning experiences that will
 equip them for success in our schools and prepare them for
                  the challenges of their future.


This vision is supported through the District goals and
Theories of Action and will be delivered through the lens of the
Technology Framework.

 Clearly define goals & expectations for the use and
  integration of technology
 Develop courses and curriculum to increase the integration
  of technology
 Increase alignment with standards based on a continuum of
  knowledge and skills
 Increase communication and planning among faculty
 Re-evaluate software for current and future needs
 Increase use of mobile devices
 Increase professional development to support teaching and
  learning
 Define infrastructure plan to meet current needs and future
  growth

 Apply the collective thinking of our school community to plan our
  work in technology:
   o Technology Leadership Team – ETDT - a group of teachers, students and
     administrators who meet on a regular basis to assess the status of the
     curriculum, instruction, assessment and professional development in the
     district.
   o Technology Think Tank –designed to open the dialogue to a broad range of
     thinkers – parents, students, teachers and administrators. Continue this
     model inviting new members to join on a regular basis to ensure inclusivity of
     all levels of expertise.
