Subject: 4.2 Nomination and Election of Vice-President of the Board of Education
Type: Action

The District Clerk will declare nominations open for the office of the Vice President of the Board of Education for the 2015-16 school year. Should  there be more than one nominee, the member with the majority of votes will thereby be appointed vice president
