Category: 4. Nominations, Election of Board Officers, Oath of Office for President and Vice President, Line of Authority Within the Board
Subject: 4.1 Nomination and Election of President of the Board of Education
Type: Action

The District Clerk will declare that nominations open for the office of the President of the Board of Education for the 2015-16 school year. Should there be more than one nominee, the member with the majority of votes will thereby be appointed president. Following the oath of office, the district clerk then turns the meeting over to the Board President
