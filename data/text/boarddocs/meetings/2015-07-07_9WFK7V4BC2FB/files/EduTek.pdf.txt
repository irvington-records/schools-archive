PROJECT DESCRIPTION
Working with the Irvington Union Free School District, (IUFSD) Edu Tek Limited (ETL) will provide
comprehensive Information Technology (IT) services at each of the district's three sites: the Dows
Lane ES (including the relocated district offices), Main Street ES and the Middle/High School campus.
This agreement will commence on July 1, 2015 and end on June 30, 2016. Edu Tek will support the

district’s Local Area Networks (LAN’s) and Wide Area Network (WANs). This service includes support
for all operating systems currently in use at all sites The IT support described will be performed by:

* One (1) on-site, full-time, Level I! System Engineer

* One (1) on-site, half-time, Level | System Engineer

Project Commencement

The anticipated project start date is July 1%, 2015.

Daily & Weekly

Edu Tek will provide One (1) onsite, full-time, Level Il System Engineer, for forty (40) hours per week,
52 weeks per year, less discretionary time described below. Their schedule will be from 8:00am until
4:30pm and they will receive % hour for lunch unless another mutually agreeable schedule is
determined. Additionally, Edu Tek will provide One (1) onsite, half-time, Level | System Engineer, for
twenty (20) hours per week, 52 weeks per year, less discretionary time described below. Their
schedule will be from 8:00am until 4:30pm, Tuesday and Thursdays and 8:00am until 12:30pm
Wednesday’s. They will receive % hour for lunch unless another mutually agreeable schedule is
determined. Twice monthly Edu Tek requests attendance at project status meetings in our
Mamaroneck office at 4:30 PM by all employees. If there are no urgent matters requiring our
immediate attention, we request that that the onsite staff be dismissed one half hour (.5) early (4:00

PM) and allowed to attend these biweekly meetings.
Transfer:

To ensure stability and integrity of its network operations the district requires that Edu Tek personnel
transfers and assignments to/from district schools meet the approval of the Superintendent of
Schools. The Superintendent of Schools reserves the right to approve and/or disapprove any
individual hired by Edu Tek to be an on-site network specialist for any reason he/she deems
appropriate. The transfer of an existing on-site network specialist to another district during the school
year must be approved by the Superintendent of Schools. The district requires that any personnel

changes being considered be effectuated at a time least disruptive to its day-to-day operations.

Discretionary e

For each full time Edu Tek Staff position held in district Edu Tek will receive two (2) weeks or ten (10)
days of discretionary time in addition to holidays (see below). For each half-time Edu Tek Staff
position held in district Edu Tek will receive one (1) week or five (5) days of discretionary time in
addition to holidays (see below). This time will be used to fill employee vacation and sickness
requests. The time may also be used to provide training to our onsite staff or to perform tasks not

Staffing Definitions

System Engineer Level One
Certifications - MCSP, CAN, CLA or equivalent experience
Skill Set:

* — Ability to install Network operating system and perform simple operating system upgrades (not
requiring any data or application movement/migration).

* Basic operating system troubleshooting

* — Standard network/local printing and print queue installation and management.

* General network Administration (adding users, groups, shares, printers).

¢ — Server hardware configuration.

* Rack device installations and cable management.

* SCSI attached storage subsystems.

* Implementation of Patch process for individual NOS.

* Non configurable/non manageable switch installations.

* Stand-alone Tape drive installations.

¢ Approved server based (non enterprise) tape backup software installation and configuration
(specific agents may require a SE Level 2).

* General server diagramming and documentation

System Engineer Level Two
Certifications - MCSE, CNE, CLE or equivalent experience
Skill Set:

* — Ability to install Network operating system and perform more complex upgrades (application and
data moves must be well defined and pre-approved)

* Advanced troubleshooting of operating system.

* Advanced network/local printing such as shared faxing and shared scanning.

* Advanced network Administration such as Directory and Domain troubleshooting.

* Cluster Hardware configurations

* Blade Server configurations

* Entry Level NAS/SAN (Fiber Attached Subsystems and Appliance NAS devices) in cluster
configurations only. Does not include additional products for load balancing or active-active
failure.

* Implementation of Patch process for Server group such as Microsoft Servers using bundled
solutions such as SUS, WUS and Zenworks.

* Entry level managed/configurable switches not requiring routing, trouncing, or multiple

manufacturer switch operation.
