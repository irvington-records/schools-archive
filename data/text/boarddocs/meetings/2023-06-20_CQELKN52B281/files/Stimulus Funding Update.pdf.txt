Summary of Federal Funds - American
Rescue Plan (ARP)
On March 11, 2021, the President signed into law the American Rescue Plan
Act of 2021 (ARP). New York State has been allocated nearly $9 billion in
ARP Elementary and Secondary School Emergency Relief (ESSER) funds.
These funds are focused on improving potential lasting pandemic impacts.

   Irvington UFSD Allocation: $464,238
    ●   Funds may cover expenses through September 30, 2024
         ○   20% of funds must be applied to assist with potential “learning loss”
