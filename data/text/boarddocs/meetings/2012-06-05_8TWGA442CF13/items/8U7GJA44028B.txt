Subject: 9 1st Read - Bullying, Harassment and Hazing Policy

The Dignity for All Students Act  requires that the Public have the opportunity to review and comment on the Bullying, Harassment and Hazing Policy prior to the Board's adoption. The Irvington Board of Education will adopt the policy at its July 10, 2012 meeting.
