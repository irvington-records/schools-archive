Irvington High School
Physical Education Department 

Grading Policy

	The Irvington Physical Education program guides students toward the development of skills needed to make decisions concerning personal health and wellness. Students are exposed to a variety of activities in class, and as a result should recognize the need for a lifelong commitment to fitness.
	Therefore, “New York State authorizes the granting of a local IHS diploma based on the successful completion of 20.5 units in an approved course of study. This includes 2 units of Physical Education (1/2 each year). Four years of Physical Education must be successfully completed” (Student Guidelines Manual).
	All students will be graded on a letter scale basis, and grades will be computed into the student’s grade point average. The following procedure will be used to determine the grade each student receives:
A maximum of 4 points will be awarded per class according to the point system below:
4 Points 
Student comes to class on time
Student is properly dressed for class
Student actively participates for the entire class period
Student exhibits good sportsmanship/citizenship
Student remains in class for the entire period

The student will lose points for different items as discussed below:
Loss of 1 point
Student arrives late to class and is unexcused 
Student exhibits poor sportsmanship/citizenship
Student leaves the class without permission
Student does not participate in warm-up activity

Loss of 1-4 points
Student does not actively participate for the entire class period

Loss of 4 points
Student is fully unprepared for class *** (i.e. NO DENIM, improper footwear)
Student is not active for the entire class period
*** If the student has sneakers but does not change for class, he/she will not be allowed to participate for the class.

An additional 5 points will be deducted from the final quarter average each time a 
             student accumulates 3 unprepared classes.

The 5 points per quarter will also be graded through assessment.  Assessment is a process used to gather information about the participant’s achievement. The primary goal of assessment is to enhance learning. Types of assessment that will be applied in our program will include exams, laboratories and self/peer evaluations.
If a student has an excused absence or medical excuse, he/she will be given the opportunity to do an alternative assignment or make up the class period to earn the points for that day.
If a student has an unexcused absence (e.g. cut), he/she will not be able to make up the points, and will lose eight (8) points towards their final grade.
At the end of the marking period, the total number of points for each day will be totaled and a number grade will be given.
