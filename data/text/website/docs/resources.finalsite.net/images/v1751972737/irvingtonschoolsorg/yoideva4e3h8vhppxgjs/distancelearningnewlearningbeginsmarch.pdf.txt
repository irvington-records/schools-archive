Dear Dows Lane Families,
We hope you are all managing during this difficult time. All of us at Dows Lane are thinking about you and
your children and wishing we were back in school.

Our teachers and Related Service Providers have been working hard to createnew learning experiences
for students to complete at home that will begin on Wednesday, March 25th. Below are details about our
plan:
        Classroom teachers will communicate daily assignments by 8:00am via an email or Google
        Classroom
               Directions will be clear and explicit
               Teachers will support learning during the school day and will track student process
        Special Area teachers will post weekly activities on the Dows Lane Website - Special Areas
               Special Area teachers will also include a Google Form link as an exit ticket
        Special Education teachers and Related Service Providers will communicate with students/
        families who receive services as per each student's plan
        Instructional Coaches are consulting with teachers to help develop At-home learning assignments
        Nurse Fleming, our Psychologists, Social Worker, and School Counselor are all available via
        email to help support the well-being of students
        Students are expected to participate and engage in the daily work assignments
               If a student does not participate, classroom teachers will contact families to check in and
               offer suggestions and assistance
        Please start your day with this video: Dows Lane Pledge of Allegiance with Dr. Kantor

If you are looking for additional activities for today and tomorrow, please visit ourDows Lane Library
Resources.




If your child is struggling with At-Home learning or needs additional support, please reach out to your
child's teacher, Related Service Provider, or us. We know that the school closure is stressful for all
involved, and we remain focused on the health and well-being of our students and staff. We are still here
to support your child, in any way we can, and we appreciate your partnership in this unusual time.

Stay healthy,
Andrea & Linda
