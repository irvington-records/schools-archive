Re: Districtwide Lead in Drinking Water Sampling 2023-2025

Dear Mr. Pittore,

On October 27, 2023, Enviroscience Consultants, LLC performed a limited water sampling event at the
Irvington Union Free School District. Samples were collected at Main Street School, Dows Lane Elementary
School, and at the Middle School & High School Campus from potable water sources selected by the district.

First draw samples were collected in wide-mouth 250 milliliter containers and during the early morning,
prior to any water sources being used. The samples were transported to New York Environmental
Laboratory and were analyzed for lead concentration. The current limit for lead in drinking water is 5 parts
per billion (ppb). There were no exceedances.

Please see the attached Outlet Inventory tables and lab reports for detailed results at each location.

Should you have any questions, please feel free to contact me.
