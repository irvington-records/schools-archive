5140 AGE OF ENTRANCE
Kindergarten
Students who are legal residents of the School District and who reside with parents or guardians
within the School District must be five (5) years of age on or before December 31st of the year of
enrollment in order to register for kindergarten. Students who turn six (6) years of age on or before
December 31st of the enrollment year will be placed in First Grade.
For the 2025–2026 and 2026–2027 school years only, students who turn six (6) years old on or before
December 31 of the year of enrollment may be permitted to enroll in Kindergarten at the discretion of
the Superintendent.
Other Grades
Admission of children to other grades shall be determined by the District using a consideration of both
chronological age and the readiness of the children to do the work of that grade, which may be
evaluated using District assessments.
Proof of Age
A student's birth certificate or other satisfactory evidence of age shall be presented at the time of initial
registration. The child shall be entered under his/her legal name.
