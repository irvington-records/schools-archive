DEBATE CLUB

Contact: Mr. Dore

(jason.dore@irvingtonschools.org)

The Debate Club welcomes all IMS students. In this club, we begin by
selecting compelling topics for discussion. Once a topic is chosen, students
delve into research to deepen their understanding of the selected topic.
Armed with knowledge, we then immerse ourselves in the art of debate.
We use the Middle School Debate Format to engage in debate as well as
evaluate our fellow debaters.

When debating, students are on teams of 3- each standing at the "podium"
(a desk) and delivering their points. At the appropriate time, students on
the opposing team or in the audience can stand and ask for a "Point of
Information" or participate in "heckling ". Join us for some fun and hone
your debate skills.

We will meet every Thursday at A103 at 8:00am.
If you are interested in participating, please join our google classroom
page.

FRENCH CLUB

French Club offers French students an opportunity to

practice speaking French in a fun and lively
atmosphere. The emphasis is on French and
francophone culture, and students will enjoy
traditional foods, watch films, play board games and
other traditional games such as pétanque (bocce ball).
French Club students perform community service by
fundraising for worthy causes, including purchasing
French books for middle schools in Sénégal and raising
money for Médecins Sans Frontiéres (Doctors without
Borders). French Club students take a leadership role
in our school-wide celebrations of National French
Week and Mardi Gras.

NATIONAL JUNIOR
HONOR SOCIETY

This is not an Open enrollment club.

NJHS is a nationally recognized honor society with a focus on
academics, leadership, integrity and community service.
Students’ cumulative GPAs will be calculated at the end of the
first marking period of their 8th grade year and invitations to
apply will be sent out to all students whose GPA meets the
NJHS requirement of 3.9 or higher.

8th grade students are continually monitored throughout the
year that they maintain a cumulative GPA of 3.9. Throughout
the remainder of their 8th grade year, prospective members
will have multiple opportunities to complete community
service projects both independently and within NJHS.
Students will be formally inducted into the NJHS during a
ceremony in the spring.

The Youthful Hearts Initiative Club

Empowering Change Through Compassion

Our Mission: At The Youthful Hearts Initiative Club, our mission is to make
a positive difference in our community by assisting less fortunate people. We
believe that even small actions can create big impacts. Together, we strive to
spread kindness and support to those in need, fostering a spirit of
compassion and unity.

What We Do:
Clothing Drives: Collect and distribute gently used clothing to families and
needy individuals.

Food Drives: Gather and provide nutritious food to local food banks and
shelters.

Inspirational Cards: Create heartfelt cards for seniors to be delivered with
their meals to brighten their day.

Care Packages: Assemble and distribute care packages filled with essential
items and comfort goods.

Community Events: Organize and participate in various events aimed at
raising awareness and supporting community welfare.

Advisor: Marianne.kennedy@irvingtonschools.org
Location: Library Second Floor Room 201
Google Classroom Code: epmu2mf
