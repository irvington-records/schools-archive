                              Help with Groceries for Your Family
Many families qualify for help buying groceries through the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance
                Program (SNAP), even if adults in the household are working.

                                       What Is SNAP?
SNAP helps eligible families buy healthy food each month using an EBT card that works like a
                                 debit card at grocery stores.

                             Why SNAP Matters for Students
   Helps ensure students have enough nutritious food at home May automatically qualify
            students for free school meals Supports health, focus, and learning

                                        Who Can Qualify?
  Eligibility is based on household size, income, and certain expenses. Many working families,
                          seniors, and people with disabilities qualify.

                                    What Can SNAP Buy?
   Fruits and vegetables, meat, poultry, fish, dairy products, bread, and other staple foods.

                                         How to Apply
                     SNAP is managed by each state. Families can apply:

            Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) | OTDA
