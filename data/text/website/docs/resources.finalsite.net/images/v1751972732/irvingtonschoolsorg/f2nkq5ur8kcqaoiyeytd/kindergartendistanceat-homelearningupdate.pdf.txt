April 8, 2020

Hello Kindergarten Families,

Following the communication you received this week from your child' kindergarten teacher, we would like to
share our thinking around Google Classroom and kindergarten Distance/ At-Home Learning:
       We initially began kindergarten Distance/At-Home Learning with emails to families, while our
       Kindergarten teachers were learning and establishing their Google Classrooms
       We are evolving in our Distance Learning, and as Dr.Harrison stated in the District Newsletter on April
       4th, we are planning to increase our interactive experiences and student-teacher engagement,and those
       experiences will be within the District's Google Classroom platform
       As a District, our 1st-12th grade students use Google Classroom, and we believe Google Classroom is
       the best way to organize assignments, communication, and learning
       Feedback and communication from teachers is more effective and efficient
       Students and families are able to find all assignments in one place
       Kindergarten students are being given the opportunity to create, collaborate, learn, and explore

We understand this is a shift for Kindergarten families and we are here to support you with Distance/At-Home
Learning and Google Classroom. Please email your child's teacher or us with any questions. We can also
arrange a phone call with a Dows Lane staff member, to walk you through Google Classroom and make
sure you are comfortable and ready to support your child.
