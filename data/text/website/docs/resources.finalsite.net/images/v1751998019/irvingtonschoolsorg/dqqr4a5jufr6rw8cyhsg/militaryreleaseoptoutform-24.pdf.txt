Irvington High School
                               Military Release Opt-out Form

This is in regards to the responsibility of IHS to provide military recruiters access to student
recruiting information. The National Defense Authorization Act requires local educational
agencies that receive federal funding to provide access to secondary school student names,
addresses and telephone listings upon a request made by military recruiters. Under this
provision, a secondary school student or parent of the student may request that such
information not be released without prior written parental consent.
If you do not want your child’s name, address and phone listing released without written prior
consent, please submit to the main office by Thursday 9/29/23.

Please DO NOT release my child’s name to military recruiters.
