Safety, Security and Procedures


District Policies
2021-22 Annual Notification of District Policies and Procedures



Visitors
Maintaining a safe and secure campus is an essential consideration and students and parents will
experience various changes in our protocols this year. As Dr. Harrison shared, visitors to schools will be
limited for indoor activities. Visitors who are permitted to enter the building for necessary activities
may be required to complete a health attestation and provide information for contact tracing purposes.

Visitor Parking
This year, we will be hosting many of our meetings and events virtually. In the event that you are
scheduled to come into the building, be advised parking is limited on campus. The main lot outside of
the high school and next to Meszaros Field is for Staff only between 7:30 am and 3:30 pm with every
spot and all curb parking assigned to a staff member. When visitors park in these staff lots, they may be
blocked into the spot by staff members who must get to class. In such cases, the visitor must then wait
until after the staff member is free in order to leave the lot. Therefore, it is important that all parents
avoid the staff lots during school hours. Visitors are asked to use the upper parking lot at the top of the
campus. For short appointments, visitors may use 15-minute parking at the island in front of the school
between the hours of 9:00 am and 1:00 pm. Students are not visitors and may never park at the island
or in the staff lots during school hours.

Masks
Students and staff must wear masks at all times when indoors, with the exception of when they are
eating lunch in the Cafeteria. Adherence to all safety protocols is critical for the well-being of all
members of our school community. Failure to comply with mask wearing protocols will constitute a
violation of the Code of Conduct and may be subject to disciplinary consequences.

Attendance
It is our belief that success in school is linked directly to attendance. Students may be ineligible to
receive credit for courses if their class absences have exceeded 20 unexcused absences (10 for a
semester-based course). This year, families will receive letters via mail regarding unexcused absences.
Families may also opt to receive automatic daily emails regarding their child’s attendance. If your child is
going to be absent, please call the attendance office at 914-269-5483 by 10:00 am. Please refer to the
IHS Student Handbook for detailed explanation. If you have any questions related attendance, please
contact Mrs. Orlando at 914-269-5422.
