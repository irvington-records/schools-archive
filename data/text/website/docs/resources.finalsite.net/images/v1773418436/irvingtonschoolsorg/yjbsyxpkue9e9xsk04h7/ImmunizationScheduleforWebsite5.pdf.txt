New York State Immunization Requirements
                        for School Entrance/Attendance
Children in New York State must be properly immunized to attend school. An official immunization record, signed or stamped by a
healthcare provider, must be submitted to the school district within 14 days of the first school day. Acceptable records include
those from a pediatrician, the New York State Immunization Information System (NYSIIS), or the New York City Department of
Health Citywide Immunization Registry (CIR).

The New York State Department of Health and the NYC Health Department endorse the AAP immunization schedule. Doses
given before the recommended ages or intervals are invalid and do not count toward the number of doses listed below. Children
in gradeless classes must meet the immunization requirements of their age-equivalent grade. Check footnotes for
vaccine-specific details.
