THE HIGH SCHOOL
    Irvington High School is located in the suburban Village of Irvington in southern Westchester
County, 20 miles north of New York City on the Hudson River. The majority of its 6,500
residents are employed in management, professional and related occupations, especially in the
                                                                                                           CONTACTS
areas of finance, science and educational services.
    Irvington High School takes pride in providing students with a rigorous college preparatory

program and broad athletic, fine arts and extracurricular offerings. Fostering a culture of
academic strength, IHS has been recognized by U.S. News & World Report and Newsweek                              Dr. Mara Ratesic
as one of America’s best public high schools. Advanced work beyond New York State Regents                         Superintendent

avail themselves of challenging work are strongly encouraged as a pathway to self-enrichment                     Mr. Scott Wynne
