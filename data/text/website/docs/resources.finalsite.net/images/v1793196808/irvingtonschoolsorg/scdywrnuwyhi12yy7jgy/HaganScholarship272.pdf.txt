Re: Hagan Scholarship — Application Now Open, Deadline December 1, 2026

A personal word from a counselor is often what convinces a qualified student to apply. The Hagan
Scholarship is a need-based merit scholarship worth up to $60,000 that gives goal-driven seniors the
opportunity to graduate college debt-free. HSF has awarded 6,920 scholarships to students attending 745
colleges and universities in all 50 states and D.C. We awarded 1,326 new scholarships this year and can
award up to 1,500 for 2027-2028; several students from the same school are often selected.

Please Encourage Seniors Who:

« Attend a U.S. public, private, charter, magnet, or STEM high school, regardless of citizenship.
Have a 3.50 CGPA or higher, are goal-driven, and willing to work to achieve their goals.
Have parents whose 2025 Adjusted Gross Household Income did not exceed $125,000.
‘Will enroll in a four-year college or university in the fall 2027 semester.

Will work 240 hours (non-family employment) from January 1, 2027, to September 1, 2027.

‘What Hagan Scholars Receive:
« Up to $7,500 each semester for up to eight semesters.
* $1,000 to purchase essential items for college.
e Free life-skills workshops, a personal Schwab brokerage account, HSF investment account to
manage, and up to $8,000 for study abroad.
« Eligibility for a Hagan Graduate Scholarship worth up to $30,000.
* No application fee. FAFSA information is not required to apply.

Recognition for Your Students and School:

When a student is awarded a scholarship, we notify the high school counselor named on the student’s
application and provide an HSF-approved announcement that can be read during your awards ceremony,
used in a school publication, or published in your local newspaper. Please ask your students to list you
as their counselor when they apply.

Three brochures are enclosed for your counseling office, scholarship coordinator, and senior teachers.
Please print additional copies from the HSF website and provide them to students you believe are
eligible. Students can apply at haganscholarships.org/application. The application deadline is
December 1, 2026. Scholarships will be awarded by May 1, 2027.

Purpose
The Purpose of the Hagan Scholarship is to
provide each recipient with the opportunity
to obtain a four-year college education and
graduate debt-free. And, to provide each
recipient with a practical understanding of
important life skills not typically covered in
the school curriculum.

Purdue University

Recipient Responsibilities

1. Must maintain a 3.25 CGPA and a four
year or less graduation schedule.
2. Must earn fourteen credit hours each fall
semester and thirty credit hours each
academic year excluding summer school.
Graduate students must maintain a 3.50
CGPA and maintain their approved
Graduation Schedule.

wo

To be fair to all recipients we will not make
exceptions to the Scholarship Criteria or to
Deadlines.

University of Kansas

Scholarship Particulars

1. The scholarship disbursement amount, up to
$7,500 each semester, will be determined by HSF
by taking the Total Cost of Attendance including
tuition, fees, room, board, books, supplies, personal
and transportation costs; then deducting the total
dollar amount of ALL scholarships, awards, grants,
waivers, Federal and institution provided work
study, and one-half of recipient’s FAFSA SAT.
Parents are not required to pay the SAI, it is used
only to determine the disbursement amount.

2. The disbursement amount will be adjusted each
semester to offset the expiration or loss of other
scholarships, awards, grants, waivers, Federal or
institution work-study, and changes in the
recipient’s FAFSA SAI.

3. The scholarship is renewable for up to seven
additional consecutive semesters if the recipient
fulfills the scholarship criteria and mandatory
workshop responsibilities.

4. First year recipients will open a Personal Schwab
Brokerage Account and receive $1,000 to purchase
essential items for college such as a computer,
printer, office supplies, cell phone, clothing, and
bedding.

5. Second year recipients will be provided with an
HSF Schwab Brokerage Account to manage until
graduation. HSF funds managed by each recipient
will be $10,000 the second year, $15,000 the third
year, and $20,000 the fourth year.

6. Third year recipients who achieve a 3.50 CGPA
are eligible for an all-expense paid study and travel
abroad experience worth up to $8,000 or the funds
can be used to study abroad for one semester.

7. Recipients must work 240 hours from January 1,
until September 1, prior to the start of each
academic year in college. Work must be regularly
scheduled non-family employment performed
under supervision for compensation.

8. Recipients graduating college while in the Hagan
Scholarship Program can apply for a Hagan
Graduate Scholarship, good for up to four
additional consecutive semesters receiving up to
$7,500 each semester.

9. There is no application fee or processing fee.

Duke University

FAQs

How many scholarships have been awarded?
6,920 scholarships have been awarded to date.

How much is the Hagan Scholarship worth?
1. Undergraduate Scholarship: Up to $60,000.
2. Graduate Scholarship: Up to $30,000.

Other Benefits, Regardless of Financial Need:

|. Free Workshops to learn important life skills.

2. First Year: Personal Schwab Brokerage Account.
$1,000 to purchase essential items for college.

3. Second Year: $10,000 HSF Schwab Account to
manage.

4. Third Year: $15,000 HSF Schwab Account to
manage. Up to $8,000 to Study/Travel abroad.

5. Fourth Year: $20,000 HSF Schwab Account
to manage.

6, After Graduation from college: Recipients will

receive all gains in their managed HSF Schwab

Accounts up to $10,000, and a Graduation Package

including a Certificate of Achievement. Walnut

Plaques will be awarded to recipients for excep-

tional achievement.

Is this a need-based or merit-based scholarship?
BOTH. Applicants must authenticate genuine
financial need in order to attend college. Recipients
are selected based upon academic ability, personal
goals, and achievements in and out of school.

Why do you have mandatory workshops?

To help recipients obtain a practical understanding
of important life skills not typically covered in the
school curriculum.

Why are recipients required to work?

The Grantor worked during high school and college
and believes each recipient will personally benefit
by contributing to the cost of his or her education.
