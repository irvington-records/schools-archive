Vendor Discovery and Proposal Request
                                                       October, 2026



1. Purpose and Desired Outcome
Irvington Union Free School District is seeking vendor proposals for a refreshed copier environment that supports
reliable building-level printing, copying, scanning, and secure release printing. The District also wants vendors to
propose innovations that may allow a meaningful reduction in classroom printers by shifting staff printing to secure
copier-based workflows.

●   Replace or refresh current copier assets using the District’s current models as the baseline model framework.
    Start date is January 2027. Ideally installations would occur over the December holiday break.
●   Evaluate whether additional strategically placed machines or enhanced models can reduce reliance on
    classroom printers and/or increase efficiency in offices.
●   Maintain the District’s PaperCut software environment and expand secure release capabilities through badge
    readers at each copier/multifunction device.
●   Improve color access, high-volume capacity, uptime, service responsiveness, and user experience across all
    schools and district offices.
●   Provide toner supplies and maintenance for office printers maintained

The District is also exploring additional copier placements and capacity upgrades that could support secure release
printing and reduce classroom printer dependency. Vendors should validate these locations during discovery and
propose the appropriate equipment class, capacity, and finishing capabilities. Click here for a current list of classroom
and office printers that we intend to keep and remove (one tab for each).

Dows Lane Elementary School
●   Could replace the Main Office copier with a high-capacity color printer.
●   Add a copier to the 2nd floor flex space.
●   Use vendor discovery to confirm the number of current classroom printers and identify printers that can be
    retired.

Main Street School
●   Increase Main Office capacity and add color capability.
●   Increase Main Office black-and-white capacity.
●   Consider an additional small unit on the 3rd or 4th floors.

Irvington Middle School
●   Update the 2nd Floor Science machine to include color.
●   Increase black-and-white capacity in both the Guidance Office and Teacher Room.
●   Add one small machine to the 1st floor and one small machine to the 2nd floor.

Irvington High School
●   Add a copier to the 1st floor near the boys’ restroom.
●   Add color capability to the Main Office copier.
●   Provide higher capacity for the large Main Office copier.



5. PaperCut, Badge Readers, and Secure Print Requirements
The proposed solution must maintain and support the District’s PaperCut software environment. The District expects
each copier or multifunction device to support secure print release and authenticated access through badge readers.

●   Include badge readers for each copier/multifunction device included in the proposal.
●   Describe compatibility with the District’s current PaperCut environment, including licensing, embedded
    application support, configuration, and ongoing support responsibilities.
●   Support secure print release, user authentication, job accounting, reporting, and print policy enforcement.
●   Identify any required network, directory, card/badge, firmware, licensing, or server requirements.
●   Provide an implementation plan that limits disruption to staff and school operations.

6. Vendor Proposal Expectations
Vendor responses should include a clear, building-by-building recommendation that addresses both baseline
replacement and possible additional placements. The District welcomes alternate recommendations when they
improve cost, reliability, workflow, or capacity, provided the vendor explains the rationale.

●   Recommended replacement model for each current copier machine.
●   Recommended device type, volume capacity, color capability, finishing options, and duty cycle for each proposed
    additional location.
●   Service and maintenance model, including response times, preventive maintenance, supplies, escalation
    procedures, and uptime commitments.
●   PaperCut and badge-reader implementation plan, including roles and responsibilities.
●   Training plan for office staff, teachers, support staff, and technology staff.
●   Lease, purchase, or managed print pricing options, with costs separated for equipment, copier or print volume
    both B&W and color, service, supplies, PaperCut-related items, badge readers, and implementation.
●   Identify purchasing source: State Contract, Piggyback contract (identify who owns contract), or Purchasing
    Cooperative. The District is currently participating in Omnia, Sourcewell, Ed Data, TIPS and BuyBoard but will
    consider others.
●   Transition plan from the current environment to the proposed environment, including migration timeline and
    decommissioning strategy for classroom printers where appropriate.



7. Vendor Questions to Address
1. What stands out about your product line or service model that is especially well suited to Irvington UFSD’s
    needs?
2. What innovations can you propose for the District’s current environment and potential shift away from classroom
    printers?
3. How would your solution support secure printing, badge release, and PaperCut integration at every copier?
4. Which machines, if any, can support poster printing or larger-format output? Please describe maximum paper
    size, media handling, speed, finishing, and cost implications.
5. Which machines can support booklet/program printing?
6. Where would you recommend adding, removing, relocating, or consolidating machines, and why?
7. How do you determine whether a location needs color, black-and-white, high capacity, finishing, scanning, or a
    smaller workgroup device?
8. What reporting can you provide to help the District monitor usage, control costs, and identify classroom printers
    that can be retired?
9. What is your service response standard during school days, and how do you handle repeated service issues or
    chronic downtime?
10. What cybersecurity, data privacy, hard-drive, firmware, authentication, and network security practices are
    included?
11. What training and change-management support do you provide to help staff adopt secure release printing?



8. Evaluation Considerations
●   Fit of the proposed fleet to building needs and anticipated print volumes.
●   Ability to reduce classroom printers while maintaining convenient and secure staff access.
●   Quality of PaperCut integration and badge-reader implementation.
●   Reliability, service responsiveness, supplies management, and lifecycle support.

●   Total cost of ownership, including equipment, copies or prints, service, supplies, software, accessories,
    implementation, and training.
●   Strength of vendor recommendations, innovation, and ability to support future changes in printing practices.
●   Past relationships and/or school district references



9. RFP Process
●   After reviewing this scope document and prior to submitting a proposal, interested vendors should schedule a
    meeting with the Director of Technology and Assistant Superintendent of Business to review the scope document
    and ask clarifying questions. Any questions that result in new scope will be disseminated as an addendum to
    other interested vendors
●   After these meetings, the District will relay a firm date for proposals to be received, likely mid November. Once
    proposals are received, the District will evaluate all proposals received and look to enter into a contract with a
    vendor for implementation for January 2027.



10. Requested Response Format
●   Executive summary of the proposed solution.
●   Building-by-building equipment matrix for current replacements and proposed additions.
●   PaperCut and badge-reader integration summary.
●   Implementation timeline and transition plan.
●   Service, maintenance, and support plan.
●   Pricing schedule with clear assumptions and optional alternatives.
●   Responses to all vendor questions in Section 6.
