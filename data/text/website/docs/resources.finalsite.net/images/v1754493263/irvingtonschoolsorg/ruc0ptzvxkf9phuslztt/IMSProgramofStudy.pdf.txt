                                Grade-by-Grade Course of Study
At IMS, students in grades 6-8 follow the course of study outlined below. Course titles in bold represent
required areas for students to incorporate into their mix of courses. Where multiple courses are listed (by
bullets), these areas represent the multiple possibilities for student in meeting the requirements of the
boldfaced areas. In the case of the Unified Arts Rotation, the letters represent each of the courses
needed to make up the specific rotation by grade.
