The Irvington School District is implementing “​P​roject ​L​ead
T​he ​W​ay”, the nation’s leader of rigorous and innovative
STEM​ (​S​cience – ​T​echnology - ​E​ngineering - ​M​ath)
curriculum.


   ● Through activity-, project-, and problem-based
     curriculum, students use structured approaches, like
     the engineering design process and employ critical thinking.
   ● Students will work on PLTW units of study for 55 minute period, weekly for the
     duration of the school year. The classroom teacher will be present for PLTW.
   ● PLTW for this year will include two strands: Engineering and Computer Science.
   ● iPads ​and apps will be integrated into all units of study.
   ● UNITS :
        ○ ENERGY: Collisions
        ○ INPUT/OUTPUT: Human Brain
