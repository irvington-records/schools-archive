GIVE-19
SPONSORED BY GIRL TALK




Give-19​ is a movement that encourages our Irvington
community to unite as one during a time of uncertainty
and isolation to support one another.
Our goal is to redefine the number ​19​, which currently is
associated with isolation, fear, and powerlessness, to a
number that corresponds to community, hope, and
action.

Please join our movement and to unite our community
through small thoughtful actions. We encourage
everyone to take part in ​Give-19​! Here are some ways
you can do so: baking cookies for a neighbor, supporting
local businesses, writing letters to patients at nursing
homes, sending thank you letters to doctors and nurses,
or anything you think will brighten someone’s day!
Here are some organizations you can send your
creations to if you need any ideas:
      Cabrini Nursing Home- ​115 Broadway, Dobbs Ferry, NY 10522
      Paws Crossed- ​100 Warehouse Ln, Elmsford, NY 10523
      Hebrew Home- ​5901 Palisade Ave, The Bronx, NY 10471

**If you have any issues or are unable to send your gifts please email
Jackie and Lara so we can help distribute if for you.


Jackie Pollack and Lara Greene have created a facebook
group chat in which individuals can post their creations.
This group chat can be found under ​Give-19 Irvington.
