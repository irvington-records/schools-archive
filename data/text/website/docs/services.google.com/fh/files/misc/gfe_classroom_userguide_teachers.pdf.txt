Getting started with
Google Classroom
A quick start guide for educators to
launch their digital classroom in 7 steps.


   Your checklist for setting up your class and engaging students:

           Sign in to Google Classroom
           To get started, sign in to classroom.google.com with your official school email.                Learn how
           If you can’t get in, contact your administrator to enable your access.



           Create your class
           Set up your digital home base. This is your central hub for announcements,                       Learn how
           assignments, and resources.


           Customize your class settings
           Go to your class settings to change the class theme, manage invite codes,                       Learn how
           and control who can post and comment on the Stream page.


           Set up your gradebook
           Go to your class settings to decide how grades are calculated, create                            Learn how
           grading categories and periods, and manage visibility for students.



           Invite your students
           Connect with your students by sharing a unique class code or by                                  Learn how
           sending email invitations directly.


           Post an announcement
           Share welcome messages, reminders, or discussion prompts with your entire                        Learn how
           class using the Stream page.


           Create an assignment
           Go to the Classwork page to create, schedule, and post assignments.                              Learn how
