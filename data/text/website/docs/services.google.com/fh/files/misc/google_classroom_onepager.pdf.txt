GEMINI IN CLASSROOM

Amplify learning impact with Gemini in Classroom,
a suite of contextual AI tools for teaching and learning
Gemini in Classroom acts as a collaborative partner, supporting educators throughout their workflows so they can
focus on the art of teaching, rather than administrative tasks. With access to over 30 Google AI tools, educators can
quickly go from idea to instruction and and seamlessly scale more tailored learning.



AI tools for educators
Plan lessons, personalize learning, and tackle
everyday tasks faster with a Gemini tab designed
for educator workflows.
 ●   Scaffolded tools and starter prompts:
     Get immediate help brainstorming real-world
     examples, developing unit plans, or creating
     vocabulary lists.
 ●   Tailored course material support: Easily
     select relevant classwork and course
     materials to make Gemini’s outputs highly
     contextual and accurate to your syllabus.
 ●   Simplified workflows: Instantly generate
     custom rubrics directly within your
     assignment creation workflow, and use class
     insights to create tailored check-in
     suggestions.

with teachers in the loop. We use educator feedback and insights to rethink and rework our features for the classroom.
