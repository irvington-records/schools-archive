    One of the benefits of going to college is meeting students from different
                                                                                         My Questions / Notes

    backgrounds. Diversity on a college campus can mean differences in race,             ______________________________________
    religion, ethnicity, gender, geography, etc. When thinking about diversity,
    consider the following…                                                              ______________________________________

                                                                                         ______________________________________

Research First                                                                           ______________________________________

qq Check out the “About” or “Fast Facts” sections on a college’s website to learn the    ______________________________________
   demographic breakdown of students attending the institution.
                                                                                         ______________________________________
Ask the Admission Office
                                                                                         ______________________________________
qq Is enrolling a diverse student body part of your institution’s mission statement?
                                                                                         ______________________________________
qq How will I find people like me and/or faculty and administrators who will
   understand my experience?                                                             ______________________________________

   sexual orientation, gender identity, and religion)?
                                                                                         ______________________________________
qq What does diversity look like in the surrounding off-campus community?
                                                                                         ______________________________________
qq What places of worship are available on and near campus (e.g., mosques,
   synagogues, churches, and prayer rooms)? Are professors supportive 		                 ______________________________________

                              FINANCIAL AID

    Paying for college is big piece of the admission process and can be a
                                                                                          My Questions / Notes

    concern. There are many financial options that help make college more                 ______________________________________
    affordable. Consider the following when inquiring about paying for college…
                                                                                          ______________________________________

                                                                                          ______________________________________
Research First
                                                                                          ______________________________________
qq All colleges must offer a net price calculator on their websites, which can 		
   help you estimate costs. You can also find tuition costs there.                        ______________________________________

qq US Department of Education’s Federal Student Aid website, studentaid.ed.gov.           ______________________________________

Ask the Admission Office                                                                  ______________________________________

qq Who can walk me and my family through the financial aid process and 		                 ______________________________________
   types of assistance (e.g., scholarships, grants, loans)?
                                                                                          ______________________________________
qq Can someone explain what the actual cost of attendance may look like once
   financial aid is taken into consideration?                                             ______________________________________

qq What is the process for applying for a scholarship at your institution?                ______________________________________

qq What employment opportunities are available on campus?                                 ______________________________________

qq If I am unable to pay the application fee, does your institution accept fee waivers?   ______________________________________

qq Is financial aid available to undocumented students or students with DACA status?      ______________________________________

qq What financial aid is available if I am a transfer student?                            ______________________________________

    the most successful students are the ones who seek help before they need it.        ______________________________________
    Ask the following about the support programs to help you succeed…
                                                                                        ______________________________________

                                                                                        ______________________________________
Research First
                                                                                        ______________________________________
qq View majors/degrees via the Department of Education’s College Navigator website
   at nces.ed.gov/collegenavigator.                                                     ______________________________________

qq Share your interests with an admission representative so they can connect them       ______________________________________
   with majors.
                                                                                        ______________________________________
qq Most college websites offer information on average class size, as well as
   department information.                                                              ______________________________________

Ask the Admission Office                                                                ______________________________________

qq What academic support services are available (e.g., advising, tutoring)?             ______________________________________

qq Does your institution have academic flexibility? How will your institution support   ______________________________________
   my academic goals?
                                                                                        ______________________________________
qq Are there opportunities to experience classes/campus life?
                                                                                        ______________________________________
qq How does your institution support undecided students?
                                                                                        ______________________________________
qq Can you speak about outcomes? First-year to second-year retention rates?
   Graduation rates?                                                                    ______________________________________
