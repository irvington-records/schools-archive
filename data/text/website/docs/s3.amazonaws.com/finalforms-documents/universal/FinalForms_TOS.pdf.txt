X. TEXT MESSAGE PROGRAM (SMS) TERMS

Program:
FinalForms operates the FinalForms Text Alerts program on behalf of the school district that has enrolled
you.

Program description:
By providing your mobile phone number and opting in, you agree to receive recurring automated text
messages from your school district through FinalForms. These messages include:
    ●​ Registration and form completion reminders, signature requests, and physical and athletic

    ●​ Athletic and activity event notifications, including schedules, practice and game changes, and
        participation reminders
    ●​ Emergency, weather, closure, and safety announcements
