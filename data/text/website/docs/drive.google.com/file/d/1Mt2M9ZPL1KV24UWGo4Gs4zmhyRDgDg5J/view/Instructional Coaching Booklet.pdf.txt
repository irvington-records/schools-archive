          What is the role of an instructional coach?
  An instructional coach is a specialist educator dedicated to strengthening teaching and
learning across classrooms. Serving as a teacher-centered partner rather than an evaluator
 or supervisor, the coach’s primary goal is to enhance instructional practice, build teacher
                    capacity, and ultimately improve student outcomes.

 Instructional coaches collaborate with teachers as trusted colleagues and critical friends,
  offering support, feedback, and opportunities for professional growth in curriculum and
instruction. They also play an important role in the intentional integration of instructional
 technology—helping educators select and use digital tools that align with learning goals,
increase student engagement, support differentiation, and foster collaboration, creativity,
                                 and meaningful feedback.

While no coach is an expert in everything, they are responsive, supportive teammates who
work alongside teachers to continuously reﬁne practice and promote high-quality learning
                                experiences for all students.
