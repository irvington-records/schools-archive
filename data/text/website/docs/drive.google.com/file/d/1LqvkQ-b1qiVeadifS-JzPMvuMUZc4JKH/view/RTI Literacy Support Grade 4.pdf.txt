Where Is That Animal?

1 Animals sometimes need to hide. They may want to avoid an animal that
hunts them. Or, they may want to sneak up on an animal they are hunting. One
way an animal can hide is by blending in with its background. An animal's
coloring can help it do this.

2 The color of some animals is the same as the background. There is alot of
snow where polar bears live. These bears are white like the snow. This makes it
hard for other animals to see them.

3 For other animals, their coloring breaks up their outline. Then, they don't stick
out against the background. These animals might have spots or stripes. A zebra's
stripes help to break up its outline in a herd. When zebras are standing or moving
as a group, their stripes blend together. So, a hungry lion has trouble picking out
any one zebra to attack.

It’s Not What You Think

1 Some animals look like an object to blend in with their background. For
example, an insect might hide by looking like a branch or a leaf. One of these
insects is the walking stick. It looks just like a stick or twig, If it stays very still, an
enemy will pass without noticing it.

2 In the movies, an actor might play the role of a real person. The actor looks
and acts just like that person. In the same way, animals can imitate other animals.
This helps them to stay safe. Sometimes they look and act like a dangerous
animal. An elephant hawk caterpillar has two false eyes to scare enemies. It raises
its head and moves from side to side like a snake.

3 Some animals look like they would not make a good meal. For instance, the
viceroy butterfly looks like the monarch butterfly. Both are orange with black
stripes and spots. However, they don't taste the same. Birds won't eat monarch
butterflies because they taste bitter. Viceroys taste good, but birds think they are
monarchs and avoid them.

What Is a Wilderness?

What do you think of when you hear the word wilderness? You might think of a
place to camp or fish. Or you might picture animals living free in the wild. Perhaps
it brings to mind a forest alive with green trees and plants. All of these ideas are on
the right track,

The Wilderness Act of 1964 defines wilderness. It is a place touched mostly by
the forces of nature and not by people. Humans are no more than visitors. In other
words, it’s an area where tlie land is in its natural state. And the government wants
to make sure that it stays that way. So, it has set off these areas to protect.

What can't people do in wilderness areas? They can't build roads or dams. They
can't cut down trees or drive motor vehicles. In fact, there can be no machines
with motors at all. And, since 1984, new mining has not been allowed in these
areas.

What can you do in these areas? You are free to enjoy all that nature has to
offer. You can swim, fish, and camp. You can glide across pure, crystal clear water
on a canoe ora raft. Or you can take a hike and perhaps do sume bird-watching.

The wilderness is very important. It has rivers and streams that provide
drinking water to many areas. It helps filter the air for better air quality. It is the
home of many kinds of animals and plants. Without this home, they might die off.
And it is an area of natural quiet, where we can get away from noise and crowds. It
is a true gift of nature.

“T'm going to call back to camp for a ladder. You keep him safe and warm until
