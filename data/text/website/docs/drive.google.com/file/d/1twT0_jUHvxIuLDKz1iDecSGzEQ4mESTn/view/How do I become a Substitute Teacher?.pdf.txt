SUBSTITUTE TEACHER REQUIREMENTS

1) What are the requirements to become a substitute teacher in the
   Irvington Union Free School District?

     All substitute teachers for the Irvington Schools must meet one of
     the following requirements:
     a. Certified teacher
     b. Hold a Bachelor’s Degree from a College or University in any
        area

2) If I meet any of the above requirements, how do I apply?

     If you meet the above requirements and want to apply to be a
     substitute teacher, you must send the following information to:
        via Email:
              Lisa.Tortora@IrvingtonSchools.Org
        Or
        via mail:

              Ms. Lisa Tortora
              Human Resources
              Irvington UFSD
              6 Dows Lane
              Irvington, NY 10533

     a. A current resume outlining your prior experiences
     b. A copy of a diploma or transcript that indicates receipt of a
        Bachelor’s Degree
     c. A copy of your certification (if applicable)
     d. Two professional letters of reference


3) What happens after I send in the information?

     a. You will be contacted to schedule an interview.
     b. Prior to your scheduled interview you must complete an
        application through the Putnam Northern Westchester BOCES
        Online Application System (OLAS) at www.olasjobs.org/lhv. You
        do not need to specifically apply to the Irvington UFSD; however,
        we must be able to access your application prior to your
        scheduled interview.
   4) I am ready to become a substitute, now what?
         Once interviewed and found to meet or exceed all of our
         qualifications, your references will be checked. You will also be
         required to do the following:

         NYS Education Law requires all new employees to a school district
         to be fingerprinted. See link below. If you have already been
         fingerprinted by NYS Education Department or NYC Board of
         Education (after June 1990), you will not need to be fingerprinted
         again.

         http://www.highered.nysed.gov/tsei/ospra/

   5) When will I be able to substitute in a classroom?

         When all of the above is completed, you will be recommended as a
         Substitute Teacher to the Board of Education. Once approved to
         work, you will be required to complete new hire paperwork and you
         will be assigned a log-in and PIN# to access AESOP (our substitute
         calling system).

         On your first day of work, the building principal will orient you to the
         policies and procedures for that building. You will also be required
         to complete additional paperwork for our payroll department.

Please direct any questions or comments to: Ms. Lisa Tortora, Personnel
Assistant at lisa.tortora@irvingtonschools.org. No phone calls please.


Substitute Teachers Salary Schedule:

All grades (1-40 days)        $140/per diem
All grades (41 days & over)   $150/per diem
