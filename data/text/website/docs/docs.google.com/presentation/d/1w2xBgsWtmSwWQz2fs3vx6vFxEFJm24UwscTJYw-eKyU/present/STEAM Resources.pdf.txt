Students cannot make accounts and shouldn't try signing in with their Google Accounts.

You can make an account using your school email. There is a way to share some lessons with
students:
How do students use Classroom? Do I have to create accounts for students?
Educators do not need to (and cannot) create student accounts for Ozobot Classroom. Classroom is
compliant with FERPA and COPPA student and child privacy laws and does not collect student data.
Instead of creating accounts, it’s easy to share Ozobot lessons with students. All Ozobot video lessons
have a ‘Share with Students’ option. Find it on the right side of the Lesson Details page.

When you make an account, you'll get access to library of resources and lessons!
https://classroom.ozobot.com/lessons?query=&sort=Newest&page=1
