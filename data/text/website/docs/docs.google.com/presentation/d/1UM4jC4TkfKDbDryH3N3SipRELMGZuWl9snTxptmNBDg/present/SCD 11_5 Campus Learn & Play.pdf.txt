     Interactive Fairy Tales with LEDs Kit
                                                          The Simple LEDs Kit and the
Example: Students in 3rd Grade Library (enrichment)
are either retelling and summarizing or writing their      MakeyMakey were used to
own fairy tale!
                                                               create this project
They are breaking the story down into four
components: First, Next, Then, Last

For each of those sections, they are writing about
two sentences, then recording each section using the
MakeyMakey Audio Sampler app.

They are drawing a scene from the story and adding
conductive components using copper tape,
conductive ink, or paper clips.

Then students will connect the MakeyMakey and do
a gallery walk to experience all of the interactive
fairy tales!
