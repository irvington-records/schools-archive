Elective: Sustainable School
The 9th grade Foundations of Research classes recently
designed a Sustainable School!

In a ﬁrst phase, students built their ideal school with a focus
on sustainability and guided by the UN Sustainable
Development Goals. Students conducted research, used
NoodleTools, and created a brochure that laid out details and
images with a Works Cited.

For the design portion, which was new this year, students built
their school using Floorplanner.com.

 Elementary Library: Interactive Fairy Tales
Students in Library Enrichment are either retelling and
summarizing or writing their own fairy tale!

They are breaking the story down into four components:
First, Next, Then, Last

For each of those sections, they are writing about two
sentences, then recording each section using the
MakeyMakey Audio Sampler app.

They are drawing a scene from the story and adding
conductive components using copper tape, conductive
ink, or paper clips.

Then students will connect the MakeyMakey and do a
gallery walk to experience all of the interactive fairy tales!   BITE-SIZE CHANGE:

 Weather and Climate                                 curriculum unit, add a creative,

 Green Screen Videos
3rd graders created their own green screen
videos using WeVideo where they became
meteorologist travel agents! Students
researched information about a particular
location, and then created their video where
they shared news about patterns in
average monthly temperature and
precipitation data, the best activity and
month of year to visit for that activity, and
things someone would need to prepare for
the temperature, precipitation, and the
activity. This project took the last three
lessons of this Science21 unit, and created
a fun and engaging way for students to
share their research and ﬁndings!

  About Stephanie                                        for more!



Stephanie Peborde Burke has been an educator for over 16 years with experience teaching middle school and high school. She
previously taught 6th Grade Science at Fox Lane Middle School, and Science Research at Fox Lane and Yorktown High Schools.
Last year she was the K-12 DICE (Design, Innovation, Creation, and Expression) Facilitator at the Blind Brook-Rye Union Free
School District where she ran the high school and middle school’s FabLab and supported K-12 teachers with experiential,
hands-on learning in all content areas. This year, she brings her experience back to Bedford/Fox Lane as part of the leadership
team, supporting K-12 teachers with STEAM and EdTech through coaching, co-teaching, and modeling active learning,
interdisciplinary experiences, enrichment, and the NYS Computer Science and Digital Fluency Standards.

Dr. Burke has been involved with the Westchester and Science and Engineering Fair (WESEF) since its inception in 2001 and
currently serves on its executive board as treasurer. She has also served on the leadership committee for the
Westchester-Rockland Junior Science and Humanities Symposium (WR-JSHS) since 2014.

In May '23, she will be graduating with her Ed.D. in Educational Technology Leadership from New Jersey City University. This
past March she defended her dissertation, The Impact of a High School Maker Education Program on Student Interest in STEM
Careers: A Mixed Methods Study. She has also presented about Maker Education at SITE, NYSCATE 2018, and NYSCATE 2022.

Dr. Burke is a nature lover, birder, Geocacher, foodie, geek, and enjoys spending time with her husband and two young children.
As a maker herself, she enjoys being creative through arts and crafts and a variety of media, including 3D designing and
printing.

    Moving from Static to
     Dynamic Learning
⊹    Static learning is learning that lacks movement, action, or change.

⊹    Dynamic blended learning happens through constant change and
     activity, growing and evolving over time
       ×   It can take place anywhere, anytime, and is personalized
           and differentiated
       ×   Learner-centered, giving students choice, embedding
           communication, collaboration, critical thinking, and
           creativity skills where they align with learning goals
       ×   Digital tools are used for more than substitution, to do and
           create things previously unconceived
