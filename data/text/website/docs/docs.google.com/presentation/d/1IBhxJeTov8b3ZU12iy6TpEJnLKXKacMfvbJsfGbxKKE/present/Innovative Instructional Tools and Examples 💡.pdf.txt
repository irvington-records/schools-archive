Social Studies Lesson: Geography
Objective: Explore how geography inﬂuences human settlements.

Sandards: Maps can be used to represent varied climate zones, landforms,
bodies of water, and resources of the Eastern Hemisphere.

Materials: Computer with Internet access or atlas, cardboard/construction
paper, clay, model trees/ﬁgures, markers

Activities: Students choose an ancient civilization and create a 3D map of the
area around a major city or important site. Students are tasked to accurately
represent features of the geography, including scale. Students will be able to
explain advantages and disadvantages of the terrain and its importance in the
civilization’s culture or development in a brief presentation.

Physical Education Lesson: DIY Games
Objective: Develop unique games and their rules

Standards: Students will demonstrate proﬁciency in selected complex
physical activities (games, sports, exercises) that provide conditioning for
each ﬁtness area

Materials: Miscellaneous sports equipment, or self-created game pieces

Activities: Based on the materials a group of students receive, they must
creatively develop a new game. Students could also create their own
game pieces/items. Students will draft the rules, explain how it would
work (maybe even illustrate it or demonstrate it using video). Each group
would then take turns running their game with the class.
Adapted from: https://thepegeek.com/2014/09/mystery-higher-order-thinking-creativity-pe/   9

 ELA Lesson: Enhanced Writers’ Workshop
Objective: Create unique content related to original student writing.

Standards: Grade 4 students: 6. With some guidance and support from adults, use
technology, including the Internet, to produce and publish writing as well as to interact
and collaborate with others; demonstrate suﬃcient command of keyboarding skills to
type a minimum of one page in a single sitting.

Materials: Arts and crafts materials, Makey Makey, Arduino, Hummingbird, Minecraft,
Storyjumper, Mixbook, Big Universe, Songsmith, UJAM, Soundcloud, Readers Theatre

Activities: Students will develop their characters (and further develop through drawing)
and plot (and further develop through 3D storyboard creation and addition of electronic
upgrades). Students will create eBooks based on their stories and a theme song and
ﬁnally record a podcast of their story script.

Adapted from: https://usergeneratededucation.wordpress.com/2013/10/28/maker-education-meets-the-writers-workshop/   11
