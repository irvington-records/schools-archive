                                      CARING FOR YOUR CHROMEBOOK
Wash your hands! Clean hands make it easier to use the Chromebook and touchpad; sweat and small particles cause damage to it
Use a clean, dry, cotton, lint-free microﬁber, chamois, or some other very soft cloth to clean the dust oﬀ the Chromebook
Use a damp, clean, cotton, lint-free microﬁber, chamois, or some other very soft cloth if a dry cloth does not clean the Chromebook well
Wipe the cloth against the Chromebook in a circular motion - be sure not to place too much pressure on the screen
Make sure that there are no small items such as a pencil or earphones on the keyboard when closing and shutting down the Chromebook
Use a can of air to blow dry any dust or foreign particles from under the keyboard
