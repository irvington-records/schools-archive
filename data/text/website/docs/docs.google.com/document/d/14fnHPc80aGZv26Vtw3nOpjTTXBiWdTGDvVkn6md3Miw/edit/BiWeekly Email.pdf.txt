From the School Counseling Department (Updated on 10/21/26)
                                      The Happenings this week…

We are truly in the thick of the fall season! The air is crisp, the autumn leaves are turning, and the jackets
are out!

Now that we are well beyond the initial rush of schedule changes, our students have settled in and are
really digging deep into their coursework as the end of Quarter 1 approaches. There is a wonderful energy
throughout the building, and our department has been buzzing with activity across every grade level:

           9th Grade Check-Ins: Ms. Pulice has been meeting individually with each of her 9th graders for
           their one-on-one check-ins, helping our newest high schoolers stay supported, grounded, and
           confident as they navigate their transition.

           10th Grade Career Day Preparation: Our sophomores are getting ready for the upcoming Career
           Day Fair! To help them prepare, Mr. Lund and Mr. DeBatte have been pushing into English
           classes to guide students in exploring their interests and selecting the career paths that excite
           them most for the trip.

           11th & 12th Grade "Inter-YOU" Workshops: We are thrilled to partner with Scott Wynne,
           Assistant Superintendent of Curriculum and Instruction, to launch our inaugural "Inter-YOU"
           interview preparation sessions! These interactive workshops will give upperclassmen actionable
           tools to nail college, job, and internship interviews. Keep an eye on your inbox for registration
           details coming soon!

           College Representative Visits: Our college visit program is in full swing! With over 110 college
           admissions representatives visiting our office this fall, our counseling suite has been lively with
           juniors and seniors connecting directly with higher ed professionals.

Whether your student is adjusting to 9th grade or finalizing their post-secondary plans, please know that
our door is always open. Thank you for your continued partnership, and we wish your family a wonderful
season ahead!

UPCOMING…

                         Please Mark your Calendars for our “Hours of Insights”…
The Counseling Department is excited to announce a series of invaluable evening/day programs designed
to support our students and their families. These sessions will cover a range of crucial topics, including
navigating the high school transition, navigating high school as it is happening, understanding the
college application and review process, and providing insight into what happens behind the scenes in
admissions committees. We encourage all parents and students to join us for these informative events,
which are specifically tailored to equip you with the knowledge and tools needed for academic success
and future planning.

IN PERSON-Partnering for Success: Your Freshman's First Quarter–Nov. 12/Time: 10-10:40 am Target
audience: 9th graders/families. This program covers topics like graduation requirements, course
selection for the next year, and how to best support their ninth grader during this foundational year.

Video Release–Course Selection Overview–Release date Dec. 14 Target audience: 8-11th grade families:
Watch the Counselors present the Course offerings for the 2027-2028 school year.

VIRTUAL-Minds Unpacked: Advice for our Neurodiverse College Bound Students-January 13/Time:
10-10:40 am Target audience: parents and guardians of our neurodiverse students. Grab a cup of coffee
and join college experts for a candid conversation on guiding your neurodiverse student through the
college transition. Together, we’ll tackle crucial questions from whether to disclose a diagnosis to
navigating the shift from K-12 IEPs/504s to higher education accommodations.

VIRTUAL–Admissions Committee Deep Dive–January 14/Time: 6:00-7:00 pm Target audience: 9-11th
graders/families. Ever wonder what it is like to sit on an Admission Committee? Join us and our expert
panelists as they walk us through a Mock admission Panel. Together we will unpack the applications and
Admit, Defer and Deny 3 applicants.

IN PERSON–Laying the Underclass Groundwork–March 11/Time: 10-10:40 am Target audience: 10th
graders/families. Get ready 10th graders—you are about to be an Upperclass student. What does that
mean? This evening will provide parents with a clear roadmap for the remaining 2 years of high school,
covering topics like course selection, standardized testing, and college and career readiness planning.

VIRTUAL–Your High School Head Start–June 10/Time: 6:00-7:00 pm Target audience: 8 to 9th
graders/families. Join Ms. Pulice for a night of High School introductions with some words of wisdom
for our expert panelists!
