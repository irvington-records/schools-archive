DOWS LANE CAFETERIA EXPECTATIONS

Act           Student will listen to the adults in the cafeteria.
Thoughtfully  Students will enter and exit the cafeteria calmly.
              Students will keep hands and feet to themselves.
              Students will walk in the cafeteria at all times to keep safe.
Self-Reliance Students will come to lunch prepared (lunch boxes, coats, etc.).
              Students will respectfully use the bathrooms.
              Students will remember their belongings when exiting.
Show           Students will be respectful and kind to the cafeteria staff, aides,
Kindness &     and monitors.
Respect        Students will use appropriate voice levels in the cafeteria.
               Students will respond promptly to safety signals (lights off).
Cooperate      Students will follow directions of all adults.
               Students will self-advocate and ask for help when needed.
               Students will follow our Allergy Aware Because We Care practice.
