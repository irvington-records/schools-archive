Dows Lane Elementary School
              Elementary Plain Language Version - Code of Conduct
                                 Irvington UFSD




This is the plain language version of the Irvington UFSD Code of Conduct; Board Policy 7310. This
document reflects the Irvington UFSD Code of Conduct and is available on the District website
and in the Dows Lane Main Office. We provide a plain language version of the Irvington UFSD Code
of Conduct to support conversations with our younger students.


STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES:
ACT THOUGHTFULLY
 ☺​   I will be kind and thoughtful of others.
 ☺​   I will act in ways that do not interfere with the teaching and learning of others.
 ☺​   I will not bring anything to school that can intimidate, threaten, or hurt others.

PRACTICE SELF-RELIANCE
 ☺​   I will be truthful.
 ☺​   I will tell an adult if there is a problem.
 ☺​   I will come to school every day prepared to do my best, leaving any physical items at home
      that might distract me or others from learning.

SHOW KINDNESS & RESPECT
 ☺​   I will treat others the way I would like them to treat me.
 ☺​   My words and actions will be kind and respectful.
 ☺​   I will not make hurtful comments about someone’s race, color, weight, national origin, ethnic
      group, religion, ability, sexual orientation, gender or sex; or treat others differently
      because of any of these characteristics.
 ☺​   I will refrain from fighting, name calling, or other behaviors that could be called bullying.
      Bullying is behavior that is repeated, causes harm to another person in a physical, emotional
      or verbal way, and/or there is a power imbalance. This includes cyber-bullying.

COOPERATE
 ☺​   I will respect personal space, property, and rights of others, and property of the school.
 ☺​   I will follow the directions of adults at school.
 ☺​   I will not use a cell phone or any internet enabled watch.
 ☺​   I will not use computers, emails, cell phone, texting, etc. to cause emotional distress
      towards other students or staff members at school or at home.
 ☺​   If I need medication at school, my family will bring it to the school nurse.

We expect students to demonstrate these responsibilities throughout the school including in
classrooms, hallways, cafeteria, playground and bus, as well as on field trips and at all school
related events.

GENERAL CONSEQUENCES FOR NOT MEETING STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES
When students fail to follow the Code of Conduct there may be consequences, including one or
more of the following:
   ▪​ Conversation and/or coaching with an adult
   ▪​ Verbal warning
   ▪​ Family contact
   ▪​ Referral to a building administrator
   ▪​ Family conference
   ▪​ Administrative alternate learning location
   ▪​ Loss of privilege
   ▪​ In-school suspension
   ▪​ Out-of-school suspension
   ▪​ Superintendent’s hearing
   ▪​ Immediate removal from setting if student behavior threatens the health and/or safety of
      self or others

STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES ON THE BUS
    ☺​   I understand that the school bus is like being at school and I am responsible for my
         behavior in the same ways that I am at school.
    ☺​   I understand that riding the bus is a privilege; by not fulfilling my responsibilities I can lose
         this privilege.
    ☺​   I will remain seated and use a quiet voice on the bus.
    ☺​   I will keep my hands, feet, and all belongings to myself.
    ☺​   I will listen to the adults on the bus.


STUDENT ATTENDANCE RESPONSIBILITIES
    ☺​   I understand that daily school attendance is important for success in school.
    ☺​   If I struggle to get to school, I will ask my teacher or another adult at school for help.

It is our hope that by acting responsibly and with kindness, students learn to problem solve and
grow in their decision making skills, as they develop as wonderful humans.
