Dear New Student & Family,

Welcome to the Irvington High School community! We are absolutely thrilled to have you join us and
look forward to getting to know you. We completely understand that transitioning to a new school can
feel a bit overwhelming, so this packet is designed to help make your arrival as smooth and seamless as
possible. Inside, you will find our bell schedule, a directory of important school staff, and other key
resources to help you get acclimated.

If you have any questions that aren't answered here, please never hesitate to reach out to our counseling
department. We are all here to support you every step of the way!

A Quick Note on Registration & Health Forms: If you are receiving this packet, it means you have
successfully completed the initial registration process at the District Office. As a final required step,
please bring all of your student medical and health forms directly to the school nurse. This must be
completed before we can officially set up your schedule and academic meetings.

Next Steps: Scheduling Your Counselor Meeting

Once your health forms have been submitted to the nurse, it is time to schedule your intake
appointment. You can do this by contacting our School Counseling Department Office Assistant at (914)
269-5432.

During this initial meeting, you and your counselor will collaborate to:

   ●​ Review your academic history and transcript.
   ●​ Register you for the appropriate classes and build your schedule.
   ●​ Discuss New York State graduation requirements.
   ●​ Answer any individual questions you or your family may have about life at IHS.




                                                                                                            2
Preparing for Your First Meeting

Your appointment will typically last about 30 minutes. To help you find your way around, your
counselor will also connect you with a current IHS student who will give you a personalized tour of the
school. Depending on your course history, you may also be asked to complete a few brief placement
exams around this time to ensure you are placed in the levels best suited for your success.

Please bring the following information with you to your scheduled appointment:

   ❏​ An official copy of your transcript (if you have attended another high school)
   ❏​ All report cards from the current school year.
   ❏​ State/Standardized Test Results (if available)
   ❏​ A school profile or additional information that may help us properly schedule your classes
   ❏​ IEP/504 documentation (if applicable)

Again, welcome to the Irvington High School Community! We are here to support you through your
transition and your remaining years in high school. Don’t hesitate to reach out-all questions are good
ones!

Warmly,

The IHS School Counseling Team

GOOGLE G-SUITE:

Irvington High School assigns every student a google account where they can access their google drive. Classroom
teachers use the google drive to share work with students. Counselors use google drive to schedule appointments
with students. This account is typically firstname.lastname@irvingtonschools.org

GOOGLE CLASSROOM:

All teachers will be using Google Classroom exclusively for posting information and accepting assignments for their
classes. At the beginning of the year, a registration code is provided to link students to their class. Students who
arrive later in the year should ask their teachers for the registration code so they can link their Irvington account with
Google Classroom. Families can sign up for weekly bulletins of information. At this time, they are not granted
access to the google classroom page.

NAVIANCE (https://student.naviance.com/irvington) & XELLO :

Naviance and SCOIR are both programs designed to increase communication, organization, and accountability
within the IHS community. Students can research colleges and careers, indicate college interest, register for events,
and eventually will create a final list of colleges through this program. IHS can use this program to track student
progress through the application process, send school documents online (including your recommendations), and
obtain admissions statistics specific to our school. If you don’t have access to either platform, please reach out to the
Counseling Office.


LOGGING IN AND CONNECTING:

Logging into the District Network:
Username: firstname.lastname (i.e. John.Doe)
Initial Password: Passwords will be set to irvington (all lower case)
If it is your first time logging into your school account you will be prompted to change your password. Your new
password needs to be at least 8 characters long and include 1 capital letter and 1 number. Your network password is
linked to your district Google account. When your network password is changed it will automatically sync your new
password with your Google account. To change your password at any point in the future, after you log-in, hit
Ctrl+Alt+Del and select ‘Change a Password.’

                                         IHS CLUBS & ORGANIZATIONS

      Irvington High School has moved to a Block Schedule and a Unit Lunch. With this, Clubs will have the option of
      meeting for part of the lunch period. Students can participate in the clubs of their choosing. If you are interested
      in starting a new club, you must secure an advisor and bring a proposal to the principal who will review it for
      approval.

                                   COMMUNITY SERVICE OPPORTUNITIES

      Many Irvington High School students complete community service hours while in high school. Students
      keep track of their service over the 4 years through Helper Helper for credits in senior year. 60 hours of
      service equals .5 credits and 120 hours equals 1 credit.

                                 IRVINGTON HIGH SCHOOL DIPLOMA TYPES*
       REGENTS DIPLOMA:

●​      Achievement of basic credit requirements (please refer to Graduation Requirement chart) - plus –
●​      Attainment of a minimum score of 65 on Regents examinations in English Language Arts, U.S. History, Global
History, Mathematics and Science or use of Multiple Pathways 4+1 Program.

REGENTS DIPLOMA WITH HONORS:

●​      Achievement of basic requirements (please refer to Graduation Requirement chart ) - plus –
●​      Attainment of 5 Regents exams with a computed average score of 90 or better as follows: 1 Math, 1 Science, ELA,
Global History, US History & Government.

**REGENTS DIPLOMA WITH ADVANCED DESIGNATION:

●​     Achievement of requirements for Regents Diploma (please refer to Graduation Requirement chart) - plus –
●​     Scores 65 or better on each of 8 Regents Examinations and LOTE Local Exam

**REGENTS DIPLOMA WITH ADVANCED DESIGNATION with annotation that denotes Mastery in Math:

●​       Achievement of requirements for Regents with Advanced Designation (please refer to Graduation Requirement
chart) - plus –
●​       Scores 85 or better on each of 3 Regents Examinations in Mathematics

**REGENTS DIPLOMA WITH ADVANCED DESIGNATION with annotation that denotes Mastery in Science:

●​       Achievement of requirements for Regents with Advanced Designation (please refer to Graduation Requirement
chart) - plus –
●​       Scores 85 or better on each of 3 Regents Examinations in Science

**REGENTS DIPLOMA WITH ADVANCED DESIGNATION with Honors:

●​       Achievement of requirements for Regents with Advanced Designation (please refer to Graduation Requirement
chart) - plus –
●​       Attainment of 8 Regents exams with a computed average score of 90 or better as follows:3 Math, 2 Science, ELA,
Global History, US History & Government, and either a 65+ in Language Exam OR 65+ in CTE program assessment .

LOCAL DIPLOMA (Students with Individualized Education Plan or section 504 Accommodation Plan only):

●​      Achievement of basic credit requirements (please refer to Graduation Requirement chart) - plus –
●​      Scores 55 or better on the 5 required Regents (or 55+ on English and Math with an approved Superintendent
Appeal)
