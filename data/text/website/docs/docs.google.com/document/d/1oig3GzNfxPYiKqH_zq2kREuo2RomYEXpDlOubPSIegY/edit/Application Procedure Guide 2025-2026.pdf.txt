NAVIANCE
Naviance will be your “go-to” tools for the college application process. At this time, all materials that your
School Counselor will send colleges on your behalf must be requested through Naviance. This means that
your Naviance College Application list should remain up-to-date at all times, and transcript and
recommendation requests must be requested through Naviance in order to be processed.
The Naviance website is: http://student.naviance.com/irvington
If you are new to Irvington and do not have a Naviance account, come to the School Counseling Office. If you
do not remember your password, follow the “forgot my password” link or come to the School Counseling
Office for assistance. As you decide to apply to colleges, you MUST update your Naviance account for each
school.

COLLEGE LIST AND MATCHING COMMON APP & NAVIANCE
Creating a good list takes thought and time. It should consist of a range of schools, ones that are likely to
accept you, others that are a target for you, and some reach schools. Don’t forget to include a range of
schools for your financial needs as well. Your prospective college list should be discussed with your counselor
before any school supplements are sent. All schools must be in your Naviance College List and you must
match your Naviance and Common Application accounts (if applicable) before transcript requests are
accepted. Matching can only happen in Naviance after the FERPA agreement is signed in the Common
Application. For more information on matching, please visit the following website:

http://www.screencast.com/t/gxEQkElyo1

REQUESTING DOCUMENTATION

Certain documentation must be sent directly from Irvington High School to the individual college (Transcript,
recommendations, etc.). We use Naviance for all transcript and recommendation requests.

TRANSCRIPTS:
When you add a school to your Naviance list, it will ask you if you’d like to request a transcript be sent on your
behalf. YOU MUST DO THIS IN ORDER FOR US TO SEND A TRANSCRIPT FOR YOU. If you want to add a
request later, you may also do so through the “Manage Transcripts” tab in Naviance. This request must be
made by the Transcript Request Deadline (listed below) in order for your counselor to guarantee that it is
sent by the deadline. Please note: Transcripts will not be sent over weekends or school breaks so please
plan accordingly. For more information on how to request transcripts, please visit the following website:
https://www.screencast.com/t/LMtSIif3e1

RECOMMENDATIONS:
Recommendation requests for teachers must also go through your Naviance account. When requesting that a
teacher write your recommendation, you may indicate that you would like it sent to all colleges, or you can
specify a specific college. As is the same with transcripts, please follow the posted request deadlines and
note that recommendations will not be sent over weekends or school breaks. Please plan accordingly. For
more information regarding recommendations, please see the following document:
https://tinyurl.com/ihsrecguide

For more information and directions: https://tinyurl.com/navrecommendationinstructions

NCAA ELIGIBILITY CENTER
If you are going to play Division I or II sports at the college level, you must sign up with the NCAA Eligibility
Center. To do so, you will have to access NCAAclearinghouse.org and follow the prompts. Once completed,
please print out the counselor transcript page and give it to your counselor. For more information on athletics
in college, please visit the following website: https://www.irvingtonschools.org/Page/4288



TEST SCORES
Don't Forget that YOU will need to send your SAT or ACT scores to all of your colleges if applicable! Most
schools that require standardized test scores need them to be sent officially. That means logging into either
Collegeboard.org or Actstudent.org and paying to have your scores sent to colleges.


Things to Note...

          ●​ If your school is test-optional, and you do not want them to see your scores, make sure that you
             don't send them. Also, make sure that it's not reported in your application.
          ●​ Some schools offer the opportunity to self report your scores. This is a nice option that only a
             select few schools will accept. You must reach out to a school directly to see if self-reported
             scores are an option. If so, double check to make sure you report them correctly. Colleges will
             request official scores be sent as soon as you enroll.



PAYING FOR COLLEGE
Applying for financial assistance is individual to each college. All schools will require that families complete
the FAFSA. Schools in New York State will also ask for families to complete the TAP. Some schools will ask for
additional information through the CSS Profile, or their own individual application. If parents live apart, a
school may also ask for a Non-Custodial form. The FAFSA, TAP, and the CSS Profile are available to complete
starting in January of your senior year. Our recommendation is for you to check the individual school
websites for instructions and deadlines. For more information on the individual financial aid forms, please
visit these sites:

FAFSA: https://studentaid.ed.gov/sa/fafsa
TAP: https://www.hesc.ny.gov/
CSS Profile: https://cssprofile.collegeboard.org/

                                YOUR COLLEGE PROCESS: REMEMBER TO…


Be true to yourself throughout the process.

Take your time with each application. Do not hit submit your applications until all revisions are made. Once
your application is submitted you CANNOT go back and make any changes.

Research the school before applying. Don’t indicate interest in a major or program if the school doesn't offer
it.

Proofread your essay – Don’t just use spell check. Do not name a school in your common application personal
statement. If you name your school in the supplemental essays make sure that you match the correct essay to
the correct application. For example, sending an essay to Columbia that says, “and that’s why NYU is the
perfect fit for me” is NOT GOOD.

Give as much thought to short answer questions as you would to your personal statement.

If there is anything unusual about your transcript (large drop in grades, etc.) be sure to mention it in some
part of your application.

Sign your application. It will not be processed without a signature.

Send your test scores to each school you are applying to (if they require them).

Send the application fee or fee waiver if you qualify for one.

Make copies of all documents for your files.

Follow up with each school to make sure they have received all parts of your application. Do not assume they
will notify you if something is missing.

Stay calm if you get notification from a school about materials missing. Check on Naviance as to when it was
sent, as well as see your counselor.
