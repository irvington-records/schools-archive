DISTRICT EQUIPMENT WAIVER FORM​

I hereby acknowledge that I was offered a District-owned helmet/shoulder pads for my use during the
upcoming season. However, I have declined this offer and instead, have elected to purchase a
helmet/shoulder pads at my sole expense or already have one.

 ●​ I understand that in order for the District to permit me to utilize my privately purchased
     helmet/shoulder pads, the helmet/shoulder pads must bear the “meets NOCSAE standards” seal and
     bear the “NOCSAE” logo on the helmet/shoulder pads for the current season.

 ●​ The District will send the District-owned      helmet/shoulder pads to a NOCSAE licensed agency
     every year for reconditioning and testing. I understand that in order for the District to permit me to
     utilize my privately purchased helmet/shoulder pads, I must send my helmet/shoulder pads every
     year, for the required reconditioning and recertification by a NOCSAE licensed agency. I must
     provide the District with proof of the recertification by a NOCSAE licensed agency, which must
     include a receipt from the agency that conducted the reconditioning and recertification of the
     helmet/shoulder pads, every year.

 ●​ I understand that in order for the District to permit me to utilize my privately purchased
     helmet/shoulder pads, my helmet/shoulder pads must be properly fitted to me.

 ●​ I acknowledge that the District has the right to inspect my helmet/shoulder pads at any time prior to
     my use, for items including, but not limited to, damage to the helmet/shoulder pads shell or liner,
     holes, loose hardware and/or loose face masks.

 ●​ I acknowledge that the District has the right to prohibit the use of my privately purchased
     helmet/shoulder pads in the event the District determines that my helmet/shoulder pads does not
     meet the foregoing criteria or is otherwise deemed by the District to be unsafe and/or improper for
     my use. In such an event, I understand that the District shall provide me with a District-owned
     helmet/shoulder pads at no cost or I may purchase my own, which I acknowledge must meet the
     above-referenced requirement/standards.

 ●​ I understand and acknowledge that no helmet/shoulder pads can prevent all head or neck injuries a
     player might receive while participating in any practice, contest or game.

 ●​ I understand and acknowledge that improper or illegal use of a helmet/shoulder pads can result in
    severe head or neck injuries, concussion, paralysis, or death to me and/or my opponent. I further
    acknowledge and agree that I will not engage in such conduct at any time. Understanding that the
    student will be subject to disciplinary action in accordance with the Code of Conduct for improper
    or illegal use of a helmet/shoulder pads while in his/her possession.

 ●​ I understand and agree that I remain solely responsible for my helmet/shoulder pads, including but
    not limited to familiarity and compliance with applicable helmet/shoulder pads warranty(ies),
    proper fitting of my helmet/shoulder pads, remediation of any damage and/or necessary repairs,
    proper care/maintenance of my helmet/shoulder pads as required by the District if my
    helmet/shoulder pads is to be used for a school activity and loss of helmet/shoulder pads.
