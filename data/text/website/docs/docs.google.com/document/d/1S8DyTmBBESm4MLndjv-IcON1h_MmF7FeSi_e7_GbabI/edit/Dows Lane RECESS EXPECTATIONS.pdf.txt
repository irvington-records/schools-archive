DOWS LANE RECESS EXPECTATIONS
Act Thoughtfully   Student will enter and exit the playground calmly
                   Student will use hands and feet for play only
                   Student will use playground equipment appropriately
                   Student will follow adult directions about snow, ice and rain.
Self-Reliance      Student will know and follow the rules
                   Student will promptly return all equipment after play
                   Students will take all personal belongings inside when
                   leaving the playground
Show Kindness &    Student will use words and language that is kind
Respect            Student will safely follow game rules
                   Student will play fairly and include everyone – practice
                   kindness.
Cooperate          Student will follow the directions and signals of all the adults
                   Student will help others keep safe and self-advocate when
                   help is needed
                   Student will stay where they can be seen by adults
Student will line up promptly and quietly upon hearing the
whistle
