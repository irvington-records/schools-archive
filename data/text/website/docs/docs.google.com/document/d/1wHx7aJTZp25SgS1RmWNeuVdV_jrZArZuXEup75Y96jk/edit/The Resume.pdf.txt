THE RESUME

                        How To Build A High School Resume

Your resume should be a basic profile of who you are based on your interests, activities and
accomplishments since starting high school.

The only information you must include is your name, address, email address and phone number. You
may also include your Social Security Number for your college applications, since that is what most
colleges use for an ID number. If you use your resume for other things (for example job applications),
you should remove your Social Security Number. You also want to include the name of your high
school. If you transferred from another high school to Irvington, you should include that information
after listing IHS.

On a resume, all information in each category is listed with most recent items first. Entries should be
chronological. The headings on your resume will differ depending on your activities, interests,
accomplishments and jobs.

Put categories first that most reflect your interests and accomplishments. For example, if you are an
avid student of history, and you received an award for a history paper or project, you would include
that toward the beginning of your resume.

You can take liberties with layout, design and typeface on the resume. You could, for example,
include your photo. Remember, however, that first and foremost, you want to keep your resume
simple and easy to read, so no strange illegible typefaces.

If possible, your resume should be one page long. So keep it simple.
