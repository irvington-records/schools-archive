Dows Lane – Setting the Tone
Morning meetings and circles are wonderful opportunities to set the tone for the day, review the daily
schedule, build class community, teach into the Habits of Mind and Character Building Framework, reflect on
learning, and build shared experiences.

A morning announcement occurs at 8:05 daily. It consists of a welcome for the day and the Pledge of
Allegiance. At the end of the day, we make announcements at 2:15 while students are packing up.

During the first two weeks of the launch of the school year, all teachers will introduce, review and reflect upon
the following expectations. ​

                                       School-Wide Expectations
Arrival              ●​ Enter quietly through the Dows Lane Main Entrance
                     ●​ Walk directly to classroom
                     ●​ Use a low voice to speak with friends while awaiting entry into the classroom
                     ●​ Follow directions of staff in the hallways
                     ●​ Be kind and respectful

Hallway              ●​ Use a low voice or silence
                     ●​ Keep quiet hands and walking feet
                     ●​ Be respectful of the learning going on inside other classrooms


Cafeteria            ●​ Walking feet, no running
                     ●​ Follow Cafeteria Expectations

Recess               ●​ Move to recess quietly
                     ●​ Be mindful of hallways and learning in classrooms as you transition
                     ●​ Follow all Recess Expectations


Dismissal            ●​ Listen to all directions
                     ●​ Follow all Bus Expectations
