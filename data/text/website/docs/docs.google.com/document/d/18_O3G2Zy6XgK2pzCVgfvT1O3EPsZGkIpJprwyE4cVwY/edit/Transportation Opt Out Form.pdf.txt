TRANSPORTATION OPT-OUT FORM
​
Where practices and competitions for athletic competitions are held off school district grounds, it is expected that
students will travel to and from such practices and competitions with their teams. However, parents may request
that they be permitted to transport their children or that their children be permitted to transport themselves to
and/or from such practices and competitions.


This form must be properly completed, signed, and returned to the Athletic Office or School Administrative Office before
any student is permitted to be transported to and/or from athletic practices and competitions as set forth above. Students will
not be permitted to “carpool” to and/or from athletic practices and competitions.

practice and/or competitions in the above-referenced manner and hereby release the school district from any and
all liability related to the same. I understand that under no circumstances may my child transport other students to
and/or from practices and competitions.* I further understand that this form only applies to the sport listed above

he/she must travel to games with his/her team. I understand that in situations where my child must travel to
