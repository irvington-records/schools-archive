Three Dark Crowns by
Kendare Blake

Told in multiple point of
views, Three Dark Crowns is a
compelling and suspenseful
novel about three sisters who
must fight to the death to
become queen. Readers who
enjoyed Hunger Games or

The Secret Life of Bees by Sue
Monk Kidd

Set in South Carolina in 1964,
The Secret Life of Bees tells the
story of Lily Owens, whose life
has been shaped around the
blurred memory of the afternoon
her mother was killed. This is a
remarkable novel about divine
female power, a story that
women will share and pass on
to their daughters for years to
come.

(e

Rain Reign by Ann M. Martin

Rose has lots of rules and routines
that she feels she must follow, which
makes it hard for other people—her
classmates, teachers, her dad-to
understand her. When her dog goes
missing in a terrible storm, she must
leave these routines behind to save
her. Filled with creative uses of
homonyms, this is a beautiful story.

Recommended by Ms. Nadler (6th
Grade ELA)

Girl with a Camera by Carolyn Meyer

This is a novel based on the life of
Margaret Bourke-White. It is very
detailed, and would be of interest to
those who have an interest in her or
her photography. Margaret's
progress and perseverance in
pursuing photojournalism at a time
when women were not in the field,
is a very good read for students.

Recommended by Dr. James (MS
Librarian)

The Notorious Benedict Arnold: A
True Story of Adventure, Heroism,
and Treachery by Steve Sheinkin

An award-winning book that
introduces young readers to the

accounts, astonishing American

and true adventure tale from
history.
