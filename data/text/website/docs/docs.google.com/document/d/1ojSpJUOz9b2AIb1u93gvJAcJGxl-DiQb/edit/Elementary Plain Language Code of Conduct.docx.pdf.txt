This is the plain language version of the Irvington UFSD Code of Conduct; Board Policy 7310.

This document reflects the Irvington UFSD Code of Conduct. This document and the complete Irvington UFSD
Code of Conduct are available on line at the District website and are available in the main office.

We provide a plain language version of the Irvington UFSD Code of Conduct to support conversations with our
younger students and their teachers and parents/guardians. Please take time to review this document with your
child.

At Main Street School, we have behavior expectations based on specific locations because we believe that being
explicit with our students regarding behavior fosters a safe and nurturing learning environment. Below, you will
find student responsibilities categorized based on our behavior expectations- act thoughtfully, practice
self-reliance, show kindness & respect and cooperate. Additionally, we have included consequences as outlined
in the Irvington UFSD Code of Conduct. For your reference, at the end of this document, we included the MSS
Behavior Matrices.

                                           STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES

ACT THOUGHTFULLY
 ☺​ I will act in ways that do not interfere with the teaching and learning of others.
 ☺​ I will not make up mean stories or spread rumors about students and adults.
 ☺​ I will not use words to threaten others.
 ☺​ I will not bring to school any object that can intimidate, threaten, hurt or harm others.

PRACTICE SELF-RELIANCE
  ☺​ I will be truthful.
  ☺​ I will dress appropriately in school.
  ☺​ I will tell an adult if there is a problem.
  ☺​ I will come to school every day prepared to do my best, leaving any physical items at home that might
       distract me or others from learning.

SHOW KINDNESS & RESPECT
  ☺​ I will treat others the way I would like them to treat me.
  ☺​ I will not make hurtful comments about someone’s race, color, weight, national origin, ethnic group,
      religion, ability, sexual orientation, gender or sex; or treat others differently because of anyone of these
      characteristics.
  ☺​ I will use kind and respectful words and not inappropriate words.
  ☺​ I will refrain from fighting, name calling, or any other behavior that could be called bullying. Bullying
      means any behavior which occurs repeatedly that causes harm to another person in a physical,
      emotional or verbal way. This includes cyber-bullying.

COOPERATE
 ☺​ I will follow the directions of adults who work in the school.
 ☺​ I will not use computers, emails, instant messages, cell phones, texting, etc. to cause emotional distress
      towards other students or school staff members at school or at home.
 ☺​ I will respect the personal space, rights and properties of others and the school.
 ☺​ If I need medication at school, my parents will bring it to the school nurse.

We expect students to demonstrate these responsibilities throughout the school including in classrooms,
hallways, cafeteria and on the playground and bus as well as on field trips and during all school related events.

Please note that smartphones and smartwatches must be turned off and stored in students' backpacks for the
entire school day. Students may use eReaders for reading purposes as long as the device is not connected to the
internet. Personal electronic devices may be used before entering the building in the morning and after dismissal
in the afternoon.

                   GENERAL CONSEQUENCES FOR NOT MEETING STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES
When students fail to follow the above responsibilities there may be consequences, including one or more of the
following:
     ▪​ Conversation with an adult about student responsibilities
     ▪​ Verbal warning
     ▪​ Complete Behavior Reflection Sheet
     ▪​ Parent contact
     ▪​ Referral to a building administrator
     ▪​ Learning session with a member of the SEL team
     ▪​ Community Reset
     ▪​ Parent conference
     ▪​ Administrative detention
     ▪​ Loss of privilege
     ▪​ In-school suspension
     ▪​ Out-of-school suspension
     ▪​ Superintendent’s hearing
     ▪​ Immediate removal from setting if student behavior threatens the health and/or safety of self or others

                      BUS CONSEQUENCES FOR NOT MEETING STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES
     ▪​ Conversation with an adult about student responsibilities
     ▪​ Verbal warning
     ▪​ Change of seat
     ▪​ Parent contact
     ▪​ Administrative detention and/or bus suspension
It is our hope that by acting responsibly and with kindness that students will be able to problem solve and learn
how to best handle challenging situations in positive ways. We encourage students to live by the golden rule and
always treat others how they themselves want to be treated.
