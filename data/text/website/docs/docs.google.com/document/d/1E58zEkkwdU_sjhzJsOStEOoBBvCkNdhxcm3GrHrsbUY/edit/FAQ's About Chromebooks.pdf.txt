●​ What is a Chromebook, and why did we choose it?
       ○​ A Chromebook is an Internet-based computer. It is fast, light and easy to
          use.
       ○​ The device compares favorably to other technologies on the market and is
          easy to support.
●​ IUFSD selected this device platform because:
       ○​ Chromebooks natively and seamlessly integrate with Google Apps for
          Education, our core platform at IUFSD for communication and
          collaboration
       ○​ Chromebooks come with an integrated keyboard and mouse, which makes
          creating products online easier for many students.
       ○​ It has a long battery life and will let students work through an entire
          school day without charging.
       ○​ IUFSD can manage the device and monitor student use.
       ○​ Chromebooks update the operating system themselves while retaining the
          ability to push out specific applications and tools to our students.
       ○​ We have the ability to lock anyone out of a stolen or lost Chromebook, thus
          making it unusable and serving as a deterrent for theft.
       ○​ Chromebooks offer a full-featured browser for working on the Internet.
       ○​ All work is saved in Google Drive. As a result, student work is accessible
          from any device.
●​ What about charging the Chromebook battery?
       ○​ Each Chromebook is issued to students with a charging cable and adapter.
          Students are required to charge their Chromebooks at home prior to
          bringing it to school, and to keep their charger at home. A fully-charged
          Chromebooks can last between 10 and 12 hours, depending on usage
          patterns.
●​ My Chromebook is not working right! What do I do?
       ○​ Hold down the power button on the keyboard on your Chromebook for 10
          seconds, or until the light goes off. Wait 2 seconds, then press the power
          button to restart your Chromebook. This takes very little time and solves
          many problems.
●​ How can I identify my Chromebook?
       ○​ The IUFSD Technology Department records and labels all 1:1
          Chromebooks by their serial number. Students can identify their
          Chromebook by its computer number, found on the back of the device.
          This tag usually begins with “ET”
●​ Is it OK to forget my Chromebook at home?
       ○​ No, students are required to bring their Chromebooks, fully charged, to
          school each day. All Chromebooks should remain inside of the Irvington
          protective case that was issued to students with their Chromebooks.
●​ Can students browse anywhere on the Web with the Chromebook?
       ○​ IUFSD uses the web proxy service Lightspeed to monitor and block
          inappropriate sites from an IUFSD 1:1 student, even at home.
          Additionally, IUFSD maintains a content filter and firewall for all
         Internet-enabled computers and equipment. Regardless of physical
         location (on campus or out), all IUFSD Chromebooks will have all Internet
         sites monitored.
●​ What about digital citizenship & cyber-safety?
     ○​ Yes. IUFSD is in compliance with the Children's Internet Protection Act
         (CIPA). We teach digital citizenship/cyber-safety topics to students
         throughout the year, centering around Digital Citizenship.
●​ Can I use my Chromebook without an Internet connection?
     ○​ In order to do so without internet access, you must have already logged
         into that Chromebook, AND have enabled offline access.
●​ What will students connect to for wireless Internet access?
     ○​ At school, students’ Chromebooks will automatically connect to the secure
         IUFSD wireless network. At home, students will need to connect to their
         own home wireless network.
●​ What if we have no wireless internet at home?
     ○​ Most Chromebooks can connect to a wired internet connection using this
         adapter. (to purchase on Amazon, click on the link)
●​ What if we have no internet at home?
     ○​ Please contact our Director of Technology, at (914)269-5540 to discuss
         opportunities for support.
●​ Do Chromebooks print to printers?
     ○​ No, not at IUFSD sites, where only IUFSD teacher computers print. At
         home, Chromebooks can print if Google Cloud Print is set up.
●​ Why is my Chromebook saying "Aw Snap!"?
     ○​ The Aw Snap! message appears if a web page crashes.
         Usually, reloading the page will fix the problem.




●​ My Chromebook has a black screen and is unresponsive, yet the blue
   power button light is on-
     ○​ Hold down the power button for 10 seconds, or until the power light on the
        button turns off. Wait 2 seconds, then press the power button again to
        turn it back on. Unless the battery is completely discharged, this usually
        solves the problem.
●​ How can I update the Chrome OS Operating System
     ○​ Go to chrome://help
      ○​ Look at the toolbar on the bottom right of your screen for this update icon.
         If you see it, click and restart from its menu.
●​ Will Google, Chromebooks or in Google Apps for Education sell or
   share our information?
      ○​ The information in Google Apps for Education is private and secure and is
         not used or sold in any way as per Google
●​ Can my students use their own Google account on the Chromebooks?
      ○​ Each student is assigned an Irvington Google account; that is the only
         account that will be used for logging into the chromebook.
●​ Will faculty or staff be able to access or control the webcam on the
   Chromebook?
      ○​ No. Faculty and staff will not have access to remote control of the
         Chromebooks in any way.
●​ How can I check for updates on my Chromebook?


       Check for updates yourself
1.​ Turn on your Chromebook.
2.​ Connect your Chromebook to Wi-Fi.
3.​ At the bottom right, select the time.

4.​ Select Settings     .

5.​ Select Menu         About Chrome OS.
6.​ Under "Google Chrome OS," you'll see which version of the Chrome operating system your
    Chromebook is using.
7.​ Select Check for Updates.
   8.​ If your Chromebook finds a software update, it will start to download automatically.


    ●​ Changing the Keyboard on your Chromebook
Switch between keyboard languages
If you have more than one keyboard language turned on, you’ll see the language you’re
using next to your account photo.
   1.​ At the bottom right, select the time.
   2.​ Select your keyboard language   .
   3.​ Choose the keyboard language you want to use.

Tip: You can also switch your language by pressing Ctrl + Shift + Space until you see the
language you want. To switch back, press Ctrl + Space.

Set your keyboard language
   1.​ At the bottom right, select the time.
   2.​ Select Settings          Advanced.
   3.​ Optional: To add another keyboard language, select Language                Add languages.
       Choose your language(s) and select Add.
   4.​ In the "Languages and input" section, select Input method              Manage input
       methods.
   5.​ Check the boxes next to the keyboard languages you'd like to use.
          1.​ Check the boxes next to the keyboard language you's like to enable.
          2.​ Uncheck the boxes next to the keyboard language you'd like to disable.
   6.​ Select Back    .
   7.​ Select the keyboard language you want to use. You’ll see "Enabled" listed under
       the current keyboard.
Optional: To see all your keyboard language options on your desktop, turn on Show
input options in the shelf.
