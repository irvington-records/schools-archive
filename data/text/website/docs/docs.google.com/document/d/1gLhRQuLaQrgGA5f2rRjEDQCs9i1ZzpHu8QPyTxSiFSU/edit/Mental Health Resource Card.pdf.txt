Mental Health Resource Card




While this by no means is a comprehensive list, it is a starting point of local and national resources that can be reached by
phone or online. In addition to these resources, REMEMBER, you have people who care about you at school who are
available to talk!

National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI) of Westchester:
    ❖​ HelpLine - (914) 592-5458 (Available Mon-Fri, 9am-3pm)
    ❖​ https://www.namiwestchester.org/

National Suicide Prevention Lifeline:
    ❖​ 988
    ❖​ suicidepreventionlifeline.org

Crisis Text Line:
    ❖​ Text HOME to 741741

Trevor Project for LGBTQIA Students:
    ❖​ Trevor Lifeline for LGBTQIA students struggling with suicidal thoughts
       1-866-488-7386 (Toll free, 24 hrs a day)
    ❖​ Confidential text line with a Trevor Project Counselor
       Text START to 678678

National Domestic Violence Hotline:
    ❖​ Hotline # 1-800-799-7233
    ❖​ Text TELL NOW to 85944

My Sister’s Place (Teen Dating Violence/Domestic Violence)
   ❖​ MSPNY.org
   ❖​ 1-800-298-7233 (SAFE)

    ❖​ Hotline # 1-800-931-2237
