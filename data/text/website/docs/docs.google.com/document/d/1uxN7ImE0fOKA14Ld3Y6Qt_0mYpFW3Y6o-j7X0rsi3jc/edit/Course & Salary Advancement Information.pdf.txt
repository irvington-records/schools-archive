                  Graduate, In-Service, and CTLE Credit Information
All requests for graduate, in-service, or CTLE credit must be submitted in Frontline no fewer than
10 working days in advance of the course commencement date.

Graduate Credit: (counts toward salary increase; employee pays for course)
  ●​ To receive salary credit, the course must be graduate level course work credited as graduate
        study by an accredited educational institution or post-secondary education and directly
        related to your current field of work in Irvington.
   ●​   Online courses which the sponsoring institution does not accept toward its own graduate
        degree programs shall be credited one (1) credit for every three (3) credits earned.
   ●​   Written evidence that the course is otherwise accepted by the sponsoring institution is
        required.
   ●​   Must be taken outside of school hours. If taken during school hours, you may not request
        graduate credit.
   ●​   Click “Graduate Credit” (Towards Salary Movement) in the approval form.


In-Service Credit: (counts toward salary increase; employee pays for course)
   ●​ Must be related to your current field of work in Irvington.
   ●​ Typically taken through providers listed in the District Catalog (i.e. various teacher centers)
      or other approved providers.
   ●​ Must be taken outside of school hours. If taken during school hours, you may not request
      in-service credit.
   ●​ No more than six (6) in-service credits may be applied to each fifteen (15)-point salary
      differential. No more than twelve (12) in-service credits may be applied to each thirty
      (30)-point salary differential.
   ●​ To qualify for one (1) in-service credit, you must complete fifteen (15) hours of instruction.
   ●​ Click “In-Service Credit” (Towards Salary Movement) in the approval form.


Workshops/CLTE Credit: (NYSED certification compliance only. No impact on salary.)
   ●​ Typically taken through providers listed in the District Catalog (i.e. BOCES), the IUFSD, or
      other approved providers.
   ●​ Those with more recent certification status must complete 100 CTLE hours every 5 years.
   ●​ The district does not keep track of these hours. You are responsible for monitoring and
      recording these hours.
   ●​ Click “Professional Development Hours” in the approval form.

                                 COURSE APPROVAL PROCESS

Course Approval: All Course Notification Forms in Frontline receive online administrative
approval from the Principal and the Assistant Superintendent for Curriculum & Instruction.

Course Notifications: Although approval is not always a requirement, the district does require
that online Course Notification Forms with attached course descriptions be submitted through
Frontline for all coursework you plan to use as credit toward salary movement. Transcripts
submitted without an approved online Course Notification Form will not be accepted toward
salary movement.

Transcripts: For graduate courses, only official transcripts will be accepted. You must collect
sealed official transcripts and submit them along with your request, or have your college forward
them directly to:

                                       Marika Chasse, Irvington UFSD
                                      6 Dows Lane, Irvington, NY 10533

Please do not send copies or unofficial transcripts to the District Office.

For in-services courses, copies of Certificates of Completion must be submitted (it does not need
to be the original certificate). If you have taken a course through Frontline catalogs (i.e. NYSUT),
then a copy of your certificate will be embedded by the catalog vendor into your online course
notification form. There is no need for you to get a certificate for those courses. We can print them
for you. Any courses outside of the Frontline catalogs will require you to send in a copy of the
Certificate of Completion.


                             REQUESTING SALARY MOVEMENT

Submission Process: When ready to be considered for a salary movement, you must submit the
following no later than November 1st:
    ●​ A completed, typed Request for Salary Movement Form listing each college, the
       completed course name and the number of graduate or in-service credits you received for
       each course. Only list the courses you want to use for your salary movement.
    ●​ Official transcripts, or indicate your transcripts were forwarded to the District Office.
    ●​ Certificates of Completion for all in-service courses.

For your convenience, an online Request for Salary Movement Form may be obtained here
Request for Salary Movement Form. This is a fillable form, so you will need to download it onto
your computer.
