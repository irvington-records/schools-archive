Individuality, Culture, and Belonging for all at Dows Lane Elementary School




 Dows Lane Mission: To create a nurturing learning environment where students thrive and develop into caring,
                                 compassionate individuals and active learners.

Whole School:
  ●​ Common language that creates a welcoming school environment for all
  ●​ Read alouds and book videos chosen for learning about individuality, respect for others
  ●​ Character Education/ SEL and monthly themes related to The Irvington Way such as
      Community, Actions, Choices, Thinking Flexibly, and Self Awareness
         ○​ Character education is woven into daily learning in all content areas
         ○​ Review, update, and reflect on our work, to be inclusive and accessible for all students
   ●​ School Counselor collaborating with every classroom with lessons
   ●​ Whole-school assemblies to build community
   ●​ Buddy-classes to increase connections and build a culture of relationships

Classroom:
   ●​ Circles and/or Morning Meetings in every classroom to build community and a culture of
       self awareness, respect for others, acceptance and honoring of differences
           ○​ Character Education/ SEL conversations with books, videos, and activities
           ○​ Read alouds and discussions to help children learn to engage in respectful
              conversations
   ●​ Read alouds that mirror our students’ cultures, National and world celebrations embedded
       into learning
           ○​ Classroom libraries that are representative of all our students and global citizenship

Small Group and Individual:
  ●​ School Counselor, Social Worker, School Psychologist, MTSS (Multi-Tiered System of
      Supports), Related Service Providers, and Administrators working with small groups or 1:1
      with students and families
  ●​ Communication and partnership with families to support students
         ○​ Positive behavior interventions and support, walk-and-talk coaching to best support
             students’ learning and growth
                ■​ Using mistakes as teachable moments

Collaborative Partnerships:
  ●​ Partnership with families to best help support students academically and social emotionally
  ●​ Collaboration with the PTSA, SCAFLD, IEF, and Boces Arts-in-Ed to enrich learning and
      support students and families
Professional Development:
   ●​ Time together as a staff to talk, discuss ideas, read articles, and plan work with students
   ●​ Time to reflect on our work, time for planning best instructional practices
   ●​ Cultivating and maintaining a growth mindset
   ●​ Continuous updates in our learning spaces and common spaces with flexible seating to best
      meet the learning needs of students
   ●​ Continual pursuit of better, always learning, changing, and growing
