Home Access Center

Our district utilizes a product called eSchoolPlus as its student
management system. Home Access Center is a portal for students
and parents that allows for access to important information such as
schedules, assignment grades, and progress reports.

In order to sign up for Home Access Center, you must use the email
address that the District has on file for you. If the District does not
have an email address on file for you (or if you need to update it to
a new address), you can contact your building’s Main Office to
correct it.

For all questions related to Home Access Center, please contact us
at homeaccess@irvingtonschools.org.
