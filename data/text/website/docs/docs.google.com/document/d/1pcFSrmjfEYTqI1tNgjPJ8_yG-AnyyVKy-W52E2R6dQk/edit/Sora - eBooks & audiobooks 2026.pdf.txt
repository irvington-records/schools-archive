Directions for getting started with Sora
Short how-to videos on using different features of Sora.

2 Things to Do to Do After Signing Up to Make the Most of Sora

   1)​ If you only want to see books that are available right now, not books you have to
       reserve/hold and wait to get:
   ●​ Go to the Explore page (binoculars icon) and click on Filters
   ●​ Click on the box for Available Titles Only

   2)​ Add your public library collection so you can search for and check out ebooks from
       both school and public libraries:
   ●​ From the Sora Home page, click on the 3 horizontal lines in the top right corner.
       Your profile should pop up.
   ●​ Click on the blue button for Add a Library
   ●​ Start typing in Irvington Public Library and click on the gray box that says
       Irvington Public Library / Westchester Library System. If asked, find our school
       and sign in with your school Google login
   ●​ Once you have set it up to search all your libraries,Sora should
       look in both our school (Southern Westchester BOCES) and the
       Westchester Public Libraries collection of books every time you
       use the search box.

Some Sora features:
* Search for books by title, author, subject, genre, and special lists.
* Adjustable font style and size.
* Highlight and annotate passages as you read.
* Download your highlights and notes to Google and share them.
* Many Sora books can be loaded onto a Kindle.

          Please speak to your school librarians if you have questions or problems.
