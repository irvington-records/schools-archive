LEAD CSE CHAIRPERSON
                                 JOB DESCRIPTION

Title: Lead CSE Chairperson
Reports to: Director of PPS
Qualifications: 5-10 years of CSE chairperson experience as a school psychologist,
special education teacher or clinician within inclusive models, 2-5 years of experience
in a formal teacher leadership role. Masters degree in School Psychology, Special
Education or related field. Doctorate degree preferred.
Summary: Full-time probationary appointment with 25 summer days. In partnership
with the Director and Assistant Director of Pupil Personnel Services, the Chairperson
provides leadership of equity and access to the CSE process, and collaborates with
the district-wide psychologist/evaluator to ensure that CSE processes are completed
within all deadlines.

Responsibilities

   ●​ Provides support to teachers on the implementation of CSE practices and
      processes, within established deadlines.
   ●​ Participates in special education department meetings and PPS planning
      meetings.
   ●​ Designs and delivers professional development for teachers relating to
      culturally responsive data inquiry and CSE decision making.
   ●​ In partnership with the Director and Assistant Director of PPS, and the
      district-wide psychologist, leads collaborative development of aligned,
      culturally responsive referral and evaluation practices.
   ●​ Supports teacher teams in the annual projection process and
      recommendations for Least Restrictive Environment in District in
      collaboration with PPS Teacher Leadership Team.
   ●​ Participates in Target Support Groups relating to CSE state compliance
   ●​ Supports the development of structures for the CSE process and practices and
      trains teachers to engage with a culturally responsive system.
   ●​ Acts as CSE Chairperson and ensures timely finalization of IEPs on assigned
      caseload
   ●​ Monitors CSE deadlines and ensures that CSE processes are running on
      schedule.
   ●​ Supports teachers during Drafting Days and with IEP writing and progress
      monitoring for purposes of enhancing inclusion.
   ●​ In collaboration with the Assistant Director supports the Extended School Year
      recommendations for the assigned CSE portfolio
   ●​ Chair and support summer CSE processes
   ●​ Monitors and reports on status of CSE process to PPS Leadership team.
   ●​ Act as backup evaluator based on certification area.
   ●​ Other duties as assigned by the Director of PPS

Other Qualifications

   ●​   Collaborative and analytical approach to resolving complex issues
   ●​   Authentic belief in the efficacy of distributed leadership and practices
   ●​   Driven by culturally responsive practices and beliefs
   ●​   Entrepreneurial spirit and iterative learner
   ●​   Commitment to inclusive adult education
   ●​   Prioritizes responsibilities and exhibits a high level of organization
   ●​   Demonstrates excellent decision making skills
   ●​   Demonstrates reflective practice and continuous improvement
   ●​   Demonstrates motivation and good work ethic
   ●​   Abides by school and District policies
   ●​   Exhibits and models excellent communication skills
   ●​   Exhibits excellent attendance record
   ●​   Demonstrates a high level of ethical behavior
   ●​   Must maintain NYS teacher or related certification
