Dear Incoming 9th Grade Family,

Welcome to Irvington High School! We are excited that you are joining our high school community, and
hope that you are as well. At the same time, students and their families can also feel uneasy about the
transition. It is important for you to know that the counseling department is here to support you, not
only through this transition, but also over the next four years.

We have multiple events geared towards both students and parents to help ease this transition.

FOR STUDENTS:

The Final Four: A look at HS Schedules
In this workshop, all 8th grade students will learn about the scheduling process for high school. We will
discuss what a typical schedule looks like, and some of the decisions that have to be made now for next
year. Students will also learn about the elective offerings for next year as well as an overview of the
honors process and support service programs.

Student Panel: Tips from the Trenches
At this in-school event, students will have the opportunity to hear from High School students and
teachers about what to expect in high school. The transition will be discussed along with tips on how to
move smoothly into high school. There will be time for 8th graders to ask questions.

Student Orientation
At our annual 9th grade orientation, students will come together for activities and discussion. They will
also participate in a dry-run of their first day of high school by walking their schedule and meeting their
teachers. By the time the first day comes, students will be ready!


FOR PARENTS:

Curriculum Night
Join us as we take a first look at the vast opportunities available to students at Irvington High School. By
traveling through short presentations, you will gain an understanding of course sequencing, graduation
requirements, and elective opportunities. The program will be catered towards curriculum at two
integral junctions: Starting high school and becoming an upperclassman.

Welcome to High School: A First Look at What’s Ahead
Parents! Meet your child’s counselor, learn what to expect over the next four years at Irvington High
School and what steps you can be taking yearly toward post-high school success!
