Irvington Athletics Equipment Use Program

The Athletics Equipment Use Program is a partnership between Irvington UFSD (“the School”)
and families (“Family”, “Parent(s)/Guardian(s)”, “Children” or “Student”).

I understand and agree to my family’s responsibility in the Irvington Athletics Equipment Use
Program which includes:
    ●​ Ensuring my child meets the expectations of equipment use as outlined in the Athletic
        Handbook which is incorporated by reference herein.
    ●​ Protecting the equipment, to the furthest extent possible, against damage, loss or theft
        while ensuring the equipment is cared for and utilized for its intended use.
    ●​ Assuming responsibility for improper use, while at school and home.
    ●​ Understanding that the student will be subject to disciplinary action in accordance with
        the Code of Conduct for misuse of the equipment while in his/her possession.
    ●​ Payment for repairs made to damaged equipment or to replace a lost or stolen equipment.
        (approximate cost is $______)

I understand and agree to the responsibilities stated above and on the Student Responsibility Form
and give my permission for _________________________________________________ to
participate in this Program.
​
    ●​ I assume full liability and responsibility for the equipment that my minor child will be
        using.
    ●​ Should loss or damage occur, I, or my child, will report it to designated school personnel
        immediately.
    ●​ The undersigned herewith agrees to assume any and all risk with respect to the use or
        possession of this equipment and also hereby release the Irvington Union Free School
        District, Superintendent of Schools, individually and in his official capacity, the Board of
        Education of the Irvington Union Free School District, its members, individually and in
        their official capacities, and/or any of the Irvington Union Free School District’s
        employees, agents and/or independent contractors, individually and in their official
        capacities from any liability, claim, suit, or expense including, but not limited to,
        negligence, for any injury or harm which may result from such use or possession
        resulting from any cause whatsoever.
