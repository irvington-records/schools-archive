                        New Student Orientation
                            August, 2025

 At our annual 9th grade orientation, students will come together for activities
and discussion. They will also participate in a dry-run of their first day of high
 school by walking their schedule and meeting their teachers. By the time the
                    first day comes, students will be ready!
