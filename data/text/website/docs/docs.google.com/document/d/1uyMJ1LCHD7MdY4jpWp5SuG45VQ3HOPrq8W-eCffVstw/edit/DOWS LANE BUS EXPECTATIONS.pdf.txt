DOWS LANE BUS EXPECTATIONS
Act             Student will keep hands and feet to themselves
Thoughtfully    Student will remain in the seated position
                Student will use inside voices (unless there is an emergency)

Practice        Student will take personal belongings with them
Self-Reliance   Student will be prepared for their stop
                Student will know and follow the rules

Show Kindness   Students will use appropriate words (no cursing, name calling,
& Respect       verbal threats, etc…)
                Students will follow the directions of the bus monitor and driver

Cooperate       Student will help others to keep safe
                Student will ask for help when needed
                Students will help keep the bus clean
