                                 National Honor Society​
                     Local Candidate Eligibility & Selection Procedures

Note: Receipt of this document does not guarantee eligibility for candidacy or membership in the NHS.


Here is an overview of our local eligibility and selection procedures. Please take the time to read
this document carefully so you understand the criteria for selection. The information below
comes directly from the official Constitution of the National Honor Society, as well as our local
by-laws.

   ●​ To be eligible for membership, the candidate must be a member of the junior or senior
      class and must have been in attendance at IHS for at least one full semester.
   ●​ In order to be eligible, juniors and seniors must have an overall weighted transcript GPA
      of 3.50 or higher. In early fall, after transcript GPAs have been calculated to include
      grades from the 2025-26 school year, every rising junior or senior who has enough
      credits, and whose GPA meets or exceeds 3.50 will receive an invitation, which will
      include detailed information about the process. Each member of this select group of
      students will then have until the beginning of November to complete the required
      paperwork demonstrating their ability to meet the additional IHS chapter of National
      Honor Society criteria for service, leadership, and character.
   ●​ In order to be considered for selection, prospective 11th grade candidates will need to
      have completed 50 hours of community service and prospective 12th grade candidates
      will need to have completed 75 hours of community service by the candidacy paperwork
      deadline. Only hours accumulated after 8th grade graduation can be counted towards the
      community service requirement. For all service you must:
          ○​ Complete the NHS Candidate Community Service form
          ○​ If you performed service hours outside of the Irvington School Community, you
              MUST provide official documentation via a hard copy organization letterhead OR
              an email verification, including specific dates/ date ranges and times, sent by your
              direct supervisor through the organization's official email address (no personal
              emails will be accepted). Helper-Helper approvals are not sufficient proof of
              service.
   ●​ In order to be selected, students must demonstrate strong character through the attributes
      of respect, responsibility, integrity, honesty, caring, citizenship, and empathy. Faculty and
      staff members assess the character of each eligible candidate based on the definitions
      provided below. It is important to note that the faculty council may take into
      consideration past events and/or occurrences that show a lack of character when
      determining a candidate’s NHS membership.
     ●​ In order to be selected, students are expected to demonstrate outstanding leadership.
        Definitions of each component of leadership appear below. Candidates should reach out
        to individuals who can speak directly to these leadership qualities.

Attributes of character:
    ●​ RESPECT (for self and for others) - Honoring others’ experiences and perspectives.
    ●​ RESPONSIBILITY - Being trusted to do what is right and act for the greater good.
    ●​ INTEGRITY - Adhering to the accepted community standards of right and wrong.
    ●​ HONESTY - Being fair and truthful in both words and actions.
    ●​ CARING - Helping others feel healthy, safe, and cared for.
    ●​ CITIZENSHIP - Upholding community responsibilities and values.
    ●​ EMPATHY - Understanding and validating the feelings of others.

Attributes of leadership:
    ●​ Fostering Collaboration - Influencing others to work jointly towards meeting a common
       goal.
    ●​ Consistency - Always acting or behaving in the same way or of the same quality.
    ●​ Initiative - The energy and desire to solve a problem (or do something) before others do.
    ●​ Communication - Ability to effectively express your ideas, thoughts, feelings to others.
    ●​ Flexibility - Willing to change or try different things.
    ●​ Conviction - Being sure that what you believe or say is true.
    ●​ Sense of Ethics - Following accepted rules of behavior.

These definitions are derived from the following source: ​
Merriam-Webster.com. Merriam-Webster, 2015. Web. 13 June 2016.
