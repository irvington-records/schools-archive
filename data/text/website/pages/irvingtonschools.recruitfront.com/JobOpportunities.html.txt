Job Openings for Irvington UFSD
							
Thank you for your interest in employment at Irvington Union Free School District and becoming a member of the Irvington Community! If you would like to apply to a job within the Arlington School District, you can search for a job by Category, Location, or Job Number. To search by job number simply enter the job number in the text box in the blue search bar and click on the green Search button and you will be directed to that job.
Once you click the green Apply Now button for a job, the system will walk you through the steps to create a RecruitFront account and application, then applying to that specific job opportunity at our District.  
Please note: Completing an application does not mean that you applied for a job. You must click the Apply Now button for the specific job to complete the process.
|  |  | The mission of the Irvington Union Free School District is to create a challenging and supportive learning environment. | 
Important Notes:
- Completing an application is required only one time and allows you to revisit RecruitFront (remember your username and password!) to update your application as necessary and apply to as many jobs as you like
- You will receive email notifications when you complete your application and another email when you’ve successfully applied to a job
- Pay close attention to the location and schedule on each posting
