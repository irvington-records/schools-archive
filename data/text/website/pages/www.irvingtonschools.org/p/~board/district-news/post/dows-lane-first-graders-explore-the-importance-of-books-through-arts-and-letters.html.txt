Dows Lane First Graders Explore the Importance of Books Through Arts and Letters
Careful observation and creative thinking came together during a recent Arts and Letters lesson as first graders in Ms. Ann Marie Reardon’s class explored the connection between arts and literature.
Students examined a painting of Laila and Majnun and considered how the artwork conveys the importance of books. Through close observation and class discussion, they shared what they noticed and listened to the different ideas and perspectives of their classmates.
Students then expressed their own thinking through writing and drawing, using creativity to build on the ideas they explored together.
The lesson provided a natural opportunity to practice The Irvington Way. By approaching the artwork with curiosity and an open mind, sharing their ideas and listening thoughtfully to others, students demonstrated the habits that help create an engaged and collaborative learning environment.
Through Arts and Letters, Dows Lane’s first graders are developing their ability to observe closely, communicate their thinking and discover how art can inspire meaningful conversations and new ideas.
