Dows Lane Celebrates National Gold Star Recognition
Dows Lane Elementary School’s national recognition continues to shine! 
Superintendent Dr. Mara Ratesic and Dows Lane Principal Dr. Andrea Kantor recently traveled to Orlando, Florida, to accept the 2026 National Gold Star School Award at the National Association of Elementary School Principals (NAESP) Gold Star Schools Awards ceremony. 
The prestigious Gold Star Award recognizes schools that exemplify excellence through three key pillars: established culture, empowered people, and optimal systems. During the ceremony, keynote speaker Dr. Miguel Cardona, former U.S Secretary of Education, celebrated schools of excellence and the leaders who foster outstanding educational communities.  
“It is such an honor to receive this award for Dows Lane. We work hard as a staff to create the most glorious culture for our students, families and each other, and I am so proud,” said Dr. Kantor. 
Congratulations once again to the entire Dows Lane community on this outstanding national recognition.
