Irvington Middle School Club Fair Showcases Ways to Get Involved
A wide range of interests, activities and ways to participate were on display during Irvington Middle School’s recent Club Fair, giving students a chance to discover what extracurricular life IMS has to offer.
Held Sept. 24 in the theater atrium, the fair welcomed all sixth graders as well as new seventh and eighth grade students. Seventh and eighth graders also had additional opportunities to sign up for clubs during lunch.
Helping lead the event were seventh and eighth grade club ambassadors, who staffed tables and spoke with their peers about the different activities available. The student-led format allowed younger and newer students to hear directly from fellow IMS students while considering which clubs best matched their interests.
With more than 30 clubs and activities represented in this year’s materials, offerings span the arts, academics, leadership, service, culture, wellness and recreation. Students can explore groups such as Art Club, Debate Club, Diversity Alliance, Environmental Club, Science Olympiad, Student Council, Video Production Club, Yearbook and many more.
The variety gives students the chance to pursue existing interests while also trying something new. Some clubs emphasize collaboration and problem-solving, while others focus on creative expression, community service, leadership or building relationships with peers.
The fair also reflected The Irvington Way by encouraging students to Be Engaged and Be Collaborative as they explored activities and interacted with one another. For the seventh and eighth grade ambassadors, the event provided an added leadership experience as they welcomed fellow students, answered questions and shared their own involvement at IMS.
Whether discovering a new interest, developing a skill or finding another way to participate in school life, students left the fair with plenty of possibilities for making the most of the year ahead.
