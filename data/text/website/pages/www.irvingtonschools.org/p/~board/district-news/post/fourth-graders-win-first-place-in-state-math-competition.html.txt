Fourth Graders Win First Place in State Math Competition
A team of fourth graders from Main Street School earned first place in New York State at the First In Math State Competition, held in Albany.
Representing MSS with determination, teamwork and outstanding mathematical thinking were Oren Fielman, Nora Mojtabaee-Zamani and Sofia Teich. They competed against top students from across the state and demonstrated exceptional problem-solving skills throughout the competition. The event challenged students to think critically, work strategically, and apply their mathematical knowledge in a fast-paced and engaging environment.
“This remarkable achievement reflects many of the attributes of The Irvington Way,” Principal Joyce Chapnick said. “Our students were courageous as they embraced a challenging competition, collaborative as they worked together as a team, engaged as they dedicated themselves to learning and practicing, and self-aware as they persevered through difficult problems and celebrated their growth as mathematicians.”
The students worked with math coach Molly Stern and fourth grade teacher Stephanie Saputo prior to the competition.
“We’re grateful for their guidance, encouragement and preparation leading up to the competition,” Chapnick said. “We are especially grateful to Ms. Saputo for accompanying the team to Albany and supporting our students throughout this memorable experience. We are incredibly proud of these students for representing Main Street School and Irvington with excellence.”
