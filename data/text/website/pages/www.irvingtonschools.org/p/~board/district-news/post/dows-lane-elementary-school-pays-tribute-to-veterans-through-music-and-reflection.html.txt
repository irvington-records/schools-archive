Dows Lane Elementary School Pays Tribute to Veterans Through Music and Reflection
The sounds of Woody Gunthrie’s “This Land Is Your Land” filled the air as students and staff at Dows Lane Elementary School gathered on Nov. 10 to recognize Veteran’s Day and express gratitude to those who have served our country.
The celebration featured a special tribute to Sgt. Anthony D’Onofrio, who served as a combat medic during the Vietnam War. In preparation for the event, music teacher Genevieve Lotze taught students across all grade levels the patriotic song, bringing the school community together through a shared musical experience.
Students and staff also recited the Pledge of Allegiance as they raised the American flag, taking a moment to reflect on the service and sacrifices of our nation’s veterans.
The celebration embodied the Irvington Way by fostering a sense of community and encouraging students to become engaged citizens who demonstrate respect, gratitude and appreciation for others. Through song and shared traditions, students had an opportunity to recognize the importance of honoring those who have served our country.
