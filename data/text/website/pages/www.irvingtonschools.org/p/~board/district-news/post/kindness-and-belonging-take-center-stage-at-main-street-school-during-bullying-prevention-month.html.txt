Kindness and Belonging Take Center Stage at Main Street School During Bullying Prevention Month
Throughout October, Main Street School students and staff have come together for a series of schoolwide experiences designed to strengthen connections, encourage inclusion and ensure every member of the community feels like they belong. 
In recognition of Bullying Prevention Month, students and staff participated in activities including a kindness paper chain, kindness clips, kindness notes, kindness circles and classroom door banners. Members of the Fifth Grade Leadership Team also made bracelets for every member of the school community.  
As part of the monthlong effort, Main Street School celebrated Unity Day, joining schools across the country in standing together against bullying. Members of the school community wore orange and white as a visible reminder of their shared commitment to kindness, friendship, acceptance and inclusion. 
The Fifth Grade Leadership Team helped plan and facilitate the school’s Unity Day activities, giving students an opportunity to lead their peers and contribute to a welcoming school environment.  
Belonging is Main Street School’s focus for the year, centered on ensuring students know they matter, are accepted for who they are and are an important part of the school community. Through classroom activities, character-building experiences and schoolwide initiatives, students are encouraged to practice kindness, stand up for others, celebrate differences and help others feel included.  
The Main Street School community also supported two organizations dedicated to preventing bullying and helping children feel connected. Funds raised beyond the cost of Unity Day T-shirts will benefit PACER’s National Bullying Prevention Center and the Be A Friend Project. Main Street School will also participate in the Be A Friend Project during the 2026-27 school year by creating messages of hope and encouragement for young people who have experienced bullying.  
These experiences put the Irvington Way into action as students demonstrate what it means to Be Kind, Be Engaged and Be Makers of Change!
