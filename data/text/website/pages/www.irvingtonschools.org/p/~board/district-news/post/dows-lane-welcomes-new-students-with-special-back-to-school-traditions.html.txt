Dows Lane Welcomes New Students with Special Back-to-School Traditions
Excitement for the new school year was in the air at Dows Lane Elementary school on Aug. 24 as incoming kindergarten students, new students and their families came together for two special back-to-school traditions.
Incoming kindergarten students started the day with a school bus ride around Dows Lane alongside a family member. During the ride, students learned more about their new school and what to expect on their first day of kindergarten. Each student also received a plush bulldog courtesy of the PTSA.
“Students were so excited to get on the big bus,” Principal Dr. Andrea Kantor said. “It’s a fun way to get ready for their first day of school.”
Later that day, Dows Lane welcomed new students and incoming kindergarten students for the PTSA’s annual Popsicles with the Principal event. Dr. Kantor and Superintendent of Schools Dr. Mara Ratesic greeted students and families as they became acquainted with the Dows Lane community.
Students had the opportunity to find other students in their classes, play on the playground with new friends and explore the school garden.
“It's a wonderful tradition,” Dr. Kantor said. “Students and families are joining our Irvington family, and we welcome them with open arms!”
Together, the two events offered Dows Lane’s newest students and families a fun and welcoming introduction to the school community as they prepare for the start of the new school year.
