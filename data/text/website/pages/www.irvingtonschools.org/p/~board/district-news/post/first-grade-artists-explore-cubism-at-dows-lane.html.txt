First Grade Artists Explore Cubism at Dows Lane
Shapes, imagination and creative expression are coming together in the Dows Lane Elementary School art room as first-grade students explore the work of Pablo Picasso and the world of Cubism.
Under the guidance of art teacher Chelly Arango, students are using Picasso’s Three Musicians as an inspiration to create their own Cubist characters. Through the project, young artists are experimenting with different shapes while making individual artistic choices to bring their ideas to life.
The lesson encourages students to look at art in new ways while using their imaginations to create original pieces that reflect their own ideas and creativity.
The project also brings The Irvington Way into the art room. As Courageous Learners, students are taking creative risks and exploring new approaches to making art. As Self-Aware Individuals, they are making thoughtful artistic choices that allow them to express themselves through their work.
From exploring a celebrated work of art to proudly sharing their own creations, Dows Lane’s first-grade artists are discovering how inspiration, imagination and individual expression can come together in the creative process.
