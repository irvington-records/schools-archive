Irvington High School Pep Rally Builds Homecoming Spirit
School spirit filled the Irvington High School gym as students, teachers and staff came together for a Homecoming pep rally celebrating the Bulldogs and Homecoming Week’s athletic events.
Music and performances from the Irvington Marching Band and color guard brought energy to the gym as students arrived and helped build excitement throughout the afternoon. The Irvington Bulldog mascot also joined the festivities on the gym floor, helping energize the crowd and build school spirit. The rally officially began with the national anthem before moving into a celebration of Irvington’s student-athletes and Homecoming events.
Director of Athletics John Buonamano then introduced the teams competing throughout Homecoming Week. Student-athletes from girls varsity soccer, field hockey, girls volleyball, girls tennis, boys soccer, girls swim and dive and cross country took to the gym floor to highlight their Homecoming Week competitions and share spirit themes for supporters attending their games. The cross country team also celebrated its recent victory and the Rivertown Cup.
The excitement continued as the school community prepared for Friday night’s football Homecoming game, with students encouraged to fill the stands in green and white. The rally also highlighted a Unified Flag Football event featuring Section One teams and the New York Giants.
Students also took part in games of musical chairs, adding some friendly competition and giving the crowd another opportunity to cheer on their classmates.
Beyond athletics, the gathering celebrated members of the school community. Phoebe Trencher was recognized as Student of the Week for the positive impact and helpfulness she brings to the classroom, while Mr. Fada was named Teacher of the Week for making students feel welcome in social studies. Homecoming Royals were also announced as part of the celebration.
Throughout the rally, students showed their Irvington pride by cheering on classmates, recognizing one another’s accomplishments and coming together in support of their school community. The celebration reflected The Irvington Way as students were encouraged to Be Engaged and Be Collaborative while supporting their peers and participating in Homecoming traditions.
With music, performances, team celebrations, games and plenty of energy from the Irvington Bulldog mascot, the pep rally brought the high school community together and celebrated the spirit of Homecoming Week.
