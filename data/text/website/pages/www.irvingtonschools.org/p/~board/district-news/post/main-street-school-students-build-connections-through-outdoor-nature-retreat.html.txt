Main Street School Students Build Connections Through Outdoor Nature Retreat
Main Street School fourth and fifth grade students spent the day learning beyond the classroom through a special Nature Connection Retreat that combined outdoor exploration, teamwork and reflection. 
Facilitated by Wayfinders on the Hudson and funded by the Irvington Education Foundation, the full-day experience on the Main Street School field engaged students in outdoor games, team challenges and guided reflection designed to strengthen their connections to nature and one another while building confidence, collaboration and communication skills.  
Fifth grade students participated in the morning, followed by fourth graders in the afternoon. Each session featured opening and closing circles led by school staff with support from Wayfinders mentors, along with a series of experiential outdoor activities.  
The retreat brought The Irvington Way to life by encouraging students to Be Courageous, Be Collaborative, Be Self-Aware and Be Engaged as they tried new activities, worked alongside their classmates and reflected on their experiences. 
The program expanded upon the popular Wayfinders sessions offered during Main Street School’s annual Health and Wellness Fair, providing every fourth and fifth grader with an opportunity to participate in hands-on nature connection and social-emotional learning activities. 
Through the experience, students strengthened relationships, practiced important interpersonal skills and connected with the natural world while building upon the values that support a strong and connected Main Street School community.
