Dows Lane and Irvington High School Students Celebrate Halloween Together
Halloween celebrations brought students from across the Irvington Union Free School District together on Friday, as Irvington High School students joined their younger peers at Dows Lane Elementary School for a morning of festive activities.
High school students visited classrooms to share Halloween stories with Dows Lane students, creating an opportunity for students from the two schools to connect and celebrate together.
The festivities continued outside, where Dows Lane students participated in a schoolwide Halloween parade. Students from third grade, second grade, first grade and kindergarten made their way around the upper field in costume as families gathered to watch.
Adding to the celebration, members of the Irvington High School marching band participated in the parade, bringing music and school spirit to the morning.
The collaboration gave high school students an opportunity to play an active role in creating a memorable Halloween experience for younger students while bringing together different parts of the Irvington school community.
Through reading, music and celebration, students demonstrated the Irvington Way by showing what it means to Be Collaborative and contribute to their school community.
