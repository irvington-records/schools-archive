Dows Lane Celebrates Multilingual Learners’ Language Proficiency Achievement
Hard work and growth in language learning were celebrated at Dows Lane Elementary School as four multilingual learners were recognized for reaching the Commanding level of English language proficiency.  
First grader Harshivram Suresh Balajee Priyanka, who speaks Tamil and English; second graders Srinika Das, who speaks Bengali and English, and Camila Jimenez, who speaks Spanish and English; and third grader Joel Brito Recio, who speaks Spanish and English, were honored during a special celebration with their families. 
The students achieved the Commanding level through their performance on the New York State English as a Second Language Achievement Test, or NYSESLAT, the state’s annual English language proficiency assessment for multilingual learners. 
At the Commanding level, students demonstrate the ability to use English across a variety of academic contexts aligned with their grade level. This includes comprehending information in different contexts, using complex grammatical structures and effectively applying academic vocabulary. 
English as a New Language teacher Paul Manzi began the celebration several years ago as a way to recognize students for reaching this important milestone and give families an opportunity to celebrate their accomplishments alongside the school community. 
The students’ achievement reflects their dedication and growth as learners and embodies the Irvington Way as they continue to be engaged and courageous in their learning. Dows Lane congratulates Harshivram, Srinika, Camila and Joel on their accomplishment.
