Our Grades 4–5 special area program offers students a well-rounded educational experience through art, physical education, music, counseling, and library, all aligned with the New York State Learning Standards. In art, students expand their use of varied media and techniques while exploring elements of design, artistic expression, and the study of diverse artists and cultures. Physical education focuses on skill development, teamwork, sportsmanship, and personal fitness, helping students build lifelong healthy habits. In music, students deepen their understanding of rhythm, melody, and notation through singing, instrumental work, and performance, while exploring a range of musical genres and cultural traditions. Counseling supports students’ social and emotional development through topics such as self-awareness, goal setting, peer relationships, and responsible decision-making. Library instruction strengthens independent reading habits while building research skills, media literacy, and digital citizenship.
Together, these experiences support the development of the whole child and reflect The Irvington Way. Students are encouraged to be courageous as they take creative and academic risks, collaborative as they work and perform with others, engaged as they explore new interests and ideas, and self-aware as they reflect on their growth and well-being. Through these special areas, students develop essential life skills while discovering their interests, strengths, and connections to the broader world.
