9-12 Program
Irvington High School’s philosophy emphasizes "purposeful learning," aiming to graduate ethical leaders who possess both the resilience and the intellectual curiosity to thrive in a global society. The High School is nationally recognized for its academic rigor, highlighted by an elite Science Research program and extensive Advanced Placement, honors, SUPA and other college credit and concurrent enrollment opportunities. The high school also offers students the opportunity to earn the New York State Seal of Biliteracy and Seal of Civic Readiness. The staff strives toward every student receiving the personal mentorship necessary to find their unique voice through a rich variety of course offerings, athletic experiences, involvement in the arts, and ample opportunity for civic engagement. Students are supported socially and emotionally through a strong support system, including a DBT-informed counseling program.
