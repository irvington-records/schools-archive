Our K–3 special area program provides students with a rich and engaging experience across art, physical education, Project Lead the Way (PLTW), music, and library, all aligned with the New York State Learning Standards. In art, students explore a variety of media and techniques while learning about elements of design, self-expression, and artists from diverse cultures. Physical education focuses on developing fundamental movement skills, teamwork, and an understanding of healthy habits. Through Project Lead the Way, students engage in hands-on STEM learning, exploring topics such as structure and function, forces and motion, and problem-solving through design. In music, students build foundational skills in rhythm, melody, and performance while exploring different genres and cultures. Library instruction fosters a love of reading while developing early research skills, digital citizenship, and an understanding of how to locate and evaluate information.
Together, these experiences support the development of the whole child and reflect The Irvington Way. Students are encouraged to be courageous as they try new skills and take creative risks, collaborative as they work and perform with others, engaged as they explore new ideas and disciplines, and self-aware as they reflect on their growth and interests. Through these special areas, students not only build important skills, but also discover passions and perspectives that enrich their overall learning experience.
