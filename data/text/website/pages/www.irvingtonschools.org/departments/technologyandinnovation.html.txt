Technology
Welcome to the Technology Department!
Technology continues to shape how we live, work, communicate, create, and learn. In the Irvington Union Free School District, we believe technology can be a valuable tool when it is used thoughtfully, intentionally, and in service of student learning.
Guided by The Irvington Way, our approach to technology emphasizes curiosity, connection, critical thinking, collaboration, creativity, and responsible digital citizenship. Technology should support strong instruction, expand access, foster creativity, and help students develop the digital fluency skills needed to navigate an increasingly complex world.
At the same time, we recognize that technology is only one tool among many. It should not replace the hands-on, interpersonal, paper-based, and discussion-rich learning experiences that are essential to student growth. Across our schools, we are committed to using technology in ways that are developmentally appropriate, teacher-directed, balanced, and aligned to clear instructional goals.
This page contains resources related to technology use in Irvington UFSD, including information about instructional tools, digital safety, family resources, and our ongoing work to ensure technology supports—not drives—teaching and learning.
