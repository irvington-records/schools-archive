Food and Nutrition
Irvington UFSD Food Services
Currently, the Irvington School District partners with Aramark to provide food services for our students and staff. The district participates in the National School Lunch program also known as Child Nutrition. Breakfast is available at the campus cafeteria for Middle and High School students between the hours of 7:30am - 9:30am and school lunch is available at all schools. Meals are free with the Universal Meals Program.
There are many ala carte items offered for sale at the Campus Cafeteria for Middle and High School students as well. Theses purchases require funds on account or payment at the register.
Please note that your child's end-of-year school meal balance may be refunded, transferred to a sibling in the district, or donated to the Irvington Bulldog Family Fund for a student in need. Please email lunch@irvingtonschools.org with your preference.
My School Bucks
The District uses My School Bucks to allow parents/guardians to create a lunch account for their student and add funds periodically to these accounts. There is a small processing fee associated with this. Alternatively, students may pay in cash or have a check applied to their account. Parents may also use My School Bucks to view student purchases your student makes.
