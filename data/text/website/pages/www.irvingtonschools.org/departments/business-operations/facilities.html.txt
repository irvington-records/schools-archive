Facilities
Welcome to the Facilities Office of the Irvington UFSD. Our custodial and grounds staff proudly maintain our facilities for eight buildings and four fields. We partner with the Village of Irvington to run recreational programs at our facilities, including athletic programs and summer camp. We also allow other outside groups to use the facilities on a permit basis and when not in use for school groups and athletics. Please see below for the facility use policy and facility use process.
