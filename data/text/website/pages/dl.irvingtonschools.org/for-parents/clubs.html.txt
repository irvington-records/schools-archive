Clubs
Clubs at Dows Lane
At Dows Lane, we offer lunchtime club opportunities in 2nd and 3rd grade.
These clubs offer enriching experiences for children during their lunch/recess. They foster collaboration and friendships through creative and innovative experiences. They are also a great way to foster a deeper connection with other students.
Students will receive information about club dates and sign up. Depending on interest, clubs are run on rotations and cycles. All clubs are voluntary and signing up should reflect student interests.
