Mrs. Nemeth's video about submitting work in Google Classroom
Top 5 tips for students on Google Classroom: 
1) Ignore the Stream. Focus on Classwork 
2) Use the browser back arrow. Otherwise, you'll end up back at the Stream.
3) Always Turn in or Mark as Done. You want all your icons gray!
4) Check in with View Your Work. This is where you see a summary of all your assignments. 
5) Bookmark the Classwork page. It's the quickest way to get straight to Classwork. 
Communication
If your child will be absent, but is able to continue their independent learning, we suggest continuing reading, writing, and practicing math facts. Please communicate with your classroom teacher and Nurse Fleming about any absences.
