Summer Reading Suggestions and Resources
As the summer approaches, one of our hopes is that students will continue to read joyously and voraciously this summer. To support this endeavor, we have created the IMS Summer Reading Recommendations which will also be posted on our school website. The IMS Library is here to support students all summer long. Our free, digital ebook and audiobook service, Sora, has thousands of books that you can read on any device. To keep up with the news through articles tailored to students, check out Newsela. These and many other resources are available on our library website page. For six simple strategies to encourage lifelong reading please read our letter about supporting your child’s summer reading. Happy reading!
