Welcome, Irvington Coders! 
The Hour of Code is an event that takes place each year during Computer Science Education Week where students all over the world take the time to practice or learn coding skills. Anyone can join in the fun regardless of coding experience! To help you out, here are some projects and activities that can get you started on your week. 
What is Creativity?
Creativity is important for coders! Watch this short video to find out more!
Do you follow our Instagram account? @BulldogBytes
If you message us a photo of yourself coding or a video explaining why you like to code, we may feature you on our account during CS Education Week!
If you make a video, include your first name only (not your last) when talking and your grade. In the comment to us, please also let us know your last name. You may also email your photo or video to Dr. Burke (stephanie.burke@irvingtonschools.org) or Mrs. Cassidy (diana.cassidy@irvingtonschools.org) to be included. In the message of the email, please include your first and last name and grade level.
We also would love to feature alumni, parents, or staff who have a connection 
and interest in coding and computer science!
