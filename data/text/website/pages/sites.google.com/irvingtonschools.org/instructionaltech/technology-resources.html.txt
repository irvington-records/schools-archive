Securly
Securly Classroom helps teachers monitor and control student devices, primarily Chromebooks, during class to ensure focus on learning and prevent distractions. It allows teachers to view student screens in real-time, lock screens to get attention, push specific websites and content, block distracting websites, share content with students, and even identify students needing help. Teachers can even unblock sites for students, eliminating the need to submit Mojo Help Desk Tickets to unblock websites.
