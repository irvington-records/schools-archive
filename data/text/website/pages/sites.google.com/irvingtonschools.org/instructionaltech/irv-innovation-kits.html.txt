Thinking about 🌶️ spicing 🌶️ things up in your classroom? 
🤔 Wondering 🤔 how to engage, enhance, and extend 
the learning of your students?
Have an 💡idea💡 but need help bringing it to life? 
Do you know you want to do something 
fun and different but not sure what? 🤷🏻♀️
Consider trying items from the Irvington Innovation Kits 
and reach out to Stephanie who will work with you
to 🧠⛈️brainstorm🧠⛈️ an amazing activity for your class!
If you would like to use some of the materials and tools from the 
Irvington Innovation Kits Lending Library in your classroom,
Stephanie can meet with you to chat about how they can be used,
and then you can sign them out for a period of time.
Stephanie is happy to support the activity/implementation through co-teaching.
To book Innovation Kits items, please reach out to Stephanie
