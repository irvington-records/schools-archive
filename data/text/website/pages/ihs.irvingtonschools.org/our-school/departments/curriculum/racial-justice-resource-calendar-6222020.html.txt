Racial Justice Resource Calendar - 6/22/2020
Dear Students and Families,
As a follow-up to the communication we shared earlier this month, we are writing to share our Racial Justice Resource Calendar.
These are recommendations and a calendar meant to help us engage with a number of the resources we shared earlier this month. The calendar is thoughtfully organized; each engaging activity can be done in about ten minutes. The activities begin today and last for a month. Individuals are encouraged to journal or dialogue with their families throughout the summer. We continue to work as a district to develop a plan in which all of us can learn more about ourselves, our experiences and our biases so that we can continue to move forward together!
Best always,
Juliet
