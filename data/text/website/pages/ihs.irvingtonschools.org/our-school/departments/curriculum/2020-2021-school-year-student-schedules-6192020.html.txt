2020-2021 School Year: Student Schedules - 6/19/2020
Dear Students and Families,
As we look ahead to the 2020-2021 school year, we are planning to release schedules on August 20th, 2020. At that time, an email will be sent with information regarding how to access individual schedules. The verification process completed in the spring certainly will have lowered the number of questions or requests for updates. 
If you are currently considering a schedule request change, you must communicate with your counselor by 6/23/20 via email. ALL schedule change requests received after this date will be addressed by the school counselors at the end August.
PLEASE DO NOT email counselors directly after 6/23/20 unless there is additional information to share after completing the form. Your counselor will contact you after 8/28/20 when they have returned to the office.  
Please note: change requests will be honored for errors in a student’s schedule. All other requests will be considered, but cannot be guaranteed.
Best always,
Ms. Juliet Gevargis-Mizimakoski & Ms. Emily Colman
