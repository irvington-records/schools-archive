Events & Workshops
Throughout the year, the School Counseling Department hosts events geared towards the current needs of our high school families.
Our Evenings of Insights…
The Counseling Department is excited to announce a series of invaluable evening/day programs designed to support our students and their families. These sessions will cover a range of crucial topics, including navigating the high school transition, navigating high school as it is happening, understanding the college application and review process, and providing insight into what happens behind the scenes in admissions committees. We encourage all parents and students to join us for these informative events, which are specifically tailored to equip you with the knowledge and tools needed for academic success and future planning.
VIRTUAL–From Now to Next: Admissions to Aid **—Sept. 10 /Time: 6:00-7:00pm** Target audience: 12th graders/families. Click Here for Admissions to Aid Presentation
IN PERSON-Partnering for Success: Your Freshman's First Quarter–Nov. 12/Time: 10-10:40am Target audience: 9th graders/families. This program covers topics like graduation requirements, course selection for the next year, and how to best support your ninth grader during this foundational year.
Video Release–Course Selection Overview–Release date Dec. 14 Target audience: 8-11th grade families: Watch the Counselors present the Course offerings for the 2027-2028 school year.
VIRTUAL-Minds Unpacked: Advice for our Neurodiverse College Bound Students-January 13/Time: 10-10:40am Target audience: parents and guardians of our neurodiverse students. Grab a cup of coffee and join college experts for a candid conversation on guiding your neurodiverse student through the college transition. Together, we’ll tackle crucial questions from whether to disclose a diagnosis to navigating the shift from K-12 IEPs/504s to higher education accommodations.
VIRTUAL–Admissions Committee Deep Dive **–January 14/Time: 6:00-7:00 pm** Target audience: 9-11th graders/families. Ever wonder what it is like to sit on an Admission Committee? Join us and our expert panelists as they walk us through a Mock admission Panel. Together we will unpack the applications and Admit, Defer and Deny 3 applicants.
IN PERSON–Laying the Underclass Groundwork **–March 11/Time: 10-10:40am** Target audience: 10th graders/families. Get ready 10th graders—you are about to be an Upperclass student. What does that mean? This evening will provide parents with a clear roadmap for the remaining 2 years of high school, covering topics like course selection, standardized testing, and college and career readiness planning.
VIRTUAL–Your High School Head Start **–June 10/Time: 6:00-7:00pm** Target audience: 8 to 9th graders/families. Join Ms. Pulice for a night of High School introductions with some words of wisdom for our expert panelists!
