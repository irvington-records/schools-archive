Driver's Ed Summer 2026
Please see the link below:
https://mariaregina.org/apps/forms2/?f=57496
Lectures for the Summer are every Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. The students must attend all three days. Each day the same class topic is offered at 8 am, 5 pm and 6:15 pm. The students have the flexibility of what time they want to attend, as long as they make it to one lecture each day. The lectures are about one hour long.
For the driving portion, you will need to schedule during orientation. The schedule will be available for you to sign up during that time. Driving is available 7 days a week 7 am-7 pm. and you must attend 3 lessons per week.
The program is 6 weeks, beginning July 7, 2026 through August 15, 2026.
