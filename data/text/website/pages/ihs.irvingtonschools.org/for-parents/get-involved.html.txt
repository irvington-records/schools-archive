Get Involved!
A strong bridge between home and school is sustained by parent involvement. Students with parents who are involved in their school tend to have fewer behavioral problems and better academic performance, and are more likely to complete high school than students whose parents are not involved. Here you can find resources to help you get involved in our school community.
