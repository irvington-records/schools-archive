The Irvington NY PTSA is an all-volunteer, non-profit organization that works to improve kids’ educational experiences, connect our community, ensure equal access to education, and advocate for change.

The Irvington NY PTSA is a volunteer-led 501c3 organization that helps shape and support the IUFSD community in four key ways:
- We fund grants for in-school programs and resources beyond the District budget.
- We run signature programs and events that bring our school community together and enrich our student experience.
- We provide assistance to District students and families in financial need through the Bulldog Family Fund.
- We advocate through our Committees including our Parent Advisory Councils (PAC) and Supporting Children & Families with Learning Differences (SCAFLD).
