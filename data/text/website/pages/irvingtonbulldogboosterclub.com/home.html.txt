The Irvington Bulldog Booster Club (IBBC) is dedicated to fostering a positive and spirited community environment by working to enhance Bulldog pride and encourage participation in and attendance at athletic programs of the Irvington Union Free School District. Together with parents, coaches, students, teachers, and administrators, the Irvington Bulldog Boosters will provide opportunities for our student-athletes and coaches and work to enhance student sportsmanship, leadership, and character. The Bulldog Boosters will seek to organize strategic fundraising to ensure important financial resources for all sports teams, leadership development, athletic programs, and athletic facilities of the Irvington Union Free School District.
As an organization solely focused on the betterment of our students and student-athletes, the Irvington Bulldog Booster Club will have no role as an advisory group to the athletic program or its administration. Matters about coaches, student athletes' playing time, budgetary considerations, and all other athletic program management decisions are within the administration's purview, and the Booster Club will have no say in these matters.
