Registered Nurse, Irvington HS
Elizabeth Basha, MS, RN
University, BA 
Elizabeth Basha, MS, RN, provides compassionate and comprehensive care to students throughout the school day. She plays an essential role in supporting student health, safety and well-being while helping students remain healthy, engaged and ready to learn. Elizabeth works collaboratively with students, families, faculty members, administrators, healthcare providers and the Athletic Department to address individual health needs and promote a safe school environment. Her responsibilities include responding to illnesses and medical emergencies, managing student health plans, reviewing medical documentation and supporting the health-clearance process for interscholastic athletics.
Known for her dedication and reassuring presence, Elizabeth is a trusted resource for the Irvington High School community. She values the opportunity to build meaningful relationships with students and families while providing thoughtful care and guidance throughout each student’s high school experience.

Certified Athletic Trainer, ATC
Hana R. Gross, ATC/Sports Coordinator
Alfred University, BS
Hana R. Gross, ATC, serves as the Certified Athletic Trainer and Sports Coordinator, supporting student-athletes across the district’s interscholastic athletic programs. She earned her Bachelor of Science degree from Alfred University and holds additional professional credentials in performance enhancement, functional movement and athletic injury care. Hana is committed to providing student-athletes with compassionate, comprehensive care through injury prevention, evaluation, emergency response, rehabilitation and safe return-to-play planning. She works closely with families, coaches, school nurses, physicians and administrators to promote the health, safety and overall well-being of Irvington’s student-athletes.
In 2024, Hana was selected as the Section 1 Athletic Trainer of the Year, an honor recognizing an athletic trainer who exemplifies dedication, selflessness and an exceptional work ethic. Her professionalism, responsiveness and unwavering commitment to student safety have made her an invaluable member of the Irvington school community.
