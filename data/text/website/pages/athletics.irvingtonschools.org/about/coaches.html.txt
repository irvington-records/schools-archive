Coaching Certification Requirements
All interscholastic coaches in New York State must meet the training and certification standards established by the New York State Education Department (NYSED). These requirements are designed to ensure that coaches are properly prepared to support student-athletes in a safe, educational, and developmentally appropriate environment.
Individuals who are not currently certified New York State Physical Education teachers may still serve as coaches but must obtain the appropriate coaching license and complete required coursework, safety training, and background clearances. NYSED provides two certification pathways—the SED Individual Evaluation Pathway and the NFHS Pathway—each with specific coursework and timelines.
The information and resources on NYSED's Website provide an overview of the requirements for both certified teachers and non-teacher coaches who are interested in coaching in New York State schools.
Coaching Opportunities & Applications
The Irvington Athletic Department is always interested in connecting with individuals who are passionate about working with student-athletes and contributing to a positive, education-based athletic experience. Our coaches play an important role in developing character, leadership, teamwork, and sportsmanship while helping student-athletes grow both on and off the field.
Coaching positions may become available throughout the school year across our middle school and high school programs. Candidates who are interested in being considered for future coaching opportunities are encouraged to review the New York State coaching certification requirements and submit an application when positions are posted via OLAS.
Qualified candidates should demonstrate strong knowledge of the sport, a commitment to student development, and the ability to support the mission and values of Irvington Athletics.
