Student Athlete Advisory Committee (SAAC)
The Student-Athlete Advisory Committee (SAAC) is a dynamic group of student-athletes committed to shaping the future of Irvington Athletics. As a member of SAAC, you’ll have the opportunity to develop leadership skills, amplify the voice of student-athletes, and play an active role in influencing the policies and decisions that impact your experience.
Through collaboration, community outreach, and advocacy, SAAC members help create a positive, lasting impact on their teams, the Athletic Department, and the entire Irvington community. Join SAAC and be a driving force for change, growth, and excellence in Irvington Athletics!
