                         Irvington Middle School PAC Meeting
                                          MINUTES
                                         May 8, 2026


In attendance: David Sottile (IMS Principal), Allyson Daley (IMS Asst. Principal), Oda Sham
(PTSA IMS VP), Heather Goodman (PTSA IMS VP and Heidi Rhodes (PTSA Grade Parent –
6th)

                                      PAC Submissions

   ●​ Do all clubs have advisors that instruct on the topic? My child joined a child but
      lots of the kids were just playing on their phones and there wasn’t much direction
      from the instructor. My child really didn’t get much out of it and ended up quitting,
      even though they were super excited about it.
   ●​ RESPONSE: Clubs are intended to be exploratory experiences that introduce students
      to interests, topics, and activities they may not otherwise have the opportunity to
      encounter. As such, clubs should include an element of instruction, guided learning, and
      collaborative discovery as part of the overall experience. If a student attends a club and
      feels that the experience does not align with these expectations, they are encouraged to
      speak directly with the club advisor. Families may also reach out to David, who can help
      facilitate a conversation with the advisor if needed. Parents and students are welcome to
      check with individual club advisors regarding the level of prior knowledge, experience, or
      participation expected for a particular club.


   ●​ When can we expect IMS to start using ParentSquare? I see other schools are
      posting on it.
   ●​ RESPONSE: ParentSquare will be effective in September for IMS.
