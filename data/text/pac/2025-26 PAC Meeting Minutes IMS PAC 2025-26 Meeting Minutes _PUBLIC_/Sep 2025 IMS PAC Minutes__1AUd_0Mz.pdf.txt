                                     PAC Submissions

   ●​ For modified sports, the coaches use the sportsYou app, but students can’t sign
      up for the app unless they are 13. How are changes in the schedule handled for
      kids not in the app and with phones banned during school hours?
      o​ RESPONSE: There are announcements made at the beginning of 9th period if there
         are changes for any modified sports. The students are aware of this and should be
         paying attention. If there isn’t an announcement, their sport should proceed as
         scheduled.
