                         Irvington Middle School PAC Meeting
                                         MINUTES
                                      February 13, 2026


In attendance: David Sottile (IMS Principal), Allyson Daley (IMS Asst. Principal), Oda Sham
(PTSA IMS VP), Heather Goodman (PTSA IMS VP), Heidi Rhodes (PTSA Grade Parent – 6th),
Sarah Harris Weiss (PTSA Grade Parent – 7th) and Barbara Trencher (PTSA Grade Parent –
8th)

                                     PAC Submissions

   ●​ The timing for the middle school clubs can be very stressful. The process at MSS
      is much more organized and easier for parents. It's also been frustrating that
      communication goes to students instead of parents. It seems like if students will
      need to be somewhere outside of school hours, there should be an email or
      infinite campus message that goes to parents rather than a request to join the
      google classroom.
   ●​ RESPONSE: Many teachers currently use Google Classroom, which is primarily
      student-facing. As a result, parents may not always receive information as directly or as
      quickly as we would like.
   ●​ The district is in the process of transitioning to a new communication platform,
      ParentSquare, which will become a primary tool for connecting with both students and
      parents. This shift is intended to improve transparency and address some of the
      limitations of Google Classroom.
   ●​ We recognize that there may be times when communication does not reach families as
      quickly as needed, especially when plans change. We are continuing to work on
      improving this. Staff have also been encouraged to support students in building
      responsibility and independence, while recognizing that parents play an important role in
      helping students stay informed and organized.


   ●​ Is it possible to do more to support students who ride to school. Something like a
      secure (weather proof, lockable shed/ shelter).
   ●​ RESPONSE: There are bike racks to lock up bikes and scooters. Weather proofing
      would need to be budgeted for and would need to go through the school district.
