                             Irvington Dows Lane PAC Meeting
                                                MINUTES
                                            February 14, 2024
                                                10:00 am

Attendees: Dr. Andrea Kantor (DL Principal), Tom Chickery (DL Assistant Principal), Katherine Cain (DL
PTSA VP), Amy Suehnholz (DL PTSA VP), Katie Bauer (DL Grade Parent), Emma Gardner (DL Grade
Parent)

PAC Submissions

Submission: The children shouldn’t be taken outside to play when the temperature is cold, less than 40
degrees. The month of January my child would complain that she was very cold when she was taken
outside to play
Response: At Dows Lane, the administrators and school nurse evaluate the grounds each day to
determine whether they are safe for outdoor recess. Dr. Kantor believes that fresh air and time/space to
move is extremely important for children. Families are encouraged to dress their children in layers, so that
they are comfortable inside and also prepared to go outside.

Submission: Why are all of the nonfiction books in the Dows Lane library from the 90s and early 2000s?
Every week [my son] brings home books about sports that are painfully outdated. Feels like such an easy
fix - to have more current “soccer stars” books, for example. So the question is - what do we need to do to
make the nonfiction books in the library more current, with copyrights let’s say 2015 and later?
Response: Dr. Kantor and Mr. Chickery are going to discuss this issue with the DL librarian Gina
Samaniego. Keeping library books current is an important goal and we will work to address this issue.
