                                 Irvington Dows Lane PAC Meeting
                                                MINUTES
                                               April 10, 2024


    In attendance: Dr. Andrea Kantor (DL Principal), Tom Chickery (DL Asst. Principal), Alison
    Lang (PTSA DL VP), Amy Suehnholz (PTSA DL VP), Emily Burley (PTSA Grade Parent), Katie
    Bauer (PTSA Grade Parent), Brittany Marcus (PTSA Grade Parent), Emma Gardner (PTSA
    Grade Parent)

    PAC Submissions

●   SCHEDULING: When making the calendar and the 1pm dismissals please keep in account the
    other days when school isn’t a full day. We have a delayed opening, 1pm dismissal, and half
    day all in a row right before break and it’s very hard for working parents.
●   RESPONSE: Those dates come from the district and not Dows Lane administrators. It is worth
    reaching out to the acting superintendent for comment if you have concerns about scheduling.
    Please note that for the purpose of receiving NYS funding, the District needs to have a certain
    number of school days, and 1/2 days count as a "day" as per NYS State.

●   ICE CREAM We’d like to understand why ice cream was added to the cafeteria menu. There
    does not seem to be enough time for children to eat their healthy options, much less fit in ice
    cream. We are worried they will just prioritize the ice cream and not eat the more nourishing
    foods. Additionally it causes the cafeteria lines to be so long that there is even less time to finish
    their foods.
●   RESPONSE: The DL 3rd graders wrote a letter advocating for ice cream. Aramark and the
    school administration agreed to try it out, but are open to feedback. Carol Stein heads up the
    Wellness Committee which discusses and helps to decide lunch and snack choices in the
    cafeteria. Any parent is welcomed to send a note to her regarding this concern or anything
    related to Aramark’s food choices: carol.stein@irvingtonschools.org
