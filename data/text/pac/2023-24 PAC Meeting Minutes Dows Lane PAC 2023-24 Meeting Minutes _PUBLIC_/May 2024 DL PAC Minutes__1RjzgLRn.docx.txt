Irvington Dows Lane PAC Meeting
MINUTES
May 1, 2024


In attendance: Dr. Andrea Kantor (DL Principal), Tom Chickery (DL Asst. Principal), Alison Lang (PTSA DL VP), Amy Suehnholz (PTSA DL VP), Emily Burley (PTSA Grade Parent), Brittany Marcus (PTSA Grade Parent), Emma Gardner (PTSA Grade Parent)

PAC Submissions 

	•	After School Programs: Why can’t Dows Lane offer more robust after school offerings on site? Many school districts (including in the Rivertowns) offer a variety of enrichment classes (often from outside providers) during the after school hours. Thanks. 
	•	RESPONSE: Any qualified individual is allowed to create an after school program. An insurance policy and building use contract are required (all details, forms, and information can be found on the District website). The school suggests any sort of local program to reach out and make arrangements to provide families with this type of service. The cafeteria is available and would sometimes need to be shared with The Girl Scouts or Chess Wizards. 
	•	District Scheduling: I am wondering if the district has considered the burdensome impact of additional days off of school during the academic year for dual-working parents (this is regarding the 2024-2025 recently adopted calendar). For dual-working parents who rely on afterschool programs such as ICC, JCC, or Mascia, we are not provided with childcare on the full days off. Therefore, taking so many days off for vacation breaks and various holidays really puts working parents in a bind. It is nearly impossible to find a babysitter who can work one full day sporadically, so our only option becomes a handful of sports day-camps that typically cost $100/child/day. Instead of taking two full weeks off in December 2024, and adding a day to the spring break, and a day to Memorial Day Weekend, and then (for reasons that are still quite baffling to parents) having 3 half-days during the final week of school, I know many, many families in the district who would prefer a school year that has less days off, less half-days, and ends much earlier in June. That way we could (in early June) either enroll our kids in full-week summer camps (which are easier to find and more affordable than single-day camps) or find a summer babysitter who works full-time Mon-Fri. It’s the sporadic days off here and there that wreak havoc for full-time dual-working families. Would it be possible for the district to poll the parent community about this? Thanks for your consideration. 
	•	RESPONSE: Those dates come from the district and not Dows Lane administrators. It is worth reaching out to the acting superintendent for comment if you have concerns about scheduling. Please note that for the purpose of receiving NYS funding, the District needs to have a certain number of school days, and 1/2 days count as a "day" as per NYS State. 

	•	MySchool Bucks: Regarding adding funds to our kid's meal accounts with MySchoolBucks: I've been looking at the MySchool Bucks Website and saw this "MySchoolBucks is not the only option to make payments to your school. To learn more about alternative free options for adding funds to student accounts, please contact your school or school district." Could we get more information? It would be nice to be able to start the school year with a larger amount of money in the account. $120 is the maximum amount that can be added at a time on their website. Thank you!
	•	RESPONSE: Children are allowed to bring in cash to put toward their Aramark card free of charge. The school will review their contract with Aramark to see why there is such a limit. Additional you can find out more by emailing Carol Stein, carol.stein@irvingtonschools.org
