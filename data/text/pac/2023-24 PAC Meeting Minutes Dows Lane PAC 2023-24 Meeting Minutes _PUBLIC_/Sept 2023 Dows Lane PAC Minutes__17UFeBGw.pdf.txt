                                 Dows Lane PAC Meeting
                                         MINUTES
                                     September 13, 2023


In person attendance: Dr. Andrea Kantor (DL Principal), Mr. Tom Chickery (DL Asst. Principal),
Katherine Cain (PTSA DL VP), Amy Suehnholz (PTSA DL VP), Alison Lang (PTSA DL VP),
Emily Burley (PTSA DL Treasurer) Emma Glazer (PTSA DL Grade Parent) and Brittany Marcus
(PTSA DL Grade Parent)

PAC Submissions

       1. Lunch: There should be a fund for kids whose parents have forgotten to put money
          on the lunch account. No child should ever be told there is no money and not given a
          lunch.

          RESPONSE:
          ● Nobody ever goes hungry. Every child receives lunch whether or not they have
            money on their account. Please note that snacks are not given out as a part of
            this complementary offering. Please always feel free to email
            lunch@irvingtonschools.org for any additional questions regarding all things
            lunch.

       2. Kindergarten Orientation: It would be great if there was a parent orientation for the
          incoming kindergarteners just to understand where their classrooms are, what is
          expected from them and how to get lunch.

          RESPONSE:
          ● Fortunately there are a few scheduled kindergarten events that all are welcomed
            to join. In the spring there is a carnival for rising kindergarteners, where they can
            tour the building and participate in games. In the summer the PTSA holds a
            "Popcilies with Principals" event for both parents and children to meet some of
            the school staff and other families. Families receive a summer mailing that
            announces their kindergartener's assigned teacher and classroom location.
            Additionally kindergarteners now receive a video message from their teacher
            before starting school. Finally Dows Lane also holds the "Welcome to
            Kindergarten" informational meeting for parents in the fall.

       3. Calendar: Could we please get a calendar of all the special days in the pdf format so
          that we can plan in advance?

          RESPONSE:
          ● Please refer to the district website's calendar. Everyone has the opportunity to
            export the school calendar to their personal digital calendar.

school puts out the "Buzz", which provides reminders for the important dates for
