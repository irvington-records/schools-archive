                                               MINUTES
                                            January 11, 2024


In attendance: Dr. Andrea Kantor (DL Principal), Tom Chickery (DL Asst. Principal), Katherine
Cain (PTSA DL VP), Alison Lang (PTSA DL VP), Amy Suehnholz (PTSA DL VP), Emily Burley
(PTSA Grade Parent), Emma Gardner (PTSA Grade Parent)

PAC Submissions

   ●   Lice: It seems like lice keeps going around our grade level. It is really expensive to mitigate the
       problem once much less twice and constantly have to purchase the preventative products. What
       can we do to minimize this problem and keep it at bay?
   ●   RESPONSE: Per the district’s medical director, it is a family’s responsibility to mitigate individual
       lice problems. The school communicates to parents about any sort of lice problem when three or
       more students report having it in a classroom. Feel free to purchase the preventative sprays on
       your child’s hair or book bag. Families are encouraged to reach out to Nurse Flemming if they
       detect lice at home.
