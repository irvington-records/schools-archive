                         Irvington Middle School PAC Meeting
                                         MINUTES
                                      October 17, 2024


In attendance: David Sottile (IMS Principal), Allyson Daley (IMS Asst. Principal), Mavis Edme
(PTSA IMS VP), Mara Gredick (PTSA IMS VP), Oda Sham (PTSA IMS VP), Sarah Harris Weiss
(PTSA Grade Parent – 6th), Barbara Trencher (PTSA Grade Parent – 7th) and Janet Kovacs
(PTSA Grade Parent – 8th)

                                     PAC Submissions

       1. Lots of the eighth grade parents are expressing support for an end of the year
          dance/party. One of the parents is an event planner and I am confident that
          there will be no shortage of volunteers. I’m happy to take the lead to corral
          them. If the school and/or PTSA can provide any type of support, that would be
          great. Being able to do it on school property would make things a lot simpler
          rather than us finding another venue and doing this.

          The fifth grade parents last year organized a similar party without school
          involvement and the cost was $25/child, which included $400 for the venue.
          Perhaps we’d need to step it up a bit to account for the age difference, but this
          gives us a ballpark idea.

          RESPONSE:
          ● The 8th grade class has the DC trip planned for April, Field Day in May and the
            Moving Up Ceremony in June. With all of these events planned already, it feels
            unnecessary to add another 8th grade event at the end of the school year.



   ●   Next PAC meeting is on November 15, 2024
