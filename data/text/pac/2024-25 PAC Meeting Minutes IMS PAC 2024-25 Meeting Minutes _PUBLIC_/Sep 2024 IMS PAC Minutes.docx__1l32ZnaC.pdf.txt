                         Irvington Middle School PAC Meeting
                                        MINUTES
                                    September 13, 2024


In attendance: David Sottile (IMS Principal), Allyson Daley (IMS Asst. Principal), Mavis Edme
(PTSA IMS VP), Mara Gredick (PTSA IMS VP), Oda Sham (PTSA IMS VP), Sarah Harris Weiss
(PTSA Grade Parent – 6th), Barbara Trencher (PTSA Grade Parent – 7th) and Janet Kovacs
(PTSA Grade Parent – 8th)

                                     PAC Submissions

       1. Can you please clarify the rule for religious conflicts with modified sports?
          Some of the coaches are telling students that if they miss practice for religious
          school they cannot play in the game the following day. I don’t think students
          can or should be penalized for exercising their religious freedoms.

          RESPONSE:
          ● There is a NY State mandate on how many practices a student must attend
            before participating in any competition.
          ● Individual teams may have set guidelines. Parents should speak to the coach
            and athletic director to understand the guidelines.

       2. Students were told they cannot move from one activity at recess to another.
          Why is this the case?

          RESPONSE:
          ● Students can move from one activity, at recess, to another, but it doesn’t happen
            with the same ease as it did last year. It was always in the plans to make access
            to the school more secure by having all the external doors locked automatically
            versus using keys (like last year). The aides are managing the basketball courts
            and the turf to closely supervise the students, to ensure a safe environment while
            the students are playing outdoors. The aides may not always be available by the
            door when a student wants to switch to a new activity.
          ● Mr. Sottile and Ms. Daley will follow up with the staff to ensure that the students
            can move from one activity to another.
