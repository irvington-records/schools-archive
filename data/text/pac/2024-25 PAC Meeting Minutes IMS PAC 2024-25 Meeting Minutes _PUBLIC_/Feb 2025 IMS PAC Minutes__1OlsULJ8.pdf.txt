                          Irvington Middle School PAC Meeting
                                         MINUTES
                                      February 14, 2025


In attendance: David Sottile (IMS Principal), Allyson Daley (IMS Asst. Principal), Mavis Edme
(PTSA IMS VP), Mara Gredick (PTSA IMS VP), Oda Sham (PTSA IMS VP), Sarah Harris Weiss
(PTSA Grade Parent – 6th), Barbara Trencher (PTSA Grade Parent – 7th) and Janet Kovacs
(PTSA Grade Parent – 8th)

                                      PAC Submissions

●​ Would it be possible to communicate dates and times for events to parents directly?
   A lot of information seems to go directly to students and they are expected to pass
   the information along. This leads to a lot of potential for miscommunication. One
   example is that the social studies classes were going outside and students needed to
   wear warm clothing - could this be shared through infinite campus rather than just
   google classroom? This is also true of music honor society performances, clubs, and
   other school-run activities.
       o​ RESPONSE: We want to encourage the students to build a level of responsibility
          and get them in the habit of taking ownership when they sign up for anything beyond
          their day to day classes.
       o​ We will share with teachers that if they are engaging with students outside of the
          school day, that’s through a club/class, to communicate with parents directly via
          e-mail.


●​ Today 2/11, my middle schooler has an Earth Science Midterm, Spanish Unit test and
   a math test. While I know some were pushed back due to the recent weather, that's a
   lot for a 13-14 year old to manage. The high school has a policy about testing days to
   avoid things like this. I do not believe the middle school has this policy. Any thoughts
   about adding one?
       o​ RESPONSE: There is a testing calendar for the teachers to use to manage how
           many tests students have in a day that is usually . But there are times when weather
           or breaks can cause unforeseen conflicts. We will follow up with the 8th grade team
           on this as well. These should be infrequent occurrences. If you find this to happen
           regularly, please reach out directly to a counselor or building administrator


●​ I imagine there are certain years when the kids’ needs are greater, such as 8th grade
   which is a transitional year. Sponsoring an event which would bring all the kids
   together in the evening, with the opportunity to dress up and have a dance party
   would be a really nice way to cap off their middle school experience. We know that
   some kids have been hosting larger scale birthday parties of this nature. From what I
   understand, the IMS administration is doing their best to manage the inevitable
   struggles around exclusivity that come with this. A grade-wide
   party/dance/celebration would be an inclusive version of something we know the kids
   are loving and really bring the kids together as they prepare for high school. There is
