                                           MINUTES
                                        December 2, 2025


In attendance: Joyce Chapnick (MSS Principal), Heather Shaughnessy (MSS Asst
Principal), Katherine Cain (PTSA MSS VP), Amy Suehnholz (PTSA MSS VP),
Lauren D’Amico (PTSA Grade Parent - 4th)

PAC Submission: Aside from the requirement that students must wear long pants and a coat
for recess if the temperature is below 32 degrees, on a cool (but not freezing) day, is there any
attempt to encourage students to wear the coat / clothing they came to school in?
Response: Yes, students must go to recess in the clothes that they came to school in. Students
must put on at least one additional layer to go outside when it is cold; however, once they are
outside playing, some students take off a layer if they get warm (although this is discouraged).
