                        Irvington Main Street School PAC Meeting

                          Lurye, Amy Suehnholz, Sarah Suehnholz

Question 1: Please provide more information on when and why digital tools are chosen over
analog tools. How do you consider trade-offs?

Response: The district has provided its “K-5 Screen Time Guidelines” which can be found here:
https://drive.google.com/file/d/1dF9pEkKe-vHZy5lNJOTeVYC11mZUHY3v/view

This document outlines the guiding principles that have been implemented at MSS.

Mr. Scott Wynne, Assistant Superintendent, has also provided answers to several technology
questions (“FAQs”), which can be found in Parent Square.

Here at MSS, we are committed to the principles the district has outlined and are mindful of
using technology only when necessary and to enhance learning. Our ELA and math curriculums
do not regularly incorporate technology (except when practicing for the NYS tests), and we do
not allow ChromeBooks to be used during “free time” or when a class has a substitute.


Question 2: I was disappointed to hear families would receive fewer report cards this year. How
are teachers planning to give feedback as to whether or not children are meeting standards,
especially since the number of parent teacher conferences remains the same?

Response: The district has moved to earlier parent-teacher conferences and two report cards
during the year. This was done after much consideration because prior feedback from teachers
and parents was that receiving a student’s report card at the same time as the conference made
the report card less impactful. We are moving to a schedule that allows feedback from the
conference and report cards to come at different times.

However, students receive feedback on their progress continuously throughout the year. The
main way this is done is through assessments from teachers, which parents are provided.
Additionally, starting next year, the district will be moving to a new universal screening system,
known as Renaissance STAR. As part of that transition, families will now be provided with their
students’ universal assessments three times a year through the Infinite Campus portal. This will
be an additional way for parents to track their student’s progress. Finally, if a student is not
meeting NYS benchmarks for their grade level, parents will be informed of that and begin
receiving assistance through the MTSS (multi-tiered system of supports) that is in place. We
encourage parents to always reach out to their classroom teacher with any questions or if you
would like more information on your student.
