                      Irvington Main Street School PAC Meeting
                                          MINUTES
                                         April 9, 2025


In attendance: Joyce Chapnick (MSS Principal), Heidi Rhodes (PTSA MSS VP), Katherine
Cain (PTSA MSS VP), Heather Goodman (PTSA Grade Parent - 5th), Emily Besidski (PTSA
Grade Parent - 4th)

PAC Submissions

   1.​ Recess and Lunch Aides

      Submission: I’m concerned about what I’ve heard about some of the school aides during
      recess and lunch. I have heard students complain that some of them often yell and blow
      their whistles loudly by students’ ears. Perhaps they need some more training on
      developmentally appropriate behaviors in children and better ways to interact and
      manage the students behavior at this age.

      Response: Thank you for sharing your concern. At Main Street School, the staff is
      committed to fostering a safe and supportive environment for all students during lunch
      and recess. There is a team of dedicated lunch aides and recess monitors who work
      closely with administration and members of the SEL team to ensure positive and
      developmentally appropriate interactions.

      Ms. Chapnick provides ongoing support and guidance to both lunch aides and recess
      monitors. This includes monthly professional learning sessions focused on child
      development, behavior management and fostering respectful communication. In addition,
      Ms. Shaughnessy and members of the SEL team are]regularly present during lunch and
      recess to offer coaching and to help address any concerns that may arise.

      In response to recent feedback, Ms. Chapnick has already addressed the specific
      concerns related to the use of whistles and is continuing to monitor the situation
      carefully. She is also in the process of hiring an additional recess monitor and a cafeteria
      monitor to further strengthen student supervision and support.

      If you or someone in the community is interested in joining the MSS team, you are
      encouraged to apply.



   2.​ Intramural Sports Teams

      Submission: I want to bring up an issue with the intramural sports teams. I think it is a
      great program and my child was excited to play. But the teams being mixed levels are
      deterring them from feeling comfortable participating. Some of the players are very
      strong players and don’t pass the ball to the weaker players and there isn’t a coach
      available to guide them to be more inclusive. My child wanted to join to play for fun and it
hasn’t been so fun for them. THey may not want to sign up next year which is
unfortunate. Perhaps there can be a different way for it to be organized such as 4th
graders and 5th graders playing on separate teams and playing others in the same
grade or an option to join an advanced level team or a beginner level team and having
those levels playing other teams on the same level.

Response: Ms. Chapnick appreciates the thoughtful feedback about the intramural
sports program. She and Ms. Shaw are so glad to hear students are excited to
participate, and they truly value insight on how they can make the experience more
enjoyable and inclusive for all students.

Ms. Chapnick has met with Ms. Shaw to discuss ways to better support students during
intramurals. As a next step, a student self-assessment survey will be developed and
used during the sign-up process. This will help students reflect on their familiarity and
comfort level with the sport, and it will allow Ms. Shaw to form more balanced groups.
The goal is to ensure that all participants, regardless of experience or ability, feel
comfortable, included and supported.

They appreciate your suggestion and will continue to explore ways to strengthen the
program to make intramurals a positive experience for every student.
