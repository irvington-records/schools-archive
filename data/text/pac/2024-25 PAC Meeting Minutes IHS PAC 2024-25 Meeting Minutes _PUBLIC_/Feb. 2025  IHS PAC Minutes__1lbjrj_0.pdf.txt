   PAC Submissions:

   1.​ Submission: ​
   A parent asks:​
               Would it be possible for the school library to open as a study space during exam periods?
               Has IHS ever considered this?


   ​
   2. Response:​
   The IHS library has been open for study during midterms and finals [ed.: according to Librarian
   Lovetta James, from 7:50-4:15] with the Academic Commons within the library designated for group
   study.

   There is also an increased number of bus pickup and drop offs that students can take advantage of to
   support their study. However, they should make sure to check the busing schedule during exam
   periods. Likewise, students should take special care to make it out to the bus pickup points because
   the normal dismissal bells are not used during testing periods. Thank you.
