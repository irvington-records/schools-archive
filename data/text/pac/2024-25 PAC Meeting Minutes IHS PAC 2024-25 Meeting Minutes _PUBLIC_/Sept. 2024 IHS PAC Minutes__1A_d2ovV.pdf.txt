                              Irvington High School PAC Meeting
                                               MINUTES
                                             Sept. 20, 2024


In attendance: Jon Hirsch (IHS Principal)
(via video teleconference): Sara Rust (IHS Assistant Principal), Joanna Anemodouras (1st
Year PTSA VP to IHS), Jorge Magallón (2nd Year PTSA VP to IHS), Amy Friedman (PTSA
Grade Parent - 9th), Gayle Katzman (PTSA Grade Parent - 11th), Maggie Kadro (PTSA Grade
Parent - 12th)


   PAC Submissions:

   1. There is a request to revive the IHS master schedule discussion. There is a contention that it was
   sidelined a few years ago. Also, it is stated that it seems clear that students “need to feed their
   growing minds and bodies as well to take a mental break”. The question is raised as to whether or
   not a system can be developed where students are not required to give up lunch in order to make
   their schedule otherwise work. [edit:This assumes the current system doesn’t allow for this].
   Response:
   The administration stated that this is a great and timely submission. The administration mentioned
   that some students (and their families) are prioritizing courses over a lunch period. The current master
   schedule allows for that.
   The administrators agree with the author that the master schedule should be revisited. One
   suggestion made by the administration is to consider mandating a lunch period. Still, the
   administration will approach the discussion about the master schedule with a willingness to consider
   the concerns of all stakeholders.
   The administrators note another issue that has become apparent. In addition to students missing
   much-needed lunch and other breaks, is that there may be too many transitions in some students’
   schedules. In any case, the administration notes that the current master schedule may be showing its
   limitations.
   2. There is a need for better traffic coordination in the morning because of backups.

   Response:

   There is a Bulldog Blast addressing the topic of traffic coordination likely to be published as early as
   Sept. 20. The administrators point out that there are many factors beyond their control. However,
   they do have suggestions to alleviate the congestion around the school.

   Many of these suggestions rely on the cooperation of parents/caregivers. They suggest that the more
   students who arrive via bus, the better. Also, the administration suggests drivers give themselves as
   little as five extra minutes for this task. Next, the administration asks drivers who are dropping off to
   more closely follow directions of the traffic monitors to maximize efficient dropoff.

   The administration notes that they have a limited amount of staff to assist in traffic duties. They
   mentioned that they have worked with the Village to adjust traffic light patterns to help with traffic
   congestion around the school. Likewise, they occasionally seek assistance from the Village Police for
some traffic concerns.

3. It is alleged the Infinite Campus app as used by IMS updates a student’s score in a more helpful
manner (e.g., 2 out of 3) compared to IHS (e.g., 2 with no context as to the possible score). Can IHS
address this?

Response:

The administration acknowledges that there have been some implementation issues. They will look
into this request and in how to, otherwise, improve this platform.
