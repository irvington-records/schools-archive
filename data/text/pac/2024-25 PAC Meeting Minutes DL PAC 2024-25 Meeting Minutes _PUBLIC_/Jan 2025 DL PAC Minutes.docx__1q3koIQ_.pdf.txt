                              Irvington Dows Lane PAC Meeting
                                              MINUTES
                                           January 15, 2024


In attendance: Dr. Kantor (DL Principal), Megan Hanson (DL VP) Amy Suehnholz (PTSA DL
VP), Melissa Kearns Lurye (PTSA DL VP) Alison Dollinger (PTSA Grade Parent – 2nd), and
Emma Gardner (PTSA Grade Parent – 1st), Randi Bleichfeld-Sirota (PTSA Grade Parent – K)

PAC Submissions

   ●​ Outdoor Recess:
      Despite overwhelming evidence that outdoor time is important for children, outdoor recess is
      canceled regularly even for what many would consider mild weather conditions here in NY. There
      is no reason why kids can't bundle up slightly to go outside and get outdoor recess. We should
      look to improve the number of days when our kids are actually permitted outdoor recess

      RESPONSE: Dows Lane’s goal is to get the kids outside everyday. Of the 76 days of school
      we’ve had this year, 6 days have been spent inside. Dr. Kantor consults with the nurse
      Bernadette before making the indoor recess call. Factors such as temperature and safety are
      considered. Finally, when weather conditions do not allow for outdoor play, "fresh air walks" to the
      tiny house are implemented.


   ●​ OUTDOOR SPACE:
      In a district that pays this many taxes (65k per student!!!), why do ANY of our playgrounds look
      like this? Basketball is arguably the most popular sport in this community. Why not re-pave the
      courts and make them nice again? We re-paved our entire parking lot and path leading to it, yet
      left it to rot? On this note, why did we spend money to replace the playground gyms with smaller,
      more juvenile ones? Our class sizes are increasing, so why replace with gyms that host even less
      children? Parents have been asking about the basketball courts for years and never receive a
      valid answer. I urge our stake-holders to look at the courts at Springhurst, their immaculate turf
      field, their huge climbing gym... why can't Irvington have these kinds of facilities? Please don't say
      cost, as our school taxes are more than theirs.

      RESPONSE:
      The current basketball courts were put in when the school was K-5, not set up for K-3. They are
      out of compliance for this age group and are quite far from the main recess area. If they were to
      be re-paved/brought back up into compliance the distance from the main play area would cause
      other safety concerns. The best recommendation is to contact the school board and ask about
      re-paving and bringing them up to compliance. In the interim, the PTSA and school will look into
      funding/possibilities to install a free standing basketball pole in the existing playground area that
      they could at least shoot at / play PIG, etc

      Since 2021 there has been a site committee composed of parents and school employees that has
      provided the administration with input about the selection of the new playground equipment. The
      school’s observation is the new structures have not yet seemed overwhelmed or overcrowded as
      many students are off playing other things such as soccer, gaga, swings. There is room to add on
      structures in the future, if you would like to spearhead this collaboration, please contact the PTSA
      or Dr. Kantor.
