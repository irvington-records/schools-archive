PAC Submissions

●       Assistant Principal: Given Ms. Wishney exited as Assistant Principal in June 2022 and Mr.
Chickery is now set to exit in November 2024, how is the District/Dow's Leadership assessing the root
issues that led to their short tenures? What adjustments will be made to defining the scope of the AP role
and/or the hiring process to best set Dow's up for retaining an AP for a longer tenure?
RESPONSE: The Assistant principal position is generally a stepping stone, and has historically
been so in this school. Most professionals who enter this position are highly motivated to progress
their careers forward. We are happy to be a school who attracts this type of talent, and are excited
to have such a qualified educator fulfilling the position that Mr. Chickery left vacant. We wish him
the best of luck in his new position.


●        Student Privacy: Does the school district provide our child’s name, date of birth, address, and
any other demographic information with the chrome book apps necessary to perform school assignments
and class lessons and/or testing services, etc…l? If so, can we please negotiate with the tech companies
and services so that infringing on our children’s privacy is not a part of obtaining these teaching and
testing tools?
RESPONSE:
Jerrod Blair, our Director of Technology and Innovation, provided the response below:
The New York State Ed Law 2d requires that any vendor that requires student or parent information for the use of their
platforms sign off on privacy documentation. Our privacy documentation is locally developed and Board of Education
Policy. The documentation (both a contract and Parent Bill of Rights) ensures the protection of student and
parent/guardian demographics to the extent possible by Federal, State, and Local policy. Attached is a fact sheet that is
distributed by the New York State Education Department and outlines the statute that we are bound to.

If there are any questions or concerns, please refer the parent to
jerrod.blair@irvingtonschools.org. Dr. Kantor echoes this policy, and set up a “Coffee with
Principals” in February to further discuss these concerns and other technology related issues
with Mr. Blair directly. The PTSA will be sure to advertise the timing on social media and through
class parents so that this can stay on everyone’s radar.
Communication: Can we please finally get a school app? Brightwheel, Seesaw, Remini, etc. This
would greatly enhance communication as many of our young children do not share ANY details of their
day. Communication is SO limited with the teachers… feeling completely in the dark about my child’s
Dows Lane experience. Having an app to share notes and photos and videos, get quick updates,
assignments, mark absent, ask questions, etc. is done just about everywhere else and makes things so
much more manageable and streamlined for the many working parents who are juggling multiple kids.
RESPONSE: Teachers are required to provide a general update to parents once a month.
Dr. Kantor is flexible with how this is provided - whether it be paper sent home, an email
or over a private social media group. She is open to exploring the suggested apps, but as
per the note above, it must comply with Ed Law 2D.
