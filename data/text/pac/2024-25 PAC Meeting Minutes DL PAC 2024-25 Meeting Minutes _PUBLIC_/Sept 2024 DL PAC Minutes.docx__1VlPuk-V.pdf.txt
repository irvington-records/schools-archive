                                    Dows Lane PAC Meeting
                                            MINUTES
                                        September 11, 2024


In attendance: Dr. Kantor (DL Principal), Tom Chickery (DL Asst. Principal), Amy Suehnholz
(PTSA DL VP), Alison Lang (PTSA DL VP), Katie Bauer (PTSA Grade Parent – 3rd), Alison
Dollinger (PTSA Grade Parent – 2nd), and Emma Gardner (PTSA Grade Parent – 1st), Randi
Bleichfeld-Sirota (PTSA Grade Parent – K)

PAC Submissions

1. Teacher Assignments: At the end of 2nd and 3rd grade, my child’s teachers asked her to
   make a list of 3 students she’d like to be placed in class with the following year. Each time,
   not a single child from the list ended up in their class. Now it doesn't matter in the scheme of
   things, but why ask our children to make a list in the first place? Clearly the list isn’t followed.
   It seems almost mean to ask for the child’s input (as if it matters) only to ignore it. Can you
   explain this process and why it’s necessary?
   RESPONSE: Teacher assignments are based on a multitude of factors including but not
   limited to helping a child feel welcomed culturally and academically, unique educational
   requirements or IEP/504 plans, honoring requests for children not to be together, honoring
   requests for families not to have a teacher their other child already had, etc… Some
   teachers seek to empower the children to provide input as one factor of many. This does not
   guarantee their request.
2. Kindergarten Orientation: Suggestion: a Kindergarten orientation where students are able
   to meet their teachers/view their classroom and drop off supplies ahead of the school year

                            😅
   (like a 2-3 hr window during the week of classroom set ups) to minimize nervous feelings
   (students and parents       ) before the start of school. Having Kindergarten students bring
   heavy supplies in their backpacks (ie: baby wipes, liquid soap etc.) can be challenging.
   Maybe suggest the heavier supplies be brought in during Open House evening?
   RESPONSE: As a school, we strive to build independence for children and encourage
   students to bring 1 item in each day and/or follow the teacher’s specific directions. We never
   want a child to carry more than they can. In addition, moving forward parents are always
   able to bring large items such as tissue boxes and wipes to the Open House. Most teachers
   have specific requests for items to be brought on certain days, and parents are
   recommended to honor those instructions. We don’t have the space or resources to move
   school supplies for 510 students, and encourage families to send 1-2 items at a time to
   school with their child.
   Playground: I believe there was discussion of replacing the playground(s?) at Dows lane
   but I’m not sure where it stands. If parents are allowed to get involved with fundraising or
   designing, please let me know. I understand that not only is the equipment roughly 30 years
   old, but if the general school population has increased, the overall size of the playground
   may need to reflect that?
   RESPONSE: The new playground is scheduled to be installed and open in the end of
   October- early November.. A formal announcement will be issued by the district closer to
that time. The damaged tree on the north lot is scheduled to be cut down on the 21st and
fully removed off site on the 28th, weather permitting. Swings will be replaced in kind.
Please note that the ADA swing is on backorder, and will likely be replaced at a later time.
