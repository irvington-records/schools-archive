                             PAC Submissions
Question: Has Dows Lane ever considered using an app for changes to
school dismissal rather than writing these requests on paper. Other districts
use apps such as School Dismissal manager
Answer : Yes we have considered this but it won’t eliminate the need for
paper. If we used an app, we would need a staff member to print the
change requests to hand to the teachers. We don’t have the staff for that at
this time.

Similar Q&A from November 2025 PAC:
Snacks: (PAC received approximately 12 submissions from 12 different parents in regards to snack
quality and wrote feedback that students (a lot in K) are procuring snacks with NO money on their
accounts): For example, I’ve put $0 dollars on my daughter’s lunch account and have asked Gloria
Estrada to put a restriction on preventing my daughter from getting any à la carte items. My daughter is
still getting chips and ice cream. How can I actually prevent her from getting à la carte
items?

Answer: The district and Aramark are not giving food to children who have restrictions. Per Andrea and
Meg’s investigation these were kindergarten students who were taking food. Communication with families
and reminding students, that they can only take food that their parents approve and snacks that are paid
for. The problem has been found and addressed. The overall experience of the cafeteria won't change
unless we make different decisions from the District and Wellness Committee. The food contract is not
school based, but district facilitated.

information.

Question: How much time does a typical kid in Kindergarten spend on screens in their
classrooms?
Answer: DL is a hands-on environment primarily, teaching through books and papers,
activities. But technology and screens are a part of young learners' lives. They will have
to know technology. Each teacher uses their discretion to ensure the balance is
appropriate. There is no “downtime” where videos are being shown but videos may be
used for movement breaks (GoNoodle, Yoga movement, songs, dance). If a parent has
a more specific questions, they should speak with their student's teacher.
