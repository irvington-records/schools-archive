PAC Submissions
SCHOOL POLICIES: What are the policies and procedures at Dows Lane for
addressing antisemitism and hate speech in school?
Answer : DL school policies are BOE District policies. Discipline and the code of
conduct is taken very seriously, we have young students, and not everything is a
consequence many are teachable moments. The school will send around their code of
conduct to families and how the school communicates. Dr Kantor will talk to Mara and
Scott for more guidance. The town has a new antisemitism task force. Here is the link to
the Irvington School Code of Conduct – the school is a resource to help and to provide
communication, any additional concerns should go to the school directly.
BEFORE CARE/AFTER SCHOOL: Many of our families in the Dows community need
before and after school care. I have lived in other Westchester schools that provide
both. Who can parents talk to about creating a BEFORE school program and weighing
in on a new AFTER SCHOOL program since the funding for Beyond the Bell has
expired?
Answer : The after school program is coordinated through the district and Scott Wynne.
DL had a grant, school paid for it this year, $100K and does not have the grant next
year. DL used to have Curious on Hudson, still have Chess but to run something new it
would need to be an organization who wants to run it. We do have someone who is
interested and is exploring the options. If a parent wants to spearhead they can make a
proposal on the website.
