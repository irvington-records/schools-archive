                                     ​PAC Submissions​

​QUESTION:​​Can Dows Lane parents be allowed to attend​​the Halloween Parade on​
 ​10/31 if the weather permits it to be held outside?​

​RESPONSE:​​Dows loves when families come, however certain​​times it's difficult as​
 ​safety is our number one priority. Having 1,000+ people at the parade is too many​
  ​people to safely manage. We agree to extend an invitation to our PTSA class parents​
   ​and PTSA committee chairs for this year.​

​QUESTION​: Will the after school program be returning​​for this school year? When will​
 ​the district be releasing information on that?​

​RESPONSE:​​(This response is directly from Scott Wynne):​​The afterschool program,​
 ​which opened for enrollment on 10/17/25 (​​link​) ),​​is organized by the District​
  ​administration, not the Dows Lane administration. The PTSA reached out to Scott​
   ​Wynne, the assistant superintendent of curriculum and instruction to share parents'​
    ​requests for the program details to be announced in September to allow families​
     ​adequate time to organize childcare and schedules. Mr. Wynne, who assumed his role​
      ​this summer, is in the process of reassessing the current after-school offerings and is​
       ​excited to explore new opportunities to provide more robust enrichment for students for​
        ​the next school year.​

 ​other Rivertown elementary schools, there is a feeling that our playground area is​
  ​significantly undersized. Do we have a plan to expand it? There’s also the issue of no​
   ​shade (tree loss) or barrier between the play set and the parking lot, which could raise​
    ​safety concerns.​

 ​side, there is no plan to change anything at the moment. We can add whatever we​
  ​want however there needs to be a plan and we need a budget. The previous site​
   ​committee completed the playground but this committee no longer exists following the​
    ​completion of the playground. Dows is happy to work with anyone who wants to take​
