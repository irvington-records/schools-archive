Question: PARKING: Is there any way to add an additional parking lot at DL? The
parking situation for families when they come to DL for vents is impossible and beyond
frustrating

Answer: Wonderful idea, all of Irvington schools have a lack of parking – this would
need to run through the building and grounds and Irvington town. The bond will be
redoing the front black top and the back basketball court, with pickleball courts, etc

Question: FIELD TRIPS: How does the school select field trips in general? How many
field trips usually happen over the year? Who makes the final decision on where
students go on their field trip? Is there a process for parents to make suggestions?

Answer: Field trips are always connected to the curriculum in some capacity and
usually booked the spring before the school year. All ideas are welcome and should go
to their child’s teacher. Additionally, they can be communicated to the grade level PTSA
representative, with Dr Kantor and Mrs Hanson, or the instructional leaders on the
grade level. Teachers are always open to hearing ideas.

Question: K MEET & GREET WITH TEACHER: Other school districts allow
kindergarteners (and other elementary grades) to see their classrooms and meet their
teachers before the first day of school. This would be a really nice way to help the kids
acclimate to next year’s classroom environment before school begins.

Answer: Classroom and teacher placement is finalized over the summer. Any additional
time for teachers to be at school would require them to go beyond their contract. All
kindergarten families are invited into school early in the school year to for breakfast in
the classroom in the fall and to meet their teacher. Additionally, Dows Lane hosts their
open house very early in the school year to meet the teachers and view the classroom.

Question: GYM CURRICULUM: Could you elaborate on how the gym curriculum is
developed and implemented? Specifically, how much time is allocated to skill
instruction versus allowing students to freely play the game (for example, basketball)?

Answer: Dows Lane follows NYS PE standards. Each gym class offers locomotive
movements (ex: how to slide), stretching, learning about muscle groups, a mini lesson,
drills/lead up games/actual games, and a closer. If a parent has more specific questions
they can reach out to Mrs Flynn or Mrs Stracuzzi.
