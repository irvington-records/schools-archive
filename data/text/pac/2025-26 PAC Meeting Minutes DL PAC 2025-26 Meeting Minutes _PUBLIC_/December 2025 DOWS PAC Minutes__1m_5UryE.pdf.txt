PARENT / TEACHER CONFERENCES: TIMING & SIGN UP PROCESS:

       ● Question - Parent Teacher conferences dates - the timing of these
       conferences are too late. If there are issues it will have gone on too long. It’s too
       close to December break for meaningful changes to be implemented and

       know the students before having the conferences. Teachers want to share
       learning data and social emotional growth. Any parent that wants to have a
       meeting with the teacher earlier or at any other time during the school year,
       should reach out to their teacher and meet with them. We will ensure teachers
       reiterate this message to parents at the open house. Additionally, Dr Kantor will
       discuss with Ms Chapnick.

LICE: (7 different parents submitted questions regarding the protocols of preventing lice
at Dows Lane, summarized concerns below):

      ● Who determines and manages the school lice policy? If parents wanted to
      discuss potential changes to it, who is the best person to direct these inquiries?

      ● I have been told from Irvington parents with older children that Dows Lane
      used to have 3rd party lice checks, could you please give background on why
      that was discontinued?

      ● Requesting a grade wide communication policy that notifies all parents as
      soon as a case is informed

      ●   What is the threshold for the school nurse to report lice to the parents?

      ● What is the Dows Lane lice protocol? Requesting this protocol be more
      public (was not run in the buzz or the lice alert handout)

      ● The CDC states “it is useful to share information with school nurses,
      teachers, parents of classmates and others about contact with head lice to limit
      spread,” where does Dow’s Lane lice policy stem from ?

      ● I understand that pursuant to the district’s doctor, that the superintendent
      does not recommend regular third party lice testing. However, having a third
      party perform lice checks will help clear up cases and raise awareness of the
      issue. Suggest allocating PTSA funds to this project, which will ultimately save
      many families time and money.

             ○ Answer to LICE question from December 2024 minutes: “Per the
             district’s medical director, it is a family’s responsibility to mitigate individual
            lice problems. The school communicates to parents about any sort of lice
            problem when three or more students report having it in a classroom. Feel
            free to purchase the preventative sprays on your child’s hair or book bag.
            Families are encouraged to reach out to Nurse Flemming if they detect
            lice at home. The school also seeks the advice of Dr. Huff, District Doctor,
            with Tribeca Pediatrics with regard to contagious illnesses.”

        Answer: Dows Lane consults with a medical consultant Dr Hough, the district
        doctor regarding lice. In addition the school works with the school nurse, Ms
        Fleming to communicate with parents. Since lice is not defined as a medical
        problem the school is not required to remediate lice. Dows Lane’s practice is
        to alert parents in a class where there are 3 or more cases. This
        communication comes home in students' back packs in a letter from Ms
        Fleming. All parents should inform the nurse if their child has lice. We will
        work on better highlighting this policy and how to best check for lice in the
        buzz and future PTSA communications. This information is also in the Buzz.
