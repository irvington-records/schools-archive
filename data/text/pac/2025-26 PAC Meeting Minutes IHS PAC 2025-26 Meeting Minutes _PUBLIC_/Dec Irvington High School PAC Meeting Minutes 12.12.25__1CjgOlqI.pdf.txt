Pac Submissions:
      1. I find it odd that we don’t have any school parties in our school. I heard a while back there was
        one and would like to see my son go to a school function. I realize that covid has done quite a
        bit of damage but why hasn’t Irvington tried to go back to that. I hear of other districts doing
        homecoming dances and a mid year party of sorts, we should have that too.

        Post Covid there was a movement to bring back dance but it was poorly attended. The
        teachers and IHS are not opposed to a school party or dance but feel it should be an
        initiative led by the students. They recommend starting with the student council to lead
        the initiative towards a school dance.

        2. First, if we have a 9th period that is allocated for extra help, why aren't teachers available for
        my son. His math teacher has only given him extra help time at the same as club. He should not
        have to choose.

        Club time does not conflict with 9th period because it’s 2:08-2:35pm. Next year they will
        build in extra help time that doesn't conflict with classes or clubs. Teachers give
        availability if they can’t do 9th period. If you still feel you cannot find time you can email
        the teacher, department chair, Jon Hirsch and Sara Rust.

        3. On regents week, when the kids don't have class to "study" why don't teachers hold review
        classes for the regents? I have only seen the science dept hold study/review classes. It appears
        the other teachers take the time off. The kids need help, many of them don't know how to study or
        prep for a whole years worth of material
Teachers during regents and exams are doing a tremendous amount of proctoring (for
example Bio has 9 testing rooms), grading and strategic planning. It is highly
recommended that teachers make time to be available but if you feel your student cannot
get time then parents should email the teachers directly.
