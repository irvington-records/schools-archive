Pac Submissions:
      1. I’m reaching out with an idea I had given the current issues with SNAP benefits. My
      child shared that if he purchases lunch and it falls under the free lunch category, there is
      a bin to put the fruit provided if the student does not eat it. I’m unsure if the bin items are
      held to resell, but had a thought that if several children are doing this and/or not taking
      advantage of the free lunch, is there a way Irvington schools could help with donations of
      the food not being used? As a social worker/therapist who works with clients who at
      times face food insecurity, I often like to think of ways our community could help,
      especially in a time like this with the government shutdown/decrease in SNAP benefits.
      This would be great for students to bring up to the student council to start a
      student group or a community service club to use extra food for families in need.
      Unfortunately, at this time the school does not have the resources to do this.

       2. In Previous PAC notes, I saw that there was an idea to put either a suggestion box or

       A few ways you can do this is:
           1.​ If they do not feel comfortable enough to go to their counselor they can go

 3. The HS has a program that tracks community service and they "advertise" you can get credit
for the service, however, they don't show the credit until the final transcript even if you have
earned the hours beforehand. This is not helpful if one of the points is to show colleges how much
community service you have. I don't really see the point of giving the credit at all at the end. I
encourage this program to be reexamined to better serve the interests of the students and
promoting community service hours.
For seniors the community service goes on the transcript at mid terms. The HS will look
into this for the future.

4. We should have a program or an after school club that helps students prepare for college
applications. Most of our community spends thousands of dollars on a college counselor ( I do
not think our counselor is really helpful with that) but really a lot of that should be done in school.
Is there something that can be done for the coming grades?
The school counselors do a tremendous amount of work to help the students with college
admissions. They are available for one on one and there is a town hall for juniors to learn
how to do essays and they can do one on one after this as well through Kenyon college.
Over the summer there is also help to kids that want additional help with a bootcamp on
the application process.
