(1)​ Question about adjusting the blocks on school computers where teachers used to rely on
   students’ phones.
 •​ Answer from Mr. Hirsch: As the issues come up, the school will address them. For
   example:
   •​ Meeting with student council to create an approved student-created playlist
      students can use, since music streaming on phones is no longer available during
      the school day.
   •​ Forensics class tools blocked on chrome books — teachers will be proactive to get
      needed sites pre-approved.
   •​ Youtube videos needed for class can be embedded in Google Classroom where
      student privacy is protected, and won’t be blocked that way.

(2)​ Schedule Changes Update:
  •​ Mr. Hirsch reviewed the purpose of the schedule change is to take teachers and
    students off the “hamster wheel” of the short class periods where students pack so
    much into their day. The schedule change is intended to allow for innovation, more
    meaningful and sustainable lives.
 •​ Mr. Hirsch discussed the due diligence schedule, school visits are starting in
    September to investigate different models in surrounding schools.
 •​ Parent and student Q&As are planned and will be broken down by graduation year.
 •​ Other schools have seen no negative impact with respect to college admissions.
 •​ School visits are comprised of a selection of members of the stakeholder groups:
    Schedule committee; students; teachers; BOE members.
