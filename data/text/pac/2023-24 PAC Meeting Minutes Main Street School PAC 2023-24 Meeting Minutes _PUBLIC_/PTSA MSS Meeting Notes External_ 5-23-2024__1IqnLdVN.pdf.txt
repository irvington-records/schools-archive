                       Irvington Main Street School PAC Meeting
                                             May 2024


Q1. I understand from my child that tag was recently banned at recess time.
Tag is a fun game and a great source of exercise for the children. Kids
should be encouraged to run around outside. If tag was being played in an
inappropriate manner, that should be addressed without banning the game
completely. Please let the children play tag again.

A1: We encourage kids to run around and play outside. There are alot of
opportunities to play games, but some games are limited at times because of
space and safety concerns.

Q2. As technology is becoming more widespread among MSS kids, I am
curious to learn in what ways the school is helping kids consider the import of
translating the values of MSS (global citizen, kindness) to online behavior. As a
parent of a 5th grader, I am troubled by various large group text threads that
exist (i.e. a full grade thread, individual classroom threads, all dominated by a
smaller subset of voices - but filled with inappropriate speech and at times,
bullying behaviors). We are speaking a lot about this at home, and wonder what
role a school can play in helping to educate and support kids as well.


A2: MSS reviews digital citizenship in library class and with push in lessons with
school counselors. Over the summer, MSS educators will be developing a series of lessons focused on
executive functioning skills. A component of these lessons will address the management of technology
and responsible use. In addition, there will be learning opportunities for parents on related topics. We
appreciate the partnership of parents here to support appropriate use of technology.


Q3. Can letters PLEASE be sent home when a child in a class has lice? Lice
has been rampant in the fourth grade this year and some families have had
to deal with it 2 or 3 times already. Once they do, they then learn only from word
of mouth that several other kids in the class had it recently. If parents aren’t
aware that their child might have been exposed, they do not know to get their
child checked and then it just keeps spreading. I understand there is a note at
the bottom of the Scoop but it’s much more helpful to know if it’s going around an
individual class. I also get that the classroom isn’t the only place where a student
is being exposed to it, but it’s at least a start.

A3: Communication about cases of lice and/or illnesses is made based on the specific situation, with
student/family privacy as a consideration. Please reach out to Nurse Diamond if you have questions
and she can help you. As mentioned above, information is included in Scoop.
