                                         February 2024

Q: Can Rice Krispies Treats and other sugary items be removed from the cafeteria?



Carol Stein heads up the Wellness Committee which discusses and helps to decide beverage, lunch and
snack choices in the cafeteria. Any parent is welcome to join this committee or find out more by emailing
Carol Stein, carol.stein@irvingtonschools.org.
