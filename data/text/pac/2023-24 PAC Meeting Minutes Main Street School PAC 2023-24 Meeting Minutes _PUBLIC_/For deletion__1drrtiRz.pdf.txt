                    Irvington Main Street School PAC Meeting
                                April 2024

1. PAC Submission: Regarding the question about if early band could be scheduled on a different
   day than the NY State testing:
   A parent can choose not to send his or her child to early band. There are several conflicts that
   require schedule adjustments and it is important to bring all of the ensemble together.
   Therefore, early band will sometimes be scheduled on the same day as the NY State test.
