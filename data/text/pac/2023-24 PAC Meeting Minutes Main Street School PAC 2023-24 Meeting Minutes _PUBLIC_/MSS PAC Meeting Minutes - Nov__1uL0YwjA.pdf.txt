                         Irvington Main Street School PAC Meeting
                                     November 13, 2023

Q1. Can you please explain the school’s understanding of what the ‘science of reading’ is and
how it comes up against the district’s current curriculum of Readers and Writers workshops?
With the rise of the ‘science of reading’ nationwide and more and more districts across states
leaving behind the Lucy Caulkins reading approaches, what direct steps is Main Street school
taking to implement more explicit teaching of reading and writing as my son will be there next
year and daughters shortly thereafter. Specifically for gen edu students. I realize reading
accommodations are made for children’s whose IEP ‘s mandate it, but what steps are being taken
to follow the science as it relates to updating how our children are taught to read and write across
the board?

A1. Explicit instruction in reading and writing is aligned to NYS standards for literacy. This is a great
conversation to have with the Asst. Superintendent of Curriculum.



Q2. Many parents would like to know if children will be allowed to play in the snow during recess
this year or only allowed on the blacktop again?

A2. Safety issue at MSS for kids to play in the snow as the floors get very wet and slippery.



Q3. Can the Moving Up Ceremony be moved up to June 21 due to the timing of Sleepaway Camp?

A3. June 25th is an instructional day of school, so there are no plans to move the ceremony. This
request has already been discussed with the Superintendent as several parents inquired about moving
the date of the Moving Up Ceremony.
