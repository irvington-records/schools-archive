2. Why does Earth Science not receive the Honors designation when taken in 8th grade? As an
accelerated class, taken 2 years ahead of when high schooler take it and it being offered as an
Honors Class at the high school, 8th graders should also be afforded the Honors designation
since the grade goes on their High School transcript and is calculated into their overall high
school GPA.

 Response: Some happen in classes or electives in highschool. There are no honors programs
in Middle School. We offer accelerated programs as standard in NY State. Most schools give
accelerated classes and you get to experience regents classes in highschool. Some additional
accelerated offered in highschool are Math, Science, Visual Arts and Language.
