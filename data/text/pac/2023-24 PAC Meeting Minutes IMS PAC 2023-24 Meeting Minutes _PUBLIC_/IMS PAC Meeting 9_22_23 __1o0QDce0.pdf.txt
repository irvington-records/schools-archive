                           Irvington Middle School Pac Meeting
                                         Minutes
                                    September 22, 2023

In Attendance: David Sottile (IMS Principal), Alysson Daley (IMS Asst. Principal) Joanna
Anemodouras (PTSA IMS VP), Mara Gredick (PTSA IMS VP), Janet Kovacs (PTSA Grade
Parent-7th), Amy Friedman(PTSA Grade Parent-6th), Amy Friedman(PTSA Grade Parent- 8th)

Pac Submission:

   ● I'm a parent to a 7th grader (and a psychologist in the community). It has
     become evident to me, through recent conversations with IMS guidance,
     teachers, and building leaders, that cell phone use at IMS is having negative
     impacts on our kids' healthy social functioning and constructive learning. It's
     also been made clear that the IMS policy prohibiting phone use during the
     school day is not something the school can enforce alone. Given the
     relationship between adolescent cell phone use and increased distraction,
     diminished student achievement and self-esteem, other school districts opted
     to implement policies of locking phones in pouches or collecting phones at the
     start of the school day.
Response:
         ○ Phones are currently not being seen in the hallways and teachers have
             eliminated the opportunity for phones and IMS has seen a lot of progress
             in the lack of cell phone usage. There is a strict code of conduct in place
             for if a student is caught on a phone:
         ○ 1. First offense is a parent letter home and the child’s name is added onto a
             spreadsheet.
         ○ 2. If the student is caught again the phone is confiscated and the parent must
             collect the phone from the main office and a letter is sent home.
         ○ 3. If the student is caught a 3rd time then the phone stays in the main office and
             the parent needs to collect the phone and meet with the Principal and Assistant
             Principal. The parent does not need to come immediately when they are free.
         ○ David and Allyson ask that you help partner with the school and let kids know of
             these rules and the importance of turning the phones on airplane mode or silent
             during school hours. PLease help reinforce the habits of not using phones during
             school time.
