                                 Irvington Middle School Pac Meeting
                                               Minutes
                                          November 17, 2023

In Attendance: David Sottile (IMS Principal), Alysson Daley (IMS Asst. Principal) Joanna
Anemodouras (PTSA IMS VP), Mara Gredick (PTSA IMS VP), Janet Kovacs (PTSA Grade
Parent-7th), Amy Friedman(PTSA Grade Parent-6th), Amy Friedman(PTSA Grade Parent- 8th)

Pac Submission:

It is my understanding —after hearing this from students on more than one occasion over the past month
— that this year’s IMS school play “Beauty and the Beast” has “gender specific roles.” As in only certain
genders can audition for certain roles, and though not all roles are gender spe it cific, some, like the two
leads, are “gender specific.”

If this is true (and I really hope it isn’t), it is not only shockingly insensitive, exclusionary, discriminatory
and incompatible with DEI, it has probably discouraged some children from auditioning. Moreover,
regardless of a student’s interest in the play, just hearing remarks/guidelines about “gender specific” roles
regarding the production reinforce harmful stereotypes that we as a society are working hard to retire.

It would be important for IUFSD administrators to investigate this claim as soon as possible so that all
students feel comfortable auditioning for any role in any play regardless of gender.

As it stands, the damage may already be done.

Response:

Any student can audition for any role. The children chose this particular play after being
given a list of several different plays.

The only guidelines that are given with trademarked material is that roles must be
portrayed as male or female when you purchase the rights; however, anyone can audition
for either role. You are not allowed to change the lyrics or dialogue but any person can
raise their hand for a gender specific role.
