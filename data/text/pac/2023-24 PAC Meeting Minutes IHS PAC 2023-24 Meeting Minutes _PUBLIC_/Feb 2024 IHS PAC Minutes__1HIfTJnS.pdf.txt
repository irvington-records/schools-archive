      ● IHS Profile - Inaccuracies a Disservice to College Bound Students: A parent asks that
          the Profile, which is apparently sent to colleges, be reviewed and corrected. The parent

      ● Yearbook - Where Do Ad Fees Go?: The parent suggests that a statement indicating the
         beneficiaries of advertising fees accompany advertising solicitations. The parent
         specifically asked if the fees defray the cost of the yearbook, if the yearbook company
         makes any donations and to whom do they go.
      Response: Yearbook advertising fees go solely to cover yearbook costs. These fees defray
            some of the costs in buying yearbooks. The fees do not generate a profit. The
         administrators did mention that students pre-order yearbooks and the school purchases
         extra copies to have on hand for purchase for eventualities.

● Pre-Calc Honors vs. AP:
   A parent asks re Fall 2024:
   is a student in Pre-Calc Honors prepared for the AP Pre-Calculus Exam? In other words,
   though Pre-Calc Honors is ‘not an official AP class’, can the student, nonetheless, take
   the AP exam? The parent alleges that they reached out to the Math Department with this
   question, but has yet to receive a response.


   Response: Students may sit for the exam. However, the Honors classes are not
   necessarily tailored to prepare students for the AP Exam. A student or caregiver can
   nonetheless have a conversation with an individual perspective instructor to find out if
   that specific Honors class is designed to prepare students for the AP-Calculus Exam.
   A student could find an Honors instructor who is amenable to guiding them to also
   preparing them for the AP Exam. Finally, the Honors student could even prepare for the
   AP Exam on their own.


   In cases where the student thinks they will be taking the AP-Calculus Exam, but are not
   in the AP Class, special arrangements for test taking may have to be made ahead of time
   and should be discussed with the student’s guidance counselor.
