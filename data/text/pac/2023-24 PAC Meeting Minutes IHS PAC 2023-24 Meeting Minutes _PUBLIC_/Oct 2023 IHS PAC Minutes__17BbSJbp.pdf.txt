                           Irvington High School PAC Meeting
                                          MINUTES
                                       October 12, 2023


In attendance (via video teleconference): Jon Hirsch (IHS Principal), Jorge Magllón (PTSA
IHS VP), Mavis Edme (PTSA Grade Parent - 9th), Tricia Kim (PTSA Grade Parent - 10th),
Maggie Kadro (PTSA Grade Parent - 11th), Kerri Warner (PTSA Grade Parent - 12th)

PAC Submissions

1. Chromebooks - Enforcement of Requirement to Use: A senior parent alleges their child’s
   chromebook is substandard. The parent also claims that the Class of 2024 has not been
   required to use chromebooks (presumably school administered devices) since the 8th
   grade. Apparently, now some staff are requiring the use of chromebooks (presumably to the
   exclusion of a student's private device) in school. The claim is made that, as a result,
   students who are not accustomed to using chromebooks, and now are required to use them,
   are at an unfair disadvantage. As a result, the request is made that IHS review and amend
   its policy to allow seniors to BYOD (Bring Your Own Devices).
   RESPONSE:
        ● The principal began by stating they understand the parent’s frustration on this
          point. He confirmed that the parent is right in saying that the “BYOD” (bring your
          own device) policy would allow students to bring private computers that could be
          better than the devices provided by the district. He also confirmed that the parent
          is right in saying that enforcement of the policy has changed. The board policy was
          put in place before the current administration was at IHS.

              He went on to explain that the current policy requires students, as a general rule,
              to use the school provided devices. The exception to this rule is the current 1
              year accommodation for some classes that necessitate the greater computing
              power of some students’ private devices (e.g., A.P. Art - emphasizing graphic
              design). He stated, however, by next year the school provided devices will be
              able to meet the needs of all classes. Therefore, this 1 year accommodation will
              no longer be needed.

              He states that when he began here last year, he decided to enforce the school’s
              policy that it was not a “BYOD” campus. The reason for this is the equity
              concerns he has regarding a “BYOD” policy. He also added that there was
              inconsistency in some staff enforcing the policy on the books and others not.
              Staff is now enforcing it more consistently. Therefore, though he found the
              parent’s concerns valid, they were not compelling enough to change the policy.
