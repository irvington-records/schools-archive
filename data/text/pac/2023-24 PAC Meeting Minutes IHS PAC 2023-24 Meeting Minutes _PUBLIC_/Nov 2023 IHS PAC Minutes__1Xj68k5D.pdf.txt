PAC Submissions

1. PAC Submissions
           Early Decision University Applications & Student Workload: A senior parent asks the
           administration to consider recommending to staff a lighter student workload around the time
           of early application deadlines for universities. The parent suggests with relatively little effort
           on the part of staff, students would be less overwhelmed and could instead focus on these
           early decision applications.


   RESPONSE:
     ● The administration replied that this is a difficult balance of being mindful of the student stress
           level and getting the school business done. It is not a simple solution to lighten the course
           load considering the students have weeks and months before to do the applications. Every
           interruption to the class (holidays, no homework, days off) slows the pace of the course, and
           the teachers feel the tension. The staff acknowledges the deadlines - for example, November
           1 is a big deadline for early admission - so no homework is given on Oct 31. He will discuss
           at the staff/counselor meeting to see what other accommodations can be made.
