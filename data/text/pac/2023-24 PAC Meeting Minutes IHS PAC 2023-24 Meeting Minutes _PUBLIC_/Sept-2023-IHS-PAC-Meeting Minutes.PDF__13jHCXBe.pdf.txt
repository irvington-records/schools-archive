 Asst. Principal), Jorge Magallón (PTSA IHS VP), Tricia Kim (PTSA Grade Parent -
                   10th), Maggie Kadro (PTSA Grade Parent - 11th)

                         In attendance (on phone):
 Mavis Edme (PTSA Grade Parent - 9th), Kerri Warner (PTSA Grade Parent - 12th)



                                                  MINUTES


PAC Submissions
    1.   Arrival, traffic and traffic signals: There is a concern that traffic at dropoff has been particularly
         acute, with a claim that it may be resulting from the traffic light at bottom of the hill. Has the issue
         been addressed by the school/Village?
RESPONSE: Carol Stein, Asst. Superintendent for Business & Operations, requested a traffic light
review to the Village of Irvington. Additional requests have been made to the police department for
assistance during dismissal of special events (i.e., IMS and IHS Open Houses). Jon Hirsch, the principal,
also noted that morning traffic congestion has noticeably decreased since earlier in the school year.


    2. Prompt Notice of Summer Assignments: There is a request that the guidance office inform
       students ASAP of end of year class placements that require summer assignments. This request
       seeks to reduce student stress. There is also a suggestion that if a student is placed by the end of
       the school year, then that student should receive notice of classes with summer assignments.
       This request is based on the presumption that the majority of students would be placed by year’s
       end.
RESPONSE: There were three notices that went out regarding class placements that will require summer
assignments. The first was posted on the website at the end of the school year in June. The second was
an email in early August. The third was an email sent out two weeks before the start of school. Jon
Hirsch will look into the viability of informing the students when they are placed by the end of the school
year, regardless if all students have been placed.
The administration will also address the special cases of students who are late to register to a class that
has summer requirements (i.e., students transferring in the summer). Mr. Hirsch added that he will
communicate the importance of these notices to his faculty in his weekly Faculty Blast.


    3. Posting of Homework: A request is made that all assignments be made on Google Classroom.
       The claim is made that all assignments are not being posted to Google Classroom. As a result,
        students, apparently, have to search for their assignments and this extra level of complexity is
        resulting in missing assignments.
RESPONSE: Mr. Hirsch will reiterate to the faculty on his weekly Faculty Blast that assignments are to be
posted to Google Classroom. He also suggested that any parent can reach out to the teacher directly if
there is an issue.
